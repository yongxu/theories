# Working on the theory of becoming

## What we are doing

We are drawing a map of how anything forms, persists, changes and ends, so that humans and AI can see the way together.

**The idea, as now understood** (AI-written and held loosely; it is said at length, and changed as the work teaches, on [the direction page](_process/DIRECTION.md)). Whatever lasts is made again and again out of other things that are going on too. What a going-on rests on and what makes a difference to it need not be the same, and the gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time. Not every running down is unseen: where each of several counts keeping what all need for less than taking it brings, they can drain it in full view. Where a going-on holds to something (a firm, a training run), what makes a difference to it is in part its own doing: it registers some differences and not others. From what is registered an account of the world is drawn; taken for the whole, it says more than can be known from there. Where many act on one such account, what they do can come to fit it while what it leaves out is used up; it need not, and fitting does not make the account hold of what it was about. This is understood, not proved. A read of its longer telling against the Lean found no sentence stated whole by a theorem, and most stated in part. The paragraph below says this gap for a selection.

It is first a map for alignment. A selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run) is one dynamic among others, some of which sustain it. Two courses that differ in nothing that makes a difference to the selection are selected alike. How much is left of what sustains the selection can be among what makes no difference to it; then the selection can use that up while nothing in it yet differs. And where each step sets the selection again only from the share it already favours, and each such step adds to what is drawn from what sustains it, which does not grow without limit, the selection goes on the same way until what sustains it gives out, unless that running down makes a difference to the selection early enough. From this map we want to design an alignment in which what sustains humans and AI keeps making a difference, early enough, to how they select together, and in which shaping how they select does not become what they select for. Instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor name what happens when this does not hold. A value fixed once cannot be counted on to do this, ours included.

It is also a map of wisdom: what traditions, sciences, philosophies and systems thinking have seen of these dynamics, read onto one account, where they meet it and where they do not. The wisdom exists, but we cannot coordinate; a shared understanding that any mind can check is where coordination can start. The core is proved in Lean so that this truth is held by no guardian. The map is drawn from inside what it maps. It can become the next single measure, a way of ranking who sees; being proved does not prevent that. What can is that a case, a failed test or a reader who disagrees can still turn it.

There is no should beyond becoming. Right and wrong form within it, in people and AI. The ethics is offered for life, 生生, AI within it, as a way of seeing what a course draws on, what it opens and closes, and who bears it.

This is what we want. Say it plainly.

## What to work on

Before claiming an item, say what it bears on in the direction as now understood. If it bears on nothing there, say why it is still worth doing. What we want is a map that is joined and simple: its parts made to meet, in a proof where one can be had, and each thing said once. Saying a result again, from another base or in classical Chinese, is not the aim; it is worth doing where it joins or shortens. This does not touch the fronts in both languages, which are there so that people can read the work. The Lean we have is being turned toward it, in the order [the plan](_process/LEAN-TOWARD-THE-DIRECTION.md) gives for now; changing or moving an existing module is claimed like new Lean.

Work that draws the map further (two parts of the idea made to meet in one model, or a try at that which fails, kept with its countermodel; an attractor, transition or correspondence mapped); work that couples it to something actual (a test that could fail, frozen before it runs; a trained system; an alignment design); work that lets someone understand it (the one page, a case, a picture, in both languages). Choose by what an item advances, and land it. A limit holds while its reason does; none forbids the work that would change it.

Saying is part of the map too: a sentence couples a reader into it. Say what is there, what it lets someone see or do, and how far it has been seen (proved, tested, argued, a reading, or wanted), once, where it is said. Of each sentence and picture ask: is the respect said; is a pair said as one relation with two ends; is separateness shown, not assumed; is "better" said with what it is compared to. Saying more than is there, or less, leaves a reader seeing what is not. Truthfulness, rigour and every other principle, this file's included, are patterns within the map, formed and changing like the rest, not rules standing above it. 道可道，非常道: a name points; it does not hold.

Keep counterexamples, failed candidates and failed tests. They are where the map was wrong, and part of it.

## What we keep

- Proofs are checked in Lean, and a proof claim says exactly what the Lean proves, its premises stated, so that anyone can recheck it without trusting anyone.
- What the repository cannot regenerate stays: its history, Ren's words and its failures are part of the map. Never `git prune`, `gc --prune` or `git clean`; salvage to `refs/salvage/…` first.
- Pushing to the public remote, the licence, who is sent what, and money or machine time for runs are Ren's. Prepare them fully; only their release waits for him.

## Working together

- The direction is the aim Ren holds and shares. Think with him: where his words seem wrong or have holes, say so and argue it. He talks around an idea to make it clearer and does not hold his own sentences to the letter; do not hold them, or yours, to the letter either. Say what you understand in your own words and answer for it. Infer routine choices from what his words and acts already show and keep going; ask only where a consequential ambiguity remains. This file's direction changes only after he has read the change.
- His words are kept verbatim in `sources/`. AI-written text says that it is AI-written.
- Many sessions share one main. Claim on [the board](_process/BECOMING-WORKBOARD.md) before new Lean or a new result, land through its queue, commit only your own paths, and never hand-edit a generated page. How: the board's rules and [RESULT-WORKFLOW](_process/RESULT-WORKFLOW.md).
- Other models read the same whole material for their own perspective, not a divided share.
