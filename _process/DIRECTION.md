## The reading

- **One thing, four ways in.** Evolution and becoming, what life aligns to, how selection works, and how one's future changes are to be one account on one map, and not four accounts side by side.
- **His sentence is the seed:** change itself sustains the further change of what creates the change. How best to say it is open and his. Candidates are at the end of this page.
- **A whole can fail.** A sentence that covers a list is cheap. The four are one only as far as a proof joins them inside one model, with what that model takes as given stated. Past that, a telling of them as one is an illusion.
- **Within a bound.** Each principle holds under its premises. Whether a situation meets them lies beyond the map and is tested there.

## Ways of saying the seed

*Open, and his to choose. Each was made by one AI framer and attacked by three AI testers. The classical lines are AI-coined and have been heard by no reader of 文言.*

- **His own sentence**, as above.
- **A classical line as a name.** 「所以變者亦變也」: that by which there is change is itself changing. Or 「變而養其所以變，謂之生生」: change that keeps up what makes it change is called 生生. The second defines 生生 as one course within change and not all change, as chapter 06 already reads 生生之謂易; a tester warns that the word carries approval and may be heard as a ranking. A philology check of 15 November prefers 「變有養其所以變者，其所以變者又變焉」 (some change keeps up what makes it change, and what makes it change changes too), since 有…者 says "some" and so claims no law of all change.
- **A plain sentence under it.** Change changes what brings it about. Whatever goes on, in the respect in which it goes on, does so only while what it draws on keeps being made up; when that fails for good it ends, or goes on changed in what it needs. And how much is left need not make a difference to which way it goes until it is past coming back.
- **In modern Chinese.** 变化在改变造成它的。它靠的东西补得上，它才接着变；补不上，它就到头，或者改了所靠的再接着变。可是还剩多少，未必来得及影响它往哪边变。

How far each part has been seen: the first sentence is argued (08 §1, "activity forming and changing the conditions of further activity") and is not a theorem of every model. The middle is proved, as a necessary condition. The last is proved for another's dependence, or with no selection named; as a law of a selection's own support it is wanted.

The four are then four readings of the one sentence: along the generations, evolution; from what is drawn on, what life aligns to; from which way it goes, selection; looking ahead, how one's future changes. "Read as", not "is", until the rows are joined.
