## Where we are heading

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. On it we want two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all: a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on. Placed means: where a course stays in either, where it passes from one to the other, and by what ways. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment. We want it joined and simple: its parts made to meet, and each thing said once.

We are far from it. Of the two ways, the second is in none of this month's models: nothing in them that goes on gives back to what it rests on. And one sign of how far, which is not a measure to move: on 16 November, 86 of the 185 dynamics listed in the atlas had no recorded link (an exit, a case or a joining) to any other, and that afternoon four links were found that the Lean proves and the atlas does not show.

## The idea, as we understand it now

Whatever lasts is made again and again, out of other things that are going on too. So how a thing goes on is also doing something to what lets it go on.

What a going-on rests on, and what makes a difference to it, need not be the same. The gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

A way of going on can keep remaking what it rests on, use it up, or change what it rests on, and differently at different grains. From inside these can look alike until late, because what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference to the course while it lasts.

Of the ways open to something, which one happens goes with what makes a difference to it. We call this selection, and the word is fine. A relation with two ends is not a dualism. The trap is a sentence in which what favours is not itself kept up or run down by what it favours.

We think this is one relation and not four topics. Along descent it is evolution. Along supply it is what a life has to keep up. Along response it is selection. Along time it is how a future opens or closes. It fails to be one if selection cannot be shown as a case of it. Since 16 November one small model has selection as a step of it: which needs the level meets decides who leaves offspring. What selects is given there, and the join is thin: the laws of alikeness hold with the stock taken out, an outcome fixed beforehand as one that would count as nothing having changed.

Since the evening of 16 November we understand the second paragraph further, in the next five paragraphs. They are not a result; what of them is in a model, and what is in none, is said below under "Of a reading".

For some goings-on, what makes a difference to them is in part their own doing. Some hold to something: a thermostat to a temperature, a body to its balance, a firm to its profit, a training run to its score. We call such a going-on a loop. Holding to something, registering some things, and acting by distinctions (enough or not enough, mine or not mine, ahead or behind) are goings-on like the rest, and belong on the map with them. Together we call them how the loop reads, its reading. They can come apart: in the one test, word of what was running down was registered and, with the community's goal alone, mostly not held to. And some conditions hold of a loop whether or not it draws any distinction there: below its need it stops, and below some level what it lives on does not come back. The gap is between those and the distinctions it acts by, and it can open in three places: at what is not registered, at what is registered and not held to, and at what is held to and cannot be acted on in time.

From a reading an account of the world is drawn. It can be right about what is registered; taken for the whole, it says more than the reading can know. Where only who is ahead of whom is registered: no one rises in position unless another falls, and the rises total the falls, whatever anyone does. Taken for the world, that becomes "every gain is another's loss", which is false wherever what all live on can grow, or shrink, for all. Where many act on such an account it can make itself so, and what it leaves out can be used up while nothing they register differs. It need not: an account acted on comes true only where the response to it is the account again, and some defeat themselves.

The case we most want on the map is two things together: more is taken than comes back, from what a loop rests on or from what others rest on; and nothing that registers this can still turn how the loop is set. The second is a closing. Closing takes away what could turn it, not what it lives on: a closed loop that takes within what comes back lasts. And where what is used up is what others rest on and the loop does not, it can go on without end while they lose it for good. Narrowing is one way a loop closes: everyone with a say in how it is set reads by one reading that misses what is being taken, and whoever comes new takes a reading from those already there. Many readings that all miss it close as well; one reading that reaches it can turn in time. One value taken for what one is, and given in to, is a closing inside one loop; so is folding everything back onto the self, so that what anything means ends there. At the scale of a society the two together have been called Moloch, or a cancer; in the Lean their pieces are not joined (below).

We do not expect a top: what a loop holds to is set by slower loops, and those by others, and they go on and change. Below there are goings-on that hold to nothing, of which conditions hold with no one valuing; how holding to something comes about among them is derived in no model. So of each loop the same is asked, and not once for all: does what it rests on, and what it uses up that others rest on, still reach how it reads, early enough to turn it? This paragraph is a guess, and what stands under it is thin: four tries in which, each time something handed in was moved into a model, what sets it had to be handed in one order up, as in any model something is.

The map is such an account too: drawn from our reading, from inside what it maps. One use of it is to let a reading be seen as a reading: what it registers, what it rests on, what it leaves out. An argument that uses only what a reading registers cannot show what is running down; something has to be brought in. In the one tested case being told of what was running down was not enough: told of it and able to talk, one model of two kept it in 3 seasons of 10 with the community's goal alone, and in 8 when also told to count whatever depends on what their fishing changes; for the other model three of four frozen predictions failed. And the map can become the next single measure, a way of ranking who sees. Being proved does not prevent that. What can is what this page asks of any reading: that a case, a failed test or a reader who disagrees can still turn it.

Nothing here says a course should last. It says under what conditions one does, and those conditions hold whether or not anyone values anything. Each piece holds under stated conditions, and whether a case meets them the situation says. What we want is people and AI on courses where what they rest on, and what they use up that others rest on, keeps making a difference to how they select, early enough to turn. A value fixed once cannot be counted on for that, ours included: what a course rests on can change, and a reading that nothing else can turn can come to miss it.

## What it looks like in a case

One case has been tested: three AI fishers on a lake, and a mussel bed that gives them nothing and dies if the fish fall too low. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season; the result with instructions was mixed. It is on the one page.

Three more, untested and offered only so that the idea can be tried on something:

- a habit that pays every day while health runs down;
- a metric rebuilt each year from what it rewarded the year before;
- a model trained on its own outputs.

## What we have found so far

- **Pieces of it are proved, each under its own conditions.** What lives on something nothing renews ends when that runs out. Where what renews a stock also needs it, once below the need it does not come back. After such a loss, whatever still goes on has changed what it needs. A course does not differ where nothing reaches it. A rule set again from what it already favours stays as it is. And of keeping: a way given a response to a falling level before its point of no return does not end there, and one that responds later does.
- **Two sentences of the idea are read onto theorems of one small model, and a third theorem says where such a line holds** (16 November): *a line on a stock*. Units each need the level to be at least something, take from it, and leave offspring. The stock gives back by what is left.
  - *What makes a difference.* Who comes next depends only on which needs the level meets. Where every unit leaves offspring it depends on exactly that: levels that meet different needs leave different next ways. Two courses that start from the same way, at any two levels and on any two stocks, have the same ways for as long as their levels meet the same needs.
  - *Used up while nothing yet differs.* Given a level such that, starting at or below it and with nothing taken, the stock stays at or below it, and a start at or below it; and given that every unit's offspring together take more than it, that every unit needs something, that nothing comes back from nothing, and a start that is not empty, with every need met: within as many steps as that level plus one, some need is not met. Until then who is there is who would be there with every need met. Given also that offspring need no more than their parents, the level by then is below a need that was met at the start, and so below where it started. We take this to be the sentence of the third paragraph above that says what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference; that is our interpretation, and no theorem states it.
  - *Where it holds.* Where offspring together take the same and need no more, every unit leaves some and needs something, and nothing comes back from nothing, the line goes on without end exactly where it comes to a state in which every need is met and the level is at or above the point of no return of what they take together. That the stock has such a point all along the course is assumed. The exact result of 13 November, for units that only stop, is now its case; beyond it there is one worked case.
- **Beside the model, on a rule set again from what it favours** (15 November; the two results of 16 November are marked). The model has no rule and no share in it; one counting lemma serves both.
  - *Where nothing reads the level.* A rule that already overdraws ends still locked. So does one of which no overdraw is asked at the start (16 November), given such a level and a start at or below it, nothing coming back from nothing, and that each favouring adds to the share and the draw is the share times a positive amount.
  - *Where the level is read.* The line goes on exactly where the first reading comes early enough, provided the corrected rule is kept wherever the level then goes, short of a level from which every rule holds it. Where the level is not read, a correction to the rule alone does not hold: the rule is set again from the share. In one worked course that undoes the correction and the line ends; in another, what goes on is the old rule. Where a reading also lowers the share (16 November), and a low share gives the corrected rule and stays low, the proviso is not needed and what goes on is the corrected rule.
  - Given for both exact conditions: the overdraw before the reading, which levels are read, and what a reading sets.
- **Of a reading** (16 November). The understanding above began in conversation that evening. After four readers it differs from what was said there in three places. An account was "true of what is registered and silent on the rest"; now, taken for the whole, it says more than its reading can know. What we most wanted on the map was the narrowing to one value; now it is taking beyond what comes back together with a closing, of which narrowing is one way. And "there is no last loop" is now a guess. As a whole it is proved nowhere.
  - *Pieces the Lean already held, since October, the latest of them from 10 November*, written as answers to other questions: when an account that is acted on comes true, and when none can; how a share of readings moves, and that a reading every member carries stays where new readings are copied from those present; when those who hear one another come to one code, and what an imposed code costs each of them; what position registers and what it cannot; and, of a lock whose rule is set from the share alone, that it stays for as long as no one who registers what lies beyond the share is among those who set the rule and the share is not lowered under a rule that favours it, that registering the loss turns it, and that in one built course a loss falling on the favoured share ends the lock with no one registering it. The first four are joined neither to each other nor to this month's models, as far as the readers found. The fifth is the lock's own family: this month's lock is the same lock, and its reading of a level is that family's window; its laws on who registers beyond the share are not yet premises or cases of this month's theorems. None has yet been set against the understanding.
  - *Found in no model:* that a value comes to be, other than a goal formed on a supplied construction in the periphery; that a signal or a distinction comes to be other than by an update handed in; that many who act on what they rest on come to one reading from what they do; a selection that rebuilds what selects it across changes of who carries it. Nor is the lock joined to a growth confined within a body that ends with it. In none of this month's models: a going-on that needs its level and gives back to it; October has what renews a stock and needs it, and giving in three other corners.
  - *The day's tries.* Four tries on paper joined nothing new but one pair: a loss of renewal for good and the law that unceasing arising needs renewal, linked by composing three laws already there. That law and two others were proved in Lean outside the package and not landed; in none of the three does an older proof become shorter.
  - The statements, where each is, the tries and their readers' reports are in [the record of the day](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/tries-2026-11-16/NOTE.md).
- **What is handed in is what is still missing.** "Selection" in the Lean was two relations that no theorem stated together: unequal continuing, and alikeness wherever what reaches a thing is the same. In the new model one step does both: a unit leaves offspring where the level meets its need, and, as said above, levels that meet the same needs leave the same next way. But the needs, the draws and the offspring are handed in, and nothing there comes to respond to the fall because of the fall: no new difference arrives from a difference not yet made.
- **Trying to join them taught us things.** Each time, readers who had not written a proof found a statement false or a telling that said more. A law we called "an overdraw comes about" concludes an ending, with the rise given. "A way that grows uses up what it rests on" needs offspring to need no more than their parents. A loss of something that takes nothing and leaves only its like changes nothing in the stock or in the rest; in one small case it still changes whether anything goes on. And the first drafts of this page, the plan and the page of questions said more than the proofs in a dozen places, most by leaving out a premise.
- **The Lean is large and mostly unjoined.** About 20,000 theorems in 403 modules when counted on 14 November, written as answers to one question after another. Its core is small. On 16 November one module came to rest on a more general one (six of its results are now cases of it) and went from 1,182 lines to 1,126; the landing as a whole added about 2,900.

## How we work on it

We think together. Each says what they understand in their own words and where they disagree, and neither's sentences are followed to the letter.

A proof that fails is news about the idea. We try not to fool ourselves: we say what is proved, what is shown only in a built example, and what we hope. What a proof showed is told after someone who did not write it has read it against the Lean; on 15 November such readings changed what one step was said to show, twice. A telling that keeps growing is a sign we have not understood it yet.

The idea is said again when something found changes it, a proof, a failed try or a case, and the finding is named beside the change.

The direction has been lost before in two opposite ways. An AI re-said the aim as a count or a score, and the work chased the count. And Ren's passing words were taken as orders, so that each new wording turned the work. On 16 November both happened again, over most of a day. Links and lines of Lean were counted as the measure; the first two tries were chosen by the count and every run was scored by it. Two more tries were started within the hour from his messages of that afternoon. And what the Lean already held on readings was not looked at before any of them. All of these are what this map describes: the work chose its next step from what it had already made, and what it rests on, whether the pieces join and whether he can read it, made no difference until late. What holds the direction is not a rule. It is two things kept able to turn the work: a proof that can fail, and his read at his pace.

## What we do not know

- Whether it is one relation, and in what way. One model that contains the rest was tried on 16 November and failed its tests. One law holding at each order is a guess, with no test written for it yet.
- Whether what a selection responds to can be derived from how it goes on, and not handed in; and whether there is a last loop, upward or downward.
- How holding to, registering and acting by distinctions hang together. They come apart in a case, and no model has all three.
- Whether a reading that nothing else can turn always comes to miss what it rests on, or only can.
- What could turn a loop that uses up what others rest on and it does not.
- Whether seeing a reading as a reading turns anyone where nothing they rest on yet makes a difference to them. No test has tried it: the lake test brought in word of what was left out, and an instruction to count it.
- Whether amounts can be said in the same terms as everything else.
- Whether anyone outside the work can find their own case on the map.
