## Where we are heading

One joined, machine-checked map of how goings-on form, persist, adapt, and end within one relational unfolding — drawn from inside what it maps, without dualism or external controllers. Following Ren's 23 November questions and framing ([his words](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-23-be-the-new-coordinator.md), [evening start](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-23-start-three-layer-roadmap.md)), the earlier AI phrase *"two ways placed with respect to each other"* and monotonic *"more and more"* are retired from the aim. The direction stands in three cooperating layers:

1. **Core Subject — The Alignment Theory of Coupled Adaptive Systems.** For any going-on (a reward, judge, learner, firm, pool, or society) embedded in shared substrates it relies on, map three coupled questions:
   - *Blindness and drain:* what a going-on draws on vs. what makes a difference to its selection.
   - *Turning in time and learning:* when epistemic discovery, pool revision, or reading pace turns a loop *before* its substrate crosses no-return — and when capture or lag locks the signal out.
   - *Composition across scales:* when adaptive loops compose (in parallel on a shared substrate or across coarse-grained scales), which viability and responsiveness properties survive, which fail, and what interface contracts preserve or evolve capability as forms change.
   Ren's 23 November question stands as the **north-star horizon** this theory sheds light on: compositional reasoning about complex adaptive systems that preserves or evolves meaningful properties and future becomings across scales while learning, changing, and coordinating.
2. **Formal Discipline — Premise Ledger and Real Bridges.** Keep the **0-axiom relational core** clean as a machine-checked premise-and-countermodel ledger (the repository's rarest artefact). In the applied periphery, allow Mathlib reals and probability openly where approximate coarse-graining, continuous rates, or stochastic learners need them so formal models can meet real learners instead of breaking at the binding.
3. **Wisdom and Ethics — 生生 Offered Beside Dynamics.** Dynamics determine what is closed, kept, or reachable; they do not entail an "ought" (`ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`). The ethics of 生生 and the crosswalk of wisdom traditions stand beside the formal spine as our offered scope and recognition lens, never forcing formal theorems to chase poetic wording.

## What we are trying next

Every milestone clears an ungameable bar before landing — **net simplification/bridge**, **genuinely derived mechanism or composition** (no sterile dictionaries, no trivial `T := σ j` re-indexings, no standalone leaves), **real-learner friction**, or **cold-read clarity** — with adversarial paper attacks before any Lean build and single-telling metadata (`records/<id>.json` as sole prose source).

1. **Phase 0 (Context & Single-Telling):** Losslessly split `DIRECTION.md` and `BECOMING-WORKBOARD.md` into compact live fronts + `< 1 MiB` archives; stop 11-way manual prose copying and `check_becoming.py` `SCOPE` bloat; freeze `results/map/`.
2. **Phase 1 — Build the Missing In-Between in Lean (Paper Attack → Lean):**
   - **1A (`PacedStock`):** Couple coarse rule-update cadences (`Pace`, `Sampling`) to fine-step stock/unit evolution (`WayUnderARule`, `Frame.blocks`, `Viability.Kernel`) to prove intra-span slack equivalence and the **Pace–Margin Sandwich** (`2N - 1` identification lower bound vs. drawdown no-return bound).
   - **1B (`AdaptiveLoops`):** Open adaptive loops with assume-guarantee interface certificates (`Viability.disturbed`, `ReadingLoop`, `PoolFromCourse`, `SelectionPool`, `RegimeMargin.Coupled.Indexed`) to prove the **Coupled Race Theorem** (epistemic turning vs. silencing/capture vs. coordination vs. stock margin) and compositional viability vs. joint-depletion failure with endogenous rule change (not dominant-kind extinction).
   - **1C (Lax Multi-Scale Abstraction & Transformative Continuation):** Slack-bounded viability simulation across `(π, σ)` (`ProjectedLaws`, `Tower`) with a witness breaking exact lumping; formal viability-preserving transformation strictly between zero-loss `Widens` and aimless churn; surgical `Flow`–stock bridge only where non-trivial.
3. **Phase 2 (Unified Map Tooling & Local Reality Checks, Rule 7 compliant):** Anchor the map on three authoritative layers (Lean receipt/`proof-uses`, `records/`, `atlas/entries/` via `atlas_index.py`), add time-indexed couplings and feedback loops to `atlas.py`'s situation checker, bind remaining local tests, and stress-test 1A/1B locally on CPU against noisy/generalizing learners.
4. **Phase 3 (Canonical Map for Cold Read):** Distill the unified spine into one `< 15`-page `CANONICAL-MAP.md` Ren can read cold without AI translation, then bring chapters up to date once.

## What we do not know

- Which interface certificates let two adaptive pools compose over shared substrates *while evolving internal forms, capabilities, and objectives*.
- What minimal slack conditions allow macro-scale viability guarantees to transfer cleanly to fine-step stochastic or gradient-trained systems (`ε`-approximate coarse-graining).
- Whether `NeedTwentySeven`'s extinction-triggered rule flip counts as a boundary case of turning or purely ecological crash, and how to formalize transformative continuation where legacy units cease as new becomings form.
- Whether `0`-axiom `Nat` bounds suffice for compositional alignment laws or where Mathlib reals/probability are strictly required in the periphery.
