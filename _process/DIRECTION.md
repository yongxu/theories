## Where we are heading

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. On it we want two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all: a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on. Placed means: where, and at which grain, a course is doing the one or the other, where it passes between them, and by what ways. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment. We want it joined and simple: its parts made to meet, and each thing said once.

*Renewing and consuming with respect to each other.* A formation is renewing where, on every condition it requires or draws on at any step, every dependence of any formation, at each step at which it holds, is met at that step and, as it then stands, at the next (`Web.RenewingIn`): that says needs stay met, not that the formation remakes anything. ## The idea, as we understand it now

Whatever lasts is made again and again, out of other things that are going on too. So how a thing goes on is also doing something to what lets it go on.

What a going-on rests on, and what makes a difference to it, need not be the same. The gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

A way of going on can keep remaking what it rests on, use it up, or change what it rests on, and differently at different grains. From inside these can look alike until late, because what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference to the course while it lasts.

Of the ways open to something, which one happens goes with what makes a difference to it. We call this selection, and the word is fine. A relation with two ends is not a dualism. The trap is a sentence in which what favours is not itself kept up or run down by what it favours.

We think this is one relation and not four topics: along descent it is evolution, along supply what a life has to keep up, along response selection, along time how a future opens or closes. It fails to be one if selection cannot be shown as a case of it. One small model now has selection as a step of it, with what selects handed in and the join thin (below).

*The next eight paragraphs take the second paragraph further. They are our understanding, first said on the evening of 16 November and changed where a read against the Lean showed it wrong or loose. No theorem states any sentence of them whole (below, "Of a reading").*

For some goings-on, what makes a difference to them is in part their own doing. Some hold to something: a thermostat to a temperature, a body to its balance, a firm to its profit, a training run to its score. We call such a going-on a loop. Holding to something, registering some things, and acting by distinctions (enough or not enough, mine or not mine, ahead or behind) are goings-on like the rest, and belong on the map with them. Together we call them how the loop reads, its reading. They can come apart: in the one test, word of what was running down was registered and, with the community's goal alone, mostly not held to.

Some conditions hold of a loop whether or not it draws any distinction there, though what it does moves where it stands under them. What it takes has to be there. And where what it takes from is renewed only by what needs it too and gives only while that need is met, once below that need it does not come back. The gap is between such conditions and the distinctions the loop acts by. It can open at what is not registered; at what is registered and not held to, or is outweighed among those who set the loop; at what removes those who would register it; and at what cannot be acted on in time. And not every running down is unseen: where each of several counts keeping what all need for less than taking it brings, they can drain it in full view (one model, below).

From a reading an account of the world is drawn. It can be right about what is registered; taken for the whole, it says more than the reading can know. Where position is all that is held to and registered (each one's amount set against the total of all): no one rises unless another falls, and the rises total the falls, whatever anyone does. Taken for the world, that becomes "every gain is another's loss", which is false of amounts wherever all can gain, or lose, the same amount: position does not move when they do.

Where many act on such an account, we think it can make itself so in one respect: what they do comes to fit it. How they select can be set again from what it already favours and stay so; those who come after can be handed it as it stands; or the acts it chooses can go on being answered as it predicts. It does not thereby come to hold of what it was about: it can be handed on for ever and leave out the very thing it was drawn about. Nor need what they do come to fit it: some responses answer every account with something other than it. And what it leaves out can be used up while nothing they register differs.

The case we most want on the map is two things together: more is taken than comes back, from what a loop rests on or from what others rest on; and nothing that registers this can still turn how the loop is set. The second is a closing. It takes away what could turn the loop, not what it lives on: a closed loop can go on without end where what it takes keeps being met. And where what is used up is what others rest on and nothing of it comes back to the loop, it can go on without end while it is gone for them for good.

Narrowing is one way a loop closes: everyone a newcomer can take a reading from reads in a way that misses what is being taken, and revising among those ways gives only more of them. Selection can favour a reading that misses it (one built world). Many readings that all miss it close as well, where pooling them reaches only what each reaches. One that reaches it turns the loop only if it reads in time and is not outweighed, and reaching does not fix which way. We think a loop can close from inside too: one value taken for what the loop is, so that whatever would revise it is weighed by it; or meaning taken to end at the self, so that nothing from beyond can turn it. Neither is in a model. At the scale of a society the taking and the closing together have been called Moloch, or a cancer; in the package's Lean their pieces are not joined in general (below).

We do not expect a top: what a loop holds to is set by slower loops, and those by others, and they go on and change. There are also goings-on that hold to nothing, of which the conditions hold too, with no one valuing; how holding to something comes about among them is derived in no model. So of each loop the same is asked, and not once for all: does what it rests on, and what it uses up that others rest on, still reach how it reads, early enough to turn it? This paragraph is a guess, and what stands under it is thin: four tries in which, each time something handed in was moved into a model, what sets it had to be handed in one order up, as in any model something is.

The map is such an account too: drawn from our reading, from inside what it maps. One use of it is to let a reading be seen as a reading: what it registers, what it rests on, what it leaves out. An argument that uses only what a reading registers cannot show a running down that changes nothing the reading registers; something the running down does change has to be brought in, from beyond what the loop and those it reads through reach. Word of it was not enough in the lake test (below); in another frozen test, on a judge that is one trained model reading a reference set in context (a judge formed by training is untested), one note of the loss turned its reading ([02 §9](../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#capture)). And the map can become the next single measure, a way of ranking who sees. Being proved does not prevent that. What can is what this page asks of any reading: that a case, a failed test or a reader who disagrees can still turn it; a reader, only if heard by those who set it.

Nothing here says a course should last. It says under what conditions one does, and those conditions hold whether or not anyone values anything. Each piece holds under stated conditions, and whether a case meets them the situation says. What we want is people and AI on courses where what they rest on, and what they use up that others rest on, keeps making a difference to how they select, early enough to turn. A value fixed once cannot be counted on for that, ours included: what a course rests on can change, and a reading that nothing else can turn can come to miss it.

## What it looks like in a case

One case is told here, the lake test: three AI fishers on a lake, and a mussel bed that gives them nothing and dies if the fish fall too low. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season. Told of it and able to talk, one model of two kept it in 3 seasons of 10 with the community's goal alone, and in 8 when also told to count whatever depends on what their fishing changes; for the other model three of four frozen predictions failed. It is on [the one page](../theories/becoming/ONE-PAGE.md).

Three more, untested and offered only so that the idea can be tried on something:

- a habit that pays every day while health runs down;
- a metric rebuilt each year from what it rewarded the year before;
- a model trained on its own outputs.

## How we work on it

We think together. Each says what they understand in their own words and where they disagree, and neither's sentences are followed to the letter.

A proof that fails is news about the idea. We try not to fool ourselves: we say what is proved, what is shown only in a built example, and what we hope. What a proof showed is told after someone who did not write it has read it against the Lean; on 15 November such readings changed what one step was said to show, twice.
