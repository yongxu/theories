## The reading

- **One thing, four ways in.** Evolution and becoming, what life aligns to, how selection works, and how one's future changes are to be one account on one map, and not four accounts side by side.
- **His sentence is the seed:** change itself sustains the further change of what creates the change. How best to say it is open and his. Candidates are at the end of this page.
- **A whole can fail.** A sentence that covers a list is cheap. The four are one only as far as a proof joins them inside one model, with what that model takes as given stated. Past that, a telling of them as one is an illusion.
- **Within a bound.** Each principle holds under its premises. Whether a situation meets them lies beyond the map and is tested there.

## What moves it, and what is drift

*Proposed, for his read.*

**Moves it:**
- a row of the table changing, by a theorem in one model that passes the test above;
- a try at a row failing, with the countermodel kept;
- a test that could fail, frozen before it runs, on a row;
- a telling that goes exactly as far as the rows.

**Drift:**
- a module that nothing else uses;
- a new view, store, score or count given as progress;
- a wording pass that moves no row;
- a telling of the four as one past the table;
- the direction said again in a second place in other words;
- a reply an AI wrote and he picked, taken as his direction.

A landing says which row it moves, or says that it moves none.

## What it leaves out

The four as one cover about half of what the chapters hold. Three things most likely to be found missing:

- **How what selects comes to be and changes.** His sentence is about the change of what creates the change. Every missing statement above holds the order, the couplings, the need and the law fixed. The root chapter's open list names this ("Self-changing conditions").
- **Losses that never come back.** The chain is about a selection and its own support. Where dependence runs one way, no statement reading only the dynamics counts the loss; counting it is the one step the ethics offers.

## A route

*Proposed and not started. New Lean is claimed on the board first.*

A reader set against five new statements on the World (the flows and webs with generations of descent) judged the target right and that route not the best. The exact laws already stand on the pair of a way and a level (`TurningInTime`, `WayOfUnits`), which is tied to the grammar and holds the lock. Its proposal:

1. Say first what is joined and what is not. This page does that.
2. Prove the chain once on the pair.
3. Carry the World to the pair by two lemmas, so that descent, balance and couplings come from the World.
4. Prove new, on the World, only the link it alone can give: that what is favoured draws more.
5. Say each conclusion as a region of the map: generating, keeping, degenerating.
6. Before proving, freeze that the built examples must come out as cases.

It fails if a World's stock takes the pair's form only under premises that built examples alone meet.

## Ways of saying the seed

*Open, and his to choose. Each was made by one AI framer and attacked by three AI testers. The classical lines are AI-coined and have been heard by no reader of 文言.*

- **His own sentence**, as above.
- **A classical line as a name.** 「所以變者亦變也」: that by which there is change is itself changing. Or 「變而養其所以變，謂之生生」: change that keeps up what makes it change is called 生生. The second defines 生生 as one course within change and not all change, as chapter 06 already reads 生生之謂易; a tester warns that the word carries approval and may be heard as a ranking.
- **A plain sentence under it.** Change changes what brings it about. Whatever goes on, in the respect in which it goes on, does so only while what it draws on keeps being made up; when that fails for good it ends, or goes on changed in what it needs. And how much is left need not make a difference to which way it goes until it is past coming back.
- **In modern Chinese.** 变化在改变造成它的。它靠的东西补得上，它才接着变；补不上，它就到头，或者改了所靠的再接着变。可是还剩多少，未必来得及影响它往哪边变。

How far each part has been seen: the first sentence is argued (08 §1, "activity forming and changing the conditions of further activity") and is not a theorem of every model. The middle is proved, as a necessary condition. The last is proved for another's dependence, or with no selection named; as a law of a selection's own support it is wanted.

The four are then four readings of the one sentence: along the generations, evolution; from what is drawn on, what life aligns to; from which way it goes, selection; looking ahead, how one's future changes. "Read as", not "is", until the rows are joined.
