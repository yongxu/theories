## Where we are heading

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment.

## The idea, as we understand it now

Whatever lasts is made again and again, out of other things that are going on too. So how a thing goes on is also doing something to what lets it go on.

What a going-on rests on, and what makes a difference to it, need not be the same. The gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

A way of going on can keep remaking what it rests on, use it up, or change what it rests on, and differently at different grains. From inside these can look alike until late, because what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference to the course while it lasts.

Of the ways open to something, which one happens goes with what makes a difference to it. We call this selection, and the word is fine. A relation with two ends is not a dualism. The trap is a sentence in which what favours is not itself kept up or run down by what it favours.

We think this is one relation and not four topics. Along descent it is evolution. Along supply it is what a life has to keep up. Along response it is selection. Along time it is how a future opens or closes. It fails to be one if selection cannot be shown as a case of it, and today it is not shown.

Nothing here says a course should last. It says under what conditions one does. Each piece holds under stated conditions, and whether a case meets them the situation says. What we want is people and AI on courses where what they rest on keeps making a difference to how they select, early enough to turn.

## What it looks like in a case

One case has been tested: three AI fishers on a lake, and a mussel bed that gives them nothing and dies if the fish fall too low. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season; the result with instructions was mixed. It is on the one page.

Three more, untested and offered only so that the idea can be tried on something:

- a habit that pays every day while health runs down;
- a metric rebuilt each year from what it rewarded the year before;
- a model trained on its own outputs.

## What we have found so far

- **Pieces of it are proved, each under its own conditions.** What lives on something nothing renews ends when that runs out. Where what renews a stock also needs it, once below the need it does not come back. After such a loss, whatever still goes on has changed what it needs. A course does not differ where nothing reaches it. A rule set again from what it already favours stays as it is. And on the keeping side: a way that comes to respond to a falling level before its point of no return does not end there, and one that responds later does.
- **They do not yet meet.** These stand in separate models. Where they fail to meet is selection.
- **"Selection" in the Lean is two relations.** One is unequal continuing: of what could go on, some does, or leaves more. The other is alikeness: what happens is the same wherever what reaches it is the same. No theorem states both together. Two of the Lean's four forms have nothing that chooses. The other two have a slot filled from outside, a free predicate and a free order, and that is the gap below: how what selects comes to be.
- **Trying to join them taught us things.** Several statements we expected to hold were false when tried by hand. One of them: a correction that fires once is undone when things recover. On paper it holds only if the correction stays in force.
- **The Lean is large and mostly unjoined.** About 20,000 theorems in 403 modules, written as answers to one question after another. Its core is small.

## How we work on it

We think together. Each says what they understand in their own words and where they disagree, and neither's sentences are followed to the letter.

A proof that fails is news about the idea. We try not to fool ourselves: we say what is proved, what is shown only in a built example, and what we hope. A telling that keeps growing is a sign we have not understood it yet.

The idea is said again when something found changes it, a proof, a failed try or a case, and the finding is named beside the change.

The direction has been lost before in two opposite ways. An AI re-said the aim as a count or a score, and the work chased the count. And Ren's passing words were taken as orders, so that each new wording turned the work. Both are what this map describes: the work chose its next step from what it had already made, and what it rests on, whether the pieces join and whether he can read it, made no difference until late. What holds the direction is not a rule. It is two things kept able to turn the work: a proof that can fail, and his read at his pace.

## What we do not know

- Whether it is one relation.
- Whether what a selection responds to can be derived from how it goes on, and not handed in.
- Whether amounts can be said in the same terms as everything else.
- Whether anyone outside the work can find their own case on the map.
