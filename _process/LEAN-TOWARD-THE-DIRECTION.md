# The Lean toward the direction

*AI-written (Fable), 15 November 2026. The order of work on the Lean as it looks now. It will change as the work teaches us. About ten minutes. It goes with [the direction](DIRECTION.md). Steps 3 to 7 were re-said on 16 November, after they were tried as one model. A first draft was attacked by another AI reader and three of its statements were tried by hand before any Lean; no person has checked it.*

## How this came up

On 14 November the whole Lean was counted and read for the first time: about 20,000 theorems in 403 modules, written as answers to one question after another, joined inside a family and mostly not between. Ren asked that the Lean we have be turned toward the direction, and said to start if the plan looked good. His words are in [the sources](../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-13-the-map-one-model.md#the-direction-14-november-2026-evening).

## What a stage is for

A stage is a try at making something the idea needs, and that is now assumed, come out as a conclusion in one model. Before a stage, someone says what they would count as nothing having changed. After it, someone who did not write the proof reads it against the Lean. Turning the Lean is not restating 403 modules, and it is not counted in modules or theorems.

## What trying on paper found

Three statements the first draft rested on were tried by hand. All three failed as first worded, and each failure is kept.

- **A correction that fires once.** "A locked rule never ends exactly where its first reading of the level comes before the point of no return" is false. On a five-step course the level is read in time; one step later it is no longer read, the rule is set again from the share, and the line ends. This page first said the level recovers there; it never rises on that course. The statement holds under the condition said in step 2 below, proved on 15 November.
- **Its one-way half holds.** A locked rule that never reads the level ends, still locked, and the fall follows from the lock holding its draw. The lock keeps an overdraw going. It does not bring one about.
- **Two lemmas that were to carry one model to another** are true only when written so exactly that they say nothing new: a dictionary between two vocabularies.
- **The first statement tried on 14 November**, a selection using up what it itself depends on, was false as first worded too; two small worlds refute it.
- **Step 2's first two tellings were wrong, one each way** (found by readers of the Lean, not on paper). Its first Lean stated the exact condition with the start held within the range where the corrected draw holds the level, and was told as joining the lock to turning in time. Readers who had not written it showed that this form adds no case with a first reading after the start to the old theorem's, and that the lock's premises did nothing in it. The session then wrote, and told Ren, that no such case exists and that the lock is not joined. The next readers showed that the bound belongs on the level after the turn; with it there the case exists and the lock's premises do work. Both were found before anything landed.
- **Steps 3 to 7, tried as one model on 16 November.** Readers found these, some before the Lean and some after it. The candidate laws of alikeness hold with no stock in them, so by themselves they join nothing. The lock's new law was first told as an overdraw that comes about; it concludes an ending, and the rise of the draw is given. "A way that grows uses up what it rests on" said more than what was first proved (`need_unmet`): without the premise that offspring need no more than their parent, the unmet need can be an offspring's greater need at a level that never fell. "Units that take nothing make no difference" is false of whether anything goes on. And the join to the web-and-flow family is a dictionary. Each is kept in [the study folder](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/NOTE.md); the two countermodels are in the Lean (`KidHigherNeed`, `NothingTakenEnds`). The first drafts of this re-saying, of the direction page and of the page of questions then said more than the Lean in a dozen places, most by leaving out a premise, most often that offspring need no more than their parent. Three readers found them before anything was committed, and two more then found four clauses still wrong. The landing itself stopped twice at its last step: once because a new entry of the atlas needs a card on the map, and once because the registering texts said that a unit's offspring take what it took without "together", which is false for a unit with two; it took three core runs.

## The order as it looks now

1. **A lock that keeps an overdraw.** Landed on 15 November (content tip `b09df328e`), in `Becoming/TurningInTime.lean`: `locked_alone_not_below`, `locked_ends_with_net_regime`, its form on the lock's own window, and two worked cases, in one of which the share rises at every step. The core run passed with every axiom report empty. A reviewer and the coordinator read it: no premise speaks of the level's course any more, and the lock's premises carry the ending; the start already overdrawing, the tie from the lock to how much is drawn, and the level making no difference are still assumed. In the worked cases how much is drawn follows only the rule.
2. **Turning in time, with a correction that stays in force.** Landed on 15 November (content tip `fb6f3cf35`), in `Becoming/TurningInTime.lean`; the core run passed with every axiom report empty, and three rounds of readers read it in scratch.
   - **For any way.** A way that comes to respond never ends exactly where the step at its first marked level leaves the level at or above the corrected kind's point of no return (`turns_in_time_iff_kept_below`, on `unending_iff_above_kept_below`). This is proved where the corrected kind is kept from that point up to below a level `c`, in one of two ways. Either `c` lies above a bound on where the level is after the turn, so the kind is kept wherever the level then goes; the earlier exact theorem, `turns_in_time_iff`, is this way with the kind kept at every level. Or from `c` up to the bound every way's step keeps the level at or above `c` and within the bound; then `c` is never below any way's own point of no return (argued from `held_above`, not a Lean statement). Also given: the way as it was draws at least a floor, from a start already past that draw's point of no return, and every way reached after the turn draws at least what the corrected kind draws. The law is a sufficient condition: whether a line can go on where no such level and bound exist is not shown.
   - **On the lock's window.** A `Locked` way that already overdraws, its level read on a band that reaches from the corrected kind's point of no return up to that level `c`, never ends exactly where the step at the first reading leaves the level at or above that point (`locked_turns_in_time_on_window_iff`). The lock's premises keep its draw at or above its floor until the first reading. On a lock a first reading after the start occurs only in the first of the two ways (argued in that law's docstring, not a Lean statement). From one start that is not locked the line goes on with no reading, so the two sides differ (`Three.unlocked_goes_on`).
   - **What stays given on the lock.** That a reading sets a rule of the corrected kind and such a rule draws one fixed amount; that every step reads; where the band lies; that from `c` up to the bound every rule's step keeps the level there, which asks nothing in the first way; the floors on the draws; the start already past the lock's own point of no return; a bound on the level after a step that is read; and the premises on giving and on the need. Nothing is concluded about whether the way that goes on is locked.
   - **Three more worked courses**, from a level of nine. Read at eight only: 9, 8, 6, 6, 2, 0, in time by the old theorem's right side, and the line ends (`Three.read_at_eight_ends`). Read from six to below ten: 9, 8, 10, 12, 12, and it goes on; from seven it ends (`Three.read_to_ten`). Read from six to below nine: 9, 8, 6, 6, 6, first read at step 1, and it goes on (`Three.held_at_six`).
   - **What goes on is not always the corrected rule.** In the last course it is, by the first way: the corrected rule is held where the band reads the level. In the one before it is not, by the second way: at ten the rule is set again from the share, and what goes on at twelve is the lock's own rule, at a level where its draw is met. So "a lock turns in time" does not say the lock was corrected.
   - **The cases we expected.** `Three.goes_on` and `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` rest on the old theorem unchanged. `Three.turned_and_ends` is a computed case and never went through it.
   - **Not tried.** A reading at some steps only. A correction to what the rule is set from, and a use that grows with the share, were tried on 16 November: items 5 and 6.
3. **One model for steps 3 to 7: a line on a stock.** Landed on 16 November (content tip `e4a81512c`): the new `Becoming/LineOnAStock.lean` and `Becoming/LineOnAStockCases.lean`, additions to `Becoming/TurningInTime.lean`, and `Becoming/WayOfUnits.lean` reworked to rest on the new module. The core run passed with every axiom report empty. Agents who had not written the Lean read it thirteen times before it landed, and each round changed what was said; [the study note](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/NOTE.md) keeps what failed. Ren said on 16 November that the aim is a map that is joined and simple, so the five steps were tried as laws of one model that extends steps 1 and 2, and not as five modules. Items 4 to 7 say what each step became.
   - **The model.** Units each need the level to be at least something, take from it, and leave offspring. The level is the regime's: what comes back depends on what is left. `WayOfUnits` is the case where each unit leaves itself, and a course is a family of `SelectionTerm.Generations`. The lock of steps 1 and 2 is not in this model; one lemma is shared with it (item 5).
   - **Step 3, the two relations behind "selection".** In the model one step has both. Unequal continuing: a unit leaves offspring where the level meets its need. Alikeness: two levels leave the same next way where they meet the same needs, and exactly there if every unit of the way has offspring to leave (`alike_iff`); along two courses from one start way, on any two stocks and under any two uses, whose levels meet the same needs step by step, the ways are the same (`ways_alike`). `need_unmet` (item 5) has a part of each: while every need is met the way is the same on every stock, and then a need is not met. What did not come out: the alikeness laws hold with no stock in them, they meet the stock only through "every need is met", and one test frozen beforehand still fires for them.
4. **A stock's own point of no return.** *No exact point where what is given is free.* In the web-and-flow family what is given is a free field, so no exact point can be stated on a level there (argued on paper, not a Lean statement), and reading the course as a web with a flow turned out to be a dictionary: under balance each one-step premise is its conclusion again. That is kept on paper and not built; a fold is owed when `Continuation` is next open. Exactness comes where what is given depends on what is left. Where every unit has offspring that together take what it took and need no more than it, every unit needs something, and nothing is given from nothing, the line never ends exactly where its course comes to a state that holds: every need met, and the level at or above the point of no return of what they take together (`LineOnAStock.unending_iff_holds`; that the stock has such a point is assumed all along the course). `WayOfUnits.unending_iff_holds` is now its case, statement unchanged; beyond it there is one worked case (`TwoForOne`). Where every unit's offspring together take more there is no such point: under item 5's premises the line ends. With one way in which the offspring of some units together take less than them, and of others more, a higher start ends where a lower one does not (`HigherStartEnds`).
5. **What is favoured draws more.** *The ending is a conclusion; the growth is given.* Given that each unit's offspring together take more than it, that every unit needs something, a ceiling the level cannot pass, nothing given from nothing, and a start that is not empty with every need met: within as many steps as the ceiling plus one there is a first step at which some need is not met, and until then the way is its plain line of descent, in which no stock appears (`need_unmet`). Given also that offspring need no more than their parent, the level at that step is below a need the start met (`falls_to_unmet`), and the net ends, at a step no law bounds (`way_ends`). Without that premise the level need not have fallen (`KidHigherNeed`) and the line can go on (`KidsNeedMore`). On the lock one lemma is shared (`TurningInTime.rising_takes_all`): a rule set again from its share ends within the ceiling plus one, still locked, with no overdraw asked at the start (`locked_ends_rising`). Given there: that the level makes no difference to the rule while the need is met, as in item 1, and that the use rises with each step of the lock, which holds where the share is a count every favouring rule raises strictly and the use is that count times a positive amount (`count_rises`). A share is still not a draw; that tie is this premise. Two built examples were set beside the model. `LineageRegimes.MultiplyingTakers` has the sizes and levels of a course on which `way_ends`' premises hold. `LineageDrawdown.Drain`, gated, has those of a course on which another law's premises hold from step 1 (`fall`: a line that already takes more than is ever given, so the overdraw is given there). The laws add nothing about either, since each example's ending already follows from its own Lean. `ReadingsSpread.Commons` was not tried.
6. **How what selects comes to be and changes.** *Not reached.* In this model no new difference arrives from a difference not yet made (`ways_alike`): needs, uses and offspring are given, and nothing comes to read the fall because of the fall. What was done is the item step 2 left open, a correction that reaches what the rule is set from. Where a reading sets the share as well as the rule, to a share off which the corrected rule is read and which stays so under it, the line never ends exactly where the step at the first reading leaves the level at or above the corrected kind's point of no return (`locked_turns_in_time_share_iff`), and from the next step on the share and the rule are the corrected ones (`share_corrected_on_course`). That a reading does this is given, and so is the overdraw before it; also given, as in step 2, that the corrected kind draws one fixed amount, the lock's premises, and the premises on giving and on the need. On the band where correcting the rule alone ended the line, the line now goes on (`Three.share_read_goes_on`). The earlier work named here before (`Closing`, `Revision`, `HeldPossible`, `Web.consumed_continues_transformed`) was not joined.
7. **Losses that never come back.** A line with no unit in the way never has one again, given that no unit outside the line leaves a unit of it (`never_in`, `gone_from`, by descent). Units that take nothing and leave only their like can be lost with the level and every other unit as they were (`takes_nothing`): such a loss is not felt through the stock. It can still change whether anything goes on: in one small case such a unit is the only one still met, and with it something goes on, without it nothing does (`NothingTakenEnds`). That this is the only way is argued from `takes_nothing`, not a Lean statement. The loss of a unit that takes something is not in the model's laws. In one worked course it is what the rest goes on by (`WayOfUnits.With.goes_on`); in another the rest ends all the same (`WayOfUnits.Against.turns_and_ends`). The modules of 9 and 10 November on what persists were joined at one lemma (`kept_of_descent`). Reading a loss still does not settle what to do about it, and counting it stays the step the ethics offers.
8. **A case from outside.** No Lean. The proved conditions are put as [questions someone can ask of a case of their own](A-CASE-FROM-OUTSIDE.md), prepared on 16 November in English. Sending it to anyone, and any test built on it, are Ren's.
9. **In the grammar's own terms, where that joins.** Not a pass of its own. A stage states its conclusion as going on without end or ending (`«不穷»`, `«亡»`) where that joins it to the root, as steps 1 and 2 did. No conclusion is said again for the sake of the idiom (Ren, 16 November: the aim is not the language, or results said again).

Re-sayings are folded where a module is already open for one of these. Folding as its own pass was tried on 18 October: it added adapters and retired nothing. On 16 November one module came to rest on a more general one and got shorter (`WayOfUnits`, 1,182 lines to 1,126, thirteen helper declarations retired), while that landing as a whole added about 2,900 lines. If the size does not come down this way, we change it.

## Where the Lean we have stands

- About forty modules hold a statement that one of the stages needs or leans on.
- A quarter to a third of the theorems are built examples and kept failures. They stay: they are where a condition is shown to matter.
- Much is needed and bears on none of the stages: general mathematics; results that say what does not follow (`World.independent`, `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, `World.standing_not_read`); other fields read in Lean; 710 theorems named in classical Chinese; and the parts of the map this order does not reach. This plan gives them no way to move, and says so.
- No module is deleted. A module taken out of the build is named on the board with the reason and can come back. On 16 November thirteen helper declarations of `WayOfUnits` (twelve lemmas and one definition) were removed when it came to rest on the general module. Only its own proofs had used them, and those now go through the general forms; one record sentence named one (`anyGoes`) and was re-said. The record's history names them and the repository's history keeps them.

## Size, and following one understanding

The repository is too large to read. As something is understood better its telling should get shorter, and each landing is a chance to point several tellings at one place. Once a few of these stages hold, it should be possible to follow one understanding through: the statement, its proof, its conditions, its example, its test, where it is told.
