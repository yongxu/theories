## What the paper tries found

Three statements the first draft rested on were tried by hand before any Lean. All three failed as first worded. Each failure is kept.

- **The centre on the pair.** "A locked way never ends exactly where its first reading of the level comes before the point of no return" is false. On the numbers of `TurningInTime.Three`, a five-step course reads the level in time, recovers, is locked again and ends. A check that fires once, or only in a band, is undone when the level recovers, because every reading step sets the rule again from the share. The statement holds on paper only with a standing reading: from the first reading on, every reading step finds the level in the window.
- **Its one-way half holds.** A locked way that never reads the level ends, still locked, and the fall follows from the lock holding its draw. It needs one new lemma (along the lock's own course, share and rule are never below the start) and one premise said plainly (the draw reads the rule; the start already overdraws). The lock keeps an overdraw. It does not bring one about.
- **The two lemmas that were to carry the World to the pair.** False as first worded: a stock cycles where renewal goes on below the need; a built example's pool is not on the pair's step; a supplied label is not blindness. Written exactly they are true and near-definitional: balance plus the pair's two equations assumed of the World. That is a dictionary between two vocabularies, not two joins.
- **The hinge on the World** (14 November, on the direction page) was false as first worded too.

## Stages

Each stage is few landings. New Lean is claimed on the board first and lands by a branch, the gate, and a fast-forward merge. A core check is one heavy run; one at a time.

1. **The lock that keeps an overdraw.** On the pair of a way and a level (`TurningInTime`): the new lemma, then `locked_ends_with_net` with the level's fall derived from the lock instead of assumed. A few dozen lines in one module. *Moves, in part:* selection with what sustains it. *Not moved if:* the fall is still a premise, or the lock's premises do no work in the proof. *Started in scratch on 15 November. Its landing is the first core check and waits for his morning.*
2. **Turning in time for a lock, with a standing reading.** Marks on configurations, with marks on levels as their case. Either the exact statement with a standing reading, or a three-way split: ends locked; in time; in time for the level and too late for the lock (`ReadInTime.deadline`). *Must come out as cases, said beforehand:* `Three.goes_on`, `Three.turned_and_ends`, `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff`. *Moves:* selection with what can still form. *Can fail:* the exact form has no natural case with the start above the window.
3. **The World's own exact point.** Not the dictionary. At or above a level the family keeps (`Flow.joint_keeps`), below it the excess lowers it while supported (`Flow.excess_lowers`), after the break it stays below (`Flow.stays_below`); the pair's regime becomes a case. *Moves:* what sustains with what can still form. *Can fail:* the sign of giving less taking on each side of the level holds only in built examples.
4. **What is favoured draws more.** A line of descent that does not shrink, its offspring standing from a family it requires, overdraws it; the overdraw a conclusion. *Must come out:* the ending of `LineageDrawdown.Drain` and one conjunct of `ReadingsSpread.Commons`, no more. *Moves:* the rows of evolution. *Can fail:* a share is not a draw (`SelectionShift.Generations.shift_step`); then the link stays put in by hand, and that is said.
5. **How what selects comes to be and changes.** One statement of the loop: what is selected forms the coupling, and the coupling changes what is selected. The earlier work it starts from: couplings formed in a course (`Closing`), a lumping changed by the course's own acts (`Revision`), a tower of rules with no top (`HeldPossible`), and the theorem used nowhere (`Web.consumed_continues_transformed`), cited where it stands. *A seventh row, proposed.*
6. **Losses that never come back.** A loss a part causes makes a difference to a selection exactly where what is lost lies within what returns to the part. Starts from the modules of 9 and 10 November on what persists. *Must come out:* `Habitat.back_which_persist`. *Must stay consistent with:* `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, `Clearing.read_not_direction`. *Can fail:* a reading does not settle the direction, and counting the loss stays the step the ethics offers.
7. **A case placed from outside.** No Lean. The premises of stages 1 to 4 as questions someone outside can put to a case of their own, the prediction frozen first. New test designs and paid runs are held since 6 November, so this waits for him.
8. **Said in the grammar's own terms.** Each conclusion of stages 1 to 6 stated also as a region of the map: going on without end, generating, degenerating (`«不穷»`, `«生成»`, `«退化»`). This is where his question about Chinese meets the Lean.

There is no stage for folding re-sayings. A re-saying is folded only inside a landing that already changes its module. A separate folding stage would be the adapter of 18 October again.

## Where the Lean we have stands

A module's place is judged by what it holds, not by what imports it.

- **On a row:** about forty modules state or support a join or a near statement.
- **Cases:** corollaries of a law proved once. Many re-sayings are not yet cases; they become so only when a stage reaches them.
- **Built examples and kept failures:** a quarter to a third of the theorems. They stay, as cases a general law must return and as the record of where a premise is needed.
- **Needed and on no row:** general mathematics; calibrations and independence results (`World.independent`, `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, `World.standing_not_read`); other fields read in Lean; the 710 theorems named in classical Chinese; and the half of the map the four do not cover. This plan gives these no way to move. That is said, not hidden.
- **Set aside:** nothing, by this plan. A module leaves the build only on his word, with its bytes and history kept.

## Size, and following one understanding

Each landing says what it lets be folded or pointed to one place. No page or index is added to find things. Once a row is joined, one can follow it from the statement to its proof, its premises, its example, its test and where it is told; that is drawn from what is stored, later.

## Signs that this plan is wrong

- A stage lands and the row's missing premise is still a premise.
- A statement is proved and its built example has to be changed to come out of it.
- Stages 1 to 4 land and the four still cannot be told as one further than before.
- The plan grows stages.
