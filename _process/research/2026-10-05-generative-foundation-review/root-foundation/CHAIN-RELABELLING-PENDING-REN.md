## Why this is Ren's decision

The [generativity chain](../../../../theories/becoming/results/GENERATIVITY-CHAIN.md) is Ren's curated argument. It changes only through its [maintenance record](GENERATIVITY-CHAIN-INDEX-RECORD.md), and changes to it are proposed to Ren rather than made over his curation ([AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md), "Ren's own voice").

On 17 October Ren asked for the old labels to go from live text ([source](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-17-ren-words-not-authority.md)):

> no need for keeping "G0", "Part I", we should rename/revise/remove a lot of these noise, just make sure you commit all before doing this. My words should not hinder the most significant direction, and we are pushing the frontier, not my frontier

That relabel (`1038a699e`) covered the chapters, records and generated pages. It left the chain alone, because the chain is his curation. Ren's answer of 19 October, recorded as [D45](DELEGATED-CHOICES.md#d45-一个说明中的读法--the-readings-in-one-account), settles the readings in one account and keeps the chain apart: it changes "only through proposals to Ren" (D45 (6)). So the chain still uses the retired labels. Its pages now read differently from the chapter, which explains those labels only in its glossary ([Retired labels](../../../../theories/becoming/history/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#retired-labels)).

## Options

**(a) Relabel in place (recommended).** Replace each label in the table with its current term, in both languages. Each entry keeps its claim, scope, order and links. C17's title "The explicit generative-axiom route" would become "The conditional alignment route" ("条件对齐路线"). G0 would be named once per entry as living significance, and the status clause A3 added would shorten to "which follows under the continuation condition (§6.15, not asserted)". P-numbers would leave claims and scopes; the records stay linked. Link labels change only where they carry a label.

**(b) Keep the labels and add a key.** The chain's opening scope gains one sentence pointing to the chapter's Retired labels table. The entries keep their wording.

**(c) Relabel the status-bearing labels only.** G0, J1–J4 and the axiom-route phrases change as in (a). P-numbers and "folding" stay as provenance.

**Recommendation: (a).** Ren's 17 October words ask for exactly this, and the chain is now the only live argument that still needs the glossary to be read. P-numbers carry no content the linked records lack. What Ren would decide is whether his curated argument is to be read in the theory's current words. The cost is one careful pass over 15 entries and their Chinese texts.

## How to answer

A reply such as "a", "b", "c" or a correction works. Ren's answer is recorded verbatim in `sources/` first. A board item then applies it as one dated entry of the maintenance record, with the chain re-rendered and checked. Whichever option is taken, the chain's historical evidence is not refreshed silently: the entry lists every field changed.
