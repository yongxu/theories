## Reconstructing the relationship before isolating a decoder

Ren's [source lineage](CORE-TREE-SOURCE-READING.md) connects accumulation, formation and knowing: a formed structure enables further structures; mind, feedback and environment are interwoven rather than separately privileged substrates; learning and generative understanding arise within that activity. The historical sources do not certify every later formalization or a universal favorable direction. They specify the positive relationship that the formalization must respect.

The preceding [receipt-identification study](../history/root-foundation/RECEIPT-IDENTIFICATION-INTEGRATION.md) validly characterized what an action/receipt pair identifies under its declared relation. Its results about outages, staleness and slow correction remain intact. The trajectory error was to make another isolated decoder condition the next foundational advance while treating the rest of the generative relationship as separate unfinished domains. That local abstraction did not establish their fundamental separateness.

The existing paid episode already contains a more connected account. Its enacted coupling both produces a return and changes the resources for continuing. The retained receipt participates in the later write and in renewal or depletion of the very capacities that make further episodes possible. The acquired score affects subsequent enacted responses. There is a loop of formation and further formative effects here, not merely a neutral world reported to an independent information processor.

The actor/plant names remain useful coordinates of this local model. This reconstruction does not claim that their boundary or sensing architecture has now been generated from nothing. It makes explicit which relationship the existing decomposition preserves, and which developmental relationship it still omits.

## One actual episode, with differentiated consequences

[GenerativeEpisode.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/learning/construction/prediction/GenerativeEpisode.lean) imports only the unchanged PredictionAction law. It reconstructs the actual intermediate states of every admitted rent → work → write → finish episode and proves their joint relations. No new state, transition, receipt override, decoder, reward or controller is installed.

Let the actual episode begin at state \(s\), with fuel \(F\), score \(q\), retained action reference and live selector/updater. Let \(a\) be the command produced by that current state, \(z\) the regime at work, and \(r\) its actual receipt. Under the existing guaranteed-availability work law,

\[
r=(a=z).
\]

The same actual episode yields all of the following together:

| Aspect of the episode | Derived relation |
| --- | --- |
| Enacted response and evidence | The work endpoint retains action \(a\) and receipt \(r\), formed by the actual coupling. |
| Acquired revision | The paid write applies the installed updater to \(q\) and the label decoded from that same \((a,r)\); under this law that label equals event-time \(z\). |
| Available fuel | Rent, work, any capture-dependent maintenance, and writing occur in their original order, with the actual capture determining the return. |
| Material opportunity | Exactly one source packet is consumed. |
| Subsequent capacity | The same \(r\) resets both leases on success or decrements them on failure. |
| Retained history | The regime and enacted action reference persist through completion; the transient receipt is then cleared. |

For rent cost \(c_r\), trial cost \(c_w\), packet energy \(e\), maintenance \(m\), and writing cost \(c_q\), the exact installed fuel expression is

\[
F'=\begin{cases}
(((F\mathbin{\dot-}c_r)\mathbin{\dot-}c_w)+e)\mathbin{\dot-}m\mathbin{\dot-}c_q,&r=1,\\
((F\mathbin{\dot-}c_r)\mathbin{\dot-}c_w)\mathbin{\dot-}c_q,&r=0.
\end{cases}
\]

Here \(\dot-\) is natural-number subtraction. Keeping its exact order matters: the general parameter type does not itself ensure that a packet covers maintenance. The existing stronger energy-conservation theorem uses its own admissibility condition; this new relation does not smuggle that condition into an allegedly unconditional equation. Actual episode existence supplies the event guards, but no episode is asserted for every arbitrary start.

One further theorem fixes the relevance of a counterexample. Under this existing work law, two actual work steps with the same enacted command and event-time regime have the same receipt. Flipping only a receipt while claiming to preserve that coupling is not another allowed work event. A different availability law, a corrupting channel, or different coupling can be a substantive alternative; it must be represented as such. The earlier stochastic outage evidence remains relevant at its separate stated implementation, and is not erased by this deterministic theorem.

The result establishes an already present causal relationship that the narrower decoder framing obscured. It does not establish that every causal record is knowledge, support is welfare, or successful local continuation is ethically good. Correct failure evidence can coexist with lost support, and a correctly updated score can still leave the next command unchanged. Formation of the operative correspondence and revision of its practical scope remain live explanatory tasks **within** generative activity.

## A branch can become a root articulation without gaining independent authority

The existing [inquiry-tree calculus](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/GenerativeClosure.lean) had already shown that derived-lemma reuse preserves semantic necessity when the basis has that status. The new [CoreTreeRearticulation.lean](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/inquiry-root/CoreTreeRearticulation.lean) establishes the stronger proof-level relation without assuming a necessity predicate or a truth valuation for the basis.

Write \(B;\Gamma\vdash p\) for the existing finite derivation judgment, retaining its local context \(\Gamma\), and let \(T(B)\) contain the closed branches already derived from \(B\), each with its original derivation. Then

\[
T(B);\Gamma\vdash p\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad B;\Gamma\vdash p.
\]

Every reused branch can be expanded into its earlier derivation. Conversely, each original basis member already occurs as a branch. The proof handles all eight existing constructors, including implication introduction with its local premise. It does not erase context or manufacture a new warrant when a branch is promoted to a reusable starting point.

More generally, two bases have exactly the same contextual derivations iff each generates every member of the other without additional local assumptions. Adding an arbitrary proposed branch is conservative exactly when it was already closed-derivable. Thus a presentation's root/branch distinction can change while its derivational content remains the same; an unsupported addition cannot hide behind that change of presentation.

This is a contribution to one mathematical practice within the generative tree. It is not a reduction of all formation, discovery, experience or philosophical revision to deduction. Conservative reuse also need not preserve proof length, storage, execution cost or the history of discovery. A substantive revision of the theory can change its conclusions; these theorems neither forbid that revision nor justify it automatically. The general proof method is established in kind, not claimed as a new discovery in logic or a proof of nondual ontology.

## What changes the continuing research path

The decoder study remains a supporting analysis. It no longer determines the governing next step. The continuing connection is **formed, potentially overlapping participation → retained and revisable distinctions of consequence → changed practical scope and further formation**. The earlier response-range construction and the actual acquired predictor each supply part of that question; they have not been silently spliced into one already completed developmental history.

The next substantive construction must trace how a distinction comes to guide participation and how its consequences can revise that very distinction or its scope. It must preserve actual continuities and effects on others, including destructive uses of accurate understanding. An analyst's favorable scalar cannot supply the significance being explained. A mere role label, retained report or increasing theorem count cannot stand in for this generative relationship.

The relevant challenges are now specific. Does the alleged acquired distinction causally participate, or is a fixed route doing the work? Does its use preserve the relevant continuing organization while exhausting another indispensable relation? Does a change in practical boundary track actual changed dependence, or only relabel it? Can a rival explain the same independently specified episode and learning history more adequately? Those tests can narrow or change the account. A missing relation in a model is first an adequacy question for that representation; an unresolved mechanism requires further explanation; a preserved counterexample to a fully stated claim can defeat that claim.

Ren's no-assumptions objective remains active. Locally supplied law, formal background and unestablished experiential or ethical relationships are still obligations, but they are not thereby entities standing outside generative reality. The advance is to reconstruct and test their relationship within the unfolding, rather than infer separateness from the limitations of an earlier decomposition.
