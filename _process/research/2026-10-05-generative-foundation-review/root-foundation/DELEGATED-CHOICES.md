## 0. How to read this record

**What a decision does and does not do.** It moves a reading from “pending Ren” to adopted or declined, with its reasons and what would change it. It changes no Lean statement or checked theorem, adds no premise to any law, and turns no reading into a proof; the theorems of a declined reading stay. If a better argument revises a decision, the reading changes, not the mathematics.

**The standard used (the AI's).**
1. **Ren's words first.** Where Ren's words settle a reading, the decision follows them and says so; where several readings fit them, it takes the one Ren's other words and the checked results support.
2. **No added premise.** It takes the reading that adds no premise: no weight, order, privileged level or boundary, and no claims, ownership or representation.
3. **What a checked result can decide, it decides.** A reading a theorem excludes is declined.
4. **Exact scope.** A reading is adopted only with its theorems' scope; an analogy is adopted as an analogy, not as an identification.
5. **Relation first.** No reading is adopted that brings in separate owners of value, privileged centers, or claims of one party on another; totalizing readings are answered through what the relation actually does.
6. **Nothing lost.** Declined readings and their reasons stay on record.

**Standard 1 restated (19 October 2026, R21; nineteenth review E5).** Ren's words are provenance, not warrant ([AGENTS.md](../../../../AGENTS.md); D45). Where a reading concerns a term of Ren's, his texts are the evidence of what the term means, and the reading stands or is revised by its place in one account of the theory, argued from the work. Until this date standard 1 read “Ren's words first … the decision follows them”; the decisions below that cite his words as settling a reading are read with this standard, and D45 revises those whose place in the account differs. The original text above is kept.

## 1. 生生 and the generative space

<a id="d1-生生的读法--how-生生-is-read"></a>
### How 生生 is read

Where: section 8, Ren's choices, first item; the records above.

**Decision.** 生生 is read as the generative space: the widening of what can form (`Web.Widens`; with the axiom as a condition, retaining and adding what has significance, `Web.widens_iff_retains_adds`). The three scopes of Ren's answer of 7 October 2026 (生 broadly; biological life and ecological flourishing; every generative process that keeps forming new organization) are read as scopes of what counts as a formation: each gives a space with its own widening, and in each 生生 is the widening of that space, not any one formation in it. G0 and C share this reading: courses are compared on possibilities (section 6.12). The readings of `living`, `Flow.More` and arising that recurs stay as readings of what is counted, not as definitions of 生生.
**Reasons.** (1) Ren names the direction by what it produces: “一种整体的，不断创造更多生的方向，而这个方向产生的可能性，多样性越高，复杂性/复杂结构的复杂度可以不断的上升，就是一个好的方向” (7 October 2026, [words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-shengsheng-ethical-direction.md)) and “being generative is its existence creates more possibilities of becoming - more future, not less” (10 October 2026). Widening what can form is that, and Ren confirmed possibilities as what can form next. (2) It adds no premise: with “better” read as widening, C is a law on level paths for every reading of `living` (`Web.paths_comparison_widens`). (3) It does not count every new organization as 生生 (G0's object is 生生): a malignant formation's events are themselves included (`Ranking.malignant_possible`), but its part lessens what others can form, so it does not widen; sharing ranks above winning by consumption (`Ranking.possible_contest`). (4) Ren said of the three scopes “我觉得这几个都很有意义，都可以是，并且可能会得到不同的范围”; keeping them as scopes does that.
**What would change it.** A reading of 生生, argued from Ren's texts, other than this one; a widening part that an argument from the work shows degenerative, or the reverse; a measure that orders mixed parts, adopted openly as a premise beyond widening (“more futures” is a partial order, and parts that each exceed the other stay unranked).

**Revision, taken by Ren (18 October 2026).** 生生 is read as generation generating generation: what forms preserves, restores, transmits or creates the conditions and generators of further forming, so a course changes what can form, and the capacity to form, along with the state. This is the definition in Ren's treatise 生生不息论 v1, under its heading “生生：生成者生成新的生成”: “这一次生成的结果，又保存、恢复、传递或创造了下一轮生成的条件与生成者。普通生成改变接下来的状态；生生还改变接下来能够形成哪些状态，以及世界能否继续发现新的形成方式” ([text](../../../../sources/06-zhishan/shengsheng/生生不息论v1/生生不息论.md)); Ren said the same on 10 October 2026: “it is about if the continuation of becoming continues itself”. The core states this structure in laws, not in one predicate: with nothing given from beyond, what can form is opened only by what the supported give (D35); where arising depletes finitely many conditions, unceasing generation needs renewal made within the unfolding (`Web.Unceasing.activeRenewer_recurs`); a whole whose parts pass persists only through the generation of its parts ([whole-persists-through-generation](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)); a renewing formation does not consume (`Web.renewing_not_consuming`). The generative space is what can form now (the treatise: “在当下，生显为尚可成为的可能性空间”; D22). The direction of 生生, “more 生” and “more future, not less”, is compared by widening what can form (`Web.Widens`), with the four cases of D2; the treatise's “生生维护的不是选项数量” agrees, since widening is inclusion, not a count of options. The rest of the decision stands: the three scopes as scopes of what is counted, courses compared on possibilities, and 生生 as the object of the ethical direction rather than any new organization. The Lean predicate `living` does not read 生生: its readings do not include further generation. **What changed.** The decision above read 生生 as the generative space, the widening of what can form. A space and the widening of a space are different things, and widening compares, between two level paths, only one component of the treatise's (state, 可成为之域, 生成能力). Renewal capacity and richness of relations (Ren, 10 October 2026) stay measures not yet read (chapter §8.2). **What would change it.** A reading, argued from Ren's texts, under which 生生 names only the direction (then 生生 is the widening, and the generative space what is widened); a case that renews the conditions and generators of its own further forming which a reading argued from Ren's texts shows is not 生生; adopting renewal capacity or richness of relations as the measure of “more”, a premise beyond widening, since it orders mixed parts. The arguments are in the [decision brief](../history/root-foundation/READINGS-PENDING-REN.md#a1-生生-d1-and-the-generative-space-d22); Ren's answer ([words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-18-central-readings-taken.md)) is its provenance, not its warrant. The original text above is kept.

**What widening counts (20 October 2026, R25; twentieth review D6, F6).** `Web.Widens` compares which members of the world's type of formations `F`, given in advance, the levels admit at given thresholds. A course that brings about a kind of formation or requirement outside `F` widens nothing on this measure, so 生生 as read in the revision above, creating the conditions and generators of further forming, can exceed what widening registers. Widening over a grown world (`Flow.GrownBy`, `Becoming.Cuts`) is open (chapter §8.1, item 5). `Flow.GrownBy` says how the conditions of one flow over fixed types of formations and conditions were attached, a structure of sharing, not a course in which the type of formations grows, so widening over a grown world needs a reading of one world's formations in another's, which no core module states (R34, 20 October 2026). Since this revision only C shares the reading: the `living` readings include no further generation, so living significance does not read 生生 (chapter §8.3). The original text above is kept.

### Generative and degenerative

**Decision.** The proposal's “generative, degenerative” name the two directions of the generative space, read at two levels that the margin theorems join. For a part, what it does to what can form: it is generative where the levels with it widen those without it, degenerative where those without it widen those with it, neutral where each includes the other, and mixed where each exceeds the other (section 6.13; `Web.Widens`, `Web.Keeps`, `Web.Mixed`; the four cases exclude one another). For a formation, what it does to the conditions of continued formation of the relations it takes part in: renewing and consuming formations (Ren asked on 9 October 2026 that the two be separated by relational structure, [words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-renewing-consuming-without-axioms.md); `Web.renewing_not_consuming`). The margin joins them: a part retains where its net draw stays within the margin and loses where it exceeds it (`Flow.retains_iff_within_margin_at`, `Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`). The reading of activity (arising recurring, ceasing recurring) is declined as the reading of “generative, degenerative”: in finite worlds the two are one part, and Ren wrote “generative和degenerate虽然都在一个becoming model上，但是他们不overlap吧”. Arising and ceasing remain the names of the activity reading. This also answers reading the four cases as generative, degenerative and neutral: widening is generative, narrowing degenerative and keeping the same neutral; mixed is not neutral but the case that needs a measure.
**Reasons.** The four cases exclude one another, so generative and degenerative do not overlap, as Ren requires; no significance, standpoint or boundary enters, so it is a reading of Part I; Ren said on 10 October 2026 “there are also neutral and generative dynamics that can be identified (degenerative/neutral/generative)”, and the three classes are widening, keeping and narrowing.
**What would change it.** A part that narrows what can form and that an argument from the work shows generative; a reading, argued from Ren's texts, that defines the pair only at the level of formations (renewing and consuming).

**Revision in the one account (20 October 2026, R34; twentieth review D2, F13)** [AI reading]. The decision read the pair at two levels, joined by the margin: parts that widen or narrow, and formations that renew or consume. The second level is dropped from the names. What the core now shows joins the run classes to the order by laws, not by identity ([the-run-classes-and-widening](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/the-run-classes-and-widening.md), `Becoming.Retaining`). In a balanced flow where only active formations take, and only from what they draw on, a part whose every draw falls on a condition renewed over the whole run keeps what can form, so it does not narrow (`Flow.Balances.includes_of_renewed`, `Flow.Balances.not_narrows_of_renewed`; a part of formations renewing over the run is a case, `Flow.Balances.includes_of_renewingIn`); and where a dependence is consumed and the levels without a part meet it later at no smaller need, a part giving each condition no more than it takes narrows (`Flow.Balances.narrows_of_consumed`). That is one direction each, under premises, and two witnesses mark the boundary: the preempting drawer is within renewal at its step and consuming over the run, and its part narrows (`Preemption.renewingAt_consuming_narrows`), so being within renewal at a step does not decide keeping; and a part can be consuming over the run while taking nothing, and keep what can form the same (`Untaken.consuming_keeps`), so consuming alone does not narrow. Renewing is keeping, not widening: a renewing part need not be generative. So "renewing" and "consuming" are not the formation-level meaning of "generative" and "degenerative"; they are classes of a formation over a run, related to the part's place in the order by the laws above. On level paths the condition, significance and C read one object, admission (`Web.paths_one_object`): the condition fixes which admissions count, and C, read as widening, orders them. **What changed.** The sentence that at the level of formations generative and degenerative are renewing and consuming formations; the four cases, the decline of the activity reading and the non-overlap stand. **What would change it.** A law identifying a run class with widening or narrowing under premises the representation already carries (the boundary witnesses show none of the two classes does alone); a measure of the capacity to form under which renewing is widening. The original text above is kept.

<a id="d3-第二个生的范围以消耗为条件的生起--the-scope-of-the-second-生-arising-that-depends-on-depletion"></a>
### The scope of the second 生; arising that depends on depletion

**Decision.** The second 生 ranges over what can form within the adopted scope (D1): formations whose requirements a course's levels meet, active or not, including dormant ones and ones not yet formed. Arising that depends on depletion is not classed on its own: it is generative where the course with it widens what can form and degenerative where it narrows it, as the comparison decides (“worse” goes through C).
**Reasons.** Classing single arisings would need a further premise and could overlap (one event both arises and depletes); the comparison decides it with no premise and keeps decomposition, scavenging and succession after a death within the account without crediting them as such. By [unceasing-generation-needs-renewal](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/unceasing-generation-needs-renewal.md), where arising depletes finitely many conditions, unceasing generation needs renewal to recur.
**What would change it.** A reading of 生, argued from Ren's texts, restricted to living formations or to one of the three scopes (the space narrows; the laws are unchanged).

**Revision in the one account (19 October 2026, D45).** The second 生 is read as further generation: what can form, together with the conditions and generators of further forming, which D1's revision makes part of 生生 (a course changes what can form, and the capacity to form, along with the state). What can form stays the part the comparison reads, so arising that depends on depletion is still classed through it: generative where the course with it widens what can form, degenerative where it narrows it. The capacity to form, renewal capacity and richness of relations among it, is not yet a measure (chapter §8.2), so it enters no comparison. **What changed.** The decision above read the second 生 as what can form only, narrower than D1's revision, which Ren took on 18 October. **What would change it.** As above; a measure of the capacity to form, which would let the comparison read it. The original text above is kept.

<a id="d4-体现--embodiment"></a>
### Embodiment

**Decision.** Embodied relata, which exchange exactly where they stand, are read as 生: formations whose living is their standing. Generation is not restricted to embodiment: held conditions, dormant formations and relations count wherever they are among what can form. Embodiment stays a premise of the laws that use it, not a definition ([ZERO-PREMISE-PLAN](../history/root-foundation/ZERO-PREMISE-PLAN.md) §1.4).
**Reasons.** The possibility reading counts formations active or not (section 6.12), so restricting generation to embodiment would drop dormancy (seed banks) and held conditions; P107 lists embodiment as a state of a part (what stands without exchanging is dormant).
**What would change it.** A reading of 生, argued from Ren's texts, as embodied life only.

<a id="d5-接续与生生不息的层次--succession-and-the-level-of-生生不息"></a>
### Succession and the level of 生生不息

**Decision.** Succession is read through the formed relations of continuation: interaction, living on, descent and conveyance (D9, D12); a tenure on the working reading continues through succession as far as the theorems of [identity-across-steps](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/identity-across-steps.md) say. A successor inherits its predecessor's loss exactly where the succession keeps the tenure: the predecessor's need met above continues in the successor, and later drawing below it undermines (`Succession.readings`); where the dependence lapses, the reading starts afresh (`Gap.lapse_starts_afresh`). 生生不息 is read at the level of the whole, as Ren's “一种整体的……方向” says: generation that does not cease, arising that recurs through renewal. In a bounded whole whose rest is idle this is return, not novelty without end (`Flow.whole_recurs_of_rest_idle`, `Flow.whole_not_new_of_rest_idle`, P111); endless novelty needs gain from beyond every bounded part (`Flow.new_beyond_every`). Lineages, members and families are levels within the whole at which continuation is read.
**Reasons.** Ren's words; P111; Ren on 25 September 2026: “we can grow exponentially till we met our need, but we can't compounding forever” ([words](../../../../sources/16-claude-code-directions/2026-09-25-the-mind-not-the-approach.md)); a persisting whole whose parts are transient needs parts to keep arising ([whole-persists-through-generation](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)), so at the whole's level “不断创造” holds, which removes the tension with Ren's words that reading it only as a lineage's continuation had.
**What would change it.** A reading of 生生不息, argued from Ren's texts, as endless novelty (it then needs unbounded gain, whose exact form P111 gives), or at the level of one member or one lineage; with succession read only at the level of members, loss is not inherited with the tenure.

**Revision, taken by Ren (18 October 2026).** 不息 keeps the reading above: arising recurs through renewal, so the source of generation does not dry up. This is the treatise's 不息, “不是永不消亡，而是不使生成之源枯竭”, and its 不绝其源, with its five tests of true 不息 as necessary conditions ([text](../../../../sources/06-zhishan/shengsheng/生生不息论v1/生生不息论.md)). 生生不息 is read as 生生 (D1) going on at the whole with more still able to form: not a return into copies of existing forms, which the treatise excludes (“不是现有形式的无限复制”). Complexity can keep rising, as Ren's answer of 7 October 2026 says (“复杂性/复杂结构的复杂度可以不断的上升”), though nothing requires it to rise; the treatise's true 不息 “不是数量的无限增加”, and Ren's “we can't compounding forever”, exclude reading it as growth. A whole that only returns has 不息 but not 生生不息. A closed cycle whose total stays one, in which arising never ceases, is such a whole (`Recycle.closed_unceasing`); under the first law a finite whole whose rest gives nothing net stays within its stock (`Flow.whole_bounded_of_rest_idle`) and, with natural-number amounts, keeps revisiting configurations it has had (`Flow.whole_recurs_of_rest_idle`). P111 says what more needs, without redefining it: under the first law a family new at every step is given more than it takes, from the rest or across its boundary, at arbitrarily late steps, and so is every bounded family containing it (`Flow.new_beyond_every`): inflow from beyond, not growth. So a part fed from beyond, as the Earth is by the sun, can have 生生不息 while the inflow lasts; the whole can have it only if it is not a finite whole under the first law, and whether the actual whole is such a whole is empirical correspondence (chapter §8.7), not a reading. Scope: return is forced here by natural-number amounts, a commitment of the representation; with real amounts a bounded whole need not revisit a configuration exactly, and what replaces return there is open (chapter §8.5, the scope of representation). Succession and the level of the whole stand as decided above. **What changed.** The decision above counted return as 生生不息. **What would change it.** A reading, argued from Ren's texts, that counts return as 生生不息 (the decision above then stands), or that requires novelty at every step rather than more still able to form (the same theorem gives what that needs); reading 不塞其流 alone as the criterion, under which a closed cycle, whose flow is not blocked, would count. The arguments are in the [decision brief](../history/root-foundation/READINGS-PENDING-REN.md#a2-生生不息-d5); Ren's answer ([words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-18-central-readings-taken.md)) is its provenance, not its warrant. The original text above is kept.

## 2. Endings, underminings, openness and held conditions

<a id="d6-终结之步的汲取与侵蚀--drawing-across-an-ending-and-undermining"></a>
### Drawing across an ending, and undermining

**Decision.** The stepwise reading's definition is neither widened nor narrowed, and its verdicts stay as theorems: drawing on a self-bearing embodied relatum across the step at which it ends breaks its own dependence (`Embodied.ending_breaks`), so on the stepwise reading the drawer's event is not living, for every drawer, with no narrower class of drawers chosen; undermining a dependence that persists unchanged leaves the relatum depending not active after the step (`Web.Dependent.inactive_below`), its ending where it was active (`Web.Dependent.ceases_below`). These verdicts are not taken as valuations of the drawers: they do not decide which drawers are worse. Whether an ending or an undermining makes a course worse is decided by the comparison on possibilities, which counts at each step what the levels admit, active or not, by what it removes from what can form, not by a verdict on the event.
**Reasons.** The verdicts are theorems of the definition, not further choices; no comparison uses `living` any longer (section 6.12), and choosing a class of drawers for it would add a premise to a reading no comparison uses; “worse” goes through C.
**What would change it.** An argument from the work for a reading of `living` in the comparison, or for a class of drawers in the stepwise reading.

<a id="d7-开放与增益--openness-and-gain"></a>
### Openness and gain

**Decision.** “Openness” names exchange beyond every finite family (as in `exposed`, at zero net). Inflow is not openness but gain, and the two are kept apart: continued change needs openness ([continued-change-needs-openness](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md)); novelty needs gain, which under the first law comes from the rest or across the boundary (P111, `Flow.rise_from_rest_or_open`, `Flow.new_beyond_every`).
**Reasons.** The theorems use the two apart: `exposed` is open at zero net. Reading openness as inflow would make “continued change needs openness” weaker than what is proved, and would merge it with the gain that P111 proves novelty needs.
**What would change it.** A reading of “open”, argued from Ren's texts, as net inflow (the readings become: continued change needs exchange beyond, novelty needs inflow; the theorems are unchanged).

<a id="d8-所持条件被表示时的估值--valuation-where-a-held-condition-is-represented"></a>
### Valuation where a held condition is represented

**Decision.** Valuation uses the possibility reading (D1): a reserve counts as a condition that keeps a formation among what can form across dormancy. The reading in which dependence transfers onto the held condition is the dynamics' reading (it satisfies `Dependent` by construction), and the readings of `living` no longer value.
**Reasons.** Section 6.12; the possibility reading counts formations active or not, which is what a reserve keeps.
**What would change it.** As for D1.

<a id="d9-以之为生世系与新的生起--living-on-descent-and-new-arising"></a>
### Living on, descent and new arising

**Decision.** Living on and descent are adopted as formed relations of continuation (relations along which N_rel transmits where that completion is used, and through which succession is read, D5), not as direct valuations. Whether bringing about new arising makes an outcome better: it ranks above its absence exactly where it widens what can form; a formation that adds no possibility ties with its absence (`Ranking.possible_tie`), and the theory adds no order to break the tie, since possibilities first would also rank a rest below uninterrupted activity (`Ranking.Pause.pause_ranked_below`), counting a pause as a loss. The value of bringing new lives into existence beyond widening stays outside the theory (section 6.6).
**Reasons.** A direct valuation adds a weight, a premise, and credits continuation as such; as relations they enter extent and significance with no weight.
**What would change it.** A valuation of descent, or a measure, adopted openly as a premise.

**Restated in current terms (20 October 2026, R36; twentieth review F11).** (3) “With the axiom read as `Unrestricted` (D16), G0 no longer needs transmission along `related`” reads: under the continuation condition (chapter §6.4), which the theory does not assert, living significance needs no transmission along `related`, so the discipline does not bear on it; the Reasons' “the axiom gives significance directly” reads the same way. ### The derived extent and conveyance

**Decision.** (1) The derived extent, with no axiom, runs backward along dependence to what a continuation depends on (the feeding closure), and forward along reach to what its exchange reaches (the reach closure); every formed relation enters only through N_rel, in the other completion. Ren's “i think there are two evolution attractor forces, one is for the self(life, and what's within that life, within ecosystem and beyond), and another one is the whole, which is also a force of evolution. and everything exists in between. so this is a relation that is self recursive - system and subsystem” (10 October 2026, [words](../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-centers-within-the-dynamics.md)) is read through the five closures: the self, the feeding closure and the influence closure nest within the system, and the reach closure lies within it too ([levels-of-closure](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/levels-of-closure.md)). <a id="d13-不损--not-harm"></a>
### Not harm

**Decision.** “Not harm” is read as retaining: a part does no harm where the levels with it retain what has significance without it (section 6.8). Retaining counts what has not yet formed (`Preemption`); not harm is not read as the stepwise reading's not undermining, which counts only dependences met. It is neither a filter on which continuations have significance nor a claim of one continuation on others: it is a relation between the levels with and without a part. Its boundary is the margin: a part loses a continuation exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin (`Flow.loses_iff_beyond_margin_at`), the form of Ren's “a phase transition with a clear boundary, not some "outside will or belief"”. A loss enters judgment through C, which ranks lossless extensions.
**Reasons.** As a filter on members' continuations, nearly every life would lose significance, for almost all life continues by ending other life; as a claim on others it would add a premise of claims (relations, not rights); retaining adds no premise under the axiom, and its boundary is Ren's phase transition; the stepwise reading would count a preemptive drawer as harmless (`Preemption.not_undermining_yet_loses`).
**What would change it.** A reading of “not harm”, argued from Ren's texts, as a constraint on acts rather than a comparison of courses.

**Restated in current terms (19 October 2026, R21; nineteenth review E5).** “Retaining adds no premise under the axiom” reads: retaining adds no premise under the continuation condition of chapter §6.15, which the theory does not assert. The retired label “the axiom” is explained once, in the chapter's glossary. The original text above is kept.

<a id="d14-损的层次与形成的个体化--the-level-of-harm-and-the-individuation-of-formations"></a>
### The level of harm and the individuation of formations

**The level (the AI's reading; Ren's words of 10 October 2026 its provenance).** Harm is read at the level of the whole, a weaker version within the system one is in, then the self (Ren, 10 October 2026, see section 0), with “the whole” the actual whole, never any center's model of it, and comparison by inclusion never licensing an imposed ending. Section 6.8 gives this order as an order of how complete a judgment is (`Web.Retains.within`). The comparison over the whole is the comparison within every region at once (`Web.includes_iff_within_every`), so reading harm at the whole misses no part's loss: what narrows within any region narrows the whole.
**Decision (the AI's): the individuation of formations**, which fixes what counts as a loss (section 6.8). Formations are individuated at both levels `Becoming.Identity` gives, each with its use. (1) To class a part as degenerative, neutral or generative (D2), formations are counted as lineages, kinds that renew: taking within renewal is then no harm, and the margin of renewal is the phase boundary of D13. (2) A member's ending is still a loss of that member's continuation: no comparison by inclusion licenses it, and a part that adds its own continuation while ending a member is mixed, unranked without a measure.
**Reasons.** Counting only members would make nearly every eating a harm, with no phase boundary at renewal, against Ren's three classes; counting only lineages would let a member's ending count for nothing, a totalizing reading; with both, Ren's phase transition has its place and no ending is licensed.
**What would change it.** An argument from the work for individuating only members or only lineages; a measure trading possibilities against each other (a further premise).

### Junctures, commuting laws and the third value

Where: chapter §1, "Where a configuration is lost" and "What a commuting law keeps apart", §3, "A family of readings within what it reads" (the liar with a third value), and the glossary; the records [every-loss-passes-a-juncture](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/every-loss-passes-a-juncture.md), [what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart.md) and [revision-with-an-undecided-value](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/revision-with-an-undecided-value.md); study section 199 ([SG](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#199-junctures-what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart-and-the-third-value-sg)). **Ren's words.** On 19 October 2026, asking that the earlier 生生不息 repository be read for what can be integrated: "please take a look of this … many insights can be integrated" (abridged, as quoted with the [review](SSBX-REPOSITORY-REVIEW.md) in commit `c599d83db`; the full message is not in `sources/`). Provenance; each reading stands on its law.

**Decision.** Adopted with exact scope. (1) **机 is a juncture.** The earlier text's 机, two continuations leading to different open futures, is read with the open future as one configuration still to be had: a juncture for `s` has a step after which `s` can still be had and one after which it cannot (`Branching.Juncture`). A profile of several possibilities at once is not formalized. (2) **护闭 and 成闭 at a juncture.** 护闭 is keeping, the largest closure that keeps `s` to be had again on its course from `s` (`Branching.keeping`, `Branching.keeping_greatest`); 成闭 is losing, the steps after which `s` is never had again (`Branching.losing_not_returns`). They are one operation at a juncture, removing the losing steps or the keeping ones. (3) **What finding the place asks.** Every loss of a returning configuration passes a juncture, up to double negation, and finding it is deciding whether `s` can still be had; asked of every process, that is the limited principle of omniscience (`Branching.lpo_of_junctures_found`). The place of a loss is there on every course; where it is is known where reach is decided, as for configurations a list holds with decided steps. (4) **Many options make no juncture** (`Branching.no_juncture_of_keeps`, `Branching.Many.no_juncture`): two options are needed and are not enough. (5) **What a symmetry carries.** A law that keeps a symmetry moving every configuration never merges what the symmetry tells apart (`Law.Commutes.apart`); to merge it a law must break the symmetry. In the Changes, 消息 never merges 陰 and 陽 changed line for line, and the waning into 坤 breaks 錯 (`Hexagram.waxWane_apart`, `Hexagram.wane_breaks`). The earlier topology's "«一» structurally does not exist" and its table equating Borsuk–Ulam with the second law are not taken; the law is. (6) **The third value 疑.** The undecided value is what revision has not decided, not a third truth and not superposition (the review's "不确定 ≠ 不可知"). The earlier "三值保守律" (U ⇏ ⊤) is `Undecided.eval_undecided` for terms without constants, and along the run `Undecided.stays_undecided`. The diagonal's gap reappears as undecidedness (`Undecided.undecided_of_moves`, `Undecided.rest_undecided`). (7) **Not read into it.** No valuation: which configuration is worth keeping, whether keeping or erasing a difference supports or damages 生生, and which sentences ought to be decided are the comparison's to say (§6.15).
**Reasons.** Each reading names the declarations it rests on and says no more than they prove. The double negation and the LPO calibration are read as what finding a juncture asks of the unfolding, not as a gap in the proof.
**What would change it.** A formal profile of openness at a juncture that the reading through one configuration misses; a process in which every loss has its juncture found without deciding reach (which would need LPO there); a law keeping a symmetry that moves every configuration and merging a pair (impossible by `Law.Commutes.apart`).

Where: [Becoming.SharedCodes](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SharedCodes.lean); the record [a-code-formed-between-formations](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/a-code-formed-between-formations.md); the chapter [CHINESE-SOURCES-AND-BECOMING](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/CHINESE-SOURCES-AND-BECOMING.md) §8 and four passages of its map (同则同之, 名无固宜, the 說文's 止戈 gloss, 书同文); canonical chapter §4; study section 210 ([SC](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#210-a-code-formed-between-formations-sc)). **What would change it.** A law of the spine under which a symmetric disagreement settles without breaking a symmetry (which `never_agrees_of_both` excludes for every law it covers); a reading of 约定俗成, argued from the 正名 and its commentaries, under which the fit is fixed by a centre rather than formed in use; evidence that the 說文's gloss did not enter later readings of 武; a model of actual exchange (probabilities over orders, codes with several forms) under which these laws fail.

Where: the chapter [THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE.md); the canonical chapter §6.5, §8.5 and §8.9; the records [what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches.md) and [widening-as-what-every-weighting-ranks](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/widening-as-what-every-weighting-ranks.md); the [significance review's literature check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#invariance-check); study section 256 ([IV1](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#256-the-step-of-invariance-what-it-reaches-across-standpoints-and-the-order-every-weighting-shares-iv1)). **Decision.** Adopted with exact scope. (1) **What wants its continuation.** Read as the centers of standpoints, the formations whose presupposition about their own continuation holds (`atCenters`). <a id="d60-the-readings-of-the-de-se-question-and-of-graded-significance"></a>
### The readings of the de se question and of graded significance

Where: the chapter [THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE](../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE.md) §4.1, §6.1, §6.2, §6.5 and §7; the canonical chapter §8.9; the records [what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches.md) and [graded-significance-and-priority](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/graded-significance-and-priority.md); the [significance review's literature check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#de-se-and-grades-check); study section 260 ([IV2](BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#260-the-de-se-question-and-graded-significance-iv2)). Decided in the one account of D45; continues D59.

**Decision.** Adopted with exact scope.
1. **De se and de dicto.** A content held de se is read as Lewis's self-ascribed property: each standpoint holds it of its own center (`selfAscribed`). A content held de dicto is one proposition from every standpoint (`shared`). The self's presupposition held de se is the property of the own closure (`ownClosure`). On these readings the egoist reply to Gewirth is the de se reading, the step across standpoints is the de dicto reading (`forced_iff_within_some`), and the two part exactly where centers' closures differ (`selfAscribed_iff_withinSome`).
2. **Standing and status.** A grade's positivity is read as standing (whether a formation counts) and the grade as status (how much), after Kagan and Sebo. The step as argued gives standing.
3. **Levels of continuation.** A formation's level in a course is read as the number of its listed items (steps) the course admits (`Weighting.level`). This is a reading of well-being as the length of admitted continuation, and it omits every other dimension of how a formation fares.
4. **Priority and sufficiency.** Priority is read as a sum of a strictly increasing transform of levels, strictly concave up to a bound (`Weighting.prior`, `Weighting.ConcaveTo`). Sufficiency is read as a transform that rises no further from a threshold (`Weighting.SufficientAt`).

**Reasons.** Each reading names the declarations it rests on and says no more than they prove. Lewis's reading is the standard one, and it makes the de se question a question about which content the presupposition has, which the laws can then answer conditionally. Standing and status is the distinction under which Williams's partiality and Taurek's numbers become one premise (invariance of status). Levels as admitted steps are the measure the comparison already reads, so priority and sufficiency are stated on the same items as C.

**What would change it.** A reading of de se content that is not a self-ascribed property (Castañeda's quasi-indicator, for instance) under which the parting law fails. An argument that the step, as argued, applies to grades. A measure of how a formation fares that is not the length of admitted continuation, under which priority would be stated differently.
