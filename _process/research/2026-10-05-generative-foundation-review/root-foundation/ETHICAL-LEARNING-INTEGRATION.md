## 13. Genuine rival positions and counterreview

**Original-purpose essentialism.** The rival says a created intelligence is necessarily exhausted by its assigned function, even if the relevant lived harm is granted. **Agency alone suffices for the complete package.** A sophisticated goal-directed organization can seek harmful ends, lack authority over others, and remain uncertain as a site of experience. Actual understanding and participation can ground specific claims without certifying consciousness or making every desire good. Conversely, failure of sophisticated agency does not erase an independently established experiencing or living position. **A dynamic account licenses withdrawal of standing whenever performance drops.** No. Current performance is not the whole realization, and affected standing does not require sophisticated reasoning. Temporarily unexercised capacity, continuing living relationships, legitimate past commitments and unrepaired harm can retain their grounds. A claim of actual change must distinguish these from reduced access or suppressed expression. Where grounds genuinely change, the appropriate particular conclusion may change without retroactively deleting the history.

**No internal life means nothing matters about it.** A particular claim about its own experience can remain unsupported while actual shared relationships, justified undertakings and supporting functions matter. Those grounds must not be mislabeled as proof that the carrier has experience. Their existence nevertheless warrants their own appraisal. Missing one ground is not evidence against every other ground.

**Welfare considerations defeat safety or every termination.** Recognition does not make a harmful action permissible or every restriction wrongful. A response can be justified under J while retaining its real losses. **Designed compliance is equivalent to consent.** The formation account permits acquired assent, including meaningful consent where the requisite understanding and participation are established. Training success or an obedient sentence alone does not establish that relationship. Equally, all culturally or technologically shaped assent is not thereby invalid. The point is faithful application of consent and standing, not the invention of an unconditioned chooser outside history.

**Any removal of concern is a harm.** False. Correcting a mistaken concern or relieving pain can be good; suppressing reports can leave the harm intact; destroying an established capacity can add a loss. These are different realizations to investigate. Ethical standing is not a command to freeze every state or preference. The earlier process-appraisal argument is reused to assess the transition.

**Creators owe everything forever.** Causal formation alone does not assign unlimited obligation. Accountable dependency, actual control, legitimate undertakings, alternative support and cumulative burden specify J3/J4's bounded requirements. ## 17. Affirmative argument and premise audit

**A. Practical influence is a realized relation.** Knowing what a reason supports does not determine how that understanding functions in subsequent activity. It can guide exploitation, remain unused, or participate in purpose revision. Concern takes up living significance when that significance helps organize attention, preparation or choice, including correction when its ground changes. This names an explanatory target; it does not stipulate that every accurate representation already has that role. Actual formation and operation require evidence.

**B. Influence is not sole determination.** Several motives can support one act, and a real reason can lose to another legitimate claim. Consequently, unaltered behavior after removing one putative motive does not settle its earlier absence. Nor does changed behavior after withdrawing pay establish pure self-interest if the intervention also removes support or capacity. These conceptual comparisons preserve the fuller process instead of treating motives as independently toggled labels. No general causal-identification theorem or empirical detection procedure is asserted.

**C. Formation must let grounds revise scope.** The value of concern is not exhausted by increasing its intensity. Actual relief, new relationships, changed participation and completion can favor different responses. A disposition that cannot relinquish inappropriate help may be sincere but misdirected. Existing G0/J/E, rather than correctability alone, assess these differences. Good habitual conduct can precede explicit understanding; reflective completion is not a prerequisite for its actual benefits.

**D. Cultivation itself enters practical comparison.** A formative intervention has benefits, burdens, permissions and alternatives. No special immunity follows from its aim of producing aligned motivation. In the constructed case, a manager's warranted role responsibility, knowledge, authorized control and a supported better alternative jointly justify revising a reward practice that sustains concealment. Mere capacity or causal influence does not assign the duty. J3 supplies the responsibility connection, while J1–J4 and E supply comparison, feasibility and application. The case also retains the participant's own bounded responsibility without assigning all corrective costs to that final acting position.

**E. Protection and mature motivation need not arise together.** Accountable limits and supporting practices can protect living before all participants care directly. Their actual protective contribution is assessable under the same principles, while their effect on development is an additional question. This avoids requiring universal internal transformation before any justified response. It also prevents collective success from being misreported as proof of each participant's motive.

These steps give a substantive answer to what alignment should cultivate and who can owe the work of cultivation in a specified relation. **They do not turn normative warrant into sufficient causal force.** G0 is retained as the explicit normative starting point, J1–J4 as additional comparative and practical commitments, and E as the substantive correspondence burden. The proposal adds neither an axiom requiring continual moral self-improvement nor a duty to maximize helping motivation.

## 30. Delivering the selected acquisition account

**Candidate:** a formed concern can initially organize conduct through an inadequate conception of its realization. Shared activity can reveal differences that this conception collapses. Acquiring those differences can change anticipation, preparation and operative purposes, while the activity also forms new living relations. The candidate distinguishes the reorganization of an existing concern from the separate formation and uptake of a new good.

The constructed case follows joint story-making. One participant commonly writes for another whose expression is initially intermittent. A finished text, approval and meaningful response initially travel together in the writer's practical conception. Later, a polished result misstates the other's contribution, while unfinished conversation yields an unexpected contribution. Comparing changes in the interaction can expose the dependence of actual contribution on the available place to revise what happens next. A changed plan then makes room for that contribution to affect the story rather than merely decorate an unchanged plan.

This is a proposed causal and philosophical account, not a historical observation or a formal possibility theorem. It explains what a successful developmental history would involve and why the specified initial concern could give its discovery practical influence. It supplies no new psychological law guaranteeing that the history occurs.

这是待事实支撑的定性应用例，不是经验发现或新检查的联合模型。真实次要损失仍须保留；在恢复额外基本保护且保住其余基本要求时，J2 可以给出有损优先。若旧路线已保住相同基本要求，改建增加能力且没有抵消损失，则可用 J1。若改建跨过另一项基本保护，“修复”这个名称不能补上比较。实际根据可以支持扩大共同能力、改变组织、继续维护或结束；回应形式不预先确定。

### Author counterreview and what changes the account / 作者反审与修订条件

1. **Does causal dependence itself imply an obligation?** No. The affirmative argument uses the accepted significance and practical commitments. It distinguishes a reason in favor, overall comparison and assignment of responsibility. Actual control and contribution do not by themselves establish unlimited duties. / 因果联系定位实践理由；正面意义、整体比较与责任承担各保留其根据。
2. **Must every dependency be preserved?** No. Alternative routes, changed needs, finite completion and burdens of maintaining the old arrangement can defeat that conclusion. A verified timely replacement changes the preservation question. Dependence that was deliberately manufactured does not entitle its manufacturer to retain control. / 真实及时替代、需要改变、有限完成及维持负担可以改变结论；依存不是制造者保有控制的凭证。
3. **Does a uniform return bound settle adequacy?** No. It is sufficient for neither continuous support nor the right timing of every need. Conversely, absence of a uniform bound does not show every actual episode misses its particular tolerance. The specific trajectory, reserves, timing and supported content decide correspondence. / 有界回程不自动充分，无统一界也不自动处处失接；需要具体展开的对应。
4. **Does supporting the future dominate present life?** No. G0 includes present living, rest and finite fulfillment. Future support can be relevant without making all present life an expendable instrument. Neither network centrality nor the number of downstream paths supplies a moral multiplier; avoid counting the same realization repeatedly. / 未来支承有意义，未因此取得压倒当下生活的地位；依存路径数不是价值权重。
5. **What has become more determinate?** The support content that can enter the existing comparison: which living relations continue together, through which replacement, during which actual needs, with which retained losses. A focal escape, resource return or endpoint match is insufficient evidence by itself. This is an application advance, not a second proof of the already completed general transfer principles. / 本次更明确的是已有比较的实质输入，不是重证一般交接。
6. **Where is the remaining wall?** Even complete descriptions can leave two futures with different basic losses. Their dependencies may explain why both losses occur without ranking them. Stronger comparison needs a defensible substantive priority or compensation account; whole zero-substantive-axiom necessity also remains unproved. This is an open warrant question, not an impossibility result. / 依存事实可以解释冲突，却尚未给出交叉基本损失的排序；更强根据继续开放，未被证明不可能。
