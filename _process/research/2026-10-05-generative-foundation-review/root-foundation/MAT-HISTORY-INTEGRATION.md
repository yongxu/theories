## Primary evidence and actual inspection

[Ferguson, Bertels and Rainey (2013)](../../../../sources/evolutionary-selection/ferguson-2013/README.md) reconstruct fuzY mutations and restore the wild-type sequence or expression in relevant backgrounds. The study connects changes in cell-surface interactions to flocculation, surface organization and compromised mats. Its time-lapse evidence revises an earlier assignment of fuzzy spreader (FS) to a bottom-growth niche: material at the bottom can have been produced at the air–liquid interface and subsequently fallen. Bottom accumulation alone is therefore a poor proxy for the location of growth in this documented case. This correction belongs to the original study.

The preserved media include FS growth (S1), FS growth across five labeled pH conditions (S4), a separate postgrowth flocculation series (S5), and a WS/fuzY-associated structural comparison (S6). Source file lengths agree with the original BioStudies deposit. [The extraction package](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/ecological-selection/mat-history/README.md) fully decodes 4,795 encoded frames and checks 44 selected frames against a separate full-video RGB hash output. Five derived contact sheets were visually inspected; crops and playback timestamps are recorded. These counts describe processing, not replication.

S1's selected frames show hanging surface-associated material and changing downward extensions, alongside accumulated material at the vial bottom. The dense sequence at encoded frames 972–1104 shows the hanging material changing and receding; it does not independently identify every detachment or track the same marked cells. The authors' fuller interpretation is repeated formation and descent of small rafts without a substantial continuous biofilm. We credit that source-reported result and distinguish it from the limited direct visual inspection.

In S4, the terminal pH 6.5 vial has relatively clear upper broth and a bottom deposit, while higher-pH vials have more diffuse broth turbidity. S5 shows a separate postgrowth contrast in clearing and accumulation. These visual correspondences support condition-sensitive spatial organization; they do not calibrate viable biomass from brightness. Some S4 differences are already present at the first encoded frame, so that frame cannot be assumed to mark equivalent initial conditions. The paper's nonsignificant 72-hour CFU comparisons also do not establish equal growth rates throughout the preceding history.

S6's legend calls the comparison WS versus WS-fuzY T443G; the visible labels instead read LSWS and LSWS with a deletion-like Δ…478 notation. The article identifies fuzY as pflu0478 and studies both point mutation and deletion. Exact movie-genotype correspondence is unresolved. We do not assign an allele-specific rate or transfer the movie label to the separately described bead-strength experiment. The original genetic interventions remain evidence at their reported scope.

## What changes in the explanatory connection

The [previous study](../history/root-foundation/MAT-CONDITIONS-INTEGRATION.md) linked respiration-generated oxygen gradients to selective consequences and distinguished developmental strength from a later endpoint. The new temporal evidence adds a necessary observation distinction: renewal of visible surface material need not be uninterrupted persistence of the material first formed. Neither automatically identifies persistence of a reproductively continuing organization. Which continuity matters depends on the actual developmental and reproductive mechanism.

These are analytical distinctions within one history of division, adhesion, matrix formation, transport, separation and continued growth. The vial, medium, pH treatment and subsequent sampling participate in that history. They are not an independent environment joined afterward to a finished organism. The current source still supplies prepared medium, exposure conditions and genetic constructs; its explanation of temporal organization does not derive their wider formation or renewal.

The proposed empirical correspondence is now:

**Inherited and current cellular organization + actual chemical and transport conditions → observed spatial development, loss and possible reformation → the protocol's endpoint criterion → sampled viable descendants and redevelopment.**

The first arrows have several primary correspondences. The [recorded bacterial genealogy and linked assay](../history/root-foundation/LINEAGE-CONTINUATION-INTEGRATION.md) already establish the later continuation connection in another experimental history. These are not one joined population record. The immediate task is to calibrate compatible connections, preserving this distinction rather than treating a literature composite as an observed life cycle.

## Observation requirements before fitting

| Proposed quantity | Evidence needed | What these movies supply |

| Terminal protocol success | The actual assay's scoring rule and terminal observation time | No scoring equivalence between small rafts and the later mutL mat criterion |
| Reproductive continuation | Linked viable samples, actual transfers, descendant identities and redevelopment | Not recorded for the movie vials |

The imaging methods specify photographs every five or ten minutes and subsequent movie compilation. The legend spans of 48 or 12 hours do not identify each encoded frame's capture time. We therefore retain playback timestamps only. Dividing the legend duration uniformly over the encoded frame count would introduce an unverified timing assumption. Thousands of compressed frames are also not thousands of independent trials.

Two candidate observation models should be distinguished when suitable data are available. An absorbing-loss model treats loss after established structure as irreversible through the assay horizon. A recurrent-formation model allows later detectable structure after loss. The published FS result makes recurrent surface material empirically relevant. It does **not** refute an absorbing model for a separately defined mature mat or for the focal mutL assay without matching those criteria. A state that merely says “surface material present” may also omit connectivity and strength needed to predict a mat's response. This is a limitation of that measurement, not a foundational counterexample to the richer relational theory.

No kinetic constants are fitted from the available edited films. The candidate comparison must use acquisition times and measurements of structure, exposure and disturbance, then forecast held-out conditions. A fitted condition-specific endpoint probability alone would preserve the explanatory gap identified by the dated transfer failure.

## Substantive counterreview and comparative credit

**Affirmative argument.** The results connect retained cellular differences and present interactions to how a structure forms, persists and changes subsequent spatial opportunities. They support the claim that consequences of activity help form the conditions of further activity. They sharpen which histories must be retained to explain the terminal developmental outcome.

**Strong rival.** The original biofilm and adaptive-radiation accounts already explain cell-surface interaction, transient rafts, pH dependence and genetic trade-offs. A conventional model supplied the same temporal, genetic and chemical information can preserve all these mechanisms. It must be the comparison target. Nothing here establishes a prediction uniquely available to our framework.

**Surviving limitations.** The movie genotype discrepancy prevents exact S6 attribution. An image showing loss of visible material does not identify dead cells, lost reproductive capacity or whole-mat failure. Similar final titers do not prove identical growth histories. Controlled pH differences do not by themselves demonstrate endogenous evolutionary change in pH regulation. Repeated physical reformation is not a new generation or a newly inherited evolutionary unit. The source's proposed phage–biofilm evolutionary arms race is not treated as a directly observed continuous coevolutionary cycle.

**Potential comparative gain.** A connected model could reduce separately fitted survival assumptions by predicting the protocol outcome from measured development, structural response and subsequent sampling. That is a testable contribution, not an achieved advantage. If an equally informed conventional model already supplies the same connection, credit that equivalence and narrow the novelty claim.
