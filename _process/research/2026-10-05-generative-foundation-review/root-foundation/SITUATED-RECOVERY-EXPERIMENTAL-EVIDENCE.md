## What was searched and what counts

Searches covered physical robot self-modeling/damage recovery, human contextual and explicit/implicit motor learning, and cellular responses to changing osmotic inputs. Included studies manipulate a relevant condition and observe subsequent regulation, learning or action. Theory-only papers and publicity are not counted as experiments. Published models, physical observations and our interpretation are kept separate. Existing biological and thermal analyses are credited below rather than counted again as new replications.

This follows the [nondual method](../../../../theories/becoming/history/NONDUAL-METHOD.md): sensing, remembered descriptions, intervention and subsequent conditions are roles within each studied activity. The experiments license particular analytical distinctions through actual apparatus and interventions. They do not require fundamentally separate mind/world domains; neither do they uniquely establish this ontology.

## Six primary studies and their actual contribution

### E1. Acquire a self-model, use it, revise after physical change

Bongard, Zykov and Lipson (2006), *Resilient Machines Through Continuous Self-Modeling*, Science 314, 1118–1121, [author-hosted paper](https://meclab.w3.uvm.edu/papers/2006_Science_Bongard.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1133687).

Read the robot article on printed pp. 1118–1121, especially model-disagreement probe selection, random-acquisition controls and physical gait execution. The robot acquired candidate body descriptions, selected informative actions and generated compensating motion after damage. The model family and task were engineered; recovery demonstrations began from a selected accurate model, and predicted motion differed from physical motion. **Supports an enacted acquisition–revision–use loop**, with actual transfer error; it does not establish universal diagnosis or exact convergence. We do not claim novelty for this established connection or reproduce the paper's raw experiments.

### E2. Recover useful action without fully locating the damage

Cully, Clune, Tarapore and Mouret (2015), *Robots that can adapt like animals*, [author manuscript, arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.3501v4), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14422).

Read manuscript pp. 1–3 and Extended Data Fig. 7; the retrieved PDF explicitly identifies itself as a pre-submission manuscript. Physical tests update a previously generated behavior-performance map and obtain compensating actions. The hexapod damage conditions report median adaptation time 66 seconds, with 5th/95th percentiles 24/134 seconds: “under two minutes” is not an every-run bound. **Supports practical recovery from informative trials without complete causal diagnosis.** The precomputed map, supplied performance measure and behavior descriptors limit any claim about unrestricted novelty or autonomous revision of final goals.

### E3. Context affects which memory is expressed and updated

Heald, Lengyel and Wolpert (2021), *Contextual inference underlies the learning of sensorimotor repertoires*, Nature 600, 489–493, [publisher page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04129-3), [data archive](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.m63xsj42r).

Read the abstract, public Extended Data 2, 6 and 7, and data/code availability; subscription main text and full supplement were not reviewed. Human perturbation/cue experiments support evoked recovery and context-dependent updating. The COIN explanation separates memory acquisition from changed expression; its latent assignments are model inferences, and some individual parameters are poorly recovered. **Supports learned, context-sensitive organization of adaptation**, not unique identification of a neural algorithm. Dryad lists participant-level records for the recovery and updating studies; no new raw-data reanalysis was performed here.

### E4. Deliberate adjustment and implicit adaptation differ within one performance

Taylor, Krakauer and Ivry (2014), *Explicit and Implicit Contributions to Learning in a Sensorimotor Adaptation Task*, J. Neurosci. 34, 3023–3032, [author-hosted paper](https://ipalab.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf2841/files/ipalab/files/taylorkrakauerivry2014.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3619-13.2014).

Read abstract, methods and discussion of instruction/feedback contrasts. In 60 participants, aiming reports and movement measurements distinguish explicit adjustments from an implicit component estimated by subtraction. That decomposition assumes additivity, and reported aiming need not mean the person discovered the rotation's cause. Implicit adaptation remained incomplete. **Supports deliberate revision alongside automatic learning**, while requiring a richer description than one undifferentiated error variable. This is evidence about means of reaching a supplied target, not the ethical justification of that target.

### E5. Adaptive movement can conflict with the understood task

Morehead, Taylor, Parvin and Ivry (2017), *Characteristics of Implicit Sensorimotor Adaptation Revealed by Task-irrelevant Clamped Feedback*, J. Cogn. Neurosci. 29, 1061–1074, [author-hosted paper](https://ivrylab.berkeley.edu/files/organized_pubs_pdfs/2017_morehead_et_al.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1162/jocn_a_01108).

Read abstract/methods and the indexed original discussion of invariant response and adaptation limits. Cursor direction was experimentally detached from hand direction, and informed participants were told to ignore it; their movements nevertheless adapted. The reported response was bounded across a broad range of offsets. **This is adverse evidence against identifying every stable learned response with adequate understanding or task success.** It preserves a distinction between what a person can report and what an acquired motor process does. It does not show that reflective correction is generally impossible.

### E6. Biological return after a step does not guarantee tracking a ramp

Muzzey, Gómez-Uribe, Mettetal and van Oudenaarden (2009), *A systems-level analysis of perfect adaptation in yeast osmoregulation*, Cell 138, 160–171, [author manuscript record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3109981/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2009.04.047).

Read original indexed summary, measurement-method excerpts and the ramp-input Results/Fig. 6 discussion. Measurements of Hog1 localization, volume and glycerol constrain feedback organization. Recovery after step changes contrasts with failure to return to baseline during the tested rising ramp, followed by recovery after the plateau. **Supports biological regulation and dependence on the pattern of change.** It does not show cells reflectively identifying their own laws, or certify our scalar estimator. Direct full-page/PDF retrieval failed; the listed sections, not the entire article or supplement, support this assessment.

**Empirical interpretation:** the central operational connections are instantiated, rather than merely mathematically imaginable. Together these experiments warrant a scoped account of situated learning and recovery. They support a mechanism class; they do not establish that all realizations obey one simple update law. The existing Lean proofs expose sufficient conditions and failure cases within their models. Experimental evidence assesses realizability and correspondence through different methods. Neither layer can be substituted for the other.

The [thermal prediction study](../history/root-foundation/PREDICTIVE-CALIBRATION-INTEGRATION.md) already tests retained dynamics against alternative predictors, with useful improvement and failed narrow intervals both preserved. New literature does not erase those negative results. A favorable result in another apparatus cannot supply its missing hard bounds.

The [joint biological specification](../history/root-foundation/BIOLOGICAL-JOINT-VALIDATION.md) remains a legitimate stronger target for a quantitative connected preparation. It is **not the minimum requirement for any empirical support of the general account**. Complementary experiments can support connected mechanisms without fabricating one shared cohort. Conversely, adding individually supported relations does not supply an unmeasured interaction, transport coefficient or whole-loop error bound. Transportability must be argued at the scope actually claimed.

## Completion and counterreview

**Completed:** this targeted empirical grounding of the operational situated-learning/revision/recovery account, with positive exemplars, explicit correspondence and adverse cases. No generic “first obtain any experimental support” task remains. The literature can ground the theoretical mechanism without a new experiment here.

**Retained stronger obligations:** exact numerical certification of a selected implementation; the separately requested full biological joint validation; unrestricted novelty and complete semantic awareness; universal or ethically justified goal convergence. These are not collapsed into one completion label. Published finite adaptation supports actual recovery under tested conditions, while infinite-time convergence remains a conditional mathematical statement.

Author counterreview asks whether favorable examples were cherry-picked, whether performance replaced understanding, and whether heterogeneous studies were concatenated into a fictitious experiment. The targeted search is not exhaustive and estimates no prevalence. E5, E6 and the existing calibration/replication failures constrain the conclusion. E2 also supplies a rival route to recovery that does not require our preferred complete diagnostic account. Conventional control, learning and physiological explanations receive credit; exclusive necessity of our theoretical vocabulary is not established.

This round adds no theorem and changes no Lean source. It assesses published experiments; it is not new laboratory execution, raw-data replication or independent-agent acceptance. Reading scope and temporary-download fingerprints are recorded without importing or redistributing the papers. [Integration checks](../history/root-foundation/SITUATED-EXPERIMENTAL-EVIDENCE-CHECKS.json) verify documentary bindings and preserved content, not scientific truth by script.

## Public data counterreview

Subsequent step, 8 October 2026: [all 16 original recovery files are now analyzed](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/inquiry-correspondence/situated-recovery/README.md). The preceding six-study round's “no new raw-data reanalysis” statement remains true of that earlier round. The new scoped result is `situated-recovery-empirical-correspondence`, with [theoretical integration](../history/root-foundation/JOINT-RISK-REVISION-INTEGRATION.md#41-public-records-now-test-a-specific-correspondence).

This author counterreview checks the strongest alternative readings. First, the measured coefficient is expressed force compensation, not directly a hidden memory state or complete situational understanding; our Lean error variable cannot be substituted for it. Second, 2,656 channel observations are nested in 16 participants, not 2,656 independent replications. Participant bootstrap and allocation permutation preserve that unit; with eight people per group, intervals remain imprecise. Baseline adjustment includes zero in the between-group interval, and the permutation comparison does not justify a robust late-group superiority claim.

Third, future-trial forecasting challenges the preferred richer curve. Its mean improvement over zero-level decay is not uniform; spontaneous data favor the simpler zero-level model among planned comparators, while evoked data favor a constant calibration mean. Four unconstrained fits have nearly unit retention and large cancelling coefficients. A nonnegative-level sensitivity is explicitly post hoc, does not erase those failures, and does not establish unique mechanism identification. The descriptive models are not the original COIN model; these results cannot be reported as a new COIN model-selection replication or its refutation.

Fourth, raw-source checks expose practical limits. Actual pre-terminal trial numbers differ from nominal plotting coordinates; the analysis uses the actual rows. A force/velocity reconstruction is close to, but not exactly the same as, the supplied adaptation coefficient (maximum absolute difference 0.024071). The source's complete preprocessing has not been independently recreated. Originals, reported values and alternative reconstructed values are all preserved; no discrepancy is hidden by replacing the primary outcome.

Finally, the analysis plan follows knowledge of the paper's findings. It is an exploratory secondary study with a recorded split, not a blind independent confirmation. All recovery participants were used; no winner or unresponsive person was removed. These checks support the claimed finite correspondence and require restraint about exact calibration, infinite limits, all-intelligence awareness and ethical conclusions. The general learning/revision mechanism retains its earlier experimental grounding. This is substantive self-review, not independent-agent acceptance.

## Context-updating counterreview

9 October 2026. Extend E3 through the [24-person reanalysis](../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/inquiry-correspondence/situated-recovery/MEMORY-UPDATING.md), not a seventh independent experiment. The published phase-specific pattern is reproduced; the new empirical delta is a direct comparison of changes and finite persistence. Retained source tables and all participants prevent convenience-sample selection, but the known published result and secondary design mean this is not preregistered confirmation.

The affirmative case is specific: the perturbation contrast grows by 0.10666 with a participant-bootstrap interval excluding zero and a three-test adjusted p=0.00138. The post-training cue contrast is positive. These support a connection between established experimental history and differentiated subsequent expression. The strong acquired-cue claim does not follow: cue-change uncertainty includes zero, and earlier versus later post-window means differ. Phase-specific significance cannot substitute for a direct change test.

A substantive rival locates some or all expressed changes in contextual retrieval/generalization rather than a separately identified latent memory update. This analysis does not distinguish those possibilities or refit COIN; it credits the original explanatory account without making its exclusive truth a measurement. Pre/post comparison also bundles training, elapsed time and accumulated tests. Washout-related cue dilution is a plausible account of a later decline, not an independently identified mechanism here. Primary change p-values are adjusted across three tests; remaining intervals and sensitivity tests are explicitly exploratory. A nonsignificant contrast is not an equivalence result.

Provenance is graded accurately: exact compressed table fragments for 24 people, plus one full original with deposited hash and byte-equivalent fragments. Twenty-three complete-file hashes are not verified by those fragments. Participant 13 has two missing trial numbers between retained Exposure rows; all analysed triplets remain consecutive, no imputation is made, and omitting that participant leaves the main descriptive pattern. Redundant table agreement and agreement with published rounded statistics support the extraction, without certifying all author preprocessing or eliminating source omissions.

This author counterreview narrows the account without discarding the positive evidence: one formation-related contrast is supported more strongly than another, current cue use is better supported than its acquired increase, and finite persistence varies. No new Lean theorem, independent participant sample, complete situational understanding or ethical conclusion is added.
