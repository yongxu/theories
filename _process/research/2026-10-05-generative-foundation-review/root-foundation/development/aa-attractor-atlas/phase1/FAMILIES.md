# The other seven families: how they were read

*AI-written, 7 November 2026, for board item AA ([the plan, §9](../PLAN.md#9-phases-size-checks)). About five minutes to read, the table aside. It says what ran, what the reads found, what the join decided and what was left out. The result is [the atlas](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/atlas/ATLAS.md); the agents' reports are kept as returned in [the first read](families-first-read.json) and [the second](families-second-read.json).*

## What ran

Ren agreed to about 14 agents, an extractor and a checker per family, on 6 November. Those 14 ran (41 minutes). Family D's pair also took the four rows the pilot left of family C.

The first read found enough that its corrections could not stand unread: 6 high, 54 medium and 48 low across 142 entries. So a second pass of 9 more ran (28 minutes), past what was agreed, and the report to Ren says so. One agent per family read each correction against the record, the home and the Lean, keeping 50 and changing 51, and added the gaps assigned at the join. Then two readers who wrote none of it read everything new, and found no high, 8 medium and 18 low. Their 22 corrections were applied after a word-by-word read, and the four that change a law's statement were checked against the Lean at the join (`Drawn.still_iff`, `Flow.not_keepsOpening_along_supportedPassing`, `Flow.Balances.keeps_of_not_arising`, `Undecided.liar_rests`).

No agent committed anything, and none ran Lean, a test suite or the gate. Each writer ran the atlas's own check on its family, and the whole passed it at the join.

## What it holds

177 dynamics, up from 11. By family: A 29, B 24, C 16, D 22, E 10, F 24, G 18, H 34. 168 rest on records and 9 are readings or open items with none. Of 284 exits, 151 are witnesses, 65 sufficient, 28 necessary and 40 exact. So most ways out are shown at a constructed example, with no condition for them, and that is worth a reader's knowing.

The six it rated high, each in an entry as first written:

- `a-cascade-along-total-dependence` said a run within a bound rests, while `Coupled.Relay.cycles`, among its own exits, stays within 3 and is never still.
- `a-stop-passed-by-a-listing-that-fixes-the-sign` listed a frozen test that REST-TR8-TR9 says cannot test this lock.
- `readings-within-what-they-read` had an open line saying no later step closes a gap, where the next step says each gap by definition.
- `overshoot-beyond-the-gap` said no law decides which exchanges oscillate, where `Flow.never_at_of_beyond` is one.

- `where-a-run-of-a-regime-comes-to-rest` copied a record saying a premise "is not checked on the data", where `experiments/margin-measured` reports the check.

Three of the six are negatives or stale lines, not slips in a law. The commonest medium was a dropped qualifier, as in the pilot.

## What the plan had wrong

PLAN's first pass was unchecked, as it said. The line that mattered most was family F's: "every one a witness, with no law of when or with what period". General laws of return stand (`Law.basin_one_cycle`, `Withholding.withholding_returns`, `Regulation.relay_threshold`, `Settling.keeps_changing_iff`, `Stock.repeat_covered_iff`, `Commutes.self_apart`). What may stand as absent is narrower: when a regime cycles, and with which period. §4.1, §4.4 and the ten-minute part are corrected. Appendix A and B stay as written, under a line saying the atlas stands where they differ. The readers' list of their errors is in the reports' `elsewhere` fields.

## What the join decided

- **Moved.** `reserve-defers-ending` to A, since its record says a reserve defers the end, which still comes. `the-last-change-of-a-run` to G, beside the rest laws. The cascade and the pool stay in D, against the plan's C, since the pool's record disclaims the rule a pool reads.
- **Merged.** H's copy of the optimum over a horizon into B's. H's sparing formation into E's thresholds. A's market protection into H's guard. The step coupling and `steps-met-again-after-a-fall` from B to H.
- **Split.** F's copy of the settling law took its own id, `a-run-round-a-loop-keeps-changing`. G keeps the settling law.
- **Dropped.** `merge-of-a-part-kept-by-the-rest`: a counting bound at every step, with no course in it.
- **Kept, with a doubt on record.** `joint-difference-making` and `closing-needs-net-draw` are mostly comparisons, and each says so. A reader looking for what closes what can form looks there.
- **Added to the closed lists.** An exit's `witness`, 08 §2's constructed example: three of the pilot's own exits had been labelled sufficient. A test's `refused`: T1N and T1R were frozen, then stopped at their header before any verdict, and "frozen" misstated them.
- **Page.** The mechanism left the family indexes, which at this size repeated the sections.

## What this does not show

- **It is not complete.** The candidates were chosen from record titles, and 222 of 506 live theorem records are cited. Those left out were read only where the table below says so. A dynamic may stand among the rest.
- **A read is not a proof of absence.** Each negative an entry states was looked for, and a looked-for negative can still be wrong.
- **Not done.** The atlas's other kinds (transitions, exits, premises, theses and tests, open items as files of their own); exits joined to CD's six conditions; English only, as in the pilot, with a Chinese name where a chapter gives one; `forbids` filled only where a home says it (11 entries). §11's form choices stay Ren's.
- **Length.** Many mechanisms pass the 1000 characters asked, the longest about 1500. The writers kept qualifiers rather than cut, because a shortening is where the errors entered.

The reads also found errors outside the atlas, in chapters, records and COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION. Those are their own landing, on the board.
