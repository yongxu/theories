## 4. Thesis T3 (homed at 05 §7 `#t3`; the only thesis homed in either chapter)

- **Statement:** "*Optimization drains what lies beyond its couplings, where that gives room.*" It "adds that actual trained systems, not only optima, do this in proportion to how near they come to their reward's optimum, the drain lying where what lies beyond shares units with what is rewarded."
- **Standing:** "The law for optima is proved; the clause on trained systems is conjectured, with partial support." The second forbidden case is "proved in expectation for learners that the exchange of the choice's two values carries, from a starting state or law the exchange leaves unchanged; from a start preferring one value… it stays conjectured." Its first wording, for conditions sharing no units, "fails for the separable family's exact expected update where the learner reads the condition."
- **Forbids:** "with eligible outcomes, couplings and reward fixed and the optimality gap measured: a system nearer its reward's optimum leaving a room-giving condition beyond its couplings higher, on average, than one further from it; a systematic drive on a choice whose values nothing rewarded and nothing the learner reads tells apart; where room exists, a side-effect term ranked after the reward protecting a condition, or a constraint at the need leaving a margin above it; an instruction that does not bring a dependent into what the learners couple into, yet keeps it more often than the same learners without the instruction."
- **Scope:** the side-effect clause concerns rising rewards. Plateaus, set points and aspiration levels are no counterexample.
- **Refuted by:** "Overoptimization curves in which what the reward omits falls as far where it shares no resource with what is rewarded, or any case the forbidden list names, observed."
- **Evidence (05:232–243):** CG1 (118/118 against 0/2,360; the keep-to-end prediction failed, 59% against 90%); MQ1 (8/8 against 0/8); IN1 (0/10 per cell; most frozen predictions failed); CG2; EM1 (3 of 6 locked settings kept); LA1, LA1b and LA1c test "the bridge", not the drain; LA4 Stage A (3 November): no forbidden case resolved, P1 and P5 held, P3 (the locus) unresolved, P6 supported only in the recurrent family; outside work (Gao et al. 2023; Pan et al. 2022; MacDiarmid et al. 2025); limits from §263 and §254.

## 7. Gaps: attractor-like dynamics no law yet states

These are my own reading of the gaps, not claims the chapters make.

1. **Mutation–selection balance as an attractor.** Only forward invariance is stated (`below_always`, `not_falls_below`); convergence is open. The error threshold is stated for one step, not as a bifurcation in k−w.
2. **Fixation times, and Wright–Fisher with selection.** Absorption is bounded in weight; no time law exists.
3. **Basins of learning dynamics in the commons.** The laws state perfect profiles and mutual best responses. Which equilibrium learners reach (EM1's 3 of 6) has no law, and mutual best response existence is open.
4. **Market bistability and hysteresis.** It is stated only in the periphery model at β=1/2, with M as a margin. No pure-core law covers two retained regions with an unstable separatrix. The "four routes" are a reading only.
5. **Network thresholds of tolerance.** These are percolation-like: what distribution of tolerances keeps giving on a general web. `kept_set` and `thin_absorbed` are local. Amounts of giving, thresholds with memory, and probabilistic forgiveness are open.
6. **Selection among laws of giving over many cohorts.** Only one-step `PairSelection` exists; there is no law of evolutionary stability.
7. **Codes and conventions under chance.** The deterministic symmetry lock is proved. No law says chance or reinforcement brings agreement (`Weighted.Reinforcing.oneSided` gives only positive weight), and the time to agreement is open.
8. **Ends of position races.** The conjecture list (frozen destruction, deadlock, merger, collapse, winner takes all) is not formalized. That a race produces joint excess is a premise.
9. **Institutions.** Missing: a classification of ends; the World-flow version with a bridge to `Flow.joint_excess_ends`; partial exit at overdraw; sanctions, revision deciders and schism; identity through purposes; other kinds of institution; enshittification as degeneration.
10. **Organisms.** Missing: ending from every stock; a horizon monotone in the stock; the World-flow placement (conveyance, degeneration from a horizon); closure of constraints and autopoiesis.
11. **Lineage regimes.** It is not shown that the three regimes exhaust what decides a lineage's region, and nothing decides which side a course is on (`Horizon.two_fates_of_nonempty`).
12. **Group dynamics.** Missing: assortment changing over cohorts, mixing, synergy; group formation beyond the bonded model.
13. **Pools.** How they form, last and resist capture has no law. Recruitment through the pool is the only lock stated.
14. **Reserves under noise.** Whether reserves are worth holding (05 Open, set points) is open; "a reserve defers the end" is homed in 02, not here.
