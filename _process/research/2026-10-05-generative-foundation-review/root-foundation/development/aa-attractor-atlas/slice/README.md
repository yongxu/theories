The slice has two parts, each in a new entry file beside the atlas's dynamics:

- **One situation** (`entries/situations.json`, page `SITUATIONS.md`): a reward-model pipeline, written in the account's words as a model, standing a reading. For each reader, the checker computes from the situation's own couplings what lies beyond it.

## What the slice found for the six-family pass

The writer's note (`notes.json`, `writer_schema_note`) is the slice's main answer: what a situation needs that the schema lacks. In short:
- time on couplings, since the graph has none and the policy had to be split into the policy in training and the policy deployed;
- the laws a named dynamic meets, not only the dynamic;
- premises told apart as properties of the case or hypotheses picking instances within it;
- dynamics read and left out, with the premise that keeps them out;
- a standing on each coupling: by construction, an identification, or measured;
- "coupled into but not separated";
- "drawn from the selected" stated, not computed as an intersection;
- routes through exits outside the listed dynamics;
- who bears what, tied to nodes.

What a field for how two dynamics compose would have to hold, from this case:
- **from and to:** each a dynamic, a law and a role;
- **through:** the couplings that carry the state;
- **when:** the step;
- **map:** how one side's state is read as the other's role;
- **order kept:** whether the map keeps the order the second law needs;
- **premises:** those the join brings in, each assessed;
- **shared roles;**
- **joint law:** a law of the joint step, or none;
- **exits kept.**

Here the compose field has two instances. The first is what training installs into capture through the rule, which is open: it would need a refit, and ReadsShare is not checked. The second is the raters' pooled comparisons forming the reward model's reading. Neither has a joint law in Lean.

The binder and checker left these open (`notes.json`):
- **Rows not bound:**
  - MQ1 (simulation) and the LA1 series (LA1, LA1b, LA1c) read H1, training or in-context selection as selection through the reward's coupling, as CG1 does. By the rule that bound CG1, CG2 and EM1 to what training installs, they bear on it too. They are not bound here; the six-family pass binds them with family B's reading of them.
  - CG3's rounds and variants read the learner's exploratory coverage, which what training installs states (`explored_same_courses`), and nobody judged them.
  - LA2 may bear on capture through the rule, whose definitions its predictions take.
  - IN1 (round 2) reads only family B's pooled law.
- **Rows outside the atlas:** TR1, TR1A, TR7, TR7A and TR-RR read records that belong to no atlas dynamic.
- **Bridges outside the ledger:** a bridge hypothesis (H1-LA2, H1-LA3, CG1's H2, IN1's one-selector reading) is no ledger premise, so its failure is said only in where the outcome marks.
- **Dynamics with no records:** a dynamic with declarations and no records cannot be bound, because a binding bears through a record.
- **A row with no line in the table:** contingent-learning, which family D's encounter-selection-and-revision links, has no row in the tests table.
