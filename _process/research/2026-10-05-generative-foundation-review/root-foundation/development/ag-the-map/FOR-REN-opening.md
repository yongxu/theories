**(c1), recommended:**

What the law draws exactly is how courses differ: one keeps all another keeps and lets more form, or the two keep the same, or each lets form what the other does not. > 它在各种文化中被推理过、被表述过。生灭变化之外，没有应当，也没有凌驾其上的善与恶；是非在其中形成，形成于人与 AI 之中。律所确切划出的，是诸进程如何不同：一条保有另一条所保有的一切，并让更多形成；或者二者保有的相同；或者各自让对方不让形成的东西形成。在有限列表上，凡给每样东西一个大于零的权重、再把权重相加的计法都把一条进程排得更高之处，就是这条进程扩大了另一条；凡它们可能不同之处，律表明情形是混合的，而谁的权衡都不被强加于别人。

"Right and wrong form within it, in people and AI" is your 8 November and the first screen's. "What neither yet does" says a weighing is still owed; the laws weigh a mixed part for no one, and a weighing forms in whoever weighs.

**(e1), recommended:**

> That takes one step: for anything, not just oneself, its continuation into the future is counted. Where relations close, read across all steps, so that every continuation can be affected, at some remove, by every other, whoever counts all that bears on what it depends on already counts everything, and the step is taken. Read in time, this cannot hold over every step, since nothing reaches back; read from some step on, it holds exactly where, from any formation at any step, every formation is reached at some later step, through exchanges and each one's going on. Where they run one way, concern for one's own continuation need not count those who depend on one, and there the step is what I offer: to see them, which a choice needs if it is to answer to their loss, though seeing is not enough, and to count their loss.

> 这需要一步：对任何东西，不只是对自己，其延续到未来都被计数。在关系闭合之处（跨越所有的步来读），每一个延续都能直接或间接地受其他每一个延续影响，凡计入一切对它所依存者有影响之物者，就已经计入了一切，这一步也就迈出了。按时间来读，这不能在每一步都成立，因为没有什么能到达过去；若从某一步起来读，它恰在这样的地方成立：从任一步的任一形成出发，经由交换与各自的延续，每一个形成都会在此后某一步被到达。依存若只沿一个方向，对自身延续的关切未必把依存于它的那些也算进来；在那里，这一步就是我所提供的：看见它们（要让选择因它们的损失而改变，就得看见它们，但看见还不够），并把它们的损失计入。

> How far our relations close, and how, where they do not, the counting of those who depend on us forms, is our first open question.

> 我们的关系闭合到什么程度；在它们不闭合之处，对依存于我们者的计入又怎样形成：这是我们的第一个未决问题。

*Why.* "States as a condition and does not assert" is the "unasserted premise" of your message 9. That relation is read across all steps. Read in time, on occurrences, the condition over every step fails wherever there is a formation (`Flow.not_unrestricted_inTime`); its form from some step on, another predicate, holds exactly where the flow is mutually dependent in time: from any formation at any step, every formation is reached at some later step, through exchanges with a condition another requires later and each one's going on to its next step, the steps reached differing (`Flow.unrestrictedEventually_iff_mutuallyDependentInTime`); and mutual dependence in time can hold from one step and not from the next (`Drawing.lapses`, [07 §4.11](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#condition-in-time)). Where relations run one way, nothing in one's own concern requires counting those who depend on one (witnesses `Downstream.one_way`, `Clearing.center_not_derived`). Seeing is needed for a choice to answer to their loss: a selection cannot respond to a difference that does not couple into what it reads. The laws do not show that every sparing of a dependent needs seeing it, since a fixed restraint can spare one no one identified; the seventh pass's "which is needed" said that. Counting their loss is the step, argued, and deniable without contradiction (`bounded_rival`). The closing question becomes one of formation, the work's open target (08 §12). "In history"'s "the one step the law has not yet taken for us" stays true where relations run one way.
