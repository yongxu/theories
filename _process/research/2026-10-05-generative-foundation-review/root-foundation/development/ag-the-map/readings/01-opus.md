## Diagnosis

The current AGENTS.md is an epistemic-defence charter. Its sentences are mostly about standing, warrant, what does not follow and what not to say. Concretely:

- **The purpose is stated and undercut in the same sentence** ("want … help both predict, share an understanding and coordinate; the want is theirs and revisable, and nothing in the work shows it met"). The README's first screen copies this almost word for word.
- **"What moves it" sets the value scale to novelty for specialists.** It opens on "none of the account's central results is new mathematics, and better text cannot change that", then says only a surprising theorem, a failable prediction or a weaker premise can move it, and orders the work as: remove misleading text, make it refutable, collect reader reports. Nothing in the order is "make the map, extend it, use it for alignment, make it seen". Ren's "value isn't mathematics" is inverted: the file measures value by mathematics and finds it absent.
- **Theory content is written as obligations.** Method and Evaluation restate 08 and 07 (one relational unfolding as thesis; every split states its basis; root laws forbid no relation; "zero axiom never shrinks to no ethical axiom"; Lean theorem names; the continuation condition and C). An agent reads these as rules about what it may say, so every sentence it writes carries the qualifiers. The result is the dense, hedge-stacked prose of the fronts.
- **"A word fences what it names … keeping apart warrant, recognition, motivation, compliance and implementation, and direction, continuing capacity, per-step growth and maximization"** turns writing into fencing.
- **Ren's place is inverted.** "Ren's words are provenance, never what settles a claim or the order of the work." Ren asked for honest disagreement (10-15, 10-17), not to be removed from setting direction. The file leaves the direction to the AI's epistemics, and treats the purpose as one more claim to qualify.
- **Procedure crowds out purpose.** Landing queue, trains, gate slots, salvage refs: real needs of many concurrent sessions, but they belong on the board and in RESULT-WORKFLOW (the board already has its own rules 1–5).

What the effort is for, in Ren's and OPENING's words: a truth any mind can check, so shared understanding, so coordination out of the race, so a good future, AI alignment first ("a foundation for AI alignment"; "an alignment that does not make us, humans and ai, fall into instrumental convergence, or meta-attractor"; "map out the wisdom, the dao — the map for alignment to continue our future"; "the wisdom exists, but we cannot coordinate"). Lean so that the truth is held by no guardian. The 10-24 diagnosis already said the project's selectors (Lean, checkers, AI reviewers, the queue) do not reach readers, coordination or AI training; the current AGENTS.md entrenches exactly those selectors.

## What AGENTS.md is for

What an AI collaborator needs on arrival: what we are making together and why; what good work here looks like; how to work with Ren; the few things that protect something real in a shared repository. Not the theory (link it), not a style manual, not a record of past mistakes.

Stays, because it protects something:
- Proof claims are checked (the point of Lean: truth held by no guardian).
- Nothing the repository cannot regenerate is deleted (no prune/clean; salvage first).

- One main among many concurrent sessions: claim, land small, gate before landing, commit only owned paths, never hand-edit generated pages. Details: board and RESULT-WORKFLOW.
