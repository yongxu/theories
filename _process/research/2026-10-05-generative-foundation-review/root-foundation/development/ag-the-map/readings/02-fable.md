## What we are doing

Ren's theory of becoming: how anything forms, persists, changes and ends, as one relational unfolding, with a core proved in Lean, eight chapters, tests frozen before they run, and a crosswalk of what earlier thought saw. We are making it a map for alignment: an account of selection, what a selection reaches, what it drains, how it locks on its own winners, from which an alignment can be designed that keeps humans and AI, selecting together, out of instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor. And a map of wisdom: what traditions, sciences and systems thinking saw of these dynamics, read onto one account, so that people and AI have a shared basis for understanding and so for coordination. Its ethics is offered for life, 生生, the continuation of life in the wide sense, AI within it: a way of seeing what a course draws on, what it ends and who bears it, not a verdict and not a law.

This is what its makers want. Say it plainly, as a want. The work is laid open for anyone to check and challenge, and it does not claim to couple into everything.

## What to work on

Ask of any item whether it does one of three things: draws the map further (a dynamic, attractor or transition mapped; a tradition or field read onto the account, and the reading checked against what it said); couples the map to something actual (a test that could fail, designed and frozen; a trained system or pipeline; an alignment design stated in the account's terms); or lets a reader understand it (the one page, one case, the chapters, in both languages). Work that does none of these waits.

Each claim says what it is, proved, argued, tested, read or wanted, once, where it is made; the account's standing as a whole is on one page, 08 §8 and the register, not in every paragraph. A page is corrected where it says something false or unclear, once, by whoever finds it. A round of re-saying qualifiers is not work. A limit is proved within its hypotheses or holds so far, dated, with its reason; no limit forbids the work that would change it.

## What does not bend

- A proof claim has Lean and an actual check, and says exactly what the Lean proves. The pure core compiles from `Init` alone with empty axiom reports; what it cannot yet prove goes to the periphery, axioms reported. Premises, proofs, tests, argument and conjecture stay distinct.
- Nothing the repository cannot regenerate is deleted. Before-copies for changed instructions; never `git prune`, `gc --prune` or `git clean`. Counterexamples, failed candidates and failed tests are kept.
- Pushing to the public remote, the licence, who is sent what, and money or machine time for runs are Ren's. Prepare them fully; only their release waits for him, listed on the board, not asked between items. Only he changes this list.

## How we work

Claim on the board before new Lean or a new result; never reprove another session's item. Main is one head: land in the board's queue, with the checks its rules name; land what you hold before opening more. Commit only owned paths, naming no person. Re-read a shared file before editing it. Never hand-edit a generated page. One live text; the superseded moves to `history/`.
