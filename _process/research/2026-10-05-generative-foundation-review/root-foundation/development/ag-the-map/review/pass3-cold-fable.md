**After the opening.** The person who directs the work believes there are invariants of becoming that any mind can check, that an absolute self is impossible, that the one step to count others is what the sages agreed on, that alignment is the most important matter now and that a shared, guardian-free understanding is where coordination can start; the meta-attractor is the name for the race that remakes the minds under it. ### 3. Where the Chinese reads wrong or unnatural

Wrong (meaning moved):

1. ONE-PAGE.zh, 根: "责任是否也这样从关系之内分化出来，没有表明" says something the English and the home do not (above, 2.5). Suggest "责任是否划定谁的损失算数，没有表明".
2. "framing" is "一种框架" in README-ZH and "一种表述方式" in ONE-PAGE.zh, which in its next sentence uses 框架 for "frameworks". 表述方式 is the better word for framing precisely because 框架 is taken; the screen should follow the one page.

Unnatural (reads as translation):

3. 耗竭 used transitively with an object: "在它所读之外耗竭什么", "必定耗竭外部之物" (README-ZH), "把外部之物耗竭到……程度" (ONE-PAGE.zh). In ordinary Chinese 耗竭 is intransitive (资源耗竭); the transitive is 耗尽 or 使……枯竭. It is the corpus's term (THE-THEORY.zh 6×, USES.zh 5×), so this is a term choice to make once, not a slip; I note it because the screen is the first sentence a Chinese reader meets.
4. README-ZH, 伦理: "本论的伦理为生命、为生生（……）而提供，是一种看法" dangles. Suggest "本论的伦理是为生命、为生生（……）而提出的一种看法：看……".
5. 更新 for "renewal" without the one page's gloss ("更新（把取走的补回来）") reads as "update" in the screen ("取自何种更新"). Keep the gloss on first use or use 再生.
6. "读到同一部论述上", "读到自身份额之外" calque "read onto / read beyond". "对照到同一部论述上", "读取自身份额之外的东西".
7. README-ZH, 有什么用: "并以其中一条为例逐问走过一遍" is ambiguous (one question? one pipeline?); the English is "worked on one [pipeline]". Suggest "并在一条这样的流水线上逐问走过一遍".
8. ONE-PAGE.zh: "本论的制作者" ×3 for "its makers". 制作者 is for films and products; for a theory 提出者, or the screen's own "主持这项工作的人" / "做这项工作的人".
9. ONE-PAGE.zh, 对齐: "锁定及时被离开" is an unnatural passive. "形成的锁定被及时走出" or "及时走出锁定".
10. ONE-PAGE.zh, 证据: "重述代理指标问题中对代理指标所不记录者之不见" is hard to parse; "……对代理指标所不记录之物的盲视".
11. Quotation marks: 「有通过，有未通过」 in 「」 on pages that otherwise use “”. Cosmetic, in both Chinese fronts.
12. Good and worth keeping: 生灭变化 as the name with its gloss; "胜出又怎样换来左右选择规则的力量"; 薪火相传 for renewal of parts; the lake explaining the four words; "好让人与 AI 能共有一种任何心智都能核对的理解，协调可以从这里起步".
