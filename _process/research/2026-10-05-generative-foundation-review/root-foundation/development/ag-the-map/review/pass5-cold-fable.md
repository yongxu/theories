## Where message 9 has a hole

Ren: "there is no warrant, ethics emerges from continuation condition, not another way around, there is no any dualistic good or evil." Three places where the sentence says less, or other, than the Lean gives, and the fronts have quietly corrected it; he should see that they did.

1. **"Emerges from continuation condition."** The condition alone ranks nothing, by construction (`Unrestricted sig` has no valence, and `ReadsNoOrder.not_entails` says no fact about the data fixes an order). What orders courses is self-concern, an order a life holds, meeting the condition where relations close (`increasingInOwn_iff_increasing`). So the ethics emerges from *continuing* (a life holding its own) *where relations close*, not from the condition; the fronts say "emerges from continuation" and are right to. Message 11 accepted the session's reading, so this is settled in practice; the record should say that "condition" in message 9 was read as "continuation", and why.

2. **It emerges on the closed half only.** Where relations run one way, concern for one's own continuation does not reach those who depend on one (`Downstream.one_way`, `Clearing.center_not_derived`), and nothing emerges there: what counts the bed is the step of invariance, argued and coherently denied, and T8's seeing, offered. The one-way case is not a corner: a selection that drains what it cannot read is one-way by definition, and so is the lake. The ethics the work is for, the alignment half, lives where it does not emerge. The fronts say this plainly ("Where relations run one way ... is the way of seeing the work offers"); message 9 reads as if the whole ethics emerges. The hole is not in the fronts but in the sentence, and a reader of the source should be told the fronts' two halves are the answer to it.

3. **"No warrant" is itself a claim with a reach.** Said beyond becoming ("no mind is bound"), it would be a should beyond becoming. The fronts avoid this: "no should stands beyond becoming, so there is no warrant binding every mind to find", argued on the thesis of one relational unfolding, which is a claim within the map that a different map could deny. That is the right form; message 9's flat "there is no warrant" is not, and the record should carry the fronts' form.

4. **"No dualistic good or evil", and widening.** Widening sorts courses three ways (widens, mixed, closes), and the laws weigh mixed courses for no one; no valence is set over becoming. But "more future" (screen, one page) and the opening's "draws a line exactly" are where a reader will hear a good; the first is a gloss self-concern earns from a standpoint, the second is Ren's own text (§1.2 above). The hole is small and his to close in the opening.

C has not gone; it is a named order used as a hypothesis in many results (`Comparison`), T5's premise relocated, and the fronts say "beyond that, C ... is a named order".
