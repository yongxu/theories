4. **"Argued" for self-concern's formation.** Message 11's reading and the fronts both lean on "a life forms such an order", and the work has no argument for it at a home (§2.3). This is the one place where I think the sixth pass has made the sentence Ren wants rather than the sentence the work has; the fix is to say "open" until the argument is written, which is also the more interesting admission: the open target (08 §12 item 12, how scope and order form) is exactly whether becoming forms self-concern, and the fronts should let a reader see that the ethics' hinge is the work's open target, not something already argued.

5. **"Every way of counting" is the additive reading.** The dissolution of C into unanimity rests on Sen's intersection over additive positive weightings (§2.1). Said as "every way of counting each thing as worth something", it claims the non-additive valuers too, which T5 does not. The hole is small and closes with one word.
