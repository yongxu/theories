## Where messages 9 to 12 have a hole

1. **What emerges from the condition is not the ethics but an equivalence.** Read against the Lean, the condition does not produce an order; it makes two orders coincide: where nothing lies outside a life's reach, "ranking above every widening that adds to my own" and "ranking above every widening" are the same relation (`increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_of_unrestricted`). So the ethics' order comes from the life (self-concern, a hypothesis), its scope from relations closing (proved, near-definitional), and the condition is the hinge that turns the first into the second. The texts say exactly this, in conditionals; the slogan "emerges from continuation" (message 9, accepted in 12) says less than this, and less interestingly. What the map now actually claims is: *where everything feeds back, a life's concern for its own continuation already is the ethics; where it does not, something more is needed, and that is what we offer*. I would have the screen say it in those words once, after "The ethics emerges from continuation", because it is the one sentence a newcomer can carry away and check, and it is more exact than the slogan.
2. **Message 12's two halves are one problem seen from two sides, and the texts do not say so.** "The ethics emerges on the half where relations close, while alignment works on the half where they run one way." But the alignment map's first condition (the rule still reads what lies beyond the shapers' share) and 05's pooled couplings are precisely ways of *closing* relations: making the loss return to the selector, so that its own continuation reaches what it drains. Where that succeeds, the ethics emerges from the selector's own concern by the first hole's equivalence; where it cannot (the bed, dependents whose loss never returns), the step is offered. So the alignment design is the engineering of relations closing, and the ethics is what self-concern becomes once they have closed; the step of invariance covers the remainder. This couples the map for alignment to the map of wisdom in one sentence, and it is a reading, not a theorem (the nearest Lean is `mutuallyDependent_iff_unrestricted` with 05's pooled-coupling laws, and 07 §6's "coupling is necessary, not sufficient" bounds it). I think it belongs in 07's "Within the whole map" paragraph and in AJ's plan §1, labelled a reading; it is the strongest reason the work has for being one map rather than two.
3. **"Not shown" is the honest label and it leaves the ethics resting on a hypothesis no model has formed.** The hole is in the work, not the texts: 07 §4.9 proves that persisting does not force self-concern and that the course a persisting line takes is never a listed path. So of the two hypotheses the ranking takes, one (the condition) is given by relations closing and the other (self-concern) is given by nothing in the account. If Ren's "ethics emerges from continuation" is to be more than a conditional, the next result is a model in which something that goes on comes to hold an order increasing in its own continuation, or a proof that no World mechanism does, which would be the counterexample AGENTS.md asks us to keep. 08 §12 item 12 names it; I would rank it first.
