## Where messages 9 to 12, or the reading in 07, have a hole

1. **The hinge rests on a choice of relation that the slogan hides.** "The ethics emerges from continuation" is carried, on the fronts, by reading a life's own continuation as its reach along *being affected*. Along *feeding*, the relation "a life goes on through what sustains it" names, mutual dependence does not give the condition (05: mutual dependence does not give mutual feeding; the clearer's world and the pair with nothing given are the witnesses), and a self-concern marked by the feeding closure is not increasing in admission wherever a listed item lies beyond it (`Weighting.exists_increasingInOwn_not_increasing_of_outside`). So the equivalence "where relations close, self-concern already ranks every widening above" holds for concern drawn along what can touch one, and fails for concern drawn along what sustains one, on the same web. That is not a defect of the Lean, which states the relation; it is a hole in how the fronts' first clause and the hinge are joined: the paragraph moves from "what sustains it" to "its reach along that relation" without saying that the second is wider than the first, and that which relation a life's own is, is open (07 thesis; 08 §12 item 12). Fix: the clause in §2.1, and in 07 §4's opening one sentence: "The relation the marking reads is a reading; along feeding, narrower than being affected, the condition can fail where relations close along being affected, and a feeding-marked self-concern then ranks less (`exists_increasingInOwn_not_increasing_of_outside`)." With it, "emerges from continuation" says exactly what it carries: a life whose concern reaches everything that can affect it ranks by widening where everything can.
2. **The reading's "depends on it" is a gloss wider than the word.** 07 "Within the whole map" and AJ §1: "Where a selector reads what it drains and depends on it, its own concern already ranks above what it widens every widening that adds to what it depends on." "Depends on" there means "lies within its closure along being affected", which the reading says exactly one sentence earlier ("bear on what the selector depends on, along being affected") and the Lean fixes (`WithinInfluence`). On the ordinary word, a selector depends on the fish and not on a rival boat; on the closure, the rival boat is within its reach too, since it exchanges with the fish. Fix: "reads what it drains, and what it drains can affect, at some remove, what it depends on". Low-medium; it is the same hole as 1 at the reading's grain.
3. **The reading says what closing dependence would and would not do, and leaves one design consequence unsaid.** It says closing dependence is a mechanism to examine, that domination also closes it, and that the order side is open. What follows for the design, and is not said: a return engineered as dependence makes the selector's concern reach the loss only under the hypothesis that the selector holds a self-concerned order; a reward, a rule or a market holds none that is shown; so for the joint selection, closing dependence buys nothing the hinge gives unless some member of it is a life with such an order, and then it is that member's concern, not the selection's, that ranks. One clause would say it: "and where it is a life in the joint selection, and not the rule, that holds the order, what the closed relation buys is that life's reach, not the selection's." Low; the plan's §1 is where it belongs.
4. **Message 12's sentence, as the source's AI note glosses it** ("the ethics emerges on the half where relations close, while alignment works on the half where they run one way"), conflates two relations that the reading now keeps apart: alignment's instruments (condition 1, pools) act on reading; the hinge needs dependence; a loss can be read and not depended on. The fronts and 07 are right; the note at the end of the source is the one place where the conflated form still stands as the accepted reading.
