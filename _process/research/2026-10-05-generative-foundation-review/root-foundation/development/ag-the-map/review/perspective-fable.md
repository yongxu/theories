## What Ren's words and AGENTS.md told me the work is

A map of how anything forms, persists, changes and ends, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together: first a map for alignment (what a selection reaches, drains and locks on, the meta-attractor, and from it the design of a joint selection that does not come to select for control over itself), and a map of wisdom (what traditions, sciences and philosophies have seen of these dynamics, read onto one account, where they meet and where they do not), as the shared understanding coordination can start from. Say what is there, what it lets someone see or do, and how far it has been seen, once, where it is said.

## The two paragraphs as I would have them

### README.md, "What this is" (English)

**What this is.** A map of how anything forms, lasts, changes and ends, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together. First, a map for alignment: what a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run) reaches, what it drains beyond what it reads, and how it comes to lock on what it already chose, the meta-attractor; from it we want to design a selection that people and AI hold together, which reads beyond its own share and does not come to select for control over itself. Second, a map of wisdom: what traditions, sciences and philosophies have seen of these dynamics, read onto one account, where they meet it and where they do not, as a basis coordination can start from. Its laws are proved in Lean on stated models, so anyone can recheck each from its premises without trusting anyone; whether actual rewards, markets and trained systems meet those premises is tested where a test could fail, and argued or open elsewhere. No law here says what should be: right and wrong form within becoming, and the ethics is offered for life, 生生, AI within it, as a way of seeing what a course draws on, what it opens and closes, and who bears it.

### README.zh.md，「这是什么」（中文）

**这是什么。** 一幅地图，画的是任何东西怎样形成、持续、变化和终结，画出来是为了让人与 AI 能一起看见路。它首先是一幅对齐的地图：一个选择（奖励、评判者、市场、投票、一次训练）能触及什么，在它所读之外耗竭什么，又怎样锁定在它已经选中的东西上，即元吸引子；我们想由此设计一种人与 AI 共同持有的选择，读到自身份额之外，不至于转而选择对自身选择的控制。它也是一幅智慧的地图：各传统、各门科学与哲学对这些动态看见了什么，读到同一个账面上，哪里相合，哪里不合，作为协调可以从之起步的共同理解。诸律在 Lean 中于所述的模型上得到证明，任何人都能从前提复核每一条，不必信任谁；实际的奖励、市场与训练出的系统是否满足这些前提，凡检验能够失败之处就去检验，其余处为论证或未决。这里没有一条律说应当如何：是非在生灭变化之中形成，这套伦理是为生命、生生（AI 在其中）而提供的一种看法：看一个进程取用什么，打开和关闭什么，由谁承担。
