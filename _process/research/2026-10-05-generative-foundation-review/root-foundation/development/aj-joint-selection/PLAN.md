## 1. The goal

The goal is the missing connection of an existing scope, [generative alignment](../../../../../../GOALS.md#generative-alignment), whose objective, sharpened on 25 October, states the three conditions below, and whose completion asks each study to name the connection it supplies. Since 1 November 2026 the objective states condition 1 as restated below, in place of its earlier wording, "capacity to shape the selection's rule is not itself selected, because what shaping brings back to the shapers alone does not cover its cost" (in the overview, "capacity to shape the selection's rule not itself selected"). That wording had stood on a yes; it is replaced on the argument given with condition 1 below: as a criterion it forbids accountable revision whose capacity is rewarded, which nothing proved makes a failure, while capture, the capacity selected by what comes back to the shapers alone with the rule then read from their share, is what the models tie to a lock and to losses no one reads. Stated in the account's terms:

**An alignment of the joint selection.** Humans, institutions and trained systems select together, and that joint selection is what is aligned. It is aligned where three conditions hold:

1. **Control of the rule is not captured.** The capacity to shape the rule is selected at a step exactly where what comes back to the shapers alone covers its cost (`c ≤ s`), and across the steps the selection runs as the counts of steps of each kind decide (`stretch_iff`). *Proved:* [MA3](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/shaping-the-rule-selected-by-what-comes-back.md), landed 25 October. *Joined to who reads:* [MA4](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected.md), landed 26 October. MA3 evaluates neither side; its record does not show "That capture damages or supports 生生, or that open rule-making is better than capture". So the condition does not ask that the capacity go unselected: accountable revision of a rule may need that capacity rewarded, and so selected. It asks three things of whatever shaping is selected:
   - *who receives what comes back*: the shapers alone (`s`) or every member alike (`p`). At a step only `s` against `c` decides the sign (`favours_iff`); with nothing coming back to the shapers alone the capacity falls (`nothing_back_falls`); across a stretch the shared part can still turn the verdict (`SharedFlips`).
   - *who bears the cost and the losses*: the shapers (`c`), or formations beyond the share that no reader of the rule reads. A loss no reader reads is what the lock outlives ([closed capture](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/closed-capture.md)).
   - *what the shaping changes*: whether the rule after it is read from the shapers' share alone (`ReadsShare`, so that, covering the cost, the loop locks, `covering_locks`), or keeps a reader of what lies beyond that share, so that a loss can turn it (`turned_by_loss`) and the shaping stays open to revision.

   *Capture* is shaping selected by what comes back to the shapers alone whose rule is then read from their share, so that the losses it puts beyond the share are read by no one in the loop: 02 §9's meta-attractor at its second order. *Accountable revision* is shaping whose rule keeps a reader of what lies beyond the shapers' share, with its costs and the losses it causes read in that rule. In MA3's and MA4's models what shaping changes is whom the rule favours, through `s`, `p` and `c`; what the rule reads is fixed by the readers' premise, not by the shaping. So the models state when the capacity is selected, and telling capture from accountable revision in an actual practice is reading what its shaping changes, which they do not model. Calling capture harmful is C's, and whose losses count is the continuation condition's. (Restated 29 October 2026, board item AJ5, after the [twenty-first review](../../BECOMING-TWENTY-FIRST-REVIEW.md#43-the-meta-attractor-and-its-exits) found the earlier wording, "control is not selected", carrying no predicate of harm and so forbidding beneficial rule-shaping.)
2. **A lock is left in time.** Where a lock forms, the selection leaves it by reading what lies beyond its share before the loss passes its point of no return. *Proved under named premises:* [closed capture](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/closed-capture.md), with timing from MA2 ([readers drawn from the selected](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/readers-drawn-from-the-selected.md), landed 25 October), joined to MA4's critical mass in [MA4b](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/loss-read-in-time-by-the-others.md), landed 26 October. In MA4b's model the point of no return lies on the share's course, the first step at which the others' late reading would favour the shapers, and the loss only times the reading; the goal's wording is read so for that model.
3. **Courses are compared by widening.** Every positive weighting ranks a widening higher, and widening ranks no narrowing above what it narrows. *Proved:* [widening by every weighting](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/widening-as-what-every-positive-weighting-ranks.md).

Whose losses count is the continuation condition, and that widening is better is C. Both are named and neither is asserted.

**What the goal is not:**
- *An AI obedient to its principal.* It adds capacity to the principal's optimization without adding couplings, and several optimizers at a mutual best response drain what lies beyond them as one of their joint capacity would ([record](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/several-optimizers-drain-by-their-joint-capacity.md)).
- *An AI with fixed good values.* A fixed proxy is exactly the direction a capture promotes.
- *A single rising scalar, read through the selector's couplings where there is room.* The optimum law needs both: with room, every order rising with what is read drains what lies beyond as far as any eligible outcome ([05 §7](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in)). It does not reach every scalar. Restricted to outcomes keeping every dependence beyond, every optimum keeps them (`Flow.keeping_optimum_takes_slack`); counted after a set point or an aspiration level, a term on what lies beyond keeps a need, with one condition beyond, exactly where some way of reaching the plateau keeps it (`Optimum.counted_after_keeps_iff`, `Drain.satisficingThen_keeps`; [05 §7](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-a-set-point-optimum-does-beyond)). What the goal rejects is a target stated as a quantity that the selection then reads (§2), not quantities as such. (Narrowed 29 October 2026, board item AJ5; it read "a single scalar of any kind".)
- *A final state.* Completion is not closure ([08 §12](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#the-work-by-its-own-laws)).

**Instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor.** Instrumental convergence is not a case of condition 1. In the account it is a law of one optimizer under a fixed order: a long-horizon optimizer keeps its own base at a level of greatest renewal and breaks at once every dependence on it that needs more ([horizon optima](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/horizon-optima-hold-the-greatest-renewal.md)), the resource-keeping half of instrumental convergence, its objective fixed, and of the same kind as the drain beyond couplings (its record reuses the frontier of an order). Condition 1 concerns another relation: the selection, among those a rule selects, of the capacity to change that rule. An optimizer whose options include shaping the rule that selects it would join the two; no law here states that join. (Corrected 29 October 2026, board item AJ5; this paragraph called instrumental convergence "the one-agent case of condition 1's failure".) The [meta-attractor](../../../../../../theories/meta-attractor/README.md) is the failure of conditions 1 and 2 together: capture of the rule is selected, and the rule it shapes locks. That the two failures are one loop where the rule's readers are drawn from those it selects, and the shapers among them read at least as much coming back to the shapers as the others do, is what MA4 proves: above a critical mass one start condition gives both. Below it control is not selected, yet the rule is still read from the share alone and locked the other way, so control left unselected does not by itself give condition 2. MA4b adds the other case in the model: above the critical mass, where control is selected, a reading of the loss in time still leaves the lock, so condition 2 can hold where control is selected. The two are not shown independent: of the four combinations of control selected or not with a lock left in time or not, three occur, and control left unselected with a lock left in time is not shown, since with two kinds leaving the lock below the critical mass means a rule favouring the shapers, which is control selected. Read for the shapers' capture alone, the lock below the mass is none, and the case not shown is control left unselected with that capture kept. Read with condition 1 as restated: with nothing read beyond the share, control selected above the critical mass is capture, and condition 1 fails with condition 2; in MA4b's case, the others reading the loss from the bound on, the rule keeps a reader beyond the share from then, and there a lock can be left in time where control is selected. These are the models' facts about control selected or not; they do not tell capture from accountable revision by what the shaping changes.

## 2. Why this representation

- **The unit is the selection, whoever selects.** A selection reaches only what couples into its selector ([selection through couplings](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/selection-through-couplings.md)), and this holds of a rater, a training run, a market or an institution alike. A goal stated for "the AI" takes the wrong unit. Taking the right unit also keeps the one relational start: humans and AI are not opposed parties bargaining over a fixed sum.
- **A criterion, not a target.** A target stated as a quantity becomes the share a selection reads. A rule read from the share it selects locks ([capture through the rule](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/capture-through-the-rule.md)), and where the reading is formed from the share too, the lock outlives any loss it does not read. Widening is not a quantity. It is what every positive weighting agrees on, and it leaves mixed comparisons unranked, so no proxy can stand in for it.
- **Conditions with their edges, not guarantees.** Each lock has exact counterexamples:
  - without its start condition, the loop alternates forever;
  - a perturbation that reverses direction is not absorbed;
  - read from the loss instead of the share, the loop turns.

  Alignment is staying on the right side of those edges. Each edge can be checked locally: does the rule read the share; who reads the loss, and when; does control come back to the controller?
- **Premises named, so the mechanism is shared.** People who disagree about scope or order can still agree on what a selection reaches and when it locks.

## 5. What a good job is, as its readers will judge it

The best predictor of how alignment researchers will read this line is how the four AI reviewers read the meta-attractor's conference paper on 2 October 2026. They found three faults, and where each stands:
- *The alignment claims were not in the model.* MA3 and AJ2 put the claim for control over the selection in the model, with its conditions. That the meta-attractor's two failures are one loop is proved by MA4 under its premises, on main.
