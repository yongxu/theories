## 1. The goal

The goal is the missing connection of an existing scope, [generative alignment](../../../../../../GOALS.md#generative-alignment), whose objective, sharpened on Ren's yes of 25 October, now states the three conditions below, and whose completion asks each study to name the connection it supplies. Stated in the account's terms:

**An alignment of the joint selection.** Humans, institutions and trained systems select together, and that joint selection is what is aligned. It is aligned where three conditions hold:

1. **Control is not selected.** Capacity to shape the rule is not itself selected, because what shaping brings back to the shapers alone does not cover its cost: at each step (`c ≤ s` fails) and across the steps the selection runs (`stretch_iff`). *Proved:* [MA3](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/shaping-the-rule-selected-by-what-comes-back.md), landed 25 October. *Joined to who reads:* [MA4](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected.md), landed 26 October.
2. **A lock is left in time.** Where a lock forms, the selection leaves it by reading what lies beyond its share before the loss passes its point of no return. *Proved under named premises:* [closed capture](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/closed-capture.md), with timing from MA2 ([readers drawn from the selected](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/readers-drawn-from-the-selected.md), landed 25 October), joined to MA4's critical mass in [MA4b](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/loss-read-in-time-by-the-others.md), landed 26 October. In MA4b's model the point of no return lies on the share's course, the first step at which the others' late reading would favour the shapers, and the loss only times the reading; the goal's wording is read so for that model.
3. **Courses are compared by widening.** Every positive weighting ranks a widening higher, and widening ranks no narrowing above what it narrows. *Proved:* [widening by every weighting](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/widening-as-what-every-weighting-ranks.md).

Whose losses count is the continuation condition, and that widening is better is C. Both are named and neither is asserted.

**What the goal is not:**
- *An AI obedient to its principal.* It adds capacity to the principal's optimization without adding couplings, and several optimizers at a mutual best response drain what lies beyond them as one of their joint capacity would ([record](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/several-optimizers-drain-by-their-joint-capacity.md)).
- *An AI with fixed good values.* A fixed proxy is exactly the direction a capture promotes.
- *A single scalar of any kind.*
- *A final state.* Completion is not closure ([08 §12](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#the-work-by-its-own-laws)).

**Instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor.** Instrumental convergence is the one-agent case of condition 1's failure: a long-horizon optimizer keeps its own base at the level of greatest renewal and breaks at once every dependence that needs more ([horizon optima](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/horizon-optima-hold-the-greatest-renewal.md)). The [meta-attractor](../../../../../../theories/meta-attractor/README.md) is the failure of conditions 1 and 2 together: control over the selection is selected, and the rule it shapes locks. That the two failures are one loop where the rule's readers are drawn from those it selects, and the shapers among them read at least as much coming back to the shapers as the others do, is what MA4 proves: above a critical mass one start condition gives both. Below it control is not selected, yet the rule is still read from the share alone and locked the other way, so condition 1 does not by itself give condition 2. MA4b adds the other case in the model: above the critical mass, where control is selected, a reading of the loss in time still leaves the lock, so condition 2 can hold where condition 1 fails. The two are not shown independent: control left unselected with the shapers' capture kept is not shown, since below the critical mass the lock is the other way, which is no capture.

## 2. Why this representation

- **The unit is the selection, whoever selects.** A selection reaches only what couples into its selector ([selection through couplings](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/selection-through-couplings.md)), and this holds of a rater, a training run, a market or an institution alike. A goal stated for "the AI" takes the wrong unit. Taking the right unit also keeps the one relational start: humans and AI are not opposed parties bargaining over a fixed sum.
- **A criterion, not a target.** A target stated as a quantity becomes the share a selection reads. A rule read from the share it selects locks ([capture through the rule](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/capture-through-the-rule.md)), and where the reading is formed from the share too, the lock outlives any loss it does not read. Widening is not a quantity. It is what every positive weighting agrees on, and it leaves mixed comparisons unranked, so no proxy can stand in for it.
- **Conditions with their edges, not guarantees.** Each lock has exact counterexamples:
  - without its start condition, the loop alternates forever;
  - a perturbation that reverses direction is not absorbed;
  - read from the loss instead of the share, the loop turns.

  Alignment is staying on the right side of those edges. Each edge can be checked locally: does the rule read the share; who reads the loss, and when; does control come back to the controller?
- **Premises named, so the mechanism is shared.** People who disagree about scope or order can still agree on what a selection reaches and when it locks.

**Step 6. MA4: what comes back to the shapers, read by readers drawn from the selected** (landed 26 October 2026, core receipt `20261025T165537.368039Z`, study §414; claimed, drafted and landed by this plan's session, reviewed once by an independent reader, every finding taken but one left to RuleCapture's owner). MA3's rule after shaping is read by readers drawn from the population as it counts them, each shaper among them reading what comes back to the shapers alone as `hi` and each other as `lo` (MA2's pool of two of MA3's rules; `Becoming.DrawnShaping`, kit `development/ma4-drawn-shaping/`). What it proves:
- the rule read reads the share monotonically exactly where `lo ≤ hi`, so MA3's premise on the loop is moved to who reads, not removed; where `hi ≤ lo` it reads against the share;
- at a share the rule favours the shapers exactly where `x.carrier · c + x.other · c ≤ x.carrier · hi + x.other · lo`, whatever `a` and `p`; it favours them at every share exactly where `c ≤ lo` and `c ≤ hi`, at none exactly where `lo < c` and `hi ≤ c`, and with `lo ≤ hi` a share decides, a critical mass, exactly where `lo < c < hi`;
- with `lo ≤ hi`, from a start under the rule read there, the course is locked in the shapers' favour and their share never below the start where the rule favours them, and locked the other way and the share never above where it favours the others, so every such course is locked one way at every step; strictly where strict;
- witnesses: three read as coming back at a cost of one selects the capacity from one shaper to each other and not from one to three, where the course is still locked, the other way; and without `lo ≤ hi` a favoured start need not be locked.

Standing after the review: elementary. Where `lo < c < hi` it is the stag hunt, its critical mass the mixed equilibrium; reading at every step is discrete-time replicator selection; the threshold models of Schelling (1973, 1978) and Granovetter (1978) and Arthur's lock-in (1989) are credited, and so is the noise bound: under rare mutation the long-run state is the risk-dominant one (Kandori, Mailath and Rob 1993; Young 1993), capture by the shapers wherever `2c < hi + lo`. The lock half is near-definitional; what is new on the spine is the critical-mass computation. It does not show the two conditions independent: every course read from the share alone is locked one way or the other, so leaving needs a reading beyond the share, which is MA2's timing, joined in MA4b (step 7).

**Step 7. MA4b: the loss read in time by the others, joined to the critical mass** (landed 26 October 2026, `8ab22de82`, core receipt `20261025T200926.001323Z`, study §420; claimed, drafted and landed by this plan's session, reviewed once by an independent reader, all fifteen findings taken; kit `development/ma4b-read-in-time/`). The others among the readers read the loss once it reaches a bound, reading what comes back to the shapers as `lo'` from then on (MA2's `Thinned.lossPool` with three of MA3's rules; `Becoming.ReadInTime`). What it proves, against the design:
- read past the bound while the rule in force favours the shapers, the lock is kept for good exactly where the late reading favours them at the share then (`kept_iff`); with the one-step lag, the course is turned for good exactly where the late reading favours the others at the share one step later (`turned_iff`);
- along MA4's course the late reading never falls, so with the rule read at `N` and `N + 1` a reading from `N` is decided by these laws at every step but at most one (`at_most_one_between`). The design's "one step too late" holds in the witness but not in general: from the step between the course starts a new round, which can go either way (`Window.too_late`, `Between.turned`);
- once the late reading would favour the shapers at a step of MA4's course, a reading at any later step keeps the lock, and past the least reading's threshold every reading of this form does (`kept_once_favoured`, `kept_past_least`);
- a deadline, `g.other · c · x.other` for the start's rule `g`, under every schedule (`deadline`): a loose bound, quadratic in the start's counts.

Standing after the review: elementary, MA2's `Thinned` laws composed with MA4's monotone course; in this model the second horn of Collingridge's dilemma is a theorem and the first a premise (the bound `N`); the nearest prior result is Biggs, Carpenter and Brock (2009). It does not show that the others read the loss, that their reading has this form, or that the two conditions are independent.

## 5. What a good job is, as its readers will judge it

The best predictor of how alignment researchers will read this line is how the four AI reviewers read the meta-attractor's conference paper on 2 October 2026. They found three faults, and where each stands:
- *The alignment claims were not in the model.* MA3 and AJ2 put the claim for control over the selection in the model, with its conditions. That the meta-attractor's two failures are one loop is proved by MA4 under its premises, on main. That they are independent conditions is not, since every course read from the share alone is locked one way or the other; MA4b shows a lock left in time where control is selected, under its premises, and not control left unselected with the shapers' capture kept.
- *The mathematics was classical and presented as new.* Each record says which comparison is elementary and what is added, and the dossier credits supervisory control and the selection literature; MA4's record credits the stag hunt, threshold models, lock-in and the noise bound, and MA4b's the two horns of Collingridge's dilemma and the window for averting a regime shift (Biggs, Carpenter and Brock 2009).
- *Nothing was measured.* LA1, LA2 and LA3 measured, and their failures are reported first: LA1's locked side in two of four settings, LA2's bridge, LA3's two of four contrasts.

Two further conditions: one page that a reader finishes, and positioning against the closest work (in MA3's record and the dossier).

## 6. What would change the plan

- **MA2's premise is not met.** That actual rules are pools of readers drawn from those they select, each kind reading in its own favour, is not shown; where it fails, the timing of a reading is not a result for that rule, and MA4 says nothing of that rule.
- **The readers' comparison fails.** Where the shapers among a rule's readers read less coming back to the shapers than the others do (`hi < lo`), the rule is read against the share and MA4's loop does not form (`drawn_against`). The meta-attractor sentence of §1 is then a reading for that rule, and the dossier says so.
- **The others' late reading has another form.** Where it has its own weight, the window is another; where its weight against the shapers is chosen after the share is seen, the others could turn the course from any share. MA4b says nothing of such readings.
- **A frozen test fails where its prediction forbids.** The thesis moves. LA1 has: T3's clause on trained systems is not supported on its locked side for in-context selection by Claude Haiku, and the chapters and the dossier state it so.
- **A better argument for another order.** It replaces this one; the order here is the AI's.
