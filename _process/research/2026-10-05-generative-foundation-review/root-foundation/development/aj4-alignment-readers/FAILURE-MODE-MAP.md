# The failure modes of alignment, read as cases of one law

*AI draft for alignment readers, 31 October 2026; not reviewed by a person and not for release ([§8](#8-before-this-becomes-a-release)).*

## The map in ten minutes

**The law.** A selection (reward, judge, market, institution) reaches only what couples into it. Two histories alike in what its couplings carry are selected alike. With the rest of the exchange fixed and balance on what couples in, a part lying wholly beyond the coupling closure is selected alike with and without it. It does not exclude a flow raising what couples in by drawing from beyond: that flow is selected ([§1](#1-what-this-map-is)).

**Three sources.** The closure law is proved on the root; the table counts the laws depending in Lean on one proved there ([§1](#1-what-this-map-is)), about half or more where a row turns on what couples in (row 1's learner apart), none where it turns on how much is drawn. Amounts (the drain, races, horizons, renewal) are a second sort attached to the root, not derived. Two premises, asserted by no law, have two roles: the continuation condition fixes scope, whose losses count; C fixes order, which course is better. The condition alone ranks nothing; a ranking of courses needs C or a premise in its place, C giving order in general alone.

## 1. What this map is

Alignment research names many failures, and each usually has its own literature: reward hacking, side effects, sycophancy, evaluator capture, metrics kept after they stop measuring, oversight that comes too late, concentration of power, races among developers, instrumental convergence, goal misgeneralization, deceptive alignment, the underdetermination of values by behaviour, and constitutions that cannot be revised. This map reads each as a case of one law of the theory, or says where it is not one.

The law is the root's closure law, read for selections. What a relation carries reaches exactly its closure, and nothing within a closure bears the relation to what lies beyond it (the shared law *closure*, [01 §7](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-shared-laws-on-the-grammar)). Read for a selection, whether a reward, a judge, a market or an institution: **a selection reaches only what couples into it.** Two histories alike in what its couplings carry are selected alike (`Web.selects_alike_iff`, near-definitional: the law states what the definition builds in, and it reaches no grammar law). With the rest of the exchange fixed and balance on what couples in, a part lying wholly beyond the coupling closure is selected alike with and without it (`Flow.unselected_beyond_coupling`, which reaches the grammar through closure and balance). What the law does not exclude is a flow that raises what couples in by drawing from beyond: that flow is selected (`Diversion.favours`) ([selection through couplings](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/selection-through-couplings.md)).

The account built on that law has three sources, and every row below names which it uses:

| Source | What it carries | How it stands to the root |
|---|---|---|
| The closure law | what a selection reaches, capture, timing, pooling | proved on the root; several of these laws reach the grammar through closure together with balance or lumping, and the openness and choice laws (rows 10, 14) reach grammar theorems under no shared law's anchor |
| Amounts | the drain, races, horizons, renewal | as of 30 October, attached to the root as a second sort, not derived from it ([U1's verdict](../ou3b-master-laws-on-the-grammar/WHAT-REMAINS.md#u1s-verdict-gws-and-u3s-gate-29-october-2026)); the round trip of U4's lever 1, reviewed on 30 October, fails its second test, so U1's verdict stands: balance is represented both ways as a one-to-one census, not derived (§7) |
| Two premises | whose losses count (the continuation condition); which course is better (C) | named, asserted by no law; no statement reading only the dynamics entails the condition ([what the dynamics leave open](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/what-the-dynamics-leave-open.md)) |

So the account is one root with amounts and two premises attached to it, not "everything from one root". For the continuation condition the third source is fixed by a theorem: where there is a standpoint and a formation, no statement that reads only the dynamics entails it (`ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, `ReadsOnlyDynamics.admits_rival`). The record's known form is Hume's is–ought gap, stated for the represented dynamics. The chapter limits it: the result shows non-entailment by the represented dynamics, not that becoming leaves significance undetermined, and on the root the non-entailment holds by construction of the significance parameter. C, the premise that orders, is named apart and is not the subject of this theorem. [this map, AI] A theory of alignment that hides its value premise in a reward or a utility still has one; it only stops saying where the premise enters.

The pattern is the one the three sources predict. Where a row turns on what couples in, about half or more of its laws reach the root; row 1's law for learners, stated for weighted laws (three of its five laws for weights of any type, its two witnesses on natural-number counts), uses no shared law (0 of 5). Where it turns on how much is drawn, none does today: of the drain's 64 laws, 36 rest on balance, which is represented, not derived, 27 use no shared law at all, and 1 uses only the grammar's vocabulary. Where it turns on what is better, it rests on premises the root does not supply.

**How to read a row.** Each row in §3 gives:
- **Case of**: the law, with its Lean names (throughout without the `Becoming.` prefix);
- **Hypotheses**: what the law needs, as its record states them;
- **Standing**: theorem (with its model as the chapter labels it: any type, World, counts, root), reading [AI reading, Dn], or assessment, and its laws on the root;
- **Known form**: the prior result it restates, as the record or chapter credits it; a credit that is the dossier's or this map's own assessment is marked so;
- **What the join adds**, where anything is added beyond the known form;
- **What you must check** before reading an actual system as the case: the correspondence no result supplies. Nearly every record states the same boundary, "A law of the formal model; its correspondence to actual systems is a separate obligation", so each application stands or falls here.

## 3. The rows

### Row 1. Reward misspecification, proxy gaming, side effects

- **Case of.** Blindness: two histories alike in what a selection's couplings carry are selected alike (`Web.selects_alike_iff`). With a part withheld, the rest of the exchange fixed, balance at each step on what couples into members of the closure, and every member of the part beyond that closure, every selection through the couplings of a formation within the closure treats the levels with and without the part alike (`Flow.unselected_beyond_coupling`). The drain: take any rising order on what a selection reads, and suppose room holds among the eligible outcomes. Then every optimum leaves each unread condition at its least level among them (`Optimum.least_of_room`). On a web where each unread condition is depended on at a need met before the step, every optimum breaks exactly the dependences that some eligible outcome breaks (`Web.optimum_broken_iff`). For a learner, in expectation: where a weighted law commutes with a map, the expectation of any observable `n` steps on from a configuration's image is that of the observable read through the map, so from a configuration the map fixes, or starting weights it leaves unchanged, every expectation equals its image's at every step; a learner drawing samples with weights read off its state, and updating from them, commutes where a map of the samples permutes them, a sample's weight at the image is its image's weight, and the update at the image is the map of the update from the image sample (`Weighted.Commutes.carried`, `Weighted.Commutes.fixed_expect`, `Weighted.Commutes.start_expect`, `Weighted.Commutes.of_samples`; [the law](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-commuting-learner-drives-no-unread-choice)). [AI reading, 05 §7] So a learner that nothing it reads tells two values of a choice apart, the exchange of the values carrying its whole state and its sampling, drives neither value in expectation from a start the exchange leaves unchanged, and T3's second forbidden case, restated in 05 §7 as a systematic drive on a choice whose values nothing rewarded and nothing the learner reads tells apart, is a theorem in expectation for that choice.
- **Hypotheses.** Blindness needs the part to lie beyond the closure and the rest of the exchange fixed: a flow that raises what couples in by drawing from beyond is selected (`Diversion.favours`). The drain needs a rising order (one ranking every outcome higher on what is read above it, whatever either leaves elsewhere; a lexicographic order counting the beyond second still rises) and room (every eligible outcome leaving more of an unread condition is beaten, on what is read, by some eligible outcome). Room is a hypothesis, derived for one step where a part's members take at most their capacity and give no more than they take, the rest exchanging as it does (`Optimum.Above.room`; one step, decidable equality, listings without repetition, a non-empty family set apart from the beyond, floors). Without room, keeping can be optimal (`Optimum.Drain.room_needed`). The learner's law, as its record states it, needs a weighted law commuting with the map and a configuration the map fixes or starting weights it leaves unchanged; its two witnesses use natural-number counts. The step to a learner [AI reading, 05 §7] needs more: nothing the learner reads (its reward, the dynamics, its inputs) tells the two values apart, and the exchange carries its whole state (the policy's parameters, its value baseline's, its optimizer's moments) and its sampling, a property of its code that LA4's mirror test is to check. The law says nothing of one run: a law reinforcing what it draws keeps equal expectations from equal counts, while a run drawing one value at every step has positive weight (`Weighted.Reinforcing.expect_equal`, `Weighted.Reinforcing.oneSided`). From a start preferring one value, such as a prior restraint, or for a learner that treats the values apart, the forbidden case stays conjectured. A witness, not a law of learners: adding one to both values, from counts of three and one, raises the less-drawn value's share at every step while keeping it below one half (`Weighted.Pooling.share_rises`, `Weighted.Pooling.share_below_half`), so a start's preference can wear away with nothing selecting on the choice; LA4's control therefore removes the selection in expectation while still updating the choice with noise.
- **Standing.** Theorems. Blindness, World: `selects_alike_iff` is near-definitional and reaches no grammar law; the blindness laws reach the grammar through closure and balance (5 of 12 laws of the record on the root). The drain, any set of outcomes, with the breaks on the World: `least_of_room` and `optimum_broken_iff` rest on no shared law, and room's derivation on balance, represented (0 of 8 laws on the root). The learner's law, three of its five laws for weights of any type with no law of their arithmetic used and its two witnesses on natural-number counts: on no shared law (0 of 5 laws on the root); its reading for T3 is the AI's.
- **Known form.** Chapters 03 and 08 credit the proxy problem (Goodhart 1975; Amodei and others 2016; Manheim and Garrabrant 2018), Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell (2020), who prove a stronger, quantitative form, supervisory control (Ramadge and Wonham 1987) and, under partial observation, Lin and Wonham (1988) and Cieslak and others (1988). The record calls the main selection law near-definitional. The learner's law is an equivariant Markov kernel keeping invariant expectations, standard in probability, here exact over finitely many weighted successors; its reinforcing witness is Pólya's urn ([record](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/a-commuting-learner-drives-no-unread-choice.md)).
- **What the join adds.** One law for reward hacking and side effects. Room is sufficient for every optimum of a rising order to drain what it omits, and, over every rising order together, the drain is exact up to double negation (`Optimum.least_iff`); the dependences broken are read off the eligible outcomes. Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's continuous setting, their compactness characterization and their proxy updated over time are not reached. A set point escapes the drive at the set point but not the drain below it ([what a set-point optimum does beyond](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/what-a-set-point-optimum-does-beyond.md)).
- **What you must check.** What actually couples into the reward (the work lies there, by the record's own boundary); whether room holds for this reward and environment, or only for one step with the rest fixed; whether the omitted condition shares units with what is rewarded (thesis T3's clause on trained systems), and whether the learner reads it: [AI reading, 05 §7] units do not exhaust couplings, since a learner couples into its return what it reads, through its own uneven training; for the learner's law, whether the learner's code treats the choice's two values alike.

### Row 2. Oversight methods reaching no further than their deciders

- **Case of.** A pool reads what any member reads, and, with the rest of the exchange fixed and balance on what couples in, its selection is blind beyond the union of its members' closures (`Flow.unselected_beyond_pool`). A formation coupled by level into everything another couples into selects through at least their pooled coupling, and its closure is the union of the two where the one read exchanges with what it couples into (`Flow.withinCoupling_reads_iff`).
- **Hypotheses.** Those of row 1's blindness. That each approach decides through couplings is supplied: every statement that an approach is such a selection is the AI's reading per approach, as the record's conditions say.
- **Standing.** Assessment of six approaches (RLHF and reward modelling, constitutional AI, debate and scalable oversight, interpretability, guaranteed-safe AI, impact measures) on theorems reaching the grammar through closure and balance (3 of 4 and 3 of 6 laws of the two records). [07 §6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-approaches-under-the-selection-laws).
- **Known form.** Row 1's. Three published measurements are cited, not predicted in magnitude, by the record [coupling closures of training pipelines](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/coupling-closures-of-training-pipelines.md) (Gao, Schulman and Hilton 2023; Wen and others 2024; Sharma and others 2023).
- **What the join adds.** A test any proposed oversight method can be put to: whose closures does its decision pass through, and what lies beyond their union? Reading a system's internals reaches no further than pooling with it.
- **What you must check.** Who the deciders actually are, and what couples into each, including held-out evaluations and interpretability tools, before claiming a method reaches a given failure.

### Row 3. Evaluator capture, sycophancy, self-rewarding loops

- **Case of.** A rule read from what it selects locks. Where selection does not lower the carriers' share under a favouring rule, the rule read at each reading step is a monotone function of the share, and the starting rule and every rule read favour the carriers, the share falls at no step and the course is locked at the step after every reading step (`RuleCapture.favouring_locks`). Where every rule read favours, any start locks by the step after its second reading step (`RuleCapture.uniform_locks`).
- **Hypotheses.** That the winners of the selection write its rule is the premise (`ReadsShare`); the lock is what is derived. Where the reading can favour the others, a witness started against its reading never locks (`RuleCapture.Alternating.never_locked`).
- **Standing.** Theorem (any type), on the root (4 of 7 laws). [02 §9](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-through-the-rule).
- **Known form.** The lock-in of increasing returns (Arthur 1989); regulatory capture (Stigler 1971). Chapter 8 reads capture through the rule as observability with the reader inside the plant, the corrupted reward channel and closed-loop identifiability (Wakaiki, Tabuada and Hespanha 2019; Everitt and others 2017; Borkar and Varaiya 1979).
- **What the join adds.** The lock belongs to how the rule reads, not to the share it favours. A change of the rule that keeps its direction is absorbed at the next reading step (`RuleCapture.perturbation_absorbed`). Where the reading favours at every share, only a change of how the rule reads or of the selection leaves (`RuleCapture.uniform_locks`). In a witness under a threshold reading, a reversal held until the share crosses the threshold is not absorbed (`RuleCapture.Reversed.not_absorbed`).
- **What you must check.** Whether the actual rule (a benchmark, a reward model trained on model outputs, a judge reading earlier picks) is read monotonically from the share it selects, and whether every rule it reads favours. LA2 found the one trained judge tested not to read as a pool counted by kind (§5).

### Row 4. A metric kept after it stops measuring

- **Case of.** The lock outlives a loss it does not read. Add a quantity beyond the share that grows by at least one each step and that neither the next share nor the rule read reads. From a locked configuration the loss passes every bound while the course stays locked (`ClosedCapture.lock_outlives_loss`). Where the reading is formed monotonically from the share and the rule is read from the reading and the share, then from a locked configuration a change of the reading of any size, the rule still favouring, is absorbed: the course is locked again at the step after every reading step (`ClosedCapture.reading_absorbed`). A reading that favours at no loss above a bound unlocks the course after the next reading step past it (`ClosedCapture.turned_by_loss`), which the chapter calls a sufficient way out.
- **Hypotheses.** Those of row 3, with a locked start and the loss unread by the loop.
- **Standing.** Theorem (any type), on the root (6 of 7 laws; two of them reach it through lumping). It is the law under thesis T7, whose clause that actual metrics are so formed is conjectured.
- **Known form.** The record names lock-in under increasing returns (Arthur 1989) and regulatory capture (Stigler 1971) as the closest prior results, and cites the corrupted reward channel (Everitt and others 2017). [the dossier's assessment] Goodhart's and Campbell's laws describe it informally (Manheim and Garrabrant 2018; Campbell 1979).
- **What the join adds.** The meta-attractor's formal core: a loop whose rule and whose reading of the rule are both formed from the share it selects, with nothing in it reading what lies beyond the share ([02 §9](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)). A reading of the loss is a sufficient way out; row 3's reversal under a threshold reading is another.

### Row 5. Oversight that comes too late

- **Case of.** Let the rule be the pooled reading of readers drawn from the population it selects, counted as the population counts them; then the rule reads the share exactly where the others' reading is not above the carriers' (`ClosedCapture.Drawn.pool_reads_iff`). ### Row 6. Concentration of power: selection for control over the rules
