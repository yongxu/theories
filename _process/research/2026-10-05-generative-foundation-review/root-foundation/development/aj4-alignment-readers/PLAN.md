## 1. The objective, represented

The objective is how the alignment community would take the work, if the work were done well. Read by the work's own law, that means **alignment researchers come to couple into the work**:
- they can read its account in an hour;
- they can check each claim against its proof or its test;
- they can rerun the tests;
- what they find can change the account.

It does not mean approval. A selection reaches only what couples into its selector, and the work's selectors (the Lean checker, the completion checks, AI reviews) take in no alignment researcher ([08 §12](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#the-work-by-its-own-laws)). Approval read as a score would be a share the work selects itself by, which is the capture it describes ([capture through the rule](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/capture-through-the-rule.md)).

MA3 gives the design rule. What comes back to the work alone, its credit, selects for shaping how it is received. What is shared selects for nothing of the kind: proofs anyone can check, tests anyone can rerun, credit given to the closest work. So the account is built on the shared side throughout.

## 3. What the account must do

The four AI reviews of 2 October 2026 faulted the meta-attractor's conference paper on three counts, and the account answers each:
- **The alignment claim is in the model.** MA3 states the conditions under which selection favours control over its own rules. The readings for alignment are marked as readings and say what each rests on.
- **What is elementary is said first.** The optimum law is Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's Theorem 1 in substance. Capture is Goodhart's and Campbell's observation given an exact form. MA3's comparison of offspring numbers is elementary. What the work adds is said apart: the conditions, their joins and their witnesses.
- **Measurement is reported as it fell.** Every test is reported, with its failures: IN1 kept no dependent its agents were not told of, two of CG2's predicted keeps failed, and SE1's forecast does not beat relative reachability or attainable utility. Predictions are frozen before any new run.

Two further rules come from how public text has failed a cold read before:
- **Plain words.** Field-standard terms are used, and no coined word, metaphor or allusion appears in the text. Each term of the work is replaced, or explained once where the page needs it.
- **No names in the argument.** Persons and organizations appear only in the section that gives credit. Citations taken from the AI's knowledge are marked so until they are checked against the sources.

## 4. Deliverables

1. **The page.** One page, readable in ten minutes: the claim, the results with their conditions and standing, the readings for alignment, what is not claimed, the tests, how to check, the closest work. Its draft is in this folder.
2. **The dossier.** A dossier for alignment researchers in the review packet's format, readable in half a day. Each item quotes its record exactly, gives the reading for alignment, its known weaknesses and neutral questions, and ends in the response form. Adding it to the packet changes the packet's scope, so it is Ren's choice.
3. **A citation check.** Each work cited is read against its source before anything is sent.

## 6. How it would show that the work is taken well

These are checks, not targets:
- a reader outside the work finds an error, a missing credit or an unstated premise, and the account changes;
- a reader reruns a test;
- a frozen prediction fails, and the thesis moves;
- a reader uses one of the conditions, a return kept or shared or a loss read in time, in their own design or evaluation.

Praise, counts and attention are not checks. Each response is answered on what it says, in the place it concerns ([08 §1](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#1-beginning-within-the-one-unfolding)).

## 7. What would change this plan

- **MA3 or MA2 does not land as fixed.** The page states the premise it then needs.
- **A reader shows that a central result is a known theorem with nothing added.** The claim of addition is withdrawn, and the credit is given.
- **The tests on trained systems fail.** The failures lead the page.
