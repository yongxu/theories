## {N}. Not going out of a region, on the root (BD2)

Board item BD2, the formal gap BD's reading of boundaries left ([note §6](development/bd-boundaries/NOTE.md#6-not-going-out-on-the-root-board-item-bd2)). The World's cut, a region no formed relation leaves, was defined apart from the grammar's closed region, and the reading's account of a line crossing a region rested on a hand argument. Both are now in the core: the grammar's not going out (`«不出»`), its laws against the closed region and on a line, the World's cut as its case by definition, and 04 §1's law of crossing read on 07 §4.1's boundaries. An independent AI reader reviewed the kit and re-checked it ([the review](development/bd-boundaries/REVIEW.md)).
