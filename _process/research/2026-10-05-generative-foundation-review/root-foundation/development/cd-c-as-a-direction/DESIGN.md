# CD: C as a direction, its order read from the difference between options

Board item CD. AI-authored, 3 November 2026. Ren's words on ranking reached this study through the coordinator. They are kept verbatim, with a reading of them, in [the fronts' owner's source record](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-03-ranking-a-cognitive-construct.md). They are provenance, not warrant.

The study asks three things:
- whether C can be stated as a direction toward widening what can form, with its comparison derived as the order that direction gives on the difference between two options;
- what "better" adds beyond naming that difference;
- whether any name for ranking, order or betterness, in the Lean, the records or 07, states a wrong meaning.

It also asks where 07, the fronts and the glossary state a comparison with no relational basis.

The first draft answered "yes, exactly" and proposed "widening what can form is the direction to take". Two independent readers refuted it (§5): a check against the Lean, and a cold reader. This note gives the corrected answer.

## 1. The finding

**C already compares by difference alone.** Given a frame and a reading of `living`, `Extension F living s t` says that `t` keeps every claim `s` realizes and realizes a living claim `s` does not. C is `∀ s t, Extension F living s t → Better s t`, with `Better` a free relation (`Becoming.Extension`, `Becoming.Comparison`). So the least relation meeting C is `Extension` on that frame and reading. Nothing about either course enters but what one keeps of the other and what it adds: no level, total or place on a scale.

**"Better" adds exactly one thing: that the side of that difference where widening lies is better.** The module says so: `Comparison` unfolds to `Extension`, "so the normative content of C lies in calling widening better". Widening itself needs no premise. That the wider is better is the whole of C, a premise not asserted, and its warrant is open. "Better" adds nothing else:
- *No measure of the difference.* A course that widens another by one formation and one that widens it by many are both just better than it.
- *No top.* A natural-number score meeting C rises at every link of an endless chain of extensions and outgrows every configuration (`Comparison.climbs`, `Comparison.outgrows`). The force is the unit step: a bounded score can meet C without this.
- *No verdict on a mixed pair.* On a finite listing, positive weightings decide a mixed pair both ways (`Weighting.mixed_either_way`). The orders that count everything agree on exactly widening (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_of_class`). Their monotonicity is C's premise relocated, so this decomposes C and does not derive it (07 §4.4).
- *No one frame.* Which frame and which reading of `living` C is read on is a reading, not C. On a branching witness, no strict partial order meets C both on realized courses and on reachable ranges (`KeptAlternative.no_comparison_both`). The whole-run and stepwise readings order some pairs differently (07 §5).

**So the ethics needs no ranking beyond the differences and that one premise.** A ranking that decides more, such as weights, a grade or a lexical priority on mixed pairs, is a further premise (07 §4.4, §5). No frame fixes an order (`ReadsNoOrder.not_entails`, immediate since "better" is free). An order enters as a reading or a premise. Where one forms, it forms within the unfolding as some position's (07 §1), and acted on, it selects (03).

**C cannot be restated as "the direction" without loss.** The draft's "widening what can form is the direction to take" loses three things.
- *It hides C's content.* It moves the one thing "better" adds into "to take", where the Lean has no counterpart. `Better` is a comparison of outcomes, and taking a move needs L, the lifting of the comparison from outcomes to moves, and J1–J4 (07 §5, §7). AGENTS.md forbids hiding weight in a definition.
- *It takes a word that already has four senses.* "Direction" already names:
  - the conditional direction of 07 §7 (the condition, C, J1–J4 and E), with the Lean's `Direction.*`;
  - 生生 as the direction in Thesis T8, which is *compared by* widening and can exceed what widening counts (07 §2);
  - the open second-order direction of 生生, which widening does not give;
  - the stronger direction hypothesis that 08 dropped.
  Calling first-order widening "the direction" blurs it with 生生, and direction with per-step growth, which AGENTS.md keeps apart.
- *It clashes with the fronts.* The one page says that "a direction follows only conditionally" (07 §6).

**What may change in C's statement.** Only its form. "Widening what can form is better" is accurate, but read alone it sounds like "more is better", with a scale and a best. Both readers found the pairwise form the most exact: *of two courses, one that widens the other is better than it*. The fronts' owner's sentence for the freeze already says what "better" adds: "One course widens another where …, which needs no premise; that the wider is better does." The pairwise statement is proposed for the next edition, through the generativity chain's maintenance record (§4). C's role sentence ("gives order", "in general alone", the self-concern exception) is exact under the glossary's one sense of ranking and stays.

## 2. The Lean, point by point

Read at `ec825595f`. No new Lean is used.

1. `Extension` is irreflexive and transitive (`Extension.irrefl`, `Extension.trans`). It is a strict partial order, and it relates two options only where one keeps all the other has.
2. On a web's level paths, with every claim counted as living, an extension is exactly a widening (`Web.paths_extension_iff_widens`, `Web.paths_comparison_widens`). On reachable ranges it is widening what can be reached, both ways and with no premise (`Reaching.widens_iff_extension`, `Reaching.comparison_widens`). The lagged order on reachable ranges meets C too, by its first disjunct (`Reaching.comparison_widensOrLater`). Its verdict where same-offset widening is silent is a premise beyond C (07 §2).
3. Where a comparison meeting C is irreflexive and read off a description of configurations, the description must tell every extension from its base (`Comparison.separates`).
4. On a finite listing with two-valued admission, take any class of orders that contains every positive weighting's order and holds only orders increasing in admission. One course widens another exactly when every order of the class ranks it higher (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_of_class`). On a web this needs both paths' admissions listed, which covers only courses whose admissions end (07 §4.4).
5. An order is increasing in its own continuation when it ranks above what it widens every widening that also adds to the items its own continuation marks (`Weighting.IncreasingInOwn`). All such orders agree in ranking one course above another exactly where the first widens the other and adds to that continuation (`Weighting.widensOwn_iff_every_increasingInOwn`). On the World model every such order ranks one case this way, with no C (`Flow.coupled_ending_ranked_below`). Where the continuation condition holds de se, each standpoint's own continuation marks every item, and the self-concerned orders are exactly the orders increasing in admission (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_unrestricted`). That is C's premise, placed more narrowly, not warranted. The join is near-definitional. It reaches C one way only, between listed level paths whose admissions end (`Web.extension_ranked_of_increasingInOwn`), and it derives neither premise (07 §4.4).

## 3. Names

The test is AGENTS.md's: rename only a name that states a wrong meaning. The meaning is the glossary's one sense, landed by the fronts' owner (5e7b118a9): *ranking, order (which course is better)* is a comparison of options as better or worse, *order* is the ethics' role word for it, and no law gives one; widening is one-way and transitive, a strict partial order in the mathematical sense, and says which course admits more, not which is better. Three finders (the Lean, the records, the prose) judged 162 names, and five refuters re-read 61 of them against their statements ([names-test.json](names-test.json)). What follows is what was decided on that reading.

**Kept, with the reason.**
