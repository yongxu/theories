# CD: C as a direction, its order read from the difference between options

Board item CD. AI-authored, 3 November 2026. Ren's words on ranking reached this study through the coordinator and are kept verbatim in [the source record](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-03-ranking-a-readers-construct.md). They are provenance, not warrant: what follows argues from the Lean and the chapters, and §3 says where it disagrees with his words.

The study asks two questions. Can C be stated as a direction of change toward widening what can form, with its comparison derived as the order that direction gives on the difference between two options? And where do 07, the fronts and the glossary state a comparison with no relational basis?

## 1. What the Lean says

Every point below is read off a declaration in [`Becoming`](../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/) at `ec825595f`. No new Lean is used.

1. **C's order reads only the difference between two options.** `Extension F living s t` says that `t` keeps every claim `s` realizes and realizes a living claim `s` does not (`Becoming.Extension`). Nothing else about `s` or `t` enters: no level, total or place on a scale. Extension is irreflexive and transitive (`Extension.irrefl`, `Extension.trans`), so it is a strict partial order, and it relates two options only where one keeps all the other has.
2. **C asks of a comparison only that it contain that order.** `Comparison F living Better` is `∀ s t, Extension F living s t → Better s t`, and `Better` is a free relation. So the least relation meeting C is `Extension` itself. This follows at once from the definition, and no theorem states it. The module says the same: `Comparison` unfolds to `Extension`, "so the normative content of C lies in calling widening better" (`Becoming.Extension`'s header; the record [comparison by widening](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/comparison-by-widening.md)).
3. **On the account's carriers, that order is widening.** On a web's level paths, an extension with every claim counted as living is exactly a widening (`Web.paths_extension_iff_widens`, `Web.paths_comparison_widens`). On reachable ranges it is widening what can be reached, both ways and with no premise (`Reaching.widens_iff_extension`, `Reaching.comparison_widens`). The lagged order on reachable ranges meets C too, by its first disjunct (`Reaching.comparison_widensOrLater`). Its verdict where same-offset widening is silent is a premise beyond C (07 §2). So C settles only the order it contains, and any order beyond that is a further premise.
4. **Every order that counts everything shares exactly this order, and no more.** On a listing of items, take any class of orders that contains every positive weighting's order and holds only orders increasing in admission, additive or not. One course widens another exactly when every order of the class ranks it higher (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_of_class`). Positive weightings decide a mixed pair, where each course admits something the other does not, both ways (`Weighting.mixed_either_way`).
5. **No order is fixed by a frame alone.** A comparison read off a description of configurations, and meeting C, needs the description to tell every extension from its base (`Comparison.separates`). A statement whose truth does not change with the order fails, for every order, wherever it entails C on a frame with an extension (`ReadsNoOrder.not_entails`). The theorem is immediate, since "better" is free, and it shows only that a frame fixes no order.
6. **No option is best along an endless chain of extensions.** A natural-number score whose strict order meets C rises by at least one at every link, and outgrows every configuration (`Comparison.climbs`, `Comparison.outgrows`). The force is the unit step: a bounded score, with values `k / (k + 1)` for example, can meet C without this.
7. **Self-concern is the same order, asked only where the difference adds to one's own continuation.** An order is increasing in its own continuation when it ranks above what it widens every widening that also adds to the items its own continuation marks (`Weighting.IncreasingInOwn`). That is C's condition with one more requirement on the difference. Every order of this kind ranks one course above another exactly where the first widens the other and adds to that continuation (`Weighting.widensOwn_iff_every_increasingInOwn`). The World model ranks one case this way, with no C (`Flow.coupled_ending_ranked_below`). Where the marking marks every listed item, the two kinds of order coincide (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing`). Read from standpoints, with each marking reading a center's closure along a relation, they coincide exactly where the continuation condition holds de se (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_unrestricted`).

## 2. The answer

**Yes, exactly.** The direction toward widening what can form relates two options in one way. One lies in the direction from the other when it keeps everything the other lets form and lets more form. That is `Extension` (point 1). C asks of any comparison exactly that it contain this order (point 2). The orders that count everything agree on exactly this order and split on the rest (point 4). So C can be stated as the direction, and its comparison derived from it. "Better" then names nothing beyond the side of a difference the direction is on.

**The proposed statement.** *C: widening what can form is the direction to take. Of two courses, the one that widens the other is the better.* The fronts can carry the first sentence alone. The glossary and 07 §4 carry both.

**C's role.** C gives order, read from the difference between two options. It puts nothing on a scale and decides no mixed difference. "In general alone" stays, with its one exception, because self-concern orders one case with no C (point 7).

**What changes.**
- *C's wording.* "Widening what can form is better" becomes the statement above. The old wording is not false: "widening" already relates two courses. But it reads naturally as "more is better", which suggests a scale and a best. The account claims neither (points 4 and 6).
- *Ranking.* An order of options is always someone's: a reader's, a selector's, a valuer's or a premise's. None is in the dynamics (point 5). An order that decides a mixed difference, such as a total of weights, a grade or a lexical priority, is a premise beyond C. Such orders decide a mixed difference both ways (point 4). The fronts' "a ranking of courses needs C or a premise in its place" becomes "an order of courses needs C or a premise in its place".
- *The self-concern case.* It stays a second premise that orders one case with no C. It is now read as C's direction asked only where the difference adds to one's own continuation (point 7). So scope and order meet in one place. The continuation condition widens whose continuation the difference must add to, and where it holds de se, self-concern's order is C's.
- *One word across the fronts.* [The collective page](../../../../../../theories/becoming/COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#1-a-direction-as-a-criterion) already calls widening "a direction, as a criterion", and 07's Thesis T8 calls 生生 the direction. C's statement would now say the same. The glossary has no row for "direction", although AGENTS.md asks that direction be kept apart from continuing capacity, per-step growth and maximization. CD adds that row.

**What is lost, exactly.**
- *Nothing in the Lean.* Every declaration and statement stands, and `Better` stays the name of the free slot.
- *A plain value word in first place.* "Is better" says at once that C is evaluative, and the new wording says it through "to take". Without "to take", "the direction" could be read as where reality goes. 08 dropped that as a thesis, because it forbids nothing measurable. So the statement keeps "to take", and the second sentence keeps "better".
- *A risk of reading "to take" as a choice rule.* C compares courses. Choosing a move needs L, which lifts the comparison from outcomes to moves, and further named premises for mixed cases (07 §5). The statement says which way the difference points, not which move to make. 07's "C ranks courses; choices take more" becomes "C orders two courses by their difference; choices take more".
- *Brevity.* The first sentence is four words longer, and the full statement is two sentences.

**Alternatives the read should weigh.** (B) one sentence: "of two courses, one that widens what the other lets form is better". It is the most exact, but it has no direction in it. (C) The old statement with a new role clause: "widening what can form is better; it orders two options by their difference only". This changes the least, but it keeps the scale reading in first place. The proposal is preferred because it states the direction and derives the comparison, which is what the Lean does.

## 3. Where Ren's words go too far

- **"Ranking is somewhat illusion."** An order of options is a reader's construct, as he says: none is in the dynamics, and a comparison exists only in a reading that tells the options apart (point 5). But it is not an illusion. Read off a difference that is really there, it is accurate, and a selection that reads it acts on it. An illusion would be a reading that gets the difference wrong.
- **"Not take dualist comparisons."** Following a direction is itself a comparison of two options: of two moves, take the one whose difference points the direction's way. A theory that guides action cannot do without that. What it can do without is a ranking of everything: a scale, a best, a complete order. C was never one (points 4 and 6). The fault was in the wording.
- **"Understanding the difference between options."** This is what C reads, and only that (point 1). Where the difference is mixed, C says nothing, and choosing takes the situation read and further named premises (07 §5). That matches his "understanding the situation and knowing the options".

## 4. The sweep

[`sweep.py`](sweep.py) searched 07, the README's first screen, the one page and the overview with their Chinese editions, the glossary, [the one argument](../../../../../../theories/becoming/ONE-ARGUMENT.md) and the collective page. The words were: better, worse, best, worst, higher, lower and their superlatives, rank in every form, optimum and its kin, superior, inferior, winner, loser, dominate, maximize, minimize, above, below, prefer, and in Chinese 更好, 更差, 最好, 最优, 最佳, 排序, 排名, 排得, 排在, 更高, 更低, 高于, 低于, 优于, 劣于, 胜者, 赢家, 输家, 优越, 最大化, 支配 and 偏好. That gave 695 hits ([`hits.tsv`](hits.tsv)). Rules sorted 324 of them: textual places 67, amounts with their basis 37, orders a theorem or the sentence names 144, and a field's own frame 76. The other 371 were read in their sentences. The rules are crude, and a hit they sorted was not read. Every class a rule assigns is a kept one, so a rule's mistake can only hide a restatement: a hit near a word the rules take for a basis, such as "score", "an order" or "reward", that does not give that hit's basis.

The hits fall into six kinds.
- **Textual** ("the cases below", "the laws above"): kept.
- **Amounts with their basis** (a level above a need, a weight above zero, a lower member of a routing): kept.
- **A field's own frame** (a reward's optimum, an optimizer, a best response, Pareto, Pigou–Dalton, priority to the worse off, POWER, a test's best choice and its score): kept as the field's.
- **An order the sentence names** (a strict partial order containing widening, a weighting's order, a valuer's ranking, a lexical order, a self-concerned order, the routes at a revision, L, J): kept. Each says whose order it is. "Rank" there means what that order puts above what. That includes C's own verdict on two named courses ("C ranks it higher", "C leaves that pair unranked"): a comparison of two options with its premise named is relational.
- **C itself stated as "is better", or "ranking" standing for any order of courses**: restated in the next edition (§5).
- **A comparison with its basis left out**: one, "the community's best course, 50 tons a month", on the one page and in the overview with their Chinese editions. In the experiment it is the course that maximizes the community's total catch over the season and after it, the bed not read ([`exact.py`](../../../../../../experiments/direction-installation/exact.py)). It is restated with that basis.

07 keeps its comparisons on a relational basis almost everywhere. Its "rank" is nearly always an order the sentence names, and its theorems on weightings, lexical priority and revisions say whose order they are.
