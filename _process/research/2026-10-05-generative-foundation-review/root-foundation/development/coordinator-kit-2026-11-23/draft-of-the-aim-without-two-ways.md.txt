## What stands on the page now

The first paragraph under "Where we are heading" has five sentences. The first, fourth and fifth stay. The second and third are the ones in question:

> On it we want two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all: a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on. Placed means: where, and at which grain, a course is doing the one or the other, where it passes between them, and by what ways.

## What would stand in their place (row DJ's wording)

> On it we want, for any going-on, a selection among them, and at any grain: what its going on does to what it and others rest on, whether it keeps that up, uses it up, adds to it or changes it; where that makes a difference to it, and where it does not; and whether what it rests on still reaches it early enough to turn it. We do not ask the map to sort goings-on into two ways and set them against each other. What it should show is what decides whether a course uses up what it rests on until it can no longer be turned, or goes on with more able to go on after it; at which grain; and how early.

The whole paragraph would then read:

> One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. On it we want, for any going-on, a selection among them, and at any grain: what its going on does to what it and others rest on, whether it keeps that up, uses it up, adds to it or changes it; where that makes a difference to it, and where it does not; and whether what it rests on still reaches it early enough to turn it. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment. We want it joined and simple: its parts made to meet, and each thing said once.

## What row DJ changed in the coordinating session's first form, and why

- **A sentence that said more than is seen is gone.** The first form said that a course that uses up what it rests on and a course that goes on with more able to go on after it "are outcomes of one relation". That is not shown. Today's module has ending and holding from one rule and one kind of step; its going on is a configuration that holds, and no unit in it adds to what it rests on. No model found has a course after which more can go on. And "one relation" is a centre by another name, which he asked about on 22 November and did not affirm. The aim now says what the map should show, and asserts nothing about how it will come out.
- **"Adds to it" is named,** since that is the part no model has.
- **"On it we want, for" in place of "We want this said of",** where "this" had nothing clear to point at.
