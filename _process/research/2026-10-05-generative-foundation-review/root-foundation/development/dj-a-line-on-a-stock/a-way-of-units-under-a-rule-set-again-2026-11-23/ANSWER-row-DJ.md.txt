## 1. What breaks it that was not seen: S3′ cannot be the base

The core run asks an empty axiom report of every declaration. The left-to-right half of S3′, in either of its forms, cannot be proved so. From it one could decide, for any decidable property `P` of the naturals, whether `P` holds somewhere or nowhere, and no proof with an empty axiom report gives that (I state this last from what is known of Lean's logic; it was not checked here).

**The course, in `LineOnAStock`'s own terms (the form "by a kind").** Units are the naturals. Every unit needs 1 and takes 1. Unit `n` leaves `[n+1]`, except the first `n` at which `P` holds, which leaves `[n+1, n+1]`; that one unit is the growing kind (decidable, since "no `m < n` has `P`" is a bounded check). Giving is 0 at 0 and `min 2 (10 - z)` elsewhere; `hi = 10`; the start is `([0], 10)`; `y` is 1 everywhere. Every premise of the proposal's list holds and is provable with `P` left open: `grows` (1 < 2), `even`, `some`, `down`, the needs, `nothing`, `high`, `Keeps` at takes 1 and 2, `Short` and `Meets` at takes 1 and 2. The level stays 10 whether the way takes 1 or 2, so every need is met at every step and the net never ends, provably. S3′ left to right would then hand back a step `k` none of whose descendants is of the growing kind, that is, no first place of `P` at or after `k`. Checking `P` below `k` (finitely many) then decides whether `P` holds anywhere.

**Over a sequence of rules** it is the same course: two rules, one under which a unit leaves two and one under which it leaves one, the rule at step `j` favouring exactly at the first `j` where `P` holds. "A step from which no rule favours" is a claim about every later rule.

**What follows.**

- Stage 1's first item ("S3′ … over any sequence of rules", then "S3 as its corollary") and stage 1a as row FM reshaped it ("S3′ alone, in `LineOnAStock`'s terms") would stop at their first step. Neither the reader of 19 November nor any search could see this: the reader read that half classically and said so; a search does not see how a thing is proved.
- S3 cannot be got from a weakened S3′ either. What can be proved of S3′ left to right is "it is not the case that no such step exists". From that, a step at which a decidable test holds does not follow without a further principle the package also does not have.
- **S3 itself looks provable, directly.** Its right side is a test at one configuration: it holds, the rule in force does not favour, and the rule read off the present share does not favour (the reader's simpler form; all decidable once `favours` is). A measure exists, in this order: a bound on the needs of the way; the room the cap leaves above what the way takes; the level. At a step whose level is below the bound, the next way's needs are at most the level, and the first falls. Where every need is met and the rule favours, the take rises and the second falls. Where every need is met, the rule does not favour and the configuration does not hold, the level falls (`Short`, as in `reaches_from`). Where it holds but the rule read off the share favours, the next step has every need met (`Meets`) under a favouring rule, so the second falls within two steps. Otherwise the test holds. This is the reader's "one nested induction, carrying the reversed lock", with the case it left open written out. It is a sketch by one writer.

So the lock does one piece of work in S3 that the read of 19 November did not see. It is still not the joining of the old laws: those are cases at one rule, where the lock's premises are trivial.
- A statement by a kind that *can* be proved: add that no unit outside the growing kind leaves a unit of it (`line_ends`' own `closed`). Then "no descendant of the growing kind" is "no unit of it present", a test at one configuration. It would have `unending_iff_holds` as its case exactly and `way_ends` in its second conjunct, and it covers offspring that take more in total and are partly of the other kind (row FM's point 2).
