## What it advances (rule 6)

- **A claim put to a test that could fail:** that the statement below can be proved with an empty axiom report under the premises listed, no more.
- **A telling a person can follow:** two built courses that differ in one unit of one need, one going on and one ending.

It derives no response of a selection beyond the one the line on a stock already has: a unit whose need is unmet leaves nothing. It does not place the two ways: no unit in it adds to what it rests on, and the going on it finds is a configuration that holds.

## The account, before any build (row FM's words, taken)

The level and the units' needs decide that one of two things comes: a step at which the configuration holds and neither the rule in force nor the next rule favours, or a step at which no need is met. The lock's premises decide that the first of them lasts. The statement of the "or" names a rule that is set again; that it is set from a share by a monotone reading does no work there.

## The module

One leaf module, working name `Becoming.WayUnderARule`, importing `LineOnAStock` and `RuleCapture`. Nothing existing is changed but the root's import and bullet, the package README's row and the checker's list.

**The model.** `U R : Type`; `need use : U → Nat`; `kids : R → U → List U`; `giving y : Nat → Nat`; `hi : Nat`; `favours : R → Prop`; `next : List U → R → R`. A configuration is `((w, g), x)`: a way, the rule in force, a level.

- the way and rule after a step: `(LineOnAStock.after need (kids g) w x, next w g)`. The next rule is set from the way present before the step, as `Pace.turn` reads.
- the level after: `LineOnAStock.left giving use w x`.
- the step is `TurningInTime.pair` of the two; `course` is its `Law.orbit`; `Goes` is `LineOnAStock.Goes need (w, x)`; `net` is `Law.stepWhile Goes step`.
- **the test** at a configuration: `LineOnAStock.Holds need use y (w, x) ∧ ¬ favours g ∧ ¬ favours (next w g)`.

Write `w_k`, `g_k`, `x_k` for the course from `s`, `T_k := taken use w_k`.

- `regimeAlong : ∀ k, (∀ j, j ≤ k → Goes_j) → ¬ favours g_k → RegimeMargin.Short giving T_k (y T_k) ∧ RegimeMargin.Meets giving T_k (y T_k) hi`

## The statements

**A. The "or" (list 1).**
`(∃ k, (∀ j, j < k → Goes_j) ∧ Test_k) ∨ (∃ n, (∀ j, j < n → Goes_j) ∧ ¬ Goes_n)`.

**B. The first outcome lasts (even, some, down, high, keepsAlong, regimeAlong, list 2).**
`(∃ k, (∀ j, j < k → Goes_j) ∧ Test_k) → «不穷» net «一» s`.
Not asked: `grows`, `needs`, `nothing`, decidability.

**C. The equivalences (lists 1 and 2), corollaries of A and B.**
`«不穷» net «一» s ↔ ∃ k, (∀ j, j < k → Goes_j) ∧ Test_k`, and `¬ «不穷» net «一» s ↔ ∃ n, (∀ j, j < n → Goes_j) ∧ ¬ Goes_n`.

**D. From a locked start (list 3; no stock premise).**
`∀ n, (∀ j, j < n → ∀ u, u ∈ w_j → need u ≤ x_j) → ∀ j, j ≤ n + 1 → favours g_j`; and its corollary: `Test_k → ∃ j, j + 2 ≤ k ∧ ∃ u, u ∈ w_j ∧ x_j < need u` (a need went unmet at least two steps before; the step found by a bounded search, `first_of_decided`).

**E. The two old laws as cases (last; dropped first if the cap is near, and then told as argued on paper).**
A lemma that at one rule (`R := PUnit`) this course is `LineOnAStock.course` with the rule carried. Then, in this module, two theorems whose statements are `LineOnAStock.unending_iff_holds`'s and `LineOnAStock.way_ends`'s, binder for binder, proved from C (with `favours := fun _ => False`) and from A (with `favours := fun _ => True`: the first outcome asks a rule that does not favour, so the second stands, and that is `way_ends`' first conjunct). They do not call the old theorems. The old theorems are not touched; making them calls is stage 2 and waits on his word.

## The measure for A

Three numbers, in this order: a bound `m` on the needs of the way; the room `c` the cap leaves above what the way takes (`hi + 1 ≤ T_k + c`); a bound `f` on the level. The claim at `m`: for all `c`, `f` and every step `k` with some need met at every step before it, the way at `k` needing at most `m`, the room `c` and the level at most `f`, one of the two outcomes holds. First a lemma: with some need met at every step before `k`, `x_k ≤ hi` (`keepsAlong`, as `within_along`).

At a step `k`, in this order:
1. No need met (decided, as `anyMet_iff`): the second outcome at `k`.
2. `x_k < m`: every unit of the next way is a kid of a unit the level met, so it needs at most `x_k` (`below_after` at `kids g_k`, with `down`). The claim at `x_k < m`, with `c` and `f` set again from `hi`.
3. Else every need is met and the way is not empty. If `x_k < T_k`: nothing is left (`step_zero`), and by `needs` no need is met at `k + 1`: the second outcome there.
4. Else `T_k ≤ x_k ≤ hi`, so `c` is not nothing. If `g_k` favours: the next way is all the kids (`after_of_met`), and it takes at least one more (`allKids_rises` at `kids g_k`). The claim at `k + 1` with `c - 1`, `f` set again, `m` kept (`below_keeps`).
5. `g_k` does not favour, and `x_k < T_k + y T_k`: the step leaves nothing or less (`step_lowers`, with `Short` from `regimeAlong`). Nothing: the second outcome at `k + 1`. Less: the claim at `k + 1` with the same `c` (`taken_allKids` at `kids g_k`) and `f - 1`.
6. `g_k` does not favour and the configuration holds. If `next w_k g_k` does not favour: the first outcome at `k`.
7. Else the rule at `k + 1` favours. The way at `k + 1` is not empty (`some`), takes `T_k`, the level has not fallen (`Meets`), every need is met there (`down`), so the way at `k + 2` takes at least `T_k + 1`. The claim at `k + 2` with `c - 1`, `f` set again, `m` kept.

In the package's style this is three nested lemmas, each by structural recursion with the outer claim handed in as a premise (as `reaches_from` takes `ih`): on `f`, on `c`, and `strong_below` on `m`. No recursion by a well-founded order.

Nothing in A uses the share, the reading or an order on rules.

## The route for B and D

**B.** From the test at `k`, by induction on `i`, at step `k + i`: the rule in force and the next rule do not favour; the way is not empty; every need is at most `x_k`; the way takes `T_k`; the level is at least `x_k` and at most `hi`. The level: one step under a draw `T` from a level `z` with `T + y T ≤ z ≤ hi` leaves at least `z` (`Meets`) and at most `hi` (`Keeps`). The rule: the reversed lock, `RuleCapture.Locked (RuleCapture.reverse M) (fun g => ¬ favours g) (fun w g => h (share w) g)`, holds at `k + 1` (from the test at `k`, `lowers` and `monotone`) and is kept by **`RuleCapture.turn_locked`** at the reversed orders (its premises: `lowers`; `ReadsShare.reverse monotone` pulled back along `share`; `upper` read backward). The carrying of the rule must be a call of `turn_locked`.

**D.** `RuleCapture.turn_locked` on ways of units, with `f w g := LineOnAStock.allKids (kids g) w` and the order on ways pulled back along `share`; at a step where every need is met the model's step is that turn (`after_of_met`). It must be a call of `turn_locked`.
