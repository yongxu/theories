# Read of the direction page's correction of 18 November against the Lean

## MUST

### M1. Line 9: "They are named and sorted on October's web" (again in line 73, "on the web, where the two ways are named", and in the head note, "found the two ways named on October's web")

**MORE: a reading told as a statement.** "They" is the two ways of line 7: "a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on". What the web names is two classes of a formation:

- `Web.ConsumingIn f := ∃ t c, W.draws t f c ∧ W.Consumed c`, with `Consumed c := ∃ g T, W.requires T g c ∧ ∀ t, T < t → W.level t c < W.need T g c` (`B/Dependence.lean:164-176`). No selecting, no turning, and no amount: `draws` is a proposition, and the module's opening says "Drawing records that a formation draws on a condition, not how much: the levels move by the process alone" (:21-22). The docstring of `Consumed` says the dependence "need not have been met at that step; `Lost` requires that". `Untaken.consuming_keeps` (`B/Retaining.lean:538`) is a built formation that is consuming and whose part takes nothing.
- `Web.RenewingIn f := ∀ t c, W.Participates t f c → W.Renewed c` (:173): every dependence on what it requires or draws on is kept. Nothing in it says the formation remakes anything.

So the classes are the nearest named things, not the two ways. The definitions the page gives next are as the Lean, so a careful reader is not misled about the Lean; what is more than the Lean is the "They".

**Replacement.** Line 9: "Two classes that come nearest them, renewing and consuming, are named and sorted on October's web, a model of formations (goings-on) that require and draw on shared conditions, each condition with a level." and, in place of "Each is said of a whole course.": "Each is said of a whole course. Neither has selecting in it, an amount, or whether the course can still be turned: the web records that a formation draws on a condition, not how much, and consuming does not ask that the dependence was ever met." Line 73: "And on the web, where those two classes are named, one statement …". Head note: "found two classes, renewing and consuming, named on October's web, which this page had not told."

### M2. Line 73, "Second": "where each one's giving rises with what is left of each and every start lies within a band, each ends exactly where it starts below a threshold set by where the others start"

**MORE: a premise told in part.** `Coupled.Indexed.ends_iff_below` (`B/RegimeMargin.lean:2687`) takes `rises : ∀ i, Rises Drawn.whole (G i)`, `band : Band G H hi`, a list covering the index, `high : a ≤ hi` and `highs : ∀ j, y j ≤ hi`. `Band` (:2425) is a premise on the step, not on the start: "The coupled step of a web keeps every state with every level within `hi` within `hi`". The sentence tells `high` and `highs` ("every start lies within a band") and not `band`. The rest of the sentence is as the Lean: whole numbers, finitely many, constant draws `H`, each giving a function of what is left of all (`step`, :2113), `Rises` (:2120) as rising with what is left of each, the threshold a function of the others' starts `y`.

Said with its weight: on paper the plain sentence probably survives without the premise (ending passes downward by `rises` alone, so a threshold exists if "no level" is allowed as one), but the Lean does not state that, and its threshold is a number up to `hi + 1` checked at one step, which needs the band kept.

**Replacement.** "… where each one's giving rises with what is left of each, the step keeps every state within a band (every level up to one bound) within it, and every start lies within that band, each ends exactly where it starts below a threshold set by where the others start."
