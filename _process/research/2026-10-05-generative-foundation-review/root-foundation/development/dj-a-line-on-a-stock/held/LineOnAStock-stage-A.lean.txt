/-!
# A line on a stock: units that leave offspring on a renewed level

AI-written, 16 November 2026 (scratch, board row DJ, stage A).

## The model

A type `U` of units, each with a need and a use (`need use : U → Nat`) and the units it leaves
(`kids : U → List U`). A way is a list of units; a level is a number. After a step at level `x`
each unit of the way in turn leaves its kids where `x` meets its need and nothing where it does not
(`after`); the level becomes `RegimeMargin.step giving (taken use w) x`, where `taken` is the total of
the uses of every unit present (`left`). The net has a next while some unit's need is met (`Goes`,
`net`, `Law.stepWhile` of `TurningInTime.pair`); `course` is the pair's orbit. `WayOfUnits` is the
case `kids u = [u]` on `U = Nat × Nat`. The way reads the level only through which needs it meets
(`after_alike`); the level reads the way only through what it takes (`left_of_taken`).

A line is a predicate `S` that offspring inherit exactly (`same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)`);
what it takes of a way is `lineTake` (the total of `use` over the units with `S`), and `lineCount`
counts its units.

## What is given and what is concluded

Given: `need`, `use`, `kids`, `giving`, a start, and for each law the premises its docstring names.

* **Who is in the next way** (`mem_after_iff`), **alike levels give the same next way**
  (`after_alike`), and **ways side by side step side by side** (`after_append`, `taken_append`).
* **Alike levels, one count, on any two stocks** (`alike_one_count`): under two givings, two uses and
  two start levels, while every unit of each generation has its need met at that step of both
  courses, both ways are the free generations of the start (`generation`). The way of any course is
  the family of a `SelectionTerm.Generations` (`generations`, `family_eq`), whose sum is what the way
  takes (`sum_eq`) and whose number born is the next way's length (`born_eq_length`, by
  `Generations.born_eq`).
* **The case of `WayOfUnits`** (`after_unit`, `taken_unit`, `goes_unit`, `course_unit`,
  `unending_unit`).
* **Rise** (`rise`): where every unit of a line is met and each unit's kids of the line take more than
  it, what the line takes rises by at least its number of units.
* **Fall** (`fall`): a line of one need `m`, whose kids of the line take no less than the unit, taking
  more than `giving z` at every `z`, with `giving 0 < m`, is gone from some step on.
* **A line that grows ends** (`line_ends`): with nothing given from nothing, the step under no draw
  keeping levels up to `hi` within `hi`, a start at most `hi`, and a line whose kids take more, whose
  needs are positive and whose kids need no more than their parent, the line is gone from some step
  on; for the line of every unit, some step has no need met and the net ends (`way_ends`). Without
  the premise on the kids' needs, from a way that is not empty with every need met, some need is
  unmet at a step `n` with `0 < n ≤ hi + 2` (`need_unmet`).
* **On a course that goes on, what the way takes is less than the level** (`taken_below`,
  `unending_taken_below`), with nothing given from nothing and every need positive.
* **Kept countermodels and worked courses**, each closed: kids that need more than their parent and
  a growing way that never ends (`KidsNeedMore`); equal in place of more (`EqualInPlaceOfMore`,
  through `WayOfUnits.With.goes_on`, and one unit that leaves itself); a share that rises while the
  number of drawers falls (`ShareRises`); a line that doubles and ends, also as a case of `way_ends`
  (`Doubling`); a higher start that ends while the lower one never does, the ways equal at steps 0
  to 2 (`HigherStartEnds`); a met unit with no kids, whose net ends with every need met
  (`NoKids`).

## What is not derived

The kinds, offspring, needs and uses are given with the carrier; nothing here makes a new kind or
changes `kids`. No exact condition for a line that leaves offspring never ending is stated;
`HigherStartEnds` shows that, with one way, whether the line ends is not monotone in the start
level, so no condition of the form "the start is at least some level" is exact for every way. Losses, units that take
nothing, the regime as a `Flow`, and the lock are not here. Nothing here is about a trained system.

Every declaration has an empty axiom report.
-/

variable {U : Type}

/-! ## 1. The model -/

/-- The way after a step at level `x`: each unit in turn leaves its kids where `x` meets its need,
and nothing where it does not. -/
def after (need : U → Nat) (kids : U → List U) : List U → Nat → List U
  | [], _ => []
  | u :: w, x => cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) [] ++ after need kids w x

/-- What a way takes: the total of its units' uses (`Becoming.total`, the total of `Prelude`). -/
def taken (use : U → Nat) (w : List U) : Nat := total w use

/-- The level after a step: `RegimeMargin.step` under what every unit present at the step takes. -/
def left (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : U → Nat) (w : List U) (x : Nat) : Nat :=
  RegimeMargin.step giving (taken use w) x

/-- Some unit of the way has its need met at the level. -/
def Goes (need : U → Nat) (s : List U × Nat) : Prop := ∃ u, u ∈ s.1 ∧ need u ≤ s.2

/-- The pair's step: the way after a step and the level after it (`TurningInTime.pair`). -/
abbrev step (need : U → Nat) (kids : U → List U) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : U → Nat) :
    List U × Nat → List U × Nat :=
  TurningInTime.pair (after need kids) (left giving use)

/-- The net: the pair's step while some unit's need is met, and nothing where none is. -/
def net (need : U → Nat) (kids : U → List U) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : U → Nat) :
    «际» (List U × Nat) :=
  Law.stepWhile (Goes need) (step need kids giving use)

/-- The course: the pair's orbit from `s`. -/
abbrev course (need : U → Nat) (kids : U → List U) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : U → Nat)
    (s : List U × Nat) : Nat → List U × Nat :=
  Law.orbit (step need kids giving use) s

/-- The kids of every unit of a way, in order: the way after a step at which every need is met. -/
def allKids (kids : U → List U) : List U → List U
  | [] => []
  | u :: w => kids u ++ allKids kids w

/-- The `n`-th generation of a way with no level in it: the kids of the kids … of its units. -/
def generation (kids : U → List U) : Nat → List U → List U
  | 0, w => w
  | n + 1, w => allKids kids (generation kids n w)

/-- What a line `S` takes of a way: the total of `use` over the units of the way with `S`. -/
def lineTake (S : U → Prop) [DecidablePred S] (use : U → Nat) (w : List U) : Nat :=
  total w fun u => if S u then use u else 0

/-- The number of units of a way with `S`. -/
def lineCount (S : U → Prop) [DecidablePred S] (w : List U) : Nat :=
  total w fun u => if S u then 1 else 0

/-! ## 2. On lists -/

theorem mem_append_left {a : U} {t : List U} : ∀ {s : List U}, a ∈ s → a ∈ s ++ t
  | _, .head s => .head (s ++ t)
  | _, .tail b m => .tail b (mem_append_left m)

theorem mem_append_right {a : U} {t : List U} (m : a ∈ t) : ∀ s : List U, a ∈ s ++ t
  | [] => m
  | b :: s => .tail b (mem_append_right m s)

theorem mem_append_cases {a : U} {t : List U} : ∀ {s : List U}, a ∈ s ++ t → a ∈ s ∨ a ∈ t
  | [], m => .inr m
  | b :: s, m => by
    have m' : a ∈ b :: (s ++ t) := m
    cases m' with
    | head => exact .inl (.head s)
    | tail _ m'' => exact (mem_append_cases m'').elim (fun h => .inl (.tail b h)) .inr

theorem append_assoc' : ∀ (a b c : List U), a ++ b ++ c = a ++ (b ++ c)
  | [], _, _ => rfl
  | x :: a, b, c => congrArg (x :: ·) (append_assoc' a b c)

theorem mem_cond {a : U} {l : List U} : ∀ {b : Bool}, a ∈ cond b l [] → b = true ∧ a ∈ l
  | true, m => ⟨rfl, m⟩
  | false, m => nomatch m

theorem mem_cond_of {a : U} {l : List U} : ∀ {b : Bool}, b = true → a ∈ l → a ∈ cond b l []
  | true, _, m => m

/-- Where `x` and `x'` meet the need `n` alike, the two tests of it are the same. -/
theorem decide_le_congr {n x x' : Nat} (same : n ≤ x ↔ n ≤ x') :
    decide (n ≤ x) = decide (n ≤ x') :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge x n).elim
    (fun lt => (decide_eq_false (Nat.not_le_of_gt lt)).trans
      (decide_eq_false fun le => Nat.not_le_of_gt lt (same.mpr le)).symm)
    (fun le => (decide_eq_true le).trans (decide_eq_true (same.mp le)).symm)

/-- A member's quantity is at most the total. -/
theorem le_total_of_mem {q : U → Nat} {a : U} : ∀ {w : List U}, a ∈ w → q a ≤ total w q
  | _, .head w => Nat.le_add_right (q a) (total w q)
  | _, .tail b m => Nat.le_trans (le_total_of_mem m) (Nat.le_add_left _ (q b))

theorem ite_le {S : U → Prop} [DecidablePred S] (u : U) (a : Nat) :
    (if S u then a else 0) ≤ a :=
  (Decidable.em (S u)).elim
    (fun h => Nat.le_of_eq (if_pos h))
    (fun h => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (if_neg h)) (Nat.zero_le a))

/-- What a line takes is at most what the way takes. -/
theorem lineTake_le_taken {S : U → Prop} [DecidablePred S] {use : U → Nat} (w : List U) :
    lineTake S use w ≤ taken use w :=
  total_le w fun u _ => ite_le u (use u)

theorem lineTake_append {S : U → Prop} [DecidablePred S] {use : U → Nat} (a b : List U) :
    lineTake S use (a ++ b) = lineTake S use a + lineTake S use b :=
  total_append _ b a

/-- A unit of the line in the way makes the line's count positive. -/
theorem lineCount_pos {S : U → Prop} [DecidablePred S] {u : U} (su : S u) {w : List U}
    (mu : u ∈ w) : 0 < lineCount S w :=
  have one : (if S u then 1 else 0) = 1 := if_pos su
  Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le Nat.zero_lt_one (Nat.le_of_eq one.symm))
    (le_total_of_mem (q := fun v => if S v then 1 else 0) mu)

/-- Every unit of a way has the line `S` or not; decided along the way. -/
theorem line_or_none {S : U → Prop} [DecidablePred S] :
    ∀ w : List U, (∀ u, u ∈ w → ¬ S u) ∨ ∃ u, u ∈ w ∧ S u
  | [] => .inl fun _ m => nomatch m
  | v :: w => (Decidable.em (S v)).elim (fun h => .inr ⟨v, .head w, h⟩) fun h =>
      (line_or_none w).elim
        (fun nm => .inl fun u m => by
          cases m with
          | head => exact h
          | tail _ m' => exact nm u m')
        (fun ⟨u, m, su⟩ => .inr ⟨u, .tail v m, su⟩)

/-- At a level, either every unit of a way has its need met, or some unit's need is above it. -/
theorem met_or_unmet (need : U → Nat) (z : Nat) :
    ∀ w : List U, (∀ u, u ∈ w → need u ≤ z) ∨ ∃ u, u ∈ w ∧ z < need u
  | [] => .inl fun _ m => nomatch m
  | v :: w => (Nat.lt_or_ge z (need v)).elim (fun lt => .inr ⟨v, .head w, lt⟩) fun le =>
      (met_or_unmet need z w).elim
        (fun all => .inl fun u m => by
          cases m with
          | head => exact le
          | tail _ m' => exact all u m')
        (fun ⟨u, m, lt⟩ => .inr ⟨u, .tail v m, lt⟩)

/-- Whether some unit of a way has its need met at `x`, computed along the way. -/
def anyMet (need : U → Nat) (x : Nat) : List U → Bool
  | [] => false
  | u :: w => decide (need u ≤ x) || anyMet need x w

theorem anyMet_iff (need : U → Nat) (x : Nat) :
    ∀ w : List U, anyMet need x w = true ↔ ∃ u, u ∈ w ∧ need u ≤ x
  | [] => ⟨fun h => absurd h Bool.false_ne_true, fun ⟨_, m, _⟩ => nomatch m⟩
  | v :: w => by
    show (decide (need v ≤ x) || anyMet need x w) = true ↔ _
    cases hv : decide (need v ≤ x) with
    | true => exact ⟨fun _ => ⟨v, .head w, of_decide_eq_true hv⟩, fun _ => rfl⟩
    | false =>
      exact (anyMet_iff need x w).trans
        ⟨fun ⟨u, m, le⟩ => ⟨u, .tail v m, le⟩,
         fun ⟨u, m, le⟩ => by
          cases m with
          | head => exact absurd (hv.symm.trans (decide_eq_true le)) Bool.false_ne_true
          | tail _ m' => exact ⟨u, m', le⟩⟩

/-- Whether some unit's need is met is decided, by `anyMet`. -/
instance goesDecidable (need : U → Nat) (s : List U × Nat) : Decidable (Goes need s) :=
  decidable_of_iff (anyMet need s.2 s.1 = true) (anyMet_iff need s.2 s.1)

/-- Strong induction on a number, by induction on a bound. -/
theorem strong_below {P : Nat → Prop} (step : ∀ m, (∀ m', m' < m → P m') → P m) :
    ∀ M m, m < M → P m
  | 0, m, lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero m)
  | M + 1, m, lt => step m fun m' lt' =>
      strong_below step M m' (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le lt' (Nat.le_of_lt_succ lt))

/-- A function of two arguments takes equal arguments to equal values. -/
theorem congr_two {α β γ : Type} (f : α → β → γ) {a a' : α} {b b' : β} (ea : a = a') (eb : b = b') :
    f a b = f a' b' :=
  (congrArg (f a) eb).trans (congrArg (fun c => f c b') ea)

/-- Two laws that agree at every configuration have the same orbit. -/
theorem orbit_congr {S : Type} {f g : S → S} (e : ∀ s, f s = g s) (s : S) :
    ∀ n, Law.orbit f s n = Law.orbit g s n
  | 0 => rfl
  | n + 1 => (congrArg f (orbit_congr e s n)).trans (e _)

/-! ## 3. Who is in the next way; alike levels; lines reach each other through the level -/

section Step

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U}

/-- **Who is in the next way.** A unit is in the way after a step at `x` exactly where it is a kid of
a unit of the way whose need `x` meets. For any `need`, `kids`, way and level. -/
theorem mem_after_iff {x : Nat} {v : U} :
    ∀ {w : List U}, v ∈ after need kids w x ↔ ∃ u, u ∈ w ∧ need u ≤ x ∧ v ∈ kids u
  | [] => ⟨fun m => (nomatch m), fun ⟨_, m, _⟩ => (nomatch m)⟩
  | u :: w =>
    ⟨fun m => (mem_append_cases m).elim
        (fun mc => match mem_cond mc with
          | ⟨e, mk⟩ => ⟨u, .head w, of_decide_eq_true e, mk⟩)
        (fun mr => match (mem_after_iff (w := w)).mp mr with
          | ⟨u', mu', le, mk⟩ => ⟨u', .tail u mu', le, mk⟩),
     fun ⟨u', mu', le, mk⟩ => by
      cases mu' with
      | head => exact mem_append_left (mem_cond_of (decide_eq_true le) mk)
      | tail _ mu'' => exact mem_append_right ((mem_after_iff (w := w)).mpr ⟨u', mu'', le, mk⟩) _⟩

/-- **Alike.** Where `x` and `x'` meet the same needs in `w`, the way after a step at `x` is the way
after a step at `x'`. For any `need` and `kids`. -/
theorem after_alike {x x' : Nat} :
    ∀ {w : List U}, (∀ u, u ∈ w → (need u ≤ x ↔ need u ≤ x')) →
      after need kids w x = after need kids w x'
  | [], _ => rfl
  | u :: w, same => by
    show cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) [] ++ after need kids w x =
      cond (decide (need u ≤ x')) (kids u) [] ++ after need kids w x'
    rw [decide_le_congr (same u (.head w)), after_alike fun v m => same v (.tail u m)]

/-- **Lines reach each other only through the level.** The way after a step of two ways side by side
is the two ways after the step, side by side, at the same level. For any `need` and `kids`. -/
theorem after_append (x : Nat) :
    ∀ w₁ w₂ : List U, after need kids (w₁ ++ w₂) x = after need kids w₁ x ++ after need kids w₂ x
  | [], _ => rfl
  | u :: w₁, w₂ =>
    (congrArg (cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) [] ++ ·) (after_append x w₁ w₂)).trans
      (append_assoc' _ _ _).symm

/-- What two ways side by side take is the sum of what each takes. -/
theorem taken_append (use : U → Nat) (w₁ w₂ : List U) :
    taken use (w₁ ++ w₂) = taken use w₁ + taken use w₂ :=
  total_append use w₂ w₁

/-- The level after a step at two ways that take the same is the same. For any giving and use. -/
theorem left_of_taken {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : U → Nat} {w₁ w₂ : List U} {x : Nat}
    (same : taken use w₁ = taken use w₂) : left giving use w₁ x = left giving use w₂ x :=
  congrArg (fun t => RegimeMargin.step giving t x) same

/-- Where every unit's need is met at `x`, the way after a step is all the kids. -/
theorem after_of_met {x : Nat} :
    ∀ {w : List U}, (∀ u, u ∈ w → need u ≤ x) → after need kids w x = allKids kids w
  | [], _ => rfl
  | u :: w, met =>
    (congrArg (fun b => cond b (kids u) [] ++ after need kids w x)
        (decide_eq_true (met u (.head w)))).trans
      (congrArg (kids u ++ ·) (after_of_met fun v m => met v (.tail u m)))

/-- The way after a step is `List.flatMap` of what each unit leaves. -/
theorem after_flatMap (x : Nat) :
    ∀ w : List U, after need kids w x = w.flatMap fun u => cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) []
  | [] => rfl
  | u :: w => congrArg (cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) [] ++ ·) (after_flatMap x w)

end Step

/-! ## 4. Alike levels, one count, on any two stocks -/

section Count

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U}

/-- While every unit of the `j`-th generation of `w` has its need met at step `j` of the course from
`(w, x)`, the way at step `n` is the `n`-th generation of `w`. Under any giving and use. -/
theorem way_generation {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : U → Nat} {w : List U} {x : Nat} :
    ∀ n, (∀ j, j < n → ∀ u, u ∈ generation kids j w →
        need u ≤ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).2) →
      (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).1 = generation kids n w
  | 0, _ => rfl
  | n + 1, met => by
    have ih := way_generation n fun j lt => met j (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt)
    show after need kids (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).1
        (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).2 = allKids kids (generation kids n w)
    rw [ih]
    exact after_of_met (met n (Nat.lt_succ_self n))

/-- **Alike levels, one count, on any two stocks.** For two givings `g`, `g'`, two uses `use`,
`use'`, two start levels `x`, `x'` and one way `w`, under one `need` and `kids`: if for every `j < n`
every unit of the `j`-th generation of `w` has its need met at step `j` of both courses, then the way
at step `n` of each course is the `n`-th generation of `w`. Neither course's way is assumed; by
`way_generation` on each. -/
theorem alike_one_count {g g' : Nat → Nat} {use use' : U → Nat} {w : List U} {x x' n : Nat}
    (met : ∀ j, j < n → ∀ u, u ∈ generation kids j w →
      need u ≤ (course need kids g use (w, x) j).2 ∧ need u ≤ (course need kids g' use' (w, x') j).2) :
    (course need kids g use (w, x) n).1 = generation kids n w ∧
      (course need kids g' use' (w, x') n).1 = generation kids n w :=
  ⟨way_generation n fun j lt u m => (met j lt u m).1,
   way_generation n fun j lt u m => (met j lt u m).2⟩

/-- The course as generations of descent (`SelectionTerm.Generations`): the first family is the start's
way, the kids of `u` at step `t` are `kids u` where the level at step `t` meets `need u` and nothing
otherwise, and the amount is the use. -/
def generations (need : U → Nat) (kids : U → List U) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : U → Nat)
    (s : List U × Nat) : SelectionTerm.Generations U where
  first := s.1
  kids t u := cond (decide (need u ≤ (course need kids giving use s t).2)) (kids u) []
  amount _ := use

/-- **The way of a course is a family of generations.** At every step the family of `generations` is
the course's way. Under any giving and use, from any start; by `after_flatMap`. -/
theorem family_eq {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : U → Nat} (s : List U × Nat) :
    ∀ t, (generations need kids giving use s).family t = (course need kids giving use s t).1
  | 0 => rfl
  | t + 1 =>
    (congrArg (fun l => l.flatMap ((generations need kids giving use s).kids t)) (family_eq s t)).trans
      (after_flatMap (course need kids giving use s t).2 (course need kids giving use s t).1).symm

/-- The family's sum is what the course's way takes. -/
theorem sum_eq {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : U → Nat} (s : List U × Nat) (t : Nat) :
    (generations need kids giving use s).sum t = taken use (course need kids giving use s t).1 :=
  congrArg (fun l => total l use) (family_eq s t)

/-- The number born at step `t` (`SelectionTerm.born`) is the length of the course's next way:
`SelectionTerm.Generations.born_eq` with `family_eq`. -/
theorem born_eq_length {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : U → Nat} (s : List U × Nat) (t : Nat) :
    SelectionTerm.born ((generations need kids giving use s).family t)
        ((generations need kids giving use s).kids t) =
      (course need kids giving use s (t + 1)).1.length :=
  ((generations need kids giving use s).born_eq t).trans (congrArg List.length (family_eq s (t + 1)))

end Count

/-! ## 5. The case of `WayOfUnits` -/

section Units

theorem cond_single {α : Type} (u : α) (r : List α) :
    ∀ b : Bool, cond b [u] [] ++ r = cond b (u :: r) r
  | true => rfl
  | false => rfl

/-- With `kids u = [u]`, `after` is `WayOfUnits.after`. -/
theorem after_unit : ∀ (w : List (Nat × Nat)) (x : Nat),
    after WayOfUnits.need (fun u => [u]) w x = WayOfUnits.after w x
  | [], _ => rfl
  | u :: w, x => (cond_single u _ (decide (WayOfUnits.need u ≤ x))).trans
      (congrArg (fun r => cond (WayOfUnits.goes x u) (u :: r) r) (after_unit w x))

/-- `taken` at `WayOfUnits.use` is `WayOfUnits.taken`. -/
theorem taken_unit : ∀ w : List (Nat × Nat), taken WayOfUnits.use w = WayOfUnits.taken w
  | [] => rfl
  | u :: w => congrArg (WayOfUnits.use u + ·) (taken_unit w)

/-- `Goes` at `WayOfUnits.need` is `WayOfUnits.Goes`. -/
theorem goes_unit (s : List (Nat × Nat) × Nat) : Goes WayOfUnits.need s ↔ WayOfUnits.Goes s :=
  Iff.rfl

/-- With `kids u = [u]`, the pair's step is `WayOfUnits`'s. -/
theorem step_unit (giving : Nat → Nat) (s : List (Nat × Nat) × Nat) :
    step WayOfUnits.need (fun u => [u]) giving WayOfUnits.use s =
      TurningInTime.pair WayOfUnits.after (WayOfUnits.left giving) s :=
  congr_two Prod.mk (after_unit s.1 s.2)
    (congrArg (fun t => RegimeMargin.step giving t s.2) (taken_unit s.1))

/-- **The course of `WayOfUnits` is the case `kids u = [u]`.** -/
theorem course_unit (giving : Nat → Nat) (s : List (Nat × Nat) × Nat) (n : Nat) :
    course WayOfUnits.need (fun u => [u]) giving WayOfUnits.use s n = WayOfUnits.course giving s n :=
  orbit_congr (step_unit giving) s n

/-- **The net of `WayOfUnits` is the case `kids u = [u]`:** the two lines never end alike. -/
theorem unending_unit (giving : Nat → Nat) (s : List (Nat × Nat) × Nat) :
    «不穷» (net WayOfUnits.need (fun u => [u]) giving WayOfUnits.use) «一» s ↔
      «不穷» (WayOfUnits.net giving) «一» s :=
  have same : (∀ n, Goes WayOfUnits.need
      (course WayOfUnits.need (fun u => [u]) giving WayOfUnits.use s n)) ↔
        ∀ n, WayOfUnits.Goes (WayOfUnits.course giving s n) :=
    ⟨fun all n => Eq.subst (motive := WayOfUnits.Goes) (course_unit giving s n) (all n),
     fun all n => Eq.subst (motive := Goes WayOfUnits.need) (course_unit giving s n).symm (all n)⟩
  (Law.stepWhile_unending_iff.trans same).trans Law.stepWhile_unending_iff.symm

end Units

/-! ## 6. Rise and what descends along a line -/

section Line

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {S : U → Prop}

/-- Where each unit `u` of the line `S` has `use u + bonus u` at most what `S` takes of its kids, and
every `S`-unit of `w` has its need met at `x`: what `S` takes of `w`, with the bonus of its units,
is at most what `S` takes of the way after the step. -/
theorem draw_rises [DecidablePred S] {bonus : U → Nat}
    (more : ∀ u, S u → use u + bonus u ≤ lineTake S use (kids u)) {x : Nat} :
    ∀ {w : List U}, (∀ u, u ∈ w → S u → need u ≤ x) →
      lineTake S use w + total w (fun u => if S u then bonus u else 0) ≤
        lineTake S use (after need kids w x)
  | [], _ => Nat.le_refl 0
  | u :: w, met => by
    have head : (if S u then use u else 0) + (if S u then bonus u else 0) ≤
        lineTake S use (cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) []) :=
      (Decidable.em (S u)).elim
        (fun h => by
          rw [if_pos h, if_pos h, decide_eq_true (met u (.head w) h)]
          exact more u h)
        (fun h => by
          rw [if_neg h, if_neg h]
          exact Nat.zero_le _)
    show (if S u then use u else 0) + lineTake S use w +
        ((if S u then bonus u else 0) + total w fun v => if S v then bonus v else 0) ≤
      lineTake S use (cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) [] ++ after need kids w x)
    rw [Nat.add_add_add_comm, lineTake_append]
    exact Nat.add_le_add head (draw_rises more fun v m => met v (.tail u m))

/-- **Rise.** For a line `S` whose units' kids of the line take more than the unit
(`grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)`): if every `S`-unit of `w` has its need met at
`x`, what `S` takes of the way after the step is at least what it takes of `w` plus the number of
`S`-units of `w`. By `draw_rises` with a bonus of one. -/
theorem rise [DecidablePred S] (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) {x : Nat} {w : List U}
    (met : ∀ u, u ∈ w → S u → need u ≤ x) :
    lineTake S use w + lineCount S w ≤ lineTake S use (after need kids w x) :=
  draw_rises (bonus := fun _ => 1) (fun u h => Nat.succ_le_of_lt (grows u h)) met

/-- With the kids of the line taking at least what the unit takes (`keeps`), and every `S`-unit of
`w` met at `x`, what `S` takes does not fall at the step. By `draw_rises` with a bonus of nothing. -/
theorem draw_holds [DecidablePred S] (keeps : ∀ u, S u → use u ≤ lineTake S use (kids u)) {x : Nat} {w : List U}
    (met : ∀ u, u ∈ w → S u → need u ≤ x) :
    lineTake S use w ≤ lineTake S use (after need kids w x) :=
  Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_right _ _)
    (draw_rises (bonus := fun _ => 0) (fun u h => keeps u h) met)

/-- A unit of the line in the way after a step is a kid of a unit of the line whose need was met.
Premise: offspring inherit the line exactly (`same`). -/
theorem line_from_line (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {w : List U} {x : Nat} {v : U}
    (mv : v ∈ after need kids w x) (sv : S v) : ∃ u, u ∈ w ∧ need u ≤ x ∧ S u ∧ v ∈ kids u :=
  match (mem_after_iff (need := need) (kids := kids)).mp mv with
  | ⟨u, mu, le, mk⟩ => ⟨u, mu, le, (same u v mk).mp sv, mk⟩

/-- Where no unit of the line has its need met at `x`, no unit of the line is in the way after the
step. Premise: `same`. -/
theorem none_after (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {w : List U} {x : Nat}
    (nm : ∀ u, u ∈ w → S u → x < need u) : ∀ v, v ∈ after need kids w x → ¬ S v :=
  fun _ mv sv => match line_from_line same mv sv with
  | ⟨u, mu, le, su, _⟩ => Nat.not_le_of_gt (nm u mu su) le

/-- **What is gone from the line does not come back.** With `same`, under any giving and use: if no
unit of the line is in the way at step `k`, none is at any later step. -/
theorem gone_from (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {s : List U × Nat} {k : Nat}
    (gone : ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s k).1 → ¬ S u) :
    ∀ j, k ≤ j → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → ¬ S u :=
  have plus : ∀ i, ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s (k + i)).1 → ¬ S u := fun i => by
    induction i with
    | zero => exact gone
    | succ i ih => exact none_after same fun u mu su => absurd su (ih u mu)
  fun j le => match le_dest le with
  | ⟨i, e⟩ => Eq.subst (motive := fun j => ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → ¬ S u)
      e.symm (plus i)

/-- Where no unit of the line has its need met at step `k`, no unit of the line is in the way at any
step after `k`. With `same`. -/
theorem gone_after (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {s : List U × Nat} {k : Nat}
    (nm : ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s k).1 → S u →
      (course need kids giving use s k).2 < need u) :
    ∀ j, k + 1 ≤ j → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → ¬ S u :=
  gone_from same (k := k + 1) (none_after same nm)

end Line

/-! ## 7. The level under a draw -/

/-- A step under a draw at least the level leaves nothing, with nothing given from nothing. -/
theorem step_zero {giving : Nat → Nat} {H x : Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (le : x ≤ H) : RegimeMargin.step giving H x = 0 := by
  show x - H + giving (x - H) = 0
  rw [sub_eq_zero_of_le le]
  exact (Nat.zero_add _).trans nothing

/-- A step under a draw `H` of which every giving falls short (`short : ∀ z, giving z < T`,
`T ≤ H`) leaves less than the level, or what is given at nothing. -/
theorem step_falls {giving : Nat → Nat} {T H x : Nat} (short : ∀ z, giving z < T) (le : T ≤ H) :
    RegimeMargin.step giving H x < x ∨ RegimeMargin.step giving H x = giving 0 :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge x H).elim
    (fun lt => .inr (by
      show x - H + giving (x - H) = giving 0
      rw [sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt)]
      exact Nat.zero_add _))
    (fun ge => .inl (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le
      (Nat.add_lt_add_left (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (short (x - H)) le) (x - H))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (Naturals.sub_add_cancel ge))))

/-- With the step under no draw keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi`, the step under any draw
does. -/
theorem keeps_any {giving : Nat → Nat} {hi : Nat} (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (H x : Nat) (le : x ≤ hi) : RegimeMargin.step giving H x ≤ hi :=
  keeps (x - H) (Nat.le_trans (Nat.sub_le x H) le)

/-! ## 8. Fall -/

section Fall

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}
  {S : U → Prop} {hi : Nat}

/-- Where the level at step `k` is below the one need `m` of the line, no unit of the line is in the
way after step `k`. With `same` and `one`. -/
theorem fall_low (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {m : Nat} (one : ∀ u, S u → need u = m)
    {s : List U × Nat} {k : Nat} (lt : (course need kids giving use s k).2 < m) :
    ∃ n, ∀ j, n ≤ j → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → ¬ S u :=
  ⟨k + 1, gone_after same fun u _ su => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le lt (Nat.le_of_eq (one u su).symm)⟩

theorem fall_from [DecidablePred S] (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {m : Nat} (one : ∀ u, S u → need u = m)
    (keeps : ∀ u, S u → use u ≤ lineTake S use (kids u)) (low : giving 0 < m)
    (s : List U × Nat) :
    ∀ f k, (course need kids giving use s k).2 ≤ f →
      (∀ z, giving z < lineTake S use (course need kids giving use s k).1) →
      ∃ n, ∀ j, n ≤ j → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → ¬ S u
  | f, k, bound, over =>
    (Nat.lt_or_ge (course need kids giving use s k).2 m).elim (fall_low same one) fun ge =>
      have met : ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s k).1 → S u →
          need u ≤ (course need kids giving use s k).2 :=
        fun u _ su => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (one u su)) ge
      have hold := draw_holds (need := need) keeps met
      have over' : ∀ z, giving z < lineTake S use (course need kids giving use s (k + 1)).1 :=
        fun z => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (over z) hold
      (step_falls over (lineTake_le_taken _)).elim
        (fun fell => (Nat.lt_or_ge (course need kids giving use s (k + 1)).2 m).elim
          (fall_low same one)
          (fun _ => match f, bound with
            | 0, bound => absurd (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le fell bound) (Nat.not_lt_zero _)
            | f + 1, bound => fall_from same one keeps low s f (k + 1)
                (Nat.le_of_lt_succ (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le fell bound)) over'))
        (fun given => fall_low same one (k := k + 1)
          (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq given) low))

/-- **Fall.** For a line `S` that offspring inherit exactly (`same`), of one need `m`
(`one : ∀ u, S u → need u = m`), whose units' kids of the line take at least what the unit took
(`keeps`), with `giving 0 < m` (`low`): from a configuration `(w, x)` at which what `S` takes of `w`
is more than `giving z` at every `z` (`over`), there is a step from which no unit of the line is in
the way. While the level meets `m` the line is met, what it takes does not fall (`draw_holds`) and
the level falls or goes to `giving 0` (`step_falls`); below `m` the line leaves nothing
(`gone_after`). -/
theorem fall [DecidablePred S] (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u)) {m : Nat} (one : ∀ u, S u → need u = m)
    (keeps : ∀ u, S u → use u ≤ lineTake S use (kids u)) (low : giving 0 < m)
    {w : List U} {x : Nat} (over : ∀ z, giving z < lineTake S use w) :
    ∃ n, ∀ k, n ≤ k → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) k).1 → ¬ S u :=
  fall_from same one keeps low (w, x) x 0 (Nat.le_refl x) over

end Fall

/-! ## 9. A line that grows ends -/

section Grows

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}
  {S : U → Prop} {hi : Nat}

/-- Every unit of the line `S` in `w` needs at most `m`. -/
def Below (need : U → Nat) (S : U → Prop) (m : Nat) (w : List U) : Prop :=
  ∀ u, u ∈ w → S u → need u ≤ m

/-- No unit of the line `S` has its need met at the configuration. -/
def NoneMet (need : U → Nat) (S : U → Prop) (s : List U × Nat) : Prop :=
  ∀ u, u ∈ s.1 → S u → s.2 < need u

/-- With `same` and `down`, every unit of the line in the way after a step at `x` needs at most `x`. -/
theorem below_after (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u))
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) (w : List U) (x : Nat) :
    Below need S x (after need kids w x) :=
  fun _ mv sv => match line_from_line same mv sv with
  | ⟨u, _, le, su, mk⟩ => Nat.le_trans (down u _ su mk) le

/-- With `same` and `down`, a way whose line needs at most `m` leaves one that does. -/
theorem below_keeps (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u))
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) {m : Nat} {w : List U}
    (below : Below need S m w) (x : Nat) : Below need S m (after need kids w x) :=
  fun _ mv sv => match line_from_line same mv sv with
  | ⟨u, mu, _, su, mk⟩ => Nat.le_trans (down u _ su mk) (below u mu su)

/-- The claim at `m`: from every configuration at most `hi` whose line needs at most `m`, some step
has no unit of the line met. -/
def Ends (need : U → Nat) (kids : U → List U) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : U → Nat) (S : U → Prop)
    (hi m : Nat) : Prop :=
  ∀ s : List U × Nat, s.2 ≤ hi → Below need S m s.1 →
    ∃ k, NoneMet need S (course need kids giving use s k)

/-- One step and the rest: a step `j` of the course from the next configuration is step `1 + j` of
the course from `s`. -/
theorem none_shift {s : List U × Nat} {j : Nat}
    (nm : NoneMet need S (course need kids giving use (step need kids giving use s) j)) :
    ∃ k, NoneMet need S (course need kids giving use s k) :=
  ⟨1 + j, Eq.subst (motive := NoneMet need S) (Law.orbit_add _ s 1 j).symm nm⟩

theorem ends_low (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u))
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) {m : Nat}
    (ih : ∀ m', m' < m → Ends need kids giving use S hi m')
    (s : List U × Nat) (high : s.2 ≤ hi) (lt : s.2 < m) :
    ∃ k, NoneMet need S (course need kids giving use s k) :=
  match ih s.2 lt (step need kids giving use s) (keeps_any keeps _ _ high)
      (below_after same down s.1 s.2) with
  | ⟨_, nm⟩ => none_shift nm

theorem ends_high [DecidablePred S] (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u))
    (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) (needs : ∀ u, S u → 0 < need u)
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) {m : Nat}
    (ih : ∀ m', m' < m → Ends need kids giving use S hi m') :
    ∀ k (s : List U × Nat), s.2 ≤ hi → Below need S m s.1 → m ≤ s.2 →
      hi + 1 ≤ lineTake S use s.1 + k → ∃ j, NoneMet need S (course need kids giving use s j)
  | k, s, high, below, ge, fuel =>
    (line_or_none (S := S) s.1).elim
      (fun nm => ⟨0, fun u mu su => absurd su (nm u mu)⟩)
      (fun ⟨u₀, mu₀, su₀⟩ =>
        have met : ∀ u, u ∈ s.1 → S u → need u ≤ s.2 := fun u mu su =>
          Nat.le_trans (below u mu su) ge
        have up : lineTake S use s.1 + 1 ≤ lineTake S use (after need kids s.1 s.2) :=
          Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_left (lineCount_pos su₀ mu₀) _) (rise grows met)
        have high' : (step need kids giving use s).2 ≤ hi := keeps_any keeps _ _ high
        have below' : Below need S m (step need kids giving use s).1 :=
          below_keeps same down below s.2
        (Nat.lt_or_ge (step need kids giving use s).2 m).elim
          (fun lt => match ends_low keeps same down ih _ high' lt with
            | ⟨_, nm⟩ => none_shift nm)
          (fun ge' =>
            have within : taken use s.1 ≤ s.2 := (Nat.lt_or_ge s.2 (taken use s.1)).elim
              (fun lt =>
                have zero : (step need kids giving use s).2 = 0 := step_zero nothing (Nat.le_of_lt lt)
                absurd (Nat.le_trans (below u₀ mu₀ su₀) (Nat.le_trans ge' (Nat.le_of_eq zero)))
                  (Nat.not_le_of_gt (needs u₀ su₀)))
              id
            have small : lineTake S use s.1 ≤ hi :=
              Nat.le_trans (lineTake_le_taken _) (Nat.le_trans within high)
            match k, fuel with
            | 0, fuel => absurd (Nat.le_trans fuel small) (Nat.not_succ_le_self hi)
            | k + 1, fuel =>
              match ends_high nothing keeps same grows needs down ih k _ high' below' ge'
                  (Nat.le_trans fuel (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_right_comm _ k 1))
                    (Nat.add_le_add_right up k))) with
              | ⟨_, nm⟩ => none_shift nm))

/-- The claim at every `m`, by strong induction. -/
theorem ends_all [DecidablePred S] (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u))
    (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) (needs : ∀ u, S u → 0 < need u)
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) (m : Nat) :
    Ends need kids giving use S hi m :=
  strong_below (P := Ends need kids giving use S hi)
    (fun m ih s high below => (Nat.lt_or_ge s.2 m).elim
      (ends_low keeps same down ih s high)
      (fun ge => ends_high nothing keeps same grows needs down ih (hi + 1) s high below ge
        (Nat.le_add_left _ _)))
    (m + 1) m (Nat.lt_succ_self m)

/-- **A line that grows ends.** With nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the step under no draw
keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), the start at most `hi` (`high`), and a line `S`
that offspring inherit exactly (`same`), whose units' kids of the line take more than the unit
(`grows`), whose units need something (`needs`), and whose kids need no more than their parent
(`down`): there is a step from which no unit of the line is in the way. By `ends_all` at the total of
the needs of `w`, and `gone_after`. -/
theorem line_ends [DecidablePred S] (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (same : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → (S v ↔ S u))
    (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) (needs : ∀ u, S u → 0 < need u)
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) {w : List U} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) :
    ∃ n, ∀ k, n ≤ k → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) k).1 → ¬ S u :=
  match ends_all nothing keeps same grows needs down (total w need) (w, x) high
      (fun _ mu _ => le_total_of_mem mu) with
  | ⟨k, nm⟩ => ⟨k + 1, gone_after same nm⟩

end Grows

section All

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}

/-- What the line of every unit takes is what the way takes. -/
theorem lineTake_true (w : List U) : lineTake (fun _ => True) use w = taken use w :=
  total_congr (fun _ => rfl) w

/-- The count of the line of every unit is the length. -/
theorem lineCount_true (w : List U) : lineCount (fun _ => True) w = w.length :=
  (total_congr (fun _ => rfl) w).trans (total_ones w)

/-- **A way whose units grow ends.** With nothing given from nothing, the step under no draw keeping
every level up to `hi` within `hi`, the start at most `hi`, every unit's kids taking more than it
(`grows`), every need positive (`needs`) and kids needing no more than their parent (`down`): at some
step no unit's need is met, and the line from `(w, x)` ends. `line_ends` at the line of every unit. -/
theorem way_ends {hi : Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (grows : ∀ u, use u < taken use (kids u)) (needs : ∀ u, 0 < need u)
    (down : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) {w : List U} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) :
    (∃ n, ¬ Goes need (course need kids giving use (w, x) n)) ∧
      ¬ «不穷» (net need kids giving use) «一» (w, x) :=
  match ends_all (S := fun _ => True) nothing keeps (fun _ _ _ => Iff.rfl)
      (fun u _ => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (grows u) (Nat.le_of_eq (lineTake_true _).symm))
      (fun u _ => needs u) (fun u v _ mk => down u v mk) (total w need) (w, x) high
      (fun _ mu _ => le_total_of_mem mu) with
  | ⟨k, nm⟩ =>
    have stop : ¬ Goes need (course need kids giving use (w, x) k) := fun ⟨u, mu, le⟩ =>
      Nat.not_le_of_gt (nm u mu trivial) le
    ⟨⟨k, stop⟩, fun never => stop (Law.stepWhile_unending_iff.mp never k)⟩

/-- Along any course, with the step under no draw keeping levels within `hi` and the start at most
`hi`, every level is at most `hi`. -/
theorem course_within {hi : Nat} (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) {s : List U × Nat}
    (high : s.2 ≤ hi) : ∀ k, (course need kids giving use s k).2 ≤ hi
  | 0 => high
  | k + 1 => keeps_any keeps _ _ (course_within keeps high k)


/-- A list that is not empty has a member. -/
theorem mem_of_ne_nil' : ∀ {l : List U}, l ≠ [] → ∃ v, v ∈ l
  | [], ne => absurd rfl ne
  | v :: l, _ => ⟨v, .head l⟩

/-- While every need is met, a way whose units' kids take more than the unit (`grows`) stays not
empty, and what it takes at step `j` is at least `j`. By `rise` at the line of every unit. -/
theorem grows_while (grows : ∀ u, use u < taken use (kids u)) {w : List U} {x : Nat}
    (ne : w ≠ []) :
    ∀ j, (∀ t, t ≤ j → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) t).1 →
        need u ≤ (course need kids giving use (w, x) t).2) →
      (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).1 ≠ [] ∧
        j ≤ taken use (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).1
  | 0, _ => ⟨ne, Nat.zero_le _⟩
  | j + 1, all =>
    match grows_while grows ne j fun t le => all t (Nat.le_succ_of_le le) with
    | ⟨ne', le'⟩ =>
      have met := all j (Nat.le_succ j)
      match mem_of_ne_nil' ne' with
      | ⟨u, mu⟩ =>
        have some : kids u ≠ [] := fun e =>
          Nat.not_lt_zero _ (Eq.subst (motive := fun l => use u < taken use l) e (grows u))
        match mem_of_ne_nil' some with
        | ⟨v, mv⟩ =>
          have inNext : v ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) (j + 1)).1 :=
            (mem_after_iff (need := need) (kids := kids)).mpr ⟨u, mu, met u mu, mv⟩
          have up := rise (S := fun _ => True) (need := need) (kids := kids) (use := use)
            (fun u _ => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (grows u) (Nat.le_of_eq (lineTake_true _).symm))
            (fun u mu _ => met u mu)
          ⟨fun e => (nomatch (Eq.subst (motive := fun l => v ∈ l) e inNext)),
           Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add le' (length_pos_of_mem mu))
             (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (congr_two (· + ·) (lineTake_true _) (lineCount_true _)).symm)
               (Nat.le_trans up (Nat.le_of_eq (lineTake_true _))))⟩

/-- **A need comes to be unmet.** With nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the step under no draw
keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), the start at most `hi` (`high`), every unit's
kids taking more than it (`grows`), every need positive (`needs`), a way that is not empty (`ne`)
and every need met at the start (`met`): at some step `n` with `0 < n ≤ hi + 2`, some unit of the way
has its need unmet. No premise on the kids' needs. By `grows_while`: were every need met up to
`hi + 2`, the way at `hi + 1` would take more than `hi`, the level at `hi + 2` would be nothing, and
a unit there would need something. -/
theorem need_unmet {hi : Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (grows : ∀ u, use u < taken use (kids u)) (needs : ∀ u, 0 < need u)
    {w : List U} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) (ne : w ≠ []) (met : ∀ u, u ∈ w → need u ≤ x) :
    ∃ n, 0 < n ∧ n ≤ hi + 2 ∧ ∃ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).1 ∧
      (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).2 < need u :=
  match first_of_decided
      (P := fun j => ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).1 →
        need u ≤ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).2)
      (Q := fun j => ∃ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).1 ∧
        (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).2 < need u)
      (fun _ => met_or_unmet need _ _) (hi + 2) with
  | .inl all =>
    have atHi := grows_while grows ne (hi + 1) fun t le => all t (Nat.le_succ_of_le le)
    have over : (course need kids giving use (w, x) (hi + 1)).2 <
        taken use (course need kids giving use (w, x) (hi + 1)).1 :=
      Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (course_within keeps high (hi + 1))
        (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.lt_succ_self hi) atHi.2)
    have zero : (course need kids giving use (w, x) (hi + 2)).2 = 0 :=
      step_zero nothing (Nat.le_of_lt over)
    match mem_of_ne_nil' (grows_while grows ne (hi + 2) all).1 with
    | ⟨u, mu⟩ => absurd (Nat.le_trans (all (hi + 2) (Nat.le_refl _) u mu) (Nat.le_of_eq zero))
        (Nat.not_le_of_gt (needs u))
  | .inr ⟨n, le, unmet, _⟩ => match n, le, unmet with
    | 0, _, ⟨u, mu, lt⟩ => absurd (met u mu) (Nat.not_le_of_gt lt)
    | n + 1, le, unmet => ⟨n + 1, Nat.succ_pos n, le, unmet⟩

/-! ## 10. On a course that goes on, what the way takes is less than the level -/

/-- **On a course that goes on, what the way takes is less than the level.** With nothing given from
nothing (`nothing`) and every need positive (`needs`), under any `kids`: where some unit's need is met
at step `n + 1`, what the way takes at step `n` is less than the level there. A draw at or above the
level leaves nothing (`step_zero`), which meets no need. -/
theorem taken_below (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (needs : ∀ u, 0 < need u)
    {s : List U × Nat} {n : Nat} (goes : Goes need (course need kids giving use s (n + 1))) :
    taken use (course need kids giving use s n).1 < (course need kids giving use s n).2 :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge (taken use (course need kids giving use s n).1)
      (course need kids giving use s n).2).elim id fun ge =>
    match goes with
    | ⟨u, _, le⟩ => absurd (Nat.le_trans le (Nat.le_of_eq (step_zero nothing ge)))
        (Nat.not_le_of_gt (needs u))

/-- Where the line never ends, what the way takes is less than the level at every step. -/
theorem unending_taken_below (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (needs : ∀ u, 0 < need u)
    {s : List U × Nat} (never : «不穷» (net need kids giving use) «一» s) (n : Nat) :
    taken use (course need kids giving use s n).1 < (course need kids giving use s n).2 :=
  taken_below nothing needs (Law.stepWhile_unending_iff.mp never (n + 1))

/-! ## 11. Fixed configurations -/

/-- A configuration the step leaves as it is stays so. -/
theorem course_fixed {s : List U × Nat} (fixed : step need kids giving use s = s) :
    ∀ k, course need kids giving use s k = s
  | 0 => rfl
  | k + 1 => (congrArg (step need kids giving use) (course_fixed fixed k)).trans fixed

/-- **A line that comes to a configuration its step leaves as it is, some need met there and at every
step before, never ends.** By `course_fixed` and `Law.orbit_add`. -/
theorem unending_of_fixed {s : List U × Nat} {t : Nat}
    (before : ∀ j, j < t → Goes need (course need kids giving use s j))
    (fixed : step need kids giving use (course need kids giving use s t) =
      course need kids giving use s t)
    (goes : Goes need (course need kids giving use s t)) :
    «不穷» (net need kids giving use) «一» s :=
  Law.stepWhile_unending_iff.mpr fun k => (Nat.lt_or_ge k t).elim (before k) fun ge =>
    match le_dest ge with
    | ⟨i, e⟩ => Eq.subst (motive := fun j => Goes need (course need kids giving use s j)) e.symm
        (Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) ((Law.orbit_add _ s t i).trans (course_fixed fixed i)).symm
          goes)

end All

  | B => by decide

theorem goes_on : «不穷» (net need kids hump10 use) «一» ([B, A, B], 8) :=
  unending_of_fixed (t := 0) (fun _ lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero _)) fixed
    ⟨A, .tail _ (.head _), by decide⟩

namespace ShareRises

      lineTake (fun u => isC u = true) use [C] = 2 ∧ taken use [C] = 2 :=
