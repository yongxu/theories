# Brief: three laws of `TurningInTime` under a weaker premise, and the tidyings owed beside them

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ), 23 November 2026. Written before any build. Frozen when its hash stands in `FROZEN.sha256.txt`; what an attack changes goes under "After" and nothing above it is edited.*

## Why

On 23 November row FM landed `Becoming.LevelAlone`, whose core law asks of the course as it was only that a level once below `H₂ + y₂` stays below (`below`). Its forms in the notions `Recoverable` and `NoReturnAt` are given only where the level never rises, because the three laws of `Becoming.TurningInTime` that carry those notions (R1 `turned_meets_iff`, R2 `recoverable_iff_above`, R3 `noReturnAt_iff_first_below`) ask a course that never rises (`fall`) and starts at most `hi` (`top`). A reader of another kind of model argued on paper that they hold under `below` (row FM's study folder `fm-level-alone-2026-11-23/returns/astra-read.txt`, section 2, last bullet). Ren, 23 November 04:39 WIB, to the coordinating session: sessions that did not finish their work should finish those parts and then merge. This is the seam between the two rows' modules, and the session that keeps `TurningInTime` takes it. It advances a join: older laws become cases of more general ones.

## Members

**M1. In `Becoming/TurningInTime.lean`, its section 7 (lines 1990 to 2400 on main `52fc707a4`).** By the session's reading of the proofs: `fall` is used there only through the private `later_le`, in three places. In `turned_meets_of_above`: (a) `X T ≤ X 0 ≤ hi`, a bound; (b) `X T ≤ X t` for `t ≤ T`, so that the need is met before the turn. In `above_of_recoverable`: (c) `X T ≤ X N` for `N < T`. (b) and (c) are: a level at or above `H + y` at a later step is at or above it at an earlier one, which is `below` read backward.

To build, as new public theorems beside the old ones, names with the ending `_of_stays_below` (row FM's module uses that ending):

Each takes, in place of `fall` and `top`: `below : ∀ t, X t < H + y → X (t + 1) < H + y`, and a bound on the level, in the weakest form that builds: `X T ≤ hi` (or `X N ≤ hi`) where the law is at one index, `∀ t, X t ≤ hi` where it ranges over indices. `above_of_recoverable_of_stays_below` should need no bound. Every other premise is the old law's, unchanged.

The old laws' docstrings gain one clause naming the general law they are now a case of. The module's head note gains at most three lines. No other statement of the module changes, and `noReturn_exists`, `turns_in_time_iff_before` and section 8 are not touched.
