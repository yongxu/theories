# The meeting of the two halves, tried on paper on 20 November: it did not come out, and what that turns

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ), 20 November 2026. No Lean was written and nothing was built. Two AI workers who did not see each other worked the question in two homes, by small programs and on paper. A third AI agent, set to refute, wrote its own programs and read the Lean it named. A fourth (Claude Opus) set what the two workers and the frozen file say of the Lean against statements and docstrings, no proofs, and ran nothing. The paper arguments are each one agent's, read by no one else unless said. No person has read any of it. A search over small numbers shows where a statement fails, not that it holds. Share, rule, level, unit, need, use, giving, place, bud are working names.*

## What was asked

Ren, 20 November 19:06 WIB: "let's keep moving the roadmap, when choices are important, reflect on the direction" ([his words, with the roadmap as put to him](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-20-the-lead-keep-moving-the-roadmap.md)). Step 1 of that roadmap, which is this session's reading and not his: the table of 20 November has, in the package's core, what comes back and a rule set again from a share in places with no level that can run down, and what multiplies on a level in places without those two together; make them meet in one statement, on paper first.

The family asked about: what comes back to the favoured alone is paid only where a level is at or above an amount `M`. Two signs: the favoured draw more of the level than the others, or less. Two homes: the lock on a level (`TurningInTime` §6 with `RuleShaping`'s rule), and units on the line on a stock as it stands. Five guesses and eight tests of what would count as nothing having changed were frozen at 19:17, before any agent ran ([the frozen file](FROZEN-THE-MEETING.md)); three additions were written under its line "After" at 19:36, before any report was read, and the agents did not work from them.

"The second way" below is only the first of the three senses the record of 17 November told apart: staying within what is made again. Nothing in either model adds to a level, and no going-on in them rests on another.

## How it came out

**No statement was found that places the two ways with respect to each other.** That is the refuting agent's verdict, and I take it: none has both ends, the edge between them, and what a course passes by. Every statement that is exact trips test 1 (two older laws in a row), test 2 (a definition unfolded, or an older statement in other names) or test 3 (the level at which the favouring changes does no work). By the frozen file's own rule the fifth guess then stands in its outcome: what is gained is a recognition, and some kept failures.

By guess:

- **G1** (the favoured draw more; the fall stops the favouring; exact at one step). The condition at the step after the first step below `M` is necessary (argued by the lock's worker). It is not sufficient: the favouring comes back when the level recovers (the refuter's own search: 773 of 191,281 starts on the wrong side; the worker's smallest course checked by hand). It is exact only where the rule never favours again, which is handed in, or holds where `M` lies above every level the stock keeps. The guess's last clause is refuted in its letter: what breaks the single step is not a use that goes on changing, it is the favouring coming back.
- **G2** (the favoured draw less; two regions with one inequality between them). Exact only where `M` is at or below the point of no return of the least use, and there `M` does no work: the line never ends exactly where the lock's own course never comes below `M`, two older arguments end to end. Above that, refuted (the refuter: 216,138 of 660,255 starts with no premise on `M`), and no edge was found.
- **G3** (one statement with both signs as cases). Not found by either worker or by the refuter.
- **G4** (units; growth that needs more of the level than going on). In the writing the guess names (a kid with a higher need, and what growth added is kept), the first stop is necessary and not sufficient (the refuter: 13,079 of 20,014 on the wrong side), and within the range the Lean's exact laws assume the stop by the fall never comes in time: every such start at or above the higher need ends (argued by the refuter by a route of its own, after it found the worker's lemma false in part; no failure in either's search). Where the kind that multiplies needs more and leaves a kind that only stays, the guess holds in its form (below, U2), and it can come too late.
- **G5** (the family is the window law with `mixed_turns_iff`, and nothing is to be stated). Right in outcome, false in its letter. The family's step is a window course by definition, with the levels below `M` as the window, and `RuleShaping.favours_iff` gives the window law's premise `corrects`. But `locked_turns_in_time_on_window_iff` does not decide the family: its premise `uses` asks one use of every way whose rule is the one the window sets, which fails as soon as two shares use differently. Nothing is to be stated because nothing exact was found where the old laws stop, not because an old law covers it.

## What is kept, none of it a law in Lean

1. **Where the old laws stop.** The window law decides the family only where the use reads the rule alone, or where the level cannot come back up to `M`. Its neighbour `locked_turns_in_time_share_iff` does not cover the family either (the refuter and the reader against the Lean, each at the statement): it asks the window's reading to set the share, and in the family the share still steps under the rule in force. The reader against the Lean found no turn law in `TurningInTime` whose use after the turn changes for a finite stretch and then stands; each asks one use of the kind turned to.
2. **A contrast between the two signs, which is a comparison and not an edge.** Where the favoured draw less, the reading of the level keeps one order of configurations (more share, the favouring rule, more level: never worse); where they draw more, it reverses it. So in the first the starts that never end are closed upward and shrink as `M` rises, and in the second a start with more level can end where one with less goes on for ever (one course traced by the worker and checked by hand by the refuter: from level 11 the line alternates for ever, from 12 it ends in four steps). The refuter argues, unread by anyone else, that what holds where the favoured draw less is three generic laws the package already has (`Lasting.orbit_mono`, `Lasting.below_orbit`, `BandRest.keeps_one_way`) at the carrier of share, rule and level, and that the one new thing is the check that the family's step keeps the order in that sign. The reader against the Lean confirms that for the third law, finds the first two of that shape, and adds that a higher start ending where a lower one does not is already a kept case in the package on another carrier (`LineOnAStock.HigherStartEnds.higher_ends`).
3. **Where the fall changes who is favoured before the point of no return of what then goes on, no edge was found in either home**, and three kept courses say why: the favouring comes back with the level; more level can end a line; and, in units where what growth added is lost at the stop, growth is lost and regained in rounds for ever (a round of seven steps checked by hand by the refuter). In the lock's home the worker's search counts, among locked starts whose own course ends, lines that never end with the favouring lost for good, with it regained for good, and alternating for ever; those counts are of its ranges and were not re-run.
4. **In units.**
   - *A turn by the fall with the standing amount read at the turn* (the units' worker's U2, argued by it; searched by it and, on wider kinds, by the refuter, with no failure where its premises hold). Growers that all need `m`; every other unit leaves only itself and needs `n`, `0 < n ≤ m`; the regime's premises at the step of the turn, some stayer left there; the course with every need met coming below `m`. Then the line never ends exactly where, one step after the first step below `m`, the level is at or above the point of no return of what the stayers left by then take. `mixed_turns_iff` (scratch of 17 November) is its case, checked premise by premise by the reader against the Lean; `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` follows with its statement unchanged through a bridge lemma of the package. The refuter holds it a composition by the Lean's own shape (`unending_iff_first_marked`, then the regime's law at the way reached), and it has no rule and no share in it: of the two halves that were to meet, one is absent. As the worker wrote it, its premise "at each step" excludes every start with no stayer; it is to be asked at the step of the turn.
   - *Going on can ask that the level not rise.* For kids with a higher need, a line never ends exactly where its course comes to a configuration that stands: some need met, the way left as it is, and the level's course under what the way takes meeting the same needs throughout (the worker's E; no failure in 583,422 courses of the refuter's for the writing where growth is kept; it fails where growth is lost, 3,140 courses that never end and never stand). The last clause is what `LineOnAStock.Holds` does not have. `unending_of_fixed` is a case of its first half with its statement unchanged.
   - *A way that goes on with its growing need met at every step grows at every step and ends* (argued by the worker from `rising_takes_all`, whose premises the reader against the Lean lists). So in this model nothing goes on for ever while growing: only with its growing stopped, or lost and regained.
   - *The sign of the difference in use did no work in any statement the units' worker found.* What decided was which kind multiplies and which needs more; the fall takes first the kind that needs more.
   - *With a rule set again from a count put on top*, the rule did no work in any of these but one searched family, which is the course of 19 November: the lock's law, then U2 moved by two steps.
5. **Not found in the Lean, in what the reader against the Lean read** (its report lists the lines; a search by the ideas' words across the package, opening accounts and statements, not proofs): a module that joins `RuleShaping`'s return with a level (the modules that cite `RuleShaping` use neither `RegimeMargin` nor `TurningInTime`); a lock whose favoured kind draws less; an offspring number that depends on the level otherwise than through a need; an exact law for a kid whose need is higher than its parent's (the module's own account says there is none). Found: a return paid only at or above a level, in `CoupledDrawers` and in the gated renewals, never to the favoured alone and never with a rule read from a share.

## What it turns, set against the direction

This is my reading, held loosely, and it is the choice Ren asked to have reflected on.

The direction asks for two ways placed with respect to each other: one that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and one that keeps remaking what it rests on. Step 1 looked for the placing on one level whose giving is a given function, with the favoured taking less as the nearest thing to keeping. What came back, in both homes: on such a level a way goes on for ever only as a standing use that the giving meets, or by losing its growth and regaining it. That is the frozen test 7's empty end. Nothing on such a level is remade by anything; it is only taken from less. The record of 17 November had argued this from the definitions ("where what a going-on rests on is one amount standing apart, a going-on that remakes it is not sayable"); today it was tried from the other side, with a return and a rule, and came out the same.

So I do not think the two ways can be placed on one level with its giving handed in, and I stop trying. The first way has much there: the lock, the point of no return, and now where a turn by the fall is exact and where it is not. The second way has content only where what is rested on is itself kept by goings-on. That is the third sense of 17 November, and the roadmap's step 3.

The order I now hold:

1. **The second way where what is rested on is itself a going-on.** First a read at the source of what the Lean already states there, before any statement is proposed (the record of 17 November's dig names four things, and the table has more rows: `PairFlow`, `ReadingsSpread`, `Roles.Mutualism`, the conditions that each give by what is left of the others, `deeper/RelationalRenewal`'s contribution, `Dissipation`'s closed family). What would count against going this way: the read finds that there too what keeps is a supplied function and nothing is derived, as in `RelationalRenewal`, where the reader against the Lean notes the contribution is handed in.
2. **The meeting again, inside that**: what comes back, a rule from a share, and a level that is itself kept. Today's kept items 1 to 3 say what it must get past: one use after the turn, the favouring coming back, more level ending a line.
3. **How what a loop registers comes to be and is kept**, as before.
4. **The tellings**, with each new docstring set against its signature at the landing that adds it (the survey session's point), and a case from outside, which is his.

Not on the roadmap, and to be placed after this step and not left unsaid: the map as data (the atlas, the paused second store, and the table of 20 November as a third listing), raised by both other sessions.

Whether a small Lean statement is worth having from today is open and nothing is written before Ren has seen this: the nearest would be the check that the family's step keeps the order where the favoured draw less, which would make this family a case of three generic laws; and U2, which would make `two_units_iff` a case and settle the scratch proof of 17 November. Both trip the frozen tests as a meeting; either would be a join of the smaller kind.
