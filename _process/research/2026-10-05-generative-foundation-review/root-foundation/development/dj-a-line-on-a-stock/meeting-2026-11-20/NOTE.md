*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ), 20 November 2026. No Lean was written and nothing was built. Two AI workers who did not see each other worked the question in two homes, by small programs and on paper. A third AI agent, set to refute, wrote its own programs and read the Lean it named. A fourth (Claude Opus) set what the two workers and the frozen file say of the Lean against statements and docstrings, no proofs, and ran nothing. A fifth AI agent (Claude Opus), who took no part, then set a first form of this note against the frozen file and the four reports: it found fifteen places that were false or said more than their source and seventeen lesser ones; they are mended here in its words where it gave words, and not read again ([its report](check-note.md.txt)). ## What it turns, set against the direction

This is my reading, held loosely, and it is the choice Ren asked to have reflected on.

The direction asks for two ways placed with respect to each other: one that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and one that keeps remaking what it rests on. Step 1 looked for the placing on one level whose giving is a given function, with the favoured taking less as the nearest thing to keeping. What came back: in units, a way goes on for ever only as a standing use that the giving meets, by losing its growth and regaining it, or (in random worlds) by a way that goes round; in the lock's home, also by the favouring lost and regained for ever. None of these remakes anything: the level is only taken from less. The standing use is the frozen test 7's empty end. The record of 17 November tells its own frozen file as saying that where what a going-on rests on is one amount standing apart, a going-on that remakes it "is not sayable", an argument from the model's definitions carried by no theorem; today it was tried from the other side, with a return and a rule, and nothing was found against it.

So, from this one family in two homes, small searches and paper arguments read by no one, I do not think the two ways can be placed on one level with its giving handed in, and I stop trying there. The first way has much there: the lock, the point of no return, and now where a turn by the fall is exact and where it is not. My reading is that the second way has content only where what is rested on is itself kept by goings-on; that is the third sense of 17 November, the one I hold, and the roadmap's step 3. No report states it. Which sense the direction means is Ren's to say.

The order I now hold:

1. **The second way where what is rested on is itself a going-on.** First a read at the source of what the Lean already states there, before any statement is proposed (the record of 17 November's dig names four things, and the table has more rows: `PairFlow`, `ReadingsSpread`, `Roles.Mutualism`, the conditions that each give by what is left of the others, `deeper/RelationalRenewal`'s contribution, `Dissipation`'s closed family). That record already has one thing that bounds it, under the premise that nothing gives from nothing, on a record that balances: "a finite family taken from at arbitrarily late steps, its exchange enclosed in finitely many conditions from some step on, is not from any step on left unfed from beyond it (`fed_from_beyond`)". So a second way in this sense is not a closed loop; it would be goings-on that pass along what comes from beyond them. What would count against going this way: the read finds that there too what keeps is a supplied function and nothing is derived, as in `RelationalRenewal`, where the reader against the Lean notes the contribution is handed in.
2. **The meeting again, inside that**: what comes back, a rule from a share, and a level that is itself kept. Today's kept items 1 to 3 say what it must get past: one use after the turn, the favouring coming back, more level ending a line.
3. **How what a loop registers comes to be and is kept**, as before.
4. **The tellings**, with each new docstring set against its signature at the landing that adds it (the survey session's point), and a case from outside, which is his.

Not on the roadmap, and to be placed after this step and not left unsaid: the map as data (the atlas, the paused second store, and the table of 20 November as a third listing), raised by both other sessions.

Whether a small Lean statement is worth having from today is open and nothing is written before Ren has seen this: the nearest would be the check that the family's step keeps the order where the favoured draw less, which, by the refuter's unread argument (the second of the three not found as one statement by the reader), would make this family a case of generic laws: a recognition, not a join; and U2, of which `two_units_iff` and the scratch's `mixed_turns_iff` would be cases, which is a join of the smaller kind. Both trip the frozen tests as a meeting. Before either were written, it would first be set against the table's other rows, which was not done.
