On places it applies only through `a ↦ (a, n − a)` with `a < n`, at `θ = ⌈n/2⌉`. A share of all `n` places has no `CarrierCounts`. Any other `θ` (the searches used `θ = 2` at every `n`) is a reading of the same shape, not `thresholdBack`.

**A2. **A3. G5-law, `covers`/`safe`: holds as a necessary condition.** The premises are `covers : c ≤ K` and `safe` from `c` up to `hi`. With `K = M`, `c ≤ M`, so `safe` asks every level from `c` up to be safe, and `c` may lie below `M`. The report's "every level from `M` up is safe" follows from this but is weaker than what the Lean asks. Its "or `c > hi`" is the docstring's own case (`safe` is then vacuous).
