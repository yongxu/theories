## U1b argued without U1a (argued, by me; unread)

Claim: W1 with the regime at what the way takes at each step where the level falls; if the level falls at a step `s`, every later way with `n` met before it takes at least what was taken at `s`. Then U0 applies.

- *`s` at a level at or above `m`.* `p_{s+1} = p_s + q_s·ia`, `q_{s+1} = p_s·jb + q_s·ib`. At `s + 1` the take less the take at `s` is `q_s(ia·a + ib·b − b) + p_s·jb·b`, not negative where `ib ≥ 1`; where `ib = 0` buds never exceed `jb` a core, so `q_s·b ≤ p_s·jb·b`. Later, `p_t ≥ p_{s+1}` and `q_t ≥ p_{s+1}·jb`, so the take is at least `(p_s + q_s·ia)(a + jb·b) ≥ p_s·a + q_s·b`.
- *`s` below `m` with `q_s ≤ p_s·jb`.* Later `p_t ≥ p_s`, `q_t ≥ p_s·jb ≥ q_s`.
- *`s` below `m` with `q_s > p_s·jb`.* Then `s ≥ 1` and step `s − 1` was at or above `m` (a step below `m` leaves exactly `jb` a core, and the start has at most that). So the level fell at `s − 1` too, and the first case applies there.

So U1b holds for every `ib`, with the regime asked at every fall; its writer's sentence "the first sentence is U0 with (a)" should be replaced.

## For the direction's sake

**Does any statement place the two ways with respect to each other? No.**

- *Both ends.* No statement has a way that uses up what it rests on at one end and a way that keeps remaking it at the other. The going sides found are: a standing use at or above its point of no return (HL, LATE, D-no-return, U2, E), which is test 7's own example; a set of fates closed upward (UP); and rounds (W3). None remakes anything.
- *The edge.* Where an edge is exact it is the old one: a level, one step after a turn, against the point of no return of a use that then stands (tests 1 and 4). Where the fall changes who is favoured before the point of no return of what goes on (`M` above it, the level able to come back; kids that need more), no edge was found in either home, and three kept failures say why: the favouring comes back (D-sufficient), more level can end a line (NUP), growth is lost and regained for ever (W3).
- *By what a course passes.* No statement has a course passing from one to the other. The alternating courses (the workers count 13,009 and 9,625 in the lock's home; the rounds in W3) are the only ones that go back and forth, and nothing is stated of them.

**What is gained, plainly.**

1. A recognition, with its limit: the family is a window course; the window law decides it only where the use reads the rule alone or the level cannot come back to `M`.
2. One structural contrast, not an edge: where the favoured draw less the reading of the level keeps the order of configurations, where they draw more it reverses it. That is why one sign has a least level for each share and the other does not. It is a check of monotonicity put through three generic laws the package already has.
3. In units, the turn by the fall with the standing amount read at the turn (U2), of which `two_units_iff` is a case; it is the 19 November paper statement with the step named and trips 1, 4 and 7.
4. Kept failures and courses: D-sufficient, NUP, G2's dichotomy, G4's first stop, U1a, the W3 round.

**If one statement had to be carried forward**, the two that come nearest, with the least premises under which my search found no failure and what to distrust:

- **UP-MM-REST.** Premises: `fA(a, not) ≤ a ≤ fA(a, favours)` and `fA` monotone in the share under each rule; the use not rising with the share; `z + giving z` not falling as `z` rises; the reading "favours exactly where `x ≥ M` and `a ≥ θ`". Distrust: it is three generic laws at one carrier; "an edge exists" means only that the going configurations are closed upward, and no edge is stated; it says nothing where the favoured draw more; "draw less" is the first sense of the second way only.
- **U2.** Premises: growers all needing `m`; every other unit leaving itself and needing `n`, `0 < n ≤ m`; nothing from nothing; start within `hi`; the step under the start's stayers' take keeping within `hi`; the course as it was coming below `m`; and the regime (`Short`, `Meets`, `n ≤ H_T + y_T`) **at the step `T` of the turn only**, with some stayer left there. Distrust: as written ("at each `T`") it excludes starts with no stayer, its own worked case among them; it is silent where the course as it was never comes below `m`; no rule and no share is in it; what goes on is one standing amount.
