(ii) Every turn law of the package asks one use `H₂` of every way of the kept kind. (iii) U2's "comes below `m`" is weaker than `unending_iff_first_marked`'s `asItWasEnds` (below `n`) and than `turns_in_time_iff`'s `short₁`/`uses₁`/`below`.

**(2) What a Lean U2 would add.** Cases with their statements unchanged: `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` (except equal pairs, one more line) and so `With.goes_on`; in scratch, `mixed_turns_iff` (439) and `mixed_turns_iff_ends` (470), per B11. Not cases: `turns_in_time_iff` and its forms (they are generic in `W`; U2 is about units), `LineOnAStock.unending_iff_holds`, `WayOfUnits.unending_iff_holds`. Shorter proofs: probably none. `two_units_iff` is now about 30 lines (transfer, `turns_in_time_iff`, arithmetic). Through U2 it would need U2's premises at a constant way, `ends_below` for "comes below `m`", `unending_unit`, and the equal-pairs line, which is not shorter (argued). The reusable part, and the one join worth stating, is the transfer: from a units way whose non-growers leave themselves, at the first marked step, to a carrier `Nat ⊕ Unit` with the kept use read at that step. `unending_iff_carrier` and `unending_grown` would be its cases. With it, U2 is a few lines. Without it, U2 is the composition written out once more.

**(3) Is the refuter right?** Yes in substance. U2 is `unending_iff_first_marked` at `marked := (· < m)`, `need := n`, then the regime law at the way reached (`ends_iff_below` with `holds`, or `unending_iff_above`). Two qualifications. First, it is that composition only on a carrier with a transfer lemma. Second, `unending_iff_first_marked`'s `asItWasEnds` must be widened to "some step of the course as it was is below the need or marked" (argued from the proof term shown; not built), or U2's premise strengthened to "comes below `n`". Is U2 an instance of `turns_in_time_iff` or `turns_in_time_iff_kept_below` with the kept kind read off the turn? Only on a carrier built from the start, where `T`, hence `H₂ := H_T` and `y₂ := y_T`, are fixed numbers. There `uses : ∀ w, Kept w → use w = H₂` holds because the carrier has one kept way, `inr ()`, with use `H_T`. `turns` and `kept` hold by construction, and `keeps` holds at `H_T` from `Keeps g H₀ hi` and `H₀ ≤ H_T` by `kept_within_more` (scratch only). Even there U2 lacks `short₁`, `uses₁`, `below`. So U2 is an instance of those laws only on the sub-family where some `H₁ ≤` every take of the course as it was and `x < H₁ + y₁` (this is `two_units_iff`'s and `mixed_turns_iff`'s family). Outside it, the widened `unending_iff_first_marked` plus `unending_iff_above` is what applies.

**(4) What to distrust in U2 as stated.**
- *`R_T ≠ []` is redundant as a premise.* `Short g 0 y` forces `y = 0`, and with `0 < n` the premise `n ≤ H_T + y_T` then fails, so the regime premises at `T` already force `H_T > 0`, hence `R_T ≠ []`. It also excludes stayers that take nothing. As a premise it hides that the line ends trivially where the turn leaves no stayer.
- *`T` is defined, not quantified.* The premises at `T` refer to the first `j` with `X_j < m`, so a Lean statement needs `Nat.find` (decidable `<`) or the premises as "for every first marked `T`". The worker's first text ("at each `T`") excluded every start with no stayer, its own worked example among them (refuter, line 11).
- *Equal pairs.* `two_units_iff` at `(m, h) = (n, H₂)` is not reached and wants its own line. At `m = n` with distinct pairs it is reached, and both sides are false.
- *"Comes below `m`" is a real premise.* It is not derivable in general: growers that take nothing and multiply (the 39,025 undecided), or growers that leave only stayers, can keep the course as it was at or above `m`, or in `[n, m)`. It is derivable (i) from `short₁`, `uses₁`, `below` by `RegimeMargin.ends_below` (705), as in `two_units_iff`; or (ii) where the take strictly rises at every step up to `hi`, by `TurningInTime.rising_takes_all`, whose `rises` premise was seen at its use near line 725 but whose statement was not opened. `LineOnAStock.need_unmet` (1011) does not reach U2's ways: its `grows` is asked of every unit, and the stayers do not grow.
- *Hidden lemmas.* `Keeps` at `H_T` and the level within `hi` at `T + 1` rest on `S/ kept_within_more` and `S/ asItWas_within`, which are not in the package. `gone_from`'s `closed` is global, so a proof should use that `R_T` is fixed, not `gone_from`.
- *Uniform needs.* Every grower needs `m` and every stayer needs `n`: U2 is silent on stayers with different needs, and on stayers that leave anything other than themselves.
