## Where the population model sits, as I read it

- **Its grain.** A population of contracts revised by a supplied schedule, each slot that is changed taking the action that pays more at that step against the same others, under a correction that acts only while a reserve covers its fee. The two actions, the payoffs, the charges, the law of revision and its schedule, the reserve's law and the law of capacity are handed in.
- **What part it is.** One region where a way of selecting helps keep up the condition that selects it: the correction makes renewing pay, and the contracts pay for the correction (the renewing ones always, the racing ones while it acts). For that mechanism both ends are stated with the boundary between them exact, under the premises the table's note gives (`exact_schedule_activation_boundary`, `schedule_shortfall_is_destructive`, `schedule_sufficiency_reaches_full_state`). The study does not say of either end that it is a meta-attractor. As I read the file, at each end what the contracts do keeps the condition that selects them: all renewing keeps the correction funded, none renewing keeps it unfunded. Whether the end with none renewing is recurrent in the page's sense the file does not settle: the page says "A frozen record, exhausted structure or absorbing failure can be invariant without recurring".
- **What the pages keep apart that sits near the direction's words.** The generative-regimes page separates a selection threshold, the boundary of the starts that approach a regime, and the boundary of what an intervention can still reach, and says a claim that two of them coincide "needs its own model and proof". It has two arrangements whose histories under the selected operation are the same and whose outcomes under an untried change are opposite, so that no classifier of that history alone "can judge both correctly". And on the collective page a faster schedule of revision can change which side of the boundary a start falls on (one six-slot example). These sit near three things on the direction page: what a going-on rests on and what makes a difference to it need not be the same; from inside, a way that keeps remaking what it rests on and one that uses it up can look alike until late; and whether what a course rests on still reaches how it reads early enough to turn it. No one has set either against the other.
- **What the file does not have.** Nothing in the file is derived from the account's root: it imports only `CollectiveDestruction`, and its mechanism is supplied. Nothing in it leaves offspring. The amount that runs down in it, the capacity, is read by nothing and can recover; what the correction needs is the reserve. The population model says nothing of how the correcting institution forms, which is its own wall. Pages not read take up how regulation forms and is revised.
