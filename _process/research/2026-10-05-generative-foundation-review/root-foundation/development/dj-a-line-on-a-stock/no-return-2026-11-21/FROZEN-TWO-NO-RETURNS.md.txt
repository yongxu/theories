## The guesses (each may be false)

- **J1. For a process whose states are the levels themselves, the two are one.** For `P : Process (C → Nat) A`, a guard `g` and a run `r`, with the feasible courses the state courses of the runs of `P` and what is asked `∀ t, g (l t)`: the course of `r` is recoverable at `N` exactly where `g` holds on `r` up to `N` and `g` is keepable from `r.state N`. No other premise.
- **J2. With that, the point of no return of a run is the first step at which its state is not keepable**, where `g` held before; and it exists exactly where the run reaches a state from which `g` is not keepable.
- **J3. The lock is a case, though its states carry more than the level.** There is a process (a level and whether the way has turned) whose runs' level courses are `Turned X giving H` where `X` is the course of the way left as it was, and for it `Recoverable (Turned X giving H) (MeetsNeed need) X N` is: the need met up to `N`, and the need keepable from the level at `N` with the way not yet turned. If so, `recoverable_iff_above` has the same statement as a statement proved through keepable.
- **J4. Something old becomes a case, or something new follows.** At least one of: a theorem of `PointOfNoReturn` gets a scratch twin with the same statement up to unfolding, proved through `Keepable` and the scratch step law; October's renewable configurations get a point of no return by the two bridges together; `readableInTime_iff` can be stated of the runs of any process.
- **J5 (against the step).** `Recoverable`'s general laws hold of any set of feasible courses, and a set of courses need not be the runs of a process. So the join holds only on the part where the feasible courses come from a process, and there it is a dictionary: nothing old becomes a case and nothing new follows. The read of 21 November that set October's renewal against keeping ended that way ("a re-packing").
- **J6 (the older files).** The four older files outside the package state recoverability of something else (control, shutdown, explanation), on carriers of their own, and none is the same notion as either of the two above.

The coordinating session knows no place where the join is stated (its search by name: no tracked Lean file and no filed scratch text has both; six atlas pages have both words, never near each other). Four things it added, taken in as tests:

1. **Which differences the try keeps fixed.** The two notions differ in three ways at once: feasible is any set of whole courses, where a process's runs are generated step by step; `keeps` is asked of a whole course, where a guard is asked at every state; and recoverable is relative to a course's past up to `N`, where keepable is from a state and has no past. J1 fixes the first two (feasible is the runs of a process; what is asked is a guard at every step) and states what becomes of the third (the past enters as "the guard held up to `N`"). The worker says, for each of the three, what its statement fixes.
2. **The direction of "is a case of".** Keepable from `s` should be recoverable at `N = 0` with feasible the runs of `P`. So keepable sits under recoverable, and recoverable is the wider notion. And it is the keepable set, not the kernel, whose edge is the point of no return: the kernel is where every course stays; the point of no return is where some course still can.
3. **Where it should fail, to be found and kept.** A family of feasible courses that is not the runs of any process, or a `keeps` that is not a guard at every step (something that need only hold in the end). The worker looks at every use of `Recoverable` in the package (`ReadOffInTime`, `TurningInTime`) and says of each whether its feasible courses are the runs of a process and whether its `keeps` is a guard at every step. A use that is neither is a result.
4. **What would make it more than a dictionary**, replacing J4's list with three named tests: (a) `NoReturnAt.recoverable_iff` stated through the keepable set; (b) the lock's point of no return on one level (the number in `RegimeMargin`) as the edge of where the guard is keepable for that level's process; (c) the scratch step law of 21 November carried over to `Recoverable`, which has no step law that was found. If none of the three comes out, it is a dictionary, and saying so is the result.
