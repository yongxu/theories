**The process.** For any course `X` of one level, any giving and any standing use `H`: a state is a step, a level and whether the way has turned. From a state not turned, staying goes to the next step with the level `X` has there, not turned; turning goes to the regime's step under `H` from the present level, turned. From a turned state every step is the regime's step. The guard is the need met at the level. So the way left as it was and the way turned are this process's two moves, and the choice is made along the way.
