## What bears on J1 to J6

- **J6 holds in what was read.** All four state recoverability of something else (a device's pending change and its safety; stop access during recovery; a past fact from a present configuration; pairs from dissipation), on carriers of their own, and none defines or uses `Recoverable`, `NoReturnAt`, `Keepable` or `Kernel`.
