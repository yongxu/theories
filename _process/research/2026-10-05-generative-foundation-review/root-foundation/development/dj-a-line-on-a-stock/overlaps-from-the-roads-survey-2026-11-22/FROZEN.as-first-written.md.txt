## Where this comes from

Row FM's survey (`_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/fm-roads-the-atlas-tells-2026-11-22/`) left with this row: six overlaps that stand and that the store does not say (`readers/CHECK-overlaps-and-walls-fable.md.txt`, "The two lists"), one candidate road (`readers/CHECK-roads-and-sample-opus.md.txt`, section 1), and a table of how blind judges placed the 56 lawed exits of the store that name a dynamic (`JUDGE-SCORED.json.txt`). Since the layer of uses between proofs landed (`ddff47bc5`), a line between two dynamics is computed wherever the proof of a theorem listed under one uses a theorem listed under the other. So what the store itself must still say by hand is only: which theorems are listed under which dynamic; where a law is another's case (a `case_of` row); and where a course leaves one dynamic for another (an exit).

## What is tried, item by item, in the store's own forms

- **E1. `ChoiceCountCapture.counted_outlives_loss` a case of `ClosedCapture.lock_outlives_loss`.** A `case_of` row on the law's existing premise row, on both dynamics that list it (`capture-by-counted-choices`, `habit-lock-in-active-inference`). The print already has the use. *Against:* the proof is more than that law with its roles fixed (a second argument of its own); or the `how` says more than the proof term; or `lock_outlives_loss` is a declaration of the dynamic the row sits on.
- **E2. Three theorems of `Junctures.lean` on no dynamic's list** (`Branching.Juncture.possiblyDrawn`, `Branching.Juncture.returns`, `Branching.PossiblyDrawn.passes_juncture`) **and three of the "more without end is unceasing" family** (`Flow.More.unceasing_covering`, `unceasing_reidentified`, `unceasing_reidentified_covering`). Each is listed under a dynamic, in its `declarations`, only where a reader shown the statement and the atlas's dynamics, and NOT told the survey's answer or what the theorem uses, names that one dynamic as the one whose own notion the statement states, such that the theorem would belong on its list whatever else is true of it. Listed, the computed layer draws whatever its proof uses; nothing else is typed. *Against a listing:* the blind reader names no dynamic, or two; or the dynamic already lists a theorem that says the same (then the page points, it does not repeat). No theorem is listed in order to carry a link.
- **E3. `PossiblyDrawn.passes_juncture` a case of `Branching.loss_passes_juncture`.** Only if E2 lists it under a dynamic that does not list `loss_passes_juncture`, and a writer shows from the proof that it is that law with roles fixed after an identification (`possiblyDrawn_iff`), and a refuter agrees. It then needs a premise row and a mapping row (binders by the lister's Lean function; a Lean run of seconds, told beforehand). *Against:* it is a use and not a case; then it is listed only (E2) and the layer shows the use.
- **E4. The one road: `Web.optimum_ends_beyond` as an exit of `what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in`.** The survey's own rule for a far end: it names a dynamic only where a judge who took no part, shown the statement and the entries and not told the hoped-for answer, names the same one. One judge has named `three-outcomes-of-a-loss`; the checker adds `loss-of-renewal-for-good`. A second blind reader is asked here. An exit is entered only if that reader names one of those two; its `to` is the dynamic both blind readers name (if they differ, none is entered); its `edge` says, in the checker's words, that the passage starts from a hypothesis that is this law's alone in its dynamic (the dependence persists and the need is no weaker at the next step), that it gives one step and not the far law's every later step, and that the proof calls no law of the far end; `strength` `sufficient`. *Against:* the second reader names another dynamic or none; or a refuter shows the edge says more than the statement.
- **Not entered, whatever the readers say, and filed as found:** the overlaps that stand only in a statement (`Juncture.returns` concluding the other dynamic's `Returns`; `TurningInTime.locked_at_class` concluding two premises of `what-a-regularity-takes-in`'s law; `Flow.More.unceasing` concluding `Unceasing`), beyond a listing under E2. The store has no form for "a statement listed here names a notion listed there": it is not a case, not a joining as the store defines one (a state read as a rule, a reading or a pool), and not an exit; the survey itself found that the four `exact` exits naming a dynamic are identifications drawn as exits, and none is added. The five walls: the store has no field for one.

## E5. A look at the store's own roads (findings only; nothing is changed by it)

Blind judges placed 23 of the 56 lawed exits that name a dynamic as a passage inside one dynamic and nothing else. Eight of the 23, taken by a rule fixed here (the 23 sorted by the survey's key, every third from the first: S01, S07, S10, S23, S30, S40, S43, S50), are opened by one reader: the exit as the store has it, the law's statement, and both entries. For each: does the statement place the course, at its conclusion, under a notion that is the far dynamic's own (and which), so that the exit's `to` is borne; or is the far dynamic named by the exit's words and not by the statement? *What would count as the store being right:* the far end is in the statement for most of the eight. *What would count against the store:* for most, the far dynamic is not in the statement. Either way it is a sample of 8 of 23 by one reader, and what follows from it (lowering a `to` to words, as three exits were lowered on 17 November) is a later landing after the writer of this file has opened each.

## What this is not

Not a way to move a count. Before: by the store's hand-kept links 81 of 186 dynamics are linked to no other; that figure is told after, as a count. No sentence of any entry's mechanism is changed. Nothing here places the two ways with respect to each other.

## How, and the cap

One blind reader (E2's placements and E4's far end, two separate questions); one writer of the rows for E1, E3 and E4, after the blind reader; one refuter of those rows; one reader for E5. Readers read; nothing is built by them. Then the writer of this file applies what is left, runs the lister for any new premise row, the validator, the tool's check, the render, the atlas's tests and the gate, and has the note checked. Cap: five agents with the note's checker, about 700,000 tokens in all; each is told to stop at 150,000.
