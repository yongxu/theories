## 21 November, how a registering comes to be: reader revision-and-forming

*As "OpenedCorrection.lean (Linked.opens)". A reader's return, unchecked; what the note mended is in the note: [note](../../read-registering-2026-11-21/NOTE.md), [report](../../read-registering-2026-11-21/read-revision-and-forming.md.txt).*

- **what registers what**: reading : X → S → O in force at each configuration. Correctable (l74): a uniform policy through reading x whose acts are acceptable and executable at x.
- **what changes it**: Along a trace of one process: OpensAlong (l89) = (∃w, Trace P x w x') ∧ ¬Correctable x ∧ Correctable x'. 'The gain may come from the reading, from executability, or both.'
- **laws where something comes to be registered**: Linked.opens (l168, built for all n, v): OpensAlong P read acceptable executable (n+2,false,v) (n+1,true,v) via [.rebuild true]. Handed in: Revising.P, read, acceptable (l128, held fixed), executable = Enabled P. It states only that a trace exists; 'Uniform-policy existence is not what a formation chooses.' General: finer_keeps (l95), with premises forget (reading x' s) = reading x s for all s and executable x ⊆ executable x', takes Correctable x to Correctable x'. coarser_opens_nothing (l107), with premises forget (reading x s) = reading x' s and executable x' ⊆ executable x, gives ¬OpensAlong. widened_correctable (l115).
- **cost and keeping**: rebuilt_starved (l175): from one unit, rebuilding leaves (0,true,v) uncorrectable. opens_after_replenishing (l182). opens_by_support (l189): the reading unchanged, the gain is in executability.
- **a registering lost**: unlinked_uncorrectable (l138): link off, no configuration correctable. starved_uncorrectable (l157): link on and no stock, none is.
- **tied to what it rests on**: Only through executability: opening needs a stock unit (linked_correctable l146 at n+1). No statement makes the stock's fall the cause of the revision.

## 21 November, a choice made from a reading: reader keeping-through-what-is-registered
