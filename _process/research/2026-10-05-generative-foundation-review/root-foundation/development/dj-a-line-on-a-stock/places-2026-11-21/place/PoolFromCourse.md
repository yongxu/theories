## 21 November, how a registering comes to be: reader readings-and-the-lock

*As "PoolFromCourse.lean (windowRead, closed_stays_closed, Borne.shapers_left)". A reader's return, unchecked; what the note mended is in the note: [note](../../read-registering-2026-11-21/NOTE.md), [report](../../read-registering-2026-11-21/read-readings-and-the-lock.md.txt).*

- **what registers what**: A pool whose readers of what lies beyond the share are present only where the course leaves them (present: A×B→Prop; ReadsThroughPresent 119).
- **what changes it**: Who reads changes with the course; what each reads is handed in. windowRead (252): readers read the loss as hL while it lies in [N,K), elsewhere off the share as hA. Stands K: loss \< K (256). Handed in: hA, hL, N, K, fB.
- **laws where something comes to be registered**: Readers register the loss from a supplied N and are lost from K by the loss itself. Borne: bear (498) and borneStep (504), with on whom the loss falls handed in (onShapers, onOthers). bear_shaped_shapers (509): the loss adds to the cost c, no premise. borne_by_shapers_favours_iff (524): favours ↔ c+l ≤ s. borne_falls (543): carriers present and the borne rule giving each carrier less than each other; the share falls strictly. Built example shapers_left (753): the lock is left with no reader of the loss. Cause: the loss, falling on what the selection already counts.
- **cost and keeping**: borne_by_others_raises (536): borne by the others, the lock is not left (others_locked 782). window_order_kept (465, monotonicity premises in the report): a share not lower draws no less on what lies beyond, and its readers are gone no later. Built example Drained.strong_undone (660).
- **a registering lost**: stands_not_readmitted (271), premise Accrues. closed_stays_closed (222), premises Raises (liftShare P), ReadsShare, upward favouring, ReadsThroughPresent, NotReadmitted, none present and locked at r. Then none present again and locked at every later step. window_removed_unregistered (332): Accrues and K ≤ loss; the removed readers' reading changes nothing. window_never_read (295): K ≤ N. Not being readmitted is a premise or follows from Accrues.
- **tied to what it rests on**: The readers' standing hangs on the loss. Not derived: 'that readers of what lies beyond a share are removed by what they read, or that any drain grows with a share'.

- **when a reading tells apart, and what can still be kept**: From the account only: window_never_read where K ≤ N, and window_order_kept. Not read as statements.
