- **what registers what**: Readers drawn from the selected: shapers read hi; others read lo below the loss bound N and lo' from N (shapedLossPool 132).
- **what changes it**: The loss crossing a bound handed in. Handed in: N, lo, lo', hi, a, p, c, the schedule reads, and the loss step fB.
- **laws where something comes to be registered**: kept_iff (155): Accrues fB, lo' ≤ hi, reads r, loss ≥ N at r, rule in force favours the shapers. Then locked in their favour from r+1 exactly where x.carrier*c + x.other*c ≤ x.carrier*hi + x.other*lo'. turned_iff (185): Accrues fB, lo' ≤ hi, reads r and r+1, loss ≥ N at r, late reading disfavours at r. Then locked the other way from r+2 exactly where the late reading disfavours at r+1. Cause: the loss accruing past N; that the others read it from N with lo' is the premise. Not derived: 'that the others read the loss at all'; readers who learn are not stated.
- **cost and keeping**: Cost: the counted inequality. Timing: kept_once_favoured (483), kept_past_least (527), deadline (631, premises in the report), in_time_earlier (354), at_most_one_between (397).
- **a registering lost**: Not stated here.
- **tied to what it rests on**: The window: from the first step at which the late reading would favour the shapers, reading the loss keeps the lock. That what lies beyond is a loss to any formation is not stated.
