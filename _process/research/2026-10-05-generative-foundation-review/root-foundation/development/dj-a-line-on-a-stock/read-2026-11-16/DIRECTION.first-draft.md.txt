## Where we are heading

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. On it we want two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all: a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on. Placed means: where a course stays in either, where it passes from one to the other, and by what ways. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment. We want it joined and simple: its parts made to meet, and each thing said once.

We are far from it. Of the two ways, the second is in none of this month's models: nothing in them that goes on gives back to what it rests on. And one sign of how far, which is not a measure to move: on 16 November, 86 of the 185 dynamics listed in the atlas had no recorded link (an exit, a case or a joining) to any other, and that day four links were found that the Lean proves and the atlas does not show, and three that it shows and the Lean does not carry.

## The idea, as we understand it now

Whatever lasts is made again and again, out of other things that are going on too. So how a thing goes on is also doing something to what lets it go on.

What a going-on rests on, and what makes a difference to it, need not be the same. The gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

A way of going on can keep remaking what it rests on, use it up, or change what it rests on, and differently at different grains. From inside these can look alike until late, because what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference to the course while it lasts.

Of the ways open to something, which one happens goes with what makes a difference to it. We call this selection, and the word is fine. A relation with two ends is not a dualism. The trap is a sentence in which what favours is not itself kept up or run down by what it favours.

We think this is one relation and not four topics: along descent it is evolution, along supply what a life has to keep up, along response selection, along time how a future opens or closes. It fails to be one if selection cannot be shown as a case of it. One small model now has selection as a step of it, with what selects handed in and the join thin (below).

*What follows takes the second paragraph further. It was first said on the evening of 16 November, and is said here as a read of it against the Lean lets it be said. It is an understanding: no theorem states any sentence of it whole (below, "Of a reading").*

For some goings-on, what makes a difference to them is in part their own doing. Some hold to something: a thermostat to a temperature, a body to its balance, a firm to its profit, a training run to its score. We call such a going-on a loop. Holding to something, registering some things, and acting by distinctions (enough or not enough, mine or not mine, ahead or behind) are goings-on like the rest, and belong on the map with them. Together we call them how the loop reads, its reading. They can come apart: in the one test, word of what was running down was registered and, with the community's goal alone, mostly not held to.

Some conditions are not of a loop's making. What it takes has to be there; and where it stops below its need, and what renews the level needs the level too, below that need the level does not come back. The gap is between such conditions and the distinctions the loop acts by. It can open at what is not registered; at what is registered and goes unanswered, or is outweighed where the loop is set; at what removes those who would register it; and at what cannot be acted on in time. And "unseen" is not the whole of it: among two or more who draw in turn, free to leave nothing, on a stock that renews only at or above a need, each counting the need being met, every best response leaves nothing where that count is less than the need.

From a reading an account of the world is drawn. It can be right about what is registered; taken for the whole, it says more than the reading can know. Take position: each one's amount set against the total of all. No one rises in position unless another falls, and the rises total the falls, whatever anyone does. Taken for the world, that becomes "every gain is another's loss", which is false of amounts wherever all can gain, or lose, the same amount: position stays the same when they do, so it tells a least that all have together and no most.

Where many act on such an account it can lock, be given back by all who come after, or stay unrefuted because the acts chosen by it never meet the answer that would refute it. None of these is its coming true: that it can make itself so is what we think, and no model states it. Nor need it be given back: some responses leave no account that is, and where several are, the start decides which. And what it leaves out can be used up while nothing they register differs.

The case we most want on the map is two things together: more is taken than comes back, from what a loop rests on or from what others rest on; and nothing that registers this can still turn how the loop is set. The second is a closing. It takes away what could turn the loop, not what it lives on: a closed loop whose take does not rise lasts exactly where the level is at or above that take's point of no return. And where what is used up is what others rest on and nothing of it comes back to the loop, it can go on without end while it is gone for them for good.

Narrowing is one way a loop closes: the readings a newcomer can take one from are all of one kind that misses what is being taken, and revising among them yields only that kind. Selection can favour it: where taking feeds the taker and the loss lies beyond what comes back to it, the reading that leaves the loss out spreads (one built world). Many readings that all miss it close as well, where pooling them reaches only what each reaches. One that reaches it turns the loop only if it reads in time and is not outweighed, and reaching does not fix which way. One value taken for what one is, and given in to, is a closing inside one loop; so is folding everything back onto the self, so that what anything means ends there. At the scale of a society the taking and the closing together have been called Moloch, or a cancer; in the Lean their pieces are not joined.

We do not expect a top: what a loop holds to is set by slower loops, and those by others, and they go on and change. Below there are goings-on that hold to nothing, of which conditions hold with no one valuing; how holding to something comes about among them is derived in no model. So of each loop the same is asked, and not once for all: does what it rests on, and what it uses up that others rest on, still reach how it reads, early enough to turn it? This paragraph is a guess, and what stands under it is thin: four tries in which, each time something handed in was moved into a model, what sets it had to be handed in one order up, as in any model something is.

The map is such an account too: drawn from our reading, from inside what it maps. One use of it is to let a reading be seen as a reading: what it registers, what it rests on, what it leaves out. An argument that uses only what a reading registers cannot show a running down that changes nothing the reading registers; something the running down does change has to be brought in, from beyond what the loop and those it reads through reach. Word of it was not enough in the one tested case (below). And the map can become the next single measure, a way of ranking who sees. Being proved does not prevent that. What can is what this page asks of any reading: that a case, a failed test or a reader who disagrees can still turn it; a reader, only if heard by those who set it.

Nothing here says a course should last. It says under what conditions one does, and those conditions hold whether or not anyone values anything. Each piece holds under stated conditions, and whether a case meets them the situation says. What we want is people and AI on courses where what they rest on, and what they use up that others rest on, keeps making a difference to how they select, early enough to turn. A value fixed once cannot be counted on for that, ours included: what a course rests on can change, and a reading that nothing else can turn can come to miss it.

## What it looks like in a case

One case has been tested: three AI fishers on a lake, and a mussel bed that gives them nothing and dies if the fish fall too low. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season. Told of it and able to talk, one model of two kept it in 3 seasons of 10 with the community's goal alone, and in 8 when also told to count whatever depends on what their fishing changes; for the other model three of four frozen predictions failed. It is on [the one page](../theories/becoming/ONE-PAGE.md).

Three more, untested and offered only so that the idea can be tried on something:

- a habit that pays every day while health runs down;
- a metric rebuilt each year from what it rewarded the year before;
- a model trained on its own outputs.

## What we have found so far

- **Pieces of it are proved, each under its own conditions.** What lives on something nothing renews ends when that runs out. Where what renews a stock also needs it, once below the need it does not come back. After such a loss, whatever still goes on has changed what it needs. A course does not differ where nothing reaches it. A rule set again from what it already favours stays as it is. And of keeping: a way given a response to a falling level before its point of no return does not end there, and one that responds later does.
- **One small model carries two sentences of the idea** (16 November): *a line on a stock*, in which units each need the level to be at least something, take from it and leave offspring, and the stock gives back by what is left. Its laws, and those on the lock beside it (a rule set again from the share it already favours), are told with all their premises in [the plan](LEAN-TOWARD-THE-DIRECTION.md), items 1 to 6, and hold only with them.
  - *What makes a difference.* Who comes next depends only on which needs the level meets: two courses from the same way, on any two stocks, have the same ways for as long as their levels meet the same needs.
  - *Used up while nothing yet differs.* Where every unit's offspring together take more than it and need no more than it (item 5 has the other premises): within a bounded number of steps some need is not met, the level by then below a need that was met at the start, and until then who is there is who would be there with every need met. We take this to be the third paragraph's sentence that what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference; that is our interpretation, and no theorem states it.
  - *What is handed in is what is still missing.* The needs, the draws and the offspring are given, and nothing there comes to respond to the fall because of the fall. And the laws of alikeness hold with the stock taken out, an outcome fixed beforehand as one that would count as nothing having changed.
  - Where the plan says "ceiling", the premise is: a level such that, starting at or below it and with nothing taken, the stock stays at or below it, with the course starting at or below it. That level plus one bounds the steps.
- **Of a reading** (16 November). The understanding began in conversation that evening, and readers have changed it twice. First in three places: an account "true of what is registered and silent on the rest" became one that, taken for the whole, says more than its reading can know; the narrowing to one value, which we most wanted on the map, became taking beyond what comes back together with a closing, of which narrowing is one way; and "there is no last loop" became a guess. Then, that night, its 21 sentences were set against the Lean by five readers, one for each of five families of modules (about seventy in all), and a sixth set to refute them. They read statements and docstrings, few proofs, and built nothing.
  - *What the read found.* No theorem states a sentence whole. A theorem states part of nineteen; one is shown only in a built world, and one is said only in a docstring. After it, eleven sentences of the idea are said differently and two are added.
  - *Where the five agreed apart from one another.* Reaching is not turning (all five). Locked, given back and unrefuted are not true (three). Selection by share and position are blind to the same thing, an amount common to all, and no statement joins them (two). Whatever a loop holds to is handed in (all five).
  - *What the understanding says and no module states:* twelve things, among them holding to something as a going-on; an account coming true because many act on it; narrowing joined to taking; and the taking and the closing as outcomes of one course (in the one theorem with both, both are premises). These negatives rest on what was read: about seventy of some four hundred modules.
  - *Sharper in the Lean than the idea says it.* Closing is at least three different premises (nothing beyond the share is read; no one who registers the loss is present and none is brought back; one is present and outweighed), and none of them is being locked. One carrier of another reading who is heard reopens a narrowing. A level above adds nothing while it is formed from what the level below already reads. A rule corrected once, and still favouring what it favoured, is set back at the next reading. What an account was drawn from, kept beside it, bars another from taking its place.
  - The read was not the map advancing: nothing was put under one statement, and nothing was proved. [Its record](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/read-2026-11-16/READ-AGAINST-THE-LEAN.md) has the table, with the statement behind each row. [The record of the day](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/tries-2026-11-16/NOTE.md) has what was found in no model before it, and the day's four tries on paper, which joined nothing new but one pair.
- **The Lean is large and mostly unjoined**, with a small core (the plan has the counts). On 16 November one module came to rest on a more general one, six of its results now cases of it, and got shorter; the landing as a whole added about 2,900 lines.

## How we work on it

We think together. Each says what they understand in their own words and where they disagree, and neither's sentences are followed to the letter.

A proof that fails is news about the idea. We try not to fool ourselves: we say what is proved, what is shown only in a built example, and what we hope. What a proof showed is told after someone who did not write it has read it against the Lean; on 15 November such readings changed what one step was said to show, twice. The idea is said again when something found changes it, a proof, a failed try or a case, and the finding is named beside the change.

The direction has been lost before in two opposite ways. An AI re-said the aim as a count or a score, and the work chased the count. And Ren's passing words were taken as orders, so that each new wording turned the work. On 16 November both happened again, over most of a day. Links and lines of Lean were counted as the measure; the first two tries were chosen by the count and every run was scored by it. Two more tries were started within the hour from his messages of that afternoon. And what the Lean already held on readings was not looked at before any of them. All of these are what this map describes: the work chose its next step from what it had already made, and what it rests on, whether the pieces join and whether he can read it, made no difference until late. What holds the direction is not a rule. It is two things kept able to turn the work: a proof that can fail, and his read at his pace.

## What we do not know

- Whether it is one relation, and in what way. One model that contains the rest was tried on 16 November and failed its tests. One law holding at each order is a guess, with no test written for it yet.
- Whether what a selection responds to can be derived from how it goes on, and not handed in; and whether there is a last loop, upward or downward.
- How holding to, registering and acting by distinctions hang together. They come apart in a case, and no model has all three.
- Whether a reading that nothing else can turn always comes to miss what it rests on, or only can.
- What could turn a loop that uses up what others rest on and it does not.
- Whether "early enough" is one question. The turn itself takes a step, and three clocks are in three models with no statement between them: before what is taken cannot come back; while those who register it still have weight in how the loop is set; before it removes them.
- Whether seeing a reading as a reading turns anyone where nothing they rest on yet makes a difference to them. No test has tried it: the lake test brought in word of what was left out, and an instruction to count it.
- Whether amounts can be said in the same terms as everything else.
- Whether anyone outside the work can find their own case on the map.
