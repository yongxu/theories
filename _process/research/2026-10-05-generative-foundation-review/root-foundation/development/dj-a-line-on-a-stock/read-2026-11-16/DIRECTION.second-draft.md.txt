## Where we are heading

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. On it we want two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all: a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on. Placed means: where, and at which grain, a course is doing the one or the other, where it passes between them, and by what ways. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment. We want it joined and simple: its parts made to meet, and each thing said once.

We are far from it. Of the two ways, the second is in none of this month's models: nothing in them that goes on gives back to what it rests on. And one sign of how far, which is not a measure to move: on 16 November, 86 of the 185 dynamics listed in the atlas had no recorded link (an exit, a case or a joining) to any other; and that day the atlas was found wrong both ways, lacking links the Lean proves (the read's record lists six) and showing three the Lean does not carry.

## The idea, as we understand it now

Whatever lasts is made again and again, out of other things that are going on too. So how a thing goes on is also doing something to what lets it go on.

What a going-on rests on, and what makes a difference to it, need not be the same. The gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

A way of going on can keep remaking what it rests on, use it up, or change what it rests on, and differently at different grains. From inside these can look alike until late, because what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference to the course while it lasts.

Of the ways open to something, which one happens goes with what makes a difference to it. We call this selection, and the word is fine. A relation with two ends is not a dualism. The trap is a sentence in which what favours is not itself kept up or run down by what it favours.

We think this is one relation and not four topics: along descent it is evolution, along supply what a life has to keep up, along response selection, along time how a future opens or closes. It fails to be one if selection cannot be shown as a case of it. One small model now has selection as a step of it, with what selects handed in and the join thin (below).

*The next eight paragraphs take the second paragraph further. They are our understanding, first said on the evening of 16 November and changed where a read against the Lean showed it wrong or loose. No theorem states any sentence of them whole (below, "Of a reading").*

For some goings-on, what makes a difference to them is in part their own doing. Some hold to something: a thermostat to a temperature, a body to its balance, a firm to its profit, a training run to its score. We call such a going-on a loop. Holding to something, registering some things, and acting by distinctions (enough or not enough, mine or not mine, ahead or behind) are goings-on like the rest, and belong on the map with them. Together we call them how the loop reads, its reading. They can come apart: in the one test, word of what was running down was registered and, with the community's goal alone, mostly not held to.

Some conditions hold of a loop whether or not it draws any distinction there, though what it does moves where it stands under them. What it takes has to be there. And where what it takes from is renewed only by what needs it too and gives only while that need is met, once below that need it does not come back. The gap is between such conditions and the distinctions the loop acts by. It can open at what is not registered; at what is registered and not held to, or is outweighed among those who set the loop; at what removes those who would register it; and at what cannot be acted on in time. And not every running down is unseen: where each of several counts keeping what all need for less than taking it brings, they can drain it in full view (one model, below).

From a reading an account of the world is drawn. It can be right about what is registered; taken for the whole, it says more than the reading can know. Where position is all that is held to and registered (each one's amount set against the total of all): no one rises unless another falls, and the rises total the falls, whatever anyone does. Taken for the world, that becomes "every gain is another's loss", which is false of amounts wherever all can gain, or lose, the same amount: position does not move when they do.

Where many act on such an account, we think it can make itself so in one respect: what they do comes to fit it. How they select is set again from what it already favours and stays so; those who come after are handed it as it stands; the acts it chooses are answered as it predicts. It does not thereby come to hold of what it was about: it can be handed on for ever and leave out the very thing it was drawn about. Nor need it be handed on: some responses hand no account back. And what it leaves out can be used up while nothing they register differs.

The case we most want on the map is two things together: more is taken than comes back, from what a loop rests on or from what others rest on; and nothing that registers this can still turn how the loop is set. The second is a closing. It takes away what could turn the loop, not what it lives on: a closed loop can go on without end where what it takes keeps being met. And where what is used up is what others rest on and nothing of it comes back to the loop, it can go on without end while it is gone for them for good.

Narrowing is one way a loop closes: everyone a newcomer can take a reading from reads in a way that misses what is being taken, and revising among those ways gives only more of them. Selection can favour a reading that misses it (one built world). Many readings that all miss it close as well, where pooling them reaches only what each reaches. One that reaches it turns the loop only if it reads in time and is not outweighed, and reaching does not fix which way. We think a loop can close from inside too: one value taken for what the loop is, so that whatever would revise it is weighed by it; or meaning taken to end at the self, so that nothing from beyond can turn it. Neither is in a model. At the scale of a society the taking and the closing together have been called Moloch, or a cancer; in the Lean their pieces are not joined (below).

We do not expect a top: what a loop holds to is set by slower loops, and those by others, and they go on and change. There are also goings-on that hold to nothing, of which the conditions hold too, with no one valuing; how holding to something comes about among them is derived in no model. So of each loop the same is asked, and not once for all: does what it rests on, and what it uses up that others rest on, still reach how it reads, early enough to turn it? This paragraph is a guess, and what stands under it is thin: four tries in which, each time something handed in was moved into a model, what sets it had to be handed in one order up, as in any model something is.

The map is such an account too: drawn from our reading, from inside what it maps. One use of it is to let a reading be seen as a reading: what it registers, what it rests on, what it leaves out. An argument that uses only what a reading registers cannot show a running down that changes nothing the reading registers; something the running down does change has to be brought in, from beyond what the loop and those it reads through reach. Word of it was not enough in the one tested case (below). And the map can become the next single measure, a way of ranking who sees. Being proved does not prevent that. What can is what this page asks of any reading: that a case, a failed test or a reader who disagrees can still turn it; a reader, only if heard by those who set it.

Nothing here says a course should last. It says under what conditions one does, and those conditions hold whether or not anyone values anything. Each piece holds under stated conditions, and whether a case meets them the situation says. What we want is people and AI on courses where what they rest on, and what they use up that others rest on, keeps making a difference to how they select, early enough to turn. A value fixed once cannot be counted on for that, ours included: what a course rests on can change, and a reading that nothing else can turn can come to miss it.

## What it looks like in a case

One case has been tested: three AI fishers on a lake, and a mussel bed that gives them nothing and dies if the fish fall too low. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season. Told of it and able to talk, one model of two kept it in 3 seasons of 10 with the community's goal alone, and in 8 when also told to count whatever depends on what their fishing changes; for the other model three of four frozen predictions failed. It is on [the one page](../theories/becoming/ONE-PAGE.md).

Three more, untested and offered only so that the idea can be tried on something:

- a habit that pays every day while health runs down;
- a metric rebuilt each year from what it rewarded the year before;
- a model trained on its own outputs.

## What we have found so far

- **Pieces of it are proved, each under its own conditions.** What lives on something nothing renews ends when that runs out. Where what renews a stock also needs it, once below the need it does not come back. After such a loss, whatever still goes on has changed what it needs. A course does not differ where nothing reaches it. A rule set again from what it already favours stays as it is. And of keeping: a way whose response to a falling level comes more than one step before the first step from which no turn keeps its need met goes on without end, and one whose response comes later ends.
- **Two sentences of the idea are read onto theorems of one small model** (16 November): *a line on a stock*, in which units each need the level to be at least something, take from it and leave offspring, and the stock gives back by what is left. Its laws, and those on the lock beside it (a rule set again from the share it already favours), are told with all their premises in [the plan](LEAN-TOWARD-THE-DIRECTION.md), items 1 to 6, and hold only with them.
  - *What makes a difference.* Who comes next depends only on which needs the level meets: two courses from the same way, at any two levels and on any two stocks, have the same ways for as long as their levels meet the same needs.
  - *Used up while nothing yet differs.* Given a level that the stock, starting at or below it and with nothing taken, stays at or below (the plan's "ceiling"), a start at or below it, and that every unit's offspring together take more than it (the plan's item 5, "What is favoured draws more", has the others): within that level plus one steps some need is not met, and until then who is there is who would be there with every need met. Where offspring also need no more than their parents, the level by then is below a need that was met at the start. We take this to be the third paragraph's sentence that what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference; that is our interpretation, and no theorem states it.
  - *What is still missing there.* The needs, the draws and the offspring are given, and nothing comes to respond to the fall because of the fall. And the laws of alikeness hold with the stock taken out, an outcome fixed beforehand as one that would count as nothing having changed.
- **Of a reading** (16 November). The understanding began in conversation that evening, and readers have changed it twice. First in three places: an account "true of what is registered and silent on the rest" became one that, taken for the whole, says more than its reading can know; the narrowing to one value, which we most wanted on the map, became taking beyond what comes back together with a closing, of which narrowing is one way; and "there is no last loop" became a guess. Then, that night, its 21 sentences were set against the Lean by five readers, each with one family of modules, and a sixth set to refute them. They read statements and docstrings, few proofs, and built nothing.
  - *What the read found.* After it, and after three readers of this page, twelve sentences of the idea are said differently and two are added. Four changes touch what he said was what he meant. An account many act on is now said to make itself so in what they do, and not thereby in what it was about; and the Lean's three ways of staying are each of one rule, one line of cohorts or one loop, never of many acting on an account. Position is each one's amount against the total of all, not who is ahead of whom, an order none of the modules read has. A reading that reaches what is being taken turns the loop only if it also reads in time and is not outweighed. And not every running down is unseen.
  - *What the understanding says and no module states:* twelve things, among them holding to something as a going-on (every held-to is handed in, but for two exceptions the list of changes names); an account that comes true because many act on it; narrowing joined to taking; and the taking and the closing as outcomes of one course (in the one theorem with both, both are premises). Nor is the lock joined to a growth confined within a body that ends with it, or to a selection that rebuilds what selects it across changes of who carries it, which has an atlas entry and no declaration. These negatives rest on what was read: about seventy of some four hundred modules.
  - *Sharper in the Lean than the idea says it* (the list of changes has each law with its premises, and [the read's record](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/read-2026-11-16/READ-AGAINST-THE-LEAN.md) the table). Draining in full view is proved of two or more who draw in turn, over a set number of steps and free to leave nothing, from a stock that renews only at or above a need: where what each counts for a step with the need met is less than what must be left to meet it, every profile of mutual best responses leaves nothing. The law nearest "a closed loop can go on" holds a take at one amount and finds one level from which the line never ends, and below which it ends; that level is a line we draw for a take we hold still. Closing is at least three different premises (nothing beyond the share is read; no one who registers the loss is present and none is brought back; one is present and outweighed), none of them being locked; and in one built course a lock is left with no one registering, a loss on the favoured share lowering it.
  - The read was not the map advancing: nothing was put under one statement, and nothing was proved. [The record of the day](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/tries-2026-11-16/NOTE.md) has what was found in no model before it, and the day's four tries on paper, which joined nothing new but one pair.
- **The Lean is large and mostly unjoined**, with a small core (the plan has the counts). On 16 November one module came to rest on a more general one, six of its results now cases of it, and got shorter; the landing as a whole added about 2,900 lines.

## How we work on it

We think together. Each says what they understand in their own words and where they disagree, and neither's sentences are followed to the letter.

A proof that fails is news about the idea. We try not to fool ourselves: we say what is proved, what is shown only in a built example, and what we hope. What a proof showed is told after someone who did not write it has read it against the Lean; on 15 November such readings changed what one step was said to show, twice. The idea is said again when something found changes it, a proof, a failed try or a case, and the finding is named beside the change.

The direction has been lost before in two opposite ways. An AI re-said the aim as a count or a score, and the work chased the count. And Ren's passing words were taken as orders, so that each new wording turned the work. On 16 November both happened again, over most of a day. Links and lines of Lean were counted as the measure; the first two tries were chosen by the count and every run was scored by it. Two more tries were started within the hour from his messages of that afternoon. And what the Lean already held on readings was not looked at before any of them. All of these are what this map describes: the work chose its next step from what it had already made, and what it rests on, whether the pieces join and whether he can read it, made no difference until late. What holds the direction is not a rule. It is two things kept able to turn the work: a proof that can fail, and his read at his pace.

## What we do not know

- Whether it is one relation, and in what way. One model that contains the rest was tried on 16 November and failed its tests. One law holding at each order is a guess, with no test written for it yet.
- Whether what a selection responds to can be derived from how it goes on, and not handed in; and whether there is a last loop, upward or downward.
- How holding to, registering and acting by distinctions hang together. They come apart in a case, and no model has all three.
- Whether a reading that nothing else can turn always comes to miss what it rests on, or only can.
- What could turn a loop that uses up what others rest on and it does not.
- Whether "early enough" is one question, as what we want says it above. The turn itself takes a step, and three clocks are in three models with no statement between them: before what is taken cannot come back; while those who register it still have weight in how the loop is set; before it removes them.
- Whether seeing a reading as a reading turns anyone where nothing they rest on yet makes a difference to them. No test has tried it: the lake test brought in word of what was left out, and an instruction to count it.
- Whether amounts can be said in the same terms as everything else.
- Whether anyone outside the work can find their own case on the map.
