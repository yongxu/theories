## The sentences

Taken from the five paragraphs of the idea that follow "Since the evening of 16 November", and the paragraph after them. Shortened only where the page gives an example.

- **S1.** For some goings-on, what makes a difference to them is in part their own doing: they hold to something, register some things, and act by distinctions.
- **S2.** Holding to something, registering, and acting by distinctions are goings-on like the rest.
- **S3.** The three can come apart: something registered and not held to.
- **S4.** Some conditions hold of a loop whether or not it draws any distinction there: below its need it stops, and below some level what it lives on does not come back.
- **S5.** The gap between what a loop rests on and what makes a difference to it can open in three places: at what is not registered, at what is registered and not held to, and at what is held to and cannot be acted on in time.
- **S6.** From a reading an account of the world is drawn. It can be right about what is registered; taken for the whole, it says more than the reading can know.
- **S7.** Where only who is ahead of whom is registered: no one rises in position unless another falls, and the rises total the falls, whatever anyone does. Taken for the world this becomes "every gain is another's loss", which is false wherever what all live on can grow, or shrink, for all.
- **S8.** Where many act on such an account it can make itself so, and what it leaves out can be used up while nothing they register differs.
- **S9.** It need not: an account acted on comes true only where the response to it is the account again, and some defeat themselves.
- **S10.** The case most wanted on the map is two things together: more is taken than comes back, from what a loop rests on or from what others rest on; and nothing that registers this can still turn how the loop is set (a closing).
- **S11.** A closed loop that takes within what comes back lasts.
- **S12.** Where what is used up is what others rest on and the loop does not, it can go on without end while they lose it for good.
- **S13.** Narrowing is one way a loop closes: everyone with a say in how it is set reads by one reading that misses what is being taken, and whoever comes new takes a reading from those already there.
- **S14.** Many readings that all miss it close as well; one reading that reaches it can turn in time.
- **S15.** One value taken for what one is, and given in to, is a closing inside one loop; so is folding everything back onto the self.
- **S16.** No top is expected: what a loop holds to is set by slower loops, and those by others.
- **S17.** Below there are goings-on that hold to nothing, of which conditions hold with no one valuing; how holding to something comes about among them is derived in no model.
- **S18.** Of each loop the same is asked: does what it rests on, and what it uses up that others rest on, still reach how it reads, early enough to turn it?
- **S19.** An argument that uses only what a reading registers cannot show what is running down; something has to be brought in.
- **S20.** An account can become the next single measure. What keeps it from closing is that a case, a failed test or a reader who disagrees can still turn it.
- **S21.** A value fixed once cannot be counted on: what a course rests on can change, and a reading that nothing else can turn can come to miss it.

## What counts (frozen now)

For each sentence, one grade:

1. *A theorem states it:* a Lean statement, with its premises, says the sentence in the model's own terms.
2. *A theorem states part of it:* with the part named, and what is handed in named.
3. *One built world shows it:* a worked example, not a law.
4. *A docstring says it:* no statement does.
5. *Not there.*
6. *A statement speaks against it:* a law or a kept countermodel under which the sentence as written fails.

A link between two families, or between a family and this month's models, counts only by the four conditions written down in the day's record of links (`tries-2026-11-16/LINKS-ON-PAPER.md`, "What counts as a link"): one statement in both sides' own definitions, not a renaming, not a shared word.

**The read has changed something only if** at least one sentence has to be said differently because the Lean is sharper than it or against it, or at least one link is found that meets the four conditions and is not already in the atlas. Otherwise it is a table: useful to have, and not the map advancing.
