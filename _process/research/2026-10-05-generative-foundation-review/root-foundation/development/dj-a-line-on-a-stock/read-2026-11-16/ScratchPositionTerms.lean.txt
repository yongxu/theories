/-!
# What the selection term reads of offspring numbers is their positions

AI-written (Fable), 17 November 2026. Scratch; not on main.

`RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w'` says that two profiles of amounts give every listed member the same
position (its amount counted once for each listed member, against the total).
`SelectionTerm.concord` and `SelectionTerm.discord` are the two halves of the selection term proper,
totals over ordered pairs of listed members. Nothing is defined here but two helpers of the proofs
(`oneAt`, `apart`); both laws are stated in the two modules' own definitions.

## What is proved

* `same_terms_of_keeps` (left to right, no decidable equality): profiles giving every listed member
  the same position have the same concordance and the same discordance with every amount.
* `keeps_at_of_terms` (decidable equality): for one listed member `p`, the same concordance and the
  same discordance with the amount that is one at `p` and nothing elsewhere give `p` the same
  position. This is the hand outline's right-to-left half, member by member.
* `keeps_iff_same_terms`: the statement as it was written on paper, with decidable equality, proved
  by that outline.
* `keeps_of_own_concord`, `keeps_iff_own_concord` (no decidable equality, no discordance, two
  amounts): the profiles give every listed member the same position exactly where each profile has
  the same concordance with `w` as the other has, and the same with `w'`.
* `keeps_iff_terms`: the statement as written with the instance removed.
* `concord_shifted_as_case`, `discord_shifted_as_case`: `SelectionShift.concord_shifted` and
  `discord_shifted`, their statements word for word, from `keeps_iff_terms` at
  `RelativePosition.keeps_add`. They need three congruences on the list beside the law
  (`keeps_of_shifted`, `concord_amount_on`, `discord_amount_on`), so no line falls.
* `kept_step`, `shift_step_terms_as_case`: read on a step of `SelectionTerm.Generations`, the same
  family, amounts alike and offspring numbers with the same positions give the same concordance and
  discordance at the step; the first two conjuncts of `SelectionShift.Generations.shift_step` are its
  case. Left to right only: a course has one amount at a step.
* `keeps_order`: the same positions keep which of two listed members has more.

## Kept witnesses

* `TwoProfiles.same_order_apart`: two profiles with the same order and not the same positions.
* `TwoProfiles.one_amount_not_enough`: two profiles with the same terms with one amount and not the
  same positions (`uniform_amount_same_terms`: with a uniform amount every two profiles have the same
  terms).

The laws hold for every list: an empty one, one member, members listed more than once. They are laws
of one family at one step. They do not say what the next generation's size is, which the common
amount sets (`SelectionShift.Generations.shift_step`), and they say nothing of a course of
generations, where a term positive at unboundedly many steps does force every generation to have a
member (`SelectionShift.Generations.never_empties_of_unbounded`). Of order they say only
`keeps_order`.

Every declaration has an empty axiom report.
-/

/-! ## Arithmetic -/

/-- Where `x` with `y'` is `y` with `x'` (the premise `common`: `x'` differs from `x` by what `y'`
differs from `y`), `x` exceeds `y` by what `x'` exceeds `y'`. Rests on `excess_add_right`. -/
theorem excess_of_common {x y x' y' : Nat} (common : x + y' = y + x') :
    excess x y = excess x' y' :=
  (excess_add_right x y y').symm.trans
    ((congrArg (fun k => excess k (y + y')) common).trans
      ((congr (congrArg excess (Nat.add_comm y x')) (Nat.add_comm y y')).trans
        (excess_add_right x' y' y)))

/-- The converse. Where `x` exceeds `y` by what `x'` exceeds `y'` (`forward`) and `y` exceeds `x` by
what `y'` exceeds `x'` (`backward`), `x` with `y'` is `y` with `x'`. Rests on `excess_balance`. -/
theorem common_of_excess {x y x' y' : Nat} (forward : excess x y = excess x' y')
    (backward : excess y x = excess y' x') : x + y' = y + x' :=
  Becoming.add_right_cancel (excess y x + excess x' y')
    ((Nat.add_add_add_comm x y' (excess y x) (excess x' y')).trans
      ((Naturals.add_congr (excess_balance x y) (excess_balance y' x')).trans
        ((Nat.add_add_add_comm y (excess x y) x' (excess y' x')).trans
          (congrArg (y + x' + ·)
            ((Naturals.add_congr forward backward.symm).trans (Nat.add_comm _ _))))))

/-- Two balances with the same `c` and the same `d` trade their first terms: from `x + d = t + c`
(`first`), `x' + d' = t' + c'` (`second`), `c = c'` and `d = d'`, `x' + t = x + t'`. Addition of
natural numbers only. -/
theorem trade_of_balances {x x' t t' c d c' d' : Nat} (first : x + d = t + c)
    (second : x' + d' = t' + c') (concordant : c = c') (discordant : d = d') : x' + t = x + t' :=
  Becoming.add_right_cancel (d + c)
    ((Nat.add_add_add_comm x' t d c).trans
      (((Naturals.add_congr (congrArg (x' + ·) discordant) first.symm).trans
          ((congrArg (· + (x + d)) (second.trans (congrArg (t' + ·) concordant.symm))).trans
            (Nat.add_comm (t' + c) (x + d)))).trans
        (Nat.add_add_add_comm x d t' c)))

/-! ## Left to right -/

/-- **Profiles with the same positions have the same terms.** Premise `keeps`: the profiles `w` and
`w'` give every listed member the same position. Then with every amount `a` the concordance of `w`
is the concordance of `w'`, and the discordance of `w` is the discordance of `w'`. No decidable
equality is used. Rests on `RelativePosition.keeps_iff_common` (the two profiles differ at every
listed member by one common amount) and `excess_of_common` (so no excess of one listed member's
weight over another's changes). -/
theorem same_terms_of_keeps {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat}
    (keeps : RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w') (a : α → Nat) :
    SelectionTerm.concord ps w a = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' a ∧
      SelectionTerm.discord ps w a = SelectionTerm.discord ps w' a :=
  ⟨total_congr_on fun p mp => total_congr_on fun q mq =>
      congrArg (· * excess (a p) (a q)) (excess_of_common (keeps_iff_common.mp keeps p q mp mq)),
   total_congr_on fun p mp => total_congr_on fun q mq =>
      congrArg (· * excess (a q) (a p)) (excess_of_common (keeps_iff_common.mp keeps p q mp mq))⟩

/-! ## Right to left, by the amount that is one at a member -/

/-- The amount that is one at `p` and nothing at every other member. A helper of the proofs below;
it needs equality of members decided. -/
def oneAt [DecidableEq α] (p q : α) : Nat := mark (q = p)

/-- A weight times the amount that is one at `p` is `p`'s weight times that amount, at every
member. Rests on `mark_cases`. -/
theorem mul_oneAt [DecidableEq α] (w : α → Nat) (p q : α) : w q * oneAt p q = w p * oneAt p q :=
  mark_cases (p := q = p) (q := fun m => w q * m = w p * m)
    (fun same => congrArg (fun r => w r * 1) same)
    (fun _ => (Nat.mul_zero (w q)).trans (Nat.mul_zero (w p)).symm)

/-- A listed member is listed at least once: over a list with `p` in it (`mem`), the amount that is
one at `p` totals more than nothing. The total is how often `p` is listed. Rests on `mark_pos` and
`le_total_of_mem`. -/
theorem listed_pos [DecidableEq α] {p : α} {ps : List α} (mem : p ∈ ps) :
    0 < total ps (oneAt p) :=
  Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (show 0 < oneAt p p from (mark_pos (p := p = p)).mpr rfl)
    (le_total_of_mem (q := oneAt p) mem)

/-- The weighted total of the amount that is one at `p` is `p`'s weight, as often as `p` is
listed. Rests on `mul_oneAt` and `total_mul`. -/
theorem weighted_oneAt [DecidableEq α] (ps : List α) (w : α → Nat) (p : α) :
    weighted ps w (oneAt p) = w p * total ps (oneAt p) :=
  show total ps (fun q => w q * oneAt p q) = w p * total ps (oneAt p) from
    (total_congr (fun q => mul_oneAt w p q) ps).trans (total_mul (w p) (oneAt p) ps)

/-- `SelectionTerm.covariation` at the amount that is one at `p`: the number of listed members times
`p`'s weight, as often as `p` is listed, with the discordance, is the total weight as often, with
the concordance. No premise. Rests on `covariation` and `weighted_oneAt`. -/
theorem covariation_oneAt [DecidableEq α] (ps : List α) (w : α → Nat) (p : α) :
    ps.length * w p * total ps (oneAt p) + discord ps w (oneAt p) =
      total ps w * total ps (oneAt p) + concord ps w (oneAt p) :=
  (congrArg (· + discord ps w (oneAt p))
      ((mul_assoc_nat ps.length (w p) (total ps (oneAt p))).trans
        (congrArg (ps.length * ·) (weighted_oneAt ps w p).symm))).trans
    (covariation ps w (oneAt p))

/-- **The terms with one amount fix one member's position.** Premises: `p` is listed (`mem`); the
profiles `w` and `w'` have the same concordance (`concordant`) and the same discordance
(`discordant`) with the amount that is one at `p` and nothing elsewhere. Then `p`'s position is the
same under both: its rise count is its fall count. Members may be listed more than once. Equality of
members is decided. Rests on `covariation_oneAt` for each profile, `trade_of_balances`, and
cancelling how often `p` is listed (`listed_pos`). -/
theorem keeps_at_of_terms [DecidableEq α] {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat} {p : α} (mem : p ∈ ps)
    (concordant : concord ps w (oneAt p) = concord ps w' (oneAt p))
    (discordant : discord ps w (oneAt p) = discord ps w' (oneAt p)) :
    riseCount ps w w' p = fallCount ps w w' p :=
  show ps.length * w' p + total ps w = ps.length * w p + total ps w' from
    Nat.eq_of_mul_eq_mul_right (listed_pos mem)
      ((add_mul_split (ps.length * w' p) (total ps w) (total ps (oneAt p))).trans
        ((trade_of_balances (covariation_oneAt ps w p) (covariation_oneAt ps w' p)
            concordant discordant).trans
          (add_mul_split (ps.length * w p) (total ps w') (total ps (oneAt p))).symm))

/-- **The statement as it was written on paper.** With equality of members decided: two profiles
give every listed member the same position exactly where, with every amount, they have the same
concordance and the same discordance. No premise on the list: it may be empty, hold one member, or
list members more than once. Left to right is `same_terms_of_keeps`; right to left is
`keeps_at_of_terms` at each listed member, which uses the terms only with the amounts that are one
at a listed member and nothing elsewhere. -/
theorem keeps_iff_same_terms [DecidableEq α] {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat} :
    RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w' ↔ ∀ a : α → Nat,
      SelectionTerm.concord ps w a = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' a ∧
        SelectionTerm.discord ps w a = SelectionTerm.discord ps w' a :=
  ⟨same_terms_of_keeps, fun same p mem =>
    keeps_at_of_terms mem (same (oneAt p)).1 (same (oneAt p)).2⟩

/-! ## Right to left, by the profiles themselves: no decidable equality -/

/-- By how much two numbers are apart, squared: the square of the excess of each over the other,
one of which is nothing. A helper of the proofs below. -/
def apart (x y : Nat) : Nat := excess x y * excess x y + excess y x * excess y x

/-- The two squares are the two crossed products with the squared difference. No premise. It is
`SelectionTerm.pair_balance` read at one pair twice, with `Reproduction.excess_cross`. -/
theorem square_balance (x y : Nat) : x * x + y * y = x * y + y * x + apart x y :=
  have none : discordPair x y x y = 0 :=
    Naturals.add_congr (Reproduction.excess_cross x y) (Reproduction.excess_cross y x)
  (Nat.add_zero _).symm.trans ((congrArg (x * x + y * y + ·) none.symm).trans (pair_balance x y x y))

/-- A number whose square is at most nothing is nothing. -/
theorem zero_of_square_le : ∀ {e : Nat}, e * e ≤ 0 → e = 0
  | 0, _ => rfl
  | e + 1, le => absurd le (Nat.not_le_of_gt (Nat.mul_pos (Nat.succ_pos e) (Nat.succ_pos e)))

/-- Numbers nothing apart are equal. Premise `zero`: their squared difference is nothing. Rests on
`Reproduction.le_of_excess_zero`. -/
theorem eq_of_apart_zero {x y : Nat} (zero : apart x y = 0) : x = y :=
  Nat.le_antisymm
    (Reproduction.le_of_excess_zero
      (zero_of_square_le (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_right _ _) (Nat.le_of_eq zero))))
    (Reproduction.le_of_excess_zero
      (zero_of_square_le (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_left _ _) (Nat.le_of_eq zero))))

/-- **Two readings of ordered pairs with the same squares and products agree on every pair.**
Premises, each a total over the ordered pairs of listed members: the squares of `F` total the
products of `G` with `F` (`first`); the products of `F` with `G` total the squares of `G`
(`second`). Then `F` and `G` agree at every pair of listed members. Rests on `square_balance` at
each pair, totalled (`SelectionTerm.pairs_add`): the squared differences total nothing, so each is
nothing (`le_total_of_mem`). -/
theorem pairs_agree (ps : List α) (F G : α → α → Nat)
    (first : (total ps fun p => total ps fun q => F p q * F p q) =
      total ps fun p => total ps fun q => G p q * F p q)
    (second : (total ps fun p => total ps fun q => F p q * G p q) =
      total ps fun p => total ps fun q => G p q * G p q) :
    ∀ p q, p ∈ ps → q ∈ ps → F p q = G p q :=
  have summed :
      (total ps fun p => total ps fun q => F p q * F p q) +
          (total ps fun p => total ps fun q => G p q * G p q) =
        ((total ps fun p => total ps fun q => F p q * G p q) +
            total ps fun p => total ps fun q => G p q * F p q) +
          total ps fun p => total ps fun q => apart (F p q) (G p q) :=
    (pairs_add ps (fun p q => F p q * F p q) (fun p q => G p q * G p q)).symm.trans
      ((total_congr (fun p => total_congr (fun q => square_balance (F p q) (G p q)) ps) ps).trans
        ((pairs_add ps (fun p q => F p q * G p q + G p q * F p q)
            (fun p q => apart (F p q) (G p q))).trans
          (congrArg (· + total ps fun p => total ps fun q => apart (F p q) (G p q))
            (pairs_add ps (fun p q => F p q * G p q) (fun p q => G p q * F p q)))))
  have zero : (total ps fun p => total ps fun q => apart (F p q) (G p q)) = 0 :=
    Naturals.add_left_cancel
      (summed.symm.trans ((Naturals.add_congr first second.symm).trans
        ((Nat.add_comm _ _).trans (Nat.add_zero _).symm)))
  fun p q mp mq => eq_of_apart_zero (Nat.le_antisymm
    (Nat.le_trans (le_total_of_mem (q := fun q' => apart (F p q') (G p q')) (a := q) mq)
      (Nat.le_trans
        (le_total_of_mem (q := fun p' => total ps fun q' => apart (F p' q') (G p' q')) mp)
        (Nat.le_of_eq zero)))
    (Nat.zero_le _))

/-- **Concordance with the two profiles themselves fixes every position.** Premises: `w` has the
same concordance with `w` as `w'` has with `w` (`first`), and `w` has the same concordance with `w'`
as `w'` has with `w'` (`second`). Then the profiles give every listed member the same position. No
decidable equality, no discordance, and two amounts only. Rests on `pairs_agree` (every excess of
one listed member's weight over another's is the same under both), `common_of_excess` and
`RelativePosition.keeps_iff_common`. -/
theorem keeps_of_own_concord {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat}
    (first : SelectionTerm.concord ps w w = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' w)
    (second : SelectionTerm.concord ps w w' = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' w') :
    RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w' :=
  have agree := pairs_agree ps (fun p q => excess (w p) (w q)) (fun p q => excess (w' p) (w' q))
    first second
  keeps_iff_common.mpr fun p q mp mq => common_of_excess (agree p q mp mq) (agree q p mq mp)

/-- Two profiles give every listed member the same position exactly where each has the same
concordance with `w` as the other, and the same with `w'`. No premise, no decidable equality. From
`same_terms_of_keeps` and `keeps_of_own_concord`. -/
theorem keeps_iff_own_concord {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat} :
    RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w' ↔
      SelectionTerm.concord ps w w = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' w ∧
        SelectionTerm.concord ps w w' = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' w' :=
  ⟨fun keeps => ⟨(same_terms_of_keeps keeps w).1, (same_terms_of_keeps keeps w').1⟩,
   fun same => keeps_of_own_concord same.1 same.2⟩

/-- **The statement as written, with the instance removed.** Two profiles give every listed member
the same position exactly where, with every amount, they have the same concordance and the same
discordance. No premise, no decidable equality; the list may be empty, hold one member, or list
members more than once. Left to right is `same_terms_of_keeps`; right to left is
`keeps_of_own_concord`, which uses the hypothesis at the amounts `w` and `w'` and only its
concordance. -/
theorem keeps_iff_terms {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat} :
    RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w' ↔ ∀ a : α → Nat,
      SelectionTerm.concord ps w a = SelectionTerm.concord ps w' a ∧
        SelectionTerm.discord ps w a = SelectionTerm.discord ps w' a :=
  ⟨same_terms_of_keeps, fun same => keeps_of_own_concord (same w).1 (same w').1⟩

/-! ## The shift laws as its case -/

/-- A profile shifted at every listed member by a common `k` (`shift`) gives every listed member
the same position. It is `RelativePosition.keeps_add`, carried from the profile `fun p => w p + k`
to any profile that agrees with it on the list. -/
theorem keeps_of_shifted {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat} {k : Nat}
    (shift : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → w' p = w p + k) : RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w' :=
  keeps_iff_common.mpr fun p q mp mq =>
    (congrArg (w p + ·) (shift q mq)).trans
      ((keeps_iff_common.mp (keeps_add ps w k).1 p q mp mq).trans
        (congrArg (w q + ·) (shift p mp)).symm)

/-- Amounts alike at every listed member (`same`) have the same concordance with any weights. -/
theorem concord_amount_on {ps : List α} {w a a' : α → Nat} (same : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → a' p = a p) :
    concord ps w a' = concord ps w a :=
  total_congr_on fun p mp => total_congr_on fun q mq =>
    congrArg (excess (w p) (w q) * ·) (congr (congrArg excess (same p mp)) (same q mq))

/-- And the same discordance. -/
theorem discord_amount_on {ps : List α} {w a a' : α → Nat} (same : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → a' p = a p) :
    discord ps w a' = discord ps w a :=
  total_congr_on fun p mp => total_congr_on fun q mq =>
    congrArg (excess (w p) (w q) * ·) (congr (congrArg excess (same q mq)) (same p mp))

/-- `SelectionShift.concord_shifted`, its statement word for word: weights shifted at every member
by a common `k` (`shift`), and amounts alike (`same`), leave concordance unchanged. Proved here from
`keeps_iff_terms` at `RelativePosition.keeps_add` (through `keeps_of_shifted`), with
`concord_amount_on` for the amounts. -/
theorem concord_shifted_as_case {ps : List α} {w w' a a' : α → Nat} {k : Nat}
    (shift : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → w' p = w p + k) (same : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → a' p = a p) :
    concord ps w' a' = concord ps w a :=
  (concord_amount_on same).trans (keeps_iff_terms.mp (keeps_of_shifted shift) a).1.symm

/-- `SelectionShift.discord_shifted`, its statement word for word: and discordance. Proved from
`keeps_iff_terms` at `RelativePosition.keeps_add` (through `keeps_of_shifted`), with
`discord_amount_on` for the amounts. -/
theorem discord_shifted_as_case {ps : List α} {w w' a a' : α → Nat} {k : Nat}
    (shift : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → w' p = w p + k) (same : ∀ p, p ∈ ps → a' p = a p) :
    discord ps w' a' = discord ps w a :=
  (discord_amount_on same).trans (keeps_iff_terms.mp (keeps_of_shifted shift) a).2.symm

/-! ## Read on a step of generations -/

/-- **The term part of `SelectionShift.Generations.shift_step`, from positions.** Premises: two
courses of generations have the same family at step `t` (`family`), amounts alike there at every
member of it (`amount`), and offspring numbers there that give every member of the family the same
position (`keeps`). Then they have the same concordance and the same discordance at `t`. Nothing is
concluded of the next generation's size. A course has one amount at a step, so only the
left-to-right half of `keeps_iff_terms` is read here (`one_amount_not_enough`). Rests on
`keeps_iff_terms`, `concord_amount_on` and `discord_amount_on`. -/
theorem kept_step {G G' : SelectionTerm.Generations α} {t : Nat}
    (family : G'.family t = G.family t)
    (amount : ∀ p, p ∈ G.family t → G'.amount t p = G.amount t p)
    (keeps : RelativePosition.Keeps (G.family t) (G.offspringNumber t) (G'.offspringNumber t)) :
    G'.concordAt t = G.concordAt t ∧ G'.discordAt t = G.discordAt t :=
  ⟨(congrArg (fun l => concord l (G'.offspringNumber t) (G'.amount t)) family).trans
      ((concord_amount_on amount).trans (keeps_iff_terms.mp keeps (G.amount t)).1.symm),
   (congrArg (fun l => discord l (G'.offspringNumber t) (G'.amount t)) family).trans
      ((discord_amount_on amount).trans (keeps_iff_terms.mp keeps (G.amount t)).2.symm)⟩

/-- The first two conjuncts of `SelectionShift.Generations.shift_step`, under its three premises
word for word (the same family at `t`, amounts alike on it, offspring numbers differing at every
member by a common `k`): the same concordance and the same discordance at `t`. It is `kept_step` at
`keeps_of_shifted`. The third conjunct of `shift_step`, the next generation larger by `k` for each
member, is not a consequence of the positions and is not proved here. -/
theorem shift_step_terms_as_case {G G' : SelectionTerm.Generations α} {t k : Nat}
    (family : G'.family t = G.family t)
    (amount : ∀ p, p ∈ G.family t → G'.amount t p = G.amount t p)
    (shift : ∀ p, p ∈ G.family t → G'.offspringNumber t p = G.offspringNumber t p + k) :
    G'.concordAt t = G.concordAt t ∧ G'.discordAt t = G.discordAt t :=
  kept_step family amount (keeps_of_shifted shift)

/-! ## What the laws do not say: order, and one amount -/

/-- **The same positions keep which listed member has more.** Premises: the profiles give every
listed member the same position (`keeps`); `p` and `q` are listed. Then `q`'s weight is below `p`'s
under `w` exactly where it is under `w'`. The converse fails (`TwoProfiles.same_order_apart`). Rests
on `RelativePosition.keeps_iff_common` and `SelectionTerm.lt_iff_of_balance`. -/
theorem keeps_order {ps : List α} {w w' : α → Nat} (keeps : RelativePosition.Keeps ps w w')
    {p q : α} (mp : p ∈ ps) (mq : q ∈ ps) : w q < w p ↔ w' q < w' p :=
  lt_iff_of_balance (keeps_iff_common.mp keeps p q mp mq)

/-- With an amount that is the same `k` at every member, any two profiles have the same concordance
and the same discordance, both nothing. No premise. Rests on `SelectionTerm.concord_alike` and
`discord_alike`. -/
theorem uniform_amount_same_terms (ps : List α) (w w' : α → Nat) (k : Nat) :
    concord ps w (fun _ => k) = concord ps w' (fun _ => k) ∧
      discord ps w (fun _ => k) = discord ps w' (fun _ => k) :=
  ⟨(concord_alike (k := k) (fun _ _ => rfl) w).trans (concord_alike (k := k) (fun _ _ => rfl) w').symm,
   (discord_alike (k := k) (fun _ _ => rfl) w).trans (discord_alike (k := k) (fun _ _ => rfl) w').symm⟩

namespace TwoProfiles

/-- Nothing and one. -/
def near : Bool → Nat
  | false => 0
  | true => 1

/-- Nothing and two. -/
def far : Bool → Nat
  | false => 0
  | true => 2

/-- The two profiles do not give the two members the same positions: nothing with two is not one
with nothing. -/
theorem not_keeps : ¬ RelativePosition.Keeps [false, true] near far :=
  fun keeps => Nat.lt_irrefl 1 (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.lt_succ_self 1)
    (Nat.le_of_eq (show 2 = 1 from
      keeps_iff_common.mp keeps false true (.head _) (.tail _ (.head _)))))

/-- **The same order, not the same positions.** A witness on two members: under `near` and under
`far` the same member has more, and the two profiles do not give the members the same positions.
So `Keeps` is not sameness of order, and the laws above say nothing of order beyond
`keeps_order`. -/
theorem same_order_apart :
    (∀ p q : Bool, near q < near p ↔ far q < far p) ∧
      ¬ RelativePosition.Keeps [false, true] near far :=
  ⟨fun p q => match p, q with
    | false, false => ⟨fun lt => absurd lt (Nat.lt_irrefl 0), fun lt => absurd lt (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)⟩
    | false, true => ⟨fun lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero 1), fun lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero 2)⟩
    | true, false => ⟨fun _ => Nat.succ_pos 1, fun _ => Nat.succ_pos 0⟩
    | true, true => ⟨fun lt => absurd lt (Nat.lt_irrefl 1), fun lt => absurd lt (Nat.lt_irrefl 2)⟩,
   not_keeps⟩

/-- **One amount is not enough.** A witness on two members: with the amount that is nothing at
both, `near` and `far` have the same concordance and the same discordance, and they do not give the
members the same positions. So the right-to-left half needs the terms with more than one amount;
`keeps_of_own_concord` shows the two profiles themselves suffice as amounts. -/
theorem one_amount_not_enough :
    (concord [false, true] near (fun _ => 0) = concord [false, true] far (fun _ => 0) ∧
        discord [false, true] near (fun _ => 0) = discord [false, true] far (fun _ => 0)) ∧
      ¬ RelativePosition.Keeps [false, true] near far :=
  ⟨uniform_amount_same_terms [false, true] near far 0, not_keeps⟩

end TwoProfiles

end PositionTerms
