## A choice made from a reading: what is stated exactly

The first and second readers, each of its own group.

1. **One act, at one situation, with no steps.** A response common over a reading (`«共应»`, `CommonResponse.lean` 88) is, for each act, a region of situations inside where that act is acceptable, the regions covering every situation and each closed under the reading. It exists exactly where every situation has an act acceptable throughout what the reading's closure reaches from it (`«共应当且仅当所闭之承遍»`, 153): for every carrier, reading and requirement, with no premise and no choice. It is a relation from situations to acts, not a rule. A rule from a reading gives one (`«依读而应则共应»`, 205, one direction). The other direction needs a choice over the readings as a named premise (`Viability.ChoiceOver`, `Keeping.lean` 156; free where the readings are finitely listed) and every reading given by some situation: `GuardHorizon.uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible` (341) and `uniform_policy_iff_common` (about 351). There is no process, no step and no time in any of these.
2. **Keeping a guard over steps: exact for plans with recall, for each number of steps.** `Keeping.KeptFor` (90) takes a process with moves, a reading of each step, a guard, which moves are allowed and a class of starts: the class is kept for `n + 1` steps where the guard holds throughout it and some move usable from the whole class leaves, whatever value the step keeps, a class kept for `n`. Where the values kept are finitely listed, a class is kept for `n` steps exactly where a plan of `n` moves branching on the values kept keeps it (`keptFor_iff_plan`, 182; given choice in general, 176; `plans_iff_choice`, 247, says how much choice that takes over all processes). Every successor of the chosen move is counted.
3. **For every number of steps: a sufficient condition only.** A family of classes within the guard, closed under some usable move whatever is kept, keeps each of its classes for every `n` (`keptFor_of_closed`, 311). Its converse was not found. The record `finite-continuation-plans` says under what it does not show: "Keeping a class for every finite horizon does not give one infinite plan."
4. **One rule from the present reading, over more than one step: not found.** `GuardHorizon.uniform_viable` (406) gives one step further from a rule whose move is admissible for `n` more steps at every configuration; only the first move comes from the reading, and the later ones are chosen configuration by configuration. So the frozen file's C2 is partly false as worded: the uniform policy is not stated over a closed set and not for ever (the second reader).
5. **Within one reading.** That situations read alike which need different acts have no common response is stated as laws, for one act (`«一之共应当且仅当一应遍»`, 194; `«遍定应共应当且仅当同读同应»`, 248, under covering and one act; `«映应共应当且仅当同读同应»`, 274), and shown in built cases (`«共应之例».EachNotCommon`, `PairsNotCommon`, `GuardHorizon.statewise_not_uniform`). Over steps: in the built case `Matching`, two starts are kept for two steps where the step keeps a bit and not where it keeps nothing (`keeping_matters`, 435); that each start alone is kept there is not stated.
6. **Two readings.** What a coarser reading keeps a finer one keeps: one direction, on each carrier (`«共应之细»`, 178; `KeptFor.finer`, 288; `GuardHorizon.uniform_finer`; `OpenedCorrection.finer_keeps`, 95). What a finer reading keeps more is shown only in built cases. An exact law of it was not found.
7. **Time.** The step at which a reading comes to tell two things apart is related to what can still be kept only in `ReadOffInTime`, on its own carrier of level courses with the feasible courses handed in (`readableInTime_iff`, 661: readable in time exactly where the first step apart comes before the point of no return). No statement joins it to `KeptFor` or to the common response. Nearest elsewhere, each a built case: a reading revised where the stock that would carry the correction is gone (`OpenedCorrection.Linked.rebuilt_starved`, 175), and, outside the package, an inquiry that crosses a floor before its response comes (`InquiryChoice.inquiry_can_cross_floor_before_response`).

## The joins that are there, and two seams

- **Joined.** `uniform_policy_iff_common` joins a rule made from a reading to the common response. `OpenedCorrection.Correctable` is defined as a uniform policy. `Kernel.keptFor` (333) and `Certificate.keptFor` (322) carry the kernel into `KeptFor`, given one move usable throughout, so that no reading does work in them. `Viability.kernel_iff_held` (65) states the kernel through the same law of the grammar that gives the common response (`«所闭之承最大»`, 142: the largest region inside a requirement that is closed under a relation). The first reader: the two "are the same grammar law … at two relations: a reading in one and the process's steps in the other. No module in this group puts the reading and the steps in one statement."
- **A seam between two things of one name.** `Inquiry`, `LabelledRoles` and `MoveOutcomeProcesses` do not use `«共应»`. They use another definition, `CommonResponse` in `inquiry-root/sufficiency/InquirySufficiency.lean` (102): one response, a finite list of acts, that completes the goal safely from every configuration a belief holds. No theorem relating the two was found. Chapter 3's sentence that a judgment through the couplings is a common response over the class alike through them has its Lean in `LabelledRoles.Web.class_judge_iff` (111), which is stated with that other definition (the first reader).
- **A seam between two ways of keeping over steps.** No theorem joins `KeptFor` to `GuardHorizon.Viable` or to the uniform policy (the second reader).

## The lock's side

The third reader, of seven modules (`LineOnAStock`, `TurningInTime`, `RuleShaping`, `RuleCapture`, `PoolFromCourse`, `ClosedCapture`, `ReadOffInTime`).

- **None uses these notions.** By a search of the seven files: no occurrence of the common response, `KeptFor`, a plan, a uniform policy, the kernel, keepable, a process, or a choice over readings. `CommonResponse` and `Viability` are among what three of them import at some remove (computed from the import lines by a script, not by Lean), and are used in none.
- **Nothing chooses.** Each model is one way as it runs: the step is a function of the state, cut where the need is not met. The lock's step takes a yes or no for whether this step reads (`Pace.turn`), and that is a schedule handed in, not chosen (the second reader).
- **What comes nearest to "any rule from this reading".** The lock's laws hold for every handed-in function meeting a premise: the window law for every correcting rule that gives a kept rule throughout the window (its right side does not name that rule), `RuleCapture`'s laws for every rule read that is monotone in the share. And `ClosedCapture.courses_alike_iff_unread` (206) is an exact law over starts alike in what the loop reads, along every schedule: their shares and rules stay equal at every step exactly where neither the next share nor the rule read depends on what lies beyond. It is of a determined law's courses staying alike, not of a choice keeping something.
- **What would have to be supplied** to read "no choice made from what the selection registers keeps the level" as a case of the common response or of `KeptFor` (listed by the third reader; nothing is proposed). For the line on a stock: the state exists; moves are missing; a reading exists only in part (`Alike` is a relation on levels relative to one way, not on states, and nothing reads a step); the class of starts and the allowed moves are missing. For the turn's window law: the state exists; the nearest thing to a move is the handed-in correcting rule, and the type of rules would be the move at a reading step; a reading is present in pieces (`windowRead`, `loopPart`, `ReadsThroughPresent`), none of them a relation on these states; the class of starts and the allowed moves are missing. The Lean has a way to make a law with moves into a process (`Viability.disturbed`, with `GuardHorizon.dViable_iff`, 230); no statement applies it to the lock's models (the second reader).
- **It agrees with this afternoon's record** in what the third reader read: the package's sentence that the lock's feasible courses are turns to one standing use is there word for word; no process with moves was found in the seven modules.

## What it draws

This is my reading, held loosely; no report states it whole.

- **The two halves of the direction's sentence are stated in two kinds of model.** When a choice made from what is registered meets what is asked is stated exactly, for one act, and for plans over a number of steps on a process with moves. What a way set from a share does to what it rests on is stated for one way as it runs, with what it registers handed in as a function. The first kind has no level that runs down. The second has no moves.
- **One law of the grammar already stands under both.** The kernel (kept whatever the moves) and the common response (one answer over what is read alike) are that law at two relations, the steps and the reading. `KeptFor` is the one notion read here that has both relations at once, and of it the exact law is for each number of steps, with recall.
- **The chapters already set the lock under the response's inputs**, in chapter 8's table, as a telling.
- **It places nothing between the two ways.** It says where the pieces are and what each lacks of the other.
