## A choice made from a reading: what is stated exactly

Items 1 to 6 are from the first and second readers, each of its own group; item 7's first sentence is from the third.

1. **One act, at one situation, with no steps.** A response common over a reading (`«共应»`, `CommonResponse.lean` 88) is, for each act, a region of situations inside where that act is acceptable, the regions covering every situation and each closed under the reading. It exists exactly where every situation has an act acceptable throughout what the reading's closure reaches from it (`«共应当且仅当所闭之承遍»`, 153): for every carrier, reading and requirement, with no premise and no choice. It is a relation from situations to acts, not a rule. A rule from a reading gives one (`«依读而应则共应»`, 205, one direction). The other direction needs a choice over the readings as a named premise (`Viability.ChoiceOver`, `Keeping.lean` 156; free where the readings are finitely listed) and every reading given by some situation (`GuardHorizon.uniform_policy_iff_common`, about 351); with choice alone, a rule from the reading exists exactly where each reading has one act good at every situation giving it (`uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible`, 341). There is no process, no step and no time in any of these.
2. **Keeping a guard over steps: exact for plans with recall, for each number of steps.** `Keeping.KeptFor` (90) takes a process with moves, a reading of each step, a guard, which moves are allowed and a class of starts: the class is kept for `n + 1` steps where the guard holds throughout it and some move usable from the whole class leaves, whatever value the step keeps, a class kept for `n`. Where the values kept are finitely listed, a class is kept for `n` steps exactly where a plan of `n` moves branching on the values kept keeps it (`keptFor_iff_plan`, 182; given choice over the values kept into plans, 176; `plans_iff_choice`, 247, says how much choice that takes over all processes). Every successor of the chosen move is counted.
3. **For every number of steps: a sufficient condition only.** A family of classes within the guard, closed under some usable move whatever is kept, keeps each of its classes for every `n` (`keptFor_of_closed`, 311). Its converse was not found. The record `finite-continuation-plans` says under what it does not show: "Keeping a class for every finite horizon does not give one infinite plan."
4. **One rule from the present reading, over more than one step: not found.** `GuardHorizon.uniform_viable` (406) gives one step further from a rule whose move is admissible for `n` more steps at every configuration, at a configuration within the guard; only the first move comes from the reading, and the later ones are chosen configuration by configuration. So the frozen file's C2 is partly false as worded: the uniform policy is not stated over a closed set and not for ever (the second reader).
5. **Within one reading.** That situations read alike which need different acts have no common response is stated as laws, for one act (`«一之共应当且仅当一应遍»`, 194, for a carrier with at least one situation; `«遍定应共应当且仅当同读同应»`, 248, under covering and one act; `«映应共应当且仅当同读同应»`, 274), and shown in built cases (`«共应之例».EachNotCommon`, `PairsNotCommon`, `GuardHorizon.statewise_not_uniform`). Over steps: in the built case `Matching`, two starts are kept for two steps where the step keeps a bit and not where it keeps nothing (`keeping_matters`, 435); that each start alone is kept there is not stated.
6. **Two readings.** What a coarser reading keeps a finer one keeps: one direction, on each carrier (`«共应之细»`, 178; `KeptFor.finer`, 288; `GuardHorizon.uniform_finer`; `OpenedCorrection.finer_keeps`, 95, where every act that could be done before can still be done). What a finer reading keeps more is shown only in built cases. An exact law of it was not found.
7. **Time.** The step at which a reading comes to tell two things apart is related to what can still be kept only in `ReadOffInTime` among the statements read, on its own carrier of level courses with the feasible courses handed in (`readableInTime_iff`, 661: given the first step apart and the point of no return, readable in time exactly where the first comes before the second). `TurningInTime`'s laws of its section 7, known to the third reader from the module's account only, relate a first marked step to the line's ending. No statement joining either to `KeptFor` or to the common response was found. Nearest elsewhere, each a built case: a reading revised where the stock that would carry the correction is gone (`OpenedCorrection.Linked.rebuilt_starved`, 175), and, outside the package, an inquiry that crosses a floor before its response comes (`InquiryChoice.inquiry_can_cross_floor_before_response`).
