Q1. What is read, of what, and what chooses.
- The reading is a map of STEPS, `sense : S → A → S → O` (Seen 73, KeptFor 90). It reads a step (where it began, the move, where it ended), not a state.
- A class of starts `B : S → Prop` is handed in.
- `Seen P sense B a o t` (73, statement read) is `∃ s, B s ∧ P.step s a t ∧ sense s a t = o`: what can stand after move a from the class, where the step kept the value o.
- `Usable P allowed B a` (84, statement read): a is allowed and enabled at every configuration of B.
- What chooses is a `Plan A O n` (100, statement read). It gives a first move and, for each value kept, a plan of n−1 moves. So it is a tree that branches on the whole sequence of values kept: it has recall.
- Handed in are the process, sense, guard, allowed and the class B. The docstring says no premise is added.
