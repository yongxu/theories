1. CommonResponse.lean (whole file, 506 lines, every statement read)
Definition, «共应» (line 88; statement and docstring read). The arguments are E : «际» α (a relation on situations: the reading, saying which situations it does not tell apart), G : A → α → Prop (the requirement: for each act a, the situations where a is acceptable and can be done), and J : A → α → Prop. «共应» E G J holds when three things hold: every J a lies inside G a; every situation lies in some J a; and every J a is closed under E («闭» E (J a)). The regions may overlap, so this is an admissible relation from situations to acts, not a rule. The opening account (lines 1-74) says so and says that one act fixed for each class "is a rule, which needs choice". The reading is supplied (line 71), and the requirement is supplied and fixed before the outcome (line 11). There is no process, no step and no time in the definition.
