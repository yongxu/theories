## 1. ReadOffInTime.lean: Recoverable, NoReturnAt, ReadableInTime, FourReaders

**Q1. What stands for "what can go on after".** The feasible courses that agree with a course so far and keep what is asked. `Recoverable feasible keeps l N := ∃ l', feasible l' ∧ AgreeUpTo l' l N ∧ keeps l'` (591–592). This is a set of courses, of one course `l` at a step `N`, and it is relative to two supplied predicates: `feasible`, which the docstring reads as "the courses the part can still take" (73–74), and `keeps`. `NoReturnAt … N := ¬ Recoverable … N ∧ ∀ n < N, Recoverable … n` (595–596).

**Q2. Opening or widening.** None found in what was read. No law concludes that a course becomes recoverable at a later step.

**Q3. Narrowing and closing (general laws).**
- `Recoverable.earlier` (601): from `Recoverable feasible keeps l N` and `n ≤ N`, conclude `Recoverable … n`. No other premise. Contrapositively, once a course is not recoverable it is not recoverable at any later step. This is the general law of no return.
- `NoReturnAt.recoverable_iff` (607), an exact two-sided law. Given `NoReturnAt feasible keeps l N`, for every `n`, `Recoverable … n ↔ n < N`.
- `NoReturnAt.unique` (615): the point of no return is one step.
- `NoReturnAt.parts` (625). From `NoReturnAt … (N+1)`: some feasible `l'` agreeing with `l` up to `N` keeps, and every feasible `l'` agreeing up to `N+1` does not.
- `noReturnAt_of_past` (632). Premises: `decided : ∀ n, Recoverable … n ∨ ¬ Recoverable … n`, and `past : ¬ Recoverable … n`. Conclusion: `∃ N ≤ n, NoReturnAt … N`.
- `readableInTime_iff` (661), exact. Given `W.FirstApart group l l' T` and `NoReturnAt feasible keeps l N`: `ReadableInTime … ↔ T < N`.
- The too-late laws `too_late_of_alike` (678), `too_late_of_unreached` (699), `too_late_within_margin` (719) and `too_late_of_own` (743). Each concludes "not readable in time, and no family read off the group's continuations reads in time". The premises of each:
  - alike: every member is admitted alike at every step up to `D`, and `¬ Recoverable … l D`.
  - unreached: balance of both courses before `D`, the same formations present, a common start, `responds` (every non-source exchanges alike wherever it is alike through `obs`), no member reached in time by `D`, and `¬ Recoverable … W.level D`.
  - within margin: `l'` admits every member and every gap is within the margin up to `D`, and the course is past return at `D`.
  - own: every kept course admits every member at every step, and `l'` admits every member at every step.

**Built example.** `FourReaders.recoverable_iff` (977): `Recoverable Feasible DwellerKept (levels .never) N ↔ N ≤ 2`. Also `FourReaders.noReturn` (1011): the point is 3, the dweller is admitted at 3 and not at 4. And `recoverable_iff_regime` (1097). The feasible courses are the clearer's four stopping courses, "supplied as flows, not generated by a stated law of exchange" (115–117).

**Q4. Forms changing.** Not found in what was read. The feasible courses are fixed, and the formations and their needs are fixed in the witness.

**Q5. Where a widening comes from.** Not applicable: no widening is stated. In the witness the renewer gives one unit while the habitat stands at 2 or more. That is a regime (`regime_point`, 1034, a built example), not a widening.

**Q6. Relates a way of selecting to narrowing.** Yes, for closing, in time. A selection read off a group's continuations can tell two courses apart no earlier than their first step apart (`TellsApartAt.not_before`, 184). It is in time exactly where that step comes before the losing course's point of no return (`readableInTime_iff`). The too-late laws give sufficient conditions for every such selection to be late. The docstring: "Which way a reading turns a course is not stated: a selection that tells the courses apart in time can select for the loss as well as against it" (117–119). Nothing relates a selection to an opening.

## 8. ReachableRanges.lean: possible_of_next, and what "possible" is

**Q1.**

## 9. TurningInTime.lean section 7: recoverable_iff_above, noReturnAt_iff_first_below, turns_in_time_iff_before

**Q1.** The `Recoverable` of `ReadOffInTime`, with:
- feasible courses: `Turned X giving H`, which is `X` turned to the standing use `H` at some step, the start included (1945);
- what is kept: `MeetsNeed need l := ∀ t c, need ≤ l t c` (1949);
- courses over one condition.

**Q3. Closing (general laws under named premises).** `recoverable_iff_above` (2087) and `noReturnAt_iff_first_below` (2100) share these premises:
- `fall : ∀ t, X (t+1) ≤ X t`
- `top : X 0 ≤ hi`
- `nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving`
- `short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H y`
- `meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H y hi`
- `keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving H hi`
- `needs : 0 < need`
- `within : need ≤ H + y`

Under them:
- `recoverable_iff_above`: `Recoverable (Turned X giving H) (MeetsNeed need) (fun t _ => X t) N ↔ H + y ≤ X N`.
- `noReturnAt_iff_first_below`: `NoReturnAt … N ↔ X N < H + y ∧ ∀ n < N, H + y ≤ X n`.
- `noReturnAt_of_below` (2148), under the same premises plus `X n < H + y`, gives `∃ N ≤ n, NoReturnAt … N`.
- `noReturn_exists` (2252): the course as it was has a point of no return within `x` steps, under the premises listed at 2240–2251.

**Q2 and Q4. A going-on continuing through a change of its way.** `turns_in_time_iff_before` (2289) is a general law and exact.

Premises:
- `AloneOutside marked alone after`, and `[DecidablePred marked]`;
- `turns : ∀ w x, marked x → Kept (after w x)`;
- `kept : ∀ w x, Kept w → Kept (after w x)`;
- `uses : ∀ w, Kept w → use w = H₂`;
- `nothing`; and, at `H₂, y₂`: `short`, `meets` and `keeps`, with `hi`;
- `0 < need` and `need ≤ H₂ + y₂`;
- `short₁ : Short giving H₁ y₁`, `uses₁ : ∀ j, H₁ ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w j)`, `below : x < H₁ + y₁`, and `high : x ≤ hi`.

Conclusion: the line from `(w, x)` never ends (`«不穷» (net need after …) «一» (w, x)`) exactly where there is a first marked step `T` of the course as it was, with the need met at and before it, and a point of no return `N` with `T + 1 < N`.

`course_turned` (2190) has no premise on the need or on `giving`. Under `AloneOutside`, `turns`, `kept` and `uses`, the pair's level course is the course as it was, turned at `T + 1`.

So this law states that the way changes, from `alone`'s ways to the `Kept` kind using `H₂`, while the line continues. Its conclusion is continuation without end, not widening.

**Q5.** Not applicable: the level is kept, not raised. `H + y` is the regime's point.

**Q6.** A turn to a standing use, which is a change of the way the course is drawn, is related to closing: in time exactly where it comes before the point of no return. The docstring: "The feasible courses are turns to one standing use; other continuations are not considered, and the marks stay given" (1933–1934). Nothing about widening.

## 10. The lock's laws of closing

**PoolFromCourse.closed_stays_closed** (222), a general law.

Premises:
- `raises : Raises (liftShare P) favours f`, that is, `∀ x g, favours g → P.le x.1 (f x g).1`;
- `monotone : ReadsShare P M hA`;
- `upper : M.le g g' → favours g → favours g'`;
- `through : ReadsThroughPresent present hA h`, that is, `∀ p g, ¬ present p → h p g = hA p.1 g`;
- `kept : NotReadmitted present f`, that is, `∀ p g, ¬ present p → ¬ present (f p g)`;
- `absent : ¬ present (paced f h reads c r).1`;
- `lock : Locked M favours hA (…r…)`, where `Locked M favours h p := favours p.2 ∧ M.le p.2 (h p.1 p.2)`.

Conclusion: `∀ k`, no reader of the beyond is present at `r + k`, the course is locked there, and neither share nor rule is below where they were at `r`.

Readers of what lies beyond, once gone, stay gone. That is carried by the premise `NotReadmitted`.

**PoolFromCourse.Borne.shapers_left** (753), a built example. The rule stays `hi` at steps 2 and 3, the share falls across 2→3 and 3→4, the course is locked at 1, 2 and 3, and it is not locked at 4. A lock can be left while the share falls (head docstring, 31–32).

**ClosedCapture.lock_outlives_loss** (307), a general law.

Premises:
- `raises : Raises P favours fA`;
- `monotone : ReadsShare P M hA`;
- `upper`;
- `accrues : ∀ p g, p.2 + 1 ≤ fB p g`;
- `locked : Locked M favours hA (a, g)`.

The loss is read by neither the selection nor the reading: the course is `paced (join fA fB) (joinRead hA) reads`. Conclusion: for every `t`, `b + t ≤` the loss, the course is locked, and neither share nor rule is below its start.

**ClosedCapture.turned_by_loss** (291), a general law.

Premises:
- `accrues`;
- `turns : ∀ p g, N ≤ p.2 → ¬ favours (h p g)`;
- `at_r : reads r = true`;
- `past : N ≤ b + r`.

Conclusion: `¬ favours` of the rule at `r + 1`.

**DrawnBeyond.Flow.Balances.breaks_beyond** (205), a general law.

Premises:
- `L.Balances W`;
- `L.GainlessOn (ds ++ rest) t`;
- `W.MetOn dep rest t`;
- `W.stock ds t + W.slack dep rest t < W.stock ds (t+1) + L.lost (ds ++ rest) t`.

Conclusion: `∃ c ∈ rest, W.Broken t (dep c) c`.

`breaks_beyond_unsettles` (271) adds `W.Dependent`. The formation then "ends, or its dependence weakens or dissolves". `breaks_beyond_ends` (287) adds that the dependence is fixed, and the formation ends.

**DrawnBeyond.Flow.favours_breaking_beyond** (293), a general law.

Premises:
- `Balances`, `GainlessOn (ds ++ rest) t` and `MetOn dep rest t`;
- `apart : ∀ c ∈ rest, c ∉ ds`;
- `listed : ∀ h c, members h → W.CoupledInto obs h c → c ∈ ds`;
- `rewards : select W.level → W.stock ds t + W.slack dep rest t < W.stock ds (t+1)`;
- `holds : select W.level`.

Conclusion: `∃ c ∈ rest, W.Broken t (dep c) c ∧ ¬ members (dep c) ∧ ∀ h, members h → ¬ W.CoupledInto obs h c`.

The `_inflow` form (272) drops `GainlessOn` and adds `L.inflow (ds ++ rest) t` to the left side.

**Q1 for the lock.** Being locked, and whether readers of the beyond are present: of one course. In DrawnBeyond: a dependence beyond that is met or broken, at one step.

**Q2.** Not found in what was read. A rise of the family is handed in as `rewards` or `exceeds`.

**Q3.** All of the above.

**Q4.** `breaks_beyond_unsettles`: the formation does not go on as it was. It ends, or its dependence changes. This is a change of a formation beyond. It is not stated as something that continues while it changes.

**Q5.** In a balanced flow, a rise is drawn from beyond, and under gainlessness by the rise plus the loss. The head says: "a rise above all the whole held needs a step at which it is open, gaining from outside it (`Flow.Balances.open_of_rise_above`)", and "only the part of a rise the inflow covers need not be drawn from beyond" (51–62). I read these two laws only as the head names them.

**Q6.** Yes, for closing:
- `favours_breaking_beyond`: a selection that holds only where the family rises above the slack holds only where a dependence outside what the group couples into is broken.
- `lock_outlives_loss` and `closed_stays_closed`: a selection read off its share stays locked while what it does not read grows, or while the readers of the beyond stay gone.

Nothing in what was read relates a selection to an opening.

## Does anything state that the set of courses still open GROWS along a course?

Not found in what was read. Every law along a course that I read says the set shrinks or stays:
- recoverability is down-closed (`Recoverable.earlier`, `NoReturnAt.recoverable_iff`);
- the kernel is closed forward (`kernel_step_iff`);
- the whole possible never grows (`Run.possible_of_next`), and a lost possibility has no way back (`Run.not_possible_returns`);
- each configuration covers its successor with a lag of one (`Run.coversLater_next`), and no successor widens with a lag over its predecessor (`Run.not_widensWithLag_next`);
- the possible stays the same where the successor covers the predecessor (`Run.possible_same_of_covers_next`);
- a longer horizon keeps every shorter one (`KeptFor.shorter`);
- once closed, the lock stays closed (`closed_stays_closed`).

The only "grows along a course" found is same-offset widening (`widens_next_iff_rising`, on determined processes), with the built examples `Rise` and `Climb`. That is what each configuration admits rising, read against the course's own delay, while the whole possible stays the same (`Rise.possible_same`). It is not a growth of the courses still open. Things grow wider only by comparison across guards or classes (`Kernel.mono`, `Keepable.mono`, `KeptFor.finer`), not across steps.
