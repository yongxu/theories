## Definitions the answers rest on

- `Web.Admits l t f` (Admission 378): `∀ c, W.requires t f c → W.need t f c ≤ l c`.
- `Web.Includes l l'` (Admission 388): at every step `l` admits every formation `l'` admits. `Web.Exceeds l l'` (392): at some step `l` admits one `l'` does not.
- `Web.Widens l l'` (Regions 157) = `Includes l l' ∧ Exceeds l l'`; `Keeps` (161) inclusion both ways; `Mixed` (166) exceeding both ways. Its docstring: "a formation or requirement of a kind not in `F` widens nothing, so growth by new kinds (as along `Flow.GrownBy`) is not compared here."
- `Flow.WithheldLevels part W l` (Admission 507): `l` changes by what the flow with the part withheld takes and gives, starting at `W.level 0`.
- `Web.IncludesAcrossFrom R l T`, `Web.KeepsOpening R l T`, `Web.KeepsWidening R l T` (EventualWidening 282, 287, 292): along continuation `R`, from `T` on nothing admitted is lost across any stretch; after every step something is admitted that "continues nothing admitted at that step"; both together.
- `Web.EventuallyWidens` (EventualWidening 99): from some step on, `l` widens `l'` on every tail.
- `Steps.KeepsWideningFrom Q N` (WideningOnTheMap 100): from `N` nothing held is lost across any stretch and after every step something not held there is held later.
- Grammar: `«生成» R L E x` (GrammarMap 274): along some branch the pattern `L` goes on and a carrying renewal `«传»` lies ahead of every point; `«传» R L E x y` (62): `L` continues from `x` to `y` while a fine line under `E` begins at `y`; `«全生成»` (80): the coarse line never ends and a carrying renewal lies ahead of every point; `«守»` (279): goes on along some branch with no carrying renewal; `«退化»` (283): ends on every branch.
- `Branching.Reachable P n s` (Recurrence 118, not opened): per `Branching.lean`'s docstring, "some course of `n` moves leads to it".
