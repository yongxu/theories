## The reading, in seven claims

**R1. A closed loop of things resting on each other cannot go on under October's premise of dissipation.** Under `Flow.Dissipative` and balance, with finitely many conditions in all, no taking recurs without end (`Dissipative.finite_taking_not_recurring`); a finite family taken from at arbitrarily late steps is not closed from any step on (`Dissipative.opens`) and, enclosed around, draws in from beyond it (`taking_draws_in`, `fed_from_beyond`). The examples of going on for ever on the web that are closed (`Cycle` and five others) fail dissipation (`witnesses_not_dissipative`). So the sense of "the second way" I gave Ren this morning ("things that are among what each other rests on", closed into a loop) is bounded by a theorem already there: such a loop goes on only as something a flow from beyond passes through.

**R2. On October's web, renewing and consuming say that needs stay met or are lost for good; they do not say that anything remakes anything, and they have no selecting in them.** (The correction of 18 November to my record; `Web.RenewingIn`, `Web.ConsumingIn`.)

**R3. What comes back to the one that gives is a law at a step, with no level in it.** In `RuleShaping`: shapers bear a cost `c`; of what shaping gives, `s` comes back to shapers alone and `p` to every member alike. The rule favours the shapers exactly where `c ≤ s` (`favours_iff`), whatever is shared (`favours_alike`); with nothing coming back and a cost their share falls at every step (`nothing_back_falls`). Where what comes back is read from the shapers' share at a threshold with `lo < c < hi`, it is a tipping point: from above the share rises at every step, from below it falls (`threshold_rises`, `threshold_falls`). Among groups the whole selects for shaping exactly where `c` times the spread falls short of `s` times it with `m q` times the spread among groups (`grouped_rises_iff`): what is shared counts as far as it is shared with one's own. `s` and `p` are constants of the rule at a step: the shared part does not grow with the number who give, and nothing can run down.

**R4. The line on a stock has units that multiply on a level that can run down, and nothing in it gives or is favoured for giving.** (`LineOnAStock`; the plan's items 3 to 7.) My try of 17 November put giving into it: with one shared level, what a unit gives makes no difference to who comes next except through which needs are met, so a kind that pays for what comes to all alike loses its share (R3's `nothing_back_falls`, by argument).

**R5. A small program of 17 November (D6) has all four together and no law came of it.** Two kinds, one that gives part of what it took; a first condition fed by a named source at every step; a second condition that offspring need and that only the givers feed; own or shared. In 193,536 exact runs what decided which line is left was who gets back what was given and who is short of what, the order of access included. Its part about selection is `favours_iff`; its part about ending is in no module read.

**R6. The nearest thing in the repository to "two ways placed with respect to each other" is the market model of 6 October, which the direction page never names.** In `experiments/market-generativity/formal/` (outside the package; rationals; the standard three axioms reported; told in chapter 5 §10): a state of three numbers, the renewing practice's share `x`, the quality `q` of the mechanism that charges for depletion, and the other practice's influence `w`. The renewing practice's advantage is `fine·q + lobby − cost` (`profits_difference`): it is selected exactly where the charge that comes back covers its cost. Influence follows the other share and erodes `q`; paid correction restores it. For a range of the erosion there are two fixed ends and a middle (`fixed_classification`, `high_fixed`, `low_fixed`, `middle_fixed`); each end attracts a neighbourhood (`high_approaches`, `low_approaches`); the middle is unstable (`middle_unstable`); coupled to a stock that the renewing share rebuilds and the other damages, the low end breaches the stock's floor in finitely many steps (`low_support_fails_finitely`, `coupled_eventual_floor_breach`) and from a box about the high end the history never fails (`high_coupled_history_never_fails`). The stock makes no difference to the selection there: only `q` does. So it is, as I read it: a selection, a rule set again through the share's influence, a cost of renewing, what comes back, a stock rested on, two ways of going on with a boundary between them, and the stock running down unseen by the selection. It has shares and not units that multiply; its mechanism is supplied; much of it is for one set of numbers.

**R7. So what is not there is narrower than "the second way has no model".** No statement that was found has, in the package's own terms and for units that multiply: a level that those who give keep up from a source that is said, a cost to them, what comes back to them depending on that level or on their share, and the two outcomes with the boundary between. The pieces are R3 (what comes back; a tipping point; no level), R4 (units and a level; no giving), R6 (all of it as shares, outside the package, for supplied numbers), and R5 (all of it in a program).
