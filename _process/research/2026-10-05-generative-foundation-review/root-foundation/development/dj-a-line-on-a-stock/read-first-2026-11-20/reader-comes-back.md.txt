## (1) R3, sentence by sentence

What the letters are (`shaped`, `RuleShaping.lean` 150 to 152): `shaped a s p c pos` is the pair of counts `⟨a + s + p, a + c + p⟩`, the first what each shaper leaves, the second what each other leaves. `a` is what each leaves without shaping, positive (`pos : 0 < a`). `s` is the part of what shaping gives that comes back to the shapers alone. `p` is the part that comes to every member alike. `c` is the cost, "read as what each other leaves more than each shaper": it is added to the others' count, and a shaper's own count is never lowered by it. All four are natural numbers handed in.

**The threshold.** `thresholdBack hi lo x` is `if x.other ≤ x.carrier then hi else lo` (458): what comes back to the shapers alone is one of two handed-in numbers, by whether the shapers are at least as many as the others. So it is a threshold in the population's two counts, placed at one to one by the definition, not located by `c`, `hi` or `lo`. `byBack` (426) makes the rule read at a reading step `shaped a (σ x) p c`, ignoring the rule in force.

- `threshold_rises` (570): `lo ≤ hi`, `c < hi`, any `reads : Nat → Bool`, a start `(x, shaped a hi p c)` with `favours x` (shapers at least as many as the others, equality included) and `0 < x.carrier`. Then for every `t` the share rises strictly from `t` to `t + 1`.
- `threshold_falls` (603): `lo ≤ hi`, `lo < c`, any `reads`, a start `(x, shaped a lo p c)` with `¬ favours x` and `0 < x.carrier`. Then for every `t` the share falls strictly.

So: every step, yes; every schedule of reading, yes; but only from starts whose rule in force is the one read at the start's share. Each theorem needs only its own strict inequality; `lo < c < hi` gives both. A start above the threshold under another rule is not covered, and with a schedule that reads late such a course can cross: `RuleCapture.Reversed.not_absorbed` (855) is a built course from carriers two to one under the lower rule, not read at the first step, that is locked the other way from step 1. That witness is of `RuleCapture.threshold` on two rules, the same construction, not of `thresholdBack`. Neither theorem says the share reaches or approaches an end; `DrawnShaping`'s account says so of its own laws in words ("not convergence to either boundary state").

**The groups.** `Grouped gs w shapes a s q c pos` (636): for every group `g` in the listing and every member `x` of it, either `shapes x = 1` and `w x` is the shapers' count of `shaped a s (q * total g shapes) c`, or `shapes x = 0` and `w x` is the others' count. So the shared part of a group is `q` for each shaper in it. `grouped_rises_iff` (663), with `equal : ∀ g ∈ gs, g.length = m`:

`discord (whole gs) w shapes < concord (whole gs) w shapes ↔ c * concord (whole gs) shapes shapes < s * concord (whole gs) shapes shapes + m * q * concordAmong gs shapes shapes`.

- "The whole selects for shaping" is the left side: over ordered pairs of members, offspring number goes with shaping more than against it (`SelectionTerm.concord`, `discord`). It is a statement about one listing and one function `w`. No next generation is in it; the module says "at a step and with no transmission across steps" (122).
- "The spread" is `concord (whole gs) shapes shapes`: the sum over ordered pairs of members of the squared excess of one's shaping over the other's; with shaping one or none, the number of shapers times the number of others.
- "The spread among groups" is `concordAmong gs shapes shapes` (`SelectionLevels` 212): the same sum over ordered pairs of groups, each read by its number of shapers.
- `grouped_alike_iff` (692) adds: every group has the same number of shapers, and shaping varies; then exactly where `c < s` (strict). `grouped_uniform_iff` (714) adds: each group all shapers or none, and the groups differ; then exactly where `c < s + m * q`.
- All rest on `SelectionLevels.rises_iff_kept` (966): for groups of equal size `m`, where `w p + K * a p = B + s * a p + q * total g a` for every member, the whole selects for the amount exactly where `K` times its spread falls short of `s` times it with `m q` times the spread among groups. There `a` is any natural-number amount, not one or none.

**The last sentence.** The module's own words (119 to 125): "`s` and `p` are constants of the rule at a step: the shared part reaches every member even with no shapers present and does not grow with their number. A gain made by the shapers and divided among the members of their group is stated among groups of equal size (`grouped_rises_iff`), at a step and with no transmission across steps; in groups of any sizes each member gains `q` for each shaper in its group, so a larger group shares more".

- True of `shaped`: `p` is a number, the same whoever is present.
- Not true of the grouped statements R3 cites one sentence earlier: there the shared part is `q * total g shapes`, which grows with the number of shapers in the group. The draft kept the module's first sentence and dropped its second.
- In the loop `s` is not a constant along the course: it is `σ x`, read from the share at each reading step (`byBack`), and `byBack_reads_iff` (431) says the rule read reads the share exactly where `σ` is monotone in it. R3's own fifth sentence says as much.
- "Nothing can run down" is not a sentence of the module. It follows from the definitions: the state is two counts and a rule, the counts are multiplied at each step, the others are always present (`other_pos`), and shapers present stay present (`present_along`, 271). There is no level, no need and no ending in the module. The share can fall.

**"What a unit gives makes no difference to who comes next except through which needs are met"** is `ways_alike_give`, exactly, once said as a statement about two courses from one way that holds for as long as their levels meet the same needs. **"So a kind that pays for what comes to all alike loses its share"** follows from nothing proved, and is more than the record says:

A unit that adds leaves the same kids as one that does not. So two kinds alike but for adding keep their numbers in step while the same needs are met: the adding kind's share neither rises nor falls. 2. *The record says less.* The correction of 16:37 to the frozen file's line 68: "so, by argument, a unit that adds is not favoured over others by it. Which outlasts the other turns on how the needs lie and on whether what is added covers what is taken."
3. *`nothing_back_falls` does not carry over as it stands.* It is of two counts each multiplied by the same held rule at every step, the others always present, with no level and no need. 4. *"Comes to all alike" is not what the level does.* In `shaped`, `p` is added to both kinds' offspring. What is added to a level reaches units only through which needs are met, so it comes alike only to units with one need.

**Is `RuleShaping`'s share the same notion as a share of units on a stock?** No, and no statement was found that joins them. `CarrierCounts` (469) is two natural numbers with the second positive; `select` multiplies each by the rule's number for it; the share order is cross-multiplication. Every member of a kind leaves the same number, no member has a need, and neither kind can end. On the line a way is a list of units, each with its own need and its own list of kids, and a unit whose need is unmet leaves nothing. The two agree, by my own working and by no theorem, only while every need is met and each kind leaves a fixed number of its own kind. No file that names `CarrierCounts` or `RuleShaping` names `LineOnAStock`, `WayOfUnits` or `RegimeMargin` (a search by name over the package's files; it misses a join made through a third file or in other words). ## (4) Is `RuleShaping`'s threshold the market model's unstable middle?

No statement makes them one, and they are alike in one respect only. Written as a fraction `x` of shapers, one step of `select` under a rule giving `A` to each shaper and `B` to each other moves `x` by `x(1 − x)(A − B) / (xA + (1 − x)B)`; the market's `shareStep` (`MarketGenerativity` 13) moves it by `η x (1 − x) g`. So at a step the two have the same sign and the same rest points (`x = 0`, `x = 1`, or no advantage), with `A − B = s − c` in the place of `g`. Read so (with `g` as `gap` writes it, `fine · q + lobby − cost`; the family of `RegimeAttraction` writes it `fine · (q − 3/8)`): the renewing practice's `cost` is `c`; `fine · q + lobby`, which the other practice pays, is `s` (a loss borne by the others is a return to the shapers, as `PoolFromCourse.bear_shaped_others` writes it); `price − levy`, common to both profits, is `a + p` and drops out, as in `favours_alike` and `profits_difference`. This correspondence is my working on paper, stated by no theorem, and it breaks in five places. (i) In `RuleShaping` what comes back is read from the present share at reading steps and the rule is held between them; in the market `q` is a state with its own law (`qualityStep`), raised by paid correction and eroded by an influence `w` that itself lags the other share, so what comes back follows the share's history through two lagging levels, and a start above the middle in share with low `q` is covered by no theorem. (ii) `thresholdBack` puts the jump at one to one by definition, between two handed-in values; the market's middle is the rest point at which `q` is exactly `3/8`, at `x = 1 − 5/(12β)`, moving with the erosion `β` (`middle`, `fixed_iff_balances`); `DrawnShaping`'s critical mass, at `c − lo` to `hi − c`, is the nearer thing in the package, and it too reads the present share. (iii) `threshold_rises` and `threshold_falls` hold from every start on their side, at every step, for all the parameters; the market's `fixed_classification` (68) holds for `0 < β ≤ 3/4`, but the two attractions are of tubes about the ends at `β = 1/2` only (`high_approaches`, `low_approaches`), and `middle_unstable` (430) says, at `β = 1/2`, that within every distance of the middle there is a state, on one side, whose share later exceeds `1/4`: it does not say the middle divides the starts, nor that such a state reaches the high end, nor anything of the other side. (iv) `RuleShaping`'s share rises strictly without a statement of where it goes, and neither kind can end; the market has ends that are rest points. (v) The market's stock (`supportStep`) is moved by the share and moves nothing back; `RuleShaping` has no stock. What the market has that `RuleShaping` lacks is exactly the lagging level `q` between the share and what comes back.

## (5) Of "a level that those who give keep up, a cost to them, what comes back depending on that level or on their share, for kinds that multiply": what `RuleShaping` has and lacks

- **Kinds that multiply: has, in a thin form.** Two kinds; at each step every member of a kind leaves the rule's number for that kind; offspring carrying the parent's kind is a premise. Whole numbers; no need; neither kind can end.
- **A cost to them: has.** `c`, a constant, written as the others' surplus.
- **What comes back depending on their share: has, in the loop.** `byBack` with any `σ` monotone in the share (`byBack_reads_iff`, `covering_locks`); `thresholdBack` is a `σ` of two values. `σ` is handed in. `DrawnShaping` derives one such `σ` from who reads.
- **A shared part depending on how many give: only in `Grouped`,** at one step, with no next step stated.
- **A level: lacks.** Nothing in the state but two counts and a rule. So it also lacks what comes back depending on a level, anything those who give keep up, any need, and any running down or ending.
- **What the shapers do: shape the rule.** They are not said to give to anything; the module's third reading (111 to 114) reads a capacity "whose gain comes to every member alike" onto `nothing_back_falls`. Taking the shapers for "the one that gives" is the draft's recognition.

## Findings

### MUST

**M1. R3, last sentence: "the shared part does not grow with the number who give".** False of `Grouped`, `grouped_rises_iff` and its cases, cited in the sentence before, where the shared part is `q` for each shaper in the member's group; and `s` is read from the share in the loop. Put instead:

> In `shaped`, `a`, `s`, `p` and `c` are numbers handed in: the shared part `p` reaches every member even with no shaper present and does not grow with their number. In the loop what comes back alone is read from the share at each reading step (`byBack`), `p` and `c` staying fixed. Only in the grouped statements does the shared part grow with those who give: it is `q` for each shaper in the member's group, at one step, with no next step stated. Nothing in the module has a level, a need or an ending: two counts are multiplied, the others are always present, and shapers present stay present.

**M2. R4, second sentence: "so a kind that pays for what comes to all alike loses its share (R3's `nothing_back_falls`, by argument)".** More than anything proved and more than the record of 17 November, for the four reasons under (3). Put instead:

> My try of 17 November put adding into it, in scratch outside the package: of two courses from one way, under any two givings, uses, gives and start levels, the ways are the same for as long as the levels meet the same needs of the way (`ways_alike_give`). In that model adding costs a unit nothing, so no kind pays there; by argument, and by no theorem, a unit that adds is not favoured by it, and which kind outlasts the other turns on how the needs lie. That a kind which leaves fewer for what comes to all alike has a falling share is `RuleShaping.nothing_back_falls`, a law of two counts multiplied by a held rule with no level and no need. It has not been stated of units on a level, where it could hold only up to the first unmet need.

**M3. R3, fifth sentence: the tipping point told without its start.** The two theorems are of courses that start under the rule read at the start's share; without that, "from above the share rises at every step" is not what is proved, and a built course of the same construction crosses. The threshold's place is also fixed by definition. Put instead:

> Where what comes back is `hi` when the shapers are at least as many as the others and `lo` when they are fewer (`thresholdBack`: a step placed at one to one by definition, `lo ≤ hi`), and the course starts under the rule read at its start: with `c < hi`, from a start with shapers present and at least as many as the others, their share rises strictly at every step; with `lo < c`, from a start with shapers present and fewer, it falls strictly at every step; under every schedule of reading (`threshold_rises`, `threshold_falls`). A start whose rule in force is not the one read there is not covered. Neither law says where the share goes.
