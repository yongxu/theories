## Verdict

R1's theorems are cited rightly, but its headline and its conclusion say more than they do: the Lean bounds **taking** (and rising, coming to stand, embodied arising) in a closed family of **conditions**, not **resting on**, and the package's own `Mutualism` is a closed pair that rest on each other for ever under a dissipative flow. R2 is right in its two negatives and wrong in one word ("lost"). No law was found of several goings-on that go on without end by passing on what comes from a said source; the halves are apart.

## 1. R1, sentence by sentence

### What the definitions ask (`B/Dissipation.lean`)

- `ClosedAt cs t` (:237): no formation **taking part** in the step from `t` (`L.present t`) takes from or gives to a condition outside the list `cs`. It is said of a family of *conditions* and one step. Formations are not members of the family (unless read with one kind of relatum, `Roles`).
- `DissipatesOn cs t` (:242): for every formation taking part, `total cs give + sign (total cs take) ≤ total cs take`: it gives the family no more than it takes, at least one whole unit less when it takes, and nothing when it takes nothing. Per formation, per step.
- `Dissipative` (:248): every family listed without repetition and closed across a step is dissipated on across it. It binds only where some finite family is closed.
- `Enclosed t` (:253): some family without repetition is closed across `t`. `Around ds t` (:259): some family without repetition holding `ds` is closed across `t`; the enclosing family may be a different one at each step.
- `Inward ds t` (:264): every formation taking part that gives to `ds` takes only from members of `ds`.
- `Flow.Balances` (`B/Flow.lean`:278): at every step and condition, level after plus taken equals level before plus given. Amounts are natural numbers of one unit.
- `FedFromBeyond ds t` (`B/Continuation.lean`:122): some formation taking part takes from a condition outside `ds` and gives to a member of `ds`.

### What the theorem does not bound (S0, S5)

1. **Resting on is not taking.** On the web a formation rests on a condition through `requires` and `need` (`Web.Supported`, `B/Dependence.lean`:134: every required need is at most the level). Taking is `Flow.take`. `Dissipative` speaks only of `take` and `give`. A need is a threshold on a level, not a share of it: any number of formations may require one standing level, and nothing in `Supported` divides it. The dig's own record says this ("Resting on something … and taking from it are apart on the web. Two of my guesses were worded as if they were one", `NOTE.md` line 18); R1 words them as one again.
2. **The package has a closed pair resting on each other for ever under dissipation.** `Mutualism.web` (`B/Roles.lean`:759): two relata, each requiring itself and the other at need one and drawing on the other (`draws`, a relation with no amount), both at level one and active throughout; each is embodied, bears on itself and is renewing (:777 to :783). `Space.mutualism` (`B/Space.lean`:379) is that web with `Flow.still`, in which nothing takes part; `Flow.still_dissipative` (:96) says that flow is dissipative; `mutualism_world` (:389) says the world is finite, nothing arises or ceases, every event is living, it is `Dependent` and `Embodied`. That the still flow balances this web is not stated there (levels constant, nothing exchanged; it holds by the definition, my reading).
3. **Going on with nothing taken.** `Flicker.flicker` (`B/Roles.lean`:1576): one relatum is all there is, the flow balances and is dissipative, and arising is unceasing; only embodiment fails.
4. **What is bounded besides taking.** A member of the family rising or coming to stand at arbitrarily late steps (`rising_opens` :651, `standing_opens` :659, `rising_draws_in` :683, `standing_draws_in` :691, `rising_not_recurring` :699), and, with one kind of relatum, embodied members arising (`Web.embodiedArising_opens`, `_draws_in`, `_not_recurring`, `B/Roles.lean`:695, :717, :725).
5. **"A flow from beyond passes through."** No source is named, no rate is stated, and none need recur: in `Lone` the pool is fed at every step and each fuel is conveyed from at exactly one step (`each_fuel_once`, `B/Continuation.lean`:622). With finitely many conditions in all there is no beyond and taking stops (S1), so what feeds a family without end is conditions without end, each a finite amount (`Frontier.frontier` :985, `Lone.frontier` :1217). That anything passes *out* is not concluded: under dissipation the family's stock falls by at least the number of takers (`Balances.runs_down` :387), and a `Flow` has no place the loss goes. Where no step is enclosed nothing follows (`Spray.spray` :1334; `SprayShuttle.spray_shuttle`, `B/Roles.lean`:1519, whose docstring says "arising recurs in a finite family into which nothing flows"; its statement was not read through).
6. **Which half of dissipation does the work.** `Dissipative` is nothing-from-nothing (`Flow.Gainless`, `B/Entropy.lean`:156) with a least loss of one unit per taking (`Dissipative.gainless` :181). Under the first alone a closed finite loop takes for ever: `Entropy.Passing.passes` (:1878: finite, balanced, `Conserving`, enclosed at every step, arising and ceasing recur, not dissipative), `Shuttle.shuttle` (`B/Roles.lean`:1262), `Cycle`, and `Overshoot.steady_continues` (below). By `Resolution`'s opening account (not opened at its statements) the least loss belongs to the fixed unit.

### Is `Dissipative` a premise a model may fail

Yes; it is a premise on a `Flow`, and the package says of its own examples which do.

- **Fail, by a theorem:** the six of `witnesses_not_dissipative`; `Brood` (`Brood.not_dissipative`, `B/Roles.lean`:1032, "giving without taking"); `Shuttle` (:1262, "the holder gives back all it takes"); `Passing` (`B/Entropy.lean`:1878).
- **Fail, by the definition, no theorem found** (searched `dissipat` in `Interdependence.lean`, `RenewalFlow.lean`): `Overshoot.steadyFlow` (each takes one and gives one within the two conditions that are all there is); the package's own flow for `RelationalRenewal.Law` (`SupportGated.flow`, `B/RenewalFlow.lean`:82: the sustaining formation gives the renewal and takes nothing, on one condition) wherever the renewal is given, `cultivation` among them.
- **Meet:** `Frontier` (:952) and `Rooted`, `Lone` (:1187), `Throughput` (`B/Roles.lean`:1356), each burning a fresh fuel at every step; `Spray` (:1327) and `SprayShuttle` (`B/Roles.lean`:1505), vacuously, no step enclosed; `Flicker` and `Flow.still`, nothing taking part; `Clearing` (`B/Clearing.lean`:136, statement not opened).

**November's models.** `LineOnAStock`, `WayOfUnits` and `TurningInTime` are not flows on a web: none names `Flow.Balances` or `Dissipative` (word counts: `Balances` 0, 0, 0; `dissipat` 0, 0, 0). Their level steps by `RegimeMargin.step giving H x = (x − H) + giving (x − H)` (`B/RegimeMargin.lean`:249; `LineOnAStock.left`, `B/LineOnAStock.lean`:146). The stock's giving is a function of what is left, handed in; there is no giver, so it is not "a giver that takes nothing" at the statements, and `Dissipative` can be neither met nor failed by them as they stand. `LineOnAStock`'s own account lists "the regime read as a `Web` with a `Flow`" as not there (:122). `RegimeMargin.FromNothing` (:252) is `g 0 = 0`, not `Gainless`. What can be said by inference from `Flow.Dissipative.one_taking_not_recurring` (`B/Dissipation.lean`:1361), and is in no statement: a balanced record of a course of the line on a web whose only condition is the level, with units taking at arbitrarily late steps, is not dissipative however the giving is recorded; to meet dissipation the record needs conditions without end from which the giving is conveyed, as `Lone`'s fuels. A course with nothing given has no giving to record (`MultiplyingTakersCase`, `B/LineOnAStockCases.lean`:92, is one; whether the web example it is set beside is stated dissipative was not checked). `LineageRegimes`' opening account (lines 99 to 100, AI-written) says the same of the web in general: "a formation's giving is not drawn from its own level unless it takes, so 'from outside' means from no listed condition."

### Is the draft's conclusion fair

In part. Fair: a family of conditions, listed without repetition, from which formations **take** at arbitrarily late steps cannot stay closed under balance and dissipation at a fixed unit, and where its steps are enclosed around it from some step on, it is not from any step on left unfed from a condition outside it. Not fair: that this bounds "things that are among what each other rests on". Whether the second way's loop is one of taking or of resting is exactly what the theorem turns on, and the Lean does not settle which was meant.

## 3. R2 against `Dependence.lean` and the correction of 18 November

`Web.RenewingIn f` (`B/Dependence.lean`:166): for every step and condition where `f` requires or draws on it, `Renewed c` (:150): every dependence of **any** formation on `c`, at each step it holds, is met at that step and, at that step's need, at the next. `Web.ConsumingIn f` (:169): at some step `f` draws on a condition that is `Consumed` (:155): some dependence on it holding at a step `T` is met at no later step.

- "needs stay met": right as a short form. It leaves out that the needs are everyone's on what `f` requires or draws on, and that a formation requiring and drawing on nothing is renewing (both in the correction, line 41).
- "or are lost for good": **not so.** `Consumed`'s docstring (:153): "It need not have been met at that step; `Lost` requires that." `Lost` (:159) is a narrower definition; `Unmet.consumed_without_loss` (:386) is a web in which nothing is ever broken or lost and the drawer is consuming. The correction says this; R2 drops it and uses the Lean's own word for the narrower thing.
- "or": the two do not exhaust the cases (`fluctuating_neither`, `deeper/RelationalRenewal.lean`:217; the correction, line 44).
- "they do not say that anything remakes anything": right at the definitions. A `Web` is `Dependence Nat F C` (`B/World.lean`:57), whose fields are `level`, `active`, `requires`, `need`, `draws` (:48 to :53); no giving.
- "they have no selecting in them": right at the definitions.
- Left out against the correction: each class is of a whole course (step classes are separate, `RenewingAt` :294); the web records that a formation draws, not how much; `RelationalRenewal` defines its own two classes for courses with fixed dependences (:132, :135), which are the web's on the static web (`static_renewingIn` :206, `static_consumingIn` :211).

## 4. The third sense (arising as something others can rest on)

The record's paragraph (`NOTE.md` line 30) at the statements:

- `Embodied.arising_lowers_on` (`B/Roles.lean`:650): balance, `DissipatesOn ds t`, `x` embodied and in `ds`, `x` arises across `t`; then the stock of `ds` falls across `t`. As told, except "to which every formation then gives less than it takes": `DissipatesOn` is no more than it takes, less when it takes, nothing when it takes nothing.
- `rises_late_of_newly_supported` is `Web.rises_late_of_newly_supported`, in `B/Gain.lean`:205. Premises: steps `at_ n` increasing, all from `T`; each `f n` requires, fixed, only members of `cs` (`RequiresWithin` :179); each `f n` is **first supported from `T`** at `at_ n` (`FirstSupported` :192). Conclusion: the stock of `cs` rises at arbitrarily late steps. No flow, no balance, no dissipation in it. It is about support at a configuration not seen since `T`, not about arising or activity.
- `KeepsWidening.passes_every_bound` is `Web.KeepsWidening.passes_every_bound`, `B/KeepingWidening.lean`:287: keeping widening by name on the actual levels from `T`, and every formation supported from `T` on requiring, fixed, only members of `cs`; then the stock of `cs` exceeds every bound at arbitrarily late steps.
- `rise_from_rest_or_open` is `Flow.rise_from_rest_or_open`, `B/Gain.lean`:279: under `Gainless` (not `Dissipative`; the latter gives it), a family without repetition whose stock rises across a step is given net by the rest (what the flow does not count) or is not closed there.
- What `Roles` states of the third sense itself: a formation depending at a positive need on an embodied relatum is active only while that relatum is (`Embodied.active_of_bearing` :441); an embodied relatum arises only as another active formation gives to it (`Embodied.arises_given` :505) and is given at least the standing it arises with (`given_of_arises` :608); with finitely many relata in all, under balance and dissipation, embodied relata do not arise at arbitrarily late steps (`embodiedArising_not_recurring` :725).

**Is "under dissipation the third sense runs the other way" right?** Of what it lists, yes. As a heading it says more. The laws price the **arising** (each embodied arising in a family dissipated on costs the family stock, so it cannot recur in a closed finite family) and **endless first support** (formations newly supportable without end, with fixed needs in a finite family, need its stock to rise without end). None prices the **resting**: once something stands, any number of formations may require it with nothing more taken (a need is a threshold; `Mutualism` is such a pair under a dissipative flow), and what is not embodied arises at no cost (`Flicker`). So "that more can then go on without more being taken is no law" is true in that no theorem states it, and also no theorem denies it; on the web's definitions it is allowed wherever the more only requires and does not take.

## 5. Is there a law of several goings-on that go on without end by passing on what comes from a source beyond them, with the source in the statement

**Not found in what was read.** What is nearest, by kind:

- **Necessary, not sufficient, with "beyond" in the statement.** `Flow.taking_draws_in` (`B/Dissipation.lean`:587), `Flow.fed_from_beyond` (`B/Continuation.lean`:180), `Flow.rising_draws_in` (:683), `Web.embodiedArising_draws_in` (`B/Roles.lean`:717): if taking (rising, embodied arising) recurs in an enclosed family then it is fed from beyond. Nothing in them passes on within the family, and no source is named.
- **Passing on, defined, with what feeds the top in the statement, over a finite stretch.** `Flow.PassesOn` (`B/Weightings.lean`:2995: each formation gives one family at most what it takes from another); `Flow.uptake_le_tiers` (:3069): what is drawn from the bottom of a hierarchy across a stretch is at most what the hierarchy held and what was given to its top; `Flow.needsSome_tiers` (:3109): a formation drawing from the bottom for longer than all the stocks together needs a giver among the class `K` that alone feeds the top. A bound and a need, not a going on without end.

## Findings

**MUST**

- **M1. R1's headline says "resting on" where the theorem says "taking".** The package's `Mutualism` (`B/Roles.lean`:759, `B/Space.lean`:379 to :397 with `Flow.still_dissipative` :96) is a closed pair resting on each other for ever under a dissipative flow. *Put instead:* "**R1. Under October's premise of dissipation a closed family of conditions cannot go on being taken from; resting on is not bounded.** Under `Flow.Dissipative` (across a step, every formation taking part gives a family that is listed without repetition and closed across that step no more than it takes from it, and at least one unit less when it takes) and balance, with finitely many conditions in all, no formation takes from any family at arbitrarily late steps (`Dissipative.finite_taking_not_recurring`)."
- **M2. R1's conclusion, "such a loop goes on only as something a flow from beyond passes through", says more than `taking_draws_in` and `fed_from_beyond`.** They ask enclosure around the family from some step on and a family without repetition; they conclude that it is not, from any step on, left with no formation that takes from a condition outside it and gives to a member; no source is named, none need recur (`each_fuel_once`), nothing is said to pass out, and where no step is enclosed nothing follows (`Spray`). *Put instead:* "So where the second way is a loop in which each **takes** from what another gives, a theorem already there bounds it: closed, it runs down within its stock (`Balances.takers_within`); going on, its conditions are not closed from any step on, and where each step's exchange with them lies within finitely many conditions they are not, from any step on, left unfed from a condition outside them (`taking_draws_in`; `Flow.fed_from_beyond`, in `Continuation.lean`). No source is named and none need recur. Where each only **rests on** the other's standing, a need the level must meet with nothing taken, the theorem does not bound it (`Mutualism`). Which of the two I meant this morning I had not said."
- **M3. R2's "are lost for good".** `ConsumingIn` asks `Consumed`, which does not ask that the need was ever met; `Lost` is the Lean's narrower definition (`B/Dependence.lean`:153 to :160; `Unmet.consumed_without_loss` :386). *Put instead:* "renewing says that every need on what a formation requires or draws on stays met, and consuming that it draws on something some need on which, as it stood at one step, is met at no later step; the need need not ever have been met, and a formation can be neither."

**LESSER**

- **L1 (S4).** "The examples … that are closed (`Cycle` and five others)": six flows on one condition in which taking recurs; two give back what they take (`Cycle`, `Repeated`), four have a giver that takes nothing (`Expansion`, `Pulse`, `Steady`, `Oscillation`). Others fail by their own theorems (`Brood`, `Shuttle`, `Passing`) and nine meet dissipation (list in section 1).
- **L2 (S2, S3).** "listed without repetition" is a premise of `opens`, `taking_draws_in` and `fed_from_beyond`; `fed_from_beyond` is in `Continuation.lean` and asks equality of conditions decided; both draw-in conclusions are denials of "never from some step on".
- **L3.** A family in these theorems is a list of conditions, not of goings-on; "a loop of things" fits only with one kind of relatum (`Roles`).
- **L4.** What does the bounding is the least loss of one unit per taking, not nothing-from-nothing: under `Gainless` or `Conserving` alone a closed finite loop goes on (`Entropy.Passing.passes`, `Shuttle`, `Cycle`, `Overshoot.steady_continues`).
- **L5.** November's three models are not flows; "the line's stock gives by what is left" is a function handed in (`RegimeMargin.step`), with no giver. `Dissipative` cannot be said of them as they stand; that any balanced one-condition record of an unending course of the line fails it is an inference from `one_taking_not_recurring`, in no statement.
- **L6.** The dig's record, line 23, "Nothing passes between the conditions, giving costs nothing and there are no units": as the Lean. Not said there: `Coupled.Indexed.fates` is a general law that concludes levels holding for ever (something at rest from the threshold on), against the record's line 17 that every general law read concludes an ending or a fact about a level; `fates` is a fact about a level, and it is one of going on.
- **L7.** `Overshoot.steady_continues` is the package's plainest closed loop of two that pass to each other; it is a built example, fails dissipation by the definition, and no theorem says so.
- **L8 (third sense).** The record's "every formation then gives less than it takes" should be "no more than it takes, and less when it takes"; its `Gain.*` names are `Web.rises_late_of_newly_supported` and `Flow.rise_from_rest_or_open` in `Gain.lean`; the latter is under `Gainless`. Its heading "runs the other way" holds of arising and of endless first support, not of being rested on.
- **L9 (R2).** Left out against the correction: the classes are of a whole course; the web records drawing, not how much; `RelationalRenewal` has its own pair for fixed dependences, equal to the web's on the static web.
- **L10.** `ReaderRebuilding` and `RuleRebuilding`, which the draft lists under "What I do not know", each have a source said in their statements (the supply; the renewal `g`) and conclude a kept region and, for the second, an exact band; neither has units that multiply, a cost of giving to the giver, or a selection, and neither is joined to `Flow`.
