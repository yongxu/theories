# R6 against the Lean: the market model

Paths: `F/` is `experiments/market-generativity/formal/`; `B/` is the package's `Becoming/`.

## Verdict

R6 is right that this model is nearer to "two ways placed with respect to each other" than anything the direction page names, and right that the page and the plan never name it. It is wrong in one clause ("the charge that comes back"), says more than the Lean in two (attraction and instability are proved at β = 1/2 only; no boundary between the two ways is stated in the state, only in the erosion parameter), and is short where the model is strongest for the direction: three later files state when a course can still be turned and when it cannot, and a fourth joins the model to the package.

## 1. The model exactly

**State** (`F/MarketGenerativity.lean` l.151-153): `x` (the renewing practice's share), `q` (quality of the charging mechanism), `w` (the other practice's influence), `budget` (the reserve that pays correction), and `stock : Fin n → Rat` (any finite number of supports). Five components, not three. The three-number state `(x, q, w)` is `RegimeBoundary.OperatingState` (`F/RegimeBoundary.lean` l.214), a projection in which correction is paid at every step and there is no error (l.216-219).

**One step** (`operatingStep`, l.197-203, from the definitions at l.9-23):

| Component | Next value | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| share | `x + η·x·(1−x)·(fine·q + lobby − cost)` | `x`, `q` |
| quality | `clip01((1 − α·u − β·w)·q + α·u − error)`, `u = 1` if `fee ≤ budget`, else `0` | `q`, `w`, `budget`, the disturbance |
| influence | `(1−μ)·w + μ·λ·(1−x)` | `w`, `x` |
| reserve | `min cap (budget − fee·u + levy)` | `budget` |
| stock `j` | `max 0 ((1−dep)·stock + (renewal+damage)·x − damage − baseline − shock)` | that stock, `x`, the disturbance |

**Supplied**: every parameter (`Parameters`, l.142-145: price, levy, cost, fine, lobby, η, α, β, μ, λ, fee, cap; per stock its depreciation, renewal, damage, baseline, and whether it is `essential`), the bounds (`Bounds`, l.147-149: `xb qb wb ε`, per stock a floor and a shock bound), and the disturbances. The file's own head says so (l.3-5): "The policy, charges, reserve levy, measurement bounds, causal stock effects and protected floors are supplied inputs."

**What follows, generally** (any parameters): under `Certificate p b` (21 inequalities, l.159-181) the rectangle `Safe p b` is kept by every step under every admissible disturbance (`rectangle_one_step` l.205, `rectangle_all_times` l.259), and a guarded history from it never stops (`safe_history_never_fails` l.299).

**What selects what, in the model's terms.**

- The share's step is a function of the share itself and of `q`, through the gap `fine·q + lobby − cost`. Price and levy cancel (`profits_difference` l.25-27). The factor `x·(1−x)` means a share of none stays none (`no_entry_at_zero` l.391; `zero_share_remains_zero`, `F/RegimeAttraction.lean` l.574).
- The stock enters no other component. `supportStep` is read only in the `stock` field of `operatingStep`. This is by definition; as a theorem it is stated for particular parameter sets: the `(x, q, w)` history of the full model from `lift s initial` is the projection's history for every `initial` stock (`coupled_histories`, `F/RegimeAttraction.lean` l.499-501; likewise `pulse_coupling_history`, `partial_coupling_history`).
- The stock does enter one thing: the guard. `guardedStep` (l.276-280) returns `none` when an essential stock is below its floor at the start or the end of a step, and `none` is kept (`failed_market_absorbing` l.291). So the stock makes no difference to which share is selected, and decides whether there is a market at all.
- `q` is eroded by `β·w·q` and restored by `α·u·(1−q)`; `u` is 1 only where the reserve at the start of the step covers the fee (`payment_guard` l.40, `later_receipt_cannot_pay_earlier_correction` l.378). The reserve is fed by a levy that both practices pay alike (levy is in `profitR` and `profitE`, l.10-11).
- `w` moves a fraction `μ` of the way toward `λ·(1−x)`: it follows the other share with a lag. The lobbying spend `lobby` is a constant in the gap and does not set `w`; `λ` is supplied (README l.22).

## 3. What the draft does not say and should

**The margin** (`F/RegimeBoundary.lean`), for general parameters, not the family. With `M = α(1−θ) − β·λ·θ` (l.8-9):

- `exact_floor_boundary` (l.20-24), for `α, β ≥ 0`, `α + β ≤ 1`, `0 ≤ λ ≤ 1`, `0 < θ ≤ 1`: with correction paid and no error, `q` stays at or above `θ` after one step from every `q` in `[θ, 1]` and every `w` in `[0, λ]`, exactly where `M ≥ 0`. A one-step statement about `q` alone.
- `exact_low_certificate_boundary` (l.155-160), for `α > 0`, `β ≥ 0`, `0 ≤ λ ≤ 1`, `0 < θ < 1`: a low certificate exists exactly where `M < 0`. A low certificate (l.52-54) gives a box `0 ≤ x ≤ xd`, `0 ≤ q ≤ qd < θ`, `wd ≤ w ≤ λ` that every step keeps (`low_box_all_times` l.247, under sign and rate premises), and inside it a positive share falls strictly (`strict_loss_of_renewing_share`).

So for general parameters the model states: a region in which the selection goes against the renewing practice at every step for ever exists exactly where `M < 0`. The README's own limit (l.86): "an invariant region, not a proved strict attractor or a complete basin boundary."

**Passing from one way to the other** (`F/RegimeTransition.lean`, `F/PartialRegimeTransition.lean`). There is such a statement, in one direction, under a change of β that is handed in:

- `exact_universal_landing_boundary` (`F/PartialRegimeTransition.lean` l.279-280), in the family, for `0 ≤ β ≤ 3/4`: every state of the unit box with a positive share reaches `Landing` (`x ≥ 3/4`, `q ≥ 1/2`, `w ≤ 1/4`) in finitely many steps, exactly where `β ≤ 5/12`. This is the three-number projection, with no stock.
- `supercritical_low_box_never_lands` (l.291-293): for `5/12 < β ≤ 3/4` there is a low box from which no state ever lands.
- `supported_finite_transition` (`F/RegimeTransition.lean` l.264-275, β = 0 for the pulse) and `supported_partial_transition` (`F/PartialRegimeTransition.lean` l.416-427, any `0 ≤ β ≤ 5/12`): for a positive share there is `N` such that, with initial stock at least the cover `K_N` (`K_0 = 5`, `K_{n+1} = (10/9)(K_n + 1 + cost)`, l.161-163), the guarded full history runs through the pulse, arrives in the safe rectangle, and then, with β back at 1/2, never fails. Sufficient, not least.
- `no_uniform_pulse_duration` (l.349): no one `N` serves every positive share. `arbitrarily_small_error_blocks_universal_landing` (`F/PartialRegimeTransition.lean` l.334): at β = 5/12 an arbitrarily small constant error leaves a positive fixed state that never lands.
- No statement was found, in the seven files, of a course that passes from the high way to the low way.

**A point of no return** (`F/FiniteSupportObstruction.lean`). `every_finite_stock_has_obstructed_positive_seeds` (l.210-214): for every stock `≥ 5` there are `N > 0` and a positive `seedLimit N = 1/(6·(3/2)^N)` such that from every share `0 < x ≤ seedLimit N`, every `q` in `[0, 1]` and every `w`, the guarded history is `none` by step `N` under every schedule of β, every error and every nonnegative shock. Not "no finite set of supports suffices": every finite stock has positive shares that no change of the erosion can save, the share's growth rate and the stock's law being fixed. `support_required_for_small_prefix` (l.132-138) gives the stock needed to survive such a prefix; `projected_recovery_and_actual_failure` (l.243-247) gives one state that lands in the projection and whose full history stops; `too_late_even_without_extra_cost` (`F/RegimeTransition.lean` l.310) is one built state. The README calls the interval conservative, "not the exact viability boundary" (l.159).

**Is anything a statement of "can still be turned"?** Yes, in three forms, none a basin boundary in the state:

1. exact in the erosion: every positive share turns exactly where `β ≤ 5/12` (projection only);
2. sufficient with the stock: enough cover carries the turn through;
3. sufficient against: a share too small for the stock cannot be turned by any erosion schedule.

Between 2 and 3 nothing is stated, except at the worst share: the stock alone lasts `n` steps at or above 5 exactly where it is at least `K_n` (`MarketInstances.cover_exact`, `F/MarketInstances.lean` l.328-330, at `x = 0`). At β = 1/2 the state has two attracting boxes and one escaping cone at the middle; no surface between two basins is stated.

## 6. R6's last sentence, taken whole

"a selection, a rule set again through the share's influence, a cost of renewing, what comes back, a stock rested on, two ways of going on with a boundary between them, and the stock running down unseen by the selection."

- *A selection*: yes.
- *A rule set again through the share's influence*: yes as a reading, with the rule keeping a memory and a restoring term, so not "set again from the share" as the lock has it.
- *A cost of renewing*: yes, in money. The stock's renewal `renewal·x` is a separate supplied coefficient; no amount passes from the cost to the stock.
- *What comes back*: no. A charge on the other.
- *A stock rested on*: yes, by the market as a whole through a guard, with the floor and which stocks count handed in. Neither practice and no step of the selection rests on it.
- *Two ways of going on*: at β = 1/2, in two boxes: from one the guarded history never stops, from the other it stops in finite time.
- *A boundary between them*: not in the state. In the erosion, yes.
- *The stock running down unseen by the selection*: yes, by definition of the step; a theorem for the example's parameters.

**What the model lacks against the direction.** Nothing lets the stock reach the selection before the floor: the charge is on a practice's share, not on the stock's level, so "early enough" is absent by construction and the guard is the only place the stock is felt. Shares, not units that multiply, and no entry from none. The mechanism, the floors and which stocks are essential are supplied; the README names why such a mechanism forms as its open question (l.50). One grain. No course from the high way to the low. Attraction at one β. The renewal of the stock has no source in amounts. And it is joined to the package only through four characterisations of any law of change, not to the lock, to turning in time, to the line on a stock or to renewing and consuming on the web.
