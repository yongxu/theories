## Place 1. `conditional/renewal` (eight files, namespace `Generative.Conditional.Renewal`)

**Mechanism supplied?** Yes. `ChannelLaw` (`RenewalAccounting` 41-45, "Supplied local law of a constraint–flow system") has four fields: `edge : Kind → Kind → Prop`, `span`, `demand`, `rate : Kind → Nat`. `RenewalPremises` 25-26: "The law itself, kinds, edges, spans, demands and rates, is the remaining supplied input. Deriving it from the generative core is roadmap Phase 4."

**The model** (`RenewalAccounting`). A configuration is a reserve and a list of tokens, each a kind and the ticks it has left (`Token` 31, `Config` 36). One tick (`channelProcess` 141-145) with action `births : List Kind`: (1) `c'.tokens = aged c.tokens ++ formed law births`; (2) `c'.reserve + formation law births ≤ c.reserve + opened law.rate c.tokens`; (3) `∀ k, k ∈ births → ∃ tok, tok ∈ aged c.tokens ∧ law.edge tok.kind k`. `Live c` (148): some token has time left. `renewingProcess` (151-152): a tick with `Live c` before it.

**Q1. What is rested on, and is its being there a going-on's doing?** A formation of kind `k` needs two things. A supporter: a token that is still there after this tick's aging, of a kind with an edge to `k` (clause 3; docstring 139-140: "Every formed token needs a support token that is still alive after aging, so it existed before this tick"). And provision: the formations' demand is paid from the reserve plus what live tokens open this tick (clause 2; `opened` 61-63, a live token of kind `k` contributes `rate k`). Both are another going-on's being or doing: tokens are formed, age and drop out (`aged` 50-53). Nothing here is a level whose giving is a function of levels handed in. What is handed in is how much a live token of each kind opens, how long it lasts, what forming it demands, and which kind can support which.

**Q2. From where, and at what cost?** From beyond, and not as a quantity of the model. Header of `RenewalAccounting` 12-13: "The reserve law is the U1 law of `ResourceContinuation` with arrival bounded by what live tokens open; raw flow outside is unlimited." `RenewalClosure` 10-11: "No organization arrives from outside after time zero; raw flows are not represented here and may remain external." The cost stated is the cost of forming, charged to the common reserve: `opening_step` (167-170, premise: one step of `channelProcess law`): `openingPotential law c' + formation law births ≤ openingPotential law c + weighted law.rate (formed law births)`. No cost of giving falls on the giver: supporting is an existence condition (one supporter may support any number of formations), and opening comes out of a span that falls by one each tick whether or not anything is formed (`weighted_aged` 84-85).

**Q3. Is there a loop?** Yes, two, and they are joined in one definition. Support: `DependenceClosed` (`RenewalClosure` 211-212), "every member depends on a member and supports a member". Provision: tokens are formed out of the reserve, and the reserve is fed only through what live tokens open. `SelfRenewing law B` (`RenewalCharacterization` 66-67) is `B ≠ [] ∧ SupportClosed law B ∧ formation law B ≤ spanOpening law B`, where `SupportClosed` (61-62) is `∀ k, k ∈ B → ∃ j, j ∈ B ∧ law.edge j k ∧ 2 ≤ law.span j` and `spanOpening` sums `rate k * span k`. The loop is found in histories and not only assumed: `late_occurrence_descends_from_realized_closure` (`RenewalClosure` 336-343) and `live_late_descends_from_realized_closure` (`RenewalLink` 288-296; premises: `X : Execution law`, `U` with `∀ k, k ∈ U`, `∀ k, law.span k ≤ D`, `Live (X.states t)`, `(U.length + 1) * D < t`) give a closed walk of support kinds each formed within the preceding `(U.length + 1) * D` ticks; `RenewalLink` 15-17 calls this "the timescale separation of Montévil and Mossio, here as a consequence rather than a definition". What the loop passes round comes from beyond it (Q2). A built example shows a provider outside every support closure: `danglingSupport` (894-898), `dangling_kind_outside_every_closure` (909-912), `dangling_support_renews_forever` (921); docstring 917-920: "Read only through support, a closure misses it. Read with provision as a second kind of dependence, `true` is generative for `false`."

**Q4. General laws concluding going on without end.** All hold of every `ChannelLaw` meeting the premises; in all of them "going on" is that *some* history stays live, not that the history that occurs does.
