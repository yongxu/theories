## Where something comes to be registered without the reading being handed a change

Six shapes, each told with what its reader says is handed in:

1. **By the going-on's own act** (`ReadingLoop.sensed_alike_iff`, a general law, the first reader): for every blanketed law, from starts with equal internal, sensory and active parts, the values sensed agree through a step exactly where, at each earlier step, the two worlds answer alike the act taken at the value sensed. So two worlds come to be told apart at the first step their answers to the formation's own act differ. Nothing is handed in beyond the law and the starts. A built explorer shows a cost: trying costs the floor once.
2. **What is kept can keep it from ever being registered** (`ReadingLoop.alike_of_kept`, general, with `floor_forgoes` on two built worlds): where every answer that would tell the worlds apart breaks a task in the second world, kept there at every step, keeping the task keeps them sensed alike. The first reader: "This is the one statement in the group found that ties what can come to be registered to what the formation keeps."
3. **A loss that reaches a fixed reading through couplings** (`ReadOffInTime`: `FirstApart`, `admits_iff_gap_within_margin`, `readableInTime_iff`; general, on a group handed in): the loss travels through couplings and crosses a margin, and so reaches a selection whose reading did not change. By its docstring, read off the continuations of what is lost, the loss is read only after its point of no return (`too_late_of_own`, under premises on what the kept levels admit that the report lists).
4. **A loss borne by those who shape the rule** (`PoolFromCourse.Borne`): a loss borne by the shapers enters the counts the selection already reads (`bear_shaped_shapers`, `borne_falls`, general); in a built example (`shapers_left`) it leaves the lock with no reader of it. Borne by the others it does not leave the lock. Who bears it and at what rate is handed in.
5. **A failure of the going-on's own act, read by a rule** (`ContinuingRevision`, with `ReferencePlan.revise_registers_failure` as the general law, the third reader): the act fails at an address not registered; after revising, probing and installing, that address is registered (in a built family). The rule that proposes is handed in, with a supplied policy and a supplied probe channel, and a blind rule leaves some failure unregistered (`frozen_misses`). The second reader read `Contacts.lean` lines 135 to 184, including the statement of `answers_iff`, from its statement and docstring with no proof, and gives it as the exact condition: a revision rule answers every failure exactly where its output contains the failed address, the rule handed in. The third names it, unread, as probably holding the general law. The third reader calls this, beside the first guess's three shapes, a fourth form: "a failure read by a rule handed in".
6. **A miss that moves the candidate held** (`Learning.index_missed`, the second reader): the held candidate changes because of a miss; the miss is the course's own doing, but only candidates already in the supplied enumeration can be held.

One more, from this row's own record and not from these readers (the coordinating session's reminder): in the course of 19 November, found by program in a model that is not in the Lean, the level reached the rule through the need of the unit the rule counts, which left nothing; the rule then no longer favoured. It is a built course in which something the rule did not read came to make a difference to it, and by an ending.

Outside the package the fourth reader found what is registered to be sometimes the effect of the thing itself: the access loss the agent's own practice causes (`GoalFormationRevision`), the outage pattern a shared dependency causes (`CommonCauseDiscovery.observation_is_effect`); and, built with the demand handed in, a rule that changes only if the old credit would not carry the demand from the fund and the new one would (`GoalRevision`).

The first reader's remark on its three shapes, which I take as the limit of this section: they "change what reaches a fixed reading, not the reading … Whether they meet 'other than by an update handed in' depends on whether the item asks for a change in what is registered or in what reaches it; this read does not decide that."

## The gap stated as an absence of registering

And where a level does set a change of rule: in `TurningInTime`'s window laws (`locked_turns_in_time_on_window_iff` and its neighbours) the level entering a window triggers the reading, and which levels are read is handed in; in `ReadInTime` the loss passing a bound handed in switches a reading handed in (the third and first readers).

## What reading costs, and what bounds it

- **A reader that reads one condition a step cannot keep everything** (`ReadingSchedule`, general laws, the first reader): a schedule keeps a condition within a margin against every balanced flow's draws exactly where it reads that condition within every so many steps (`keeps_iff_within`, under the premises the report lists); the windows within which one schedule reads distinct conditions are bounded together (`density`); and a stretch left unread lets a part's draw take enough that a formation admitted without the part is lost (`Balances.loses_of_unread`).
- **In what was read, stock enters as a precondition, not as content** (the second reader): it is drawn per sample, per slot and per training step; in `ResponseLearning` a program is kept only while each teacher has at least seven units and each fresh formation is supplied twenty-five.
- **In one constructed process** (`ContinuingRevision`, the third reader), every run repeats exactly where the yield is at least nine, and with the return cut no unending run exists; these are laws of that process, not of every model. In a built example, forming a distinction can use up the last support for using it (`opens_without_correction`).

## A registering lost

Losing is general and exact, by the second and third readers: where the old relation is an equivalence, any pair the new one joins and the old told apart gives a selection through the old that is not through the new (`Learning.Training.lost_choice`, in `Revision.lean`); a reading read off the old one through a supplied map, with nothing more executable, opens no correction (`coarser_opens_nothing`); in `SharedCodes`, a form every formation holds at a sense stays through every order of hearings under every rule (`held_stays`); no inverse restores what a readout merged (`Readout.no_restore`). A merged distinction comes back only from a form kept outside or from a coining, which is a bare supplied step. In built cases closing is undone by rebuilding, given stock; in `ResponseLearning` both losing and repairing are built examples. The first reader qualifies it for the lock: that "in the general laws not being brought back is a premise … not derived"; and the second did not find, in its group, that a reader removed is not brought back. ## What it shows, set against the direction

This is my reading, held loosely; no report states it.

The item as the page words it was not found met in three of the four groups, and the first reader left it undecided for one law. No law read has a reading change itself with nothing handed in, and none has a fall in what a going-on rests on cause a new registering without the reading of it handed in.

What the Lean does have, drawn already and in several places, is how a difference comes to reach a going-on: by its own act (`sensed_alike_iff`); by a failure of its own act, where a rule handed in has an output that contains the failed address (`Contacts.answers_iff`, read by one reader at its statement); by a loss travelling through couplings, readable in time exactly where the first step at which it can be told comes before the point of no return, on a group handed in; and within what one reader can read in time. It has, as a general law in the readings group, that where every answer telling two worlds apart breaks a task kept in the second, keeping it keeps them sensed alike. And it states the gap itself as an absence of registering in at least three general laws.

These are pieces of the gap the idea names, between what a going-on rests on and what makes a difference to it. They are stated on different carriers, and no reader was asked whether they join. Two would have to be read against each other before anything is proposed: the condition under which a rule answers every failure, and the lock's law on a level (`locked_turns_in_time_on_window_iff`), whose reading of the level is handed in. In the first the rule is handed in too, so it cannot derive what the second hands in; what could be asked is whether the second's handed-in reading is a case of the first's rule, so that one thing is handed in where two were. That is not known, and it is the next thing I would look at, on paper, with what would count against it written first.
