## 1. Learning.lean

1. **What registers what.** A course `o : Nat → O` is read only through its prefix `prefixOf o t` (l.150). An extrapolation is a function handed in, `pred : List O → Option O` (l.203). In extrapolation by elimination, what reaches the next step is a state: the index of the current candidate, `indexOn H` (l.351), over an enumeration `H : Nat → List O → Option O` that is handed in. In the training section the selection goes through the data (`Training.Through (AgreeOn D)`, l.931, 947). In the section on maps, a selection through a map `r` is a selection through its lumping (`selects_through_lumping`, l.993).
2. **Does it change, and by what.** The index changes exactly where the current candidate misses: `index_missed` (l.364), `(refuted : MissesAt (byElimination H) o t) → index H o (t+1) = index H o t + 1`, and `index_kept` (l.368). So the cause of the change is the miss, a difference between what was proposed and the value the course took. The enumeration `H`, and with it every candidate that can be held, is handed in and never changes; in the induction laws it is `lassos` (l.674). The map through which the course is read does not change. Nothing changing it was found in what was read.
3. **A law in which something not registered comes to be registered.** The nearest are laws in which a proposal comes to be right, not laws in which a new difference comes to be registered:
   - `index_le_of_accords` (l.393), a general law. Premises: `H` and `o` (variables), `[DecidableEq O]`, `(holds : Accords (H j) o)`. Conclusion: `∀ t, index H o t ≤ j`. "The extrapolation by elimination never passes a candidate that accords."
   - `held_byElimination_bounded` (l.759), a general law. Premises: `{next : S → S} {s} {l : List S} (held : ∀ n, Law.orbit next s n ∈ l) (reg : S → O)`, with values decided. It misses fewer than `tri l.length` times and proposes at every step from `l.length` on. "given neither the configurations nor their number".
   - `held_rest_unrefuted` (l.778). Coming to rest is only `¬¬∃ T`; the docstring says the step "is not stated here".
   - What is handed in: the enumeration `lassos`, the map `reg`, decided values, and a world held by a list. The cause is the miss. That is something the course does to the proposal, not a teacher, though only candidates the enumeration already holds can be reached. `diagonal_misses` (l.298) and `merged_unrested` (l.816) are general laws: on its diagonal course no extrapolation rests, over every merging of classes. The docstring reads this as "no top level: learning at the next level up is again learning, and again incomplete."
4. **Cost, who bears it, what keeps it.** No stock or draw occurs in this module (not found in what was read). The rest is kept only while the course stays a lasso: `lasso_of_rest` (l.730), where it rests the course repeats.
5. **A registering lost.** Two laws:

## 5. LearnedGrouping.lean

1. **What registers what.** A state holds a formed apparatus and a `log` of observation slots (l.66). The grouping is `bond s.log`, the majority of the transitions logged (Segmentation). 2. **Change.** The log changes by reception. `receive event s` (l.159) draws one unit and appends the slot; without stock it does nothing. So are the majority threshold and the decoder; the module account says "The sounds, the slots, the majority threshold, the decoder, the encounters and the prepared formation are supplied". `broadenAfterFailure raw more s = bif (parse s raw).isNone then expose more s else s` (l.176), docstring "A supplied policy: where the utterance does not parse, receive further slots."
3. **A law of coming to be registered.** Yes, in built examples only:
   - `reception_changes_parse` (l.386): every trace of reception from `ready 28` along `context.map some` ends where the utterance, unparsed and unanswered before, parses to `intended` and is answered. Handed in: the context, the stock (28), the threshold, the decoder.

   - `parsed_not_broadened` (l.451): a parsed message does not broaden.

4. **Cost, who bears it, what keeps it.** Each slot received draws one unit of the receiver's stock: `receive_stock` (l.180), `total_kept` (l.351), a general law over every trace, "keeps stock plus drawn". `support_used` (l.409), a built example: stock falls from 14 to 4. No later loss of the log is modelled (not found).

3. **A law of coming to be registered.**

Conclusion: `(∀ X i j, runBy always es X i m = runBy always es X j m) ↔ ∀ i j, origin m es i = origin m es j`. This is agreement coming to be, not a distinction. The order is handed in.
   - **The one step that makes a form is `coin`** (l.1344). "A formation *coins* the form `x` for the sense `m` ... Hearing makes no form (`hearBy_held`); coining does." It is a definition, supplied, with no cause stated.

"A coined form, heard by all, brings back a merged distinction." Handed in: the coining, the broadcast order, the rule `always`.
