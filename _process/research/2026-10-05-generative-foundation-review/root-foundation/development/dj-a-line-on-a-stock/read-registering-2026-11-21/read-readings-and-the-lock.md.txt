### ReadingLoop.lean
1. **What registers what.** (a) A supplied reading `B : M → W → Prop` of the world by the state, kept by a supplied `update : M → A → O → M` from a supplied `sense : W → O` (`joint`, 157; `KeptBy`, 166). (b) The history of acts and values sensed, whose reading is `Possible` (324), "where a course from a start, taking the history's acts and sensing its values, could be". (c) In `Loop` (544), a listed set of candidate laws `predict : H → S → A → S`; the internal part "holds the candidates kept and the last value sensed" (`law`, 575).
2. **Change and by what.** (a) by the handed-in update. (b) by recording, `record` (320), which is the identity of what is sensed appended; `possible_least` (334): `Possible` is the least reading kept by recording holding at the starts (premises: `KeptBy world sense record B`, `∀ w, start w → B [] w`). (c) by elimination against the world's answer: `Loop.kept_iff` (589), "at configuration `n + 1` from a start, a candidate is kept exactly where it was listed and predicts, at each step before `n`, the value sensed after it". Handed in: the list `hs`, each candidate's `predict`, `choose`, and the world's `answer` and `move`. "elimination, not weighting"; "how candidates are proposed" is left out (opening account, 'Modelling choices').
3. **Comes to register.** Yes, in the sense of two worlds coming to be told apart, by a general law: `sensed_alike_iff` (442), for every blanketed law `b`, from `x`, `y` with `x.internal = y.internal`, `x.sensory = y.sensory`, `x.active = y.active`: for every `n`, the sensed values agree at every `k ≤ n` exactly where, at each `k < n`, `b.nextSensory (b.course x k).sensory (b.course x k).active (b.course x k).external = b.nextSensory (b.course x k).sensory (b.course x k).active (b.course y k).external`. So a difference of worlds comes to be sensed at the first step at which the two worlds answer the formation's own act differently: the cause is the world's answer to what the formation does. Nothing is handed in beyond the blanketed law and the two starts. In `Loop`, that answer then drops the candidates it refutes (`kept_iff`). The built example `Untested` shows the formation's own act deciding whether the difference arrives: trying the untried act first separates the worlds (`explorer_up`, 858; `explorer_down`), and a worst-case chooser rests and never tells them apart (`rests`, 815; `alike_in_both`, 828).
4. **Cost.** In the built example, "Trying costs the floor once": in world `down` the value sensed is low at step one and at no other step (`explorer_low_once`, 873, no docstring of its own; docstring text of the preceding block). A general law over every formation on these two worlds: `floor_forgoes` (889), premises `∀ s a w, b.nextSensory s a w = reply s a w`, `∀ s a w, b.nextExternal s a w = w`, and `∀ n, (b.course ⟨i, s, a, .down⟩ n).sensory ≠ .low`; conclusion: at every `n`, sensed alike in both worlds and the known act taken in `down`. Docstring: "Keeping the floor forgoes what only the untried act shows." Its general form `alike_of_kept` (508): where every answer telling two worlds apart breaks a task `K` in the second, and `K` is kept at every step in the second, the values sensed agree at every step. Who bears it: the formation (it senses low). What keeps it: once a candidate is dropped it stays dropped (`kept_iff` is a conjunction over all earlier steps).
5. **Lost.** Candidates are only dropped, never re-listed (`kept_iff`). "The reading admitting every world" and "the reading admitting no world" are kept by every update (`everything_kept`, 258; `nothing_kept`, 268): being kept is not being informative. Candidates predicting alike along the course are kept alike (`kept_alike`, 624): "the formation's model lumps the candidates its own course does not tell apart."
6. **Tied to what it rests on.** `alike_within` (478) and `alike_of_kept` (508) tie it: where keeping a task is exactly not receiving the answers that would tell the worlds apart, keeping the task keeps the worlds unregistered. This is the one statement in the group found that ties what can come to be registered to what the formation keeps. `rests_of_unrefuted` (672): premises `L.answer s a e = s`, `L.move s a e = e`, `L.choose hs s = a`, `∀ h ∈ hs, L.predict h s a = s`; conclusion: the formation rests at once, same candidates, act and value at every step.

3. **Yes: a law in which the loss comes to make a difference, from a supplied bound.** `kept_iff` (155): premises `Accrues fB` (every step adds at least one to the loss), `lo' ≤ hi`, `reads r = true`, `N ≤ (course … s r).1.2` (the loss has reached its bound at `r`), and the rule in force at `r` favours the shapers; conclusion: locked in the shapers' favour at every step from `r + 1` exactly where `x.carrier * c + x.other * c ≤ x.carrier * hi + x.other * lo'` at the share at `r`. `turned_iff` (185): premises `Accrues fB`, `lo' ≤ hi`, `reads r = true`, `reads (r + 1) = true`, the loss at its bound at `r`, the late reading disfavouring at the share at `r`; conclusion: locked the other way at every step from `r + 2` exactly where the late reading favours the others at the share at `r + 1`. ### ClosedCapture.lean (lock_outlives_loss, turned_by_loss)
1. A pair: the share `a` and what lies beyond it `b` (a loss); a rule read `h`.
2. The reading `h` is handed in. Where the reading is formed from the share (`readers`, 412, `mind`), "the rule and its reading together are one rule read from the share" (`both_reads`, 425).
