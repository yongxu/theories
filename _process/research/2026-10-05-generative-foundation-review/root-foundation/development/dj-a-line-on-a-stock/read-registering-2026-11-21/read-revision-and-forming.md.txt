## 1. Revision.lean (`Registration`, `Revising`, `RevisionRuns`)

**Q1. What registers what.** What is registered is a lumping at each configuration, `alike : S → X → X → Prop` (`Registration.Keeps`, l72). The module account says "its index is not an outside selector". A selection reaches through it as `Learning.Training.Through`. In the built process `Revising.P` (l153), the reading is a coordinate of the state: `read s b = if s.2.1 then b else false` (l159), and `alike s b c := read s b = read s c` (l161). The link bit `s.2.1` decides whether the represented alternative is told apart.

**Q2. Does it change, and by what.** It changes by a move of the one process: `.rebuild b` sets the link to `b` and draws one unit of stock: `∃ n, s.1 = n + 1 ∧ t = (n, b, s.2.2)` (l155). The local laws are supplied: "These local laws are supplied" (docstring l150-152). Which move is taken is handed in as the controller `choose` (l234). Its docstring (l231-233): "It reads no hidden target bit. Its preference for forming the link is supplied, not derived from the old reading."

**Q3. A law where a difference not registered comes to be registered.**
- *Built example:* `Revising.controlled_revision` (l256). On the run `controlled` (the orbit of `next` from `(0,false,false)`): `controlled.act 1 = .rebuild true`, `controlled.state 2 = (0,true,false)`. Selection of `b = true` is not through `alike (state 0)` and is through `alike (state 2)`. Correction is not enabled at state 2. Then `act 2 = .replenish`, `act 3 = .correct`, and the response goes from `false` to `true`. **What is handed in:** the process `P`, the controller `choose`, the start. **The cause is the supplied controller, which reads the stock and the link status.** It is not the difference that was unregistered. `Revising.old_cannot_select_rebuild` (l199) states that it could not be: for every `n` and every `select : Bool → Prop` with `Through (alike (n,false,false)) select`, `select true ↔ select false`. "The old lumping cannot distinguish the two reconstruction choices, although one forms and the other removes the link."
- *General law:* `RevisionRuns.history_gains` (l412). Premises: any process `P`, any run `r`, any `obs : S → X → V`, `same : obs (r.state 0) x = obs (r.state 0) y`, `different : obs (r.state 1) x ≠ obs (r.state 1) y`, `1 ≤ t`. Conclusion: some `select` is through `AlikeUpTo r (fun s a b => obs s a = obs s b) t` and not through the reading at the start. **What is handed in:** the reading `obs` at every configuration, and the premise `different`, that the reading at step 1 already tells `x, y` apart. The law says that keeping the history keeps a gain. It does not say where the gain comes from. The `AlikeUpTo` docstring (l105-106): "Reading it as a usable present record requires an actual retention mechanism; this definition does not provide one."
- *General law:* `Learning.Training.keeps_through_iff` (l41). Premises: `old` reflexive, symmetric and transitive. Conclusion: `(∀ select, Through old select → Through new select) ↔ (∀ x y, new x y → old x y)`. Also `Registration.retained_gains` (l127): if `old x = old y` and `fresh x ≠ fresh y`, then some selection is through the pair `(old, fresh)` and not through `old` alone.

**Q4. Cost, who bears it, what keeps it.** Rebuilding draws one unit (`rebuild_enabled_iff` l178: enabled iff `∃ n, s.1 = n + 1`). Correction draws one unit and needs the link (`correction_enabled_iff` l183).
- `opens_without_correction` (l210, built): "Forming a distinction can consume the last support for using it". From `(1,false,false)`, rebuilding reaches `(0,true,false)`, where the distinction is through and correction is not enabled.
- `opens_with_correction` (l218, built): with a unit left, correction is enabled.
- The account and the statements `covered_not_enacted` (l344) and `linked_not_enacted` (l350) say that a course is feasible exactly where the link process admits it and the stock covers it (`feasible_iff` l337, statement not read). Stock alone does not enact, and the link alone does not.
- What keeps the registering: the link stays under `.replenish` and `.correct`, and only `.rebuild false` removes it (`P`, l153-156). Keeping the link costs nothing. Using it costs stock.

**Q5. Lost.**
- `Learning.Training.lost_choice` (l56, general). Premises: `old` reflexive, symmetric and transitive, with `new x y` and `¬ old x y`. Conclusion: `∃ select, Through old select ∧ ¬ Through new select`. "constructively, without deciding all selections or all histories."
- `Revising.closes_choice` (l224, built): "Actual closing destroys a formerly available distinction, even with support left." `(2,true,false) --rebuild false--> (1,false,false)`, after which correction is not enabled.
- Can it be undone? In this model, yes: `rebuild_then_close` (l204, built) is one trace that replenishes, rebuilds true, corrects, replenishes and rebuilds false. Forming and closing are both moves, each drawing stock. The account: "Both opening and closing are executable; neither is selected inevitably."

**Q6. Tied to what it rests on.** Through cost only. Forming the link draws on the stock, and `controlled_revision` shows the formed distinction unusable until replenishment. The controller `choose` reads the stock ("replenish when empty"), but it is supplied. No statement makes the stock's level, or its running down, the cause of a change in what is registered.

**Q1.** What makes a difference to a way is which of its units' needs the level meets. The account (l33): "What makes a difference to a way is its own response: two levels are `Alike` to `w` where the way after a step at each is the same."
**Q2.** `need` and `kids` are fixed. The way's units change: at each step, the units whose needs are met leave their kids. So the set of needs present, and with it which level differences make a difference next, changes by which carriers are born. The account cites `need_in_after` and `needs_after_iff`: with kids needing what their parent needs and every unit leaving a kid, the needs in the next way are those of `w` met at the level. Those statements were not read. This is a selection among carriers, each with a fixed need.
**Q3. General law:** `ways_alike` (l547). Premises: one `need` and one `kids`; any givings `g, g'`, uses `use, use'`, start levels `x, x'`, way `w`, and `n`; and `alike`: `∀ j < n, ∀ u ∈ (course need kids g use (w,x) j).1, (need u ≤ level j ↔ need u ≤ level' j)`. Conclusion: `∀ j ≤ n`, the two ways are equal. Docstring: "So a difference in the ways, new kinds included, arrives only after a step at which the levels met different needs of the way. Nothing is asked of the givings, the uses or the stock." This is a law of where a new difference **cannot** come from. It is not a law of a registering coming to be.
**Q4-Q5.** Not read beyond this.
**Q6.** The level is the stock. The way registers the stock only through which needs it meets, so a running down of the level that crosses no need makes no difference to the way (`ways_alike`). This states the gap the idea names, as an absence of registering.

## 14. TurningInTime.lean (the three laws where a reading sets the rule or the share)

**Q1.** A window on the level, `[N, K)`, with a reading `hL : A × Nat → R → R`, and in the share form `sA : A × Nat → R → A`. These set the rule, or the rule and the share, at levels the window reads (`windowAfter`, `windowAfterShare`). The account (l235-236): "`AloneOutside` and the marks are given, not derived: which levels make a difference to the way is a premise of every law that uses them."

**Q2.** The rule (and the share) change when the stock's level enters the window. The window, its reading `hL` or `sA`, and `reads` are handed in.

**Q3.**
- `locked_turns_in_time_on_window_iff` (l1633, general). Premises:
  - `raises : Raises P favours fA`, `monotone : ReadsShare P M hA`, `upper` (favouring kept upward);
  - `every : ∀ n, reads n = true`;
  - `corrects : ∀ p g, N ≤ p.2 → p.2 < K → kept (hL p g)`;
  - `uses : ∀ v, kept v.2.1 → use v = H₂`, `least : ∀ v, H₂ ≤ use v`;
  - the regime premises `nothing`, `short` (H₂, y₂), `meets`, `keeps`;
  - `needs : 0 < need`, `within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂`, `pos : 0 < c`, `low : N ≤ H₂ + y₂`, `covers : c ≤ K`;
  - `safe` (every way's step keeps `[c, hi]` within `[c, hi]`), `short₁` (H₁, y₁);
  - `locked : Locked M favours hA (w.1, w.2.1)`, `draws : ∀ v, P.le w.1 v.1 → M.le w.2.1 v.2.1 → H₁ ≤ use v`, `below : x < H₁ + y₁`;
  - `high`: one step after a step the window reads, the level of the lock's own course is at most `hi`.

  Conclusion: the net of `windowAfter` from `(w, x)` never ends (`«不穷» … «一»`) iff there is a `T` such that, on the course as it was (`asItWas (windowAlone fA hA reads)`), the need is met and the level is outside `[N,K)` at every `j < T`, the need is met at `T`, the level at `T` is in `[N,K)`, and the level at `T+1` is at least `H₂ + y₂`. **What is handed in:** the window `[N,K)`, its reading `hL` with `corrects`, and `every`. The docstring: "What stays given: `corrects`, `every`, `low` and `covers`".
- `locked_turns_in_time_share_iff` (l1817, general). The same shape with `windowAfterShare`. Its premises are `sets : ∀ p g, N ≤ p.2 → p.2 < K → Low (sA p g) ∧ kept (hL p g)`, `lowRule : ∀ a g, Low a → kept (hA a g)` and `lowStays : ∀ a g, Low a → kept g → Low (fA a g)`; `uses` (Low and kept → `use = H₂`); the regime premises; `needs`, `within`, `short₁`, `locked`, `draws`, `below`, and `high : x ≤ hi`; with "no premise on where the window lies". Only the "if" side has a worked case (`Three.share_read_goes_on`).
- `share_corrected_on_course` (l1896, general). Premises: `sets`, `lowRule`, `lowStays`, any `left`, any `s`, a `T` with `before` (no step before `T` reads) and `read` (step `T` reads). Conclusion: `∀ k`, the pair's course at `T+1+k` has a `Low` share and a `kept` rule. Nothing about the regime is asked.

  What each hands in, from the account (l267-269): "the correction reaches the share because that reading sets it (`sets`, given); a share that falls under the rule the window reads, so that the rule read off it changes, is not tried."

**Q4.** The cost is the use `H₂` of the kept kind against the regime's giving. "The overdraw of the course as it was stays given" (docstring of l1817). What keeps the correction: `lowStays` (Low under a kept rule stays Low) and `lowRule`, both premises.
**Q5.** Not in these three laws. The lock, a lost reader, belongs to the readings group.
**Q6.** Yes. This is the one place in my group where what the going-on rests on (the stock's level) is what sets the change of the rule or the share. The level falling into `[N,K)` triggers the reading, and whether the line goes on is decided by where that first read falls relative to `H₂ + y₂`. Which levels are read is handed in, not formed.

## The guesses

**G1** (every change of what is registered is an update handed in, a window on a level handed in, or a selection among carriers with fixed readings; nothing comes to be registered because of what it did).
- *For:* TurningInTime's three laws are a window on a level handed in (`corrects`, `sets` and `every` given; "AloneOutside and the marks are given, not derived"). LineOnAStock is a selection among carriers with a fixed `need` (`ways_alike`). Revision and OpenedCorrection change the reading by a move (`rebuild`) chosen by a supplied controller (`choose`: "Its preference for forming the link is supplied"). RouteFormation's new record is supplied (`encounter`).
- *Against, in letter:* in ContinuingRevision/ReferencePlan a new distinction comes to be registered after **the going-on's own act failed** at the unregistered address. See `fail_step_at` (l190, mismatch at `n+1`), then `new_available_choice` (l317), with the general `ReferencePlan.revise_registers_failure` (RP l446). The failure is what the going-on did. But it reaches the registering only through a supplied revision rule (`propose`), a supplied policy (`selected`) and a supplied probe channel, and `frozen_misses` (RP l460) shows a blind rule does not register it. So it is a fourth form: "a failure read by a rule handed in". The reading is a coordinate of the configuration changed by a move of the same process. It is not an update function handed in.

**G2** (learning, codes and inquiry: the feedback, teacher or sample is handed in; none ties what is learned to what the learner rests on). Only RouteFormation is in my group.
- *For:* `encounter` adds "a supplied code's value" (l389), and `unobserved_keeps_wrong` (l545) keeps the wrong program without it.
- *Partly against, as cost only:* `discrepancy_no_parts` (l539) and `repair_account` (l534) tie the repair to parts and stock. ContinuingRevision ties the next registering's renewal to the stock (`no_run_without_return` l476, `repeats_iff` l576). Neither ties what is learned to the stock.

**G3** (losing is general and exact; acquiring is conditional on something supplied; the same asymmetry as closing and opening).
- *For:* the general laws of loss are `lost_choice` (Revision l56: any merge of a pair the old relation told apart loses a selection) and `coarser_opens_nothing` (OpenedCorrection l107: nothing read off the old reading through a map opens a correction). The general laws of gain are conditional on a supplied difference: `history_gains` (l412) needs `different` at step 1, and `retained_gains` (l127) needs `fresh x ≠ fresh y`. `old_cannot_select_rebuild` (l199) says the old lumping cannot itself select forming the new one.
- *Against "what is merged stays merged":* in `Revising.P` closing is undone by `rebuild true` given stock (`rebuild_then_close` l204). In ContinuingRevision, retirement (`retired_choice` l374) is part of every cycle. No statement found brings a retired contact back.

**G4** (outside the package). Not in my group; not read. The nearest inside the package is ReferencePlan's account ("Acquired mismatch puts an address into the next executable plan"). Its mechanism is supplied ("revision policy … supplied").

**G5** (whatever changes a reading is a slower reading handed in one level up).
- *For:* HeldPossible's `RuleTower.process` (l543): "Every rule changes only as the one above it allows, and none is the last; the scheme (`base`, `up`) is fixed." `adaptive F G` changes the rule by a `G` handed in (`adaptive_keeps_rule_iff` l454). `Reaching.not_step_of_grows` (l150): along a stated process the whole possible never grows, so any change of registering lies within the possible of a fixed law. The account: "A change with no fixed scheme … not stated." TurningInTime's window is a reading of the level handed in.
- *Against the exact shape "one level up":* in Revision, OpenedCorrection and ContinuingRevision the change of reading is not a slower reading one level up. It is a move of the same process, with the reading a coordinate of its configuration (Revision account: "its index is not an outside selector"). What is handed in is the process's law and a controller or policy at the same level. That is still "handed in", so the item's wording "other than by an update handed in" is not met by any place in my group.

**Already there / not sayable.** In what was read, no general law has a difference come to be registered *because of what it does to the going-on* without a supplied rule or reading. The nearest are:
