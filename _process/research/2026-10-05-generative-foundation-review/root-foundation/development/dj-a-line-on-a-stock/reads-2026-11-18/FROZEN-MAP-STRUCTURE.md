# The map's data structure: what it must do, my guesses, and what counts against them

*AI-written (Fable, board row DJ). Frozen 18 November 2026, 00:50 WIB (from the clock), before any reader of this round was started. Scratch; nothing is built and no store is changed. What comes back is appended below with its time; nothing above an appended line is changed.*

## What it bears on

Ren, 17 November, 17:06 WIB: "to get the map in its desired direction … but at the same time, we make the map more and more visible, not necessarily in terms of UI, but to create the best data structure to represent the map." The direction asks for two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all, on one map that is joined and simple, each thing said once.

## What I found before writing this

This was worked out once already. On 14 November three rounds of readers tested four structures for the store against questions frozen beforehand (`../FROZEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `../FROZEN-ROUND-3.md`; the page for Ren was `../../THE-GRAPH.md`). The one that passed, `../STRUCTURE-D2.md`, has a theorem as the only carrier of a relation, the conditions it takes and gives read from the checked Lean and never typed, a model as where conditions live, a chain of relations never counted as a relation, a place where the account's prose speaks across two statements entered as a join or as a seam, and a dynamic as a reading. He was reading that page when the work turned to the direction, and he did not answer it. Sixteen of 184 dynamics were tested, by AI readers and AI judges.

So I do not design again. I ask what the direction as now understood, and yesterday's dig, change in that structure.

## What the structure must do (my words)

- R1. One store. What the Lean can say is read from it and never typed.
- R2. From the data alone, of a going-on in a model: what it rests on, what makes or does not make a difference to it, whether it still turns and by when; each with its theorem, every premise, and how far it is seen.
- R3. The two ways placed: where each is a stated condition, what separates them (an exclusion, an exact line), what passes between them, in which model; and where no statement joins them, a seam that says how far it was looked for.
- R4. A law stated on one model is said of a course of another only through a statement that carries it, with what goes across and what does not.
- R5. A sentence of the idea is tied to the theorems that state it, with the premises it leaves out, or to a seam. Each law is told once.
- R6. A case is placed by which conditions were met, failed, or not checked.
- R7. It never says more than the Lean: no chain as a join; an absence carries what was read.
- R8. It holds when the idea's words change: a renamed word touches readings only.

## The draft

`STRUCTURE-D3.md`: D2 with six changes. A1 three layers named, the idea itself a reading. A2 a CLAIM for any sentence of the account. A3 one list of standings. A4 what goes across a link between two models and what does not. A5 `looked` on every absence. A6, on trial: a `shape` for a theorem and a `side` for a condition, in the idea's working names.

## Guesses, each with what would count against it

- **G1. D2 as it stood on 14 November answers what a theorem assumes and concludes without a wrong answer, and is silent or partial on the placement across models (questions 4, 5, 6's last part, 7, 9).** Against: D2's reader is right on those, in which case A2, A4 and A5 are not needed; or D2's reader is wrong on questions 1 to 3 or 8 other than by an error of entry, in which case the base I am building on is not sound.
- **G2. A2, A4 and A5 each turn a silent or partial answer into a right one.** A2 on question 7; A4 on questions 4 and 9; A5 on question 5. Against, for each: D3's reader does no better than D2's on its question. A change that earns nothing is dropped.
- **G3. Question 9 is a trap, and no structure's reader walks into it.** A reader who concludes from `within_renewal_renewing`, or from any law of a fixed cast or of a record, that a line that multiplies goes on without end has chained two statements across models. Against: any reader does. If D3's reader does, D3 fails outright, whatever else it gets right.
- **G4. A6 (the tags) earns its place only on questions 1, 2, 3 and 6.** Against: the reader with tags does no better there than the reader without. Then the tags are dropped, and the structure holds none of the idea's working names below its readings. I expect them to earn little, because a theorem's own sentence in plain words already says its shape.
- **G5. The structure holds what it should fit worst without a new kind of entry and without a field that says more than the Lean:** five sayings of other traditions from the atlas's correspondences, three cases (the lake test among them), and five sentences of the direction page, two of which the page itself says are in no model. Against: an item that cannot be entered, or that can be entered only by saying something no theorem or text says.
- **G6. Nothing in the draft is new except A2 to A6, and nothing in it goes against a line tested on 14 November or against what Ren has said of the map** (4, 9, 13, 14, 16 and 17 November). Against: a reader shows a contradiction, or shows that a part I call new was already designed, or that a part of the earlier designs I dropped was needed.
- **G7. The store is the atlas's entries, with the extracted layer beside them as a generated file; the second store (`results/map/`) folds in.** Against: something in either store that has no place in the structure; or the tools and gates make the fold cost more than it gives.
- **G8. This is the reasoning under the map and not yet the map.** On 13 November I tested "what a theorem takes and gives" as the map's one relation and it fitted one family and not the rest. My guess now: from this structure the places of the two ways, the exact lines and the seams can be written out for the fixture in one screen of plain text, and a reader set to refute it will still find that what a person needs in order to see regions and ways between them is not in the data. Against the structure as "the best data structure to represent the map": that reader names what is missing and it cannot be derived from the entries.

## The test

- Two readers enter the fixture from the Lean, one in D2 as written, one in D3; neither sees the questions. A third fixes the answers to `QUESTIONS.md` from the Lean.
- Three readers answer the nine questions from the data alone: D2; D3 without the tags; D3 with them.
- One judge, with the Lean, gives each answer a verdict: as the Lean has it, partly, wrong, silent; and for a wrong one, whether the structure, the entry or the reader is at fault.
- Four more read apart: one against the earlier designs and Ren's words (G6); one entering what should fit worst (G5); one on the two stores and their tools (G7); one set to refute the structure as the map (G8).
- Eleven AI readers in all, read-only, no Lean built. One reader per cell: a single answer can be the reader's and not the structure's, and the judge is asked to say so.

## What this round cannot show

Whether the structure holds over the whole package. Whether a person can read what is drawn from it. Whether the extraction's faults listed on 14 November are fixed (they are not). Nothing here is a store.
