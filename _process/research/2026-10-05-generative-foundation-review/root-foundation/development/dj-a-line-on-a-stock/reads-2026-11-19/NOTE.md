# A lock on units, worked out on paper: what was tried on 19 November and what came of it

*AI-written (Claude Fable), 19 November 2026. No Lean was written and nothing was built. Everything below is argued on paper, or searched on small numbers, by one AI writer and eight AI readers. Three more AI readers, who took no part, checked a first draft against the reports and the Lean's statements, and the writer rewrote it. A fourth, who took no part, checked the rewrite against the same; what it marked is mended here, and the sentences changed after that check have been read by the writer only. No person has read it. A search over small numbers shows where a statement fails, not that it holds. Unit, way, share, rule, level, need are working names.*

## What was asked

The direction page said on 18 November that no model has all three together: units that multiply, a rule set again, and a level they need. I asked Ren whether to work out on paper the one join that would put them in one model; his answer was "Yes, on paper". What would count against it was written first (`FROZEN-LOCK-ON-UNITS.md`), and no Lean was to be written before he had seen the result.

## What the page had left out

The Lean already has a rule set again, a share that rises and a needed level in one law, in no chapter: a locked way whose share is one number that every favouring rule raises strictly, and whose use is that number times a fixed positive amount, on a level its going on needs and that cannot pass a bound, ends, still locked, within that bound plus one steps (`TurningInTime.locked_ends_rising` with `count_rises`). Given there also: that the level makes no difference to the share or the rule while the need is met, and the lock's own three premises. The plan tells it in its item 5; I wrote that entry and did not read it again on the 18th. So told, nothing in it has a need of its own or leaves offspring. The law itself takes a share of any kind, which matters below.

## The model

Units, each with a need and a use, as in the line on a stock (`LineOnAStock`), except that what a unit leaves depends on the rule in force. At a step, each unit whose need the level meets leaves its offspring under that rule and the others leave nothing; every unit present takes, and the level steps as in the line on a stock; the rule is set again by a reading, monotone in the share, of the way present before the step (the frozen paper said "of the new way"; the programs and the readers follow the Lean's order). A locked start is one whose rule favours and is not above what the reading of its share gives.

## What I wrote beforehand, and how it came out

Four statements: the lock is kept while every need is met (L-a); where each unit's offspring together take more under a favouring rule, a need goes unmet within the level's bound plus one steps (L-b); afterwards the lock does not decide the outcome (L-c); and a guess at the exact condition for two kinds (L-d). And seven things that would count against them.

**The fifth held against it: it is a dictionary.** Two readers who read the proofs, and a third who read statements only, each without the others' reports, on paper. L-a is the lock's own law (`RuleCapture.turn_locked`, as `TurningInTime.locked_alone`) on ways of units, with one lemma restated with a rule carried (about ten lines afresh, by one reader). L-b is the lock's law followed by the argument of `LineOnAStock.need_unmet` (two readers; the third says so only for the two-rule model the programs ran). One reader found no premise new, with one change of scope: the growth is asked only under favouring rules. Another found that no older proof gets shorter, and that `need_unmet` becomes a case of L-b only by a rule that does nothing. Two things I wrote were wrong:

- I said `locked_ends_rising` with `count_rises` would become a case. It runs the other way. Units that all have one positive need, each of whose offspring together take more under every favouring rule, from a start that is not empty, fit `locked_ends_rising` as it stands, with the way of units in the share's place and the share a count (one reader premise by premise, with a search of about 8 million small courses, by a program that was not kept, in which nothing failed; two others in sketch; for a share that is a proportion it does not stand as written). By that one reader, with one need the level cannot reach the rule before the end, since a level below the need leaves no unit: the three are then together in form only.
- I said "the use rises with the share" would come out as a conclusion. It does not. What stands in its place is a premise of the same kind: under a favouring rule a unit's offspring together take more than it. The Lean's kept course `LineOnAStock.ShareRises`, which has no rule in it, read as a course of this model (two readers): one kind's part of what is taken rises from 2 of 4 to 2 of 2 because the other leaves nothing, what the way takes falls, and the line never ends. A share is still not a draw.

The other six:

- *A small instance fails:* none found, in my program (L-a 1,824,162 and L-b 948,072 instances; it asked the lock's premise only at the ways a course visits, and odd needs only) or in a reader's wider one. Two readers add that with two rules this could not have come out otherwise.
- *The lock does no work:* it does one thing, keeping the rule under which the units multiply. The pairs I kept did not meet the lock's premise as the Lean states it; a reader found pairs that do (one is re-run in `check_courses.py`).
- *The lock on units is assumed:* for a share that counts the favoured units, that the share is not lowered under a favouring rule, at a step where every need is met, follows from "each favoured unit leaves a favoured unit"; a proportion asks more. That the offspring take more stays given.
- *Only one outcome after the first unmet need:* with offspring needing no more than their parent, three were found: the line ends still locked; it ends with the lock left; the lock is left and the rest goes on. Without that premise a fourth, a line that never ends still locked, which is the Lean's kept `KidsNeedMore`. In every example my programs kept of the third, in all 608 a reader found from starts that hold, and in 1,040 of its 1,100 from starts that overdraw, the rule's change made no difference to the course: the remaining units leave the same under both rules.
- *It is already there:* not found by a reader who went by content. That reader's verdict is "near": "three things come close, each from another side", which three it does not list. Its table has eight places, among them the law above, the line on a stock, chapter 2 §9 and §10, chapter 5 §12 and chapter 7 §4.12 and §4.14 (the table is in `reader-sweep.md`, which is not filed). Statements were read in about a dozen of the 406 files and the rest only swept by word.
- *L-d is false:* as I wrote it, yes. With the premise that `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` and `TurningInTime.unending_iff_above` carry, the two sides agreed on all 1,217,496 instances my program ran. By the refuting reader it is then not `two_units_iff` itself but three known pieces in a row.

## What came out of the read instead

One reader proposed without seeing my statements. Four more were then set to break three of its proposals; its two plainest statements (a lock's `need_unmet`, and "no way of units goes on locked") had no reader of their own; the proposer and one reader call the first a composition, and one reader calls the second, at one rule, `way_ends` word for word.

**1. One course on which the running down reaches the rule with nothing handed in to read it.** It is one course, run by program, in a model that exists on paper and in Python only. A favoured unit needs 27 and takes 14, from a level of 28 that gives back as much as is taken, so the start holds. Under the favouring rule it leaves itself and one small unit (need 1, use 1), and small units double; under the other rule each leaves itself. The share is the number of favoured units. The levels go 28, 28, 26: at 26 the favoured unit's need is unmet and it leaves nothing. Then 18, with six small units and the share nothing; then the rule set again no longer favours, and twelve small units take 12 from a level of 24 that gives 12 back, without end. With the rule held favouring, the same start ends at its sixth step. A reader found it, with 39 like it from starts that hold, in a search built to find them (40 among 211,689 starts); `check_courses.py` re-runs this one and its twin.

What is and is not new in it. Chapter 2 §11 already calls an ending of some units a turn for a way of units. Chapter 2 §10 already has a worked course on which a lock is left when the favoured kind bears a loss (`PoolFromCourse.Borne`); there the loss is a count nothing needs, and the cost to the favoured kind is handed in. Here the loss is of a level the units draw down and need, and the rule's change is what lets the rest go on. How often such courses occur was not measured on one footing: in the search that checked every premise of the condition below, none occurred in six families (among 4.6 million locked starts of instances in which, on the ways checked, the share is not lowered under a favouring rule; how many of these met every need at the start was not counted) and 162 in a seventh made to look for them. The proposer found none in 502,215 starts where every unit multiplies under the favouring rule: the turn came in time only where growth is slow against the stock. One checking reader adds, as a reading: in this course the rule stops favouring only because the unit it counts is the one that needs most.

**2. An exact condition, a candidate, in which the lock does not do the joining.** As proposed, with its premises: under a favouring rule each unit's offspring together take more than it, under any other they take the same and are not none; offspring need no more than their parent; every unit needs something; the share does not rise under a rule that does not favour; the reading is monotone, at every step, and favouring is kept upward; the stock gives nothing from nothing and keeps its regime along the course. Then a way of units never ends exactly where its course comes, with some need met at every step before, to a configuration that holds and at which the rule in force does not favour and the rule read off the present share is not above it.

- A reader searched it in 45,272,833 small starts, in worlds its proposer had not run, each premise checked by the program (some on a grid of ways only; five of the seven families by sample), and found no disagreement. The proposer's gloss of it in words was found false as worded.
- The reader who set it against the Lean found no false step in either half on paper, the left-to-right half read classically; in the style of the Lean's files that half is one nested induction, more than its proposer counted. As proposed, neither older law is its case. With two premises on the stock restated, `LineOnAStock.unending_iff_holds` is its case exactly, and `way_ends` in its second conjunct only (that the line does not go on without end).
- The same reader found what it is made of. First, a statement about the line on a stock alone, with no rule in it: where some units' offspring take more and the others' take the same and are not none, offspring need no more than their parent, every unit needs something, and the stock's premises hold along the course, the line never ends exactly where its course comes to a holding configuration with no descendant of the growing kind. The two older laws would be its cases where every unit grows (`way_ends`, in its second conjunct only) and where none does, with the stock's premises restated as for the candidate. One reader, on paper; searched on 358,358 starts by a program that was not kept. Second, a test at one configuration for "from here no rule favours": one direction of it is `turn_locked` with the order reversed, the other is argued from two of the candidate's premises.
- So, by that reader, applying the test its proposer wrote beforehand, the candidate as a statement about a lock is the lock-free statement with a certificate. What the course in 1 shows, that the fallen level reaches the rule through who is left, is not in the equivalence; it is only in a last clause on how early the lock can be left.
- Proved on top of the two older laws, nothing old would retire. They retire only if their sections are proved again with offspring that depend on the step, which the proposer (speaking of the candidate) put at about five hundred lines reworked.

**3. Two small things.** A lemma under the two proofs that share `rising_takes_all`: as proposed it is a wrapper; restated without the rise, three old proofs become cases or get shorter, about nine lines fewer before docstrings and about level after them, by one reader's count on paper. And the one-need instance above as a corollary of about a hundred lines, which adds a case and joins nothing.

## What I make of it

Nothing tried made the join I named on the 18th as a theorem about the lock: with one need it is an instance in form only, with needs that differ what I wrote was two older results end to end, and in the candidate, by one reader, the lock does not do the joining. No statement of when a course like the one in 1 happens was found. One checking reader's view, which I share: correct the direction page; write no Lean from the exact condition now; and before any Lean for the course, there is a question no proof answers, which is Ren's: whether a rule that stops favouring because the units it counts were the first to go without is what the idea means by turning.

If Lean were written, the options as I see them (the list is mine, not a reader's), each with what would count as nothing having changed: the course with its twin as a kept case (the refuting reader names it in its smallest landing; no reader costed it; my test: nothing has changed if it is filed and no law comes of it); the lock-free statement (the reader who found it does not propose it; if it holds, it would join two laws of the line on a stock, `way_ends` in half, and it says nothing of a lock; that reader's test: nothing retires if it is proved on top of them); the small lemma, when those files are next open (one checking reader's view; about level in lines).

## What it does not show

That any of this holds: nothing was built. Whether leaving a lock because the favoured units ran out of what they need is what the idea means by turning. And nothing here bears on the second way the direction names: no unit in this model adds to what it rests on.
