## The question

Every law of November is about one course as it runs: the net ends or it does not. "Can still be turned" compares a course with others open from the same place. October's root has names for that. For a process (configurations, moves, a step) and a guard (what is asked):

- **K, kept as it goes:** from this configuration no course leaves the guard (`Viability.Kernel`).
- **R, kept by a choice from what it reads:** there is a way of choosing the move from a reading of the configuration such that the course so chosen from here never leaves the guard. This is my wording, for one start; the Lean's `UniformPolicy` asks one choice good at every configuration, and `GuardHorizon.Viable` lets the process answer a move in more than one way. Readers say how far mine is the Lean's.
- **Kp, kept by some course:** some run from here stays within the guard (`Viability.Keepable`).

K is within R, and R within Kp, for every reading; a finer reading gives a wider R. Along a course that ends, each region is left at some step. The direction page says three clocks are compared by no statement that was found.

November's courses have no moves in them. To ask the question a process has to be made, and that is my construction, not the Lean's:

- **On `Three`** (a way that takes five or three from a level that gives back `min z (12 − z)`, need one): the configuration is the take in force and the level; the move sets the take for the next step.
- **On the units of 19 November** (two kinds, two rules, a level): the configuration is how many of each kind, the rule in force and the level; the move sets the rule for the next step. This is the order the Lean's lock has: the rule in force at a step was set from what was read before it.
- **Readings tried:** nothing (one rule for ever); the share, by a monotone reading with favouring kept upward (the lock's own class); the share, by any function; the share and the level; the whole configuration (which is Kp).
- **Guards, one for each grain:** something goes on (some unit's need is met); a unit of the favoured kind is present with its need met; the same for the other kind; the level is at least `b`, for someone else who rests on it and takes nothing.

2. **The gap is trivial.** "Kept by some course, but by no choice from what it reads" comes out empty, or as everything that is not kept as it goes, in every case and for every reading tried.

5. **Grain adds nothing.** At every grain the regions are those of the way, cut down to the configurations where that kind is present.
6. **The convention decides.** If letting the move set the rule for this step, and not the next, changes which regions are empty, the finding is about that convention and is told as that.
7. **No sentence in the regions' terms holds in two of the models by one argument.**
