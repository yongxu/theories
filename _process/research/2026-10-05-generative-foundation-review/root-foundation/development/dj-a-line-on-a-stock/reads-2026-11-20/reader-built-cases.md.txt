What the regions add is a step on one course, the step from which no course keeps a guard, and for two guards the Lean already states it as `NoReturnAt` (`FourReaders.noReturn`, `Three.noReturn`), both from November. ## Test 1, second half: do the regions sort differently from renewing and consuming?

Differently, in both directions, and in neither direction do they sort anything that the ending of each formation does not.

- *Same regions, different class.* `Renewable`'s runs at their shared start (Lean: `kept_part_not_renewal`; with the three other runs, three classes). `malignant` and `stoppingMalignant` at steps 0 and 1 (Lean for one class, by hand for the other). This direction is forced: a region is a fact of a configuration (`Kernel.run`; `Keepable` by its definition), so it cannot tell apart two courses while they stand at one configuration.
- *Same class, different regions.* `FourReaders`' `s1`, `s2` and `never`: every formation has one class on all three, and the dweller's keeping is entered for good at step 2, entered at step 3, and lost at step 3. But the dweller's ending already tells `never` from the other two, and the near gauge's tells `s1` from `s2`.
- *The step.* Where the regions say something the endings do not, it is a step on one course: no course keeps the guard from a step before the guard fails. The gap is 0 in `Renewable` and for `malignant`; 1 for the dweller and for `Three` (both Lean); 1 for the second relay and 2 for the listener (by hand); 0 for the gauges and the first relay. No two courses in these cases differ in that gap while agreeing in when they end.

**What `kept_part_not_renewal` already settles.** Three things, at one configuration. That no course leaves a formation's own guard decides neither its class nor another's fate: two runs of opposite class share it. A second guard, at the same configuration, is in Kp and not in K, and the witnesses are the same two runs: the renewing run is why it is in Kp, the consuming run why it is not in K. And so a region is of the configuration and the guard, while the class is of the run. It does not settle anything about steps, being stated at the start only.

One relation follows from the definitions in one line each, and is my working, not a Lean statement. Along a run on which a formation is renewing, each need on a condition it takes part in is met at every step, so the run itself keeps that guard from each of its steps. Along a run on which it is consuming, some need on a condition it draws on is unmet at every step after some step, so the run stands outside that guard's Kp from then on. Neither converse holds: `dipping` leaves Kp and is not consuming; `fluctuating` is never in Kp and is not consuming.

## What the table shows that the Lean does not state in one place, and what it does not

It shows four things. The Lean names regions and classes together in one theorem only, `kept_part_not_renewal`, at one configuration; for `FourReaders` and the laws both can be had by hand, and they sort differently. The step from which no course keeps a guard comes before the guard's own failing in a few rows only (the dweller, `Three`, the second relay, the listener), and the Lean has the first two as `NoReturnAt`. Every case where K\* holds for a guard that can fail is a family in which the turn or the stop is for good; in the Lean's one process here K is everything or nothing and Kp is the guard. And the Lean keeps two conventions for when a reading can still act, with one built case where the step between them decides.

It does not show a middle region, since no case has a choice from a reading. It shows no pair of courses that the regions tell apart and the endings do not. It says nothing of units that multiply or of a rule set again from a share: the lock's courses in `Three` are placed by their levels only. It gives no class for `Three`, which has no web. And it is a table of built cases worked by hand: it shows where a statement fails, not that one holds.
