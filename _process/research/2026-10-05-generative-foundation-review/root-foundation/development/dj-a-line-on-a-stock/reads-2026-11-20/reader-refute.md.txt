# Set to refute: the regions of "can it still be turned"

## Verdict

The programs are right as far as I could check them; the numbers in `AFTER-PROGRAMS.md` reproduce. What they mean is less than the file's last section suggests. On the model the Lean's lock laws are about (a favouring rule, and a rule turned to under which what is taken stands), "kept by some course" is "the standing rule, held from here, keeps it" in 267,313 of 267,598 kept configurations, and a reading adds nothing: there the table is a dictionary of `WayOfUnits.unending_iff_holds` one step on. Tests 3 and 7 trip; test 6 trips in its letter at one guard; tests 2 and 4 pass only in the letter (4: the lock is not assumed, since the model lets the share fall at an unmet need, and it does no work); tests 1 and 5 could not have failed in a model where one move ends what another keeps and one unit can be lost while another goes on. Three small things are worth keeping, none of them a statement.

## 1. The programs (no error found)

**LESSER. `reach`, `gfp`, `run_policy`, `kept_by_reading`, `kept_mono`, `classify`, `clocks`: no wrong answer found.** How: by reading, and by `refute.py check` on all 5,184 instances of the standstill family (bounds 8 and 12, the Lean's order, guard "something goes on"):

The three points asked. *A repeated configuration on the path counted as "for ever":* right. Every configuration on the path had its reading fixed when it was visited, and the partial choice only grows along a branch, so the course from the repeated configuration retraces the path. *True at once in K:* right, since K says every sequence of moves keeps, whatever is already fixed. *Complete:* both moves are tried at an unfixed reading with a fresh copy of the choice. *`const` when only rule 1 held keeps:* yes, counted (`0 in ths`); 185 of the sweep's 215,992. *The lock's own course in `clocks`:* threshold 1, starts with at least one F and rule 1 in force, so the start is `Locked` in `RuleCapture`'s sense for that reading.

**LESSER. `mono` is wider than "the lock from here".** `kept_mono` tries every threshold, also those above the start's share, down to "never favour". Under such a reading a start with the favouring rule in force is not `Locked`. And the clocks table times only two things: the first step with rule 0 in force, and the first configuration no course keeps. ## 6. The clocks

**LESSER, MUST NOT be told as a finding. "Stopped favouring at or before the point of no return: 0" is forced in this family.** With threshold 1 on a count the rule stops favouring only when no F is left; under the favouring rule in force that needs F unmet and no O leaving an F; from then no F ever returns and the course is the standstill on the O that remain. For O alone the standstill is the best course (any other only multiplies or removes them; more units take more, and the step rises in the level), so if any course keeps the configuration at which the reading moved, the lock's own course does. In chapter 2 §10's words every such turn is the second way a lock is left: a step at which the share is not kept. Under the Lean's three premises a locked course never leaves (`turn_locked`), so the Lean's lock laws have no such row.

**LESSER. 34,611 against 144 is the choice of ranges.** Where F needs no more than O: 0 turns. Where F needs more and something multiplies: 144 against 16,503. In 19 November's instance over all starts: 18 against 10 (need 27), 13 against 7 (need 26). With a large F: 979 against 3,815, and 3,818 against 12,274.
