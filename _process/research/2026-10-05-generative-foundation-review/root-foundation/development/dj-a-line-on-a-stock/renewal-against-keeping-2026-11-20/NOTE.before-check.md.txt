*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ), 20 November 2026. A read on paper: no Lean was written and nothing was built or run. One AI agent answered six questions at the statements; a second, set to refute and having taken no part, checked the first's main claims at the source and applied four tests frozen beforehand. Every bridge, instantiation and counterexample below is argued on paper by one or both of them and checked by no build. No person has read any of it. Negatives are "not found in what was read"; each report lists what it read. Renewable, keepable, live are the files' own names.*

## What was asked

A read earlier the same evening ([its record](../read-kept-by-goings-on-2026-11-20/NOTE.md)) found, outside the package, the eight files of `lean/conditional/renewal/` (6 and 7 October) with an exact law of what a supplied law admits. I wrote a guess beside it, marked unchecked: that the two ways might be placed by one relation between a law's self-renewing population and a way of selecting among the histories the law admits. Six questions, five guesses (P1 to P5, the fifth against my own) and four tests of what would count as nothing having changed were frozen at 20:53, before either agent ran ([the frozen file](FROZEN-THE-PLACING.md.txt)). At 21:00, before any report was read, I added what would count as the find pulling the order; the reader did not see it and the refuter did.

## How it came out

**Nothing is placed by it, and my guess does not hold.** The refuter's verdicts, which I take: test 1 (a renaming) tripped; test 2 (already said) not tripped in its letter; test 3 (not placed) tripped; test 4 (drift) tripped; the fifth guess holds with one clause corrected. Its plain answer to whether any statement, writable without a new model, says where a course passes from a way that keeps remaking what it rests on to a way that uses it up and can no longer be turned: no.

- **The bridge is a re-packing.** `Renewable law c` is `Keepable (channelProcess law) Live c`: the renewal files' `History` and the package's `Run` have three fields of the same types over the same `Process` (both agents, at the structures; the lemma on paper, not written). By it no named law of either side becomes a case of a law of the other or gets shorter. Through a second bridge the reader found (a run of the files' own guarded process), three renewal proofs are instances of general constructions of the package; the refuter calls them run plumbing (prepend a step; stay at a fixed point; a falling number allows no run), none shorter, and one of them was already recorded as the same statement on 16 October.
- **What it would place is keepable against not keepable.** That says nothing of selecting (both agents). The reader's case against the drift test was that the renewal model has a reserve, supporters and re-forming in it. The refuter: that is true of the model and not of what the relation places, which names only the process, the guard and the configuration; what speaks of supporters and of demand against opening is the closure theorem, and it is of a law, not of a configuration or a course.
- **Why the renewable stretch is not the second way and its end not the first**, by the files' own theorems as the refuter assembled them: a history can lie in the renewable stretch and form nothing; renewability is then lost with the reserve unchanged; nothing in the eight files selects; no deadline follows the loss in a law that has a population; and whether a configuration is renewable is not decided anywhere (the files' README lists it as not yet characterised).

## What is kept

1. **A small gap in the package, on paper.** For `Keepable` the package has the start (`Keepable.start`) and, for every course, the step law of the kernel (`kernel_step_iff`). Not found stated: that a guard is keepable from a configuration exactly where it holds there and some step leads to one it is keepable from; and that where every step keeps the guard's complement, every step keeps not being keepable. The renewal files have both for their own process (`renewable_iff_step`, `renewability_not_generated`). The second is not a fact of every process as the renewal file words it: the reader gives a process of the package on which a guard is lost and regained, re-checked by the refuter. Both lemmas are short, by the refuter, and neither is a meeting of the two sides.
2. **Where each thing stands over.** The lock is stated over its own determined step, not as a choice among a process's moves (both agents). `NoReturnAt` is typed over courses of levels and does not apply to configurations as it stands. The package does have choices of a process's move from a reading, for any process (the reader names two; the refuter adds three more), all for keeping a guard on every successor.
3. **The pieces lie on opposite sides, and what would have to be new is five things** (the refuter, with where the package has each): a rule and a share for a policy on which kinds are formed (the construction of a policy exists for any process; the rule, the share and three premises do not); a cost of supporting borne by the supporter (none found on either side; the package has a cost of using a level); a finite source (the package has one level with a cap and its giving handed in); a selection among histories (the package has it on courses of levels only, all supplied); and a decision of whether a configuration is renewable (not proved in the renewal files; the package has it for one level). "One law with both changes the channel process or the lock's step, which is a new model."
4. **What to distrust when the renewal files are told as "the second way"** (the refuter): they say that some history stays live; supporting costs nothing; the source is unlimited, and it is what is rested on, while only the tokens are remade; the law is supplied; live means one token of any kind; and the standing renewal is one configuration repeated.
5. **The receipt is of 7 October and may not pass today.** The renewal folder's own checker pins the hash of a file (`Depletion.lean`) that a comment of 16 October changed. On paper it would stop at its own assertion. The refuter read two hashes and an assertion and ran nothing; I have not run it either.

## What it turns

By the fourth of the conditions I wrote at 21:00, the drift test tripping means the work does not go on with these files. They are filed here as a part of the map, with what was read of them, and the order goes back to where it stood before the find. I withdraw the guess of the earlier record.

What the evening's three tries say together is my reading, held loosely, and no report states it. The meeting was tried on one level with its giving handed in; the package was read for things kept by goings-on; October's renewal law was set against the package's keeping. Each time the pieces were found on opposite sides: on one side a level that can run down, use, units that multiply, a lock; on the other, kinds that re-form each other from a source with no limit, at no cost, with nothing selecting. Nothing found joins them, and by the refuter nothing can without a new model.

So the next step in the order, the meeting where the level is itself kept, is not a join of what exists. It is a model. One model that contains the rest was tried on 16 November and failed its tests, and I have proposed a model three times since and withdrawn it. I will not start one from this note. What I do next is smaller: write down, on paper and by myself, the least set of things such a model must have (from item 3 above), which existing laws would have to come out as its cases with their statements unchanged, and what would count as it being one more model beside the others. That page goes to Ren before any agent or any Lean is spent on it, since whether work on this row goes on by models at all was listed as his to say.
