**Of what is rested on, and of remaking: the case against tripping, and why I do not think it stands.** The reader's last sentence under P5 is that the law "does have a reserve, supporters and re-formation, so 'what is rested on' and 'remaking' are in the model". ## 6. The plain answer

**After this read, is there any statement, writable without a new model, that says where a course passes from a way that keeps remaking what it rests on to a way that uses it up and can no longer be turned? No.**

What is writable, and has not been written on either side, is this [P]: along any history of a supplied channel law the renewable configurations are an initial stretch, and where renewability is decided along the history and fails somewhere there is one first step at which it fails; the configuration there can still be live. That is the third clause, "can no longer be turned", for the guard "some token is alive". By the files' own theorems the stretch before that step is not "keeps remaking" (a course can lie in it and form nothing), the step is not "uses up" (the reserve can be the same or more), and nothing selects (test 3). The package already has such a point, decided and located, for one level under a standing use: by the account at the head of `B/TurningInTime.lean` section 7 (1914–1935), `recoverable_iff_above` and `noReturnAt_iff_first_below`, for a level course that never rises and starts at most `hi`, under `FromNothing`, `Short`, `Meets` and `Keeps` at `H` with `y` and `hi` and a positive need at most `H + y`. I read that account and `turned_meets_iff`'s docstring, not those two statements.

**What would have to be new, and whether the package has the piece.** I list what is missing; I propose none of it.

| Piece | In the renewal files | In the package, and where |
|---|---|---|
| **A policy on births** | None. The step is a relation and `births` is free (`R/RenewalAccounting.lean` 141–145) | The construction exists for any process: `closedBy` (`B/Labelled.lean` 93). Also `GuardHorizon.UniformPolicy` (`B/GuardHorizon.lean` 326), `Keeping.Plan` (`B/Keeping.lean` 100), `ReadingLoop.keeping_policy_iff` (`B/ReadingLoop.lean` 247). The last three choose for keeping a guard on *every* successor, which on the channel process means against any loss of reserve. What is not there for this law: a rule type and its order, a share on configurations, `f` and `h` as functions (the reserve clause is an inequality, so a function needs the reserve fixed too), and proofs of `Raises`, `ReadsShare`, `upper` (`B/RuleCapture.lean` 145, 150, 169). The reader's list is right |
| **A cost of supporting** | None. A birth needs `∃ tok, tok ∈ aged c.tokens ∧ law.edge tok.kind k` (145): the supporter is not spent, counted or limited, and one token supports any number of births in a tick (`free_formation_lives_long`, `R/RenewalPremises.lean` 265, forms `m` tokens on one seed). The only costs are `demand` on the reserve and aging. `R/README.md` 95 lists "several required supporters" as not done | A cost of *using a level* is there: `LineOnAStock.taken`, `left` (`B/LineOnAStock.lean` 143, 146); `RelationalRenewal.Law.level` (`L/deeper/RelationalRenewal.lean` 246); `Renewable.process` (`B/Labelled.lean` 552). ## 7. What to distrust in the renewal files when they are told as "the second way"

Each is read off the file unless marked.

1. **It says some history stays live.** `RenewsForever` and `Renewable` are existentials (`R/RenewalCharacterization.lean` 70; `R/RenewalConfigurations.lean` 43). "Nothing here says which history occurs, that a system reaches the steady configuration, or that continuation is favorable" (`R/README.md` 97). A law with a self-renewing population also admits, from every configuration, the history that forms nothing and dies [P, by `History.idle` and `agedN_empty`].
2. **Supporting costs nothing.** Table above. A population is "closed under support" by an existential on edges; no supporter is ever short of support to give.
3. **The outside flow is unlimited, and it is the flow, not the population, that is rested on.** The steady configuration "renews forever, with any reserve, including none" (`R/README.md` 49; `steady_renews` at any `R`; `steadyExecution` at reserve 0, `R/RenewalCharacterization.lean` 96–103). What it remakes is its own tokens. What each token opens, `rate` a tick, comes from outside at no limit and is remade by nothing in the model. So "keeps remaking what it rests on" is true of the supporters and not of the source [P: my reading of the step].
4. **Growth uses nothing up.** A kind with `demand < rate · span` is a net source: forming more of it opens more (`excess_supported_by_closure_renews`, `R/RenewalSufficiency.lean` 343, where `formation law C` copies of such a kind `q` a tick cover a closure `C` whose own kinds may fall short; premises `C ≠ []`, positive spans at most `N` for `C` and `q`, `C` support-closed, `q` supported from `C`, `law.demand q + 1 ≤ law.rate q * law.span q`). The first way, read as a multiplying that draws down what all rest on, cannot occur in this law by multiplying. A reserve falls only by forming kinds that fall short [P].
5. **The law is supplied.** `edge`, `span`, `demand`, `rate` (`R/RenewalAccounting.lean` 41–45). "Deriving it from the generative core is roadmap Phase 4" (`R/RenewalPremises.lean` 26–27). No kind arises or changes. The equivalence needs finitely many kinds (`infinitely_many_kinds_break_the_equivalence`, 139).
6. **The reserve clause is an inequality.** Any amount of reserve may vanish at any step (144). So the package's every-successor notions (`Viable`, `KeptFor`) ask, on this law, for keeping against any loss of reserve, and are not renewability.
7. **`Live` is a thin guard:** one token of any kind with `remaining ≠ 0` (148). `renewal_keeping_theorem` (703; premises `U`, `complete`, any `K`) is the form that can name what must be kept, and the files' own example is that a closure can renew for ever while the kind designated as correction cannot be kept (`lapsing_correction_cannot_be_kept`, 982).
8. **The standing renewal is one configuration repeated** (`steady_fixed`, `R/RenewalSufficiency.lean` 126). Nothing in it turns, widens or reads.
9. **"Cannot be created from within"** (`renewability_not_generated`) is within the law's own steps; what "outside" is, is not modelled (`renewable_of_more` adds tokens and reserve by hand).
10. **Its standing as proved is a receipt of 7 October.** The checker as written would not pass today (section 0), for a one-line comment. I rebuilt nothing.
