## (A) The form chosen, and why

**The general statement is a simulation with an invariant on configurations** (`unending_carrier`). Its premises are asked only at configurations with `Inv`, and `Inv` is of the pair (way and level), not of the way alone. Why this form:

1. *The two transfers that exist are its cases with nothing left over.* `WayOfUnits.unending_iff_carrier` is the case `W := Bool`, `way := twoWay`, `Inv` true everywhere; its three ingredients (`after_twoWay`, `taken_twoWay`, `goes_twoWay`) are exactly the three premises `ways`, `takes`, `goes`. The scratch's `unending_grown` is the case `W := List U`, `way c := c ++ R`, `Inv` "every unit of the part is of the line". Both are built with their statements unchanged (`unending_iff_carrier_case`; `unending_grown_case`, checked against the scratch by `rfl` in the check module). So this was a claim that could fail, and it did not.
2. *A way that turns needs the level in the invariant.* For growers and stayers the agreement "some unit's need is met exactly where `n` is" fails at a way with no stayer at a level in `[n, m)`. Before the turn such a way is harmless (the level is at or above `m`); whether it is still harmless at the turn depends on the step. An invariant of the way alone cannot say this; one of the configuration can ("the configuration is the course as it was at a step before `T`, or the way has a stayer").
3. *The reader's three missing things.* (i) No transfer for ways that turn: `unending_turned`. (ii) The turn laws ask one take of the kept kind for every way: on the carrier `List U` with `turned`, the kept kind is "the way is the stayers of the `T`-th generation", one way, so `uses` holds by construction and `kept` because stayers leave themselves (`turned_stayers`). (iii) A premise weaker than `asItWasEnds`: `unending_iff_marked_at` asks only a step `T` with no marked level before it.

**The carrier is `List U` itself, not the reader's `Nat ⊕ Unit`.** With the step `turned m S kids`, `alone := allKids kids` and `marked := (· < m)`: `AloneOutside` holds by definition (`turned_outside`), and the carrier's course as it was is literally U2's `X_j` (`wasLevel`), so no bridge between two courses is needed. `turned` at a way all of whose units are growers is the scratch's `grown` (argued, by `LineOnAStock.strip_all`; not stated as a theorem).

**What `unending_turned` is not.** `unending_iff_carrier` and `unending_grown` are cases of `unending_carrier`, not of `unending_turned`: in both, a unit beside the line may itself be of the line (equal pairs; a unit of `R` that is of `S`), and then there is no stayer in the sense "not of `S`".

**Argued, not built:** that without the premise `stayer` the two sides of `unending_turned` can differ (a grower alone at a level in `[n, m)`: no unit's need is met, the carrier's need is, and under no take the carrier's level does not fall).

## (B) Where the statement given had to change

The statement built as `units_turn_iff` is the brief's, with the step `T` explicit:

- *"R_T is not empty": dropped, as the reader said.* It is derived inside the proof from `short`, `needs`, `within` (`RegimeMargin.Adjusted.draw_pos`). Consequence, said in the docstring: where the turn leaves no stayer the premises do not hold and `units_turn_iff` concludes nothing.
- *"`T` the first step below `m`": stated with an explicit `T`*, premises `before : ∀ j < T, m ≤ X_j` and `belowT : X_T < m`. "The course as it was comes below `m` at some step" is then `belowT`; it is a premise and is not derived. `units_turn_iff_first` is the form with the step found: premise `comes : ∃ N, X_N < m`, the regime asked at every first step below `m` (there is one such step, `first_unique`), `y` a function of the step, and the right side `∃ T, (∀ j < T, m ≤ X_j) ∧ n ≤ X_T ∧ X_T < m ∧ H_T + y T ≤ X_{T+1}`, which is `two_units_iff`'s form.
- *No premise was missing and no conclusion was false.* `Keeps` is asked at the start's stayers' take, as given; `Keeps` at `H_T` and the level at `T + 1` within `hi` come from `kept_within_more`, `asItWas_within` (copied from the scratch of 17 November) and a new lemma `stayers_take` (the stayers of every generation take at least what the start's take).
- *Not used, against the paper argument:* `gone_from` (its `closed` is global, as the reader warned). The proof uses that the stayers' way is fixed (`turned_stayers`).
- *Spelled as a definition:* "every unit of every generation of `w` not of `S` leaves only itself and needs `n`" is `Stays S need kids n w`. `R_T` is `strip S (generation kids T w)` (`LineOnAStock.strip`), `H_T` is `taken use` of it.

## (C) The cases, and the findings

**`two_units_iff`: a case of (B) except where the two pairs are equal, as predicted.** At `S u := (u = (m, h))` every generation is the way, the stayers are `[(n, H₂)]` and take `H₂`, and "comes below `m`" is `RegimeMargin.ends_below` from `short₁`, `below`, `nothing`. Where `(m, h) = (n, H₂)` both units are growers, no stayer is left, and (B)'s `short` and `within` cannot hold of a take of nothing: the premise that does not match is the regime at `H_T = 0`. There both sides are false; the left side needs the separate line `one_need_ends`. The statement is `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff`'s, checked by `rfl`. The proof is longer than the package's (about 60 lines against about 30), as the reader expected.

**`mixed_turns_iff`: a case of (B) only where `n < m`. This is wider than "equal pairs" and was not in the reader's or the worker's account.** Three mismatches:

1. *The line `S` of `mixed_turns_iff` is not decidable and (B) asks a decidable `S`.* The growers used are `fun u => need u = m`, which is decided.
2. *Where `n = m` no decided predicate here separates the line from `R`* (a unit of `R` may be of the line, and `mixed_turns_iff` does not ask otherwise). With `need u = m` as the growers every unit is a grower, no stayer is left, and (B) does not reach. Both sides are false there; the left by `one_need_ends`. So the worker's sentence "`mixed_turns_iff` is a case of U2, by an instantiation that can be written" holds for `n < m` and not for `n = m`.
3. *Its premise `ne : R ≠ []` is not used* (it follows from `take`, `short`, `needs`, `within`). To keep the statement and have no unused-variable warning, the premise is carried without a name: `(_ : R ≠ [])`. This is the only difference from the scratch's signature, and `ScratchUnitsTurnCheck.mixed_same : @ScratchUnitsTurn.mixed_turns_iff_case = @ScratchMixedWay.mixed_turns_iff := rfl` builds, so the two statements are the same proposition.

"Comes below `m`" for `mixed_turns_iff` is derived from `short₁`, `below`, `keepsUse` by a new generic lemma `comes_to_nothing` (from `LineOnAStock.step_lowers`); the levels of the whole way and of the part are joined by `was_beside`.

**The separate line, `one_need_ends`:** where every unit of every generation needs `m` and the course as it was comes below `m`, the net ends. It is what (B) is silent about (a turn that leaves no stayer). A form of (B) that asked the regime only where a stayer is left, with "a stayer is left" in the right side, would have equal pairs as a case with no separate line; I did not build it. Argued, not built: `mixed_turns_iff` at `n = m` would still not be its case, since its `keeps` is at `H₂`, and with no stayer counted that form would ask `Keeps` at a take of nothing, which is stronger.

**The kept example.** `Example.from_one`, `Example.from_two` compute the courses (`[g]` at 8: ways `[g]`, `[g, r]`, `[g, r, r]`, `[r, r]` at levels 8, 8, 7, 4, then fixed; `[g, g]` at 8: `[g, r, g, r]` at 4, `[r, r]` at 0). `Example.one_by_law` and `Example.two_by_law` get the same two verdicts from `units_turn_iff` at `T = 2` and `T = 1`. This start has no stayer, the case the refuter found excluded by the first paper wording, and a grower leaves a stayer, the case `mixed_turns_iff` does not reach.
