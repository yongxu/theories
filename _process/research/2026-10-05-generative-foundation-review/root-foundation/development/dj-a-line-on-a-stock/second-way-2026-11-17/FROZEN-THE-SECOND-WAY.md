# The second way, tried in the line's terms: what is tried and what would count against it

## What it bears on

The direction wants two ways of going on placed with respect to each other: a way of selecting that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and a way that keeps remaking what it rests on. The page says the second "is in none of this month's models: nothing in them that goes on gives back to what it rests on". This is the third item under "What we are trying next". A try of 16 November (`tries-2026-11-16/giving/report.json`) found: for members that are always there, "members give" is the stock's giving renamed, so nothing is new there; it is new only where the givers are units of the way, which come and go by what the level meets.

## The model tried

`LineOnAStock` as it is (`need use : U → Nat`, `kids : U → List U`, way `w : List U`, level `x`; way after a step `after need kids w x`; level after `step giving (taken use w) x = (x - T) + giving (x - T)`), with one more thing a unit does: `give : U → Nat`. Two orders are examined, since the earlier try found that the order matters:

- **L, on what is left:** `z = x - taken use w`; next level `z + giving z + (total of give u over u in w with need u ≤ z)`.
- **B, before the take:** the same with `need u ≤ x`.

`give = 0` is the line on a stock.

## What I guess (C1 to C5)

- **C1, no difference.** Along two courses from one way with the same `need` and `kids`, under any two givings, uses, gives and start levels, whose levels meet the same needs of the way step by step, the ways are the same. Read: in this model what a unit gives cannot make a difference to who comes next except through which needs are met.
- **C2, the order of needs decides.** In a world of two kinds, givers (need `a`, use `t`, give `b`, each leaving itself) and takers (need `m`, use `c`, each leaving `k ≥ 2` takers), with the stock giving nothing of itself or giving a rising amount: (i) where `m ≤ a` the net ends from every start, and the givers go unmet no later than the takers; (ii) where `m > a` there is an exact condition, in the numbers at the step the takers first go unmet, for the net never to end, and it says that what is left at that step still meets the givers' need; (iii) there are numbers where a higher start ends and a lower start never ends (the takers have had longer to grow when the fall reaches them).
- **C3, the region.** A configuration from which a way with givers goes on without end, said as `Holds` is said for the line (way not empty, every need met, the level at or above what is needed for the net take), and its exact form.
- **C4, cases.** The line on a stock is `give = 0`. October's `RelationalRenewal.Law` (a giver kind that needs the level, a taker) is the two-unit case in one of the orders, up to the phase shift the earlier try found; which of its theorems would become cases, and how many lines they are.
- **C5, the turn is because of the fall.** In C2 (ii) the way changes at the step the takers' need goes unmet, with no reading or update handed in. Whether that is a case of `TurningInTime`'s kept-below laws (`turns_in_time_iff_kept_below`, `unending_iff_above_kept_below`) or of `ClosedCapture.turned_by_loss`, or cannot be.

## What would count against it (frozen)

2. The dichotomy differs in kind between the orders L and B, not by a shift: then the claim is about the order, not about giving.
3. Every statement that holds is an existing theorem under a renaming (`RegimeMargin`'s holding region and `ends_iff_below`, `Coupled.ends_iff_below`, `MutualActivation.saves`, `Relay.cycles`, `ClosedCapture.turned_by_loss`, `TurningInTime`): then nothing is new, and what is owed is an atlas link at most.
4. No older proof would become a case and get shorter: then it is a piece added beside the others, said as that, and not landed as a join.
5. C2 (ii)'s condition is its own conclusion handed in (for example "the level never falls below `a`").
6. C1 is false, or true only because `after` never reads the level's law: if the second, it is said as what it is, the alikeness law with one more thing that makes no difference.

My sentence "a growing way that ends and one that goes on under one comparison" holds only for one kind on a stock that gives nothing, and no theorem sets it against `need_unmet`.

Nothing is handed in as a mark or a reading: the mark is the level being below `m`, and the cut is `after` itself. `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` is re-derived from it with its statement verbatim (`same_statement` by `rfl`); if the package's theorem rested on this, about 21 lines would go from `WayOfUnits` against about 470 of general parts coming in below it.
