# 17 November 2026: the second way tried in the line's terms, and what was left

*AI-written (Fable, board row DJ), filed 17 November 2026. A record of a try, kept with what failed. No result is landed by it: the two Lean modules here are text, outside the package, and nothing in the package or the atlas changed.*

**What it bears on.** The direction wants two ways of going on placed with respect to each other: one that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and one that keeps remaking what it rests on. The direction page said the second "is in none of this month's models". This was the first try at it after Ren's word of 17 November that we have the direction and can start working on it ([his words](../../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-16-no-corners-the-interconnected-map.md)). **How it came out, in short** (these four paragraphs were rewritten on the afternoon of 17 November, after another AI session, the one coordinating the landings, set the first wording against the Lean and found premises left out; the first wording is in the repository's history, and that session's result is beside this note as `check-landings-17-nov.result.json`). The guess mostly failed as something new: units that add to the level and leave only themselves, beside units that only take and need at least as much, are (on paper and in search, not in Lean) the line's own course on a stock whose giving is renamed; a try of 16 November had found that for members that are always there. Two statements were left and both are proved in scratch.

One (`covered`): where every unit has offspring, offspring need no more than their parent, and every unit adds to the level at least what its offspring together take, a way that is not empty and whose needs are met by what is left goes on without end. It does not hold the second way: what a unit adds comes from nowhere in the model, and where offspring together take more than their parent each unit must add more than it takes, so a growing way that goes on there is a way of sources. That last step is an argument from the definitions, not a theorem; with nothing taken a way doubles and goes on with nothing added (`OneKind.unending_iff`).

The other (`mixed_turns_iff`, in three forms) is in the line as it is: a part whose units all have one need and that can grow, written before units that each leave only themselves and all have one need, positive and no greater than the part's; under the stock's premises at what those units take, up to a ceiling the start is within, with that need at most what those units take plus the level from which giving meets it (`within : n ≤ H₂ + y₂`), and a premise under which the course with the part never cut comes below those units' need at some step (the third form also asks that the part is not empty). Then whether the rest goes on is decided at the first step at which the part's need is unmet and the next, with no mark or reading handed in. `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` is its case with the statement unchanged. It is not landed. The kept example's `five_by_law` is of the way `[k, g]`, the part written first, though the module's header says `[g, k]`; no lemma there says the order of a way makes no difference.

The frozen file's 11:55 section says (line 80) that where what a going-on rests on is one amount standing apart, a going-on that remakes it "is not sayable". That is an argument from the model's definitions, carried by no theorem. And its "not tried", of goings-on that rest on each other (line 82; the direction page had "That is not tried"), was too wide: readers the same afternoon reported goings-on that rest on each other stated in the Lean in part, in separate modules (conditions that each give by what is left of the others, with nothing passing between them and no units), with the join to a line that multiplies still missing ([the dig's record](../dig-2026-11-17/NOTE.md)).

**Not done.** Neither module is in the package. The reader's repairs to the second (the order of the way in the kept example; saying that the premises entail the cut) are not made. Whether to land it as a join is open.
