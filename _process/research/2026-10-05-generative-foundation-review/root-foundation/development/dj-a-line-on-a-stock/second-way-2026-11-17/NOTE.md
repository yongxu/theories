# 17 November 2026: the second way tried in the line's terms, and what was left

*AI-written (Fable, board row DJ), filed 17 November 2026. A record of a try, kept with what failed. No result is landed by it: the two Lean modules here are text, outside the package, and nothing in the package or the atlas changed.*

**What it bears on.** The direction wants two ways of going on placed with respect to each other: one that uses up what it or others rest on and can no longer be turned, and one that keeps remaking what it rests on. The direction page said the second "is in none of this month's models". This was the first try at it after Ren's word of 17 November that we have the direction and can start working on it ([his words](../../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-16-no-corners-the-interconnected-map.md)). Of the words used here (line, stock, need, giving) he said the same morning that they are probably not the right words, and that he would not dictate so early. They are working names of one model's parts.

- `search/`: the programs and their outputs. Searches are searches, not proofs.

**How it came out, in short.** The guess mostly failed as something new: units that add to the level beside units that only take are the line's own course on a stock whose giving is renamed, as a try of 16 November had already found. Two statements were left and both are proved in scratch. One, a way that grows and goes on where each unit adds at least what its offspring take, does not hold the second way: what a unit adds comes from nowhere in the model, and a growing way that goes on is a way of sources. The other is a turn that comes from the fall itself for a part that can grow beside units that leave only themselves, in the line as it is; `WayOfUnits.two_units_iff` is its case with the statement unchanged, and it is not landed. What the try shows about the model is on the frozen file's last section: where what a going-on rests on is one amount standing apart, remaking it cannot be said.

**Not done.** Neither module is in the package. The reader's repairs to the second (the order of the way in the kept example; saying that the premises entail the cut) are not made. Whether to land it as a join is open.
