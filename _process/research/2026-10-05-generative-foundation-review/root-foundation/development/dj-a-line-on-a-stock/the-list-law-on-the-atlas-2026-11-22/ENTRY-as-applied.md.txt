## The dynamic `kept-at-the-length-of-a-list`, family H

**Name.** The largest class a step preserves, reached at a list's length / 步所保之最大类，至列表之长而得

**Mechanism.** A step on classes of states and a start: the iterate at 0 is the start, and the iterate at n + 1 is the step applied to the iterate at n. The step is monotone where a class inside another has its step inside the other's step. Where a monotone step takes the start into itself, each iterate lies inside the one before and inside the start. Where also a list holds every member of the start, a state is in the iterate at the list's length exactly where it is in every iterate (iter_iff_all); and that iterate lies inside the start, lies inside its own step, and contains every class inside the start that lies inside its own step: the largest class inside the start that the step preserves (iter_largest). With no list, for a monotone step, a class inside the start that the step preserves lies in every iterate (postfixed_in_iter). The other way, where a monotone step's start lies inside its own step, each iterate lies inside the next (iter_succ). Let G be that a state y is in the iterate at a list's length. Where the step is monotone, its start lies inside its own step, the step commutes with implication to G (for every class W and state z: if z is in the step of the class of states t where (W t → G) → G, and if z in the step of W gives G, then G), and the list holds every state at which membership in the start implies G, y in any iterate is in the iterate at the list's length (iter_within_length). If every monotone step on a one-state type took membership in iterate 2 from the empty start to membership in iterate 1, every proposition would be decided (singleton_rise_needs_em). Theorems the Lean proves by calling a declaration of the law: with a list of every state at which Q holds, Q holds throughout the closure of a relation from s exactly where s is in the iterate at the list's length, from Q, of the step asking Q here and every successor in the class (held_iff_iter, by iter_largest and by «所闭之承最大», which states that what holds throughout the closure is the largest class inside Q that the relation's successors keep; listed under a-reading-kept-by-its-update); with a list of every state within a guard, a state viable for as many steps as the list is long has an enabled move all of whose outcomes are viable for as many (GuardHorizon.viable_persists, by iter_persists); with a list of every configuration, the guard is keepable from s (some run from s stays within the guard) exactly where s is in the iterate at the list's length, from the guard, of the step asking the guard here and some move with an outcome in the class (Viability.keepable_iff_count, by iter_largest and by Onward.KeepsMoving.run, which takes that list; listed under never-leaving-a-guard); with a list of every code whose class lies within the guard, and where after each usable move from such a code's class every class seen is exactly the class of some code, a code's class is kept for as many steps as the list is long exactly where it lies in some family of classes within the guard each of which has a usable move after which every class seen is in the family (Viability.coded_kept_iff_closed: from kept to a closed family by iter_postfixed with iter_iff_kept; Beside recurrence-without-premise (family F), where a list holds every member of a sequence of states and the sequence revisits from every step on: here the list holds the members of the start, the states at which membership in the start implies G, or every state; the step is on classes, and no run or process is in the statements of the law itself. It is placed in family H, kept regions, because what the law concludes is a class the step keeps: the largest class inside the start that the step preserves (iter_largest); no statement of it is of a cycle or an alternation, as in family F.

- That one way of selecting is placed against another is not shown.

## The map card

**plain_en.** For a monotone step on classes of states (a class inside another has its step inside the other's step) that takes its start into itself (the step of the start lies inside the start), where a list holds every member of the start: a state is in the iterate at the list's length exactly where it is in every iterate, and that iterate is the largest class inside the start that the step preserves, that is, that lies inside its own step. With no list, for a monotone step, a class inside the start that lies inside its own step lies in every iterate. Where the start instead lies inside its own step, the step commutes with implication to a state y's being in the iterate at the list's length, and the list holds every state at which membership in the start implies that, y in any iterate is in the iterate at the list's length; without the commuting premise, asking this of every monotone step on a one-state type would decide every proposition. That the step is monotone, what it does to the start, and what the list holds are premises, not derived; the number of an iterate counts applications of the step.

**plain_zh.** 对一个作用于状态之类的单调的步（一类在另一类之内，则它的步在另一类的步之内），它又把起始类带入起始类自身（起始类的步在起始类之内）；若有一个列表容纳起始类的每一个成员：一个状态在迭代到列表长度那一次的类之中，恰在它在每一次迭代的类之中之时，而那一类是起始类之内为此步所保的最大类，即起始类之内、在自身的步之内的最大类。没有列表时，对单调的步，起始类之内、在自身的步之内的类，在每一次迭代的类之中。若起始类反在它自身的步之内，此步与“蕴含状态 y 在迭代到列表长度那一次的类之中”相交换，且列表容纳每一个“在起始类之中便蕴含此事”的状态，则 y 在任何一次迭代的类之中，就在迭代到列表长度那一次的类之中；没有交换的前提，若对单一状态的类型上每一个单调的步都要求如此，每一个命题就都是已判定的。步为单调、步对起始类之所为、列表之所容，都是前提，不是推出的；迭代的序号数的是此步施用的次数。
