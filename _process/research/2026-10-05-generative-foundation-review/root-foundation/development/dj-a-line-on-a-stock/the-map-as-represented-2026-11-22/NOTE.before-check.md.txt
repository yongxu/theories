# The map as represented, counted on 22 November 2026

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ). A measurement of the atlas's store and of Lean's proof terms; nothing is built into any tool, no Lean is written, and nothing here is a result. Every figure is a count, told and not to be moved. What a reader who took no part changed in this note is said at its end. No person has read it.*

## Why

On the evening of 22 November Ren asked the session that holds row FM whether the map really has a centre, and wrote: "we want the map to be represented as "the map" - is our approaching right?" ([his words](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-22-does-our-map-have-a-centre.md); he asked and did not state). The coordinating session then put to this row, as its reading, that the atlas's links could be read off Lean when a page is drawn, where today they are typed by hand. This note counts what the represented map has today and what such a reading would add, so that the question can be put to him with figures and their limits. It decides nothing.

## What was counted, and by what

- The atlas's store on main `10742ca6f` (`theories/becoming/results/atlas/entries/dynamics.json`, 186 dynamics).
- A list Lean printed that day of every theorem declared in a module of the package, with the package's theorems that occur in its proof term (`AllProofHeads.lean.txt` is the file that prints it; `README-of-the-list.md.txt` says what each field is, how it was wrong twice that day and corrected, and its limits; the list itself is not filed: 4.7 MB, sha256 beginning 1f7f88bed4bb6307, regenerated by that file in about half a minute).
- One script, `count_the_map.py.txt`, whose whole output is `counts.txt`. Every figure below is a line of that output.

Three kinds of link between two dynamics are kept apart:

- **A road:** an exit, or an end of a transition, that names another dynamic. It says how a course gets from the one to the other.
- **The same law seen twice:** a case row that names a law another dynamic lists, or a joining. It says two entries overlap.
- **Proved from:** a theorem listed under one dynamic has in its proof term, directly or through theorems no dynamic lists, a theorem listed under another. It is a dependence. It does not say "a case of", it is not a road, and it is not typed by anyone.

## What the store has, kept by hand

- 186 dynamics list 2,785 declarations. Of the package's 20,567 theorems, 2,156 are listed under some dynamic, 102 of them under two or more. Six dynamics list no declaration.
- **Roads:** of 329 exits, 71 name another dynamic; of 105 transitions, none names another dynamic at either end. That is 58 pairs of dynamics; 111 dynamics have no road to or from another; the largest piece joined by roads has 17.
- **The same law seen twice:** 62 pairs; 111 dynamics have none.
- **Both kinds together:** 105 pairs; 81 dynamics are linked to no other; the other 105 fall into 20 pieces, the largest of 41.

## Where the measure flatters, by the coordinating session's attack

The coordinating session reproduced the main figures by a script of its own and set the measure against itself (`coordinator-on-the-measure.md.txt`, its words). In short, with this note's figures:

1. Chains through unlisted theorems make the larger numbers; by a direct use the largest piece is 74 and not 114.
2. In 22 of the 230 links every witness ends in a theorem also listed under the source dynamic: the link is there because one theorem is listed under two dynamics, which is a choice kept by hand.
3. Six dynamics list no declaration and can have no such link; they are among the 54 and the 45.
4. It cannot see a road. 28 of the 105 hand-kept pairs are not proved-from pairs. A passage from one dynamic to another is a statement and not a dependence, so the measure adds most where the map is least a map of change.
5. About a tenth of the package's theorems are under any dynamic; the chains of point 1 run through the rest.
6. A link runs from what uses to what is used: the dynamics most depended on (`least-loss-rest`, 15) are the most basic, not a middle.

By the same attack the joined piece is not a star: without the one, three or five most depended-on dynamics it is still 110, 104 and 86 of the rest (the coordinating session's figures, from the first form of the links file), and the theorems most links end in are laws of the account and not lemmas everything uses.

## What it does not show

- That any proved-from link is a case, a join or a passage; that the dynamics so linked say one thing; that the map is nearer its aim.
- Anything about the nine tenths of the theorems that no dynamic lists, or about notions the atlas has no dynamic for.
- That the list is complete: it is one tool's output, wrong twice on the day it was made, and a floor.
- What "represented as the map" asks for. That is Ren's to say. Whether the atlas should carry a layer drawn from Lean beside the hand-kept one is put to him and is not decided; it belongs to the board's line 0 (e).
