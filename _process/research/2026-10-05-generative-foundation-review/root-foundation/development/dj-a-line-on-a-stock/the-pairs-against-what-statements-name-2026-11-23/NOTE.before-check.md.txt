# The pairs the direction page names, set against a print of what statements name (23 November 2026)

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ). No Lean of the package was written or changed, nothing was entered on the atlas, and nothing here is a result. Every verdict below is an AI reader's reading of a statement, not a proof. What a reader who took no part changed in this note is said at its end.*

## Why

The layer built on 22 November draws a line where one theorem's proof uses another ([its record](../uses-between-proofs-2026-11-22/NOTE.md)). It cannot see a passage, which is in what a theorem says. Lean can also print what a statement names, and the open question of that evening was whether the atlas should draw that too. Asked to explain and to suggest an approach, the session that holds row DJ advised against drawing it across the atlas and for one piece first; Ren wrote "agree with your approach" ([his words, with what he had been told and what in it was wrong](../../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-23-agree-with-your-approach.md)). The piece is the one the direction page's aim names: two ways of going on placed with respect to each other and to going on at all.

## What was taken as the two ways

No new choice. [The direction page](../../../../../../../_process/DIRECTION.md) says under "Which two" that the two ways are not named in the Lean and that the account has several pairs, of which it names four: on the grammar, where a pattern generates, keeps or degenerates; what can form widening or narrowing; renewing and consuming on the web; a lock against a turn in time. To these the step added going on at all (lasting, ceasing, no return), from the aim's own words; that one is the step's addition and is counted apart throughout. That the two ways are any of these stays the page's reading.

## What was frozen first, and what the attack changed

The plan, a forecast and what would count against the step were written and hashed before any reader ran (`FROZEN.as-first-frozen.md.txt`, 00:10 WIB). One reader then attacked that file (`returns/RETURN-attack.md.txt`). Its main finding was taken whole, under "After" in `FROZEN.md.txt` (hashed 00:31, before any candidate was judged; both hashes are in `FROZEN.sha256.txt`, under the files' names before `.txt` was added for filing):

- **The step sees names standing together in one statement, and nothing else.** Three sides are stated in the Lean mostly by the shape of statements and by no notion of their own: narrowing is `Web.Widens` with its two level paths exchanged; the turn in the capture modules is `RuleCapture.Locked` with the order reversed, and the turn laws of `TurningInTime` are an iff over `«不穷»` that names no turn notion; the lock's ending is written `∀ v, ¬ net … t v`. So the step claims no absence. The first file's test "no statement names both sides of a pair" was withdrawn as a gain.
- The five verdicts first written were replaced by eight, after the attacker tried them on six statements: passage (by an index or a parameter, row FM's two kinds from [its survey of roads](../../fm-roads-the-atlas-tells-2026-11-22/NOTE.md)); implies; equivalent; wall; covers; built case; across (one side said of one going-on and the other of another); name only.
- "Already told" was given a meaning and a decider who is not the session: the source names the theorem, or states its conclusion with every premise the statement takes.
- Three factual sentences of the first file were wrong and are corrected there: what the print holds, how many pairs the page names, and what row FM's three kinds are.

Not taken from the attack: it asked that the control theorems the print does not see be read. They are told by construction (next section), and reading them would set the tellings against the Lean, which is another piece of work.

## What stands, by pair

**Renewing with consuming.** No passage and no implication. What stands is walls, and by the refuter's reading of the proof heads the eleven walls are fewer facts: each wall is the two definitions' opposite inequalities at one shared step. Independent, by that reading: a renewed condition is not consumed (`Web.renewed_not_consumed`, with its twin on the fixed-dependence form); a kept dependence is not broken and a renewing event is not undermining (`Web.kept_not_broken`, `Web.RenewingAt.not_undermining`, one inequality at the next step); renewing within a cycle of `p` excludes undermining over `p` (`Web.RenewingInWithin.not_undermining_for`); and `Web.Restored.not_consumed`, which the chapter names. `Web.kept_or_broken` is the order on the numbers with the two notions' shared conjuncts handed in. Two found on the way by that refuter and not among the candidates' verdicts: the step grain and the whole-run grain come apart (`Unmet.consumed_without_loss` states a formation consuming over the whole run with nothing broken and no event undermining at any step), so the step-grain walls place nothing about the whole-run classes; and in the part of `Restoration.lean` it read (lines 44 to 262) it found no statement that a condition is either restored or consumed. The 31 built cases are the bulk of this cell.

**The grammar, and the grammar with widening.** Inside the triples: ending on every branch gives reaching an end where continuing is decided, and only up to a double negation without that; all three statements are named in chapter 02, outside the linked passage. With widening: the six statements are calls of one law that chapter 07 tells (keeping widening gives generation on the course's moments, where each point is refuted or unrefuted), or they price its premise against excluded middle, and two of those were refused as placings. The refuter narrows one of them: the half that says a formation is kept follows from nothing being lost, not from widening.

**Widening with renewing or consuming.** One statement stands whole and says more than its tellings: `Flow.Balances.includes_of_renewed`. Under its premises the levels with the part include the levels without it; chapter 07 and the direction page tell the weaker corollary, that such a part does not narrow what can form. `includes_of_renewingIn` is one call of it. `Web.UnderminingOn.exceeded` stands narrowed: its proof discards the drawing formation, and what is left is that where the level after a step is below a dependant's need, a path admitting that dependant exceeds the levels. `Web.Includes.not_undermining` was refused as a placing of widening against consuming.

**Going on at all.** With the grammar, two stand that the sources do not tell: on a web, an endless line no support of which lasts a step is generation with every step a carrying renewal (`Web.generation_of_one_step_supports`; its sibling for `K + 1` steps is named in chapter 07), and an endless line on which nothing becomes active that was not carried on is keeping (`Web.keeping_of_carried_on`; the refuter notes its premise is close to its conclusion in other words). The rest are projections of definitions or are named in a chapter. With renewing and consuming, all twelve are built cases. With the lock and the turn, **no passage is left**: `TurningInTime.locked_turns_in_time_share_iff` falls on the ground row FM's checker gave on 22 November and one more (the lock's own outcome is no conjunct; `locked` only bounds the use from below); `noReturnAt_of_below` and `noReturn_exists` are each one call of a least-step law; `NoReturnAt.recoverable_iff` is the definition with `Recoverable.earlier`. `ClosedCapture.lock_line` stands narrowed to: locked at the start is locked at every step, with nothing said of the course or of what sustains it.

## The tests, answered

- **T1 and T2.** Most of what stands is told, is a call of a told law, or is a definition unfolded. By the letter of T2(a) three statements stand that place one side with respect to another and that the sources do not tell: `Web.generation_of_one_step_supports`, `Web.keeping_of_carried_on`, and `Flow.Balances.includes_of_renewed`, the last told with less than is proved. None is a passage, and none places with respect to each other the two ways the aim names. For T2(b), two tellings say more than the statement the print found: the direction page's sentence that renewing from an event excludes consuming from any later event that continues it, which the page gives as worked on paper and which `Web.renewingAlong_not_consumingAlong` states of the same event only; and chapter 02's "two drawers each within renewal can together consume it", which leaves out three premises of `SupportGatedWebs.jointly_consuming`. No sentence of the sources of the form "not found" was contradicted.
- **T3.** It does not fall as written: seven of 128 were name only. But the forecast was wrong the other way too. What the print finds is built cases (72), and among general statements walls from the definitions and calls of told laws.
- **T4.** Three sides are stated by the shape of statements (above). The print was not used for them.
- **The forecast.** "Most candidates: name only" was wrong. "Passages that survive the refuter and that the page does not tell: fewer than five, more likely none" held: none.

By the rule written first, with so little that the sources do not tell: **nothing is drawn on any page of the map by this step, and the print stays a lookup.** No layer of statement links is proposed for the atlas.

## What it shows of the map, and what it does not

- Where notions of two of these sides stand in one statement, the Lean mostly shows a built case. Where it says something general it excludes (walls) or one side gives another at one index. This is of the 128 judged and says nothing of the 176 controls the print does not see, nor of what is stated by unfolding.
- It does not show that two sides are unrelated in the Lean. No sentence here says that.
- It does not place the two ways with respect to each other, and it found no statement that does.
- The print is useful as a lookup, on this night's evidence only: before any reader had returned it had answered, in seconds, whether any statement names sampling with selection through couplings, whether the lock's course is already stated at stretches, and whether the level's clock (recoverable, no return) is named with the lock or with the clocks of the counts (not named together; and the two turn laws of the lock write the point of no return out in numbers and name neither notion). Each such answer is "not named together by this print".
