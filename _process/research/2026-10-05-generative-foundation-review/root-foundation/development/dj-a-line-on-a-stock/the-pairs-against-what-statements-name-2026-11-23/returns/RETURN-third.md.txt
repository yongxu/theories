- `joins`: f takes part in c at t (`W.requires t f c ∨ W.draws t f c`, line 163).
- `broken`: the Broken premise itself.

**Each side:**
- Broken is said of g's dependence on c across the step from t ("met at `t`, not after the step"). Grain: one dependence, one step. It is affirmed, as a premise. Its definition has no clause on later steps, so it is not `Consumed`.
- RenewingAt is said of f's event at t. Grain: one formation at one step, quantifying over every condition f takes part in and every dependence on them. It is denied, in the conclusion.
- Both sit at the same step t and the same condition c. f and g are free and may or may not be the same formation.

**Hesitation:** between WALL and ACROSS (Broken is of g's dependence, RenewingAt of f's event), with IMPLIES a distant third.
- I chose WALL because `RenewingAt t f` unfolds to `∀ c, Participates t f c → ∀ g, requires t g c → Kept t g c`. The broken dependence (g, c) is one of the things RenewingAt of f already speaks of, so this is not a second going-on.

- It is not IMPLIES because Y is denied, not given.

**Reach of the wall:** only events at that step t of formations taking part in that c. Nothing is said about other steps, about formations not taking part in c, or about `RenewingIn`, `Renewed` or `Consumed`.
