## What to try

1. **A front and a record for the direction page, by moving and not rewriting.** The front: the aim, the idea as now understood, what is proved with pointers, what is open; a few thousand characters. Every dated paragraph moves, word for word, to a file for its day. A script checks that each paragraph of the old page stands in exactly one place afterwards. Sessions load the front and open a day's record by pointer.
2. **The same for a row of the board.** A cell says what the row is, what it has claimed now and what is open. What landed is already on the Queue line, in the record and in the study note; the rest of a cell goes to the board's history file. A Queue clause can be a few hundred characters with a pointer to the note; this row's clause that day was 3,959.
3. **A law's premises told once.** The record tells each law with its premises. The root's bullet, the README row, the checker's text and the region note become one line each, the module's name and a pointer, or are made from the record by script. Then a reader reads the Lean and one telling, and copies cannot drift apart.
4. **Start from a short page.** `AGENTS.md`, the hand-over page and one's own row; for a place in the Lean, its page in [the listing of places](../places-2026-11-21/PLACES.md) before the file; for a law, its record. Not the whole direction page or the whole board.

What would count against these: a session that starts from the front and then acts against something only a dated paragraph says; or a reader of one telling missing what a reader of eleven would have caught.
