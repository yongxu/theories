## What the Lean already held beside the evening's understanding

*As the readers reported them, with the declaration names they gave. The AI holding this row has read the head notes of `ReadingsSpread`, `ReflexivePrediction`, `PoolFromCourse` and `ReadInTime` and the statements in `RelativePosition`, and not the proofs. None of these has yet been set against the understanding on the direction page, and none of these laws is a premise or a case of this month's theorems; this month's lock is the same lock, and its reading of a level is `PoolFromCourse`'s window. All are in the package's `Becoming/` folder.*

- **An account that is acted on** (`ReflexivePrediction`, 30 October; atlas `a-prediction-read-by-what-it-predicts`). A prediction published and read by what it predicts is right exactly where the response to it is itself (`Right`). Where the response moves every value, none is right (`none_right_of_moves`). Where a response keeps a finite chain and its order, publishing again from the bottom reaches the least right prediction (`bottom_rests`); where two are right, the start decides which is reached (`ThresholdResponse`). No reading and no many are in it.
- **Readings that spread and stay** (`ReadingsSpread`, 10 November; atlas `readings-revised-spread-and-locked`). Each kid carries its parent's reading as updated by what the parent sensed; the update is handed in (`Generations.RevisesBy`). The share of a region of readings moves by selection and by revision (`share_step`). A region of readings that holds every member stays where each revises from a member of its own cohort and revising within the region stays within it (`fixed_of_present`). Built worlds: the other reading's carrier leaves no kid and the narrow reading is carried by all from then on (`Contact.removed_locked`); a narrow reading spreads (`Commons.cleared_spreads`).
- **Codes that come to agree** (`SharedCodes`, 19 October; atlas `a-code-formed-between-formations`, `merged-distinctions-stay-merged`). An exact condition under which hearings bring every start to agreement (`agrees_every_start_iff`); an imposed code costs each holder exactly the distinctions it merges (`impose_loses_iff`); a form every formation holds stays (`held_stays`).
- **The lock and who reads beyond the share** (`PoolFromCourse`, 10 November; `DrawnShaping`; `ReadInTime`, 26 October; `ClosedCapture`). From a locked step with no reader of what lies beyond the share present, where none is brought back and the selection does not lower the share under a favouring rule whatever lies beyond, the course is locked at every later step (`PoolFromCourse.closed_stays_closed`). In the same module a loss borne by the favoured side lowers the share and the lock is left with no reader at any step (`Borne.shapers_left`). With the readers drawn from the selected, a rule read from the share alone is locked one way or the other (`DrawnShaping.drawn_locked_either`). Where the others read the loss once it reaches a bound, there are exact conditions for the course being kept or turned (`ReadInTime.kept_iff`, `turned_iff`). A lock outlives a loss nothing in the loop reads, and a reading of the loss turns it (`ClosedCapture.lock_outlives_loss`, `turned_by_loss`).
- **Position** (`RelativePosition`; `Optimum`, the `InTurn` laws; 19 to 22 October; atlas `position-does-not-protect`, `a-race-for-position-draws-everything`). Over a family of parts the rises in position total the falls, whatever the amounts did (`change_balances`, `rise_needs_fall`). A change keeps every position exactly where every amount changes by one common amount, so positions give no bound on the total from above (`keeps_iff_common`, `keeps_exceeds`). Among two or more who draw in turn, what a drawer leaves places it no lower than leaving nothing only if it is nothing, given that its own later draw is the same, that what passes on of what it leaves is at most that, and that all draw no less later (`InTurn.position_keeps_nothing`). In one built world where something left would stop what stands next, the drawer keeps it (`Stopping.perfect_keeps`, the kept countermodel). The same positions can go with what is taken staying within what is given (`RaceWithin.same_positions_apart`).
- **A rule put in a locked rule's place** (atlas `a-counter-rule-read-from-its-own-share`; `RuleCapture.uniform_locks`, `locked_stays`). If it too is read from the share it selects, no lower at a share no lower, selection does not lower its carriers' share under a favouring rule, and every rule it reads favours its carriers, it is locked from the step after its second reading step. Said of a counter-rule this is an AI's interpretation of those laws.
- **A reading revised by a move** (`OpenedCorrection.Linked.opens`, 30 October): rebuilding a link from stock revises the reading and opens a correction in one step. The move is handed in.
- **Not found in any model:** a value that comes to be; a signal or distinction that comes to be other than by an update handed in; many coming to one reading from what they do; a selection that rebuilds what selects it across changes of who carries it (atlas `self-reproducing-selection-across-carriers`, no declaration); in this month's models, anything that goes on and gives back to what it rests on.
