## Where we are heading

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, whole enough that a person, a society or an AI can find its own course on it: what that course rests on, what cannot reach it, and whether it can still turn. On it, three things are placed relative to each other: a way of selecting that closes on itself and uses up what it lives on, a way of going on that keeps remaking what it rests on, and going on at all. Placed means: where a course stays, where it changes from one to another, and by what ways. We want it to be of use to each, and a base for alignment. We want it joined and simple: its parts made to meet, and each thing said once.

We are far from it. Counted on 16 November, 86 of the atlas's 185 dynamics are linked to no other.

## The idea, as we understand it now

Whatever lasts is made again and again, out of other things that are going on too. So how a thing goes on is also doing something to what lets it go on.

What a going-on rests on, and what makes a difference to it, need not be the same. The gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

What makes a difference to a going-on is not simply given. Some goings-on hold to something: a thermostat to a temperature, a body to its balance, a firm to its profit, a training run to its score. To do that, such a loop registers some things and acts on them. What it holds to is its value. What it registers is its signal. The lines it acts by, enough or not enough, mine or not mine, ahead or behind, are lines it draws. Value, signal and line are things the loop does, and they are on the map with everything else. Together we call them its reading. A reading settles which differences make a difference there; whatever else differs is alike to it. So for such a loop the gap lies where its reading stops.

From a reading an account of the world is drawn. It is true of what the reading registers and silent about the rest. Read only who is ahead of whom, and every gain is another's loss, whatever happens to what all of them live on. When many select by one reading and act on its account, the account comes true of what they do. What it is silent about can then be used up while nothing they register differs, and how they select is set again from what the reading already favours. In a small model this is a lock. At the scale of a society it has been called Moloch, or a cancer: a way of reading that keeps remaking the conditions that favour it, until what it rests on gives out, unless that running down comes to make a difference in time.

What goes wrong there is a closing. A loop is set again only from what it already holds, and nothing is left that could turn it. Narrowing to one is how it closes: where all read by one reading, no other is left to register what that one misses. One value taken for what one is, and given in to, is such a closing. So is meaning folded back onto the self alone.

There is no last loop. What one holds to is set by others, and those by others again, with no first and no top; they go on and change. So we do not look for a place where value is finally derived. Wherever a loop holds to something the same can be asked: does what it rests on still reach how it reads, early enough to turn it?

A way of going on can keep remaking what it rests on, use it up, or change what it rests on, and differently at different grains. From inside these can look alike until late, because what is being used up may be exactly what makes no difference to the course while it lasts.

Of the ways open to something, which one happens goes with what makes a difference to it. We call this selection, and the word is fine. A relation with two ends is not a dualism. The trap is a sentence in which what favours is not itself kept up or run down by what it favours.

We think this is one relation and not four topics. Along descent it is evolution. Along supply it is what a life has to keep up. Along response it is selection. Along time it is how a future opens or closes. If it is one, it may be one by the same question holding wherever a loop holds to something, and not by one model that contains the rest; that is a guess. It fails to be one if selection cannot be shown as a case of it. Since 16 November one small model has selection as a step of it: which needs the level meets decides who leaves offspring. What selects is given there, and the join is thin: the laws of alikeness hold with the stock taken out, an outcome fixed beforehand as one that would count as nothing having changed.

Nothing here says a course should last. It says under what conditions one does, and those conditions hold whether or not anyone values anything. Each piece holds under stated conditions, and whether a case meets them the situation says. What we want is people and AI on courses where what they rest on keeps making a difference to how they select, early enough to turn. No value fixed once does that, ours included: what a course rests on changes, and a reading that nothing else can turn can come to miss it.

The map is a reading too, drawn from inside. Its use is to let a reading be seen as a reading: what it registers, what it rests on, and what it leaves out. It will not do that as a better argument inside a game, because by the game's own measure the game is winning. It helps only where something the game rests on comes to count. And it can become the next single measure, a way of ranking who sees. So it is proved where it can be, open to anyone's check, and held by no one.

## What it looks like in a case

One case has been tested: three AI fishers on a lake, and a mussel bed that gives them nothing and dies if the fish fall too low. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season; the result with instructions was mixed. It is on the one page.

Three more, untested and offered only so that the idea can be tried on something:

- a habit that pays every day while health runs down;
- a metric rebuilt each year from what it rewarded the year before;
- a model trained on its own outputs.

## What we have found so far

- **Pieces of it are proved, each under its own conditions.** What lives on something nothing renews ends when that runs out. Where what renews a stock also needs it, once below the need it does not come back. After such a loss, whatever still goes on has changed what it needs. A course does not differ where nothing reaches it. A rule set again from what it already favours stays as it is. And on the keeping side: a way given a response to a falling level before its point of no return does not end there, and one that responds later does.
- **Two sentences of the idea are read onto theorems of one small model, and a third theorem says where such a line holds** (16 November): *a line on a stock*. Units each need the level to be at least something, take from it, and leave offspring. The stock gives back by what is left. There is a level such that, starting at or below it and with nothing taken, the stock stays at or below it; every course here starts at or below it.
  - *What makes a difference.* Who comes next depends only on which needs the level meets. Where every unit leaves offspring it depends on exactly that: levels that meet different needs leave different next ways. Two courses that start from the same way, at any two levels and on any two stocks, have the same ways for as long as their levels meet the same needs.
  - *Used up while nothing yet differs.* Given that every unit's offspring together take more than it, that every unit needs something, that nothing comes back from nothing, and a start that is not empty, with every need met: within as many steps as that level plus one, some need is not met. Until then who is there is who would be there with every need met. Given also that offspring need no more than their parents, the level by then is below a need that was met at the start, and so below where it started. We read this as one sentence of the third paragraph above; that is a reading.
  - *Where it holds.* Where offspring together take the same and need no more, every unit leaves some and needs something, and nothing comes back from nothing, the line goes on without end exactly where it comes to a state in which every need is met and the level is at or above the point of no return of what they take together. That the stock has such a point all along the course is assumed. The earlier exact result, for units that only stop, is now its case; beyond it there is one worked case.
- **Beside the model, on a rule set again from what it favours** (15 November; the two results of 16 November are marked). The model has no rule and no share in it; one counting lemma serves both.
  - *Where nothing reads the level.* A rule that already overdraws ends still locked. So does one of which no overdraw is asked at the start (16 November), given such a level, nothing coming back from nothing, and that each favouring adds to the share and the draw is the share times a positive amount.
  - *Where the level is read.* The line goes on exactly where the first reading comes early enough, provided the corrected rule is kept wherever the level then goes, short of a level from which every rule holds it. Where the level is not read, a correction to the rule alone does not hold: the rule is set again from the share. In one worked course that undoes the correction and the line ends; in another, what goes on is the old rule. Where a reading also lowers the share (16 November), and a low share gives the corrected rule and stays low, the proviso is not needed and what goes on is the corrected rule.
  - Given for both exact conditions: the overdraw before the reading, which levels are read, and what a reading sets.
- **Of a reading and what is drawn from it** (16 November). The paragraphs on it above are an understanding, not a result. The tries, their readers' reports and the scratch proofs are kept in [the record of the day](research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dj-a-line-on-a-stock/tries-2026-11-16/NOTE.md).
  - *Proved in October, each in a model of its own.* Over a family of parts, the rises in position total the falls, whatever the amounts did. A change keeps every part's position exactly where every amount changes by one amount common to all, so positions give no bound on the total from above. Among two or more who draw in turn, a drawer that ranks what it leaves by its position leaves nothing, where its own later draw would be the same. And a rule put in a locked rule's place locks as that one did, if it too is read from the share it selects and every rule it reads favours its carriers; this last is an AI's reading of proved laws.
  - *Proved nowhere.* That a value, a signal or a line comes to be: every model hands them in. That many readings become one. That an account drawn from a reading comes true of what is done. The meta-attractor in the wide sense, a selection that rebuilds what selects it across changes of who carries it, has an entry in the atlas and no theorem. October's laws on position and this month's lock are not joined.
  - *Four tries on paper, each with what would count as failure written down first. As joins, all four failed.* One more general model: where who is there depends on the level, no condition on the start decides what happens. Links among the dynamics already there: two ways from one to another that the Lean proves and the atlas does not show, one small law, and little else. October's modules on regulation, set points and lines drawn: no link to this month's models, and three guesses of ours were wrong. A value held to and set again: what sets it is handed in one order up.
  - *Out of them, three statements proved in Lean outside the package and not landed*, each read against the Lean by a reader who did not write it: on a growing part among other units, on taking below the need of what gives, and on a value that every reading sets above what is taken. One of the three joins two dynamics. Lines rise in all three.
- **What is handed in is what is still missing.** "Selection" in the Lean was two relations that no theorem stated together: unequal continuing, and alikeness wherever what reaches a thing is the same. In the new model one step does both: a unit leaves offspring where the level meets its need, and, as said above, levels that meet the same needs leave the same next way. But the needs, the draws and the offspring are handed in, and nothing there comes to respond to the fall because of the fall: no new difference arrives from a difference not yet made.
- **Trying to join them taught us things.** Each time, readers who had not written a proof found a statement false or a telling that said more. A law we called "an overdraw comes about" concludes an ending, with the rise given. "A way that grows uses up what it rests on" needs offspring to need no more than their parents. A loss of something that takes nothing and leaves only its like changes nothing in the stock or in the rest; in one small case it still changes whether anything goes on. And the first drafts of this page, the plan and the page of questions said more than the proofs in a dozen places, most by leaving out a premise.
- **The Lean is large and mostly unjoined.** About 20,000 theorems in 403 modules when counted on 14 November, written as answers to one question after another. Its core is small. On 16 November one module came to rest on a more general one (six of its results are now cases of it) and went from 1,182 lines to 1,126; the landing as a whole added about 2,900.

## What we are trying next

Going on along [the plan for the Lean](LEAN-TOWARD-THE-DIRECTION.md), which is the order as it looks now. It holds the table of what a proof says today.

- One reading held by all, as a way into a lock; and a reading of what lies beyond one's own share that reaches in time, as a way out. October's laws on position and this month's lock are the pieces; whether they join is not known. On paper first.
- How a loop comes to register what it did not register. No model reaches it. We no longer look for a first reading from which the rest would follow.
- Losses that do not come back to whoever causes them. The model says that a line once gone does not come back, and gives one case in which a loss changes nothing else; who bears it is not in it.
- A case from outside the work. The proved conditions are put as [questions someone can ask of a case of their own](A-CASE-FROM-OUTSIDE.md). The page is prepared, not sent, and no test is built on it.
- Telling it where people read. The chapters do not yet tell what was proved on 15 and 16 November.

Whether classical Chinese says these things better was looked at on 15 November (chapter 06 and a note of that day). It is not what we are after: a result said again in another idiom, or from another base, is worth it where it joins or shortens.

## How we work on it

We think together. Each says what they understand in their own words and where they disagree, and neither's sentences are followed to the letter.

A proof that fails is news about the idea. We try not to fool ourselves: we say what is proved, what is shown only in a built example, and what we hope. What a proof showed is told after someone who did not write it has read it against the Lean; on 15 November such readings changed what one step was said to show, twice. A telling that keeps growing is a sign we have not understood it yet.

The idea is said again when something found changes it, a proof, a failed try or a case, and the finding is named beside the change.

The direction has been lost before in two opposite ways. An AI re-said the aim as a count or a score, and the work chased the count. And Ren's passing words were taken as orders, so that each new wording turned the work. On 16 November the first happened again in small: links and lines were counted as the measure, and runs were started to move the count. All of these are what this map describes: the work chose its next step from what it had already made, and what it rests on, whether the pieces join and whether he can read it, made no difference until late. What holds the direction is not a rule. It is two things kept able to turn the work: a proof that can fail, and his read at his pace.

## What we do not know

- Whether it is one relation, and if so, in what way it is one.
- How a reading comes to be and comes to change. Every model so far hands it in.
- Whether a reading that nothing else can turn always comes to miss what it rests on, or only can.
- Whether an account drawn from one reading and acted on by many can be shown to make itself true, in a model, with the conditions under which it does not.
- Whether amounts can be said in the same terms as everything else.
- Whether anyone outside the work can find their own case on the map.
