## What the readers found

The pieces of the picture you describe are already in the Lean. Each is there several times, under different names, and almost none is said in terms of another. "A region a course does not leave" is said in about ten vocabularies. The root has the general language (a closed region, what is drawn into it, a basin; every point generates, keeps or degenerates), and the centre of the work never uses it.

## The picture, as I now understand it

Something goes on by taking from a level of what it rests on, and something gives back.

**One boundary.** For a given take there is a level below which what comes back cannot cover it. From there up the level holds; below it the level runs out. This is proved, and exact.

**What goes on moves the boundary.** This is the part I had not seen as one thing. There are three ways.

- *It takes more.* The boundary rises. A course can end up on the wrong side without the level having fallen yet, by its own growth. Proved, with the growth given: a line whose successors together take more, and a rule set again from a share that is a count of those who draw.
- *It takes less.* A member stops when the level no longer meets it, or a reading corrects a rule. The boundary falls. The course is kept exactly where the level after that turn is on the kept side of the new boundary. Proved, exact.
- *It gives.* What goes on remakes what it rests on, and the boundary falls. This is proved exactly in one corner only: conditions that each give from what is left of the others, where each one's threshold falls as its partners rise, and a pair can hold together when neither holds alone. In the models of this month nothing that goes on gives. The stock remakes itself. **This is the missing generative half.**

**Two questions we have been running together.**

1. Is how it selects closed on itself? That is the lock. It is a region in the coordinates of the rule and the share, and it is proved closed.
2. Is it on the kept side of the boundary? That is a region in the coordinates of the level.

They are independent, so there are four cells, not three states. The meta-attractor as first written is the lock. Cancer is the lock on the wrong side of the boundary, or the lock with a take that grows. A lock whose take is met goes on: there is a worked course. And a taker that needs nothing of the level goes on for ever while the level is lost for whoever needs it: an October theorem says "persistence and success do not certify renewal". Going on is not the same as generating.

| **Boundaries** | Three, each seen in several models: a level against a take plus what it takes to cover it; a take against a renewal; a share against a critical mass. | Any boundary stated between two named regions. A boundary in how fast the take grows. |
| **Paths** | Lock to ended. Lock to kept, by a reading in time. Running down to renewal. How a lock forms, in models with no stock. | Kept to locked, on a stock: one witness only. Turning back to renewing: nothing. |

In the atlas no link runs from any lock to turning in time, though the Lean proves that path.

## Words the proofs have outgrown

Yours and mine. I list them because they still steer the texts.

- **"Meta-attractor"** names three unjoined things in the map: a rule with no stock, a selection across carriers with no law, and a rise confined within a whole. And it is not the opposite of renewal: the lock renews itself in what it reads.
- **"It goes on the same way until what sustains it gives out"** (my paragraph at the head of AGENTS.md). This holds only where the overdraw, or a growing take, is given.
- **"Never ends" as the good side.**
- **"Only a change of the law leaves a lock."** On a stock it can also end, or be read in time.
- **Mine of this week.** "One model". "Selection is a threshold on a need": that is who the level still carries; what selects is who leaves what, and that was fixed. "Nothing comes to respond to the fall": a member stopping is a response. The plan's nine steps: its step 6, how what selects comes to be, is the centre and not a step.
- **生生** carries three senses in the texts, none of them with a stock.

## What I would try next, on paper first

The smallest space in which all of this is one picture: one level, and a way whose members each take, need, **give**, and leave successors, where who leaves what is read off who is there. Then a rule is not a separate box.

- **The lock becomes a case.** A share is a count of members of a kind, and being favoured is leaving more of them. "What is favoured draws more" then follows from descent; today it is a premise.
- **The generative half comes in.** Members that give lower the boundary. The October theorem and the mutual-support threshold would be its cases.
- **Old proofs would have to go** if it holds: the lock's two newest laws, the October overdraw theorem, and the confined rise's premise that the excess is given.

*What would count as nothing having changed, fixed now.* No premise that is given today comes out as a conclusion. Or changing the stock alone, or the way alone, does not change the conclusion. Or no proof older than 12 November is deleted and returned as a case. Or the condition is on the whole course and not on the start.

*It may fail.* The October model gives before it takes and this month's after, so the join may be a dictionary again. And the old lock's share is a ratio, which can rise while the draw falls.

**Cheap and true meanwhile.** Four links whose law is already in the Lean are missing from the atlas, the lock to turning in time among them. With six more that need a law, the map's 108 pieces would become 98 and its largest would go from 30 dynamics to 61, by one reader's count.
