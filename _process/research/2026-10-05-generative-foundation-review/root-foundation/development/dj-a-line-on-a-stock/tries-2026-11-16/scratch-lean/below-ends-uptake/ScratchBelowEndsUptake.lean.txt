import Becoming.Continuation

/-!
# Below the need of what gives to it, taking from a condition does not go on

AI-written (Fable), 16 November 2026. Scratch; not on main.

`Becoming.Continuation` proves the loss of renewal for good on a flow: where whatever gives to a
condition depends on it at a need of at least `m` and is supported where it gives, a level below
`m` stays below (`Flow.stays_below_at`) and nothing is given to it (`Flow.given_zero_below`).
`Becoming.Flow` proves that taking at arbitrarily late steps needs giving
(`Flow.uptake_needs_replenishment_from`). This module makes the two meet in one law, and keeps
what the law does not say.

* **The law, on one condition.** From a step `T` at which the level of `c` is below `m`, with, at
  every step from `T` on, the flow balanced on `c` and whatever gives to `c` depending on it at a
  need of at least `m` and supported where it gives: it is not the case that something is taken
  from `c` at arbitrarily late steps (`Flow.below_ends_uptake_from`). Under balance everywhere,
  only active formations giving and activity needing support, the premises are those of
  `Flow.stays_below` (`Flow.below_ends_uptake`). So formations that take from `c` at their first
  active step do not arise at arbitrarily late steps (`Flow.below_ends_arising_taking`), and `n`
  steps of joint excess from a level below `n + m` end the taking from a level that may be at or
  above the need (`Flow.joint_excess_ends_uptake`).
* **The same law of a family read as one.** With the coupled gate of
  `Flow.family_stays_below_at`, nothing is given to a family whose stock is below `m`
  (`Flow.family_replenishment_zero_below`, the family form of `Flow.given_zero_below`); from such
  a step on the stock stays below and nothing is given (`Flow.family_unreplenished_below_from`),
  and taking from the family does not recur (`Flow.family_below_ends_uptake_from`).
* **How much.** Over any `N` steps from `T` the stock after them, with all that was taken across
  them, is the stock at `T`, and something is taken at fewer than `m` of those steps
  (`Flow.family_uptake_within_below_from`).
* **A step from which on nothing is taken.** That there is one is not refuted
  (`Flow.family_uptake_stops_unrefuted_below_from`). The step is not exhibited.
* **What the law does not say: arising.** "Below the need for good, arising is not unceasing" is
  false. In `Sprouting` every premise of the law holds from step one, the level standing at one
  below the renewer's need of two, and a formation that depends on the condition at need one and
  takes nothing arises across every even step (`Sprouting.arises_for_ever_below`). The law holds
  of taking, and of arising only where the arising takes.
* **Each premise is needed.** One giver and one taker at every step, with the level, the giver's
  need and its activity as parameters: something is taken at every step, one premise failing and
  the other four holding. The gate (`OnePremiseLess.gate_needed`); the level below the need
  (`OnePremiseLess.below_needed`); activity needing support (`OnePremiseLess.support_needed`);
  only active formations giving (`OnePremiseLess.acting_needed`); balance
  (`OnePremiseLess.balance_needed`).
* **Checks, not laws to land.** The paper record's proof from `Flow.stays_below`,
  `Flow.given_zero_below` and `Flow.uptake_needs_replenishment_from` alone
  (`Flow.below_ends_uptake_by_the_three_laws`). `Flow.given_zero_below` and the one-condition law
  as the family laws at the family of the one condition (`Flow.given_zero_below_as_case`,
  `Flow.below_ends_uptake_from_as_case`), and `Flow.family_stays_below_at` with its step "nothing
  is given" by `Flow.family_replenishment_zero_below` (`Flow.family_stays_below_at_as_case`).

No new structure carries the laws: each is stated in the definitions of `Becoming.Flow` and
`Becoming.Continuation` as they stand, and the witnesses are small flows and webs. No ethical
predicate, ranking or axiom occurs.
-/

namespace Becoming

variable {F C : Type}

namespace Flow

variable {L : Flow F C} {W : Web F C}

/-! ## A family of conditions read as one -/

/-- **Nothing is given to a family whose stock is below the need of what gives to it**, at one
step. Premises, all at the step `t`: whatever takes part and gives to a member of the family is
supported there (`givers`); whatever takes part and gives to a member depends on some member at a
need of at least `m` (`gated`, the coupled gate); the family's stock is below `m` (`below`). Then
the family's replenishment across the step from `t` is zero. A giver's need is at most the level of
the member it depends on, which is at most the stock. The family form of `Flow.given_zero_below`;
the step `none` inside the proof of `Flow.family_stays_below_at` proves the same inline. -/
theorem family_replenishment_zero_below {ds : List C} {m t : Nat}
    (givers : ∀ d, d ∈ ds → L.GiversSupported W t d)
    (gated : ∀ f, f ∈ L.present t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → 0 < L.give t f d →
      ∃ e, e ∈ ds ∧ W.requires t f e ∧ m ≤ W.need t f e)
    (below : W.stock ds t < m) : L.replenishment ds t = 0 :=
  total_eq_zero fun d mem => total_eq_zero fun f present => eq_zero_of_not_pos fun gives =>
    match gated f present d mem gives with
    | ⟨e, inside, depends, high⟩ =>
      Nat.lt_irrefl m (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_trans high (Nat.le_trans
        (givers d mem f present gives e depends)
        (le_total_of_mem (q := W.level t) inside))) below)

/-- **From a stock below the need on, the stock stays below and nothing is given.** Premises, each
at every step from `T` on: the flow balances on the family (`balances`); whatever takes part and
gives to a member is supported there (`givers`); whatever takes part and gives to a member depends
on some member at a need of at least `m` (`gated`). And at `T` the family's stock is below `m`
(`below`). Then at every step `t` from `T` on the stock is below `m` and the family's replenishment
across the step from `t` is zero. `Flow.family_stays_below_at` at the offset of `t` from `T`, then
`Flow.family_replenishment_zero_below` at `t`. -/
theorem family_unreplenished_below_from {ds : List C} {m T : Nat}
    (balances : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W ds t)
    (givers : ∀ t, T ≤ t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → L.GiversSupported W t d)
    (gated : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → f ∈ L.present t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → 0 < L.give t f d →
      ∃ e, e ∈ ds ∧ W.requires t f e ∧ m ≤ W.need t f e)
    (below : W.stock ds T < m) :
    ∀ t, T ≤ t → W.stock ds t < m ∧ L.replenishment ds t = 0 :=
  fun t later => match Nat.le.dest later with
    | ⟨n, sum⟩ =>
      have low : W.stock ds t < m := Eq.subst (motive := fun s => W.stock ds s < m) sum
        (family_stays_below_at below n (fun k _ => balances (T + k) (Nat.le_add_right T k))
          (fun k f _ => gated (T + k) f (Nat.le_add_right T k))
          fun k _ => givers (T + k) (Nat.le_add_right T k))
      ⟨low, family_replenishment_zero_below (givers t later) (fun f => gated t f later) low⟩

/-- **Below the need of what gives to it, taking from a family does not go on for ever.** Premises,
each at every step from `T` on: the flow balances on the family (`balances`); whatever takes part
and gives to a member is supported there (`givers`); whatever takes part and gives to a member
depends on some member at a need of at least `m` (`gated`). And at `T` the family's stock is below
`m` (`below`). Then it is not the case that the family's uptake is positive at arbitrarily late
steps. Nothing is given from `T` on (`Flow.family_unreplenished_below_from`), and uptake at
arbitrarily late steps needs replenishment at some step from `T` on
(`Flow.uptake_needs_replenishment_from`). Nothing is asked of who takes, of what a taker depends
on, or of any step before `T`. -/
theorem family_below_ends_uptake_from {ds : List C} {m T : Nat}
    (balances : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W ds t)
    (givers : ∀ t, T ≤ t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → L.GiversSupported W t d)
    (gated : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → f ∈ L.present t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → 0 < L.give t f d →
      ∃ e, e ∈ ds ∧ W.requires t f e ∧ m ≤ W.need t f e)
    (below : W.stock ds T < m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < L.uptake ds t :=
  fun recurs => uptake_needs_replenishment_from balances recurs fun t later =>
    (family_unreplenished_below_from balances givers gated below t later).2

/-- **How much is taken below the need: what the family held, and no more.** Under the premises of
`Flow.family_below_ends_uptake_from` (balance on the family, givers supported and the coupled gate
at a need of at least `m`, each at every step from `T` on; the stock below `m` at `T`), over any `N`
steps from `T`: the stock after them, with everything taken from the family across them, is the
stock at `T`; and the steps among them at which anything is taken number fewer than `m`. The
balance over a stretch (`Flow.accumulates_on`) with nothing given, and each step that takes taking
at least one unit (`count_le`). -/
theorem family_uptake_within_below_from {ds : List C} {m T : Nat}
    (balances : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W ds t)
    (givers : ∀ t, T ≤ t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → L.GiversSupported W t d)
    (gated : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → f ∈ L.present t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → 0 < L.give t f d →
      ∃ e, e ∈ ds ∧ W.requires t f e ∧ m ≤ W.need t f e)
    (below : W.stock ds T < m) (N : Nat) :
    W.stock ds (T + N) + total (steps T N) (L.uptake ds) = W.stock ds T ∧
      count (steps T N) (L.uptake ds) < m :=
  have whole : W.stock ds (T + N) + total (steps T N) (L.uptake ds) = W.stock ds T :=
    (accumulates_on T N fun t from_ _ => balances t from_).trans
      ((congrArg (W.stock ds T + ·) (total_eq_zero fun t mem =>
        (family_unreplenished_below_from balances givers gated below t
          (between_of_mem_steps mem).1).2)).trans (Nat.add_zero _))
  ⟨whole, Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_trans (count_le (L.uptake ds) (steps T N))
    (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_left _ _) (Nat.le_of_eq whole))) below⟩

/-- **That taking stops for good is not refuted.** Under the premises of
`Flow.family_below_ends_uptake_from` (balance on the family, givers supported and the coupled gate
at a need of at least `m`, each at every step from `T` on; the stock below `m` at `T`), it is not
the case that there is no step from `T` on from which on the family's uptake is zero at every step.
It rests on the law of rest as far as can be refuted (`Amounts.rests_unrefuted`) at whole-number
amounts: from `T` on the stock never rises, and it falls by at least one across each step at which
anything is taken. The step itself is not exhibited: the premises do not say when the last taking
comes (argued, not proved here: a step exhibited under these premises would say, of every course
of a level that never rises, from which step it is constant). -/
theorem family_uptake_stops_unrefuted_below_from {ds : List C} {m T : Nat}
    (balances : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W ds t)
    (givers : ∀ t, T ≤ t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → L.GiversSupported W t d)
    (gated : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → f ∈ L.present t → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → 0 < L.give t f d →
      ∃ e, e ∈ ds ∧ W.requires t f e ∧ m ≤ W.need t f e)
    (below : W.stock ds T < m) : ¬¬ ∃ S, T ≤ S ∧ ∀ t, S ≤ t → L.uptake ds t = 0 :=
  fun never =>
    have step : ∀ t, T ≤ t → W.stock ds (t + 1) + L.uptake ds t = W.stock ds t :=
      fun t later => (balances t later).over.trans ((congrArg (W.stock ds t + ·)
        (family_unreplenished_below_from balances givers gated below t later).2).trans
          (Nat.add_zero _))
    Amounts.rests_unrefuted (M := Amounts.whole) (s := W.stock ds) (ε := 1)
      (P := fun t => 0 < L.uptake ds t) (m := W.stock ds T + 1)
      (fun t later no => no (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_right _ _) (Nat.le_of_eq (step t later))))
      (fun t later positive no => no (Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_left positive _)
        (Nat.le_of_eq (step t later))))
      (Amounts.whole_within (W.stock ds T))
      fun N from_ no => never ⟨N, from_, fun t later =>
        eq_zero_of_not_pos fun positive => no ⟨t, later, positive⟩⟩

/-! ## One condition -/

/-- **Below the need of what gives to it, taking from a condition does not go on for ever**, from a
step on. Premises, each at every step from `T` on: the flow balances on `c` (`balances`); whatever
takes part and gives to `c` is supported there (`givers`); whatever takes part and gives to `c`
depends on `c` at a need of at least `m` (`gated`). And at `T` the level of `c` is below `m`
(`below`). Then it is not the case that something is taken from `c` at arbitrarily late steps (the
uptake of the family of `c` alone is what is taken from `c`). Proved from three laws as they stand:
the level stays below `m` (`Flow.stays_below_at`, at the offset of each later step from `T`), so
nothing is given to `c` (`Flow.given_zero_below`), and uptake at arbitrarily late steps needs
replenishment at some step from `T` on (`Flow.uptake_needs_replenishment_from`). Nothing is asked
of who takes, of what a taker depends on, or of any step before `T`. -/
theorem below_ends_uptake_from {c : C} {m T : Nat}
    (balances : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W [c] t)
    (givers : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.GiversSupported W t c)
    (gated : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c →
      W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    (below : W.level T c < m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < L.uptake [c] t :=
  fun recurs => uptake_needs_replenishment_from balances recurs fun t later =>
    match Nat.le.dest later with
    | ⟨n, sum⟩ => given_zero_below (givers t later) (fun f => gated t f later)
        (Eq.subst (motive := fun s => W.level s c < m) sum
          (stays_below_at below n (fun k _ => balances (T + k) (Nat.le_add_right T k))
            (fun k f _ => gated (T + k) f (Nat.le_add_right T k))
            fun k _ => givers (T + k) (Nat.le_add_right T k)))

/-- **Below the need of what gives to it, taking from a condition does not go on for ever.**
Premises, those of `Flow.stays_below`: the flow balances the web (`balances`); only active
formations give (`acting`); a formation is active only where the conditions it then depends on
support it (`dependent`); at every step, whatever takes part and gives to `c` depends on `c` at a
need of at least `m` (`gated`); at `T` the level of `c` is below `m` (`below`). Then it is not the
case that something is taken from `c` at arbitrarily late steps. The case of
`Flow.below_ends_uptake_from` in which the premises hold at every step, givers being supported
because they are active (`Flow.Acting.giversSupported`). -/
theorem below_ends_uptake (balances : L.Balances W) (acting : L.Acting W)
    (dependent : W.Dependent) {c : C} {m : Nat}
    (gated : ∀ t f, f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c →
      W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    {T : Nat} (below : W.level T c < m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < L.uptake [c] t :=
  below_ends_uptake_from (fun t _ => balances.on [c] t)
    (fun t _ => acting.giversSupported dependent t c) (fun t f _ => gated t f) below

/-- **So arising that takes does not go on for ever below the need.** Under the premises of
`Flow.below_ends_uptake` (balance, only active formations giving, activity needing support, every
giver to `c` depending on `c` at a need of at least `m`, the level of `c` below `m` at `T`), it is
not the case that formations taking from `c` at their first active step arise at arbitrarily late
steps. The arising enters only through the uptake it brings (`Flow.uptake_pos`); of arising that
takes nothing the conclusion is false (`Sprouting.arises_for_ever_below`). -/
theorem below_ends_arising_taking (balances : L.Balances W) (acting : L.Acting W)
    (dependent : W.Dependent) {c : C} {m : Nat}
    (gated : ∀ t f, f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c →
      W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    {T : Nat} (below : W.level T c < m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t f, N ≤ t ∧ L.ArisesTaking W [c] t f :=
  fun arising => below_ends_uptake balances acting dependent gated below fun N =>
    match arising N with
    | ⟨t, _, later, _, present, takes⟩ =>
      ⟨t + 1, Nat.le_succ_of_le later, uptake_pos present takes⟩

/-- **Joint excess that brings the level below the need ends the taking.** Premises: balance
(`balances`); only active formations give (`acting`); activity needs support (`dependent`); at
every step, whatever takes part and gives to `c` depends on `c` at a need of at least `m`
(`gated`); across each of `n` consecutive steps from `T` more is taken from `c` than is given to
it (`excess`); at `T` the level of `c` is below `n + m` (`start`). Then it is not the case that
something is taken from `c` at arbitrarily late steps. The excess brings the level below `m` at
`T + n` (`Flow.below_of_excess`), and from there `Flow.below_ends_uptake`: the course leaves from
a level that may be at or above the need. -/
theorem joint_excess_ends_uptake (balances : L.Balances W) (acting : L.Acting W)
    (dependent : W.Dependent) {c : C} {m : Nat}
    (gated : ∀ t f, f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c →
      W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    {T n : Nat} (excess : ∀ k, k < n → L.given (T + k) c < L.taken (T + k) c)
    (start : W.level T c < n + m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < L.uptake [c] t :=
  below_ends_uptake balances acting dependent gated (T := T + n)
    (below_of_excess balances (ds := [c]) excess start)

/-! ## Checks: the paper record's proof, and the laws of one condition as cases of the family's -/

/-- A check, not a law to land. The statement of `Flow.below_ends_uptake`, proved from the three
laws the paper record names and from no law of this module: the level stays below `m`
(`Flow.stays_below`), so nothing is given to `c` (`Flow.given_zero_below`), and uptake at
arbitrarily late steps needs replenishment at some step from `T` on
(`Flow.uptake_needs_replenishment_from`). -/
theorem below_ends_uptake_by_the_three_laws (balances : L.Balances W) (acting : L.Acting W)
    (dependent : W.Dependent) {c : C} {m : Nat}
    (gated : ∀ t f, f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c →
      W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    {T : Nat} (below : W.level T c < m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < L.uptake [c] t :=
  fun recurs => uptake_needs_replenishment_from (T := T) (fun t _ => balances.on [c] t) recurs
    fun t later => match Nat.le.dest later with
      | ⟨n, sum⟩ => given_zero_below (acting.giversSupported dependent t c) (gated t)
          (Eq.subst (motive := fun s => W.level s c < m) sum
            (stays_below balances acting dependent gated below n))

/-- A check, not a law to land. The statement of `Flow.given_zero_below`, proved as
`Flow.family_replenishment_zero_below` at the family of the one condition `c`. -/
theorem given_zero_below_as_case {m t : Nat} {c : C} (givers : L.GiversSupported W t c)
    (gated : ∀ f, f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c → W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    (below : W.level t c < m) : L.given t c = 0 :=
  family_replenishment_zero_below (ds := [c]) (of_single givers)
    (fun f present => gate_single (gated f present)) below

/-- A check, not a law to land. The statement of `Flow.family_stays_below_at`, proved as it stands
in `Becoming.Continuation` except that the step "nothing is given" is
`Flow.family_replenishment_zero_below`. -/
theorem family_stays_below_at_as_case {ds : List C} {m T : Nat} (below : W.stock ds T < m) :
    ∀ n, (∀ k, k < n → L.BalancesOn W ds (T + k)) →
      (∀ k f, k < n → f ∈ L.present (T + k) → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → 0 < L.give (T + k) f d →
        ∃ e, e ∈ ds ∧ W.requires (T + k) f e ∧ m ≤ W.need (T + k) f e) →
      (∀ k, k < n → ∀ d, d ∈ ds → L.GiversSupported W (T + k) d) →
      W.stock ds (T + n) < m
  | 0, _, _, _ => below
  | n + 1, balances, gated, givers =>
    have before := family_stays_below_at_as_case below n
      (fun k lt => balances k (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt))
      (fun k f lt => gated k f (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt))
      fun k lt => givers k (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt)
    have none : L.replenishment ds (T + n) = 0 := family_replenishment_zero_below
      (givers n (Nat.lt_succ_self n)) (fun f => gated n f (Nat.lt_succ_self n)) before
    Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt
      (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_right _ (L.uptake ds (T + n)))
        (Nat.le_of_eq ((balances n (Nat.lt_succ_self n)).over.trans
          (congrArg (W.stock ds (T + n) + ·) none))))
      before

/-- A check, not a law to land. The statement of `Flow.below_ends_uptake_from`, proved as
`Flow.family_below_ends_uptake_from` at the family of the one condition `c`: a gate on `c` is the
coupled gate on that family (`Flow.gate_single`). -/
theorem below_ends_uptake_from_as_case {c : C} {m T : Nat}
    (balances : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W [c] t)
    (givers : ∀ t, T ≤ t → L.GiversSupported W t c)
    (gated : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → f ∈ L.present t → 0 < L.give t f c →
      W.requires t f c ∧ m ≤ W.need t f c)
    (below : W.level T c < m) : ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < L.uptake [c] t :=
  family_below_ends_uptake_from balances (fun t later => of_single (givers t later))
    (fun t f later present => gate_single (gated t f later present)) below

end Flow

/-! ## What the law does not say: arising that takes nothing goes on below the need -/

namespace Sprouting

/-- A renewer of one condition, a taker from it, and a sprout that depends on the condition at a
lower need and neither takes nor gives. -/
inductive Formation where
  | renewer
  | taker
  | sprout

open Formation

/-- The odd steps. -/
def odd : Nat → Prop
  | 0 => False
  | 1 => True
  | n + 2 => odd n

/-- No doubled step is odd. -/
theorem not_odd_double : ∀ n, ¬ odd (2 * n)
  | 0 => fun no => no
  | n + 1 => not_odd_double n

/-- The step after a doubled step is odd. -/
theorem odd_after_double : ∀ n, odd (2 * n + 1)
  | 0 => trivial
  | n + 1 => odd_after_double n

/-- The renewer and the taker take part across the first step only; after it nothing takes
part. -/
def present : Nat → List Formation
  | 0 => [renewer, taker]
  | _ + 1 => []

/-- Across the first step the renewer gives one unit and the taker takes two. The sprout takes
nothing and gives nothing at any step. -/
def flow : Flow Formation Unit where
  present := present
  take _ f _ := match f with
    | renewer => 0
    | taker => 2
    | sprout => 0
  give _ f _ := match f with
    | renewer => 1
    | taker => 0
    | sprout => 0

/-- The condition stands at two at step zero and at one from step one on. Each formation depends on
it: the renewer and the taker at need two, the sprout at need one. The renewer and the taker are
active at step zero only; the sprout is active at the odd steps. -/
def web : Web Formation Unit where
  level t _ := Witnesses.profile [2] 1 t
  active t f := match f with
    | renewer => Witnesses.profile [True] False t
    | taker => Witnesses.profile [True] False t
    | sprout => odd t
  requires _ _ _ := True
  need _ f _ := match f with
    | renewer => 2
    | taker => 2
    | sprout => 1
  draws _ f _ := match f with
    | renewer => False
    | taker => True
    | sprout => False

/-- The flow balances the web: across the first step two are taken and one is given, and the level
goes from two to one; after it nothing is taken or given and the level stays at one. -/
theorem balances : flow.Balances web
  | 0, () => rfl
  | _ + 1, () => rfl

/-- Only active formations give: the one giver, the renewer, gives across the first step, where it
is active. -/
theorem acting : flow.Acting web
  | 0, renewer, _, _, _ => trivial
  | 0, taker, _, _, _ => trivial
  | 0, sprout, _, _, gives => absurd gives (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)
  | _ + 1, _, _, no, _ => nomatch no

/-- Activity needs support: the renewer and the taker are active only at step zero, where the level
of two meets their need of two; the sprout is active only at odd steps, where the level of one
meets its need of one. -/
theorem dependent : web.Dependent
  | 0, renewer, _, _, _ => Nat.le_refl 2
  | 0, taker, _, _, _ => Nat.le_refl 2
  | 0, sprout, dead, _, _ => nomatch dead
  | _ + 1, renewer, dead, _, _ => nomatch dead
  | _ + 1, taker, dead, _, _ => nomatch dead
  | _ + 1, sprout, _, _, _ => Nat.le_refl 1

/-- The gate at need two: the one giver to the condition, the renewer, depends on it at need
two. -/
theorem gated : ∀ t f, f ∈ flow.present t → 0 < flow.give t f () →
    web.requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ web.need t f ()
  | _, renewer, _, _ => ⟨trivial, Nat.le_refl 2⟩
  | _, taker, _, gives => absurd gives (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)
  | _, sprout, _, gives => absurd gives (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)

/-- Only active formations take, and only from conditions they draw on: the one that takes, the
taker, takes across the first step, where it is active and draws on the condition. -/
theorem taking : flow.Taking web
  | 0, renewer, _, _, takes => absurd takes (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)
  | 0, taker, _, _, _ => ⟨trivial, trivial⟩
  | 0, sprout, _, _, takes => absurd takes (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)
  | _ + 1, _, _, no, _ => nomatch no

/-- The sprout arises across every doubled step: inactive there, active at the next. -/
theorem sprout_arises (n : Nat) : web.Arises (2 * n) sprout :=
  ⟨not_odd_double n, odd_after_double n⟩

/-- **Below the need of what gives to a condition, arising can go on for ever.** In this flow and
web every premise of `Flow.below_ends_uptake` holds with the need `m` at two and `T` at one: the
flow balances the web, only active formations give, activity needs support, at every step whatever
takes part and gives to the condition depends on it at a need of at least two, and at step one the
level is below two. Beyond the law's premises, only active formations take, and only from
conditions they draw on. The gate is not idle, and the level comes below the need through one step
at which more is taken than given: at step zero the level is not below two, the renewer takes part
and gives, and less is given than taken. At every step the sprout depends on the condition at a
need below two and takes nothing from it. Arising is unceasing (`Web.Unceasing`): the sprout
arises across every even step. And, as the law says, it is not the case that something is taken
from the condition at arbitrarily late steps. So "below the need for good, arising is not
unceasing" is false; what the law ends is taking. -/
theorem arises_for_ever_below :
    flow.Balances web ∧ flow.Acting web ∧ web.Dependent ∧
      (∀ t f, f ∈ flow.present t → 0 < flow.give t f () →
        web.requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ web.need t f ()) ∧
      web.level 1 () < 2 ∧ flow.Taking web ∧
      (¬ web.level 0 () < 2 ∧ renewer ∈ flow.present 0 ∧ 0 < flow.give 0 renewer () ∧
        flow.given 0 () < flow.taken 0 ()) ∧
      (∀ t, web.requires t sprout () ∧ web.need t sprout () < 2 ∧ flow.take t sprout () = 0) ∧
      web.Unceasing ∧ ¬ ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < flow.uptake [()] t :=
  ⟨balances, acting, dependent, gated, Nat.lt_succ_self 1, taking,
   ⟨Nat.lt_irrefl 2, .head _, Nat.zero_lt_one, Nat.lt_succ_self 1⟩,
   fun _ => ⟨trivial, Nat.lt_succ_self 1, rfl⟩,
   fun N => ⟨2 * N, sprout, Nat.le_mul_of_pos_left N Nat.zero_lt_two, sprout_arises N⟩,
   Flow.below_ends_uptake balances acting dependent gated (c := ()) (m := 2) (T := 1)
     (Nat.lt_succ_self 1)⟩

end Sprouting

namespace OnePremiseLess

/-- A giver (`true`) and a taker (`false`) take part at every step: the taker takes one unit from
the one condition and the giver gives it `gives` units. -/
def flow (gives : Nat) : Flow Bool Unit where
  present _ := [true, false]
  take _ f _ := match f with
    | true => 0
    | false => 1
  give _ f _ := match f with
    | true => gives
    | false => 0

/-- The condition stands at `level` at every step. The giver depends on it at need `need` and is
active exactly where `live` holds; the taker depends on nothing, draws on the condition and is
active at every step. -/
def web (level need : Nat) (live : Prop) : Web Bool Unit where
  level _ _ := level
  active _ f := match f with
    | true => live
    | false => True
  requires _ f _ := match f with
    | true => True
    | false => False
  need _ _ _ := need
  draws _ f _ := match f with
    | true => False
    | false => True

/-- Whatever the giver gives, something is taken from the condition at every step. -/
theorem uptake_recurs (gives : Nat) : ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < (flow gives).uptake [()] t :=
  fun N => ⟨N, Nat.le_refl N, Nat.zero_lt_one⟩

/-- Where the giver gives one unit, the flow balances the web at any constant level: one is taken
and one is given at every step. -/
theorem balances (level need : Nat) (live : Prop) : (flow 1).Balances (web level need live) :=
  fun _ _ => rfl

/-- Only active formations give, where the giver is active if it gives anything. -/
theorem acting {gives level need : Nat} {live : Prop} (alive : 0 < gives → live) :
    (flow gives).Acting (web level need live)
  | _, true, _, _, positive => alive positive
  | _, false, _, _, positive => absurd positive (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)

/-- Activity needs support, where the level meets the giver's need if the giver is active: the
taker depends on nothing. -/
theorem dependent {level need : Nat} {live : Prop} (met : live → need ≤ level) :
    (web level need live).Dependent
  | _, true, active, _, _ => met active
  | _, false, _, _, no => nomatch no

/-- The gate at need `m`, where the giver's need is at least `m` if it gives anything: the giver
is the only formation that gives. -/
theorem gated {gives level need m : Nat} {live : Prop} (high : 0 < gives → m ≤ need) :
    ∀ t f, f ∈ (flow gives).present t → 0 < (flow gives).give t f () →
      (web level need live).requires t f () ∧ m ≤ (web level need live).need t f ()
  | _, true, _, positive => ⟨trivial, high positive⟩
  | _, false, _, positive => absurd positive (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)

/-- **The gate is needed.** The level stands at one, below two; the giver depends on the condition
at a need of zero, is active, and gives one at every step. The flow balances the web, only active
formations give, activity needs support and the level is below two at step zero; the gate at need
two fails, the giver's need being below two; and something is taken from the condition at every
step. -/
theorem gate_needed :
    (flow 1).Balances (web 1 0 True) ∧ (flow 1).Acting (web 1 0 True) ∧
      (web 1 0 True).Dependent ∧ (web 1 0 True).level 0 () < 2 ∧
      (¬ ∀ t f, f ∈ (flow 1).present t → 0 < (flow 1).give t f () →
        (web 1 0 True).requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ (web 1 0 True).need t f ()) ∧
      ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < (flow 1).uptake [()] t :=
  ⟨balances 1 0 True, acting fun _ => trivial, dependent fun _ => Nat.zero_le 1,
   Nat.lt_succ_self 1,
   fun gate => Nat.not_succ_le_zero 1 (gate 0 true (.head _) Nat.zero_lt_one).2,
   uptake_recurs 1⟩

/-- **The level below the need is needed.** The level stands at two; the giver depends on the
condition at need two, is active, and gives one at every step. The flow balances the web, only
active formations give, activity needs support and the gate at need two holds; the level is not
below two at any step; and something is taken from the condition at every step. -/
theorem below_needed :
    (flow 1).Balances (web 2 2 True) ∧ (flow 1).Acting (web 2 2 True) ∧
      (web 2 2 True).Dependent ∧
      (∀ t f, f ∈ (flow 1).present t → 0 < (flow 1).give t f () →
        (web 2 2 True).requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ (web 2 2 True).need t f ()) ∧
      (∀ t, ¬ (web 2 2 True).level t () < 2) ∧
      ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < (flow 1).uptake [()] t :=
  ⟨balances 2 2 True, acting fun _ => trivial, dependent fun _ => Nat.le_refl 2,
   gated fun _ => Nat.le_refl 2, fun _ => Nat.lt_irrefl 2, uptake_recurs 1⟩

/-- **Activity needing support is needed.** The level stands at one, below two; the giver depends
on the condition at need two, is active all the same, and gives one at every step. The flow
balances the web, only active formations give, the gate at need two holds and the level is below
two at step zero; the giver is active where the level does not meet its need; and something is
taken from the condition at every step. -/
theorem support_needed :
    (flow 1).Balances (web 1 2 True) ∧ (flow 1).Acting (web 1 2 True) ∧
      (∀ t f, f ∈ (flow 1).present t → 0 < (flow 1).give t f () →
        (web 1 2 True).requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ (web 1 2 True).need t f ()) ∧
      (web 1 2 True).level 0 () < 2 ∧ ¬ (web 1 2 True).Dependent ∧
      ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < (flow 1).uptake [()] t :=
  ⟨balances 1 2 True, acting fun _ => trivial, gated fun _ => Nat.le_refl 2,
   Nat.lt_succ_self 1,
   fun supported => Nat.not_succ_le_self 1 (supported 0 true trivial () trivial),
   uptake_recurs 1⟩

/-- **Only active formations giving is needed.** The level stands at one, below two; the giver
depends on the condition at need two, is never active, and gives one at every step all the same.
The flow balances the web, activity needs support, the gate at need two holds and the level is
below two at step zero; the giver gives where it is not active; and something is taken from the
condition at every step. With `OnePremiseLess.support_needed`: of the two premises that make
givers supported, neither can be dropped. -/
theorem acting_needed :
    (flow 1).Balances (web 1 2 False) ∧ (web 1 2 False).Dependent ∧
      (∀ t f, f ∈ (flow 1).present t → 0 < (flow 1).give t f () →
        (web 1 2 False).requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ (web 1 2 False).need t f ()) ∧
      (web 1 2 False).level 0 () < 2 ∧ ¬ (flow 1).Acting (web 1 2 False) ∧
      ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < (flow 1).uptake [()] t :=
  ⟨balances 1 2 False, dependent (fun dead => nomatch dead), gated fun _ => Nat.le_refl 2,
   Nat.lt_succ_self 1, fun acts => acts 0 true () (.head _) Nat.zero_lt_one,
   uptake_recurs 1⟩

/-- **Balance is needed.** The level stands at one at every step, below two, while the taker takes
one at every step and nothing is given: the level does not change by what is taken and given. Only
active formations give (nothing gives), activity needs support, the gate at need two holds
(nothing gives) and the level is below two at step zero; the flow does not balance the web; and
something is taken from the condition at every step. -/
theorem balance_needed :
    (flow 0).Acting (web 1 2 False) ∧ (web 1 2 False).Dependent ∧
      (∀ t f, f ∈ (flow 0).present t → 0 < (flow 0).give t f () →
        (web 1 2 False).requires t f () ∧ 2 ≤ (web 1 2 False).need t f ()) ∧
      (web 1 2 False).level 0 () < 2 ∧ ¬ (flow 0).Balances (web 1 2 False) ∧
      ∀ N, ∃ t, N ≤ t ∧ 0 < (flow 0).uptake [()] t :=
  ⟨acting fun positive => absurd positive (Nat.lt_irrefl 0), dependent (fun dead => nomatch dead),
   gated fun positive => absurd positive (Nat.lt_irrefl 0), Nat.lt_succ_self 1,
   fun balanced => Nat.succ_ne_self 1 (balanced 0 ()), uptake_recurs 0⟩

end OnePremiseLess
