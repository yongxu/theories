/-!
# Scratch: a use that reaches `hi` leaves nothing, and a held value set above the take

AI-written (Fable), 16 November 2026. Scratch; not on main.

Two statements, the second resting on the first. Take, level, value, use, giving and reading are this
model's labels for parts of its configurations and steps, not terms of the root.

## 1. The reach lemma

A level `X` that steps by `RegimeMargin.step giving (D t)`, the use `D t` any amount at each step.

* Where the use is at least the level, the step leaves nothing, with nothing given from nothing
  (`step_takes_all`).
* Where the step under no use keeps every level up to `hi` within `hi` and the start is at most `hi`,
  every level is at most `hi` (`levels_within`, by `TurningInTime.kept_within` at each step).
* So, with all three premises, a use of at least `hi` at step `n` leaves the level nothing at step
  `n + 1` (`reach_levels`).
* On the pair's course, whatever the way after a step (`after` any function of way and level), these
  are `pair_within` and `reach`.
* `TurningInTime.rising_takes_all` restated and proved from `reach` by `TurningInTime.rises_from`
  (`rising_takes_all_by_reach`), and `TurningInTime.rising_meets_level` restated and proved with
  `pair_within` in place of the private `takes_at_hi` (`rising_meets_level_by_within`).

## 2. A value that every reading sets above the take

`Pace.paced` at a fast configuration of a take `x` and a level `y`, and a held value `m`. The fast step is
`ClosedCapture.join A fB`: the take moves to `A x m`, whatever the level is.

* **The take's rise** (`set_above_rises`), for any step `fB` of the level. Premises: the take after a
  move is at least whatever the take and the value both are at least (`low`); below the value the take
  rises strictly (`raises`); every reading sets the value above the take as it stood or above the take
  as moved (`above`); every `L` steps in a row hold a reading step (`gaps`). Then at step `k * (L + 1)`
  the take and the value are both at least `k`.
* **The ending** (`set_above_takes_all`), where the level steps by
  `RegimeMargin.step giving (use x m)` with a use at least whatever the take and the value both are at
  least (`least`): with nothing given from nothing, the step under no use keeping every level up to `hi`
  within `hi`, and the start's level at most `hi`, the level at step `hi * (L + 1) + 1` is nothing. By
  `set_above_rises` at `k := hi` and `reach_levels`.
* **As it was asked** (`set_above_ends`): the level under the take as it stands, the value read from
  the configuration before the step (the order `Pace.turn` has), the move `Regulation.Between` and
  rising strictly below the value, every reading above the take: some step `n ≤ (2 * hi + 1) * L` has
  the level nothing. `Between` gives `low` (`low_of_between`), and a take strictly nearer the value
  wherever they differ gives `raises` (`raises_of_nearer`). The other order of taking
  (`set_above_ends_moved_first`) and the reading after the step, in either order of taking
  (`set_above_ends_read_after`), end by the same step.
* **Each premise dropped, a course that never ends** (`Needed`): the take not rising below the value
  (`Needed.not_rising`), the value set at the take and not above it (`Needed.not_above`), no reading
  step (`Needed.not_read`), something given from nothing (`Needed.from_nothing`), a level not kept
  within `hi`, or a start above it (`Needed.no_cap`), and a take that falls below both the take and the
  value (`Needed.not_low`).

What is not here: that the level stays nothing after it is nothing; a least step; a bound that is
reached for `L` above one; the ending as the end of a line under a need (`TurningInTime.net`).

Every declaration the scratch build audits (the public ones) has an empty axiom report, and each
private lemma is used by one of them.
-/

/-! ## 1. The reach lemma -/

/-- **A use of at least the level leaves nothing.** With nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), where
the level `x` is at most the use `d` (`takes`), `RegimeMargin.step giving d x` is nothing: the use takes
all there is, and nothing is given from nothing. No premise on `hi`. -/
theorem step_takes_all {giving : Nat → Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) {d x : Nat}
    (takes : x ≤ d) : RegimeMargin.step giving d x = 0 :=
  (congrArg (fun z => z + giving z) (sub_eq_zero_of_le takes)).trans
    ((Nat.zero_add (giving 0)).trans nothing)

/-- **A level that steps by the regime's step, under any uses, stays within `hi`.** A level `X` that
steps by `RegimeMargin.step giving (D t)` at each step `t` (`step`), where the step under no use keeps
every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`, `RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi`) and the start `X 0` is at
most `hi` (`high`): every `X t` is at most `hi`. By `TurningInTime.kept_within` at each step. Nothing is
asked of the uses `D`. -/
theorem levels_within {giving : Nat → Nat} {hi : Nat} (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {X D : Nat → Nat} (high : X 0 ≤ hi)
    (step : ∀ t, X (t + 1) = RegimeMargin.step giving (D t) (X t)) : ∀ t, X t ≤ hi
  | 0 => high
  | t + 1 => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (step t))
      (kept_within keeps (D t) (levels_within keeps high step t))

/-- **The reach lemma, on a level and its uses.** A level `X` that steps by
`RegimeMargin.step giving (D t)` at each step `t` (`step`), with nothing given from nothing (`nothing`),
the step under no use keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`,
`RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi`) and the start `X 0` at most `hi` (`high`): where the use at step `n`
is at least `hi` (`reaches`), the level at step `n + 1` is nothing. The level at step `n` is at most
`hi` (`levels_within`), so at most the use, which takes all of it (`step_takes_all`). Nothing is asked
of the uses at other steps. -/
theorem reach_levels {giving : Nat → Nat} {hi : Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) {X D : Nat → Nat} (high : X 0 ≤ hi)
    (step : ∀ t, X (t + 1) = RegimeMargin.step giving (D t) (X t)) {n : Nat} (reaches : hi ≤ D n) :
    X (n + 1) = 0 :=
  (step n).trans (step_takes_all nothing (Nat.le_trans (levels_within keeps high step n) reaches))

/-- **On the pair's course every level is at most `hi`.** On the course of
`pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)` from `(w, x)`, the way after a step any
function `after` of the way and the level: where the step under no use keeps every level up to `hi`
within `hi` (`keeps`) and the start's level `x` is at most `hi` (`high`), the level at every step `t` is
at most `hi`. `levels_within` read on the pair. On the course as it was
(`after := fun w _ => alone w`), at `t := hi`, it is the first half of the private
`TurningInTime.takes_at_hi`. -/
theorem pair_within {after : W → Nat → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {hi : Nat}
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) {w : W} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) (t : Nat) :
    (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).2 ≤ hi :=
  levels_within keeps
    (X := fun t => (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).2)
    (D := fun t =>
      use (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).1)
    high (fun _ => rfl) t

/-- **The reach lemma, on the pair's course.** On the course of
`pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)` from `(w, x)`, the way after a step any
function `after` of the way and the level: with nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the step under no
use keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`, `RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi`) and the
start's level `x` at most `hi` (`high`), where the way at step `n` uses at least `hi` (`reaches`), the
level at step `n + 1` is nothing. `reach_levels` read on the pair. No premise says how the use came to
be at least `hi`: a rise at every step (`TurningInTime.rising_takes_all`, restated below as
`rising_takes_all_by_reach`) is one way, and a way whose next way reads the level is allowed. -/
theorem reach {after : W → Nat → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {hi : Nat}
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {w : W} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) {n : Nat}
    (reaches : hi ≤
      use (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) n).1) :
    (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) (n + 1)).2 = 0 :=
  reach_levels nothing keeps
    (X := fun t => (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).2)
    (D := fun t =>
      use (Law.orbit (pair after (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).1)
    high (fun _ => rfl) reaches

/-- **`TurningInTime.rising_takes_all`, restated, from the reach lemma.** The statement is that law's,
word for word: on the course as it was from `(w, x)`, with nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the
step under no use keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), the start's level `x` at most
`hi` (`high`), and the use rising strictly at each of the first `hi` steps of `alone`'s orbit from `w`
(`rises`), the level at step `hi + 1` is nothing. By `reach` at `n := hi` on the course as it was
(`after := fun w _ => alone w`), its `reaches` from `TurningInTime.rises_from` through
`TurningInTime.asItWas_way`. -/
theorem rising_takes_all_by_reach {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {hi : Nat}
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {w : W} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1))) :
    (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) (hi + 1)).2 = 0 :=
  reach (after := fun w _ => alone w) (use := use) (w := w) (n := hi) nothing keeps high
    (Eq.subst (motive := fun v => hi ≤ use v) (asItWas_way alone _ (w, x) hi).symm
      (rises_from rises hi (Nat.le_refl hi)))

/-- **`TurningInTime.rising_meets_level`, restated, with `pair_within` in place of the private
`takes_at_hi`.** The statement is that law's, word for word: under `keeps`, `high` and `rises` as in
`rising_takes_all_by_reach`, there is a step `j` at most `hi` of the course as it was from `(w, x)` at
which the level is at most the use of the way `alone` taken `j` times, the use below the level at every
step before it. The proof is that law's, with the level at step `hi` at most `hi` by `pair_within` and
`hi` at most the use there by `TurningInTime.rises_from`. -/
theorem rising_meets_level_by_within {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {hi : Nat}
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) {w : W} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1))) :
    ∃ j, j ≤ hi ∧
      (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) j).2 ≤
        use (Law.orbit alone w j) ∧
      ∀ i, i < j → use (Law.orbit alone w i) <
        (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) i).2 :=
  match first_of_decided
      (P := fun t => use (Law.orbit alone w t) <
        (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).2)
      (Q := fun t =>
        (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) t).2 ≤
          use (Law.orbit alone w t))
      (fun _ => Nat.lt_or_ge _ _) hi with
  | .inl all => absurd (Nat.le_trans (pair_within (after := fun w _ => alone w) (use := use) keeps high hi)
      (rises_from rises hi (Nat.le_refl hi))) (Nat.not_le_of_gt (all hi (Nat.le_refl hi)))
  | .inr ⟨j, le, takes, before⟩ => ⟨j, le, takes, before⟩

/-! ## 2. A value that every reading sets above the take -/

section HeldValue

variable {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} {fB : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat}
  {reads : Nat → Bool}

/-- The fast configuration after one turn is the fast step's, reading or not. Needed by
`level_steps`. -/
private theorem turn_fast (f : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat × Nat) (h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat) :
    ∀ (b : Bool) (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat), (Pace.turn f h b p).1 = f p.1 p.2
  | true, _ => rfl
  | false, _ => rfl

/-- One turn keeps whatever the take and the value both are at least. Where the take after a move is
at least whatever the take and the value both are at least (`low`) and every reading sets the value
above the take as it stood or above the take as moved (`above`): after one turn, reading or not, from a
take and a value both at least `k`, both are at least `k`. Needed by `reading_raises` and
`lower_kept`. -/
private theorem turn_lower (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m) {k : Nat} :
    ∀ (b : Bool) (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat), k ≤ p.1.1 → k ≤ p.2 →
      k ≤ (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b p).1.1 ∧
        k ≤ (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b p).2
  | true, p, kx, km =>
    ⟨low p.1.1 p.2 k kx km, (above p.1 p.2).elim
      (fun set => Nat.le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt kx set))
      (fun set => Nat.le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (low p.1.1 p.2 k kx km) set))⟩
  | false, p, kx, km => ⟨low p.1.1 p.2 k kx km, km⟩

/-- One turn from a take below the value raises what both are at least. Where below the value the take
rises strictly (`raises`) and every reading sets the value above the take as it stood or above the take
as moved (`above`): after one turn, reading or not, from a take at least `k` and below the value, the
take and the value are both at least `k + 1`. Needed by `reading_raises`. -/
private theorem turn_raises (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m) {k : Nat} :
    ∀ (b : Bool) (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat), k ≤ p.1.1 → p.1.1 < p.2 →
      k + 1 ≤ (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b p).1.1 ∧
        k + 1 ≤ (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b p).2
  | true, p, kx, below =>
    have moved : k + 1 ≤ A p.1.1 p.2 :=
      Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt kx (raises p.1.1 p.2 below))
    ⟨moved, (above p.1 p.2).elim
      (fun set => Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt kx set))
      (fun set => Nat.le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt moved set))⟩
  | false, p, kx, below =>
    ⟨Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt kx (raises p.1.1 p.2 below)),
      Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt kx below)⟩

/-- A reading turn and the turn after it raise what the take and the value both are at least. Under
`low`, `raises` and `above`: from a take and a value both at least `k`, after a reading turn and one
more turn, reading or not, both are at least `k + 1`. After the reading turn the take is at least `k`
and the value at least `k + 1`; where the take is then below the value the next turn raises both
(`turn_raises`), and otherwise the take is at least `k + 1` already and the next turn keeps both
(`turn_lower`). Needed by `reading_step`. -/
private theorem reading_raises (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m)
    (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m) {k : Nat} (b : Bool)
    (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) (kx : k ≤ p.1.1) (km : k ≤ p.2) :
    k + 1 ≤ (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b
        (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h true p)).1.1 ∧
      k + 1 ≤ (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b
        (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h true p)).2 :=
  have moved : k ≤ A p.1.1 p.2 := low p.1.1 p.2 k kx km
  have set : k + 1 ≤ h p.1 p.2 := (above p.1 p.2).elim
    (fun lt => Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt kx lt))
    (fun lt => Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt moved lt))
  (Nat.lt_or_ge (A p.1.1 p.2) (h p.1 p.2)).elim
    (fun below => turn_raises (fB := fB) raises above b
      (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h true p) moved below)
    (fun reached => turn_lower (fB := fB) low above b
      (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h true p) (Nat.le_trans set reached) set)

/-- Along the course, what the take and the value both are at least is kept. Under `low` and `above`:
where at step `t` the take and the value are both at least `k`, they are at every later step.
`Pace.stays_turned` at `turn_lower`. Needed by `gap_raises`. -/
private theorem lower_kept (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m)
    (c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) {k t : Nat}
    (lower : k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c t).1.1 ∧
      k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c t).2) (j : Nat) :
    k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (t + j)).1.1 ∧
      k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (t + j)).2 :=
  Pace.stays_turned (f := ClosedCapture.join A fB) (h := h) (reads := reads)
    (Q := fun p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => k ≤ p.1.1 ∧ k ≤ p.2)
    (fun b p held => turn_lower low above b p held.1 held.2) c lower j

/-- Two steps after a reading step, what the take and the value both are at least is one more. Under
`low`, `raises` and `above`: where step `r` reads (`at_r`) and the take and the value at step `r` are
both at least `k`, at step `r + 2` both are at least `k + 1`. `reading_raises` on the course, by
`Pace.paced_succ` twice. Needed by `gap_raises`. -/
private theorem reading_step (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m)
    (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m)
    (c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) {k r : Nat} (at_r : reads r = true)
    (lower : k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r).1.1 ∧
      k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r).2) :
    k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (r + 1 + 1)).1.1 ∧
      k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (r + 1 + 1)).2 :=
  have one : Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (r + 1) =
      Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h true (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r) :=
    (Pace.paced_succ c r).trans (congrArg
      (fun b => Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h b
        (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r)) at_r)
  have two : Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (r + 1 + 1) =
      Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h (reads (r + 1))
        (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h true
          (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r)) :=
    (Pace.paced_succ c (r + 1)).trans
      (congrArg (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h (reads (r + 1))) one)
  Eq.subst (motive := fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => k + 1 ≤ q.1.1 ∧ k + 1 ≤ q.2) two.symm
    (reading_raises low raises above (reads (r + 1))
      (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r) lower.1 lower.2)

/-- Over `L + 1` steps, what the take and the value both are at least is one more. Under `low`,
`raises`, `above` and a reading step in every `L` steps in a row (`gaps`): where at step `t` the take
and the value are both at least `k`, at step `t + (L + 1)` both are at least `k + 1`. Some step `r` from
`t` to below `t + L` reads; the lower bound is kept up to `r` (`lower_kept`), is one more at `r + 2`
(`reading_step`), and that is kept up to `t + (L + 1)`. Needed by `set_above_rises`. -/
private theorem gap_raises (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m)
    (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m) {L : Nat}
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true)
    (c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) {k t : Nat}
    (lower : k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c t).1.1 ∧
      k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c t).2) :
    k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (t + (L + 1))).1.1 ∧
      k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (t + (L + 1))).2 :=
  match gaps t with
  | ⟨r, tr, rL, at_r⟩ =>
    match le_dest tr,
      le_dest (show r + 1 + 1 ≤ t + (L + 1) from Nat.succ_le_succ (Nat.succ_le_of_lt rL)) with
    | ⟨a, ea⟩, ⟨b, eb⟩ =>
      have atR : k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r).1.1 ∧
          k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c r).2 :=
        Eq.subst (motive := fun n => k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c n).1.1 ∧
          k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c n).2) ea.symm
          (lower_kept low above c lower a)
      Eq.subst (motive := fun n => k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c n).1.1 ∧
        k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c n).2) eb.symm
        (lower_kept low above c (reading_step low raises above c at_r atR) b)

/-- **Where every reading sets the value above the take, the take and the value are both at least `k`
at step `k * (L + 1)`.** On `Pace.paced` at a fast configuration of a take and a level and a held value,
the fast step `ClosedCapture.join A fB`: the take `x` under the value `m` moves to `A x m`, and the level
steps by any `fB`. Premises: the take after a move is at least whatever the take and the value both are
at least (`low`; `Regulation.Between m x (A x m)` gives it, `low_of_between`); below the value the take
rises strictly (`raises`); every reading `h p m` is above the take `p.1` as it stood or above the take
`A p.1 m` as moved (`above`), whatever else the reading reads, the level included; and every `L` steps
in a row hold a reading step (`gaps`). Then for every `k`, at step `k * (L + 1)` the take and the value
are both at least `k`. By induction on `k` through `gap_raises`. Not asked: that the take does not pass
the value, that it does not move away from the value from above, anything of the level's step, or
anything of the start. -/
theorem set_above_rises (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m)
    (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m) {L : Nat}
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true) (c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) :
    ∀ k, k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (k * (L + 1))).1.1 ∧
      k ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c (k * (L + 1))).2
  | 0 => ⟨Nat.zero_le _, Nat.zero_le _⟩
  | k + 1 =>
    Eq.subst (motive := fun n => k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c n).1.1 ∧
      k + 1 ≤ (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A fB) h reads c n).2) (Nat.succ_mul k (L + 1)).symm
      (gap_raises low raises above gaps c (set_above_rises low raises above gaps c k))

end HeldValue

/-- The level of the two-level course steps by the regime's step under the use of the take and the
value there. On `Pace.paced` with the fast step
`ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)`, by `Pace.paced_succ` and
`turn_fast`. Needed by `set_above_takes_all` and `Needed.from_nothing`. -/
private theorem level_steps {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat} {reads : Nat → Bool}
    {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : Nat → Nat → Nat} (c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) (t : Nat) :
    (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h reads c
        (t + 1)).1.2 =
      RegimeMargin.step giving
        (use (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h
            reads c t).1.1
          (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h
            reads c t).2)
        (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h
          reads c t).1.2 :=
  (congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2) (Pace.paced_succ c t)).trans
    (congrArg Prod.snd (turn_fast _ h (reads t) _))

/-- `hi * (L + 1) + 1` is at most `(2 * hi + 1) * L` where `L` is at least one. Needed by `ends_by`. -/
private theorem step_bound {hi L : Nat} (pos : 1 ≤ L) : hi * (L + 1) + 1 ≤ (2 * hi + 1) * L :=
  Nat.le_trans
    (Nat.add_le_add
      (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.mul_succ hi L))
        (Nat.add_le_add_left
          (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.mul_one hi).symm) (Nat.mul_le_mul_left hi pos)) (hi * L)))
      pos)
    (Nat.le_of_eq ((Nat.succ_mul (2 * hi) L).trans
      (congrArg (· + L) ((congrArg (· * L) (Naturals.two_mul hi)).trans (add_mul_split hi hi L)))).symm)

/-- Where every `L` steps in a row hold a reading step (`gaps`, which makes `L` at least one), an
amount that is nothing at step `hi * (L + 1) + 1` is nothing at some step at most `(2 * hi + 1) * L`.
Needed by `set_above_ends`, `set_above_ends_moved_first` and `set_above_ends_read_after`. -/
private theorem ends_by {reads : Nat → Bool} {hi L : Nat}
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true) {X : Nat → Nat}
    (ended : X (hi * (L + 1) + 1) = 0) : ∃ n, n ≤ (2 * hi + 1) * L ∧ X n = 0 :=
  match gaps 0 with
  | ⟨r, _, rL, _⟩ =>
    ⟨hi * (L + 1) + 1,
      step_bound (Nat.succ_le_of_lt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.zero_le r)
        (Eq.subst (motive := fun z => r < z) (Nat.zero_add L) rL))),
      ended⟩

/-- `Regulation.Between m x (A x m)` at every take and value gives `set_above_rises`'s `low`: the take
after a move is at least whatever the take and the value both are at least. Only the two lower bounds of
`Between` are used. -/
theorem low_of_between {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} (between : ∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (A x m)) :
    ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m :=
  fun x m _ kx km => (Nat.le_total m x).elim
    (fun mx => Nat.le_trans km ((between x m).1 mx).1)
    (fun xm => Nat.le_trans kx ((between x m).2 xm).1)

/-- A take that moves strictly nearer the value wherever they differ, the gap `gap (A x m) m` below
`gap x m` (`nearer`), rises strictly below the value: `set_above_rises`'s `raises`. Below the value a take
that did not rise would be no nearer. What `nearer` says from above the value is not used. -/
theorem raises_of_nearer {A : Nat → Nat → Nat}
    (nearer : ∀ x m, x ≠ m → gap (A x m) m < gap x m) : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m :=
  fun x m below => (Nat.lt_or_ge x (A x m)).elim id
    (fun back => absurd
      (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (nearer x m (Nat.ne_of_lt below))
        (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Regulation.gap_of_ge (Nat.le_of_lt below)))
          (Nat.le_trans (Optimum.sub_le_sub_left_of_le back m)
            (Nat.le_of_eq (Regulation.gap_of_ge (Nat.le_trans back (Nat.le_of_lt below))).symm))))
      (Nat.lt_irrefl _))

/-- **A held value that every reading sets above the take leaves the level nothing at step
`hi * (L + 1) + 1`.** On `Pace.paced` at a fast configuration of a take `x` and a level `y` and a held
value `m`: the take moves to `A x m`, the level steps by `RegimeMargin.step giving (use x m)`, and at a
reading step the value becomes `h (x, y) m`. Premises: the take after a move is at least whatever the
take and the value both are at least (`low`); below the value the take rises strictly (`raises`); every
reading is above the take as it stood or above the take as moved (`above`); every `L` steps in a row hold
a reading step (`gaps`); the use is at least whatever the take and the value both are at least (`least`:
the take as it stands, `use := fun x _ => x`, and the take as moved, `use := A`, both meet it); nothing
is given from nothing (`nothing`); the step under no use keeps every level up to `hi` within `hi`
(`keeps`, `RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi`); and the start's level is at most `hi` (`high`). Then the
level at step `hi * (L + 1) + 1` is nothing. The take and the value are both at least `hi` at step
`hi * (L + 1)` (`set_above_rises`), so the use there is, and `reach_levels` gives the rest. Not
concluded: that the level is nothing at no earlier step, or that it stays nothing. -/
theorem set_above_takes_all {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat} {reads : Nat → Bool}
    {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : Nat → Nat → Nat} {hi L : Nat}
    (low : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ A x m) (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m ∨ A p.1 m < h p m)
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true)
    (least : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ use x m)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat} (high : c.1.2 ≤ hi) :
    (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h reads c
      (hi * (L + 1) + 1)).1.2 = 0 :=
  have risen := set_above_rises (fB := fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2) low raises
    above gaps c hi
  reach_levels nothing keeps
    (X := fun t => (Pace.paced
      (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h reads c t).1.2)
    (D := fun t => use
      (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h
        reads c t).1.1
      (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2)) h
        reads c t).2)
    high (level_steps (A := A) (h := h) (reads := reads) (giving := giving) (use := use) c)
    (least _ _ hi risen.1 risen.2)

/-- **A held value that every reading sets above what is taken ends the stock: the statement as it was
asked, in the order of taking and reading `Pace.turn` has.** On `Pace.paced` at a fast configuration of
a take `x` and a level `y` and a held value `m`: the take moves to `A x m`, the level steps under the
take as it stands, `RegimeMargin.step giving x y`, and at a reading step the value becomes `h (x, y) m`,
read from the configuration before the step. Premises: the take after a move lies between the take and
the value (`between`, `Regulation.Between m x (A x m)`); below the value the take rises strictly
(`raises`); every reading sets the value above the take, for every fast configuration `p` and value `m`,
`p.1 < h p m` (`above`); every `L` steps in a row hold a reading step (`gaps`: `Pace` has no such
premise, so it is a hypothesis here); nothing is given from nothing (`nothing`); the step under no take
keeps every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`, `RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi`); and the start's
level is at most `hi` (`high`). Then at some step `n` at most `(2 * hi + 1) * L` the level is nothing:
the step `hi * (L + 1) + 1` (`set_above_takes_all` at `use := fun x _ => x`), which is at most
`(2 * hi + 1) * L` since `gaps` makes `L` at least one. Of `between`, only that the take after a move is
not below both the take and the value is used (`low_of_between`); strict nearing is asked only from
below the value, and a take that moves strictly nearer wherever it differs from the value meets `raises`
(`raises_of_nearer`). -/
theorem set_above_ends {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat} {reads : Nat → Bool}
    {giving : Nat → Nat} {hi L : Nat}
    (between : ∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (A x m)) (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m)
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat} (high : c.1.2 ≤ hi) :
    ∃ n, n ≤ (2 * hi + 1) * L ∧
      (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step giving p.1 p.2)) h reads c
        n).1.2 = 0 :=
  ends_by gaps
    (X := fun n => (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step giving p.1 p.2)) h
      reads c n).1.2)
    (set_above_takes_all (use := fun x _ => x) (low_of_between between) raises
      (fun p m => .inl (above p m)) gaps (fun _ _ _ kx _ => kx) nothing keeps high)

/-- **The other order of taking: the take moves first, and the level steps under the take as moved.**
`set_above_ends` with the level's step `RegimeMargin.step giving (A x m) y` in place of
`RegimeMargin.step giving x y`; every premise and the conclusion are otherwise the same.
`set_above_takes_all` at `use := A`, whose `least` is `low_of_between`. -/
theorem set_above_ends_moved_first {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat}
    {reads : Nat → Bool} {giving : Nat → Nat} {hi L : Nat}
    (between : ∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (A x m)) (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m)
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat} (high : c.1.2 ≤ hi) :
    ∃ n, n ≤ (2 * hi + 1) * L ∧
      (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (A p.1 m) p.2)) h reads c
        n).1.2 = 0 :=
  ends_by gaps
    (X := fun n => (Pace.paced
      (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (A p.1 m) p.2)) h reads c n).1.2)
    (set_above_takes_all (use := A) (low_of_between between) raises
      (fun p m => .inl (above p m)) gaps (low_of_between between) nothing keeps high)

/-- **The other order of reading: the value is read from the configuration after the step.** The
reading at a fast configuration `p` under the value `m` is `h` of the fast configuration after the step,
`h (ClosedCapture.join A … p m) m`, in either order of taking (any `use` that meets `least`). `between`,
`raises`, `gaps`, `nothing`, `keeps` and `high` are `set_above_ends`'s, `least` is
`set_above_takes_all`'s, and `above` says of `h` what it says there, `p.1 < h p m` for every fast
configuration `p` and value `m`; here it is used at the configuration after the step, so the value is set
above the take as moved. The conclusion is the same. `set_above_takes_all` with the second branch of its
`above`. -/
theorem set_above_ends_read_after {A : Nat → Nat → Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat}
    {reads : Nat → Bool} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : Nat → Nat → Nat} {hi L : Nat}
    (between : ∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (A x m)) (raises : ∀ x m, x < m → x < A x m)
    (above : ∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < h p m)
    (gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + L ∧ reads r = true)
    (least : ∀ x m k, k ≤ x → k ≤ m → k ≤ use x m)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat} (high : c.1.2 ≤ hi) :
    ∃ n, n ≤ (2 * hi + 1) * L ∧
      (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2))
        (fun p m =>
          h (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2) p m) m)
        reads c n).1.2 = 0 :=
  ends_by gaps
    (X := fun n => (Pace.paced
      (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2))
      (fun p m => h (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2) p m) m)
      reads c n).1.2)
    (set_above_takes_all (use := use) (low_of_between between) raises
      (fun p m => .inr (above
        (ClosedCapture.join A (fun p m => RegimeMargin.step giving (use p.1 m) p.2) p m) m))
      gaps least nothing keeps high)

/-! ## 3. Each premise dropped, a course that never ends

Six small courses. Each theorem states the premises of `set_above_ends` (for `not_low`, of
`set_above_takes_all`) that its course meets, and that the level is never nothing; the premise left out
is the one the course does not meet. -/

namespace Needed

/-- A stock that gives nothing at any level. -/
def barren (_ : Nat) : Nat := 0

/-- A stock that gives one from nothing, and nothing from any other level. -/
def spring (z : Nat) : Nat := 1 - z

/-- A stock that gives as much as is left. -/
def doubling (z : Nat) : Nat := z

/-- The tent on four: what is left, or what it lacks of four, whichever is less. -/
def tent (z : Nat) : Nat := min z (4 - z)

/-- The take that never moves. -/
def stay (x _ : Nat) : Nat := x

/-- The take that moves to the value at once. -/
def atOnce (_ m : Nat) : Nat := m

/-- The take that rises by one below the value, and falls to nothing at or above it. -/
def fall (x m : Nat) : Nat := if x < m then x + 1 else 0

/-- The reading that sets the value one above the take. -/
def oneAbove (p : Nat × Nat) (_ : Nat) : Nat := p.1 + 1

/-- The reading that sets the value at the take. -/
def atTake (p : Nat × Nat) (_ : Nat) : Nat := p.1

/-- The schedule that reads at every step. -/
def always (_ : Nat) : Bool := true

/-- The schedule that never reads. -/
def never (_ : Nat) : Bool := false

/-- A configuration that the turn at every step takes to itself is the course's at every step. Needed
by `not_rising`, `not_above` and `not_read`. -/
private theorem fixed_course {f : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat × Nat} {h : Nat × Nat → Nat → Nat}
    {reads : Nat → Bool} {c : (Nat × Nat) × Nat} (fixed : ∀ t, Pace.turn f h (reads t) c = c) :
    ∀ t, Pace.paced f h reads c t = c
  | 0 => rfl
  | t + 1 => (Pace.paced_succ c t).trans
      ((congrArg (Pace.turn f h (reads t)) (fixed_course fixed t)).trans (fixed t))

/-- The take that moves to the value at once lies between the take and the value. Needed by the
courses of this section that use `atOnce`. -/
private theorem between_atOnce : ∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (atOnce x m) :=
  fun _ m => ⟨fun mx => ⟨Nat.le_refl m, mx⟩, fun xm => ⟨xm, Nat.le_refl m⟩⟩

/-- The schedule that reads at every step has a reading step in every one step. Needed by the courses
of this section that read at every step. -/
private theorem always_gaps : ∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + 1 ∧ always r = true :=
  fun t => ⟨t, Nat.le_refl t, Nat.lt_succ_self t, rfl⟩

/-- **Without the strict rise below the value, a course that never ends.** The take never moves
(`stay`), which is `Regulation.Between`; every reading sets the value one above the take; every step
reads; nothing is given at any level, so nothing from nothing, and the step under no take keeps every
level up to one within one; the start has take nothing, level one and value one. The level is one at
every step. Only `set_above_ends`'s `raises` is not met. -/
theorem not_rising :
    (∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (stay x m)) ∧
    (∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < oneAbove p m) ∧
    (∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + 1 ∧ always r = true) ∧
    RegimeMargin.FromNothing barren ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps barren 0 1 ∧
    ∀ t, (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join stay (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step barren p.1 p.2)) oneAbove
      always ((0, 1), 1) t).1.2 = 1 :=
  ⟨fun x m => Regulation.Between.refl m x, fun p _ => Nat.lt_succ_self p.1, always_gaps, rfl,
    RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl,
    fun t => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2)
      (fixed_course (f := ClosedCapture.join stay (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step barren p.1 p.2))
        (h := oneAbove) (reads := always) (c := ((0, 1), 1)) (fun _ => rfl) t)⟩

/-- **With the value set at the take and not above it, a course that never ends.** The take moves to
the value at once (`atOnce`), which is `Regulation.Between` and rises strictly below the value; every
reading sets the value at the take (`atTake`); every step reads; nothing is given at any level; the
start has take nothing, level one and value nothing. The level is one at every step. Only
`set_above_ends`'s `above` is not met. -/
theorem not_above :
    (∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (atOnce x m)) ∧ (∀ x m, x < m → x < atOnce x m) ∧
    (∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + 1 ∧ always r = true) ∧
    RegimeMargin.FromNothing barren ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps barren 0 1 ∧
    ∀ t, (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step barren p.1 p.2)) atTake
      always ((0, 1), 0) t).1.2 = 1 :=
  ⟨between_atOnce, fun _ _ lt => lt, always_gaps, rfl, RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl,
    fun t => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2)
      (fixed_course (f := ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step barren p.1 p.2))
        (h := atTake) (reads := always) (c := ((0, 1), 0)) (fun _ => rfl) t)⟩

/-- **With no reading step, a course that never ends.** The take moves to the value at once; every
reading would set the value one above the take; no step reads (`never`); nothing is given at any level;
the start has take nothing, level one and value nothing. The level is one at every step. Only
`set_above_ends`'s `gaps` is not met, at any `L`. -/
theorem not_read :
    (∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (atOnce x m)) ∧ (∀ x m, x < m → x < atOnce x m) ∧
    (∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < oneAbove p m) ∧
    RegimeMargin.FromNothing barren ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps barren 0 1 ∧
    ∀ t, (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step barren p.1 p.2)) oneAbove
      never ((0, 1), 0) t).1.2 = 1 :=
  ⟨between_atOnce, fun _ _ lt => lt, fun p _ => Nat.lt_succ_self p.1, rfl,
    RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl,
    fun t => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2)
      (fixed_course (f := ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step barren p.1 p.2))
        (h := oneAbove) (reads := never) (c := ((0, 1), 0)) (fun _ => rfl) t)⟩

/-- Under `spring`, the step from the level one leaves one under every use. Needed by
`spring_level`. -/
private theorem spring_one : ∀ d, RegimeMargin.step spring d 1 = 1
  | 0 => rfl
  | d + 1 => (congrArg (fun z => z + spring z)
      (sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.succ_le_succ (Nat.zero_le d)))).trans rfl

/-- The level of `from_nothing`'s course is one at every step. Needed by `from_nothing`. -/
private theorem spring_level : ∀ t,
    (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step spring p.1 p.2)) oneAbove
      always ((0, 1), 1) t).1.2 = 1
  | 0 => rfl
  | t + 1 =>
    (level_steps (A := atOnce) (h := oneAbove) (reads := always) (giving := spring)
      (use := fun x _ => x) ((0, 1), 1) t).trans
      ((congrArg (RegimeMargin.step spring _) (spring_level t)).trans (spring_one _))

/-- **With something given from nothing, a course that never ends.** The take moves to the value at
once; every reading sets the value one above the take; every step reads; the stock gives one from
nothing and nothing from any other level (`spring`), and the step under no take keeps every level up to
one within one; the start has take nothing, level one and value one. The level is one at every step.
Only `set_above_ends`'s `nothing` is not met. (The take still passes every amount, since the course
meets `set_above_rises`'s premises; that is argued here, not a Lean statement.) -/
theorem from_nothing :
    (∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (atOnce x m)) ∧ (∀ x m, x < m → x < atOnce x m) ∧
    (∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < oneAbove p m) ∧
    (∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + 1 ∧ always r = true) ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps spring 0 1 ∧
    ∀ t, (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step spring p.1 p.2)) oneAbove
      always ((0, 1), 1) t).1.2 = 1 :=
  ⟨between_atOnce, fun _ _ lt => lt, fun p _ => Nat.lt_succ_self p.1, always_gaps,
    RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl, spring_level⟩

/-- The region `no_cap`'s course stays in: the value is the take or one above it, and the level is at
least twice one more than the take. -/
def Ahead (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) : Prop :=
  p.1.1 ≤ p.2 ∧ p.2 ≤ p.1.1 + 1 ∧ p.1.1 + 1 + (p.1.1 + 1) ≤ p.1.2

/-- Every turn of `no_cap`'s step keeps `Ahead`, reading or not: the take becomes the value, at most
one more; a reading sets the value one above the old take; and twice what the take leaves is at least
twice two more than the old take. Needed by `ahead_course`. -/
private theorem ahead_kept (b : Bool) (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) (inside : Ahead p) :
    Ahead (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step doubling p.1 p.2))
      oneAbove b p) :=
  have left : p.1.1 + 1 + 1 ≤ p.1.2 - p.1.1 := Naturals.le_sub_of_add_le
    (show p.1.1 + (p.1.1 + 1 + 1) ≤ p.1.2 from Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg Nat.succ (Nat.add_comm p.1.1 (p.1.1 + 1)))) inside.2.2)
  have next : p.2 + 1 ≤ p.1.2 - p.1.1 := Nat.le_trans (Nat.succ_le_succ inside.2.1) left
  have level : p.2 + 1 + (p.2 + 1) ≤ p.1.2 - p.1.1 + (p.1.2 - p.1.1) := Nat.add_le_add next next
  match b with
  | true => ⟨inside.2.1, Nat.succ_le_succ inside.1, level⟩
  | false => ⟨Nat.le_refl p.2, Nat.le_succ p.2, level⟩

/-- `no_cap`'s course is in `Ahead` at every step, under every schedule of reading. Needed by
`no_cap`. -/
private theorem ahead_course (reads : Nat → Bool) : ∀ t,
    Ahead (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step doubling p.1 p.2))
      oneAbove reads ((0, 2), 1) t)
  | 0 => ⟨Nat.le_succ 0, Nat.le_refl 1, Nat.le_refl 2⟩
  | t + 1 => Eq.subst (motive := Ahead) (Pace.paced_succ ((0, 2), 1) t).symm
      (ahead_kept (reads t) _ (ahead_course reads t))

/-- **With a level not kept within `hi`, or a start above it, a course that never ends.** The take
moves to the value at once; every reading sets the value one above the take; every step reads; the stock
gives as much as is left (`doubling`), nothing from nothing; the start has take nothing, level two and
value one. The level is above nothing at every step: it is at least twice one more than the take
throughout (`Ahead`). At `hi := 0` the step under no take keeps the level nothing at nothing, and only
`set_above_ends`'s `high` is not met, the start's level being two; at `hi := 2` the start is within `hi`
and only `keeps` is not met, the step under no take taking two to four. -/
theorem no_cap :
    (∀ x m, Regulation.Between m x (atOnce x m)) ∧ (∀ x m, x < m → x < atOnce x m) ∧
    (∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < oneAbove p m) ∧
    (∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + 1 ∧ always r = true) ∧
    RegimeMargin.FromNothing doubling ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps doubling 0 0 ∧
    RegimeMargin.step doubling 0 2 = 4 ∧
    ∀ t, 0 < (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join atOnce (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step doubling p.1 p.2))
      oneAbove always ((0, 2), 1) t).1.2 :=
  ⟨between_atOnce, fun _ _ lt => lt, fun p _ => Nat.lt_succ_self p.1, always_gaps, rfl,
    RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl, rfl,
    fun t => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.succ_pos _) (ahead_course always t).2.2⟩

/-- The region `not_low`'s course stays in: four configurations, the level four in each. -/
def Cycle (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) : Prop :=
  p = ((0, 4), 0) ∨ p = ((0, 4), 1) ∨ p = ((1, 4), 1) ∨ p = ((0, 4), 2)

/-- A reading turn of `not_low`'s step keeps `Cycle`: the first configuration goes to the second, the
second to the third, the third to the fourth, the fourth to the third. Needed by `cycle_course`. -/
private theorem cycle_kept (p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat) : Cycle p →
    Cycle (Pace.turn (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2))
      oneAbove true p)
  | .inl e => .inr (.inl ((congrArg (Pace.turn
      (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2)) oneAbove true) e).trans rfl))
  | .inr (.inl e) => .inr (.inr (.inl ((congrArg (Pace.turn
      (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2)) oneAbove true) e).trans rfl)))
  | .inr (.inr (.inl e)) => .inr (.inr (.inr ((congrArg (Pace.turn
      (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2)) oneAbove true) e).trans rfl)))
  | .inr (.inr (.inr e)) => .inr (.inr (.inl ((congrArg (Pace.turn
      (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2)) oneAbove true) e).trans rfl)))

/-- `not_low`'s course is in `Cycle` at every step. Needed by `not_low`. -/
private theorem cycle_course : ∀ t,
    Cycle (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2))
      oneAbove always ((0, 4), 0) t)
  | 0 => .inl rfl
  | t + 1 => Eq.subst (motive := Cycle) (Pace.paced_succ ((0, 4), 0) t).symm
      (cycle_kept _ (cycle_course t))

/-- In `Cycle` the level is four. Needed by `not_low`. -/
private theorem cycle_level {p : (Nat × Nat) × Nat} : Cycle p → p.1.2 = 4
  | .inl e => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2) e
  | .inr (.inl e) => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2) e
  | .inr (.inr (.inl e)) => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2) e
  | .inr (.inr (.inr e)) => congrArg (fun q : (Nat × Nat) × Nat => q.1.2) e

/-- **With a take that falls below both the take and the value, a course that never ends.** The take
rises by one below the value and falls to nothing at or above it (`fall`), so below the value it rises
strictly; every reading sets the value one above the take; every step reads; the stock is the tent on
four, nothing from nothing, and the step under no take keeps every level up to four within four; the
start has take nothing, level four and value nothing. The takes run nothing, nothing, one, nothing, one,
and so on, and the level is four at every step. Only `set_above_takes_all`'s `low` is not met (so
`set_above_ends`'s `between` is not): at take one and value one the take falls to nothing. -/
theorem not_low :
    (∀ x m, x < m → x < fall x m) ∧ (∀ (p : Nat × Nat) (m : Nat), p.1 < oneAbove p m) ∧
    (∀ t, ∃ r, t ≤ r ∧ r < t + 1 ∧ always r = true) ∧
    RegimeMargin.FromNothing tent ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps tent 0 4 ∧ fall 1 1 = 0 ∧
    ∀ t, (Pace.paced (ClosedCapture.join fall (fun p _ => RegimeMargin.step tent p.1 p.2)) oneAbove
      always ((0, 4), 0) t).1.2 = 4 :=
  ⟨fun x _ lt => Eq.subst (motive := fun z => x < z) (if_pos lt).symm (Nat.lt_succ_self x),
    fun p _ => Nat.lt_succ_self p.1, always_gaps, rfl, RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl, rfl,
    fun t => cycle_level (cycle_course t)⟩

end Needed
