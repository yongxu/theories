/-!
# A growing line takes everything with it where nothing else needs less than it

AI-written (Fable), 16 November 2026. Scratch; not on main.

On `LineOnAStock`'s model (units with needs, kids and uses on a level with a giving), with nothing
added to the model.

## What is proved

* `TurningInTime.rising_floor_takes_all`: on any orbit whose level steps by `RegimeMargin.step` under
  a take, with nothing given from nothing, the step under no take keeping every level up to `hi`
  within `hi` and a start at most `hi`: where the take is at least a quantity that rises at each of
  the first `hi` steps, the level at step `hi + 1` is nothing. `TurningInTime.rising_takes_all` is its
  case where the quantity is the take (`rising_takes_all_as_case`).
* `falls_below`: for any decidable `S` whose units' kids of `S` take more than the unit, with the
  units of `S` on the course needing at most `m`, `0 < m`, the same three premises on the giving and
  the start, and a unit of `S` in the start way: within `hi + 1` steps the level is below `m`, at
  least `m` at every step before, a unit of `S` in the way at every step up to it. Nothing is asked of
  the other units, and no closure. `ends_all` (so `line_ends` and `way_ends`) follows from it without
  `ends_high` (`ends_all_as_case`).
* `takes_the_whole`: with every unit of `S` needing exactly `m` and every unit not of `S` needing at
  least `m`, at that step no need is met and the net has ended, within `hi + 1` steps.
  `one_need_ends` is its case at the line of every unit (`one_need_ends_as_case`).
* `line_gone_within`: without the premise on the other units and without a unit of the line at the
  start, a closed line of one need is gone from a step at most `hi + 2`; the rest may go on
  (`OutsideNeedsLess`).
* `line_way`: while every unit of a closed line is met, the way stripped of the units not of the line
  is the generation so stripped.

## Kept countermodels and worked courses

* `OutsideNeedsLess`: a unit outside the line that needs less than the line. Every other premise of
  `takes_the_whole` holds, the line is gone from step 6, and the net never ends.
* `ProperLine.no_line_unit`: no unit of the line in the start way. Every other premise holds and the
  net never ends.
* `EqualInPlaceOfMore.all_but_grows_whole`: kids that take what the unit takes. Every other premise
  holds and the net never ends.
* `OutsideNeedsMore`: a unit outside the line that needs more than it. Every premise holds and the
  net ends at step 2 with a way that is not the generation of the start way: `one_need_ends`' last
  conjunct cannot be had as it stands.

Every declaration has an empty axiom report.
-/

universe u

namespace Becoming
namespace TurningInTime

/-- **Against a level that cannot pass `hi`, a take that is at least a quantity rising for `hi` steps
leaves nothing after `hi + 1` steps.** On the orbit of any `next` from `s`, read by a level, a take
and a quantity `q`: with nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the step under no take keeping every
level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), the level after a step being `RegimeMargin.step giving` under
the take, at every configuration (`steps`), `q` at most the take at every configuration (`floor`),
the start's level at most `hi` (`high`), and `q` rising strictly at each of the first `hi` steps of
the orbit (`rises`): the level at step `hi + 1` is nothing. Every level of the orbit is at most `hi`
(`kept_within`, step by step); `q` at step `hi` is at least `hi` (`rises_from`); so the take there is
at least the level, nothing is left, and nothing is given from nothing. Nothing is asked of the take
but that it is at least `q`: it may fall. -/
theorem rising_floor_takes_all {W : Type u} {next : W → W} {level take q : W → Nat}
    {giving : Nat → Nat} {hi : Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (steps : ∀ t, level (next t) = RegimeMargin.step giving (take t) (level t))
    (floor : ∀ t, q t ≤ take t) {s : W} (high : level s ≤ hi)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → q (Law.orbit next s j) < q (Law.orbit next s (j + 1))) :
    level (Law.orbit next s (hi + 1)) = 0 :=
  have within : ∀ j, level (Law.orbit next s j) ≤ hi := fun j => by
    induction j with
    | zero => exact high
    | succ j ih => exact Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (steps (Law.orbit next s j))) (kept_within keeps _ ih)
  have takes : level (Law.orbit next s hi) ≤ take (Law.orbit next s hi) :=
    Nat.le_trans (within hi) (Nat.le_trans (rises_from rises hi (Nat.le_refl hi)) (floor _))
  (steps (Law.orbit next s hi)).trans
    ((congrArg (fun z => z + giving z) (sub_eq_zero_of_le takes)).trans
      ((Nat.zero_add (giving 0)).trans nothing))

/-- **`rising_takes_all`, as a case.** The statement of `TurningInTime.rising_takes_all`, proved from
`rising_floor_takes_all` on the course as it was, with the quantity the use itself: the level's step
is `RegimeMargin.step giving` under the use by definition, and the use along that course is the use
along `alone`'s orbit (`asItWas_way`). The private `takes_at_hi` is not used. -/
theorem rising_takes_all_as_case {W : Type u} {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat}
    {hi : Nat} (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {w : W} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1))) :
    (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) (hi + 1)).2 = 0 :=
  have way := asItWas_way alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x) (w, x)
  rising_floor_takes_all (next := asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x))
    (level := Prod.snd) (take := fun c => use c.1) (q := fun c => use c.1) nothing keeps
    (fun _ => rfl) (fun _ => Nat.le_refl _) (s := (w, x)) high
    fun j lt => Eq.subst (motive := fun v => use v <
        use (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x) (j + 1)).1)
      (way j).symm
      (Eq.subst (motive := fun v => use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use v) (way (j + 1)).symm (rises j lt))

end TurningInTime

/-! ## 1. The level comes below the need of a growing line -/

section Falls

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}
  {S : U → Prop} {hi m : Nat}

/-- A unit of `S` whose kids of `S` together take more than it (`grows`) has a kid of `S`: what `S`
takes of its kids is positive, and a positive total has a positive member (`total_pos`). -/
theorem line_kid [DecidablePred S] (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) {u : U}
    (su : S u) : ∃ v, v ∈ kids u ∧ S v :=
  match total_pos (q := fun v => if S v then use v else 0) (kids u)
      (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.zero_le (use u)) (grows u su)) with
  | ⟨v, mv, positive⟩ => ⟨v, mv, (Decidable.em (S v)).elim id fun h =>
      absurd (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le positive (Nat.le_of_eq (if_neg h))) (Nat.lt_irrefl 0)⟩

/-- With the step under no take keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), from a level at
most `hi` (`high`) every level of the course is at most `hi`. `TurningInTime.kept_within`, step by
step. -/
theorem level_within (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) {s : List U × Nat} (high : s.2 ≤ hi) :
    ∀ j, (course need kids giving use s j).2 ≤ hi
  | 0 => high
  | j + 1 => TurningInTime.kept_within keeps _ (level_within keeps high j)

/-- **While the level meets `m`, a unit of `S` stays in the way.** For any decidable `S` (no closure
is asked) whose units' kids of `S` take more than the unit (`grows`), every unit of `S` in a way of
the course needing at most `m` (`below`), and a unit of `S` in the start way (`mem`, `line`): if the
level is at least `m` at every step before `n` (`before`), a unit of `S` is in the way at every step
up to `n`. A unit of `S` met at a step leaves a kid of `S` (`line_kid`, `mem_after_iff`). -/
theorem line_while [DecidablePred S] (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u))
    {s : List U × Nat}
    (below : ∀ j u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → S u → need u ≤ m)
    {u₀ : U} (mem : u₀ ∈ s.1) (line : S u₀) {n : Nat}
    (before : ∀ j, j < n → m ≤ (course need kids giving use s j).2) :
    ∀ j, j ≤ n → ∃ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 ∧ S u
  | 0, _ => ⟨u₀, mem, line⟩
  | j + 1, le => match line_while grows below mem line before j (Nat.le_of_succ_le le) with
    | ⟨u, mu, su⟩ => match line_kid grows su with
      | ⟨v, mv, sv⟩ => ⟨v, (mem_after_iff (need := need) (kids := kids)
            (w := (course need kids giving use s j).1) (x := (course need kids giving use s j).2)).mpr
          ⟨u, mu, Nat.le_trans (below j u mu su) (before j (Nat.lt_of_succ_le le)), mv⟩, sv⟩

/-- **The level comes below the need of a growing line within `hi + 1` steps.** For any decidable `S`
(no closure is asked, and nothing is asked of the units not of `S`): with each unit of `S` having
kids of `S` that together take more than it (`grows`), nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the
step under no take keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), `0 < m` (`pos`), the start
level at most `hi` (`high`), every unit of `S` in a way of the course needing at most `m` (`below`),
and a unit of `S` in the start way (`mem`, `line`): there is a step `n ≤ hi + 1` at which the level is
below `m`, the level at least `m` at every step before `n`, and a unit of `S` in the way at every step
up to `n`. Were the level at least `m` at every step up to `hi + 1`, the units of `S` would be met and
one of them present at each step (`line_while`), so what `S` takes would rise at each step (`rise`,
`lineCount_pos`); what the way takes is at least that (`lineTake_le_taken`); so the level at `hi + 1`
would be nothing (`TurningInTime.rising_floor_takes_all`), below `m`. The step is the first with the
level below `m` (`first_of_decided`). -/
theorem falls_below [DecidablePred S] (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u))
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (pos : 0 < m) {s : List U × Nat} (high : s.2 ≤ hi)
    (below : ∀ j u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → S u → need u ≤ m)
    {u₀ : U} (mem : u₀ ∈ s.1) (line : S u₀) :
    ∃ n, n ≤ hi + 1 ∧ (∀ j, j < n → m ≤ (course need kids giving use s j).2) ∧
      (course need kids giving use s n).2 < m ∧
      ∀ j, j ≤ n → ∃ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 ∧ S u :=
  match first_of_decided
      (P := fun j => m ≤ (course need kids giving use s j).2)
      (Q := fun j => (course need kids giving use s j).2 < m)
      (fun j => (Nat.lt_or_ge (course need kids giving use s j).2 m).symm) (hi + 1) with
  | .inl all =>
    have before : ∀ j, j < hi + 1 → m ≤ (course need kids giving use s j).2 :=
      fun j lt => all j (Nat.le_of_lt lt)
    have zero : (course need kids giving use s (hi + 1)).2 = 0 :=
      TurningInTime.rising_floor_takes_all (next := step need kids giving use) (level := Prod.snd)
        (take := fun c => taken use c.1) (q := fun c => lineTake S use c.1) nothing keeps
        (fun _ => rfl) (fun _ => lineTake_le_taken _) high
        fun j lt =>
          match line_while grows below mem line before j (Nat.le_of_lt (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt)) with
          | ⟨_, mu, su⟩ =>
            have up : lineTake S use (course need kids giving use s j).1 + 1 ≤
                lineTake S use (after need kids (course need kids giving use s j).1
                  (course need kids giving use s j).2) :=
              Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_left (lineCount_pos su mu) _)
                (rise grows fun v mv sv =>
                  Nat.le_trans (below j v mv sv) (before j (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt)))
            Nat.lt_of_succ_le up
    absurd (Nat.le_trans (all (hi + 1) (Nat.le_refl _)) (Nat.le_of_eq zero)) (Nat.not_le_of_gt pos)
  | .inr ⟨n, le, low, before⟩ => ⟨n, le, before, low, line_while grows below mem line before⟩

end Falls

/-! ## 2. The line's part of the way -/

section Way

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}
  {S : U → Prop}

/-- With the kids of units of `Z` all of `Z` (`inZ`), all the kids of a way stripped of `Z` are, so
stripped, all the kids of the way, so stripped. `strip_after` at a need of nothing, where the way after
a step is all the kids (`after_of_met`). -/
theorem strip_allKids {Z : U → Prop} [DecidablePred Z] (inZ : ∀ u v, Z u → v ∈ kids u → Z v)
    (w : List U) : strip Z (allKids kids (strip Z w)) = strip Z (allKids kids w) :=
  (congrArg (strip Z) (after_of_met (need := fun _ => 0) (kids := kids) (x := 0) (w := strip Z w)
      fun _ _ => Nat.le_refl 0)).symm.trans
    ((strip_after (need := fun _ => 0) inZ 0 w).trans
      (congrArg (strip Z) (after_of_met (need := fun _ => 0) (kids := kids) (x := 0) (w := w)
        fun _ _ => Nat.le_refl 0)))

/-- For a line `S` (`closed`: no unit outside it leaves a unit of it): where every unit of `S` in `w`
has its need met at `x`, the way after the step, stripped of the units not of `S`, is all the kids of
`w` so stripped. A unit of `S` leaves its kids; a unit not of `S` leaves, met or not, no unit of
`S` (`strip_all`). -/
theorem line_after_met [DecidablePred S] (closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u) {x : Nat} :
    ∀ {w : List U}, (∀ u, u ∈ w → S u → need u ≤ x) →
      strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (after need kids w x) = strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (allKids kids w)
  | [], _ => rfl
  | u :: w, met =>
    have head : strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) []) =
        strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (kids u) :=
      (Decidable.em (S u)).elim
        (fun su => congrArg (fun b => strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (cond b (kids u) []))
          (decide_eq_true (met u (.head w) su)))
        (fun ns => (strip_all (Z := fun v => ¬ S v) fun v mv sv =>
            ns (closed u v (mem_cond mv).2 sv)).trans
          (strip_all (Z := fun v => ¬ S v) fun v mv sv => ns (closed u v mv sv)).symm)
    (strip_append (Z := fun v => ¬ S v) (cond (decide (need u ≤ x)) (kids u) [])
        (after need kids w x)).trans
      ((congrArg (· ++ strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (after need kids w x)) head).trans
        ((congrArg (strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (kids u) ++ ·)
            (line_after_met closed fun v mm => met v (.tail u mm))).trans
          (strip_append (Z := fun v => ¬ S v) (kids u) (allKids kids w)).symm))

/-- **While every unit of a line is met, the line's part of the way is the line's part of the
generation.** For a line `S` (`closed`), under any `need`, giving and use, from any start: where at
every step before `n` every unit of `S` in the way has its need met (`met`), the way at step `n`
stripped of the units not of `S` is the `n`-th generation of the start way so stripped. No giving, use
or level appears in the generation, and nothing is asked of the units not of `S`: whether they are met
makes no difference to the line's part. By `line_after_met` and `strip_allKids`, step by step.
`way_while_met` is the like statement for the whole way where every need is met. -/
theorem line_way [DecidablePred S] (closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u) {w : List U} {x : Nat} :
    ∀ n, (∀ j, j < n → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).1 → S u →
        need u ≤ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).2) →
      strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).1 =
        strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (generation kids n w)
  | 0, _ => rfl
  | n + 1, met =>
    have inZ : ∀ u v, ¬ S u → v ∈ kids u → ¬ S v := fun u v ns mv sv => ns (closed u v mv sv)
    (line_after_met closed (met n (Nat.lt_succ_self n))).trans
      ((strip_allKids inZ (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).1).symm.trans
        ((congrArg (fun l => strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (allKids kids l))
            (line_way closed n fun j lt => met j (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt))).trans
          (strip_allKids inZ (generation kids n w))))

end Way

/-! ## 3. Where nothing else needs less, everything ends with the line -/

section Whole

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}
  {S : U → Prop} {hi m : Nat}

/-- **A growing line of one need takes everything with it where nothing else needs less than it.**
For any decidable `S`: with every unit of `S` needing `m` (`one`), `0 < m` (`pos`), each unit of `S`
having kids of `S` that together take more than it (`grows`), every unit not of `S` needing at least
`m` (`outside`), nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the step under no take keeping every level up
to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps`), the start level at most `hi` (`high`), and a unit of `S` in the start
way (`mem`, `line`): there is a step `n ≤ hi + 1` such that the level is at least `m` at every step
before `n` and below `m` at step `n`; the net from `(w, x)` reaches the course's configuration at step
`n` in `n` steps; that configuration conditions nothing, no need being met there; and, where no unit
outside `S` leaves a unit of `S` (closure, asked by this last conjunct only), the way there stripped
of the units not of `S` is the `n`-th generation of `w` so stripped (`line_way`). By `falls_below`
(which rests on `rise` and `TurningInTime.rising_floor_takes_all`): at the first step with the level
below `m` no need is met, every need being at least `m`; before it a unit of `S` is in the way and
met (`Law.stepWhile_steps_iff`, `Law.stepWhile_none_iff`). `one_need_ends`' conjunct that the way
there is the generation of `w` is not concluded and does not hold: a unit not of `S` that needs more
than `m` can be unmet at a level that meets `m` (`OutsideNeedsMore.way_not_generation`); where every
unit is of `S` it follows from the second conjunct by `way_while_met` (`one_need_ends_as_case`).
Without `outside` the net can go on
(`OutsideNeedsLess.all_but_outside`); without a unit of `S` in the start way it can go on
(`ProperLine.no_line_unit`); with kids that take what the unit takes in place of more it can go on
(`EqualInPlaceOfMore.all_but_grows_whole`). -/
theorem takes_the_whole [DecidablePred S] (one : ∀ u, S u → need u = m) (pos : 0 < m)
    (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) (outside : ∀ u, ¬ S u → m ≤ need u)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {w : List U} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) {u₀ : U} (mem : u₀ ∈ w) (line : S u₀) :
    ∃ n, n ≤ hi + 1 ∧
      (∀ j, j < n → m ≤ (course need kids giving use (w, x) j).2) ∧
      (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).2 < m ∧
      «迭» (net need kids giving use) n (w, x) (course need kids giving use (w, x) n) ∧
      (∀ v, ¬ net need kids giving use (course need kids giving use (w, x) n) v) ∧
      ((∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u) →
        strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (course need kids giving use (w, x) n).1 =
          strip (fun v => ¬ S v) (generation kids n w)) :=
  match falls_below (s := (w, x)) grows nothing keeps pos high
      (fun _ u _ su => Nat.le_of_eq (one u su)) mem line with
  | ⟨n, le, before, low, units⟩ =>
    ⟨n, le, before, low,
     (Law.stepWhile_steps_iff n).mpr ⟨fun j lj => match units j (Nat.le_of_lt lj) with
       | ⟨u, mu, su⟩ => ⟨u, mu, Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (one u su)) (before j lj)⟩, rfl⟩,
     Law.stepWhile_none_iff.mpr fun ⟨c, _, lc⟩ => Nat.not_le_of_gt low (Nat.le_trans
       ((Decidable.em (S c)).elim (fun sc => Nat.le_of_eq (one c sc).symm) (outside c)) lc),
     fun closed => line_way closed n fun j lj u _ su =>
       Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (one u su)) (before j lj)⟩

/-- **A growing line of one need is gone within `hi + 2` steps, whatever else is in the way.** For a
line `S` (`closed`: no unit outside it leaves a unit of it), with every unit of `S` needing `m`
(`one`), `0 < m` (`pos`), each unit of `S` having kids of `S` that together take more than it
(`grows`), nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), the step under no take keeping every level up to
`hi` within `hi` (`keeps`) and the start level at most `hi` (`high`); nothing is asked of the units
not of `S`, and no unit of `S` need be in the start way: there is a step `n ≤ hi + 2` from which no
unit of `S` is in the way. With no unit of `S` in the start way that is step 0 (`gone_from`);
otherwise the step after the level comes below `m` (`falls_below`, `gone_after`). `line_ends` states
no such step; in place of one need it asks that the units of `S` need something and that every kid of
a unit of `S` need no more than it, so neither law's premises contain the other's. Whether the net
ends is not concluded: on `OutsideNeedsLess` it does not. -/
theorem line_gone_within [DecidablePred S] (closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u)
    (one : ∀ u, S u → need u = m) (pos : 0 < m)
    (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u))
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    {w : List U} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) :
    ∃ n, n ≤ hi + 2 ∧
      ∀ k, n ≤ k → ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use (w, x) k).1 → ¬ S u :=
  (line_or_none (S := S) w).elim
    (fun nm => ⟨0, Nat.zero_le _, gone_from closed (k := 0) nm⟩)
    (fun ⟨_, mem, line⟩ =>
      match falls_below (s := (w, x)) grows nothing keeps pos high
          (fun _ u _ su => Nat.le_of_eq (one u su)) mem line with
      | ⟨n, le, _, low, _⟩ => ⟨n + 1, Nat.succ_le_succ le,
          gone_after closed fun u _ su => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le low (Nat.le_of_eq (one u su).symm)⟩)

end Whole

/-! ## 4. Laws of `LineOnAStock` as cases -/

section Cases

variable {need : U → Nat} {kids : U → List U} {use : U → Nat} {giving : Nat → Nat}
  {S : U → Prop} {hi : Nat}

/-- **`one_need_ends`, as a case.** The statement of `LineOnAStock.one_need_ends`, proved from
`takes_the_whole` at the line of every unit: an empty way ends at step 0; otherwise its first unit is
of the line, no unit is outside it, and the level being at least the one need at every step before
the end, every need is met until then and the way there is the generation (`way_while_met`).
`need_unmet` is not used. -/
theorem one_need_ends_as_case {n : Nat} (one : ∀ u, need u = n) (pos : 0 < n)
    (grows : ∀ u, use u < taken use (kids u)) (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) {w : List U} {x : Nat} (high : x ≤ hi) :
    ∃ m t, m ≤ hi + 1 ∧ «迭» (net need kids giving use) m (w, x) t ∧
      (∀ v, ¬ net need kids giving use t v) ∧ t.1 = generation kids m w :=
  match w with
  | [] => ⟨0, ([], x), Nat.zero_le _, (Law.stepWhile_steps_iff 0).mpr
      ⟨fun _ lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero _), rfl⟩,
      Law.stepWhile_none_iff.mpr (fun ⟨_, mu, _⟩ => nomatch mu), rfl⟩
  | _ :: w' =>
    match takes_the_whole (S := fun _ => True) (fun u _ => one u) pos
        (fun u _ => Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (grows u) (Nat.le_of_eq (lineTake_true _).symm))
        (fun _ ns => absurd trivial ns) nothing keeps high (.head w') trivial with
    | ⟨m, le, before, _, steps, stops, _⟩ => ⟨m, _, le, steps, stops,
        way_while_met fun j lj u _ => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (one u)) (before j lj)⟩

/-- With `closed` and `down`, a start way whose line needs at most `m` keeps it so at every step of
the course: `below_keeps`, step by step. -/
theorem below_along (closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u)
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) {m : Nat} {s : List U × Nat}
    (below : Below need S m s.1) : ∀ j, Below need S m (course need kids giving use s j).1
  | 0 => below
  | j + 1 => below_keeps closed down (below_along closed down below j) _

/-- **`ends_all`, as a case.** The statement of `LineOnAStock.ends_all`, proved by the same strong
induction with `falls_below` in the place of `ends_high`: from a configuration at most `hi` whose line
needs at most `m`, either no unit of the line is in the way, or (`falls_below`, the line needing at
most `m` along the course by `below_along`) some step has the level below `m`, from where the claim
below `m` applies (`ends_low`). `ends_high` is not used. -/
theorem ends_all_as_case [DecidablePred S] (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving)
    (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u)
    (grows : ∀ u, S u → use u < lineTake S use (kids u)) (needs : ∀ u, S u → 0 < need u)
    (down : ∀ u v, S u → v ∈ kids u → need v ≤ need u) (m : Nat) :
    Ends need kids giving use S hi m :=
  strong_below (P := Ends need kids giving use S hi)
    (fun _ ih s high below => (line_or_none (S := S) s.1).elim
      (fun nm => ⟨0, fun u mu su => absurd su (nm u mu)⟩)
      (fun ⟨u₀, mu₀, su₀⟩ =>
        match falls_below grows nothing keeps
            (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (needs u₀ su₀) (below u₀ mu₀ su₀)) high
            (below_along closed down below) mu₀ su₀ with
        | ⟨n, _, _, low, _⟩ =>
          match ends_low keeps closed down ih (course need kids giving use s n)
              (level_within keeps high n) low with
          | ⟨k, nm⟩ => ⟨n + k, Eq.subst (motive := NoneMet need S) (Law.orbit_add _ s n k).symm nm⟩))
    (m + 1) m (Nat.lt_succ_self m)

end Cases

/-- Four kinds (the paper record's `a`, `b`, `c` and `O`). -/
inductive Kind
  | A
  | B
  | C
  | O

open Kind

/-- `A`, `B` and `C` need seventeen, `O` one. -/
def need : Kind → Nat
  | A => 17
  | B => 17
  | C => 17
  | O => 1

/-- `A` takes one, `B` two, `C` three, `O` one. -/
def use : Kind → Nat
  | A => 1
  | B => 2
  | C => 3
  | O => 1

/-- `A` leaves `B`; `B` leaves `C`; `C` leaves four `A`; `O` leaves itself. -/
def kids : Kind → List Kind
  | A => [B]
  | B => [C]
  | C => [A, A, A, A]
  | O => [O]

/-- The units of the line: `A`, `B` and `C`. -/
def isLine : Kind → Bool
  | A => true
  | B => true
  | C => true
  | O => false

/-- Giving `min z (17 - z)`. -/
def giving (z : Nat) : Nat := min z (17 - z)

/-- No unit outside the line leaves a unit of it: `O` leaves `O`. -/
theorem closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → isLine v = true → isLine u = true :=
  fun u v mv sv => match u, v, mv, sv with
    | A, _, _, _ => rfl
    | B, _, _, _ => rfl
    | C, _, _, _ => rfl
    | O, _, .head _, sv => sv
    | O, _, .tail _ mm, _ => nomatch mm

/-- Every unit of the line needs seventeen. -/
theorem one : ∀ u, isLine u = true → need u = 17 :=
  fun u su => match u, su with
    | A, _ => rfl
    | B, _ => rfl
    | C, _ => rfl
    | O, su => absurd su Bool.false_ne_true

/-- Each unit of the line has kids of the line that together take more than it: two against one,
three against two, four against three. -/
theorem grows : ∀ u, isLine u = true → use u < lineTake (fun u => isLine u = true) use (kids u) :=
  fun u su => match u, su with
    | A, _ => by decide
    | B, _ => by decide
    | C, _ => by decide
    | O, su => absurd su Bool.false_ne_true

/-- Step 1 of the course from `([A, O], 17)`. -/
theorem at1 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 1 = ([B, O], 17) := rfl

/-- Step 2, from step 1 by one step. -/
theorem at2 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 2 = ([C, O], 17) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at1).trans rfl

/-- Step 3, from step 2 by one step. -/
theorem at3 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 3 = ([A, A, A, A, O], 17) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at2).trans rfl

/-- Step 4, from step 3 by one step. -/
theorem at4 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 4 = ([B, B, B, B, O], 17) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at3).trans rfl

/-- Step 5, from step 4 by one step: of the nine the way takes, eight are given back. -/
theorem at5 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 5 = ([C, C, C, C, O], 16) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at4).trans rfl

/-- Step 6, from step 5 by one step: sixteen does not meet the line's seventeen, and `O` is left. -/
theorem at6 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 6 = ([O], 6) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at5).trans rfl

/-- Step 7, from step 6 by one step. -/
theorem at7 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 7 = ([O], 10) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at6).trans rfl

/-- Step 8, from step 7 by one step. -/
theorem at8 : course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 8 = ([O], 17) :=
  (congrArg (step need kids giving use) at7).trans rfl

/-- **Kept countermodel: a unit outside the line that needs less than the line. Every other premise
of `takes_the_whole` holds, the line is gone from step 6, and the net never ends.** On
`min z (17 - z)` with `hi = 17` and `m = 17`, the line of `A`, `B`, `C`: nothing is given from
nothing; the step under no take keeps every level up to seventeen within seventeen; no unit outside
the line leaves a unit of it; every unit of the line needs seventeen, which is positive; each unit of
the line has kids of the line that together take more than it; the start seventeen is at most
seventeen; `A`, of the line, is in the start way `[A, O]`. The premise that every unit not of the line
needs at least seventeen fails: `O` needs one. By computation the course from `([A, O], 17)` is
`[B, O]`, `[C, O]`, `[A, A, A, A, O]`, `[B, B, B, B, O]` at seventeen, `[C, C, C, C, O]` at sixteen,
then `[O]` at six, at ten, and at seventeen from step 8 on, which the step leaves as it is. No unit of
the line is in the way from step 6 on (`gone_from`), and the net never ends (`unending_of_fixed`): the
line is gone and `O` goes on at a level back at seventeen. -/
theorem all_but_outside :
    RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving ∧ RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 17 ∧
    (∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → isLine v = true → isLine u = true) ∧
    (∀ u, isLine u = true → need u = 17) ∧ 0 < 17 ∧
    (∀ u, isLine u = true → use u < lineTake (fun u => isLine u = true) use (kids u)) ∧
    17 ≤ 17 ∧ A ∈ [A, O] ∧ isLine A = true ∧
    ¬ (∀ u, ¬ isLine u = true → 17 ≤ need u) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 1 = ([B, O], 17) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 2 = ([C, O], 17) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 3 = ([A, A, A, A, O], 17) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 4 = ([B, B, B, B, O], 17) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 5 = ([C, C, C, C, O], 16) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 6 = ([O], 6) ∧
    course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) 7 = ([O], 10) ∧
    (∀ k, course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) (8 + k) = ([O], 17)) ∧
    (∀ k, 6 ≤ k →
      ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) k).1 → ¬ isLine u = true) ∧
    «不穷» (net need kids giving use) «一» ([A, O], 17) :=
  ⟨rfl, RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl, closed, one, by decide, grows, Nat.le_refl 17, .head _, rfl,
   fun h => absurd (h O Bool.false_ne_true) (by decide),
   at1, at2, at3, at4, at5, at6, at7,
   fun k => (Law.orbit_add _ _ 8 k).trans
     ((congrArg (fun c => course need kids giving use c k) at8).trans (course_fixed rfl k)),
   gone_from closed (k := 6) (fun u mu su =>
     match u, (Eq.subst (motive := fun c : List Kind × Nat => u ∈ c.1) at6 mu : u ∈ [O]), su with
     | _, .head _, su => absurd su Bool.false_ne_true
     | _, .tail _ mm, _ => nomatch mm),
   unending_of_fixed (t := 8)
     (fun j lt => match j, lt with
       | 0, _ => ⟨A, .head _, by decide⟩
       | 1, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at1.symm ⟨O, .tail _ (.head _), by decide⟩
       | 2, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at2.symm ⟨O, .tail _ (.head _), by decide⟩
       | 3, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at3.symm ⟨A, .head _, by decide⟩
       | 4, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at4.symm ⟨B, .head _, by decide⟩
       | 5, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at5.symm
           ⟨O, .tail _ (.tail _ (.tail _ (.tail _ (.head _)))), by decide⟩
       | 6, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at6.symm ⟨O, .head _, by decide⟩
       | 7, _ => Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at7.symm ⟨O, .head _, by decide⟩
       | j + 8, lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_of_ge (Nat.le_add_left 8 j)))
     (Eq.subst (motive := fun c => step need kids giving use c = c) at8.symm rfl)
     (Eq.subst (motive := Goes need) at8.symm ⟨O, .head _, by decide⟩)⟩

/-- **On the same world, by `line_gone_within`:** the premises that law asks hold (it asks nothing of
`O`), so the line is gone from a step at most nineteen. By `all_but_outside` it is gone from step 6. -/
theorem gone_by_law :
    ∃ n, n ≤ 19 ∧ ∀ k, n ≤ k →
      ∀ u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use ([A, O], 17) k).1 → ¬ isLine u = true :=
  line_gone_within (need := need) (kids := kids) (use := use) (giving := giving)
    (S := fun u => isLine u = true) (hi := 17) (m := 17) closed one (by decide) grows rfl
    (RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl) (Nat.le_refl 17)

end OutsideNeedsLess

/-- **Kept countermodel for `one_need_ends`' last conjunct: every premise of `takes_the_whole` holds,

           absurd (goes 2 (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (by decide) (Nat.le_add_left 3 n))) (by decide),
   rfl⟩
