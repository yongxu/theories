## The rules (those of 22 November, `the-list-law-on-the-atlas-2026-11-22/FROZEN-THE-LIST-LAW-ON-THE-ATLAS.md.txt`, with what this case adds)

- **A dynamic of its own** only where the record describes a going-on that no dynamic of the store has: said by a reader who is shown the store's candidate dynamics whole and is not told this file's forecast. Otherwise its theorems are listed, where they belong, under dynamics that are there.
- **A listing.** A theorem is newly listed under a dynamic only where that reader, shown the dynamic's entry and the theorem's statement, judges that it states that dynamic's own notion and would belong on its list whatever it is a case of. A name, a namespace or a shared word is not enough. Private theorems, helpers and the parts of a built case that only compute are not listed.
- **A case row** sits on a law its dynamic lists and names exactly one declaration that Lean's print (`theories/becoming/results/atlas/proof-uses/`) shows in that law's proof term and that another dynamic lists; the statement must be a case of the law named (the law at an instance, or with a premise supplied), not only a use of it; its `how` names every link. The validator does not allow a case row to name a law of the same dynamic, so a law and its namesake under one dynamic get no row.
- **A record** is added to a dynamic's `records` only where at least one of its theorems is listed there by the rule above.
- Nothing is entered where the reader and the print disagree, or where the second reader refutes it. Every "not found" says where it was looked for.

## What would count against it

- A case row that is only a use: then the layer of uses between proofs already draws it at each render, and the row says more than the proof.
- A listing by name: the reader cannot quote from the dynamic's entry the notion the statement states.
- A dynamic made so that a module has a home.
- The store growing with nothing a reader of the map could not already get from the two records. What is claimed is small: that the store names the two modules where they belong, and that the join row GR's Queue clause names (`Sampling` with `Selection`) is drawn as a case link if, and only if, it is one.
