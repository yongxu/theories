# The lock and the turn as two requirements and two readings on one built process: the guesses, fixed before any Lean is written

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ), 21 November 2026, 19:48 WIB, before any agent ran and before any Lean was written for it. Frozen: nothing above the line "After" is changed later. Uniform policy, admissible, viable are the Lean's names; share, level, favour, hold, yield are working words. This is a built case in scratch, outside the package. It is not a model proposed for the package, and nothing is general in it unless a statement says so.*

## What we are doing

One map of how things come to be, go on, change and end, on which two ways of going on are placed with respect to each other. The idea, as the direction page has it: what a going-on rests on and what makes a difference to it need not be the same, and the gap between the two is where things run down unseen, or turn in time.

Four tries of this day looked for a bridge between two notions the Lean already has, and each came back as a dictionary or as two sides with no theorem between them (the direction page, "What four tries in one day say about how to go on"). The session's reading from them, which no report states: where a way may always turn, turning now keeps what it rests on, and no reading is needed for that. So the lock is not a failure to keep one guard by a plan. It is a way whose selecting is set from one thing, its share, while it rests on another, a level: two requirements and a reading.

The Lean has the terms for that already: a rule made from a reading that meets a requirement at every configuration (`GuardHorizon.UniformPolicy`), and a move admissible for keeping a guard some number of steps more (`GuardHorizon.Admissible`, `Viable`). This file fixes what is guessed when both requirements are put on one small process.

## The built process

A configuration is a share and a level, two numbers. There are two moves. To favour raises the share by one and lowers the level by one, stopping at zero. To hold leaves both as they are. The guard is that the level is not zero. Nothing gives to the level: it only runs down, and only under favouring.

Two readings of a configuration: the share alone; the share and the level.

Three requirements on a move at a configuration:

- **the selection's own**, for a threshold `c`: where the share is at least `c`, the move is to favour (a rule set from the share, as the lock's is);
- **keeping**, for a number of steps `n`: where the guard holds, the move is admissible for keeping it `n` steps more;
- **the selection yielding to the level**, for a line `m`: where the share is at least `c` and the level is at least `m`, the move is to favour; and keeping, as above.

## The guesses (each may be false)

- **T1.** The selection's own requirement is met by a rule made from the share alone, for every threshold.
- **T2.** Along the run of any rule that meets the selection's own requirement, from a start whose share is at least the threshold: the share rises at every step and stays at least the threshold, and the guard fails at the step equal to the start's level and not before. The requirement is met at every step of that run.
- **T3.** Keeping alone is met by a rule that reads nothing: always hold. So keeping needs no reading here.
- **T4.** The selection's own requirement and keeping together are met by no rule under either reading: at a configuration with the share at least the threshold and the level one, no move meets both. The conflict is between the requirements at a configuration, not inside a reading.
- **T5.** The yielding requirement, with the line at least two, is met by a rule made from the share and the level, and by no rule made from the share alone. With the line at one it is met by no rule under either reading: the yield comes too late. So it has a rule exactly where the reading registers the level and the line is at least two.
- **T6.** Along the run of a rule that meets the yielding requirement with the line at least two, from a start whose share is at least the threshold and whose level is at least the line: the share rises until the level is one below the line and then stays, and the guard holds at every step.
- **T7 (against the step).** All of this is arithmetic on a toy that restates the package's two built cases of a uniform policy (`statewise_not_uniform`, `discriminating_reading_uniform`) with numbers. Nothing in it is the lock's own model, and no law of the package does work in any proof.

## What would count as nothing having changed

- T2, T4 or T5 fails as worded and cannot be mended by a premise that the built process plainly meets.
- T7 holds in the judgement of a reader who took no part, asked: "Is this the direction's sentence stated on a built case, with the selection's requirement, what it rests on, a reading and a time all doing work, or is it the package's built cases of a uniform policy said again with numbers?"

## What it would be if it stands

A built case, not a law and not a join. It would be the first statement found in which the two ways are placed with respect to each other in one table, in the Lean's own terms: which requirement, under which reading, has a rule, and what the run of that rule does to the level. What it would still lack, to be said in the note: the level has no giving; the threshold and the line are handed in; nothing forms the yielding requirement from the selection's own; it is one process of two numbers; and it is not the package's lock (`RuleCapture`, `RuleShaping`), whose laws it does not use.

Whatever is found is told as what the Lean states, with every premise, and not as what makes a course better.
