## The built process

A configuration is a share and a level. To favour raises the share by one and lowers the level by one, stopping at zero. To hold leaves both. The guard is that the level is not zero. Nothing gives to the level. Two readings: the share alone; the share and the level. Three requirements on a move at a configuration, on the package's own `UniformPolicy`, `Admissible` and `Viable`, unchanged: the selection's own (where the share is at least a threshold `c`, favour); keeping (where the guard holds, the move is admissible for `n` steps more); and the selection yielding to the level from a line `m` (where the share is at least `c` and the level at least `m`, favour; and keeping). The reader: the definitions are the frozen file's, none bent.
