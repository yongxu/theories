## Definitions and what I chose

- **`next` (line 121) and `proc` (line 126).** A configuration is `Nat × Nat`, a share and a level. The move `true` favours: it leads to `(s.1 + 1, s.2 - 1)`, and since subtraction on `Nat` stops at zero, so does the level. The move `false` holds and leaves `s`. The process is `⟨fun s a t => t = next s a⟩`, the direct form, as in `Drain`.
- **`above` (line 129).** The guard is `s.2 ≠ 0`.
- **Readings.**
  - `shareRead s := s.1` (line 132): the share alone.
  - `bothRead s := s` (line 135): the share and the level.
  - For T1 and T3 I also used the reading into `Unit`, which keeps nothing.
- **`selReq c s a` (line 141).** Defined as `c ≤ s.1 → a = true`. It says nothing below the threshold.
- **`keepReq n s a` (line 145).** Defined as `above s → GuardHorizon.Admissible proc above n s a`.
  - I chose to restrict it to configurations where the guard holds. Without that restriction no rule could exist at all: at level 0 the guard fails for every horizon, both moves lead to level 0, and `UniformPolicy` asks the requirement at every configuration of `Nat × Nat`. The frozen wording "where the guard holds" says the same thing.
  - `Admissible` and `Viable` are used unchanged.
- **`yieldReq c m n s a` (line 150).** Defined as `(c ≤ s.1 → m ≤ s.2 → a = true) ∧ keepReq n s a`.
- **`orbit obs policy s k` (line 155).** The run of a rule: step 0 is `s`, and step `k+1` is `next (orbit k) (policy (obs (orbit k)))`.
- **Helpers.** `geq` (line 27) is `true` exactly where `c ≤ x` (`geq_complete`, `geq_sound`). `both` (line 55) is "and" with its four cases written out. The arithmetic facts are proved by direct recursion, with no `omega` or `simp`.
- **Moves that keep the guard.**
  - `closed_above` (line 164): the set `above` is `GuardHorizon.Closed` under holding.
  - `viable_above` (line 170): every configuration with the level not zero is `Viable` for every `n`. Calls `GuardHorizon.closed_viable`.
  - `hold_admissible` (line 176): holding is admissible wherever the level is not zero.
  - `favour_admissible` (line 183): favouring is admissible wherever the level is at least 2.
  - `favour_at_one` (line 190): at level 1, favouring is admissible for no `n`, whatever the share. Calls `GuardHorizon.viable_guard`.
  - `adm_of` (line 195): a move is admissible where the level is not zero if it favours only where the level is at least 2.
