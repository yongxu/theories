# The table of what the repository already has toward the two ways: columns and rows, fixed before any cell is filled

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row DJ). Fixed 20 November 2026, 13:27 WIB by the clock, at Ren's "Yes, the table". The table proves nothing. It places statements that exist, by eight properties, so that which combinations are there and which are not can be seen. Each cell is to be taken from a statement of the Lean (or, for a program, from its code and stored output), by two AI readers who do not see each other's cells; where they differ a third settles it from the statement. Unit, level, share, rule, giving are working names.*

## Columns

Each cell is **Y** (yes), **P** (in part) or **N** (no), with the declarations that carry it, their lines, and at most thirty words.

- **a. Multiplies.** Y: some things in the model leave offspring or grow in number by a law of the step. P: their number is a supplied list, a weight or a premise, or grows only in a built course. N: nothing multiplies.
- **b. A level that can run down.** Y: an amount that things in the model need or take from, and that can fall to where a need is unmet or the taking stops. P: an amount that only grows, or a count or loss that nothing needs. N: none.
- **c. Gives at a cost.** Y: something that rests on or takes from that level also gives to it, and a cost of the giving to the giver is in the statement. P: it gives, leaves or forgoes with no cost stated; or bears a cost, but what it gives does not go to a level. N: nothing gives.
- **d. The source is said.** Y: where what is given comes from is in the model (a supply, a source, a part of what the giver took). P: a renewal or a law of what is left is handed in, with no giver. N: from nothing, or not said.
- **e. What comes back.** Y: a statement says what comes back to the one that gives, keeps or holds back, or states a selection between those who do and those who do not. P: the two only stand side by side in built courses. N: neither.
- **f. A rule set again from a share.** Y: a rule or a selection is set again from the share or count of what it selects. P: a rule is revised some other way (a best response, a schedule, a reading handed in). N: no rule changes.
- **g. Both outcomes, with the boundary exact.** Y: going on and ending (or kept and lost, rising and falling) are both stated, with "exactly where", as a general law. P: two one-sided laws; or exact only at one step, in a built case or for supplied numbers. N: one outcome or none.
- **h. No return, or a turn.** Y: a statement of a step or a place after which no course among those open keeps what is asked, or of a turn that comes in time. P: in a built case only. N: none.

And for each row: **standing** (core: in the package with every axiom report empty; package: in the package otherwise; outside: Lean outside the package; scratch: Lean built once and not landed; program: not Lean), **reach** (a general law; a family with parameters; a built example; supplied numbers), and **told in** (chapter and section, if any).
