1. **MUST. Pages can give the wrong reason why a theorem has no row.** Files: `tools/research/atlas.py:2119-2123` (the section's "*No row in the print.*") and `atlas.py:2223-2228` (the terrain's "What the print was made from").
   - **What goes wrong.** When the print does not bind the receipt, every listed receipt theorem without a row is said to be "declared or changed after the print was made". That is false for a theorem whose module is not among `modules_behind`; for those the printer simply has no row. "Changed" is also wrong: a theorem whose proof changed still has a row.
   - **The bound case says nothing.** When the print binds, the same theorems get no word in their section at all. A section can then say "None found" while one of its listed theorems was never read. A6 asks the section to say so in both cases.
   - **How likely.** On the earlier list I count 10 such theorems, all in `RelationalRenewal`, so on the real print (which includes that module) it may be 0. It is still wrong behaviour.
   - **Fix, step 1.** In `_proof_uses`, keep each receipt declaration's module, in the same comprehension as `kinds`: `decl_module = {d['name']: m.get('module') ...}`.
   - **Fix, step 2.** In `_add_uses`, store it per dynamic: `u['no_row'] = {'in_modules_behind': [...], 'other': [...]}`, splitting `not_read[NOT_READ[0]]` by whether `decl_module[n] in behind`.
   - **Fix, step 3.** In `_uses_rows`, drop the `if b and` guard and write the two parts with different words:
     - for theorems in a module that is behind: "`X` is declared in a module changed since the print was made (declared or renamed after it): a use it makes is not shown, and its having no line says nothing."
     - for the rest: "`X` is a theorem of the core receipt that the print has no row for: no line can be computed for it, and its having no line says nothing."
   - **Fix, step 4.** Split the terrain's `stray` sentence in the same way.

2. **MUST. The terrain says less than the printer follows.** File: `atlas.py:2209-2211`.
   - **What goes wrong.** The sentence reads "The printer follows one call, and Lean's own auxiliary declarations under a theorem's name". The printer (its `ABOUT` at `proof_uses.py:92-95`, and `n.isPrefixOf u` at line 151) also counts as a theorem's own uses those of every theorem a person declared under its name. So a line "`u`, in `t`" can be true only of `t.sub`'s proof. The heading sentence "the proof of one theorem, as written, uses another" is then more than the line is, and the page does not say so.
   - **Second omission.** The print's own `ABOUT` warns that a structure field that is a proposition gets a row. A proof that uses such a field is listed as using a theorem, though what it uses is an assumption. The terrain does not carry this limit.
   - **Fix:** replace that clause with: "The printer follows one call, and every declaration under a theorem's own name (Lean's auxiliary ones, and any theorem a person declared under that name), counting what they use as the theorem's own; a field of a structure that is a proposition has a row, so a proof that takes it from the structure it is given shows as a use, though what it uses is an assumption and not a proved theorem; a use it does not follow is not shown, so where no line is, none was found".

3. **LESSER. The direct-use line puts the dynamic before the theorem.** File: `atlas.py:2139`.
   - **What goes wrong.** `From [Y]: \`u\`, in \`t\`` reads as a use coming from dynamic Y. A1 says every line is written theorem first, with the dynamic only as where the used theorem is listed. The words attacker's sample, which A8 adopted, has "From", so A1 and A8 disagree here.

5. **LESSER. A chain can be dropped although no line on the page shows its end.** File: `atlas.py:2014`.
   - **What goes wrong.** `shown` holds the ends of direct uses whatever dynamic they were shown under. Suppose `t1` (here) uses `u` directly and `u` is listed under y1 and y2, but `t1` is also listed under y2. The direct use then shows only for y1. A chain `t2 → … → u`, whose dynamics include y2, is still removed, so no line here shows `u` for y2. This is rare.
   - **Fix:** `shown = set(direct)` (the pairs `(y, u)`). Then filter by end and dynamic: keep `c` with `[y for y in c['dynamics'] if (y, c['chain'][-1]) not in shown]` as its dynamics, and drop it only when that list is empty.
