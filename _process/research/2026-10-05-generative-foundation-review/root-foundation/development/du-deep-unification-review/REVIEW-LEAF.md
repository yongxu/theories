# The leaf's review, and what was taken

One independent AI review of the first scratch version (22 theorems, 31 October 2026). It found 13 issues: none blocking, eight to fix, five notes. Each disposition below is in the revised leaf (9 theorems in 11 declarations, every axiom report empty).

1. **"Decided by the net" overclaimed.** The first version varied only the net, with the class fixed at the one, and on one net the class decides as well (`descent_no_outside`, `member_outside`). *Taken:* the pairs are now named `«续无外随网»` and `«族无外随网»`, and the docstring says that the condition varies with the class too.
2. **"Contingent as every such statement is" was false** (`«续无外则族无外»` is a theorem). *Taken:* the clause is removed.
3. **C's non-entailment was misworded.** A statement false everywhere entails C vacuously; the review asked to say "a frame", not "the root", and to say "fails at that datum for every order". *Taken:* the statement now reads "no statement reading no order, and holding at a datum with an extension, entails C".
4. **`reach_of_beyond`'s docstring** said "sublist" and omitted a hypothesis. *Taken.*
5. **The generic full-net and empty-net witnesses were immediate**, and models both ways already exist. *Taken:* they are dropped. The pairs are built from `LineOfDescent.Endless.descent_no_outside`, `TwoLines.other_line_outside` and `ThirdThing.fixed_class`. REVIEW.md §2's table row is corrected.
6. **`Comparison.contingent` repeated the existing witnesses.** *Taken:* it is dropped. COVERAGE keeps C's existing witnesses and adds only the non-entailment.
7. **`ReadsNoOrder` repeats `ReadsOnlyDynamics`' pattern.** *Taken in part:* it stays in the leaf, and its docstring now names the pattern. A generic definition below both would edit a low module, which is proposed for that module's next change.
8. **The L witnesses were deterministic**, so they never exercised responses with several outcomes, and they stood beside `LiftModel` instead of inside it. *Taken:* `LiftWitness` is dropped. `LiftModel.met_and_failed` is a non-vacuous meeting on `LiftModel`'s frame, its failing taken from `lift_needs_live_middle`. COVERAGE adds `some_outcome_does_not_lift` for the rule's substance.
9. **The helpers repeated existing lemmas.** *Taken:* `«及之传递»` and `ListTests.or_true_iff` replace the six helpers; the reviewer's compiled copy confirmed this.
10. **`increasing_iff_by_one`'s docstring said "C"** where the Lean is about `Increasing`. *Taken:* the docstring now says "being increasing in admission", and the module docstring gives the bridge to C on level paths (`Web.paths_extension_iff_widens`).
11. **Placement** (note). *Taken:* the docstring names the destination in `Becoming.Weightings`. The widening law cannot move up to the grammar, since without a finite listing `«广»` is not generated by one-item steps.
12. **Names** (note). `«全网»` and `«空网»` are gone with finding 5. `ReadsNoOrder` and `WidensByOne` are kept.
13. **J1–J4** (note). *Recorded* in REVIEW.md, as a proposal for the premise card.
