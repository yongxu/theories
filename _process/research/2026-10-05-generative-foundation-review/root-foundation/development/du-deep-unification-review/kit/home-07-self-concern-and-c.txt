**Theorem** (any type; root; Web) **and Conditional** (self-concern and the condition, on the root or held de se, as hypotheses). *Self-concern and C under the condition.*

An order is self-concerned, increasing in its own continuation (`Weighting.IncreasingInOwn`, [§4.4](#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks)), when it ranks above what it widens every widening that adds to the items its own continuation marks. Being increasing in admission (`Weighting.Increasing`) asks this of every widening.

- **Where the two coincide.** On a listing, every order is self-concerned exactly when it is increasing in admission if and only if the marking marks every listed item; a listed item left unmarked gives a self-concerned order that is not increasing in admission (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_marks`, `Weighting.exists_increasingInOwn_not_increasing`). Read from standpoints, with each standpoint's marking reading a relation, the two coincide from every standpoint, on every listing and for every order, exactly where that relation holds of every standpoint and item (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_all`).
- **Joined to the condition.** With the marking reading what the line under a class from the standpoint's center reaches along a relation, that totality is the condition on the root, `«续无外»` ([§4.5](#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root); `Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_noOutside`). With it reading the center's closure along a relation, it is the continuation condition held de se (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_unrestricted`, composing `withinClosure_iff_reached` and `ownClosure_unrestricted_iff_noOutside`). Both joins are near-definitional: they locate where the two coincide and prove no property of reach. One half of each also stands alone: where the condition holds they coincide (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_of_noOutside`, `Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_of_unrestricted`), and a listed item the center's line does not reach parts them (`Weighting.exists_increasingInOwn_not_increasing_of_outside`).
- **To C between listed level paths, one way.** With admission decided and what two level paths admit listed, an order increasing in admission ranks every extension between them, every claim counted as living (`Web.extension_ranked_of_increasing`). So where the de se condition holds, with each (step, formation) item marked by its formation, a self-concerned order ranks every extension between two such paths, as C (`Comparison`) asks there (`Web.extension_ranked_of_increasingInOwn`). Listing confines this to courses whose admissions end: a listed path admits nothing from some step on (`Web.admits_nothing_from_of_listed`), and once there is a formation no list holds what every level path admits (`Web.admits_need`, `Web.not_listsAdmitted_every`), so a listing of every level path leaves no extension (`Web.no_extension_of_listsAdmitted_every`).
- **On the nets.** Along 缘, self-concern and being increasing in admission coincide on the endless line of descent and part on two family lines with no condition between them, whose first birth reaches exactly its own family (`increasingInOwn_with_net`, `TwoLines.first_line_reach_iff`). Along the converse of 缘, the direction of the relations a self is drawn along in §4.1–4.3, they coincide in descent that ends and part on the endless line from every center (`increasingInOwn_with_net_converse`), as the condition does ([08 §7](08-METHOD.md#r-premises-met-and-failed)).

A finite listing, two-valued admission and an order not assumed complete or transitive; on a web, admission decided by a supplied reading and what both paths admit listed ([record](../results/records/self-concern-and-c-under-the-condition.md); [`Becoming.PremiseWitnesses`](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/PremiseWitnesses.lean)).

This result locates where self-concern gives what C asks, one way: where the continuation condition held de se holds, admission is decided, and the two courses compared are level paths whose admissions are listed, so that both admit nothing from some step on. It does not derive C. In general C alone orders, and self-concern and the condition both remain hypotheses; the condition varies with the net, the class and the direction. The ethics rests on its two premises with two roles, the continuation condition for scope and C for order.

**What is not shown:**
- C on all of a web's level paths from self-concern: the link holds between listed paths, courses whose admissions end, and a form asking every level path's admissions listed holds only vacuously (`Web.not_listsAdmitted_every`);
- the converse, that C on level paths makes an order increasing in admission on a listing: a course on a listing need not be what any level path admits;
- that self-concern fails to rank an extension on a web where the condition fails, which is proved on listings only.
