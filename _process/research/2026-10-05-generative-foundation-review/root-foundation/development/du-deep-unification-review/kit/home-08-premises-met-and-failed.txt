**Theorem** (root, on supplied nets; frames; any type) **and witnesses**. *Premises met and failed: the condition on the root, C and L.*

The premise card asks of each premise a model meeting it and one failing it, or a theorem that no statement of a stated kind entails it. For the condition's root form, C and L this is given here. Most of the models are the package's own (the line of descent of `Becoming.GrammarScope`, the nets of `Becoming.GrammarCases`, `LiftModel`); what is new is the pairs.

- **The condition on the root varies with the net.** At the one, the endless line of descent meets `«续无外»` from its first birth, and two family lines with no condition between them fail it from the first family's first birth (`«续无外随网»`, through `TwoLines.other_center_outside`). In families the line meets `«族无外»`, and a third thing apart from two joined families fails it (`«族无外随网»`, `ThirdThing.first_member_outside`). It varies with the direction too: along the converse of 缘, descent that ends meets it from its last occurrence and the endless line fails it from every center (`«逆缘续无外随网»`, through `LineOfDescent.Last.converse_no_outside` and `LineOfDescent.Endless.converse_outside`); along 缘 the endless line fails it from every later member's occurrences (`LineOfDescent.Endless.later_outside`). So no law that holds of every net settles either form.
- **A statement reading no order entails C only where it fails.** A statement about some data and an order reads no order where its truth does not change with the order (`ReadsNoOrder`). If such a statement entails C (`Comparison`) at a datum on a frame with an extension, it fails at that datum for every order (`ReadsNoOrder.not_entails`). This is immediate, since "better" is free: it shows that a frame fixes no order. It follows the pattern of `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails` for the condition's free form, with the order in the place of the significance.
- **L is met and failed** on `LiftModel`'s frame: from configuration 0 the move reaching 3 is lifted above the move reaching 1 by the larger configuration, and not the reverse (`LiftModel.met_and_failed`). That the choice of the rule is substantive is `LiftModel.some_outcome_does_not_lift`.
- **Widening on a listing is generated by widening by one item.** With equality of items decided, `a` widens `b` on the listed items exactly when a chain of widenings, each by one listed item alone, leads from `b` up to `a` (`Weighting.widensOn_iff_reach`). So an order transitive on courses is increasing in admission exactly when it ranks every widening by one item above what it widens (`Weighting.increasing_iff_by_one`).

Each net's relation, class and center are supplied. The frame and the order of C and L are free. Widening is over a finite listing with two-valued admission, decided equality of items being a modelling assumption ([record](../results/records/premises-met-and-failed.md); [`Becoming.PremiseWitnesses`](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/PremiseWitnesses.lean)).

**What is not shown:**

- that C or L is warranted or unwarranted: meeting and failing say what each asks of an order, and the ethics' two premises stay premises, the continuation condition for scope and C for order;
- widening over an infinite listing, or with equality of items undecided.
