# The centre sentence: four versions for Ren's read

*AI-written, 13 November 2026. None is applied. The sentence stands in AGENTS.md, whose direction changes only after Ren has read the change, and on the first screen of four fronts.*

## As it stands

> It is first a map for alignment: what a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run) reaches, what it drains beyond what it reads, how it locks on what it already chose. From it we want to design an alignment that keeps humans and AI, selecting together, out of instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor: a joint selection that reads beyond its own share and does not come to select for control over its own selection.

Sixty-eight words. A doer (a selection), a knower's verb (reads), a place (beyond), a self (its own) and control.

## 0. Short, in the frame of his words to the map session

On 13 November, about 11:35 WIB, Ren said to the map session (kept in `sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-08-representation-to-eight.md`): "They're just like dynamics within dynamics, or maybe just different dynamics that interact with each other … that God itself is a pattern that interact with other patterns. Instead of something transcendental. And there's absolutely no need for any dualism or hard separation with boundaries … I hope if there are something that's like wrong currently, those things can also be corrected so it does not affect future sessions to default back to the dualism."

The second paragraph of AGENTS.md is where sessions start. A short form in that frame, written by this session and read by no one else:

> It is first a map for alignment. A reward, a judge, a market, a vote or a training run is one dynamic among the dynamics it goes on through. Courses alike in what couples into it are selected alike, so what it goes on through can fall while nothing in it differs; and where it is set from the share it has favoured, it stays as it was until that gives out, unless the fall comes to make a difference in time. From this we want to design an alignment in which what humans and AI select through together stays coupled, in time, to what they go on through, and is not shaped only by what comes back to those who shape it.

About 115 words. It keeps what version 1 proves and drops the optimum's clause, which the one page can carry. "It" is the selecting, said as one dynamic among others and not as a doer over a beyond. It still has "selected", "favoured" and "we … design".

## 1. From the theorems' own statements, with the Lean check folded in

Written by the session that ran the scan, then corrected clause by clause by the turning-in-time session against `SelectsThrough`, the optimum theorems (`least_iff`, `least_of_room`), the lock's theorems and `TurningInTime`. Read by those two sessions and no one else.

> It is first a map for alignment. Where courses are selected, by a reward, a judge, a market, a vote or a training run, through what couples into the selecting, courses alike in that are selected alike. So a condition they all go on through can fall, until it first changes which go on, while nothing in the selecting differs. Where the selecting is at an optimum of an order rising with what is selected, and whatever stands higher in that condition is bettered by something eligible, the condition is at the least any eligible course leaves it. Where the criterion is set from the share alone and already favours the share that sets it, it stays so and neither falls; the courses then end where the condition gives out, unless its fall comes to make a difference while what is left still holds. From this we want to design an alignment in which what humans and AI together select through stays coupled, in time, to what they go on through, and in which shaping the criterion does not rise by what comes back to those who shape it.

About 180 words. Each clause is a theorem's statement with its premise. It names no doer and no beyond. It still has "selected", "criterion", "favours", "bettered" and "we … design". It is exact and heavy: a reader meets four conditionals before the aim.

## 2. The same, shorter, as first drafted

> It is first a map for alignment. Where courses are selected, by a reward, a judge, a market, a vote or a training run, which of them go on depends only on the differences that couple into the selecting: courses alike in those go on alike. So a condition they all go on through can fall while nothing in the selecting differs. Where the selecting is carried to its best, that condition falls as far as any course lets it. And where the criterion is set from the share that went on under it, the same share comes back, and the fall makes no difference. From this we want to design an alignment in which what humans and AI together let go on stays coupled to more than the share already favoured, and in which shaping the criterion does not rise by what comes back to those who shape it.

About 140 words. Lighter, and in four places it says more than the theorems (the note lists them): it gives a definition's hypothesis as a result, drops "until it first changes which go on", drops the optimum's premise, and overstates the lock. It also leaves time out of the aim.

## 3. The earlier attempt, of 12 November

By the turning-in-time session ([its page](../tt-turning-in-time/drafts/FOR-RENS-READ.md)), which names it as the attempt that kept the shape:

> It is first a map for alignment: which differences make a difference to a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run), which conditions it depends on and uses up while nothing in it differs, and how, renewed from what its own choosing brings back, it goes on as it was. From it we want to design an alignment that keeps humans and AI, selecting together, out of instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor: a joint selection to which what it depends on can make a difference while it still stands, and which does not come to be renewed from its own choices alone.

About 100 words, close to the present length. "A selection" is still the one thing at the centre, with "its own choosing" and "its own choices alone".

## What the choice is

Version 0 is the one short enough for AGENTS.md and in his own frame; it has not been checked against the Lean clause by clause as version 1 was. Version 1 is the only one checked, and it says nothing the theorems do not. It is too long for a second paragraph of AGENTS.md as it stands. Two ways to use it: keep its first two sentences and its aim in AGENTS.md and let the one page carry all of it; or let AGENTS.md say the centre as chapter 02 §11 now does ("a line that stays alike in a respect ends where the net ends, or ends while the net goes on") and give the selection as its first case. Either is for Ren's read.
