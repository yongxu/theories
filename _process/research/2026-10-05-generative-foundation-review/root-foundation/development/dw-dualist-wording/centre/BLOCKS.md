# The blocks

*As landed in the English on branch dw-centre, 13 November 2026, after the turning-in-time session's Lean check, the coordinator's reading against Ren's words, four cold readers and three second readers against the chapters.*

Two relations are kept apart by two phrases: what **makes a difference to** a selection (`DependsOnlyOn`, `AlikeThroughUpTo`: it couples in) and what **sustains** it (what its going on needs). Whether the need is met always makes a difference; how much is left above the need can make none.

- **C1.** A selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run) is one dynamic among others, some of which sustain it.
- **C2.** Two courses that differ in nothing that makes a difference to the selection are selected alike. How much is left of what sustains the selection can be among what makes no difference to it; then the selection can use that up while nothing in it yet differs.
- **C3.** And where each step sets the selection again only from the share it already favours, the selection goes on the same way until what sustains it gives out, unless that running down makes a difference to the selection early enough.
- **A.** From this map we want to design an alignment in which what sustains humans and AI keeps making a difference, early enough, to how they select together, and in which shaping how they select does not become what they select for.
- **N.** Instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor name what happens when this does not hold. (On the pages: "a correspondence and not a theorem".)
- **T** (one-line descriptions). the differences that make a difference to a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run), how what sustains it can run down while that makes no difference to it, and how it can go on the same way until that gives out, or turn early enough
- **O** (the optimum). where a selection is at its best that ranks an outcome above another whenever the first is no lower on anything that makes a difference to it and higher on something, what makes no difference to it stands as low as any allowed outcome leaves it, given room (wherever an allowed outcome leaves it higher than another does, some allowed outcome is no lower than that one on anything that makes a difference and higher on something)
- **M.** Where what a selection raises comes to set the selection, the loop is what the work calls the meta-attractor.
- **L** (closed form). Its closed form, in which what is selected next and the rule (how the selection selects at a step) as each step sets it again both follow from the share and the rule alone, how the rule is set again being formed from the share too, so that a loss makes a difference to neither, can lock (02 §9) under three conditions: selection does not lower the favoured share; a share not lower sets again a rule not lower; and a rule not lower than a favouring one still favours. Then a course once locked stays locked whatever the loss, and if the loss grows by at least one unit each step, it passes every bound and the lock holds.
- **L-short.** Its closed form, where what is selected next and the rule (how the selection selects at a step) as each step sets it again follow from the share and the rule alone, how the rule is set again being formed from the share too, can lock: on stated models, where selection does not lower the favoured share, a share not lower sets again a rule not lower, and a rule not lower than a favouring one still favours, a locked start stays locked however large a loss grows that makes no difference to either (02 §9).
- **G.** Read on the map, it asks two things. Where shaping the rule (how they select) rises by what comes back to those who shape it, alone or through the groups they are in (02 §9), something other than their share still makes a difference to the rule, so that a loss that makes no difference to their share can turn it. And a lock, once formed, gives way early enough: something other than the share comes to make a difference to the rule before the loss passes its point of no return. Where both fail, the meta-attractor has closed and holds.
