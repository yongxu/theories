## 2. The five candidates

The three questions for each candidate:
- **Q1:** does the law forbid an outcome, or can its premises only be measured through that outcome?
- **Q2:** can existing data decide it at no cost?
- **Q3:** does a rival predict differently?

The laws named are proved in the core with empty axiom reports. `LineageRegimes`, `WindowedWaiting` and `PairSelection` are at receipt 20261101T024359.312453Z.

**A. A share rising while the stock falls (EL1).** Laws: `SelectionShift.Generations.{next_of_selection, last_term_zero, never_empties_of_unbounded, goes_on}`, `Horizon.two_fates_of_nonempty`, `Shrinking.positive_then_empty`, `LineageRegions.Courses.positive_but_last`.
- **Q1.** It forbids two things, and both are counting identities:
  - a positive selection term at a step whose next generation is empty, since there every offspring number is zero;
  - an empty generation after a positive term.

  `never_empties_of_unbounded` needs unboundedly many steps, and no finite record has them. `goes_on` needs exact transmission at every step, and `ExactOnce.exact_once_ends` shows exactness once is not enough. An apparent violation would just be a miscount.
- **Q2.** The data are consistent but decide nothing. In Sanchez and Gore 2013, small yeast populations collapsed while cooperator frequency kept rising, which is the pattern `positive_but_last` witnesses. In Fiegna and Velicer 2003, one fate of *M. xanthus* cheater mixes was extinction of the whole population.
- **Q3.** The rival predicts the same and forbids more. The evolutionary-suicide literature covers this (Matsuda and Abrams 1994; Gyllenberg and Parvinen 2001; Webb 2003):
  - under its assumptions, suicide needs a discontinuous transition to extinction;
  - suicide is impossible in Gurtin–MacCamy age-structured models;
  - Webb classifies the modes: sudden, or gradual with added stochasticity.

  EL1's witness fits both modes and classifies neither.
- **Defeater:** an EL1 law whose forbidden outcome is not an identity. There is none in the index entries for `SelectionShift` and `LineageRegions`, or in their record.
