- **The histories.** `sampled N f (k + 1) h` sums, over the samplings `w` of `N` draws from `h` of the first kind and `N - h` of the second (weight `weight h (N - h) w`), the weight times `sampled N f k (ones w)`: the next generation starts from the sampled count. It is linear in the reading (`sampled_add`, `sampled_mul`) and monotone in it on the counts a population can have (`sampled_le`, since a sampling of `N` draws has at most `N` of the first kind, `ones_le`).
- **The count kept.** One generation: `N` times `Σ weight · ones` is `N · h · N^N` (`expected_ones` with `h + (N - h) = N`), so `Σ weight · ones = h N^N` (`weighted_ones`, cancelling `N`; `N = 0` checked directly). Over `k + 1` generations, the inner total is `ones w · (N^N)^k` by induction, so the outer is `h N^N (N^N)^k`.
- **Absorbing ends.** From none, `weight 0 N` vanishes on every sampling with a draw of the first kind, so the total is `N^N` times the reading at none (`from_none`); from all, `N - N = 0` (`sub_self_zero`) and likewise (`from_all`).
- **Variation ends.** `varied N c` is one where `0 < c < N`. From varied `h`, each sampling's weight times the varied histories after it, with its weight times the bound `M` where it loses (`lost`) or fixes (`whole`) the first kind, is at most its weight times `M`, since after losing or fixing nothing is varied (`varied_step`). Summed: the varied histories with `((N - h)^N + h^N) M` are at most `N^N M = (r + 2) M`, and each power is at least one (`one_le_pow`), so the varied histories are at most `r M`.
- **Fixation.** For counts `c ≤ N`, `N · fixed c ≤ c ≤ N · fixed c + N · varied c` (`fixed_le`, `count_le`); sum through `sampled` and use `sampled_count` and `varied_vanishes`.
- **Witness.** `TwoMembers`: `N = 2`, `h = 1`: the four samplings weigh one each; one fixes, one loses, two stay varied; `2 · 1 ≤ 1 · 4 ≤ 2 · 1 + 2 · 2` (`r = 2`).

**Self-review.** The fixation share `h/N` with no selection is Kimura's neutral fixation probability (Kimura 1962; it follows from the count being a martingale and the ends absorbing), here stated as counts of histories with an explicit geometric bound on what stays varied. The bound's rate `(N^N − 2)/N^N` is far from the true one (the heterozygosity falls by `1 − 1/N` per generation), but the statement needs only that the varied share vanishes, and it does with no premise beyond `N ≥ 2`. The linearity lemmas make `sampled` a positive linear functional over histories, so the two inequalities are pointwise inequalities on counts summed. What is open is drift with selection in one draw, where the count is not kept on average and the fixation share is no longer the starting share.
