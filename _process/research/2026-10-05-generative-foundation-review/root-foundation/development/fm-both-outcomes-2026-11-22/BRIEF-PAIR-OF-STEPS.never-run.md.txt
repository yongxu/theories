### Module 1: `Becoming.ScratchPair` (imports `Becoming.PredicateIteration` only), namespace `Becoming.Iteration`

For two steps `Φ Ψ : (S → Prop) → S → Prop` on one type and two starts `V₀ W₀`:

```lean
/-- Wherever every point is in X or in Y, every point is in the step of X by Φ or in the step
of Y by Ψ. -/
abbrev OneOrOther (Φ Ψ : (S → Prop) → S → Prop) : Prop :=
  ∀ X Y : S → Prop, (∀ t, X t ∨ Y t) → ∀ z, Φ X z ∨ Ψ Y z

/-- Wherever no point is in both X and Y, no point is in both the step of X by Φ and the step of
Y by Ψ. -/
abbrev NeverBoth (Φ Ψ : (S → Prop) → S → Prop) : Prop :=
  ∀ X Y : S → Prop, (∀ t, ¬ (X t ∧ Y t)) → ∀ z, ¬ (Φ X z ∧ Ψ Y z)
```

(names are yours to change if they clash; check the package for clashes first.)

1. `iter_or`: from `∀ z, V₀ z ∨ W₀ z` and `OneOrOther Φ Ψ`: `∀ n z, iter Φ V₀ n z ∨ iter Ψ W₀ n z`.
