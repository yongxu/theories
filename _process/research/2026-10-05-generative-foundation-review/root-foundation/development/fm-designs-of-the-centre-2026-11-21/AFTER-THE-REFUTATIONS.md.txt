## The result

**No design is the centre, and none is a law that joins.** Fifteen refuters, one verdict in different words: each design survives, if at all, as a built case worth keeping with one or two small lemmas. My own reading of the eight (`READING-OF-THE-EIGHT.md`, written before the refuters ran and not shown to them) proposed a centre in two layers. Both layers are refuted as that:

- **Layer 1, the comparison law.** True, constructive, needs no premise beyond itself, and not in the package in that generality (two refuters, by text scan of the declarations). But in each use the design made of it the work is in the local condition, which is the old content: with a one-point process it unfolds to `Closed` and repeats `closed_viable`'s induction; with a clock it is a ranking function, the form needed is of endpoints and not of keeping, and the new chain would be about 90 lines against about 31 (an estimate); on one process under a preorder it is a step that keeps an order, iterated. "The three uses meet nowhere but a six-line induction."
- **Layer 2, the web with a finite source and a cost.** Its third case (kept only with kinds coming and going) is real and survives tokens that last two or three steps, by more than one mechanism. But it is carried by which kinds are named as the formation: in all 97 such laws found, the named kinds leave out a kind they rest on, and that kind stands by itself, the same at every step. With every kind named the case does not occur (an argument on paper, 0 failures on 448,258 laws). "Closing" can be deleted from every statement. The step of no return is not one: an open course from that step brings the formation back. Nothing is used up.

## What is kept (each a built case or a small lemma, none read by a person)

- **No return at a full level.** In three built cases the step past return is at full stock: what is lost is who gives. Confirmed by blind rebuild in two (the six-configuration machine; the race between level and menu). No law of the package was found whose point of no return is not a low level (one refuter's question; its answer is in `refute/E-work-opus/RETURN.md`).
- **Compulsory change needs both a finite cap and a cost** (in the web model, at the grain of the law; argued, searched, not in Lean), and is a gap between a fractional standing population and a whole one, closed by taking turns.
- **Where the lock comes about in a model (a kind that needs its own act), compulsory change cannot be**: the two exclude each other there.
- **Selecting among futures does not preclude widening.** A course that gives up one possible future at its first choice and then gains one more kept guard at every step for ever (refuting a design's negative reading). With kinds without end, each formed from the one before, the only keeping course keeps moving to a fresh kind.
- **Where one move dominates, no reading does work.** In four designs a rule that registers nothing keeps everything any course keeps (ties to the giver; the largest allowance; every affordable reading; a dominating move). The built cases where a reading does work are the ones with no dominating move.
- Small lemmas not found in what was read: the pullback of the largest closed region along a reading; the core of a step as that law on pairs; the fill bound (doubling against slots); "under free choice, what is kept at a level is kept at every higher level"; a standing population from a course (the minimum over a tail).

## What it teaches about how to go on (mine, held loosely)

1. **Designing before the whole Lean is known repeats what is there.** Seven of eight designers, and I, proposed as new what the package states in general. The listing of places covers 158 modules, by the questions of four reads. A read of all 406 by the questions this round raised is owed before another design: which places have moves, a reading, a share, a level that runs down, a giving that is a going-on, a cost to the keeper, an irreversibility that comes about, both outcomes, a comparison, a rank.
2. **Every test of "does the law do work" was passed by the built cases and failed by the general statements.** What survived every refuter is concrete: machines in which every part was tested by removal. That suggests going from the cases up: put the three or four surviving built cases side by side and ask what the least statement is that all of them are cases of, with the package's existing general laws (the rank, the order-keeping threshold, covering) as the tools, and not a new model.
3. **The comparison is worth landing only as a removal.** If one law makes `closed_viable`, the three orbit lemmas, `Covers.ensures` and the forward half of `Bisimulation.trace` its cases, the package says one induction once and not six times. That is rule 4 (each thing said once), not the centre. The scratch module will show whether it comes out.
