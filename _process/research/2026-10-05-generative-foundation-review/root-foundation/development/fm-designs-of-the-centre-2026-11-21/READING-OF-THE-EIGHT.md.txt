## What I see across them (my reading, to be attacked)

1. **Every design that got anywhere used a comparison.** C: a preorder with a one-sided simulation. D: `Dominates` (every move from below is matched by the top move from above). G1: Lemma 1 (keepable is upward closed in the level under free choice). B: the kept set is an up-set, and moves are closed under min, sum or max. G2: the full repertoire dominates every restricted one. A: kept levels of the others alone are upward closed. None of them saw the others. The package has bisimulation on frames and no law that carries keeping from one process to another (the DJ session's search, by name).

2. **F's block 1 names why five joins were dictionaries.** The largest-closed-region law gives what is kept. The running down is a rank that falls: a least region. The package's keeping side is all the first, its lock-and-level side all the second, and one cannot be unfolded into the other. What they share is comparison: a course that goes on is one a loop is carried into; a course that ends is one carried into a clock. C's two derivations are exactly these two: `closed_viable` by the one-point loop, `locked_ends_rising` by a clock.

3. **The step of no return is not a low level.** E: past return at full stock. G1: the level at the menu's exit is at the cap in 3,906 courses. B: holding both partners gives the ground's renewing act away. A: the setters' extinction. What is lost is *who gives*, and it is lost by a selecting that forms only from what is there. This is the meta-attractor's content (a selection removing what it rests on) inside a model, and it answers T6 better than any order on levels.

4. **"Closing" is the lock, said without a share.** B's `Closing` (forms no kind absent), G1's `F`-first (the menu only narrows), E's incumbent priority, A's allowed-from-share: in each, what can be selected next is set from what was selected. A share is one way of doing that. B and G1 derive the irreversibility from it in one line; A derives it from the share by `CarrierCounts.raises`.

5. **B's third case is the nearest thing to his sentence.** "It is also not holding exactly the same, dynamics, forms changes over time, but the continuation may continue": in case (iii) no population can stand, every course that keeps the formation has each of its kinds absent infinitely often and forms an absent kind infinitely often, and every selection that only re-forms what is there ends. And it needs both a finite source and a cost: free support, or no cap, and holding still is always possible. The second way there is not remaking; it is forming what is not there. What it lacks: it is rare and of one mechanism in what was searched; it may be an artefact of tokens lasting one step; "more and more future" (new kinds) is not in it.

## A candidate for the centre, in two layers (mine, untested)

- **Layer 1, the law that joins.** Keeping for n steps is carried along a one-sided comparison of guarded processes. Its two simplest instances are the loop and the clock. Cases to come out by a call: `GuardHorizon.closed_viable`, `Keeping.keptFor_of_closed` (through `KeptFor` as `Viable` on classes, which I withdrew as a dictionary on its own), `TurningInTime.locked_ends_rising`, the upward closure of what is kept (G1's Lemma 1, B's K1). If this holds in Lean with old statements unchanged, it is a law between two processes with a case on each side.
- **Layer 2, the statement with both outcomes.** On a web with a finite source and a cost: a selecting that forms only what is present (closing) keeps a formation exactly where it reaches a configuration above one that stands; where none stands and the formation can still be kept, it is kept only by forming what is absent, and every closing selection passes a step of no return, which can be at a full level. E's machine is the smallest built case with a reading in it.

What would make this one more model beside the others: layer 1 turning out to be `closed_viable` generalised with nothing crossing; layer 2's third case being an artefact of the one-step tokens; or the two layers having nothing to do with each other (layer 1 used nowhere in layer 2's proofs).
