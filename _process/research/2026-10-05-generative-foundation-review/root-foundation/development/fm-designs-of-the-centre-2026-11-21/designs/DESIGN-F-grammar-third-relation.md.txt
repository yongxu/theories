# Design F: the grammar's law of the largest closed region, at the relations a selection brings

*AI-written (Claude Fable 5.2, one of eight designers, key F-grammar-third-relation), 21 November 2026. Paper and Python only: no Lean was run, nothing is proved, nothing outside this folder was written. Way, share, level, reading, twin are working names. Every negative about the Lean is "not found in what I read".*

**The short answer to the angle.** A relation exists at which "locked" and "kept" are the same law, and it comes in three forms that are one by a small theorem. But the guess that the two ways are the inside and the outside of that law's largest closed region is false: in the built family it holds for 0 of 1,298 ways tried, and on all frames of four configurations it fails in 203,180 of 301,440. What blocks it is said exactly in part 3 (four blocks, each with an instance). What is gained is three links and one statement about first steps; by my own judgment that is links, not a centre.

## 1. The design in ten lines

1. **Configurations**: any carrier `S`. No level, share or unit is in the statement; they appear only in the built family used to test it.
2. **A step**: a relation `N` on `S`, the course's step under the way of selecting (a function where one course is spoken of). The end is in the step: a region `G` (what it rests on is still there) whose outside is closed under `N`.
3. **What is registered**: a map `read : S → O`; `E` is its sameness (a class in the grammar's sense).
4. **The way of selecting** is `N` itself, where `N` is a rule from the reading: `N s = step s (ρ (read s))`.
5. **The one law** (already in the package): `K R P := «承» («逆» («所闭» R)) P`, the largest region inside `P` closed under `R`.
6. **Its relations here**: `N` (kept: `K N G`); `E` (the common response); `E ∪ N`, the reading and the step together (what holds of everything read alike with anything on the way); `N ⊗ N` on pairs, with `E` as the region asked for (never read apart).
7. **Link 1 (pullback)**: `K (E ∪ N) P` is `K (像 read N) (classes wholly inside P)` read back along `read`: the law at the reading's own grain.
8. **Link 2**: `«同行» R E` is `«闭» (R ⊗ R) E`; for a fixed successor the root's core `«核» R E` is the law at `R ⊗ R`.
9. **Main statement**: a course either goes on, or enters `X := K (E ∪ N) (not kept)` at one first step `n_T` and never leaves it; every twin that goes on is read apart from it by a step `n_sep ≤ n_T`; and `n_T ≤ n_end` where the end is registered.
10. **And exactly when the two ways are inside and outside**: where nothing locked goes on, `G ⊆ Λ ∪ K (E ∪ N) G` exactly where `G \ Λ` is closed under `E ∪ N`; otherwise a step enters the lock, or a twin is outside `G`, or a step leaves `G` outside the lock.

## 2. Definitions

Existing (`Grammar.lean`, `GrammarLaws.lean`, `CommonResponse.lean`): `«际»`, `«逆»`, `«闭»`, `«所闭»`, `«承»`, `«像»`, `«同行»`, `«核»`, `«续»`, `«有后»`, `«不穷»`, `«类»`.

```
-- existing, as an abbreviation only
K (R : «际» α) (P : α → Prop) : α → Prop := «承» («逆» («所闭» R)) P      -- ∀ t, «所闭» R s t → P t

-- new 1: two relations together
«兼» (R S : «际» α) : «际» α := fun x y => R x y ∨ S x y

-- new 2: a relation run on two occurrences at once
«双» (R : «际» α) : «际» (α × α) := fun p q => R p.1 q.1 ∧ R p.2 q.2

-- new 3: a relation read as a region of pairs
«域» (E : «际» α) : α × α → Prop := fun p => E p.1 p.2
```

A **frame** is `(S, N, read, G)` with `N : S → S`, `read : S → O`, `G : S → Prop`; `E s s' := read s = read s'`; `T := «兼» E (Law.step N)`.

- `Kept := K (Law.step N) G` (the course goes on).
- `X := K T (fun s => ¬ Kept s)` (closed at the reading's grain: nothing read alike with anything on the way goes on).
- `KeptAtGrain := K T G`.
- For a region `F` closed under `E` (the selection goes on as before, or has ended): `Λ F := K T F`.
- `n_end s`: the least `n` with `¬ G (orbit N s n)`. `n_T s`: the least `n` with `X (orbit N s n)`. `n_sep s s'`: the least `n` with `read (orbit N s n) ≠ read (orbit N s' n)`.
- A **twin** of `s` is an `s'` with `read s' = read s`.

With moves (`step : S → A → S`, a rule `ρ : O → A`): `N_ρ s := step s (ρ (read s))`.

## 3. The main statement, premises, proof sketches, and what blocks the guess

### Link 1 (pullback). No premise.

```
theorem «读之拉回» (read : α → κ) (N : «际» α) (P : α → Prop) (s : α) :
  K («兼» (fun x y => read x = read y) N) P s ↔
  K («像» read N) (fun k => ∀ x, read x = k → P x) (read s)
```

*Sketch.* (←) `Q s := K (像 read N) P∀ (read s)` lies inside `P` (`«所闭之承在内»`), is closed under `E` (same reading) and under `N` (a step of `N` is a step of the image, and the right side is closed by `«所闭之承闭»`); so it lies in the left side by `«闭在内则在所闭之承»`. (→) `R̄ k := ∃ s, read s = k ∧ K T P s` lies inside `P∀` (the left side is closed under `E` and inside `P`) and is closed under `像 read N` (from `read x = k`, `N x u`: `x` is in by closure under `E`, `u` by closure under `N`); so it lies in the right side by `«闭在内则在所闭之承»` again. The largest-region law is called twice, at two relations; nothing follows by unfolding. For `P` closed under `E` the composite "alike, then a step" gives the same region: `K (E;N) P = K (E ∪ N) P` (0 differences); for `P` not closed under `E` it need not (39,165 differences among the triples checked).

### Link 2 (never read apart). 

```
theorem «同行即双之闭» : «同行» R E ↔ «闭» («双» R) («域» E)                      -- by «同行之逐点»
theorem «双之承最大»   : K («双» R) («域» E) is the largest relation inside E that R goes alike across
theorem «定后则核即双之承» (fixed : «定后» R) (symm : «则» E («逆» E)) :
  «核» R E u u' ↔ K («双» R) («域» (fun x y => E x y ∧ ((∃ v, R x v) ↔ (∃ v, R y v)))) (u, u')
```

*Sketch of the third.* With a fixed successor, `«有常» R S` for symmetric `S` says: of two related occurrences both have a next or neither, and the nexts are related; that is closure of `S` under `«双» R` inside "both or neither has a next". The largest such inside `E` is the right side. For a law (every occurrence has a next) the extra clause is empty and `«核» R E = K («双» R) («域» E)`. Off a fixed successor the two differ: where every occurrence has a next, `K («双» R) («域» E)` lies inside the core and can be strictly inside (the core asks for some matching next, the paired step for all pairs of nexts), as kept-whatever lies inside kept-by-some-course.

### Main statement (one course, and where the two ways lie)

**Premises.** (p1) `N` is a function (*structural*). (p2) `E` is the sameness of a map (*structural*). (p3) the outside of `G` is closed under `N`: what has ended stays ended (*structural*). (p4) the outside of `G` is closed under `E`: nothing that has ended is read alike with something that has not (*structural, and it does work*: without it clause (c) fails). For part (B): (p5) `F` closed under `E` (*structural*: how it selects is a function of what it registers); (p6) `Λ F ∩ Kept = ∅`, nothing locked goes on (***the thing itself***: see block 1).

**(A) Dichotomy with first steps.** For every `s` with `G s`, exactly one holds:

- (I) `Kept s`: the course goes on, and no configuration of it is in `X`.
- (II) the course ends, and:
  - (a) *[no premise beyond p1, p2]* for every twin `s'` of `s` with `Kept s'`: `n_sep s s' ≤ n_T s` (if the two are never read apart, the course never enters `X`);
  - (b) once in `X` the course stays in `X`, and so does everything read alike with anything on it;
  - (c) *[p3, p4]* `n_T s ≤ n_end s`: it enters `X` no later than it ends; so with (a), every twin that goes on is read apart from it at some step `≤ n_T s ≤ n_end s`.

**(B) Exactly when the two ways are the inside and the outside.** Under p5 and p6: `G ⊆ Λ F ∪ KeptAtGrain` ⟺ `G \ Λ F` is closed under `T`; and then `Kept = KeptAtGrain = G \ Λ F`. Where it fails there is `s` in `G \ Λ F` with (d1) `N s ∈ Λ F` (the lock comes about on a course), or (d2) a twin of `s` outside `G` (what it rests on is not registered), or (d3) `N s ∉ G`.

**(C) A rule chosen from the reading.** For moves `step`, a reading and `G`: the family `{ K (E ∪ N_ρ) G : ρ }` has a largest member, reached by one rule; the family `{ K N_ρ G : ρ }` need not.

*Sketch of (A).* `Kept` is closed under `N` and inside `G`; `X` is closed under `E` and `N` and inside the complement of `Kept` (the three clauses of `«所闭之承最大»` at `T`). (I) and (II) exclude each other since `Kept` is closed under `N`. (a): for `n < n_sep`, the two courses are read alike at `n`; the twin's configuration is kept; were `s`'s in `X`, closure of `X` under `E` would put a kept configuration in `X`. (c): the outside of `G` is closed under `T` by p3, p4 and is inside the complement of `Kept`, so it lies in `X` (largest); the course is outside `G` at `n_end`. That a least `n_T` exists needs the usual decision along the course (`first_of_decided` in the package). Exhaustiveness of (I)/(II) is classical.

*Sketch of (B).* (⇐) `G \ Λ` closed under `T` and inside `G` lies in `KeptAtGrain` (largest). (⇒) by p6 and `KeptAtGrain ⊆ Kept`, `Λ` and `KeptAtGrain` are disjoint, so `G \ Λ = KeptAtGrain`, which is closed. The three kinds: a `T`-step out of `G \ Λ` is an `N`-step or an `E`-step, into `Λ` or out of `G`; an `E`-step into `Λ` is impossible since `Λ` is closed under `E`. Without p6 the left side can hold and the right fail (8,360 frames at four configurations; the smallest is in part 6).

*Sketch of (C).* The union of `E`-saturated regions each kept by a rule is kept by the rule that takes, on each class, the rule of a region containing that class (a class lies wholly in or out of each). Below the grain a class can hold two configurations needing different moves.

### What blocks the guess, exactly

1. **The law gives closure, never the running down.** "Uses up what it rests on" is a least region (a rank falls), and the law is of largest ones. In a finite frame a rank falling along `N` inside `Λ ∩ G` exists exactly where `Λ ∩ Kept = ∅` (0 disagreements in 389,872 frames), so premise p6 *is* the level's fall. Instance: one configuration in `G`, `N` the identity, `F` everything: locked and going on. In the built family 81 configurations are locked and go on.
2. **The outside of a largest closed region is not closed.** A course can enter the lock (d1): 890 such steps under the way that reads the share; the package's own `RuleCapture.favouring_locks` is a course that locks at its first reading step. So "outside the lock" is not a way of going on; it is where courses still enter one region or the other.
3. **Kept is not closed under the reading.** Twins with different fates exist wherever the middle is not empty (8,498 configurations under the share-reading way, summed over the 120 parameter sets; 5,064 of the 23,760 (law, guard, reading) triples at four configurations). `Kept` is the law at `N`; only `KeptAtGrain ⊆ Kept` is the law at `T`.
4. **With the end in the step, no class lies inside "favours".** `K T (rule favours)` is empty in all 120 parameter sets: every class of one share holds a level too low to go on. The region has to be "favours, or has ended", which is what `TurningInTime` already does for constancy (`«至终有常»`, up to the net's end). `RuleCapture.Locked` is closed at `T` only because its step has no end in it (as I read `turn_locked` and `TurningInTime.locked_closed`, whose steps are total).

## 4. Which of R1 to R7

- **R5, in part.** A step of no return, as the one entry into `X`, relative to what the reading leaves alike. With moves, "no course keeps it" is the package's viable region; the two closings are not ordered (part 6: `n_T` before, at, and after the step out of the viable region are all inhabited).
- **R6, first half.** The reading does work in (A)(a), (c), (B)(d2) and (C). Its revision is not in the design.
- **R4, not derived.** The lock is a region of the law, and whether it is non-empty is not concluded. In the built family it comes about from "the next rule is read from the share reached".
- **R1, R2, R3, R7: left out.** They are in the built family only (units that multiply, a level that gives back by what is left, others that give back), and handed in there. No statement relates a way of selecting to more being able to go on after it.

Why this cut: the angle asked whether one law of the grammar is already the centre at a third relation. It is not; what the cut keeps is exactly what that law can carry.

## 7. Where it is weakest, and what would refute it

- **It is not a centre.** No level, share or multiplying is in any statement; the first way's "uses up" is exactly what the law cannot give (block 1), and R7 is untouched. I would file it as three links and one refuted guess.
- **(A) may be nothing more than the old law in a row.** A reader who shows that (a) and (c) follow from `ReadOffInTime`'s `NoReturnAt` laws by taking "feasible" to be "read alike so far" would make it a dictionary. I did not read that module.
- **`X` is a strange region.** It is "ends, as far as the reading can ever tell", not "can no longer be turned by a move". A finer reading with the way unchanged makes `X` larger, so the course is "closed" sooner; and on most ending courses `n_T = 0`. The statement bites only in the middle region.
- **The equal-time twin is too narrow.** (a) compares two courses step for step; a rule from a reading is tested against configurations met at different times, which is why `n_T` can come after the last `n_sep` (five points, gap 2).
- **Link 2 is stated for a fixed successor only**, and its third statement was checked by program, not derived line by line for partial successors.
- **The third factor of the hinted relation** ("who it thereby favours": the share's order composed in) was not tried; only `E;N` and `E ∪ N`.
- **Refuted by**: a frame breaking Link 1, (A) or (B) (none in about 1.7 million checks, all on at most five configurations; a search shows where a statement fails, not that it holds); either link found already stated in the package; or a way of putting the level's fall under `«所闭之承最大»` at some relation, which would undo block 1.
