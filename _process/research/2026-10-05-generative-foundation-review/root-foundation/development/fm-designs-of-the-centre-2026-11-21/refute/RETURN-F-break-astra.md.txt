## 1. Verdict in five lines

Design F survives as a law that joins two descriptions of safety, and as a built case worth keeping.  
It falls short of the centre. Its strongest negative is a locked course whose stock *rises* while the premise “nothing locked goes on” holds.  
The pullback and paired-step links survived. So did (A) and (B) under their stated premises on every five- and six-point frame.  
(C) as stated is false. The toy’s finer rule also fails to factor through the current reading with the moves the design specifies.  
The first-step result bounds when a kept twin can still look alike. It does not locate the last opportunity to turn.

## 3. MUST, then LESSER

**MUST**

1. **The first false step in (C) is constructing a rule on every observation.** Its proof chooses an action on each class contained in a winning region. It supplies no action elsewhere. The one-state empty-action instance breaks the conclusion even classically. For arbitrary observation types, selecting witnesses simultaneously on covered classes also needs a choice argument. For comparison, `Keeping.lean` names this obligation `ChoiceOver`. The designer’s search fixed two actions.
2. **T4 runs, but the finer rule reads at a different time from `Nρ(s) = step(s,ρ(read(s)))`.** With parameters `(2,9,2,1,1,1)` and window 8, `(1,2,8,C)` and `(1,2,9,C)` have identical current readings, including the rule in force and threshold bit. Their next states are `(2,1,7,O)` and `(2,1,8,C)`. The declared moves set the next rule. A single action at that current reading cannot give both outputs. The implemented rule reads the intermediate configuration after spending. Both resulting fixed successor functions are legitimate inputs to (A) and (B).
3. **A path in `T` can switch twins and then follow the other twin’s course.** Of the reported 195 locked, ending starts outside `X`, only **183** have a kept twin anywhere on their actual orbit. At `(2,9,2,1,4,0)`, the orbit `(2,2,0,C) → DEAD` has none, yet starts outside `X`. Likewise the 8,498 middle configurations include **1,202** with no opposite-fate twin at the current reading. And `n_T = n_end` does not imply a kept course looks alike until the end. The six-point witness in part 5 has already distinguished its only kept twin three steps earlier.
4. **The reported “d3 alone” total uses a different d1 from (B).** The statement says `N(s) ∈ Λ`. The program counts d1 only if `N(s) ≠ DEAD` as well. Here `DEAD ∈ Λ`. Under the written definition all **1,298** toy failures have both d1 and d3. Only **286** have a live entry into the lock. For example, at `(2,6,2,1,0,0)`, window 1, `(0,0,0,O) → DEAD` witnesses both written conditions. The abstract exit lemma survives. Its proof only needs `Λ` to be saturated, which follows from its definition even if `F` is unsaturated.
5. **A falling rank need not be the stock.** At `(2,6,2,1,3,0)` the share rule satisfies p6. The locked course is `(1,0,5,C) → (2,0,6,C) → (2,0,4,C) → (2,0,0,C) → DEAD`. A rank falls on its internal edges while the first step raises stock from 5 to 6. The toy also supplies no separate cost to the giver for giving. At `(0,2,6,O)`, changing the contribution coefficient from 0 to 1 raised the next stock from 4 to 6 with the same need of 2 and the same successor counts. R3 and the R7 claim about what more can go on are unestablished even in the toy.
6. **The actual “share” reading includes the rule in force.** In the T4 parameter set, the reported reading `(c,o,g)` gave **70** live locked configurations. Using just `(c,o)` gave **0**, with the course unchanged. The claim that no class lies inside “favours” is also false literally. Every live class with `g=C` lies inside that predicate. What is empty is its largest invariant subset. In the first toy, changing “favours” to “favours or ended” already changed the live lock from 0 to **8,400** across the grid, while live entries stayed at 0. The two toy versions changed both the state and the lock predicate.
7. **T3 measures different things under the same word “idle.”** I reproduced the four counts **83, 86, 12, 30** out of 120. The 86 concern consulting a stock threshold in the rule. Stock is still used to decide death. The 12 concern freezing both births and losses of the other kind. Suppressing births alone also gave 12 idle sets, suppressing losses alone gave 30. Multiplication and finer reading therefore mattered in most sets, contrary to the summary. At `(2,6,2,1,0,1)`, `(0,1,1,O)` ended normally and survived with births alone suppressed. At the same parameters, refining the reading at window 1 put `(0,1,0,O)` in `X` with exactly the same successor function. At `(2,9,2,1,1,1)`, the kept flags under the share, constant-C and constant-O rules were `(true,false,true)` at `(0,1,2,O)` and `(false,false,true)` at `(1,2,8,C)`. These are changes of outcome or region. None makes (A) or (B) fail under its premises.

**LESSER**

- In Link 2’s third sketch the missing step is symmetry of the right-hand kernel. Swapping the two coordinates preserves both the paired step and the synchronized symmetric guard, so it preserves their largest closed region. For T5’s old coarsest-*class* statement, being an equivalence relation also needs justification, available from `Grammar.lean`’s `«核为类»`. The largest-region theorem by itself states closure and maximality.
- For `RuleCapture.locked_stays`, `turn_locked` supplies the substantive one-step lock. For `LineOnAStock.unending_iff_holds`, the right side supplies a **later** holding state and a safe prefix. Applying maximality to holding states alone does not put the start in the kernel. The prefix must be traversed and continued safety justified under the theorem’s stock premises. Similarly, `TurningInTime.locked_closed` uses `Raises`, `ReadsShare` and upward preservation of favouring. Totality alone explains no closure under a specified reading.
- The four-point “p3 dropped” total of **7,512** includes **5,376** courses also missing p4. Exactly **2,136** fail with only p3 missing. The **301,440** denominator for (B) at four points counts frames satisfying p6, out of **385,024** examined.
- The paper gives sample toy arrows but omits the update equations and ablation definitions needed to reconstruct the full grid. I used the program as extra specification only after rebuilding the abstract model.

**Yes, for transferring a safety calculation between descriptions.** Link 1 lets one compute on observation classes despite successive edges using different representatives. Those conclusions do work beyond invoking closure at a single relation. As statements already present, they were **not found in what I read**, listed in part 6. For the centre the answer is **no**. ## 5. The specific questions

**(a) Links 1 and 2.** On five and six points respectively, Link 1 had **0/76,800** and **0/153,600** failures. Of the 2,400 sampled graphs at each size, 539 and 520 had both a missing successor and branching. For Link 2 I also enumerated **all 7,776 and 117,649 partial functions**, including total maps. I used the full pair predicate and every symmetric predicate formed by deleting one unordered pair, including a diagonal. Reducing by relabelling gave 1,936 and 7,436 actual comparisons, representing 124,416 and 2,588,278 labelled comparisons. Synchronized core failures were **0** at both sizes. Omitting successor availability produced **74,400** and **1,561,824** labelled differences. Two points already separate the cases. With `0→0`, terminal 1, and both points read alike, raw paired safety contains all four pairs and the core contains only the diagonal. With `0→{0,1}, 1→0` and identity reading, paired safety is empty and the core is the diagonal.

**(c) The complement and the rank.** On five and six points respectively, I checked **45,400,000** and **7,255,941,120** labelled `(N,E,G,F)` frames by symmetry. Of these, **36,341,200** and **5,928,647,040** satisfied p6. Cover, closure, the claimed equality when covered, and the exit classification had **zero** disagreements with (B). The reported two-point example reproduced `cover=True, closed=False`.

The independent rank calculation repeatedly removed sinks from the induced graph on `Λ ∩ G`. It disagreed with p6 **zero** times. **Block 1 is the finite acyclic-graph ranking fact.** Closure of `Λ` and determinism identify an infinite internal path with a locked kept orbit. In its absence the longest remaining internal path gives a natural rank. On a deterministic locked course one can read the rank directly as the remaining number of steps to exit `G`. Determinism matters to that identification. With `0→{0,1}, 1→1, G={0}, F=S`, output was `Kept=∅` although the internal loop at 0 forbids a decreasing rank.
