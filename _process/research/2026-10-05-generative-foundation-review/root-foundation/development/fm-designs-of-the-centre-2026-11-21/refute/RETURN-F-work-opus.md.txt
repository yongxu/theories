**(b) Is Block 1 the standard duality? Does the package have the rank side as a general law?**

- **It is the standard duality.** On one side is the largest closed region, the invariance kernel or safety set `νZ. G ∧ □Z`, certified by an invariant (Park induction; Aubin's invariance kernel). On the other is the least region, the attractor or reachability set `μZ. ¬G ∨ □Z`, certified by a ranking function or variant (Floyd's well-founded method for termination; the attractor with ranks in games). For a deterministic system and a decided target the two are complements. In a finite deterministic frame, "every course from an `N`-closed `C` ends" is equivalent to "a rank falls along `N` inside `C ∩ G`", the rank being the hitting time of `¬G`.
- **The lock is not used.** My search shows that nothing of `Λ` is used beyond its closure under `N` (part 2).
- **The package has the rank side as general laws.** `Lasting.lean` gives:
  - `Reaches` as the largest funnel and the least basin (`reaches_iff_funnel`, `reaches_iff_basin`);
  - `Variant` and `Variant.reaches` (a numeric level lowered by every step outside the target);
  - `orbit_reaches_iff_variant` (a determined law and a decided target: the orbit meets the target within `n` exactly when a variant is at most `n` at the start);
  - `reaches_iff_not_lasts` (reaching and lasting outside the target are complements; `dual_needs_decision` shows the decision is needed);
  - `avoids_iff_kernel` (never reaching is the invariance kernel of the complement);
  - `Drains`, `Drains.along`, `Drains.leaves` and `exhausts` (a level falling by one per step within the guard: exhaustion).
- **Further places of the rank side.**
