## 1. Verdict in five lines

1. It survives as **a built case worth keeping** with two small new lemmas. It is not a centre, and not a law that joins.
2. The strongest reason: every step of the main theorem's proof is an old pattern or plain arithmetic. The closure along descent is `never_in`/`kept_of_descent`, the fall with no giver is `RegimeMargin.ends_beyond_giving`, and the turn is `LineOnAStock.none_after`. The new arithmetic is doubling against the slots. The choice among moves, the reading and θ do no work in (a) or (b): every argument there works the same for any choice. The lock's three parts are premises: a fixed F-first priority (`FFirst`), F closed under kids (H1), and F unable to go on alone (H3).
3. One clause is false as stated. (b)(II) says "no F unit is in w_{D+1}". It fails in 4,437 of 4,437 case-(II) courses where a unit outside F leaves an F kid, because the premise that `LineOnAStock.none_after` needs is missing.
4. New work found: the fill bound t̂ (exclusion by doubling against the slots) and Lemma 1 (Viable is upward closed in the level under free choice). Neither is found in what I read. Neither is used to bring an old law in as a case, and Lemma 1 is used by no main statement.
5. T5 fails within the model. `ways_alike` would need a handed-in giving the model does not have, no cap, and no slots. It would also need a `pick` that is not a `Selecting`. Even then, Lemma 3 is `ways_alike`'s own induction with one extra congruence (applying `pick` to equal candidates).

## 4. The question

Does a general law of the design do work that the old laws do not? **A little, at one place, and not at the centre.**

The one piece of new work in the main theorem is the fill lemma inside (b). Under a selection that forms F first, with F met, closed under kids, doubling and of one need, the count obeys `n_{t+1} ≥ min N (2·n_t)`, so F's candidates fill the slots by t̂ = ⌈log₂(N/n₀)⌉. No law in what I read has a capacity on the number of units:

- `Exclusion.lean`'s competitive exclusion works through taking from a level.
- `TurningInTime.locked_ends_rising` has a share that rises strictly without a cap.
- `LineOnAStock` has no slots.

Take the slots out and (b) is empty; take the doubling out and it fails, as the design's own H2 row shows.

Everything around the fill lemma is old law in the design's names:

- The ending after the exit: closure plus a fall with no giver.
- The turn: `none_after`, with its premise missing.
- Lemma 3: `ways_alike` with `pick` put in and taken out.

Lemma 1 is new and true: Viable is upward closed in the level under free choice. It is the controlled counterpart of `ways_alike` (below), but no main statement uses it. The parts the brief asks to come about (the favouring, its permanence, the fall after the exit) are the premises FFirst, H1 and H3. The choice among moves and the reading do no work in any law, only in built instances.

## 5. The specific questions

**(a) Is anything left in (a), and is (b) more than an exclusion lemma followed by the old ending?**

Nothing of substance is left in (a). Its two directions are:

- "⇐": a unit in w_{t+1} is the kid of a unit met at t. That is `LineOnAStock.mem_after_iff` (317) plus `Sublist.subset`, with no premise.
- "⇒": Lemma 2 at E+1.

No selection, slot, reading or multiplying appears in its proof. With F = ∅, which meets H1 and H3, (a) reads "never ends ↔ something is formed at every step".

(b) is exactly an exclusion lemma followed by old endings:

- the fill bound gives E ≤ t̂, and then (a) ends the course;
- or D comes first, and then `none_after`'s pattern applies (it needs `closed`; see MUST 1).

The race is not sorted by any condition on the start; D and E are read off the course. The only new content of (b) is the bound t̂ and the fact that the race is settled by it.

Known names: a cooperator–cheater (public-goods) model with a carrying capacity. A non-giving type with higher fecundity fixes and the population collapses (evolutionary suicide, the tragedy of the commons in evolutionary biology). If the resource falls first, the cheaters crash and the producers persist. The fill time is deterministic growth to capacity.

**(b) Lemma 1 against the Lean, and as a comparison between two starts.**

Not found in what I read:

- `GuardHorizon.lean`: account and every statement; its only transport is `coverage_transports`, which is about `Covers`, not `Viable`. `Viable` is used only in `GuardHorizon` and `Reserve` (grep).
- `Viability.Kernel.mono`, `JointKeeping.Keepable.mono` and `Keeping.KeptFor.mono` are monotone in the guard or the class, not in the state.
- `RegimeMargin.step_mono` (1112), `Adjusted.step_mono` (1152) and `run_rises` (1182) order runs of a level that has no way and no moves.
- `LineOnAStock.block_le` (431) and `after_length_le` (453) give only the length form.
- `ReachableRanges.Simulation` (1804) is local to one built process.

As a comparison between two starts:

```
-- lo 0 = (w, x),  hi 0 = (w, x'),  x ≤ x'
-- lo (j+1) = (ms j, level' (lo j)),  hi (j+1) = (ms j, level' (hi j))
-- Move s m := Goes s ∧ m.Sublist (cand s) ∧ m.length ≤ slots
theorem same_moves_higher_start (le : x ≤ x') (ms : Nat → List U)
    (legal : ∀ j, j < n → Move (lo j) (ms j)) :
    ∀ j, j ≤ n → (hi j).1 = (lo j).1 ∧ (lo j).2 ≤ (hi j).2 ∧ (j < n → Move (hi j) (ms j))
```

It follows by induction from three facts:

- `Goes` is monotone in the level.
- `after need kids w x <+ after need kids w x'`: the case split of `block_le`, stated as a sublist. The sublist form is not found.
- `level'` is monotone in x for a fixed way: the design's analogue of `RegimeMargin.step_mono`, where the draw `taken use w` is the same because the ways are the same.

Viable then transports because the step is determined by the move.

Set beside `ways_alike` (`LineOnAStock.lean` 547):

- `ways_alike` gives the same ways when the levels meet the same needs, under a fixed "form all" pick. `HigherStartEnds` shows that a higher level can change the way and end the course.
- The two-start form gives the same ways because the moves are the same: under free choice the higher start discards what its extra level offers.

The general law the Lean should state is `Viable` transported along a simulation: R s s' gives guard s → guard s', and every enabled move at s is matched at s' into R-related successors. Lemma 1 is its case at "same way, level no lower". It is known as monotone control systems (Angeli–Sontag), or simulation preserving safety in games.

**(c) Lemma 2 against `never_in` and `absent_step`.**

`LineOnAStock.never_in` (664): `(closed : ∀ u v, v ∈ kids u → S v → S u) (none : ∀ u, u ∈ s.1 → ¬ S u) : ∀ j u, u ∈ (course need kids giving use s j).1 → ¬ S u`.

- Instantiate `S := (· ∉ Z)`, where Z is the closure of w's kinds under kids.
- `closed` is "Z is closed under kids", read contrapositively. `none` says every unit of w is in Z.
- It is stated on the line's "form all" orbit, not on a process with moves, so it does not apply as is.
- On the design's course the same law comes from `SelectionTerm.Generations.kept_of_descent` (`ReadingsPersist.lean` 293) with:
  - `first := w`;
  - `kids t u := if need u ≤ x_t then (kids u).filter (· ∈ m_t) else []`;
  - `Q := (· ∈ Z)`, with `keeps` from Z being closed under kids.
- The members agree with the course's; the counts do not, because `Generations.kids` reads a unit's value, not its position.

`ReadingsSpread.absent_step` (330), on that G with `read := id` and `inR := (· ∉ Z)`:

- Absent at t (every member in Z) gives carriers at t+1 equal to `revisedIn t`.
- `revisedIn t = 0`, because a descent from a parent in Z to a kid outside Z would contradict closure.
- So one step of narrowing is `absent_step` plus closure, and all steps together are `kept_of_descent`.

Lemma 2's first sentence is `mem_after_iff` with `Sublist.subset`. Its ending half is `ends_beyond_giving`'s argument with a varying draw of at least one. Nothing in Lemma 2 uses the moves.

**(d) Lemma 3 against `LineOnAStock.ways_alike`, the instantiation.**

Lemma 3 at `pick c x = p c` reads: if ∀ j < n, ∀ u ∈ w_j, (need u ≤ x_j ↔ need u ≤ x'_j), then ∀ j ≤ n, w_j = w'_j. The proof is `w_{j+1} = p (after … w_j x_j) = p (after … w_j x'_j)` by `after_alike`.

For `ways_alike {g g' use use' w x x' n}` to be its case, the design's course at `p = id` must be `LineOnAStock.course need kids g use`. That needs four things:

- `level' s = RegimeMargin.step g (taken use s.1) s.2`. The model has no g, and `min cap (z + min z given)` is `z + g z` only for g = 0 with cap ≥ z. No `give` gives `min z (10-z)`.
- `p = id` admitted as a `Selecting`. `fits` fails for every finite `slots`.
- No cap.
- Then `next σ = TurningInTime.pair (after need kids) (left g use)` pointwise, by `rfl`.

So the instantiation goes through only at g = g' = 0, in a model with `fits` removed. The general `ways_alike` needs the third model of part 2. Even there, Lemma 3 is proved by the same induction as `ways_alike`: `ways_alike` cannot be called inside it, and it adds nothing at `p = id`. That is the "put in and taken out" T5 warns of.

The registering ρ does not appear in Lemma 3. A pick that reads the level breaks it (section 2).

**(e) "F is formed first" is the way of selecting: is the lock handed in? What is concluded that is not in H1 to H5?**

Yes: the lock's three ingredients are handed in.

- FFirst is a fixed priority at every step. At the step where F's candidates reach N, it literally says "form no unit outside F".
- H1 makes the exit absorbing.
- H3 makes the absorbed state end. The design marks this "close to handed in".

Nothing is set again from a share: `RuleCapture.Locked` is not in the model.

What is concluded beyond H1–H5 and FFirst:

- (i) when the menu runs out: E ≤ t̂, from H2, H5 and the slots;
- (ii) that by t̂ the race between D and E is settled;
- (iii) that the exit can come at a level up to the cap, so that keepability is not a threshold along the course;
- (iv) in the family, which side holds, read from the start (part (c)).

So the timing and the exhaustiveness of the race come about; the favouring, its permanence and the ending do not.
