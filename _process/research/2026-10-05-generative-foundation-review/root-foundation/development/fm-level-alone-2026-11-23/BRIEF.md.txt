## What we are doing, and why this piece

The repository draws a map of how anything forms, persists, changes and ends, with its core proved in Lean so that no one has to be believed. One paragraph of it is about a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a training run) that uses up what sustains it "while nothing in it yet differs … unless that running down makes a difference to the selection early enough". In the Lean module `Becoming.TurningInTime` every law of turning in time takes the reading as given (a set of marked levels) and says when that reading is in time. This piece asks the question over **all** readings of the level that stay silent at the level the course rests at. It is a claim that can fail: a small course without one premise refutes it (case C3 below). It makes no older law a case; say so and do not dress it up.
