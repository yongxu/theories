## The sentences (AGENTS.md, the paragraph "It is first a map for alignment")

- **A.** Two courses that differ in nothing that makes a difference to the selection are selected alike.
- **B.** How much is left of what sustains the selection can be among what makes no difference to it; then the selection can use that up while nothing in it yet differs.
- **C.** Where each step sets the selection again only from the share it already favours, and each such step adds to what is drawn from what sustains it, which does not grow without limit, the selection goes on the same way until what sustains it gives out,
- **D.** unless that running down makes a difference to the selection early enough.
