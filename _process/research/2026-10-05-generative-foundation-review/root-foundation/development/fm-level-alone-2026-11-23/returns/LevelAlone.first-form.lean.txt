import Becoming.TurningInTime

/-!
# A reading of the level alone, over every place it could fire

AI-written (Claude Fable, board row FM), 23 November 2026.

## What the package already has

`Becoming.TurningInTime` steps a way and a level as a pair and cuts the pair's step where the level is
below a need (`TurningInTime.net`). Its laws of turning in time named here take the reading as given,
a set of marked levels with the way after a step at each (`AloneOutside`, `turns`, `kept`), and say
when that reading is in time (`unending_iff_first_marked`, `unending_iff_above`,
`turns_in_time_iff_kept_below`, `recoverable_iff_above`). No statement over all readings was found
there: the module was searched for a statement that quantifies over the marks or over the way after a
step, not read whole.

## What this module adds

Way, level, need, use, reading and correction are this model's labels, not terms of the root.

* **A reading by where it fires** (`readBy`): at a marked level the way after a step is one fixed
  correction `t` of the way, elsewhere `alone` of it. It meets `AloneOutside` (`readBy_outside`); with
  every corrected way of a kind `Kept` (`sets`) the way after a marked step is of that kind
  (`readBy_turns`), and with `alone` keeping the kind too (`stays`) every step keeps it
  (`readBy_kept`).
* **The level of the course as it was** (`level`), and its fall (`level_never_rises`): where the step
  under no use does not fall as the level rises (`mono`), the use never falls along `alone`'s orbit
  (`up`), and the start's own step does not raise the level (`still`), the level never rises.
* **One step, in the draw** (`next_above_iff_draw`): under `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `mono`,
  `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi` and `0 < H₂ + y₂`, a step under the use `u` from the level `x` leaves at least
  `H₂ + y₂` exactly where `u + y₂ ≤ x`.
* **Over every marking that is silent at the start's level** (`silent_at_rest_iff_next`,
  `silent_at_rest_iff_recoverable`, `silent_at_rest_all_end_iff`, `silent_at_rest_iff_draw`). Under
  the premises named at each law, with `D` the first step at which the level of the course as it was
  differs from the start's: some marking that does not mark the start's level keeps the line going
  exactly where the level at step `D + 1` is at or above `H₂ + y₂`; exactly where the level course as
  it was is recoverable at step `D + 1`; and, with `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi`, exactly where the use at step `D`
  and `y₂` together are at most the level at step `D`. No such marking keeps the line going exactly
  where that course has a point of no return at or before step `D + 1`. The `+ 1` is the lag: a
  reading that fires at a level sets the way after that step, so the step from that level is still
  taken under the use as it was.
* **Three worked courses.** `Twelve`: no marking silent at twelve keeps the line going, though the
  level at the first step that differs is still at or above `H₂ + y₂`. `Seven`: some marking silent at
  seven keeps the line going. `NotMonotone`: a giving that does not meet `mono`, on which the line
  does not go on without end under the marking at the first level that differs and never ends under
  the marking at the next level.

## What is handed in, and what is not shown

Handed in: the correction `t` and that it sets a way of the kept kind (`sets`), which `alone` keeps
(`stays`), so the laws range over where a reading fires and not over what it does; the regime at the
kept kind's use (`uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps`, `needs`, `within`); the cap on the
level (`keeps₀`, `high`); `mono`; a use that never falls (`up`) and rises strictly for `hi` steps
(`rises`); a start whose own step does not raise the level (`still`); and the step `D` with the
level at the start's before it and not at it (`rest`, `differs`). That such a step `D` exists is not
derived. One premise asked follows from another: `keeps` from `keeps₀`, by `kept_within`.

Not shown: anything of a reading that reads more than the level (the way, or the levels before), of
a marking that marks the start's level, of a correction that differs from one marked level to
another, or of a correction that takes effect at the step it fires on (the pair's step has the lag
built in; `Twelve.level_at_five_six` states two numbers and no course without the lag). No condition
on the regime is given for which side of the laws a course falls on; the two sides are shown
inhabited by `Twelve` and `Seven` only. That `mono` cannot be dropped is shown on one course
(`NotMonotone`), not in general, and no premise other than `mono` has a course here that fails
without it. That an actual selection meets these premises is a recognition no law here gives.

The laws make no older law a case. They are proved from `unending_iff_first_marked`,
`unending_iff_above`, `rising_takes_all`, `kept_within`, `asItWas_way`, `recoverable_iff_above` and
`noReturnAt_of_below` of `Becoming.TurningInTime`, for each marking in turn (`reading_in_time_iff`);
what is added is that the level never rises, so that among the markings silent at the start's level
the one at the first level that differs turns earliest and no later turn finds a higher level.

Every declaration has an empty axiom report.
-/

namespace Becoming
namespace LevelAlone

open TurningInTime

universe u

variable {W : Type u}

/-! ## 1. A reading by where it fires, and the level of the course as it was -/

/-- **A reading by where it fires and one correction**: at a marked level the way after a step is `t`
of the way, at any other level `alone` of it. -/
def readBy (marked : Nat → Prop) [DecidablePred marked] (t alone : W → W) (v : W) (x : Nat) : W :=
  if marked x then t v else alone v

/-- Outside the marked levels the way after a step of `readBy marked t alone` is `alone` of the way.
No premise. -/
theorem readBy_outside (marked : Nat → Prop) [DecidablePred marked] (t alone : W → W) :
    AloneOutside marked alone (readBy marked t alone) :=
  fun _ _ unmarked => if_neg unmarked

/-- Where every corrected way is of the kept kind (`sets`), the way after a step of
`readBy marked t alone` at a marked level is of the kept kind. -/
theorem readBy_turns {marked : Nat → Prop} [DecidablePred marked] {t : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (alone : W → W) (v : W) {x : Nat} (at_mark : marked x) :
    Kept (readBy marked t alone v x) :=
  Eq.subst (motive := Kept) (show t v = readBy marked t alone v x from (if_pos at_mark).symm) (sets v)

/-- Where every corrected way is of the kept kind (`sets`) and `alone` keeps the kind (`stays`), the
way after a step of `readBy marked t alone` from a way of the kept kind is of the kept kind, at every
level. -/
theorem readBy_kept {marked : Nat → Prop} [DecidablePred marked] {t alone : W → W}
    {Kept : W → Prop} (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (v : W)
    (x : Nat) (kind : Kept v) : Kept (readBy marked t alone v x) :=
  if at_mark : marked x then readBy_turns sets alone v at_mark
  else Eq.subst (motive := Kept) (show alone v = readBy marked t alone v x from (if_neg at_mark).symm)
    (stays v kind)

/-- **The level of the course as it was** from the way `w` at the level `x₀`, after `j` steps: the way
stepping by `alone` and the level by `RegimeMargin.step giving` under the way's use. -/
def level (alone : W → W) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : W → Nat) (w : W) (x₀ : Nat) (j : Nat) : Nat :=
  (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) j).2

/-- The level of the course as it was one step on is `RegimeMargin.step giving` from the level, under
the use of the way `alone` taken that many times. No premise. Needed by `level_never_rises` and
`silent_at_rest_iff_draw`. -/
private theorem level_succ (alone : W → W) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : W → Nat) (w : W) (x₀ j : Nat) :
    level alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1) =
      RegimeMargin.step giving (use (Law.orbit alone w j)) (level alone giving use w x₀ j) :=
  congrArg (fun v => RegimeMargin.step giving (use v) (level alone giving use w x₀ j))
    (asItWas_way alone _ (w, x₀) j)

/-- Where the step under no use keeps every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps₀`) and the start's
level is at most `hi` (`high`), every level of the course as it was is at most `hi`; by `kept_within`,
step by step. Needed by `reading_in_time_iff`. -/
private theorem level_within {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {hi : Nat}
    {w : W} {x₀ : Nat} (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (high : x₀ ≤ hi) :
    ∀ j, level alone giving use w x₀ j ≤ hi
  | 0 => high
  | j + 1 => kept_within keeps₀
      (use (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) j).1)
      (level_within (alone := alone) (use := use) (w := w) keeps₀ high j)

/-- A course that never rises at any step (`fall`) is not higher at a later step. Needed by
`silent_at_rest_iff_next`. -/
private theorem falls_to {X : Nat → Nat} (fall : ∀ t, X (t + 1) ≤ X t) {j : Nat} :
    ∀ {T : Nat}, j ≤ T → X T ≤ X j
  | _, .refl => Nat.le_refl _
  | _, .step le => Nat.le_trans (fall _) (falls_to fall le)

/-! ## 2. The fall, and one step in the draw -/

/-- **The level of the course as it was never rises, under a use that never falls.** Where the step
under no use does not fall as the level rises (`mono`: `z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z'` for `z ≤ z'`),
the use never falls from one step of `alone`'s orbit from `w` to the next (`up`), and the step under
the start's use does not raise the start's level (`still`): at every step the level one step on is at
most the level. By induction: the first step is `still`; after it, what a use at least as great
leaves of a level at most as high is at most as much, and `mono` carries that to the step. `mono`
says that the step under no use keeps the order of levels. `RegimeMargin` has no name for that of its
one-level step: it names it of a web's law (`RegimeMargin.Coupled.Indexed.KeepsBelow`), and has it of
the one-level step from a stronger premise, that giving itself does not fall as what is left rises
(`RegimeMargin.step_mono`). `TurningInTime.Three.giving` meets `mono` (`cap_mono` at twelve); argued
here, not a Lean statement: `z + min z (12 - z)` is `min (2 * z) 12` up to twelve and `z` above, while
that giving itself falls above six. -/
theorem level_never_rises {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) :
    ∀ j, level alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1) ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ j
  | 0 => still
  | j + 1 =>
    Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1)))
      (Nat.le_trans
        (mono _ _ (Nat.le_trans
          (Optimum.sub_le_sub_right_of_le (level_never_rises mono up still j) _)
          (Optimum.sub_le_sub_left_of_le (up j) _)))
        (Nat.le_of_eq (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ j).symm))

/-- **One step leaves at least `H₂ + y₂` exactly where the use and `y₂` together are at most the
level.** With nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), giving short of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`) and
meeting it from `y₂` up to `hi` (`meets`), the step under no use not falling as the level rises
(`mono`), `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi` (`fits`) and `0 < H₂ + y₂` (`pos`): for every use `u` and level `x`,
`H₂ + y₂ ≤ RegimeMargin.step giving u x` exactly where `u + y₂ ≤ x`. The only-if direction uses
`nothing`, `short` and `pos` (below `u + y₂` the use leaves nothing, or less than `y₂` at which giving
is short of `H₂`); the if direction uses `meets`, `fits` and `mono` (at `y₂` left the step gives at
least `H₂ + y₂`, and no less from more left). -/
theorem next_above_iff_draw {giving : Nat → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi : Nat}
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (fits : H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi) (pos : 0 < H₂ + y₂) (u x : Nat) :
    H₂ + y₂ ≤ RegimeMargin.step giving u x ↔ u + y₂ ≤ x :=
  ⟨fun above => (Nat.lt_or_ge x (u + y₂)).elim
    (fun below => absurd above (Nat.not_le_of_gt ((Nat.lt_or_ge x u).elim
      (fun lt => Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt
        (Nat.le_of_eq ((congrArg (fun z => z + giving z) (sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt))).trans
          ((Nat.zero_add (giving 0)).trans nothing))) pos)
      (fun ge =>
        have left : x - u < y₂ := Naturals.lt_of_add_lt_add_left (k := u)
          (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq (add_sub_of_le ge)) below)
        Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.add_lt_add left (short _ left))
          (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm y₂ H₂))))))
    id,
   fun enough =>
    Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm H₂ y₂))
        (Nat.add_le_add_left
          (meets y₂ (Nat.le_refl y₂) (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm y₂ H₂)) fits)) y₂))
      (mono y₂ (x - u) (Naturals.le_sub_of_add_le enough))⟩

/-! ## 3. Over every marking that is silent at the start's level -/

/-- For one marking, turning in time under a use that rises: with every corrected way of the kept kind
(`sets`), which `alone` keeps (`stays`) and whose ways use `H₂` (`uses`); nothing given from nothing
(`nothing`); giving short of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`), meeting it from `y₂` up to `hi` (`meets`), the
step under `H₂` (`keeps`) and the step under no use (`keeps₀`) each keeping the level within `hi`; a
positive need (`needs`) at most `H₂ + y₂` (`within`); the use rising strictly at each of the first
`hi` steps of `alone`'s orbit from `w` (`rises`); and the start's level at most `hi` (`high`): the
line from `(w, x₀)` under `readBy marked t alone` never ends exactly where there is a step `T` of the
course as it was, the need met and no level marked before it, the need met and the level marked at
it, and the level at `T + 1` at or above `H₂ + y₂`. The proof follows `turns_in_time_iff_kept_below`'s
from its line `ends` on: that the course as it was comes below the need is here by `rising_takes_all`
(`nothing`, `keeps₀`, `high`, `rises`, `needs`), where that law has it from a standing overdraw; the
line from the way after the marked step is by `unending_iff_above`, the level there within `hi` by
`level_within`; the split is `unending_iff_first_marked`. Needed by `silent_at_rest_iff_next`. -/
private theorem reading_in_time_iff {marked : Nat → Prop} [dec : DecidablePred marked]
    {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat}
    {H₂ y₂ hi need : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving H₂ hi)
    (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (high : x₀ ≤ hi) :
    «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
        (w, x₀) ↔
      ∃ T, (∀ j, j < T → need ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ j ∧
              ¬ marked (level alone giving use w x₀ j)) ∧
        need ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ T ∧ marked (level alone giving use w x₀ T) ∧
        H₂ + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ (T + 1) :=
  have ends : ∃ n, (Law.orbit (asItWas alone
      (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) n).2 < need :=
    ⟨hi + 1, Eq.subst (motive := fun z => z < need)
      (rising_takes_all nothing keeps₀ high rises).symm needs⟩
  have above : ∀ T, marked (level alone giving use w x₀ T) →
      («不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
        (pair (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)
          (Law.orbit (asItWas alone
            (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) T)) ↔
        H₂ + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ (T + 1)) :=
    fun T markedT => unending_iff_above (readBy_kept sets stays) uses nothing short meets keeps
      needs within (readBy_turns sets alone _ markedT) (level_within keeps₀ high (T + 1))
  (unending_iff_first_marked (readBy_outside marked t alone) ends).trans
    ⟨fun ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, onward⟩ =>
      ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, (above T markedT).mp onward⟩,
     fun ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, enough⟩ =>
      ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, (above T markedT).mpr enough⟩⟩

/-- **Some marking that is silent at the start's level keeps the line going exactly where the level
one step after the first that differs is at or above `H₂ + y₂`.** With every corrected way of the kept
kind (`sets`), which `alone` keeps (`stays`) and whose ways use `H₂` (`uses`); nothing given from
nothing (`nothing`); giving short of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`), meeting it from `y₂` up to `hi`
(`meets`), the step under `H₂` (`keeps`) and the step under no use (`keeps₀`) each keeping the level
within `hi`; a positive need (`needs`) at most `H₂ + y₂` (`within`); the step under no use not falling
as the level rises (`mono`); the use never falling along `alone`'s orbit from `w` (`up`) and rising
strictly at each of its first `hi` steps (`rises`); the step under the start's use not raising the
start's level (`still`), which is at most `hi` (`high`); and the level of the course as it was at the
start's level `x₀` at every step before `D` (`rest`) and not at step `D` (`differs`). Then there is a
decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way
after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where `H₂ + y₂ ≤ level … (D + 1)`: the level
after the step taken from the first level that differs, still under the use as it was. For each
marking the proof goes through `unending_iff_first_marked` (`reading_in_time_iff`). Only if: a marking
silent at `x₀` first marks at a step `T` not before `D` (`rest`), and the level at `T + 1` is at most
the level at `D + 1` (`level_never_rises`, from `mono`, `up` and `still`). If: the marking of the one
level the course has at step `D` does not mark `x₀` (`differs`), first marks at step `D` (`rest`,
`differs`), and the need is met up to there (`within`, `level_never_rises`). -/
theorem silent_at_rest_iff_next {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving H₂ hi)
    (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) (high : x₀ ≤ hi)
    (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀) :
    (∃ (marked : Nat → Prop) (_ : DecidablePred marked), ¬ marked x₀ ∧
        «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
          (w, x₀))
      ↔ H₂ + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ (D + 1) :=
  have fall := level_never_rises (alone := alone) (w := w) mono up still
  ⟨fun ⟨marked, dec, silent, never⟩ =>
    match (reading_in_time_iff (dec := dec) sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps keeps₀ needs
        within rises high).mp never with
    | ⟨T, _, _, markedT, enough⟩ => (Nat.lt_or_ge T D).elim
        (fun lt => absurd (Eq.subst (motive := marked) (rest T lt) markedT) silent)
        (fun ge => Nat.le_trans enough (falls_to fall (Nat.succ_le_succ ge))),
   fun enough =>
    have met : ∀ j, j ≤ D → need ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ j := fun _ le =>
      Nat.le_trans within (Nat.le_trans enough (falls_to fall (Nat.le_succ_of_le le)))
    ⟨fun x => x = level alone giving use w x₀ D, inferInstance, fun same => differs same.symm,
      (reading_in_time_iff sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps keeps₀ needs within rises
        high).mpr
        ⟨D, fun j lt => ⟨met j (Nat.le_of_lt lt), fun same => differs (same.symm.trans (rest j lt))⟩,
          met D (Nat.le_refl D), rfl, enough⟩⟩⟩

/-- **Some marking that is silent at the start's level keeps the line going exactly where the level
course as it was is recoverable one step after its level first differs.** Under
`silent_at_rest_iff_next`'s premises (`sets`, `stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps`,
`keeps₀`, `needs`, `within`, `mono`, `up`, `rises`, `still`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`): there is a
decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way
after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where the level course as it was is `Recoverable`
at step `D + 1`, with its turns to the standing use `H₂` as the feasible courses and the need met at
every step as what is asked. By `silent_at_rest_iff_next` and `recoverable_iff_above`, whose `fall` is
`level_never_rises` and whose `top` is `high`. -/
theorem silent_at_rest_iff_recoverable {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving H₂ hi)
    (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) (high : x₀ ≤ hi)
    (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀) :
    (∃ (marked : Nat → Prop) (_ : DecidablePred marked), ¬ marked x₀ ∧
        «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
          (w, x₀))
      ↔ Recoverable (Turned (fun j => level alone giving use w x₀ j) giving H₂) (MeetsNeed need)
          (fun t _ => level alone giving use w x₀ t) (D + 1) :=
  (silent_at_rest_iff_next sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps keeps₀ needs within mono up
      rises still high rest differs).trans
    (recoverable_iff_above (X := fun j => level alone giving use w x₀ j)
      (level_never_rises mono up still) high nothing short meets keeps needs within).symm

/-- **The wall: no marking that is silent at the start's level keeps the line going, exactly where the
level course as it was has a point of no return at most one step after its level first differs.**
Under `silent_at_rest_iff_next`'s premises (`sets`, `stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`,
`keeps`, `keeps₀`, `needs`, `within`, `mono`, `up`, `rises`, `still`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`):
under every decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` it is not so that the line from `(w, x₀)` never
ends, the way after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where there is a step `N ≤ D + 1`
that is the point of no return (`NoReturnAt`) of the level course as it was, with its turns to the
standing use `H₂` as the feasible courses and the need met at every step as what is asked. Only if:
the level at `D + 1` is below `H₂ + y₂` or not; below, `noReturnAt_of_below` gives the point; not
below, `silent_at_rest_iff_next` gives a marking whose line never ends. If: by
`silent_at_rest_iff_recoverable` a marking whose line never ends makes the course recoverable at
`D + 1`, so at `N` (`Recoverable.earlier`). -/
theorem silent_at_rest_all_end_iff {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving H₂ hi)
    (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) (high : x₀ ≤ hi)
    (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀) :
    (∀ (marked : Nat → Prop) [DecidablePred marked], ¬ marked x₀ →
        ¬ «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
          (w, x₀))
      ↔ ∃ N, N ≤ D + 1 ∧
          NoReturnAt (Turned (fun j => level alone giving use w x₀ j) giving H₂) (MeetsNeed need)
            (fun t _ => level alone giving use w x₀ t) N :=
  ⟨fun all => (Nat.lt_or_ge (level alone giving use w x₀ (D + 1)) (H₂ + y₂)).elim
    (fun below => noReturnAt_of_below (X := fun j => level alone giving use w x₀ j)
      (level_never_rises mono up still) high nothing short meets keeps needs within below)
    (fun above =>
      match (silent_at_rest_iff_next (t := t) sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps keeps₀ needs
          within mono up rises still high rest differs).mpr above with
      | ⟨marked, dec, silent, never⟩ => absurd never (@all marked dec silent)),
   fun ⟨_, le, point⟩ marked dec silent never =>
    point.1 (Recoverable.earlier
      ((silent_at_rest_iff_recoverable sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps keeps₀ needs within
        mono up rises still high rest differs).mp ⟨marked, dec, silent, never⟩) le)⟩

/-- **The draw form: some marking that is silent at the start's level keeps the line going exactly
where, at the first step whose level differs, the use and `y₂` together are at most the level.** Under
`silent_at_rest_iff_next`'s premises (`sets`, `stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps`,
`keeps₀`, `needs`, `within`, `mono`, `up`, `rises`, `still`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`) and
`H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi` (`fits`): there is a decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line
from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where the use
of the way `alone` taken `D` times and `y₂` together are at most the level of the course as it was at
step `D`. By `silent_at_rest_iff_next`, and `next_above_iff_draw` at the step from `D` (`level_succ`,
through `asItWas_way`). -/
theorem silent_at_rest_iff_draw {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving H₂ hi)
    (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) (high : x₀ ≤ hi)
    (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀) (fits : H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi) :
    (∃ (marked : Nat → Prop) (_ : DecidablePred marked), ¬ marked x₀ ∧
        «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
          (w, x₀))
      ↔ use (Law.orbit alone w D) + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ D :=
  (silent_at_rest_iff_next sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps keeps₀ needs within mono up
      rises still high rest differs).trans
    (Eq.subst (motive := fun z => H₂ + y₂ ≤ z ↔
        use (Law.orbit alone w D) + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ D)
      (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ D).symm
      (next_above_iff_draw nothing short meets mono fits (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le needs within) _ _))

/-! ## 4. Three worked courses -/

/-- A way of the worked courses is a draw and whether it has been returned; what it uses is the
draw. -/
def draw (v : Nat × Bool) : Nat := v.1

/-- The way's step as it was in the worked courses: a returned draw stays as it is, any other rises
by one. -/
def rise (v : Nat × Bool) : Nat × Bool := if v.2 then v else (v.1 + 1, false)

/-- Along `rise` from a draw `a` not returned, the way after `j` steps is the draw `a + j`, not
returned. Needed by `draw_rises`. -/
private theorem rise_orbit (a : Nat) : ∀ j, Law.orbit rise (a, false) j = (a + j, false)
  | 0 => rfl
  | j + 1 => congrArg rise (rise_orbit a j)

/-- Along `rise` from a draw `a` not returned, the draw rises strictly at every step. Needed by the
worked courses, for `up` and `rises`. -/
private theorem draw_rises (a j : Nat) :
    draw (Law.orbit rise (a, false) j) < draw (Law.orbit rise (a, false) (j + 1)) :=
  Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg draw (rise_orbit a j)))
    (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.lt_succ_self (a + j))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg draw (rise_orbit a (j + 1))).symm))

/-- `z + (n - z)` does not fall as `z` rises, at every `n`. No premise. Needed by `cap_mono`. -/
private theorem cover_mono (n : Nat) {z z' : Nat} (le : z ≤ z') : z + (n - z) ≤ z' + (n - z') :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge n z).elim
    (fun lt => Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (z + ·) (sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt))))
      (Nat.le_trans le (Nat.le_add_right z' (n - z'))))
    (fun ge => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (add_sub_of_le ge))
      ((Nat.lt_or_ge n z').elim (fun lt => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_lt lt) (Nat.le_add_right z' _))
        (fun ge' => Nat.le_of_eq (add_sub_of_le ge').symm)))

/-- **Giving `min z (n - z)` meets `mono`**: `z + min z (n - z) ≤ z' + min z' (n - z')` for `z ≤ z'`,
at every `n`. No premise. Where `z'` is at most `n - z'` the right side is `z' + z'`, and the left is
at most `z + z`; otherwise the right side is `z' + (n - z')`, and the left is at most `z + (n - z)`
(`cover_mono`). -/
theorem cap_mono (n z z' : Nat) (le : z ≤ z') : z + min z (n - z) ≤ z' + min z' (n - z') :=
  if h : z' ≤ n - z' then
    Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add le (Nat.le_trans (RegimeMargin.Peaked.min_le_left' z (n - z)) le))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (z' + ·)
        (show z' = min z' (n - z') from (if_pos h).symm)))
  else
    Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_left (RegimeMargin.Peaked.min_le_right' z (n - z)) z)
        (cover_mono n le))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (z' + ·)
        (show n - z' = min z' (n - z') from (if_neg h).symm)))

namespace NotMonotone

/-- Giving two at one, three at three, two at five and at six, and nothing at every other level. -/
def giving : Nat → Nat
  | 1 => 2
  | 3 => 3
  | 5 => 2
  | 6 => 2
  | _ => 0

/-- The correction: a return to a draw of two. -/
def back (_ : Nat × Bool) : Nat × Bool := (2, true)

/-- This giving does not meet `mono`: one left gives two, three in all, and two left gives nothing,
two in all. -/
theorem not_mono : ¬ ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z' :=
  fun mono => absurd (mono 1 2 (by decide)) (by decide)

/-- From the draw two, not returned, at the level eight, the draw rising by one a step, the level of
the course as it was is eight at the start and after one step, and seven after two: seven is the
first level that differs. -/
theorem first_differs : level rise giving draw (2, false) 8 0 = 8 ∧
    level rise giving draw (2, false) 8 1 = 8 ∧ level rise giving draw (2, false) 8 2 = 7 :=
  ⟨rfl, rfl, rfl⟩

/-- **Marked at seven, the first level that differs, the line does not go on without end.** From the
draw two, not returned, at the level eight, a need of one, the correction a return to the draw two: at
step six of the pair's course the need is not met (its levels run 8, 8, 7, 6, 4, 2, 0; those before
step six are not stated). Proved on the net itself; no law of this module applies (`not_mono`). -/
theorem marked_at_seven_ends :
    ¬ «不穷» (net 1 (readBy (fun x => x = 7) back rise)
      (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (draw w) x)) «一» ((2, false), 8) :=
  fun never => absurd (Law.stepWhile_unending_iff.mp never 6) (by decide)

/-- Marked at six, the pair's step leaves the returned draw two at the level three as it is. Needed by
`marked_at_six_goes_on`. -/
private theorem held : ∀ n, Law.orbit (pair (readBy (fun x => x = 6) back rise)
    (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (draw w) x)) ((2, true), 3) n = ((2, true), 3)
  | 0 => rfl
  | n + 1 => congrArg (pair (readBy (fun x => x = 6) back rise)
      (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (draw w) x)) (held n)

/-- **Marked at six, one step later, the line never ends.** From the draw two, not returned, at the
level eight, a need of one, the correction a return to the draw two: the need is met at each of the
first four steps of the pair's course (its levels run 8, 8, 7, 6; not stated one by one), and at step
four it is the returned draw two at the level three, which the pair's step leaves as it is (`held`).
Proved on the net itself, by `unending_from` at step four; no law of this module applies
(`not_mono`). With `marked_at_seven_ends`: on this course the line never ends under a marking later
than the one at the first level that differs, under which it does not go on without end. -/
theorem marked_at_six_goes_on :
    «不穷» (net 1 (readBy (fun x => x = 6) back rise)
      (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (draw w) x)) «一» ((2, false), 8) :=
  (unending_from 4).mpr ⟨by decide, (Law.stepWhile_unending_iff (s := ((2, true), 3))).mpr fun n =>
    Eq.subst (motive := fun s : (Nat × Bool) × Nat => 1 ≤ s.2) (held n).symm (by decide : 1 ≤ 3)⟩

end NotMonotone
