import Becoming.TurningInTime

/-!
# A reading of the level alone, over every marking that does not mark the start's level

AI-written (Claude Fable, board row FM), 23 November 2026.

## What the package already has

`Becoming.TurningInTime` steps a way and a level as a pair and cuts the pair's step where the level is
below a need (`TurningInTime.net`). Of its laws named here, two take the marks as given, a set of
marked levels with the way after a step at each (`unending_iff_first_marked`,
`turns_in_time_iff_kept_below`), and two speak of one kept kind or of one course of levels
(`unending_iff_above`, `recoverable_iff_above`). No statement over all markings was found there: the
module was searched for a statement that quantifies over the marks or over the way after a step, not
read whole.

## Words

Way, level, need, use, reading, marking and correction are this model's labels, not terms of the root.
A *marking* is a set of levels whose membership is decided (`marked` with `[DecidablePred marked]`). A
marking is *silent at a level* where it does not mark it. *The line never ends* says `«不穷»` of the
pair's net from the start; its negation is said "it is not so that the line never ends", and no end is
exhibited by it. *The course as it was* is the pair's course with `alone` as the way's step at every
level; `level … j` is its level after `j` steps. *The corrected use* is `H₂`, the use of the ways of
the kept kind. *The lag*: a marking at a level sets the way after that step, so the step from a marked
level is still taken under the use as it was; it is the `+ 1` of `D + 1` below. In the worked courses
a way is a *draw* and whether it has been returned, and what it uses is the draw; the name
`next_above_iff_draw` has the word for the use `u`.

## What this module adds

* **A reading by the levels it marks and one correction** (`readBy`): at a marked level the way after
  a step is one fixed correction `t` of the way, elsewhere `alone` of it. It meets `AloneOutside`
  (`readBy_outside`); with every corrected way of a kind `Kept` (`sets`) the way after a step at a
  marked level is of that kind (`readBy_turns`), and with `alone` keeping the kind too (`stays`) every
  step keeps it (`readBy_kept`).
* **The level of the course as it was** (`level`), and premises under which it never rises
  (`level_never_rises`): the step under no use does not fall as the level rises (`mono`), the use
  never falls along `alone`'s orbit (`up`), and the start's own step does not raise the level
  (`still`).
* **One step, by the use** (`next_above_iff_draw`): under `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `mono`,
  `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi` and `0 < H₂ + y₂`, a step under the use `u` from the level `x` leaves at least
  `H₂ + y₂` exactly where `u + y₂ ≤ x`.
* **Over every marking that is silent at the start's level: one law, and two calls of it.** With `D`
  the first step at which the level of the course as it was differs from the start's, and the
  premises named at each law: there is a marking silent at the start's level under which the line
  never ends, exactly where the level at step `D + 1` is at or above `H₂ + y₂`.
  `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below` asks one fact of the course as it was (`below`): where its
  level is below `H₂ + y₂` at a step, it is below at the next. Its argument is: once below, it stays
  below; so if the level is below `H₂ + y₂` one step after the first level that differs, it is below
  one step after every later level, and those are the levels a marking silent at the start's level
  can first mark. `below` is weaker than a level that never rises: it follows from one and does not
  ask one. `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_le` has `below` from `H₂ ≤ use w` (`stays_below_of_le`), with
  no premise on the shape of giving; a return to the start's own use is `H₂ = use w` and is inside
  it; that the level never rises is not shown there. `silent_at_start_iff_next` has `below` from
  `level_never_rises`, for any corrected use, under `mono` and `still`; that the level only falls, so
  that the earliest marking finds the highest level, is the argument of this call and not of the law.
* **Three more forms, under `silent_at_start_iff_next`'s premises.** Such a marking exists exactly
  where the levels of the course as it was are recoverable at step `D + 1`
  (`silent_at_start_iff_recoverable_next`) and, with `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi`, exactly where the use as it was
  at step `D` and `y₂` together are at most the level at step `D` (`silent_at_start_iff_use`). Under
  no such marking does the line never end, exactly where those levels have a point of no return at or
  before step `D + 1` (`silent_at_start_none_iff_no_return`).
* **Four worked courses.** `Twelve`: under no marking silent at twelve does the line never end,
  though the level at the first step that differs is still at or above `H₂ + y₂`. `Seven`: under some
  marking silent at seven the line never ends. `WithoutMono` and `WithoutStill`: see the next section.

## What `mono` and `still` are for

`silent_at_start_iff_next` holds for any corrected use, and asks `mono` and `still`, through
`level_never_rises`. Each of the two has one course here that meets every other premise of that law
and not it, and on which the law's two sides are apart: `WithoutMono` and `WithoutStill`. On both the
corrected use, five, is above the course's first use, one. `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_le` asks
neither `mono` nor `still`; it asks `H₂ ≤ use w`. That each is needed is shown on its one course, not
in general, and whether either can be weakened is not shown.

## What is handed in, and what is not shown

Handed in: the correction `t` and that it sets a way of the kept kind (`sets`), which `alone` keeps
(`stays`), so the laws range over which levels a marking marks and not over what the correction does;
the regime at the kept kind's use (`uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `needs`, `within`); the cap on
the level (`keeps₀`, `high`); a use that never falls (`up`) and rises strictly for `hi` steps
(`rises`); the step `D` with the level at the start's before it and not at it (`rest`, `differs`;
where `D = 1`, `rest` says nothing, so the course need not rest at the start's level at all); and, law
by law, `below`, or `H₂ ≤ use w`, or `mono` and `still`. That such a step `D` exists is not derived.
`mono` is not a named notion of `RegimeMargin`'s one-level step, as far as searched; see
`level_never_rises`.

Not shown: anything of a reading that reads more than the level (the way, or the levels before), of
a marking that marks the start's level, of a correction that differs from one marked level to
another, or of a correction that takes effect at the step from the marked level (the pair's step has
the lag built in; `Twelve.level_at_five_six` states two numbers and no course without the lag). No
condition on the regime is given for which side of the laws a course falls on; the two sides are
shown inhabited by `Twelve` and `Seven` only. For no premise other than `mono` and `still` is a
course given on which a law fails without it. The forms in `Recoverable` and `NoReturnAt` are not
given under `below` or under `H₂ ≤ use w`: `recoverable_iff_above` and `noReturnAt_of_below` ask a
course that never rises. That an actual selection meets these premises is a recognition no law here
gives.

The three laws (`silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`, `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_le`,
`silent_at_start_iff_next`) make no older law a case, and neither do the three further forms. They
are proved from `unending_iff_first_marked`, `unending_iff_above`, `rising_takes_all`, `kept_within`,
`asItWas_way`, `recoverable_iff_above` and `noReturnAt_of_below` of `Becoming.TurningInTime`, and
`Recoverable.earlier` of `Becoming.ReadOffInTime`, for each marking in turn (`reading_in_time_iff`).
What is added is `below`: with it the question over every marking silent at the start's level is one
inequality on the course as it was.

Every public declaration has an empty axiom report; the private helpers are not reported on their
own, and each is used, directly or through another helper, by a public one.
-/

open TurningInTime

universe u

variable {W : Type u}

/-! ## 1. A reading by the levels it marks, and the level of the course as it was -/

/-- **A reading by the levels it marks and one correction**: at a marked level the way after a step is
`t` of the way, at any other level `alone` of it. Whether a level is marked is decided
(`[DecidablePred marked]`). -/
def readBy (marked : Nat → Prop) [DecidablePred marked] (t alone : W → W) (v : W) (x : Nat) : W :=
  if marked x then t v else alone v

/-- Outside the marked levels the way after a step of `readBy marked t alone` is `alone` of the way.
No premise but decided marks. -/
theorem readBy_outside (marked : Nat → Prop) [DecidablePred marked] (t alone : W → W) :
    AloneOutside marked alone (readBy marked t alone) :=
  fun _ _ unmarked => if_neg unmarked

/-- Where every corrected way is of the kept kind (`sets`), the way after a step of
`readBy marked t alone` at a marked level (`at_mark`) is of the kept kind. Marks are decided. -/
theorem readBy_turns {marked : Nat → Prop} [DecidablePred marked] {t : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (alone : W → W) (v : W) {x : Nat} (at_mark : marked x) :
    Kept (readBy marked t alone v x) :=
  Eq.subst (motive := Kept) (show t v = readBy marked t alone v x from (if_pos at_mark).symm) (sets v)

/-- Where every corrected way is of the kept kind (`sets`) and `alone` keeps the kind (`stays`), the
way after a step of `readBy marked t alone` from a way of the kept kind (`kind`) is of the kept kind,
at every level. Marks are decided. -/
theorem readBy_kept {marked : Nat → Prop} [DecidablePred marked] {t alone : W → W}
    {Kept : W → Prop} (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (v : W)
    (x : Nat) (kind : Kept v) : Kept (readBy marked t alone v x) :=
  if at_mark : marked x then readBy_turns sets alone v at_mark
  else Eq.subst (motive := Kept) (show alone v = readBy marked t alone v x from (if_neg at_mark).symm)
    (stays v kind)

/-- **The level of the course as it was** from the way `w` at the level `x₀`, after `j` steps: the way
stepping by `alone` and the level by `RegimeMargin.step giving` under the way's use. -/
def level (alone : W → W) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : W → Nat) (w : W) (x₀ : Nat) (j : Nat) : Nat :=
  (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) j).2

/-- The level of the course as it was at step `j + 1` is `RegimeMargin.step giving` from its level at
step `j`, under the use of the way `alone` taken `j` times. No premise; by `asItWas_way`. Needed by
`level_never_rises`, `stays_below_of_le` and `silent_at_start_iff_use`. -/
private theorem level_succ (alone : W → W) (giving : Nat → Nat) (use : W → Nat) (w : W) (x₀ j : Nat) :
    level alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1) =
      RegimeMargin.step giving (use (Law.orbit alone w j)) (level alone giving use w x₀ j) :=
  congrArg (fun v => RegimeMargin.step giving (use v) (level alone giving use w x₀ j))
    (asItWas_way alone _ (w, x₀) j)

/-- Where the step under no use keeps every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps₀`) and the start's
level is at most `hi` (`high`), every level of the course as it was is at most `hi`; by `kept_within`,
step by step. Needed by `reading_in_time_iff`. -/
private theorem level_within {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {hi : Nat}
    {w : W} {x₀ : Nat} (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi) (high : x₀ ≤ hi) :
    ∀ j, level alone giving use w x₀ j ≤ hi
  | 0 => high
  | j + 1 => kept_within keeps₀
      (use (Law.orbit (asItWas alone (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) j).1)
      (level_within (alone := alone) (use := use) (w := w) keeps₀ high j)

/-- Where a course of numbers `X` that is below `c` at a step is below `c` at the next (`below`), and
`X` is at or above `c` at a step `T` (`above`), it is at or above `c` at every step `j ≤ T`. One step
back at a time; whether `X` is below `c` at a step is decided, by the order on `Nat`. Needed by
`silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`. -/
private theorem above_before {X : Nat → Nat} {c : Nat} (below : ∀ j, X j < c → X (j + 1) < c)
    {j : Nat} : ∀ {T : Nat}, j ≤ T → c ≤ X T → c ≤ X j
  | _, .refl, above => above
  | _, @Nat.le.step _ m le, above => above_before below le
      ((Nat.lt_or_ge (X m) c).elim (fun lt => absurd above (Nat.not_le_of_gt (below m lt))) id)

/-- Where `H₂` is at most the use of `w` (`le`) and the use never falls from one step of `alone`'s
orbit from `w` to the next (`up`), `H₂` is at most the use at every step of that orbit. Needed by
`stays_below_of_le`. -/
private theorem use_from {alone : W → W} {use : W → Nat} {w : W} {H₂ : Nat} (le : H₂ ≤ use w)
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1))) :
    ∀ j, H₂ ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w j)
  | 0 => le
  | j + 1 => Nat.le_trans (use_from le up j) (up j)

/-! ## 2. Where the level never rises, and one step by the use -/

/-- **The level of the course as it was never rises, under a use that never falls.** Where the step
under no use does not fall as the level rises (`mono`: `z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z'` for `z ≤ z'`),
the use never falls from one step of `alone`'s orbit from `w` to the next (`up`), and the step under
the start's use does not raise the start's level (`still`): at every step `j`,
`level … (j + 1) ≤ level … j`. By induction: the first step is `still`; after it, what the use leaves
at step `j + 1` is at most what it leaves at step `j`, since the level did not rise and the use did
not fall, and `mono` gives the same order once giving is added.

This is the premise `fall` of `turned_meets_iff`, `recoverable_iff_above`,
`noReturnAt_iff_first_below` and `noReturnAt_of_below` of `Becoming.TurningInTime`, said of the
course `fun j => level … j`. In that module, where a search for those names found `fall` supplied
(to `recoverable_iff_above` in `turns_in_time_iff_before`, to `noReturnAt_iff_first_below` in
`Three.noReturn`), it is from the private `asItWas_falls`, which asks a standing overdraw: every use
on the course at least `H₁`, giving short of `H₁` below `y₁`, and the start below `H₁ + y₁`. No call
was found in another module of the package's `Becoming` folder (searched by name, not read whole).
Here `recoverable_iff_above` and `noReturnAt_of_below` are called with this law as `fall`
(`silent_at_start_iff_recoverable_next`, `silent_at_start_none_iff_no_return`); the other two are
not called.

`mono` says that the step under no use keeps the order of levels. No name for that of the one-level
step was found in `RegimeMargin` (searched, not read whole): it names it of a web's law
(`RegimeMargin.Coupled.Indexed.KeepsBelow`), and has it of the one-level step from a stronger premise,
that giving itself does not fall as what is left rises (`RegimeMargin.step_mono`).
`TurningInTime.Three.giving` meets `mono`: `cap_mono 12` is that statement, used so in
`Twelve.mono_and_still`. Argued here, not a Lean statement: `z + min z (12 - z)` is `min (2 * z) 12`
up to twelve and `z` above; that giving itself falls above six, so `RegimeMargin.step_mono`'s premise
does not hold of it. -/
theorem level_never_rises {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) :
    ∀ j, level alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1) ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ j
  | 0 => still
  | j + 1 =>
    Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1)))
      (Nat.le_trans
        (mono _ _ (Nat.le_trans
          (Optimum.sub_le_sub_right_of_le (level_never_rises mono up still j) _)
          (Optimum.sub_le_sub_left_of_le (up j) _)))
        (Nat.le_of_eq (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ j).symm))

/-- From a level below the use and `y₂` together, one step leaves less than `H₂ + y₂`. With nothing
given from nothing (`nothing`), giving short of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`) and `0 < H₂ + y₂` (`pos`):
for every use `u` and level `x` with `x < u + y₂` (`below`),
`RegimeMargin.step giving u x < H₂ + y₂`. Where `x` is below `u` the use leaves nothing and nothing is
given; otherwise it leaves less than `y₂`, at which giving is short of `H₂`. Nothing is asked of how
`u` compares with `H₂`. Needed by `next_above_iff_draw` and `stays_below_of_le`. -/
private theorem step_below {giving : Nat → Nat} {H₂ y₂ : Nat}
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (pos : 0 < H₂ + y₂) {u x : Nat} (below : x < u + y₂) :
    RegimeMargin.step giving u x < H₂ + y₂ :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge x u).elim
    (fun lt => Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt
      (Nat.le_of_eq ((congrArg (fun z => z + giving z) (sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt))).trans
        ((Nat.zero_add (giving 0)).trans nothing))) pos)
    (fun ge =>
      have left : x - u < y₂ := Naturals.lt_of_add_lt_add_left (k := u)
        (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq (add_sub_of_le ge)) below)
      Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.add_lt_add left (short _ left))
        (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm y₂ H₂)))

/-- **One step leaves at least `H₂ + y₂` exactly where the use and `y₂` together are at most the
level.** With nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), giving short of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`) and
meeting it from `y₂` up to `hi` (`meets`), the step under no use not falling as the level rises
(`mono`), `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi` (`fits`) and `0 < H₂ + y₂` (`pos`): for every use `u` and level `x`,
`H₂ + y₂ ≤ RegimeMargin.step giving u x` exactly where `u + y₂ ≤ x`. The only-if direction uses
`nothing`, `short` and `pos` (`step_below`). The if direction uses `meets`, `fits` and `mono`: with
`y₂` left the step gives at least `H₂ + y₂`, and no less from more left. `meets` is used at `y₂`
itself only, which is why `fits` is asked and no bound on `x`. The use `u` is any number; on the
course as it was it is the use as it was (`silent_at_start_iff_use`). -/
theorem next_above_iff_draw {giving : Nat → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi : Nat}
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (fits : H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi) (pos : 0 < H₂ + y₂) (u x : Nat) :
    H₂ + y₂ ≤ RegimeMargin.step giving u x ↔ u + y₂ ≤ x :=
  ⟨fun above => (Nat.lt_or_ge x (u + y₂)).elim
    (fun below => absurd above (Nat.not_le_of_gt (step_below nothing short pos below))) id,
   fun enough =>
    Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm H₂ y₂))
        (Nat.add_le_add_left
          (meets y₂ (Nat.le_refl y₂) (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm y₂ H₂)) fits)) y₂))
      (mono y₂ (x - u) (Naturals.le_sub_of_add_le enough))⟩

/-! ## 3. Over every marking that is silent at the start's level -/

/-- For one marking, when the line never ends, under a use that rises: with marks decided
(`[DecidablePred marked]`); every corrected way of the kept kind (`sets`), which `alone` keeps
(`stays`) and whose ways use `H₂` (`uses`); nothing given from nothing (`nothing`); giving short of
`H₂` below `y₂` (`short`) and meeting it from `y₂` up to `hi` (`meets`); the step under no use
keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps₀`); a positive need (`needs`) at most `H₂ + y₂`
(`within`); the use rising strictly at each of the first `hi` steps of `alone`'s orbit from `w`
(`rises`); and the start's level at most `hi` (`high`): the line from `(w, x₀)` under
`readBy marked t alone` never ends exactly where there is a step `T` of the course as it was, the need
met and no level marked before it, the need met and the level marked at it, and the level at `T + 1`
at or above `H₂ + y₂`. The `+ 1` is the lag: the step from the marked level is still taken under the
use as it was. The proof follows `turns_in_time_iff_kept_below`'s from its line `ends` on: that the
course as it was comes below the need is here by `rising_takes_all` (`nothing`, `keeps₀`, `high`,
`rises`, `needs`), where that law has it from a standing overdraw; the line from the way after the
marked step is by `unending_iff_above`, whose `keeps` is `kept_within` from `keeps₀`, the level there
within `hi` by `level_within`; the split is `unending_iff_first_marked`. Needed by
`silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`. -/
private theorem reading_in_time_iff {marked : Nat → Prop} [dec : DecidablePred marked]
    {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat}
    {H₂ y₂ hi need : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (high : x₀ ≤ hi) :
    «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
        (w, x₀) ↔
      ∃ T, (∀ j, j < T → need ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ j ∧
              ¬ marked (level alone giving use w x₀ j)) ∧
        need ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ T ∧ marked (level alone giving use w x₀ T) ∧
        H₂ + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ (T + 1) :=
  have ends : ∃ n, (Law.orbit (asItWas alone
      (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) n).2 < need :=
    ⟨hi + 1, Eq.subst (motive := fun z => z < need)
      (rising_takes_all nothing keeps₀ high rises).symm needs⟩
  have above : ∀ T, marked (level alone giving use w x₀ T) →
      («不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
        (pair (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)
          (Law.orbit (asItWas alone
            (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) (w, x₀) T)) ↔
        H₂ + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ (T + 1)) :=
    fun T markedT => unending_iff_above (readBy_kept sets stays) uses nothing short meets
      (fun _ le => kept_within keeps₀ H₂ le) needs within (readBy_turns sets alone _ markedT)
      (level_within keeps₀ high (T + 1))
  (unending_iff_first_marked (readBy_outside marked t alone) ends).trans
    ⟨fun ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, onward⟩ =>
      ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, (above T markedT).mp onward⟩,
     fun ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, enough⟩ =>
      ⟨T, before, atT, markedT, (above T markedT).mpr enough⟩⟩

/-- **Under some marking silent at the start's level the line never ends, exactly where the level one
step after the first that differs is at or above `H₂ + y₂`; asked of the course as it was: once below
`H₂ + y₂`, below at the next step.** With every corrected way of the kept kind (`sets`), which `alone`
keeps (`stays`) and whose ways use `H₂` (`uses`); nothing given from nothing (`nothing`); giving short
of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`) and meeting it from `y₂` up to `hi` (`meets`); the step under no use
keeping every level up to `hi` within `hi` (`keeps₀`); a positive need (`needs`) at most `H₂ + y₂`
(`within`); the use rising strictly at each of the first `hi` steps of `alone`'s orbit from `w`
(`rises`); the start's level at most `hi` (`high`); the level of the course as it was at the start's
level `x₀` at every step before `D` (`rest`) and not at step `D` (`differs`); and that level, at
every step at which it is below `H₂ + y₂`, below `H₂ + y₂` at the next step (`below`). Then there is
a decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way
after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where `H₂ + y₂ ≤ level … (D + 1)`. The `+ 1` is
the lag: it is the level after the step taken from the first level that differs, still under the use
as it was.

`below` is weaker than a level that never rises: it follows from one (as in
`silent_at_start_iff_next`), and `stays_below_of_le` gives it from premises that ask nothing of the
shape of giving. That the level never rises is not asked here and not used; no course is worked in
this module on which `below` holds and the level rises. `rest` says nothing where `D = 1`: the course
need not rest at `x₀` at all.

The argument: once below, it stays below; so if the level is below `H₂ + y₂` one step after the
first level that differs, it is below one step after every later level. For each marking the proof
goes through `unending_iff_first_marked` (`reading_in_time_iff`). Only if: a marking that does not
mark `x₀` first marks at a step `T` not before `D` (`rest`), with `H₂ + y₂ ≤ level … (T + 1)`; were
the level at `D + 1` below `H₂ + y₂`, it would be below at `T + 1` (`below`, through `above_before`).
If: the marking of the one level the course has at step `D` does not mark `x₀` (`differs`) and first
marks at step `D` (`rest`, `differs`); `below` read backward from `D + 1` puts `H₂ + y₂` at or below
the level at every step up to `D`, so the need is met up to there (`within`). -/
theorem silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop}
    {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (high : x₀ ≤ hi) (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀)
    (below : ∀ j, level alone giving use w x₀ j < H₂ + y₂ →
      level alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1) < H₂ + y₂) :
    (∃ (marked : Nat → Prop) (_ : DecidablePred marked), ¬ marked x₀ ∧
        «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
          (w, x₀))
      ↔ H₂ + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ (D + 1) :=
  ⟨fun ⟨marked, dec, silent, never⟩ =>
    match (reading_in_time_iff (dec := dec) sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps₀ needs within
        rises high).mp never with
    | ⟨T, _, _, markedT, enough⟩ => (Nat.lt_or_ge T D).elim
        (fun lt => absurd (Eq.subst (motive := marked) (rest T lt) markedT) silent)
        (fun ge => above_before below (Nat.succ_le_succ ge) enough),
   fun enough =>
    have met : ∀ j, j ≤ D → need ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ j := fun _ le =>
      Nat.le_trans within (above_before below (Nat.le_succ_of_le le) enough)
    ⟨fun x => x = level alone giving use w x₀ D, inferInstance, fun same => differs same.symm,
      (reading_in_time_iff sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps₀ needs within rises high).mpr
        ⟨D, fun j lt => ⟨met j (Nat.le_of_lt lt), fun same => differs (same.symm.trans (rest j lt))⟩,
          met D (Nat.le_refl D), rfl, enough⟩⟩⟩

/-- **Where every use is at least the corrected use, a level below `H₂ + y₂` is below it at the next
step.** With nothing given from nothing (`nothing`), giving short of `H₂` below `y₂` (`short`),
`0 < H₂ + y₂` (`pos`), `H₂ ≤ use w` (`le`) and the use never falling from one step of `alone`'s orbit
from `w` to the next (`up`): at every step `j` of the course as it was with
`level … j < H₂ + y₂`, `level … (j + 1) < H₂ + y₂`. The use at every step is at least `H₂` (`le`,
`up`, through `use_from`); a level below `H₂ + y₂` is then below the use and `y₂` together, and the
step from it leaves less than `H₂ + y₂` (`step_below`). Nothing is asked of giving at `y₂` or above,
and that the level never rises is not concluded. -/
theorem stays_below_of_le {alone : W → W} {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ : Nat}
    {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (pos : 0 < H₂ + y₂) (le : H₂ ≤ use w)
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1))) :
    ∀ j, level alone giving use w x₀ j < H₂ + y₂ → level alone giving use w x₀ (j + 1) < H₂ + y₂ :=
  fun j lt => Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ j))
    (step_below nothing short pos
      (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le lt (Nat.add_le_add_right (use_from le up j) y₂)))

/-- **Under some marking silent at the start's level the line never ends, exactly where the level one
step after the first that differs is at or above `H₂ + y₂`; where the corrected use is at most the
course's first use.** Under `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`'s premises but `below` (`sets`,
`stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps₀`, `needs`, `within`, `rises`, `high`, `rest`,
`differs`), with the use never falling from one step of `alone`'s orbit from `w` to the next (`up`)
and `H₂ ≤ use w` (`le`): there is a decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line
from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where
`H₂ + y₂ ≤ level … (D + 1)`. The `+ 1` is the lag, as there. No premise on the shape of giving is
asked: neither `mono` nor `still`. A return to the start's own use is `H₂ = use w` and is inside
`le`. It is a call of `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`, with `below` from
`stays_below_of_le` (`nothing`, `short`, `needs`, `within`, `le`, `up`). -/
theorem silent_at_start_iff_next_of_le {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}

  silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps₀ needs within

    (stays_below_of_le nothing short (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le needs within) le up)

/-- **Under some marking silent at the start's level the line never ends, exactly where the level one
step after the first that differs is at or above `H₂ + y₂`; for any corrected use, where the level
never rises.** Under `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`'s premises but `below` (`sets`, `stays`,
`uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps₀`, `needs`, `within`, `rises`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`),
with the use never falling from one step of `alone`'s orbit from `w` to the next (`up`), the step
under no use not falling as the level rises (`mono`), and the step under the start's use not raising
the start's level (`still`): there is a decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line
from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where
`H₂ + y₂ ≤ level … (D + 1)`. The `+ 1` is the lag, as there. Nothing is asked of how `H₂` compares
with the use of `w`. It is a call of `silent_at_start_iff_next_of_stays_below`, with `below` from
`level_never_rises` (`mono`, `up`, `still`): a level at most one that is below `H₂ + y₂` is below it.
`WithoutMono` meets every premise here but `mono`, and `WithoutStill` every premise but `still`; on
each the two sides are apart. -/
theorem silent_at_start_iff_next {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}

/-- **Under some marking silent at the start's level the line never ends, exactly where the course as
it was is recoverable one step after its level first differs.** Under `silent_at_start_iff_next`'s
premises (`sets`, `stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps₀`, `needs`, `within`, `up`,
`rises`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`, `mono`, `still`): there is a decidable marking that does not mark
`x₀` under which the line from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way after a step being
`readBy marked t alone`, exactly where the levels of the course as it was are `Recoverable` at step
`D + 1`, with their turns to the standing use `H₂` as the feasible courses and the need met at every
step as what is asked. The `+ 1` is the lag, as at `silent_at_start_iff_next`: the marking of the
level at step `D` sets the way after that step, so the step from `D` is still taken under the use as
it was. By `silent_at_start_iff_next` and `recoverable_iff_above`, whose `fall` is
`level_never_rises`, whose `top` is `high` and whose `keeps` is `kept_within` from `keeps₀`. It is
not given under `below` alone or under `H₂ ≤ use w`: `recoverable_iff_above` asks a course that never
rises. -/
theorem silent_at_start_iff_recoverable_next {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop}

    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (high : x₀ ≤ hi) (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀)

    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) :

/-- **Under no marking silent at the start's level does the line never end, exactly where the course
as it was has a point of no return at most one step after its level first differs.** Under
`silent_at_start_iff_next`'s premises (`sets`, `stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps₀`,
`needs`, `within`, `up`, `rises`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`, `mono`, `still`): under every decidable
marking that does not mark `x₀` it is not so that the line from `(w, x₀)` never ends, the way after a
step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where there is a step `N ≤ D + 1` that is the point of no
return (`NoReturnAt`) of the levels of the course as it was, with their turns to the standing use `H₂`
as the feasible courses and the need met at every step as what is asked. The left side is `¬ «不穷»`
for each such marking; no end is exhibited. `N = 0` is not excluded: a course not recoverable at its
start has its point there. The `+ 1` is the lag, as at `silent_at_start_iff_next`. Only if: the level
at `D + 1` is below `H₂ + y₂` or not, decided by the order on `Nat`; below, `noReturnAt_of_below`
gives the point (its `fall` is `level_never_rises`, its `keeps` is `kept_within` from `keeps₀`); not
below, `silent_at_start_iff_next` gives a marking under which the line never ends. If: by
`silent_at_start_iff_recoverable_next` a marking under which the line never ends makes the course
recoverable at `D + 1`, so at `N` (`Recoverable.earlier`), where it is not. -/
theorem silent_at_start_none_iff_no_return {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop}
    {giving : Nat → Nat} {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (high : x₀ ≤ hi) (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) :
    (∀ (marked : Nat → Prop) [DecidablePred marked], ¬ marked x₀ →
        ¬ «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»

      ↔ ∃ N, N ≤ D + 1 ∧
          NoReturnAt (Turned (fun j => level alone giving use w x₀ j) giving H₂) (MeetsNeed need)

  ⟨fun all => (Nat.lt_or_ge (level alone giving use w x₀ (D + 1)) (H₂ + y₂)).elim
    (fun below => noReturnAt_of_below (X := fun j => level alone giving use w x₀ j)
      (level_never_rises mono up still) high nothing short meets
      (fun _ le => kept_within keeps₀ H₂ le) needs within below)
    (fun above =>
      match (silent_at_start_iff_next (t := t) sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps₀ needs
          within up rises high rest differs mono still).mpr above with
      | ⟨marked, dec, silent, never⟩ => absurd never (@all marked dec silent)),
   fun ⟨_, le, point⟩ marked dec silent never =>
    point.1 (Recoverable.earlier
      ((silent_at_start_iff_recoverable_next sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps₀ needs within
        up rises high rest differs mono still).mp ⟨marked, dec, silent, never⟩) le)⟩

/-- **Under some marking silent at the start's level the line never ends, exactly where, at the first
step whose level differs, the use as it was and `y₂` together are at most the level.** Under
`silent_at_start_iff_next`'s premises (`sets`, `stays`, `uses`, `nothing`, `short`, `meets`, `keeps₀`,
`needs`, `within`, `up`, `rises`, `high`, `rest`, `differs`, `mono`, `still`) and `H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi`
(`fits`): there is a decidable marking that does not mark `x₀` under which the line from `(w, x₀)`
never ends, the way after a step being `readBy marked t alone`, exactly where
`use (Law.orbit alone w D) + y₂ ≤ level … D`. The use here is the use as it was at step `D`, the use
of the way `alone` taken `D` times, not `H₂`: that is the lag, here without a `+ 1`, since the step
from the first level that differs is still taken under it. By `silent_at_start_iff_next`, and
`next_above_iff_draw` at the step from `D` (`level_succ`, through `asItWas_way`). -/
theorem silent_at_start_iff_use {t alone : W → W} {Kept : W → Prop} {giving : Nat → Nat}
    {use : W → Nat} {H₂ y₂ hi need D : Nat} {w : W} {x₀ : Nat}
    (sets : ∀ v, Kept (t v)) (stays : ∀ v, Kept v → Kept (alone v)) (uses : ∀ v, Kept v → use v = H₂)
    (nothing : RegimeMargin.FromNothing giving) (short : RegimeMargin.Short giving H₂ y₂)
    (meets : RegimeMargin.Meets giving H₂ y₂ hi) (keeps₀ : RegimeMargin.Keeps giving 0 hi)
    (needs : 0 < need) (within : need ≤ H₂ + y₂)
    (up : ∀ j, use (Law.orbit alone w j) ≤ use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (rises : ∀ j, j < hi → use (Law.orbit alone w j) < use (Law.orbit alone w (j + 1)))
    (high : x₀ ≤ hi) (rest : ∀ j, j < D → level alone giving use w x₀ j = x₀)
    (differs : level alone giving use w x₀ D ≠ x₀)
    (mono : ∀ z z', z ≤ z' → z + giving z ≤ z' + giving z')
    (still : RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x₀ ≤ x₀) (fits : H₂ + y₂ ≤ hi) :
    (∃ (marked : Nat → Prop) (_ : DecidablePred marked), ¬ marked x₀ ∧
        «不穷» (net need (readBy marked t alone) (fun w x => RegimeMargin.step giving (use w) x)) «一»
          (w, x₀))
      ↔ use (Law.orbit alone w D) + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ D :=
  (silent_at_start_iff_next sets stays uses nothing short meets keeps₀ needs within up rises high
      rest differs mono still).trans
    (Eq.subst (motive := fun z => H₂ + y₂ ≤ z ↔
        use (Law.orbit alone w D) + y₂ ≤ level alone giving use w x₀ D)
      (level_succ alone giving use w x₀ D).symm
      (next_above_iff_draw nothing short meets mono fits (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le needs within) _ _))

/-! ## 4. Four worked courses -/

/-- A way of the worked courses is a draw and whether it has been returned; what it uses is the
draw. -/
def draw (v : Nat × Bool) : Nat := v.1

/-- The way's step as it was in the worked courses: a returned draw stays as it is, any other rises
by one. -/
def rise (v : Nat × Bool) : Nat × Bool := if v.2 then v else (v.1 + 1, false)

/-- Along `rise` from a draw `a` not returned, the way after `j` steps is the draw `a + j`, not
returned. No premise. Needed by `draw_rises`. -/
private theorem rise_orbit (a : Nat) : ∀ j, Law.orbit rise (a, false) j = (a + j, false)
  | 0 => rfl
  | j + 1 => congrArg rise (rise_orbit a j)

/-- Along `rise` from a draw `a` not returned, the draw rises strictly at every step. No premise.
Needed by the worked courses, for `up` and `rises`. -/
private theorem draw_rises (a j : Nat) :
    draw (Law.orbit rise (a, false) j) < draw (Law.orbit rise (a, false) (j + 1)) :=
  Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg draw (rise_orbit a j)))
    (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.lt_succ_self (a + j))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg draw (rise_orbit a (j + 1))).symm))

/-- `z + (n - z)` does not fall as `z` rises, at every `n`. No premise but `z ≤ z'` (`le`). Needed by
`cap_mono`. -/
private theorem cover_mono (n : Nat) {z z' : Nat} (le : z ≤ z') : z + (n - z) ≤ z' + (n - z') :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge n z).elim
    (fun lt => Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (z + ·) (sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt))))
      (Nat.le_trans le (Nat.le_add_right z' (n - z'))))
    (fun ge => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (add_sub_of_le ge))
      ((Nat.lt_or_ge n z').elim (fun lt => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_lt lt) (Nat.le_add_right z' _))
        (fun ge' => Nat.le_of_eq (add_sub_of_le ge').symm)))

/-- **Giving `min z (n - z)` meets `mono`**: `z + min z (n - z) ≤ z' + min z' (n - z')` for `z ≤ z'`
(`le`), at every `n`. No other premise. Where `z'` is at most `n - z'` the right side is `z' + z'`,
and the left is at most `z + z`; otherwise the right side is `z' + (n - z')`, and the left is at most
`z + (n - z)` (`cover_mono`). -/
theorem cap_mono (n z z' : Nat) (le : z ≤ z') : z + min z (n - z) ≤ z' + min z' (n - z') :=
  if h : z' ≤ n - z' then
    Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add le (Nat.le_trans (RegimeMargin.Peaked.min_le_left' z (n - z)) le))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (z' + ·)
        (show z' = min z' (n - z') from (if_pos h).symm)))
  else
    Nat.le_trans
      (Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_left (RegimeMargin.Peaked.min_le_right' z (n - z)) z)
        (cover_mono n le))
      (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (z' + ·)
        (show n - z' = min z' (n - z') from (if_neg h).symm)))

  | _ => 0
