## Mine, tried on paper by one checker, built by no one

Both of the file's outcomes are one state repeated. The package has had since 27 October that along a course that comes back to one configuration at arbitrarily late steps nothing read off its configurations keeps widening (`Run.not_keepsWidening_of_returns`, `HeldPossible` l. 193), keeping widening being of a chain of predicates: nothing held is lost at any later step, and after every step something not held there is held later (`Reaching.KeepsWidening`, `ReachableRanges` l. 311). So, on paper, with the file's history taken as such a course, on neither outcome does anything read off the three numbers keep widening. That is by the standstill, whatever the carrier.

Of the carrier less follows than I first wrote. No list holds all that a chain keeping widening ever holds (`Reaching.KeepsWidening.not_listed`, l. 583, 27 October; for a listed type of formations `Web.KeepsWidening.not_listed`, `EventualWidening` l. 339, 25 October). So where a list holds the carrier, no chain of predicates on the carrier keeps widening (`Reaching.not_keepsWidening_of_listed`, l. 757, says it of what is possible within a horizon along a course). A list holds the carrier in this morning's either-or, by its premises; in `RegimeMargin.Adjusted.fates`, within its band; and in this file from its first step, for a start with no more renewing than slots (`count_history`, l. 73, under that premise; `post_step_bounds`, l. 570, bounds the reserve and the capacity only). It holds it in none of three exact statements this read's questions and this note name, each an iff between lasting for ever and a condition of the start, on a level that can rise without bound: `ResponseCapacity.exact_no_investment_viability` (l. 318), `Stock.repeat_covered_iff` (l. 319), `Optimum.kept_iff_within` (l. 2342). And a listed carrier forces no standstill: under a determined step it forces only a course that comes round again, and the package has a run within a band that is never still (`RegimeMargin.Alternation.alternates`, l. 1467); under a schedule that varies in time, as the file's does, not that either. What makes both of the file's outcomes standstills is its step, which is supplied.

Nor is keeping widening, by its letter, a test of the second way. A chain over what no list holds (the steps gone by, a counter of generations, the levels up to a support that rises) keeps widening on a course whose state stands still or only piles up; the lasting side of `ResponseCapacity` l. 318 has it wherever demand is positive. What the second way entails is not mine to say, and the direction page already says, of one fixed process read in a space given beforehand, that what is possible never grows while what a configuration admits and what is held can.
