OPENED **Q9. What a list holds, and the regime threshold.** Neither named list theorem has the exact boundary as its unchanged conclusion. `PredicateIteration.iter_iff_all` (97–100, 153–155) assumes a monotone predicate step, `Φ V₀ ⊆ V₀` and a list containing every `V₀` state. It concludes `iter Φ V₀ all.length y ↔ ∀ n, iter Φ V₀ n y`. For the increasing iterate, `iter_within_length` (209–213, 255–257) assumes monotonicity, `W₀ ⊆ Ψ W₀`, commutation with implication to `G = I all.length y`, and `∀ x, (W₀ x → G) → x ∈ all`. It concludes `∀ n, I n y → I all.length y`. October’s `r₀` counts remaining contributors. It is neither a list covering states nor a bound obtained by exhausting such a list. Listing the states in Q5 gives a different, coarser bound.

**The scalar regime law.** `RegimeMargin.ends_iff_below` (741–743) says `(∃ n, (Law.run (step g H) x).state n = 0) ↔ x < H+y`. It assumes `0<H+y`, `Short g H y`, `FromNothing g`, `Meets g H y hi`, `Keeps g H hi` and `x≤hi` (definitions at 249–265). October can fund from zero reserve and fail forever with positive reserve. Even the same reserve and count give opposite outcomes when the schedule changes. Substituting the triangular potential for `x` moves the difficulty: at unit speed a positive sub-fee potential can stay constant all the way to the destructive state. It does not reach zero. The unchanged scalar theorem stands beside the boundary.

**Two closer regime statements.** `RegimeMargin.Crossing.toward` 1005–1009 raises or lowers a second coordinate by one toward a target of 4 or 0 set by the residual level. `Adjusted.step` 299 feeds the **new** giving into a level after a standing draw. `Crossing.crosses` 1062–1066 has actual opposite fates: from `(4,6)` the level stays at least 7 after two steps, from `(5,0)` it stays zero. `Adjusted.fates` 1670–1679 even puts both stationary outcomes in **one** statement under monotone adjustment, `RestsAtDraw`, a band and both initial bounds. Its dividing value is computed by a test for resting below draw (1643–1645). October uses the **old** contributors, a conditional fee, and new private revision counts. These are related two-coordinate laws, not a renaming.

There is a restricted **paper case** of `RegimeMargin.Coupled.Indexed.ends_iff_below_within` (2814–2819, 2960–2969, 2984–2987). Fix a *constant* positive quota, `1≤N` and `fee≤N`. Use two coordinates `(r,b)`, clipping inputs to `N,fee`. The projected step is `(allocated N k (fee≤b) r, min fee (b+r))` by 50 and 57. It preserves coordinatewise order: both count branches are monotone in count, switching up can only increase the output, and the second coordinate is a clipped sum. List the two coordinates, take band and upper count `N`, hold `b` fixed and vary `r≤N`. Then `EndsUnder` of the count means eventually constantly zero. By October 524 and 553 this is exactly funding failure. The package gives a threshold in the *initial count*, computed by bounded runs. October identifies it as the first count with `fee ≤ b+available k r r` on that band. Such a count exists here because the first payment at `r=N` covers `fee≤N`. This is a case for **existence of a threshold**. The package conclusion does not compute the contribution sum, and an arbitrary supplied schedule is not one fixed quota. The more general scalar search `leading_exact` (1550–1552) similarly needs downward closure and an already exact decision procedure.
