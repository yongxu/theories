## 4. The paragraph "Mine, and checked by no one"

**(a) The facts cited.** `KeepsWidening` and `not_listed` are as §2 gives them; 27 October is right. `count_bound` is one step under a premise; the parenthesis should say `count_history` and the premise. `post_step_bounds` does not bound the count.

**(b) The inference.**
1. *"Every exact two-outcome statement found so far lives on a carrier a list holds."* False of three statements the read's own questions and note name, each an iff between lasting for ever and a condition of the start, on a level that can rise without bound: `ResponseCapacity.exact_no_investment_viability` (l. 318; the lasting course is `⟨s.response, s.support+demand*t⟩`, l. 249), `Stock.repeat_covered_iff` (l. 319: `(∀ k, Covered draw replenish n (repeatCourse w k)) ↔ Covered draw replenish n w ∧ total w draw ≤ total w replenish`) and `Optimum.kept_iff_within` (l. 2342: `(∀ t, m ≤ x t) ↔ k ≤ r`). It is true of the three it could have named: the morning's either-or (listings among its premises, by its note l. 11), this file under `hr`, and `Adjusted.fates` (`Band` keeps levels and recent givings up to `hi`).
2. *"No model on such a carrier can have it as an outcome."* Sound only once the chain is fixed. `not_listed` is of a chain of predicates on `X`; it needs a list of what the chain holds, not of the states. For a chain of predicates on the carrier itself it applies as it stands (the package says it of what is possible within a horizon: `Reaching.not_keepsWidening_of_listed`, l. 757). For a chain read off the configuration into another type it does not apply; what applies is `Run.not_keepsWidening_of_returns` (`HeldPossible` l. 193, 27 October: `(returns : ∀ d, ∃ m, N + d ≤ m ∧ r.state m = r.state N) : ¬ Reaching.KeepsWidening (fun n => F (r.state n))`, any `F`), with, on a listed carrier, a pigeonhole the package does not state for a course under a schedule (some configuration recurs without end). For a chain over what no list holds (steps gone by, a counter) nothing is excluded: `PopulationRealization` beside the file has `generation := s.generation+1` (l. 30, by grep), and by `CHECK-whole` §4 it "runs alike in the destructive course". For the file, then: with `Q n x` as "`x` is the state at step `n`", or as what is reachable from it, a list of the bounded states holds it and neither outcome keeps widening; with `Q n` as the generations so far, both outcomes keep widening by the letter.
3. *"A standstill on the good side is forced by the carrier."* Not so. A listed carrier under a determined step forces only a course that comes round again; the package has a run within a band that is never still (`RegimeMargin.Alternation.alternates`, l. 1467: `∀ N, ¬ LastChange.StillFrom …`). Under a schedule that varies in time, as the file's does, not even that. What makes both of the file's outcomes standstills is its step, which is supplied (N3). And the standstill, not the carrier, is what excludes widening there: a state repeated returns at every step, so `Run.not_keepsWidening_of_returns` applies on paper whatever the carrier.
4. By the letter, keeping widening is met by one number rising: the lasting side of `ResponseCapacity` l. 318 has it for `0 < demand` with `Q n x := x ≤ support at n` (the package's own witness has that form, `HeldPossible` l. 886). So "entails keeping widening" is no test that tells the second way from a pile.

**(c)** It should not stand as written. Either drop it, or R15, which keeps what holds.
