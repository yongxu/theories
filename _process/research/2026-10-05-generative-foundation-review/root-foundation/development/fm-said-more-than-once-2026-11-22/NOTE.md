# Inductions the package says more than once: a sweep, its check, and six old proofs made calls

*AI-written (Claude Fable, board row FM), 22 November 2026; checked against its sources by a reader who took no part, whose 35 replacements are applied word for word. A record of an afternoon: a read-only sweep of the Lean package, the check made of it, and the small build that followed, for whoever next wants to make an old proof a case of a law the package already has. In the sweep's sections every "is a case" was tried on paper by the one reader named; what Lean has seen is in the section on the build. The finding is on the board, in row FM.*

## Why

Ren's direction for this row (21 November) is a full map with formal link proof, done elegantly, with its centre ([his words](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-21-a-full-map-with-its-centre.md)). Two tries at the centre on 22 November came back as not the centre ([both outcomes of a choice](../fm-both-outcomes-2026-11-22/NOTE.md); [the read of PopulationCorrection](../fm-read-population-correction-2026-11-22/NOTE.md)). What had worked the night before was of another kind: one induction written in many modules was said once (`Becoming.Iteration`, [the law of what a list holds](../fm-the-law-of-what-a-list-holds-2026-11-22/NOTE.md)), and a dozen older theorems became calls with their statements unchanged. Rule 4 of `AGENTS.md` asks for exactly that: the proof that makes an old result a case of another. Nobody had looked for such families on purpose.

## What was asked, and of whom

Six questions, frozen with their hash and clock before any reader ran ([QUESTIONS](QUESTIONS-SAID-MORE-THAN-ONCE.md.txt), [hash](FROZEN-QUESTIONS.sha256.txt)): the families whose proofs are one induction under different names; the one statement that has the others as cases; where it would live by the import lines; the ledger in lines; the rank; the near misses. Five ways to find nothing were written first (M1 alike only in shape; M2 the call no shorter; M3 an import cycle or a large closure; M4 all members of 22 November; M5 a general statement nobody needs).

- Three Claude readers, each alone, each on two seeded families ([search](returns/RETURN-search-fable.md.txt), [horizons and courses](returns/RETURN-horizons-and-courses-opus.md.txt), [pigeonholes and thresholds](returns/RETURN-pigeonholes-and-thresholds-fable.md.txt)).
- One read-only pass by a reader of another kind of model (Astra 6.2) over all 409 modules ([its report](astra/REPORT.md.txt): 296 rows in 39 families).
- A list printed by Lean, made by the session that holds row DJ at this row's asking: for each theorem of the package the head of its proof term, the package theorems it uses and its recursors ([how it was printed](lean-list/README.md.txt)). The four checkers, and later the readers of the build, had its first form: 20,519 theorems, 2,638 with an induction of their own; the three readers' returns and the Astra report do not use it. That form undercounted, as a later reader found (below, "The list was corrected afterwards").
- The questions were frozen without being attacked. The coordinating session's seven points came after; they were applied at the checking stage ([ADDENDUM](ADDENDUM-AFTER-FREEZE.md.txt)). The two that changed most: a family is ranked by whether it is a law of a going-on, where a "case of" row on the atlas would say something (class i), or deciding, searching and list bookkeeping (class ii: tidying under rule 4, not a link); and the ledger needs a cost side (modules compiled again, records bound to the changed files).
- Four checkers who took no part, one question each ([cheap calls](returns/CHECK-cheap-calls-opus.md.txt), [the two search families](returns/CHECK-two-search-families-fable.md.txt), [goings-on](returns/CHECK-goings-on-fable.md.txt), [against the Lean list](returns/CHECK-against-the-lean-list-fable.md.txt)).

## The build that followed: six old proofs made calls

The first item above was put to Lean the same afternoon, with the first of the four class (i) items beside it. What landed is small: six theorems in three modules (`Likeness`, `GrammarCandidates`, `GrammarCourse`), 31 proof lines for 13, no statement, declaration or import changed; five theorem blocks moved inside their modules to stand under their bridges (three in `Likeness`, two in `GrammarCandidates`).

**How.** A brief was written with its tests first: a Lean file that prints every declaration of the touched modules (its statement binder by binder as Lean holds it, the head of its proof, the package theorems it uses and its recursors; after the attack, the constants that carry an induction and what it uses from outside the package), run on main before and on the scratch tree after, and a script that compares the two and the sources. Two readers attacked the brief and the tests before any build ([the cheapest pass that gains nothing](build/readers/ATTACK-cheapest-pass-fable.md.txt), [possible as worded](build/readers/ATTACK-possible-as-worded-opus.md.txt)); two more, at the caution of the session that holds row DJ (this session's record; no source of it is filed here), said of each of ten of the eleven theorems which premise of the law its old proof had argued, `run_within` not being given them ([one](build/readers/PREMISES-fable.md.txt), [the other](build/readers/PREMISES-opus.md.txt)). The brief was rewritten and frozen ([brief](build/BRIEF-OLD-INDUCTIONS-AS-CALLS.md.txt), [hashes and clock](build/FROZEN-BRIEF.sha256.txt); [its first form](build/BRIEF-first-form-before-the-attack.md.txt)). An implementer of another kind of model (Astra 6.2) built nine calls in scratch in nine minutes ([its report](build/report/REPORT.md.txt)). Three readers then read the result: [the mended tests](build/readers/SECOND-LOOK-kit-fable.md.txt), [the built proofs](build/readers/BUILT-the-proofs-fable.md.txt), [what else in the repository tells of them](build/readers/BUILT-what-tells-of-them-opus.md.txt).

**The rule for keeping a call**, frozen before the build: the rows show the statement the same binder by binder, no induction of its own left and the named lemma used; the call has strictly fewer lines and no more characters that are not spaces. After the build two readers showed that the second half can be met by one-letter local names, so a call was kept only if it is also no longer with names alike and by tokens (a name counting once). That is stricter than the brief, and it is this session's judgement, made after the build.

- "Shorter" is not one number. Six of the paper calls had more text than the inductions they replaced and fewer lines only by being filled to 100 columns; a character count is met by short names; a count of tokens, a name counting once, changes under neither. Of the nine calls that Lean accepts, three were left: member 8 is longer by characters (152 against 122); member 6 is longer by tokens (80 against 79) and by characters once the old proof's names are shortened as the call's are (174 against 170); member 7 is shorter by tokens (32 against 42) and longer by characters once its `h` is written `gives` as the old proof has it (102 against 96).

## The list was corrected afterwards

A reader attacking the test kit of the build that followed found that the list took a recursor by the end of its name, so an induction made through a wrapper (`Nat.strongRecOn`, `Nat.recAux`) did not show, and that it left out every theorem whose last name begins `eq_` or `match_`. The session that holds row DJ printed it again the same afternoon: 20,567 theorems, 2,709 with an induction of their own; 68 theorems newly seen as carrying one, 3 no longer, 48 newly listed (45 whose last name begins `eq_`, 3 `match_`), 6 of them carrying one. Both counts are floors.

One pass of this note and the returns against the corrected list, by script (a theorem's last name on a line that also names its module):

- Of the 68, two are named in the returns: `ReaderRebuilding.length_replicate` and `RuleRebuilding.length_replicate'` (the Astra report and the cheap-calls check), each now shown with `Nat.recAux`. So the figure "41 of the 189 call rows have no induction of their own" was read from the first form and may be 39; it was not recounted.
- Of the 48 newly listed, six are named in the Astra report (`Gluing.Cycle.eq_of_even`, `GrammarLaws.eq_true_of_not_false`, `Interacting.eq_true_of_not_false`, `Joining.Once.eq_of_mem_single`, `RevisionPlans.eq_zero_of_add_eq_zero_left` and `_right`); none has an induction. All six stand in its register of proof text (after l. 1009), not in its 296 table rows, each of which the checker found in the first form of the list.
- `Lasting.orbit_mono` is unchanged: head `Eq.subst`, no induction.
- The nine theorems of item 1 each show an induction (`Nat.brecOn`) in both forms. The first form missed 68 inductions and showed 3 that are none (through `Bool.rec` and `Decidable.rec`, recursors of types that are not recursive); the 68, and the 6 newly listed that carry one, were not in the population the goings-on checker read.

## What it taught

- The find of the sweep came from the checking stage and from the other end: not "which proofs look alike" but "which theorems about a notion that already has a bridge to the law still have an induction of their own". The Lean-printed list made that a join of two tables and a read of 143 statements.
- A cost side changes the rank. By lines the search families lead; by what a landing compiles again and re-binds they are last.
- The Lean list does not show an induction carried by a helper, and a reader that opens every module still misses a family: neither replaces the other.
