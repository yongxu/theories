## What we are doing

The repository draws a map of how anything forms, persists, changes and ends, with its core proved in Lean so that any mind can check it (`AGENTS.md` at the repository root says this in full; read its first section and its seven rules). The present aim of this row: a full map whose links are Lean theorems, each thing said once. A link counts when an OLD proof becomes a call of a more general law, its statement unchanged, its own induction gone, and the call no longer than what it replaces.

On 22 November a law landed, `Becoming.Iteration` in `Becoming/PredicateIteration.lean`: a monotone step on predicates, applied again and again from a start. A read-only sweep then found, on paper, that some theorems of 13 October to 6 November still carry an induction that this law already has, in modules where the bridge from the notion to an iterate is already landed. Nobody has built any of it. This build finds out, member by member, whether Lean agrees and whether the call is shorter.

## The rules

1. **Only the nine proofs change.** No statement, binder, docstring, import, `open`, `variable`, `universe`, attribute or notation is added, removed or altered. No declaration is added (no helper, no `private` or `local` anything) and none is removed or renamed. No file is put in the after tree but copies of the six modules. A name from `Becoming.Iteration` is written qualified where `open Iteration` is not already in scope at that place.
2. **The statement keeps its lines character for character.** The proofs now are in equation form (`| 0 => …`); a term proof needs ` :=`, which is added at the end of the statement's last line and nothing else goes on that line. The proof is every line after it, to the next blank line. No line of a changed member is wider than 100 characters. Names are written as the package writes them.
3. **A theorem may move inside its own module only where this brief says so**, as a whole block (docstring, statement, proof): members 1 and 2 with `AlikeTo.down_to` (Likeness), member 4 with `«核则不可致诘»` (GrammarCandidates), member 6 with `kernel_iff_lasts` (Lasting). The three blocks that move with a member move byte for byte, each still followed by a blank line. If no member that needs a move is kept, the blocks go back where they were.
4. **A member is kept as a call only if all of these hold** (the comparison's line `R4 member n` says PASS):
   - its row shows no induction of its own, every name listed for it in `members.json` under `uses` is in its `uses`, and none listed under `not_uses`;
   - nothing among what it uses from outside the package needs looking at (the comparison prints the names);
   - its proof has **strictly fewer lines** than before **and no more characters that are not spaces**;
   - width, docstring and statement lines as in rules 1 and 2, and no other command inside its block;
   - member 3 only if member 1 is kept.
   **Otherwise it is put back byte for byte.** For a member that fails only on length, first get the shortest call you can to build, record its text and its two counts in the report, and then put it back: that Lean accepts the call is a fact worth having even where the old proof stays.
5. **The whole build**: the comparison's checks C0 to C10 all pass on the last sources (what each tests is below).
6. **No verdicts.** The report says what was done and what Lean and the scripts printed. Whether a member is "a case", "a join" or "worth it" is not for the report.

`/Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/kit/Rows.lean` prints, for every declaration of the six modules (private ones too): name, kind, universe names, and its statement binder by binder as Lean holds it; and for every theorem the head of its proof term, the package theorems the term uses, the constants in it that carry an induction (a recursor or companion of a recursive type, and the toolchain's wrappers such as `Nat.recAux`, `Nat.strongRecOn`, `WellFounded.fix`), and everything it uses from outside the package that is not a type or a constructor; all following Lean's auxiliary declarations under the theorem's own name. ## What would make this build gain nothing, said before it runs

- **A call that is not shorter.** Fewer lines can be had by filling lines; so the measure is lines **and** characters. On paper, with the checker's terms, members 4, 6, 7 and 8 have more characters than the inductions they replace (247 against 216, 222 against 207, 139 against 96, 194 against 122): unless a shorter term is found, each is put back, and what the build then shows of it is only that Lean accepts the call.
- **A call that needs anything new** (a lemma, a statement, an import, an `open`): not a call of a law the package already has.
- **An induction that has only moved**: into a wrapper, a helper, another module's copy, or a theorem three calls down. The rows print what each proof uses inside and outside the package; member 6 is barred from the route through `kernel_certificate` for this reason.
- **Member 3 without member 1** would be a call of an induction of 14 October, not of the law.
- Even if all nine are kept, three things are not shown and are not to be said in the report or anywhere on its strength: member 10's target has the same statement up to `(run T x).state n` for `orbit next s n` (one of two twins calling the other, not a case of something more general); member 7's statement is the law's `iter_within` hypothesis for hypothesis, but its old proof was one direct induction and the law's is two (a case of the statement, not the same argument); and the build adds no statement and no row to the atlas.

- Put the changed copy of a module at `/Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/after/Becoming/<Module>.lean` (start from a byte copy of the package file). Write nowhere else except `/Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/report/`.
- Build named modules while working: `/Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/kit/run-after.sh <n> <Module> [<Module> …]` (`<n>` a fresh number each time; it waits for any other build, gate or core run, builds once, logs to `after/build-<n>.log`). - When the sources are as you mean to leave them: `/Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/kit/run-stub.sh after` (builds the stub and every module in the after tree with its axiom audit, writes the sources' hashes, prints `after/rows.txt`; modules between a changed module and the stub are compiled again, which is expected), then `python3 -B /Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/kit/compare.py > /Users/ren/tmp/fm/build13/report/compare.txt`. Repeat both after any change to a source, so that the last `rows.txt` and `compare.txt` are of the last sources (C0 fails otherwise).
- Do not edit anything under `/Users/ren/theories`, run no git command that writes, and change nothing in `kit/` or `before/`. If a script in `kit/` is wrong, say so in the report and stop there for the check it gets wrong; do not mend it.
