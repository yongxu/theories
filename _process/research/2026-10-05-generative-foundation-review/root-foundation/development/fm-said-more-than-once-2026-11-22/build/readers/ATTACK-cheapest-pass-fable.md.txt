## 2. The holes, each with its cheapest exploit and the smallest change that closes it

**H1. An induction through a wrapper the lister does not name. OPENED.** `Nat.recAux` (`Init/Data/Nat/Basic.lean:38`), `Nat.strongRecOn` and `Nat.caseStrongRecOn` (`Init/WF.lean:246, 255`), `WellFounded.induction`, `WellFounded.recursion`, `WellFounded.fixF` (`Init/WF.lean:92, 87, 98`), `Nat.div.inductionOn` (`Init/Data/Nat/Div/Basic.lean:59`) all exist in the toolchain (v4.29.1); none ends in a listed suffix, and none is a package theorem, so it appears in **no field of the row**. Evidence that this is live: main's list has `Becoming.Regulation.lateReturn_course_balances | head Nat.strongRecOn | recursors ['Eq.rec'] | inductions []` and `Becoming.TimedResponse.levels_alike_before | head Nat.strongRecOn | recursors [] | inductions []` (sources: `induction t using Nat.strongRecOn`, `Regulation.lean:1063`, `TimedResponse.lean:104`). Functional induction (`f.induct`) is a further candidate; how it would show I could not determine without Lean.
*Exploit:* the term in section 0; or `by intro n; induction n using Nat.strongRecOn with …`.
*Closes it (Rows.lean; one rerun of the before rows):* print what the term uses outside the package, so nothing is invisible, and count a recursor by the type being recursive, not by a list of exceptions:

**H5. The line rule counts a proof begun on the statement's line short. OPENED** (simulation, member 8, text of the paper call with `:= fun n s held =>` on the statement's last line: `proof lines 4 -> 1`, for three and a half lines of proof; `statement text same False`, a fact). "So the proof begins on a new line" is a consequence drawn, not a rule. The pressure is exactly here: the brief says members 4, 7, 8, 9 are undone by one more line. Also: a statement line ending `:= by` with no later `:=` or `|` gives `p = None` and `compare.py` dies at `ea - pa` (`:76`) with no output at all.

```python

want = [l.rstrip() for l in src_b[sb:pb]]
```

## 4. Which matter for this build

Eleven small proofs, terms on paper, an implementer who follows the letter.

**Matter (the honest path meets them):**

- **H5** proof on the statement's line: the one-line margin of members 4, 7, 8, 9 is where a layout change is tempting, and rule 3's letter permits it for 4 and 8.

- **H9** `all pass` over a kept member that breaks rule 4, and **H11** rows of an earlier source: slips, not exploits, and each is a few lines of script.

**Cheap to close, unlikely from this implementer:** H1 and H2 (but the lister's blindness is a fact about main already, so `inductions after []` is not yet the fact it is named), H3, H4, H10.
