- `MonotoneStep Φ` (l. 83): `∀ {X Y}, (∀ z, X z → Y z) → ∀ y, Φ X y → Φ Y y`.

The brief's list of premises is right for these three lemmas.

## Across the ten

**The same sentence of the law.**
- *A class inside the start that the step preserves lies in every iterate* (`postfixed_in_iter`): 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9. Within it: **1 and 4** are twins (a two-sided matching step on pairs; K a bisimulation, K a symmetric constant relation); **3** is 1 at the identity; **6** is the sentence at one named class (the kernel) for the region step; **8** for the course step of a restricted process, the start everything; **9** the degenerate end, K the start itself. `GuardHorizon.closed_viable` and `Tolerance.kept_set`, already calls, are the same sentence with K given by hypotheses, as 4 and 8 are.
- *A start that contains its first step: each iterate lies in the one before* (`iter_pred`): 2 and 5, twins, and the same two steps as 1 and 4.
- *… so every iterate lies in the start* (`iter_within`): 7 alone.
- Not of the law: 10.

**How the old proof holds the premises, in three kinds** (this is what separates "a case" from "shorter"):
1. the premises are the theorem's hypotheses: 7 (both, by name), 4 (three hypotheses make `inside` and `post`), 8 (`post` is the hypothesis, repacked), 9 (`post` is the hypothesis), 1 (`Alike` is "some class with `post` holds the pair");
2. the premises are a fact about a named class that the old proof wrote out inline: 6 (`kernel_step_iff.mp`), 3 (`Bisimulation.eq`);
3. the premise is the shape of the step: 2, 5 (`start` is the first conjunct).
In all nine `mono` was inlined in the step case, except 7 (a hypothesis) and 9 (absent: the old proof did not need it).

**Claims I would not let stand as written.**
