## 4. "Strictly fewer lines and no more characters"

Coded as worded (`:57`, `:119`, `:137-138`): the proof is every line after the statement's lines to the first blank line; all whitespace is dropped; the docstring and statement are not counted, nor the ` :=`. Ways round it that the code refuses: text on the statement's line (H5), text after a blank line (C6), a line over 100 (`wide`), a local macro or helper (C1, R4; but see H3).

What is left is what the two counts measure. Counts by script on the real after sources and main; "one-letter" renames the old proof's own local names (`alike`, `step`, `joined`, `claims`, `forth`, `back`, `kept`, `admitted`, `reach`) to one letter; "refilled" joins the old proof's words and wraps at 100 columns (a count of text, not a layout Lean checked); a token is a name, a number or a symbol, a name counting once.
