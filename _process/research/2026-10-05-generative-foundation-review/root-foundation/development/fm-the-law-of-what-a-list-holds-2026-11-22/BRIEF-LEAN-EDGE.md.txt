## The argument on paper (found no hole by two readers; not checked in Lean)

Let `all` list every configuration and `N = all.length`. A witness of `Viable P guard N s` is a tree: at each node the guard holds, a move is chosen, and every outcome of that move is a child, down to depth `N`. Call a node *fresh* when the configurations on the path from the root down to it are at pairwise different places of the list (or, with equality decided, are pairwise different). A fresh path has at most `N` nodes, so at a fresh node at least one step is left: the guard holds there and the tree gives an enabled move. Each outcome of that move either extends the path freshly, and is the configuration of a fresh node; or stands at a place already on the path, and is then the configuration of an earlier fresh node. So the configurations of fresh nodes form a set closed in `GuardHorizon`'s sense, holding `s`. One way to say it without trees: `K t` holds when some path of configurations at pairwise different places of the list ends at `t`, each configuration on it viable for `N` less the number before it.
