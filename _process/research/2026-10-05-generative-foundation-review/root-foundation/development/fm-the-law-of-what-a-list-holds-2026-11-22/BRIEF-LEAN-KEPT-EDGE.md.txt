### Part 1. The law


```lean

    (mono : ∀ {X Y : S → Prop}, (∀ z, X z → Y z) → ∀ y, Φ X y → Φ Y y)
    (start : ∀ y, Φ V₀ y → V₀ y)
    {all : List S} (every : ∀ x, x ∈ all) {y : S} :
    iter Φ V₀ all.length y → iter Φ V₀ (all.length + 1) y
```

Say whether `start` is needed, and whether anything beyond `mono`, `start` and `every` is. No decision of equality, nothing listed but the configurations.

Then what follows, each in a line or two:
- the chain falls: `iter Φ V₀ (n + 1) y → iter Φ V₀ n y`;
- `iter Φ V₀ all.length y ↔ ∀ n, iter Φ V₀ n y`;
