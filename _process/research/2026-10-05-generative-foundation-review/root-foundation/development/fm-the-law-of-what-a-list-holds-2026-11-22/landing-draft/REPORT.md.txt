> # PredicateIteration: finite bounds on repeated steps on predicates
>
> `Iteration.iter` starts with a predicate and applies the same step once at each successor. For a step that preserves inclusion (`Iteration.MonotoneStep`) and takes the start inside itself, a list covering the start bounds the descent. At its length the predicate is the largest class inside the start that lies inside its own step (`Iteration.iter_largest`). If the start is one step from all states, a list of every state of length n + 1 gives persistence at n (`Iteration.iter_persists_from_all`). Given a list member, `remove_mem` gives a list one shorter that still contains every original member different from it.
>
> For a monotone step whose first step contains the start, write G for membership at y after as many steps as the list is long. If the step commutes with implication to G (`Iteration.CommutesWithImplication`) and the list covers every state where membership in the start implies G, membership at y after any number of steps implies G (`Iteration.iter_within_length`). This module gives no unconditional shortening for rising steps.

> # RisingIteration: when a rising predicate can be bounded
>
> `Iteration.iter` applies a step repeatedly from a starting predicate. If all states are listed, every monotone step commutes with implication to False (`Iteration.commutes_false`). Suppose also that the start lies inside its first step. At any state reached one step past the list length, membership at the list length holds under double negation (`Iteration.rise_not_not`). Where that membership follows from its double negation, it holds (`Iteration.rise_of_stable`).
>
> An unconditional shortening would decide every proposition, even if required only from the empty start at the second step on a one-state type (`Iteration.singleton_rise_needs_em`).

The choice bridge fits before `closed_viable`. The inclusion and orbit bridges fit before `kept_set`. The two viability equivalences call `closed_viable` by its package name.

I named the repeated step or exact trace length in `kernel_iff_iter`, `iter_region_iff_lasts`, `iter_iff_continues`, `iter_iff_reachable`, `returns_iff_reachable_count`, `drawn_iff_not_reachable_count`, `iter_iff_inquiry`, `keepable_iff_count`, `rise_iff_trace` and `iter_eq_orbit`.
