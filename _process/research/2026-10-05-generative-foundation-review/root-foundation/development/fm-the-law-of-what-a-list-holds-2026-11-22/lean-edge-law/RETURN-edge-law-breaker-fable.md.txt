## 4. The reach argument

**By program it cannot fail, and that is the reason it needs no decision.** `reaches` is built from "or", "exists" and "and" over atomic facts. Its truth at a world depends only on that world, so the edge at each world is the classical statement about one graph.

| Frame, points | Instances | N+1 → N fails | N → N − 1 fails | N − 1 → N − 2 fails |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| one world, 2 / 3 / 4 (40,000 random) | 64 / 4,096 / 40,000 | 0 | 0 | 16 / 384 / 906 |
| line of two, 2 / 3 | 729 / 531,441 | 0 | 0 | 270 / 65,610 |
| fork, 2 / 3 (40,000 random) | 15,625 / 40,000 | 0 | 0 | 7,800 / 7,011 |
| line of three, 2 | 4,096 | 0 | 0 | 1,792 |

`loseStep` has "for every enabled move" inside it, an implication whose truth depends on later worlds; that is where the two part.

**`reach_shorten_at_places` in plain words.**
- G is whatever one wants to conclude. The cover says of each point: its place is unspent, or a route of at most n + 1 steps from it is already enough for G.
- At y with a route of at most n + 2 steps: if y's place is spent, the recorded sufficiency takes the route in hand and finishes.
- If y is in the target, the route is short already.
- Otherwise the route begins y → t. Spend y's place and record for y the continuation in hand ("n + 1 steps from y suffice"). Other spent points keep theirs, padded by `reach_pad`.
- Recurse at t with one place fewer and one step fewer. The new continuation says "a route of n steps from t, with y → t in front, is a route of n + 1 from y".
- With no place left, every point is spent, so the first alternative applies.
- A return to a spent point feeds the remaining route to the continuation recorded at the first visit. That is the loop cut.
- Nothing is decided, because equality is never asked: which place a point holds comes from its membership proof, and the route is a witness in hand.

I found no fault in the term. Two reservations:
- **It is not a case of the law.** It repeats the induction of `extend_at_places` with the conclusion as a parameter. The law gives only its double negation, since `¬ reaches n` is the falling chain of `regionStep` at `¬ W₀` with nothing decided.
- **It is one above its edge.** N → N − 1 held in every instance. I expect that to be provable; no proof is written or built.
