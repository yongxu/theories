## 3. The proof of `extend_at_places`, in my words

**Statement.** Given a list `left` of length n and a cover, every configuration kept for n steps is kept for n+1.

**What the cover says.** Every configuration either still has a place in `left`, or is already known to be kept for n+1 steps, which is one more than the horizon now in hand. At the start the list is `all` and `every` puts each configuration in the first alternative.

**Base, n = 0.** The list is empty, so the cover's first alternative is impossible and it gives "kept for 1 step" outright.

**Step.** Take y kept for n+1 and ask the cover about y.
- If it answers "kept for n+2", that is the goal.
- If it answers "y has a place in `left`", `remove_place` takes out that one occurrence and leaves `rest` of length n. Every member of `left` is either y (the removed occurrence) or in `rest`.
- The cover is rebuilt one level down: a configuration in `rest` keeps its place; y itself is covered by the hypothesis in hand (kept for n+1); a configuration covered as "kept for n+2" is kept for n+1 by `horizon_pred`.
- y's own witness gives the guard, an enabled move, and each outcome kept for n steps. The induction at n, with `rest` and the new cover, lifts each outcome to n+1. The same move then shows y kept for n+2.

**Why a place may be spent once.** When y's place is spent, y is recorded at horizon n+1. Every later question about y is at a smaller horizon, which `horizon_pred` answers from the record. So y never needs a place again. The list's length equals the remaining depth, and each descent spends at most one place, so places cannot run out before depth does. A duplicate of y left in `rest` is harmless: it only keeps the weaker alternative true.

**Why no decision of equality.** Nothing is ever compared.
- The case split is on the cover's own disjunction, a proof eliminated into a proposition.
- The place removed is the one the membership proof points at, by recursion on that proof.
- "z = y" arises only from the head case of membership, as data.
- The proof never asks for a first repeat on a branch, which was the difficulty point 2 of `AFTER-LEAN-EDGE.md` foresaw. It proves "kept for N implies kept for N+1" by the invariant, not the brief's set of fresh nodes.

`horizon_persists` then reads the move off the extra step. I found no fault. The recursion is structural on n and the log reports no axioms.
