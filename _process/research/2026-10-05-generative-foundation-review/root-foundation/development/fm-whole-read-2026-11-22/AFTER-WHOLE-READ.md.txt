**Mine, on paper, not checked by anyone.** For a process with every configuration in one list and equality of configurations decided, the region may be had with nothing else decided and no listing of moves. Take a witness of `Viable N s` with `N` the list's length: a tree of moves and outcomes of depth `N`. Let `K` hold the configurations that stand at a node whose path from the root repeats no configuration. Such a path has at most `N` nodes, so at such a node at least one step is left: the guard holds there and the tree gives an enabled move, and each outcome either extends the path without repeating, and is in `K` by its own node, or repeats a configuration earlier on the path, which is in `K` by that earlier node. So `K` is `Closed` and holds `s`, and `closed_viable` then gives every horizon. The one decision used is whether an outcome equals a configuration already on the path. If that is right, the statement is
`Viable P guard all.length s ↔ ∃ K, Closed P guard K ∧ K s`,
with the other side by definition: where it fails, no choice of moves keeps the guard for as many steps as there are configurations. **That would be an exact edge with moves in it: a closed region on one side, a bound on every plan on the other.** With a reading, through `keptFor_iff_viable`, it would be of classes, where a list of all classes is not to be had for predicates without extensionality, which the package does not admit: so the classes would have to be carried as listed data, and that is a design question, not a detail.

What would count as nothing gained, to be frozen with the statement when the returns are in and before any Lean: the law is already in the package under another name; it needs a premise that is the conclusion (a listed witness of the region); neither of the two old laws comes out as a case with its statement unchanged; or it is of configurations only and says nothing of a reading or of the lock.
