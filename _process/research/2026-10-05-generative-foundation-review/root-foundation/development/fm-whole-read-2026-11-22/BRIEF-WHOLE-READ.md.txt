## How to read a module

The package is `experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/`. For each module in your batch:

1. Read its opening account (the `/-! … -/` block at the head).
2. Read **every declaration's statement** (the signature of each `theorem`, `def`, `structure`, `inductive`, `abbrev`, up to `:=`) and its docstring. You need not read proof bodies; read one only where a statement cannot be understood without it.
3. Answer the fourteen questions below for that module.

Each answer begins with **one letter** saying how you know:

- `S` — from a **statement** you read. Name the declaration and its line: ``S: `Lasting.orbit_reaches_iff_variant` (l. 412): one sentence saying what it states, with its premises.``
- `D` — from a docstring or the opening account **only**; you did not find or read a statement that says it. Say where.
- `B` — only in a **built case** (a named instance, a witness, an example), not in a general law. Name it and its line.
- `N` — **Not found in what was read**, having read every statement of the module.
- `U` — **Not reached**: you did not read enough of the module to say.

An answer may have two parts (`S: … B: …`). Keep each to a few sentences. Use the Lean's own names. Do not interpret: say what the statement says, with its premises. "General" means stated for every process, law, relation or the like; "built" means of one particular model.

## The fourteen questions (the fields of your return, in this order)

1. `states` — What the module states, in two sentences: its headline law or laws, and whether they are general or of one built model.
2. `moves` — Is there a process with **moves** (a choice among steps: `Process S A`, a policy, a plan, an allowed set), and a law about what some choice or every choice keeps?
3. `reading` — Is there a **reading** (an observation, a class of things read alike, a rule made from what is registered), and a law in which what is registered decides something?
4. `share` — Is there a **share** (a count or fraction of one kind among several) and a rule or favouring **set again from it**?
5. `level` — Is there a **level** (a stock, a reserve, a condition with an amount) that can **run down**, with what comes back depending on what is left?
6. `giving` — Is what gives to that level, or what keeps a condition met, **itself a going-on of the model that can be lost** (a unit, a kind, a formation), as against a function or premise handed in?
7. `cost` — Does keeping or giving **cost the one that does it** (an offspring forgone, an act spent, its own standing drawn on)?
8. `multiplying` — Are there **units that leave units**, or kinds that form kinds, whose number changes by a law of the step?
9. `no_return` — Is there a **point of no return** or an irreversibility (once lost not regained; from here no course keeps it)? Is it **concluded or a premise**? Is it at a low level, or at the loss of something that gives?
10. `both_outcomes` — Is there a statement with **both outcomes** (an "exactly when", a dichotomy) of going on and ending, or of keeping and losing, for one model? General or built?
11. `comparison` — Is there a law that **carries** something between two processes, two starts or two ways (a simulation, an order kept by the step, "what this keeps that keeps", a covering, a dominating move, a set closed upward)?
12. `rank` — Is there a law of **ending within a bound**, a rank or variant that falls, a drain, a deadline?
13. `closed_region` — Is there a law of a **region closed under steps** (an invariant, a kernel, a certificate, a largest closed region, a fixed point, a standing population)?
14. `forms_change` — Is there a statement or built case in which **the going on continues while what is present changes** (a relay, an alternation, kinds coming and going), or in which **more can go on after** (a widening)?

Then one more field:

15. `how_read` — In one line: which of the fourteen you answered from statements, which from docstrings or the account only, which you did not reach; and how much of the module you read (all statements; statements to line N; the account only).
