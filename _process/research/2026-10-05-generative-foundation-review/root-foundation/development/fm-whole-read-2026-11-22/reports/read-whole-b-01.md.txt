Things the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre:
(1) Admission's Flow.support_passes_on (l.282) is a general relay law. In a balanced flow where only active formations give, activity needs support, requirements never loosen and no formation is supported for more than K+1 steps in a row, gains that recur are (up to double negation) given by formations that themselves gained support after any T. This is a going on carried by changing formations. With it, Admission's unsupported_stays and nothing_supported_stays are a concluded absorbing end at the loss of the givers, with built witnesses showing that each premise is needed (Restoring.*). So one module states both a continuing relay and an absorbing end, though not as one exactly-when.
(2) Admission's Balances.supported_iff_within_margin is an exact boundary: a part's net draw must stay within the margin of the levels without it. Its closes_nothing, opens_nothing and changes_nothing compare two level paths by inclusion.
(3) Amounts gives the general loss law and the least-loss law of rest for any ordered amounts. It also gives le_by_exchange, a comparison of two balanced counts by their exchanges. In these laws the replenishment is a handed-in sequence and never depends on what is left.
(4) Actuation's omitted_route_fails is built, not general: a plan selected from a list of configurations that omits the open-route configuration completes where it was selected and has no trace where the route is open. In this one model, what was registered decides the selection, and the selection fails on what it left out.
(5) In ActiveReading, which reading is used (whole-run, or every active event) decides which course a comparison satisfying C must or may rank higher.
(6) Arising's RenewedBy and activeRenewer_recurs make renewal a recurring activity of formations of the web. Its relation `renews` is handed in.
