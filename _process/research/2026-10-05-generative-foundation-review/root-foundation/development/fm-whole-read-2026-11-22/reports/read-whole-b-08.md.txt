Points the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre:
(1) ContinuingRevision.success_recurs and enabled_by_success: in that built model, what returns to the stock is tied to the process's own successful act, not only to a parameter. repeats_iff is an exact "goes on without end exactly when yield >= 9", stated for every yield. no_run_without_return holds for every controller.
(2) ContinuedClasses: whether a formation counts as renewing or consuming is fixed by the continuation (what continues what), not by names. Consuming passes backward through a merge and renewing passes forward into every branch of a split (consumingAlong_iff, renewingAlong_iff). Widening is likewise read along a continuation: Bud.readings shows a stretch that widens by name and adds nothing along a bud. This is a general law about reading a change of forms, but it has no dynamics of levels running down.
(3) Continuity.balancing_iff: balance along a relation is exactly conservation on every family the relation closes. Teleport shows that conservation of the total alone does not give it.
(4) Copying.copying_iff and lineage_all_iff are general laws of units that leave units, drawing on a stock or pool that is handed in. Nothing in these modules makes that stock a going-on given by the units themselves.
