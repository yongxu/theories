Things in these modules that the fourteen questions miss and that bear on the centre:
1. Dissipation.lean is a general drain law on flows, not on a built model. Its premises are balance and dissipation on a family of conditions. Under them, the family's stock falls each step by at least the number of formations taking from it (Balances.runs_down, l. 387), and the takers over all later steps number at most the stock (Balances.takers_within, l. 406). So taking from the family is not recurring (Balances.taking_not_recurring, l. 427), and recurring taking under dissipation forces every finite family to be open (Dissipative.opens, l. 452). Where the family is enclosed around, the opening is inflow from beyond (taking_draws_in, l. 587). It counts formations taking ('takers'), but no rule or favouring is set again from that count. Nothing in it says that what gives is itself lost when the stock runs down.
2. Direction.lean states as a general schema that, between a widening move and a narrowed one, the admission that differs lies beyond the acting group's coupling closure. It is selected through the pooled coupling of every pool holding that formation and of no pool within the closure (Direction.widening_selected_by_pool, l. 160). In plainer words, a selection reaches what sustains others only through a member coupled to it. Its docstring (l. 327-328) says the theorem does not say that such a selection occurs.
3. Dispatch.lean, of one built installation model, has a reserve that only runs down: drawn units are kept even when the act fails. Its gate registers only that the credential equals the epoch, so a capture accepted as current passes (captured_epoch_passes, l. 403): what the reading registers decides, and the gate does not reach who the actor is.
4. Diagonal.lean states in general that completing a family of readings within what it reads never closes and never returns, and that no finite stock holds what it misses. This is a general 'more after' law, but it is about readings, not about a going-on that draws on a stock.
5. Descent.lean adds living on and descent as relations for valuation. In its built cases a relabelling of which condition is which relatum disconnects living on (Mutualism.relabel_sustenance, l. 289), so the identification decides connection there.
