Things the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre:
1. Exclusion `outrun_reading_alike` (l. 173) is a general law. Once a part outruns a gated renewal, every score that reads the condition only after the end scores two flows alike, however a member's act differs. A member's restraint is invisible to such a selection, and the account says its own take is then the only difference the score can reward.
2. Exclusion `continuing_excludes` (l. 246) is general competitive exclusion within the bound `W.level T c + 1`. Exhaustion `seeking_exhausts` (l. 154) is general: a take that rises each step, under gated renewal, drives the level below the need within a stated window. From then on every dependent ends, renewers included. The rise is a premise: the account says it is "not derived" (l. 43).
3. Exclusion `admission_alike` (l. 485) is built: a comparison of level paths by what they admit does not separate a course with exclusion from one with sharing.
4. Experiment `Confound.confounded` (l. 345) and `varied_refutes` (l. 353) are built. A wrong model of what is irrelevant is never excluded while acts move in step with what the reading leaves out, and varying the act excludes it. `keepHistory_fixes_nothing_else` (l. 190) is general.
5. Extension `Comparison.climbs` and `Comparison.outgrows` (l. 88, l. 100) are general. Any natural-number score that ranks every extension above its base climbs without bound along an endless chain of extensions. The account says itself that ranking courses by widening is "taken, not shown".
6. FaultEnvelopes `copied_roster_same` (l. 167) is general: copied witnesses warrant nothing more. `availability_iff_separated` (l. 129) is an exact "exactly when" for a certificate, not for going on.
7. ExchangePremises `listed_iff_lpo` (l. 223) is general: listing what exchanges in every flow over Nat is exactly the limited principle of omniscience.
