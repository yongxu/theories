Four things the fourteen questions miss bear on the centre. (1) FreeEnergyGuarantees.RiskAndNeeds.restraint_unread is general. Under the outrun premises, every score of level histories that reads the condition only from T+n on scores a chooser's course and a restrained course alike. It is a selection to which restraint makes no difference once the running down has happened, a near-statement of the centre's "makes no difference to the selection". Beside it, need_ends says any chooser whatever, with any model and preferred level, ends. (2) Founding.Flow.confined_ends and ConfinedRise.ends are general. A lineage raised by selection within a whole, and confined to a family of conditions in excess whose renewal is gated on the family, has no member after T+n, whatever selection had raised it to. Founding.Nested.raises_iff_among (Sequestered) gives one way that a formed whole keeps selection within from turning the selection among wholes. Body.spread_outlasts is a built case where the lineage that is not confined goes on after the conditions it rose on have ended. (3) Frame's not_within_of_closed with Invariant.reach gives a general "no course leaves a region closed under the steps". ReturnsAlong.whole says that one returning element whose closure is the whole makes every closure the whole. (4) FreeEnergyGuarantees.SharedModels has three built cases. Foragers sharing one model and alike preferences end together (Exceeding), while the same forager alone continues. Two regulators reading the shared level continue with the level alternating, and three regulators end at once (three_end). These are both outcomes in built cases, decided by the joint draw, which no one chooser's preference reads.
