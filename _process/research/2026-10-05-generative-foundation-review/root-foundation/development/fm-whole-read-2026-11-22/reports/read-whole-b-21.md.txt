These are the grammar's own two outcomes, on some branch («延») and on every branch («尽»), and they are exclusive («延尽不并»). Telling them apart at every point of every model is exactly excluded middle («延尽之定当且仅当排中», «全三域之定当且仅当排中»). So the line between going on and ending is, by a theorem, where a course is not decided.
