I read all three modules whole: every statement and docstring of Identity.lean (2507 lines, read in four parts to the end), IdleRemoval.lean (268) and Imbalance.lean (207). No question was left unreached. The names these modules use from elsewhere (Web.Census, Flow.More, Consumed, Lost, Stutter, SameAt, withRefinement, the grammar's «闭», «亡», «生», «迭») were taken from how they are used here; I did not open the modules that define them. These returns have not been checked by another reader. I ran no Lean, build, test or gate, as the brief asks, so the full suite was deliberately not run.

Three things the fourteen questions miss bear on the centre:

1. Identity.lean bears directly on the second outcome, forms changing while the going on continues, because it proves that identity across steps is a reading. Which formation renews or consumes, whether undermining happens, and whether arising is unceasing all change under a re-identification that keeps every step's relations (`Turnover.reidentified`, l. 1515; `Dormancy.classes`, l. 2177; `Succession.readings`, l. 2027). `TideFlip.naming_meets_premises` (l. 1600) shows that a naming alone can meet every premise of `Unceasing.activeRenewer_recurs`. What survives every one-to-one reading is the count: under a census, More gives unceasing arising under every re-identification (l. 405), and under every continuation without splits (l. 643). `Layered.both` (l. 1958) is a built case of a lineage that is always active while its members arise without end. The module says outright that a web has no relation of part to whole.

2. The census balance under a continuation (`Census.balanceFrom`, l. 847) and its covering forms (l. 1155–1351) are general laws of how the number of formations changes across a step under splits and merges. They are the nearest thing here to 'units that leave units', but nothing in them has a cost or a level.

3. IdleRemoval.lean and Imbalance.lean are both equivalences between two representations. Removing idle steps keeps every premise and every total. Counting the imbalance as an inert relatum makes balance hold by construction, and turns balance into an activity premise at the added relatum. They bear on the centre only in showing that balance and step-timing are features of the representation. Neither has a level that runs down, a point of no return or a selection.
