Bearing on the centre:
- OneStep is a completeness result for one step and for runs. It says exactly which per-step patterns of taking, giving, takers and givers any world can show, condition by condition. It is a fixed frame of what the spine's flow laws allow, not a model of a selection. Its `standing_iff` (l. 1418) is the exact one-step law of coming and ceasing to stand. Its `step_*_iff` laws (l. 3333 to 3400) say that, without running down, the listed family can change freely from step to step, while running down forces continuity up to order (`Continues`). That is a general statement about how far what is present can change across steps.
- Onward says that within finitely many configurations, going on without end is exactly coming round to a return, decidable where branching is finite. Over all processes, runs from every family that keeps moving are exactly dependent choice. Its `KeepsMoving` and `within` are a general notion of a region from which some move stays inside. It has no stock, giver or cost: a run going on is by recurrence, not by forms changing.
- Neither module joins a selection to the running down of a level.
