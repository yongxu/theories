Points the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre:

(1) OptimumSeason is one built season. Its sharpest content is order-relative. bay_drained says every optimum of every order rising with the catch leaves the bay empty. It is general over orders but rests on the built Shared move. unshared_optima and unshared_no_room show the same bay kept or emptied among the optima when the order reads only the ground's catch. This is a reading deciding nothing about what it does not count, stated on one model. Its weighted bounds (mix_bay_catch, mix_kept) cover rational policies only. Real probabilities are said in the account to follow by continuity and are not stated.

(2) OverExchange adds no new law. Each theorem re-states a law from another module with balance, acting and taking discharged by withRest, and dependence discharged by ReadOffExchange. Several names its opening account cites are not declared in this file: World.withRest_closed_iff, ExchangeLaw.readWorld_closed_iff, World.withRest_counted, withRest_balanced, withRest_dependent_iff. Commuting exchanges not joined keeps every world premise (normalize_premises), keeps census existence (Reorders.census, reorder_census) and keeps the count of steps at which a family's stock rises (commuted_rise_count). So these readings cannot tell reorderings apart.

Which such class is one's own makes no difference to reach. That bears on the centre's question of what a reading registers. It has no stock, moves or ending.
