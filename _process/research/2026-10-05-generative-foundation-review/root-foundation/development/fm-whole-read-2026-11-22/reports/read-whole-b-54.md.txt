Four things bear on the centre that the fourteen questions do not catch:

1. **ReachableRanges: a general no-return for possibilities.** For every process, the whole possible never grows along a course (`Run.possible_of_next`). A step that loses a possibility can never be undone by reaching back (`Run.not_possible_returns`). This is a point of no return about what can still be admitted, not about a level.

2. **ReachableRanges: widening.** It gives a general order for a widening (`Reaching.Widens`, `WidensOrLater`, `KeepsWidening`, which needs a range no list holds). It shows that same-offset widening never ranks above a configuration that is wider now and starts a course (`not_above_wider_now`). Whether one ranks a range that keeps widening above one that is wider now is a premise beyond C (`WiderNow.comparison_undecided`).

3. **ReadInTime: a selection locked by reading from its share, turned only by a reading beyond it.** The selection sets its rule from its own share, and the lock is broken only by a reading beyond the share (the loss read against a bound). The window is exact at every step but at most one, closes once the late reading would favour the shapers, and has a deadline that holds under every schedule. But the "loss" is only a count read against `N`. It is not something that sustains the selection, and nothing in the model runs down or gives out, so the level and giving fields are N there.

4. **ReachedCascade: no level or loss at all.** What the first `k` couplings register decides every later act and every selection through the acts.
