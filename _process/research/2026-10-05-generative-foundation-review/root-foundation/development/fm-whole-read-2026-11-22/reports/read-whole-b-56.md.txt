1. ReadingsPersist.lean states, in general and with no premise beyond settling, that selection by persistence read off the pool its own course gives registers no removal (survivorsPersist_of_settled, l. 569). Read off a fixed pool, the same selection is turned by every removal (not_allPersist_of_removes, l. 578). Habitat.clearedBack_survivors_pass (l. 1357) is a built case: a course removes both lines, the selecting line included, and still passes that selection. The selection is turned only where what is lost is the giver to the selecting line's own source, and only through the line's own persistence (back_which_persist, l. 1273). This is close to the centre's first outcome: a selection that uses up what it rests on and is not turned by it. The general law takes settling as a premise. That finitely many lines always settle is argued in the account, not stated in Lean.
