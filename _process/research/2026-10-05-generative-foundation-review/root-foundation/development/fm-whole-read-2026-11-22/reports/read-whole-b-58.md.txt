Points the questions miss that bear on the centre:
(1) In RegimeMargin a condition's giving goes back to itself. In the coupled web, giving reads what is left of every condition but takes nothing from any of them, so it costs the giver nothing. Only the standing draw takes. So "cost" is N even where conditions sustain one another.
