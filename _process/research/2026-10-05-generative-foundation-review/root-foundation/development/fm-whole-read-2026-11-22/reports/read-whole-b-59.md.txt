Three things in this batch bear on the centre and fit the fourteen questions badly.
(1) Regulation.no_narrower_band (l. 886) is a general impossibility over every rule, not over one model. Any rule that reads ℓ steps late (ReadsLate, l. 678) and keeps its course within [a,b] under every rest moving the level by at most d a step must have a + 2(ℓ+1)d ≤ b. lateReturn_band (l. 1152) shows the bound is attained. This is a law about how late a regulating reading registers a running down, which is the 'early enough' of the direction, stated for one condition. relay_threshold (l. 1989) is an exact dichotomy between settling and oscillating without end for every flow that relays late.
(2) RelativePosition shows that positions (relative shares) do not register what ends the parts. excess_iff_spread (l. 469) says positions certify joint excess only through the spread and never certify that a step takes within what it gives. RaceWithin.same_positions_apart (l. 679) gives two built flows with the same positions at every step and opposite fates. race_ends_greatest (l. 430) is general: with gated renewal, n steps of joint excess end every part that depends on the condition at a need of at least m, the greatest included, for good.
(3) Rejoining.changes_through_coupling_apart (l. 233) and stays_beyond_response (l. 273) are general laws about when a difference made by an act can close. It closes only through a formation outside the part that couples the courses apart, and beyond the responsive reach it stays.
Regions.Omitted.loses_own (l. 308) is a built case: renewal judged over a represented region that omits a requirement coexists with the formation losing its own support through its own draw.

None of these modules has units that leave units, kinds that form kinds, or a Process S A with a law about every choice of step. In Regulation and Rejoining nothing that gives is itself something that can be lost. In RelativePosition it is, through the gated premise of race_ends_greatest.
