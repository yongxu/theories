Bearing on the centre, not caught by the fourteen questions:
1. **Resolution.** The general non-recurrence laws (losses_bound, quantum_bound, exchange_bound, growth_bound, fall_bound) are a running-down law with the stock as a falling measure. In an enclosed flow that is dissipated on, the count of changes of a given size is bounded by a multiple of the stock at T. refinement_feeds_witnesses (l. 2506) says that in every built case where change continues without end, what keeps it going is a giver that gives without taking: the unit's refinement, counted as a formation. So in this module, continued change is shown only where something is fed from outside the counted exchange.
2. **Response.** RespondsThroughCoupling (l. 110) is a supplied premise: a formation's exchange is decided by what couples into it. Loop.returns (l. 607) is a built case in which an act's cost comes back to the actor. It shows only when the rest responds, not when the rest is held fixed.
3. **ResponseLearning.** The receiver forms its program only from what it registers of the sender's responses (learn_through_interface). A faithfully transmitted program can lose a distinction the task makes, and the loss is kept in every generation (distinction_lost_every_cohort, succession_keeps_lost_distinction). A collapsed program changes once and then stays (limited_changes_then_stays). This is near the account in which "what is registered decides and what is left out is not recovered". But each successor starts from a fresh stock of 25 units, so nothing runs down across generations. Also, a peer's demonstration can repair the error (peer_repairs), so the loss is not irreversible in general.
4. **Restoration.** Consumption is defined, not derived from a dynamic: consumed_iff makes it exactly a dependence that never returns. Slowing.slowing_recovery is a built case in which the condition always returns, is never consumed, and no cycle length bounds the return. That bears on telling a slowing recovery from a loss, which the module's account leaves as an empirical question.
