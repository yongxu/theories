Four things here bear on the centre that the fourteen questions do not quite catch. (1) In Roles, embodiedArising_not_recurring (l.725) and its _on/_from forms say that under balance and dissipation embodied arising cannot recur in a closed finite world or in a family dissipated on. The built cases Shuttle, Throughput, SprayShuttle and Flicker each drop one premise and keep arising going. Throughput's arising goes on only by drawing in a fresh fuel at each step (Throughput.draws_in l.1401). That is a premise-by-premise map of when a going-on in which what stands changes can continue. (2) In Revision, Revising.opens_without_correction (l.210) is a built case in which forming a finer distinction uses the last stock needed to act on it. Revising.covered_not_enacted and linked_not_enacted keep discrimination, stock and enactment apart. (3) In RevisionRoutes, above_at_some_value (l.285) and above_of_more_value (l.291) say generally that once the value of the law's later acting is large enough, a route that keeps the law more surely stands above letting the revision through, and it stays above as the value grows. every_value_loss_iff_of_route (l.353) gives the exact condition under which the revision stays maximal at every value and every loss. (4) In Retaining, Preemption.within_renewal_yet_loses (l.475) and renewingAt_consuming_narrows (l.494) show that a stepwise class counts only dependences that have already formed, so a part can close what could have formed while no formed dependence registers it. Retaining has no Process with moves; its comparisons are between levels with and without a part (WithheldLevels).
