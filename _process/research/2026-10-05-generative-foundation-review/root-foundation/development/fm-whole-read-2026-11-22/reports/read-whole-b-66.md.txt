RuleCapture.lean (897 lines), RuleHandoff.lean (561) and RuleRebuilding.lean (1961, read in four parts to its end). I read proof bodies only where a statement needed them, which was mainly the decide witnesses. I opened Gated.lean (KeptBy, kept_along, change_located) and Pace.lean (turn, paced, stays_turned) to understand names the statements use. Nothing was left unreached.

Where answers were thin:
- RuleCapture has no stock that runs down, no cost and no giver that is a going-on. Its "share" is the share order on CarrierCounts. Its "moves" are only an arbitrary reading schedule and a perturbation, not a Process with choices.
- RuleHandoff is one finite built protocol. Its laws hold of every state of that protocol, but it is not a general law. I found no theorem saying presence never grows along an arbitrary trace: the statements cover one handoff, repeated handoffs and traces of handoffs only.
- RuleRebuilding is one family of nets. Its laws are stated for every value of its parameters (tenure, teaching age, renewal, untaught, strength, source), so they are not general laws over processes. Its renewal is a constant parameter, not a going-on. Its end clause (Ends: level 0 or the take beyond level plus renewal) is a modelling choice of the step; the account says so.

What the questions miss that bears on the centre:
1. RuleCapture's ReadsShare is close to the selection part of the centre. Locked, locked_stays, uniform_locks and Reversed.not_absorbed together say that a rule set again from the share it favours locks, and that only a change of the law or of how the rule reads leaves the lock. But nothing in it draws on anything that sustains the selection: no level, no running down.
2. RuleRebuilding comes closest to joining a selection-like door with a level that runs down. The door counts the teachers; the members draw on the level by the take. It has an exact iff of going on and ending («稳者恒持当且仅当在带», «稳开二试», «隙之律»). Its no-return («无持者不复», «无持者改不行») is concluded at the loss of the teachers, the holders who keep the rule held. It has a case where the going-on continues after the rule is lost: open entry, «无持而门开众在». It also has a case where it continues while every holder is replaced: «域中任满皆新», with «同规随类» reading continuation at a class.
3. RuleHandoff's no_count_only_decision says a coarse count of roster, presence and reviews cannot give the integration decision. That bears on the reading question: what is registered is too coarse to decide.
4. I saw no join between these modules and Flow or Lasting. RuleRebuilding's account says no law in it uses Flow.
