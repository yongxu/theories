Things the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre:
(1) Stock.lean has a general law of going on without end, stated exactly. repeat_covered_iff and product_repeat_iff say a course repeated without end is carried exactly when its first pass is covered and its return covers its draw; carried_iff says the same for an unending run. Its general running-down law is FallsBy with exhausted_within. But in Stock, replenishment is always a function of the action (replenish : A → Nat), never of the level left or of a giver that can be lost.
(2) Substitution.lean is the closest to the centre in this batch. For every RelationalRenewal.Law meeting its premises, persists_consuming states two outcomes at once: the sustaining formation, which renews the shared condition, ends for ever, while the focal consumer stays active at every step and changes its dependence from the shared condition to an alternative. Web.consumingIn_kept_iff, a law of every web, says when consumption survives every change of the consumer's own dependence. The alternative is a fixed level (needF) with no cost stated, which the opening account calls an application duty.
