Three things the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre.
(1) Training.Commons puts in one built model a reward that reads the stock, a renewal gated on the stock, and a rest that outruns the renewal. Whether restraint is selected turns on where the reading falls against what the rest takes, at any weight (renewed_restraint_settles_iff, outrun_restraint_unsettled, outrun_drawing_settles). Which settled law is reached is decided by the search's start or its neighbourhood, not by the reward (start_decides, outrun_neighbourhood_decides). In general, outrun_settles_iff says a reading after the rest has outrun the renewal changes no optimum at any weight. This is near the selection-uses-up-what-it-rests-on centre, but stated for a selection over act laws, and whatever runs down rests on Flow.outrun_reading_alike.
(2) Tolerance has exact, general restoration laws for any law that keeps inclusion. worst_start reads restoration off the course from total withholding. withheld_kept and total_stays are concluded laws of no return when no formation gives after total withholding. restores_iff (no clear chain of n waits) is an exactly-when for giving under givesFirst. On webs, web_tolerating and giftWeb make the givers formations whose own support can be lost, so giving is a going-on of the model, not a function handed in.
(3) Transactions has a reserve drawn by every transaction, the gate's load included even when the gate is closed, and never renewed. redundancy_exhausts shows faithful redundancy using up the reserve so that no completed move is left. That is a cost of checking borne by the one that checks, in a built model.
