Three things in this batch bear on the centre and the questions do not quite catch them.

(1) Units.lean, in its DependenceWebs section, has chain_ends_at_once (l. 508). It is a general coextinction law: where activity needs support, and the first link of a chain of positive dependences on embodied relata has ended from T, every link has ended from T. So the loss of what gives is carried down the chain. The first link's ending is a premise; nothing there concludes it. Next to it, Feeding.endless (l. 455) is a built case in which unfed consumption does not end, because a finer unit feeds it. And consumed_continues_transformed_in_unit (l. 396) says that a formation still supported after a loss has weakened or dissolved its dependence. That is a formation going on with its dependence changed, stated in general.

(2) ValuationFrames.Renewable.readings_differ (l. 258) is a built pair of runs. In the escalating run the focal formation consumes. In the dipping run it does not, because the condition returns, and the stepwise and whole-run readings take in different events. This is a built keeping-versus-losing contrast that the both_outcomes question only partly catches.

(3) Undecided.lean is the logic of revision, not of levels. The information order in which "once decided, stays decided" holds (stage_mono, Le.decided) is an irreversibility of gaining, not of loss. And moves_not_monotone (l. 737) says that a revision that never rests needs a map that is not monotone in information. That may bear on when a selection's revision cannot settle.
