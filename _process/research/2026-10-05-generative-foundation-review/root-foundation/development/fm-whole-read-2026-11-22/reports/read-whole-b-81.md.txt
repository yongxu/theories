Four things the fourteen questions miss that bear on the centre.
(1) Wholes.lean, Whole.family_excess_ends (l. 323). A whole of ending, listed parts ends, and no part arises from T+n on. This follows where the joint uptake of a family of conditions exceeds its replenishment for n steps, from a stock below n+m. The family's renewal is gated: whatever gives to a member depends on some member at need at least m. The parts' arising also depends on the family. The whole need not be a taker. Four witnesses (WholeWithin, WholeUnending, WholeUngated, and WholeExceeding as positive case) show that the excess, ending parts and the dependence of arising are each needed. The module says that the need for the gate and for the finite listing is not shown. This is the nearest thing in the batch to running down what sustains and then ending, but it is about one course of a given Web, not about a selection.
(2) WindowedWaiting.lean, keeps_split (l. 454) and restores_and_answers_iff (l. 516). Over every one-step law of a pair that keeps mutual giving, keeping inclusion forces either never restoring after one withholding or never answering a constant withholder. Only agreement does both, and it does not keep inclusion. A window of two does both while keeping inclusion, but only by widening the state (windowTwo_*). Under every window, absorbing one error excludes answering a pattern spaced at two (not_absorbs_and_answers_spaced). These are trade-offs between being able to turn back and being able to respond, and no question asks about that directly.
(3) WideningOnTheMap.lean, the logic calibrations. Getting from widening to generation for every chain is exactly weak excluded middle (forward_iff_wem). The converse is exactly excluded middle (back_iff_em). Even with decided holding it is Markov's principle (markov_of_keepsWidening_of_generation). So whether more can go on after is exhibited or only not refuted is itself a premise.
(4) WholeAmounts.lean. The whole-number reading that makes falling ranks end (not_falls_ever) is not available where amounts fall without end (Amounts.no_whole_reading). Over dense amounts, a bounded family can be new at every step without gain.
