Findings. (1) No law of ending within a bound, no falling rank, in any of the three; the two earlier passes' N on `rank` stands for each. `SharedCodes.Path.rank` (l. 1545) is an index of three nodes used in `least_least` (l. 1585), not a variant of a step. `boundedInTime` (Significance l. 204) bounds which steps have significance, not the time within which anything ends. (2) SharedCodes.lean: `edge` S, `rank_side` N. `run_kept_iff` (l. 515) and `run_keeps_apart_iff` (l. 840) are one-step exactly-whens of keeping against losing, on `Run (process adopt)`, with the move (the hearing `r.act n`) inside the condition. The losing side says only that agreement, or the hearer's telling apart, does not hold at step n+1. The keeping side has a closed region in separate statements (`run_agreement_kept` l. 495 under hearings `Closed` for the group, a sufficient condition narrower than the exact one; `all_agree_rest` l. 529 under every hearing). So this module is a case of an exact edge with a closed region and no rank, with moves in the statement. A reading in the brief's sense is not in the condition: the condition is on the forms themselves. (3) Significance.lean: `edge` S only in a weak form, `rank_side` N. Its exactly-whens (`Web.retains_iff_includes` l. 309 and its siblings) are equivalences between two ways of saying one comparison of two level paths of a `Web`, under the hypothesis `Unrestricted sig`. They are not of one process's course, have no step, no closed region, no moves. The exact boundary by a margin that the opening account tells of (`Flow.Balances.retains_iff_within_margin`, `loses_iff_beyond_margin`) is not declared in this module (it is named as of `Becoming.Retaining`); I did not open it. (4) Tail.lean: `edge` N as an exact edge. `TailRuns.indicators_sum` (l. 687) is a dichotomy of every run's level (vanishes from some step on, or never), exclusive and exhaustive, but it gives no condition on the process, start or configuration that says which: the outcome is decided by the two rules handed in (`FalseRule`, `VanishRule`, omniscience as rules). Neither side is a closed region or a rank.
