### `no_return`

- pass b: S, in two pieces, with no single world-level theorem joining them. (a) Concluded for every census of every world meeting a selection that binds counting (`counting_under_sound` l. 809, `counting_binds_iff` l. 861, `CountsOf.company_binding` l. 2734): 0 < a t -> 0 < n t. That is concluded from the premises of balance, embodiment and acting (or taking with dissipation). S: `lpo_of_finite_ends` (l. 1559): if every finite world meeting the eight with decided activity had a step after which nothing arises, LPO would hold. `lpo_of_census_ends` (l. 5085): the same for a step after which nothing ceases or arises, in every world with a change bound and the company condition.
