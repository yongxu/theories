## Dephasing.lean  (453 lines)

### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: B: `plus_kept_iff` (l. 340) equates keeping the starting plus response at any positive time with vanishing of the starting cross-term sum in every run of this law.
- pass b: S (built): `dephase_keeps_response_iff` (l. 211): dephasing keeps the plus response exactly where m.zo + m.oz = 0. `plus_kept_iff` (l. 340) gives the same along every dephasing run. This is about keeping or losing a response, not about going on or ending.

### `no_return`
- pass a: B: `no_restore_along` (l. 372) concludes that no map from a positive-time state recovers both distinct starts of the merged dephasing runs. This is loss of information.
- pass b: S (built, concluded): `no_uniform_dephase_inverse` (l. 200) and `no_restore_along` (l. 372): no map restores the prepared and the coherent control from their dephased or later states. `not_lossless` (l. 363). The loss is of the cross terms, not of a giver. Contrast `returns` (l. 416): recording returns every configuration after two steps.

### `rank`
- pass a: B: `settles` (l. 306) bounds time to rest by one dephasing step.
- pass b: S (built): `settles` (l. 306): every run of the dephasing law is, from step 1 on, the dephased start; it rests within one step.

### `closed_region`
- pass a: B: `diagonal_invariant` (l. 310) and `mass_invariant` (l. 315) give invariant fibers for the dephasing process.
- pass b: S (built): `diagonal_invariant` (l. 310), `mass_invariant` (l. 315), `x_norm_invariant` (l. 282), `z_norm_invariant` (l. 286): fixed diagonal, weight and norm are Invariant regions of these laws.

### `moves`
- pass a: S: `operations_trace_iff` (l. 249) identifies endpoints for any list of endomorphism moves with their ordered fold. B: X and Z each keep the norm (`x_preserves_norm`, l. 134; `z_preserves_norm`, l. 137).

### `reading`

## DrawHistory.lean  (272 lines)

### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: S: `history_decides` (l. 149) maintains at least x under H when Meets, Keeps and H+y≤x≤hi hold, and reaches g 0 forever after T when H′ exceeds all giving up to hi for an initial T≥x−H′+1 steps then returns to H≥g 0. These are sufficient histories. B: `Loop.two_paths` (l. 247) gives the two outcomes from eight.
- pass b: S: `history_decides` (l. 149) is a conjunction of two outcomes under the same later draw H for the same start x: under the raised-then-returned schedule the level is g 0 from T on, and under H all along it never falls below x. `histories_differ` (l. 162): with g 0 < x the first is strictly below the second at every step from T. `two_levels` (l. 129) gives a held level and an inflow rest under one draw. It is a dichotomy decided by history, not an exactly-when. B: `Loop.two_paths` (l. 247), `Loop.bounds` (l. 259).

### `no_return`
- pass a: S: `inflow_kept` (l. 74) concludes conditional trapping at the low level. `leaving_needs_a_lower_draw` (l. 85) produces an intervening draw below g 0 for every actual departure. This is a necessary condition for departure.
- pass b: S: `inflow_kept` (l. 74): a level at g 0 at step T, with every draw from T on at least g 0, is g 0 at every later step. `leaving_needs_a_lower_draw` (l. 85): a level at the inflow at T that is not the inflow at T + k had a draw below g 0 at some step between. `history_decides` (l. 149): after draws above every giving for T ≥ x - H' + 1 steps and then H ≥ g 0, the level is g 0 forever. These are concluded, from the premise that later draws stay at least g 0. They are at a low level (the inflow rest), not at the loss of something that gives. The account says leaving is not shown to be recovery.

### `rank`
- pass a: S: `collapses` (l. 112) gives the deadline x−H+1 in natural subtraction assuming g z<H throughout z≤hi and x≤hi.
- pass b: S: `collapses` (l. 112) is an explicit deadline: under a draw above every giving up to hi, the level from x ≤ hi is the inflow from step x - H + 1 on (through RegimeMargin.Adjusted.taken_whole). `inflow_from` (l. 101): once the run is at or below H at step m, it is the inflow from m + 1. B: `Loop.bounds` (l. 259): under draw 4, every level up to 8 is 1 from step 9.

### `closed_region`
- pass a: S: `inflow_rests` (l. 61) makes inflow a fixed point if H≥g 0. `two_levels` (l. 129) also keeps the orbit above its high start given Meets, Keeps and H+y≤x≤hi.
- pass b: S: `inflow_stays` (l. 66): with g 0 ≤ H, g 0 is a fixed point of the step. `inflow_kept` (l. 74): {g 0} is kept under any schedule of draws at least g 0. `two_levels` (l. 129) / RegimeMargin.holds: the band at or above x is never left, with Keeps g H hi a premise.

### `moves`

- pass b: N: no Process with moves, policy or allowed set. The schedule of draws Hs is a function handed in. Read as choices: inflow_kept (l. 74) holds for every schedule whose draws from T on are at least g 0, and history_decides (l. 149) is about one schedule, raised for T steps and then brought back.
