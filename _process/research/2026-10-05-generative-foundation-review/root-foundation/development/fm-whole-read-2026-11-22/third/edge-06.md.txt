## Drift.lean  (1554 lines)

### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: S: `Replacement.valueBy_partition` (l. 992) gives an exact three-way division into fixed, lost and still mixed at every horizon given Steps. `lost_weight` (l. 285) and `whole_weight` (l. 395) give the two pure outcomes of one generation weights q^n and p^n.
- pass b: S: `Replacement.valueBy_partition` (l. 992): with Steps, every history ends fixed, lost or varied, and their weights total T^k. `fixation` (l. 635) and `Replacement.fixationBy` (l. 1095) bound the weight of fixing on both sides, with the varied vanishing. `lostBy_bounds` (l. 1214) does the same for losing. These are general, as weights over histories, not an exactly-when for one course. B: `TwoMembers.one_generation` (l. 1531), `Favoured.two_members` (l. 1544).

### `no_return`
- pass a: S: `sampled_none` (l. 478) and `sampled_all` (l. 491) conclude that every weighted final-count reading from an endpoint stays concentrated there. `Replacement.valueBy` (l. 662) stipulates a self-step at nonmixed counts; `valueBy_stays` (l. 687) extends it to all horizons.
- pass b: S: the ends are absorbing. `sampled_none` (l. 478): from 0, every history keeps none. `sampled_all` (l. 491): from N, every history keeps all. `Replacement.valueBy_stays` (l. 687): a population of one kind stays. Each is concluded from the definitions, at the loss or fixation of a kind; a lost kind leaves no offspring. The counterpart: `falls_back_ge` (l. 305) and `Replacement.lost_ge` (l. 1508) / `lost_geBy` (l. 1108): losing the favoured kind is never ruled out from a mixed count.

### `rank`
- pass a: S: `varied_vanishes` (l. 573) bounds still-varied history weight by r^k when r+2=N^N and h≤N. `Replacement.varied_vanishesBy` (l. 1073) gives the round bound with common total T, a lower bound c on mixed upward weights, c≤T, and r+c^(M+1)=T^(M+1).
- pass b: S: `varied_vanishes` (l. 573): with r + 2 = N^N, the histories still varied after k generations weigh at most r^k. `Replacement.varied_vanishesBy` (l. 1073): at most r^m after m rounds of M + 1 steps, with r + c^(M+1) = T^(M+1). `climbsBy` (l. 1038): fixing within k steps weighs at least c^k. These are geometric bounds on the weight still undecided, not a variant falling along one course.

### `closed_region`
- pass a: S: `sampled_none` (l. 478), `sampled_all` (l. 491) and `Replacement.valueBy_stays` (l. 687) make the pure populations absorbing in their respective models.
- pass b: S: the absorbing ends (`sampled_none` l. 478, `sampled_all` l. 491, `valueBy_stays` l. 687). Also a conserved quantity: `Replacement.potentialBy_harmonic` (l. 813) and `valueBy_potential` (l. 821): with Steps, the potential keeps its weighted value over any number of steps.

### `moves`
- pass a: S: `Replacement.valueBy_mixed` (l. 676) sums weighted up, down and unchanged successors at a mixed count. `Replacement.valueBy_stays` (l. 687) keeps a nonmixed start for all steps. These are weighted alternatives.
- pass b: N: there is no Process with moves, policy or allowed set. Samplings and histories are all counted and weighed (Typicality.courses, sampled, valueBy); none is chosen.

### `reading`
- pass a: S: `Replacement.valueBy_potential` (l. 821) gives the weighted reading of final potential for any starting count and horizon from its initial value under Steps.
- pass b: N: `sampled N f k h` (l. 434) and `valueBy … f` (l. 662) total a function f of the final count, which the docstrings call a reading. No law has what is registered deciding something.
