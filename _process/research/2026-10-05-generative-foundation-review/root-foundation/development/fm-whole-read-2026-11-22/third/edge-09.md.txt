## GrammarConditionals.lean  (691 lines)

### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: B: `«不穷与亡不同核之例»` (l. 595) exhibits an endless start and an ending start in one net, outside one another's core. S: `«亡者异当且仅当有后»` (l. 43) gives the exact condition for alteration under F once ending under E is assumed.
- pass b: S: «亡而两向有常则异当且仅当通» (l. 138): for what ends under E, with F a class constant under R and its converse, altering under F exactly where it passes through (never-ending going and coming); «亡者异当且仅当有后» (l. 43): what ends under E alters into a continuation under F iff it has a successor under F. General.

### `no_return`
- pass a: S: `«线不返则短于所列»` (l. 264) assumes each indexed place lacks a positive continuation back to itself. `«无环则无俱有»` (l. 430) concludes no mutual reach under acyclicity.
- pass b: S: «不返而有限则非不穷» (l. 287): where no place on the line from x reaches itself and a list holds every place of that line, the line is not endless (concluded; constructively no end is exhibited); «断则无相续» is cited elsewhere. Not at the loss of something that gives. B: «亡而息之例» (l. 49): an end with nothing after it ceases and does not alter.

### `rank`
- pass a: S: `«线不返则短于所列»` (l. 264) proves n<l.length for an n-step line listed in l with no return at any place. `«不返而有限则非不穷»` (l. 287) gives negated endlessness under the corresponding reachable-set premises.
- pass b: S: «线不返则短于所列» (l. 264): a line of n continuation steps none of whose places reaches itself, with all places in a list l, has n < l.length (pigeonhole bound on a non-returning line).

### `closed_region`
- pass a: B: The step relation in `«相须之例»` (l. 671) preserves the first two bits of every allowed successor. Each agreeing pair admits such successors.
- pass b: S: «有后而两向有常则通» (l. 115): with a successor under F and F constant both ways, x passes through; B: «相须之例» (l. 671): a joint class constant while neither class alone is; «不穷与亡不同核之例» (l. 595).

### `moves`

### `reading`
- pass a: S: `«核同则同不穷»` (l. 560) carries endlessness under a class E to any occurrence in the same forward core. B: `«生于核下可变之例»` (l. 577) puts a beginning and a nonbeginning in the same forward core.
- pass b: S: «核同则同不穷» (l. 560): for a class E, what the core puts with an occurrence whose line under E never ends also has a never-ending line; «无异当且仅当不分续» (l. 361): nothing alters from E under F exactly where F parts no continuation (up to double negation). B: «生于核下可变之例» (l. 577): beginning is not kept along the forward core.
