### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: S: `GrammarLaws.trace_on` (l. 2419) gives a later regional ending or a k-step continuation from x, for each k, with unit stage advance, forward closure, and decisions of ending or successor after N. Its alternatives need not be exclusive.
- pass b: S, general, for return vs being drawn in: `«定后于有后则入当且仅当不返»` (l. 1527): with the successor fixed within x's closure and some successor, «入» R x ↔ ¬ «及» R x x. `«有限遍后定后则定前当且仅当皆返»` (l. 1736). `«四比之周»` (l. 2776) gives four cases for two relations. N for a dichotomy of going on and ending.

### `no_return`
- pass a: S: `«入当且仅当有不返»` (l. 1478) proves that reaching a closed region excluding the start is equivalent to reaching a point from which the start is unreachable. `«定后于有后则入当且仅当不返»` (l. 1527) equates this with no self-return if x has a successor and successors are unique throughout its closure.
- pass b: S, concluded, general: `«入当且仅当有不返»` (l. 1478): x is drawn in (some closed region it lies outside holds something it reaches) exactly where it reaches some y that does not reach back. `«有后不返则入»` (l. 1522). `«定后于则返者不入»` (l. 1512): with the successor fixed within x's closure, an x that reaches itself is not drawn. This is about reach, at no particular level and not at the loss of something that gives.

### `rank`
- pass a: S: `«亡者不在长迭»` (l. 1329) excludes a longer path from m once a k-step path ends, given uniqueness of the successor wherever paths to those endpoints may diverge. `«尽则不屡»` (l. 1792) excludes recurring F-steps on an eventually Q-line assuming inductive ending of Q-closure followed by F.
- pass b: S: `«尽则不屡»` (l. 1792): falls that end (the inductive «尽» of Q-courses then F) do not recur along a line of Q steps, with no decision; no converse is established (account). S: `«线有限不并则回»` (l. 1647): a line held by a list, merging nothing at its places, comes back to its start within the list's length. Count bounds: `«线终不过所列»` (l. 1374), `«间无生则间亡不过所覆»` (l. 2282). ### `closed_region`
- pass a: S: `«所闭之证»` (l. 300) certifies exact closure by membership of the start, step closure, and inclusion in the start's closure. `«闭含所闭»` (l. 294) places this closure in every closed region holding the start.

S: `«定后遍后则核为其内最粗同行之类»` (l. 2643): with a fixed, total successor, the core of a class is the coarsest class inside it that R goes alike across.

### `moves`

### `reading`
