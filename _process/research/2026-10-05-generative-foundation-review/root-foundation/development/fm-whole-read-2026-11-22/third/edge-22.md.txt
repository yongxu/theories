### `both_outcomes`

- pass b: S: `relay_threshold` (l. 1989): for 0<a, a≤b, M>0, (ℓ+1)M ≤ b+1: every relaying flow from every start held below a settles in [a,b] iff a+(ℓ+1)M ≤ b+1. Past it, `held_oscillates` gives crossing without end. This is general over all flows L' W' c' and is a dichotomy of settling versus oscillating, not of going on and ending. `no_narrower_band` with `lateReturn_band` give the exact band.
