### `moves`

### `reading`
- pass a: S: `Generations.ByNumber` (l. 371) reads each member by its offspring number. With that reading `ByNumber.sum_eq` (l. 428) gives next generation size as the current total and `discord_none` (l. 432) gives zero discordance.
- pass b: S: `Generations.ByNumber` (l. 371) reads each member by its offspring number (amount t p = offspringNumber t p). `Breeding.Combination` (l. 1010) and `Breeding.Normal` (l. 1015) make a breeding value from carried amounts, fitted by normal equations (a regression). The read amount decides how its mean moves: `Breeding.response_rises_iff` (l. 1151) says that with d > 0 and exact transmission the mean breeding value never falls and rises exactly where its self-concordance is positive. `Generations.ByNumber.rises_exact_iff` (l. 469) says the mean offspring number rises exactly where 0 < concordAt t. The reading does not decide who leaves offspring: kids are handed in.

## RoomNet.lean  (949 lines)

### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: S: `«温不穷当且仅当有给»` (l. 632) gives the equivalence for every room with margin at least two; `unended_at_one` (l. 661) gives the exceptional margin for any heater.
- pass b: B (built family, for all m and c): `«温有常当且仅当有给»` (l. 606): for m ≥ 1, warmth is a constant class under `net m c` iff 1 ≤ c. `«温不穷当且仅当有给»` (l. 632): for m ≥ 2, no line of warmth from the set level ends iff 1 ≤ c. `unended_at_one` (l. 661): at m = 1 none ends either way, so the margin of two is needed.

### `no_return`
- pass a: S: `net_open` (l. 126) forces every open successor to remain open; `cold_step` (l. 202) concludes the open, zero-level unheated state has only itself as successor.
- pass b: B, concluded from the rule: `cold_after` (l. 197): warmth ends at the unheated room's open occurrence at level 1 (its only successor is cold). `cold_step` (l. 202): the cold open occurrence steps only to itself. `cold_rests` (l. 587): it is 全守. `net_open` (l. 126): an open window stays open. The loss is at the low level (level 0), not the loss of something that gives.

### `rank`
- pass a: S: `cools` (l. 193) and `net_open` (l. 126) give a forced unit decrement at positive open unheated levels. `empties` (l. 769) separates the open level j from any warm heated occurrence at stage j under positive margin and heater.
- pass b: B: `cools` (l. 193) loses one unit a step, and `cools_to` (l. 625) reaches level 1 from every open occurrence at j+1. `empties` (l. 769): an unheated room open at j is told from every warm heated occurrence at stage j, because the window empties it in j draws. `«开窗以温层如其余乃分»` (l. 781): at margin n+1, stage n holds and stage n+1 tells them apart. No general variant law.

### `closed_region`
- pass a: S: `Matched.closed` (l. 328) makes the open and shut set-level states closed under the matched thermostat for every positive margin.
- pass b: B: `Unheated.closed` (l. 225), `Matched.closed` (l. 328: the set level, shut or open, is closed under the matched net), `Overshooting.closed` (l. 413), `Overshooting.closed_open` (l. 504). `shut_rests` (l. 570): with no window, every room at its set level is 全守. `cold_rests` (l. 587). `«给如所取则全守»` (l. 390): the matched thermostat is 全守 at every positive margin.

### `moves`
- pass a: B: `net_shut` (l. 132) allows the window to stay shut or open. `«平衡守而有终»` (l. 290) gives keeping and a reachable end from the same unheated start at three.
- pass b: B: `net` (l. 81) is a relation of alternatives: a shut room may stay shut or open its window (`net_shut`, l. 132), and an open window stays open (`net_open`, l. 126). It is not a `Process S A` here. `heated_warm` (l. 171): with m ≥ 1 and c ≥ 1, every successor is warm (every branch keeps warmth). `Unheated.keeps` (l. 238) keeps along the shut branch, while warmth ends along the window's (`Unheated.ends`, l. 243).

### `reading`
- pass a: S: `heater` (l. 70) and `next` (l. 73) give c iff the post-exchange level is below m, otherwise zero. `before_at_one` (l. 851) leaves zero from one if the heater reads before exchange.
- pass b: B: the classes `alike` (warm or not, l. 97) and `sameLevel` (l. 88), and the heater reading the level left (`heater`, l. 70). `«开窗则一层可分»` (l. 716): with the window in the net, the first stage of the level tells the unheated room from every heated one. `«有给之室以温不可分»` (l. 793): warm or not puts two heated rooms in one core class. `«不开窗则同核»` (l. 698): with no window, every room at its set level is one core class at the level class, whatever its heater.
