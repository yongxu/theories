### `both_outcomes`
- pass a: S: `Flow.possible_iff_covers` (l. 3704) says some capacity-respecting flow permits drawing throughout a stretch longer than initial stock iff the active feeder class covers demand at every step. It assumes decidable identities and activity, distinct listings, a nonempty family and an outside consumer active throughout. This is finite-stretch feasibility.
- pass b: S, general. `Flow.possible_iff_covers` (l. 3704): some flow lets f, outside the class, draw past the stock exactly when, at every step of the stretch, the active members' capacity covers the step's demand; otherwise every need is vacuous (`Flow.needs_of_class_short`, l. 3688). `Flow.needs_giver_iff_short` (l. 2965), `Flow.needs_giver_iff_shortfalls_pos` (l. 3593) and `Flow.needs_iff_through_short` (l. 4775) give need exactly when there is a shortfall. `Flow.feedsAlone_iff` (l. 2518) gives feeding alone exactly when the member is active and its capacity covers the demand. B: `Capacities.second_alone_first_not` (l. 5131), `SharedCopies.none_needed` (l. 5306).

### `no_return`
- pass a: S: `Flow.passed_le_drop` (l. 3016) entails that a zero supply stays zero across each balanced, unfed pass-on step. B: `OwnStores.second_store_spent` (l. 5474) proves zero subsequent store fall at every t ≥ 1 on the specified path, and `second_spent` (l. 5479) concludes zero giving by that present feeder for 1 ≤ t < 3 in every `FedFromStores` flow on the stretch.
- pass b: S: `Flow.coupled_ending_ranked_below` (l. 6125) concludes, from its premises, that no generation after T + n has a member: an ending at the loss of the family of conditions that gives, via GroupEnding. `Flow.passed_zero_of_exhausted` (l. 3023) concludes nothing is passed on at one step from the premises unfed and stock zero: a low level, one step, not stated as never regained. `Weighting.ended_below` (l. 6108) takes as a premise a course admitting nothing marked late. ### `rank`
- pass a: S: `Flow.needsSome_without_end` (l. 3735) gives a deadline for replenishment. If a family balances from T, only active class members give to it, and a present active consumer draws positively at every step, some member gives in each window of `stock N + 1` steps starting at N ≥ T.
- pass b: S: `Flow.needsSome_without_end` (l. 3735) is a deadline: drawing at every step from T, some member of K gives within the next `stock N + 1` steps after every N. `Flow.needsSome_on` (l. 1911): drawing for more steps than the stock needs giving within the stretch. `Flow.uptake_le_tiers` (l. 3069): what is drawn from the bottom of a hierarchy is at most what it held plus what was given to its top. `Flow.coupled_ending_ranked_below` (l. 6125) bounds the ending at T + n from the excess over n steps and a stock below n + m.
