### What Parts 7 and 8 add (each sentence is a theorem, premises stated)

### Rule 2 boundaries

- `gaussian_seq_youdenJ_wall`, `gaussian_seq_sample_complexity_lower_bound`, and `sequential_gaussian_lag_sample_wall` bound measurable events on the **`T`-sample sum `gaussianSeqLaw`** (and equivalently on the sample mean via `gaussianSeqMean_map_eq`); a formal sufficiency theorem over general `ℝ^T` statistics is not proved in Lean here.
- `sequential_gaussian_lag_sample_wall` relies on the unit-rate sampling premise `(T : ℝ) ≤ t` (at most one sample per unit continuous time before crossing `x_crit`).
- `convPow` models `T`-fold convolution of independent, identically distributed additive Gaussian observations with fixed variance `v`; it does not model autocorrelated noise, non-stationary drift within the `T`-sample window, or closed-loop adaptive threshold updates.
- Pairing `gaussian_seq_lab_witness` or `sequential_gaussian_lag_sample_wall` with `B5` trajectories remains a recognition (Rule 2), not a proof that the simulator's observations are i.i.d. Gaussian.
