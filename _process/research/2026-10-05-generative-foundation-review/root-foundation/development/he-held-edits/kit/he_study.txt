Edits held "for the next full rebuild" slip when a rebuild comes without them: OC5's approved wording for `Becoming.GrammarPath` missed WRP2's rebuild of that module. So the coordinating session asked for one landing that rebuilds the package once, on purpose, after a rehearsal outside the repository, and applies every edit held for a rebuild ([notes](development/he-held-edits/DESIGN.md)).
