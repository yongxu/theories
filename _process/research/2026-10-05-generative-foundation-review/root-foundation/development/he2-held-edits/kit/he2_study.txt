## {N}. The held edits, in one near-full rebuild (HE2)

HE (§455) showed that edits held for a rebuild land best together, on purpose, after a rehearsal. HE2 does that for seven edits. The board's HE2 row listed them after HE, each written and checked outside the package by its owner. Most of them change GrammarLaws or the grammar's modules, which almost every module imports, so each one alone would rebuild most of the package. One lander gathered the owners' kits and applied them in one pass ([design](development/he2-held-edits/DESIGN.md)).
