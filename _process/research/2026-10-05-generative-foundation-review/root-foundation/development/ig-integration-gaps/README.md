# IG: where the account is not yet one picture (integration scan, 3 November 2026)

AI-written (a Claude Code session, with twenty Claude Opus subagents, read-only). Reading time: this page 3 minutes; [the gap map](GAP-MAP.md) about 35 minutes, its five leading items and its general cause (the last two sections) about 10.

**Why.** Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's (2020) theorem on misaligned proxies stood in the account only as a known result it "restates more weakly": their stronger result and their remedy sat outside the picture, and the account's own analogues of that remedy were never joined to it (that case is now board item ZH). The question was whether there are more such cases, and where the account is not yet one coherent whole.

**How.** Nine readers, each on one slice:
- the chapters in five slices: 01–02, 03, 04 with the evolution line, 05, 06–07;
- 08 with the known-results table, the third-parts audit, UE's plan and the sciences dossier;
- the alignment line;
- the fronts;
- a sweep from the fields' side, about forty established frameworks each searched for in the repository.

They looked for four kinds of gap:
- a framework cited but not contained;
- a field missing;
- the account's own results stating one thing without being joined;
- unity claimed and not shown.

One skeptic per reader then tried to refute each of its findings against the whole repository. Of 96 findings, 91 survived, most of them narrowed (`partly`), and 5 were refuted as already integrated. One agent merged them into [the gap map](GAP-MAP.md); a completeness critic then said what the scan missed and checked eight further findings itself ([CRITIC](CRITIC.md)). [`findings.json`](findings.json) holds every finding with its verdict, the skeptic's reason, and the corrected wording where given. [`scan.js`](scan.js) is the workflow as run.

**Standing.** Every item is one reader's finding checked by one skeptic, so it is a lead, not a verdict. A session taking an item re-reads its sources first. Literature named from the readers' prior knowledge is marked so in the map and was not re-read.

**What it found, in one paragraph.** The four general causes are in the map's last section:
- a prior-art verdict ("restated", "near-definitional") is taken as the end of the work, so 08 §8 records what the account owes a framework but not what the framework holds that the account lacks;
- studies map a framework or prove a join, and the mapping never reaches the home section;
- each model has its own carrier, and a resemblance is recorded instead of proved, so a join between two landed items has no owner;
- generated and secondary text is not re-synced after a correction.

**Taken so far.** On the coordinator's order, items 5.1 (its text half), 1.1, 1.4 and 1.5 landed before the freeze, in the same landing as this folder. The other items wait on the board's order.
