# PF2's three 07 §4.6 edits: the IV3 line's answer (25 October 2026)

The text reviewed is `development/pf-path-and-crux/round2/texts_pf2.py` at commit `0567ffe4d`. It was read against `development/pf-path-and-crux/tree2/Becoming/GrammarPath.lean`. Every Lean name the three edits cite was read in full: `«出类随行»`, `«出类随线»`, `«出类不随则不至终有常»`, `CleanStep.not_constant_to_end`, `FiniteWake.constant_to_end`, `FiniteWake.not_constant`, `FiniteWake.not_held` and `FiniteWake.wake_follows`. The glosses of the three laws and of the four `FiniteWake` theorems match their statements, hypotheses included.

**Answer: yes, with the changes below.** Take them as written or in words that say the same, with no second look needed. The Open item needs one of the two courses in 4.

1. **The witness sentence reads as a law.** The sentence "in a finite net without cycles, the class holding a member and part of what follows it is constant up to the net's end though not constant, …" can be read of every finite net without cycles. `CleanStep` is such a net, and its class holding a member and part of what follows it is not constant up to the net's end. Suggested: "in a finite net without cycles, a class holding a member and part of what follows it can be constant up to the net's end though not constant, not holding the member's effects, while something outside it follows every step of its line that goes on (`FiniteWake.constant_to_end`, `FiniteWake.not_constant`, `FiniteWake.not_held`, `FiniteWake.wake_follows`)".

2. **"Close".** The phrase is "read at a member the class does not close". The chapter's term for `«所缘同于»` is holding a member's effects, and the glossary gives "closed" other senses (a closed route, closed capture). Suggested: "read at a member whose effects the class does not hold". The module doc and the docstring of `«出类随行»` are not chapter text, but the same change would serve there.

3. **Label the intention reading where it is used.** The sentence "A self drawn by what one intends, holding only part of one's effects, can still pass P2 (`FiniteWake`)" cites a Lean name for a reading. `FiniteWake`'s own doc calls that a reading, and the bullet's labels come only after the sentence. Suggested, as the module doc already does it: "Read as a self drawn by what one intends [AI reading; nothing in the Lean marks an intended effect], a class holding only part of one's effects can still pass P2 (`FiniteWake`), so P2 does not exclude it and P4 carries the crux; such a class, constant up to the net's end, has something outside it follow every alike step that goes on ([above](#r-what-leaves-a-class-follows-its-line))."

   Optional: "which makes intention decisive for what may be done" overstates the doctrine. The same sentence goes on to cite its proportionality condition, which weighs more than intention. Suggested: "which lets what is intended, as against only foreseen, bear on what may be done".

4. **The Open item.** It has two problems, both checked in [review_pf2_open_item.lean](review_pf2_open_item.lean). That file copies the definition from `GrammarPath`, sits outside the package, was checked with Lean 4.29.1 and reports no axioms.
   - **The two-block clause has no Lean in `tree2`.** The clause is "with its counterpart for what the class holds, is constancy up to the net's end for a class of two blocks". It is true where the two blocks are given by a two-valued tag (`two_blocks`). For a class known only to have two blocks, the backward direction must, as far as I can see, decide which block an effect is in. So a core proof needs the blocks given decidably, for example by a tag. Either land such a theorem in `GrammarPath` and cite it, or drop the clause.
   - **The question is already answered.** "Whether a weaker condition suffices for classes that divide the outside more finely" is not open. In a net of three blocks, a member and an alike occurrence each condition something in a different outside block (`ThreeBlocks`). Something outside follows every alike step that goes on, and nothing inside is conditioned. Yet the class is not constant up to the net's end. For finer classes the condition is the definition itself, "alike to that effect" included.

   **What does stay open** [AI reading]. Constancy up to the net's end constrains every block of a class, the outside included:
   - Under two blocks it asks little of the self's block: only that something outside follow each alike step that goes on.
   - But it asks the same of everything outside. By `two_blocks`, where one outside occurrence conditions something in the self's block, every outside occurrence that goes on must do so too.
   - Dividing the outside more finely frees the outside, and asks instead that what is alike to each effect follow.

   So which class P2 reads, and how finely it divides what one leaves out, fixes how much P2 asks of the agential self. Whether P2's "a regularity of its own" should constrain only the self's block is part of the same question. A possible Open item: "**How finely a self's class divides what it leaves out** ([§4.6](#future-path)): constancy up to the net's end constrains every block of a class, the outside included, and asks that what is alike to each effect leaving a block follow every alike step that goes on (`«出类随行»`); so the division of what one leaves out fixes how much P2 asks of the agential self. Which class P2 should read, and whether it should constrain only the self's block, is open."
