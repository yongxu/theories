## A check: self-concern through dependence (23 October 2026, IV3)

This is an AI check by one session (board item IV3), made after the Lean was drafted in scratch and before it landed. It asks what the literature already has for the route through dependence (THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE §4.4) and what the formal setting adds. **Verdict: every ingredient is known. What is new is where they land: within the centers' common closure the de se question decides nothing, the means-end step reaches a copy only when it is alone, and an order increasing only in its own continuation already ranks the ending of what everything needs below every course keeping the standpoint.**

|---|---|---|

| Reasons transmit from an end to its means | Kant's hypothetical imperative; Raz, "The Myth of Instrumental Rationality", *Journal of Ethics and Social Philosophy* 1 (2005), with its symposium, reasons to take the steps that facilitate an end; Kiesewetter, "Instrumental Normativity: In Defense of the Transmission Principle", *Ethics* 125 (2015), transmission to what an end requires; White, "Transmission Failures", *Ethics* (2017); Kolodny, "Instrumental Reasons" (2018) | Drawn along need (necessary means), the presupposition reaches exactly what is needed (`forced_needs_iff`); with copies it reaches none, only the class (`needs_iff_only`).
