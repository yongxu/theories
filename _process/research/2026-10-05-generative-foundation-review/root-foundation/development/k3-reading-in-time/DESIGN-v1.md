## What K3 adds (new core leaf `Becoming.ReadOffInTime`; imports BeyondCoupling, ConditionInTime, TimedResponse, Propagation)

1. **A selection read off continuation, dated.** `Web.ReadOffContinuationsBy W group T select`: decided by each member's admission at every step up to `T`. Laws: it is `W.SelectsThroughAll thresholdOnly group T` (iff); it is a whole-run one (`.readOffContinuations`); monotone in `T`; every such selection selects `l, l'` alike iff every member is admitted alike at every step up to `T`.
2. **When it reads: the first step apart.** `Web.FirstApart W group l l' T`: some member's admission differs at `T`, none at any earlier step. Laws: before `T` every selection read off the group's continuations up to that step selects alike; at `T` one separates (the continuation class up to `T`); unique; exists wherever admission alike at each step is decided and some step differs (least search, constructive).
3. **The route: TimedResponse's potential route is J3's dated relation.** J3's `AffectsBefore` with coupling by level added (`Flow.AffectsBeforeBy L W obs`: requires at `y.1` or `obs y.2 c`; the threshold case is J3's, `iff`); read through either course (`Flow.StepInTimeEither L L' W obs`, `WithinTimeEither`; with `L = L'`, `obs = thresholdOnly` it is J3's `StepInTime`, iff). `ReachedInTime … g T`: a source occurrence `x`, an occurrence `z` within `x`'s closure in time, and `z` affecting `(u, g)` with `u ≤ T`. **Law:** with the same formations present in both courses, `ReachedBy … g T → ReachedInTime … g T`; where the source goes on (`source t h → source (t+1) h`), the converse; so the two dated relations are one. (Proof: induction on `Spread`; converse by the closure invariant "source or reached by a spread condition at or before".)
4. **The reading not before the route (light cone).** Under TimedResponse's deadline premises up to `D`: where no source occurrence's closure in time meets a member by `D`, every selection read off the group's continuations up to `D` selects the two courses alike; so a first step apart `T` has the closure meeting some member by `T` (¬¬; with reaching decided, outright). Bound's whole-run law is the limit; Latency's `Relay` attains the bound.
5. **How strongly: the gap against the margin.** A member admitted at `t` in one course is admitted in the other wherever the gap on each condition it requires is within its margin there (level − need): `admits_of_gap_within_margin`. Where the loss lowers each required condition, exactly: admitted in the lowered course iff every gap is within the margin. So a selection read off continuation reads a difference that has arrived only once the gap crosses some member's margin; with Propagation's `gap_le_carried`, not while the most that can arrive is within it. Route (when it can arrive at all) and margin (when what arrives is read) are the two lower bounds.
6. **The point of no return on the same base.** `Web.Recoverable feasible keeps l N`: some feasible course agreeing with `l` at every step up to `N` (pointwise, no funext) keeps (`keeps` any property of a course, e.g. a dweller admitted at every step). Down-closed in `N`. `Web.NoReturnAt feasible keeps l N`: not recoverable at `N`, recoverable at every earlier step. Laws: unique; recoverable at `n` iff `n < N`; at `N − 1` the courses part: a feasible course agreeing up to `N − 1` keeps, and every feasible course agreeing up to `N` loses — 02 §2's juncture (机) read on courses (near-definitional).
7. **The law in time.** `Web.ReadOffInTime W group feasible keeps l l'`: the first step apart comes at a step from which the losing course is still recoverable. **Laws:** (a) given the first step apart `T` and the point of no return `N`, in time iff `T < N` (near-definitional); (b) **too late from alikeness**: where every member is admitted alike at every step up to `D` and the losing course is past return at `D`, the reading is not in time; (c) **too late from the route**: under the deadline premises, where no source occurrence's closure in time meets the group by `D` and the loss is past return at `D`, no selection read off the group's continuations reads it in time (composition of 4 and (b)); (d) **too late from the margin**: where every gap up to `D` is within the members' margins and past return at `D` (composition of 5 and (b)).
8. **Witnesses** (`Gauged`, concrete, one loss and three readers). A clearer draws a habitat of 3 one unit a step; it can stop at any step `k` (courses `k = 0, 1, 2, 3+`, each balanced). A dweller needs the habitat at 1. `near` gauges the habitat at need 2, `far` at need 1, `keeper` takes from a store of 1 that the dweller gives to while admitted, needing it at 1. Courses keeping the dweller: `k ≤ 2`; point of no return of the cleared course: step 3. First steps apart (cleared vs spared): `near` 2 (in time), `far` 3 (at the point: not in time), `keeper` 4 (past it). J3's closure in time from `(0, clearer)` meets `near` and `far` at step 1 and `keeper` at step 2: the route arrives before the reading; the margin decides when it is read. The same loss, the same courses, read in time or late by where the reader couples, as MA2's same readers read in time or late by when.
9. **Joins named.** Bound (dated: its whole-run law the limit), J3 (the relation is the route), TimedResponse and Latency (the light cone, one link a step), Junctures (point of no return is a juncture on courses; the law has `turned_at_juncture`'s shape: both are cases of `answer_alike_before`), RegimeMargin and drawdown (the loss runs out by the ledger; in the witness the point is where the habitat falls below the dweller's need with nothing renewing from nothing), Propagation and 02 §5's margin (how strongly), MA2/MA4b (their reading step `N`, supplied there, is here the first step apart of the others' continuation reading; their deadline is the point of no return; a reading, the models differ).

## Which law this is a case of

The reading's timing is a case of TimedResponse's deadline law (`answer_alike_before`): nothing read through couplings differs before a route arrives. The point of no return is 02's juncture read on courses. The in-time law is their composition with the dated selection.
