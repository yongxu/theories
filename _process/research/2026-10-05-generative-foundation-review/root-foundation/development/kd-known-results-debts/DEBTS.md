## How it was made

Four AI readers each took three rows of 08 §8's table (the last two rows together), read each row's home section and records, and listed what the closest prior frameworks state that the account does not yet. They searched the chapters, records and Lean before calling anything absent and marked how they knew each framework result. Four further AI readers, one for each drafter, tried to refute every item: already in the account, misstated, or unsupported. The workflow script is [kit/known-results-debts.js](kit/known-results-debts.js), and the raw results are in [workflow-results.json](workflow-results.json). The column in 08 was written from the checked cells, with two changes made after reading the evidence below. Haldane's load was dropped, because it is one line of arithmetic on `Mutation.kept_share`. The ranking-function converse was worded as the record's open item.

The framework results come from the readers' knowledge of the literature ("prior knowledge" below), except where a record of the account names the gap. That is the depth at which each one is known here: none was checked against its source paper.
