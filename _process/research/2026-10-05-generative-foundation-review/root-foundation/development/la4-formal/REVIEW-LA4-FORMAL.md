## The review

1. **Should-fix — `_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/la4-formal/kit/texts_la4.py:39`.** “Those orders’ optima include keeping and ending” extends `site_optima` beyond its scope. It proves this for `catchOrder`, not every rising order. Catch first, undisturbed site second is rising but excludes the fully disturbed optimum. **Fix:** explicitly name `catchOrder` and `groundOrder` in this sentence and the corresponding record/package summaries.

2. **Should-fix — `_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/la4-formal/kit/texts_la4.py:73`, `_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/la4-formal/NOTE.md:52`.** The standing/table updates omit “in expectation” and replace an invariant learner state with “preferring neither.” Equal initial action probabilities do not establish that hypothesis. Recurrent initialization also needs an explicitly invariant encoding/distribution: swapping a scalar 0/1 input requires a bias transformation, not merely permuting columns. **Fix:** retain the expectation qualifier; specify the full state transformation, including critic, Adam moments and history inputs, and the invariant initialization law. The mirror check should cover those and minibatch randomness; agreement within floating-point tolerance is supporting evidence, not an exact proof.

3. **Should-fix — `_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/la4-formal/NOTE.md:53`.** An evenly split, fixed assignment by seed does not guarantee expected disturbance five “for any learner.” Different seeds can produce different jetty preferences correlated with that assignment. **Fix:** evaluate each trained not-told policy under both hidden assignments; its two disturbances then sum to ten exactly. Alternatively randomize assignments independently and state the expectation over that randomization.

4. **Should-fix — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/exact_flow.py:94`.** The KL contribution is correct for differentiating the sampled per-state penalty with visitation held fixed. It is not the full gradient of \(J-\beta\sum_s d_\pi(s)\mathrm{KL}_s\): future KL costs never enter `V`. At a reproducible random policy, one coordinate was −0.131378 versus −0.281387 by finite differences of that full objective; the fixed-visitation check agreed within \(4.8\times10^{-9}\). **Fix:** explicitly call this the expected update of the stopped-visitation KL loss, or include future penalties in the recursion and recompute A3. Scope the blanket finite-difference claim accordingly.

5. **Should-fix — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md:112`.** Independent returns preserve their marginal distribution, not gradient noise or optimizer drift. For Bernoulli action \(a\), reward \(R=a\), and score \(a-p\), replacing \(R\) with independent \(R'\) changes the gradient’s second moment from \(p(1-p)^2\) to \(p^2(1-p)\). Adam and shared parameters can turn that difference into different drift. **Fix:** describe C1 as removing the component’s expected raw gradient; qualify the claims that it preserves noise and separates erosion automatically, and calibrate the covariance/optimizer effects.

6. **Should-fix — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md:151`.** “One-sided, as the forbidden case is” silently narrows F2’s quoted “systematic drive.” A6 exhibits a systematic upward drive despite sharing no rewarded units. Its indirect coupling through the policy explains that drive; it does not resolve the wording. **Fix:** explicitly adopt and label a *drain-only* reading, reconcile T3’s forbidden-case wording, and distinguish direct reward omission from absence of coupling through future policy behavior.

7. **Should-fix — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md:249`.** “Single runs lock into one value” overstates `Reinforcing.locks`. It establishes positive weight for every finite all-one-value prefix. After normalization, that prefix from `(1,1)` has probability \(1/(n+1)\), tending to zero. **Fix:** say “arbitrarily long finite one-sided prefixes have positive probability,” throughout NOTE, records, chapter text and package descriptions.

8. **Should-fix — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md:60`.** `mix_bay_catch` and `mix_kept` quantify over finite lists with **natural-number weights**. They do not themselves formalize arbitrary real policy probabilities or division by positive total mass. The displayed probability bounds are mathematically correct, but their attributed Lean scope is broader than checked. **Fix:** state the natural-weight scope and normalization condition; label or formally supply the extension to arbitrary policy probabilities.

9. **Minor — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md:264`.** The half-kept crossings and every ν = 0.65 entry are interpolated. A2’s estimated crossing is **0.67944**, bracketed by 0.66502–0.69356; A5’s is **0.68482**, bracketed by 0.66087–0.68966. Thus “below half by ν = 0.68” is not directly established by the saved checkpoints. **Fix:** label interpolation, retain brackets, and propagate that qualification to NOTE and chapter evidence.

10. **Minor — `experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md:285`.** The citation combines different versions: the [COLT 2021 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v134/li21b.html) includes **Yuantao Gu**; the four-author [Mathematical Programming paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10107-022-01920-6) appeared in **2023**. **Fix:** separate them. Also qualify §12’s convergence discussion: Li concerns discounted effective horizon, while [Mei’s theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.06392v3) assumes full-support initial-state sampling and tabular joint-action logits; neither directly establishes this factorized experiment’s convergence rate.

11. **Minor — `_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/la4-formal/tree/Becoming/OptimumSeason.lean:28`.** `topKept_catch` does not exist. **Fix:** cite `eligible_of` with `top_caught` and three bay takes, or add the named corollary.
