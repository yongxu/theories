## In brief

*On laws of nature: in part.* "Law" covers three standings, and the account holds two of them. Its grammar laws hold of every relation and class, so they forbid nothing. Its conditional laws are theorems on named premises; each premise holds in one constructed world and fails in another. A law of nature in thermodynamics' sense is a third kind: a contingent regularity of the actual world, stated in measured quantities, that rules out observations. The account has this kind only as theses (T1–T8), conjectured, untested or partly tested. Its own verdict is that with thermodynamics it "is level or states less" (08 §8). It takes the first and second laws in as premises in the periphery and does not derive them. Its ethics is no law of nature by its own result.

*On the full picture of evolution: yes for the form, no for the course.* One account can state each mechanism of evolution as an exact conditional law with its premises and a counterexample where it fails. That is reachable, and the evolution line is already partly there. Which course actually happens is not given by laws: the account's laws of evolution say what a history allows and forbids, "not which happen" (04 §6). Read on the account itself, a knower's picture is bounded by what couples into it, so a "full picture" held by one knower conflicts with the account's own law of knowing. Closing the real gaps (amounts on the root, an organism's model, tests run) needs models in measured quantities and data, not further theorems alone.

## 1. What the account already states

1. **The grammar forbids nothing.** "The laws with no premise exclude no unfolding beyond what the representation excludes, and each premise of the conditional laws holds in one constructed world and fails in another" (OPENING, note `no-law`; `World.contingent`). The DU review (31 October) draws the consequence: the contentful laws are conditionals on contingent hypotheses, proved independent.
2. **The edge is at amounts.** Of the 118 central declarations, ten are derived on the root, one holds under a stated hypothesis and seven are counting. The other 100 stand at a named distance, 18 of them "on amounts" (08 §7, record [one-root-to-its-edge](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/one-root-to-its-edge.md)). U4b drew the line: amounts is the distance for a statement that uses the dynamics and constrains numbers the grammar has no terms for.
3. **Thermodynamics enters as premise and correspondence.**
   - A quantity carried only by exchanges balances exactly where nothing produces it, so a locally conserved quantity such as energy balances (01 §8.4, periphery).
   - Read in available work, the exergy balance with the second law gives the spine's first law, and destroyed exergy is its record. The second law alone gives no dissipation (02 §4, periphery, `EntropyProduction.secondLaw_not_dissipative`).
   - "Entropy" as the record is a reading [D45]. Balance as the first law with locality is a correspondence, and "dissipation as stated is stronger than the statistical second law" (08 §12).
4. **Strength is what a claim forbids.** "A theory of the world is judged by what it forbids"; strength and standing are kept apart (08 §9). The Landauer–Bennett unification is "kept as a law, since as a thesis it forbids little" (08 §9).
5. **Where the account reaches farther, and where not.** Of 21 containments the third-parts audit read, four forbid something the framework leaves open, three of them distinct. Price's equation and Fisher's theorem joined to the flow have such a third part; drift, fixation and mutation have none. With thermodynamics, recurrence, control, constructor theory and causal sets the account "is level or states less", and "it does not yet reach farther than the sciences taken together" (08 §8).
6. **The ethics is not read off the dynamics.** No satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the continuation condition (07 §1, `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`). That C does not follow either is named as a premise (ONE-PAGE, 07 §4). Its non-entailment is in DU's scope, on its branch, not on main.
7. **Evolution in counting form.** Price's equation, Fisher's theorem in its exact case, Hamilton's rule and drift hold in counting form (04 §3–§6). The drift laws weigh histories by the ways of drawing them and do not say which happen (04 §6). Evolution "names particular mechanisms ... it is not every change" (04 §8).
8. **The evolution line decides boundaries, not fates.** EL's head result is the boundary of the region of selection terms that decide whether a family goes on. "Which case a course is in, these laws do not decide", and under each of three ending regimes the stock alone decides neither fate (EL's design note, `LineageRegimes.stock_forces_neither`, scratch Lean).
9. **Knowing reaches what couples in.** A selection reaches exactly what couples into its selector; this is near-definitional, and its force lies in the premise that a selector selects through just those couplings (03 §1).
10. **What keeps widening is fed from beyond.** A finite balanced world under the first law has no course that keeps widening, and a finite family whose widening goes on is given net by the rest or is not closed (07 §2, `Flow.whole_not_keepsWidening`). 08 §8 reads this as open-systems thermodynamics and the measure of open-ended evolution.
11. **Not yet modelled.** An organism and an institution have no model yet, and several theses' tests are still to run (ONE-PAGE).

## 2. Three standings of "law"

| Standing | What holds | What it forbids | In the account |
|---|---|---|---|
| A law of the grammar | every relation and class | nothing beyond the representation | the root's laws (01, 02 §1) |
| A conditional law | the implication, in every world | where its premise holds, what its conclusion excludes | most laws, each premise shown contingent |
| A law of nature (thermodynamics' kind) | a contingent regularity of the actual world, in measured quantities | observations: a machine of the second kind, heat flowing up unaided | theses T1–T8 only, conjectured; thermodynamics itself as periphery premise |

The second row has a law's shape in the sense statistical mechanics has one. There too, theorems on modelling premises (Hamiltonian dynamics, typical initial conditions) become physics once actual systems are shown to meet those premises. The account has proved the theorems and named the premises, which is the first half. Showing the premises hold is the second half, and that is 08 §12's open programme. A conditional law becomes a law of nature for a domain when its premise is shown to hold there. Thermodynamics' force also comes from its quantities (energy, entropy, temperature), and those lie at the root's edge (item 2).

So the reading is: **the account states laws about any unfolding, and law schemas that become laws of nature for a domain once their premises are shown to hold there. It does not yet state a law of nature it can call its own.** The OPENING applies the same standard to rival accounts. It denies that the struggle for power, competition or the closed self are laws, because each fails in one constructed world (note `no-law`). By that standard, its own laws are either necessary and empty of what happens, or conditional on premises that fail somewhere.

## 3. The picture of evolution

**What is reachable.** Every mechanism of evolution can be stated on one root as an exact conditional law: transmission, variation, selection, drift, fixation, kin selection, degeneration and abundance destroyed. Each would carry its premises, a counterexample where it fails, and a map of which premises hold where. Price, Fisher, Hamilton and drift are already in counting form (item 7). EL makes the boundary between going on and ending exact at the counting layer (item 8). This is a full picture of the *form* of evolutionary explanation, and the third parts (item 5) show where it can forbid more than one field alone does.

**What is not, for three reasons.**

1. **The course is historical.** Which variants arise, in which conditions, is not given by the laws. The account's own laws say so: the drift laws do not say which histories happen, and EL's laws do not decide which case a course is in (items 7, 8). Explanation can still be complete in form: given the history, say why it went as it did and what it ruled out. Prediction of the course from the laws alone is not available, and nothing in the account promises it.
2. **No knower holds the whole.** By 03 §1 a selection reaches only what couples into it, and the account itself is held by selectors (people, AI). Applying the law to its own knowers is a reading, not a theorem about them. The reading is this: a picture complete for every knower contradicts the law of knowing, and a picture complete for none is what the law predicts. Where widening goes on, what can form next was not listed before (item 10).
3. **Theorems are not the bottleneck.** The open items are amounts on the root, a model of an organism, and the theses' tests (items 2, 11). Each needs models in measured quantities, data and runs. More Lean on the present carriers adds standing to what is stated; it does not reach those.

**The aim restated.** Not "the full picture of evolution", but this: *one account in which every mechanism of evolution is stated with its exact conditions and counterexamples, which says what a history allows and forbids, and which says what it cannot predict and why.* That forbids more than any one field's statement of a mechanism, which is the standard 08 §9 sets.

## 4. Where Ren's words seem wrong

- "Our work is also laws of nature, as thermodynamics": the account says otherwise of itself (item 5). Its proved laws are of the grammar or conditional; its claims about the actual world are theses. The parallel with thermodynamics holds for the statistical-mechanics shape (§2), not for the standing.
- "The full picture of evolution ... if we push far enough": the form can be complete, the course cannot (§3). "Push far enough" read as more formal work misses where the gaps are.
- "And how it can be understood": this part holds up best. Saying exactly what each mechanism needs and where it fails is a way of understanding evolution that no single field gives.
