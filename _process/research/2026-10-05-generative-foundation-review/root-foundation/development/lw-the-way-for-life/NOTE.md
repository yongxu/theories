## 2. What the proposal changes, and what it keeps

It changes the standing of the ethics' two premises. It does not change their roles or any proof.

- **Given up as a demand: a warrant that binds every possible mind.** None comes from the dynamics or from logic. No statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the condition ([07 §1](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)), by construction of the representation. A perspectivist holding significance relative all the way down denies the condition consistently (`bounded_rival`), and the step of invariance can be denied coherently ([07 §4.2](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#step)). The method also excludes a good owned outside the unfolding ([07 §1](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)); that is the method's verdict, not a proof. Whether a stronger warrant exists stays an open question, since giving up the goal proves no impossibility (GPT astra's point). The ethics does not wait on it.
- **Kept universal: the laws and the self's presupposition.** The laws hold for every relation and every class; generation never overlapping degeneration is one of them. The self's presupposition (P1) is that acting presupposes some continuation has significance.
- **Stated as life's stance: the scope.** It is read de se under a class that holds one's effects. Under P1–P4, concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of what one conditions, past one's end and across lines fed by conditions one affects, with no step of invariance ([07 §4.6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)). Its crux is P4. What §4.6 does not reach stays the step's: others counted beyond their part as a condition, present beings beyond every closure, and whatever no chain joins. That remainder is given up as a demand on every mind and kept as an open question.
- **Unchanged: the order.** C still ranks, and it still needs its own reason. Wanting life to continue gives a guard: keep off the courses that end on every branch, and stay above the points of no return. In the core's terms that is a plan keeping a guard ([05 §10](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-market-protected-continuation)). It does not give that the wider course is better, and both readings agree on this.
- **Kept: everything proved and stated.** That covers every proved law, every record's statement, the two premises' roles (scope and order) and their naming wherever the ethics is summarized, and the conditional results at their scope.

## 4. What would make this wrong

- **A warrant binding every possible mind is found.** An argument that no agent can coherently deny the step of invariance would be one. The open question would then be answered, and the target restored.
- **P4 fails for actual selves.** If one's effects are not one's own in the sense of whose losses count, the de se scope reaches less than life, and T8's stance form overstates what it covers.
- **No reason for C can be given from life's stance.** The order then stays a bare premise, as now.
