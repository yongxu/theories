2. **High — “none comes from the dynamics or from logic” exceeds the stated scope unless qualified.**

   This occurs in the owner’s reading, A1, A4 and A8. A1 says:

   > “No verdict binding every possible mind is claimed: none comes from the dynamics … or from logic”

   The live theorem concerns **significance supplied as an untied predicate**. The chapter explicitly limits its conclusion:

   > “non-entailment by the represented dynamics, not that becoming leaves significance undetermined or needs the condition.”

   [07-SIGNIFICANCE.md:18](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md), [07-SIGNIFICANCE.md:23](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)

   `bounded_rival` supports consistent denial of the condition. It does not prove that every possible philosophical grounding fails. The chapter itself keeps the privileged-relation question open. [07-SIGNIFICANCE.md:254](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)

   Replace A1’s whole negative sentence with:

   > No verdict binding every possible mind is established here. With significance supplied as the untied predicate of §1, where a standpoint and a formation exist, neither logic alone nor a satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the continuation condition; `bounded_rival` supplies a coherent denial. This does not rule out a stronger philosophical warrant.

   For the shorter repetitions in A4 and A8, replace:

   > “none comes from the dynamics or from logic”

   with:

   > “the continuation condition follows neither from logic alone nor from the represented dynamics alone when significance is supplied as an untied predicate”

   Chinese replacement:

   > 在意义作为未与其他内容绑定的谓词而给定时，延续条件既不能单从逻辑推出，也不能单从所表示的动力学推出

   **A5 is substantially better:** it already says “alone” and identifies the condition rather than every possible verdict. It should nevertheless add the same qualification about the untied predicate.

   This matters for the programme too: the live warrant record explicitly says, “Universality is not … full Unrestricted significance.” Failure to derive that condition does not by itself defeat every version of the universal target. [universal-practical-warrant.md:57](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/universal-practical-warrant.md)

3. **Medium — A2 and A5 describe the extension of self-concern as an extension of the condition’s scope.**

   Words at issue:

   > “P2 and P4, which carry the condition's scope past one's own end”

   Chinese:

   > “P2 与 P4，它们使该条件的范围越过自己的终结”

   The condition already covers anything’s continuation, from every standpoint, at every step. Its scope does not await P2 or P4. [07-SIGNIFICANCE.md:121](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)

   What §4.6 extends is **one’s concern for one’s own continuation**, reaching part of what the condition already includes. [07-SIGNIFICANCE.md:405](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)

   Replace that clause in A2 and A5 with:

   > P2 and P4 of 07 §4.6, which, together with P1 and P3, extend de se self-concern to everything downstream of one's acts, past the end of one's organism

   Chinese:

   > 07 §4.6 的 P2 与 P4，它们与 P1、P3 一起，使从己的自我关切到达自身行动下游的一切，越过自身有机体的终结

   **The draft’s treatment of the remainder is sound.** The live text expressly assigns to the step what the path does not reach, including others counted beyond their causal role and whatever no chain joins. [07-SIGNIFICANCE.md:407](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)

4. **Medium — The guard language conflates an aim with a supplied guard and its preservation.**

   Words at issue in the owner’s reading, A1 and A4:

   > “a wish … gives a guard, keeping off the courses that end on every branch”

   > “05 §10, a plan keeping a guard”

   The cited result characterizes **never leaving a supplied guard**, given a certificate containing the start. It neither selects the guard nor establishes that a suitable certificate or plan exists. [05-COLLECTIVES.md:321](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md)

   The chapter separately says that “which guards mark what each activity needs is supplied,” and that keeping a part is not renewal. [05-COLLECTIVES.md:85](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md)

   Thus avoiding degeneration can be a motivated aim, but the cited theorem does not establish its identification with a particular guard or its feasibility. “Not ending on every branch” also must not silently become “preserved on every admissible history.”

   Replace A1’s guard clause with:

   > a wish that life go on motivates seeking to preserve its necessary conditions; §10 of chapter 5 characterizes preservation of a supplied guard under a certificate, without establishing that a suitable certificate exists or that widening is better.

   Replace A4’s added sentence with:

   > Wanting life to continue motivates investigating and preserving its necessary conditions; it does not by itself establish either ethical premise or a feasible plan for keeping those conditions.

   Chinese:

   > 希望生命延续，促使人探究并保持其必要条件；它本身既不确立任何一个伦理前提，也不确立保持这些条件的可行方案。

   **The narrower point is sound:** wanting continuation does not establish C.
