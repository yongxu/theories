## scope-order

### scope-order 0: A1 (high; stands)

- **Words:** The stance rests on what acting presupposes ([§4.6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path), P1), not on a wish that life go on; such a wish gives a guard, keeping off the courses that end on every branch ([05 §10](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-market-protected-continuation)), not that the wider course is better.
- **Problem:** The sentence contrasts P1 with a wish: the wish gives only a guard and not that the wider course is better. A careful reader takes the contrast to mean that P1 supplies what the wish lacks, which is the order. But P1 is the self's presupposition, and with invariance it is exactly the condition, a scope premise. So the sentence implies that order comes from the scope side, which is the derivation the scope/order rule forbids implying. The thesis never says that only C gives order.
- **Evidence:** 07-SIGNIFICANCE.md line 223: the condition "is exactly the presupposition sub-true and invariant at every standpoint (`unrestricted_iff_presupposed_along`)". Line 213: "The condition alone ranks nothing ... and every ranking needs C." Line 657: "the condition fixes scope and orders nothing; C alone ranks." Line 314: self-concern ranks only coupled endings, and "C's premise there is self-concern's monotonicity: placed more narrowly, not warranted." AGENTS.md line 21: "What ranks lies in calling widening better (C) alone."
- **Proposed:** The stance rests on what acting presupposes ([§4.6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path), P1), not on a wish that life go on; such a wish gives a guard, keeping off the courses that end on every branch ([05 §10](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-market-protected-continuation)). Neither the wish nor P1 gives that the wider course is better: that is C's alone. (The guard finding below rewrites the middle clause; the last sentence stays in both versions.)
- **Verdict:** stands. T8's stance includes '(order: C)'. So 'The stance rests on what acting presupposes (P1), not on a wish ... such a wish gives a guard ..., not that the wider course is better' invites the reading that P1 supplies the ranking the wish lacks. P1 is the self's presupposition, on the scope side: 07 line 223 makes the condition exactly the presupposition plus invariance. 07 line 213 says 'every ranking needs C', and line 657 says 'C alone ranks'. The added sentence removes that implicature, and its 'C's alone' matches line 657.
- **Better replacement:** The stance rests on what acting presupposes ([§4.6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path), P1), not on a wish that life go on; such a wish gives a guard, keeping off the courses that end on every branch ([05 §10](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-market-protected-continuation)).
