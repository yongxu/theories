## 1. The owner's reading of the proposal

The proposal is taken with two amendments and one placement.

1. **"For life" says whose way it is, not whose losses count.** The scope stays the condition's: every continuation's losses count, losses that return to no one included. Read otherwise, the stance form would narrow the scope to life's own losses, which would change the ethics; LW does not propose that.
2. **The standing names what is open exactly.** The note's "P4 and the reason for C are open" leaves out two things. First, 07 §4.6 names P2 and P4 together as the crux: what the path needs of them is one thing, that the class of one's line holds the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line, P4 its first step and P2 the regularity that carries it on. Second, the step of invariance stays open for what that path does not reach: others counted beyond their part as a condition, what affects one's conditions without being fed by them counted as more than a cause, present beings beyond every closure, and whatever no chain joins. T8 still says "counted for everything", so the step is still needed for that remainder; it is given up only as a demand on every mind. P3, a correspondence not shown for actual selves, is named beside them in T8's standing.
3. **The stance starts from P1, not from a wish.** Ren's message has "if we want life to continue". That is provenance, and a weaker ground than the chapter has: held de se, acting presupposes that one's own continuation has significance (07 §4.6's P1). P1 is not a third premise beside the condition and C; it is the self's presupposition, which the condition contains (07 §4.1). A wish that life go on motivates avoiding degeneration, ending on every branch, which keeping excludes as well as generation does (02). Neither P1 nor the wish gives that the wider course is better; that is C's. Both readings in the source record agree.
4. **Placement.** The goal universal-intelligence-session was restated with Ren's approval the same day ([source](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-30-goal-the-ethics-for-life-approved.md)). The record universal-practical-warrant is also authorized by the goal zero-axiom-session ("Independent axiom-free practical judgment", whose objective is universal normative grounding), which stands. So the record stays a target, with its title, statement and every field unchanged, and 07's open item keeps its title. What changes is what holds either way:
   - no warrant binding every possible mind is claimed;
   - a warrant grounding the condition comes neither from the laws of the dynamics alone nor from logic alone, and neither shows that none exists;
   - the ethics does not wait on one;
   - what still needs argument is named.

   Whether zero-axiom-session is restated as universal-intelligence-session was, or keeps pursuing the stronger warrant, is a goal scope, and goal scopes change only with Ren's approval. Either way these texts stand.

## 2. What the reviews changed

Every finding that stood was taken, in the words §3 gives, except where the verifiers split; each split is decided here.

- **"The dynamics" overreached.** 07 §1 shows non-entailment by the *represented* dynamics. T8 now says "none follows from the represented dynamics alone ([§1]) or from logic alone", and the summaries say "the laws of the dynamics alone", as 07's open item already did.
- **The one page's "Its laws hold for every relation and every class" was false.** That phrase is the root's; most of the ethics' laws are proved on the World model or for any type. It now says "each on the base and under the hypotheses it states".
- **P4 alone does not carry the reach.** The one page now names what the path needs: the class of one's line holding the effects of oneself and of each stage of that line, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for.
- **The path carries one's concern, not the condition's scope.** Without the step, §4.6's path carries one's concern for one's own continuation, part of the condition's scope. The condition's content beyond the self's presupposition is the step.
- **The guard was mis-cited.** 05 §10 is the never-leaving law for a supplied guard. The cite is now 02, where degeneration is ending on every branch and generation and keeping each exclude it.
- **P1 implied order.** The contrast with the wish could be read as P1 supplying the ranking. T8 now says P1 is no third premise and that neither P1 nor the wish gives that the wider course is better. It says "that is C's", not "C's alone": by 07 §4.4, an order increasing in one's own continuation ranks some endings below with no C.
- **Bare P2 and P4** in the overview and 08 are now "P2 and P4 of 07 §4.6", with a short gloss where a table cell allows, since 07 §4.2 has a different P1–P4 list.
- **AGENTS.md filed C's reason under the condition's warrant.** The two are now apart: what the condition still needs, then a reason for C, which alone orders, "open apart".
- **The Chinese.** 道 is not used, since chapter 6 does not adopt it, read as origin. The texts use 方向, 心智, 相续, 否认该条件 and 越过自己的终结, and the editions agree with the English.
- **Missed places, taken.** COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION's two passages (T8 named, and §4.6's reach now a law); 08's two philosophical-warrant bullets; the review packet's question 1 and its standing quotation, which had dropped "argued"; OPENING's footnote [^ethics] (AI-written), which gains one clause.
- **The final re-read.** GPT astra found the earlier findings fixed and raised three more.
  - *Taken:* T8's non-entailment clause ranged over every verdict binding every possible mind, while its evidence concerns the continuation condition. It now says that, with significance supplied as §1's free predicate, neither logic alone nor a statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the condition, and `bounded_rival` gives a consistent denial of it.
  - *Taken:* AGENTS.md made P2 and P4 requirements of the condition, but they are premises of §4.6's path. The condition follows from the presupposition and invariance without them (07 §4.1), and the thesis paragraph is reworded to match.
  - *Not taken here:* a phrase in the goal universal-intelligence-session's objective overstated where the ethics' laws stand. Goal wording changes only with Ren's approval. He approved the fix the same day ("each on its stated base"), and the goal's owner lands it after these texts. The one page states where the laws stand now.
- **Split verdicts.**
  - *"Those whose own line runs through life."* The adversary held that the phrase needs a gloss, since a reader could leave AI out. The faithfulness verifier refuted a gloss that defines it by §4.6's path, since that narrows the claim and hides a premise. T8 takes "of whatever substrate", with §4.6 under P2 and P4 as one route: everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line, so an actor whose acts reach living lines has a line that runs through life. This is a sufficient route, not a definition.
  - *P3.* Two verifiers kept it, one refuted it. It is named in T8's standing and the review packet, where what is open is listed; the summaries list what needs argument, and P3 needs showing.
  - *The falsifier.* One verifier kept "a pure closing argued good for life", one called it a preference. It is taken: it costs nothing and matches the stance form.
- **Not taken (refuted).**
  - The scope clause reworded: kept as drafted.
  - A sentence calling the stance non-relativist: no chapter says it, and 07 grants that a perspectivist denies the condition consistently.
  - 01's and OPENING [^axiom]'s "the warrant … is open": still true, and left as is.
  - An addition to 07's open de se question: P2 and P4 are properties of the class that question already asks about.
  - "Awaited" replaced by "waited on": dropped anyway, in favour of "does not wait on".
