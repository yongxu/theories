## B2. Its home section, 07's Open item

Current:

> - <a id="r-universal-practical-warrant"></a>**A universal practical warrant**: grounding practical reasons with zero substantive premises, as one account of ends, selection, knowing and revision within becoming; supplied local norms, behavioural convergence or fuller description do not substitute for it. Since, where there is a standpoint and a formation, no satisfiable statement reading only the dynamics entails the condition ([§1](#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)), such a warrant, where it would ground the condition, cannot come from the laws of the dynamics alone. ([record](../results/records/universal-practical-warrant.md))

Proposed:

> - <a id="r-universal-practical-warrant"></a>**Whether a universal practical warrant exists**: grounding practical reasons for every possible mind with zero substantive premises, as one account of ends, selection, knowing and revision within becoming; supplied local norms, behavioural convergence or fuller description do not answer it. The ethics does not wait on it: its laws hold for every relation and every class, and its stance is life's (T8, [§4.6](#future-path)). Where there is a standpoint and a formation, no satisfiable statement reading only the dynamics entails the condition ([§1](#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)), and a perspectivist denies the condition consistently (`bounded_rival`); so such a warrant, where it would ground the condition, comes neither from the laws of the dynamics alone nor from logic alone. Not seeking it proves it no impossibility. ([record](../results/records/universal-practical-warrant.md))
