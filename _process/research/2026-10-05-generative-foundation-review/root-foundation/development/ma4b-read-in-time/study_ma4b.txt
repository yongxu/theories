## {N}. The loss read in time by the others, joined to the critical mass (MA4b)

MA4 (§414) joined the capacity to shape the rule with the readers drawn from the selected. With the rule read from the share alone, every course from the rule read at its start is locked one way or the other (`drawn_locked_either`). So control not selected does not by itself give a lock left in time: leaving needs a reading beyond the share. MA2 (§401) gave one: the others among the readers read a loss once it reaches a bound (`ClosedCapture.Thinned`). It was not joined to MA4's critical mass. MA4b joins them. It is step 7 of AJ's plan and states the joint selection's second condition, a lock left in time, in this model. It serves the alignment account (07 §6, its owner) and AJ4's dossier, which must say when a reading beyond the share is in time and what it presupposes.
