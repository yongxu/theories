## What holds already

The account is relational where it matters, and says so in its own terms. 08 §1 requires every distinction (subject and object, actor and patient, observer and world, inside and outside, fact and value) to state its basis; says nonduality erases no difference in experience, history, access, control, consent or consequence; and denies a cosmic subject, an aggregate interest and an administrator. The overview and 08 say twice that nonduality is a requirement of method and proves neither the ontology nor an ethical necessity. 08's thesis of one relational unfolding forbids "an intrinsic value, a self that goes on closed, or a law outside what it governs". "Independent owners of value" and "a fixed common utility" appear only as what is not assumed. The self is a center whose boundary is drawn along a relation, and the contested step is stated as holding "however it is drawn". COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION keeps rivalry as a theorem beside the removal of a fixed sum. "Agent" is the free-energy principle's term, attributed, for the internal and active parts of a blanket, and 08 says activity presupposes no agent. "Independent" and "separate" are conditional independence and separation in their technical senses.

## The renaming test

08 §10 renames a landed name only where it states a wrong meaning. "Master" states rank and rule: a law set over others that command it, where 08 §7 measures only proof dependence and a law of the root, holding of every relation, forbids nothing. "Obey" states a command and its compliance, where a predicate holds of counts, as `ListingHolds` beside it already says. "Good" states a value, where the definition is successor safety, and "harm" calls a loss a harm, which no law of the map does; "belongs to" states ownership, where the law says only that no part alone makes the difference.

## What is kept, and why

- **The record id, until the end.** `research.py rename-id` renames a record with a redirect and `former_ids`, so `master-laws-on-the-grammar` becomes `shared-laws-on-the-grammar` (anchor `r-shared-laws-on-the-grammar`) as the last step, after OU7's rebuild: U4c, BD2, OU7 and DU all edit this record in flight, and renaming it first would send each of them round a rename.
- **Machine keys.** The anchor `r-master-laws-on-the-grammar` until then, 08's anchor `master-laws-and-connectivity`, COMPLETION's `#master-laws`, the data file `master-laws.json`, the JSON fields and category values (`master_laws`, `master law`, `no master law`), `master_laws.py` and the study folders' names. A key is not a statement: it is matched, not read. Renaming them would break every link from the studies, the receipts and history, which are kept byte for byte. New anchors (`shared-laws-and-connectivity`, COMPLETION's `#shared-laws`) are added beside the old ones (with the heading's old slug `7-master-laws-and-connectivity`, which two studies link), so that new links read rightly; the old names become aliases of the record.
- **"Significance".** Called a dualist word in conversation on 24 October. As used, `sig` is a free predicate, the continuation condition and C are unasserted premises, and significance is read de se as lying within what a center continues with along a relation (07 §1, the glossary). That states its relational basis and hides nothing: the premise is named as a premise. Renaming it would change the statement of a premise, which is not a naming change. Kept.
- **"Agent"**, the free-energy principle's word, attributed; **"self"**, a center with its boundary drawn along a relation; **"independent"**, **"separate"**, **"control"**, technical senses; **"Admits"** in `Admission`, the mathematical sense (what a level can hold).
- **OPENING.** Its good–evil line reads evil relationally (a part enlarging itself by closing the world of others), and its note marks the naming a reading. Version 13 belongs to another session; any finding there goes to that owner, not edited here.
- **`Actuation.Command`**: a command input to an actuator, an actual relation in the model.
