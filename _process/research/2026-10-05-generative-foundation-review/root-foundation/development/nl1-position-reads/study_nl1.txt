## NNN. What position reads (NL1)

```text
Result(s): new record what-position-reads.
Delta: two profiles of amounts give every part of a family the same position exactly where every part's amount
       differs between them by one amount common to all (RelativePosition.Keeps, keeps_iff_common): positions read
       the amounts up to a common amount. Adding an amount to every part keeps every position and adds it to the
       total once for each part (keeps_add); taking the least part's amount from every part keeps every position
       and leaves the spread above the least part (keeps_least). So with given positions the total is at least the
       spread (spread_le), every bound is exceeded (keeps_exceeds), and every profile with those positions has its
       total above r exactly where the spread is above r (exceeds_iff_spread). Witness Lasting: the renewer gives two
       units at every step, as in Race, and its two racers have the race's positions (Lasting.keeps), the same greatest part and the same rise of the first;
       it balances, gives only while active and keeps activity supported; what is taken is within what is given and
       every part is active at every step, while in Race every part is inactive from step two on
       (Lasting.same_positions_apart).
Premises / correspondence: none for the counting laws (whole-number amounts, a finite family with a part).
       Correspondence: competition read as position, the opening's "winning".
Evidence: core receipt RECEIPT (passed).
Boundary: the composition with the joint-excess law (no position certifies that no more is drawn than is renewed,
       and positions certify that more is drawn only where the spread alone exceeds it) reads two laws together and
       is not one theorem on a flow; ranks, shares and signed amounts are not treated.
Output → input: OPENING [^no-law] (what each leaves out; two of the features, read as laws of position, refuted by
       constructed flows); chapter §4.3 and the record table; record relative-position-is-zero-sum (its next step,
       positions read otherwise, partly taken: what positions read of amounts).
```

**What was built.** In `Becoming.RelativePosition`: `Keeps`, `length_pos_of_mem`, `regroup`, `keeps_iff_common`, `keeps_add`, `keeps_least`, `spread_le`, `keeps_exceeds`, `exceeds_iff_spread`; and the witness `Lasting` (`flow`, `web`, `balances`, `acting`, `dependent`, `racers`, `drawn_common`, `keeps`, `drawn_second`, `drawn_renewer`, `first_greatest`, `same_positions_apart`). `Keeps` is stated with the counts the module already had: the change from `a` to `b` keeps a part's position where its rise count is its fall count. The proofs avoid `omega` and the `Nat` lemmas whose reports carry `propext` (`Nat.add_right_cancel`, `Nat.add_sub_assoc`, `Nat.sub_add_cancel`), using the prelude's `add_right_cancel`, `add_sub_of_le` and `Naturals.sub_add_cancel`.

**Why this pushes the note.** The note said that "winning need not measure what lasts" rested on one constructed example, and that no struggle, competition or sovereign self being among the laws was "read off the list of laws, not a theorem". A list read off shows what is listed, not what follows: a feature absent from the list could still be derived from it. A law holds in every world, so a feature that fails in a constructed world is not a law. Read as laws of position, two of the features are now refuted that way: "the greatest continue" fails in `Race` and holds in `Clearing` (already proved, not cited by the note), and "position measures what lasts" fails in `Lasting` against `Race`. The general law says why the second fails everywhere and not only in the witness: what positions read of amounts is everything except one common amount, and the total drawn, which balance compares with what is given, is exactly what that leaves out above the spread.

**Self-review.** (1) The exactness is two-sided: `keeps_iff_common` is an equivalence, and `exceeds_iff_spread` gives the lower bound with its attainment, so "positions bound the total only from below, by the spread" is stated, not read. (2) The spread is not nothing: positions alone can force a total above what is renewed, where the parts' amounts are spread wider than what is renewed. The note therefore says "never certify that no more is drawn than is renewed", not "measure nothing", and leaves the opening's body ("need not measure anything of what lasts", Ren's voice) unchanged; the stronger reading ("no position certifies that anything lasts") is offered as a proposal, not applied. (3) The witness reads positions among the two that take, not the renewer: with the renewer in the family, a common shift would need it to draw, which it does not. (4) `Lasting` and `Race` have the same renewal (two units at each step the renewer takes part) and the same positions among the racers at every step; the separation is carried by the totals alone (the race's racers draw two more per step jointly), which is the law's point. (5) Board item RP1 (drawers in turn ranking position draw everything, in `Optimum.InTurn`, for OPENING `[^race]`) is the complement: it derives the joint excess from ranking position under stated premises, where this item shows what position reads of the total; neither reproves the other. (6) The composition with `Flow.joint_excess_ends` is read, not proved as one statement; the record's `does_not_show` says so.
