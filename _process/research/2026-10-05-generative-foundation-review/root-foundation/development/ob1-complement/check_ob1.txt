## A check: what dependence alone carries of a level (21 October 2026, OB1)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of the conjecture chapter §2.6 and §8.1 item 3 kept open: that observation is no second relation beside dependence, in the sense that the supplied `obs` could be derived from dependence. **Verdict: the mathematics is known, in four forms. A nondecreasing function is the sum of its threshold layers; a filter given by a positive Boolean function commutes with threshold decomposition; circuits without negation compute exactly the monotone Boolean functions, and with each input and its negation every function; dual-rail logic carries each signal with its complement so that monotone gates compute any circuit. What the spine's form adds is the reading on its own relations, checked with a converse each way: dependence, with activity needing support at the step, carries of a level exactly the responses rising with it and a fall only where some level falls, and with a complement at a fixed total it carries everything. Conservation supplies the complement wherever a closed pair holds a fixed total.** The conjecture is settled at the step of the exchange: true where the response rises with the level or the level has such a complement, false elsewhere (supplying a deficit with nothing holding what the level does not).
