# Dependency audit of the Becoming spine (plan §九 9.1)

Measured at `0cc1aafb4` (sources match the commit): 306 spine modules, 13,828 theorems (core ring 13,068, instances 581, applications 179). Run by an Opus subagent for the one-unfolding plan; the data are in [REPORT-DATA.json](REPORT-DATA.json). The per-theorem table `audit.json` (12 MB) is regenerated by the two commands below and is not kept in the repository.

```
python3 -B _process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/one-unfolding-root/audit/dump.py <dumpdir>
python3 -B _process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/one-unfolding-root/audit/audit.py <dumpdir>
```

## Validation

- `World.law_iff_em`: statement and proof both use only `active`. The proof builds its counterexample world with `World.of`, which supplies every field, but reads only `active`; a naive closure would report all eight.
- `CeasesToBe.not_comes`: no field. `Flow.still_dissipative`: {present, take, give}. `World.rests_unrefuted`: {level, active, present, take, give} with Balanced and Dissipative. All three match their sources.
- Known over-count: `World.premised_rests_unrefuted` shows all eight premises through `Premised`; its source uses three (balance, dissipation, embodiment).

## Method and limits

- 525 definitions repackage fields (their value applies a base constructor: `World.web`, `World.of`, witness webs). The main count does not descend into them: a field counts where something projects it out (`w.web.active` counts `active`). The upper bound descends into all of them except eight pass-throughs read by hand. The two counts differ for 550 theorems; the true set lies between them.
- Building a record is recorded as a construction (2,869 statements, 3,238 proofs), not as reading its fields. No spine theorem destructures a base record.
- The audit works on constants, not proof terms: a hypothesis bundled as a list (`Premised`, `Meets`) counts every member. Theorems over a general `Web` or `Flow` count their fields even where `World` is not mentioned.

## What it says for the plan

Two thirds of the spine (9,370 of 13,828) reads no field of the old base at all: these theorems are about frames, processes, runs, numbers and lists, and the question for them is only whether their statements can be put in the grammar's terms. Of the rest, the single largest group reads one field, `active`, which is a two-valued distinction along a run: exactly the material of continuation (续) under a distinction. Only 313 (upper bound 605) read all eight fields. The lifting of plan §九 9.2 is therefore smaller than the 13,828 suggests, and it starts where the new centre is: the `active`-only theorems and the continuation family above.
