**Classical-text review of plan version 9: 续 and the texts**

Quotations marked [R] were checked in the repository. Those marked [M] come from memory and need checking.

## 1. Verdict

On the classical side, version 9 fits the texts to the root rather than reading the root out of them. The Buddhist texts were added after the thesis was fixed. What the record says (2026-10-23-continuity-as-root.md) is that "classical Chinese already holds it." The plan quotes the lines that agree with it. Passages in the same files that reject 相續 (continuity) as a ground go unregistered, and a commentator's lines are attributed to the root text. In Chinese Buddhism 续 is a contested doctrine: 唯识 (and, by my memory, 经部) defend it, and 中观 refutes it in the very 中论 chapters the plan cites as support. The formal side is the formal reviewer's job. Two textual points bear on it: the texts contain simultaneous and mutual 缘, which breaks "time = transitive closure", and they define 缘 by its result.

## 2. Findings

**F1. The 中论 explicitly rejects 相續 as the ground of 不常不断.** *Textual error, overclaim.*
- **Where.** §5.2 uses 「不常不斷」 (观因缘品) and lists 观成坏品 only as "论成坏".
- **观业品 [R].** 「如芽等相續…從相續有果，先種後有果，不斷亦不常」 is the opponent's view. The verse answer is 「若如汝分別，其過則甚多…於義則不然」, and 青目 asks "為滅已相續？為不滅相續？"
- **观成坏品 [R].** The opponent again: 「因果相續故，不斷亦不常」, which 青目 glosses "生滅故不常，相續故不斷". That is the plan's thesis word for word. The chapter ends with 「三世中求有，相續不可得」, and 青目 adds "有有相續，皆從愚癡顛倒故有，實中則無".
- **观三相品 [R].** The plan's summary "不能各自成立" (cannot each be established alone) drops half the argument. The marks fail both singly and together: 「云何於一處，一時有三相？」
- **Fix.** Register 中论 chapters 7, 17 and 21 as a counter-reading. Cite the actual precedent instead: 成唯識論 「「恒」言遮斷，「轉」表非常，猶如瀑流」 [R], together with 種子 "恒隨轉…一類相續" [R]. Then either answer the dilemma (the plan's 缘 is not an act that needs the condition to be present when the result arises) or record it as open. Remove "不常不断" from the supporting chains.

**F2. The 起信论 is read against itself.** *Textual error.*
- **The elision.** §5.2 quotes "念念相續……則知心相生住異滅……". The ellipsis drops "未曾離念故，說無始無明。若得無念者" [R]. In the text, continuity from thought to thought is beginningless ignorance. 相續相 and 相續識 are marks of 不覺, and the goal is to "滅相續心相" [R].
- **俱時.** It means each thought has all four marks at once, and they are 無自立 (not self-standing). The plan's "从不同区间读出" (read from different intervals) says the opposite. The formal 四相之定 also makes the four exclusive positions.
- **Scope.** The text speaks of 心相 (marks of mind), inside a 唯心 frame ("三界虛偽唯心所作" [R]).
- **Fix.**
  - Register the mind-only scope as 定域, and the 相續 = 無明 sign as a reversal.
  - Treat "心起者，無有初相可知" [R] as a model property: no event is 生.
  - The plan's reading "初起…終盡…中隨轉…前後別" belongs to 俱舍论 卷五 on 相續 [M]. Cite that, not the 起信论. The plan currently lists 俱舍 only as the dualist 有部 view.

**F3. The 金师子章 attributions are wrong, and 六相 are synchronic.** *Textual error.*
- **Commentary, not 法藏.** FO2-KS.txt is T1881, "五臺山真容院沙門承遷註" [R]. These lines appear only in the notes (FO2-KS-C) and are absent from 法藏's text (FO2-KS-P) [R]:
  - "金喻一真之性，師子喻緣起事法" (§三 戊, §5.4)
  - "理本無生"
  - "顯法界中無孤單法" (§5.2)
  
  The note even quotes 清涼, who came after 法藏. The repository's own 中华本 already attributes the 孤單 line to 承遷.
- **成/坏 are not 生/亡.** §一 cites "諸根合會是成相，諸緣各住自位是壞相" for the four positions of one continuation. 法藏's own definition in 五教章 is "壞相者，諸義各住自法，不移動故" [R]: part and whole at one time, the 舍/椽 (house and rafter) example. 中华本 records 壞相 as "不是跨步之亡" (not a perishing across steps).
- **Fix.** Take the 相分 from 法藏's text: "以金收師子盡。金外更無師子可得" and "師子雖有生滅，金體本無增減" [R]. Register alongside it the nondual "主伴交輝，理事齊現" [R].

**F4. 等无间缘, and 缘 that is not temporal.** *Textual error, formal.*
- **Where.** §4.3 correction 2 explains the fire passed on by "佛典称等无间缘".
- **Restricted to mind.** The corpus restricts 次第緣 (= 等无间缘) to mind and mental factors: "次第緣除…阿羅漢最後心心數法，餘過去現在心心數法" [R]. The 中论 then refutes it: 「滅法何能緣？故無次第緣」 [R].
- **Simultaneous 缘.** The texts also hold simultaneous and mutual 缘: 成唯識論 "三法展轉因果同時…亦如蘆束更互相依" [R], and 五教章 "因果同時" [R]. Such cycles make "时 = 缘之传递闭包" (§4.1) a preorder, not an order.
- **Fix.** Drop the 等无间缘 gloss for fire. Read time only off the acyclic part of 缘, or name a simultaneous reading (俱有) separately.

**F5. Words that carry a split.** *Dualism.*
- **分别 for E.** In these texts 分别 is 妄念 (deluded thought): "一切諸法唯依妄念而有差別" [R], and "凡夫分別為有" [R]. That is exactly the "judge's choice" the plan denies E is. Rename it, for example 類 after 墨经 "有以同，類同也" [R].
- **事 as carrier.** 事 is defined against 理 in 华严 usage, and the plan sets 理 = 有常之核 over 事. That rebuilds the pair it registers as 相分 in 金师子章. Either show the two pairs differ, or register the plan's own pair.
- **相.** 四相 and 六相 use 相 as mark (lakṣaṇa), not the reciprocal 相 X of §4.2.
- **缘 has more senses.**
  - The Abhidharma 四缘 include 缘缘, the condition that is an object of cognition. That is a knower/known split.
  - 荀子 "缘天官" means "relying on".
  - 养生主 "緣督以爲經" means "following along" [R].
  
  State which sense 缘 takes, and exclude 所缘缘 (the object-of-cognition condition).
- **Others.** 判据 (criterion, which implies a judge), 载体 (carrier versus carried), and 观法 / 主读法 (which imply an observer) should go, or each state its relational basis.

**F6. 荀子 and 墨经 read as ontology.** *Textual error.*
- **荀子 is naming, not ontology.** The 一实 passage ends "此事之所以稽实定数也。此制名之枢要也" [R]. It is a rule for counting referents when making names. What individuates is place: "状同而为异所者…谓之二实". The grounds of sameness and difference are "缘天官" and "约定俗成" [R]. Record that the criterion is the 所 (place) of the 天官 relation, plus convention.
- **墨经 化 is in an agentive list.** 经上 lists 化 among the kinds of 爲: "爲，存、亡、易、蕩、治、化", and 爲 is defined through 知 and 欲 [R]. 中华本 already notes this.
- **Polysemy to register:**
  - "生，刑與知處也": 生 here means living, not arising.
  - "已，成、亡": one word for both completion and perishing.
  - "盡，莫不然也": a quantifier ("all"), not an ending.
  - "窮，或有前不容尺也": a spatial limit.
  - 中论 ch.7's 生生 (本生/生生): the regress that the plan's 续之续 resembles. Address it.

**F7. Pairing and power are fitted.** *Overclaim.*
- **兼/體 is whole and part.** "體，若二之一，尺之端也" [R] describes part and whole, the endpoint of a measure. 兼 in "兼三材而兩之" [R] joins three things. Neither gives functional quasi-projections, which Tarski–Givant pairing needs.
- **所 is not shown as power.** "各指其所之" [R] means "the direction it heads". In 荀子, 所 is place.
- **Fix.** Mark both as conjectures that need a corpus case with functional projections.

**F8. Smaller textual points.**
- **周氏's six gates.** 孔疏 reports 周氏's 相因/相须/相病 classification and then rejects it: "故不用周氏之義" [R].
- **动静有常.** The line sits in the 天尊地卑…貴賤位矣 sentence [R], whose head the plan registers as 相分.
- **两者同出而异名.** 王弼 reads it as "在首則謂之始，在終則謂之母" [R]: positions on one course, not a relation and its converse.
- **Rule 3.** "凡位之是其位，由其际之凡然定" is the May drafts' newly composed classical-style sentence, quoted as if it were classical.

- The rules (separating verse from 青目 and the text from its notes; tagging each reading with its commentary; not taking over the soteriology) are right. They are just not applied yet.

## Important passages the plan misses

- 中论 ch.1, 青目: "若一則無緣，若異則無相續", with the grain and sprout examples. Also the verse 「因是法生果，是法名為緣」, with 青目 "緣成由於果" (a condition is a condition only through its result).
- 中论: ch.17 and ch.21 on 相續 (above); ch.10 「若燃是可燃，作作者則一」 (the fire and fuel parallel to 火传); ch.19 「因物故有時」; ch.24 「眾因緣生法…亦是中道義」.
- 五教章: 六相 and "椽是因緣，由未成舍時無因緣，故非是緣" (a rafter is a condition only in the finished house).
- 成唯識論: "恒轉如瀑流" (the passage quoted in F1) and "如炷生焰、焰生焦炷" (the flame and wick).
- 起信论:
  - "依一心法，有二種門…二門不相離" is the nondual form.
  - The asymmetry to register is "依如來藏故有生滅心".
  - 同相/異相 with the example of clay vessels of one fine dust: coarsening, in the text itself.
- 金师子章: 十世隔法異成門 (the nine times held in 一念) and 說無生 "但是金生".
- 墨经: "同，重、體、合、類" with the 经说 glosses (four kinds of 同, one E each); 经说 "始當無久"; "化，若鼃爲鶉".
- 荀子: "验之所缘以同异" (荀子's own 验).
- 王夫之 外传 卷五:
  - "萬變之理，相類相續而後成乎其章" is close to 续 = 缘 ∧ E, and to 成文.
  - "繼之則善矣，不繼則不善矣" is an evaluative reading of 续, relevant to the deferred ethics.
- 孔疏 序卦: "二二相耦，非覆即變" (pairing by reversal or complement).
- [M] 俱舍论 卷五, the 相續 reading of the four marks; 郭象 on 火传, "命續而不絕".
