Reading chosen
--------------
1. Along the steps of one determination (for ComesToBe / CeasesToBe, p : Nat -> Prop).
   carrier  Nat (the step index)
   缘       Steps.next s t := t = s + 1  (step s is a condition of the next step)
   class    Steps.alike p s t := (p s <-> p t)  (the two-valued class of p; a 类: Steps.alike_class)
2. On a web (for Arises / Ceases / Unceasing).
   carrier  Nat x F (occurrence = step, formation)
   缘       Steps.along x y := y.1 = x.1 + 1 /\ y.2 = x.2  (the same formation's next step)
   class    Web.activityClass W x y := (W.active x.1 x.2 <-> W.active y.1 y.2)  (a 类)
   Omission: no dependence, drawing or flow is taken as 缘; what conditions an occurrence here is only
   the same formation's previous step. The web's positions are exactly the positions of each
   formation's activity read along the steps (Web.sheng_iff_steps, Web.wang_iff_steps).
   Not invented: the modules compare activity at t and t+1 of one formation and nothing else.
Along the steps 缘 is one-to-one, so an ending at t is a beginning at t+1 (Steps.wang_iff_sheng_next);
PLAN 4.3 "灭与补" (end_not_beginning) is about many-to-many 缘 and does not apply here.

The excluded-middle calibrations, exactly
-----------------------------------------
* «四相之定当且仅当排中»: deciding 生-or-有前 at every occurrence of every carrier, relation and
  class is EM. positions_iff_em: still EM when restricted to determinations along the steps (the
  -> direction is new: firstThen Q at step 1 begins iff not Q, has a predecessor iff Q).
* World.law_iff_em: the finite-world law ArisingRecurs <-> CeasingRecurs, asked of all finite
  worlds, is EM. So the two are equivalent as principles; checked in scratch (probe, empty axiom
  reports): World.law_iff_positions := law_iff_em.trans positions_iff_em.symm, and
  World.law_iff_grammar_positions := law_iff_em.trans «四相之定当且仅当排中».symm. These would go in
  Space (or below it) when Space's owner wants them; I did not edit Space.
* What this says about the unfolding: deciding a position at one step is deciding whether activity
  changes across that step (positions_iff_change). In law_decides, the ceasing that the law hands
  over is an ending of an active occurrence (Web.ceases_iff_wang), and the undecided world carries Q
  at one step's position, as firstThen Q does. In law_of_em, EM is used four ways; only one,
  persists_of_em, is local, and it needs only decided positions (persists_of_positions). The other
  three (em on CeasingRecurs, exists_not_of_em, quiet_of_once) decide questions about unboundedly
  many steps, which no single position decides. Not shown: whether, for a fixed finite world,
  decided positions imply the law or the converse.

Not connected, and why
----------------------
* Rises, Falls, Still, Depletes, RenewedBy, ActiveRenewer, stock and every law about them
  (depletion_not_recurring, Unceasing.rises, substitution_defers, ...), and Change's counting laws
  (marks, rise/fall, over_stretch, held_over_stretch, keep_pace, coming_recurs_iff,
  arising_recurs_iff, unceasing_iff): they read amounts and counts; the grammar has no amounts.
* LPO results named in Change's header live elsewhere; continues_in_core_iff_buqiong is where
  settling (deciding whether a run changes again) meets the core, but no LPO calibration is proved.
* Recurs, Accretion, Attrition: recurrence over arbitrarily late steps has no grammar term yet.
