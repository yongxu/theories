  Imports: each module adds only `import Becoming.Grammar`. No existing statement or proof is changed.

Reading
  Carrier: occurrences `Nat × F`, the pair (step, formation).
  缘: `Web.continuationStep R x y := y.1 = x.1 + 1 ∧ R x.1 x.2 y.2`, one step of the continuation
      relation `R : Nat → F → F → Prop` that both modules already use (`Web.Continues`, `Continuing`, `Linked`).
  Classes (each a «类», proved):
    `Web.activityClass W` = "active or not" (alikeBy of activity), the class of `Continues`/`Continued`/
      `ArisesFrom`/`CeasesFrom`/`Lineage`/`Continuing` (the flame's "flame or not" in the grammar);
    `«一»` for `Linked` (nothing asked of activity);
    `Web.dependenceClass W c` = "requires c or not", the class of `holdingOn` and tenure (`MetAboveAlong`).
  Scope note: under "active or not", two inactive occurrences linked by R also continue each other; so the
  instance theorems for lines say "from an active occurrence" (a line from an active occurrence stays active).
  That is a property of the class, stated in every statement where it matters.

Not connected, and why
  The census balance and its halves (Census.balanceFrom, arisingFrom_of_ceasingFrom, ceasingFrom_of_arisingFrom),
    forces_iff_noSplit, forced_iff_more, Lineage.unique/ends: counting laws over listed formations; the grammar
    has no counting. Only their premises (NoSplit, NoMerge, ArisesFrom, CeasesFrom, Lineage) are connected.
  KeepsActive/KeepsTenure, renaming laws, Turnover, Succession, Dormancy, World.continues_as_alike: these compare
    two namings; the grammar's counterpart would be a map of carriers. Only carry/lines (line_reidentify) are done.
  IncludesAcross/ExceedsAcross/WidensAcross: they rest on Linked, now linked_iff_line; their own laws were not restated.
  «有常», «核», «位核», «异», «复生», «生生», «粗»: no old notion of these two modules matches them exactly
    (e.g. no module notion of a finer class telling continuations apart); not forced.
