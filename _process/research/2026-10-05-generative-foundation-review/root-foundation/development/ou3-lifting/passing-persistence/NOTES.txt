READING
Carrier: occurrences Nat × F, (t, a) = formation a at step t (carrier equality is the grammar's
model commitment). 缘: `Web.continuationStep R` for a continuation R the module already reads.

PatternPassing. R = `Web.passingContinuation passes`: a at t+1 continues b at t where b = a
(carrying on) or passes t b a. Classes: pattern's class = `V.activityClass` (alike in carrying
the pattern); carrier class = `V.carrierClass` = activityClass ∧ same formation, finer.
Persistence. R = the names (fun _ g f => g = f), the identity premise of Becoming.Identity.
Persisting changes the class, not 缘: holding = `(W.persisting h).activityClass`; state class
`W.stateClass h` = alike in activity and in holding (active / dormant / absent), finer.

NEW DECLARATIONS, PatternPassing (18 theorems, 3 defs added; general lemmas in namespace Becoming)
- marked_step_of_apart: any carrier/relation; if each 续 step keeps class K or carries mark P,
  a line (相续) whose ends K tells apart has a P-step, with its place on the line. Constructive
  (uses the split, not excluded middle).
- line_within_class: a line under a class E has E-alike ends.
- alters_of_begins: 生 under F at y and 续 under E from x to y give 异 R E F x y.
- Web.passingContinuation, Web.sameFormation, Web.carrierClass (+ _class, _finer).
- Web.byPassing_iff_continues: ByPassing ↔ every carrying occurrence after a step Continues
  (Identity's sense) under the passing continuation.
- Web.byPassing_iff_hasPred: ByPassing ↔ every carrying occurrence at t+1 has 有前 under the
  pattern's class (nothing begins under it after step 0).
- Web.arises_iff_sheng_carrier / ceases_iff_wang_carrier: Arises/Ceases ↔ active ∧ 生/亡 under
  the carrier class.
- Web.passesAt_of_alters: 异 (pattern class, carrier class) from a carrying occurrence ⇒ PassesAt.
  One way only: PassesAt counts passing to oneself, no alteration.
- Web.alters_of_passes: passing to another formation, both ends carrying ⇒ 异.
- Web.passing_fire (火传, 庄子「指窮於爲薪，火傳也」): a carrier ceasing while it passes ⇒ 亡 under
  carrier class ∧ ¬亡 under pattern class ∧ 异. The «亡于细者不必亡于粗» shape.
- Web.ceases_of_pattern_ends: carrying occurrence with 亡 under the pattern class ⇒ Ceases
  (from «亡于粗则亡于细»).
- Web.ByPassing.line_back: under ByPassing, an occurrence at S+k+1 lies on a 相续 under the
  pattern's class from a carrying occurrence at S.
- Web.ByPassing.passes_within_by_line: exactly the statement of ByPassing.passes_within, derived
  from line_back + marked_step_of_apart (carrier class tells the line's ends apart by Brief).
- Web.ByPassing.begins_passed_by_grammar: exactly ByPassing.begins_passed, derived via
  byPassing_iff_hasPred + alters_of_begins.
- PatternRelay.shifted_fire: every odd step of `shifted` is a 火传 step.
- PatternRelay.fire_shape: «则» carrier pattern ∧ 亡 under carrier at (1,0) ∧ ¬亡 under pattern:
  the grammar's «亡于细者不必亡于粗» inside the module's witness.
  (In PatternRelay.web each carrier passes at its FIRST step, so its last occurrence ends under
  both classes: not a 火传 witness.)

NEW DECLARATIONS, Persistence (17 theorems, 1 def added)
- begins_finer: dual of «亡于粗则亡于细» (生 under coarser ⇒ 生 under finer), any carrier.
- Web.stateClass (+ _class, _finer: finer than holding).
- Web.wang_state_of_change / sheng_state_of_change: activity changing across a step ⇒ 亡 before /
  生 after under the state class (no premise).
- Web.ceases_iff_wang_state / arises_iff_sheng_state (activity within holding): Ceases/Arises ↔
  active ∧ 亡/生 under the state class.
- Web.ceases_of_persisting_ceases: forward half of ceases_persisting_iff, from «亡于粗则亡于细».
- Web.arises_of_persisting_arises: forward half of arises_persisting_iff's first part, from
  begins_finer.
- Web.fallsDormant_alters / wakes_alters: falling dormant (waking) is 亡 (生) under the state
  class and 异 under holding: falling dormant is not ceasing, waking is not arising.
- Web.names_line_same: a line along the names keeps one formation.
- Web.unending_of_holds: holding at every step ⇒ 不穷 under holding.
- World.arises_fixed_iff_sheng / ceases_fixed_iff_wang: World.arises_fixed_iff / ceases_fixed_iff
  composed with Identity's arisesFrom_iff_sheng / ceasesFrom_iff_wang: an arising (ceasing) fixed
  by conservation is 生 (亡) under 缘 = continuationStep w.Conserves, class activity.
- Hibernation.positions: «则» state holding; 亡 under state ∧ ¬亡 under holding at (0,hibernator);
  at the dormant step (1,hibernator) 住 under holding and both 生 and 亡 under state; 不穷 under
  holding from (0,hibernator).
- Seed.held_classes: same 缘, two choices of h: 不穷 under holding by dependence at every step;
  亡 at (3,()) under holding by reserve.

NOT CONNECTED, AND WHY
- Web.begins_within: an instance of Web.Covered.arises_within; reads no continuation.
- ByPassing.trace, present_back, present_earlier, present_of_le, empty_stays, ends_within: about
  presence at a step (∃ a carrier), and a step is not an occurrence; no grammar term. trace's
  content is covered by line_back.
- Persistence: steady_held, named_throughout, renewingIn_held, consumingIn_held, the
  re-identification and world-naming laws (fixed_iff, still_of_apart, classes_fixed, Shuttle,
  Lineage): these are about namings of the carrier, which the grammar takes as a model commitment
  (carrier equality), not about classes on a fixed carrier. persisting_census counts lists.
- The converse halves of ceases_persisting_iff / arises_persisting_iff are not coarsening laws
  (they need "not holding after", a fact about the coarse class); left as they are.
- Candidate grammar laws for the Grammar owner: marked_step_of_apart, line_within_class,
  alters_of_begins, begins_finer (all stated for every carrier and relation).
