## Findings, most serious first

1. **Row 2's return step in `step_back` is a round trip.** The local `first` was, term for term, `returns_iff_reaches.mpr`, so the `.inl` branch was `mpr (mp returns)`. It added a grammar dependence and no content, since `Returns P s` already is the conclusion at `x = s` by definition. That is the kind of detour §十三 forbids. The case split itself (`«所闭当且仅当同一或及»`, `«及当且仅当一步而所闭»`, through `reach_iff_withinClosure`) is a genuine, near-definitional instance, like `Invariant.reach`, and `loss_passes_juncture` uses return's content only through `step_back`. But its type still names `Returns P s`, so "its dependence on return now goes through the grammar" was false: it reaches the grammar through the closure. Fix: use `returns` directly in the `.inl` branch and drop the claim. The count is unchanged.
2. **The reason for rows 3 and 4 covered only half of the lasso.** It ruled out `«定后于线有限则末返»` for lacking the bound. The other half of `Learning.lasso_of_held` is the periodicity after a revisit, proved by an `orbit_add` chain, and it follows from `«迭之接»`, `«迭之定»` and `steps_orbit`, just as OU3b derived `Law.orbit_mul` from `«迭之周»` and `«迭之定»`. Fix: derive the periodicity that way (the bound stays pigeonhole, and rows 3 and 4 then reach with content, 32/30/6/50), or write why the `orbit_mul` precedent does not apply.
3. **Landing break: the UE9 record still binds the moved laws to `ComparingCourses`.** `comparing-two-courses-on-what-they-admit.json` lists both names in its proof reference to `ComparingCourses`. The registry checks for "declaration not found" and "not elaborated in Becoming.ComparingCourses" would fail, and re-pointing never changes declaration lists. Fix: move both names to a `GrammarLaws` proof reference and binding in that record, with a history note.
4. **Row 1 is a genuine instance; the new lemma has two small issues.** `Lumped π (π ∘ next)` says that `π ∘ next` factors through `π`, which is the image's fixed successor, the same standing as OU3b's `image_determined_iff`. The lemma is correctly stated; both directions were checked. (a) It put the grammar side first, against the bridges' orientation (`returns_iff_reaches`, `determined_iff_fixed`, `goAlikeUnder_iff_alike`); naming it `lumped_iff_fixed_image` with the sides swapped would match. (b) It sat under a heading and a module note that call the section's declarations moved and unchanged, though it is new.
5. **A docstring said more than its proof.** `ReachableRanges` said "widening is covering with lag zero"; the proof shows only that widening's inclusion half covers at lag zero. Write "what widens covers with lag zero".
6. **The strict join's statements are clean; its placement was undocumented.** The statements are identical apart from `Type` to `Type u`, `ComparingCourses`' uses resolve unchanged, the headers of `Reaching.WidensOrLater.trans` and `.irrefl` are unchanged and type-check, and no conflicting name exists. But they are the module's only ASCII-named declarations directly in `Becoming`, and the header named neither them nor the new consumers. Add a sentence. Keep them typed on `α → α → Prop`: restated over `«际»`, every consumer would reach the grammar under the measure's `«` test.
7. **A wrong cross-reference** in the audit's opening ("row 7" for the relation laws).
