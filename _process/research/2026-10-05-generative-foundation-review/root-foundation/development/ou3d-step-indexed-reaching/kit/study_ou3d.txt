## {N}. Step-indexed reaching on the grammar (OU3d)

After OU3c ([§433](#433-the-return-lumping-and-order-anchors-on-the-grammar-ou3c)), EC6's audit ([audit](development/ec6-no-master-law/AUDIT.md), *Step-indexed reaching*) found three of the fifty central declarations on no master law reading the `n`-fold composition of a relation: giving that waits on giving spreads within `n` waits, waiting on all restores exactly where no chain is that long, and a course carries every bound on realizing a bundle. Closure carries no step counts, and the grammar had `«迭»` and its composition but neither reaching within `n` steps nor a law of what holds after `n` rounds. The coordinating note ([WHAT-REMAINS](development/ou3b-master-laws-on-the-grammar/WHAT-REMAINS.md), *After EC6's audit*) decided that this is a real law, to be stated beside `«迭»` and reviewed before any consumer bridges to it.

**The review, and what was taken.** One independent read-only review by a separate AI agent ([REVIEW-OU3d](development/ou3d-step-indexed-reaching/REVIEW-OU3d.md)); every finding was taken.
- A leading 或 reads "as far as can be refuted" (`«或屡»`), so staying or a step is `«静或缘»`.
- 守 is `GrammarMap`'s keeping, so the law with memory is `«存承之迭»`.
- The last step is `«迭之末步»`, as `«相续之末步»` names a line's.
- The composition's bound runs `m + n`, the brief's and the consumers' order, so `after_course` needs no commutation.
- The header no longer lists a law's run among the pending bridges, since a run already is a course whose rounds hold by definition, and it says only that `Withholding`'s closed forms have these shapes.

The review re-proved the real consumers' statements from the laws (23 declarations, every axiom report empty). That check is kept, adapted to the final names, as the second step's evidence.

**Self-review.** (1) Each law was read against the brief: composition is the audit's first part; `«承之迭»` is its iterate law in the exactly-`n` form, `«存承之迭»` the within-`n` form with memory. (2) The memory law is derived from the law without memory, not proved beside it (board rule 9). (3) Not done: the items in Boundary.
