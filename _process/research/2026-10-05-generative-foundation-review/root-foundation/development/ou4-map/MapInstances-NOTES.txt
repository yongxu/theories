Which region each regime reaches
  generation   the shifts (witness); in general generation needs renewal beyond every step and, under passing, unceasing arising (one way)
  keeping      the name classes when endless (Web.itself), and any endless line whose formations carry on as themselves from some step (one way)
  degeneration consumption with the dependence held, family excess (Wholes/Continuation), stopped passing: proved as not 不穷 (outside generation and keeping) constructively; reaching an end (退化) follows where going on is decided on the line (degenerates_of_gone). That step is exactly where the map's boundary «生败之定当且仅当排中» sits, so the one-way, refutation form is the honest constructive statement.
  新而终       the relay (witness); a stopped relay would read the same but was not proved separately (relay suffices).

Not connected, and why
  RenewingIn / RenewingAlong: read conditions, not whether a line goes on; Map.renewal_undecided shows it is compatible with 新而终 and with 生成. No implication to any region.
  ConsumingIn / ConsumingAlong: a drawer need not depend on what it draws (Dependence witnesses keep the drawer active), so consumption alone implies no region; only with the dependence held on the line (consumed_held_not_endless).
  Name classes: RenewingIn = RenewingAlong itself (existing renewingAlong_itself_iff); along itself the map has no generation, so these regimes read only as keeping or degeneration.
  GroupEnding: its laws are about generations as lists and offspring counts (selection among groups); its ending on the base is Flow.family_childless, i.e. the family-excess law already connected; groups as a class would need a carrier of groups, not occurrences, so not read here.
  Lasting, Capture, ClosedCapture: laws of guards/levels, shares and rules on configurations, not lines of occurrences under a class; no honest choice of fine/coarse class found.
  Brief carriers do not force generation on a given line (observation, not proved): with overlapping carriers a line can be continued only by passes to carriers already active, while the arisings lie on parallel lines; passes_within gives a passing on the line, not a 传.
  Observation about the map: 不穷 asks every branch to go on, so in the relay the carrier's last occurrence ends the line (degeneration) although the pattern is present at every step; the region is a line's, not presence's.

Modelling choices: discrete steps, formations a fixed index type, Prop-valued activity, natural-number levels and needs.
