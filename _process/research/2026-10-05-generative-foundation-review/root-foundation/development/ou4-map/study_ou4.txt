**Why two grains.** Under one class the grammar's renewal is either impossible (under the one) or trivial (under identity every occurrence with a successor renews). What the theory needs to separate is the renewal of parts that keeps a whole going (cells and an organism, flames and a fire, members and an institution) from turnover that ends it. With the parts' class finer than the pattern's, a step that continues the pattern while a part begins is a carrying renewal, and the region of a point is read off the pattern's line: it goes on and keeps renewing (generation), goes on without renewing (keeping), or ends (degeneration). The meta-attractor of the plan is a generating pattern: the pattern conditions the beginnings of its own parts (the condition of each new part lies in the pattern's class), and it goes on through them.
