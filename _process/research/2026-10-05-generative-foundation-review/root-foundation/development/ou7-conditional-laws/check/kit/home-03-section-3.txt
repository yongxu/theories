**Theorem** (root; a family of models). One rule makes the nets of a family of rooms. A room's level, in whole units, moves one unit a step toward its surroundings, at its set level m while the window is shut and at 0 once it opens; a heater, reading the level after that exchange, gives c wherever it is below m. So m is the margin, counted in the open window's draws of one: the unheated room (c = 0) is thermal equilibrium; the matched thermostat (c = 1) gives what the window draws, the overshooting one (c = 2) more. The window's opening is in the net as an alternative, and the rooms are compared as rules, side by side. While no window opens, every room at its set level is one core class at the level, at every margin. With the window in the net, at every positive margin, the level's first stage tells the unheated room from every heated one; under warm or not, at a margin of n + 1 draws, stage n holds and stage n + 1 tells them apart, and two heated rooms stay one core class. Lean: `«不开窗则同核»`, `«开窗则一层可分»`, `«开窗以温层如其余乃分»`, `«有给之室以温不可分»`; [record](../results/records/rooms-tested-by-a-window.md).

- **The toy derived.** At a margin of one draw, the branches `«恒温与平衡»` stipulates are the rule's steps, and its verdict follows from the stage law there (`toy_branches`, `toy_verdict`). At a margin of three, warm or not first tells the rooms apart at the third stage.
- **Regions** (at warm or not, and the level). At a margin of three the overshooting thermostat at its set level generates along the window's branch and keeps along the shut one, a model case of [the mixed region](02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains) (`«给过所取则生守相杂»`); the unheated room keeps and reaches an end, and once the window opens degenerates while renewing (`«平衡守而有终»`, `«开窗则平衡新而尽»`). At every positive margin the matched thermostat is 全守 (`«给如所取则全守»`), as every room is while no window opens (`shut_rests`). At a margin of three, where the net branches, the overshooting thermostat generates and keeps from some point on together, so the single-line law needs its condition (`Overshooting.single_line_tested`).
- **A conditional law.** At every positive margin, warmth is a constant class under the net exactly where the heater gives (`«温有常当且仅当有给»`). From a margin of two on, no line of warmth from the set level ends exactly there (`«温不穷当且仅当有给»`); at a margin of one none ends, heater or not (`unended_at_one`). Across rooms the converse of `«有常则往不穷»` fails (`joint_converse`).
- **The window as a regime.** With the window open, the rule is [the regime's step](02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime) with the heater's giving: the unheated room is cold from its margin's step on, at a positive margin the matched thermostat holds its set level, and at a margin of three the overshooting course alternates 3 and 4 (`Unheated.window_cold`, `Matched.window_held`, `Overshooting.alternation_shifted`). Each course returns, so none keeps widening ([07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-where-the-possible-grows)); a coarse line is the possible with warmth held (`line_iff_held`).
- **The reading order.** Read before the exchange, at a margin of three the window moves both thermostats' levels (`before_window_moves`).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Equilibrium and regulation rest alike until the net holds a disturbance. What tells them apart is how each answers the window's draw, and the stage at which warm or not first shows it counts the draws that empty the unheated room's warmth. A heater that gives exactly what is drawn keeps, in the region of rest; one that gives more renews at every step once the window opens: regulation has no region of its own.

**Open.** An instance of the rooms on the World's flows, by which [the laws of regulation](02-MAP.md#r-two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading) would apply to them formally; a heater that reads late; gusts; the regions and the cold occurrence at margins other than three; the toy's first half on its own relation; the variants checked by hand only (the window, the threshold, the reading order, and «通» at the overshooting thermostat's set level); the Hennessy–Milner direction.
