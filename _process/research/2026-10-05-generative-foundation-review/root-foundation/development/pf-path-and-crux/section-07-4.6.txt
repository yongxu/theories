### 4.6 The future without the step: one path

The step of invariance extends scope to losses that return to no one. Read with time, those losses include everything after every present center has ended, the future that the condition's "continuation into the future" names. This section joins §4.3 and §4.5 into one path that reaches part of that future with no step of invariance, and says where it can be denied.

<a id="r-what-a-line-holds-and-a-regularity-takes-in"></a>
**Theorem** (root; any type, and witnesses). *What a line must hold, and what a regularity takes in.*

- **What a line must pass, its class must hold.** Where a region, given by a decided predicate, is left under a class only into a set of occurrences, a line under that class from inside the region to outside holds a member of that set alike to its start, and reaches it (`«所经必同»`). Where the class holds none of them alike to the start, nothing outside is reached (`«所经不同则不及»`).
- **Holding a member's effects.** Say a class holds a member's effects where everything the member conditions is alike to it (`«所缘同于»`). Under a constant class, everything downstream of anything alike to such a member is alike to it and lies on a line under the class from there, so a line that reaches it reaches all that follows (`«常闭则后皆续»`, `«常闭则所及皆及»`). Given constancy, holding a member's effects is equivalent to everything downstream of it being alike to it (`«常类之闭当且仅当后皆同类»`): the backward direction is the one-step case, and the forward direction is constancy's work.
- **Up to the net's end.** A class is *constant up to the net's end* where whatever an alike occurrence conditions, this one conditions something alike to it, unless this one conditions nothing (`«至终有常»`); a line under it ends only at an occurrence that conditions nothing. Constancy gives it (`«有常则至终有常»`). A relation under which a line ends at an occurrence that still conditions something lacks it (`«亡而网续则不至终有常»`). Under it the same laws hold, for a member that conditions something where the start is another occurrence alike to it (`«至终常闭则后皆续»`, `«至终常闭则所及皆及»`, `«至终常类之闭当且仅当后皆同类»`).
- **Witnesses.** Neither premise can be dropped:
  - where a member's line runs through a condition but the member's effects leave its class, a constant class holding the member with the condition does not reach what the condition feeds (`Unclosed.not_taken_in`);
  - where the class is not constant even up to the net's end, a class holding a member's effects need not have what follows them alike to it (`Inconstant.not_all_follow`).

  In a finite net with two families, each of two members in turn:
  - the one is not constant (`FiniteFamilies.one_not_constant`);
  - the class of one family, narrower than the one, is constant up to the net's end, takes in everything downstream of the family's first birth and reaches nothing of the other family (`FiniteFamilies.family_narrower`, `FiniteFamilies.family_constant_to_end`, `FiniteFamilies.family_takes_in_all`, `FiniteFamilies.other_family_outside`);
  - the organism's class is not constant up to the net's end, its line ending at old age while the net goes on (`FiniteFamilies.member_not_constant_to_end`).

([record](../results/records/what-a-line-holds-and-a-regularity-takes-in.md), [`Becoming.GrammarPath`](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/GrammarPath.lean))

**Argument** [AI reading; each step cites its Lean, and the joining of the steps is argued]. Premises:

- (P1) Held de se, acting presupposes that one's own continuation has significance ([§4.1](#decomposition)).
- (P2) The self whose continuation is presupposed is a line under a class on the root ([§4.5](#condition-on-the-root)), and that class is constant up to the net's end: a regularity of its own, whose line ends only where the net itself ends.
- (P3) One's line runs through the conditions one affects.
- (P4) The class of one's line holds one's effects: everything one conditions is alike to oneself.

Steps:

1. *Need does not reach the future.* A formation active at no step of the stretch a continuation is read across is needed only where nothing lets that continuation go on (`Flow.needs_iff_none_of_inactive`). Read with time, the feeding closure holds only what gives by then (`Flow.gives_of_withinFeedingUpTo`), and read across every step it overreaches (`Overlapping.closure_overreaches`) ([§4.3](#r-need-and-feeding-read-with-time)). So the future is reached, if at all, along a line, not along need.
2. *A line goes on past a member's end.* Under a coarser class, what continues an occurrence at which a finer line ends is reached (`«过亡而续»`). A family line is constant and carries the line with its core through each birth while each member's line ends (`TwoLines.core_through_birth`). Where two family lines share nothing, even the line under the one from one family reaches no member of the other (`TwoLines.other_line_outside`; [01](01-ROOT.md#r-the-source-texts-test-systems)). In a finite net the family's class is still constant up to the net's end, and the organism's is not (`FiniteFamilies`, above). In time, self-concern alone is kin-shaped.
3. *The class must hold one of the conditions the line passes* (`«所经必同»`). The shared-condition witness is a case: a class holding both families' members without the condition reaches no member of the other family (`SharedCondition.affecting_member_outside`).
4. *What one's class holds with one, it carries on to all that follows.* Under P2 and P4, everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line, and so does everything downstream of whatever one's class holds with one (`«至终常闭则后皆续»`, `«至终常闭则所及皆及»`). Through a condition one affects, this reaches the other lines' members it later feeds (`SharedCondition.affecting_reaches_other`). Through a common cause it reaches none (`SharedCondition.fed_outside`), and what only affects the condition can be left out (`ThirdAffects.finer_class_reaches`).

*Conclusion.* Under P1–P4, one's concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of what one conditions: across lines, through the conditions one affects, and past one's own end. It needs no step of invariance and does not choose the one. Given the regularity of P2, P4 is equivalent to that reach (`«至终常类之闭当且仅当后皆同类»`). So the reach is one premise, holding one's effects, carried on by the regularity. The regularity does real work: without it, holding one's effects gives nothing beyond the first step (`Inconstant`), and without P4 the regularity gives nothing beyond the class (`Unclosed`).

*What the path does not reach.* Each center counting from another's standpoint beyond its part as a condition. What affects one's conditions without being fed by them, counted as more than a cause. Present beings beyond every closure. Whatever no chain joins. These remain the step's.

*Where it can be denied.*

- **P2 and P4 are the crux.**
  - The organism's class fails P2 wherever its last stage conditions anything (`«亡而网续则不至终有常»`): its line ends while what it conditions goes on, so it is no regularity of its own. Denying P2 is coherent, since one can presuppose the significance of a line that ends.
  - Denying P4 is also coherent: one can hold only some of one's effects as one's own. But the effects that are one's own later stages are themselves conditioned through what one takes in, so such a partial holding draws its line by the identity in question [AI reading].

  The questions the path leaves, put to philosophers in the [review packet](../review/README.md): *in acting, does one presuppose the significance of a line that ends, or of the regularity one's acts keep going?* And *are one's effects one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for?*
- **P3 is a correspondence.** The laws of renewal support it ([04](04-LIFE.md#r-generation-is-replenishment)), but it is not shown for actual selves. Nor is it shown that the actual net is joined, or in which direction.
