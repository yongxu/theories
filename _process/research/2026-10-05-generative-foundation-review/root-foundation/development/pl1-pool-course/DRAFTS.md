## Record `a-pool-that-recruits-through-itself`

**Title.** A pool that recruits through itself / 经自身招募之合池

**Statement (en).** A pool's reach lasts with its members. A pool that admits its next members through its own pooled coupling has the same members under two histories up to the first step it couples them apart, and never couples them apart exactly when the pool along the other history never does. So it comes to couple into a loss only as its course without the loss would.

**Statement (zh).** 合池之所及随其成员而存。经自身合池耦合接纳下一批成员之合池，在两历史下直至其初次耦合二者有别之步成员皆同；其永不分别二者，恰当另一历史下之合池永不分别之。故其耦合进一损失，只如无此损失之历程本会如此。

**Laws.**
1. A member that has coupled two histories apart up to a step keeps the pool apart at every later step at which it stays; where every member at a step couples them alike up to it, every selection through the pool there selects them alike. (`Becoming.Web.apart_while_member`, `Becoming.Web.selects_alike_of_members`)
2. Under recruitment through the pool (`Becoming.Web.RecruitsThrough`) and the same members at the start, the members are the same under both histories at every step up to and including the first at which the pool couples them apart, and the pool along either history separates them there; it never separates them exactly when the pool along the other never does, the members are then the same at every step, and every selection through the pool at every step selects them alike. (`Becoming.Web.SameMembers`, `Becoming.Web.RecruitsThrough.members_alike`, `Becoming.Web.RecruitsThrough.first_apart`, `Becoming.Web.RecruitsThrough.members_alike_always`, `Becoming.Web.RecruitsThrough.alike_iff`, `Becoming.Web.RecruitsThrough.selects_alike`)
3. The exits bind: a keeper alone, recruiting through itself, never couples a lost habitat apart; a pool admitting a reader where the habitat reads empty does not recruit through itself and couples the loss apart at once; a pool that recruits through itself and admits the reader at step 2 in every history couples the loss apart there, through a member the course without the loss holds too. (`Becoming.PoolCourse.alone_through`, `Becoming.PoolCourse.alone_never_apart`, `Becoming.PoolCourse.called_not_through`, `Becoming.PoolCourse.called_apart`, `Becoming.PoolCourse.scheduled_through`, `Becoming.PoolCourse.scheduled_apart`)

**Hypotheses.** No axiom. Recruitment through the pool and the same members at the start are hypotheses of the theorems. Membership is any claim about histories at each step; no count, weight or rule of aggregation is assumed. Coupling is read as in Testing (admission and the levels coupled in by level, at every step up to the one read).

**Does not show.** That any actual pool recruits through its own coupling (correspondence); how fast or whether a pool comes to couple into a loss its course would reach (the scheduled witness shows only that it can); anything about the rule a pool reads (ClosedCapture, MA2).

**Correspondence.** Co-optation and self-perpetuating boards; hiring by current staff on their own criteria; evaluators chosen by the evaluated. The exit is an admission the pool's coupling does not fix, such as an outside evaluator with its own access and criteria (CR1, section 7).

**Next.** Pools whose recruitment reads part of what lies beyond; a join with ClosedCapture and MA2 (a pool whose rule and whose members are both formed through what it reads); an independent review.
