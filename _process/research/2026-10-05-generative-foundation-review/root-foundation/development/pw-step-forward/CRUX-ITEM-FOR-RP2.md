## Item: a line that ends, or the regularity one's acts keep going

**Lean to copy.**

- `Flow.needs_iff_none_of_inactive`, `Flow.gives_of_withinFeedingUpTo`, `Overlapping.closure_overreaches` (`Becoming/Weightings.lean`)
- `«过亡而续»`, `«核有续当且仅当有不穷»`, `«续即缘当且仅当含族»` (`Becoming/GrammarScope.lean`)
- `«有亡之续则不有常»` (`Becoming/Grammar.lean`)
- `TwoLines.core_through_birth`, `TwoLines.other_line_outside`, `SharedCondition.affecting_member_outside`, `SharedCondition.affecting_reaches_other`, `SharedCondition.fed_outside`, `ThirdFed.constant_takes_third` (`Becoming/GrammarCases.lean`)

**Meaning.** The theory's ethics separates scope (whose losses count: the continuation condition) from order (which course is better: C). Scope reaches past a self only through the step of invariance, which the theory does not assert. The question here is how far scope reaches *without* the step, starting from one premise few deny: in acting, one presupposes that one's own continuation has significance (held de se).

- Read along what one's continuation needs, it reaches nothing after one's end. Need reaches only what is active within the stretch it carries, and a dependence closure read with time holds only those whose lives overlap one's own.
- Read along the line one's continuation runs on, the theory's root gives more. Every subject is read as continuation relative to a class. Under a coarser class than an organism's, the line goes on past the organism's end. A family line can carry it through each birth while reaching no other family. Where lines run through a shared condition they affect, a constant class holding that condition reaches the members of other lines that the condition later feeds; a mere common cause reaches no one. These last two are shown in small models only.

**The theory's reading** [AI reading; standing: argued, with theorems at each step, and steps 3–4 witnessed, not general]. Under three premises the self's presupposition reaches what its conditions later feed, across lines and past its end, with no step of invariance:

- (P1) the de se presupposition;
- (P2) the self is a line under a *constant* class, a regularity of its own;
- (P3) one's line runs through its conditions.

The narrow self, the organism, is a class under which a line ends, and such a class is provably not constant. So the disagreement with the critic of the step comes down to P2. *Use:* if P2 holds, the future-generations part of the ethics needs no premise beyond the self's own presupposition. If it fails, that part rests on the step. ([07 §4.5](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#condition-on-the-root); the path as proposed for 07 §4.6 is in `DRAFT-07-4.6.md` beside this file.)

**Known weaknesses.**

- [AI] Denying P2 is coherent: one can presuppose the significance of a finite life that ends. The path does not refute the critic; it relocates the disagreement.
- [AI] P2 assumes that "constant" (whatever an alike occurrence becomes, this one becomes something alike) is the right formal sense of "a regularity of its own". Other senses of regularity may give other answers.
- [AI] Steps 3–4 are shown only in small constructed models with no amounts. The general law is open.
- [AI] P3 (one's next stage is conditioned through what one takes in) is a correspondence, supported by the theory's renewal laws, not shown for persons. Whether the actual web of conditions is joined, and in which direction, is not shown either.
- [AI] What the path never reaches: counting from another's standpoint as an end, beings that affect one's conditions without being fed by them, and present beings beyond every closure.

**Literature to verify and cite** (AI-recalled; check each before use):

- Parfit, *Reasons and Persons* (1984): identity is not what matters; continuity and connectedness in degrees.
- Johnston, *Surviving Death* (2010): the self as wide as the relations it lives by; goodness as living on in others.
- Scheffler, *Death and the Afterlife* (2013): much of what we value presupposes that others live on after us (the "collective afterlife"). This is the nearest existing statement of the regularity side.
- Williams, "Persons, Character and Morality" (1976): categorical desires that give reason to go on.
- Næss, "Self-Realization: An Ecological Approach to Being in the World": the ecological Self. RP2's check found no record of a 1987 journal printing. Cite the Keith Roby Memorial Lecture (Murdoch University, 12 March 1986) and its reprint in Sessions (ed.), *Deep Ecology for the 21st Century* (Shambhala 1995).
- Perry (1979) and Lewis (1979) on the essential indexical and de se content.
- Gewirth, *Reason and Morality* (1978), for the step itself.

**Questions.**

1. In acting, does one presuppose the significance of a line that ends, or of the regularity one's acts keep going? Is this a real alternative, or does it confuse the content of a presupposition with its bearer?
2. Is "a class under which no line ends" (constancy) a fair formal reading of "a regularity of its own", and does the narrow self's failing it count against the narrow self?
3. Scheffler argues that our valuing presupposes a future beyond us. Does that support P2, or only show that we value what we cannot reach de se?
4. If the path holds, its scope in time is kin-shaped until lines share conditions. Is a scope that follows what one affects, and not whom one is related to, a defensible basis for obligations to future people?
