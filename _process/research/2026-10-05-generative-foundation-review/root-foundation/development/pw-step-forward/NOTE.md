# PW: the step forward in time

What self-concern reaches after its own end, and what the root offers there. Provenance: [the discussion of why this theory exists](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-24-why-this-theory.md). ## 1. Why this question

The ethics turns on the step of invariance ([07 §4.2](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#step)). After the route through dependence ([07 §4.3](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#dependence)), the step still does two things: each center counting from another's standpoint, and what lies beyond every center's closure, such as the dweller of `Clearing`.

The claim of this note: the largest part of the second, and the part the purpose most needs, is **the future**. That is what continues after every present center has ended. OPENING's ethics closes on it ("care for the next generation, and for the future that comes after us"), and the condition itself speaks of "continuation into the future". The race does its worst damage over time: drawing down what later lives need. So the question the work most needs answered is whether anything reaches the future without the full step.

## 2. Need does not reach past one's end

Drawn along need, the presupposition reaches exactly what the continuation needs (`forced_needs_iff`). A continuation across a stretch ending at `T` needs nothing that is active only after `T`. Any possible course that agrees with it up to `T` and differs after keeps it continuing. On the flow laws of need this is visible in their form: need is read at the steps of the stretch across which the consumer draws (`Flow.needs_giver_iff_short`, `Flow.least_giving`).

So the route through dependence, read along need (its warranted reading, since the means-end step transmits significance to what an end needs), reaches contemporaries and predecessors. **It never reaches successors.** The future is the dweller of `Clearing`, moved into time.

## 3. The feeding closure reaches the future only by ignoring time

`Flow.FedFrom g h` holds where `h` feeds `g` at some step, any step, and `Flow.WithinFeeding` is its closure. So, with lives that overlap, the closure runs forward: `h` feeds `f` while `f` lives, and `k`, which arises after `f` has ended, feeds `h` later. Then `k` lies within `f`'s feeding closure. But `f`'s continuation does not need what feeds `h` after `f` has ended. The means-end warrant follows need, not the timeless closure.

Two consequences:

- Reading the feeding closure as what self-concern warrants overstates it toward the future.
- The empirical reading of 07 §4.3 (D64), that human beings lie within one another's feeding closures, gives a shared scope among those who live at once. It does not give scope over those who come after.

This is a point for the owner of the IV3 line and of D64. It changes no theorem.

## 4. What the root offers: the self as a line

The root reads every subject as continuation relative to a class: `«续» R E`, an occurrence a condition of the next and the two alike under `E` ([01](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md)). Take that reading seriously for the self whose continuation acting presupposes.

- **The relation is fixed; the class is not.** The privileged-relation objection (P2 of 07 §4.2: no law fixes which relation the self is drawn along) is half answered by the root itself. Continuation runs along `R`, being a condition of what comes next. What the dynamics leave open is the class. The objection becomes a question of which class.
- **Fine classes end; constant ones do not.** A line under a class fine enough to pick out one organism ends, and a class under which some line ends is not constant (`«有亡之续则不有常»`). Under a class constant for `R` the line never ends after its first step (`«有常则续不尽»`, `«有常则相续不尽»`). The core of a class is the greatest symmetric constant relation inside it (`«根»`): the regularity the line itself carries.
- **What acting presupposes is about what follows.** To act is to presuppose that what follows the act has significance. Under the organism's class nothing of the self follows its end. Under a coarser class the line goes on through new members: a line of descent, a practice, a community, the living as a whole. A whole active from some step on needs parts arising at arbitrarily late steps, and parts outside every finite list (`Web.Whole.needs_arising`, `Web.Whole.needs_new`).
- **So, held de se, the question is the class of one's own line.** If the self's line is its organism's, the presupposition stops at its end. If it is a line under a class the self shares with what continues after it, then the presupposition, held de se and with no step of invariance, reaches successors along that line.

This is the forward analogue of Næss's ecological Self. Næss draws the self wide in space, through what feeds it. Here the self is drawn long in time, through what continues it. OPENING's `[^self]` already reads identity this way ("going on beyond one's cycle of life … in the larger life one was always part of") and labels it a reading. This note says where that reading does ethical work: the future, where nothing else but the full step reaches.

## 5. Strength and weakness

**For.**

- The root's own account of subjects makes the class, not the relation, the open variable, so the dynamics do privilege `R`.
- The core is fixed by the dynamics. "Which class" therefore has a non-arbitrary candidate: the core of the class an agent acts under, the regularity its acts keep going. Where an agent acts for what goes on after it (children, work, practices, the conditions others live by), the regularity it acts within is not its organism's.
- Parfit: what matters is continuity and connectedness, which come in degrees and pass to successors. Seen from the root, identity is one class among many, not what matters.

**Against.**

- Prudential concern held de se tracks the organism (fission cases, the dread of one's own death). The critic answers that one's line is one's organism's, and the argument fails there. This is the same crux as before (07 §4.2's open question), now stated as a question of classes. It is not resolved, only moved.
- A line under a coarse class reaches successors in one's own line, not every future being. The future being descended from others is still reached only by the full step, unless lines join: shared conditions make one living whole, and its line is everyone's.
- Whether the core of a living line is wider than its organism is a formal question, not settled here. If a living line's core is trivial, the argument has no non-arbitrary class to stand on and rests on choosing a class.

## 6. What changes if it holds

- The step's work narrows to three parts:
  - each center counting from another's standpoint;
  - present beings beyond every closure;
  - future beings in no one's line.
  The future in one's own line is reached by a class, not by invariance.
- The question for the philosophers in the review packet becomes concrete: *is the self whose continuation one presupposes in acting the organism, or the line one acts within?*
- For the purpose: it speaks to the commons over time, where the race draws down what later lives need and the route through dependence is silent.

## 6a. What GS1 makes exact (added 24 October 2026)

GS2's owner took candidate (3) and pointed to two laws of `Becoming.GrammarRegularity` (GS1, core receipt `20261023T192022.431748Z`, not yet landed):

- `«守理则不穷»`: a step continuing under the core is followed by another under it.
- `«亡前理变»`: where `E` is a class, the step into an end leaves the core.

So the organism's end leaves the core of the organism's class. §5's last point then splits into two cases. Let `E′` be a coarser class, such as descent, under which the line goes on past that end.

- **Within the core.** If the step from the member's last moment to what continues it lies within `E′`'s core, the dynamics fix the class that carries the line past the end, and §5's second argument for stands.
- **Outside the core.** If that step lies only within `E′` and outside its core, the line goes on past the end under a class the dynamics do not single out. Then the reach past one's end rests on choosing the class, and the crux of 07 §4.2 returns unanswered.

GS2's two descent witnesses are to say which case holds, and where.

## 7. Candidate theorems, for the owners

None is claimed here. Each goes to its owner on the board.

1. **Need ends at the end** (`Becoming.Weightings`, the IV3 line). With possible courses closed under variation after `T`, a continuation across a stretch ending at `T` needs nothing active only after `T`.
2. **The timeless closure overreaches** (the owner of `Becoming.Interdependence`). A witness: three formations overlapping in turn, the last within the first's feeding closure and not needed by it.
3. **On the grammar** (GS2): the significance of a line under a class. Held de se of a line under `E`, the presupposition reaches what continues under `E` after a member's end. A model of descent in which a class with ending members has a core whose line goes on through new members, and one in which the core is trivial, so that both sides of §5 have a witness.
