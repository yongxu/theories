## Ranked findings

**F1. No accountable sender, purpose, consent or logistics** (README.md, README.zh.md, CLASSICAL opening).
- At issue: "Return them to whoever sent you the packet. Reviews are kept with the work; whether a reviewer is named is the reviewer's choice."
- Problem: The repository is public (github.com/yongxu/theories), so "kept with the work" means published, and the cover never says so. It also does not say whether AI systems will read and act on the comments. There is no deadline, no reply address, no word on what the reviewer gets back, and no reason why this recipient was chosen. Together with "made with AI", it reads as spam or data collection.
- Fix: In the email and the cover's first lines, give the responsible person (name, background, contact), the purpose of the work (including the ethics and alignment aims), why this recipient, a return date, a plain consent statement ("stored publicly; read by AI agents under X's direction; you choose whether you are named or quoted"), and a promise to report back what changed. ## Verdict
The packet is not ready to send. The disclosures are candid, the structure is sound, and the English response forms work. But a first-time recipient meets an anonymous AI project with no stated purpose, no consent terms and no deadline (F1). They are asked for days of work under a "half a day" label (F2), in the theory's private vocabulary and the project's own bookkeeping (F4, F5). And the four dossiers no longer agree with each other or with the chapters (F3). In order: freeze a version and re-sync all four dossiers, waiting for or settling IV3l and OU6. Add a named sender, purpose, consent and logistics to the email and the cover. Cut each dossier to what only that expert can judge, with an honest time estimate and a one-hour path. Fix the cover's overclaim and honesty line, and verify every claim taken "from memory". Then send PDFs with a plain reply template, and the re-coding as a separate .xlsx.
