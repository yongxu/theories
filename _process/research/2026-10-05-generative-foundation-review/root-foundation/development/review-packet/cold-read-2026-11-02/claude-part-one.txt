**S.** This is the landing text of an unfinished, not yet human-reviewed theoretical project, which offers a general account of how anything comes to be and passes away, adds an ethics resting on two explicitly unasserted premises, and backs its formal results with Lean proofs and its empirical theses with pre-registered tests.

**Q1. What is it? (sure)**
An AI-made, not yet human-reviewed framework modelling becoming as occurrences conditioning occurrences, with persistence defined as continuation under a chosen sameness class. Lean-checked theorems, mostly on discrete models, cover renewal, collapse from joint over-taking, what a selector can reach, evolution and optimisation. An ethics is added conditionally through two unasserted premises: one for whose losses count, one for ranking courses. Empirical theses got mixed test results. By its own report, no central result is new mathematics.

Rests on: "It was made with AI, the Lean proofs included."

**Q3. What does its ethics rest on?**
- **The continuation condition (sure).** It is for scope, meaning whose losses count. "by itself it ranks nothing."
- **The step of invariance (unsure).** This is the condition's content beyond what acting for oneself presupposes. It is what counts losses that return to no one.
- **C (sure).** It is for order, meaning which course is better. The widening relation needs no premise. "calling it better is C". Mixed cases stay unranked.
- **Acting for oneself, a possible third support (unsure).** The boats example ranks a collapse below keeping the stock "with no C", using the boats' own continuation. The text does not count this as a premise. It also sits oddly with the first screen's "alone gives order".
- **Further premises (unsure).** "Choices need further premises, named". This page does not name them.

**Q4. What does it not claim? (all sure)**
- That becoming is good: "without assuming that becoming is good".
- That either premise is true: "the theory asserts neither".
- A universal verdict: "no verdict binding every possible mind is claimed".
- That the axiom reports support the modelling: "That concerns logic only".
- New mathematics: "finds none of the central results new mathematics".
- That passing a frozen test measures the thesis: "A frozen rule fixes how a verdict is scored, not that it measures what the thesis names".

**Q5. What would show it wrong?**
1. **The theses: the text does not let me say.** It asserts "Eight theses about actual systems name what refutes them", but gives no refuter on these pages. I would follow the theses link; it does not resolve here. (sure)
2. **Failed tests.** T1 failed in four settings and T5 failed every frozen rule. The text does not say what those failures did to the account, so I cannot tell what a failure costs it. (sure)
3. **T3.** A trained system with room that does not drain what lies beyond its couplings. This is my inversion of the thesis, not the text's test. (guessing)
4. **Reach of the formal work.** A becoming shown not representable in the grammar, or laws that vanish without discrete steps and whole-number amounts. The text does not say either would count as refutation. (unsure)
5. **The ethics: the text does not let me say.** The premises are unasserted and their warrant is open. The text does not say what would refute T8, or whether T8 is one of the eight theses "about actual systems". (sure)

**Q6. What is new in it? (sure of what the text says, unsure of its worth)**
"the work adds a ledger of premises, each shown holding in one model and failing in another, and an ethics with scope and order apart". So the new thing is bookkeeping plus a separation, not results. The prior-work check was AI-run and "not exhaustive".

My own remarks, from outside the text:
- Separating who counts from how to rank is familiar in moral philosophy.
- The recurrence law looks like the elementary fact that an injective map on a finite set is a permutation.

Do I hold it as one thing? **Partly.**
- I can retell one thread: continuation, collapse, limited reach, an open question, then premises.
- The text itself says "one account only in part", and most laws are not on the root. The ethics is added by design.
- The laws seem to supply the ethics' description of losses, not its values.
- The opening promises how "evaluation" forms within the unfolding, yet no law on this page addresses that.
- The evolution results feel like a catalogue beside the thread rather than a load-bearing part of it.

**Q8. Where I got lost, stopped believing or had to reread**
1. "So whose losses count turns on how far what each continues with reaches, which the dynamics leave open and the continuation condition, a premise, fixes; calling the wider course better is a second premise, C." The "So" did not feel earned, and I had to parse the middle clause several times.
2. "In a fished stock, the boats' own continuation ranks a collapse that ends them below keeping the stock, with no C; the condition adds losses that return to no boat, such as the seabirds'; ranking courses where the boats are not at stake needs C." This is the clearest sentence on the ethics, but it shows a ranking without C, against the first screen's "alone gives order".
3. "With significance a free parameter, no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the condition, by construction" If significance is left free by stipulation, this restates the is/ought gap rather than finding anything. I discounted it.
4. "The root and the ethics are one account only in part: held de se (each center of itself), the condition is a statement on the root, met by some nets and failed by others: everything lies on every center's line under the one of that relation (`«续无外»`); along a net with no cycle it holds only with one center" I lost the meaning here entirely.
5. "Under both premises the ethics argues one direction, thesis T8: for life, the way is 生生, life in the whole and its continuing generation (生发, not the map's generation) [AI reading, D1], compared by widening; no verdict binding every possible mind is claimed" The ethical conclusion is a labelled AI gloss on a Chinese term. Mixed cases are unranked and I expect most real choices to be mixed, so I stopped believing it gives "one direction" of any use. The text does not show the step from the premises to T8.
