**R2. The structure, again**

I now organize it around three questions:

1. **What makes something continue?** Relations and a chosen respect of sameness define continuation. Comparing parts with patterns distinguishes renewal from persistence without renewal and ending. The “core” concerns distinctions preserved by the relation.
2. **What sustains continuation, and what can detect its loss?** The World model adds quantities, activity, thresholds and exchange assumptions. Resource laws describe support and collective depletion. Selection laws describe what can be observed, learned or rewarded. Their intersection produces the central problem: a system can select successfully while losing conditions outside what its selection reads. Evolution, optimization and collective action supply particular developments.
3. **Which continuations should matter, and what should be done?** Dynamics leave the represented significance parameter free. The continuation condition supplies universal scope; C prefers widening possibilities. Self-concern supplies a narrower conditional route. Practical commitments and correspondence to actual systems are needed for decisions and AI alignment.

The philosophical thesis proposes that these belong to one relational reality. Classical readings interpret that proposal and vocabulary. Proof records, countermodels, calibrations and empirical theses assess different parts of it.

**Partly.** I now see a stronger connection between resource dependence, observation and collective failure. But the whole remains joined partly by interpretation: most quantitative laws are not derived on the root, significance has not been lifted onto it, and the ethical premises remain unwarranted. The overview helps me distinguish the connections established from the connections sought.

**R3. Where I reread or lost the thread**

> “A class is *constant* where whatever one member conditions, a member alike to it conditions something alike; under it no continuation ends.”

I cannot see from this formulation alone why matching conditional successors guarantees that continuation does not end.

> “Where the rule's readers are drawn from those it selects and only those it disfavours read the loss, the same reading of the loss turns the lock when it comes in time and leaves it once the selection has left those readers no more than its carriers”.

I lost track of the readers, carriers and timing; I need a small example before the theorem’s verbal description.

> “What finite records can settle has an exact form: with no premise the verifiable classes of histories are exactly the open ones; that every refutable class is closed is exactly double-negation elimination, a calibration; recurrence can be neither verified nor refuted.”

“Open,” “closed,” the logical calibration and the scope of the recurrence claim arrive faster than their meanings can be established here.

> “The strongest argument for the step is that what is one's own is a region drawn along a relation no law of the dynamics fixes; its last inference is valid by a theorem, the rest is argued, and its soundness is not shown”.

I understand the admitted uncertainty, but do not yet see why the absence of a dynamically fixed boundary supports extending significance across boundaries.

> “This shows non-entailment by the represented dynamics, by construction, not that becoming leaves significance undetermined”.

This usefully stopped an inference I had been tempted to make: a freely chosen formal parameter does not establish metaphysical independence.
