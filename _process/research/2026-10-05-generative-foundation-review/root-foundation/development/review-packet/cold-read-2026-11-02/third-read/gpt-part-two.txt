**R2. The structure, again**

I now see three connected projects, with tests and interpretations attached:

```text
Relational grammar
  → continuation relative to a chosen sameness
  → distinctions between persistence, renewal and ending
  → proposed interpretation: everything belongs to one relational unfolding

Additional modelling assumptions
  → flows, resources, requirements and renewal
  → observation, generalization and selection
  → evolution constrained by support
  → collective depletion, optimization and self-maintaining rules

These results + correspondences to actual systems
  → empirical theses and tests
  [The correspondences remain incompletely established.]

A separate evaluative construction
  → significance supplied, or interpreted through relational closure
  + continuation condition: universal scope
  + C: preference for widening
  + practical commitments: choices beyond widening
  + correspondence to actual life and AI
  → conditional ethical and alignment direction
```

The self-concern and invariance arguments attempt to warrant part of the ethical construction. They are not another proved connection from the dynamics. Classical texts supply terminology and interpretive comparisons, rather than operations or independent confirmation. The premise ledger and proof records document the dependencies across these projects.

**Partly.** I now understand more of the connective tissue: resource dependence limits persistence; coupling limits selection; neither limit determines whose losses matter. That is a coherent organizing problem. But the ontology, most model results and the ethics still do not depend on one another strongly enough for me to hold them as one explanatory theory. The overview makes that separation more explicit, particularly when it says significance’s central laws are not derived from the root.
