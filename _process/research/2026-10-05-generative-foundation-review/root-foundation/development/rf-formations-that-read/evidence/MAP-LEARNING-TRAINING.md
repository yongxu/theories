# What exists, region: learning-training (Claude Opus 5.5 reader, read-only, 29 October 2026)

Evidence for the [RF plan](../PLAN.md): one of three mapping reports of what the Theory of Becoming already proves, kept as returned. Every declaration name was checked to exist by its reader. Statements are the reader's one-sentence glosses; the Lean governs.

## Summary
Region 1 is pure core (prelude only, empty axiom reports) except Becoming.FallibleInquiry and the LearningCorrespondence bridge (periphery). ResponseLearning is an applications-ring leaf, and only the package root imports Becoming.Training. The records are recorded-checked; most were reviewed only by their author. The region establishes these laws. No prefix fixes the next value. Every extrapolation has a diagonal course on which it never rests. Elimination comes to rest in worlds a list holds, a rest that is unrefuted but never shown. The identification bounds are exact (N+N′−1; 2N−1 optimal; a bound buys silence; elimination's rest is at least cubic). Learnability is observability, which is tracking in the limit. Generalization holds only where a lumping ties an input to the data. Training is selection through the data and through the learner's own courses.
(a) Nothing on mesa-optimization. Candidates are arbitrary ExchangeLaws. No law treats a candidate that is itself a selection by an internal score, or relates such a score to RewardsThrough/Settles. The nearest results are not joined to Training: the ChoiceCountCapture chooser (locks against its standing score, outlives an unread loss), Optimum, and Commons.
(b) Off its courses, a law is installed exactly where the representation's lumping ties a history to a course. Elsewhere some same-course law takes any amount there, responding or not. This is existential over all laws that read through the lumping, not over a restricted class. Exploring steps are not installed, and there is no probabilistic bound under distribution shift.
(c) Meta level: elimination over merged classes, with no top level; systems that change their own rule (adaptive, RuleTower, Pace); in the periphery, the updating route as the rule. There is no theorem of improvement or transfer.
(d) Revision is eliminative: fitting rules are kept, answers are safe and greatest, a misspecified class settles confidently wrong, and records only narrow. A lumping change keeps every selection iff new ⊆ old. Three-valued revision settles at the least fixed point. There is no contraction, no repair of the candidate class, and no calibration certified from inside.
(e) Identification needs a bound on states or a listed world, with decided values. The diagonal world exceeds any given bound. Merge is learnable, yet its start is unidentifiable. Settling is verified only in the limit; recurrence is not verified even there (under Markov).
(f) Implicit bias appears only structurally: a search rests where no neighbour is better, the start decides among settled laws, and empty neighbourhoods install what no weight does. 03:431 and 08:646 still list implicit bias as not shown.
(g) No law has acts depend on being read. There is only selector blindness and same-course laws that differ off-course; 07:645 says nothing about deception.
Wording issues: 07:524 drops 'by every training'; 07:526 omits distinctness and present takers; the 03:178 reading drops Markov and ByRecord; Experiment.lean:20 cites a nonexistent name. Two read-only astra (high) passes cross-checked this map; I re-verified every name they added.

## Gaps
- Inner alignment (mesa-optimization): no predicate for a candidate act law that is itself a selection or optimum by an internal score over modelled outcomes, and no theorem relating such an inner score to the outer RewardsThrough or Settles. No module but the root imports Becoming.Training, and Training imports neither Optimum nor ChoiceCountCapture, so neither the counted chooser's lock (ChoiceCountCapture.counted_locks) nor an optimizer's drain beyond its couplings (Optimum.least_of_room) is stated of a trained law.
- Goal misgeneralization is named only in prose (the what-training-installs correspondence; 08-METHOD.md:460) as the 'formal kernel' of the on-course law. No Lean statement says that an installed inner target agrees with the reward on the courses and differs off them; the only existing support is the existence results free_where_unlumped and response_free_where_unlumped.
- Deception: no act law reads whether it is read (for example, whether a selector's coupling or reading schedule covers the current step). No theorem says whether training through a reader's couplings selects for or against such laws, and there is no account of incentives. What exists is selector blindness (trained_alike_beyond_closure, Flow.act_unselected, Web.Excludes.not_of_alike, Flow.unexcluded_beyond_coupling) and the existence of same-course laws that respond differently off-course.
- Restricted candidate classes: the off-course freedom laws construct their alternatives in the class of all nonanticipating laws that read through the lumping. Nothing states what a restricted, parameterized class (an architecture) installs off its courses, that is, an inductive-bias law beyond the Determines condition of Learning.Training.fit_right_iff.
- Assistance: no helper formation reads another's acts to infer what its selection favours and then acts to serve it. Available are Web.anticipates_iff (an information condition), Flow.withinCoupling_reads_iff (reading couplings is pooling), and the D68 reading resting on Optimum.mutual_as_one (an assistant optimizing its principal's signal adds capacity and no coupling).
- Meta-learning: there is no task family, no theorem that a learned update route or a merged enumeration learns faster or transfers across tasks, and no join of Training with adaptive systems (training a rule-changing law). merged_bound only composes the bound; merged_unrested shows there is no top.
- Self-model: no formation models or predicts its own law or acts. The core has only limits: answer_exceeds_bound, diagonal_open, keepHistory_fixes_nothing_else, adaptive_identifiesAlike_iff and Undecided.
- Probabilistic and optimization theory: no PAC or VC sample bound, no stochastic or gradient search, and no probabilistic guarantee under distribution shift (ReferencePlan.drift_fails is a Boolean witness). ConditionalWeights gives counting identities only, and which basin a given start reaches, beyond rest by step B + 1, is not derived.
- Belief revision: no AGM-style contraction or expansion, no repair or expansion of a candidate class that lacks the truth, and no non-monotone revision of predicate beliefs. 'Correction is not calibration' is an assessment without core Lean; its theorems are periphery, over the reals.
- Identification: the exact rest step of elimination is not shown, other enumeration orders are not covered, and worlds no list holds (the diagonal's open world) have no positive identification law.
- Institutions and networks: Region 1's learning laws are stated for one learner. There is no law of several learners training through pooled couplings, nor of an institution's learning: pooling exists only for selections (SelectionPool), and ResponseLearning is a named construction in the applications ring, not a general law of transmission.
- Evaluation schedules: in ReadingSchedule and Pace the reading schedule is supplied; no law lets the read formation shape, anticipate or evade the schedule.
- Correspondence: which lumping and neighbourhoods an actual architecture has, and whether an actual system was trained this way, are empirical questions. They are untested beyond the LA series (one trained model, Claude Haiku).
