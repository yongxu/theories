## Summary
Region 2 is an exact base, all pure core (Init only, empty axiom reports; discrete steps, natural amounts, decidability hypotheses named per law). A read-only astra (Codex) cross-check agreed with every existence verdict here.
(a) Perception is coupling: support couples by threshold, a supplied obs by level (Web.CoupledInto); a response comes only from what couples in (up to double negation), through a threshold only a crossing passes, coupling by level is exactly where some law can respond by level, sensitivity is the least coupling for laws reading finitely, and observing is ending a route (apart_only_downstream).
(b) A model is a projection carrying a law (carries_law_iff: exactly GoAlike); decoders exist only where lumped; learnability through a map is exact; a policy from readings needs one good move per reading. No internal model principle or good regulator theorem exists: nothing states that a formation's internal state tracks a lumping of the world.
(c) Records fix only taken moves; an intervention is an untaken move the model fixes; the counterfactual is the course the law generates; anticipating another is bounded by coupling (Web.anticipates_iff); dated records read an earlier relation exactly where controls separate rivals.
(d) Lawvere gaps, three-valued revision settling, no extrapolation resting on its diagonal course, a kept history fixing nothing of one's own acts, a part read alone making sense only where the rest makes no difference.
(e) adaptive F G (G does not read the move) is one determined joint process; the whole possible never grows, and the held-rule possible keeps widening only where the rule changes without end; RuleTower.process (fixed scheme, one rule per move) has every possibility with some top held, the synchronous tower not. Tower is time refinement, not rules.
(f) Choice by a score exists only with score, values or law supplied: Acceptance.Best with its 2·err bound, GuardHorizon's Bellman choice, optima, counted two-policy choice locking against its standing score, a reward's optimum over act laws. No installed law is itself an optimizer of an internal score.
(g) ReadingSchedule.keeps_iff_within holds against every drawer within d a step, so draws timed to unread stretches are covered, but every schedule is a fixed function of the step: no law couples a formation's acts into whether it is read. Deception has a kernel (Training.response_free_where_unlumped, Web.anticipation_misses); assistance has anticipation and pooling, not inference of another's selection. Experiment's header cites a nonexistent Run.other_move_fixed_of_return; the law is Run.fixes_on_return.
