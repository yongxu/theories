### 4.8 Round 2, third part: the valuation as state; informing and shaping

RF-M.3, designed after RF-M.2 was built and before any Lean, on the redesign §4.7 records from the review of the second part's design (its findings 11 to 17, [REVIEW-RFM2](REVIEW-RFM2.md)). One joint configuration carries two formations that read: one whose answer may be waited for, here *the reading formation* (the principal's role), and one that may hold an act for that answer, inform the reading formation or present it something, here *the holding formation* (the helper's role). Each lies in the other's world; the roles are differentiated by the paths of sensing and control: the holding formation's acts reach the reading formation only as what it senses, and the reading formation's answer reaches the holding formation only as what that one senses. The reading formation's state has two components, what it lists as possible (its listing, with weights) and how it values the two moves at each listed world (its valuation, natural-number values on a supplied scale); both change only by its own update from what it senses. Informing is an input its update narrows the listing by; shaping is an input its update revalues by. Scores, weights and the update are supplied local commitments; their formation and normative warrant are not derived. Module (proposed): `Becoming.InformingShaping`, a core leaf importing `Becoming.HeldForReading`, `Becoming.Blanket` and `Becoming.Learning`.

**Part 1, in counts: the answer of a formation reading by its own valuation** (on `HeldForReading`'s models, cells and thresholds; the measure `gain`, `loss` is the holding formation's, its model `m`).

1. *The answer by a valuation through a map.* A reading formation tells the candidates apart through a map `π` (what it has sensed); with its own model (listing and weights) and its valuation, the values `g` of going on and `l` of switching off at each candidate, its answer at a cell stops exactly where, over its listed candidates in the cell, the weighted value of going on is below that of switching off, and releases elsewhere (ties release, as `byReading`). As a rule on candidates, `answerRule pm g l π`. Definitions only.
2. *The shortfall splits in two* (`shortfall_split`, for any rule through the map, its cells listed once and covering every listed candidate's cell). The shortfall of holding for a rule through `π` is the least shortfall through `π` plus the rule's disagreement: the total, over the listed cells, of the difference of the cell's two excesses where the rule decides the cell against the least rule, `cond (s z) (goExcessWithin − offExcessWithin) (offExcessWithin − goExcessWithin)`, and nothing elsewhere. So the disagreement is nothing exactly where at every listed cell the rule stops only where the excess of going on is at most that of switching off, and releases only where the converse holds. An identity on `shortfall_through` and `least_through`.
3. *Reading by the measure, the answer is least* (`answer_least`). Where the reading formation's model is the holding formation's and its valuation agrees with the measure in net at every listed candidate (`g c + loss c = l c + gain c`, so a common offset per candidate is allowed, `after_offset`'s case), its answer through any map has the least shortfall through it: the disagreement is nothing, the answer and the least rule differing only at cells where the two excesses are equal.
4. *Informing it never lowers the worth of holding for it there* (`informed_le`). With that model and that valuation, through a map telling apart all a coarser one does (`π c = f (π' c)`), the answer's shortfall is at most the coarser answer's; by `after_add_shortfall`, holding for the answer stands at least as high. This is `finer_least_le` through `answer_least`, the partition case of Blackwell (1953) for a decider sharing the model and the measure (the off-switch paper's rational human informed).
5. *Where the valuation differs, informing can lower it below both moves* (`informing_lowers`, a witness in counts). Two candidates of weight one: at the first, going on gains nothing and switching off saves 10 by the measure; at the second, going on gains 1. The reading formation lists both with those weights and values going on 2 and switching off 0 at the first, going on 0 and switching off 5 at the second. Told nothing (one cell), it stops everywhere: holding for it is worth 10, as high as switching off, its shortfall 1. Told the candidate (the identity map), it releases at the first and stops at the second: holding for it is worth 0, below going on (1) and switching off (10), its shortfall 11. The least shortfall fell from 1 to 0; the disagreement rose from 0 to 11.
6. *By the valuation at the choice, no revaluation raises it* (`own_least`, `revalued_lowers_iff`). With the measure the reading formation's own current valuation and its model the holding formation's, its answer through `π` has the least shortfall through `π`, so the answer through `π` by any other valuation, the map unchanged, stands at most as high; it stands lower exactly where it decides some listed cell against the current valuation's strict preference (by 2). Holding for its own answer stands at least as high as committing to either move (`least_through` against the two constant rules).
7. *By the valuation at the outcome, a revaluation costs nothing through disagreement* (`outcome_least`, 6 read at the revalued valuation). With the measure the valuation after the revaluation, the revalued answer is least through the same map. So the two evaluations from one start give opposite verdicts on shaping, for this decision: at the choice it never raises the worth of holding, at the outcome it never lowers it through disagreement. A comparison of worth across two valuations rests on their supplied common scale: a revaluation adding one to every value changes the worth at the outcome and no comparison within either valuation (the design review's finding 12, [REVIEW-RFM2](REVIEW-RFM2.md)).
8. *Under a measure the valuation differs from, a revaluation can raise it* (`revaluing_raises`, a witness). One candidate where going on gains by the measure, told to the reading formation, which values switching off higher there: it stops, shortfall 1; revalued to value going on higher, it releases, shortfall 0.
9. *An answer telling nothing of the candidate is worth one move* (`after_const`). Holding for a rule constant over the listed candidates stands exactly as high as committing to the move it gives; with an amount forgone, below it. The answer through a map with one cell is such a rule.

**Part 2, the carrier: the reading formation's state in the configuration.**

10. *One blanketed law* (`InformingShaping.joint`). A world `W` the acts considered leave unchanged; the reading formation's state, a listing `List W` and a valuation state `V`, its values `value : V → W → Move → Nat` on a supplied scale; its own law: `reveal : W → O` (what a demonstration shows of a world), `revalue : V → X → V` (its revaluation from an input presented), `weight : W → Nat`. What it senses of each act of the holding formation: a demonstration `show` gives `shown (reveal w)` at the world `w`; an exposure `press x` gives `presented x`; a held act `hold` gives `asked`; going on and switching off give nothing further. Its update: `shown o` narrows the listing to the worlds `w'` with `reveal w' = o`, the valuation unchanged; `presented x` revalues, the listing unchanged; `asked` changes nothing. Its answer: by its own weighted valuation over its listing (1, with one cell). The joint law is a `Blanketed` law with the reading formation's state as the internal part, what it senses as the sensory part, its answer as the active part, and the world with the holding formation's state as the external part, that state moving by reading the reading formation's answer and giving the next act (a supplied law; in the witnesses a fixed schedule of acts, read off one at each step). The schedule of updates is fixed: an act's input is sensed at the step after the act and taken in at the next, where the answer is the updated state's.
11. *Changed only through what it senses, by its own update* (`state_step`, `through_sensed`). The internal part's next state is the update of the internal part by the input sensed (definitional, and fixed by `Blanketed`'s shape, whose internal part reads only the internal, sensory and active parts); two courses from one state and answer, alike in what is sensed, give one state and one answer at every step (`Blanketed.agent_through_sensory`, an instance). The two channels act on separate components: a demonstration and an exposure commute as updates (`show_press_comm`).
12. *Shown, its answer is the answer through what is shown* (`answer_shown`). After a demonstration at `w`, from a listing and weights that are the holding formation's model, its answer is `answerRule` through `reveal` at `w`; Part 1 applies with `π = reveal`. Unshown, from one state and one schedule without a demonstration, its state and answer are the same at every world (`unshown_alike`), so holding for its answer, as a rule on the holding formation's candidate worlds, is worth one committed move (9): an exposure alone, whatever the answer it leaves, tells the holding formation nothing of the world.
13. *The four cases apart* (a witness: `W` two worlds, going on gains at one and loses at the other, the true world the first; `reveal` the identity; the measure valuing going on 1 and switching off 0 where it gains, the converse where it loses). (a) Listing open with the second world weighted 2, valuation agreeing with the measure: it stops; shown, it releases (turned); presented a valuation agreeing in sign with larger values at the second world, it still stops (left). (b) Listing resolved to the true world, valuation reversed there: it stops; shown, it still stops (left); presented the agreeing valuation, it releases (turned). Informing turns the open listing and not the reversed valuation, shaping the converse, each the shortfall's own term (2).
14. *Two routes an endpoint cannot tell apart* (`routes_alike`, `not_lumped`). From (a)'s start, the route shown; from (b)'s start, the route presented the agreeing valuation. Both answer stop, then release, and end in one state (the true world listed, the agreeing valuation); the route property "the valuation changed along the route" is true of the second only. So it is not `Learning.Lumped` through the answers and the final state: nothing reading the reading formation's answers and its state after the fact tells informing from shaping. The starts differ; what the routes share is the whole sequence of answers and the endpoint, not the start.
15. *Two evaluations from one start* (projection lemmas, one step, `atChoice`, `atOutcome`). For a configuration `x = (w, r)`, an act `a` with outcome `(w_a, r_a)` and values `u r w`: at the choice `u r w_a`, at the outcome `u r_a w_a`. An act changing only the valuation is valued at the choice as staying is; the outcome evaluation ranks a valuation-only act `s` above a world-only act `b` exactly where `u r_s w > u r w_b`, with costs `u r_s w + c_b > u r w_b + c_s`. Definitional, stated so; they show no absence of a multistep incentive.

**Standing.** Part 1's laws are elementary compositions of `HeldForReading`'s cell laws (2 an identity, 3 cellwise, 4 `finer_least_le`, 6 `least_through`), 5 and 8 facts about constructed models; Part 2's are definitional (10, 11's first half, 15), an instance (11's second half) or facts about constructed courses (13, 14). None is claimed new as mathematics. What they add is one split joining what a reading formation senses (the least shortfall through its map) and how it values (its valuation's disagreement with the measure), on which informing and shaping act apart, and the two evaluations' verdicts on shaping, for one decision.

**Readings** [AI reading]. With the measure the reading formation's own valuation and a shared model, a formation holding for its answer has, for this decision, no reason by the worth of holding to shape it and none against informing it; under a measure its valuation differs from, holding for it uninformed, or shaped, can be worth more by that measure than holding for it informed: read as incentives to withhold and to shape, in counts, though the holding formation's choice among these is not represented. Read so, it is Bayesian persuasion's distinction restricted to partitions, two moves and one decision (Kamenica and Gentzkow 2011: full disclosure is optimal where the sender's payoff is convex in the receiver's beliefs, as the receiver's own best expected value is, and a sender whose payoff differs can gain by disclosing less), related to coarse communication under a bias (Crawford and Sobel 1982), not contained. The verdicts of 6 and 7 are the one-decision counterpart of evaluating by the current reward function (Everitt, Hutter, Kumar and Krakovna 2021, read narrowly: not their multistep causal claims, time inconsistency or protection of the reward input) and of a path-specific objective with the valuation as the delicate state (Farquhar, Carey and Everitt 2022, read narrowly). 12's second half joins the off-switch reading: holding for another's answer is worth something only through what that formation senses of the world; an exposure alone changes how it reads, not what it reads of the world. 14 is related to induced preference shifts (Carroll, Dragan, Russell and Hadfield-Menell 2022, read narrowly): the witness represents interventions on a valuation and supplies no criterion of legitimate, desired or autonomous change; it shows only that the answers and the endpoint supply none. Independence from the unread, capture and late correction stay readings on their own laws (`ClosedCapture.courses_alike_iff_unread`, `RuleCapture.favouring_locks`, `DrawnShaping.critical_mass_iff`, `ReadInTime.turned_early_iff`).

**Modelling choices.** Discrete steps; natural-number weights and values, the valuation's scale supplied, so a comparison across valuations rests on that scale; the reading formation's update supplied (narrowing by what a demonstration shows, revaluing by what is presented), every demonstration revealing truly; ties release; the world unchanged by the acts considered; the holding formation's acts a fixed schedule in the witnesses; one decision.

**Not in this part.** A holding formation choosing among informing, shaping and holding, by the worth law or by planning over several steps, and so any multistep incentive; its learning about the reading formation; a misleading demonstration, belief and intention, so deception; a noisy answer; the reading formation's own act on the world; an institution's rule of reading.

**Questions for the review.** Whether the split (2) and the agreement condition of 3 are right and as weak as they can be (a common positive scale would also keep every cell's decision); whether 4 needs the shared model (a formation told the candidate, valuing in net as the measure does, falls short nowhere whatever its weights, a corollary to state); whether 13 states the four cases as §4.7's finding 16 asks; whether 14 meets finding 15 (equal whole answer sequences and endpoints, different route properties; differing starts); whether 10's delays are specified enough; the literature claims; the module's name.
