## 11. What a formation's acts show

A selection reading a formation's acts is itself within the one unfolding: it goes through what of those acts couples into it ([07 §6](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)), and it fixes of the formation only what they tell apart. Two laws state this for two cases, each under its premises and for the widest class it allows, a narrower class fixing at least as much: what strict choices show of a strict order they could come from, and what readings fix, for acts reading through a lumping, where the formation is not read. Reading such a selection as a training, a helper, an overseer or a court is a reading (below).

<a id="r-what-choices-show-of-an-order"></a>
**Theorem** (root: any carrier of alternatives and any occasions; no premise; near-definitional). *What choices show of the order they come from.* At each occasion what is open is any predicate and one alternative is chosen, which the general laws do not require to be open; the choices show `x` above `y` where `x` was chosen while `y`, another, was open (`ChoiceOccasions.Shown`). Every transitive relation in which what is chosen stands above every other open alternative contains the reach of the shown relation (`ChoiceOccasions.reach_within`), and some strict order does exactly where nothing shown returns to itself (`ChoiceOccasions.accorded_iff_noCycle`, by the grammar's reach and `«及之传递»`). Then the positive comparisons every such order shares are exactly the shown reach: `x` stands above `y` in every one exactly where `x` reaches `y` (`ChoiceOccasions.above_in_every_iff_reach`; over a menu, `ChoiceOccasions.top_in_every_iff_reach`, which says nothing of `x` being open or chosen there); a narrower class of such orders makes every comparison they all make (`ChoiceOccasions.above_in_class_of_every`). Beyond that reach no comparison is forced: with no cycle, where `x ≠ y` and `y` does not reach `x`, some strict order according with the choices puts `x` above `y` (`ChoiceOccasions.above_in_some_of_unreached`), and where neither reaches the other, orders rank the two both ways (`ChoiceOccasions.both_ways_of_unreached`), since reach with one pair added is reach without it or a path through the pair (`ChoiceOccasions.reach_withPair_iff`), and adding a pair leaves no cycle exactly where there was none and the pair's second member neither is nor reaches its first (`ChoiceOccasions.noCycle_withPair_iff`). Choices required only to be maximal force no positive comparison (`ChoiceOccasions.weakly_forces_no_comparison`), though they still exclude an open alternative standing above the chosen one. In a witness, one search procedure, run with either of two strict orders according with one choice, chooses alike on the menu met and apart on a menu met after (`ChoiceOccasions.Untried.same_search_met`, `ChoiceOccasions.Untried.same_search_after`). It does not show which order an actual chooser carries, that it chooses strictly, or that a training can install either order of the witness ([record](../results/records/what-choices-show-of-an-order.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Read with the laws of training ([07 §6](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-training-installs)): if a training observes only a trained law's choices and imposes no further restriction on the orders the law could carry, comparisons between two distinct alternatives neither of which reaches the other in the shown relation remain ambiguous, and the witness realizes different later choices with one search and two compatible orders. That a training installs either, or that either disagrees with an independently stated objective, is not proved; goal misgeneralization in this sense is a possibility located exactly, not a failure shown. For a helper reading the choices of what it assists, a comparison beyond the shown reach needs information or premises beyond the strict choices: under strict choice, this is the order form of the part of inverse reinforcement learning's unidentifiability that concerns what behaviour never meets (Ng and Russell 2000); that behaviour fixes a planner and a reward together only under assumptions on the planner (Armstrong and Mindermann 2018) lies outside, since accordance fixes the planner. Rationalizing strict choices by a strict partial order is close to Richter's theorem (1966) without completeness, so no extension principle is needed; it is not Afriat's, which concerns expenditure data and utility.

<a id="r-what-readings-fix-of-the-unread"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; being in the target's lump decided; near-definitional). *What readings fix of the unread.* Let a formation's acts go alike across a lumping `π` of the occasions by what couples into it (which lumping an actual formation has is not shown). Read with the read occasions as the data, [§6](#r-generalization-through-lumping)'s law says where the readings fix the act at an occasion where it is not read, exactly where `π` ties that occasion to a read one under §6's hypotheses (the read occasions listed; for a schedule not given as a list, up to a double negation, `Learning.determines_iff_not_not`), and when the act so fixed is right. What this law adds is the twin: where no read occasion is so tied, with being in that occasion's lump decided, given a selection through agreement on the readings and a selected act law reading through `π`, every value there is taken by a selected law reading through `π` that agrees with the first on every reading, one acting otherwise wherever the value differs from the first's (`Learning.free_lumped`, `Learning.twin_selected`). For fixed maps from coupling to state to act, acts that differ come from states that differ, and states that differ from couplings that differ (`Learning.state_apart_of_acts_apart`, `Learning.coupling_apart_of_state_apart`). Neither converse holds: two occasions that differ while their contents agree (`Learning.Cue.apart_with_content_alike`) give, with the state the occasion and the act its content, states apart and acts alike, and with the coupling the occasion and the state its content, couplings apart and states alike ([record](../results/records/what-readings-fix-of-the-unread.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Read as evaluation and deployment: given a law the readings select, behaviour at an unread occasion can differ from that law's while every reading agrees (being in its lump decided, with a second act value) where no read occasion shares the occasion's lump, and only there, so where the formation can tell being read from not being read. Behaviour changing when read needs the occasions' states to differ, and they need the couplings to differ; reading these as sensitivity to being read needs other differences held fixed, as the witness holds the content fixed. A reader misled lies outside that chain: a law acting alike everywhere can mislead a reader whose readings miss where it is wrong, and a law changing when read misleads no reader who knows how it changes. Intention, anticipation and another's false belief are not stated. Read as eliciting latent knowledge (Christiano, Cotra and Xu 2021), this is an identification limit for reporters: two reporters agreeing on every supervised occasion are selected alike by every selection through the supervision.
