## 12. The reading loop

A formation that senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises is one law within the unfolding, not a subject facing a world: its internal part reads what goes on through what it senses, and what it does is part of what goes on ([§1](#r-the-markov-blanket-as-a-coupling-structure)). Four laws state that loop under their premises: when a reading of the world is kept by the formation's own update, and which reading its own history carries; what a course chosen through a model tests of the laws it keeps; what acts cannot tell of a model and an order; and when a prediction read by what it predicts is right. What a reader of the loop's acts fixes of it is [§11](#what-the-acts-show)'s.

<a id="r-a-reading-kept-by-its-update"></a>
**Theorem** (root for closure: any carrier and relation; any types for the loop; near-definitional, with one general law). *A reading kept by its update, and the reading its own history carries.* A region kept along every finite reach of a relation is a closed region, and conversely (`ReadingLoop.kept_iff_closed`, by `«闭之及»`). On one carrier of world and state, let the world become what a relation admits under the act its state gives and the state be updated from that act and the value sensed (`ReadingLoop.joint`). Where a supplied reading of the world by the state is kept by the update under every act (`ReadingLoop.KeptBy`), a policy keeps a task at every configuration reached from one where the reading holds, and goes on from each such configuration, exactly where each state's act can be taken at every world its reading admits and leads only where the task holds (`ReadingLoop.keeps_continues_iff`); successor safety alone (`ReadingLoop.keeps_iff_good`) is met by an act no world can take. Given choice over the states, such a policy exists exactly where each state has one such act (`ReadingLoop.keeping_policy_iff`). Acts good for every two worlds a reading admits need not be one act good for all (`ReadingLoop.PairwiseShared.two_share`, `ReadingLoop.PairwiseShared.none_for_all`), and being kept is not being informative: the readings of every world and of none are kept by every update (`ReadingLoop.everything_kept`, `ReadingLoop.nothing_kept`, `ReadingLoop.nothing_sound`). With the state the history of acts and values sensed, the worlds possible after a history are kept by recording and are the least reading so kept that holds at the starts (`ReadingLoop.possible_kept`, `ReadingLoop.possible_least`); a policy acting from its history keeps a task at every configuration reached from a start exactly where, at every history it generates, its act leads only where the task holds from every world possible after it, and goes on exactly where its act can be taken at each (`ReadingLoop.history_keeps_iff`, `ReadingLoop.history_continues_iff`). These verify a given policy; a condition for one to exist that ties histories to the task's futures is not proved ([record](../results/records/a-reading-kept-by-its-update.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. The law is a safety composition, related to possibilistic belief interpretations of regulation (Virgo, Biehl, Baltieri and Capucci 2025), whose closure is under the policy chosen, as `keeps_of_good` takes it, where `KeptBy` asks it under every act; with [§9](#r-when-to-inquire-and-when-to-respond)'s choice from readings it is requisite variety along a loop. The good regulator theorem of Conant and Ashby (1970) concerns simplest optimal regulation; nothing of optimality or of a regulator's having to model what it regulates is proved here. The history form says what a regulator acting from what couples in must meet: safety and enabledness at every world compatible with its own history and the starts. A coarser reading can suffice where its further worlds ask compatible acts.

<a id="r-what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test"></a>
**Theorem** (any types, on a blanketed law; equality of the values sensed decided where stated; a witness, with one law over every formation on its worlds). *What a course chosen through its model does not test.* For every blanketed law, from one internal, sensory and active start, two worlds give the formation one sensed course through step `n` exactly as long as, at each step before `n`, they answer alike the act it takes at the value it senses (`ReadingLoop.sensed_alike_iff`), and so one internal and active course (`Blanket.Blanketed.agent_through_sensory`). Where, at each step before a horizon, an answer of the second world to the first course's act and value that differs from the first world's breaks a task on the values sensed, a formation keeping the task through the horizon in the second world senses alike in both through it (`ReadingLoop.alike_within`; at every step, `ReadingLoop.alike_of_kept`). A formation keeping candidate laws, each predicting the value sensed next from the value sensed and the act, drops those the latest answer refutes and chooses its act from those kept (`ReadingLoop.Loop.law`): at configuration `n + 1` the candidates kept are exactly those listed that fit the answers to the acts of the steps before `n`, the latest answer taken in at the next step (`ReadingLoop.Loop.kept_iff`); a listed candidate predicting as the world answers in a region the world's moves keep is never dropped from a start there (`ReadingLoop.Loop.faithful_kept`, `ReadingLoop.Loop.fitting_closed`); candidates of one listing predicting alike on those steps are kept alike (`ReadingLoop.Loop.kept_alike`); and where the world answers the act chosen with the value sensed and stays, the formation chooses that act from the candidates listed, and every one of them predicts that answer, the formation rests at once on a model its course never refutes (`ReadingLoop.Loop.rests_of_unrefuted`). In a witness of two worlds alike on a known act and apart on an untried one, high in one and low in the other, a formation choosing by the worst case over the candidates it keeps (`ReadingLoop.Untested.byWorst_iff`, the worst the least rank predicted, `ReadingLoop.Untested.worst_le`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.worst_attained`) keeps both, takes the known act and senses the middle value at every step in either world, never sensing the high value of the first (`ReadingLoop.Untested.rests`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.alike_in_both`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.never_high`), the true candidate kept in each (`ReadingLoop.Untested.true_kept`). One trying the untried act first separates the worlds and rests on the true candidate in each, sensing the low value at step one and at no other step in the second (`ReadingLoop.Untested.explorer_up`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.explorer_down`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.explorer_low_once`). And every formation on those worlds, whatever its internal part, update and choice of act, that never senses the low value in the second senses alike in both and takes the known act at every step there, and from a start not sensing high never senses high in the first (`ReadingLoop.Untested.floor_forgoes`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.floor_never_high`) ([record](../results/records/what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. A formation's model is revised only by what its own course exposes, and that course is chosen through the model: the lumping the model keeps is the lumping its own choices make. The witness is a deterministic case of a model right along the course its choices take and wrong off it, never refuted there, related to self-confirming equilibrium (Fudenberg and Levine 1993) and incomplete learning (Rothschild 1974); its criterion is a specified one-step worst case, the special case of maxmin over candidates (Gilboa and Schmeidler 1989), and none of those works' theorems is reproduced. Beside [§6](#r-what-seeking-cannot-separate), where no experiment the formation can choose separates two hypotheses, here one does, and a formation choosing through its model need not choose it. Where every separating answer breaks a task, no formation keeping the task learns which world holds: a deterministic two-run obstruction, beside safe-exploration methods that guarantee exploration under regularity and reachability assumptions (Sui, Gotovos, Burdick and Krause 2015; Turchetta, Berkenkamp and Krause 2016). What tells such worlds apart comes from beyond the formation's own selection: an act that risks the task, or a reading from outside ([§11](#r-what-readings-fix-of-the-unread), [05 §8](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers)).

<a id="r-acts-alike-from-a-model-or-from-an-order"></a>
**Theorem** (any types for the law; a witness). *Acts alike from a model or from an order.* For two blanketed laws reading one kind of value and act, a relation between their configurations that their steps keep, under which the values sensed and the acts are one, gives from related starts the same values sensed and acts at every step, so no selection through those tells the two apart (`ReadingLoop.observed_alike`). On the worlds of the previous law, two specified formations, one keeping both candidates with a ranking of high above the middle and one keeping the true candidate with a ranking of the middle above high, have the same acts and values sensed at every step in the first world (`ReadingLoop.Untested.model_or_order`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.order_rests`). Both rankings, read as orders, accord with a training occasion choosing the middle over the low value and rank high and the middle oppositely (`ReadingLoop.Untested.both_accord`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.apart_beyond`), as [§11](#r-what-choices-show-of-an-order) has it in general: with no cycle shown, the positive comparisons every according strict order shares are the shown reach. Started with the true candidate alone, the first takes the untried act and senses high from step three on, while the second keeps the known act (`ReadingLoop.Untested.informed_turns`) ([record](../results/records/acts-alike-from-a-model-or-from-an-order.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. One observed course can leave an unresolved model and a reversed ranking indistinguishable: a training, an overseer or a helper reading the acts cannot tell a formation held by what its model does not separate from one held by how it ranks. This illustrates the ambiguity between beliefs and preferences in inferring what a formation values from what it does (Armstrong and Mindermann 2018, for planner and reward; Reddy, Dragan and Levine 2018, for beliefs about dynamics). Starting the first with the true candidate changes its course and leaves the second's, so whether informing helps depends on which holds: a reason for assistance to read more than acts, not a general assistance theorem, and informing is represented by its outcome, not by an act within the law.

<a id="r-a-prediction-read-by-what-it-predicts"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; the natural numbers `0` to `n` for the chain; near-definitional, with one general law). *A prediction read by what it predicts.* A published prediction read by the response before the outcome is right exactly where the response to it is itself (`ReflexivePrediction.Right`). Where the response moves every value, no published prediction is right, and no predictor is right on every admitted response once one admitted response moves every value, even a predictor reading the response itself (`ReflexivePrediction.none_right_of_moves`, `ReflexivePrediction.no_predictor_right`); on two values negation is such a response (`ReflexivePrediction.not_moves`); given choice over the admitted responses, a predictor right on every one exists exactly where each leaves some value (`ReflexivePrediction.predictor_iff`). Publishing at each step the outcome of the step before, a step's prediction is right exactly where the next step publishes it again, and a right prediction, once published or once the outcome, is published at every later step (`ReflexivePrediction.rests_iff_right`, `ReflexivePrediction.right_rests`, `ReflexivePrediction.reached_rests`). On the values `0` to `n`, under a response keeping them and their order, repeated publication from the bottom stays on the chain, rises, stays at or below every right prediction and is right by step `n` (`ReflexivePrediction.on_chain`, `ReflexivePrediction.rises`, `ReflexivePrediction.below_right`, `ReflexivePrediction.bottom_rests`): the least right prediction, reached within the chain's length. In witnesses, a threshold response has two right predictions, the one reached decided by where publication starts (`ReflexivePrediction.ThresholdResponse.reached_by_start`); Boolean negation leaves no value fixed, and publication alternates for ever (`ReflexivePrediction.Negating.alternates`); and a response reversing the order can leave one fixed (`ReflexivePrediction.Reflecting.reverses`, `ReflexivePrediction.Reflecting.middle_right`) ([record](../results/records/a-prediction-read-by-what-it-predicts.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Self-fulfilling and self-defeating prophecy (Merton 1948): a public prediction is one more input to what it predicts. In the bounded scalar setup a continuous response mapping a closed interval into itself has a correct public prediction (Grunberg and Modigliani 1954; Simon 1954), an existence, not its reaching by repeated publication; here a response keeping a finite chain and its order is reached from the bottom within the chain's length (Tarski 1955 for the fixed point). The bandwagon and the underdog effect read as responses keeping and reversing the order. The fixed points are related to performative stability (Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dünner and Hardt 2020), defined there by risk minimization on the distribution a deployed model induces; where retraining returns the outcome exactly it is this orbit, and no general correspondence is proved. Several right predictions, where publication starts deciding, read as self-fulfilling runs (Diamond and Dybvig 1983); the witness is a threshold response, not a model of coordination. A formation whose act negates its prediction of that act has no right self-prediction: the one-step form of [§6](#r-readings-within-what-they-read)'s self-reading gap, whose course form is [§5](#r-learning-by-refutation)'s diagonal.
