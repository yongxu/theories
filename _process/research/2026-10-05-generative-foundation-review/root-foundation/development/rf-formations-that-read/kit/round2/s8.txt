<a id="r-a-revision-that-opens-a-correction"></a>
**Theorem** (any types; an application in the ring, on the core's conditions; near-definitional, with witnesses on Revising's process). *A revision of a reading that opens a correction, along one process.* At a configuration, a correction is possible through the reading in force where a uniform policy exists whose act at each reading is acceptable at every situation giving it and executable there; given choice over the readings, exactly where each reading has one such act (`OpenedCorrection.Correctable`, `OpenedCorrection.correctable_iff`). A revision opens a correction from `x` to `x'` where a trace of the process leads from `x` to `x'`, no fitting uniform policy exists at `x` and one exists at `x'`, with one acceptability throughout (`OpenedCorrection.OpensAlong`). A revision to a reading the old one is read off, losing no executability, closes nothing, and one to a reading read off the old, gaining no executability, opens nothing (`OpenedCorrection.finer_keeps`, `OpenedCorrection.coarser_opens_nothing`); with every act counted acceptable, a configuration with an executable act is correctable already (`OpenedCorrection.widened_correctable`), so an opening counts only with the acceptability fixed. On Revising's stock-and-link process, with the correction acceptable where it is needed and replenishing where it is not, and executability the process's enabling: with the link off no configuration is correctable, with it on and a unit of stock every one is, and with it on and no stock none is (`OpenedCorrection.Linked.unlinked_uncorrectable`, `OpenedCorrection.Linked.linked_correctable`, `OpenedCorrection.Linked.starved_uncorrectable`); so rebuilding the link from two units opens the correction in one step, rebuilding it from one does not, and rebuilding then replenishing from one does (`OpenedCorrection.Linked.opens`, `OpenedCorrection.Linked.rebuilt_starved`, `OpenedCorrection.Linked.opens_after_replenishing`). Uniform-policy existence is not what a formation chooses; a supplied controller's run is Revising's `controlled_revision` ([record](../results/records/a-revision-that-opens-a-correction.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. For an institution, the link is its rule of reading what its members report, by which line rather than how many, the stock what that reading draws on, and the correction the repair a report calls for. Revising an observation map is one formal mechanism that may take part in organizational learning; its relation to double-loop learning (Argyris and Schön 1978) depends on whether inquiry changes a governing norm or assumption, and here the acceptability is fixed and the law concerns uniform-policy existence along a course. Replacing a finer reading by a coarser one, as by a summary measure, losing no executability, opens no correction.
