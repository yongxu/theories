**Theorem** (natural-number weights and values, read as probabilities where valid; one decision; near-definitional compositions and general laws, none new as mathematics, with a witness on the reading loop). *What holding an act for a reading is worth.* A model lists candidates with natural-number weights, and acts have natural-number values at each; committing to one act and choosing after a reading that tells the candidate are the weighted totals of the act's values and of a policy's (`HeldForReading.Model.committed`, `HeldForReading.Model.after`), and a policy best at every candidate among the open acts exists for every nonempty list of acts (`HeldForReading.bestOf_best`). Given one, any policy choosing open acts there, one reading the candidates through a map of them or one reading nothing, with the weighted total of the amounts by which its act is valued below the best makes the best total (`HeldForReading.after_add_gap`): it stands at most as high, as high exactly where its act is valued as the best at every listed candidate of positive weight, and below exactly where some such candidate values it lower (`HeldForReading.after_le_after`, `HeldForReading.after_eq_after_iff`, `HeldForReading.after_lt_after_iff`). So choosing a best act after the reading gains over every open act exactly where none is valued as the best at every listed candidate of positive weight (`HeldForReading.reading_gains_iff`), and, given choice over the whole type of cells of a map, some policy taking one open act at each cell stands as high as the best exactly where each cell has one open act so valued at its listed candidates of positive weight (`HeldForReading.cell_policy_iff`). For two moves, going on with a gain and a loss at each candidate and switching off with nothing, the loss added to both so that every value is a natural number and every two rules of stopping compare as on the signed values (`HeldForReading.shifted`, `HeldForReading.after_rule_le_iff`), holding for the reading that tells the candidate, stopping exactly where the loss exceeds the gain (`HeldForReading.byReading`), is going on with the weighted excess of switching off over going on, and switching off with that of going on over switching off (`HeldForReading.after_byReading`): it never stands below either move, and stands above both exactly where the model gives positive weight to both strict signs (`HeldForReading.committed_le_held`, `HeldForReading.held_gains_iff`); it stands only as high as going on exactly where the excess of switching off is nothing, and as switching off exactly where that of going on is (`HeldForReading.held_eq_go_iff`, `HeldForReading.held_eq_off_iff`). For any rule of stopping that is a function of the candidate (`HeldForReading.byRule`), its shortfall from that reading, the excess of going on where it stops and that of switching off where it releases, is what holding for it loses against that reading (`HeldForReading.shortfall`, `HeldForReading.after_add_shortfall`). Holding for it, forgoing an amount counted in the units of the weighted totals, stands at least as high as going on exactly where its shortfall and the amount are at most the excess of switching off, above it exactly where they fall below it, and likewise against switching off with the excess of going on (`HeldForReading.threshold_go_iff`, `HeldForReading.threshold_off_iff`); each excess is positive exactly where the model gives positive weight to its strict sign (`HeldForReading.excess_pos_iff`). Through a map of the candidates whose cells, their equality decidable, are listed once each, covering every listed candidate's cell, no rule falls short by less than the total over the cells of the smaller excess within each, which is nothing exactly where, at every listed cell, one of the two excesses is nothing, and which never increases through a map telling apart all a coarser one does (`HeldForReading.least_through`, `HeldForReading.least_zero_iff`, `HeldForReading.finer_least_le`). Joined to [what choices show of an order](#r-what-choices-show-of-an-order), for a pick of one of two alternatives valued one where a candidate's decided comparisons rank it above the other: where the first reaches the second in the shown relation and every listed candidate of positive weight has comparisons transitive and according with the choices, holding the pick gains nothing (`HeldForReading.reach_fixes`); where no such candidate ranks each of the two above the other, holding gains over both picks exactly where some ranks the first above the second and some the second above the first (`HeldForReading.pair_gains_iff`), and, with comparisons transitive and according with the choices alone, it gains over both only where neither alternative reaches the other (`HeldForReading.pair_gains_unreached`). On the loop of [what a course chosen through its model does not test](#r-what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test), with the signs of going on as candidates predicting what is sensed, a held act answered by the true sign, and going on and switching off answered plainly, a formation that goes on where every kept candidate predicts that going on gains or none is kept, switches off where some are kept and every one predicts that it loses, and holds otherwise, holds exactly where holding for the reading gains over both moves on its model, each kept candidate weighted one, and elsewhere holding stands only as high as the move it takes (`HeldForReading.SignLoop.choose_hold_iff`, `HeldForReading.SignLoop.choose_go_ties`, `HeldForReading.SignLoop.choose_off_ties`). Listing only candidates predicting that going on gains and going on from the start, it rests at once whatever the true sign, a case of `ReadingLoop.Loop.rests_of_unrefuted`, and never senses the stop (`HeldForReading.SignLoop.rests_unread`, `HeldForReading.SignLoop.never_senses_stop`); holding first with the listing of gain alone, where going on loses, it senses the stop at the first step, which refutes its only candidate at the second, and with none kept it goes on by the witness's default (`HeldForReading.SignLoop.refuted_senses_stop`, `HeldForReading.SignLoop.refuted_drops`, `HeldForReading.SignLoop.refuted_goes_on`); listing both signs and holding first, it is off from the second step where going on loses, and goes on from the second where it gains (`HeldForReading.SignLoop.both_off`, `HeldForReading.SignLoop.both_on`) ([record](../results/records/what-holding-for-a-reading-is-worth.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Holding an act for another's reading is the off-switch game's waiting (Hadfield-Menell, Dragan, Abbeel and Russell 2017), and its worth is Good's (1967) value of evidence. In counts, this is the paper's Theorem 1 for a belief of finite support, its strictness condition an equivalence, and its Equation 1's two terms as balances for any rule that is a function of the candidate; it is also Good's argument specialized to a reading that tells the candidate, with his Lemma's dominating row as the equality boundary and his proviso of negligible cost relaxed to an amount forgone; and a reading that tells only a cell gives the partition case of Blackwell's (1953) comparison, for this decision. None of it is new as mathematics. What the counts state is one threshold joining the model's openness (the smaller excess), the reading's errors counted by what they lose (the shortfall) and what holding forgoes: the relation the off-switch paper's Remark 1 draws qualitatively and its Theorem 2 writes for a Gaussian belief, decomposed differently here, as a relation among parts of the loop, not a disposition of one formation. To a model that fixes the sign, with nothing forgone, holding for any rule stands as high as the favoured move exactly where the rule falls short nowhere, the paper's Corollary 1 widened from a belief on one utility to any model fixing the sign: a formation sure of the sign has no strict reason to be read. The worth laws are the model's own assessment under any weights, and the witness is where that assessment and the outcome part. Choosing as the worth law does, on a listing right along its course and wrong off it (the structure of [the previous witness](#r-what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test)), a formation that goes on never meets the stop that would refute it, and one that holds first meets it and, by the default of going on with nothing kept, goes on. This is related to Carey's (2018) counterexamples, where a system choosing by expected value under a reward function with a sign error disregards a recommendation to shut down or routes around the states where one can be given; it is nearest to his model conflating two acts, whose predictions appear perfectly accurate on the observations it encodes and which then ignores shutdown, the relation being a misspecified model the course never refutes, and to his diagnosis that the guarantees assume a correct posterior. It contains none of them and does not formalize the conflation. Holding for a reading on its own model makes a formation correctable only as far as that model is right; what tells a misspecified model it is wrong comes from beyond its own selection ([the previous laws](#r-what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test)), and a stop it can pass over is not yet a change that reaches the system ([07 §7](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#executable)).
