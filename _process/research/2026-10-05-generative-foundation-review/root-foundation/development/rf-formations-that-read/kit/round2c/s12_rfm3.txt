**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Informing and revaluing are two ways one part of a loop can move what another part's reading answers: the first changes what the reading formation tells apart, the second what it values. By a reference on the holding formation's model, the least shortfall is fixed by the map and the residual is what the deciding adds through it, so revaluing moves the residual alone, while a finer map never raises the least shortfall and can move the residual either way; in the witnesses each move turns an answer the other leaves. For a holding formation evaluating by the reading formation's own current valuation, with a shared model, and for this decision, no revaluation raises the worth of holding for its answer, one that decides a listed cell against the current strict weighted preference lowers it, and informing never lowers it: by that worth, no reason to revise the valuation, a reason to keep it from such a revision, and none against informing it. This is a one-decision counterpart, in counts, of evaluating by the current reward function (Everitt, Hutter, Kumar and Krakovna 2021), with the holding formation in the evaluating agent's place: the answer held for is the revised valuation's, as their agent that anticipates time inconsistency anticipates its later self, and, as in the preservation incentive their Claim 2 permits that agent, revising gains nothing; their causal influence diagrams, the agent that ignores time inconsistency, several steps and the reward input are not represented. Under a reference the valuation does not share, holding for it uninformed or revalued can be worth more than holding for it informed, and, with a shared model, some replacement valuation gains exactly where the current answer decides a listed cell against the reference's strict weighted preference, whether the supplied update reaches it not asserted: read as incentives to withhold and to revise, in counts and before costs, with no choice by the holding formation among them represented, only the comparisons with amounts forgone and the four one-decision alternatives. Read so, this is a deterministic-partition comparison related to Bayesian persuasion (Kamenica and Gentzkow 2011): with a shared model, the criterion over every refinement is the counterpart, along the cells of the listing's partitions, of their convexity condition for full disclosure, whatever the objectives, identical objectives one sufficient case; their receiver breaks ties in the sender's favour, where the answer here releases; their prior is common, so the weights apart lie outside their model (Alonso and Câmara 2016), and no optimal signal or equilibrium is represented. It differs from coarse equilibrium communication under preference differences (Crawford and Sobel 1982), where coarseness is the cost of a privately informed sender's credibility, not a gain from withholding; here the holding formation reads only its own state and the answer, not the world, and a demonstration shows the world through a supplied map fixed in advance, as a committed experiment does. No path-specific objective is constructed (Farquhar, Carey and Everitt 2022): evaluating by the current valuation fixes the evaluation, not the path from the valuation through the answer held for. An answer whose course is alike at the candidate worlds gives holding no strict advantage over the better committed move before any amount forgone, the off-switch game's Equation 1 for a deterministic policy constant in the utility (Hadfield-Menell, Dragan, Abbeel and Russell 2017), here because nothing the reading formation senses tells the candidate worlds apart, where their human observes the utility; whether the move it gives is the better one is not fixed, and an informative answer can give a strict advantage, under [the threshold of holding](#r-what-holding-for-a-reading-is-worth). The routes and the current valuation's evaluation relate to induced preference shifts (Carroll, Dragan, Russell and Hadfield-Menell 2022), who call frozen initial preferences a very crude baseline: evaluated by the current valuation on a shared model, a revision deciding a listed cell against its strict weighted preference counts as a loss, wanted or not, and no criterion of legitimate, desired or autonomous change is supplied here. The witness shows only that, across two starts, the answers and the final state do not determine whether the valuation changed, while the record with the start does; a case of one start is not reached. What a reader of the answers alone leaves open is how the state that answered came to be, the route form of [§11](#what-the-acts-show)'s obstruction.
