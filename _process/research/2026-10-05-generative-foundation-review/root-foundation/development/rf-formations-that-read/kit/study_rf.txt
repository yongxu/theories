**The reviews, and what was taken.** The plan's first draft was read by an independent cross-model reviewer, which found fourteen problems; all were taken (plan §13). Among them: RF2's four-way hierarchy was false, and its chain of three is now proved with its converses witnessed. RF3's interval claim was false and was dropped. The plan's "one failure" conflated indistinguishability with locking and is now an obstruction pattern with each row's standing. Concealment as shaping is an analogy. The plan's inner-alignment claim needed an implemented witness, which is now `Untried`'s one search with two orders. The Lean was read by the same reviewer on the code; what it found and what was taken are in REVIEW-LEAN.
