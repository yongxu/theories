What the nine designs share, said once:

- **Stages.** Stage 0 is done here: mirrors of the Lean's definitions, checked against its evaluated witnesses and by enumeration on small cases; simulated systems; and the power check. Stage A (small trained systems) and Stage B (language models through an API, open-weight models, public data or people) are runs, on Ren's yes, with calls and tokens stated and any download named.
- **What a verdict can say.** Contradicted, passed, or void. A unit whose premise check fails is void, not refuted. Premises are measured apart from the outcome. Where the Lean's statement has an equality boundary, ties carry no verdict or have their own rule.
- **What a verdict rests on.** Only differences the power check resolves against the system the test may meet: the law's model with noise, readers fixed by what they read (position, label, cue, history), all-or-nothing and threshold agents, and hybrids of the law and a rival. Where a rival passes every contrast, a control is added. Where none can be added, the design says the rival is not separated.
- **The unit.** For model runs it is the call, each a fresh call unless the design carries a course. Versions are pinned. Pooled counts are descriptive only, and the theory's own model family is reported apart. Two computations in [`tr2.py`](tr2.py) show why. With calls answering ten items alike, counting items as units raises a false alarm from 2.7% to 57.7%. With arms blocked rather than interleaved, a 5-point drift turns into a false difference in 53% of runs.
- **The harness.** OpenRouter as in [VD1 and VD2](../valuer-agreement/README.md) (the key stays in Ren's interactive shell and is never printed; a spend guard), or the Claude CLI as in LA1 to LA3, which needs a smoke run first, since the installed CLI is newer than their pins.
- **The freeze, later.** Predictions, verdict rules, exact values and code are frozen in one commit, then read by an independent reader before any run. The lessons of earlier freezes ([experiments/README](../README.md#freezing-a-test-on-a-trained-system)) are met test by test in the full designs.
