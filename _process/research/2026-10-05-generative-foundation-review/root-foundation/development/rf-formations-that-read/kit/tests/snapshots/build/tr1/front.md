**TR1 (RF1): what choices show of an order.** Does a chooser pick by strict dominance under one strict order? RF1 is a pure-core theorem ([03 §11](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-choices-show-of-an-order)): some strict order accords with choices exactly where their shown relation has no cycle. No run refutes it; TR1 can refute only that correspondence, predicting nothing beyond the shown reach. Forbidden: a cycle among strict picks, each presentation its own occasion. Stage 0 (nine fresh calls each): the law's model is contradicted at most 0.03 (near-ties); no rival passes over 0.002; order-led hybrids pass, position lean reported; a stable error at 1% of presentations is caught 0.51. Stage B: 15,120 calls, on Ren's yes.
