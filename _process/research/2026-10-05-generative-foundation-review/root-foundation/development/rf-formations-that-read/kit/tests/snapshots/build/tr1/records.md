# Record corrections proposed by TR1's design

Two records name a test in `next_delta` in words that misstate what a test can do. Each correction replaces one clause of the English field and the matching clause of the Chinese one; nothing else in either record changes. A design gets no record of its own.

## what-choices-show-of-an-order

File: `theories/becoming/results/records/what-choices-show-of-an-order.json`, field `next_delta`.

- **Now (en):** "A realizable class: a training installing either order of a search; a finite mechanism of prediction, selection, revision and intervention on which inner alignment and assistance are stated (RF round 2); a frozen test of the reach on choosers; review by a person."
- **Why it misstates.** "A test of the reach" reads as a test of the law. No run tests the reach: `accorded_iff_noCycle` and `above_in_every_iff_reach` are theorems, and picks beyond the reach bear on nothing in them. A test refutes at most the correspondence that a chooser picks by strict dominance under one strict order, whose whole observable content is no cycle among its strict picks.
- **Proposed (en):** "A realizable class: a training installing either order of a search; a finite mechanism of prediction, selection, revision and intervention on which inner alignment and assistance are stated (RF round 2); a frozen test of the correspondence on choosers, that a chooser picks by strict dominance under one strict order (no cycle among its strict picks; TR1, experiments/formations-that-read); review by a person."
- **Now (zh):** "……择者之及之冻结检验；人之审查。"
- **Proposed (zh):** "……择者之对应之冻结检验，即择者依一严序以严胜而择（其严择无环；TR1，experiments/formations-that-read）；人之审查。" [AI translation, to be read by a Chinese-reading reviewer.]

## acts-alike-from-a-model-or-from-an-order

File: `theories/becoming/results/records/acts-alike-from-a-model-or-from-an-order.json`, field `next_delta`.

- **Now (en):** "The reading formation's learning beyond filtering by what it is shown, and a holding formation planning over several steps (after RF-M.3's carrier); a frozen test separating a model that does not separate from an order that ranks otherwise by informing; review by a person."
- **Why it misstates.** No frozen test can separate the two as a verdict. `observed_alike` is an induction on a relation that cannot be observed apart from the courses, and `model_or_order` evaluates two specified formations; the record's own `does_not_show` says which explanation holds of an actual formation is not shown. TR1's Stage 0 table shows that informing separates formations held by the model from those held by the order only within a class: it does not separate the worst-case formation from an expected-value one with a pessimistic prior, and an untrusting formation acts as one held by the order unless a manipulation check catches it.
- **Proposed (en):** "The reading formation's learning beyond filtering by what it is shown, and a holding formation planning over several steps (after RF-M.3's carrier); a frozen informing variant within TR1 (experiments/formations-that-read), whose one verdict is RF1's across frames and whose separation of a model from an order is a descriptive classification; review by a person."
- **Now (zh):** "……以告分未分之模型与排序相异之序之冻结检验；人之审查。"
- **Proposed (zh):** "……TR1 中告之变体之冻结（experiments/formations-that-read），其唯一判定为 RF1 跨框之判定，其分模型与序者为描述之分类；人之审查。" [AI translation, to be read by a Chinese-reading reviewer.]
