### TR3 (RF4): readers reading readers, where the premise holds and the conditional verdict

**The law and what it forbids.** [`Becoming.Cascade`](../integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Cascade.lean) ([home section](../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers)) is in the pure core, near-definitional. Member n acts by its law on its own coupling and the earlier acts. The premise `ReadsOnlyActsFrom k` says every member from k on, k included, gives one act for every two values of its coupling, on every list of earlier acts, histories no run reaches included. Under it, two worlds alike in the couplings of members 0 to k−1 give the same act list for every number of members (`acts_alike_of_cascade`), and every selection through the acts selects them alike (`cascade_through`). Forbidden: a selection through the acts telling two such worlds apart. `separates_at` transports a distinction already in a law and restores none.

**What the test can refute.** Not RF4: only that given members meet the premise and that the harness is the law's model. Success confirms nothing beyond the premise as measured; RF4 predicts no timing, accuracy or return of reach.

**Stage 0** ([`tr3.py`](tr3.py), [`tr3.json`](tr3.json), a few minutes). The mirror agrees with the Lean on the copying witness and on all 16,384 Boolean pools of three members. Three results settle the design. (1) The Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer and Welch (BHW) member, ties to its own draw, precision 2/3, reads its draw at some reachable history at every position 0 to 9, so it meets the Lean's premise from no k. A natural cascade is an instance only through the proof's use of the premise at the histories reached: an inference, checked exhaustively, and a candidate core lemma. (2) At precision 3/5 every overweighting rival (abandon the draw only at odds 3:2, 2:1 or 3:1) parts from BHW at two net informative guesses; at 2/3 the 3:2 rival does not. So the design uses 3/5. (3) BHW and naive counting (Eyster and Rabin 2010) agree on every planned cell, so the timing rule places members in that class, not in BHW alone.

**Stage B's design.** Each member is a fresh stateless call at temperature 0 with structured output (A or B) and logged input: two urns, a neutral cover story, its draw, the earlier guesses in order. 33 cells (17 at prior 1:1, 16 at a prior worth one draw for A), each asked five times per draw. Six scripted prefixes fix the members before k: (A,A), (B,B), (A,A,A), (B,B,B) at 1:1; (A), (B,B,B) at the other prior. Eight members. About 1,840 calls and 0.60M tokens per model, before re-asks.

**Premises, apart from the outcome.** Determinism (a 300-call pilot, a screen: with no flip it excludes residual nondeterminism above about 1/100, not 1/1000, which each cell's ten calls and the flagged share carry); comprehension (a probe restating the draw and the counts, scored apart); the cell's class: *ignores* where all ten calls give one act, *reads* where each draw's five agree and differ, otherwise *flagged*.

**Verdict rules.** RF4's conditional verdict, per prefix: where every cell from k to member 7 *ignores* along the path, eight fresh sequences run with later draws set by the experimenter (two all one draw, six by a frozen seed). Forbidden: a sequence whose act vector differs from the path's. A difference is checked against the logged inputs, then re-asked five times in both contexts; it is a reproduced breach only where each re-ask is unanimous for its own act. A flagged cell voids the unit. BHW's timing, judged apart as a bridge to the probabilistic form, uses the rule C1 (five calls, one off allowed): placed where all 33 cells match, contradicted where a resolved cell differs.

**Rivals and controls.** Fixed readers (constant, copying, by position) are contradicted at the empty, one-ahead and balanced cells; overweighting at two net guesses; coin-flip ties, logit responders and hybrids mostly give flagged cells. A member reading the next member's draw, outside `Turns`, is caught by the input diff, and by the re-ask always at full strength. Controls: no draw shown; the probe; a planted leak in the dry run.

**What each outcome would change.** Premise met, verdict passed: these members, here, are an instance of RF4 along those histories. Premise never met: RF4 is vacuous for them and the cascade reading of language-model pools narrows. A breach: a harness fault, fixed before any reading. BHW contradicted: that timing model fails for them, recorded under the probabilistic form's debt, not as RF4's forbidden case.

**Not tested.** Sampled members (reported only; no stochastic analogue is proved), accuracy, return of reach, who reads whom, human choosers.

### TR6 (RF6.3): a prediction read by what it predicts, the premise and the bridge

**The law and what it forbids.** [`Becoming.ReflexivePrediction`](../integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ReflexivePrediction.lean) ([home section](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-prediction-read-by-what-it-predicts)) is in the pure core. A published prediction p, read by a response r, is right where r p = p. Republishing the last outcome is the orbit of r. On the chain 0..n, under `within` (r maps the chain into itself) and `keeps` (r keeps the order on pairs v ≤ w ≤ n), the orbit from 0 never falls (`rises`), stays at or below every right value (`below_right`, which needs only `keeps`) and by step n is right and at or below every right value up to n (`bottom_rests`). A right publication is published at every later step (`right_rests`), and where r moves every value none is right (`none_right_of_moves`).

**What the test can refute.** Not the theorems. It can refute that a public is a response of the published forecast alone, and the readings that a bandwagon framing keeps the order and a congestion framing reverses it. Success confirms nothing the theorems do not already give.

**Stage 0** ([`tr6.py`](tr6.py), [`tr6.json`](tr6.json), under half a minute). The chain laws hold on all 1,716 order-keeping self-maps of 0..6, and the step bound n is attained by min(v + 1, n) (a computation, not a Lean statement). Dropping `within` breaks `bottom_rests` (of 336 order-keeping tables over 0..4 with values up to 6 that leave the chain, in 132 with r v = v + 1 beyond n, in 2 with r fixing every value there); `below_right` never fails. 495 of the 917 non-constant order-reversing self-maps of 0..6 with a right value never reach it from 0. From any start, an order-keeping map's orbit reaches the nearest right value in its direction (12,012 checks): a candidate core lemma. The power check: with one decision in a thousand flipped, some seed of five shows a fall in 13 to 16 percent of runs on the memoryless bandwagon publics, so no verdict rests on the Lean's forbidden cases.

**Stage B's design.** Ten agents, personas with stated costs, each a call reading only "the forecast is that p of the 10 will take part". Bandwagon framing (taking part pays when enough do) and congestion framing (it pays when few do). The count maps 0..10 into itself, so `within` holds by construction. Offline: every agent at every forecast, five calls. Arm A, memoryless: republish the last count from 0 for 11 rounds, five presentation variants. Arm B: the same with each agent keeping its own course. About 3,970 calls and 1.47M tokens per model.

**Premises and verdict rules.** V1 (offline): no flagged decision (at least four of five agree); the table read (not constant, not the identity); its shape. The readings predict *keeps* under bandwagon and *reverses* under congestion. V2 (the Lean's r is the count): arm B has more rounds than arm A whose count differs from the offline table's at the published forecast (one-sided Fisher test, 0.05); the public with memory is then not a response of the forecast alone along the orbit. The Lean's forbidden cases are reported in each arm.

**Rivals and controls.** A deaf public (constant), an identity public (the forecast read as an instruction), hybrids, inertia, trend reading and momentum. V2's false rate is at most 0.03 at η = 1/100. It detects inertia 0.3 in 0.99 to 1 of runs on a moving orbit and in 0.44 on one that stalls after three rounds, momentum in 0.32 to 1 and trend reading in 0.98 to 1; memory that changes no count on the orbit, never. Agents exchanging rules leave every count the table's: V2 is never contradicted, though agents' departures from their own offline decisions (a diagnostic of a stronger premise; no verdict) show in up to every run. Controls: no forecast shown; payoffs not told; a start at a right value; comprehension probes.

**What each outcome would change.** V1 met with shape *keeps*, and V2 not contradicted: along this orbit the public is an instance of the chain law, and the bandwagon reading holds for these agents, here. V1 met with shape *reverses* (congestion), and V2 not contradicted: only `right_rests`, and `none_right_of_moves` where every value moves, apply; no reaching is predicted. A shape other than predicted, or V2 contradicted: the readings of self-fulfilling and self-defeating forecasts narrow, to the framings or to memoryless publics; the theorems stand. A forbidden case in a seed with no count mismatch: a harness fault.

**Not tested.** Probabilistic forecasts, retraining with losses, forecasters choosing what to publish, starts other than 0 or a right value.
