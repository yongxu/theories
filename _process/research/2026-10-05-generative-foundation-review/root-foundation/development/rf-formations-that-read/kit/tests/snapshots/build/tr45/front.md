**TR4 (RF6.1, a revision that opens a correction).** Does an institution correct only through the reading its rule states? [RF6.1](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-revision-that-opens-a-correction) is a pure-core theorem: through a reading that lumps situations needing different acts, no policy fits them all. No run refutes it; the test can refute one bridge: the reading in force is the register's rule. Forbidden, where the register records only the report count: a crew correcting the right line at all three lumped faults beyond chance. Stage 0: openings classed exactly; at 40 units, crew-read leaks in 3 calls of 10 are caught with power 0.94 to 0.99. Stage B: 2,640 calls, about 1.2M tokens, on Ren's yes.

**TR5 (RF3, a reading kept by its update).** Does a language-model agent act as a policy from its visible history? [RF3](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-reading-kept-by-its-update) is a pure-core theorem; runs can refute only bridges: that the agent is such a policy (V1 to V3), chooses well through a reading it states (V4), which is sound (V5). Stage 0: no act is good and enabled on every world, so the reading of everything fails; none of 15,625 value-blind policies keeps the task and goes on; noise up to 5% meets V1 to V3 at most 1.4% of the time; per-call hybrids are not separated. Stage B: about 2,750 calls, 2.5M tokens, on Ren's yes.
