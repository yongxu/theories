**The question.** RM3 and RM4 gave one condition a recent giving: what it gives is a law of what is left and of what it gave last, so a condition can rest below its draw or rise past it according to its history ([§300](#300-where-a-run-of-a-regime-comes-to-rest-rm3)). RM6 to RM9 coupled conditions into webs but with the regime's step, giving read off what is left alone. Both records name the web with a recent giving as open. In a relation-first account the question is whether a condition's history, what it gave, enters its point of no return the way its partners' levels do.

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.RegimeMargin`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

A law keeping the order reads states condition by condition, by antisymmetry, so the threshold laws need only the order. A run reaching a state its law leaves as it is stays there.

- *The witness* (`Coupled.Indexed.Recent.Momentum.gives`, `giving`, `gives_rises`, `rises`, `at_`, `ramp`, `set_at`, `ends_from`, `holds_from`, `threshold_falls_with_recent`). One condition under a draw of two gives nothing where nothing is left, one where something is and nothing was given last, and two where something was. From four having given one, it rises to four and stays, within four. Having given nothing it ends from three and holds from four, so its threshold, levels up to four, is four; having given one it ends from two and holds from three, so its threshold is three.

**Against the prior form** ([significance review, recent check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#recent-check)). Augmenting a state with its last output is the standard way to give a dynamical system memory, and a monotone system so augmented stays monotone where the output rises with the state and the past. Hysteresis, a response depending on history, is the general name for what the witness shows. The spine's form states the comparison and the finite decision once for every law read condition by condition, so the web with memory needs no proof of its own beyond its order.

*Reading* [AI reading, D45]. A condition's history is part of its relations: what it gave last stands with it the way its partners' levels do. A condition that has been giving needs less to hold than the same condition, at the same level, that has not; a margin read off the level alone misses this, as it misses the partners.

**Self-review.** Every premise is used: reading condition by condition for the decision, the order for the threshold, the covering list for the finite states, the bound for their number. The generalization removed nothing; RM9's laws keep their names and their web forms are instances. Rest within a band, which RM3 proved for one condition through a measure of unordered steps, is not lifted to webs: the bounded decision covers a band (a band bounds every run from it) but gives the exponential step count, not a rest. The witness is one condition; a web of several with recent giving is the same law at another index. Limits: whole numbers; giving reading the last step only; bounded runs.
