**The question.** RM1 ([study §270](#270-the-margin-of-a-regime-giving-read-off-the-level-under-a-standing-draw-rm1)) proved the margin of a regime with giving read off what a standing draw leaves, alone. The field tests then found the level alone is not the state: recent giving adds beyond it (MQ4, [study §277](#277-a-state-of-level-and-recent-giving-in-the-field-mq4)), and with one lag nothing more is needed in the median stock (MQ5, [study §280](#280-a-lag-and-the-draws-response-in-the-field-mq5)), where the lag, through the balance, is giving moving toward the draw. Thesis T2 named the regime's step and the frozen rest as the state's two ends and left "that the regime's point stays the long-run point" without a Lean result. And MQ3's frozen check F3 ([study §272](#272-the-margin-of-a-regime-in-the-field-mq3)) found that under a held catch the regime's point did not separate fates: of 754 episodes below it, 63% fell. Two questions: do RM1's laws need giving read off the level alone, and what does an adjusting giving do to the point?

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.RegimeMargin`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *Giving that adjusts* (rule 9: generalized below). `Adjusted.step A H (x, r) = (x - H + A (x - H) r, A (x - H) r)`: the draw takes first; the giving is a law `A` of what is left and of the recent giving, and it is the next recent giving. The regime's step is the case `A e r = g e`: along every run its level is the regime's run (`orbit_regime`), and the regime satisfies every premise the general laws name (`regime_staysShort`, `regime_staysMet`, `regime_fromNothing`, `regime_keeps`). The frozen rest is the case `A e r = r`. `Between`: the adjusted giving lies between the recent giving and the regime's (`frozen_between`; `Between` takes in every partial adjustment, at any rate from holding the giving to taking the regime's); `Settles`: it moves wherever it differs from the regime's.
- *Where the state rests.* Where `A e H = H` exactly where `g e = H` (`RestsAtDraw`), a state `(x, r)` with `H ≤ x` is left as it is exactly where `r = H` and the regime's step leaves `x` (`rest_iff`), and on the run, the run from `(x, r)` stays at `(x, r)` at every step exactly where `r = H` and the regime's run from `x` stays at `x` (`rests_iff`). Every `Between` giving that `Settles` rests at the draw exactly where the regime gives it (`Between.restsAtDraw`), so the same holds of it (`Between.rests_iff`). The frozen rest leaves every level at or above the draw as it is with a recent giving at the draw (`frozen_rests`).
- *Below the point of no return.* With RM1's `Short g H y`, a recent giving short of the draw and `StaysShort` (where the recent and the regime's giving fall short of the draw, so does `A`): each step keeps the level below the point and the recent giving short, and lowers a positive level (`step_below`); after `n` steps the level is at most `x - n` where `A` gives nothing from nothing (`orbit_bound`), so it is nothing after `x` steps (`ends_below`) and stays nothing (`stays_ended`, from `from_zero`). Whatever `A` gives from nothing, with `y` positive, what the draw leaves falls by one a step (`left_falls`, `left_below`, `left_bound`), so from `x - H` steps on the level is at most the draw (`taken_whole`).
- *At or above it.* With RM1's `Meets g H y hi`, a recent giving meeting the draw and `StaysMet`, a step from a level at or above `H + y` does not lower it and keeps the recent giving at the draw (`step_holds`), so the run never falls below its start (`holds`, with the band kept, `Keeps`; `holds_from`, with no top where giving meets the draw from `y` on). Exactness on the state: from a level up to `hi` whose recent giving falls short of the draw exactly where the level is below `H + y`, the condition ends exactly where the level starts below `H + y` (`ends_iff_below`); every `Between` giving keeps both sides (`Between.staysShort`, `Between.staysMet`), so the same holds of it (`Between.ends_iff_below`). RM1's `ends_below`, `stays_ended`, `holds` and `ends_iff_below` are now the regime's case, each a line through `orbit_regime`.
- *Witnesses* (`Crossing`). Giving gated at two (nothing below two, four from two on) under a draw of three: the point of no return is five (`root`, `meets_from`). `toward` moves the recent giving one unit a step toward the regime's and rests only there (`toward_between`, `toward_settles`). `crosses`: the regime's step ends the condition from four and holds it from five; under `toward`, from four with a recent giving of six the level is at or above seven from the second step on, and from five with a recent giving of nothing it is nothing from the second step on. `frozen_stays`: the frozen rest is `Between`, and its run from four with a recent giving of three stays at four at every step, where the regime's step ends the condition from four.

**Against the prior forms** ([significance review, adjusted-giving check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#adjusted-giving-check)). The mathematics is known in its kind. A partial adjustment relaxes a quantity toward its target at a rate, and its rest points are the target's (Nerlove 1958); delay-difference models carry a level and a lagged term, and their equilibria are those of the equilibrium yield curve (Deriso 1980; Schnute 1985); with a second state variable a threshold is a curve in the state's plane, not a level, and transients carry states across it (Hastings 2004). The spine's form is exact over every adjustment with named properties, not one rate: the rest law needs exactly that the adjustment rests at the draw where the regime gives it, and the fates need exactly that giving stays on the side of the draw where the recent and the regime's giving both lie; each premise is shown needed by a witness on the run, and RM1's laws are the case reading what is left alone. MQ5's law, under a standing draw met in full, reduces through the balance to `A e r = (φ − λ) r + (1 − φ) g e + λ H`, a weighting of the recent giving, the regime's giving and the draw with `φ = 0.96` and `λ = 0.55`; over the reals it satisfies `RestsAtDraw` (since `φ < 1`), `StaysShort` and `StaysMet`, but neither `Between` (it pulls toward the draw) nor `FromNothing`. This is a correspondence by premises, since the Lean is in whole numbers.

*Reading* [AI reading, D25, D45]. RM1's point of no return was stated as a level, and T2 said that point stays the long-run point. RM2 says what of it survives once the condition carries what it gave. The point stays the point of rest: wherever a condition whose giving adjusts comes to rest at or above the draw, it rests where the regime's step would leave it, at any rate of adjustment, as long as the adjustment settles at the draw where the regime does. It no longer separates fates by the level alone. A condition below the point still ends where its recent giving is short of the draw, and one above it still holds where its recent giving meets the draw. Where recent giving lies on the other side, memory decides: what was given before can carry a condition across the point from below, and its absence can end one from above. MQ3's F3 failure fits this reading: under a held catch, a third of the episodes below the regime's point did not fall. The reading is post hoc. It predicts that, under a held catch, below-point episodes whose recent giving falls short of the catch fall at every step more often than those whose recent giving meets it; that prediction is untested and needs a frozen test on stocks no analysis has read. For the theory's margin, a margin is a distance of the state from the point, read with the recent giving, not of the level alone.
