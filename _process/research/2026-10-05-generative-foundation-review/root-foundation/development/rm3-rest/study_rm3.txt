**The question.** RM2 ([study §299](#299-the-regimes-point-over-every-adjustment-of-giving-rm2)) proved where a run of an adjusting giving can rest: at or above the draw, only at the regime's point. It left open whether a run comes to rest, and how fast. The last-change laws ([the last change of a run](../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/the-last-change-of-a-run.md)) say that for every process of decidable configurations in a finite world, settling is exactly the limited principle of omniscience (`LastChange.lpo_iff_settles`), and they name as open a premise the core accepts under which settling is decided. Two questions: under what premise does a run of a law come to rest with no omniscience, and does every run of a regime with adjusting giving come to rest?

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.LastChange` and `Becoming.RegimeMargin`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *A law's run with a bounded mark* (`LastChange`). A run of a law is fixed by its law: where the law leaves a configuration as it is, the run stays there (`still_of_fixed`, through `Law.orbit_mul`), so whether it changes at a step is decided. Where a quantity `q` marks the changes of a law's run (`Marks q`: never falling, rising wherever the configuration changes) and stays at most `B` along it, with `B` at most `d` above its start, the run is still from some step at most `d` (`still_within`): each change uses up at least one of the `d`. A run still from `N` after one step is still from `N + 1` (`still_succ`).
- *The regime's run.* Where giving rises with what is left, the regime's step keeps the order of levels (`step_mono`), so by `Lasting.orbit_mono` a run whose first step raises the level never lowers it, and one whose first step lowers it never raises it. Within a band (`Keeps g H hi`) the level, or its distance below the start, is a bounded mark: every run from a level up to `hi` is still from some step at most `hi` (`rests_within`).
- *The adjusted run.* Where the adjustment rises with what is left and with the recent giving (`Adjusted.Rises`), the adjusted step keeps the order of states (`Adjusted.step_mono`). A run whose first step lowers neither the level nor the recent giving never lowers either (`run_rises`); within a band (`Adjusted.Band`: the step keeps every state with level and recent giving up to `hi` at a level up to `hi`) it is still within `2 hi - (x + r)` steps (`rests_rising`, the mark the sum of level and recent giving, `in_band`). A run whose first step raises neither never raises either and is still within `x + r` steps (`run_falls`, `rests_falling`). A step that does neither, from a state whose recent giving is at most its level, lowers `unordered` (`unordered_falls`). At or above the draw the level moves by the new recent giving less the draw, so a step that raises the level and lowers the recent giving, or the reverse, leaves the new recent giving strictly between the old and the draw. Below it the new state lies on the line where level and recent giving are equal, from which every step raises or lowers both. `unordered` is at most `hi + 2` after the first step (`unordered_le`, `given_le_level`), so by induction (`rests_from`) every run from a level and a recent giving up to `hi` is still from some step at most `3 hi + 3` (`comes_to_rest`). Where the adjusted giving rests at the draw exactly where the regime gives the draw, a state is left as it is exactly where it is at rest (`fixed_iff`, `AtRest`): at the draw, with a level at or above it that the regime's step leaves as it is (RM2's `rest_iff`), or below the draw with level and recent giving equal and the giving from nothing left giving it again. Where nothing gives from nothing, that rest below the draw is nothing, by the definition of `FromNothing`. `unordered_falls` needs no property of the giving, and no ordering of the start is needed.
- *Witnesses.* `Alternation.alternates`: giving that is two from nothing and nothing from more does not rise with what is left. Under a draw of one it keeps every state up to two within two and rests at the draw where the regime does, yet the regime's run from one, and the adjusted run from one with a recent giving of two, move between one and two at every step and are never still. `Crossing.rises_without_rest`: the gated giving rises and rests at the draw where the regime does, but with no band the run from five rises by one at every step. `Adjusted.rests_short`: a constant giving of one rises, keeps every state up to one within one, and rests from a level of one with a recent giving of one under a draw of two, below the draw.

**Against the prior forms** ([significance review, rest check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#rest-check)). The mathematics is known in its kind. Compensatory renewal (Beverton and Holt 1957), giving that rises with what is left, settles monotonically, while overcompensating renewal, giving that falls as what is left rises, cycles and can be chaotic (May 1976); `Alternation.alternates` is the smallest such cycle in whole numbers. For monotone maps of the plane, orbits of cooperative maps are eventually componentwise monotone under regularity conditions (Smith 1998; Hirsch and Smith 2005). The spine's form is exact in its premises and constructive. The adjusted step is a cooperative map of the plane that need not be one to one. The eventual monotonicity comes from the step's own structure, which moves the level by the new giving less the draw, with an explicit bound on the steps before it, and it fails for monotone maps without that structure: exchanging level and recent giving keeps the order and cycles. With no omniscience the rest is reached and found by deciding at each step whether the law leaves the state as it is. Rising giving and the band are each shown needed by a witness on the run.

*Reading* [AI reading, D45]. RM2's regime point said where a condition whose giving adjusts can rest. RM3 says it does come to rest wherever its giving rises with what is left and with what was given before, and its state stays within a band. Within a bounded number of steps it holds at a level the regime's step leaves as it is, with its recent giving at the draw, or it rests below the draw, which is nothing where nothing is given from nothing. Settling then needs no knowledge of the whole future: at each step it is decided whether the law leaves the state as it is, and the steps it can take before that are counted. Where giving falls as what is left rises, as in overcompensating renewal, a condition kept within its band need not settle at all. For thesis T2, under the premises the point is where runs end, not only where they can rest. Whether MQ5's measured giving rises over its band is not checked: its law weighs the recent giving by 0.41 and the regime's giving by 0.04, both positive, so it rises in both exactly where the regime's giving rises with what is left.
