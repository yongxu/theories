**The question.** RM2 ([study §299](#299-the-regimes-point-over-every-adjustment-of-giving-rm2)) proved that the point of no return is exact on the state where the recent giving lies on the level's side of the draw. It found that on the other side the point is crossed both ways, and it left the threshold in the plane of level and recent giving open. RM3 ([study §300](#300-where-a-run-of-a-regime-comes-to-rest-rm3)) proved that every run of an adjustment rising in both arguments within a band comes to rest, at the regime's point or below the draw. The question: which states end below the draw, and is there a threshold in the plane?

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.RegimeMargin` and `Becoming.LastChange`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *One rest* (`LastChange`). A run still from step `T` stays at its configuration at `T` (`still_stays`), so a run still from two steps is at one configuration at both (`still_same`).
- *Down-closed fates.* `RestsBelow A H p`: the run from `p` is still from some step with its level below the draw. Where the adjustment rises with what is left and with the recent giving (`Rises`) and keeps a band (`Band`), the runs from two ordered states stay ordered (`Lasting.orbit_mono`) and both come to rest (`comes_to_rest`), so a state at or below one whose run rests below the draw rests below it too (`falls_of_below`). Within the band whether a run rests below the draw is decided at step `3 hi + 3` (`falls_iff`, through `still_same`).
- *The threshold.* `leading b k` is the length of the run of `true` with which `b` starts among its first `k` values (`leading_le`, `leading_true`, `leading_false`). `threshold A H hi r` is `leading` of the decision at step `3 hi + 3` over the levels up to `hi`, at most `hi + 1` (`threshold_le`). Since the fates are down-closed, for a level and a recent giving up to `hi`, the run rests below the draw exactly where the level is below the threshold (`falls_iff_below`). For a larger recent giving the threshold is no higher (`threshold_falls`): a state at the larger threshold's level with the smaller recent giving lies below one whose run rests below the draw. `fates` gathers the fates: below the threshold a run rests below the draw with level and recent giving equal, at a recent giving the adjustment returns from nothing (so at nothing where it gives nothing from nothing), and from it on at the draw with a level the regime's step leaves as it is, within `3 hi + 3` steps.
- *The regime's case.* Where giving rises with what is left and RM1's premises hold (giving short of the draw below `y`, meeting it from `y` up to the band's top, nothing from nothing), with the band reaching the point, the threshold is `H + y` at every recent giving up to `hi` (`threshold_regime`). Below the point the regime's run is at nothing after as many steps as it stood (RM1's `ends_below`), and the draw is positive there (RM2's `draw_pos`), so the run rests below the draw. From the point on the level never falls below its start (RM1's `holds`). Since the same levels up to `hi` lie below both, and both are at most `hi + 1` with the point within reach, the two are equal.
- *Witness* (`Notch.no_threshold`). Giving that is two from one and nothing from any other level does not rise with what is left. Under a draw of two its step keeps every state up to four at a level up to four. From four the run is at nothing from the second step on, and from three it is at three with a recent giving of two from the first step on. So the run from four rests below the draw while the run from three, below it, does not, and no least level separates them.

**Against the prior form** ([significance review, threshold check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#threshold-check)). The mathematics is known in its kind: for order-preserving maps the boundary between basins of attraction is unordered, so in the plane it is a curve along which one coordinate falls as the other rises (Hirsch and Smith 2005, from memory). The spine's form is exact in whole numbers within a band. The basins are those of resting below the draw and resting at the regime's point, and they are decided at a fixed step. The curve is the least level found by a finite search, with no omniscience. Its regime case is RM1's point, and a witness shows that without rising giving no curve exists.

*Reading* [AI reading, D45]. For thesis T2, the point of no return is a curve in the state's plane, not a level: under an adjustment rising in both, at a fixed level less recent giving never turns a fall into a hold. The F3 reading of RM2 predicted that below-point episodes whose recent giving falls short of the catch fall more often than those whose recent giving meets it. For such adjustments that is the direction the threshold law fixes, deterministically and within a band. MQ5's law rises in both exactly where its regime's giving rises with what is left, which is not checked, and the field frequency is untested. A margin is then a state's distance from this curve, read with the recent giving.

**Self-review.** The fate first chosen was reaching nothing, which needs nothing from nothing for its decision. Resting below the draw needs no such premise and is the fate `comes_to_rest` divides, so it replaced the first. `threshold_regime` first took a positive draw. It was dropped: below the point the draw is positive by RM2's `draw_pos`, and from the point on nothing falls, so the premise cost nothing. Nothing from nothing stays in `threshold_regime`, since with `y` zero and giving from nothing at least the draw, a level below the draw holds. The band is part of the threshold's definition, which searches to `hi + 1`. The connectivity check was planned before the first checker run: every new law is stated on a run or read by one that is, and `leading`'s laws are read by `falls_iff_below`. Limits: the reals, a further lag, coupled conditions, and the field frequency of the curve's direction are not stated, and the witness's giving is chosen.
