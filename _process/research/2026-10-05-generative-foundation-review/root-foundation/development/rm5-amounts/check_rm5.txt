## A check: the regime in any amounts, and its runs over the reals (21 October 2026, RM5)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written. It asks which of the regime's laws are artefacts of whole-number amounts. **Verdict: the mathematics is known. Orbits of order-preserving planar maps are eventually componentwise monotone, so bounded orbits converge. The spine's form needs none of the general theorem's regularity conditions, because the adjusted step moves the level by the new giving less the draw. So every step that is neither up nor down moves the recent giving toward the draw, with no property of the adjustment. The core states this shape constructively in any linearly ordered amounts with a draw. The periphery adds convergence over the reals, to a rest where the adjustment is continuous, with the fates down-closed and falling with the recent giving. What whole numbers add is only that the rest is reached in finitely many steps and the fate decided at a fixed step.**
