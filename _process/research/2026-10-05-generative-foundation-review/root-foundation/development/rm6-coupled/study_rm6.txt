**The question.** A condition's margin, its distance above the point of no return, has so far been a quantity of the condition under its own draw: the regime's, not the level's (RM1, [study §270](#270-the-margin-of-a-regime-giving-read-off-the-level-under-a-standing-draw-rm1)). Every regime record leaves coupled conditions open. In a relation-first account that is the gap that matters most: what one condition gives back can depend on what is left of another. So the question is whether a condition's point of no return is still its own once its giving reads what stands with it, and which laws carry the coupling.

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.RegimeMargin`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *The coupled step* (`Coupled.step`). Two levels; draws `H₁` and `H₂`, each taken first, as much as there is; each next level is what the draw left of it with its giving, `g₁` or `g₂` of what is left of the first and of the second. It is stated in any amounts with a draw (RM5's `Drawn`). The step reads a state only through what the draws leave (`Coupled.step_of_left`). A condition *ends* where, from some step on, its level is nothing (`Coupled.Ends`, for the first or the second by the part read).
- *Mutual support keeps the order* (any amounts). Where each giving rises with what is left of each, a state at or below another in both levels stays so after a step (`Coupled.step_mono`) and along the runs (`Coupled.run_below`). So a condition ending from a state ends from every state below it (`Coupled.ends_of_below`): the states from which it ends are down-closed, those from which it holds up-closed.
- *Within a band* (any amounts). Where the coupled step keeps every state with both levels within `hi` within `hi` (`Coupled.Band`), mutual support gives within each draw at every state of the band (`Coupled.within_draw`): the top state must stay within the band, and every other gives no more than it. So what the draws leave of each never rises along a run (`Coupled.left_falls`, through `Coupled.left_le`), and where a step leaves it as it was the run is still from the next step (`Coupled.still_of_left`).
- *Rest and the threshold* (whole numbers). What is left of both is a whole number that falls at every step that changes it, so a run within the band is still from some step at most `2 hi + 1` (`Coupled.rests_by`, `Coupled.comes_to_rest`), and whether the first condition ends is decided at that step (`Coupled.ends_iff`). For each partner's level `y` up to `hi`, `Coupled.threshold` is the least level whose run is not nothing there, found by RM4's finite search. The first condition ends from `(x, y)` exactly where `x` is below it (`Coupled.ends_iff_below`), and a larger partner's level has a threshold no higher (`Coupled.threshold_falls`). At its rest the first is nothing below the threshold and something from it on (`Coupled.fates`).
- *Dependence drains* (any amounts; whole numbers for the count). Where the second gives nothing wherever nothing is left of the first, a step from a state whose first level is within its draw leaves the second what its draw leaves of it (`Coupled.drains`). In whole numbers, while the first stays within its draw from step `N`, the second falls by its draw at every step and, its draw positive, is nothing from step `N + y` on, `y` its level at `N` (`Coupled.ends_after`). So the first's end ends the second (`Coupled.ends_of_partner`), and with dependence both ways and both draws positive the first ends exactly where the second does (`Coupled.ends_together`). No mutual support is needed for these.
- *The roles exchanged, the regime recovered.* Exchanging the two conditions, their draws and the arguments of their giving exchanges the levels at every step (`Coupled.run_swap`), so every law of the first is one of the second (`Coupled.ends_swap`). Where the first's giving reads only what is left of itself, its level is the regime's run under its draw whatever its partner does (`Coupled.first_regime`): RM1's laws are the uncoupled case.
- *Witnesses.* `MutualActivation.saves`: each gives one where at least one is left of each, under draws of one and a band up to two. From two the first ends with its partner at nothing and holds with its partner at two; its threshold is three (beyond the band) at a partner of nothing and two at a partner of two. `MutualInhibition.breaks_order`: each gives one only where nothing is left of the other. Within a band up to two each holds at two alone, and from two and two both end. So the first ends from a state above one from which it holds, and its threshold rises from two to three as its partner rises. `Coupled.alternation_never_rests`: RM3's `Alternation` giving read as the first's keeps a band, yet the run never rests, so the band without mutual support gives no rest. `Relay.cycles`: each gives two only where enough is left of the other; both givings rise with what is left of each, and each gives nothing where nothing is left of the other. Under draws of one each alone ends from every level; from two and three the run moves between two and three and three and two for ever, within three, neither ending and the run never still.

**Against the prior form** ([significance review, coupled check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#coupled-check)). Order preservation under mutual support is the defining property of cooperative (monotone) systems, and the down-closure of the states that go extinct is standard there (Smith 1995; Hirsch and Smith 2005, from memory). In models of obligate mutualism the boundary between extinction and persistence is a curve along which each partner needs less as the other has more (Dean 1983, from memory), and coextinction of dependents follows the loss of their partner (Koh et al. 2004, from memory). Competitive systems keep a different order, one coordinate reversed (Smith 1998), which is why the inhibition witness breaks the product order. Bounded orbits of planar cooperative maps are eventually monotone only under regularity conditions; the coupled step is not one-to-one, and `Relay.cycles` is a cooperative step whose bounded orbit cycles between two unordered states. What the spine adds is the exact, constructive form under the draw: the threshold decided by a finite search with no omniscience, the drain counted, the uncoupled case the regime itself.

*Reading* [AI reading, D45]. A condition's point of no return is not its own. Under mutual support it is a falling function of what stands with it: the more its partner has, the less it needs, and support can carry a condition across its own point. Under dependence the loss of one is the loss of the other, within as many steps as the dependent has. Under inhibition the fates lose their order: more of the other can end a condition that holds alone. A margin measured on one condition alone, with its partners held as they were, misreads the margin wherever the coupling is strong. For T2 this means the three numbers are a single condition's: where a condition gives from what is left of a partner, the partner's level is part of its state, so T2's claim that no fourth variable matters in the median condition says that coupling is weak there, which is untested.

**Self-review.** The band is a strong premise here. Under mutual support it bounds every giving by its draw (`Coupled.within_draw`), so within it no level above its draw rises, and the threshold and rest describe how far conditions fall before support holds them. Growth beyond a band is where `Relay`'s cycle lives, and there no threshold is stated; deciding ending there would need a bound on the run in place of the band. The first plan defined ending as reaching nothing at some step. It was changed to staying nothing from some step on, since a partner can give to a condition at nothing; with that reading no nothing-from-nothing premise is needed for the threshold. The threshold is the first condition's; the second's is the first's with the roles exchanged. The rest bound `2 hi + 1` counts what is left of both, at most `2 hi`, and is not claimed tight. Mutual support is needed for the order and the threshold (`MutualInhibition`), and the band for rest (`Relay`); mutual support alone without a band gives no rest, and a band alone without support gives none (`Coupled.alternation_never_rests`). The connectivity check was planned before the checker ran: every new law is stated on a run or read by one that is. Limits: two conditions, not a web; the regime's step, not the adjusted one with a recent giving; whole numbers for the threshold; the reals and continuous time not stated.
