## A check: thresholds beyond a band (22 October 2026, RM9)

An AI check (one session, 22 October), made while the Lean was written, of what RM8 ([web-rest check](#web-rest-check)) left open: thresholds on coupled webs beyond a band. **Verdict: the mathematics is known in its kind. A deterministic map on a finite set is eventually periodic within the set's size, and properties of finite-state runs are decided by bounded checks. The spine's form states this constructively on a web read condition by condition, with no equality of functions, and applies it to the margin: where the run from the top level stays bounded, each condition's point of no return is exact and falls as the others rise, beyond any band.**

| Known form | Where | Bearing |
