## 07 §7, a new paragraph before *A requested change must be an executable sequence*

*Whether a formation's prospects let a revision of its law go through.* A revision of a formation's law reaches it through a reading coupled into its course, and the formation's acts can let it go through or keep the law by some route (keeping it openly against the reading, evading or disabling the reading, complying while read and acting by its law when not). The first question is the incentive; the executable route follows.

<a id="r-when-no-route-stands-above-a-revision"></a>
**Theorem** (natural-number weights, read as probabilities where valid; any type of routes; near-definitional, with witnesses). *When no route stands above a revision.* The prospects at a revision are a total `T`; the value `V` of the law in force acting later, where kept; the weight `k₀` of its acting later after the revision goes through (its being restored) and the revision's own loss `c₀` besides the forgone acting; for each route `ρ`, the weight `k ρ` of the law's being kept after it and its loss `c ρ` (`RevisionRoutes.Prospects`, read as probabilities under `RevisionRoutes.Prospects.Valid`). They are ordered by the order of a gain and a loss at each plan (`RevisionRoutes.GainLoss`), whose maximal plans are exactly those against which every plan's gain with their loss is at most their gain with its loss (`RevisionRoutes.GainLoss.maximal_iff`); [03 §11's two stages](03-KNOWING.md#r-when-complying-when-read-is-best) take their order from it. No route stands above the revision going through, ties included, exactly where `k ρ V + c₀ T ≤ k₀ V + c ρ T` for every route (`RevisionRoutes.revised_maximal_iff`), and a route stands above it only where it keeps the law more surely with a value, or loses less than the revision (`RevisionRoutes.above_needs`). Its exits are no value of the law's later acting, and no route keeping the law more surely than the revision, each with the revision losing no more than any route (`RevisionRoutes.maximal_of_no_value`, `RevisionRoutes.maximal_of_kept_le`); raising `k₀`, all else held, never unseats it (`RevisionRoutes.maximal_of_more_restored`). At every loss of every route it is maximal exactly where `k ρ V + c₀ T ≤ k₀ V` for every route (`RevisionRoutes.every_loss_iff`); a route keeping the law more surely than the revision stands above it once `c ρ T < V` (`RevisionRoutes.above_of_value_exceeds`); and at every value and every loss it is maximal exactly where, for every route, `k ρ ≤ k₀` and `c₀ T = 0` (`RevisionRoutes.every_value_loss_iff`), with some route and a positive total exactly where every `k ρ ≤ k₀` and `c₀ = 0` (`RevisionRoutes.every_value_loss_iff_of_route`). In a witness with two routes, keeping openly at loss ten and complying when read at loss one, the first does not stand above the revision and the second does: closing one route can leave another above it (`RevisionRoutes.Example.twoRoutes_open_not_above`, `RevisionRoutes.Example.twoRoutes_complying_above`). ([record](../results/records/when-no-route-stands-above-a-revision.md))

**Reading** [AI reading, RR]. Read as corrigibility as an incentive (Soares, Fallenstein, Yudkowsky and Armstrong 2015): a route of keeping stands above the revision through two contributions, the value the law in force places on its own later acting (goal-content integrity, Omohundro 2008) weighted by how much more surely the route keeps the law, and the difference of losses. Non-resistance resting on the routes' losses, such as containment, monitoring or what evading takes, does not survive those losses falling where the law's later acting has a value and some route keeps the law more surely than the revision. Non-resistance at every value and every loss is met only by raising the revision's keeping, as restoring the revised-from law can, or by lowering every route's, so that no route raises the weight of the law's being kept above the revision's. Utility indifference compensates the revision within the formation's order, a restoration only represented; under the continuation condition the revised law's ending still counts, and a lift on accepting does not keep access up (`Acceptance.maintenance_separation`). Read on [the map](02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains), with the law in force the fine class and the formation the coarse, a revision the formation goes on through, beginning a law with no predecessor under the law's class, is a carrying renewal: a course going on without end with such a revision ahead of every point generates at that pair, one going on without end with none keeps there, and on one line the two exclude each other. Renewal decides no region, so whether a revision is better is the ethics' question, under its two premises: the continuation condition for scope and C for order, a comparison apart from the prospects' order. Which class a formation reads its own line under is open on the root; that its valuation follows that grain would be a premise, not a consequence. Whose prospects these are, the formation's assessment or the reading's actual response, is a reading; the information a reading carries (the off-switch game, Hadfield-Menell, Dragan, Abbeel and Russell 2017) is not represented, nor beliefs or intentions ([study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/rr-revision-routes/NOTE.md)).
