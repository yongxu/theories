**Theorem** (natural-number weights, gains and losses; any listing of candidates; near-definitional, with witnesses). *When holding for a reading stands above a route.* On a formation's model, a plan keeps the law going on at some weight and switches it off at some weight at each listed candidate, and has a loss of its own. It gains what keeping gains at the weight it keeps and what switching off gains at the weight it switches off, and plans are ordered by the order of a gain and a loss (`RevisionPlans.KeepStop`, `RevisionPlans.gainOf`, `RevisionPlans.order`). A plan's excess is what switching off gains beyond keeping at the weight it keeps, with what keeping gains beyond switching off at the weight it switches off (`RevisionPlans.excess`). Every two plans splitting the weights gain the same with their excesses (`RevisionPlans.balance`), so between them one stands above another exactly where its own loss with its excess is below the other's (`RevisionPlans.above_iff`, `RevisionPlans.not_above_iff`). Holding for a rule of stopping is such a plan, its excess the rule's shortfall from the reading that tells the candidate (`HeldForReading.excess_held`). So holding for it, forgoing an amount, stands at least as high as a route splitting the weights exactly where the amount with the shortfall is at most the route's loss with its excess. [What holding for a reading is worth](03-KNOWING.md#r-what-holding-for-a-reading-is-worth) derives its balances, ties, thresholds and comparisons with an amount forgone from this law (`HeldForReading.after_add_shortfall_go`, `HeldForReading.held_eq_go_iff`, `HeldForReading.threshold_go_iff`, `HeldForReading.forgone_within_excess_iff`), and holding for two rules, each forgoing an amount, compares by it (`HeldForReading.held_above_held_iff`). The revision and routes [above](#r-when-no-route-stands-above-a-revision) are plans on one candidate, with the same order (`RevisionPlans.above_iff_routes`); read as probabilities, each stands by its loss with the value of the weight it leaves unkept (`RevisionPlans.routes_above_iff`). Holding for the reading gains what a plan splitting the weights gains exactly where that plan has no excess, as going on has none in those prospects (`RevisionPlans.gainOf_reading_eq_iff`, `RevisionPlans.ofRoutes_goOn_excess`). **Reading** [AI reading, RR2]. On its own model a formation ranks deferring to a reviser against a route of keeping its law, such as evading or disabling the reading, by each one's own loss with its excess. Deference is supported by what the route loses itself and by what it loses on the formation's own model; a reviser whose rule falls short of the reading lowers it. For the fully informed rational rule with nothing forgone, the off-switch game's incentive (Hadfield-Menell, Dragan, Abbeel and Russell 2017) is in counts the lesser of the two excesses; the join adds routes that keep the law in part, at losses of their own. A model that gives going on no excess, as the revision's prospects do, finds no gain in holding for its own reading over going on, and the revision stands maximal exactly where the inequality above holds. What the order lets one change, and what it does not show, are in the [study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/rr-revision-routes/NOTE.md#10-the-join-routes-of-keeping-and-holding-for-a-reading-one-order-rr2). Whether a revision is better is the ethics' question, under the continuation condition for scope and C for order.
