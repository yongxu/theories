## 1. 生生

**The two senses.** In the grammar, `«生生»` is renewal without end: the greatest family each member of which conditions a beginning whose line goes on and returns to the family ([01 §6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#renewal-and-grains), `Grammar.lean`), under which `«生生不息»` holds by construction. In the ethics, 生生 is life in the whole and its continuing generation, compared by widening ([07 §2](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md), T8). The glossary's row "renewal, renewal without end (复生, 生生)" gives both in one row, and four live passages write the formal sense without its guillemets.

**Decision.** The formal sense is always written `«生生»`, glossed "renewal without end", as every theorem name already writes it; plain 生生 is the ethics' sense only. The two are not merged and neither is renamed: the formal word names a structure, the ethical one a direction argued under the two premises (the condition for scope, C for order), and 01's reading D1 already says the grammar's word names only renewal without end. The corpus sense of 生生不息, and the classical senses of 「生生之謂易」 and 《老子》「生生之厚」, are kept apart where 06 and the review packet discuss them; they are quotations, not the theory's terms.

## 2. 理

**The two uses.** 03 §6 and the glossary give 理 as a lumping that models structure and change at once, one the step goes alike across. The grammar's theorem names (`«守理则不穷»`, `«亡前理变»`, `«有亡之线两端理异»`, `«足以定理»` and its kin) and 06 §八 ("守值而承载律之最粗归并") use it for a class's core.

**Decision: one sense, 03's, and the core a 理 where the step makes it one.** 03's 理 is a lumping the step goes alike across. In the grammar, going alike is `«同行»`, `R˘;E;R ⊆ E` (01 §7, "Lumping"). The core of `E` is the greatest symmetric relation inside `E` that is constant under `R` (`«根»`), and the core of a class is a class (`«核为类»`). Constancy and going alike are different relations: with the successor fixed (`«定后»`), constancy gives going alike (`«有常定后则同行»`); with a successor everywhere, going alike gives constancy (`«同行遍后则有常»`). So, for a class `E`:
- Where the step's successor is fixed, the core of `E` is a class inside `E` that the step goes alike across: a 理 in 03's sense, read relationally.
- Where every occurrence also has a successor, every class inside `E` that the step goes alike across lies in the core, so the core is the coarsest such class, the 理 of `E`.
- Off a fixed successor the core need not go alike: where one occurrence steps to two, the core of the identity relates the first to itself and the step does not go alike across it. Where an occurrence has no successor, a class can go alike without lying in the core.

The composition and the first counterexample are checked against the package's own declarations, outside the package (`review/CoreAndLi.lean`, every axiom report empty, its log beside it). 01 §4 says this in a theorem paragraph of its own, labelled as composed from the declarations it cites and checked outside the package, inserted before the theorem whose names write 理. It is an added paragraph, so no record citing the section drifts.

The root's names on cores that write 理 mean the core of the relation they are stated for: `«守理则不穷»`, `«守理之线不亡»`, `«亡前理变»` and the definition `«足以定理»` are stated for every relation `E`, not only for classes, and `«有亡之线两端理异»` and `«合有常则足以定理»` take classes. That is shown a 理 where `E` is a class and the successor fixed, and the 理 of `E` with a successor everywhere too. The texts say this where the names are used, and the glossary row says it. This is the residue of the second sense: the names keep their wording, which is exact there and names the core elsewhere.

`«从缘生非文法之理»` (`GrammarCandidates`, cited in 06) uses 理 for "law". Its docstring says both are conditions on a model, not laws.

**Proposed to the owners, not done here (Lean, not texts).**
- A declaration for `Becoming.GrammarLaws` stating the composition, as `review/CoreAndLi.lean` checks it, so that 01 §4 can cite it in place of the check.
- A renaming that would remove the residue: the core names to 核 (`«守核则不穷»`, `«亡前核变»`, `«足以定核»` and their kin), and `«从缘生非文法之理»` to `«从缘生非文法之律»`. By AGENTS' naming rule a rename needs a name that states a wrong meaning, and where `E` is not a class or the successor is not fixed these do.

Each would land at its module's next Lean change, through its owner (the grammar's for `Grammar`, `GrammarLaws` and `GrammarRegularity`, `GrammarCandidates`'s), with the records citing them rebound.

06 §八 reads 理 from 文 along a determined law, where the core of the value class goes alike (`Law.alike_iff_core`). There "只在此义" was read as fixing 理 to the core. It is narrowed to the compound 文理, and the 理 read from 文 is called the 理 of the value class. 06 §八's later "文同与理在根之文法中同为一个结构" becomes "文同与文之理（值之类之理）".
