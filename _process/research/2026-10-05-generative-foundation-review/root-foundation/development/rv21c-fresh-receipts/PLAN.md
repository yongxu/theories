## The kit

- `plan_receipts.py`: the periphery receipts to rerun, read from every record's proof bindings at the run base (`out/receipts.json`); the older-layout receipts classified file by file; the earlier receipts' reuse of steps no run folder keeps. Run again after the re-points, it must find only the failed checkers.
- `kit.py`: the list (from `out/receipts.json`, else the 25 found on 31 October) and shared helpers.
- `stale_all.py`, `stale_bound.py`: read-only staleness scans; `run.sh` checks the pointer scan finds nothing the plan left out.
- `bindings.py`: which records bind each receipt, read before the runs and again after the merge.
- `run_periphery.py`: the stale checkers one at a time, on the slot cache. A failure keeps whatever run folder it wrote and restores its pointer; a checker failing before it writes its receipt has no stamp in `status.json`, and its output in `out/<stem>.log` is its kept log.
- `verify_runs.py`: the core receipt is fresh with nothing reused or copied; each passing periphery receipt reuses only this slot's steps, keeps every log, and every source and checker file it names is main's (`--no-write` re-checks that against a later main); reused steps counted by origin; folder sizes.
- `compare.py`: old against new, by declarations, axiom reports and printed signatures.
- `repoint_all.py`: `tools/research/repoint.py --receipt` for each passing checker. A pointer pinned by a reference follows the rewrite.
- `axiom_texts.py`: a record's axiom sentences brought to its renewed receipt's reports (`--check` in gates.sh, the rewrite in land.sh), from `AXIOM-SPECS.json`: templates over the counts, reports entries checked against both receipts, and `holds` entries with their reason; every mention of an axiom or a count of reports in such a record and its home section must lie in an entry; the swaps' other guards (declarations and signatures, the older audit total) are checked before any rewrite, and check_repoint.py stops on a rewritten record the swaps left. `axiom_tests.py` (a gate step) tests it on real data, each finding of its two reviews of 31 October as a case, from `axiom-tests-fixture.json`.
- `swap_stamps.py`: the stamp swap in `validation_scope`, guarded by `compare.json`; with `--dates` the older records' passing date too, guarded by `older.json`; every record left, swapped or kept is listed in `out/swap.json` and named in the study, the board and the commit.
- `check_repoint.py`: every changed record differs from the committed one only by bookkeeping.
- `texts.py`: 08 §3's receipt sentence (the fresh receipt's source hashes and counts must equal the core receipt's at the run base) and the study section, every figure from the data; `--check` compares only, before the head is claimed.
- `repoint_texts.py`: re-points the records drifting by RV21c's own edits only.
- `board_rv21c.py`: the head clause, the landed clause and the history row, counts from `out/status.json`, the landing date and the coordinator's order given.
- `run_older.py`: the older-layout chain (formal, evolution, development), each checker in place, a failure copied into `out/older-failed/` and restored, the unbound receipts stale in the same files listed.
- `readme.py`: the fresh core receipt's README, written into its run folder from the runs.
- `rehearse.sh`: a throwaway rehearsal on a few small checkers.
- The landing scripts, run in order:
  - `run.sh WORKTREE ASIDE`: the runs, with no head clause, committed on branch `rv21c`; before any run, the older-layout plan checked; then `gates.sh`.
  - `gates.sh WORKTREE ASIDE`: an axiom report changed and an older-layout checker not renewed route to the coordinator (a ruling given as `ACK_AXIOMS` or `ACK_OLDER` lets the gate pass and is named in the texts); a receipt still stale other than a failed one; 08 §3. On success, `ASIDE/gates-passed` holds the run commit.
  - `claim_head.sh WORKTREE ASIDE ORDER`: requires the gates passed on the branch as it stands, re-checks the bound bytes and 08 §3 on main, checks the order sentence and RV21c's row on copies of main's board and history file, then a board-only commit on main.
  - `[RV21C_CACHE=…] land.sh WORKTREE ASIDE CORE_UTC`: both swaps as ruled (a landing with SWAP=0 or SWAP_DATES=0 stops); merge main, re-read the bindings, re-point, texts, render, checks, a paths guard, commit.
  - `release_head.sh ASIDE REASON`: frees the head if `land.sh` stops after the claim; `reset_land.sh WORKTREE ASIDE` puts the worktree back, the attempt kept in ASIDE.
  - `remerge.sh WORKTREE ASIDE`: if main moves by board-only commits between `land.sh` and `ff.sh`.
  - `ff.sh COMMIT MERGED_MAIN`: move the shared checkout's kit folder aside first.
