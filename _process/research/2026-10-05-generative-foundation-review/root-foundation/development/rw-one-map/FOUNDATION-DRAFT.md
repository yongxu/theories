## 1. One map: lines, and what holds between them

The account's root has one kind of symbol, a relation between occurrences (际), read as condition (缘). Something that goes on is a **line** (相续): occurrences continuing one another under a sameness (类). An occurrence in a line begins (生), abides (住), alters (异) or ends (亡): the four marks, "none standing alone" (01 §3). A pattern goes on (延) or ends (尽), through the turnover of its parts (传) or without it (守) (02 §1). The account's own title is these four: how anything forms, lasts, changes and ends.

Hypothesis: the map's whole vocabulary is this.

- A **line** is anything that goes on under a sameness: a thing (a pattern, a stock it lives on, a rule, a reading) or a relation's holding (that it draws on the stock; that the rule is rebuilt from its results; that a loss reaches what the rule is read through).
- Of every line the same four things are asked: what makes it **form**, what makes it **last**, what makes it **change**, what makes it **end**.
- Lines condition one another. That is the map's topology: which line's forming, lasting, changing or ending is among the conditions of which other's.

The earlier prototypes' "places" are then combinations of marks on a few lines ("the rule's lean lasts; the stock it does not go by is ending; what would turn it has ended"). Their eleven "questions" are lines, and their "answers" are marks. There is one map because there is one set of lines for the whole account, not a list per family: the families are neighbourhoods of it and meet where they share a line.

## 2. Three things kept apart: reasoning, situation, and where they meet

The method chapter already says it (08 §9): "how far a claim has been seen is whether it is proved under named premises, supported by frozen tests, conjectured or argued; the two are kept apart. A thesis adds no theorem, a refutation removes none."

- **Reasoning.** A law says: IF these lines are at these marks (and these amounts compare so), THEN that line is at that mark. It speaks of any situation that meets the IF and of no situation in particular. It is proved on a model, or argued, or a reading.
- **Situation.** A particular case, actual (a field, a run, a record) or built (a Lean witness is a constructed case): which actual thing is which line, and what was observed of each line, when, and how.
- **Where they meet.** For a case and a law: is the case's thing really the law's line (the bridge); is the IF met; what the THEN predicts; what was observed. Three things can each be wrong while the others are right: the law, the observation, the bridge. The atlas's bindings already mark a failure as the law's, the binding's or the measurement's.

On the map: the lines are the ground. Laws are drawn on it as ties between lines. Cases are drawn on it as what was seen of each line. A test is a place where a tie and a sighting can be compared.

## 3. The store

Five kinds of record, in the account's own words.

1. **lines**: id; what it is (a thing, or a relation between two things); what form, last, change and end mean for it; the Lean predicates and ledger premises that state each, per model, and whether two models' predicates are joined by a proof or only by a reading.
2. **laws**: the Lean name; IF (line: mark, ...) and any comparison of amounts or times in plain words; THEN (line: mark); the kind (forms, lasts, changes, ends, or a border between two marks); how far seen and on which model; the atlas items it carries.
3. **cases**: actual or built; which thing is which line; sightings (line, mark, when, how, source).
4. **meetings**: a case and a law: the bridge, the IF assessed, the THEN predicted and observed, and what failed if anything did. A thesis is a meeting claimed for a whole kind of case.
5. **names**: what a field or a tradition calls a line, a mark or a law, with how it stands to the account (agrees, holds in a narrower place, diverges).

## 4. Words

No word for a role that fits only persons (not "those with a say", not 人). Use the account's own short words where they are plain: forms, lasts, changes, ends (生 住 异 亡); goes on, ends (延, 尽); condition (缘); reach (及); draws, gives (取, 给); carrying renewal, keeping (传, 守). A coined phrase is used only where the account has none.
