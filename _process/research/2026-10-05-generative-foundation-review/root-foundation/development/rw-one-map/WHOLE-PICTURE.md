## One cause under most of it

The map has two levels and is missing the one a person reads.

- At the top, 8 families. Below, 183 dynamics, each a theorem's mechanism in the theorem's words.
- Nothing in between: no **places**. A place is a condition something can be in: renewing within what it is given, drawing past it, a rule rebuilt from its own results, past the point of no return, ended, cycling, at rest.
- Of the atlas's 321 exits, 251 end in a sentence, not in a node. Only 73 of the 183 dynamics touch another; 110 touch none. The meta-attractor's closed form has no line in or out.

Three of your points follow from this one gap.

1. **No map.** Without places there is nothing to draw but boxes with counts (the terrain) or documents joined to documents (the connection page).
2. **Hard to read.** The prose has been doing the joining the map has not done, so every front grew.
3. **Dualism.** The families are sorted by how things come out: five are named as failures (ending, drains, capture, locks, stagnation) and one as the good place. Then every page denies that it judges. "Good or bad" is denied 39 times on the site; "ranks nothing" 75 times.

## Where I'd argue with you

- **Wikipedia.** The unit cannot be the 183 dynamics; that gives 183 unreadable pages. It has to be places, concepts and cases, about 40, each with a plain lead. And one linear page must stay, because an encyclopedia carries no argument.
- **The map will show a hole.** Drawn truthfully, the centre of our concern is thinly connected. I think we draw the hole.
- **Plain against non-dual.** "Lock", "drain" and "capture" are your words, and also the ones a stranger understands at once. Neutral coinages add vocabulary. My trade: everyday verbs for what happens, the field's known name as an alias, and what we want said separately, in the first person, not hidden in the map's names.
- **AGENTS.md is plain to us, not to a stranger.** Its second paragraph needs an example. The site needs a second register.
- **AI.** Once per page as authorship. But the mark stays where it carries information: 327 of the 345 readings of outside traditions rest on AI recall of a text not stored.

## The plan, in order

1. **Places.** Add the middle layer: about 30 to 40 places, named by what is happening. Assign each exit to one. Start with the capture chain, about ten places.
2. **The map.** Places as nodes, changes as arrows, line style for how far seen, blank areas shown as blank. The traditions' names as an overlay on the same picture.
3. **Articles.** About 40 short pages, one template: plain lead, example, how far seen, leads to, reached from, known as. One index.
4. **The home.** At most 250 words, the map, three doors. Limits said once. A real one page of about 500 words. Everything now there moves to its home; nothing is deleted.
5. **How we know it works.** No scores. Word caps as tests. A front is done when a person reads it for five minutes and says back what it is: you first, then two or three people outside.
