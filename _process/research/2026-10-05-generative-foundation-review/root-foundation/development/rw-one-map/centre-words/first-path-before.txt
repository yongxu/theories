SHEET lead_en: A map of how anything forms, lasts, changes and ends, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together. Its first path: what picks among things by a rule (a reward, a judge, a market, a training run) can use up what the rule does not count, can lock itself in where the rule is updated from its own picks, and in built examples loses what would have read the loss. One way out is proved of the course itself: where the loss gets back to the rule in time, the rest count it, and the judgments together go against what the rule favoured at two updates running, the rule turns. No theorem drawn says what brings a loss to get back to the rule or to be counted. Every other way out drawn needs something given from outside, or is shown only in a built example or as a reading.

PATH lead_en: A reward, a judge, a market, a vote or a training run picks among things by a rule. This path follows what can happen to such a rule, and what turns it.
PATH lead_zh: 奖励、评委、市场、投票、训练，都是按一条规则在取舍。这条路讲的是这样的规则可能走到哪里，什么能让它转向。
CAST 0: the rule :: what the picking goes by: a reward, a score, a price, a count of votes.

CAST 1: an update :: the rule set again; between updates it stays as it was. What it is set from differs from step to step, and each step says: its own picks, or judgments.
        重定 :: 把规则重新定一次；两次重定之间，规则不变。照什么来定，各步不同，每一步自己会写明：照它选出来的结果，或者照各方的判断。
CAST 2: locked; the lock :: the rule favours one side and the next update would favour it no less; from there neither the favour nor that side's share falls.

CAST 3: the favoured side, its share and its lead :: what the rule picks more of. Its share is how much of the whole it has; its lead is the same thing told as how far ahead of the rest it is.

CAST 4: the rest :: what is picked among besides the favoured side. Where the rule is set from the judgments of what it picked (steps 7, 9 and 14), the rest judge too, and weigh by their numbers.

CAST 5: the loss :: how much has been used up of something the rule leaves out. What lives on that thing bears it. It is still called the loss once something counts it.
        损耗 :: 规则没算进去的那样东西已经被耗掉多少。承受后果的，是靠这样东西延续的。后来有哪一方把它算进去了，它也还叫损耗。
CAST 6: what reads the loss, or counts it :: anything among what sets the rule whose judgment changes with the loss.
        读得到损耗的，把损耗算进去的 :: 定规则的各方里，判断会随损耗而变的一方。
CAST 7: what sets the rule :: those the rule is set from: from their judgments in steps 7, 9 and 14; in steps 8 and 15, from whether each of them keeps going.

CAST 8: the shapers :: what spends to change the rule in its own favour.
        改规则的一方 :: 花代价把规则往自己这边改的一方。
CAST 9: of the course itself; given from outside :: the first: proved under the conditions its sentence gives, with nothing else put in. The second: proved only with a further premise put in, which the step names: that the loss goes on growing, a way of judging, a measure, a limit, or where the loss falls. Which premises are marked as given from outside is the map's own marking, not a difference between two kinds of proof.
        过程自己的；过程之外给定的 :: 前一种：在句子写明的条件下得到证明，不用另外放进什么。后一种：要另外放进一条前提才得到证明，放进的是什么，那一步会写明：损耗照旧在涨，某一种评法，某一种量法，某一条限制，或者损耗落在哪一方身上。哪些前提标作“过程之外给定的”，是这张图自己的标法，不是证明分成了两类。
CAST 10: a built example :: a small case made up and worked out exactly, in which the thing happens. It shows that it can happen, not that it must.
        造出来的例子 :: 专门搭出来、算得清的小例子，里面确实发生了这件事。它说明这件事可能发生，不说明一定发生。

BOX b1.outside (row b1: Where the rule comes from)
  name: Set from outside | 规则由外面定
  says: The rule does not depend on its own results. Whatever it favours gains ground, and only a change to the rule changes what gains. | 规则不随自己的结果变。它偏向什么，什么就占上风；只有规则变了，才会换一方占上风。
  eg: Such as an entrance exam set by a ministry: schools fill with those good at that exam. | 比如部里统一出的入学考试：学校里都是会考这个试的。
BOX b3.checkers (row b3: What counts when the rule is set)
  name: Even the checks are its own | 查错的也在圈里
  says: The ways a cost could come to count (courts, press, statistics, audit) are filled by what the rule favours, or breaking them is what gets rewarded. | 代价本来可以经法院、报纸、统计、审计这些途径入账；这些途径也由规则偏向的一方占着，或者，弄坏它们正是受奖励的事。
  eg: Such as an auditor chosen and paid by the firm it audits. | 比如审计师由被审计的公司挑、由它付钱。
BOX d2.handed (row d2: Lasting through what carries it)
  name: Handed on | 换了一茬，格局还在
  says: The same pattern goes on while what carries it is replaced: it is handed on at least once in every stretch as long as one carrier lasts. | 承载它的换了一茬又一茬，同一个格局还在：每过一茬那么长的时间，至少往下传了一次。
  eg: Such as a craft passed from master to apprentice, or an office whose way of doing things survives every change of staff. | 比如师傅传徒弟的手艺；或者一个办公室，人都换过了，做事的路数没变。
