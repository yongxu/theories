## 1. What the path says, in my words

Anything that chooses (a prize, a market, a vote) can wear down something it depends on, so long as how much is left changes nothing in what it chooses. If each revision of its way of choosing goes by who is already ahead, the leader stays ahead. In made-up examples the wearing-down removes those who would have judged against it; then it is stuck for good. Ways out: the damage changes the others' judgments and together they go against the leader twice running (proved); or a cap, damage falling on the lobbyists, counting everyone who started, protecting needs (each needs an extra assumption). Too late: objectors outvoted, or damage felt only once past saving. Not known: what starts a rule feeding on its own results, what brings damage to those who decide, whether checks get captured.
