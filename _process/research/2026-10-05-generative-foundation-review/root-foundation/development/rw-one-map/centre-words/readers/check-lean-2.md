**S2. `=== path lock step 9`, `=== arrow b2.felt>b2.counted (en)` and `(tag_en)`, `=== box b2.heard (says_en)`, step 14.** The new texts say "the loss changes the rest's judgments" and "goes on changing those judgments at every later update". Two things follow.
- Nothing requires `lo' ≠ lo`, so the loss need not change any judgment. `turned_iff` and `kept_iff` hold either way.
- Nothing goes on changing. Past N, the reading is the same `lo'` at every later update.
So "changes" SAYS MORE (it adds a premise the Lean lacks), and "goes on changing" SAYS SOMETHING ELSE. The old "count the loss" matched the Lean better.
*Fix, step 9:* "If, once the loss has passed a set amount, the rest judge in a later way, and the judgments taken together that way go against what the rule favoured at this update and the next, the rule turns. The turn is for good provided the rest go on judging that way at every later update and judge no more for the favoured side than that side does." Use the same substitution in the arrow ("Suppose the rest, once the loss has passed a set amount, judge in a later way at every later update …"). Step 14 becomes: "by the update from which the rest judge in the later way". Box b2.heard: "Some that are not favoured judge in a later way once the loss has passed a set amount … or began to too late." The same applies to want_en and lead_en (outside my part).
