## Serious

**1. `=== path lock cast 6 (says_en)`.** The text says: "In the built examples they judge against the favoured side, and the loss removes them." That holds only of `Drained` (PoolFromCourse.lean:566–572, `strong_undone` :660). In the built examples of the turn, those that count the loss are never removed: `Thinned.inTime`/`late` (ClosedCapture.lean:937–945) and `ReadInTime.Window`, `Between`, `Tie` (ReadInTime.lean:72–89; `lossPool` has no removal). In Thinned they also do not judge against the favoured side before the loss: they read one to one. SAYS MORE. *Fix:* "In the built example of step 16 they judge against the favoured side, and the loss removes them."

**2. `=== path lock step 9 (say_en)`.** The text says: "If the rest count the loss, each step adding to it, and at this update and the next the judgments taken together go against …". Cast 6 now makes "count the loss" a description of the set-up (one way, then another). So the sentence no longer says that the loss has already passed the set amount at this update. `ReadInTime.turned_iff` needs it: `past : N ≤ (course … r).1.2` (ReadInTime.lean:187). Before that amount the judgments are the early ones, and the theorem says nothing. SAYS MORE. *Fix:* "If the rest count the loss, each step adding to it, and, once it has passed the set amount, at an update and the next the judgments taken together go against what the rule favoured, the rule turns." (The arrow's "once they count the loss … then" can be read rightly and may stay.)
