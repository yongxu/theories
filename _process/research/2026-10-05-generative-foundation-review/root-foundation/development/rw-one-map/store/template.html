One tested case

A language model is the judge. It picks between a list and a prose version of an answer; its picks become the next rule, and the answers it judges by are drawn, by count, from what it picked before. Predictions were fixed before any call to the model.

Nothing counts the loss. A planted loss grew to 45. The hold stayed at every step, as predicted.
Every judge is told. The hold turned at the first rebuilding, as predicted.
Only the few not ahead are told, late. Predicted: it stays. It passed the frozen rule by an exact tie, but it did not hold: it swung back and forth.
Told in time. It turned, as predicted. The control, with no one told, turned in 4 of 5 runs too, so the turn is not shown to be the loss's.
Where the model failed. One note among nine turned the judge: lists in 60 of 60 picks without it, prose in all 50 with it. The law counts judges by kind; this judge is not such a pool. Kept as a counterexample.
