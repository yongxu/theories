YOUR SHEET. It is made of blocks, one per record of a JSON data file (a card of one dynamic, a case of one test, an arrow of the map, a path, a test laid on the map). A block starts with "=== <file>: <what the record is>", then context lines in parentheses (what the record is about and the Lean statements it reads), then one line per text field, "[field] text", as the text will stand. const res = await parallel(args.map(u => () =>
