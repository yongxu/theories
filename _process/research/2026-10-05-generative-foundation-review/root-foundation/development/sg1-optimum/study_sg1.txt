**The question.** Chapter §7.1 named one place where prior art was stronger than the spine: Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell ("Consequences of Misaligned AI", NeurIPS 2020) prove that optimizing a proxy which references only some attributes of a utility drives the others to their lower bounds (their Theorem 1), and characterize when optimization must make the utility arbitrarily low (their Theorem 2, by compactness of the feasible high-utility set), while `Becoming.DrawnBeyond` only accounted what a rise draws. The question was what an optimum, not a rise, does beyond what couples in, stated on the spine with its accounting, and how far the result reaches against theirs ([significance review, optimum check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#optimum-check)).

**What was built** (`Becoming.Optimum`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *The drive, exactly.* Outcomes are the levels after a step (`C → Nat`), the eligible outcomes any set of them, an optimum a member of `Requirement.Frontier` (eligible, nothing eligible ranked above it). An order is rising with a family `ds` where it ranks above every outcome exceeding another on `ds` (as high at each condition of `ds`, higher at one), whatever either holds beyond (`Optimum.Rising`). `Optimum.least_iff`: every rising order has its optima at the least level beyond, condition by condition, exactly where every eligible outcome standing above another at a condition beyond is not unbeaten on `ds` (double negation, no decision used). `Optimum.least_of_room` is the direction with room stated positively; `Optimum.Shared.room` gives room from shared units (the eligible outcomes closed under moving a unit from a condition beyond that holds one into `ds`, with `ds` apart from the beyond and holding a condition). The family's stock is a rising order (`Optimum.rising_total`), and so is any order ranking above wherever a rising one does (`Optimum.Rising.mono`): a lexicographic order with the family first, counting the beyond after, drains it all the same (`Optimum.Drain.counted_after_drains`).
- *What breaks.* `Web.optimum_broken_iff`: where the levels after a step are an optimum of a rising order with room, and each condition beyond is depended on at a need it meets, a dependence beyond is broken exactly where some eligible outcome leaves its condition below the need: of the dependences beyond, the optimum breaks the most any eligible outcome breaks. `Web.optimum_ends_beyond`: with activity needing support and the dependence as it was, the formation is not active after the step, it is no member of a group all of whose couplings lie in `ds`, and no member couples into its condition.
- *How much.* `Flow.Balances.family_le_iff`: of two balanced steps from equal totals, each gaining nothing on the whole, one leaves the family's stock at most where the other does exactly where it leaves the beyond's stock with the loss at least where the other does. `Flow.top_iff_least`: among such moves, those with the highest family stock are exactly those leaving least beyond with the loss.
- *Keeping.* `Flow.keeping_optimum_takes_slack`: restricted to the outcomes keeping every dependence beyond at its need, a rising order with room among them, each need attained by one of them, has its optimum break nothing and, in a balanced step gaining nothing on the whole, raise the family with the loss by exactly the slack.
- *Witnesses* (`Optimum.Drain`, on `Diversion`: three units between the source and the store, the dweller needing two of the source). The store's optimum among the splits drains the source and the dweller ends, unseen by the taker (`drained_ends`, from the general law). Where lowering the source frees nothing for the store, keeping the source is an optimum and not at the least level (`room_needed`). An order satisfied at a store of one is not rising, and both the keeping and the draining outcome are among its optima (`satisficing_either`). Among the splits keeping the need, the optimum is `DrawnBeyond.WithinSlack`'s world and takes exactly the slack of one (`kept_takes_slack`, from the general law).

**Against the prior result.** Their Theorem 1 is `Optimum.least_of_shared` in continuous form: a continuous proxy strictly increasing in the referenced attributes, a constraint strictly increasing in each attribute (so lowering an unmentioned attribute frees room for a referenced one), and a convergent optimization path. What the spine's form adds, each a statement checked here: (1) the drive over every rising order, not one continuous scalar proxy, with orders that count the beyond after the proxy included; (2) an exact characterization on any set of outcomes, room being necessary as well as sufficient for every rising order to drive (`least_iff`), where their Theorem 2 characterizes, by compactness, when utility must fall arbitrarily low; (3) no utility over the beyond: the degradation is read as dependences broken and formations ended, exactly those some outcome breaks; (4) the conservation identity: the beyond with the loss falls by exactly the family's rise; (5) keeping each dependence at its need uses the slack and no more, a weaker restriction than their Proposition 2, which holds every unmentioned attribute where it was. What the spine does not reach: their continuous setting and limits of optimization paths, the compactness characterization itself, and the interactive improvement of their Theorem 3 and Propositions 4 and 5 (a proxy updated over time). Mild optimization (Taylor 2016, quantilizers) is the satisficing witness's side: it does not drive the beyond to its least level, and it does not protect it either, since the outcome ending the dweller is among its optima. Karwowski et al. (ICLR 2024) explain Goodhart's law in reinforcement learning by the geometry of the occupancy polytope, a linear proxy optimized over it moving to a vertex; the spine's statement is order-theoretic, with no linearity. Skalse et al. (NeurIPS 2022) show that over all stochastic policies a non-trivial pair of rewards is never unhackable; the spine says which conditions an optimum lowers and which dependences it breaks, not whether a proxy can be safe.

*Reading* [AI reading, D25, D45]. Optimizing harder through the same couplings reaches no further (§7.1), and it lowers what lies beyond as far as any outcome does, wherever lowering it leaves the family room to rise. The blindness and the competition for the same units are both needed: blindness alone leaves the beyond unselected (`Flow.unselected_beyond_coupling`), and only with room does an optimum go to the floor (`room_needed`). An order that counts the beyond, but after the proxy, keeps nothing; an order keeping each dependence first uses the slack and no more. That such an order separates outcomes differing only beyond, and so goes through a member coupled into what lies there, is `Direction.widening_selected_by_pool`. In IN1's lake the fishers who were told the community's goal and the direction but not the bed, talking before each month, kept it in no season (0 of 10, round 2's R2); read through these laws, the pooled members' order rose with the catch and the bed lay beyond every member's coupling (a post hoc reading, not a verdict of the experiment).

**Self-review.** The drive is near-definitional: an optimum is not beaten, room supplies a beater wherever something beyond stands above its least level, so nothing does. Its content is the exact iff on any set of outcomes, with room as a hypothesis that can fail (`room_needed`), and the spine's reading of the degradation as breaks and endings exactly (`optimum_broken_iff`). The conservation identity is `beyond_falls_exactly` applied to two moves; the keeping law composes the drive on the keeping set with `MetOn.stock_eq`. Room is the substantive premise and is supplied; deriving it from a law of exchange (the actor's takes and gives under `ExchangeLaw`, with the rest responding) is not done. The verdict on the relation-to-known-results table's row 6 stays "known result restated"; what changes is that the chapter no longer names a place where prior art is stronger on how far optimizing degrades what lies beyond, only the continuous, compactness and interactive results the spine does not state.
