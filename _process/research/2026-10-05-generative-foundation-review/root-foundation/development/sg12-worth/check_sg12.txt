## A check: valuing what is left at any worth (22 October 2026, SG12)

An AI check (one session, 22 October), made while the Lean was written, of what SG11 ([left check](#left-check)) left open: whether valuing what is left above the worth of the draw keeps what depends on the condition. **Verdict: the mathematics is known in its kind. A finite-horizon optimum stays on the path of greatest yield and departs from it near the end, the turnpike, and a quantity measured only at a fixed time is managed for that time, Goodhart's law. The spine's form is exact for every order rising with what is drawn and `v` times what is left. Above one, every optimum holds a level of greatest renewal through the horizon and regrows at the last step, the optima are exactly these courses, and what keeping a dependence forgoes is `(r - q) n` whatever `v`. With SG5 and SG11, every worth in whole numbers gives the same verdict on the path.**

| Known form | Where | Bearing |
|---|---|---|
| The turnpike: a finite-horizon optimum stays near the path of greatest growth or yield and departs from it only near the end | Dorfman, Samuelson and Solow, *Linear Programming and Economic Analysis* (1958); McKenzie (1963) (from memory) | Exact here: every optimum holds a level of greatest renewal at every step to the horizon and departs at the last step alone, to drain (`Optimum.frontier_holds`), freely (`Optimum.frontier_holds_left`) or to regrow (`Optimum.frontier_holds_valued`) |
| A terminal (salvage) value on the state left at the end | Finite-horizon optimal control; Clark, *Mathematical Bioeconomics* (1976) (from memory) | Above the worth of the draw, it sets the end level, not the path (`Optimum.valued_most_iff`, `Optimum.frontier_valued_iff`) |
| A measure becoming a target: what is measured at a fixed time is managed for that time | Goodhart (1975) (from memory) | A dependence above every level of greatest renewal is broken at the first step and restored only at the end (`Web.frontier_rebuilt_valued`, `Optimum.Holding.logistic_valued`) |
