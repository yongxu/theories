## A check: steps met again after a fall (22 October 2026, SG13)

An AI check (one session, 22 October), made while the Lean was written. It asks what SG7 ([stepwise check](#stepwise-check)) left open: whether a coupling counting every step a need is met, wherever, keeps the need as the count from the start does. **Verdict: the form is the spine's own: a per-step reward for a constraint holding, with no memory of a breach, is common in constrained optimization and reinforcement learning, but the exact threshold here, over every order rising with the worth, is not a known statement. A fall met again loses the coupling only at the steps below, so every optimum keeps the need exactly where `r + e < q + c + m` and `e < c`. That is strictly more than the count from the start needs, and a witness shows the tie at `r = q + c`.**

| Known form | Where | Bearing |
|---|---|---|
| A reward or cost for each step a constraint holds or is violated, no memory of a breach | Constrained Markov decision processes, Altman (1999) (from memory) | The coupling counted at every step met (`Optimum.metCount`); every optimum keeps the need where `r + e < q + c + m` and `e < c` (`Optimum.frontier_keeps_met`) |
| A constraint whose breach forfeits what follows | Absorbing or terminal violation states (from memory) | SG7's count from the start, threshold `r ≤ q + c` (`Optimum.frontier_keeps_stepwise`); against it a fall met again ties at `r = q + c` (`Optimum.Holding.burst_met`) |
