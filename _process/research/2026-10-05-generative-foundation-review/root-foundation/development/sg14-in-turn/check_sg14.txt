## A check: drawers in turn, each ranking courses (22 October 2026, SG14)

An AI check (one session, 22 October), made while the Lean was written. It asks what SG10 ([drawn-first check](#drawn-first-check)) left open: whether the others' rule is itself a best response when the others too rank courses. **Verdict: the mathematics is known in its kind. In the dynamic commons with linear payoffs, where any player can take the whole stock, the subgame-perfect equilibrium exhausts the stock at once, and with alternating moves the argument is backward induction. The spine's form is exact for every perfect profile, rules reading the step and the level, over any law of what stands, with no discounting, and read on the web as dependences broken. That restraint is no mutual best response over courses either, as it is not at one step (SG3) or along myopic steps (SG4), is the reading.**
