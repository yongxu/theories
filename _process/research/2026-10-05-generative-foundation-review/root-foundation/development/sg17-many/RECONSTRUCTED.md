# SG17 kit: what was rebuilt after the working folder was lost

At the landing, a shell chain removed the SG17 worktree after a failed fast-forward of main (another session had
committed to main), before the kit had been copied out; the kit's files were untracked and were lost with the folder.
Every committed result survived on branch `sg17-many` (code 2dac9c895 and 52533f8b3, receipts
20261022T040129.591948Z and 20261022T040548.209667Z, landing 63781794e, connectivity fd7777921), and the branch was
merged with main (e23ded544, the board conflict resolved by main's rows with SG17's row moved as at the landing).

The kit was rebuilt in the same session:

- `many.lean`, `web.lean`, `witness.lean` and `check_sg17.txt` are cut from the landed `Optimum.lean` and significance
  review; `study_sg17.txt` from the landed study section 332 with its placeholders restored.
- `build_tree.py` and `optimum_doc.py` were rewritten; run on main's `Optimum.lean` before SG17 they produce the landed
  `Optimum.lean` byte for byte (checked with `cmp`).
- `integrate.py` was rewritten; its four edits on main's files before SG17 give the landed `Becoming.lean`,
  `check_becoming.py`, `README.md` and `README.zh.md` exactly (checked).
- `apply_text.py`, `board_part.py` and `section9_audit.py` were regenerated by replaying, on SG15's kit, the same
  transformations the landing used, including the later removal edits; `make_record.py` was rewritten in its final form
  (the alias and length fixes the first check asked for included). These were not rerun: their outputs are landed.
- `repoint.sh` lists the records the landing re-pointed, read from `_process/architecture/rebinds.jsonl`.
- `connectivity.log` is the check rerun on the merged tree. The checker logs are not kept; the two receipts under
  `becoming-checks/` are the evidence of both runs. The render, check and link logs of the landing are not kept; the
  checks pass on the merged tree.
