## A check: room read off the exchange (21 October 2026, SG2)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of the premise SG1 left supplied ([optimum check](#optimum-check)): room, that lowering a condition beyond leaves what couples in room to rise. Thesis T3's standing named it a supplied premise, and chapter §8.1 item 10 kept its derivation from a law of exchange open. **Verdict: the mathematics is known. Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's Theorem 1 assumes a resource constraint strictly increasing in each attribute, which is room supplied; saturation of capacity at an optimum is the saturated cut of a maximum flow and the binding constraints of a corner solution. What the spine's form adds is where the constraint comes from, checked: over one step of exchange, a part's members taking at most their capacity and giving no more than they take, the rest exchanging as it does, room holds with no further premise, every optimum of every rising order stands at each condition beyond at what the rest leaves less the part's capacity, and a condition from which the part may take nothing is untouched.** The drain at an optimum lies exactly where the part shares units with what lies beyond, by its capacity there; this is new as a statement on the spine, not as mathematics.
