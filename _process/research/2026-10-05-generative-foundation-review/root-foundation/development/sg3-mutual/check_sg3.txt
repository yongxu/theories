## A check: several optimizers at a mutual best response (21 October 2026, SG3)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of chapter §8.1 item 10's open question, how much each formation takes when several draw, as a law of the formations rather than a supplied flow, for formations that optimize ([room check](#room-check)). **Verdict: the mathematics is known. That users of a common resource, each gaining from what it takes and bearing at most a share of the loss, overdraw it is the tragedy of the commons and the open-access result, and in common-pool resource games the equilibrium appropriates more than the group optimum. The spine's case is the limit where no optimizer registers the condition at all: checked, at a mutual best response a condition beyond every optimizer's family falls by the optimizers' whole joint capacity, wherever something stands each takes its whole capacity, and one part made of all of them, by any rising order, would leave it at the same level. That coordination among optimizers none of whom couples into a condition keeps nothing there is the form's own statement, immediate from the law and not a new kind of mathematics.**

| Each herder gains the whole of an added animal and bears a share of the overgrazing, so each adds animals until the commons is ruined | Hardin, "The Tragedy of the Commons", Science 162 (1968) (from memory) | The share borne is nothing where the condition lies beyond every optimizer's family; the drain is the joint capacity (`Optimum.mutual_beyond`) |
