**The question.** SG2 ([study §288](#288-room-read-off-the-exchange-sg2)) read room off one step in which one part optimizes and the formations outside it exchange as they do, and found every optimum beyond at what the rest leaves less the part's capacity. Chapter §8.1 item 10 kept open how much each formation takes when several draw, as a law of the formations rather than a supplied flow: `Becoming.JointDraw` states which dependence the joint draw breaks and that restraint by the excess keeps it, with the flows supplied. Thesis T3 names selections of many kinds, a market or natural selection among them, where many formations optimize at once. The question was what several optimizers do together beyond what couples into each of them, when each optimizes alone.

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.Optimum`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *A mutual best response.* A formation alone is a part (`Optimum.alone`, `Optimum.alone_self`). Several formations, the optimizers, each have their own family, beyond and order. A step is a mutual best response where each optimizer taking part acts within its capacity in it and the levels after are an optimum of its order among the outcomes open to it alone, every other formation, optimizer or not, exchanging as it does in that step (`Optimum.MutualBest`).
- *Restraint is no mutual best response.* At a condition beyond an optimizer's family where something stands after a mutual best response, that optimizer takes its whole capacity and gives nothing there (`Optimum.MutualBest.full`, from SG2's `Above.full` with the floor nothing).
- *Several optimizers beyond, exactly.* At a condition beyond every optimizer's family, the levels after a mutual best response stand at `l c + restGiven − restTaken − capacity`, where the rest is the formations outside every optimizer and the capacity is the optimizers' joint capacity (`Optimum.mutual_beyond`). Where nothing stands, balance and capacity alone give it: what the step takes is what the rest takes and at most the joint capacity, and what it gives is at least what the rest gives. Where something stands, every optimizer takes its whole capacity and gives nothing, and balance gives the level exactly.
- *As one.* The condition stands where every optimum of one part made of all the optimizers, by any order rising with any family apart from it, would leave it (`Optimum.mutual_as_one`, with SG2's `optimum_beyond`). It is lowered below where the rest leaves it exactly where the optimizers have some capacity there and something is left (`Optimum.mutual_lowers_iff`), and on a web a dependence on it, met at the step, breaks exactly where its need exceeds that level (`Web.mutual_broken_iff_capacity`).
- *Witness* (`Optimum.TwoTakers`). Two takers, each of capacity one at a source standing at two, each ordering by the store, and a dweller. Both drawing is a mutual best response (`joint_best`, from `joint_within`, `joint_optimum` and `gives_at_most_one`); the source falls by the joint capacity of two, to nothing (`joint_drains`, derived from the general law); each take is within the slack of a need of one, and together they leave the source below it (`within_slack_jointly_below`). The step in which the first taker restrains and the second draws, leaving a unit at the source, is no mutual best response, whatever the listings (`restrained_not_mutual`, from `MutualBest.full`).

**Against the prior forms** ([significance review, commons check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#commons-check)). The mathematics is known. Hardin's tragedy of the commons: each herder, gaining the whole of an added animal's yield and bearing only a share of the overgrazing, adds animals until the commons is ruined. Gordon's open-access fishery: effort enters until the resource's rent is dissipated. In common-pool resource games the Nash equilibrium appropriates more than the group optimum (Ostrom, Gardner and Walker 1994). The spine's case is the limit where no optimizer registers the condition at all, so no one bears any share: the drain is the whole joint capacity, the same at a mutual best response as under one optimizer of that capacity. What the form adds is exactness over any orders rising with any families, no payoff over the condition for anyone, and the statement that coordination among optimizers none of whom couples into the condition keeps nothing there (`mutual_as_one`). Ostrom (1990) found commons kept by communities through rules, monitoring and sanctions; on the spine these act through coupling (the condition entering what the users register) and through capacity, the only two terms of the law.

*Reading* [AI reading, D25, D45]. A commons that none of its users couples into is drained by the sum of their capacities, whether they act each alone or together. How many optimize changes nothing but the sum; agreement among them changes nothing at all. What keeps it is a coupling, bringing the commons into what at least one user registers, so that the law here no longer applies to it, or a capacity lowered; a rule restraining the users is a capacity lowered by the rule. This is T3's drain stated for several optimizers, and it sharpens the alignment reading of §7: many systems each optimizing a proxy blind to the same dependence drain it as one system with all their capacity would, and coordinating them without coupling any of them into that dependence protects nothing.

**Self-review.** The laws are short once SG2's are in place: `MutualBest.full` is `Above.full` applied to each optimizer alone, and `mutual_beyond` adds a bookkeeping of the joint takes; the case where nothing stands needs no optimality at all. Their content is that item 10's open question, how much each formation takes when several draw, is answered for optimizers at a mutual best response, with the exact level and the invariance under coordination. Limits. Existence: that a mutual best response exists is not shown in general, only in the witness; where the outcomes open to an optimizer depend on the others, a step may have none. Conditions within some optimizer's family are not stated: there the optimizer's order registers the condition, and the classical commons, where each bears a share, lies there. One step, as in SG2; a course, and how learners come near a mutual best response, are not stated. The witness's capacities and orders are chosen; nothing says which optimizers an actual commons has.
