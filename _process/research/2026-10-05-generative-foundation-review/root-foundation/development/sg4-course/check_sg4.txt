## A check: a course of re-optimizing steps against a renewal (21 October 2026, SG4)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of chapter §8.1 item 10's open question, an optimizer choosing again at each step of a course with the rest responding ([commons check](#commons-check)). **Verdict: the mathematics is known. That open access with a harvesting capacity above a stock's greatest renewal collapses it, and with critical depensation ends it for good, is fisheries economics; the maximum sustainable yield is the largest draw a stock renews; a sole owner ranking the course keeps what open access drains; pumping above an inflow empties the store and is then limited to the inflow. The spine's case is optimizers that register nothing of the condition and rank each step: checked, over a course of re-optimizing steps a dependence beyond is lost exactly where the draw there exceeds the renewal and kept exactly where it does not, for good, with nothing required of the renewal below the need beyond its being at most `r`; what the renewal does below the need decides only the drawers' own take, bounded where it stops there and at least the renewal a step where it does not; several optimizers jointly above the renewal lose it at mutual best responses at every step. That T1's joint excess, supplied to `Flow.joint_excess_ends`, follows for optimizers, and that for a draw the same at every step T1's premise that renewal stops below the need is not needed, are the form's own steps, short once SG2's law holds at every step.**
