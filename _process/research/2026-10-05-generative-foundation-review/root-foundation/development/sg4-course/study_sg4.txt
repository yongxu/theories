**The question.** SG2 ([study §288](#288-room-read-off-the-exchange-sg2)) read room off one step and found every optimum beyond at what the rest leaves less the part's capacity; SG3 ([study §290](#290-several-optimizers-at-a-mutual-best-response-sg3)) found several optimizers at a mutual best response draining by their joint capacity. Both are one step. Chapter §8.1 item 10 kept open an optimizer choosing again at each step of a course, with the rest responding. Thesis T1 says that what persists through many renewal cycles of a condition it depends on, where renewal stops below a need, has had the joint draw within the renewal, and its laws take the joint excess as given (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`); that each drawer within the renewal does not keep the joint draw within it was shown by a constructed pair (`Pair.own_not_joint`). The question was what a course of optimum steps does to a condition beyond that renews, whether T1's joint excess follows for optimizers rather than being supplied, and which premises the loss needs.

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.Optimum`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *Levels renewed and drawn.* A level `x` with `x (t + 1) = x t + g t − d t`. Where at most `r` is given at every step, however low the level, and more than `r` drawn, the level never rises (`Optimum.never_rises`, `Optimum.level_le_start`), falls by at least one a step while positive (`Optimum.falls_over`), stands below a positive need `m` at every step `t` with `x 0 < m + t` (`Optimum.ends_within`), and once below stays below (`Optimum.level_stays_below`). Where exactly `r` is given at or above the need and at most `r` drawn, a level starting at the need never falls (`Optimum.kept_at_need`); drawn `k` at every step, with at most `r` given below the need, it stays at the need exactly where `k ≤ r` (`Optimum.kept_iff_within`). Two subtraction lemmas replace core's, which report `propext`.
- *What is drawn.* What stands with everything drawn is the start with everything given (`Optimum.level_add_drawn`), so where the level ends no higher than it started, everything given was drawn (`Optimum.given_le_drawn`). Over `n` steps at most `x 0 + r · n` is drawn (`Optimum.drawn_within`); within the renewal `k · n` (`Optimum.kept_drawn`). Above the renewal, the gate decides. Where nothing is given below the need, at most `x 0 + r · x 0`, however long the course (`Optimum.ended_drawn_le`), so a positive draw is bounded exactly where `r < k` (`Optimum.drawn_bounded_iff`), and over `n` steps with `x 0 + r · x 0 < r · n` keeping draws more than draining (`Optimum.keeping_draws_more`). Where at least `a` is given at every step, at least `a · n` (`Optimum.drawn_at_least`), without bound for `a > 0` (`Optimum.drawn_unbounded`); with `a = r`, as much as keeping draws.
- *A course of re-optimizing steps* (`Optimum.Reoptimizes`): at every step the levels after are an optimum of the part's order among the outcomes open to it from the levels before, the rest exchanging as it does. At a condition beyond, each step's level is the level before with what the rest gives, less what it takes and the part's capacity, or nothing (`Optimum.course_beyond`, SG2's `optimum_beyond` at each step); with several optimizers at mutual best responses at every step of a balanced course, less their joint capacity (`Optimum.mutual_course_beyond`).
- *On the web.* Over a re-optimizing course, with the rest renewing by exactly `r` at a positive need and by at most `r` below, and `k` taken there with the capacity, a dependence at that need, met at the start, is lost (`Web.Lost`: broken at some step, the level below that need ever after) exactly where `r < k` (`Web.course_lost_iff`, from `Web.lost_iff` and `Web.lost_over`); the condition stays at the need exactly where `k ≤ r` (`Web.course_kept_iff`); nothing is drawn beyond the start with `r` a step (`Web.course_drawn_within`). The gate: where whatever gives to a condition is supported and the givers outside the part depend on it at a need of at least `m`, nothing outside the part gives across a step from below `m` (`Flow.restGiven_unsupported`); gated, a course above the renewal draws a bounded amount (`Web.course_ended_drawn`); ungated, with at least `a` given at every step, the dependence is lost all the same while at least `a` a step is drawn, without bound (`Web.course_drawn_unbounded`). Several optimizers at mutual best responses whose joint take there exceeds the renewal lose it, however small each one's capacity (`Web.mutual_course_lost`).
- *Witness* (`Optimum.Renewing`). On `Diversion`'s places, a taker of capacity `k` draws from the source into the store and the dweller renews the source by `r` while it stands at its need of two, by nothing below. The taker's draw is the store's optimum at every step (`course_optimum`, `course_reoptimizes`), and the source's steps are read off the general law (`source_beyond`). From a source at its need the dweller's dependence is lost exactly where `r < k` (`lost_iff`, from `Web.course_lost_iff`); with a positive capacity the store is bounded exactly there (`store_bounded_iff`), gains `k` a step within it (`store_kept`), and with `r < k` holds less after a long course than a taker of capacity `r` choosing the same way (`keeping_holds_more`). Two takers of capacity one, each ordering by the store, at mutual best responses at every step (`joint_course_best`), lose the source renewed by `r < 2` (`joint_course_lost`, from `Web.mutual_course_lost`), though alone a taker of capacity one keeps a source renewed by one (`alone_kept`); the course in which one restrains, none of whose steps is a mutual best response (`restrained_course_not_best`, from `MutualBest.full`), holds more in the store after `n > 2 s₀` steps (`restraint_holds_more`).

**Against the prior forms** ([significance review, course check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#course-check)). The mathematics is known. Gordon's open-access fishery dissipates the resource's rent; Clark's dynamics show that open access with a harvesting capacity above the stock's greatest renewal drives it to collapse, and with critical depensation, below which the stock does not renew, to extinction from which it does not return. Where the renewal is an inflow that does not depend on the stock, as with groundwater recharge, pumping above it empties the store and is then limited to the inflow as it comes (Theis 1940). Schaefer's maximum sustainable yield is the largest draw a stock renews; Scott's sole owner, ranking the whole course, keeps the stock where open access does not; Levhari and Mirman's dynamic commons shows several harvesters at an equilibrium of the course taking more than a single owner. The spine's case: optimizers who register nothing of the condition and rank each step, against a renewal fixed at `r` at or above the need. What the form adds: the threshold is exact in both directions over any orders rising with any families (lost exactly where `r < k`, kept exactly where `k ≤ r`), the loss is for good and needs nothing of the renewal below the need beyond its being at most `r`; what the renewal does below the need decides only the drawers' own take, bounded where it stops and at least the renewal a step where it does not. T1's joint excess, a premise of `Flow.joint_excess_ends`, follows for optimizers from the optimum and the capacity, as does `Pair.own_not_joint`'s gap between each drawer's own count and the joint draw, and for a draw the same at every step T1's own premise that renewal stops below the need is not needed.

*Reading* [AI reading, D25, D45]. An optimizer choosing again at every step by an order blind to a renewing condition keeps it exactly while its capacity there, with what the rest takes, stays within the renewal; capacity above the renewal ends what depends on it for good, within a count of steps set by how far it stood above the need, whatever the renewal does below the need. Whether the optimizer's own measure ever registers that loss depends on something else, on whether the renewal depends on the level it drains. Where it does (the gate), the drawer's own take is bounded, and more capacity gathers less over a long course than a capacity at the renewal. Where it does not, the renewal goes on flowing to the drawers after the dependence is gone: they draw at least what keeping would have drawn, without bound, and nothing in their measure registers the loss at all. Several optimizers each within the renewal, none of whom alone would end it, end it together at mutual best responses, and the restraint that would keep it is no mutual best response at any step. For §7 this is T3's drain along a course: raising the capacity of systems optimizing proxies blind to a renewing dependence is safe exactly up to the renewal, the threshold set by the joint capacity rather than any one system's; above it what depends on the condition ends, and whether the systems' own returns fall with it is a separate matter of the renewal, so their returns are no signal of the loss. What keeps the condition within the law's terms is a coupling, bringing it into what at least one optimizer registers (so that its order no longer rises with the family alone), or capacity held within the renewal.
