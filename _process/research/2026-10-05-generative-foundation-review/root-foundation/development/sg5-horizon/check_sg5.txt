## A check: courses ranked over a horizon (21 October 2026, SG5)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of chapter §8.1 item 10's open questions, an optimizer ranking courses rather than steps and a renewal growing with the level ([course check](#course-check)). **Verdict: the mathematics is known. A stock's balance over a horizon (what is harvested is the start less the end with everything grown) is bookkeeping; that the yield a level sustains is its renewal, and the largest is taken at the level of greatest renewal, half of room in the logistic law, is Schaefer's; that a sole owner ranking the whole course keeps the stock is Scott's; that a finite horizon with no value on what is left ends by drawing everything is standard bioeconomics; that managing a forage stock for its own yield leaves its predators' needs uncounted is the ecosystem critique; that a long-horizon agent keeps its resources is the instrumental-convergence argument. The spine's form: the balance with the shortfall holds for every course and every renewal bounded by `r`, not only optimal ones; the courses drawing the most are characterized exactly, in both directions; every optimum of every order rising with what is drawn is covered, not one objective; and the dependences on the condition are sorted exactly: kept to the horizon where every level of greatest renewal meets the need, broken at the first step where every such level lies below it, and not registered by the order by the draw where some lie above and some below (the gated witness). That this answers the far-sighted objection to T3's drain, with far-sight keeping exactly the resource base of the optimizer's own draw, is the form's own step.**
