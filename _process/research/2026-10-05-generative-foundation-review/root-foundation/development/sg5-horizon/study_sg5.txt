**The question.** SG4 ([study §291](#291-a-course-of-re-optimizing-steps-against-a-renewal-sg4)) found that an optimizer choosing again at each step, blind to a renewing condition, ends what depends on it exactly where its capacity exceeds the renewal. Its optimizer ranks steps. The obvious objection is the far-sighted one: an optimizer that ranks whole courses would keep the resource its later draws need, as Scott's sole owner keeps the stock that open access drains, and as the instrumental-convergence argument in alignment expects a long-horizon agent to keep its resource base. Chapter §8.1 item 10 kept open an optimizer ranking courses and a renewal growing with the level. The question was what far-sight keeps, exactly, and what it still loses.

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.Optimum`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *Generalized below SG4* (rule 9). What stands with everything drawn is the start with everything given wherever each step leaves at most the level with what is given (`Optimum.level_add_drawn_of_within`); SG4's `level_add_drawn`, for a course whose steps take `d`, is now its case.
- *What a course draws, leaves and forgoes.* With at most `r` given at each step, everything given with everything it falls short of `r` is `r · n` (`Optimum.sum_add_shortfall`); so what is drawn across `n` steps, with what stands after them and the shortfall, is `x 0 + r · n` (`Optimum.drawn_add_shortfall`), and across `n + 1` steps, counting the shortfall after the first, `x 0 + g 0 + r · n` (`Optimum.drawn_add_shortfall_after`). Hence no course draws more (`Optimum.drawn_le_most`), and a course draws exactly that much where `r` is given at every step after the first and nothing stands after the last (`Optimum.most_iff`).
- *On the flow.* Under balance what is drawn is what is taken (`Flow.Balances.drawnAt_eq`, `Flow.Balances.sum_taken`), so a balanced course's take, level after and shortfall add to its start with `r` a step (`Flow.Balances.taken_add_shortfall`); of two balanced courses from one level one takes at most what the other takes exactly where the other's level after with its shortfall is at most the one's (`Flow.Balances.taken_le_iff`); two given alike at every step and ending alike take the same, whatever levels they pass through (`Flow.Balances.taken_eq_of_alike`). A balanced course whose giving is a law `R` of the level and whose take is at most `k` is among the open courses (`Flow.Balances.open_course`).
- *Courses ranked.* The open courses from `s` (`Optimum.OpenCourse`: each step leaving at most the level with what `R` gives and drawing at most `k`), what a course draws (`Optimum.drawnOver`), the order by it (`Optimum.byDrawn`), and the course `holding s y n`, at `y` from the first step to the `n`-th and at nothing after. Where `R y = r` and `y ≤ s + R s`, the holding course draws the most (`Optimum.holding_most`); with `0 < n`, `s + R s ≤ y + k` and `y + r ≤ k` it is open (`Optimum.holding_open`) and an optimum (`Optimum.holding_frontier`); and then every optimum of every order rising with the draw stands where `R` gives `r` at every step to the `n`-th and at nothing after the last (`Optimum.frontier_holds`).
- *On the web.* Under the same premises, a dependence whose need lies above every level where `R` gives `r`, met at the start, is broken at the first step by every optimum and its condition stays below the need through the horizon (`Web.frontier_broken`); one whose positive need every such level meets stands at every step to the `n`-th and is broken at the last (`Web.frontier_kept`).
- *Witnesses* (`Optimum.Holding`, on `Diversion`'s places: a taker draws from the source into the store, the dweller gives the source what `R` gives at its level and depends on it at need two). Under the logistic law `s (2 − s)`, which renews one unit at one and nothing at none or from two on, from a source at two the course holding it at one balances and is an optimum with capacity two, and the dweller's dependence is broken at the first step and unmet through the horizon (`logistic_broken`, from `Web.frontier_broken`). Under a gate renewing one unit at every level from one, the courses holding the source at two and at one are both optima with capacity three and take the same (`gated_alike`, from `Flow.Balances.taken_eq_of_alike`); the first keeps the dweller to the horizon, the second breaks it at the first step.

**Against the prior forms** ([significance review, horizon check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#horizon-check)). The mathematics is known. That the yield sustained at a level is the renewal there, and the maximum sustainable yield is taken at the level of greatest renewal, half of room in the logistic law, is Schaefer's; that a sole owner valuing the whole course keeps the stock is Scott's; that a finite horizon with no value on what is left ends in depletion is standard in Clark's bioeconomics; that single-species management for a forage stock's own yield leaves its predators' needs uncounted is the ecosystem critique (Pikitch and others 2012). The spine's form: the identity is conservation over the horizon, so it holds for every course and every renewal bounded by `r`, not only for optimal ones; the characterization of the courses drawing the most is exact in both directions; every optimum of every order rising with the draw is covered, not one objective; and the levels that the order registers are named exactly, those of greatest renewal, with what that leaves for each dependence on the condition: kept to the horizon where every such level meets its need, broken at the first step where every such level lies below it, and not registered by the order by the draw where some lie above it and some below (the gated witness).

*Reading* [AI reading, D25, D45]. Ranking whole courses changes when a dependence ends and at which level the condition is held, not whether a dependence the optimizer's draw does not renew is kept. What a course draws registers its levels only through what is renewed there, so every optimum of a far-sighted order rising with it holds the condition where the renewal is greatest (which of those levels it holds is the order's further business), which keeps what depends on the condition at that level or below to the horizon and breaks, at the first step, whatever needs more; and at its horizon it draws everything, ending every positive need there. Against SG4's step-ranking optimizer, which with capacity above the renewal ends a dependence at the need within a count of steps set by how far the condition stood above it, the course-ranking optimizer holds that dependence to its horizon wherever every level of greatest renewal meets its need. For §7 this answers the far-sighted objection to T3's drain: lengthening an optimizer's horizon keeps exactly the resource base of its own draw, at the level that serves the draw, and none of the dependences beyond that need the condition higher; instrumental convergence to keeping one's resources is real, and it is not alignment with what else depends on them. What keeps a dependence above the levels of greatest renewal within the law's terms is, as in SG4, a coupling that brings it into what the order rises with, or a capacity held below what the draw-down needs.

**Self-review.** The identity is elementary (the conservation of SG4's `level_add_drawn` with the shortfall added), and the characterization of the courses drawing the most is a corollary; the weight lies in what it settles: that far-sight registers levels only through the renewal, for every order rising with the draw and every renewal law bounded by `r`. The premises were checked for necessity before landing: a first form required the capacity to cover the start with what is given there; only what the first step draws down to `y` is needed, over a horizon of at least two steps (a one-step horizon is SG2's single step), and the laws were restated so. Reach (`y` within the start with what is given there) and the last step's capacity (`y + r ≤ k`) are needed for the exact form: without reach the optimum rebuilds from below, and without the last step's capacity the drain at the horizon spreads over several steps and leaves the levels of greatest renewal earlier; neither optimum is stated. Above-every-level (`∀ z, R z = r → z < m`) is needed for "every optimum": where some level of greatest renewal meets the need, the course holding it is an optimum that keeps the dependence (the gated witness). Limits. An order valuing what is left at the horizon, or discounting later draws, is not covered; with a discount the optimum may hold below the levels of greatest renewal (Clark's result that high discount rates drive a stock lower). Several optimizers ranking courses are not stated. The witnesses' laws and capacities are chosen.
