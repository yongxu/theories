## A check: what keeping a dependence forgoes over a horizon (21 October 2026, SG6)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of what SG5 ([horizon check](#horizon-check)) left open: what keeps a dependence whose need lies above every level of greatest renewal, and how much. **Verdict: the mathematics is known in each part. That a penalty enforces a constraint exactly when it exceeds the constraint's value to the objective is the exact-penalty principle; that leaving forage for predators lowers yield is the multispecies trade-off; that a side-effect penalty's coefficient trades task reward against impact is the impact-measure literature. The spine's form: the value of keeping is exact, the renewal given up at the need for each step and the need left at the end, for every renewal law bounded by `r` everywhere and by `q` at or above the need; the thresholds hold for every order rising with the worth and are attained on both sides; the break is a law of the dependence web; and where the need lies above every level of greatest renewal the amount grows linearly with the horizon, so every coupling fixed in total is outweighed by a long enough horizon. That this sharpens SG5's answer to the far-sighted objection, with far-sight eroding every fixed coupling for what else depends on the condition, is the form's own step.**

| Known form | Where | Bearing |
|---|---|---|
| A penalty beyond the value of a constraint makes the constrained optimum an unconstrained one (exact penalty) | Zangwill, "Non-linear Programming via Penalty Functions", Management Science 13 (1967) (from memory) | With a lump-sum coupling of keeping, the threshold is the value gap itself, exact on both sides and attained (`Optimum.frontier_breaks_below`, `Optimum.frontier_keeps_above`, `Optimum.continued_breaking_frontier`, `Optimum.continued_kept_frontier`) |
| Managing a prey's harvest for what its predators need lowers the prey's yield | May, Beddington, Clark, Holt and Laws, "Management of Multispecies Fisheries", Science 205 (1979) (from memory) | Keeping a dependence at its need forgoes, against the single-optimizer yield, `(r − q) n + m` over a horizon of `n + 1` steps (`Optimum.keeping_le_most`, `Optimum.frontier_keeps`) |
| Forage fish left for predators: the yield ecosystem-based management gives up | Pikitch and others, *Little Fish, Big Impact*, Lenfest Forage Fish Task Force (2012) (from memory) | The yield forgone is no fixed sum: it grows with the horizon where the predators' need lies above every level of greatest renewal (`Optimum.forgone_grows`, `Web.frontier_breaks_long`; `Optimum.Holding.logistic_coupled`, forgoing `n + 3` over `n + 2` steps) |
| A side-effect penalty's coefficient trades task reward against impact; too small a coefficient permits the side effect | Krakovna and others, "Penalizing Side Effects Using Stepwise Relative Reachability" (2019); Turner, Hadfield-Menell and Tadepalli, "Conservative Agency via Attainable Utility Preservation", AIES (2020) (from memory) | For a lump-sum coupling of keeping a dependence through the horizon, the coefficient needed is exact and grows with the horizon at the renewal forgone a step; a coupling added step by step is not stated |
