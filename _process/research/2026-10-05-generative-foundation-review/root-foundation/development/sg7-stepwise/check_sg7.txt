## A check: a coupling added for each step a dependence is kept (21 October 2026, SG7)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of what SG6 ([keeping check](#keeping-check)) left open: a coupling added for each step a dependence is kept rather than for the whole keeping. **Verdict: the mathematics is known in each part. That a finite horizon's end changes the last period's choice, and in strategic play unravels cooperation from the last period back, is the end-game effect of finitely repeated play; that an owner with a finite horizon draws the stock down at the end is terminal depletion; that a side-effect penalty may be applied at each step is the stepwise form of impact measures. The spine's form: the rate a coupling a step needs to keep the dependence at every horizon is exact and free of the horizon, the renewal forgone at the need a step, for every renewal law bounded by `r` everywhere and by `q` at or above the need; the thresholds hold for every order rising with the worth and have optima in every case; the end effect is exact, below the need, and stays at the last step wherever the coupling exceeds the renewal forgone a step, nothing unravelling because each met step's coupling is direct; and the break is a law of the dependence web. That this answers SG6's erosion, a coupling sized to a step not being outrun by a longer horizon as one sized to a horizon is, is the form's own step.**

| Known form | Where | Bearing |
|---|---|---|
| In finitely repeated play, cooperation sustained by later play fails at the last period and unravels to the first by backward induction | Luce and Raiffa, *Games and Decisions* (1957); Selten, "The Chain Store Paradox", Theory and Decision 9 (1978) (from memory) | With a coupling a step above the renewal forgone, the end effect stays at the last step (`Optimum.frontier_met_before_end`, `Web.frontier_breaks_last`): each met step's coupling is direct, so nothing unravels |
| An owner with a finite horizon depletes the stock at the end (terminal depletion) | Clark, *Mathematical Bioeconomics* (1976) (from memory) | Below the need, the horizon's last step draws the need itself and every optimum breaks the dependence within the horizon (`Optimum.frontier_breaks_at_end`, `Optimum.continued_ending_stepwise_frontier`) |
| Side-effect penalties applied at each step, for lost reachability or attainable utility | Krakovna and others, "Penalizing Side Effects Using Stepwise Relative Reachability" (2019); Turner, Hadfield-Menell and Tadepalli, "Conservative Agency via Attainable Utility Preservation", AIES (2020) (from memory) | A coupling a step at least the renewal forgone a step and above the need keeps the dependence at every horizon (`Optimum.frontier_keeps_stepwise`, `Web.frontier_keeps_stepwise`), where a coupling of the whole keeping is outweighed (SG6); the rate needed, `r − q`, is exact and free of the horizon |
