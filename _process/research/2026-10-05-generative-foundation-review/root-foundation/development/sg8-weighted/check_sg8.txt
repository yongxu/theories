## A check: an order weighting later draws less (21 October 2026, SG8)

An AI check (one session, 21 October), made while the Lean was written, of what SG5 ([horizon check](#horizon-check)), SG6 and SG7 ([stepwise check](#stepwise-check)) left open: an order weighting later draws less. **Verdict: the mathematics is known. Weighting later utility less is discounting; with a constant rate the level an optimum holds is the modified golden rule's, where the marginal renewal equals the rate; and a sole owner finding extinction optimal where the rate exceeds the greatest rate of renewal is Clark's result on overexploitation. The spine's form: the laws hold over every order rising with the weighted draw and any weighting, one whose rate changes from step to step included, through a reference course whose level gains the most at each step; the threshold for ending the base at once is exact, the tie admitting both ending and holding; and the break is a law of the dependence web. That far-sight keeps the optimizer's own base (SG5) only where its weighting falls slower than the base renews is the form's own step.**
