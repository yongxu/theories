**The question.** SG5 ([study §292](#292-courses-ranked-over-a-horizon-sg5)) and SG8 ([study §295](#295-an-order-weighting-later-draws-less-sg8)) found the optima of a far-sighted order holding a level of greatest (weighted) gain, where the start reaches such a level in one step. Item 10 of §8.1 left open the optimum where it does not: a base already drawn far below. Does a far-sighted optimizer rebuild it, how fast, and does what depends on the base come back with it?

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.Optimum`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *Generalized below SG8* (rule 9). `GainsMost w R k s n h`: at each step to the `n`-th the level `h` leaves gains, weighted, at least what the level of any course open from `s` leaves there does. An open course leaving nothing that gains the most is an optimum of the order by the weighted draw (`Optimum.frontier_of_gainsMost`), and every optimum of every order rising with that draw leaves nothing weighted and gains at each step what it does (`Optimum.frontier_gains_of_most`). A course whose level gains at least what any level does gains the most (`Optimum.gainsMost_of_greatest`), and SG8's `weighted_frontier` and `frontier_gains_weighted` are now this case, their statements unchanged.
- *The rising course.* `rise R y s t` is the level a course from `s` rising as fast as what is given allows, up to `y`, reaches after `t` steps; `rebuilding R y s n` stands there to the `n`-th step and at nothing after. It stands at most at `y`, stays there once there, and draws nothing at a step after which it is below `y` (`rise_le`, `rise_stays`, `rise_of_below`, `rise_below_before`); from a start at most `y`, where the next level rises with the level below `y`, it is open with a capacity covering `y` with its renewal and what is given at nothing (`rebuilding_open`).
- *Nothing open rises faster.* Where `z + R z` rises with `z` below `y`, every course from `s` at most `y` whose steps leave at most the level with what is given stands at each step at or below the rising course, or the rising course stands at `y` (`Optimum.le_rise`).
- *The optimum.* Where at each step to the `n`-th the weighted gain rises with the level up to `y` and is greatest at `y`, the rebuilding course gains the most (`Optimum.rebuilding_gainsMost`) and is an optimum (`Optimum.rebuilding_frontier`). Where the gain rises strictly below `y`, every optimum stands at the rising course's level at every step from the first to the `n`-th where that level is below `y`, and draws nothing at the step before (`Optimum.frontier_rebuilds`); on the web, every dependence on the condition as it stands at the start whose need that level meets by the `j + 1`-th step is restored within `j + 1` steps (`Web.frontier_rebuilds`, through `Web.RestoredWithin` of `Becoming.Restoration`). Where `y` gains more than every other level, every optimum stands at `y` wherever the rising course does (`Optimum.frontier_rebuilt`). With every weight one the premises read, after multiplying by one, as a renewal rising with the level up to `y` and greatest there.
- *Witness* (`Optimum.Holding`, on `Diversion`'s places). Under a law doubling every level up to eight and giving nothing above (`doubling`, `doubling_low`, `doubling_high`, `doubling_grows`), from one, with capacity sixteen, over `n + 3` steps: where `w (t + 1) ≤ w t < 2 w (t + 1)`, the rebuilding course is an optimum, and every optimum draws nothing at the first two steps, stands at two and four, stands at eight at every later step to the horizon's last, and restores the dweller, which needs two, at the first step (`doubling_rises`, `doubling_rises_strictly`, `doubling_top`, `doubling_above`, from `doubling_gain_le`, `doubling_gain_lt`, `twice_mul`, `mul_lt_of_pos`); where `w t = 2 w (t + 1)`, the rebuilding course and the course ending the source at once are both optima (`doubling_outpaced_le`); where `2 w (t + 1) < w t`, every optimum ends the source at once (`doubling_outpaced`, through SG8's `frontier_exhausts`) (`doubling_rebuilds`).

**Against the prior forms** ([significance review, rebuilding check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#rebuilding-check)). The mathematics is known: where the objective is linear in the harvest, the optimal path from a stock below its optimal level is the most rapid approach path, harvesting nothing until the level is reached and then holding it (Spence and Starrett 1975; Clark 1976). The spine's form: the law holds for every order rising with the weighted draw, not one sum, and for any weighting whose gain rises with the level; every optimum, not only some, rebuilds where the gain rises strictly; the reference is any course gaining the most against every open course, so the earlier holding laws are its case; and the restoration is a law of the dependence web, through the theory's notion of a dependence restored. In the witness the strict premise is shown needed: at the tie the course ending the base at once is an optimum.

*Reading* [AI reading, D25, D45]. SG8 bounded far-sight: a weighting falling faster than the base renews ends it. SG9 gives the other side. Where the weighting falls slower, so that holding more of the base gains more, far-sight does more than keep the base where it is: from wherever it was left, it rebuilds it as fast as it can renew, drawing nothing meanwhile, and what depends on the base comes back with it. For §7 this says what a far-sighted optimizer does with damage already done to what it draws on: it repairs it, at the rate the base allows and no faster, and only for its own base and what the base's level happens to carry. A dependence needing more than the level the optimizer's order favours is not restored by this: the rebuilding stops at `y`.

**Self-review.** The laws rest on one comparison, `le_rise`: nothing open rises faster than the rising course while below `y`. Everything else is SG8's comparison through `GainsMost`, which was introduced so that the rising course, whose level at a rebuilding step does not gain the most among all levels but only among those an open course can reach, could serve as the reference; SG8's laws were then re-derived as its case, so the reference course is generalized below rather than duplicated. The premises were checked for necessity. The strict rise of the gain is needed for every optimum to rebuild: at the tie the witness has the course ending at once as an optimum. `s ≤ y` sets the case: where `s` is above `y`, `y` is within one step's reach and SG8's laws apply. That the next level rises with the level below `y` is used for `le_rise`; where it does not (an overcompensating renewal), a course lower now may stand higher later, and the rising course need not be an optimum; this is not stated or shown by a witness. A first form stated only the steps below `y`; that every optimum stands at `y` where `y` gains more than every other level was added, so that in the witness every optimum stands where the rebuilding course does at every step. Limits. The optimum where the next level does not rise with the level, or the gain has several peaks, an order valuing what is left at the horizon, steps met again after a fall, and several optimizers ranking courses remain open. The witness's law and capacity are chosen.
