## 1. What is asked, and what the account already has

The request has three parts:
- the account stated as one, with everything that can follow from the root shown as following from it;
- a map of where a law takes in what the root does not supply: the situation, value, what is perceived and observed, amounts, chance;
- the root carried into judgment made from within a situation.

The account already defines that judgment. 03 §3: "A judgment through the couplings is a common response over the class alike through them." 03 §9 states its law: a policy reading an observation meets the declared conditions exactly where every observation class admits a common admissible action. SJ adds no notion. It states the law once, on the root, and maps what the law takes in.

The root's laws hold for every relation, so they forbid nothing ([DU](../du-deep-unification-review/REVIEW.md)). The law below says what form a response made from a reading must have, and where none can exist. It does not say what the response should be. The requirement is supplied, and fixed before the outcome.

## 2. The finding

**Judgment is viability indexed by acts.** The grammar already has the kernel in all but name. Along a relation's closure, what holds throughout (`«承» («逆» («所闭» E)) P`) is the largest region inside `P` closed under `E`. At a process's steps, this is the viability kernel, `Viability.Kernel`: the largest certificate, choice-free, already counted as reaching the grammar through closure (master laws: market-protected continuation, law 1). A response common over a reading is a family of regions, one per act. Each region lies inside the act's requirement and is closed under the reading. So a common response exists exactly where the acts' kernels cover the situations. With one act this is the kernel law. With an act index it is 03 §3's judgment. SJ1 states the kernel on the grammar once and the covering law beside it. It re-proves Viability's kernel laws from the kernel law (rule 9).

**The same law, stated elsewhere.**
- **Ring, by choice over the readings:** `GuardHorizon.uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible`, with `GuardHorizon.uniform_iff` and `OpenedCorrection.correctable_iff` as one-line applications.
- **Core, the condition rearranged pointwise, the converse choice:** `ReadingLoop.keeping_policy_iff` (a relation from states to worlds), `Viability.keptFor_iff_plan_of_choice`, `ReflexivePrediction.predictor_iff`, `HeldForReading.cell_policy_iff`.
- **Periphery, on `Classical.choice`:** `InquiryChoice.observed_class_policy_iff` (03 §9), `CorrectionInfrastructure.uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible`.
- **One act, the kernel:** `Viability.Kernel`, `Certificate`, `kernel_iff_certificate`, `kernel_certificate`, `Certificate.kernel`, `kernel_largest`.
- **One act per situation, the lumping law:** `Learning.Lumped`, through the image law (`Learning.lumped_iff_fixed_image` from `«像定后当且仅当同行»`). Its finite and sectioned forms are `Becoming.lumped_upto_iff` and `Readout.decodes_iff_of_section`.
- **Its witnesses, at models:** each situation having an act is not enough (`GuardHorizon.statewise_not_uniform`). Pairs sharing an act are not enough (`ReadingLoop.PairwiseShared`, `AssurancePlans.Routes.pairwise_common` and `no_common`, `AssurancePlans.Regulation.no_merged_move`).
- **The need for choice:** `Viability.plans_iff_choice` (03 §9). Asked of every process, assembling one response for each observation is exactly choice over the observations, free only where they are listed.

**What the law takes in.** The law has two parameters, each a kind of input at the edge where acting meets the situation. A third input enters only where a rule fixed in advance is wanted.

| Input | What comes in |
|---|---|
| the sameness `E`, a reading as a relation | perception and observation: what couples in |
| the requirement `G a` | what is acceptable at each situation, from the situation and from value; what can be done there (capacity, executability) |
| choice over the readings | one act fixed for each class, where a rule is wanted |

Value enters the requirement through premises. The continuation condition gives scope (whose situations and losses count), and C gives order (widening what can form is better). Between C's ranking and a requirement stand further choices, each a premise of the practical results where it is used (07 §5): a reading of `living`, the lifting L, and the commitments J1–J4. Making a comparison into a requirement ("undefeated at each situation") has its own calibration, weak excluded middle (`FrontierFrames.choice_exists_iff_weakly`). Weights enter the law only through their support, which says which situations count. Their magnitudes, and the amounts valued, enter a second law, the comparison of policies (`HeldForReading`). Revision is a family of the law's instances indexed along a course (`OpenedCorrection`), or the update of a different law (`ReadingLoop.KeptBy`).

**The root's edge is wider.** Most inputs the account takes in enter laws with no reading and no act:
- the modelling commitments (01 §10; the premise card, 08 §4);
- the carrier and the dynamics, with balance and amounts (the [one-root-to-its-edge](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/one-root-to-its-edge.md) record's distances);
- run existence (dependent choice, 08 §5);
- noise in the dynamics (05 §9).

The map links these and does not restate them. "The edge" keeps U4's sense: where each central declaration stands against the root.

## 4. SJ1: judgment from a reading, on the root (proposed Lean)

**Basis** (the grammar's owner's choice). Situations are occurrences of the grammar's carrier `α`. A reading is a relation `E : «际» α`. The requirement is a family of regions indexed by acts, `G a : α → Prop`, with the act an index outside `«际»`. The one-carrier encoding is used only for law 5, where `«像定后当且仅当同行»` needs it, and a witness shows it is needed there. No separate subject is given: the reading and the situations it reads are relations and occurrences of one carrier. The omission is named: the reading's own formation is not represented here (RF's loop represents it).

**Terms.** One definition, `«共应» E G J`, the grammar's owner's provisional name: a covering family of regions, each inside `G a` and closed under `E` (`«闭»`). The regions may overlap. So a common response is a closed admissible relation from situations to acts, not a rule. Two definitions encode law 5: `«应际»`, the response relation on `S ⊕ A`, and `«读应»`, a map's reading on that carrier. No new residual. What holds at every situation alike is `«承» («逆» E)`.

**Laws** ([scratch/sj1/Becoming/CommonResponse.lean](scratch/sj1/Becoming/CommonResponse.lean), imports `Becoming.GrammarLaws` only):

1. *The kernel.* `«所闭之承最大»`: along the reading's closure, what holds throughout is the largest region inside `P` closed under the reading, for every relation. Its parts are `«所闭之承在内»`, `«所闭之承闭»` and `«闭在内则在所闭之承»`. Along the reading itself, without the closure, `«诸承在内当且仅当自反»` and `«诸承闭当且仅当传递»` hold, each "exactly" quantified over every region. `«闭在内则在承»` needs no hypothesis.
2. *The covering law.* `«共应当且仅当所闭之承遍»`: for every relation and requirement, a common response exists exactly where the acts' kernels cover the situations. There is no choice, decision or hypothesis. For a reflexive and transitive reading it holds along the reading itself (`«自反传递则共应当且仅当承遍»`). Witnesses that the closure is needed: `NotReflexive` (transitive, not reflexive) and `NotTransitive` (reflexive and symmetric, not transitive).
3. *A rule and the readings.* A rule from what a map reads, meeting the requirement, gives a common response with no choice (`«依读而应则共应»`). The converse needs choice over the readings and every reading given by some situation. It is the bridge `GuardHorizon.uniform_policy_iff_common`, where the cases are. `GuardHorizon.ungiven_reading_not_uniform` shows the second hypothesis does work while choice holds. The need for choice is `Viability.plans_iff_choice`, cited. No rule law is added: a rule from a relational reading is `Viability.ChoiceOver` at one relation (REVIEW-2, overlap 2).
4. *Finer readings, none, and all.* `«共应之细»` (no hypothesis); `«静之共应当且仅当遍»`; `«一之共应当且仅当一应遍»` (under `«一»`, on an inhabited carrier, one act meets the requirement everywhere).
5. *The functional case.* `«应际同行当且仅当同读同应»` (no hypothesis), `«遍定应共应当且仅当同读同应»` and `«遍定应共应当且仅当像定后»` (through `«像定后当且仅当同行»`). At an act map it is `«映应共应当且仅当同读同应»`, whose right side is `Learning.Lumped` unfolded. [scratch/sj1-checks/Bridges.lean](scratch/sj1-checks/Bridges.lean) checks the link by `.symm`, so the bridge needs no theorem. Witnesses: `NotCovering` (not total), `NotOne` (not functional), `OneCarrier` (situations and acts on one carrier: the image is determined and no response exists).
6. *Not one at a time, nor pairs.* `EachNotCommon` and `PairsNotCommon`, both through `«一之共应当且仅当一应遍»`.

**Counted as one result.** The content is the kernel on the grammar, the covering law, the two exactness laws, the sum-carrier lumping law, and the witnesses. The rest are lemmas: one-line unfoldings (`«自反则承在内»`, `«传递则承闭»`, `«闭在内则在承»`, `«共应之细»`, `«依读而应则共应»`) and compositions (`«遍定应共应当且仅当像定后»`, `«映应共应当且仅当同读同应»`).
