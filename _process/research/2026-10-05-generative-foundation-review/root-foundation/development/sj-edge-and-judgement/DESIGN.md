## 1. What is asked, and what the account already has

The request has three parts: the account stated as one, with everything that can follow from the root shown as following from it; a map of where a law takes in what the root does not supply (the situation, value, what is perceived and observed, amounts, chance); and the root carried into judgment made from within a situation.

The account already defines that judgment. 03 §3: "A judgment through the couplings is a common response over the class alike through them." 03 §9 states its law: a policy reading an observation meets the declared conditions exactly where every observation class admits a common admissible action. SJ does not add a notion. It states the law once, on the root, and maps what the law takes in.

The root's laws hold for every relation, so they forbid nothing ([DU](../du-deep-unification-review/REVIEW.md)). The law below says what form a response made from a reading must have, and where it cannot exist. What the response should be is not its business. The requirement is supplied and fixed before the outcome.

## 2. The finding, narrowed by the review

**One law, stated in many places.** The law of a common response over a reading's classes is stated or applied in these places:

- **Core, by choice over the readings:** `GuardHorizon.uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible`, with `GuardHorizon.uniform_iff` and `OpenedCorrection.correctable_iff` as one-line applications. These are ring modules.
- **Core, choosing over their own index, where the fiber condition is choice itself:** `ReadingLoop.keeping_policy_iff`, `Viability.keptFor_iff_plan_of_choice` and `ReflexivePrediction.predictor_iff`. `HeldForReading.cell_policy_iff` has a real fiber with weights' support. All are in the core.
- **Periphery, on `Classical.choice`:** `InquiryChoice.observed_class_policy_iff` (03 §9) and `CorrectionInfrastructure.uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible`.
- **Its functional case, one act per situation:** `Learning.Lumped`, already proved from the image law (`Learning.lumped_iff_fixed_image` from `«像定后当且仅当同行»`). Its finite and sectioned forms are `Becoming.lumped_upto_iff` and `Readout.decodes_iff_of_section`.
- **Its calibration:** `Viability.plans_iff_choice` (03 §9). Asked of every process, assembling one response for each observation is exactly choice over the observations, and it is free only where they are listed.

The pure core has no statement of the law that reads no model. The grammar already holds its parts. What holds at every situation alike under a sameness `E`, for an act `a`, is `«承» («逆» E) (G · a)` (`«承»`, GrammarLaws). A response common over `E`'s classes is a family of regions, one per act, each inside the requirement and each closed under `E` (`«闭»`).

**What the law takes in.** The law has three parameters, and each is a kind of input at the edge where acting meets the situation:

| Parameter | What comes in | Where the account states it |
|---|---|---|
| the sameness `E` (or a reading as a relation) | perception and observation: what couples in | 03 §1, where sameness is derived from couplings (`Web.selects_alike_iff`, a World theorem); `Readout.no_restore` (any map) |
| the requirement | (a) what is acceptable at each situation, from the situation and from value; (b) what can be done there: capacity, executability, enabling | (a) a supplied predicate; (b) `OpenedCorrection` keeps it apart, so a correction can open by resources, not reading (`Linked`) |

Value enters the requirement only after two steps. First, scope: the continuation condition says whose situations and losses the requirement ranges over. Second, order: C ranks courses, and a comparison becomes a requirement ("undefeated at each situation"). That second step has its own calibration, weak excluded middle (`FrontierFrames.choice_exists_iff_weakly`, 07 §5). Two more things sit beside the law and are not its parameters. Weights enter the law only through their support, which says which situations count. Their magnitudes, with the amounts valued, enter a second law: the comparison of policies (`HeldForReading`'s worth laws, `after_lt_after_iff`, `finer_least_le`). Revision is a family of the law's instances indexed along a course (`OpenedCorrection`'s `reading : X → S → O`), or the update of a different law (`ReadingLoop.KeptBy`).

**The root's edge is wider.** Most inputs the account takes in enter laws with no reading and no act. These include:
- the modelling commitments (01 §10; the premise card, 08 §4);
- balance and amounts (the [one-root-to-its-edge](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/one-root-to-its-edge.md) record's distances);
- the carrier and the dynamics;
- run existence (dependent choice, 08 §5);
- noise in the dynamics (05 §9).

The map links these and does not restate them. "The edge" keeps U4's sense: where each central declaration stands against the root. The map reads part of that edge from the side of what a response takes in.

## 4. SJ1: judgment from a reading, on the root (proposed Lean)

**Basis** (the grammar's owner's choice). Situations are occurrences of the grammar's carrier `α`; a reading is a relation `E : «际» α`, and the requirement is a family of regions indexed by acts, `G a : α → Prop`, the act an index outside `«际»`. The family states the law with no encoding to undo; the one-carrier encoding is used only for law 5, where `«像定后当且仅当同行»` needs it, and stated there. This generalises ReadingLoop's form from a supplied invariant reading to any relation. No separate subject is given: the reading and the situations it reads are relations and occurrences of one carrier, and the split's omission is named (the reading's own formation is not represented here; RF's loop represents it).

**Terms.** No new residual: what holds at every situation alike under the reading is `«承» («逆» E) (G a)` (the grammar's owner has withdrawn «皆»). A response common over a reading within a requirement is a covering family of regions, one per act, each inside `G a` and closed under the reading (`«闭»`). Whether that structure recurs often enough to earn a name is left to the scratch; if it does, the grammar's owner names it for what it is (not "judgment" and not "licensed"; 03 §9's "admissibility is not permission").

**Laws** (checked in the review's scratch, imports `Init` only, empty axiom reports except two `decide` lemmas, which become constructive proofs):

1. *What holds at every situation alike* lies inside the requirement exactly where the reading is reflexive, is closed exactly where it is transitive, and contains every response closed under the reading inside the requirement, with no hypothesis.
2. *Existence, no hypothesis.* For every relation `E`, a response common over `E` within the requirement exists exactly where every situation has an act in what holds at every situation `«所闭» E` relates (proof through `«闭含所闭»`). Witnesses that the closure is needed: a reading that is not reflexive (empty on one point) and one that is not transitive (the path 0–1–2–3).
3. *A rule* (not in the leaf; at the re-proof site, beside `GuardHorizon.uniform_policy_iff_fiber_feasible`, as the grammar's owner prefers to moving `ChoiceOver` or widening the leaf's imports). With the reading given as a relation from readings to situations, a rule from readings exists exactly where every reading has an act holding throughout what it relates, given `Viability.ChoiceOver`; for readings every situation gives, the condition is law 2's. Witness: with no situation and no act, a reading has no act. Its need is `Viability.plans_iff_choice`, cited; it stays a lemma unless one sentence says what the need says of the unfolding (03 §9's: assembling one response for each possible observation is choosing among observations, free only where they are listed), which is already that calibration's, so here it is a lemma.
4. *Finer readings.* A response common over `E` is one over every `E' ⊆ E`, with no hypothesis (`uniform_finer`'s form). Under `«静»`, a response exists exactly where the requirement is total.
5. *The functional case.* With one act per situation, a response exists exactly where `Learning.Lumped` holds of the reading and the act map. On a carrier holding situations and acts apart, with the reading the kernel of a map, this is `«像定后当且仅当同行»` read as in `Learning.lumped_iff_fixed_image`, extended from `π ∘ next` to any act map. Witnesses show what each hypothesis does: one carrier (`statewise_not_uniform`'s own example, where the image is determined and no response exists); not total; not functional.
6. *Pairs are not enough.* Every two related situations sharing an act does not give a response: `PairwiseShared` read on `«一»`, re-proved, not a new witness.

**Built in scratch** ([scratch/sj1/Becoming/CommonResponse.lean](scratch/sj1/Becoming/CommonResponse.lean), 1 November 2026, `scratch_build.py`: 27 theorems in 40 declarations, every axiom report empty, no warning; imports `Becoming.GrammarLaws` only). Law 1: `«自反则承在内»`, `«承皆在内当且仅当自反»`, `«传递则承闭»`, `«承皆闭当且仅当传递»` (each "exactly" quantified over every region, the fixed-region directions their own lemmas), `«闭在内则在承»`, `«所闭之承在内»`, `«所闭之承闭»`. Law 2: `«共应当且仅当所闭之承遍»`, `«序则共应当且仅当承遍»`. Law 4: `«共应之细»`, `«静之共应当且仅当遍»`. Law 3's forward half, with no choice: `«则之共应»` (a rule gives a common response). Law 5, on a sum carrier: `«应际同行当且仅当同读同应»` (the lumping condition unfolded, no hypothesis), `«定应共应当且仅当同读同应»`, `«定应共应当且仅当像定后»` (through `«像定后当且仅当同行»`).
