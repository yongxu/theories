# How evaluation forms: what is shown, read and supplied

AI-written note, 2 November 2026; reading time about ten minutes. It answers the item in the [cold read of the fronts](../review-packet/COLD-READ-2026-11-02.md): how evaluation forms is defined and read, not derived; either the fronts say that much or the work shows more. Its home is [07 §1](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#what-forms-and-what-is-supplied). No new Lean: every declaration below was already checked, and this note says how they stand to one another. Two independent AI readers tried to break its first version, and most of what follows comes from what they found ([REVIEW-5](REVIEW-5.md)). No person has read it.

## 1. The decision

The first half of the choice, made precise: the fronts should say what is shown and what is not, and the work does not now show more. The promise in "how evaluation forms" was larger than the work, and this withdraws part of it openly. What stays is still something: laws about what evaluation is made of, a reading with no free predicate, and an exact statement of what is brought in.

The word covers three things, and there is a limit on one of them. Laws cover how dependences arise and how rules and codes spread. A reading ties significance to a center. The rest is supplied, and of the free parameter among it a theorem says the represented dynamics cannot fix it. "The one evaluative parameter supplied" is exact for the one free predicate in the statement of significance; the rest of what is evaluative and supplied is listed in §2.

**What the sorting is.** It sorts the account's statements, not what is in the unfolding. *Basis:* each standing is a relation between a statement and its model: proved of it, read onto it, or taken as a hypothesis. *Supplied* means entering as a hypothesis, stated by writers and readers who are themselves formations; nothing comes from outside. *Scope:* what is represented. *Omits:* holding a standard from within, coming to care (which 07 §1 reads as able to recognize a prior reason and to create new ones), any time of holding, and recognition, motivation and compliance. A split of statements cannot show that the things are two.

**What a standard is.** Here, as in the [glossary](../../../../../../GLOSSARY.md), what something is read against, so that it can be met or missed: a requirement on a level, an acceptable set of acts, a task. A selection rule, a code or an order read off choices is one only under a reading [AI reading, new].

The Lean is about thresholds, counts, codes and orders. That the third, fourth and fifth rows concern standards at all is a reading.

**Read.** A center's own region is its least boundary along a relation (`Web.selfValuation_own_least`), which is a law about closures. Taking what lies within that region as what has significance from the center (`selfAscribed (ownClosure rel) center`) is the one form of significance with no free predicate; its relation, its centers and the identification are still brought in. It takes no time of holding: the step in `sig` is the step of the continuation valued, and `ownClosure` ignores it. So what forms, on this reading, is the relation, not the holding.

- *A formal link does run from the first standing to this one, through the relation.* Along feeding, a center's closure reads what requires what (`Web.selfValuation_feeding_own`), so a dependence that forms or lapses moves the tied region, and care bounded by feeding from each step permits ending what feeds no more (`Flow.feedingFrom_permits`). That is a need changing the reach, not a need being a significance.
- *Whether the condition holds, so tied, is settled net by net*: it holds on one net and fails on another (`«续无外随网»`, root, on two constructed nets).
- *Which relation, and which centers.* No law here picks them. `Becoming.Boundaries` says of the relation "not derived here"; 07 §4.2's P2 takes that as a premise; the center is not derived along dependence (`Clearing.center_not_derived`). 07 §4.3 argues, as a reading, that the relation is not free, since a continuation is carried by what feeds it and what it needs.

**Supplied.** The free `sig`, where it is not so tied; the reading and the requirement in the law of a common response; the task in the reading loop (`ReadingLoop.keeps_continues_iff`); the set level in the organism's family (`Becoming.ReaderRebuilding`); the reference valuation and the model in the comparison of answers; and, for the ethics, the condition for scope, C for order, a reading of `living`, L, A, D, E, J1–J4 and the weights.

**The limit, on the free parameter.** Where there is a standpoint and a formation, no satisfiable statement reading only the dynamics excludes a rival or entails the condition (`ReadsOnlyDynamics.admits_rival`, `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, any type). This holds by construction: significance is a parameter nothing else in the representation reads. So it shows non-entailment by the represented dynamics, not that becoming leaves significance undetermined or needs the condition. It does not reach the tied reading, which is fixed once its relation and centers are given.

## 3. What would join them

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. No law says that a standard which formed is a significance, and none could alone. A standard is a threshold, a set of acts or a task; a significance is a predicate on standpoints, steps and formations; calling one the other is an identification, which a law stating it would take as its reading. Three positions stay open.

1. If a standard's reach is what significance is, then, held de se along a relation, the condition is true or false of a net: it holds exactly where every center's closure under that relation is the whole (`ownClosure_unrestricted_iff`) and fails given a point outside (`bounded_is_rival`, of which the tied reading is an instance no Lean declaration states). Its warrant is then no longer the open question. The Lean states this of the significance, not of any standard: nothing yet maps a threshold, rule or code to a relation and a center.
2. If it is not, a standard's reach counts neither for nor against the condition.
3. If acting presupposes significance (07 §4.2's P1), the question is the de se one that section leaves open. [Route (b)](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#route-b) (07 §3.3) is the nearest thing the chapter has to a join: with no condition, "should" is what a center does once its represented boundary matches what it depends on, which gives a reason of its own, without warranting it.

No theorem here chooses among them. Whether standards that actually form are narrower than the condition is empirical. The selection laws say which carriers spread, not what a standard is about, and the share of a rule's shapers is not bounded by what comes back when shaping costs nothing (`RuleShaping.c_zero_favours`).

This leaves both premises' warrant open: the continuation condition for scope, C for order.

## 4. The sentences for the fronts

> Under stated conditions, laws show where a new dependence comes from, how the shapers of a rule gain share, how a code comes to be shared, and what a response within a requirement takes. They do not show that a standard which formed is a significance, or that one course is better than another. Significance is either a free parameter, which by construction no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics can fix, or read as what lies within a center's closure along a relation no law here picks. Scope is the continuation condition and order is C, both premises.

The openings should change, not carry a caveat: "of how life and knowing take shape within it, and how far evaluation does". Narrowing *evaluation* to *standards* in the opening would promise again what §2 does not show. The same holds for "Positions and standards form" in the overview. 07 §1's title and first sentence keep their wording because records cite that section; the home block says how much the title covers.

## 5. Where the work could show more

None of these is needed for the fronts to be accurate, and none is attempted here.

- **A standard generated by dynamics that supply none.** No record states a law in which a requirement, rule, task or goal is generated by dynamics that supply none. `requires` is a field of `Web`, and the laws on forming anew classify its values under a hypothesis on where it comes from. Where a goal or code goes from none to one, the source, feedback law or goal type is supplied (`GoalFormationRevision.experience`, off the Becoming spine, record `formed-goal-revision-continuation`; `CodeAcquisition`). Agreement goes from none to one only among codes already held. The search was by the records' statements, so this is "none found", not "none". The candidate is self-maintenance: a formation whose going on depends on its own activity, its set level formed where `ReaderRebuilding` supplies it.
- **The map from standards to relations and centers.** Which kinds of standard yield a relation and a center, so that §3's first position is a statement about standards. A cheap witness would test it: carriers selected by what comes back whose rule's reach is the whole.
- **A time of holding,** without which "significance forms" cannot be stated directly: a standpoint indexed by when it holds, as pairs could encode. A search of three modules found no law doing this, so this is limited evidence.
- **Which relation a center draws along.** 07 §4.2 to §4.5 hold the material.
- **The free parameter.** Not available from the dynamics, by the limit in §2, unless something else in the representation reads significance, and that would be a new premise, stated as one. The tied reading already is significance from the dynamics, given a relation, centers and an identification. What no law gives is the identification.
- **Not by proof.** P1 and the de se question are open in 07, and argument, not Lean, is their route.
