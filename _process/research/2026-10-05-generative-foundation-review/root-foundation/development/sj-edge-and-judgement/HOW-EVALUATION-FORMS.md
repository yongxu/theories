## 1. The decision

Neither half of the choice is right as put. The word *evaluation* in "how evaluation forms" covers four things, and they have four standings. The work shows laws for the first, reads the second, is given the third, and proves of the fourth that the represented dynamics do not reach it. So the fronts should neither promise the formation of evaluation whole nor say that it is only defined. They should say which part is which, in the sentence of §4.

The cold reader's phrase, "the one evaluative parameter supplied", is exact and stays. What it does not say is how much of what people mean by evaluation lies outside that parameter.

## 3. What joins them, and what does not

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. No law joins the first standing to the second. Nothing proved says that a standard which formed is thereby a significance, and that step is where the word *evaluation* does its work. What can be said exactly is how the two would stand if joined. A standard held de se along a relation is the continuation condition exactly where every center's closure under that relation is the whole (`ownClosure_unrestricted_iff`); where something lies outside, it is a rival (`rival_of_outside`). Standards that form by what comes back are bounded by what comes back. So, read this way, what forms in the unfolding is in general a rival of the condition, not the condition. This is one reason the condition cannot be offered as a description of how evaluation forms: it asks more than what forms, and that is why it is a premise. The clearer's witness at 07 §1, with its significance bounded in time, shows the difference in one world.

This reading does not settle the premises' warrant. It says only where the difficulty lies: not in showing that standards form, which the laws above do in part, but in what would make one standard's reach better than another's.

## 4. The sentence for the fronts

> Under stated conditions, laws show how needs form, how standards are shaped and gain influence, and what a response within a standard takes; that any of it has significance they do not show: significance is supplied, where no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics fixes it, or read from a center's closure along a relation no law picks.

The openings that promised "how life, knowing and evaluation form" keep their scope only with this sentence near them, or with *evaluation* narrowed to *standards*. The sentence says *shaped*, not *form*, for standards, because of the first item of §5, and confines the last clause to the supplied parameter, as 07 §1 does (from an independent read against 07).

## 5. Could the work show more?

Three things could be shown, each with its limit.

- **A standard forming from none.** Each law in §2 starts from something standard-like already there: a carried capacity to shape, a rule, a task, a hearing, a threshold. A search of the records by what they say found no law whose start has no standard and whose end has one, and the one constructed goal formation (`GoalFormationRevision`, in the history) states its practices, feedback and response law as explicit local premises. A law of that kind would be new work on the Becoming spine, with a claim row, and would strengthen the first standing. The search was by the records' statements, so this is "none found", not "none".
- **Which relation a center draws along.** A law picking it from what the center's going on takes would turn part of the second standing into the first. The nearest material is what requirement and need fix above. Not attempted here.
- **Significance from dynamics alone.** Not available in this representation, by the theorems in §2, unless the representation changes so that something else reads significance. Such a change would be a new premise, and would have to be stated as one.

None of these is needed for the fronts to be accurate. The sentence in §4 stands whichever of the first two is later shown; only its clause on the relation would narrow.
