## 1. Method: which claims map, and how a mapping corrects the map

**Kinds of claim.** The repository already has a tested classification, 06 §二's eight classes, run over 4,116 units with a Lean pipeline behind two of them (`GrammarCandidates`, `GrammarConditionals`). Opus's five kinds (structure, dynamics, evaluation, ground, practical wisdom) are a coarser cut of the same thing; I would not introduce a second vocabulary beside one that has been exercised. Map them and add one split:

| Class (06) | What the claim says | What a mapping can produce |
|---|---|---|
| 甲 definition | a term fixed by others (「X，Y也」; Engels's "metaphysical" vs "dialectical" thinking) | a definition in the grammar or a reading; no claim |
| 乙 grammar theorem | holds of every relation (有无相生; "the two sides of a contradiction depend on each other") | a root theorem, a theorem under a logical principle (as 有无相生 under ¬¬Q → Q), or a countermodel |
| 丙一 law under Z | holds where a structural premise holds (穷则变; "quantity passes into quality") | the Z stated, the law "Y exactly where Z" or "Z then Y", the web where Z fails; the source's universal form becomes 定域 |
| 丙二 model law | holds in a kind of model (counts, whole numbers) | the model named; the correspondence obligation |
| 丁 evaluation | ends in good, better, higher, progress | recorded and counted, never a law; where the evaluation rides on a mechanism, the mechanism is mapped and the evaluation marked as the source's, with the reading (C or another order) it would need |
| 戊 divergence | a non-relational layer, or a direction asserted unconditionally (06 §六's four layers: a prior ground, a fixed nature or value, a will or single ruler, contest and rank) | the layer named, the same tradition's internal answer beside it, the spine's witness that the representation needs no such layer |
| 己 contrary reading | the source argues against the account's form | kept; answered or left open |
| 庚 narrative | argument scaffolding, examples | counted; its content reclassified |

The split the pilot forces is inside 丙一 and 丁: **向** (which way a course goes: a rise reverses, a level locks, a pattern goes on) against **愈** (that where it goes is better). The map states 向 only as a scoped law and 愈 only as a reading named where used (07 §1, §4). Dialectics habitually fuses them: "development", "higher stage", "the negation of the negation" each carry both. Splitting is the first operation on every dialectical claim, and the fusion is itself a finding: it is where the map says the tradition asserted an order as a fact.

Opus's "practical wisdom" kind (situation → dynamic → advice → where it fails) is not a kind of claim but a kind of use, and the joint reading already built its home: situations, views and bindings (§2.3). A tradition's advice is a binding of a named situation to a dynamic and an exit; it belongs in that layer, not in the correspondence file.

**What separates a mapping from a word match.** Three tests, each refusable by the checker:

1. *Deletion.* Replace the source's word by the mechanism the entry claims for it, in the map's terms (what reads what, what is drawn, which class separates). If the correspondence disappears, it was a word match. 06 §7.1's rule, 同语不等于同指，也不等于相承, is the principle; this makes it an operation.
2. *A forbidden case.* Every entry with a relation names what the mapped law forbids that the source leaves open, or what the source states that the law does not yet (the crosswalk's `account_adds` and `source_adds`, which exist and are often empty). An entry with neither is a reading, labelled `agreement_of: reading`, not a law.
3. *The countermodel.* Every 定域 names the web where the source's universal form fails (Z fails, Y fails), as 06 §四's table does column by column, and says whether the source itself admits the scope (06 §六 shows every non-relational layer answered from inside its own tradition). A 定域 that cannot name its countermodel is not a correction of the source; it is the map's format.

And astra's point from the joint reading, kept as a validator rule: agreement does not compose. Two traditions each 相合 with one theorem are not shown to agree with each other.

**How the residue changes the map.** Three routes, each already practised once, each to be recorded in the data rather than in prose:

- *A claim the map does not give, that can be stated.* It becomes a candidate law with its Z, proved or refuted in a leaf module, landing as a record whose home section the chapter gains. 06 §7.2 did this 62 times from the 中华脉络's gaps ("古文所问 / 所得之律"). The entry that asked for it is re-pointed from `fresh-reading` or `not-carried` to a carrying target, and the record gains `asked_by: [cx-id]`. That back-link is the one new field records need; without it the reader cannot see where a tradition moved the map.
- *A world claim.* It becomes a `forbids` on the dynamic it bears on, and a test with state `named` (a new state beside designed/frozen/run/refused, since new designs are held until the first readers report).
- *A mistake in the map.* Most often the prose saying more than the Lean, which 08 §8 lists four of and WB found more; a tradition read closely is a reader of the map's prose. The correction goes to the home, then the restatement scan (the main_drift pattern), and the entry records `corrects: map-prose` with the commit.

On the entry: `moved_the_map: {kind: law | forbids | correction | blank, to: <record, dynamic, board row or commit>, date}`, and the mirror `scoped_the_source: true` where the relation is 定域 with a named countermodel. A generated page per tradition then has two columns, 「所问 → 所得」 and 「所陈全称 → 所定之域」, and a third, 「留白」, the blanks the tradition named that nothing yet answers. If a pilot's first column is empty, the pilot has failed its first purpose, and that is reported, not hidden under a full second column.

**What should not be mapped, and how that is kept.** Quantitative claims with no counterpart (the modern-philosophy file already declines Marx's reproduction schemes for this reason); claims about particular historical formations (capitalism, 1848) as laws, which map only as forms with the particular said to be unmapped; a tradition's ground (matter primary, Geist, 理气), which is 戊 and gets the four-layer treatment, never a target; practice and cultivation (06 §四 lists 修、工夫 among the inexpressible); evaluations as laws. Kept by the group's `not_mapped` list with reasons (it exists), by `basis: ai-knowledge` entries carrying no verified quote, and by the ledger's third column, so a declined mapping is visible as declined rather than absent. And one thing is never the purpose: validation. 相合不是印证 holds in both directions; a tradition agreeing with a theorem adds nothing to the theorem, and the theorem adds nothing to the tradition's standing.

Where I think the brief's framing has a hole: "correcting the map itself" will, in the honest case, be rare. Most of what a tradition gives is scope the other way, and the next most is a named blank. The success measure should count blanks named and overclaims removed, not only laws added; otherwise the pilot will be pushed toward finding laws.

## 2. Signifiers: many per node

**Distinguish three kinds; the argument.** A coined name claims only its definition: it is anchored to a Lean declaration or a dynamic and means exactly that (08 §10, the 名表's rules). A tradition's term carries two thousand years of its own senses, and the entry's first duty is 原文义 before the structural reading (06 §7.1). A statement is a sentence with a standing. Folding terms into names is already happening and already misleading: `dynamics.json` has six `zh` names, and 无明 is a Buddhist term read onto `locked-seeking` with a sense 06 §十一 explicitly distinguishes from the 起信论's; 元吸引子 is a modern coinage; 消息之律 is a 文言 name taken from the 易; 偏蔽，复通 is a 文言 phrase. One key, three kinds, no provenance. The two-name-systems note (06 §三) is the same lesson one level down: 窮、通、復 mean one thing in the 名表 and another in the grammar's «» names, resolved by a rule ("«» takes the grammar's sense"), which only works because the two systems are told apart.

**The structure.** Nothing new is invented for terms: a tradition's term pointing at a node with a relation *is* a correspondence entry. The signifier layer is mostly generated from what exists, plus one typed field.

```
dynamics.json / a record            (the node: declarations + record are the signified)
  names: [                          立名, coined by the map
    {text:"相待", lang:"lzh", script:"Hant", simplified:"相待", pinyin:"xiāngdài",
     anchor:"Becoming.«能所相待»", from:{quote:"長短相較，高下相傾", locator:"老子 2", era:"先秦"} | "new-combination",
     not_carried_senses:["中论之待（待缘而成，空）"], coined_by:{who:"AI (model, date)"}, status:"named"},
    {text:"correlatives", lang:"en", anchor:"...", ...},
    {text:"相待（两端）", lang:"zh-Hans", ...}
  ]
  | names: {status:"未立名", reason:"..."}    an explicit state, displayed as such
  statements: [                     經/說/證 per law, at the record
    {kind:"經", lang:"lzh", text:"能有，則所有；所有，則能有。", checked_against:"Becoming.«能所相待»", fidelity:"coded|uncoded"},
    {kind:"說", lang:"zh-Hans", text:"..."}, {kind:"說", lang:"en", text:"..."},
    {kind:"證", declarations:[...], record:"..."}
  ]

correspondences.json entry           傳統之名, one typed field added
  term: {text:"矛盾", lang:"zh-Hans", script:"Hans", translit:null, source_lang:"zh"} | null
  (relation, targets[] with role/part, source.basis, sense, source_adds, account_adds: as today)
  provenance: {who:"AI (model, date)" | "person (initials, date)", text_read:"read-in-repo"|"ai-knowledge"}

generated: signifiers index
  by term  → nodes it points at, each with relation and part        (one word, several nodes)
  by node  → its names (all languages) + terms pointing at it       (one node, many words)
```

Many-to-many falls out: 矛盾 → `«能所相待»` (相合, the two ends of one relation), `«有相则非亦有相»` (相合, a mark and its complement), `relative-position-is-zero-sum` (定域, strict opposition only for position), `two-sided-regulation` (定域, opposing exchanges settle or oscillate), and 争 (not expressible: 06 §四, "二方之胜，其 Y 会排出结局之序"). That is why 矛盾 gets no name: the map has five nodes where the tradition has one word, and a coined 矛盾 would be a word match built into the data.

**Provenance.** Who wrote a name or a reading (model and date, or a person's initials; no names on fronts, per the standing rule), and for a term whether the text was read (`read-in-repo`, 855 entries today) or recalled (`ai-knowledge`, 328). Quotes only verbatim from a stored text, checked by script, as the crosswalk brief already requires; a `lzh` 經 is itself a claim about the Lean and gets a `fidelity` mark, since 06 §7.3's coding exists for exactly this (the 經 introduced fewer referents than English prose, 0.09 against 0.81; under one discipline controlled English matched it under one coder; so the gain is discipline and genre, not 文言 as such, and a 經 is not trusted for being 文言).

**Where it fits.** `names` replaces `name` on dynamics (today's `name.en` becomes the `en` name; the six `zh` are reclassified: 无明 to a term entry, the rest to `lzh` or `zh-Hans` names with provenance). `statements` belong at the record, which is the law's home, not the dynamic's, since a dynamic bundles several laws with different premises; the atlas copies. `term` and `provenance.who` go on correspondences. The crosswalk brief's `basis` and `reads` stay. All of this touches AA's files, so it is a proposal to AA with a TM-owned prototype (`signifiers.json` and a generated index page in TM's folder) that AA can merge or refuse.

**Coining a 文言 name well.** The 中华本's six rules carry over as they stand: one concept one name, one name one concept; structural words only, none social, financial or of rank; anchored to a current Lean declaration; a frozen classical quotation where one exists, 先秦 first; the senses not carried written out; coined in 繁体, with no collision after simplification. To them, from the two-name-systems note and the eleven 虚词 conventions, three more. A name is given at the node, not at a theorem with four premises (`lock_outlives_loss` is not named; 元吸引子 is). The 虚词 are reserved for statements and never enter a name (則、故、若、必、或、皆、莫、莫不、屡、不、未 each fix a logical structure in the 凡例; a name containing one would assert a structure). And where the grammar already uses the character in another sense, the 名表 yields (the existing rule). When no name is coined: where the nearest word is among the 37 不采之字 (道、气、天、心、性、仁, each carrying a layer the method excludes); where 06 §九 says no single character is exact (耦合入、归并、拓宽、关口 are two-character words by necessity); where the concept is a count or a technicality (`(B+1)^n` has no 文言 name and needs none); and where the pilot's own word fuses nodes (矛盾, 发展). 未立名 is then written, with its reason, and displayed as a blank, not filled by translation.

**Is 文言 first a good choice?** For 立名, yes, and the repository gives the reasons: the load-bearing words are two-place relation words (待、资、与、取、容), the 「X，Y也」 definition form exists, and sense drift is traceable in texts. For defining, no: the definition is the Lean, and 經 is a statement of it, 說 an explanation, each with a fidelity obligation. For display, no: a reader sees names in their language, and showing 相待 first to an English reader, or to a Chinese reader who knows the 中论, invites the reverse word match (importing Madhyamaka into a root theorem about the two ends of a relation). Opus read Ren's "首选" as naming, not display; I agree and would write it into the schema (`names` is a set with no display order; the page chooses by the reader's language). On "然后再其他语言与文化": for coined names this order is fine and nearly empty (the map will coin few names in Sanskrit or German). For terms there is no order: pratītyasamutpāda, enantiodromia and Aufhebung arrive as traditions' terms with their relation and provenance, and a Sanskrit term that reaches a node a 文言 name does not fit is not behind anything. So the order applies to 立名 only.

**Language codes.** BCP-47: `lzh` (Literary Chinese, ISO 639-3) with `script: Hant` for 文言 names and 經; `zh-Hans` and `zh-Hant` for modern Chinese; `en`; `sa`, `pi`, `grc`, `la`, `de`, `ar`, `bo`, `ja` as terms need them. `pinyin` for `lzh`/`zh`; `translit` for the rest. Today's bare `zh` is retired, since it is exactly the key that cannot tell 文言 from modern Chinese.

**When does an extreme reverse, lock, oscillate?** The map has one model where this is a question and not a slogan: the regime, one condition under a standing draw `H`, giving `g` a law of what is left, in whole numbers, `step g H x = (x − H) + g (x − H)`. What is proved: where `g` rises with what is left, the step keeps the order of levels (`RegimeMargin.step_mono`) and every run within a band rests (`rests_within`); where giving falls as what is left rises, a run can cycle for ever (`Alternation.alternates`, a witness); below the point of no return the condition ends and the point is exact (`ends_iff_below`). The record of where a run rests already names the neighbours: compensatory renewal settles, overcompensating renewal cycles (Beverton and Holt 1957; May 1976). So the candidate is a restatement in whole numbers with exact premises, not new mathematics, and it answers the question in this model exactly.

*Candidate R1 (no reversal under compensation).* Premises: `∀ a b, a ≤ b → g a ≤ g b`. Statement: for every `H` and `x`, either the run from `x` never falls (`∀ n, orbit n ≤ orbit (n+1)`) or it never rises; the disjunction is decided by `x ≤ step g H x`, so it is constructive. Proof: `step_mono` with `Lasting.orbit_mono`, the two lines `rests_within` already uses. Contrapositive, the form that matters: *every reversal of a regime's run (a rise followed by a fall, or the mirror) exhibits two levels `a < b` with `g (a − H) > g (b − H)`*: giving that falls as what is left rises. So 物极必反 in this model is overcompensation and nothing else; under compensatory giving an extreme never turns, it locks (rests at a level the step leaves as it is, or ends). It generalizes `rests_within` by dropping the band and sits below `Alternation`, which becomes its necessary-condition witness. It forbids: a reversal in any system meeting the regime's premises whose giving rises with the level.

*Candidate R2 (period at most two under strong overcompensation).* Premises: the step itself falls with the level on the band (`∀ a b, a ≤ b → b ≤ hi → step g H b ≤ step g H a`) and `Keeps g H hi`, `x ≤ hi`. Statement: `∃ N ≤ 2 hi + 2, ∀ n ≥ N, orbit (n + 2) = orbit n`: the run rests or alternates between two levels for ever. Proof: `step ∘ step` is monotone, so the even and odd subsequences are each monotone and bounded in ℕ, hence still within `hi` steps (the `LastChange.still_within` pattern). Known for decreasing maps; here constructive and with the bound. Note the premise is on the step, not on `g`: with `g` merely falling, `(x − H) + g (x − H)` is unimodal, and R2 does not apply.

*Candidate R3 (longer cycles need a step neither rising nor falling): a witness.* `H = 1`, `g 0 = 3`, `g 1 = g 2 = 0`, band `hi = 3`: the run from 1 goes 1 → 3 → 2 → 1, period three; `g` is not monotone, `step` is not antitone, and `Keeps` holds. With R0, the pigeonhole already stated for webs (`Coupled.Indexed.ends_iff_within`: a run within `B` repeats within `(B+1)^n` steps), every run on a band is eventually periodic with period at most `hi + 1`, so the trichotomy is complete in this model: period one is a lock (rest, or the end at nothing), period two is reversal at every step, longer periods are oscillation proper, and which of the three a run enters is read off how giving moves with what is left.

What this leaves open, honestly: coupled regimes, where support beyond a band sustains a cycle (`Coupled`, witness) and the two-dimensional analogue is Hirsch and Smith's eventual monotonicity, not stated here; noise, where the exit from a margin is a Kolmogorov bound and the drawn chain leaves locks by drift; real amounts; and hysteresis. On hysteresis the map has more than the brief assumes: in the plane of level and recent giving the point of no return is a falling curve, exact for each recent giving and not rising as the recent giving rises (`RegimeMargin.Adjusted.falls_iff_below`), and `Crossing.crosses` is the witness that what was given carries a condition across the regime's point. That is hysteresis stated: the threshold depends on the direction of approach. What is absent is the loop under a parameter moved and moved back, which is CY (b). All of R0–R3 and the hysteresis note belong to CY, AA's planned item, as its design seed; TM should not claim the Lean without AA's word.

**Quantity into quality**, stated: in whole numbers, for a fate `P` on levels, exact-checkable and down-closed, there is a least level separating the fates (`leading_exact`); the source's "every quantitative change reaches a node" is false where the fate is not down-closed (`Notch.no_threshold`). A frozen test is nameable and not designable now: Ricker-type stocks (overcompensating) against Beverton–Holt-type stocks (compensating) differing in whether harvested levels oscillate about the point, which is R1's forbidden case in the field.

**The negation of the negation**, stated: the return of a pattern through renewal of its parts is `«化»` read at two grains, and 06 §三's 「因灭而续」 (`«因灭而续»`) is the same structure in the 中论's words: `x` ends under identity, `y` begins, and one relation is continuation under the coarse class, all three at once, each relative to its class. ## 4. Alignment, briefly

The map is first for a selection: what it reaches, what it drains beyond what it reads, how it locks. The pilot lands on all three.

- *Reach.* 外因通过内因 has the form of `Unread`: what lies beyond the share moves the loop only where it couples in. The dialectical hope that a captured selection will reverse itself from its own contradictions is, under the map's premises, false in a precise way: `ReadsShare` (the rule read monotonically from the share) is the compensatory case, R1's premise, so the course does not reverse; it locks and outlives the loss (`lock_outlives_loss`). Reversal in the regime needs giving that falls with the level; in the capture loop nothing plays that role unless a reader beyond the share enters (`turned_by_loss`), in time (`ReadInTime`). That is the joint selection's second condition said in the pilot's words: the exit is not dialectical, it is a reading.
- *Drain.* Quantity into quality is the critical mass and the window: the joint selection of humans and AI must read a loss before the share passes the step from which the late reading would favour the shapers; after it, every reading of that form keeps the lock, and at most one step lies between.
- *Lock.* The negation of the negation as a selection that removes its own readers (07 §4.12, `RemovesItsReaders`, `survivorsPersist_of_settled`): read off the course, selection by persistence registers no removal. The return needs openness, a reader beyond the pool, which is the first condition of the joint selection.
- *Words.* The signifier layer is itself alignment work. Names are a code formed among the map's users (05, `a-code-formed-between-formations`, `synonyms-and-coined-forms`); a word match between 矛盾 and 相待 would let a Chinese reader and an English reader believe they agree on a law when they agree on a word. Keeping terms apart from names, with relations, is what keeps agreement in structure from passing as agreement in meaning, between humans, and between humans and AI reading the same map.
