**1. Method: map claims together with what could correct their mapping**

I agree with Ren’s priority. The map becomes useful when a source can expose a distinction it erased, a premise it silently supplied, or a mechanism it cannot yet explain.

There is already an excellent precedent. The earlier definition of 通 captured continuation but lost “往来”; the revised reading requires continuation along both the relation and its converse. The counterexample that continues forward from a bounded beginning remains. This changed the articulation itself. It did not merely add a correspondence. See [06, `tong-and-xi`](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#tong-and-xi).

I would modify Opus’s five kinds. Structure, dynamics, evaluation, ground and practical wisdom are useful shelves, but they mix *subject matter* with *what an utterance does*. Practical wisdom can contain a causal assertion, a recommendation and a constitutive claim in one sentence. A structural assertion can be empirical.

Use two independent classifications:

| What the claim does | What mapping can produce |
|---|---|
| Defines or distinguishes | A definition, explicit interpretation, competing definitions, or evidence that the proposed translation collapses a distinction. A definition itself proves nothing about its instances. |
| Asserts a structural or constitutive connection | A theorem under an explicit interpretation; a conditional theorem; a countermodel to that interpretation; or an argued connection that the current representation cannot assess. |
| Explains or predicts a course | A scoped model law, a mechanism with application obligations, an empirical conjecture and its possible refuter, or a named missing mechanism. |
| Evaluates, recommends or trains attention | A reading formed within becoming: who evaluates, what is noticed, what is counted, what is ranked, which action is proposed and who bears its consequences. Its descriptive claims can be investigated separately. |
| Makes an ontological or methodological commitment | An argument, its strongest rival, what explanation it admits or excludes, and a condition of revision. It need not manufacture an observational prediction. |
| Presents experience, practice, narrative or expressive form | A situated description and selected correspondences, with the practice or work retained beyond them. Some content properly remains unmapped. |

Alongside this, record its **subject**, quantifiers, temporal form and force: existence, necessity, possibility, tendency, explanation, instruction, retrospective interpretation. “Can reverse,” “eventually reverses” and “reverses repeatedly” require different entries.

The unit of work should be a **claim within an argument**, not an isolated sentence. Preserve the passage, the problem it addresses, its interlocutor, and the claims it depends on. This matters particularly when a source states a position in order to refute it. The OU2 outcomes already expose such cases.

A correspondence worth having needs five things:

1. **An independently written source sense.** Explain the claim without the map’s vocabulary first.
2. **A translation of roles and relations.** Which source terms become which objects, predicates, changes and observations?
3. **The exact consequence preserved.** What follows in the source’s argument, and what follows after translation?
4. **A discriminating failure.** What case would defeat the correspondence, rather than merely require a more ingenious gloss?
5. **A residue with a destination.** What remains unrepresented, and what work could answer it?

This develops [08’s five-step test](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#five-step-test). In particular, toggling predicates in a weak model establishes non-entailment there. It does not establish that a richer account permits that toggle.

Two corrections to the proposed method follow.

First, “a premise not yet found, a world claim to test, or a mistake” is incomplete. Other possibilities are:

- a missing kind of relation or state;
- an inadequate observation or grain;
- a mistranslated claim;
- several incompatible but defensible readings;
- a question the map does not seek to answer.

Second, a conditional theorem does not necessarily give **exactly** where a source’s universal claim holds. It may supply only a sufficient condition. Retain the crosswalk’s `agreement`, `scope`, `divergence` and `contrary-reading`, but add a separate logical-strength field: `equivalence`, `sufficient`, `necessary`, `witness`, `non-entailment`, or `incompatible-under-premises`.

A countermodel corrects a source’s **unrestricted formal reading** immediately. Correcting a claim about actual societies or organisms additionally requires showing that the countermodel respects the source’s intended domain. Otherwise the map has corrected its translation, not the tradition.

I propose a revision record attached to each consequential residue:

```text
source claim and edition
mapping version and interpretation
what the source distinguishes that the map loses
strongest competing reading
counterexample or other reason for revision
affected definition / model / law / application / wording
proposed change
disposition and reason
before and after references
result, proof receipt or empirical evidence, when available
remaining residue
```

Dispositions should include `definition-revised`, `model-extended`, `law-added`, `source-reading-narrowed`, `mapping-rejected`, `outside-scope` and `still-open`. “Resolved” must say what was resolved. A new theorem answering a reconstructed question need not exhaust the original passage.

This would make two histories visible: **where traditions changed the map**, and **where the map limited a proposed universal reading**. Section 06’s [record of classical gaps](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#zh-gaps) already supplies material for the former.

Keep word matches, disputed translations and unsuitable material in an exclusion ledger with reasons and source references. Do not delete them or count them as successful correspondences. Nor should absence of a correspondence imply deficiency: a ritual, poem or account of suffering need not earn its place by becoming a theorem.

**2. Signifiers: distinguish expressions, senses, claims and their bindings**

Ren is right that one assertion can have several expressions. I would qualify the proposed account of “a node, the signified, anchored by Lean.” A node is a revisable articulation. A theorem anchors one formal claim; it does not settle the full referent of a tradition’s term.

The existing data already supports the beginning of this design. I counted 177 dynamics with English names and six with `zh` names. The difficulty is not the language-keyed dictionary itself. It is that one string per language cannot represent several names, competing senses, authorship or the history of a translation.

Keep three kinds of expression:

| Kind | What it binds to | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|
| **Map name — 立名** | A specific concept, dynamic or claim | A convention adopted by this map, with a naming system and version |
| **Source term — 原文之名** | A sense in a particular work or passage | Connected to map targets through correspondences, not automatically installed as their synonym |
| **Statement — 陳辭** | A versioned claim | A formulation whose scope and hypotheses can be compared with other formulations |

經、說、證 are then presentation roles, not three interchangeable translations. 經 states concisely; 說 explains the interpretation, premises and failures; 證 links the exact formal statement and its evidence. An argued reading has no fabricated 證: its panel should say where the argument stands.

A small companion expression store would suffice:

```json
{
  "id": "expr-correlatives-lzh-01",
  "kind": "map-name",
  "text": "兩端相待",
  "language": "lzh-Hant",
  "register": "controlled-classical",
  "naming_system": "becoming-grammar",
  "status": "proposed",
  "bindings": [{
    "target_kind": "claim",
    "record": "candidate-theorems-of-the-corpus",
    "part": "Becoming.«能所相待»",
    "relation": "names"
  }],
  "authorship": {
    "kind": "AI",
    "author": "astra",
    "operation": "coinage"
  },
  "source_basis": "new-combination",
  "excluded_senses": ["mutual causal production"]
}
```

This is a schema illustration, not a naming decision.

Source expressions additionally need a source-sense identifier, edition, locator, original wording, and `basis`: text read in the repository, external text actually read, secondary account, or AI memory. A quotation requires checked text; AI memory gets a paraphrase and `quote: null`. Separate **source authorship** from **who wrote the mapping**.

Statements need `claim_ref`, `claim_version`, author, language, presentation role, and a review of whether the formulation preserves the claim’s hypotheses and quantifiers. A shorter 經 that omits a premise is an abbreviation with an explicit expansion, not an equivalent statement.

Many-to-many belongs in the binding records:

- One written form can have several sense IDs.
- One sense can have partial correspondences to several nodes.
- One node can have several expressions in the same language.
- Disputed readings remain separate edges.
- Textual descent and translation are distinct from structural agreement.

A particularly useful collision is **相待** itself. In 06’s grammar family it concerns the two ends of one relation. In the historical 中华本, fourteen·two names `Flow.MutuallyDependent`: every member lies in every member’s influence closure. These are different structures. They must not become identical because their names match. The broader warning is already explicit in [06, `two-name-systems`](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#two-name-systems).

For integration:

- Preserve `dynamics.json`’s `name` dictionary during migration as the compatibility display field.
- Add references to expressions and preferred-expression IDs.
- Keep the crosswalk’s `sense`, `reading`, `source_adds`, `account_adds`, target roles and provenance.
- Give substantive residues stable IDs and links to revision records.
- Let AA choose the eventual file division and generate the display fields and pages.
- Derive proof standing from result records; naming or translation must never upgrade it.

The old “one concept, one name; one name, one concept” discipline should become **one preferred name per sense, language and naming system**. It remains valuable for controlled writing without forbidding aliases or pretending historical vocabulary is univocal.

For 文言 coinage, I would follow this order:

1. Fix the referent and scope before choosing the word.
2. Check both current grammar usage and the 中华本’s historical 名表.
3. Prefer an existing suitable expression; otherwise disclose a new combination.
4. Give the sense taken and the senses excluded.
5. Check any claimed historical example against its stored passage.
6. Write the corresponding 經 and expand it into 說; inspect whether the elegant name concealed a hypothesis.
7. Check collisions in both scripts and in ordinary syntax.

The eleven function-word conventions should remain explicit: 若 introduces a condition; 則 states implication; 故 additionally commits to the antecedent as a ground of the inference; 必 ranges over every open branch; 或 supplies a branch; 皆 ranges over members; 莫 denies existence; 莫不 is weaker than 皆 constructively; 屢 means arbitrarily late recurrence, without a frequency bound; 不 negates; 未 records what the present reading leaves unresolved. These are controlled conventions, not discoveries that all historical uses obey them.

Do not coin a name when the referent remains unsettled, when it bundles several dynamics, or when the apparent classical elegance depends on a misleading historical association. A descriptive phrase and `name_status: uncoined` are sufficient.

**文言 first is a good editorial choice for naming and compact controlled statements.** It is not established as the uniquely faithful language of definition or the best first display for every reader. Section 06’s fidelity assessments found that disciplined English could match much of the advantage attributed to 經, with coder disagreement and no human reading result establishing superiority. Keep formal definitions, full modern explanations and reader choice alongside it.

Use language tags such as `lzh-Hant` for the preferred classical form, `lzh-Hans` for an explicitly maintained simplified rendering, `zh-Hans` and `zh-Hant` for modern Chinese, and `en` for English. Other language tags belong to actual expressions—`grc`, `de`, `ru`, `sa`, `pi`, for example. Culture, school and period are separate metadata. Do not relabel all existing `zh` strings as 文言 without reading them.

**3. The pilot: opposition, thresholds and the fate of an extreme**

The pilot should begin with a question the source and the map can both get wrong:

> What mechanism makes an opposed relation persist, reverse direction, cross into another regime, repeat, or end?

These outcomes are not an exhaustive trichotomy of reversal, lock and oscillation. A course can also run away, settle without crossing, branch, or terminate.

**The first law: several claims hidden in “opposites.”**

**Engels—memory, unchecked.** The usual triad names interpenetration of opposites, transformation of quantity into quality and conversely, and negation of the negation. “Unity and struggle of opposites” should not silently be treated as his exact formulation; the pilot needs edition-specific wording.

Split the first cluster into at least four claims:

- Opposed terms are intelligible through a relation.
- Opposed tendencies belong to one organization.
- Their interaction produces change.
- Their interaction necessarily transforms that organization.

Only the first has an immediate grammar landing.

**Proved, global existential relation.** `Becoming.«能所相待»` says that something conditions something exactly when something is conditioned. It rearranges the witnesses of one relation. It proves neither mutual causal production nor conflict. `«二际可偏举»` supplies two separately given relations, one inhabited and the other empty. `«同根则不可偏举»` obtains the implication when the second relation is pointwise equivalent to the converse of the first. These are checked in [GrammarCandidates.lean](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/GrammarCandidates.lean) (line 49), registered in `candidate-theorems-of-the-corpus`, and housed at [06, `r-candidate-theorems-of-the-corpus`](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-candidate-theorems-of-the-corpus).

This is a useful reading of correlativity. Calling it “the unity of opposites proved” would substantially overstate it.

**Proved, a particular form of opposition.** `RelativePosition.change_balances` and `rise_needs_fall` concern a finite list and natural-number amounts. Position is represented by the amount multiplied by list length against the total; a rise in that position requires a fall elsewhere. This is not a theorem about all conflicts, nor a theorem about ranks or shares. The record expressly leaves the dynamics producing joint excess unproved. See `relative-position-is-zero-sum`, [05’s home](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-relative-position-is-zero-sum), and [RelativePosition.lean](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/RelativePosition.lean) (line 98).

The missing content is substantive: how opposing activities constitute and reproduce their relation, and how their effects change that relation. It is not supplied by the existential grammar theorem.

**Lenin—memory, unchecked.** His notes treat the division of a unity into contradictory aspects as central; they distinguish conditional or relative unity from the struggle of opposites treated as absolute, and describe development as involving more than simple repetition. Split these into:

- an analytical instruction to reconstruct internal differences;
- a universal claim about the persistence of struggle;
- a claim about the temporal form of development.

The first can correspond to 08’s method, as a reading of method. The other two need definitions of struggle and development. An endless renewing pattern in `GrammarMap` does not establish either. Conversely, a motionless model refutes only a formalization that equates struggle with observable state change, not every possible interpretation of Lenin.

**Mao—memory, unchecked.** I would separate six bundles rather than make one “矛盾论” node.

1. **Universality:** contradiction throughout processes and their development. This is not a grammar theorem. A useful reconstruction must identify contradiction independently of whatever change occurs; otherwise every course satisfies it by renaming.
2. **Particularity:** different processes and stages require analysis of their specific contradictions. This meets the map’s insistence on actual relations and local hypotheses, at the level of method.
3. **Principal contradiction:** one contradiction can organize the development of a complex situation. The map has thresholds, dependencies and bottlenecks, but no general theorem selecting a unique principal contradiction.
4. **Principal aspect:** dominance within a contradiction can determine the character of a situation, and dominance can change. Relative-position counting is a neighbour; it supplies neither this determination nor the mechanism changing dominance.
5. **Identity and struggle:** opposed aspects depend on one another and can transform into one another under conditions, while struggle has a different temporal standing. Correlativity, mutual dependence, feedback and transformation must be separate mappings.
6. **Antagonism:** antagonism is one form of contradiction, not its universal form. Do not translate every tension into a zero-sum contest or every remedy into removal of one side.

The third and fourth bundles expose an important missing distinction: **largest magnitude, necessary condition and decisive intervention are different things**. Two symmetric constraints may jointly determine a fate without one being uniquely principal. A future model should allow such a case. It should also say whether “principal” is an explanatory claim about the process or a practical judgment about where an actor can intervene.

A promising residue is therefore: **how the relation that organizes a process changes through the activities it organizes**. This touches changing couplings, readers and rules. It does not yet justify a new universal “contradiction drives development” law.

**When does an extreme reverse, remain locked, or oscillate?**

The snapshot already gives sharply different answers.

**Settling without crossing—proved.** With natural-number levels, balance at the condition, exchange opposing departure from `m`, and exchange within the gap at every step from `T`, `Flow.settles_at` reaches `m` by the initial gap and stays there. `Flow.stays_between_at` shows that the level does not cross `m`. Corrective motion is therefore not necessarily transformation into the opposite.

**Persistent crossing—proved in a delayed relay.** For a balanced relay reading `ℓ` steps late, with step size `M`, band `a ≤ b`, positive `a` and `M`, and floor condition
`(ℓ + 1)M ≤ b + 1`, `Regulation.relay_threshold` says:

\[
\text{every held start below }a\text{ eventually remains in }[a,b]
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
a+(\ell+1)M\le b+1.
\]

With a start held at `a−1` and `b+2 ≤ a+(ℓ+1)M`, `held_oscillates` gives visits above `b` and below `a` after every time. This is repeated crossing, not a proved exact period. Both results belong to `two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading`, [02’s home](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading); the exact statements are in [Regulation.lean](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Regulation.lean) (line 1989).

**Capture despite increasing loss—proved.** `ClosedCapture.lock_outlives_loss` uses a locked start, selection that does not lower the share under a favouring rule, a rule read monotonically from that share, favouring preserved upward, and an unread count increasing by at least one each step. The count grows beyond every bound while the lock persists. The unreadness is built into the joined dynamics.

`turned_by_loss` instead requires a reading whose resulting rule never favours once the count reaches `N`, plus an actual reading step after the bound. It proves non-favouring at the following step. It does not say that large loss automatically creates that reading. See `closed-capture`, [02’s home](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture), and [ClosedCapture.lean](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ClosedCapture.lean) (line 291).

**Rest or cycling depends on the law—proved.** `RegimeMargin.rests_within` requires giving nondecreasing in what remains, a preserved bounded band and an initial level inside it. The run settles. `Adjusted.comes_to_rest` handles the particular level/recent-giving model with adjustment increasing in both arguments, the band and its stated rest identification, within `3hi+3` steps. The `Alternation.alternates` witness drops increasing giving and cycles between one and two. See [where-a-run-of-a-regime-comes-to-rest](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/where-a-run-of-a-regime-comes-to-rest.md).

These results already defeat the inference “pressure grows, therefore reversal follows” within their respective models. They also show why an extreme’s magnitude alone is insufficient: what reads it, the response law, delay, memory and available continuation matter.

A precise **candidate**, not proved or run here, could organize one remaining elementary boundary:

> Let `f` be an order-reversing map on the finite chain `{0,…,N}`. Every orbit is eventually fixed or alternates between two states. In particular,
> \[
> n\ge2N\Longrightarrow f^{n+2}(x)=f^n(x).
> \]

Its premises are a complete scalar state, a fixed deterministic law, a finite chain, and order reversal of the **whole next-state map**. Proof route: `f²` preserves order; its orbit is monotone and stabilizes within `N` changes.

It forbids eventual periods greater than two under those premises. It would generalize the finite scalar form of the `Alternation` witness and sit beside the monotone settling argument. I found no such general declaration in the targeted search; this is an elementary candidate, not a novelty claim.

It still does not force an extreme to reverse: a constant map is order-reversing and can hold the extreme. Crossing a specified opposite region needs an additional bound on `f` there. Delayed relays require their history as state and need not satisfy this scalar hypothesis. This is a useful candidate for CY only if the existing laws do not already answer its intended question.

**Quantity into quality.**

**Engels—memory, unchecked.** The claim includes quantitative changes producing qualitative transformation and the converse. It should be split into accumulation within a regime, a transition out of it, and changed quantitative relations after transition.

The map already has a precise scoped instance. For

\[
x_{t+1}=(x_t-H)+g(x_t-H)
\]

with natural subtraction, `RegimeMargin.ends_iff_below` assumes: giving below `y` is short of `H`; nothing gives from nothing; giving meets `H` above `y` within the preserved band; `0<H+y`; and the start is at most `hi`. It proves eventual zero exactly when `x₀<H+y`. See `margin-of-a-regime`, [02’s home](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime), and [the declaration](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/RegimeMargin.lean) (line 740).

Here the qualitative distinction is a fate—ending versus not ending—specified independently of the threshold calculation.

The more general checked law is `RegimeMargin.leading_exact`: a downward-closed fate over bounded natural-number levels, with an exact Boolean check, has an exact threshold. `Notch.no_threshold` retains the band but supplies a fate where level four ends and level three does not, ruling out that threshold form. These belong to [threshold-in-the-state-plane](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/threshold-in-the-state-plane.md).

This is already a Lean-ready answer; it needs no renamed duplicate theorem. It does **not** establish that every quantity has a qualitative threshold, or that an arbitrary categorical label is a physical phase transition.

The challenging residue is that the relevant quantity or kind may itself change. Same level, different recent giving can yield different fates. Hysteresis requires distinguishing collapse and recovery under changing control; one irreversible ending is not yet that law. These are CY’s existing questions. The pilot should strengthen their specifications, not claim them by analogy.

**Negation of the negation.**

**Engels and Hegel—memory, unchecked.** Readings involving determinate negation, preservation and transformation must be kept apart from Boolean negation and from a simple return to the starting state. The familiar three-word historical caricature should not organize this pilot.

Three distinct forms are available:

- **Logical:** the checked `«相待之还当且仅当双非可还»` equates the return from a complement’s mark to the original mark, for all carriers and predicates, with double-negation elimination. It concerns logic, not development through time.
- **Across one step:** `CeasesToBe.not_comes` and `ComesToBe.not_ceases` relate a predicate’s ceasing or coming to its negation’s change. Their converses require stability at the relevant endpoint. See [negation-change-and-stable-cases](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/negation-change-and-stable-cases.md) and [Change.lean](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Change.lean) (line 274).
- **Historical transformation with preservation:** two-grain continuation can express something retained while carriers change. `«传则异»` proves carrying renewal alters the fine class; generation excludes degeneration, but renewal alone can still end. See [02, `r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains`](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains).

None establishes a necessary second negation, advancement or improvement.

An elementary candidate limit is precise: for an involution `n` and any irreflexive relation `≺`,
`∀x, ¬(x ≺ n(n x))`. Exact return cannot also be strict advancement in that relation. This is a proposed formal limit, not a checked declaration cited here.

A stronger developmental law cannot yet be stated honestly without specifying what negates, what survives, what changes, and what “higher” means. Preserve that as a named blank. Do not hide the missing content inside the definition of negation.

**4. What this gives alignment**

The immediate contribution is a better account of **which pressure can reach which selection, through what change and in time for what**.

The dialectics pilot would prevent four consequential shortcuts:

- Co-constitution does not imply that both parties’ continuation is read.
- Conflict does not guarantee correction.
- Increasing unread loss does not guarantee escape from capture.
- Repeated reversal does not guarantee widening or anything judged better.

It also directs attention toward what the present capture models often supply: how readers form, acquire access, become organized and change the rule that selects them. The unchecked Hegel and Marx residues are useful questions here, even before they support any correspondence.

The newer reader-removal result makes the connection concrete. `survivorsPersist_of_settled` assumes that after some common point each line either persists or is absent. Selection read off surviving lines then passes the course, regardless of earlier removals. `not_allPersist_of_removes` rejects a removal when read against the fixed pool. These are checked in [ReadingsPersist.lean](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ReadingsPersist.lean) (line 520) and stated at [07, `r-a-selection-removing-its-readers`](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-a-selection-removing-its-readers).

That is a model of selection failing to register its removed readers. It is not yet the full meta-attractor: selection by share coming to select control over its own selection.

For joint human–AI selection, the design implication is to preserve the trace of excluded readers and unresolved objections, connect losses beyond the selected share to correction, and distinguish access from timely influence. These are proposed design directions, not a proved universal alignment.

The account itself evaluates nothing. People and AI form evaluations within it; a mapping records what those evaluations read and do. 生生 is the direction offered for life, AI within it. No dialectical law supplies that direction.

**5. Failure modes and the first work**

The largest danger is assimilation: the map becomes able to translate every objection while changing nothing. The safeguard is a residue that remains visible until a specific revision answers it.
