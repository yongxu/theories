# Why the language keeps falling back

Ren asked whether something in the repository, the Lean or the writing misleads us. Yes. The fall is not one session's slip. It is built in at three places, and each feeds the next.

## 1. In the Lean: the root's language stops at the root

| Measured | Count |
|---|---|
| Named declarations in the package | 27,330 |
| Named in the root's terms (`«缘»`, `«核»`, `«生生»` and the like) | 777, under 3% |

| Root terms in `World`, `Optimum`, `RegimeMargin`, `ReadOffInTime` | 0 |
| Root terms in the five lock modules (`Pace`, `RuleCapture`, `ClosedCapture`, `RuleShaping`, `PoolFromCourse`) | 0 |

Above the root, each model was written in the words of the field it came from. The lock modules' commonest words in declaration names are reads, locked, threshold, rule, favours, pool, loss, window, share. The stock's are draw, margin, regime. The drain's are optimum, eligible, better.

The centre's own law is the root's law under another name. `GoAlike` ("what is alike under a description goes alike") is the root's constancy (有常) stated for a process, and `Unread` is its use in the lock. They are tied by proof (`identifiesAlike_iff_unread`). No name says so.

Two things follow. Prose written from the Lean inherits the borrowed words: one reader today called the fronts "the Lean's premises read aloud". And the form of the Lean primes the picture: a state is a tuple of coordinates, with their dependence added afterwards by a step function. I drew four boxes because the model is a tuple.

## 3. In the process: corrections were applied to words

- **Word tests.** The style checker tests words (market words, negatives, stock phrases), and the memory of Ren's corrections is largely a list of words to avoid. A banned word is replaced by another with the same grammar. The one page has "beyond" 14 times and "outside" twice; the redrawn map has "beyond" 0 times and "outside" 16 times.
- **"Plain words" heard as "ordinary English nouns".** After Ren's "too many words, AI/own vocabulary" (11 November), the root's terms were treated as jargon to keep off the fronts. But the root's terms are plain: condition, respect, alike, renews, stops. What was removed was the one vocabulary that does not name a thing apart from its conditions.

- **The AI sessions.** We are trained mostly on text that names things first, and each session starts from the texts in part 2 and from a memory index I wrote in the same borrowed words ("lock and stock", "a selection that wears away its own oversight").

## What I would change

1. **One base statement in the root's terms**, kept in one place. Every front states the centre by it; field words appear only as glosses. The rewritten page is a draft of this.
2. **Test constructions, not words.** Read a sentence by the four rules (is the respect said; is a thing named before its conditions; is separateness shown; who judges). Stop using word lists as the test for dualism.
