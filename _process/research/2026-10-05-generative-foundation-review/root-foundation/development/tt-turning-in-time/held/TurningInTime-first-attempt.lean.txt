Whatever goes on, goes on under conditions (缘), and in some respect (类). This module states, on
the grammar and then on one small model, when a going on that uses up a condition it depends on
turns before the condition is gone, and when it ends without having differed. It is the centre of
the account said in the grammar's own terms: going alike (`«同行»`), constancy (`«有常»`), the core
(`«核»`), `n` steps (`«迭»`), ending (`«亡»`) and a line that never ends (`«不穷»`). The words other
fields use for the same thing (a rule, a share, a stock, its readers; a lock; oversight) are names
for aspects of it in one case and are not used here.

## On the grammar (every carrier, every relation)

A class can go alike under a relation and still not be constant. With a fixed successor the gap
between the two is exactly whether there is a next (`«局定后则有常当且仅当同行可续»`, in
`Becoming.GrammarLaws`). Three laws here say what lies in that gap.

* Alike occurrences stay alike for as many steps as both take, where the class goes alike
  (`alike_steps`).
* A class that holds alike an occurrence that goes on and one that has no next is not constant
  (`not_constant`), and its core tells the two apart (`core_apart`): the core keeps every step
  (`core_step`) and every number of steps (`core_steps`).

So what a class does not tell now, and its core does, is what it depends on without its differing.

## The model: one going on, one condition, two marks

A `Course` is a going on taken in one respect, how it goes on (a `Way`), under one condition with a
level (a natural number). It needs the level to be at least `need`: an occurrence below it is a
condition of nothing (`cond`, the model's 缘). Each step uses `use` of the level, as much as there is,
and the condition gives back `giving` of what is left (`left`, which is `RegimeMargin.step`). How it
goes on next is `alone` of how it goes on, except at a level between two marks, `low ≤ x ≤ high`,
where it is `withLevel` of how it goes on and the level (`renew`). `high` is the level at and below
which the condition's fall makes a difference to it. `low` is the level needed by whatever carries
that difference to it. That the carrier needs a level of the same condition is the model's reading of
"what could carry the difference is among the conditions being used up"; it is a definition here,
not a theorem.

* **Which differences make a difference.** Two levels at which it goes on renew one way differently
  only where one of them lies between the marks (`differs_between`). The respect goes alike exactly
  where no two such levels renew a way differently (`goesAlike_iff`).
* **No level between the marks.** No level at which it goes on lies between the marks exactly where
  `high < low` or `high < need` (`unmarked_iff`). Then the respect goes alike (`goesAlike_of_unmarked`),
  it is constant exactly where the going on needs nothing of the condition (`constant_iff`), and its
  core tells a level at which it goes on from one at which it has ended (`core_tells`).
  `high < need` is the case in which the fall makes a difference only through the going on itself:
  whatever lies between the marks has then already ended (`between_ended`).
* **Used up while nothing in it differs.** Along a line none of whose levels lies between the marks,
  the way after `n` steps is `alone` applied `n` times (`way_alone`), the same from every level
  (`alike_along`). Where giving falls short of what each of those ways uses, from a level below that
  use's point of no return the line reaches, within as many steps as the level stood, an occurrence
  with no next (`used_up_alike`).
* **The last level from which it can turn.** Where every way uses at least `H`, from a level below
  `H`'s point of no return the line ends within as many steps as the level stood, whatever its ways
  do (`ends_below_least_use`).
* **The exact condition.** In the setting `Turning` (ways of two kinds; those that use less use
  `H₂`, stay so at every renewal, and the condition holds under `H₂` from its point of no return up to
  `hi`; the others use at least `H₁`, and by itself none of them comes to use less), from a way not
  using less and a level below `H₁`'s point of no return, the line never ends exactly where at some
  step, the way not yet using less, the level lies between the marks, what it renews into there uses
  less, and the level left by that step is at or above `H₂`'s point of no return
  (`Turning.goes_on_iff`; on the grammar, `Turning.never_ends_iff`).
* **Three courses from one start** (`Witness`). With `RegimeMargin.Peaked.giving`, a use of five as
  before and three once turned, from the level eight: with the marks at seven and eight it turns at
  once and never ends (`Witness.in_time`); with the marks at five and six it turns one step later, the
  level left is two, and it ends (`Witness.too_late`); with no level between the marks it ends
  without having differed (`Witness.unturned`).

## What is assumed and what is not shown

The marks, the need and the two kinds of way are given, not derived. `Turning.kept` (once it uses
less it keeps doing so at every renewal) is a premise; that a way so turned is kept by what renews it

section Grammar

variable {α : Type u} {R E : «际» α}

/-- The core keeps a step: where the core relates two occurrences and one is a condition of a third,
the other is a condition of something the core relates to it. -/
theorem core_step {u u' v : α} (core : «核» R E u u') (r : R u v) :
    ∃ v', R u' v' ∧ «核» R E v v' :=
  («有常之逐点» R («核» R E)).mp («根» R E).2.1 u u' core v r

/-- The core keeps every number of steps. -/
theorem core_steps : ∀ (n : Nat) {u u' v : α}, «核» R E u u' → «迭» R n u v →
    ∃ v', «迭» R n u' v' ∧ «核» R E v v'
  | 0, _, u', _, core, here => by
    cases (show _ = _ from here)
    exact ⟨u', rfl, core⟩
  | n + 1, _, _, _, core, ⟨_, r, rest⟩ =>
    match core_step core r with
    | ⟨w', r', core'⟩ =>
      match core_steps n core' rest with
      | ⟨v', rest', core''⟩ => ⟨v', ⟨w', r', rest'⟩, core''⟩

/-- **What goes on and what has no next are apart in the core of every class.** -/
theorem core_apart {u u' v : α} (goes : R u v) (ends : ∀ v', ¬ R u' v') : ¬ «核» R E u u' :=
  fun core => match core_step core goes with
    | ⟨v', r', _⟩ => ends v' r'

/-- **A class that holds alike what goes on and what has no next is not constant.** -/
theorem not_constant {u u' v : α} (alike : E u u') (goes : R u v) (ends : ∀ v', ¬ R u' v') :
    ¬ «有常» R E :=
  fun constant => match «有常则可续» constant u u' v alike goes with
    | ⟨v', r'⟩ => ends v' r'

/-- **Where a class goes alike, alike occurrences stay alike for as many steps as both take.** -/
theorem alike_steps (goes : «同行» R E) : ∀ (n : Nat) {u u' v v' : α}, E u u' →
    «迭» R n u v → «迭» R n u' v' → E v v'
  | 0, _, _, _, _, e, here, here' => by
    cases (show _ = _ from here)
    cases (show _ = _ from here')
    exact e
  | n + 1, _, _, _, _, e, ⟨w, r, rest⟩, ⟨w', r', rest'⟩ =>
    alike_steps goes n («同行之逐点».mp goes _ _ e w w' r r') rest rest'

end Grammar

/-! ## One going on, one condition, two marks -/

/-- One going on under one condition that it uses up. `Way` holds how it goes on: the classes of the
respect in which it is taken. A natural number is the level of the condition. -/
structure Course (Way : Type u) where
  /-- The level its going on needs: an occurrence below it is a condition of nothing. -/
  need : Nat
  /-- The lower mark: the level needed by what carries the level's difference to it. -/
  low : Nat
  /-- The upper mark: the level at and below which the condition's fall makes a difference to it. -/
  high : Nat
  /-- How it goes on next where the level makes no difference: from how it goes on alone. -/
  alone : Way → Way
  /-- How it goes on next at a level between the marks. -/
  withLevel : Way → Nat → Way
  /-- What a way uses of the condition at each step. -/
  use : Way → Nat
  /-- What the condition gives back from what is left. -/
  giving : Nat → Nat

namespace Course

variable {Way : Type u} (c : Course Way)

/-- A level lies between the marks. -/
def Between (x : Nat) : Prop := c.low ≤ x ∧ x ≤ c.high

instance decBetween (x : Nat) : Decidable (c.Between x) :=
  show Decidable (c.low ≤ x ∧ x ≤ c.high) from inferInstance

/-- How it goes on next: from the level where the level lies between the marks, else alone. -/
def renew (k : Way) (x : Nat) : Way := if c.Between x then c.withLevel k x else c.alone k

/-- The level after a step: what the way uses is taken first, as much as there is, and the condition
gives back from what is left (`RegimeMargin.step`). -/
def left (k : Way) (x : Nat) : Nat := RegimeMargin.step c.giving (c.use k) x

/-- One step of the going on. -/
def step (s : Way × Nat) : Way × Nat := (c.renew s.1 s.2, c.left s.1 s.2)

/-- The model's 缘: an occurrence whose level meets the need is a condition of its step, and one
below the need is a condition of nothing. -/
def cond : «际» (Way × Nat) := fun s t => c.need ≤ s.2 ∧ t = c.step s

/-- The respect: alike in how they go on, whatever the level. -/
def alike (_ : Course Way) : «际» (Way × Nat) := fun s t => s.1 = t.1

/-- How it goes on after `n` steps. -/
def way (s : Way × Nat) (n : Nat) : Way := (Law.orbit c.step s n).1

/-- The level after `n` steps. -/
def level (s : Way × Nat) (n : Nat) : Nat := (Law.orbit c.step s n).2

/-- It goes on through its first `n` steps: each of them starts at a level that meets the need. -/
def Lasts (s : Way × Nat) (n : Nat) : Prop := ∀ j, j < n → c.need ≤ c.level s j

instance decLasts (s : Way × Nat) (n : Nat) : Decidable (c.Lasts s n) :=
  show Decidable (∀ j, j < n → c.need ≤ c.level s j) from inferInstance

/-- No level at which it goes on lies between the marks. -/
def Unmarked : Prop := ∀ x, c.need ≤ x → ¬ c.Between x

theorem alike_class : «类» c.alike :=
  ⟨fun _ => rfl, fun e => Eq.symm e, fun e e' => Eq.trans e e'⟩

/-- The model's 缘 has a fixed successor. -/
theorem cond_fixed : «定后» c.cond :=
  «定后之逐点».mpr fun _ _ _ r r' => r.2.trans r'.2.symm

theorem renew_outside {k : Way} {x : Nat} (out : ¬ c.Between x) : c.renew k x = c.alone k :=
  if_neg out

theorem renew_between {k : Way} {x : Nat} (between : c.Between x) :
    c.renew k x = c.withLevel k x :=
  if_pos between

theorem way_shift (s : Way × Nat) (n : Nat) : c.way s (n + 1) = c.way (c.step s) n :=
  congrArg Prod.fst (Law.orbit_shift c.step s n)

theorem level_shift (s : Way × Nat) (n : Nat) : c.level s (n + 1) = c.level (c.step s) n :=
  congrArg Prod.snd (Law.orbit_shift c.step s n)

theorem way_add (s : Way × Nat) (m n : Nat) :
    c.way s (m + n) = c.way (Law.orbit c.step s m) n :=
  congrArg Prod.fst (Law.orbit_add c.step s m n)

theorem level_add (s : Way × Nat) (m n : Nat) :
    c.level s (m + n) = c.level (Law.orbit c.step s m) n :=
  congrArg Prod.snd (Law.orbit_add c.step s m n)

/-! ### On the grammar: steps, ending, a line that never ends -/

/-- An occurrence ends under the one, nothing continuing it in any respect, exactly where its level
is below the need. -/
theorem ends_iff {t : Way × Nat} : «亡» c.cond «一» t ↔ t.2 < c.need :=
  ⟨fun ends => (Nat.lt_or_ge t.2 c.need).elim id
      (fun ge => (ends ⟨c.step t, ⟨ge, rfl⟩, trivial⟩).elim),
   fun lt ⟨_, ⟨ge, _⟩, _⟩ => absurd ge (Nat.not_le_of_gt lt)⟩

/-- `n` steps of the model's 缘 from `s` end at `t` exactly where the going on lasts through them and
`t` is where the step, taken `n` times, leaves `s`. -/
theorem steps_iff : ∀ (n : Nat) {s t : Way × Nat},
    «迭» c.cond n s t ↔ c.Lasts s n ∧ t = Law.orbit c.step s n
  | 0, s, t =>
    ⟨fun here => ⟨fun j lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero j), (show s = t from here).symm⟩,
     fun ⟨_, e⟩ => show s = t from e.symm⟩
  | n + 1, s, t =>
    ⟨fun ⟨w, r, rest⟩ => by
      cases r.2
      exact ⟨fun j lt => match j, lt with
          | 0, _ => r.1
          | j + 1, lt => Nat.le_trans (((steps_iff n).mp rest).1 j (Nat.lt_of_succ_lt_succ lt))
              (Nat.le_of_eq (c.level_shift s j).symm),
        ((steps_iff n).mp rest).2.trans (Law.orbit_shift c.step s n).symm⟩,
     fun ⟨lasts, e⟩ =>
      ⟨c.step s, ⟨lasts 0 (Nat.succ_pos n), rfl⟩,
        (steps_iff n).mpr ⟨fun j lt => Nat.le_trans (lasts (j + 1) (Nat.succ_lt_succ lt))
            (Nat.le_of_eq (c.level_shift s j)),
          e.trans (Law.orbit_shift c.step s n)⟩⟩⟩

/-- At each number of steps the going on has lasted through them, or it ended at an earlier one. -/
theorem lasts_or_ended (s : Way × Nat) :
    ∀ n, c.Lasts s n ∨ ∃ j, j < n ∧ c.Lasts s j ∧ c.level s j < c.need
  | 0 => .inl fun j lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero j)
  | n + 1 =>
    match lasts_or_ended s n with
    | .inl lasts =>
      (Nat.lt_or_ge (c.level s n) c.need).elim
        (fun lt => .inr ⟨n, Nat.lt_succ_self n, lasts, lt⟩)
        (fun ge => .inl fun j lt =>
          (Nat.lt_or_eq_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt_succ lt)).elim (lasts j)
            (fun e => Nat.le_trans ge (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (c.level s) e.symm))))
    | .inr ⟨j, lt, lasts, ended⟩ => .inr ⟨j, Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt, lasts, ended⟩

/-- The line from an occurrence never ends, in the grammar's sense under the one, exactly where its
level meets the need at every step. -/
theorem never_ends_iff {s : Way × Nat} : «不穷» c.cond «一» s ↔ ∀ n, c.need ≤ c.level s n := by
  constructor
  · intro endless
    have lasts : ∀ n, c.Lasts s n ∧ c.need ≤ c.level s n := fun n => by
      induction n with
      | zero =>
        have first : c.Lasts s 0 := fun j lt => absurd lt (Nat.not_lt_zero j)
        match endless s (.inl rfl) with
        | ⟨_, ⟨ge, _⟩, _⟩ => exact ⟨first, ge⟩
      | succ n ih =>
        have next : c.Lasts s (n + 1) := fun j lt =>
          (Nat.lt_or_eq_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt_succ lt)).elim (ih.1 j)
            (fun e => Nat.le_trans ih.2 (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (c.level s) e.symm)))
        have reach : «相续» c.cond «一» s (Law.orbit c.step s (n + 1)) :=
          «及之单调» (fun x y r => (⟨r, trivial⟩ : «续» c.cond «一» x y)) _ _
            («迭则及» n ((c.steps_iff (n + 1)).mpr ⟨next, rfl⟩))
        match endless _ (.inr reach) with
        | ⟨_, ⟨ge, _⟩, _⟩ => exact ⟨next, ge⟩
    exact fun n => (lasts n).2
  · intro all y reached
    have at_step : ∃ n, y = Law.orbit c.step s n :=
      match reached with
      | .inl e => ⟨0, e.symm⟩
      | .inr line =>
        match «及即迭».mp («及之单调» (fun _ _ r => r.1) _ _ line) with
        | ⟨n, steps⟩ => ⟨n + 1, ((c.steps_iff (n + 1)).mp steps).2⟩
    match at_step with
    | ⟨n, e⟩ => exact ⟨c.step y, ⟨Nat.le_trans (all n) (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg Prod.snd e.symm)), rfl⟩,
        trivial⟩

/-! ### Which differences make a difference -/

/-- **The respect goes alike exactly where no two levels at which it goes on renew a way
differently.** -/
theorem goesAlike_iff : «同行» c.cond c.alike ↔
    ∀ k x x', c.need ≤ x → c.need ≤ x' → c.renew k x = c.renew k x' :=
  ⟨fun goes k x x' ge ge' =>
    «同行之逐点».mp goes (k, x) (k, x') rfl (c.step (k, x)) (c.step (k, x')) ⟨ge, rfl⟩ ⟨ge', rfl⟩,
   fun same => «同行之逐点».mpr fun s s' e t t' r r' =>
    show t.1 = t'.1 from
      (congrArg Prod.fst r.2).trans
        (((congrArg (fun k => c.renew k s.2) (show s.1 = s'.1 from e)).trans
          (same s'.1 s.2 s'.2 r.1 r'.1)).trans (congrArg Prod.fst r'.2).symm)⟩

/-- **A difference of level makes a difference only between the marks**: two levels that renew one
way differently do not both lie outside them. -/
theorem differs_between {k : Way} {x x' : Nat} (differs : c.renew k x ≠ c.renew k x') :
    c.Between x ∨ c.Between x' :=
  if between : c.Between x then .inl between
  else if between' : c.Between x' then .inr between'
  else absurd ((c.renew_outside between).trans (c.renew_outside between').symm) differs

/-- **No level at which it goes on lies between the marks exactly where the upper mark is below the
lower mark or below the need.** -/
theorem unmarked_iff : c.Unmarked ↔ c.high < c.low ∨ c.high < c.need :=
  ⟨fun un => (Nat.lt_or_ge c.high c.low).elim .inl fun low_le =>
    (Nat.lt_or_ge c.high c.need).elim .inr fun need_le =>
      (Nat.le_total c.low c.need).elim
        (fun le => absurd ⟨le, need_le⟩ (un c.need (Nat.le_refl _)))
        (fun le => absurd ⟨Nat.le_refl _, low_le⟩ (un c.low le)),
   fun h _ ge between => h.elim
    (fun lt => Nat.not_le_of_gt lt (Nat.le_trans between.1 between.2))
    (fun lt => Nat.not_le_of_gt lt (Nat.le_trans ge between.2))⟩

/-- **With no level between the marks, the respect goes alike.** -/
theorem goesAlike_of_unmarked (un : c.Unmarked) : «同行» c.cond c.alike :=
  c.goesAlike_iff.mpr fun _ x x' ge ge' =>
    (c.renew_outside (un x ge)).trans (c.renew_outside (un x' ge')).symm

/-- **With no level between the marks, the respect is constant exactly where the going on needs
nothing of the condition.** -/
theorem constant_iff (k : Way) (un : c.Unmarked) : «有常» c.cond c.alike ↔ c.need = 0 :=
  ⟨fun constant =>
    match «有常则可续» constant (k, c.need) (k, 0) (c.step (k, c.need)) rfl ⟨Nat.le_refl _, rfl⟩ with
    | ⟨_, ge, _⟩ => Nat.eq_zero_of_le_zero ge,
   fun none => («有常之逐点» c.cond c.alike).mpr fun u u' e _ r =>
    have ge : ∀ x, c.need ≤ x := fun x => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq none) (Nat.zero_le x)
    ⟨c.step u', ⟨ge _, rfl⟩,
      show _ = (c.step u').1 from
        (congrArg Prod.fst r.2).trans
          ((c.renew_outside (un _ (ge _))).trans
            ((congrArg c.alone (show u.1 = u'.1 from e)).trans
              (c.renew_outside (un _ (ge _))).symm))⟩⟩

/-- **The core of the respect tells a level at which it goes on from one at which it has ended**,
though the respect holds them alike. -/
theorem core_tells {k : Way} {x x' : Nat} (ge : c.need ≤ x) (lt : x' < c.need) :
    ¬ «核» c.cond c.alike (k, x) (k, x') :=
  core_apart (v := c.step (k, x)) ⟨ge, rfl⟩ fun _ r => absurd r.1 (Nat.not_le_of_gt lt)

/-- **Where the fall makes a difference only below what the going on itself needs, whatever lies
between the marks has already ended.** -/
theorem between_ended (own : c.high < c.need) {t : Way × Nat} (between : c.Between t.2) :
    «亡» c.cond «一» t :=
  c.ends_iff.mpr (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt between.2 own)

/-! ### Renewing alone -/

/-- **Along a line none of whose levels lies between the marks, the way after `n` steps is `alone`
applied `n` times.** -/
theorem way_alone (s : Way × Nat) : ∀ n, (∀ j, j < n → ¬ c.Between (c.level s j)) →
    c.way s n = Law.orbit c.alone s.1 n
  | 0, _ => rfl
  | n + 1, out =>
    (c.renew_outside (k := c.way s n) (out n (Nat.lt_succ_self n))).trans
      (congrArg c.alone (way_alone s n fun j lt => out j (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt)))

/-- **With no level between the marks, lines from one way at any two levels are alike at every step
both take.** -/
theorem alike_along (un : c.Unmarked) (n : Nat) {k : Way} {x x' : Nat} {t t' : Way × Nat}
    (line : «迭» c.cond n (k, x) t) (line' : «迭» c.cond n (k, x') t') : c.alike t t' :=
  alike_steps (c.goesAlike_of_unmarked un) n (show c.alike (k, x) (k, x') from rfl) line line'

/-! ### The level under what the ways use -/

/-- Nothing is left of nothing. -/
theorem left_zero (nothing : FromNothing c.giving) (k : Way) : c.left k 0 = 0 := by
  show (0 - c.use k) + c.giving (0 - c.use k) = 0
  rw [Nat.zero_sub, show c.giving 0 = 0 from nothing]

/-- Below the point of no return of a use `H`, a step by a way that uses at least `H` lowers a
positive level. At a standing use this is `RegimeMargin.Adjusted.step_below`. -/
theorem left_lt {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y) (nothing : FromNothing c.giving)
    {k : Way} {x : Nat} (use : H ≤ c.use k) (pos : 0 < x) (below : x < H + y) :
    c.left k x < x := by
  show (x - c.use k) + c.giving (x - c.use k) < x
  cases Nat.lt_or_ge x (c.use k) with
  | inl lt =>
    rw [sub_eq_zero_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt), show c.giving 0 = 0 from nothing]
    exact pos
  | inr ge =>
    have back : x - c.use k + c.use k = x := Naturals.sub_add_cancel ge
    have under : x - c.use k < y :=
      Nat.lt_of_add_lt_add_right (n := c.use k)
        (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq back) below)
          (Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_right use y) (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.add_comm (c.use k) y))))
    have less : c.giving (x - c.use k) < c.use k := Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (short _ under) use
    exact Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le (Nat.add_lt_add_left less _) (Nat.le_of_eq back)

/-- **Below the point of no return the level falls at every step**, by one at least while anything
is left, along a line each of whose ways uses at least `H`. -/
theorem falls {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y) (nothing : FromNothing c.giving)
    {s : Way × Nat} (below : s.2 < H + y) :
    ∀ n, (∀ j, j < n → H ≤ c.use (c.way s j)) → c.level s n + n ≤ s.2 ∨ c.level s n = 0
  | 0, _ => .inl (Nat.le_refl _)
  | n + 1, uses =>
    have gone : c.level s n = 0 → c.level s (n + 1) = 0 := fun zero =>
      (congrArg (c.left (c.way s n)) zero).trans (c.left_zero nothing _)
    match falls short nothing below n (fun j lt => uses j (Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt)) with
    | .inr zero => .inr (gone zero)
    | .inl le =>
      (Nat.eq_zero_or_pos (c.level s n)).elim (fun zero => .inr (gone zero)) fun pos =>
        have lt : c.level s (n + 1) < c.level s n :=
          c.left_lt short nothing (uses n (Nat.lt_succ_self n)) pos
            (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_right _ n) le) below)
        .inl (Nat.le_trans
          (Nat.le_of_eq ((congrArg (c.level s (n + 1) + ·) (Nat.add_comm n 1)).trans
            (Nat.add_assoc _ 1 n).symm))
          (Nat.le_trans (Nat.add_le_add_right (Nat.succ_le_of_lt lt) n) le))

/-- Along such a line the level is never above where it started. -/
theorem falls_le {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y) (nothing : FromNothing c.giving)
    {s : Way × Nat} (below : s.2 < H + y) (n : Nat)
    (uses : ∀ j, j < n → H ≤ c.use (c.way s j)) : c.level s n ≤ s.2 :=
  (c.falls short nothing below n uses).elim
    (fun le => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_add_right _ n) le)
    (fun zero => Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq zero) (Nat.zero_le _))

/-- Along such a line nothing is left after as many steps as the level stood. -/
theorem gone {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y) (nothing : FromNothing c.giving)
    {s : Way × Nat} (below : s.2 < H + y) (uses : ∀ j, j < s.2 → H ≤ c.use (c.way s j)) :
    c.level s s.2 = 0 :=
  (c.falls short nothing below s.2 uses).elim
    (fun le => Nat.eq_zero_of_le_zero
      (le_of_add_le_add_right s.2 (Nat.le_trans le (Nat.le_of_eq (Nat.zero_add s.2).symm))))
    id

/-- **Used up while nothing in it differs.** With no level at which it goes on between the marks,
giving short of `H` below `y`, and every way that renewing alone passes through using at least `H`:
from a level below `H + y`, within as many steps as the level stood the line reaches an occurrence
that nothing continues, and its way there is `alone` applied that many times, as from every other
level (`alike_along`). -/
theorem used_up_alike {H y : Nat} (un : c.Unmarked) (short : Short c.giving H y)
    (nothing : FromNothing c.giving) (needed : 0 < c.need) {k : Way} {x : Nat}
    (uses : ∀ j, H ≤ c.use (Law.orbit c.alone k j)) (below : x < H + y) :
    ∃ n t, n ≤ x ∧ «迭» c.cond n (k, x) t ∧ «亡» c.cond «一» t ∧ t.1 = Law.orbit c.alone k n :=
  match c.lasts_or_ended (k, x) x with
  | .inl lasts =>
    have zero : c.level (k, x) x = 0 :=
      c.gone short nothing (s := (k, x)) below fun j lt =>
        Nat.le_trans (uses j) (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg c.use
          (c.way_alone (k, x) j fun i lt' => un _ (lasts i (Nat.lt_trans lt' lt))).symm))
    ⟨x, Law.orbit c.step (k, x) x, Nat.le_refl x, (c.steps_iff x).mpr ⟨lasts, rfl⟩,
      c.ends_iff.mpr (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq zero) needed),
      c.way_alone (k, x) x fun j lt => un _ (lasts j lt)⟩
  | .inr ⟨j, lt, lasts, ended⟩ =>
    ⟨j, Law.orbit c.step (k, x) j, Nat.le_of_lt lt, (c.steps_iff j).mpr ⟨lasts, rfl⟩,
      c.ends_iff.mpr ended, c.way_alone (k, x) j fun i lt' => un _ (lasts i lt')⟩

/-- **The last level from which it can turn.** Where every way uses at least `H` and giving is short
of `H` below `y`, from a level below `H + y` the line reaches, within as many steps as the level
stood, an occurrence that nothing continues, whatever its ways do. -/
theorem ends_below_least_use {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y)
    (nothing : FromNothing c.giving) (needed : 0 < c.need) (least : ∀ k, H ≤ c.use k)
    {s : Way × Nat} (below : s.2 < H + y) :
    ∃ n t, n ≤ s.2 ∧ «迭» c.cond n s t ∧ «亡» c.cond «一» t :=
  match c.lasts_or_ended s s.2 with
  | .inl lasts =>
    ⟨s.2, Law.orbit c.step s s.2, Nat.le_refl _, (c.steps_iff s.2).mpr ⟨lasts, rfl⟩,
      c.ends_iff.mpr (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt
        (Nat.le_of_eq (c.gone short nothing below fun _ _ => least _)) needed)⟩
  | .inr ⟨j, lt, lasts, ended⟩ =>
    ⟨j, Law.orbit c.step s j, Nat.le_of_lt lt, (c.steps_iff j).mpr ⟨lasts, rfl⟩,
      c.ends_iff.mpr ended⟩

/-- Where every way uses at least `H`, a level once below `H`'s point of no return is never higher
again. -/
theorem below_stays {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y) (nothing : FromNothing c.giving)
    (least : ∀ k, H ≤ c.use k) (s : Way × Nat) {n : Nat} (below : c.level s n < H + y) (i : Nat) :
    c.level s (n + i) ≤ c.level s n :=
  Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (c.level_add s n i))
    (c.falls_le short nothing (s := Law.orbit c.step s n) below i fun _ _ => least _)

/-- Where every way uses at least `H` and the line never ends, the level is at or above `H`'s point
of no return at every step. -/
theorem above_of_never_ends {H y : Nat} (short : Short c.giving H y)
    (nothing : FromNothing c.giving) (needed : 0 < c.need) (least : ∀ k, H ≤ c.use k)
    {s : Way × Nat} (endless : ∀ n, c.need ≤ c.level s n) (n : Nat) : H + y ≤ c.level s n :=
  (Nat.lt_or_ge (c.level s n) (H + y)).elim
    (fun below =>
      have zero : c.level s (n + c.level s n) = 0 :=
        (c.level_add s n (c.level s n)).trans
          (c.gone short nothing (s := Law.orbit c.step s n) below fun _ _ => least _)
      absurd (endless (n + c.level s n))
        (Nat.not_le_of_gt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq zero) needed)))
    id

end Course

/-! ## The exact condition -/

/-- The setting in which the condition is exact. Ways are of two kinds: those that use less (`Less`)
and the rest. A way that uses less uses `H₂` and keeps using less at every renewal, and under `H₂`
the condition holds from its point of no return `H₂ + y₂` up to `hi` (`RegimeMargin.holds`'s
premises). A way that does not use less uses at least `H₁`, below whose point of no return `H₁ + y₁`
giving falls short, and renewing alone it does not come to use less. -/
structure Turning {Way : Type u} (c : Course Way) (Less : Way → Prop) (H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi : Nat) :
    Prop where
  /-- Nothing gives from nothing. -/
  nothing : FromNothing c.giving
  /-- A way that does not use less uses at least `H₁`. -/
  more : ∀ k, ¬ Less k → H₁ ≤ c.use k
  /-- Giving falls short of `H₁` below `y₁`. -/
  short₁ : Short c.giving H₁ y₁
  /-- Renewing alone, a way that does not use less does not come to. -/
  alone : ∀ k, ¬ Less k → ¬ Less (c.alone k)
  /-- A way that uses less uses `H₂`. -/
  less : ∀ k, Less k → c.use k = H₂
  /-- Once it uses less it keeps doing so at every renewal. -/
  kept : ∀ k x, Less k → Less (c.renew k x)
  /-- Giving falls short of `H₂` below `y₂`. -/
  short₂ : Short c.giving H₂ y₂
  /-- Giving meets `H₂` from `y₂` up to `hi`. -/
  meets : Meets c.giving H₂ y₂ hi
  /-- Under `H₂` the step keeps every level up to `hi` within it. -/
  keeps : Keeps c.giving H₂ hi
  /-- The lesser use is not more than the other. -/
  least : H₂ ≤ H₁
  /-- The going on needs something of the condition. -/
  needed : 0 < c.need
  /-- What it needs is not above the lesser use's point of no return. -/
  within : c.need ≤ H₂ + y₂

namespace Turning

variable {Way : Type u} {c : Course Way} {Less : Way → Prop} {H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi : Nat}

/-- Every way uses at least the lesser use. -/
theorem uses_least [DecidablePred Less] (t : Turning c Less H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi) (k : Way) :
    H₂ ≤ c.use k :=
  if less : Less k then Nat.le_of_eq (t.less k less).symm
  else Nat.le_trans t.least (t.more k less)

/-- Once a way on the line uses less, every later one does. -/
theorem less_onward (t : Turning c Less H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi) (s : Way × Nat) {j : Nat}
    (less : Less (c.way s j)) : ∀ i, Less (c.way s (j + i))
  | 0 => less
  | i + 1 => t.kept _ (c.level s (j + i)) (less_onward t s less i)

/-- **From a way that uses less, at a level from the lesser use's point of no return up to `hi`, the
line never ends**: its level is `RegimeMargin`'s run under the standing use `H₂`, which holds
(`RegimeMargin.holds`). -/
theorem holds (t : Turning c Less H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi) {s : Way × Nat} (less : Less s.1)
    (low : H₂ + y₂ ≤ s.2) (high : s.2 ≤ hi) (n : Nat) : c.need ≤ c.level s n :=
  have run : ∀ n, Less (c.way s n) ∧
      c.level s n = Law.orbit (RegimeMargin.step c.giving H₂) s.2 n := fun n => by
    induction n with
    | zero => exact ⟨less, rfl⟩
    | succ n ih =>
      exact ⟨t.kept _ _ ih.1,
        (congrArg (fun H => RegimeMargin.step c.giving H (c.level s n)) (t.less _ ih.1)).trans
          (congrArg (RegimeMargin.step c.giving H₂) ih.2)⟩
  Nat.le_trans t.within (Nat.le_trans low
    (Nat.le_trans (RegimeMargin.holds t.meets t.keeps low high n).1 (Nat.le_of_eq (run n).2.symm)))

/-- On a line that starts from a way not using less, up to each step either no way has used less, or
there is a first step at which one comes to. -/
theorem first_less [DecidablePred Less] (s : Way × Nat) (start : ¬ Less s.1) :
    ∀ n, (∀ j, j ≤ n → ¬ Less (c.way s j)) ∨
      ∃ j, j < n ∧ ¬ Less (c.way s j) ∧ Less (c.way s (j + 1))
  | 0 => .inl fun j le => by
    cases Nat.eq_zero_of_le_zero le
    exact start
  | n + 1 =>
    match first_less s start n with
    | .inl never =>
      if less : Less (c.way s (n + 1)) then .inr ⟨n, Nat.lt_succ_self n, never n (Nat.le_refl n), less⟩
      else .inl fun j le => (Nat.lt_or_eq_of_le le).elim (fun lt => never j (Nat.le_of_lt_succ lt))
        (fun e => by cases e; exact less)
    | .inr ⟨j, lt, before, after⟩ => .inr ⟨j, Nat.lt_succ_of_lt lt, before, after⟩

/-- **The exact condition.** From a way that does not use less, at a level up to `hi` and below the
point of no return of what it uses, the line never ends exactly where at some step, the way not yet
using less, the level lies between the marks, what it renews into there uses less, and the level left
by that step is at or above the lesser use's point of no return. -/
theorem goes_on_iff [DecidablePred Less] (t : Turning c Less H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi) {s : Way × Nat}
    (start : ¬ Less s.1) (below : s.2 < H₁ + y₁) (high : s.2 ≤ hi) :
    (∀ n, c.need ≤ c.level s n) ↔
      ∃ j, ¬ Less (c.way s j) ∧ c.Between (c.level s j) ∧
        Less (c.withLevel (c.way s j) (c.level s j)) ∧ H₂ + y₂ ≤ c.level s (j + 1) := by
  constructor
  · intro endless
    have above := c.above_of_never_ends t.short₂ t.nothing t.needed t.uses_least endless
    match first_less (c := c) s start s.2 with
    | .inl never =>
      have zero : c.level s s.2 = 0 :=
        c.gone t.short₁ t.nothing below fun j lt => t.more _ (never j (Nat.le_of_lt lt))
      exact absurd (endless s.2) (Nat.not_le_of_gt (Nat.lt_of_le_of_lt (Nat.le_of_eq zero) t.needed))
    | .inr ⟨j, _, before, after⟩ =>
      have renewed : Less (c.renew (c.way s j) (c.level s j)) := after
      if between : c.Between (c.level s j) then
        exact ⟨j, before, between,
          (congrArg Less (c.renew_between (k := c.way s j) between)).mp renewed, above (j + 1)⟩
      else
        exact absurd ((congrArg Less (c.renew_outside (k := c.way s j) between)).mp renewed)
          (t.alone _ before)
  · intro ⟨j, before, between, turned, enough⟩ n
    have earlier : ∀ i, i < j + 1 → ¬ Less (c.way s i) := fun i lt less =>
      before ((add_sub_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt_succ lt)) ▸ t.less_onward s less (j - i))
    have after : Less (c.way s (j + 1)) :=
      show Less (c.renew (c.way s j) (c.level s j)) from
        (congrArg Less (c.renew_between (k := c.way s j) between)).mpr turned
    have top : c.level s (j + 1) ≤ hi :=
      Nat.le_trans (c.falls_le t.short₁ t.nothing below (j + 1) fun i lt => t.more _ (earlier i lt))
        high
    have onward : ∀ i, c.need ≤ c.level s (j + 1 + i) := fun i =>
      Nat.le_trans (t.holds (s := Law.orbit c.step s (j + 1)) after enough top i)
        (Nat.le_of_eq (c.level_add s (j + 1) i).symm)
    cases Nat.lt_or_ge n (j + 1) with
    | inr ge => exact (add_sub_of_le ge) ▸ onward (n - (j + 1))
    | inl lt =>
      cases Nat.lt_or_ge (c.level s n) c.need with
      | inr ge => exact ge
      | inl ended =>
        have stays := c.below_stays t.short₂ t.nothing t.uses_least s
          (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le ended t.within) (j + 1 - n)
        have same : n + (j + 1 - n) = j + 1 := add_sub_of_le (Nat.le_of_lt lt)
        exact absurd (Nat.le_trans enough (Nat.le_trans (Nat.le_of_eq (congrArg (c.level s) same).symm)
          stays)) (Nat.not_le_of_gt (Nat.lt_of_lt_of_le ended t.within))

/-- **The exact condition, on the grammar**: the line never ends (`«不穷»` under the one) exactly
where it turns in time. -/
theorem never_ends_iff [DecidablePred Less] (t : Turning c Less H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi) {s : Way × Nat}
    (start : ¬ Less s.1) (below : s.2 < H₁ + y₁) (high : s.2 ≤ hi) :
    «不穷» c.cond «一» s ↔
      ∃ j, ¬ Less (c.way s j) ∧ c.Between (c.level s j) ∧
        Less (c.withLevel (c.way s j) (c.level s j)) ∧ H₂ + y₂ ≤ c.level s (j + 1) :=
  c.never_ends_iff.trans (t.goes_on_iff start below high)

/-- **With no level at which it goes on between the marks, the line ends.** -/
theorem unmarked_ends [DecidablePred Less] (t : Turning c Less H₁ y₁ H₂ y₂ hi) (un : c.Unmarked)
    {s : Way × Nat} (start : ¬ Less s.1) (below : s.2 < H₁ + y₁) (high : s.2 ≤ hi) :
    ¬ «不穷» c.cond «一» s :=
  fun endless =>
    match (t.never_ends_iff start below high).mp endless with
    | ⟨j, _, between, _, _⟩ => un _ (c.never_ends_iff.mp endless j) between

end Turning

/-! ## Three courses from one start -/

namespace Witness

/-- Two ways: as before (`true`), which uses five, and turned (`false`), which uses three. Alone, a
way renews as it is; between the marks it turns. Giving is `RegimeMargin.Peaked.giving`: it rises with
what is left to four at four and falls to nothing at eight. -/
def course (low high : Nat) : Course Bool where
  need := 1
  low := low
  high := high
  alone := id
  withLevel := fun _ _ => false
  use := fun k => bif k then 5 else 3
  giving := RegimeMargin.Peaked.giving

/-- The setting of the exact condition holds of these courses, whatever the marks: under five giving
is short at every level below four (and beyond), under three the point of no return is six and the
condition holds from six to eight. -/
theorem turning (low high : Nat) : Turning (course low high) (· = false) 5 4 3 3 8 where
  nothing := RegimeMargin.Peaked.fromNothing
  more := fun k not => by
    cases k with
    | false => exact absurd rfl not
    | true => exact Nat.le_refl 5
  short₁ := RegimeMargin.short_of_below rfl
  alone := fun _ not => not
  less := fun k less => by cases less; rfl
  kept := fun k x less =>
    if between : (course low high).Between x then (course low high).renew_between between
    else ((course low high).renew_outside between).trans less
  short₂ := RegimeMargin.short_of_below rfl
  meets := RegimeMargin.meets_of_below rfl
  keeps := RegimeMargin.keeps_of_below rfl
  least := by decide
  needed := Nat.succ_pos 0
  within := show 1 ≤ 3 + 3 from by decide

/-- **In time.** With the marks at seven and eight, from the level eight the fall makes a difference
at once, the level left is six, and the line never ends. -/
theorem in_time : «不穷» (course 7 8).cond «一» (true, 8) :=
  ((turning 7 8).never_ends_iff (s := (true, 8)) (by decide) (by decide) (by decide)).mpr
    ⟨0, by decide, by decide, rfl, by decide⟩

/-- **Too late.** With the marks at five and six, from the level eight it turns one step later, at
the level six; the level left is two, below the lesser use's point of no return, and the line ends at
its third step, turned. -/
theorem too_late : (course 5 6).way (true, 8) 2 = false ∧
    «迭» (course 5 6).cond 3 (true, 8) (false, 0) ∧ «亡» (course 5 6).cond «一» (false, 0) :=
  ⟨rfl, ((course 5 6).steps_iff 3).mpr ⟨by decide, rfl⟩, (course 5 6).ends_iff.mpr (by decide)⟩

/-- **Unturned.** With no level between the marks, from the level eight the line ends at its third
step, its way as it was. -/
theorem unturned : (course 9 0).Unmarked ∧
    «迭» (course 9 0).cond 3 (true, 8) (true, 0) ∧ «亡» (course 9 0).cond «一» (true, 0) :=
  ⟨(course 9 0).unmarked_iff.mpr (.inl (by decide)),
    ((course 9 0).steps_iff 3).mpr ⟨by decide, rfl⟩, (course 9 0).ends_iff.mpr (by decide)⟩

end Witness
