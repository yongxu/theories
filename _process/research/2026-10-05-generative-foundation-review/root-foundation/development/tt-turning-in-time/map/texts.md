# The texts: where the central pattern is already said, and in what words

Reader of the texts (not of the Lean). Read only; nothing built or run. Line numbers are of the
working tree at main `372705add` (the pages are hand-written unless section 4 says otherwise).
Where a page names a Lean declaration that bears on the design, I copied its Lean statement from
PKG with grep/sed (file:line given) so the designer has it exact; every other Lean name below is
as the page writes it, its statement not read by me. Quotations from the pages are verbatim; "…"
marks a cut inside a quotation.

The pattern, in the task's letters: (a) relation and class; (b) going alike (同行) without constancy
(有常): among alike occurrences one has a next and another none, and the core (核) or another class
tells what the class does not; (c) a line the same in a respect to its end while what it depends on
runs down (level under a draw, point of no return); (d) it turns before the end only if what the
respect does not tell comes to make a difference while there is a next, and what would bring that
difference is often itself running out.
