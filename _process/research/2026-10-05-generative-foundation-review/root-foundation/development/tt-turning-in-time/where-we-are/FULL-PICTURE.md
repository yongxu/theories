# Where we are, what this can be, and how ready we are

Put together from my own measurements and four readers, each of whom read the same whole core from one side without seeing the others: [an alignment researcher](alignment.md), [a systems thinker](systems.md), [a philosopher](philosophy.md) and [a reader of the formal structure](formal.md). The brief they were given: `tmp/r7/where-we-are/BRIEF.md` (not kept here). Nothing in the repository was changed, built or run. Sources outside the repository are named from the readers' memory, not reopened.

## In short

The direction is sound. All four readers said so in nearly the same words: sound, but carried out in a way that hides it. The work is not ready to send. What separates it from ready is narrow: one model, one page, one picture, a few outside readers, one experiment. None needs new mathematics. What it needs is a centre, and stopping the breadth until the centre is closed.

## Where we are

**The minimal root.** The root is a language and its logic: a relation on one set, seen through a way of taking things as the same. Every one of its theorems holds of every relation, so none can state a threshold, a lock or a drain. "Everything derives from a minimal root" is already true in the only sense available (there are no axioms; the content sits in each model's definitions and each theorem's hypotheses), which is why it feels empty. Measuring unity as "distance from the grammar" was the wrong yardstick, and weeks went to it. The sound form of the early hope is one pattern that everything instances (理一分殊), not one premise from which everything follows.

**The one pattern.** Three readers found it separately, each in their own terms:

- formal: *a part that carries its own law*. A selection is a part of the world that runs by itself; what it leaves out cannot move it, and it can still use that up.
- systems: *a slow thing steers a fast flow and is rebuilt from that flow's results*. It appears six times in separate formal homes (reader and level, rule and members, rule and share, view and what its acts bring back, code and what is heard, pattern and carriers). The work has never said these are one form.
- philosophy: *closure*. What goes on is a region closed under its own conditions, and what such a region does not read cannot turn it.

**Are the parts separate?** At the centre, yes. The lock (a rule kept by its own picks) and the stock (what is drawn on and renews) are joined by words, not by a lemma; one is an open-growth model and the other a conservation model. On the published map, by the systems reader's count, 84 of 105 links move along one quantity or mark its threshold, 12 change the wiring (9 of them need something given from outside), and 6 have one quantity driving another, of which 4 are the joints of the central loop: three built examples and a reading. So what is proved is motion on fixed wiring. The places where structure and dynamics turn into each other are premises and examples. That is the exact answer to "how are structures and dynamics transformed and related": it is the part not yet done.

**Structure and dynamics.** Structure is the slow process. It limits what the fast flow can do; the fast flow, added up, rebuilds it or wears it away. A thing lasts where what steers its flows is rebuilt by those flows. The root has the pieces (a class the relation keeps constant; renewal at two grains; a rule that changes only at reading steps). Pace is not yet a quantity in the root, and how a loop first closes is everywhere a premise.

**The ethics and alignment are one question here.** The finding of route (b) (lasting selects only on what comes back; nothing shown forms a reading wider than what returns) and the central loop's ways back are the same result. In the systems reader's words: read as far as you draw. The open target (how scope forms) and the thinnest moment on the map (forming) are one gap: how a correcting loop comes to close. The map has not shown this.

**Novelty and evidence.** No new mathematics, as the work's own review says. What is new would be a formal version of what the alignment field argues only in words: a selection that wears away its own oversight ("gradual disempowerment"). The tests do not test what is distinctive: the fishers test no law, and no test updates a model's weights.

**Mass.** About 7,000 commits in five weeks, 8,500 markdown files, about 4 million words under the theory's folder, a 14,000-word front page, a "one page" that opens with a reading time of half an hour.

## The dualism, settled

The root is not dualist, and its own laws already state the rule. The dualism sits in three later constructions: value as a free slot beside the dynamics; amounts beside relations; a map of separate bands and yes/no boxes. And in the wording, where a sentence is dualist when its subject is nowhere shown as formed.

Four rules, each already a law in the Lean:

1. **Say the respect.** Nothing begins, lasts or ends except under a class. Every "same", "inside" and "loss" names its respect and who bears it.
2. **One relation, two ends.** Draw one line or one loop, never two boxes with an arrow added.
3. **Independence is a finding.** Claim that two things are separate only where it is shown.
4. **Valence enters by comparison, never by definition.** Stability and the lock are one structure, judged differently. Name who judges.

What it costs: no "outside" without "of what"; no "counts" or "loss" without "for whom"; no "emerges" where we mean "we hope to show". Of the traditions, 不一不异 fits best. Non-dual is not "all is one": "joint" must not blur who sets the rule, who is picked and who bears.

**The ethics in three sentences kept apart.** Shown: persisting filters choices toward keeping one's own line; that is prudence within a closure. Not shown: that anything comes to respond to a loss that never returns to it. Wanted: that choosers do, ourselves among them; this is a vow (愿), itself a pattern within becoming, which enters, then spreads, locks or dies. To make it exact, drop the value slot: a loss counts in a choosing where a difference in it changes what is chosen. Then ask whether such a response forms and lasts with nothing evaluative supplied. For alignment readers the same sentence is a design requirement, which the field accepts; it does not read metaethics.

## What this can be

One pattern, said once: anything that keeps going is a part that runs by its own law inside what it draws on. It is rebuilt from its own results. What it does not read cannot turn it, and it can still use that up. Whether it is turned in time depends on whether what registers the using-up still stands, and still weighs, when the rule is rebuilt.

The commons, the proxy problem, lock-in, capture, a tumour and gradual disempowerment are regimes of that one loop. The ethics (read as far as you draw) and alignment (keep the way back alive in the joint selecting of humans and AI) are the same question asked of it.

As a piece of work:

- **One model, one theorem, one condition.** State: share, stock, rule. The loop closes with nothing assumed where the level at which the fall shows lies below the level that those who can tell need in order to stand. The way back is a race among three times: the fall showing, those who can tell coming to outweigh at two resets running, and the stock passing its point of no return. Expected true; composition, not new mathematics; the nearest module is `PoolFromCourse`, with `RegimeMargin`'s step in place of the assumption `Accrues`.
- **Two pictures.** Wiring (what influences what: loops and the stocks they share) and a portrait (regions in four quantities: margin, return per turn, timing, resolution).
- **One experiment** on trained systems: a closed evaluator loop with real training, the round after which bringing the dissenting raters back no longer turns the judge forecast beforehand. Or first a recorded case told on the form (the systems reader suggests northern cod).
- **One note**, ten to fifteen pages, one language, a dozen Lean statements checked against the prose by a person. The repository becomes the archive behind it: the atlas as proved instances and named exceptions, the crosswalk as known names first.

## How ready, and what would make us ready

Not ready. In order:

1. **Choose the centre.** The loop is the door, not "how anything forms". This is a decision and costs nothing.
2. **One page before any build**: the loop and the ethics in the four rules' language, for Ren's read.
3. **Close the loop in one model** in Lean. One board item, one session. Then prove or refute each "instance" claim.
4. **Redraw the map on that model**: one wiring picture, one portrait, one language per page.
5. **Readers from beyond**: three or four people, five pages, before more text. Who is sent what is Ren's.
6. **The experiment.** Paid runs and new test designs are held; lifting the hold for this one is Ren's.

What to stop meanwhile: counting distance from the grammar; new families, crosswalk groups and map rounds; rounds of AI readers scoring sentences.

## Is something wrong in the thinking?

Not in the aim. Three things, by the philosopher's reading and mine:

- **The work is on its own first path.** Its rule (prove, qualify, let AI readers score) is set from its own picks; what it does not read (can a person read it, is anything new) has grown. Ren's corrections were absorbed: three times he asked for the instructions to change and each AI rewrite kept the defensive frame. This is mostly ours, the AI sessions', not his.
- **"Dualist" carried too much.** It became a test of words and produced a private language. It is a test of constructions.
- **Two sentences overshoot.** "No right or wrong, just dynamics": right and wrong are real as formed things; there is none from nowhere. "The ethics emerges from continuation": today a programme, not a finding.

And one from me: steering by verdict after a build, with outside readers and runs both on hold, leaves the work with no return from beyond itself. A small sketch read early does more than a round judged late.
