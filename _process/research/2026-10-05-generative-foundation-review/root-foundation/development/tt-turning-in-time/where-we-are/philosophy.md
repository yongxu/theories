# Settling the dualism: a philosopher's reading

*Outside texts are cited from memory, unchecked.*

**In short.** The root is not dualist; its own laws already state the principle Ren seeks. The dualism sits in three later constructions: value as a free slot beside the dynamics, amounts beside relations, and a map of separate bands and yes/no boxes. The direction is sound; the writing hides it, being still organized by questions it says it has dropped.

## 1. What the dualism is

In Ren's words "dualist" names four things: two kinds given first and joined afterwards ("me" and "world"; "registration"); a standard outside what it judges ("correct", "significance", "warrant"; 诚实"也在map之中，而不是map之上"); a valenced pair that takes a side (good and evil, ranking); a self standing alone (self and environment have "never been two"). The common error: one end of a relation is taken as standing by itself (自立).

Four things look alike. A theory needs three: a distinction within one process, relative to a respect; two roles of one relation; two ends of one quantity. The fourth is the error, wherever it is assumed and not found: two kinds, each specifiable with the other absent, that then interact.

A non-dual account names, for each pair, the one relation and the respect, and keeps every difference that does work. It must not merge the two into a One, posit a ground, or draw an ethics from relatedness alone (08 §1).

## 2. Where the theory itself is dualist

- **Relation, relata.** Elements of a carrier come first; equality on it is used, not derived (08 §9). *Declared. Open, minor.*
- **Class.** Sameness is in a respect; no mark stands alone (`«四相无自立»`, 01 §3). *The best non-dual device here. But classes are supplied. Open.*
- **Root, World.** Amounts are not read off relations; balance "represented, not derived" (08 §7). *A second primitive, conceded. Open.*
- **Selector, selected.** In closed capture the rule is formed from what it picks, its readers by the course (02 §9, §10). *Settled formally: the central result is itself non-dual. Not in wording.*
- **Knower, known.** "one law within the unfolding, not a subject facing a world" (03 §12). *Settled formally; undone by the map's band "A view and how things are".*
- **Fact, value.** `sig` is a free predicate; "better" is free; self-concern is a hypothesis in every proof (07 §1, §4). *The split, built into the types. Read as own closure, value becomes reach, as 07 §4.7 admits. Open; the main one.*
- **Self, other.** Asks whether my effects are "my own" (07 §4.7). *Honestly argued. But stretching "mine" over the village is monism (all is one), not non-duality.*
- **Model, actual; proved, not.** Kept apart; their meeting shown in tests. *Needed. In wording, a rank on every sentence.*
- **Structure, dynamics.** A structure is a class the relation keeps constant (01 §4). *Settled at the root. The map draws boxes and arrows as two kinds, and its web of roles falls into three unconnected pieces.*
- **Human, AI.** Both formations. *Whether an AI has a continuation of its own is unsaid.*
- **Map, mapped.** 08 §12 applies the laws to the work. *Said, not acted on (§7).*

## 3. Where the wording and the map are dualist

- *Agent acts on object*: "a selection … reaches".
- *Inside and outside as places*: "couples into it"; "beyond" (14 times, one page); "Set from outside"; "given from outside".
- *A yes and a no as two boxes*: "What it does comes back" / "Nothing it does comes back".
- *Valenced words*: "winners", "loss", "ways out".
- *A standard over the thing judged*: "Must anyone hold the condition?"; "counts", with no one named who counts.

English grammar is not the fault: a sentence is dualist when its subject is nowhere shown as formed. Rewrites:

1. "A selection … reaches only what couples into it; … can drain what lies beyond."
   → A choosing changes only with what it reads. Followed to its limit, it runs down whatever else it uses.
   取舍只随所读而变；读不到的，再怎么增减，取舍照旧。照此取到极处，用着而没读到的，就用到底。
   Lost: the picture of a boundary. Gained: "beyond" becomes "makes no difference to", checkable.
2. "winning comes to buy power over the rule"
   → Where what the rule picks more of also sets the rule, the two hold each other in place.
   规则多取谁，谁又来定规则：相因而固。
   Lost: Ren's political edge. Gained: neither side is the agent.
3. "Shaping the rule costs" / "Shaping the rule pays".
   → One line: what comes back to the shapers alone, against what shaping costs them.
   改规则者独得之所返，比其所费：不及，相当，过之。
   Lost: two names. Gained: the wanted place and the lock are one quantity at two values.
4. "A view and how things are"; "Checked against the thing itself".
   → A view and what comes back to it; "Just tried where it could have failed".
   所见与所返；刚试过，本可不合。
   Lost: the realist phrase readers trust. Gained: what 03 §12 says.
5. "What counts a loss that returns to no one is the one step"
   → The bed's death brings nothing back to the fishers. We want choosers, ourselves among them, whose choosing still changes with it. That is our want, not a law.
   贻贝床之死，于渔者无所返。我们所愿：取舍者（我们在内）仍为之而动。此是愿，不是律。
   Lost: the step's logical place. Gained: someone is named who counts.

## 4. How to settle it

The work's own laws make the rule.

1. **Say the respect.** Nothing begins, lasts or ends except under a class. Every "same", "inside" and "loss" names its respect and its bearer.
2. **One relation, two ends.** Two terms are a pair where one cannot be had without the other (`«能所相待»`, 06 §四). Draw one line or one loop, never two boxes with an arrow added.
3. **Independence is a finding.** Describing two parts separately loses exactly their relation (`separation_faithful_iff`, 01 §9.2). Claim separateness only where shown.
4. **Valence enters by comparison, never by definition.** Stability and the lock are one structure, judged differently (06 §十). Name who judges.

Of the traditions 不一不异 (neither one nor different) fits best, as proved here: where nothing conditions itself, under identity nothing continues, and under some class every condition is a continuation (`«非一非异»`, 06 §三). Zhang Zai, of pairs: 两不立则一不可见，一不可见则两之用息 (without the two the one is not seen; without the one the two stop working). Whitehead's misplaced concreteness: a box is a class, not a thing. 对立统一 (unity of opposites) fits the map's rows, where one quantity crosses a mark, not the primitives. The two truths (conventional and ultimate) help least: "there are no two realities" (Ren, 12 Oct). Spinoza's modes posit a ground; 一即一切 (one is all) denies the one-way dependence the work proves.

The cost: no "outside" without "of what"; no "counts" or "loss" without "for whom"; no "emerges" for "we hope to show".

## 5. The ethics

Coherent, if three sentences are kept apart.

- *Shown.* Persisting filters choices: those left are consistent with keeping one's own line above ending it (07 §4.9). That is prudence within a closure; selection by share is blind beyond it (07 §4.14).
- *Not shown.* That any formation comes to respond to a loss that never returns to it.
- *Wanted.* That choosers do. This is a vow (愿), itself a pattern within becoming: it enters as a variation, then spreads, locks or dies.

So "the ethics emerges from continuation" is a programme, not a finding. The "step of invariance" is the same vow in the vocabulary of warrant, which Ren refused on 8 November.

It can be made exact. Drop the value slot. A loss *counts in* a choosing where a difference in it changes what is chosen; the map's first path already defines counting so. Then ask: with nothing evaluative supplied, does a response to non-returning loss form (say by 推己及人, its own class not telling another's case from its own), and does it last? A model and a frozen test could show it; it fails if every such response is supplied or removed. So far the results say selection by return does not favour it and can favour its opposite; it must form another way.

Spinoza reaches others through shared advantage and stops at animals: route (b) and its limit, proved here. Jonas takes the step but grounds it in a good in being, refused here. Varela, Thompson and Di Paolo supply what is missing: an order arises as a precarious system regulates toward its own margin, not as a survivor filter; the pieces lie unjoined (02 §8, 03 §10, 04 §8). Cheng Hao and Wang Yangming hold the response is already there, only obstructed. Śāntideva has the work's exact shape: no boundary is privileged, and compassion is generated as a vow, not derived.

The alignment frame is the same move and holds: a target standing apart is a standard over the thing judged; the joint selection asks what the loop reads and whether it can still be turned. Its risk is monist: "joint" must not blur who sets the rule, who is picked and who bears.

## 6. The early hope

As "axioms from which everything follows" it was confused: a calculus true of every relation forbids none, and content enters with the situation. The sound form is 理一分殊: one pattern, instanced, not one premise, deduced. Ren, 24 October: "truth meets situationality". The pattern is not the word "continuation", which a rival can drop (08 §9). It is closure: what goes on is a region closed under its own conditions, and what such a region does not read cannot turn it. The core, 生生, the lock and a view kept on its known road are this one form; closure already carries 42 of 126 central rows. Balance is a second form beside it.

## 7. Direction

Sound, and hidden by its execution. Three things are wrong, none of them the aim.

- The work is on its own first path. Its rule (prove, qualify, let AI readers score) is set from its own picks; what it does not read (can a person read it, is anything new) has grown. Three times Ren asked for AGENTS.md to change; "each AI rewrite kept the defensive frame" (9 Nov).
- "Dualist" carries too much. It became a test of words, which left the dualism and produced a private language, and a line between West and China that 06 §六 does not support.
- "No right or wrong, just dynamics" overshoots; no non-dual tradition concludes it. Right and wrong are real as formed things; there is none from nowhere.
