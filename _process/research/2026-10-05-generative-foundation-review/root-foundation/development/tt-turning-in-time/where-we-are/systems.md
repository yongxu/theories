## 2. Structure and dynamics

Structure is what changes slowly compared with what you are watching (Bertalanffy: structures are slow processes). The slow thing limits what the fast flow can do: a constraint (Pattee; Montévil and Mossio 2015). The fast flow, added up in a stock, rebuilds the slow thing or wears it away. So each makes the other, and structure at one pace is flow at the next slower one (Simon 1962). A thing lasts where its constraints are rebuilt by the flows they steer. A regime (a region a course stays in) changes when the slow thing drifts until the fast flow settles elsewhere, often one-way.

Already here: what a level is, a class the relation goes alike across (01 §4; 03 §6): lumpability in exact form, a real asset; a pattern going on through renewal of its parts, at any grain (01 §6; 02 §1); a rule changing only at reading steps (02 §8); the closed loop in small (04 §8; 05 §12).

Missing:
- The root's class describes; it does not constrain. Constraint lives in the World's needs and in `Pace`'s rule, apart.
- Pace is not a quantity in the root: a grain is a class, not a rate.
- Change of wiring is mostly assumed (§3); forming is the thinnest moment: how a loop first closes is a premise (links 18, 41, 86).

## 3. The integrating form

**Units.** Things that keep going, each with a level (how much stands) and a pace (how fast it turns over). A stock, a rule, a view, a code and a body are one kind of unit. A rule is a unit whose level is read by flows that do not use it up.

**One relation.** "x's level enters y's change": the root's condition. Two marks: an amount moves, or the level is only read; and it runs slow to fast (steers) or fast to slow (rebuilds).

**Four quantities**, already on the sheet: by my reading each of the 17 proved boundaries is a threshold in one. Margin: level against need, taking against giving (6). Return per turn, against one (5). Timing: a delay against a deadline (2). Resolution: what can be told apart (4).

**The four moments.** Forming: a loop closes. Persisting: each turn returns at least what it spends. Changing: a slow unit moves and the fast flow settles elsewhere. Ending: the margin is gone and the loop opens.

**The central case.** A unit draws on more than it reads; its rule is rebuilt from its own results; the draw wears away what would have read it. The ethics' scope is the same statement: read as far as you draw. So the open target (how scope forms) and the thin moment (forming) are one gap: how a correcting loop comes to close.

**Test on the 105 links** (my reading). 84 move along one quantity or mark its threshold: margin 28, return 22, timing 8, resolution 26. 12 change the wiring; 9 of these need something given from outside. 6 have one quantity driving another, such as a falling margin removing readers; four are the joints of the central loop: three built examples and a reading. 3 do not fit (50, 100, 105). So what is proved is motion on fixed wiring. The two places where structure and dynamics turn into each other are premises and examples.

**What the sheet cannot show.** The bands are five views of one loop, and band E is its general statement, filed as a fifth subject. The arrows mean "this state leads to that"; a systems reader expects "this influences that". Transitions have few loops; influence is all loops. Hence the arrows are hard to follow.

**Topology and dimensions.** Two pictures, one meaning of arrow in each. Wiring: its shape is its loops and the stocks they share. Portrait: regions in the four quantities, with edges and one-way crossings. Three or more dimensions are real as these coordinates, with pace as a ladder; a 3D graph adds nothing. The Lean defines the quantities model by model; their unity is my reading.

**The other serious form** is Holling's adaptive cycle, nested by pace. I would not choose it: it asserts an order of phases, and no law here gives one.

## 5. What is known, what is added

Known under my fields' names: joint excess (the commons, overshoot); the point of no return (the fold, May 1977); a selection reaching what couples in (requisite variety, Ashby 1956); the core (lumpability, Kemeny and Snell 1960; Simon's near-decomposability is not named in chapters or crosswalk); the lock (success to the successful, Senge 1990; Arthur 1989).

**A rule rebuilt from its own results that removes what would have corrected it.** My fields hold this as diagrams and special models, not one theorem.
- Drift to low performance (Meadows 2008): the goal is reset from past results, so the correcting gap shrinks.
- Shifting the burden (Senge): the quick fix wears away the capacity for the real correction. The crosswalk marks it not carried, for want of an outside helper; without the helper it is `PoolFromCourse`.
- Nearest formal statement: Ensign and others 2018. Patrols go where incidents were recorded, and incidents are recorded only where patrols go; in an urn model the allocation locks whatever the true rates.

The account adds: the exact condition under which a lock ignores a loss (`Unread`); the count deciding whether a late reading turns the rule (`ReadInTime.kept_iff`); that no step needs anyone to aim at the removal (02 §10); exits sorted by what each needs; premises shown failing one at a time. That is rigour on small whole-number models, not new mechanism, as 08 §8 says. My fields have and the account lacks: rates, hysteresis, building up, calibrated cases.

## 6. Language

Besides §3's level, pace, margin and return per turn: Stock 存量: how much of something stands. Loop 回路: influence that returns to its start. Reinforcing, balancing 增强、调节: a loop returning more of a change, or less. Reads 读: a level enters a change without being used up. Constraint 约束: a slow thing limiting a fast flow. Weighting, for "rule": what a selection goes by.

Rewritten:
1. "A weighting responds only to stocks whose levels reach it; followed to its best, it draws every unread stock down to the lowest level any allowed outcome leaves."
2. "Several outflows, each smaller than the inflow, can sum to more than it; where the inflow needs the stock above some level, enough such steps stop the inflow and everything needing that level."
3. "A reinforcing loop turns only while a balancing loop still closes through the stock it draws down: that level must reach the weighting, with enough weight, before the stock falls below where it regrows."

Misleading in my fields: system and environment (the describer draws the line; the central law is about what crosses it); controller and controlled; goal; externality; trap, tragedy, collapse; attractor (suggests a pull); positive and negative feedback (heard as good and bad).
