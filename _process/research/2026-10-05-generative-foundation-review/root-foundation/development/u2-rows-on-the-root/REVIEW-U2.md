# U2's design: the independent review and how each finding is taken

AI review, 29 October 2026. One independent reviewer, read-only, given the design, the scratch and its sources, not the designer's conclusions. It rebuilt the scratch (48 theorems in 68 declarations, no axioms), reran the dependency check, and checked all 24 record ids and coverage regions. It found that every reading's hypotheses match its row exactly, so nothing was weakened or strengthened. Its findings concern what the design claimed about the readings, and placement: 5 errors, 14 should-fix, 2 wording groups.

Every finding is taken. The scratch now has 77 theorems in 120 declarations, every axiom report empty ([log](check/scratch-5.log)), and the dependency check reports what each reading keeps in other terms ([log](check/deps.log)). The [design](DESIGN.md) is rewritten to match.
