# The free-energy principle as part of the theory, explaining more than the principle

29 October 2026. AI-written plan under [UE](PLAN.md), for Ren's request that the free-energy principle become an integral part of the whole theory and that the theory explain more than the principle ([source](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-29-fep-integral-explains-more.md)). It sets what "integral" and "explains more" mean here, what stands now, and the items that close the gap, each claimed on the board before its Lean.

## 1. What the request asks, in checkable terms

- **Integral.** Each quantity and structural claim of the principle is stated in the theory, with its premises, in Lean where it is a law, and connected to the spine rather than standing beside it:
  - belief: the posterior over hidden causes given a record;
  - surprisal: the negative logarithm of a record's probability;
  - variational free energy, its bound on surprisal and the bound's equality exactly at the posterior, with the decompositions (complexity less accuracy; divergence from the posterior plus surprisal);
  - expected free energy, with its risk and ambiguity, or epistemic and pragmatic, terms;
  - the Markov blanket: internal, sensory, active and external states with sparse coupling;
  - self-evidencing, and the existence claim that whatever persists appears to minimize free energy.
- **Explains more.** The theory states, in the principle's own terms, what the principle leaves open or claims beyond what holds. Each such statement is a theorem with its hypotheses, a condition that would refute its reading, and its standing (known in a neighbouring field, or not). The principle's claims are placed where they hold and where they do not. Where the principle states more than the theory can (continuous time, densities, Langevin flows, the Helmholtz decomposition of a steady-state flow), the plan says so.

1. **UE16. What free-energy minimization does not guarantee** (the third parts; pure core; landed 29 October as `Becoming.FreeEnergyGuarantees` with two records, [identical cells](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/identical-cells-under-a-descent-treating-them-alike.md) at 05 §9 and [risk minimization](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/risk-minimization-on-a-shared-renewal.md) at 05 §1, study section 449). On an explicit model of each claim, in the principle's terms:
   - *Identical cells* (Friston, Levin, Sengupta and Pezzulo 2015, candidate 1): a deterministic descent that commutes with exchanging cells keeps an exchange-fixed start exchange-fixed, so identical cells minimizing a free energy symmetric in them never part. What parts them is a start or a law that breaks the symmetry, as the paper's random initial expectations did.
   - *Risk minimization and needs* (Albarracin and others 2024, candidate 2): where others draw more than a gated renewal gives, an agent's need on that condition ends whatever its policy, so in particular whatever policy minimizes its risk; the agent's model cannot change that, and no later reading selects its restraint.
   - *Shared models and preferences* (candidate 3): agents with one generative model and alike preferences, each indexical (its own store), each within what a step gives, can together exceed it and end the renewal they depend on.
   Each with its hypotheses, its refutation condition and its standing: known results (symmetry breaking; the commons) brought into the principle's terms. This changes UE1's record from a restatement to a containment with third parts, each forbidding a statement in the principle's literature.
