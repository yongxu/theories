# UE: a unified explanation of reality (plan)

25 October 2026. AI-authored plan for the objective ([source](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-25-unified-explanation-of-reality.md)). It covers:

- the objective in the account's terms, and why that representation;
- what there is already, and the ordered path;
- the structural gaps;
- what would show the objective failing.

The [board](../../../../../BECOMING-WORKBOARD.md) holds the claims, and results are stated in their records and home sections, not here.

## 1. The objective

The theory already claims universal invariants of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away, and its first priority is an account of the whole of becoming ([scope](../../../../../../GOALS.md#theory-of-becoming)). A unified explanation of reality asks more than invariants: that the established frameworks of the sciences be its cases. It sharpens that aim and starts no second theory.

In the account's terms, the theory explains reality as one unfolding where four things hold.

1. **Containment.** Each domain's established framework is a case of the grammar's laws under premises named on the [premise card](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#premise-card). The domains include the physics of dissipation, life and evolution, inference and cognition, collectives and markets, and computation. A containment has three parts:
   - the framework's premises, set on the card;
   - an instance or equivalence theorem in Lean;
   - something the theory forbids that the framework leaves open.

   A containment without the third part is a restatement and is recorded as one. The containment also lists, as a debt, what the framework states that the theory cannot yet.
2. **Representation.** The models the laws are proved on (processes, the World, the counting layer) are represented in the grammar, so that a law is one law and not one per model. This is the lifting OU and EC carry.
3. **Premises from weaker ones.** Each premise a containment needs is derived from a weaker one where possible, or calibrated:
   - balance from the first law (done, [01](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-balance-from-the-first-law));
   - the least loss of dissipation from the second law in its stochastic form (UE3);
   - conditional weights from counting (UE2);
   - a framework's own premise read as a state of the law of exchange.
4. **Its own knowing.** The explanation contains the knower inside the unfolding:
   - what a knower can settle, verify and refute ([03](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#refutation));
   - where a law needs a classical principle ([08](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#calibrations));
   - what no family of readings can say of itself ([03](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-readings-within-what-they-read)).

   An explanation of reality that cannot say where its own knowing stops is not one of reality.

## 2. Why this representation

- **Unification as explanation.** One account in the philosophy of science holds that a theory explains by deriving many conclusions from few patterns of argument. Its known difficulty is that a bare conjunction of laws "unifies" everything. The answer is stringency: patterns that constrain what may be filled into them. The containment's third part is that stringency, stated as what is forbidden.
- **Farther means forbidding more, not covering more.** The free-energy principle covers every persisting thing and, by its proponents' account, forbids nothing as a principle; its content lies in its process theories. A theory reaches farther than a framework where it states what the framework states and forbids something the framework leaves open. Covering more with a weaker statement goes less far.
- **The debts are the work queue.** For the free-energy principle they are surprisal, belief and continuous time ([UE1 note](../ue1-fep-bridge/NOTE.md#3-its-premises-on-the-premise-card) §3).
- **The relational start stays a start.** That whatever is, is how it relates within one unfolding is the theory's philosophical thesis ([08](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)), and nonduality is a requirement of method. The objective claims no proof of the ontology.

## 3. What there is

- **Restatements awaiting their third part.** Every central result is, by the method chapter's own review, near-definitional or a known result restated ([08 §8](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)). The restated frameworks include:
  - finite Poincaré recurrence, Landauer and Bennett;
  - ranking functions and Lyapunov;
  - opportunity sets;
  - Blackwell, Pearl and the proxy problem;
  - the commons and positional goods;
  - Gold, Fine and Wilf;
  - the is–ought gap;
  - Perron–Frobenius;
  - Schrödinger, Prigogine and Petri nets;
  - Price, Fisher and Hamilton in counting form.

  These are containments with their first two parts and, mostly, no third.
- **Unifications claimed as theses.** Four results known in substance are claimed as one law across fields ([08 §9](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#what-the-unifications-claim)): selection through couplings across reward, training, natural selection, prices and votes (T3); C as the whole of the order (T5); one merge across computation, heat, extinction and forgetting; and overshoot, externality and Goodhart's law as one law of the optimum. As theses they forbid what the source fields leave open, and their tests are the objective's evidence.
- **The first new containment, a restatement so far.** The free-energy principle (UE1), in kit: its premises on the card, and `Becoming.ChoiceCountCapture`. Its review found the kit sound and the third part, as first stated, not surviving:
  - the lock of a rule read from the count of its choices is the lock-in of increasing returns (Arthur 1989), which active inference's own simulation of devaluation already shows (Friston and others 2016, §5.2), and counting the action taken is psychology's value-free habit;
  - the contrast with counting the posterior at the time of choosing concerns a rule active inference does not use: it counts the end-of-trial posterior, close to the executed policy;
  - the pooling result is a reading of the pool law under its hypotheses.

  What the kit adds is exactness for a simplified model and the edges of that exactness (a moved count, a stale rule, a change of score read late). A real third part needs a statement in the principle's literature that the theory forbids. The first candidate, a claim that belief sharing lets a collective respond to what no member couples to, was searched for and not found ([search](../ue1-fep-bridge/THIRD-PART-SEARCH.md): 16 claims from 15 papers). Seven claims are forbidden under a natural reading by other laws of the theory: symmetry (01's commuting law, 05 §9's shared codes) and joint excess. Each is a known result, and each forbids a verbal generalization, not a paper's simulation ([corrections](../ue1-fep-bridge/NOTE-CORRECTIONS.md) §3).

- **What the audit found (UE0, [audit](../ue0-third-parts/AUDIT.md)).** Of the 21 rows of 08 §8 and §9, four have a third part, three of them distinct:
  - gated joint excess (row 7);
  - the optimum laws (row 20, which row 19's drain is);
  - weakly, the order of what can form (row 5).

  Beyond those rows, so does Price, Fisher and Hamilton joined to the flow, which 08 §8's table lacks. Every third part joins a framework that lacks conservation, renewal over time or a threshold to a premise or law of the theory's own, and each forbidden statement is already known in a neighbouring field: critical depensation, the Malthusian bound, bioeconomic overharvesting, pinwheel scheduling. So the theory reaches farther than each framework alone, and not farther than the sciences taken together. That fixes what the objective can claim. Its contribution so far is the joining itself, one account with its premises in one place. Reaching farther than the sciences together needs results no neighbouring field states. The candidates:
  - the second-order direction of 生生 (UE4);
  - the audit's ranked unproved third parts: the pace of selection bounded by the inflow, a counting bound on surviving expected joint excess, what lies beyond falling along improvement;
  - frozen tests that could fail.

## 4. The path

Ordered by what each containment would show. Each item is claimed on the board before work, reviewed independently before landing, and lands through the queue.

1. **UE1, the free-energy principle** (claimed). The most developed rival unification of self-organization and cognition, and the one offered as the basis of collective human and AI intelligence.
2. **UE0, the audit of the third parts.** For each restated framework in 08 §8, does the theory forbid something the source field leaves open? Where it does, state it as a thesis with its refutation. Where it does not, record the restatement. This tells the theory where it reaches farther and where it does not, before it claims more.
3. **UE2, conditional weights and surprisal** on the counting layer the core already has (`Becoming.Weighted`: the sum over histories exact, every weighting a premise; `Becoming.Typicality`: Chebyshev, Chernoff and the counting law of large numbers):
   - Bayes's rule as the weight of the courses agreeing with a record;
   - the share of a set of courses as the counting form of surprisal;
   - the variational bound in the periphery, where logarithms are needed;
   - the chance of leaving what a continuation needs, bounded by its margin over its spread.

   This pays the free-energy principle's main debt.
4. **UE3, stochastic thermodynamics.** Every least loss physics supplies is a theorem there ([08 §8](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#beyond-the-table)). Deriving the World's dissipation from a fluctuation theorem would derive a premise from a stated physics.
5. **Constructor theory.** Its laws say which transformations are possible, which impossible, and why. The theory's admission and widening speak of what can form. The containment would read tasks as admissions and test whether widening is the order constructor theory's possible tasks carry.
6. **Causal sets.** The root's precedence is the reach of the condition relation, strict exactly where the net has no cycle ([01](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#one-kind-of-symbol)), and a causal set is a locally finite order. The containment would state the root's laws on causal sets and ask what continuation relative to a class adds to an order: patterns that last, on spacetime read as order.
7. **Assembly theory and the adjacent possible.** The second-order direction of 生生, a range whose widening keeps widening, is the theory's least formalized part ([07](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-along-a-reading)). Assembly theory measures selection by the steps that build an object, and the adjacent possible states how the possible grows. Containing them would give 生生 its formal second half.
8. **Quantum probability.** Contextuality already holds as gluing under parity ([03](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-gluing-under-parity)). Quantum probability needs UE2 and more, so it comes last.

## 5. The structural gaps

The overview's open list names what a full explanation must face ([§10](../../../../../../theories/becoming/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#10-what-is-not-shown-and-what-is-open)):

- **Self-changing conditions and presence in time,** which the grammar cannot state. This is the deepest gap: a full explanation of reality has to state conditions that change themselves, and the present.
- **Real amounts and continuous time,** partly measured (XL2).
- **A faithful representation of every becoming in the grammar,** open.
- **The fit of the test systems** (decay, a vortex, an organism, an institution, a belief system), modelled since GS3 and not yet confronted with data.

## 6. What would show the objective failing

- A containment whose third part stays empty after an honest search: there, the theory restates the framework, and the record says so.
- A framework's central result that cannot be stated in the grammar under any named premise: an expressive limit. Self-changing conditions and presence in time are the known candidates.
- A frozen test of a forbidden statement that fails, for example UE1's test of whether a count of choices reverses under a standing score.
- Readers from the contained field finding a containment trivial or wrong. The human review is the check no internal selector can run ([08 §12](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#the-work-by-its-own-laws)).

## 7. What it serves

- **Shared understanding.** Readers who hold a unifying framework can check a containment in their own terms. A unified account is where coordination across fields can begin: prediction aligns understanding, and aligned understanding lets people coordinate.
- **Alignment.** The free-energy principle is offered as the basis of collective intelligence. The selection laws say what any such basis can reach, and AJ's conditions, stated for every selection, carry into each framework of mind the theory contains.
