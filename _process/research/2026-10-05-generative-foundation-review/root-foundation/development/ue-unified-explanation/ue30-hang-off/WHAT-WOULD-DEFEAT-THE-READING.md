# What would defeat the central reading

The figures read [the shared-laws data](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/data/shared-laws.json), a measurement of Lean dependency at core receipt `20261102T051236.940686Z`, and a walk over the Lean constants this note describes. *About twenty minutes to read; its home in 08 §9 is the short form.*

## The gap

"That reading a subject as continuation relative to a class *explains* how it goes on is the theory's claim, argued from the laws on the models" ([01](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md)). Nothing said what would defeat it, though an argued philosophical thesis owes "what it forbids and what would refute it" ([08 §2](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md)). The cold read of 2 November 2026 found that neither reader could say what refutes a thesis, even after an hour, and [its report](../../review-packet/COLD-READ-2026-11-02.md) names this gap too.

This note takes [08 §1's five steps](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#five-step-test), written for a claim that two things are constitutively joined, because they fit. Its tests are the ones UE set itself ([plan §2](../PLAN.md#2-why-this-representation)): that a theory explains "by deriving many conclusions from few patterns of argument", and "Farther means forbidding more, not covering more".

## The answer

No single thing would refute the account as a whole, because it is not one claim. The sentence above says four things, and they differ in what could defeat them.

| The reading as | Defeated by | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|
| a language | nothing | holds, and forbids nothing |
| a faithful carrying of a domain's law | a proof | processes and the World's relations carried; amounts not, since no reading of the selected relations gives the count; the rest open |
| a class named for a domain | a measurement or an intervention | T2 has this form [AI reading, new]: of three frozen tests, one passed |
| an explanation, against its strongest rival | a rival deriving the same laws from fewer patterns, or a field working without the reading reaching 08 §8's results first | on a first count, a tie; no such result in hand |

The first sense is the one the sentence is most easily heard in, and it explains nothing. The fourth is the one the sentence claims, and on a first count it ties: the pattern explains, if it does, under either name, and the word adds nothing.

## Step 1. Four claims in one sentence

- **A language.** Anything that goes on can be read as continuation relative to some class.
- **A faithful carrying.** A domain's law can be carried into the grammar and back with nothing its explanation needs lost.
- **A class named for a domain.** This relation and this class, named apart from the course, are what this subject's going on turns on.
- **An explanation.** More than its strongest rival, this reading is what derives these laws and finds new ones.

## Step 2. The strongest alternative

The rival keeps every model and every theorem and drops the reading. Field by field it has stocks and flows with balance and thresholds, labelled transition systems with simulation, bisimulation and quotient, supervisory control under partial observation, and opportunity sets. Its strongest form is one account: a relation algebra with conservation, stating all of these with no word for continuation.

Such a rival loses no exclusion. Continuation is a definition: `def «续» (R E : «际» α) : «际» α := «合» R E`, the meet of a relation and a class ([`Becoming.Grammar`](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Grammar.lean)). Whatever a law states with it can be stated by unfolding it. Nor does the rival have fewer axioms, since "a **Reading** changes no theorem". So a rival's exclusions cannot serve as a defeater.

That exempts nothing. Entropy and fitness are definitions too, and no theorem needs either word. The plan's account counts patterns, not words, and a rival made by unfolding the word guarantees a tie and reaches nothing first, so it decides nothing. What decides is a different store: one deriving the same laws from fewer patterns, which would make the reading's pattern dispensable, or a field working without the reading and reaching first what the reading was to find.

## Step 3. A change in the thing, or in its label

Calling a course "continuation" changes nothing in the course. So the question of each law is whether its derivation uses the reading's pattern, and whether a specialist would have foreseen it. Both can be checked.

## Step 4. The countermodel that matters

The class is free. Under the one class every relation is its own continuation (`«一之续»`), any single condition is a continuation under some class (`«任一缘可为续»`), and identity is constant under every relation (`«静有常»`). So the reading alone excludes no course.

This is relevant and no toy, because it is what the free-energy principle's existence claim faces: it "forbids nothing alone, as its proponents concede", and forbidding "needs a weighting fixed apart from the course, or a law of the courses themselves" ([the page](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/FREE-ENERGY-PRINCIPLE.md)). The theory found the same levels for a blanket's existence ([03 §1](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket)): with parts read from the course every family has one, and a partition fixed apart from the family can be refuted, even by one run. The reading has those levels too:

| The relation and class are | What is excluded |
|---|---|
| read off the course | nothing |
| named apart from the course | that the class is constant for the relation: two members alike under it going on differently refute that |
| joined to a law with content | what that law's hypotheses exclude |

## Step 5. What would defeat each

**A language: nothing could.** Any carrier, relation and class will do. It is a choice of words, and under UE's test it goes no farther than the existence claim. The account should say so where it makes the claim.

**A faithful carrying: a proof.** It is defeated for a domain by a proof that no faithful representation exists, or by a carrying that needs a primitive beyond a relation and a class. No measurement bears on it.

- Processes are carried both ways, and the World's relations; the World's amounts are not ([01 §8.6](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-world-represented-both-ways)). Every other model, and every becoming, is open.
- The account has already shown what this row asks for. Amounts are "the root's second sort": "balance represented by selected unit matchings, not derived" ([01](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-amounts-by-unit-matchings)), and no reading of the selected relations gives the count (`World.standing_not_read`). Until the sort is derived from relations, this sense is conceded there. The [audit](../../ue0-third-parts/AUDIT.md) found "The third parts are the conservation joins", so this is no marginal case.
- The thesis just above the home paragraph in 08 §9 is of configurations, laws and parts. Amounts are not among them, and the home now says so.
- Extending the grammar after each failure is what 08 §1 warns of: "do not widen "the full condition" after each objection". An extension in relations answers the failure. Amounts as a second primitive concede it.

**A class named for a domain: a measurement or an intervention.** Name a relation and a class for a domain before the data. The claim is that what is alike under the class goes on alike, as far as anything measured shows. Three things defeat it: two members alike under the class going on differently beyond measured error; another class of no more variables leaving less of the next state unaccounted for; or the course changing when what the class does not read is changed, the relation held fixed. The third is the strongest, because a class can close in observation through a confound and fail under intervention.

T2 has this form [AI reading, new]: a renewable condition's state is its level, its recent giving and one lag, and "a fourth variable removing a tenth of the residual variance" is among what it forbids ([02](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#t2)). Of its three frozen tests one passed, all three on fish-stock records, so none is an intervention. A failure defeats the class named, not the reading, which could always name another. That is the immunity of a free class, so naming another class after a failure is a new claim with its own frozen test, not a rescue. No single failure defeats the general claim. Named classes failing across the domains tried would take its support away. T2 is the one thesis read so here, and T6's and T7's tests may have this form too. Where no class but equality closes, the reading has no pattern to speak of in that domain.

**An explanation, against its rival: a comparison.** Count the patterns. The account's own count is the five shared laws of [08 §7](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#shared-laws-and-connectivity), each stating "a mechanism that many central results share". The rival has each under a known name.

| Shared law | The rival's name for it |
|---|---|
| Closure | reachability, the reflexive-transitive closure |
| Balance | conservation, stocks and flows |
| Finite runs | finite recurrence, termination by a ranking function |
| Lumping | lumpability, bisimulation, quotient by a congruence |
| Order | monotonicity of opportunity sets |

Whether a class goes alike is the lumping row, and constancy meets it on determined, total relations (`«有常定后则同行»`, `«同行遍后则有常»`). Only finite runs' `«尽则不或屡»` reaches continuation, at the one class `«一»`, so this grain cannot show a sixth. So on this first count the rival has no fewer patterns and no more. A tie does not make the reading false. It means the pattern explains, if it does, under either name, and the word adds nothing. The count is the AI's, at the grain of shared laws and not argument by argument. A finer count might find that some row is dispensable, and a rival with fewer would then defeat the claim. Field by field the reading's store is smaller, but so is that of any general relational account, the rival's included.

The proofs' routes can be measured, with care. The shared-laws data give the route through any grammar constant. A walk over the Lean constants at commit `2cab11114`, from each law's declarations through statements and proofs to `«续»` or `«相续»` ([walk_continuation.py](walk_continuation.py)), gives the route through continuation itself.

- *Through continuation itself.* Of the 3,700 laws the measure reads, 885 reach the grammar and 331 reach continuation, in 68 records. Some of those forbid through their hypotheses: that unceasing generation needs renewal, that more generation needs more renewers, that no finite part bears taking, and in part that continued change needs openness. They reach it through `«尽»` at the one class `«一»`, in the finite-runs law `«尽则不或屡»` and `Amounts.falls_end`, so continuation there is the relation itself (`«一之续»`). 82 of the 331 reach it only so, among them all 21 of the first three's that reach it and 7 of the fourth's 15.
- *A selected sample.* Take the nine records of [08 §8's](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#third-parts) four third parts and the principle's five: closing-needs-net-draw, own-count-misses-joint-excess, what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in, what-bounds-offspring-numbers, identical-cells-under-a-descent-treating-them-alike, risk-minimization-on-a-shared-renewal, the-rest-outruns-the-renewal, what-seeking-cannot-separate and a-code-held-as-counts. They hold 75 laws. None reaches continuation, 74 reach no grammar constant, and the other is a statement added at a model. The sample favours the rival, because 08 §8 describes them as joins to conservation, renewal or thresholds. The data tag 28 of them on balance and 47 on no shared law, so these figures do not say what forbids rests on.
- *The theses' proved halves.* T1's margin and T6's unseen distance reach the grammar in none of 14 laws. T7's lock and selection through couplings reach it in 12 of 19, and continuation in none.
- *Central declarations.* Of the 117, 45 reach the grammar.
- *The other way.* Three of the nine records, holding thirty laws, turn on a class a law keeps: cells alike stay alike, hypotheses tied under every experiment, agents related by a renaming. That is the reading's pattern in the laws' own terms, though their proofs use no grammar constant. The literature has it as symmetry (Curie 1894), identification (Rothenberg 1971) and conventions symmetry does not select (Lewis 1969).

So the routes say less than 331 suggests. Laws that forbid pass through continuation in one body of work and not in another, and most of those named pass through it only where the class excludes nothing. None of this shows that a law needs the reading, since a definition can be unfolded. On `«尽则不或屡»`'s route the rival's name is termination by a ranking function, and under a named class, as on the rest theorem's route, it is reachability within a class. The measure reads Lean, not meaning, and says so: "Reaching no anchor says none occurs there, not that the law is no case of a shared law".

Over time the comparison runs on the results 08 §8 names: "A theorem a specialist would not foresee, a prediction that could fail and does not, or a premise derived from something weaker". None is in hand, and of the root's laws a bounded search found: "A concurrency theorist given the identification above would foresee each one." ([UE6, round 2](../../ue6-beyond-the-sciences/ROUND-2-ROOT.md)) A field working without the reading that reaches such a result first takes the point, so this defeat is comparative and needs no date. A result counts only if its derivation uses the pattern. Reaching alone is cheap: `Becoming.ModelStatements.optimum_broken_reading` reaches the grammar as a statement added at a model.

## What the claim then is

The sentence as written should not stand unqualified, and "argued from the laws on the models" says more than this supports.

1. As a language, the reading cannot be wrong, and it forbids nothing. Under UE's test it goes no farther than the principle's existence claim.
2. As a carrying it is open, and conceded where amounts come in until that sort is derived from relations.
3. Named for a domain, a relation and a class can be wrong, and T2 shows the form that takes.
4. As an explanation it ties, on a first count, with a rival that has no word for continuation. The pattern explains, if it does, under either name, and the word adds nothing. A result of 08 §8's kind reached through the pattern, and not first by a field without it, would change that.

What the reading adds so far is one vocabulary, one set of questions (which relation, which class, at which grain, whether the class is constant), and the form of the question where the ethics turns: whether one's own line runs under a class that holds one's effects ([07 §4.6](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)). It does not settle that question, and the ethics rests on its two premises either way, the continuation condition for scope and C for order.

[The plan](../PLAN.md#2-why-this-representation) names the difficulty: "a bare conjunction of laws "unifies" everything. The answer is stringency". The reading alone has no stringency. The laws have it: three of these nine records as a class a law keeps, the rest under their own hypotheses. What goes farther than the principle is the page's five laws, and as written their proofs use no grammar constant, though elsewhere laws that forbid do pass through continuation, mostly at the one class.

## A law and an observation

A law with content says that under its hypotheses its conclusion holds, and its check is what it stands or falls by. An observation reaches it only through a stated correspondence. So a conclusion failing in a domain shows that the domain does not meet the hypotheses or that the correspondence misreads them, not that the theorem is false. A thesis claiming that the domain meets them does fail there: 08 §1's "evidence against a stated claim". The fronts need this because a reader asks what would refute a theorem, and the answer is not a measurement.

## What this does not say

- It does not say the reading is false or the work idle. A tie on derivations leaves the vocabulary and the questions, which a reader may value for their own sake.
- It does not touch the theorems, which no reading can.
- It does not close the fourth sense, and no result of 08 §8's kind is known to be impossible.
- It is an AI's argument about an AI's account. It uses two accounts of explanation, patterns of argument for the fourth sense and intervention for the third, and a philosopher of science may find that too narrow.
