UE31b's design landed: the adult-loss cultures read by cell, frozen before any of their values is read

UE31 decided nothing on its primary group, since the archive's 40 adult-loss cultures (Data Set 2) hold 13 censuses each under the loss, too few for rules fitted culture by culture. UE31b reads them with the four replicates of one strain at one set loss under one law, ten cells of 36 rows, with UE31's rules imported unchanged at that unit (the models, the excess and its chance level, the robust test, the rival's sign and its band, the median of per-loss medians, the gate and the combination) and the forecasts out of sample by leaving one culture out of its cell. The recording is checked with a frozen consequence: if the recruits exceed the pupae by more than half an animal in more than 12.5% of rows, a share set between a recording as the protocol says with count errors and one whose adults were counted before the manipulation, no verdict is read and no cell's numbers are written. Week 12's entry after the treatment is read by a frozen rule: from the 0.27 loss on, the one holding fewer adults; at 0.04, where the treatment may add adults, the one at the position its strain's cultures from 0.27 on mostly show.

The dry runs, 1,000 synthetic archives of the experiment's shape for each of 18 generators, state the power before the run, and the answer expected is written down. In the field's worlds the next step goes to the field's state and three numbers suffice on the course (H1 failing in 94% of archives where the life cycle is longer), which at this shape rests on H1 and on V5 not failing. The sign cannot go to the field's model at ten cells, by counting: the cost of UE31's majority rule for T2's sign. In the field's worlds it went to T2 only where the turning point lies outside the constants' band, falsely, in 2 of 1,000 archives; under T2's law as near the field's densities as that law comes it went to T2 in 22%, all of them where the rival predicts the negative sign in six cells or more, which in every field world but the one whose turning point lies outside the band it never does. Replicates that differ lean toward the field's state (lpa-jars). The recording check lets through every archive recorded as the protocol says with the count errors probed, up to a tenth of the pupae missed, and stops every archive whose adults were recorded as counted. Two independent readers reviewed the design, and the second checked the revision; their findings were taken, among them seven errors: the recording and T2 narrowed (the first reader), and the sign's power at the field's densities, the recording check's threshold, the gate's cause, week 12's rule at the lowest loss and the onset's transient read as a shortfall of the state (the second).
