## 1. Method

**What counts as a third part.** A statement counts only if it passes five tests.

1. *On topic.* It bears on what the source framework states: offspring numbers for Price's equation, unreferenced attributes for the proxy problem. A conservation consequence about something the framework does not speak of is not counted against it.
2. *Observable.* It concerns an observation or course of the systems the framework covers, not a derivation, a proof technique or a choice of words.
3. *Permitted by the source.* The framework, taken as the union of the sources the row names, does not already exclude it. A row naming two sources is compared with both.
4. *Excluded by the theory.* A Lean statement excludes it under premises the theory names: a World premise, a local hypothesis on the card, or the gate.
5. *Not a reformulation.* A constructive proof, a discrete or whole-number version, a proof without decidable equality, or a combination of known results in one vocabulary does not count unless it excludes something the source permits.

A law stated for every relation where the source assumes a structure covers more, but it forbids nothing more within the source's own domain. Such generality is recorded and not counted.

**Grades.** Each third part is graded twice.

- *What carries it.* Either an added premise does the work, the forbidden course being that premise's content read in the source's terms ("premise-carried"), or a law combines the premise with the source's structure in a way neither has alone.
- *Whether it is known elsewhere.* Whether the forbidden statement is established in some neighbouring field, which would make it new to the contained framework and not to the sciences.

**Evidence.** Statements were read in the home sections of chapters 01–08 and in the records under `theories/becoming/results/records/`. Every declaration cited for a third part was checked to exist in the pure core's sources. The literature verdicts come from the AI's prior knowledge. They also draw on the repository's own prior-art checks of 20–23 October ([significance and prior-art review](../../BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md)), which checked several sources on the web; this audit made no new web search. Where a row's verdict rests on one of those checks, the section says so.

## 2. The table of 08 §8

### 2.1 Coming and ceasing keep pace

- **Source.** The demographic balancing equation: the population at the end equals the population at the start, plus births, less deaths. Trace theory (Mazurkiewicz 1977): independent actions commute, and runs equal up to such commutations reach the same state.
- **Theory.** [Home](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace). Over any stretch, the amount at the end with all it fell is the amount at the start with all it rose. For a decided determination over a finite family, coming to be recurs exactly when ceasing does. One list covering the active formations from some step on is enough. Declarations: `held_over_stretch`, `keep_pace`, `coming_recurs_iff`, `rising_recurs`, `Web.unceasing_iff_covering`, `Web.change_readings`; the witnesses `World.separate` (accretion, attrition); `Interleavings.LocalLaw.run_of_courses`; for wholes, `Covered.needs_new`.
- **Third part: no (restatement).** These laws are telescoping, and the alternation of rises and falls in a two-valued sequence. In a finite family, arising without end and without ceasing would make the census grow without bound, which the balancing equation with a bound already forbids. The record says the three readings are one change by six definitional unfoldings. Local laws reaching the same configuration from the same own courses is the confluence of trace theory. A whole that outlasts parts which each end needs new parts: that is the demographic fact that a population of mortal members persists only through births.

### 2.2 Recurrence and attraction

- **Source.** An injective self-map of a finite set is a permutation, so every state recurs (finite Poincaré recurrence). Logical irreversibility is non-injectivity, and erasing one bit sends entropy into the environment (Landauer 1961). Keeping the history makes any computation logically reversible (Bennett 1973).
- **Theory.** Homes: [recurrence](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-recurrence-without-premise), [attraction](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-attraction-is-not-returning), [a part's merge kept by the rest](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-merge-of-a-part-kept-by-the-rest). Declarations: `Law.lossless_iff_returns`, `Law.returns_iff_keeps`, `Law.rise_not_lossless`, `Law.present_forgets`, `Law.withHistory_never_returns`, `Law.withHistory_unlisted`, `Law.drawn_iff_not_returns`, `Law.reachable_absorbing`, `Law.loss_needs_merge`, `Law.Lossless.narrowing_spreads`, `Law.Lossless.reset_unlisted`.
- **Third part: no (restatement).**
  - "A quantity that never falls rises only under a law that merges" is finite recurrence.
  - "A bounded recorder cannot keep its whole history and go on" is the pigeonhole principle read with Bennett's tape.
  - The one candidate is `reset_unlisted`: a part reset at every step, inside a whole whose law merges nothing, needs a whole no list holds. Against Landauer alone, which assumes an ideal bath, this forbids repeated erasure inside a finite closed reversible whole. But it is the conjunction of Landauer's count with finite recurrence, two of the row's own sources. It is also the counting shadow of the finite-bath correction (Reeb and Wolf 2014), as the record says.
  - Proving recurrence without decidable equality changes the logic and excludes no course.

### 2.5 The order of what can form

- **Source.** Opportunity sets ranked by inclusion with strict monotonicity (Pattanaik and Xu 1990), and with further axioms by cardinality. The framework compares sets. It says nothing of how an agent's action changes another's set.
- **Theory.** Homes: [four cases](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-four-cases-of-a-part), [closing needs a net draw](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-closing-needs-net-draw), [own widening](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-own-widening-within-the-margin).
- **Third part: yes, weak (an identity under named premises; premise-carried).**
  - *Forbidden.* In a balanced flow whose formations require thresholds on levels, with needs fixed and the rest's exchange held as it was, no part narrows what another formation can form unless its cumulative net draw on some condition that formation requires exceeds the margin left by the levels without the part. So a part that gives every condition at least what it takes closes nothing. And a part's widening of its own options is mixed for the whole exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin of some other formation admitted without it.
  - *Declarations.* `Flow.Balances.supported_iff_within_margin`, `Flow.Balances.supported_of_net_give`, `Flow.Balances.admits_of_net_draw`, `Flow.Balances.closes_nothing`, `Flow.widens_iff_within_margin_at`, `Flow.Balances.loses_iff_beyond_margin`; own widening `Flow.Balances.widens_iff_others_within`, `Flow.Balances.mixed_iff_other_beyond`, `Flow.Balances.mixed_of_tight`, `Flow.Balances.widens_of_untaken_beyond`.
  - *What the source leaves open.* Any change of another's opportunity set, with or without a draw. For option-keeping in AI, Turner, Smith, Shah, Critch and Tadepalli (2021) say they do not yet understand when power-seeking disempowers others. The own-widening law answers this for outcomes reached by thresholds on conserved amounts. The 20 October check found no statement of it in the power-seeking literature ([option-keeping check](../../BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#option-keeping-check)).
  - *What would refute it.* A dependent losing admission while the part's measured net draw on every condition it requires stays within the margin, needs and the rest's exchange unchanged. That would refute a premise (balance, requirement as threshold, the withheld-levels counterfactual), not the law: under the premises the law is an identity with no free parameter.
  - *Thesis.* None. T1 reads the regime's margin, not the withheld-levels margin.
  - *Frozen test.* MQ1 found the identities exact in 456 records; they cannot fail. The testable content is the premise that needs are fixed, and MQ1 and MQ2 found it false under a standing draw: the need is the regime's and rises with the draw ([margin check](../../BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#margin-measured-check)). A useful test would ask whether option gains of agents in a shared-resource environment with measured margins close others' options exactly beyond those margins, with the regime's need, not the condition's, read as the margin.
  - *Known elsewhere.* The Lockean proviso and Pareto improvement from slack have the same structure on holdings and utilities; the law is the balance equation substituted into the admission inequality (08 §8: near-definitional).

### 2.7 Joint excess and position

- **Source.** The tragedy of the commons (Lloyd 1833; Hardin 1968): users of a common resource, each bearing only part of the cost of its use, overuse it to ruin. Positional goods (Hirsch 1976): relative standing is zero-sum. Neither specifies how the resource renews. With compensatory regrowth, a commons grazed down recovers once grazing stops. Neither says whether position protects.
- **Theory.** Homes: [joint excess](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess), [position](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-relative-position-is-zero-sum), [the rest outruns the renewal](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-the-rest-outruns-the-renewal), [what training installs](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-training-installs), with the regime form in [the margin of a regime](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime).

And once the other drawers alone outrun the renewal, no score reading the condition after that point settles a learner on restraint, at any weight (need one).
  - *Declarations.* `Flow.joint_excess_ends`, `Flow.stays_below`, `Flow.ends_below`, `Flow.ends_at_race_end`, `Flow.race_ends_greatest`, `Flow.outrun_below`, `Flow.outrun_ends`, `Flow.outrun_reading_alike`, `Training.outrun_settles_iff`; the family form `Flow.family_excess_ends`; the regime form `RegimeMargin.ends_iff_below`, `RegimeMargin.root_above_need`; `Pair.own_not_joint` is a witness, not an exclusion.

The forbidden statements are new to the row's sources and not to population ecology or threshold games ([restraint check](../../BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#restraint-equilibrium-check)).

Against instrumental convergence (Omohundro 2008; Turner and others 2021), which is often read as implying that a far-sighted agent preserves its environment, (f) and (g) forbid preservation beyond the optimizer's own base.

### 5.1 Price, Fisher, Hamilton and selection among groups

- **Source.**
  - Price's equation (1970), an identity: the change of a mean is a selection term plus a transmission term.
  - Fisher's fundamental theorem (1930): in the exact case, the change of mean fitness is its variance over its mean. Fisher himself left the rest to "the deterioration of the environment", which Price (1972) and Frank and Slatkin (1992) read as the partial change.
  - Multilevel selection and Hamilton's rule (1964, 1975): selection among groups goes for a trait where groups differ in it and those with more leave more.

  These are identities and statistical laws. They permit any sequence of offspring numbers: exact transmission of offspring number, with positive variance, for ever in a closed population, and selection among any groups whose outputs differ.
- **Theory.** Homes: [evolution on descent](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent), [what bounds offspring numbers](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-what-bounds-offspring-numbers), [Hamilton's rule](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-selection-with-returns-to-groupmates), [groups on coupled conditions](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-groups-whose-conditions-are-coupled).
- **Third part: yes (carried by balance, dissipation and whole units; the theory's own law joining Price's division to the flow; known as the Malthusian bound).**
  - *Forbidden.* In a family closed to inflow and dissipated on, a lineage reproduces for at most as many steps as the family's stock holds units, and offspring number cannot be transmitted exactly at every one of them: at some step before the stock runs out, Price's transmission term lowers offspring number by more than selection and transmission's rise raise it together. And groups whose members all depend on one coupled, gated family in joint excess end together, leaving nothing for selection among them.
  - *Declarations.* `Becoming.Reproduction.Flow.born_le_given`, `Flow.number_within_take`, `Flow.born_within_stock`, `Flow.reproducing_within`, `Becoming.Reproduction.Flow.lineage_ends`, `Flow.closed_lineage_lowered`, `Flow.closed_not_exact`; the witness `Becoming.Reproduction.Relay.relay` shows dissipation is needed. For groups: `Becoming.GroupEnding.Flow.coupled_end_together`, `Becoming.GroupEnding.Flow.all_end`, `Becoming.Flow.family_excess_ends`. Related but near-definitional: `Becoming.Flow.unsupported_childless`, and `Becoming.SelectionTerm.Flow.support_not_against` (a 0–1 reading of support is never selected against, since only the supported leave descendants).
  - *What would refute it.* A closed, dissipating population, with no inflow and each offspring costing at least one unit, whose offspring numbers stay exactly transmitted, with positive variance, for more generations than its stock in units. Or groups on one coupled, gated resource in excess whose survival differs.
  - *Thesis and test.* No thesis carries it, and there is no frozen test. A test in closed microcosms (batch culture without feeding), with Price's transmission term measured, can be designed, but it is of low risk: the outcome is the Malthusian bound. The commons test CG1 measured the coupled-family prediction (patch with neighbour) as an identification.
  - *Known elsewhere.*
    - Malthus (1798), and resource-limited growth in the chemostat;
    - Fisher's own deterioration of the environment, which the theory locates in Price's transmission term with a bound in the stock;
    - correlated extinction in metapopulations (Levins 1969; Hanski 1998);
    - the requirement that group selection act on groups whose fates differ (Wilson and Sober 1994).

  The record says the chapter "adds no new population-genetic prediction".
- **Not counted.** Price's equation, the selection term, Fisher's theorem in the exact case and with breeding values, and Hamilton's rule in counts (`Becoming.price`, `Becoming.SelectionTerm.covariation`, `Becoming.Reproduction.Generations.ByNumber.rises_exact_iff`, `Becoming.Reproduction.Breeding.response`, `Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_returns`) are counting forms of the classical laws.

## 7. What the audit shows

1. **The third parts are the conservation joins.** Every third part found arises where a framework is joined to balance, dissipation, a renewal law, threshold requirements or the gate, which it lacks. Some lack conservation (opportunity sets, Price's equation, Fisher's theorem, supervisory control, power-seeking). The static proxy problem has a resource constraint but no renewal, horizon or second optimizer. Others lack a threshold (Hardin's commons, multilevel selection). Frameworks that already carry conservation or are themselves identities give the World premises nothing to add. These are the demographic equation, finite recurrence, ranking functions, Prigogine, Petri nets and depensation, and for them a third part needs a new law, not a premise.

## 8. Candidate third parts not yet proved, ranked

Ranked by how much each would forbid, the largest forbidden set and the clearest route to refutation first. None is claimed here; each would need a board claim before Lean.

1. **Adaptation paid for by the flow.**
   - *Statement.* In a family whose inflow across a step is at most `I`, under balance and dissipation with each offspring given at least one unit, the cross-multiplied response of any carried amount's mean to selection across that step is bounded by `I` and the amount's spread. It would follow from Cauchy–Schwarz in counts (`Becoming.Reproduction.covariance_sq_le`) with the offspring bounds (`Flow.number_within_take`, `Flow.born_within_stock`) opened to an inflow, as [a rise past the inflow](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-rise-past-the-inflow-is-drawn-from-beyond) bounds a stock's rise.
   - *It would forbid,* against Price, Fisher and the breeder's equation, every response to selection faster than the flow can pay for, for every trait of every population read on the World.
   - *Known neighbour:* Haldane's cost of natural selection (1957), which bounds the rate of gene substitution by the reproductive excess.
   - *Test:* microbial evolution under a controlled nutrient inflow, with the ceiling on the per-generation response frozen in advance.
2. **Survival under expected joint excess, by counting.**
   - *Statement.* With renewal and draws weighted over courses (`Becoming.Weighted`), the weight of the courses on which a gated condition survives `n` steps of expected joint excess from below `n + m` is bounded by its spread over the square of its deficit, by Chebyshev in counting form (`Becoming.Typicality`; UE2's margin over spread).
   - *It would forbid* survival above that bound. That gives T1 a probabilistic third part, against the deterministic commons and against population viability analysis, which estimates rather than bounds.
   - *Test:* the survival fraction of yeast replicates under a predicted expected excess near the point of no return (Dai and others' data), against the frozen bound.
3. **The drain along improvement, for orders read through the total.**
   - *Statement.* In a balanced step on a whole that gains nothing, among orders that rank an outcome higher only where the family's total is higher, a higher-ranked outcome leaves what lies beyond, with the loss, no higher. Along every improving search of such a reward (`Training.Improving`) the beyond with the loss never rises. It would follow from `Flow.Balances.family_le_iff` and `Flow.top_iff_least`.
   - *It would forbid,* against Goodhart and Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell, whose theorem concerns limits, an improvement of such a proxy that raises what lies beyond. That turns part of T3's clause on approach into a theorem for a stated class. It is near-definitional, and Karwowski and others (2024) show that the fall need not be monotone outside such a class.
   - *Test:* a design with the units model frozen, reading the unrewarded conditions along training.
4. **A coupling rate for several optimizers over a horizon.**
   - *Statement.* At mutual best responses over a horizon, with each optimizer given `c` for each step a dependence is met, every perfect profile keeps the dependence exactly where the couplings cover the renewal forgone at the joint capacity, and breaks it within a long horizon below that rate. It would extend `Optimum.frontier_keeps_stepwise` with `Optimum.mutual_beyond`.
   - *It would forbid,* against Ostrom's design principles (empirical) and Pigou's static tax, a commons kept below the rate or broken above it.
   - *Known neighbour:* Pigouvian taxes in the dynamic commons.
5. **Joint option-keeping within joint margins.**
   - *Statement.* Where several parts each widen their own options, the whole widens exactly where their joint net draw stays within every other admitted formation's margin, and each part's own draw within the margins does not suffice. It would join the own-widening law with the joint draw (`Flow.Balances.broken_iff`, `Flow.Balances.broken_shared`).
   - *It would forbid,* against multi-agent power-seeking (Turner and Stavrianos 2021, who leave the general relation open), joint disempowerment within joint margins, and no disempowerment beyond them.
   - It would be an identity under the premises, as row 5 is.
6. **Set-point choosers and joint excess** (UE1b, claimed). An order greatest at a set point of what couples in has no drain through maximization, yet several such choosers, each within renewal, end a gated condition they jointly overdraw, and none sees it through its own couplings. *It would forbid,* against the free-energy principle, a collective of self-evidencers with characteristic states keeping a jointly overdrawn gated condition.
7. **A feeder beyond the blanket.**
   - *Statement.* A formation ends through the failure of a feeder lying outside its coupling closure, while every selection through its couplings, any update of beliefs from its blanket states included, selects the two courses alike until its support fails. It would join `Flow.Balances.consumption_ends_with_feeders`, `Flow.unselected_beyond_coupling_responsive` and the strict inclusions of `levels-of-closure`.
   - *It would forbid,* against the single Markov blanket, a self-evidencer registering before its end the failure of a feeder that couples into it by support alone.
   - *Known neighbour:* `Web.break_not_bounded` already states the unobserved-stock case.
8. **Maintenance bounded by free energy** (UE3).
   - *Statement.* Under a stated stochastic thermodynamics, a part reset at every step inside an enclosed whole holding free energy `F` keeps itself at most `F` over `kT ln 2` times the bits merged a step, without inflow. It would join `Law.Lossless.reset_unlisted` with `EntropyProduction.DestroysAtLeast`.
   - *It would forbid,* against Schrödinger and Prigogine, maintenance beyond that count without throughput.
   - Its larger value is that it derives the least loss from a stated physics: a premise from a weaker one, not only a third part.

*Unranked.* The second-order direction of 生生 (UE4), constructor theory and causal sets: none has a definition or a target statement yet from which to read what it would forbid.
