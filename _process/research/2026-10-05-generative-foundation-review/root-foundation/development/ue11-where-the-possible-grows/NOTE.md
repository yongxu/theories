## 1. The question

[UE10](../ue10-what-can-be-reached/NOTE.md)'s third law: along a course of one fixed process nothing is added to the whole possible, a lost possibility has no way back, and the possible within a horizon moves with the actual. 07's reading of it ends: growth of the possible itself would need a process that changes, which no law states ([07 §2](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-a-course-keeps-reachable)). 01's Open item on self-changing conditions leaves unstated "a change of the law of change with no fixed law above it, or a space of rules not given with the carrier" ([01 Open](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#open)). The UE plan names self-changing conditions its deepest structural gap, and its item 7 asks how the possible grows.

So: in what sense can the possible grow, what does its growth need, and can a change of the law of change with no top be stated?

The answer, in short. Under any process nothing is added to the possible under it across its own moves. So the possible with a part held, read through the moves holding the part, grows only across steps that change the part, and from the step at which the part stays for good nothing is added to it. It keeps widening, in 07's form, only where the part changes without end; where it keeps widening, read on the course's moments in the grammar, from no moment is the course's line under the part unending, and where each step's holding is also decided, lines under the part begin after every moment. Endless change of the part does not suffice, and a course that only returns never keeps widening, though the whole possible can exceed the actual along it and the held possible can widen across a step. A rule changing its own rule, and a tower of rules in which every rule changes only as the one above it allows and none is the last, are each one process: nothing is added to its whole possible along any course. On the root, for every carrier and relation, reach over a sequence of relations growing coarser that each step meets lies on a line under one of them; so every possibility of the tower is had by changing only the state and the rules below some height, and a tower changing every rule at once has a possibility needing the whole tower. On two witnesses the possible relative to what is held keeps widening while every formation stays possible at every step, relative to a rule raised at every step and relative to the rules from every height of a tower, each rule changing once as the change climbs; on both, every moment ceases under what is held. On a third, the rule raised at every other step, renewal without end holds as well. **[AI reading]** The whole possible of a stated process never grows; besides the possible within a horizon, which moves with the actual (UE10), what grows is the possible relative to what is held, and only across steps that change what is held; keeping it growing needs what is held to change without end.

## 2. The laws

Throughout, `adm : S → X → Prop` says what each configuration admits, and the possible under a process `Q` from `s` is UE10's `Reaching.Possible Q adm s`: what some configuration `Q` reaches from `s` admits. "Keeps widening" is 07's form read from the course's first step (UE10's `Reaching.KeepsWidening`); the forms from a later step follow by applying the laws to the course from that step (`Parting.after`), which the module does not state.

### Law 1. A move adds nothing to the possible under its process

For any process `Q`, what is possible under `Q` from a configuration `Q` reaches is possible from the first (UE10's `Reaching.Possible.of_reach`). So where something possible under `Q` from `t` is not possible from `s`, `Q` does not reach `t` from `s`, and a step from `s` to `t` is not a move of `Q` (`Reaching.not_reach_of_grows`, `Reaching.not_step_of_grows`).

### Law 2. Keeping widening needs departures without end

Along a course, where every step of a stretch is a move of `Q`, what is possible under `Q` at the stretch's end is possible at its start (`Run.possible_of_stretch`). So where the possible under `Q` keeps widening, from every step some bounded stretch is not made of moves of `Q` throughout (`Run.departs_of_keepsWidening`), and where every step from some step on is a move of `Q` it does not keep widening (`Run.not_keepsWidening_of_within`). The bounded form gives the plain one ("from no step on are all steps moves of `Q`") with no premise; the converse, asked of every process along one course, would give Markov's principle (the review's P2). Along a course that comes back to one configuration at arbitrarily late steps, nothing read off its configurations keeps widening, the possible under any process and the possible with any part held included (`Run.not_keepsWidening_of_returns`).

### Law 3. The possible with a part held grows only where the part changes

- *The part held.* For a relation `Same` on configurations, read as a part held unchanged, the moves holding it are the moves of `P` with `Same` between their two configurations (`Reaching.heldMoves`), and the possible with the part held is the possible under them (`Reaching.PossibleHeld`), included in the whole possible (`Reaching.PossibleHeld.possible`; `Reach.of_heldMoves`). Onward's steps within a family and Junctures' steps after which a configuration can still be had are instances, by definition (`Onward.within_eq_heldMoves`, `Branching.keeping_eq_heldMoves`).
- *On the root.* With `Same` in the place of the grammar's class `E`, any relation, a move holding the part is a continuation under it (`«续»`, `Reaching.steps_heldMoves_iff`); what the moves holding it reach is the start or the start's line under it (`«相续»`, `Reaching.reach_heldMoves_iff`); the possible with the part held is what the start or its line under the part admits (`Reaching.possibleHeld_iff_line`); and holding a weaker part is coarsening (`«粗化»`) read on processes (`Reach.heldMoves_mono`).
- *Held move by move, and as at the start.* Where the part is a class, what the moves holding it reach is the same as the start in the part (`Reach.same_of_heldMoves`, from the root's `«线内同类»`), and holding it move by move is holding it as it is at the start, both ways: what the moves holding it reach from `s` is what the moves into configurations the same as `s` reach (`Reach.heldMoves_iff_sameAsStart`, through `Trace.heldMoves_sameAsStart` and `Trace.sameAsStart_heldMoves`). Not in general: for "adjacent or equal", which is not transitive, the moves holding it reach `2` from `0`, and `2` is not the same as `0` (`AdjacentHeld.differs`); for `≤`, which is not symmetric, holding it as at the start reaches `3` from `0`, and holding it move by move does not (`OrderHeld.differs`).
- *Where it grows.* Where something possible with the part held at the next step is not possible so at the present one, the step changes the part (`Run.changes_of_heldGrows`). From the step at which the part stays for good, nothing is added to the possible with it held (`Run.possibleHeld_of_stays`).
- *Where it keeps widening.* It keeps widening only where the part changes without end: from every step some bounded stretch does not hold the part throughout (`Run.changes_of_heldKeepsWidening`), so it does not keep widening along a course whose steps hold the part from some step on (`Run.not_heldKeepsWidening_of_stays`). Where whether each step holds the part is decided, a step changing it is located within any stretch across which the possible with it held grows (`Run.held_change_located`), and, where it keeps widening, from every step on (`Run.held_change_recurs_of_keepsWidening`).
- *Read on the course's moments.* With the part between two moments in the place of the class, where the possible with the part held keeps widening, from no moment is the course's line under the part unending (`«不穷»`; `Run.not_unending_of_heldKeepsWidening`, through `Steps.buqiong_iff`); where holding is also decided, a line under the part begins (`«生»`) after every moment (`Run.begins_of_heldKeepsWidening`, through `Steps.hasPred_iff`).
- *And an unlisted whole.* It keeps widening only where no list holds the whole possible from the course's start (`Run.not_heldKeepsWidening_of_listed`, with UE10's `Reaching.KeepsWidening.not_listed`).
- *Not sufficient.* In `Oscillating` the rule changes at every step and every formation is possible from every configuration, so no list holds the whole possible; yet the possible with the rule held does not keep widening, since what it opens with the rule at `1` it loses with the rule back at `0` (`Oscillating.converse_fails`).
- *A course that returns.* `Oscillating`'s course returns every two steps (`Oscillating.returns`), so its first configuration comes back at arbitrarily late steps (`Oscillating.comes_back`). At every step the whole possible exceeds what the configuration admits (`Oscillating.exceeds_now`), and the possible with the rule held widens across the course's first step (`Oscillating.held_widens`); it does not keep widening (`Oscillating.returning_has_both`, from law 2's `Run.not_keepsWidening_of_returns`). Exceeding the actual, and widening across a step, a course that only returns has; keeping widening it has not.

### Law 4. A rule changing its own rule

On `adaptive F G`, a state going on under a rule that itself goes on, a step keeps the rule exactly where `G` leaves it as it is (`adaptive_keeps_rule_iff`). So along a course the possible with the rule held keeps widening only where `G` changes the rule without end (`Run.adaptive_rule_changes_of_keepsWidening`), located where rules are decided (`Run.adaptive_rule_change_recurs_of_keepsWidening`), while nothing is added to the whole possible (UE10's `Run.possible_of_next` at `adaptive F G`).

### Law 5. Every possibility with some member of a sequence held; the tower of rules

- *On the root.* For every carrier and relation: on a sequence of relations `E n` growing coarser (each included in every later one), where every step of `R` is alike under some member, whatever `R` reaches lies on a line under some member (`«及在渐粗列之相续»`). So for any process whose every move holds some member of such a sequence, what it reaches it reaches with some member held (`Reaching.reach_held_of_sequence`), and every possibility is possible with some member held, and conversely (`Reaching.possible_iff_held_of_sequence`).
- *The tower.* A configuration is a state and a sequence of rules, the rule at height `k` its `k`-th entry; the move `0` changes the state as the rule at height `0` allows, and the move `k + 1` changes the rule at height `k` as the rule at height `k + 1` allows, the state and every other rule kept (`RuleTower.process`). Every rule changes only as the one above it allows, and none is the last; the scheme, `base` and `up`, is fixed, as the law of any stated process is.
- *Every possibility had below some height.* The move `a` keeps the rules from height `a` up (`RuleTower.step_sameFrom`; `RuleTower.SameFrom`, a class, `RuleTower.sameFrom_class`, growing coarser with the height, `RuleTower.SameFrom.mono`), so every possibility of the tower is possible with the rules from some height up held, and conversely (`RuleTower.possible_iff_held`): each is had by changing only the state and the rules below some height.
- *The premise is needed.* The same scheme with every rule changed at once (`RuleTower.synchronous`) has a possibility needing the whole tower: with each rule growing by the one above it, `2` is possible from all rules at `1`, but not with the rules from any height up held (`Synchronous.tower_law_fails`).
- *Along a course.* Where the possible with the rules from height `n` up held keeps widening, moves above `n`, the only moves that can change a rule from height `n` up, recur from every step on, located with no decision of the rules, since each move names the one height it can change (`Run.ruleTower_move_above_recurs_of_keepsWidening`). The moves at most `n` reflect that possible, so a trace ending where `x` is possible with the rules from height `n` up held started where it was, or passes a move above `n` after which it is (`RuleTower.reflected`, `RuleTower.held_origin`, an instance of the gate law `Gated.origin`). Where from some step on the rules from height `N` up stay, so do the rules from any height `n ≥ N` up, and from that step nothing is added to the possible with them held (law 3, `Run.possibleHeld_of_stays`): a top, the rules from some height up, fixed for good stops the growth relative to it. Nothing is added to the whole possible (UE10's `Run.possible_of_next` at `RuleTower.process base up`).

### Law 6. Witnesses: open-ended relative to what is held, with the whole possible fixed

- *A raised rule.* In `RaisedRule` a configuration is a rule `n` and a position `x`; a move raises the rule by one or moves the position to any `x' ≤ n`, and `(n, x)` admits `x`. Along the course raising the rule at every step, from rule `0` at position `0`, the possible with the rule held at step `k` is what lies at most `k` (`RaisedRule.held_iff`, from `RaisedRule.held_trace`), so it keeps widening (`RaisedRule.held_keeps_widening`), while from every configuration of the course every formation is possible (`RaisedRule.whole_every`, through `RaisedRule.reach_raise`). The rule changes at every step (`RaisedRule.rule_changes`); the held laws require only that it change without end. Read on the course's moments under the rule held, no moment renews (`«复生»`, `RaisedRule.not_renews`), so every moment ceases (`«息»`, by `«自不复生则息»`; `RaisedRule.ceases`).
- *A tower copying down.* In `CopyDown`, the tower whose move `k + 1` copies the rule at height `k + 1` down to height `k`, the move `0` changing nothing and the state never moving, a configuration admits `x` where its rule at height `x` stands above `x + 1`. Along the course that copies down to height `k` at step `k`, the possible with the rules from height `n` up held at step `k` is what lies below `n` or below `k` (`CopyDown.held_iff`), so for every height `n` it keeps widening (`CopyDown.held_keeps_widening`), while from every configuration of the course every formation is possible (`CopyDown.whole_every`). Each rule changes once, at its own step, and the change climbs (`CopyDown.changes_iff`): no rule changes without end, yet the rules from every height up do. Read on the course's moments under the rules from any height up held, no moment renews, so every moment ceases (`CopyDown.not_renews`, `CopyDown.ceases`).
- *Growth with renewal without end.* In `RaisedEveryOther`, `RaisedRule`'s process with the rule raised across every even step and kept across every odd one (`RaisedEveryOther.course`), the possible with the rule held keeps widening (`RaisedEveryOther.held_keeps_widening`), from every configuration of the course every formation is possible (`RaisedEveryOther.whole_every`), and, read on the course's moments under the rule held, renewal without end (`«生生»`) holds at every even moment (`RaisedEveryOther.renews_without_end`), so moment `0` does not cease (`«生生不息»`; all three at once, `RaisedEveryOther.both`).

## 3. What it shows, and what it does not

**Shows:**
- Three senses in which the possible grows, apart. Within one fixed process the possible within a horizon moves with the actual (UE10); the possible relative to what is held grows only where what is held changes (law 3); and the whole possible never grows along a course of a stated process (UE10), however many levels of rule the process carries (laws 4 and 5).
- What keeping the possible widening relative to what is held costs: endless change of what is held, read in the grammar as no unending line under what is held and, where holding is decided, lines under it beginning after every moment; and an unlisted whole possible from the start. These do not suffice (`Oscillating`), and a course that only returns never keeps widening, though it can have the whole possible exceeding the actual and the possible relative to what is held widening across a step (`Oscillating.returning_has_both`).
- That a change of the law of change with no top is statable, as one process with a fixed scheme, adds nothing to its whole possible, and has each of its possibilities had with a top held, wherever each move keeps the rules from some height up (law 5); and that the premise is needed (`Synchronous`).
- That growth relative to what is held and a fixed whole possible go together, relative to one rule (`RaisedRule`) and relative to every height of a tower (`CopyDown`), with every moment ceasing under what is held on both; and that renewal without end can hold with them (`RaisedEveryOther`): growth relative to what is held and renewal without end are different relations.
- The held possible on the root: the possible along the line under the part, with the tower law a law on lines for every carrier and relation; and, for a class, holding move by move the same as holding as at the start, which in general it is not.

**Forbids:**
- growth of the possible under a process across one of its own moves (law 1);
- anything read off a course's configurations keeping widening along a course that comes back to one configuration without end (law 2);
- the possible with a part held keeping widening along a course whose steps hold the part from some step on, or, where it keeps widening, a line under the part unending from some moment (law 3); and anything added to it after the step from which the part stays (law 3);
- the possible with the rules from height `n` up held keeping widening along a course of the tower on which moves above `n` stop (law 5);
- growth of the whole possible along a course of any stated process, a rule changing its own rule and a tower with no top included (UE10, read on laws 4 and 5).

**Does not show:**
- a sufficient condition for keeping widening relative to what is held, beyond the witnesses;
- where a change of a general part is located without deciding each step's holding: without a decision, law 3 gives a bounded stretch that does not hold the part throughout, not a step. With each step's holding only weakly decided (refuted, or its refutation refuted) the step is still located, and with no decision, asked of every process, locating is exactly weak excluded middle (RECHECK-UE11, Q8, a probe); by AGENTS.md this is a lemma of the item, not a calibration, since it says nothing further about the unfolding;
- that a move above `n` changes a rule: where `up` lets a rule stay, the move `n + 1` can return to its configuration (RECHECK-UE11, Q7, a probe);
- a change with no fixed scheme, or a space of rules not given with the carrier: any stated process is a fixed relation;
- whether the actual unfolding is a course of a process whose configurations can be stated in advance (Longo, Montévil and Kauffman 2012). **[AI reading]** Law 1 says what such a statement would cost: if the possible grows along the actual course relative to every candidate process, no candidate carries the course.

## 4. Standing and neighbours

Every law is near-definitional or a witness: laws 1 and 2 are `Reaching.Possible.of_reach` and its iteration over a stretch, and the returning law their reading on a course that comes back; law 3 is their instance at the held moves, and law 4 its instance at the adaptive rule; the root law of law 5 is an induction on reach, and the tower law its instance. The content is in the reading and the witnesses. The neighbours, as the review graded them and as checked on 27 October 2026 by web search where the review asked for a check (the re-check verified the bibliographic data again and corrected two details, §6):

- **Open-ended novelty** (Banzhaf and others 2016, *Theory in Biosciences* 135:131–161). They classify novelty against a system's model and meta-model: variation, novelty within the model; innovation, novelty that changes the model, "thereby growing/shrinking the possibilities of variation"; emergence, novelty that changes the meta-model (the classification read in the authors' summary for ALife 2016; the journal text was not reachable). Law 3 is their innovation restated as a law on any process: the possible with the model held is the possibility of variation, and it grows only across a change of the model. In their account this holds by definition, and so it does here: restated, near-definitional.
- **Open-ended evolution** (Taylor and others 2016, *Artificial Life* 22:408–423; Bedau and others 2000). The York workshop's report summarizes Banzhaf's talk, novelty defined with respect to a model and practically unbounded if the number of levels may grow, and McMullin's on Popper, that continuing inheritable change at the highest control levels, newly dominating levels included, may be critical to openness: the informal neighbour of a top fixed for good stopping the growth relative to it (law 5). Bedau and others pose open-endedness as an open problem.
- **Unbounded evolution and innovation** (Adams, Zenil, Davies and Walker 2017, *Scientific Reports* 7:997). They define innovation as trajectories not observed in isolated systems, those running under fixed rules, and find in cellular automata whose rules vary that state-dependent rules alone give open-ended evolution in a scalable way. A state-dependent rule is an instance of `adaptive F G`; their innovation compares trajectories with fixed-rule systems, law 3 compares what is possible with the rule held, so the two are neighbours, not one statement.
- **Reflective towers** (des Rivières and Smith 1984, ACM Symposium on LISP and Functional Programming, 331–347; Wand and Friedman 1988, *Lisp and Symbolic Computation* 1(1):11–37). A procedurally reflective language has an infinite tower of processors, implemented finitely: a computation's degree of introspection is the least height above which every level only runs the default processor, an implementation need terminate only on computations of finite degree, and a single step raises the degree by a finite amount, by one in 3-LISP (read in the draft of des Rivières and Smith kept in the PARC archive). That is, in substance, the tower law's finite support: each move keeps the rules from some height up, so each possibility is had with the rules from some height up held, and a step changing every level, as in `Synchronous`, breaks it. Law 5 restates it on processes, on the root, with its premise shown needed.
- **The adjacent possible** (Kauffman 2000, *Investigations*) and **no entailing laws** (Longo, Montévil and Kauffman 2012, arXiv:1201.2069). UE10 restated the adjacent possible for a prestated space; UE11 says where the possible grows on a prestated space: never the whole possible, and relative to what is held only across a change of what is held. A space that cannot be prestated is outside the module (§3).
- **Open-endedness relative to an observer** (Hughes and others 2024, ICML). Novelty and learnability for an observer; the possible relative to what is held is a structural analogue on the side of novelty, with learnability not modelled.
- **Levels of learning** (Bateson 1972, *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*): Learning 0 to IV, each a change in the process of the level below. That is the tower's scheme, with no law of possibility; the tower law gives one.

No neighbour found states law 3 or the tower law as a law on any process, or on the root.

## 8. Reading [AI reading, D45]

If the one unfolding were one stated process, nothing would be added to its whole possible along its course: what a course adds is the actual. The possible within a horizon moves with the actual, and the possible relative to what is held grows only where what is held changes; keeping it growing needs what is held to change without end. A tower of rules with no top adds nothing to its whole possible either, and each of its possibilities is had with a top, the rules from some height up, held. So 生生不息, read at the whole as 生生 going on with more still able to form (D5, as revised on 18 October), needs no growth of the whole possible, and D5 excludes reading it as growth. More still able to form, which D5 reads as not a return, is not the whole possible exceeding the actual, nor the possible relative to what is held widening across a step: `Oscillating`'s course only returns and has both (`Oscillating.returning_has_both`). It can be the possible relative to what is held keeping widening, as on the witnesses, which no course coming back to one configuration without end has (`Run.not_keepsWidening_of_returns`). The root theorem `«生生不息»` says that renewal without end does not cease; read on a course's moments, keeping the possible widening relative to what is held needs that no line under what is held goes on without end and, where holding is decided, that lines under it begin after every moment. Renewal without end and growth relative to what is held are different relations: on both witnesses every moment ceases under what is held (`RaisedRule.ceases`, `CopyDown.ceases`), and on a third, the rule raised at every other step, both hold with every formation possible throughout (`RaisedEveryOther.both`).
