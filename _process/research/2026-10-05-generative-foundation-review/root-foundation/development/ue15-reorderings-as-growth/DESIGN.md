# UE15: a world's reorderings as the growth of its order of exchanges, and presence on the World (design)

28 October 2026. AI-written design for a board item to claim after UE14 lands, under [UE](../ue-unified-explanation/PLAN.md): its first aim, representation, asks that a law be one law and not one per model, and its structural gap of presence in time is answered by UE14 on causal-set growth only ([UE14](../ue14-the-present/NOTE.md)). This item asks whether the World's own order of change is causal-set growth, so that UE14's laws of presence are laws of the World by instance and not by analogy.

## 1. The question

01 §9.6 reads the World's shared index of steps as a choice among interleavings: exchanges whose footprints are disjoint commute, and what shows of the index is the partial order of joined exchanges (`Footprinted.Precedes`), every renumbering keeping it reached by commuting exchanges not joined (`Footprinted.sorts_iff`), and a world so ordered agreeing with a reordering (`Footprinted.converse`). UE13 and UE14 read causal-set growth: a course bears one element at a time, each with a stem as its past, and the orders of birth of the courses growing a configuration of one kind are its natural labellings (`Presence.natural_iff_course`; UE14 states its main laws on the courses themselves, `Presence.every_course_iff` and the course forms). Both are Mazurkiewicz's theorem on one side (the core's `rearranges_iff`). The design asks whether they are one structure: whether the reorderings of a world's stretch are exactly the courses of growth of its order of exchanges.

## 2. The laws to prove

1. **The exchanges of a stretch form a causal set.** For a footprinted world `r` and `m` steps from the first, `exchanges r m : Causet` with `m` elements and precedence `r.Precedes` restricted to them; it is `Ordered` (transitive by `Precedes.trans`, naturally labelled by `Precedes.lt`). Near-definitional.
2. **Its natural labellings are the reorderings.** A renumbering of the stretch is a natural labelling of `exchanges r m` exactly where it keeps the order of joined exchanges (`Keeps`), since joined and later is the order's generator and a renumbering increasing along each generator increases along every chain. With `sorts_iff`, the natural labellings are exactly the renumberings reached by commuting exchanges not joined, and with `natural_iff_course` exactly the orders of birth of the courses of causal-set growth from the empty set growing a configuration of its kind. So **a world's reorderings of a stretch are the courses of growth of its order of exchanges**; with `converse`, every world taking the same exchanges in such an order agrees with one. A representation theorem, by composition.
3. **UE14's laws on the World, by instance.** With the order decided on the stretch (joining decided gives it, by bounded search over chains of later steps: a lemma to prove), an exchange precedes another exactly where it is taken before it in every reordering; the present of a reordering at a stage, the exchanges taken that precede none taken (UE14's `MaximalBorn`), is an antichain, and every antichain given by a test is one; an exchange is present when another is taken, on some reordering exactly where it is a link below it and on every reordering exactly where that is the one exchange it is linked to (no decision for the latter). Each is UE14's law applied to `exchanges r m`, stated once as the instance.
4. **What presence means on the World.** Where the footprint contains the exchanges, an exchange `i` present at `j`'s step (no exchange `i` precedes taken between them) leaves every relatum it touched shown at `j`'s step as right after its own step (`Web.SameAt (i + 1) … j x`): what a condition left is still there when what it conditions is taken. Each step between touches none of `i`'s relata, since one touching one would be joined to `i` and so preceded by it, and a relatum untouched is unmoved (`Contains`). The converse fails on a witness: an exchange joined to `i` only through a dependence leaves `i`'s relata as they were while ending `i`'s presence. So presence at a step is stronger than persistence, and the gap is the dependence reading of joining.
5. **Witnesses.** The vee as a world: one exchange touching `x` and `y`, then one touching `x` and one touching `y`; the two later exchanges commute, and the first is present at the last step on one reordering and not on the other. The dependence witness for law 4's converse.

## 3. What it would show, and what it would not

It would show that on the World the step index's order, read through its reorderings, is causal-set growth of the order of exchanges, so UE14's answer on presence in time holds on the World's stretches where joining is decided and the footprint contains the exchanges, and that presence at a step gives the physical persistence of what a condition left. It would not show presence on processes in general (a process has no footprint), continuous time, or the present of a center; infinite stretches stay out (the stretch is finite, as in `sorts_iff`).

## 4. Sources and standing

Mazurkiewicz (1977) traces and their configurations; the correspondence between the linear extensions of a trace's dependence order and its interleavings is standard in concurrency theory (Diekert and Rozenberg, eds., The Book of Traces, 1995), as is reading a trace's prefixes as its configurations (event structures: Nielsen, Plotkin and Winskel 1981). Laws 1 to 3 are expected to be near-definitional or instances; law 4 is a lemma with a witness. What is new would be the reading: the World's interleavings as causal-set growth, so one law of presence across the two models.
