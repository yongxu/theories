# UE15: a world's reorderings as the growth of its order of exchanges, and presence on the World

28 October 2026. AI-written note for board item UE15, with the module `Becoming.ReorderingGrowth` in [tree/Becoming/](tree/Becoming/ReorderingGrowth.lean). It states what the Lean proves, what it shows and does not, and the standing of each law. The [design](DESIGN.md) gave the plan; the module was drafted to it ([DRAFT-NOTES.md](DRAFT-NOTES.md)). An independent review ([REVIEW-UE15.md](REVIEW-UE15.md)) found the Lean sound and the note wrong in one place, the gap between persistence and presence; every finding is taken (§7), and a re-check's are too (§8).

## 1. Summary

UE14 answered the root's open entry on presence in time on causal-set growth only ([01 §1](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-present-on-causal-set-growth)). The World reads its shared index of steps as one choice among interleavings: with a footprint containing the exchanges, exchanges not joined commute, and what shows of the index is the order of joined exchanges ([01 §9.6](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-order-of-exchanges-is-the-invariant)). This module shows that on a world's stretch the two are one structure, so UE14's laws hold on the World by instance and not by analogy.
- **The representation.** The exchanges of the first `m` steps, ordered by the order of joined exchanges, form a causal set. Its natural labellings are exactly the renumberings of the stretch keeping the order of joined exchanges. So, where the footprint contains the exchanges, a renumbering of the stretch is the numbering of some reordering exactly where it is a natural labelling of the stretch's exchanges, and exactly where it numbers them by a course of causal-set growth from the empty set: the numberings a world's reorderings give a stretch are exactly the orders of birth of the courses of growth of its order of exchanges. The correspondence is between numberings: reorderings giving one numbering agree over the stretch (`Footprinted.reorder_determined`), and one course carries several numberings where the order has automorphisms. A world whose footprint contains its exchanges, taking the same exchanges in the order of such a course, agrees with a reordering.
- **The decisions on the World.** Joining decided on the stretch decides the order of exchanges on it, by search over the steps between; joining weakly decided weakly decides it.
- **The laws of presence on the World.** With no decision, an exchange is taken before another on every reordering exactly where its preceding it is not refuted, and with that precedence decided exactly where it precedes it. With precedence weakly decided, one is taken before another on some reordering exactly where they differ and the other does not precede it; every present is an antichain of the order of exchanges, every antichain given by a test is the present of some reordering, and two exchanges are present together on some reordering exactly where neither precedes the other, and on every reordering, with no decision, exactly where, doubly negated, they precede the same exchanges of the stretch. An exchange is present when another is taken on some reordering exactly where it is a link below it (precedence weakly decided), and on every reordering exactly where that is the one exchange of the stretch it is linked to (no decision).
- **What presence means on the World.** On the world as it is, an exchange is present when another is taken exactly where it precedes it and no exchange between is joined to it. So, where the footprint contains the exchanges, it leaves every relatum it touched shown, when the other is taken, as right after its own step; so on the reordering. The converse fails wherever what the earlier exchange touched persists while an exchange between is joined to it: one joined through a dependence, or ones that move what it touched and move it back, with no dependence and the least footprint.
- **Witnesses.** The vee as a world, whose order of exchanges is UE14's vee and on which presence when the last exchange is taken varies with the reordering, the two numberings being by one course; and two worlds for the converse's failure, one joined through a dependence and one moving a relatum and moving it back.

**[AI reading, D45]** On the World the present of UE14 is the present of a reordering: the exchanges taken by a step that no exchange taken by then follows in the order of joined exchanges. Whether what an exchange left is still there when a later one is taken is then a question the World answers: on the world as it is, presence gives persistence of everything the exchange touched, and the converse fails wherever an exchange between is joined to it while what it touched persists: through a dependence, through a touch that does not move it, or through a move that a later exchange between undoes. So what presence adds to persistence is that nothing between is joined to the earlier exchange, and what every reordering keeps of it is the one link, as on causal-set growth.

## 2. The laws

The module has 62 theorems in 76 declarations, every axiom report empty ([scratch-9.log](scratch-9.log)). Names are in the namespace `Becoming.ReorderingGrowth`. `r` is a footprinted world (`Footprinted`: a flow, a web and a footprint), `m` the length of the stretch from step `0`.

### Law 1. The exchanges of a stretch form a causal set

`exchanges r m` has `m` elements, the exchange across `i` preceding that across `j` where both lie in the stretch and `r.Precedes i j` (`exchanges`). It is a causal set (`exchanges_ordered`): transitive by `Precedes`'s own constructor, each element preceded only by earlier ones by `Precedes.lt`. Near-definitional.

### Law 2. Its natural labellings are the reorderings

- *Keeping joined pairs keeps the order.* A renumbering increasing along each joined pair, the earlier first, increases along every chain of them (`keeps_precedes`).
- *Natural labellings.* `NaturalLabelling (exchanges r m) π σ` holds exactly where `π` renumbers the stretch (`Reindexes 0 m π σ`) and keeps the order of joined exchanges (`r.Keeps 0 m π`), with no hypothesis on the footprint (`natural_iff`).
- *The representation, as one theorem.* Where the footprint contains the exchanges, a renumbering of the stretch is the numbering of some reordering exactly where it is a natural labelling of the stretch's exchanges, and exactly where some course of causal-set growth from the empty set relabels them onto its configuration at the stretch's size (`reordering_iff_course`, by `Independent.keeps`, `natural_reordering` and UE14's `natural_iff_course`). The correspondence is between numberings: reorderings giving one numbering agree over the stretch (`Footprinted.reorder_determined`), and one course carries several numberings where the order has automorphisms (`VeeWorld.one_course_two_numberings`).
- *Reorderings as numberings.* A reordering of the stretch (`Reordering`: commutations within it, each of two exchanges not joined in the world as it then stands) numbers the exchanges by where it takes them (`place ts`); where every two consecutive exchanges are joined, the only reordering is the world as it is (`reordering_nil`); each reordering is a natural labelling (`reordering_natural`), each natural labelling is a reordering's (`natural_reordering`, by `Footprinted.sorts`), and so whatever is read on the stretch holds on every natural labelling, or on some, exactly where it holds on every reordering, or on some (`every_reordering_iff`, `some_reordering_iff`).
- *The worlds.* A world whose footprint contains its exchanges, taking across step `π i` the exchange `r` takes across `i`, touching what it touches, showing what it touches after the step as `r` does and starting as `r` does, where `π` numbers the stretch by a course of growth, agrees over the stretch with a reordering (`course_converse`, by `Footprinted.converse`).

### Law 3. The decisions on the World

- *The last link.* An exchange precedes a later one exactly where it is joined to it, or precedes an exchange between them joined to it (`last_of_precedes`, `precedes_iff_last`).
- *Deciding.* Joining decided for the pairs of the stretch decides the order of exchanges on it (`precedes_decided`, by `precedes_decided_below`, search over the steps between with `Decided.exists_below`); joining weakly decided weakly decides it (`precedes_weakly`, by `precedes_weakly_below` with UE14's `weakly_exists_below`). Precedence weakly decided on the stretch weakly decides the precedence of its exchanges as a causal set (`exchanges_weakly`).
- *Links.* Below an exchange of the stretch, the links of the stretch's causal set are the links of the order of exchanges (`links_exchanges`).

### Law 4. The laws of presence on the World

Each is a law of `Becoming.Presence` on `exchanges r m`, carried to the World's reorderings by law 2; each needs the footprint to contain the exchanges except `present_unrelated`. Three lemmas say that which exchange a numbering takes first, its present at a stage and presence when an exchange is taken are read on the stretch (`before_stretch`, `maximalBorn_stretch`, `presentAt_stretch`).
- *Taken before on every reordering.* With no decision, exactly where the precedence is not refuted (`every_reordering_not_not`, UE14's `every_iff_not_not`); with the pair's precedence decided, exactly where it holds (`precedes_iff_every_reordering`: Szpilrajn's extension theorem in Dushnik and Miller's intersection form, read on the World); with joining decided on the stretch, likewise (`precedes_iff_every_reordering_of_joined`).
- *Taken before on some reordering.* With precedence weakly decided on the stretch, exactly where the two differ and the other does not precede the one (`before_some_reordering`, UE14's `before_some_iff`); two different unrelated exchanges are taken in either order (`either_order_reordering`).
- *The present.* The present of any numbering at a stage is an antichain of the order of exchanges, with no hypothesis (`present_unrelated`, UE14's `maximalBorn_unrelated`). With precedence weakly decided, every antichain of the stretch given by a Boolean test is the present of some reordering at some stage (`antichain_present_reordering`), and two exchanges are present together on some reordering exactly where both lie in the stretch and neither precedes the other (`together_some_reordering`). With no decision, they are present together on every reordering exactly where, doubly negated, they precede the same exchanges of the stretch (`together_every_reordering_not_not`, UE14's `together_every_iff_not_not`).
- *Presence when an exchange is taken.* With precedence weakly decided, the exchange across `i` is present when that across `j` is taken on some reordering exactly where `i` is a link below `j` in the order of exchanges (`present_some_reordering`, UE14's `present_some_iff_link`); with no decision, on every reordering exactly where it is a link below `j` and a link below no other exchange of the stretch (`present_every_reordering`, UE14's `present_every_iff_only_link`).

UE14's laws that take a `Decidable` instance of the whole precedence (`prec_iff_every`, `past_every_iff`, `together_every_iff`, `present_every_iff`) are not instanced: the module builds no `Decidable` data, and the decisions it uses are Prop-valued (`Decided`, `WeaklyDecided`), which a Boolean or `Decidable` joining gives, and a Prop-valued decision cannot give `Decidable` data constructively. Of their forms with no decision, `every_iff_not_not`, `together_every_iff_not_not` and `present_every_iff_only_link` are instanced (`every_reordering_not_not`, `together_every_reordering_not_not`, `present_every_reordering`); `past_every_iff` has none in UE14.

### Law 5. What presence means on the World

- *Presence on the world as it is.* Where one exchange precedes another, it is joined to an exchange after it and no later than the other (`first_of_precedes`); so the exchange across `i` is present when that across `j` is taken, on the world as it is, exactly where it precedes it within the stretch and no exchange between is joined to it (`present_identity_iff`).
- *A relatum unmoved step by step is shown alike* across the stretch (`sameAt_of_unmoved`).
- *Presence gives persistence.* Where the footprint contains the exchanges and the exchange across `i` is present when that across `j` is taken on the world as it is, every relatum `i` touches is shown at step `j` as right after `i`'s step (`persists_of_present`): a step between touching it would be joined to `i`, so preceded by it and taken before `j`, against presence; a relatum a step does not touch is unmoved across it. On a reordering on which `i` is present, the reordered world shows them so at the step it takes `j`'s exchange to (`persists_of_present_reordering`, by `Footprinted.precedes_place`, `reorder_shows` and `reorder_contains`).
- *Not conversely, through a dependence.* In a world of three exchanges with an empty flow (`Witnesses.emptyFlow`), the first and the last change the relatum `0` and the middle one changes the relatum `1`, which needs `0` throughout (`ByDependence.world`; its footprint contains the exchanges, `ByDependence.contains`, and the dependence is met, `ByDependence.dependent`). The middle exchange touches nothing the first touches and is joined to it through the dependence (`ByDependence.joined_first_middle`), and to the last likewise (`ByDependence.joined_middle_last`); every relatum the first touches is shown when the last is taken as right after the first, yet on no reordering is the first present when the last is taken, since it is not a link below it (`ByDependence.persists_not_present`, by UE14's `link_of_present`). Every two consecutive exchanges are joined, so its only reordering is the world as it is (`ByDependence.only_reordering`, by `reordering_nil`).
- *Not conversely, with no dependence and the least footprint.* In a world of four exchanges each touching only the relatum `0`, which rises, rises, falls back and rises, with no dependence (`MovedBack.world`, `MovedBack.contains`), the footprint touches only what moves, so it is the least footprint containing the exchanges (`MovedBack.touches_moved`); every two exchanges are joined (`MovedBack.joined`), so its only reordering is the world as it is (`MovedBack.only_reordering`), and what the first touched is shown when the last is taken as right after the first, yet the first is present then on no reordering (`MovedBack.persists_not_present`).
- So presence at a step is stronger than persistence: what it adds is that nothing between is joined to the earlier exchange, and the gap is every way an exchange between can be joined to it while what it touched persists: a dependence, a touch that does not move it, or a move undone later. A footprint may touch a relatum it does not move, since containing the exchanges constrains only what it leaves untouched.

### Law 6. The vee as a world

One exchange changing the relata `0` and `1`, then one changing `0` and one changing `1`, no dependence (`VeeWorld.world`, `VeeWorld.contains`). The first is joined to each later one through a shared relatum (`VeeWorld.joined_first_second`, `VeeWorld.joined_first_third`), the later two are not joined (`VeeWorld.apart`), so the second does not precede the third (`VeeWorld.not_second_third`) and within the three steps only the first precedes, the second or the third (`VeeWorld.vee_of_precedes`). Its order of exchanges is UE14's vee (`VeeWorld.prec_iff_vee`), so its natural labellings are the vee's (`VeeWorld.natural_iff_vee`, by UE14's `natural_congr`). Commuting the later two is a reordering (`VeeWorld.reordering`), and the first exchange is present when the third is taken on that reordering and not on the world as it is (`VeeWorld.presence_varies`). The two numberings are by one and the same course, its two later births matched to the two later exchanges the other way round (`VeeWorld.one_course_two_numberings`, with `VeeWorld.swap_below` and `VeeWorld.vee_swap`): what varies is the matching of births to exchanges.

## 3. What it shows, and what it does not

Shows:
- On a world's finite stretch from its first step, with a footprint containing the exchanges, the numberings the reorderings give are exactly the orders of birth of the courses of causal-set growth of the order of exchanges: one structure read in two models, by a composition of two landed theorems.
- UE14's main laws of presence (taken before, the present as an antichain, present together, presence when taken) hold on the World's reorderings by instance, each with exactly its decision.
- Presence on the world as it is is precedence with no exchange between joined to the earlier one; it gives persistence of what an exchange touched, on the world and on every reordering on which it holds; persistence does not give presence, since an exchange between can be joined to it without leaving a trace on what it touched.

Does not show:
- anything about processes in general, which have no footprint, continuous time, infinite stretches, stretches from a step other than the first, or the present of a center;
- the laws of UE14 taking a `Decidable` instance of the whole precedence, on the World (three of their forms with no decision are instanced, and `past_every_iff` has none);
- OU's representation theorem for the World ([what remains after OU3b](../ou3b-master-laws-on-the-grammar/WHAT-REMAINS.md)): this is one aspect of it, the interleavings;
- that a footprint is given by the World: it is supplied, and the order of exchanges is relative to it;
- which presence or reordering is better.

## 4. Readings

**[AI reading, D45] Presence in time on the World.** The open entry asks whether a condition must still be there when what it conditions arises. On the World, read as UE14 reads presence and with the footprint containing the exchanges, it has the same answer as on causal-set growth: on some reordering exactly at a link, on every reordering exactly at the one link, and between them a choice of interleaving. What the World adds is what presence means there: an exchange present when another is taken has left everything it touched as it left it, and an exchange can leave all that unchanged without being present, where an exchange between is joined to it without leaving a trace on what it touched, through a dependence for instance. So what every interleaving keeps of presence is defined from the order of joined exchanges, as on causal-set growth from precedence, and which presence holds between the two bounds is the choice of interleaving the step index makes.

**[AI reading, D45] One law across two models.** The UE plan's first aim asks that a law be one law and not one per model. Here the laws of presence are stated once, on causal-set growth, and hold on the World's stretches by instance through one representation; the World's reorderings and the courses of growth are the same numberings of the same order.

## 5. Standing

Near-definitional laws and compositions, three lemmas (deciding the order of exchanges by search over the steps between; presence on the world as it is; presence giving persistence) and witnesses. Known in the theory of traces: the linear extensions of a trace's dependence order are exactly its interleavings (Mazurkiewicz 1977; Diekert and Rozenberg, eds., The Book of Traces, 1995), and the prefixes of a trace correspond to the down-sets of its dependence order, the configurations of its event structure (Nielsen, Plotkin and Winskel 1981). In causal set theory the natural labellings are the linear extensions, read as the paths of sequential growth to a causal set (Rideout and Sorkin 2000). Law 2 composes the World's form of the first (`sorts_iff`, landed) with that second fact as UE14 states it, and follows at once from the two; a light search by the review found no paper connecting traces with causal-set growth. Law 5's persistence is an elementary consequence of the footprint containing the exchanges; reading it as what presence means on the World is new here. New is the reading: the World's interleavings as causal-set growth, so one law of presence across the two models.
