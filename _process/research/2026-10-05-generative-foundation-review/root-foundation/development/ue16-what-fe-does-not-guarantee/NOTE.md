## 1. Summary

UE1 contained the free-energy principle as a restatement: its premises on the premise card, its habit read as a lock, and no third part, nothing the theory forbids that the principle leaves open. A search of the principle's literature ([search](../ue1-fep-bridge/THIRD-PART-SEARCH.md)) found three claims, graded (b), that landed laws of the theory forbid under a natural reading: that identical cells minimizing free energy are guaranteed to reach unique places (candidate 1; Friston, Levin, Sengupta and Pezzulo 2015), that risk minimization ensures an agent's needs are met (candidate 2, weak; Albarracin and others 2024), and that shared generative models and preferences make each agent's goal the group's (candidate 3; Friston, Ramstead and others 2024; Da Costa and others 2025). This module states each forbidding on an explicit model of the claim, in the principle's terms, by applying the landed law:
- **Identical cells.** Under a descent that moves every cell by one rule, from its own state and a field read from the configuration, never from a cell's number, two cells alike at the start are alike at every step, and all alike stay all alike: equal states get equal successors. Where in addition no exchange of cells changes the field, the descent commutes with exchanging neighbouring cells, so the run from the exchanged start is the exchanged run; and among configurations of one number of cells, the minima of a free energy symmetric in the cells come in exchanged pairs, and a unique minimum has its cells alike. In a witness two alike cells never reach the least free energy, and a start one unit apart parts them to either end.
- **Shared models and preferences.** In the held pair of a set point, landed in `Becoming.SetPointOptimum`, each of two holders takes, at the two steps they take part, one generative model's choice of least risk at its own store, a condition of the web, at no risk, each store at its set point at every step, while the two together end the pool's renewal, neither coupling into the pool. Alike preferences, each over one's own, are not one goal. A goal over the shared level is no guarantee either: two regulators of the level, each counting only its own take, continue; three end at the first step.
- **Risk and needs.** An agent choosing the first amount of least risk, under its generative model, has its need on a condition end whatever its model and preference, where the rest, without it, outruns a gated renewal; with need one no reading of the condition after the end tells its choice from restraint, and at the last outrun step, from a level of at most one, it takes nothing; and from the rest's end on an expectation at no risk at or above the need is above the level reached. These apply landed laws with a chooser the laws do not read: they forbid the wording "ensures", not the authors' gloss.

**[AI reading, D45]** The free-energy principle explains a system's persistence by its gathering evidence for its own model. What the forbiddings show, in its own terms, is where that explanation stops: minimizing free energy cannot break a symmetry its law keeps, cannot read a drain it does not couple to, and cannot turn alike preferences for one's own into a preference over the joint outcome, while a preference over the joint outcome, each counting only its own take, guarantees nothing either. The theory states each by a law the principle does not state: the commuting law, the outrun law, joint excess and the held pair of a set point. So these are the third parts UE1 lacked, each a known result (symmetry breaking; the commons) brought into the principle's terms.

## 2. The laws

### Law 1. Identical cells (`IdenticalCells`)

- *Alike cells stay alike, under any descent.* A configuration is the list of the cells' states; `descent update field` moves every cell by one rule from its own state and its field. Two neighbouring cells alike at the start are alike at every step, and from all cells alike every two neighbours stay alike (`two_alike_stay_alike`, `all_alike_stay_alike`, through `nth_map` and `nth_replicate`); no hypothesis on the field is needed beyond its reading no cell's number.
- *The descent with an exchangeable field.* Where no exchange of neighbouring cells changes the field (`Exchangeable`), as the summed field of the other cells does not (`othersField`, `othersField_exchangeable`), the step commutes with exchanging cells `k` and `k + 1` (`exchangeAt`, `total_exchangeAt`, `map_exchangeAt`, `length_exchangeAt`, `descent_commutes`).
- *The commuting law, applied.* A start fixed by the exchange stays fixed along the run (`keeps_exchange_fixed`, by `Law.Commutes.keeps_fixed`); the run from the exchanged start is the exchanged run (`exchanged_run`, by `Law.Commutes.iterate`), and rests are exchanged rests (`rests_exchanged`); a configuration is exchange-fixed exactly where the two cells are alike (`alike_of_fixed`, `fixed_of_alike`).
- *Minima.* A free energy summed from one energy of each cell and its field is symmetric (`Symmetric`, `freeEnergy`, `freeEnergy_symmetric`). Among configurations of one number of cells its minima come in exchanged pairs (`minimum_exchanged`), a unique minimum has its cells alike (`unique_minimum_alike`), and a minimum with two neighbouring cells apart is not unique (`apart_not_unique`). Across numbers of cells a summed free energy is uniquely least only at no cells (`unique_across_sizes_empty`), which is why minima are compared within one size.
- *The witness* (`TwoCells`): each cell moves one unit away from the other's state, staying at a tie. The step commutes (`step_commutes`); from `[1, 1]` the cells rest (`alike_rests`), and from any two alike cells the free energy is 6 at every step, above the nothing of `[0, 3]` (`alike_energy`, `pair_of_alike`, `alike_never_least`, with `run_length`); from `[1, 2]` the cells part to `[0, 3]` and from `[2, 1]` to `[3, 0]` (`parts`, `exchanged_parts`); both have the least free energy, so no two-cell configuration is the unique minimum among two-cell configurations (`minima`); other configurations reach nothing too, no cells, one cell at 3, two cells at 0 and 4 (`other_minima`).

### Law 2. Risk and needs (`RiskAndNeeds`)

- *The chooser.* A `Chooser` has the amounts it may take (`first`, `others`), a model `predict` of the next level from its reading and an amount, and a preferred level; its risk is the distance `gap` of the prediction from the preferred level, and `chosen` is the first amount of least risk (`Chooser.risk`, `Chooser.chosen`, `chosen_minimizes`, `chosen_mem`); restraint, taking nothing, is one chooser (`restraint`, `restraint_chosen`). The forager is one model: it reads its own store, uses two a step, prefers three, and takes two at no risk (`forager`, `forager_choice`).
- *The need ends whatever the chooser* (`need_ends`, by `Flow.outrun_below` and `Flow.outrun_ends`): under the World premises (balance, activity needing support, only active giving), the gate, and the rest taking jointly more than a step gives at each of `n` steps from a level below `n + m`, the level stays below `m` from then on and a member outside the rest, depending at a need of at least `m`, ends, whatever chooser gives its takes.
- *No later reading selects restraint* (`restraint_unread`, by `Flow.outrun_reading_alike`): with need one, every score reading the condition only from the rest's end on scores the course of a risk-minimizing member outside the rest and the course of restraint alike. With need one, at the last outrun step, from a level of at most one, a member outside the rest that takes part takes nothing (`outside_takes_nothing`); the earlier outrun steps follow by the same count, not stated.
- *An expectation is not the course* (`expectation_not_course`): where a choice predicts, at no risk, a level at least the need, the level reached is below the prediction, from the rest's end on.
- *A witness at need three* (`Outrun`): the forager takes two at no risk, the rest takes three against a renewal of two, and from step one the forager is inactive by `need_ends`, as it is when restrained (`ends_either_way`).

In `need_ends` and `restraint_unread` the chooser's hypotheses are not used: the takes are not premises of the ending, which is their content, and also why they forbid only the wording "ensures".

### Law 3. Shared models and preferences (`SharedModels`)

- *The held pair, in the principle's terms.* `holder` reads its own store, loses one a step and prefers one; in `Optimum.HeldPair` each holder's take, at the two steps the holders take part, is its choice of least risk at its own store, at no risk, and the model's prediction is the store's next level (`holder`, `heldPair_choices`); each store is at its set point at every step (`Optimum.HeldPair.held`). So each goal is met while each takes within what a step gives the pool, the two take more, the pool's renewal ends from step two, and neither holder couples into the pool (`held_not_one`, by `Optimum.HeldPair.held_end` and `Flow.held_excess_ends`).
- *Foragers at a stipulated store.* `store` is a stipulated reading, three at every step for every member, not a condition of the web. The takes of `Exceeding`'s two takers are the forager's choices at the two steps they take part, and the store is kept there (`exceeding_choices`, `store_kept`); the same forager keeps within its own draw while the pair is in joint excess and every member ends from step 2, and alone with the same renewer it continues (`alike_not_one`, by `Exceeding.own_not_joint`, `Exceeding.all_end` and `Alone.all_continue`).
- *One goal over the shared level* (`OneGoal`): two takers read the shared level and prefer it at four (`regulator`, `regulator_choice`); they take two and then nothing, by turns, and every member continues (`level`, `flow`, `web`, `balances`, `level_two`, `dependent`, `acting`, `gated`, `continues`). Each regulator counts only its own take: three of them take six against a level of four and a renewal of two, and every member ends from step one (`Three`, `threeLevel`, `threeFlow`, `threeWeb`, `threeBalances`, `threeActing`, `threeDependent`, `threeGated`, `three_end`).

## 3. What it shows, and what it does not

Shows:
- that the three claims, read as guarantees, are false on explicit models in the principle's terms, each by a landed law of the theory: the commuting law (with, for never parting, only a descent reading no cell's number), the held pair of a set point and the outrun law;
- what parts identical cells (a start that differs, a rule reading a cell's number, or noise), what separates alike preferences from one goal (preferences over the joint outcome, which by themselves guarantee nothing), and what an agent's need depends on beyond its policy (what the others draw from a gated renewal).

Does not show:
- that the papers' simulations fail: they break the symmetry by random initial expectations and have no shared draw (the search's grading; the 2015 paper's cells start from small random expectations and noisy receptors, checked at the source by the review);
- that the outrun law forbids the authors' gloss of candidate 2: it forbids the wording "ensures";
- anything of the principle's apparatus beyond risk in counting form: densities, variational free energy (UE17), the ambiguity and epistemic terms (UE19), softmax and precision, multi-step policies, learning, reserves between an agent and a renewal, the blanket (UE18), continuous time and noise;
- exchanges of cells other than neighbouring ones;
- that a goal over the shared level keeps a collective going: two regulators continue, three end.

## 4. Readings

**[AI reading, D45] The guarantees, read where they fail.** Each claim, as its authors gloss it, is an expectation under a model or a description of a simulation, and as such is not forbidden. Read as a guarantee, which is how its sentences are worded, each fails exactly where the theory's law says: identical cells under a law that reads no cell's number, alike needs each held at its own store on a shared renewal, a need on a condition others outrun. The free-energy principle has no law of symmetry, no conservation of what passes between thing and world and no renewal law (the premise card's rows on these), so it cannot state where its explanation of persistence stops; the theory can, and these are the cases.

**[AI reading, D45] What this changes for UE1.** UE1 was a restatement. With these, the containment has third parts in the plan's sense: statements in the principle's literature that the theory forbids, each with its hypotheses, a refutation condition (below) and its standing. Claim 3's forbidding was already landed, as the held pair of a set point (UE1b); what UE16 adds there is the reading in the principle's terms, each take a generative model's least-risk choice. They are known results in neighbouring fields, so the theory reaches farther than the principle here, not farther than the sciences together.

## 5. Standing and refutation

- **Identical cells.** Symmetry breaking needs an asymmetric cause (Curie 1894); pattern formation from a homogeneous state needs an instability amplifying perturbations (Turing 1952); identical processors running one deterministic program cannot single one out (Angluin 1980), whose core form is `Relabel.EquivariantChoice.withholds_of_full_tie`. The reading fails if the paper's scheme, run from exactly identical expectations with no noise, differentiates: that would mean a term reading a cell's number in its descent. The search names that run as a cheap frozen check.
- **Shared preferences, risk and needs.** The commons (Lloyd 1833; Hardin 1968) and pivotality (Palfrey and Rosenthal 1984): where the rest alone outruns the renewal, no member outside it is pivotal, as the outrun record reads it; in joint excess each holder can be. The readings fail for agents with no shared gated renewal, or with preferences over the joint outcome that also count the others' takes; `OneGoal` shows a joint reading counting only one's own take guarantees nothing.
- **What is new.** The models in the principle's terms (the descent, the chooser by risk, the holders' choices on the held pair), the elementary laws on minima within one size, `two_alike_stay_alike` in its general form, `expectation_not_course`, `outside_takes_nothing` and the witnesses; `keeps_exchange_fixed`, `exchanged_run`, `need_ends`, `restraint_unread` and `held_not_one` apply landed laws.
