## Findings

**R1 (S). Candidates 4–6 lack the hypotheses of the code law** (table row "Communicating agents…"; §3's closing paragraph; F11 taken in part). As written, the page implies that the code law forbids communicating agents from coming to share one model, without conditions.

The law forbids agreement only under three conditions:
- the start is symmetric under exchanging the formations together with a renaming of forms;
- the law keeps both that exchange and that renaming;
- at the sense in question, the renaming moves every form.

This is 05 §9's row, and the record's statement reads: "no law privileging neither form nor formation settles a symmetric disagreement". In the same row, a preferred form or an order of hearing breaks the symmetry and brings agreement.

UE1's search also finds that the papers' systems lie outside the hypotheses. The duet gives both agents one model. The federated-inference simulation starts from random parameters. The ecosystems paper describes no system of its own.

*Fix:* for the row's relation, write "graded (b) by UE1's search: forbidden from a start symmetric under exchanging the agents with a renaming of forms, under a law keeping both; the papers' simulations lie outside; not stated in the principle's terms here (§3)". For the paragraph, write "…under the laws of a code formed between formations (05 §9): from a start symmetric under exchanging the agents with a renaming of forms, a law keeping both never brings them to agree where the renaming moves every form. The papers' simulations share one model or start asymmetric, and are not refuted. None is stated…".
