30 October 2026. The Lean is a new section of the periphery module [`FreeEnergyReals`](../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/free-energy/FreeEnergyReals.lean), which now also imports the core's `Becoming.FreeEnergyCounting`; no core file changes. Revised after an independent review and a re-check (§6).

## 1. Summary

Two modules state the free-energy principle's central bound. UE17's periphery module `FreeEnergyReals` states it over Mathlib's reals: for a nonnegative belief `q` with total one and nonnegative joint weights `p`, `q` zero wherever `p` is, the free energy `Σ q (log q − log p)` is at least the surprisal `−log Σ p`, with equality exactly at the posterior ([record](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/free-energy-bounds-surprisal.md)). UE21's core module `Becoming.FreeEnergyCounting` states, with no axiom, for natural counts `n` (total `N`) and natural weights `a` (total `A`) over listed hypotheses, `N ^ N * ∏ a ^ n ≤ A ^ N * ∏ n ^ n`, equal exactly in proportion ([record](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/free-energy-bound-in-counts.md)). That the second is the first, read through the logarithm, for the belief `n / N`, was a correspondence by hand: UE21's NOTE §3 and the first item of what free-energy-bound-in-counts does not show name the theorem joining the two as missing, and UE21's review asked for it at `FreeEnergyReals`' next change (REVIEW-UE21 F6, with F7(a) for that module's docstring, which still called the counting form "not proved here").

UE22 proves the join. For counts `n` over hypotheses listed once, with a positive total `N`, natural joint weights `a` with total `A`, and no count where a weight is nothing (the support), the belief in counts `q = n / N` (`countBelief`) has

  `N · (freeEnergy q a − surprisal A) = log (A ^ N * ∏ n ^ n) − log (N ^ N * ∏ a ^ n)`  (`gap_counts`),

the right side being the logarithms of the two sides of the core's counting bound, cast to the reals. From this identity:

- **The two bounds are one at a belief in counts**: each holds exactly where the other does (`surprisal_le_freeEnergy_iff_bound`, whose proof uses neither bound, only the identity and the logarithm's order). From the core's laws, the real bound at such a belief holds (`surprisal_le_freeEnergy_of_counts`, from `FreeEnergyCounting.bound`), is reached exactly where the counts are in the core's proportion `n h * A = a h * N` (`freeEnergy_eq_surprisal_iff_of_counts`, from `FreeEnergyCounting.bound_eq_iff`), which is exactly where the belief is the posterior (`proportional_iff_posterior`), and is strict away from proportion (`surprisal_lt_freeEnergy_of_counts`, from `FreeEnergyCounting.bound_lt_of_not_proportional`).
- **The proofs do not pass through UE17's argument.** The dependency audit ([DependencyAudit.lean](DependencyAudit.lean), [log](dependency-audit.log)) reads theorem bodies, stops on an unknown target and has a positive control. None of the join's twelve theorems reaches `surprisal_le_freeEnergy`, `term_le`, `term_eq_iff`, `term_eq_iff_of_pos`, `freeEnergy_eq_surprisal_iff`, `freeEnergy_eq_record_surprisal_iff`, `Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos` or `Real.log_lt_sub_one_of_pos`, while UE17's `surprisal_le_freeEnergy` reaches `term_le` and `Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos`. Every statement about Mathlib's logarithm reaches `x + 1 ≤ exp x` (`Real.add_one_le_exp`), which is `log y ≤ y − 1`, through the definition of `Real.log` as the inverse of `exp` (`Real.expOrderIso`). With that definition left unexpanded, the join's theorems reach neither `Real.add_one_le_exp` nor `Real.add_one_lt_exp`, and UE17's bound reaches both. So, for beliefs in counts, the real bound is the core's counting law plus the algebra and order of the logarithm, not UE17's argument. The reviewer found, with the same definition unexpanded, that the series bounds of `exp` the proofs reach hold for nonnegative arguments only, so that they use that `exp` increases (REVIEW-UE22 F1).
- **The log ratio is `N` times the divergence from the posterior** (`divergence_counts`, through `freeEnergy_eq_divergence_add_surprisal`), so the counting bound's left side is its right side times `exp (−N · D(n / N ‖ a / A))` (`counts_eq_mul_exp`).
- **In the core's generative model.** The gap reads the joint weights up to a factor (`gap_div`: dividing every joint weight by one amount `c ≠ 0` shifts the free energy and the surprisal by `log c` together). So against UE17's joint shares, each a hypothesis's prior weight times the record's weight under it over the whole weight, and the record's share, `N` times the gap is the logarithm of the ratio of the two sides of `FreeEnergyCounting.bound_given_record`, whose total is the record's weight (`gap_counts_given_record`). The whole weight drops out, which settles the difference of normalization UE21's modelling note left ("both bounds then shifting by its logarithm"). From the core's laws the bound holds there (`record_surprisal_le_freeEnergy_of_counts`, from `FreeEnergyCounting.bound_given_record`), is reached exactly where every count over the total is the same ratio as the hypothesis's share given the record (`freeEnergy_eq_record_surprisal_iff_of_counts`, from `FreeEnergyCounting.given_record_eq_iff_share`), which as real numbers is where the belief is that share, UE17's form (`freeEnergy_eq_record_surprisal_iff_toReal_of_counts`, through `Share.same_iff_toReal_eq` and `whole_restrict`), and is strict elsewhere (`record_surprisal_lt_freeEnergy_of_counts`).
- **What each hypothesis does.** The hypotheses listed once are needed for the identity itself: with `true` listed twice and every count and weight one, `N` times the gap is `−log 2` while the log ratio is `0`, the real side summing over the set of hypotheses and the core's over the listing (`Witness.gap_counts_duplicate`). Off the support the identity can fail (`Witness.gap_counts_off_support`: counts one and one against weights nothing and one, where under `log 0 = 0` `N` times the gap is `−2 log 2`, so the real bound fails, while the counting bound's left side is nothing and its right side one, so the core's bound holds strictly). It holds off the support exactly at one count, or where every weight is nothing and one hypothesis holds every count (by hand, in REVIEW-UE22 F2; 69 of the 1,989 off-support small cases, all of these); at one count against weights totalling one the real form is at equality while the core's bound is strict (`Witness.one_count_off_support`), so off the support the two cases of equality can part even where the identity holds (against weights totalling two or more both are strict, and against weights totalling nothing both are at equality; RECHECK-UE22 N1). At total nothing both sides of the identity are nothing (a probe in REVIEW-UE22 F6), so the positive total serves the reading as a belief and the case of equality, where counts of total nothing would be in proportion to every weighting.

**[AI reading, D45]** So for beliefs in counts, on the support and over hypotheses listed once, the principle's bound is one law in two forms (`surprisal_le_freeEnergy_iff_bound`): the free energy of the belief exceeds the record's surprisal by exactly `1 / N` times the logarithm of the ratio of the counting bound's right side to its left, and the cases of equality and of strictness are the core's. The real form adds the logarithm, the beliefs whose shares are not ratios of counts and the joint weights whose ratios are irrational. On beliefs in counts, on the support and over hypotheses listed once, its bound, equality and strictness add no law to the core's, while the core's bound also holds off the support and for any listing, where the real form does not follow it. With `n` as observed counts and `a / A` as a model, `counts_eq_mul_exp` is the identity of the method of types (Cover and Thomas 2006, Theorem 11.1.2; Csiszár and Körner 1981): a sequence with these counts is less probable under the model than under its own frequencies by the factor `exp (−N · D)`, so the free-energy gap, the log ratio per count, is the divergence by which the model misses the frequencies.

## 3. What it shows, and what it does not

Shows:
- for beliefs in counts over hypotheses listed once, with a positive total and on the support, the exact identity between the real free-energy gap and the logarithm of the counting bound's ratio, and so that the two bounds hold there exactly together;
- the real bound, its equality at the posterior and its strictness elsewhere, at such beliefs, derived from the core's counting laws, not through UE17's Gibbs lemmas, and reaching `Real.add_one_le_exp` (`x + 1 ≤ exp x` for every `x`) only through the definition of the logarithm (§1);
- the same identity, bound, equality (as a ratio and as real numbers) and strictness in a core generative model, against UE17's joint shares and record share, the whole weight dropping out;
- that the identity can fail with a hypothesis listed twice or off the support, so neither hypothesis can be dropped, and that where it holds off the support the two cases of equality can part.

Does not show:
- beliefs whose shares are not ratios of counts, or densities: the real bound for them still rests on `log x ≤ x − 1` (`term_le`); a limit from beliefs in counts is not taken;
- joint weights whose ratios are irrational: the join needs natural joint weights or, through `gap_div`, natural weights divided by one amount, a case no theorem states;
- listings with repetition: the identity itself fails there (`Witness.gap_counts_duplicate`), the real side summing over the set of hypotheses and the core's over the listing, while the core's bound holds for any listing (UE21's `Witness.duplicate_share` shows a repeated hypothesis breaking the join to the share);
- a record weighing nothing, which the hypotheses exclude (`total_pos_of_counts`), so UE21's degenerate case (`nothing_record_reach_all`) is not read through the logarithm;
- a minimization at one fixed total, where the bound may be unreached (UE21's `Witness.total_two_unreached`); over count beliefs of every total the least free energy is the surprisal, reached exactly in proportion, which follows from the laws above but is not stated as one theorem;
- complexity and accuracy read from the core's own shares, or any process by which a system lowers its free energy.

## 4. Standing and refutation

- **The identity** is near-definitional: the logarithm of a product of powers is the sum of the counts times the logarithms, and the rest is rearrangement. That the counting ratio is `exp (N · D)` is the method of types' identity for the probability of a sequence of a given type (Cover and Thomas 2006, Theorem 11.1.2 in §11.1, there in base 2; Csiszár and Körner 1981). Gibbs' inequality (Gibbs 1902), the nonnegativity of the divergence (Kullback and Leibler 1951), is the weighted mean inequality with the belief's shares as weights and the ratios of the posterior to the belief as values; with rational weights that is Cauchy's unweighted arithmetic–geometric mean inequality (Cauchy 1821) by repetition (REVIEW-UE21 F8), which is the equivalence the identity makes exact.
- **New here.** That `FreeEnergyReals`' bound, with its equality and strictness, at beliefs in counts is a consequence of a law of the pure core with an empty axiom report, by proofs that do not pass through UE17's Gibbs argument (the audit); that the two bounds are one there as a theorem; and that UE17's and UE21's statements in the core's generative model coincide there, the normalization by the whole weight dropping out. Neither is a new inequality.
- **Refutation.** A proved identity has no counter-model. What could be wrong is its reading: the free-energy reading needs a positive total, the support and the hypotheses listed once, each a hypothesis of the Lean. The last two are needed for the identity itself (`Witness.gap_counts_duplicate`, `Witness.gap_counts_off_support`). At total nothing both sides are nothing, so positivity serves the reading and the case of equality.
