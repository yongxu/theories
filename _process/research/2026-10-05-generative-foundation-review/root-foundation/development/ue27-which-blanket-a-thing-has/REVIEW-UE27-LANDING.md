1. **High. The page's §4 makes the formation itself the agent, without the hypothesis.** `theories/becoming/FREE-ENERGY-PRINCIPLE.md` §4: "Every formation is, by construction, the agent of a blanketed law whose sensory course is its own course through its couplings, which forbids nothing of its course."
   - Why: in `couplingLaw` the internal part is an arbitrary model `M` with an arbitrary `update`; the formation contributes only the sensory values. The Lean review (findings 3 and 12) and DESIGN §3 A5 and A7 say that the agent is a model fed by what couples in, not the formation or its own record. 2. **High. "Only a sparse partition ... can be refuted" contradicts `whole_iff`, and no Lean theorem states the "only".** At fault:
   - the new record's `statement.en`: "a blanket's existence can be refuted only under a sparse partition fixed apart from the course" (and `statement.zh`: "毯之存在唯于独立于历程而定之稀疏之分下可被驳");

   - the study's standing (`kit/study_ue27.txt`): "only a sparse partition fixed apart from the family can be refuted";
   - the `regions.json` note: "a blanket's existence constrains only under a sparse partition fixed apart from the course";
   - the record's `related[2]`: "here the coupling form, refuted only under a partition fixed apart from it" (see also finding 4).
   - Why: the whole-configuration reading is degenerate and fixed apart from every family, and by `whole_iff` it gives a law determined part by part exactly to the families whose law is determined. So it refutes every other family: `clocked_witness`'s family of two courses parting at their first step has no law determined part by part under it, and a single run that revisits a configuration and leaves it two ways is refuted by it. An injective reading likewise requires determinedness (`injective_consistent`). The chapter's own sentence ("A sparse partition fixed apart from the family, on a law the same at every step, can be refuted, even by one run") has no "only" and is right.
   - Replacement (record statement): "...; a degenerate reading of the configuration refutes no family whose law is determined, while a sparse partition fixed apart from the family can be refuted, even by one run." (zh: "……；构型之退化之读不驳任何其律确定之族，而独立于族而定之稀疏之分可被驳，一运行即可。") For the In brief, see finding 5, and for §4, finding 10; for the study, "for a family whose law is determined, a degenerate reading is never refuted; a sparse partition fixed apart from the family can be".
