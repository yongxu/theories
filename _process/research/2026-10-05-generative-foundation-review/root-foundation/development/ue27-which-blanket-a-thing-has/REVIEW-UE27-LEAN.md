# UE27: review of the scratch Lean `Becoming.BlanketCouplings`

AI-written by an independent reviewer, 1 November 2026. It reviews the scratch module `tree/Becoming/BlanketCouplings.lean` (branch ue27-kit, merged with main cc2074912) against its design ([DESIGN](DESIGN.md)), the repository's rules and the modules it builds on. The reviewer's probe is in `review-lean/`.

## Summary

The module builds as stated (31 theorems in 55 declarations, every axiom report empty), and every Lean statement is true and says what its own docstring says, up to findings 8 and 11. No statement is vacuous or circular. The problems are in what the header and the design read into the theorems:

- **High (finding 1).** The general join is a construction with a clock on the world's side and an arbitrary environment. By the module's own C1, its existence forbids nothing. The design's "a formation's couplings are such a map", which puts the join at the level that can be refuted, has no support in the Lean.
- **Medium.** A is near-definitional, and its closed loop reads both sides off one course (2). "Every World formation" holds in a narrower sense: the agent is a model, the World's own case is the open loop, and `obs` is supplied, not given by the web (3). Level 2 is misstated as an existence claim, and the line between C2 and C3 is the partition's sparsity, not when it is chosen (5). "Read the step" should be "read the course" (6). "Has a blanket" in C2 means a partial law with moves (7). Two theorems are one step weaker than the Lean gives (8). A6 is absent, so 03 §1's reading is half a theorem (12).
- **Low.** ThresholdDrain is its case, checked (4); names (9); namespace placement (10); the "cases" of `PartDependsOnlyOn` (11); a "forbids" docstring (13); hygiene (14); counting (15).

Counts: 1 high, 7 medium, 7 low.

## Findings

1. **High. The join is itself a blanket whose existence forbids nothing; it is not a "map fixed apart from the family" at C's third level.** `couplingLaw`'s external part holds the step (`Nat × X`), and `update`, `policy`, `lev` and `env` are arbitrary. No course of a World is tested against the law: the law is built from the coupling values, so every formation of every web, with any model, policy and environment, has it by construction. By the module's own C1, a blanket with a clock on the world's side constrains nothing. DESIGN §3 ("A formation's couplings are such a map, fixed by the web before any course … The coupling form thus matches `Blanket`'s weighting form, where a course is forbidden only by a weighting fixed apart from it") and the planned §4 page line ("A formation's couplings are such a partition, and under them every formation is a blanketed agent") put A at the level that forbids. Nothing in the Lean supports that. Fix: in the header, the record, 03 §1 and the page, say that A is a construction, not a test. Under its couplings every formation is the agent of a blanketed law by construction, and having that law forbids nothing of its course. A's content is what the sensory part carries (`sensory_course`) and that what the agent tells apart passes through it (`blanket_iff_through`, `internal_through_couplings`). Drop "such a map/partition" and the `forbids_iff` analogy.

2. **Medium. A is near-definitional too, and the closed loop adds no content beyond `agent_through_sensory`.** `couplingLaw` sets the sensory part's next state to `value t (lev t x)` by definition. `Web.ValuesThrough` is the hypothesis that `value t` is a complete invariant of `Web.AlikeThrough` at `t`. So `sensory_course` is the definition read along the clock (`clock`). `blanket_iff_through` is `sensory_course` plus `Web.alikeThrough_iff` (the hypothesis read backwards) one way, and `Blanket.agent_through_sensory` the other. In the closed loop `ownLevels` is read off the same course as the blanket (`ownLevels` at `n` is `lev n` of the course's own external part), so the iff relates two quantities of one course. It says that the sensory part carries exactly the coupling values and that the active part follows the sensory course. It does not say that the agent's course is fixed by a level history given in advance; only the open loop (`selectsThrough`) says that. DESIGN §5 lists A among the new results with B and C as the near-definitional ones. Fix: standing in the record and on the page: "near-definitional: the general form of `ThresholdDrain.blanket_iff_through`, any web, model, policy and environment". Wherever a text says "every reading of its internal course is a selection through its couplings" without "in the open loop", add it. The header already does this (line 22-23); check the record, 03 §1 and the page.

3. **Medium. "Covers every World formation whose admission is decided and whose couplings are listed" (header, lines 24-26) is right only in a narrower sense than it reads.** (a) The internal part is an arbitrary model `M` with `update` and `policy`, not anything of the World. The agent is a model fed by what couples into the formation, not the formation's own record (A6 was not done, finding 12). (b) A `World` is one history (`World.history : Run …`), so its own case is the open loop with `l := w.web.level`. The closed loop, with `env` reading the act, is a model beside the World, not the World's exchange; DESIGN A5 says so, and the header should too. (c) The couplings by level `obs` are supplied, not given by the web. `Testing`'s header calls a coupling by level "supplied"; the web gives only admission (`Web.Admits` from `requires` and `need`). So the header's "the join takes the formation's couplings, given by the web" (line 55-56) and DESIGN §3's "a formation's couplings are such a map, fixed by the web before any course" are inaccurate for `obs`. The probe checks that the World instance typechecks (`world_selects`, below). Fix (header and record): "for every world `w` and formation `g` of it, with `obs` any couplings by level, admission at `g` decided and `obs g` listed (`Web.listed_values`, `W := w.web`), any model fed by what couples into `g` is the agent of a blanketed law … ; the World's own case is the open loop at `w.web.level`". In line 55-56, write "the join takes the formation's couplings: admission, given by the web, and the couplings by level `obs`, supplied".

4. **Low (checked). `ThresholdDrain.blanket_iff_through` is A's case, but only through a course correspondence, and the header's description of it is off.** The probe (`TD.threshold_case`, empty axiom report) derives `ThresholdDrain.blanket_iff_through`'s exact statement from `blanket_iff_through` at `X := Nat` (the supply), `lev t x := fun _ => x`, `env _ _ _ x := x - 1`, `value t l := Nat.blt 0 (l ())`, `update m o _ := m && o`, `policy _ _ a := a` and `obs := fun _ _ => False`. It takes three steps: `values` (the threshold coupling as `ValuesThrough`); `corr` (the instance's course matches `ThresholdDrain.law`'s part by part, with the external part `(n, supply)` against `supply`); and `own_eq` (the own levels are `fun _ => e - s`, by `ThresholdDrain.external_course`). So "the case of its witness's web" is true. But the instance law is not `ThresholdDrain.law`: its external part is `Nat × Nat`, and the join is the case only after a course correspondence. The header's "with the external part a supply in place of a step and a level history" (line 26-27) reads as if the step were dropped. Fix (header line 26-27): "`ThresholdDrain`'s join is its case at the witness's web: the environment state is the supply, beside the step, and the two courses correspond part by part (checked in the review's probe)". Whether `ThresholdDrain`'s direct proof goes stays with `Blanket`'s owner (DESIGN §6).

5. **Medium. Level 2 is misstated as an existence claim, and "chosen after / fixed apart" is not the line the Lean draws.** The header's lines 41-44 state the theorems accurately: constant, always; injective, only where consistent; whole configuration, exactly there. But DESIGN §1 (Purpose 2, "every family whose law is determined has one"), §3 C2's heading ("constrain only determinedness") and §3's summary 2 ("a family has one exactly where the law it shows is determined, and under a constant map always") do not. Under a reading chosen after the family, the constant reading always gives a law determined part by part (`const_consistent`). So the existence of a blanket under some post-hoc reading constrains nothing, not even determinedness. Determinedness is what an injective reading requires (`injective_consistent`), and only that one way: the probe's `injective_converse_fails` shows that under the identity reading, which is injective, `ReadsExternal`'s run is consistent and not consistent part by part. Moreover, `inner` and the constant reading are themselves fixed apart from every family. What separates C2's readings from C3's is the partition's content, not when it is chosen. C2's partitions are degenerate: everything in one part, or nothing in any part. C3's partition is sparse, with the internal part barred from reading the external part that moves it. Fix: rewrite the three levels as (1) readings of the course (its label and step, or its future: finding 6): every family has a blanket; (2) readings of the configuration: a degenerate one gives every family a law determined part by part (constant), or every family whose law is determined (the whole configuration). An injective one gives a blanket only where the law is determined, and not always there (`ReadsExternal` under the identity); (3) a sparse partition fixed apart from the family can be refuted, even by one run. Keep "chosen after the family" only as the reason (1) and (2) constrain nothing: whoever may pick the reading after seeing the family can pick a degenerate one. Add `injective_converse_fails` (or its one line) to the module beside `injective_consistent`, since the page's sentence "an injective map only where" otherwise reads as an iff.

6. **Medium. "With parts that may read the step, every family of courses has a blanket" (header line 38-40) is inaccurate in both directions.** `ofFamily`'s external part holds which course (`r`) as well as the step, and its sensory part holds the next value. (a) The step alone is not enough for a family. The probe's `clock_only_fails` gives two courses alike at steps 0 and 1 and parting at step 2. Under the same reading shape with only the step in the external part (`⟨x n, x (n+1), (), n⟩`), they have no law determined part by part: the world's side reads `(false, (), 0)` at step 0 for both and must go to `true` and to `false`. (b) The step is not needed. The probe's `ofFuture` is a blanketed law with no clock and no label: its sensory part holds the course's future (`Nat → α`), shifted each step, and the internal part takes its head. Its course from `⟨x 0, drop 1 x, (), ()⟩` has `x n` as its internal part at every `n` (`ofFuture_course`). So the line is between readings of the course (its label, its step, its future) and readings of the configuration, as DESIGN §2's definition half says ("or something of the course's future"). For a single course (`ρ := Unit`) the step does suffice, so DESIGN §4's page line "if the parts may read the step, every course has one" is right as written for one course. Fix (header): "With parts that may read the course, not only the configuration (which course and the step, as here, or its future), every family of courses has a blanket". `ofFamily`'s docstring is already right. Consider renaming `ofFamily` to `clocked` (finding 9).

7. **Medium. "Has a blanket" / "gives one" in C2 means a law determined part by part (possibly partial, with the family's moves), not a `Blanketed`.** `BlanketConsistentFamily H` is, by `admitted_iff`, "admitted by some process determined part by part". That process may be partial (`ShownBy H`) and keeps the family's moves `M`. A `Blanketed` is total and has no moves. Only one direction joins them (`Blanketed.courses_consistent`), and the converse is left out (B4). For C1 the module does build a `Blanketed` (`ofFamily`), so "has a blanket" is literal there. For C2 (`const_consistent`, `inner_iff`, any `M`) it is the part-by-part law, not a blanket in `Blanket`'s sense. The header's "a constant map always gives one" (line 42) and DESIGN §4's page line "every determined law has one" read as a `Blanketed`. Fix: in the header's C2 sentence and on the page, write "a law determined part by part" (once glossed: "the blanket's form on the law a family shows, partial and keeping moves"), or restrict C2's statements to families with the trivial move and say the law is partial.

8. **Medium. `internal_through_couplings` and `selectsThrough` are one step weaker than the Lean gives.** Levels alike through the couplings up to `n` fix the sensory course up to `n + 1`, hence the internal course up to `n + 2`: `internal_through_blanket` at `n + 2` needs exactly the blanket before `n + 2`, which `blanket_iff_through` gives. The module states `k ≤ n + 1` and readings up to `n + 1` (it weakens through `Nat.le_succ_of_le`). The probe's `internal_tight` and `selects_tight` prove the `n + 2` forms with the module's own proof shape (build result below). `ThresholdDrain.any_model` uses the tight count (blanket before 4, readings up to 4). Fix: state both at `n + 2` (change `k ≤ n + 1` to `k ≤ n + 2` and drop `Nat.le_succ_of_le`; in `selectsThrough`, `k ≤ n + 2`), and docstrings "up to `n + 2`". Otherwise the record would state a bound that is not tight.

9. **Low. Names.**
   - `agentNext` and `worldNext` are projections of any configuration (its internal and active parts, its sensory and external parts), not next states. They read wrongly wherever they are applied to a present configuration, and the docstrings ("The agent's next state: the internal and active parts") state a wrong meaning. Use `agentParts` and `worldParts` (or `autonomousParts`, as `Blanket`'s header calls internal and active "the principle's autonomous states"). `agentReads` and `worldReads` are right.
   - `inner` is "the whole-configuration reading" in every docstring. In the package, `inner` already names the inner four lines of a hexagram (`Hexagram.inner`, HexagramLaws) and an inner product (Dephasing), and "inner" suggests the internal part, which the reading fills but does not mean. Use `whole` (`whole_iff`, `ReadsExternal.whole_consistent`).
   - `ofFamily` is used only as "the clocked reading" (`clocked_consistent`, `clocked_witness`). Rename it `clocked` (with `clocked_course`).
   - `unmoved` is an environment step that leaves the level history as it is. As a bare name in the namespace it says nothing of what is unmoved: use `levelsStay` or `exogenous`. `openLaw` is fine but `openLoop` matches every docstring ("the open loop").
   - The theorem `selectsThrough` repeats the definition `Web.SelectsThrough`'s name in lower case and states a reading of the internal course. `internal_selectsThrough` says which.
   - "Autonomous law" (header line 49) uses "autonomous" for a law the same at every step, while `Blanket`'s header uses "autonomous states" for the internal and active parts, the principle's sense. Write "a law the same at every step", as the rest of the header does.
   - The header cites `Mechanism.DependsOnlyOn` and `HexagramSequences.runsOrder_reads` by module. The Lean names are `Becoming.DependsOnlyOn` and `Becoming.Hexagram.runsOrder_reads`. Write the Lean names, with the module in words if wanted, so that a name search or an index finds them.
   - `ReadsExternal`, `parting`, `levelsAt`, `couplingLaw`, `startAt`, `PartDependsOnlyOn`, `PartConsistentFamily`, `BlanketDetermined` and `BlanketConsistentFamily` are exact.

10. **Low. `Blanketed.blanketDetermined`, `Blanketed.step_alike` and `Blanketed.courses_consistent` live at `Becoming.BlanketCouplings.Blanketed.*`, not at `Becoming.Blanket.Blanketed.*`.** They are declared inside `namespace BlanketCouplings`. By Lean's field-notation rule, `b.courses_consistent` for `b : Blanket.Blanketed …` looks only in `Becoming.Blanket.Blanketed`, and will not find them; the module itself always writes the explicit form. Records, the page and LEAN-INDEX must cite the full names. A later `Blanket.Blanketed.step_alike` would make the short name ambiguous under `open Blanket`. Fix: rename them in the module's own namespace (`blanketed_determined`, `blanketed_step_alike`, `blanketed_courses_consistent`), or keep them and cite them fully. (Not built: an inference from the declarations and Lean's rule.)

11. **Low (checked). `PartDependsOnlyOn`'s three "cases" (docstring, header line 29-32).** In the probe, `case_dependsOnlyOn` and `case_goAlike` are `Iff.rfl`: definitional cases. `case_determined` needs a proof, since `Determined` binds one configuration and `PartDependsOnlyOn P id id` two with an equation, so it is an equivalence, not a case. All three hold only at `Type`, since the module fixes `{S M K K' : Type}` while `DependsOnlyOn`, `GoAlike` and `Determined` are universe-polymorphic. Fix (docstring): "`DependsOnlyOn P π` is the case `ρ := id` and `GoAlike P π` the case `ρ := π`, both by definition; `Determined P` is equivalent to the case where both are the identity". Optionally make the section's types universe-polymorphic (`Type u`), which costs nothing here.

12. **Medium. A6 is absent, so the page's §5 line and part of 03 §1's reading stay open.** DESIGN §3 A6 (the formation's own record as the internal part) was optional and is not in the module. By DESIGN §4, "its own record, rather than a model, as the internal part" stays on the page's §5 list and in the record's `next_delta`. 03 §1's reading ("a formation's support and observed levels are its sensory states, what it keeps of them its internal states") becomes a theorem only for its first half. With `Web.listed_values`, the sensory part is exactly (admission, the listed levels), and A gives that. The second half, "what it keeps of them", is not a theorem: the internal part is an arbitrary model `M` with an arbitrary `update`, not something the formation keeps in the World. Fix: 03 §1's new sentence says "any model fed by what couples into the formation, with any update and policy, is the agent of a blanketed law whose sensory course is the formation's own course …". The record's `does_not_show` gains "the formation's own record as the internal part", and `next_delta` keeps it, the threshold-reading policy's supply and a person's review.

13. **Low. `clocked_witness`'s docstring says "The clocked reading forbids nothing".** DESIGN §4 sets "No 'forbids' wording anywhere for C1 or C2". The theorem is one witness; the "nothing" is `clocked_consistent`, for every family. Fix: "**The clocked reading holds where the family is not consistent**: …". The section heading "A reading fixed apart from the course refutes a blanket on one run" should say "can refute": the whole-configuration reading is fixed too and refutes nothing (finding 5).

14. **Low. Hygiene, fragility and redundancy.**
   - The binder ascriptions `(step : y = b.step x)` in `Blanketed.blanketDetermined` and `(held : h = run)` in `ReadsExternal.inner_consistent` rely on the bodies of `Assurance.ofMap` (`t = f s a`) and of the family predicate unfolding by definition. A change of `ofMap` to `f s a = t` would break them silently in the elaborator, not in meaning. Acceptable, but name `Assurance.ofMap`'s orientation in a comment, or match on `Eq.symm`-neutral forms.
   - `Blanketed.step_alike`, `clock`, `open_external` and `ReadsExternal.internal_course` rely on `course (n + 1)` unfolding to `step (course n)` (`Law.orbit`), as the whole package does. That is fine, but it is the one place a change of `Law.orbit` would reach.
   - `open_external` has no docstring.
   - `ReadsExternal.internal_course` is a computation by `rfl` and should not be counted as a law.
   - `pair_eq` and `unit_eq` are general utilities in a leaf; `Weighted.pair_eq` already exists for `Nat` (with a tactic proof). No clash, since the namespaces differ.
   - No `simp`, `omega`, `decide`, `by` blocks or `injEq` occur; the scratch build and the probe's audits are empty. Nothing in the file invites an axiom in a later edit, except that a reflex to replace `parts_eq` by `Parts.mk.injEq` or `congrArg Prod.fst` by `Prod.mk.inj` would bring `propext`. The header says so; keep it.

15. **Low. What counts as a result (standing).** Of the 31 theorems:
   - **Lemmas** (not results): `parts_eq`, `pair_eq`, `unit_eq`, `PartDependsOnlyOn.of_le`, `PartConsistentFamily.mono`, `BlanketDetermined.of_le`, `BlanketConsistentFamily.mono`, `clock`, `open_external`, `open_levels` and `ReadsExternal.internal_course`.
   - **Corollaries:** `shownBy_blanketDetermined_iff` (two applications of the general iff), `Blanketed.step_alike`, `clocked_consistent` (`courses_consistent` plus `ofFamily_course`), `internal_through_couplings` and `selectsThrough` (A3 plus `internal_through_blanket`), and `ReadsExternal.not_blanketed` (`courses_consistent` plus `not_consistent`).
   - **Near-definitional laws:** A (`sensory_course`, `blanket_iff_through`; finding 2) and B (`shownBy_partDependsOnlyOn_iff`, the part-by-part `shownBy_determined_iff`; `admitted_iff`; `BlanketDetermined.determined`; `Blanketed.blanketDetermined`; `Blanketed.courses_consistent`).
   - **New:** only the C classification, once restated as in finding 5. Its witnesses are near-definitional (`ReadsExternal` is built so that the internal part reads the external one; `HexagramSequences` has the same kind).

   The record should count one result, with three parts (A, B, C), all near-definitional, the classification being the delta. It should not count 31 theorems. Fix: DESIGN §5's standing, the record's `standing` and the page's claim-table row say so.
