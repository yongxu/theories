# UE28: review of the scratch Lean

AI-written by an independent reviewer, 1 November 2026.

**Summary.** The new section is correct Lean: every theorem proves what its statement says, the build is clean and the axiom reports are empty. The trouble is what the World reading claims beyond that. The general part is sound and near-definitional: one course, read with the step as the external part, is consistent part by part exactly where its agent is determined (`readAlong_consistent_iff`). The World reading, though, says "the value of what couples into it" and "its act", and nothing in the Lean ties either to the World. `levelRun_admitted_iff` takes any value and any act course. `Web.ValuesThrough` compares values at one step only, so a value carrying the step meets it, and with that value the phased drain's level is the internal part of a law determined part by part. With the step as the act, every run is admitted. The steady feed passes only because its level never repeats, so it would pass with any feed and any act. So the witness offered for half of the delta shows nothing. Each of these has a small fix, and a probe checks each one in the pure core: a cross-step value condition met by `listed_values`' value, which turns the iff into a statement on the World's records alone; an alternating drain-and-feed witness that is determined and not vacuously; and `rowAct` read with presence. The probe's numbers: 19 theorems, 41 declarations, every axiom report empty, no warnings (`review-lean/`, log `review-lean/probe.log`). The probe builds against a copy of the scratch `BlanketCouplings.lean` in `review-lean/tree/Becoming/`. Do not commit that copy or `review-lean/tree/.build/`. Count: 3 high, 4 medium, 6 low.

## Findings

### 1. High: "the value of what couples into it" is not in the theorem, and `ValuesThrough` cannot put it there: a value carrying the step admits the phased drain

**What is wrong.** `levelRun W g value act` takes any `value : Nat → (R → Nat) → O`, and `agentDetermined_iff_exchange` and `levelRun_admitted_iff` assume nothing of it. Their docstrings still say "the value of what couples into it". DESIGN item 5 adds: "Where `W.ValuesThrough obs g value`, two steps' sensory states agree exactly where their levels admit `g` alike and agree on what couples into `g` by level". That is false. `ValuesThrough` is `∀ t x y, value t x = value t y ↔ …`: it compares values at one step and says nothing about `value j x = value k y` for `j ≠ k`, which is exactly the comparison `AgentDetermined` makes. The package already shows this (`Web.states_by_reading`: the level with the step also gives the values). In the probe, `clockValue t _ := t` gives the values of what couples into the phased drain's `g` (`clockValue_values`). Under it the agent is determined (`clock_agentDetermined`), and the phased drain's level is the internal part of a law determined part by part (`clock_admitted`). So `PhasedDrain.not_admitted` holds only for the `Unit` value, not for "the value of what couples in". The witnesses' own `values` theorems are proved but no theorem uses them.

**Fix.** State the World reading with a value that gives the values across steps, and say why in the text. The probe does it in about thirty lines, every axiom report empty:

- `ValuesAcross W obs g value := ∀ j k x y, value j x = value k y ↔ (W.Admits x j g ↔ W.Admits y k g) ∧ ∀ c, obs g c → x c = y c`, with `ValuesAcross.through` (it gives `ValuesThrough`);
- `listed_valuesAcross`: `listed_values`' value `(admits t x, cs.map x)`, the one the header already recommends, meets it;
- `levelRun_admitted_iff_records`: under `ValuesAcross`, the run is admitted exactly where, at any two steps alike on `g`'s level, alike in admitting `g`, alike on the conditions coupled into `g` by level and alike in the act, the exchange into `g` cross-sums alike and the next acts agree. This version mentions no value at all: it is a statement on the World's records.

Adopt the last as the World theorem, or keep `levelRun_admitted_iff` and add it. Replace DESIGN item 5's sentence with: `Where the value gives the values across steps (ValuesAcross; listed_values' value does), two steps' sensory states agree exactly where their levels admit g alike, each at its step, and agree on what couples into g by level. ValuesThrough compares values at one step only, and a value carrying the step meets it (Web.states_by_reading), under which every run passes.` In `PhasedDrain.not_admitted`'s docstring, add "read with a value carrying no step".

### 2. High: "its act" is any supplied course, and with the step as the act every run is admitted

**What is wrong.** The general theorems quantify over every `act : Nat → A`. `rowAct` appears only in the two witnesses. The probe's `clockAct_admitted` takes any web, any formation and any value, with `act := fun t => t`. The run read by `levelRun` is then admitted by a law determined part by part, balanced or not. DESIGN item 5 says "The act reading is supplied". Yet `levelRun_admitted_iff`'s docstring ("exactly where the exchange into it is read off its level, the value of what couples into it and its act"), the header bullet and the proposed 03 §1 text all say "its act", which reads as the formation's act in the World. `AgentDetermined`'s docstring, "read by no clock", hides the same freedom: the internal, sensory and active courses may each carry the step.

**Fix.** State the World theorem with the World's act, not any course. Take `act := presentRow presence cs L g`, where `presentRow presence cs L g t := match presence t with | true => rowAct cs L g t | false => ([], [])` and `presence` decides `g ∈ L.present t` (see finding 5; matching on a Bool needs no `if` and no choice). Or keep the general form and say what it allows. `AgentDetermined`'s docstring: `A law the same at every step on the agent's side: no clock beyond what the internal, sensory and active courses carry; a course carrying the step meets it at every pair of steps.` `levelRun_admitted_iff`'s docstring: add `for the act course supplied; with an act or a value carrying the step every run passes, so the World's reading takes the act from the flow and the value with no step in it`.

### 3. High: the condition is tested only where states recur, and the steady feed passes vacuously

**What is wrong.** `AgentDetermined` compares pairs of steps alike on the internal, sensory and active states. So along one run it constrains nothing at steps where these never recur. `SteadyFeed`'s level is `t`, so it never repeats. The probe's `steadyFeed_any` shows that its agent is determined with every value and every act course. A feed that is irregular, or not read off anything, into a strictly rising level would pass just the same. So `SteadyFeed.agentDetermined` and `course_eq` cannot show what DESIGN §2.C item 9 and the header take from them ("Being fed by what does not couple in breaks nothing: what matters is whether the exchange into the level is read off the level…"). Any exchange into a level that never repeats is "read off" it. Nowhere in the prose is the test said to bite only at recurrences. Without that, a reader takes a law that admits most runs of growing levels for a constraint (AGENTS.md: "A law admitting every outcome explains nothing").

**Fix.** Replace `SteadyFeed` with the probe's `Alternating`, or add it beside. In `Alternating`, `h` takes one unit from `g` when on and gives one when off, its phase its own level. The flow balances (`balances`), `h` does not couple into `g` (`uncoupled`), `g`'s level goes 1, 0, 1, 0, … with steps 0 and 2 alike on it (`repeats`), and the agent is determined (`agentDetermined`, the next level read off the level through `gLevel_inj`). It differs from `PhasedDrain` only in its period: two phases pass, three fail. That shows the line non-vacuously. Add one sentence to `readAlong_consistent_iff`'s docstring, the header bullet and the 03 §1 paragraph: `The condition is tested only at steps where the internal, sensory and active states recur; a course where they never recur meets it, whatever moves it.` If `SteadyFeed` stays, its docstring should say that its level never repeats, so every feed and every act pass.

### 4. Medium: the law's world side is the run read by the step, an open loop, and that is not said where it matters

**What is wrong.** `alongLaw`'s `nextSensory _ _ t := s (t + 1)` reads only the step. So the world's side is a lookup of the given course. It admits every sensory course, and the act moves nothing on it: the law is the open loop at the run's own history. In a World, a formation's act moves the levels of what it takes from and gives to, some of which may couple into it. Along one run that dependence is absorbed into the given course and does not show. The rest of the World, the other relata's levels and exchanges, is not in the configuration at all: it enters only through the step. The header says "the world's half holding of every such course", but `alongLaw`'s docstring and the proposed 03 §1 text say nothing of this. The DESIGN (§4) calls the result "a World instance of UE27's third level, a partition fixed by the formation's own records apart from the run". It is half that. The world's side is a reading of the course by the step: UE27's first level, for one course, where the step suffices. Only the agent's side is a partition fixed apart from the run. And with a value or an act carrying the step (findings 1 and 2), it falls entirely into the first level.

**Fix.** Add to `alongLaw`'s docstring: `Its world's side reads only the step: the sensory part's next state is the given course at the next step, the act moving nothing there. It is the open loop at that course, it admits every sensory course, and the law's content is its agent's side.` Add to the header bullet, after "the world's half holding of every such course": `, the world's side the run read by the step, the World's other records entering only through it`. Replace DESIGN §4's last sentence: `Its world's side is a reading of the course by the step (UE27's first level, for one course, where the step suffices), its agent's side a partition fixed by the formation's records apart from the run (the third level); with a value or an act carrying the step it falls entirely into the first.`

### 5. Medium: `rowAct` is not the formation's act: it ignores presence, and no witness exercises an act

**What is wrong.** `rowAct cs L g t` reads `g`'s take and give rows whether or not `g ∈ L.present t`. `Flow.taken` and `Flow.given` sum only over the relata present, so an absent formation's row counts for nothing, yet `rowAct` compares it. The design review (finding 5e) asked to "put presence with the act"; DESIGN §6 marks finding 5 taken, but presence is not in the Lean. In the probe's `AbsentRow`, no relatum is present at any step, every level is constant and the flow balances. `g`'s row nevertheless has one unit from `h` at every first phase. Under `rowAct` the agent is not determined (`not_agentDetermined`: at steps 1 and 2 the level, value and row agree, the next rows do not). Under a constant act it is (`constant_agentDetermined`). So the reading refutes a formation that does nothing and is moved by nothing. In both witnesses `g` is absent and its rows are zero, so the act clause is never exercised. The DESIGN's "the relata present, itself included" holds only where `g` is present.

**Fix.** Define the act with presence, as in finding 2 (`presentRow`), and say in its docstring: `the formation's act: its rows on listed relata where it takes part in the step, nothing where it does not`. In the DESIGN write "the relata present, itself among them where it is present". Optionally add a witness where `g` is present and acts, for example `g` giving to itself at one phase, so that the act half is tested once.

### 6. Medium: where the formation's own level couples into it, the sensory part copies the internal part, and only the step keeps the reading standing

**What is wrong.** `levelRun`'s sensory course is `value t (W.level t)`, the whole level function. Where `obs g g` holds, or `g` bears on itself (`Web.SelfBearing`: it requires its own standing, so admission at `g` reads `g`'s level), the sensory state at `t` is partly a function of the internal state at `t`. A blanket's sensory part should not read the internal part except through the active part. Here the world's half is met only because the step tells every two steps apart (finding 4). DESIGN §3 says self-bearing formations "are included along one run, since the step carries the world's half". That is accurate, but it presents a degenerate world side as an inclusion, and the Lean and the header say nothing.

**Fix.** Add to `levelRun`'s docstring: `Where g's own level couples into it, by level or through support where it bears on itself (Web.SelfBearing), the sensory part reads the internal part at the same step; the world's half then holds only because the step tells the steps apart.` In DESIGN §3, replace "Self-bearing formations … are included along one run, since the step carries the world's half; across runs they are not covered" with: `For self-bearing formations, whose own level couples into them, the sensory part reads the internal part; along one run the step still meets the world's half, so the reading stands there only by the step, and across runs it does not.`

### 7. Medium: the texts need more change than DESIGN §5 lists, and the UE27 record's sentence on single runs must be reconciled

**What is wrong.**
- 03 §1's blanket Reading (D45) ends: "that what it keeps of them, its own record, can be the internal part is not shown". UE27's home section says: "the closed loop is a model beside it, and the agent is a model, not the formation's own record". DESIGN §5 rewrites the first clause of the Reading only, and leaves both sentences stale.
- The UE27 record's `correspondence` says that under the time-unrolled blanket "a single run constrains nothing under it". UE28 refutes a single run (`PhasedDrain`). The two are compatible, since UE28's law is the same at every step on the agent's side and the time-unrolled blanket does not require that. A reader needs that said.
- The record gains five laws, but DESIGN §5 keeps its statement unchanged, so the statement no longer covers its laws.
- The proposed wordings say "read off" for a pairwise `↔`, and "along a run" where the Lean has one run.

**Fix (proposed wording, after findings 1–3).**
- 03 §1 Reading, replacing from "what it keeps of them its internal states" through "is not shown": `what it folds of them its internal states, and the blanket condition is that … at most that (Blanket.Deaf.lumps). That a formation's support and observed levels are a blanketed agent's sensory states is a theorem wherever its admission is decided and its observed levels listed, by construction, with a model as the internal part ([below](#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket)). Its own level is such a part along one run, the step on the world's side and its couplings read with no step in their value, exactly where, at any two steps alike on its level, on what couples into it and on its act, its next levels and next acts agree; the test bites only where these recur, and across runs, and for its other records, nothing is shown.`
- UE27's home section: replace "and the agent is a model, not the formation's own record" with `and the agent here is a model; the formation's own level as the internal part, along one run, is the next paragraph`.
- The new paragraph: `Along one run, read with the step as the external part, a course is admitted by a law determined part by part exactly where its agent is determined: at any two steps alike on the internal, sensory and active states, the next internal and active states agree (BlanketCouplings.readAlong_admitted_iff). The world's side is the run read by the step, so it holds of every such course and the act moves nothing on it. Read on a balanced World run with a formation's level internal, its couplings' value (read with no step in it) sensory and its act active, the condition is that at any two steps alike on its level, on its admission, on the levels coupled into it and on its act, what the relata present give to it and take from it cross-sum alike and the next acts agree, pairwise, with no subtraction (BlanketCouplings.levelRun_admitted_iff…). It is tested only where these recur. A drain in three phases from what does not couple in fails it (BlanketCouplings.PhasedDrain.not_admitted), and a drain and feed in two phases from the same source meets it (BlanketCouplings.Alternating…), so the line falls at whether the level's next value is fixed by the level, what couples in and the act, not at whether what moves it couples in.`
- UE27 record `correspondence`: after "a single run constrains nothing under it", add `; a single run can refute a law the same at every step on the agent's side, read by a partition fixed apart from it (ReadsExternal; for a formation's level, PhasedDrain), which the time-unrolled blanket does not require`.
- UE27 record `statement`: add a clause such as `; along one run, with the step on the world's side, a formation's level is the internal part exactly where its next level and act agree wherever its level, what couples into it and its act agree`.
- The principle's page §5 item: `a World formation's records other than its level, rather than a model, as the internal part of a blanketed law; its level across a family of runs, or with the step off the world's side`. In §1's blanket line, add `along one run, the step on the world's side and the couplings read with no step in their value, its own level is the internal part exactly where its next level and act agree wherever its level, that value and its act agree, which a drain in three phases fails`.
- Throughout: write "agree wherever … agree" or "fixed pairwise by", not "read off", and write "one run", not "a run".

### 8. Low: names

- `AgentDetermined` is a property of three courses. In this module, "Determined" names properties of laws (`Determined`, `BlanketDetermined`) and "Consistent" names properties of families (`PartConsistentFamily`, `BlanketConsistentFamily`). So `readAlong_consistent_iff : BlanketConsistentFamily … ↔ AgentDetermined …` reads as a law-to-family bridge, when it is the agent half of `PartConsistentFamily` on one course. Use `AgentConsistent` (or `AgentConsistentAlong`).
- `readAlong` does not say what it does, which is to put the step in the external part. Use `stepExternal` or `withStep`. UE27's `clocked` is the family version, with which course and the step.
- `levelRun` is not a run, and `World.run` exists. Use `levelParts` or `levelReading`.
- `Two` sits at the module's top level for two witnesses. Use `TwoRelata`, or put it under a shared witness namespace.
- `alongLaw` follows `readAlong`; rename it with it (`stepLaw`).

None is landed, so this is claim time (AGENTS.md: "Name at claim time").

### 9. Low: `alongLaw_course` does not say what it leaves out

**What is wrong.** Its docstring does not say that the converse, functions from `AgentDetermined` alone, is not stated, or why. Building the functions needs a next state at every reading the course never shows, and a search over the steps for those it does show. In general that needs choice. The easy direction, from given functions to `AgentDetermined`, follows from `alongLaw_course`, `blanketed_courses_consistent` and `readAlong_consistent_iff`, but nothing states it.

**Fix.** Add, as `blanketed_courses_consistent` does: `The converse, functions from AgentDetermined alone, needs a next state at every reading the course never shows and a search over its steps for those it does, and is not stated.`

### 10. Low: `PhasedDrain` wording, and a missing `uncoupled`

**What is wrong.** "`g` couples in nothing" (the docstring of `value`) reads as `g` coupling into something. The intended sense is that nothing couples into `g`. "Gives the unit back" (DESIGN item 8) suggests that `h` holds the unit, but balance credits nothing to the taker: `h`'s level is its phase, kept by self-exchange, and the unit taken from `g` is not in it. The header contrasts the witnesses as "from what does not couple in", but only `SteadyFeed` has `uncoupled`.

**Fix.** Write `nothing couples into g: its sensory part is the one value`, and `at its second phase it gives g one unit`. Add `PhasedDrain.uncoupled` (`¬ web.CoupledInto (fun _ _ => False) Two.g Two.h`, the same three-line proof as `SteadyFeed.uncoupled`).

### 11. Low: header housekeeping

**What is wrong.** The rewritten line "model; a formation's own level as the internal part is the last item below. `ThresholdDrain`'s join…" runs to about 136 characters, against the module's 100. The modelling-choices paragraph does not name the new section's choices. The opening's "what is new is the classification of when a blanket's existence constrains" does not say how the new section bears on it.

**Fix.** Rewrap the line. Add to the modelling choices: `for a formation's level, one run with the step as the external part, the value and act courses supplied (the World's reading: a value with no step in it, the act its rows where present), amounts in Nat under balance`. After "the classification" add `, to which a formation's level along one run adds a World case`.

### 12. Low: the World theorem is stated on `Web` and `Flow`, not on a `World`

**What is wrong.** The docstrings say "a World's run", but the theorems take `L : Flow R R`, `W : Web R R` and `L.BalancedHistory W.level`. That is a World by `World.of R L W` (`World.of_web`, `World.of_flow`, both `rfl`) with balance as a local hypothesis. A reader of 08 §2's "(World)" label should be told that.

**Fix.** Add to `levelRun`'s docstring `(a World by World.of R L W, balance a hypothesis)`. The home section's label can read `(World, balanced at every step; any type for the reading along one course)`.

### 13. Low: 08 §8 may want a correspondence clause

**What is wrong.** DESIGN §4 leaves 08 §8 unchanged. The along-one-run condition, though, is the standard consistency condition for one trajectory to be generated by a time-invariant deterministic state machine whose state, input and output are given: equal state and input at two times give equal next state and output. It is known in system identification and automata realization. Saying so would keep the standing honest, since the condition is near-definitional and not new as mathematics.

**Fix.** Add one clause to 08 §8's "Which blanket a thing has" sentence: `a formation's level along one run is the internal part exactly under the familiar consistency condition of one trajectory with a time-invariant deterministic state machine, here stated on the World's exchange, pairwise`.
