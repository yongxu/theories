## 0. The question in the account's terms

UE4 formalized the long run of widening and keeping widening (`Web.KeepsWidening`: from `T` on, along a continuation, nothing admitted is lost and something keeps being opened), and named the next step: a first law tying keeping widening, or what carries it, to the World's premises ([UE4 §3.1, §3.3](../ue4-second-order/DESIGN.md#31-candidate-a-the-long-run-eventual-widening-keeping-widening); [record, Next](../../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/eventual-widening.md#next--下一步)). The UE plan asks such a law to be a result no neighbouring field states, since the [UE0 audit](../ue0-third-parts/AUDIT.md) found the theory farther than each framework alone and not farther than the sciences together. "Farther" means forbidding more.

Every law here is a property of one course or one flow and what it needs. None compares two courses, so none is the second-order comparison 07 leaves open. Each is a first law in UE4's sense: it ties keeping widening (1, 2), or what carries a widening (3, 4), to balance, the first law, upkeep or brevity. No ethical predicate, ranking or axiom enters, and no law ranks keeping widening, being opened by the opened, or generation above anything. Ranking by keeping widening or by being opened by the opened runs against widening wholesale (`OwnCondition.not_monotone`, `Saturated.reverses`). Generation is independent of widening both ways (law 4's witnesses: a closed cycle generates with no widening, and a range widening by name is kept); no ranking by generation is proposed, and none is shown to reverse widening.

## 2. Law 1: the gain bridge

**Statement** (World; `W : Web F C`, `L : Flow F C`, `cs : List C`).
- *Keeping widening by name on the actual levels*: `W.KeepsWidening Web.itself W.level T`. On the actual levels admission is support (`Web.supported_iff_admits`, `Iff.rfl`).
- *Requirements fixed within a finite family*: `req : ∀ t f, T ≤ t → W.Supported t f → W.RequiresWithin cs f`. Only formations admitted from `T` on are constrained.
- *Passes every bound*: `∀ K M, ∃ t, M ≤ t ∧ K < W.stock cs t` (`Web.KeepsWidening.passes_every_bound`); so the stock rises at arbitrarily late steps (`Web.KeepsWidening.rises_late`).
- *Given from beyond*: with `gainless : L.Gainless` and `Distinct cs`, `∀ M, ∃ t, M ≤ t ∧ ((L.withImbalance W).NetGiver cs t none ∨ ¬ L.ClosedAt cs t)` (`Web.KeepsWidening.from_rest_or_open`). The flow need not balance: the rest is counted.
- *Corollaries.* With `L.Balances W` as well, the rest gives nothing net, so the family is open at arbitrarily late steps (`Web.KeepsWidening.open_late`). In a finite world (a list without repetition holding every condition), under balance and the first law, no course keeps widening by name with requirements fixed (`Flow.whole_not_keepsWidening`): the whole's stock never rises (`Flow.whole_rise_from_rest`), and the bridge says it must pass every bound.

**Route.** Through finite lists of steps, with no sequence chosen:
1. *Below*: a list of steps whose configurations of `cs` are pairwise distinct, longer than the number of configurations of `cs` totalling at most `K`, holds a step at which the stock exceeds `K` (`Web.exceeds_of_distinct_configs`, from `search` over the list, `config_within` and `length_le_of_sub`). This is the pigeonhole inside `Web.exceeds_of_firsts`, stated for a list of steps rather than an increasing sequence; `exceeds_of_firsts` follows from it with its statement (`Web.exceeds_of_firsts_of_distinct_configs`).
2. *The list*: for every `n`, keeping widening gives `n` steps from `T`, all at most some `S`, with pairwise distinct configurations (`Web.KeepsWidening.distinct_configs`, by induction on `n`). At the step: keeping opening at `S` gives `U ≥ S` and `g` supported at `U`, not at `S`. Nothing lost makes `g` unsupported at every earlier listed step `s` (else supported at `S`), and `Web.supported_of_config` with `req` then makes the configuration at `U` differ from each listed one.
3. Apply 1 to the list of length `N + 1`, from `T + M` (`KeepsWidening.later`); `rises_between` gives the late rise; `Flow.rise_from_rest_or_open` gives the first law's half.

**Constructive need.** None. Keeping opening supplies each new step and formation positively; the only decision is the order of numbers on finitely many listed steps. UE4 expected this ("a proof through finite lists of formations would need neither a choice of witnesses nor decided support").

**What each premise does** (each witness meets the other premises and fails the conclusion). Nothing lost: the cycle keeps opening by name with requirements fixed within `[false, true]`, a list without repetition, under the first law and balance, while the family is closed and its stock is 1 at every step (`ClosedCycle.bounded_while_opening`). A finite family: the rising web, each formation `n` requiring its own condition `n`, keeps widening by name with fixed requirements, and no finite family's stock passes its length (`OpeningChain.fixed_but_unbounded`). Fixed needs: needs that loosen (formation `n` needs two before step `n`, one from it on) admit ever more formations at a stock of one (`Loosening.bounded_while_widening`). The first law: a balanced one-condition world fed from nothing keeps widening by name with fixed requirements while its family is closed and never given from beyond (`Source.from_nothing`). The witness of the premises together, `Ladder` (`Ladder.frontier_witness`): condition `0` stands at `t + 1` at step `t`; formation `some n` requires `n + 1` of it and is supported from step `n` on; a carrier `none`, requiring nothing, is active throughout; each condition `c + 1` holds one unit up to step `c`. Under the frontier flow the carrier alone takes part: it takes the unit standing at condition `t + 1` and gives it to condition `0`. The flow balances and meets the first law, the course keeps widening by name with requirements fixed within `[0]`, and `[0]` is open at every step: the frontier of fresh conditions is what the bridge says must exist.

**Neighbours and grade.**
- *The theory's own laws.* The bridge is `Flow.newly_supported_from_rest_or_open` read for a course keeping widening, with sequences of first supports replaced by lists. A joining lemma.
- *Thermodynamics and dynamical systems.* That a bounded closed system recurs (finite Poincaré recurrence) and that sustained organization needs throughput (Schrödinger; Prigogine's dissipative structures) are the bridge's content; UE0 row 11 grades the theory's "fed from beyond" a restatement of these.
- *Open-ended evolution.* Adams, Zenil, Davies and Walker (2017) define unbounded evolution against the Poincaré recurrence time of an isolated system, and Bedau and others (2000) list "determine what is inevitable in the open-ended evolution of life" as open problem 6. The finite-world corollary says one such thing in the theory's terms: no unbounded kept widening, under fixed finite requirements, in a finite balanced world under the first law. Known.
- *The adjacent possible.* The TAP equation, `M_{t+1} = M_t (1 − μ) + Σ_{i ≥ 2} α_i C(M_t, i)` (Cortês, Kauffman, Liddle and Smolin, arXiv 2204.14115, checked), has no resource, maintenance or conservation term. With `μ = 0` (nothing lost) it lets kept types grow without bound in any world. Where each type is a formation with fixed thresholds on finitely many conserved stocks, the bridge forbids that growth unless the stocks are fed from beyond: a third part against TAP, carried by premises TAP lacks (conservation, thresholds), and known in thermodynamics.
- *Assembly theory* (Sharma and others, *Nature* 2023): no balance of building blocks is stated; the same third part, the same grade.
- *Autocatalytic sets.* A RAF set (Hordijk and Steel) is food-generated by definition: every reactant is produced from a food set supplied from outside. The bridge's "fed from beyond" is the same requirement for growth, not only for persistence; RAF theory states existence, not growth laws (the searches found none on growth).

**Grade: restatement.** It forbids nothing a physicist or an open-endedness theorist would not foresee.

## 3. Law 2: renewers multiply

**Statement.**
- *Upkeep*: `L.TakesWhileSupported W cs T := ∀ t f, T ≤ t → W.Supported t f → f ∈ L.present t ∧ 0 < total cs (L.take t f)` : every formation supported from `T` on takes part and takes at least a unit a step from `cs`.
- *Law*: `W.KeepsWidening Web.itself W.level T → L.TakesWhileSupported W cs T → L.More cs` (`Web.KeepsWidening.more`). With `∀ t, T₀ ≤ t → L.BalancesOn W cs t`, the replenishment of `cs` is bounded from no step on (`Web.KeepsWidening.replenishment_unbounded`); with `L.Capacity cs g` as well, so are its renewers: `∀ M, ¬ ∀ t, T₀ ≤ t → L.renewers cs t ≤ M` (`Web.KeepsWidening.renewers_unbounded`).

**Route.** `KeepsWidening.gathers` gives, for every `K`, `K` distinct formations admitted together at some `S ≥ T`; nothing lost keeps them supported at every `t ≥ S`; upkeep makes them takers at `t`; the count lemma gives `K ≤ L.takers cs t`. That is `Flow.More cs`, and `Flow.More.replenishment_unbounded_from` and `Flow.More.renewers_unbounded_from` conclude.

**The count lemma.** `length_le_count : Distinct xs → (∀ a, a ∈ xs → a ∈ ps ∧ 0 < q a) → xs.length ≤ count ps q`. UE4 expected it to need decided equality of formations or a double negation. It needs neither: by induction on `xs`, split `ps` at the head of `xs` (`List.append_of_mem`), and the rest of `xs` lies in the two pieces because its members differ from the head (`mem_remove`, whose premise `¬ b = a` the distinctness of `xs` supplies positively). It is the lower bound beside `count_le_of_sub`.

**Constructive need.** None.

**What each premise does** (each witness meets the other premises and has at most one renewer a step). Upkeep: under the frontier flow only the carrier takes part (`Ladder.upkeep_needed`). Capacity: the carrier gives all that is taken from condition `0`, and one more (`Ladder.capacity_needed`). Balance: nothing gives, so nothing renews (`Ladder.balance_needed`). Nothing lost: the cycle keeps opening by name under upkeep with capacity one (`ClosedCycle.renewers_bounded_while_opening`). The witness of the premises together meets the first law as well (`Ladder.upkept_witness`): under upkeep every supported formation takes part, takes a unit from condition `0` and gives it back, and the carrier also brings the unit standing at condition `t + 1`; each gives at most two, and the renewers of `[0]` are bounded from no step on. The first plan, a source formation giving from nothing, broke the first law; law 1 says a witness meeting it must feed the family from beyond, as the ladder's frontier does.

**Neighbours and grade.**
- *The theory's own law.* `Flow.More.renewers_unbounded` (more takers without end needs renewers without bound) read for keeping widening; the new step is that a range keeping widening by name under upkeep *is* more without end.
- *Semi-endogenous growth.* Jones (1995, *JPE*, "R&D-based models of economic growth") replaces Romer's scale effects with an idea production function `Ȧ = δ L_A^λ A^φ`, `φ < 1`: sustained exponential growth needs a growing number of researchers. Bloom, Jones, Van Reenen and Webb (2020, *AER*) find research productivity falling wherever they look (the researchers needed for Moore's law are more than 18 times those of the early 1970s). Law 2 is not this result. In Jones's model with a constant number of researchers `A` still grows without bound (`A^{1−φ}` grows linearly), more and more slowly; ideas, once found, are kept for free. Law 2 concerns growth at all, not its rate, and its mechanism is upkeep: under upkeep even unbounded growth at a falling rate needs renewers without bound. Against Jones's model as stated this is a third part carried by a premise the model lacks.
- *Where that premise is known.* With knowledge depreciating at rate `d`, `Ȧ = δ L_A^λ A^φ − d A` and a constant `L_A` give a finite steady state `A* = (δ L_A^λ / d)^{1/(1−φ)}`, so unbounded `A` needs unbounded `L_A` (an argument from the model; knowledge obsolescence is measured by Caballero and Jaffe 1993). The burden of knowledge (B. F. Jones 2009, *Review of Economic Studies* 76) is an upkeep-like mechanism for the same pattern: accumulated knowledge raises what each new innovator must carry. In microbiology Pirt's maintenance coefficient (1965) makes the substrate needed scale with the biomass maintained; in population ecology this is the Malthusian bound UE0 names.
- *The adjacent possible, TAP and assembly theory* speak of types or objects, not of renewers, so law 2's forbidden statement is not on their topic (UE0's first test).

**Grade: restatement**, with a premise-carried third part against Jones 1995 that the depreciation and maintenance literatures already state. A reading for the [AI]: law 2 offers one mechanism, upkeep of an accumulating range, by which Bloom and others' falling research productivity could arise with ideas no harder to find; the burden-of-knowledge literature already proposes a close cousin.

## 4. Law 3: the uniform form of support passing on

**Statement** (local form, every hypothesis at one step):

```lean
theorem Flow.gain_given_by_recent_gainer_at {S K : Nat} {f : F}
    (balances : ∀ c, W.requires (S + K) f c → L.BalancesOn W [c] (S + K))
    (givers : ∀ c, W.requires (S + K) f c → L.GiversSupported W (S + K) c)
    (unloosened : W.Unloosened (S + K) f)
    (brief : ∀ g, ¬ ∀ i, i ≤ K → W.Supported (S + i) g)
    (gains : W.GainsSupport (S + K) f) :
    ¬ ¬ ∃ c g u, W.requires (S + K) f c ∧ W.Rises (S + K) c ∧ g ∈ L.present (S + K) ∧
      0 < L.give (S + K) g c ∧ W.Supported (S + K) g ∧ S ≤ u ∧ u < S + K ∧ W.GainsSupport u g
```

The global form takes balance, only active giving, activity needing support, requirements never loosening and brevity everywhere (`Flow.gain_given_by_recent_gainer`). Subtraction never appears: the gain is at `S + K` and the window is `[S, S + K)`.

**Route.** `GainsSupport.rises` gives, up to double negation, a required condition that rises; `rise_given_by_supported_at` gives a supported giver positively; brevity at `S` with `comes_within_not_not` gives, up to double negation, the step in the window at which the giver gained support. `Flow.support_passes_on` is a corollary: take a gain at `t ≥ N + (T + 1) + K`, write `t = (N + (T + 1) + m) + K`, and use the window ending at `t`, with no subtraction (`Flow.support_passes_on_of_recent`, the derivation rule 9 asks for).

**Constructive need.** Two double negations, each locating something: the required condition that rose (a search over every condition), and the step at which the giver gained support (deciding support at each step of the window). With support decided and the gainer's requirements at the step listed and decided, the law is positive (`Flow.gain_given_by_recent_gainer_decided`, by `search`, `upto_or_fails` and `came_between`).

**What it fixes.** UE4's `Flow.OpenedByOpened L W T` asks every gain after `T` to be given by a formation that gained support after `T`. The uniform law gives something else:
- *a recency bound, not a fixed origin.* A gain right after `T` may be given by a formation that gained support before `T` and is still supported. What brevity forces is that a gain at `S + K` is given by one that gained in `[S, S + K)`. Definition: `Flow.OpenedByOpenedWithin L W K`: gains recur, and for every `S`, every gain at `S + K` is, up to double negation, given on a required rising condition by a supported formation that gained support in `[S, S + K)`.
- *the double negation inside*, or support decided.

Relations proved: brevity with the World premises and recurring gains gives `OpenedByOpenedWithin K` (`Flow.openedByOpenedWithin_of_brief`); it is monotone in `K` (`Flow.OpenedByOpenedWithin.mono`); it gives the lagged form of the old definition, gains from `T + K` on given by gainers from `T` on (`Flow.OpenedByOpenedWithin.lagged`); and the old definition at every `T` gives it with `K = 1` (`Flow.openedByOpenedWithin_of_opened`). Witnesses: the closed cycle meets brevity with `K = 1` (`ClosedCycle.opened_within`); the relay meets `OpenedByOpenedWithin 1` without brevity, so brevity is sufficient, not necessary (`OpeningChain.carriers_within`); the single giver meets every other premise and is opened by the opened within no `K`, the premise dropped being brevity (`OpeningChain.brevity_needed`). The old definition excludes a carrier the new one admits: in the lagged relay formation `t` gives at step `t + 1`, so every gain is given by the formation that gained support two steps before, and it is opened by the opened within two and at no origin in the old form (`OpeningChain.within_not_fixed_origin`).

**A consequence for UE4's candidate C** [AI reading]. Where every support is brief, every balanced flow with recurring gains is opened by the opened. So the property distinguishes carriers only where some support lasts, as the single giver's does: what UE4's `OpeningChain.carried` separates is a lasting carrier from brief ones.

**Neighbours and grade.** In a population with a maximal lifespan `ω`, every parent alive at `t` was born after `t − ω`; that is the law with "giving" for "parenting", plus the theory's own law that support comes from the supported. The core's `Web.begins_within` is the same fact for patterns. **Grade: near-definitional.** Its value is the fixed form.

## 5. Law 4: the map law

02's open item asks for "a law relating the map's 生成 to widening what can form"; UE4 proposed reaching it through gains of support ([UE4 §3.3](../ue4-second-order/DESIGN.md#33-candidate-c-what-carries-a-widening-openings-that-were-opened)). The proposal there, the opening relation alone as 缘, makes every step a carrying renewal by construction (no formation opens itself), so its 全生成 is "an opening tree that never ends", a definition rather than a law. The version below keeps "carrying on" as a step of the continuation, so that renewal and keeping can differ.

**The reading.** Carrier: occurrences `(t, f)`. For levels `l`, the web read through what `l` admits (`Web.admitting W l`: active where admitted) gives the coarse class "admitted or not" (`(W.admitting l).activityClass`) and the fine class "admitted alike and the same formation" (`(W.admitting l).carrierClass`). 缘 is one step of a continuation `R` (`Web.continuationStep R`).

**Statements.**
1. *Nothing lost is the admitted pattern never ending*: `W.IncludesAcrossFrom R l T ↔ ∀ S f, T ≤ S → W.Admits (l S) S f → «不穷» (continuationStep R) (W.admitting l).activityClass (S, f)` (`Web.includesAcrossFrom_iff_endless`). Forward: an admitted occurrence reached is admitted and late, and inclusion across one step gives it an admitted successor. Back: a line that never ends reaches an admitted occurrence at every later step (`reaches_onward`), and a line is a link chain.
2. *By name, kept*: a course keeping widening by name is, at every admitted occurrence from `T`, 全守 and not 全生成 (`Web.KeepsWidening.kept_by_name`, from 1, `Web.itself_keeps` and `Web.itself_not_generation`). Widening by name and generation are disjoint.
3. *Openings as carrying renewals*: `L.Opens W t g f` (`g` takes part and is supported, gives on a condition `f` requires that rises, and `f` gains support across the step). Along "carry on, or open" (`Web.passingContinuation (L.Opens W)`) on the actual levels, from a supported occurrence, a carrying renewal into `(t + 1, f)` is exactly `L.Opens W t g f` (`Flow.opening_carries_iff`, from `Web.carries_iff`).
4. *Every gain is one*: under balance and active giving at the step, activity needing support and unloosened requirements, every gain of support is, up to double negation, the end of a carrying renewal from a supported occurrence (`Flow.gain_carried`).
5. *Brief supports make lasting lines generation*: where no formation is supported at `K + 1` consecutive steps, every supported occurrence whose line along "carry on, or open" never ends is 全生成 (`Flow.generates_of_brief`). With 1: a range losing nothing along "carry on, or open" from `T`, its supports brief, is generation at every supported occurrence from `T` (`Flow.generates_of_includes_brief`).

**Route.** 1 unfolds link chains as lines (`Web.linked_iff_line` and its proof's pattern); 3 is `carries_iff` with the fine class; 5 follows successors (each exists by 不穷) for at most `K` steps: each step carries the formation on or opens another; `K` carrying-on steps would keep one formation supported at `K + 1` consecutive steps.
