# UE8: a dossier for scientists (plan)

AI-written, 26 October 2026, for board item UE8, under [UE](../ue-unified-explanation/PLAN.md) (a unified explanation of reality, one of the main objectives since 25 October).

## Why

The UE plan's §6 names the check no internal selector can run: readers from the contained fields finding a containment trivial or wrong. UE0 found the theory reaching farther than each framework it contains taken alone and not farther than the sciences taken together, and UE6's three rounds found no result that no neighbouring field states. Both are AI judgments against the literature an AI knew. The [review packet](../../../../../../theories/becoming/review/README.md) asks people in five fields to judge what a machine cannot: classical texts, Buddhist thought, logic, philosophy and alignment. No dossier asks scientists, though most of the theory's claims of containment, its third parts and its frozen tests concern their fields.

Purpose served (board, "Purpose first"): shared understanding with human readers, and the unified explanation, whose containments only readers from those fields can confirm or reject.

## What

A dossier in the packet's format, as [ALIGNMENT.md](../../../../../../theories/becoming/review/ALIGNMENT.md) and [PHILOSOPHY.md](../../../../../../theories/becoming/review/PHILOSOPHY.md) are written:

- **Opening sections:** what this is, in plain terms, how long it takes with the items to read if one has one hour, how it was made, what the theory says of itself, the conventions, the terms.
- **Each item:** a theorem, containment, thesis or test that bears on a science. It gives:
  - its **Meaning** in words;
  - **Lean** for the central items, copied by script;
  - **Standing and use**;
  - **Known weaknesses**, quoting a recorded weakness or marked [this dossier, AI];
  - neutral **Questions**.
- **A response form** at the end.

Readers: physicists (thermodynamics and stochastic thermodynamics); evolutionary biologists and population geneticists; ecologists and resource economists; computational neuroscientists working on active inference and the free-energy principle; control theorists and researchers of complex systems and open-ended evolution.

The parts, in outline (the drafter refines them):

1. What the theory claims of the sciences: 08 §8's verdict and what survives; the third parts; UE6's verdict; the unifications of 08 §9.
2. Physics: the least loss read in available work (entropy production, the fluctuation theorem in expectation); Landauer's count in the core; rest from a least loss or a finite reading; recurrence; balance from the first law with locality.
3. Evolution: what bounds offspring numbers (Price and Fisher joined to the flow); the pace of selection within what flows in; coming and ceasing keep pace; loops; fed from beyond.
4. Ecology and renewable resources:
   - joint excess under the gate;
   - the margin of a regime;
   - the optimum laws and T3's law for optima;
   - T1 and T2 with their frozen tests on yeast and fish stocks;
   - T6 with the seabird tests.
5. Mind and inference: the free-energy principle's containment (UE1, a restatement with its third part open, and the third-part search); conditioning by restriction; the exit from a margin; orders with a set point; selection through couplings.
6. Order and open-endedness: eventual widening; what keeps widening (UE7, once landed); the root's laws as the known mathematics of simulation and lumping.

Ethics (T4, T5, T8) is the philosophy dossier's, and trained systems (T3's clause on them, T7, LA1 to LA3) the alignment dossier's; items here point to them rather than repeat them.

## How it is checked

As the other dossiers are:

- every `Quote [path]: "…"` and every backticked Lean name is checked by the packet's [`check_dossiers.py`](../review-packet/check_dossiers.py);
- Lean statements are copied by [`lean_statements.py`](../rp2-philosophy/lean_statements.py) and compared with the sources;
- every work cited outside a quotation is checked against a publication record, logged in this folder;
- an independent AI reader reviews the dossier in two passes before it is offered.

## Where it goes

It is drafted here, as `SCIENCES.md`, not in the packet. A sixth dossier changes the scope of the packet Ren chose, as the fifth did ([AJ4's record](../../../../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-25-alignment-dossier.md)), so whether it joins the packet is his choice. It is listed under Decisions pending Ren once it has passed review, and is not asked of him between items. If it joins, it moves to `theories/becoming/review/SCIENCES.md`, its links are rewritten for that place, and a row is added to the packet's cover (EN, ZH), which RP's owner holds. Sending the packet stays Ren's step.
