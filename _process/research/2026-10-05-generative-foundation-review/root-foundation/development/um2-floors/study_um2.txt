**The question.** UM's record left open which profiles are perfect where a floor binds ([§335](#335-one-model-of-drawers-in-turn-and-one-law-of-exact-thresholds-um)). Its drain needed no floor anywhere, but its proof used only that some drawers take whatever they are given.

**What was built** (new laws of `Becoming.Optimum`, pure core, every axiom report empty).

- *Nothing passes a drawer that leaves nothing* (`Optimum.InTurn.pass_through`, `Optimum.InTurn.after_draw_nothing`). Of whatever drawer `d` is given, drawers `d, …, d + c - 1` leave nothing where one of them leaves nothing; where drawer `u` leaves nothing at every later step, every drawer after `u` draws nothing later.
- *Drawing to its floor, by position* (`Optimum.InTurn.floor_after`, `Optimum.InTurn.floor_through`). In a revision, a drawer after one that leaves nothing later, and a drawer before one that leaves nothing now, leaves its floor: what it keeps does not come back to it, so by `kept_returns` it keeps nothing above the floor.
- *Every perfect profile is the floor profile* (`Optimum.InTurn.revisions_reach_floors`, `Optimum.InTurn.perfect_at_floors`, `Optimum.InTurn.floors_perfect`, `Optimum.InTurn.revised_at_floors`, `Optimum.InTurn.levels_through`, `Web.drained_broken`). With two drawers `u < v` free of any floor: drawers after `u` come to their floors once `u` leaves nothing later, `u` once `v` leaves nothing at the step, and drawers before `u` once `u` does. That is three rounds a step back, two where `u` is the first drawer, so revision from any profile reaches the floor profile. A perfect profile, its own sequence of revisions, is the floor profile, which is perfect. UM's `pass_of_first`, `others_draw_nothing`, `revisions_end`, `perfect_ends`, `levels_of_first`, `perfect_of_zero` and `revised_draw_all` are removed, the case with no floor anywhere (rule 9).

**Against the prior form** ([significance review, floors check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#floors-check)). In extraction games with capacity constraints, conservation can be an equilibrium only where some player's restraint benefits that player. The spine's form makes that condition exact for movers in turn: two players without a limit already make every subgame-perfect profile the one where all take as much as they can.

*Reading* [AI reading, D45]. Restraint needs all but one of those who share a base to be limited in what they can take. Two who can take everything are enough to make taking everything everyone's best response, whatever the others' limits.

The round count is the induction's, three a step, two where the first drawer is free; not claimed tight. Where at most one drawer is free, perfect profiles away from the last step are characterized only by `kept_returns`.
