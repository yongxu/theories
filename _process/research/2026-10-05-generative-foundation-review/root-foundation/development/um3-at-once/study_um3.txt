**The question.** The record's last open item was drawing at once with a rule of sharing. In turn, what a drawer leaves goes to the next drawer; at once, it goes into what stands next, of which the drawer has a portion.

- *Drawing at once* (`Optimum.AtOnce.sumTo`, `pool`, `next`, `drawn`, `later`, `levels`, `Revises`, `Perfect`, `ReturnsLess`, and the sums' laws `sumTo_congr`, `sumTo_zeros`, `sumTo_zero`, `sumTo_set`). Each drawer is given its portion of the level; what all leave, added, stands next by `G`. A drawer's later draws depend on what it leaves only through its portion of what stands next, the others' leavings added.
- *Every perfect profile draws everything* (`Optimum.AtOnce.drawn_step`, `later_less`, `revisions_drain`, `drains`, `levels_drained`, `Web.at_once_broken`). Where the others draw everything later, what a drawer keeps returns to it, through its portion, less than one for one, so by the strict law it keeps nothing. Over a sequence of revisions this takes one round a step back, the others at the same step entering only as an added amount; a perfect profile is its own sequence. Drawing everything is perfect under the same premise, so the law is not vacuous.

**Against the prior form** ([significance review, at-once check](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#at-once-check)). The spine's form states the exact condition, a portion returning less than one for one, and derives the drain from the same law as the drawers in turn.

*Reading* [AI reading, D45]. In turn or at once, a drawer keeps only what returns to it. In turn, what it leaves goes to the next; at once, it goes back to it only through its portion. Where that portion returns less than it left, keeping is never its best response, however far-sighted.

**Self-review.** Every premise is used: the return condition for the strict law, no floor so that drawing everything is the floor. What lies in no portion does not stand, a modelling choice; where portions cover the level it is no restriction. Drawing at once where a portion returns at least one for one, with floors, or under sharing rules that depend on the others' claims, is not covered.
