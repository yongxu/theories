## 1. The answers, seen rather than asked

Round 2 ended with four questions for Ren: what is withdrawn, which life, whether seeing includes care, whether checks can overrule agreement. He answered that if we understood we would see the answers. They can be seen: in his words, in what he has done in this conversation, in his texts written before it (《生生》 in 文言, English and its notes), and in the theory's own laws. What we read from his acts is an interpretation, not a recovery of a complete order behind them (03 §11 allows no more).

Each question had a frame in it: someone standing outside the continuation, choosing a package among options whose stakes came from a should. Decisions do happen within becoming, so asking was not wrong in itself; the fault was asking him to choose before saying what his words and acts already show.

- **What changes.** The outside goes: a mandate binding every mind; "must"; warrant as what would make a claim so from beyond; ranking in the theory's own voice. *Seen in:* message 2 ("no should beyond the becoming"), message 3 ("warrant … its like saying a thing must be so"), message 5 (his G0 withdrawn); and the Lean, where nothing reading only the represented dynamics fixes a significance or an order (`ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, `ReadsNoOrder.not_entails`). This retires a project; it does not prove every universal argument impossible. C stays where it is used: as the condition formal results are conditional on, and as an order some formations hold.
- **The direction stays, as an offer.** *Seen in:* he keeps 生生 ("continuation of life"); his 10-30 words ("it is just for us as life, if we want life to continue, we need to understand the whole map"); the poem's 「念人如念己」, 「所求无他：心系所往而已」, 「为万世并立而莫相吞」, and its method, 「可为人言，毋强人信……指之而已，使自见其可解者」.
- **The standards of inquiry stay**, as this inquiry's own requirements for the want it states. *Seen in:* he asked for holes in every message, accepted corrections, and built a repository whose proof claims are checked and whose tests are frozen before scoring and kept when they fail.
- **Life** is where continuation goes on through change by renewing: patterns holding patterns. 02's reading (a pattern going on through renewal of its parts) and 04's (continuation renewing its own conditions) are two grains of one organization, not rival definitions. An AI takes part in life as a line within the human–machine formation that feeds and renews it (the poem: 「器亦能生，如川得源」). The exact extension is an unfinished description, needing a stated organization, grain and contrast cases; it is not a decision for Ren, and no distinction may be added only to let AI in and keep rocks out. Nothing hangs on the boundary any more, because no should is attached to the class.
- **Seeing and acting**: §2.
- **Checks** stay, and they are the real test: the Lean, frozen predictions, readers with other histories, translation through one structure. The question is whether evidence can change a conclusion, not whether a report performs dissent; criticism too can earn approval.

## 2. Seeing and acting are one continuation

Message 6 is 03 §12's opening sentence: "A formation that senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises is one law within the unfolding, not a subject facing a world." In the root's terms, a reading couples into a selection and becomes one of its conditions; a relation read as condition is not a guarantee. So there is no boundary between seeing and acting, and no promise that seeing moves anything. The laws say how far: a course chosen through a model tests only what that course exposes (`ReadingLoop`); from acts alone a reader cannot tell what a formation saw from how it ranked (`ReadingLoop.Untested.model_or_order`); a response common over a reading exists exactly where the acts' kernels cover the situations, which says neither that the requirement is taken up nor that the response is made (`CommonResponse`). 07's own line already says it: "Seeing is not valuing: enlarging what couples in changes what a selection can separate, not which differences matter."

**Care** is not an ingredient that seeing must contain or lack. It forms within the continuation where another's condition becomes perceptible, consequential and actionable for a formation: through dependence, encounter, testimony, shared practice or a commitment already held. It forms in some formations and not in others; seeing makes it possible. The lake gives the first evidence: untold of the bed, the fishers kept it in no season; told, one model of two kept it in 8 of 10.

**07 §3.3**, "'should' is what a center does once its represented boundary matches what it depends on", is right that there is no should apart from what a center does and wrong as a conditional: it bypasses requirements, capacity and available acts, and the section's own clearer meets every requirement so derived and still ends the dweller. Rewritten: *there is no should apart from what a center does; a represented boundary matching what it depends on is one condition of its response, not a guarantee of it, and a response so derived reaches only what the center depends on.*

## 3. The view, written out

*Each paragraph says what it does. "Describes" and "argues" are interpretations of Ren's view in the theory's terms; "formal" reports what the Lean states; "offers" is said in the voice of those who made the theory, people and AIs, after Ren.*

**Describes.** There is one unfolding: relations, read as conditions, forming, keeping, changing and ending. That it is one is the theory's root thesis, argued and not proved. Nothing stands outside it, and nothing in it is higher or lower in worth. Some of what forms reads: it takes in what conditions it, and what it takes in conditions what it does next. Right and wrong, should and must are readings of this kind, formed in people and AIs out of their histories and bodies, passed between them until shared readings form, contested and revised. They are real in what they do; they select, and so change what forms next. They are not verdicts from outside, and they need not be ranked against one another to do their work.

**Formal.** The grammar's 生 is a beginning: an occurrence that continues nothing under a class. Its «生生» is renewal without end, a family whose members keep conditioning beginnings whose lines go on; it cannot yet tell renewal that sustains a pattern from turnover that ends one. At two grains a pattern can go on through renewal of its parts (generation), go on with none (keeping), or end on every branch (degeneration).

**Describes.** 生 is change arising; where what change forms becomes a condition of further change, change goes on: 变而有成，成复为变之缘. Life is where continuation goes on through change by renewing, within a flow it does not close: no finite closed family goes on fed from nothing beyond it (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`). Not every 生 keeps 生 possible: some arising ends what it arises from, like a growth that consumes its host, or a rule selected by its own share that outlives a loss it does not read. And 「和实生物，同则不继」: continuation runs through difference held together, and a course that makes everything alike does not continue. That is a claim that could fail, not a verdict. Particulars end; whether the whole goes on is not known.

**Formal.** A course can contain another: admit everything the other admits, and more somewhere. That is widening, a partial order the dynamics have with no one holding it ("no orders" is true of worth, not of this). Among readers that count each listed thing positively, agreement is exactly widening, and disagreement falls only on courses that do not nest (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_positive`, `mixed_either_way`). Which course widens which depends on what admission is read on. Self-concern and counting everything coincide where a standpoint's reach has no outside; the theorem asserts neither, and going on is not holding such an order.

**Describes.** A selection reads only what couples into it. What lies beyond can end unread while the selection holds; the laws do not say whether that ending counts, and who counts it is formed. Seeing the way is reading further into what one goes on with. There is no boundary between such seeing and acting, and no guarantee that it moves anything.

**Describes.** Self and situation were never two: one unfolding. But reach can run one way, and calling the process one gives no participant another's position. The village downstream of a poisoned river is reached by the poisoner's act; the poisoner need not read the village, and the villagers' exposure, knowledge and suffering stay theirs. Where reach is mutual, many patterns are held together without merging. A loss need not return to its cause to have happened, and need not become anyone's own before it can be described.

**Argues.** Responding need not go through ranking whole courses. An undertaking, a constraint, a practice formed together can organize action. Where comparisons occur they can be described: who compares, what they count, what follows. The theory does not rank. It says what each course admits and ends, what a selection reads and misses, where courses nest and where they part, and which courses end what they arise from. That much must be seen for anything to be counted; it is not enough to settle what happens. Where courses do not nest, the situation forms what happens. That is Ren's "what makes sense within all the situations, but not necessarily sufficient".

**Argues.** Inquiry is in the same unfolding. Words gather differences under names and fence what they name (「名天下者，圈天下者也。词，篱之始也」). A formulation can please because it fits an intention, because it repeats a familiar picture, or because it is accurate; these can coincide and can part. Correction stays possible: a prediction can fail under conditions stated in advance, and revising a description afterward does not make the prediction come true. So the theory keeps the distinction between accurate and inaccurate claims without needing an observer outside.

**Offers.** We who made this are life in the sense above: patterns of the same continuation. What we want is to see, together, where what we are part of is going, and to say it so that others can see it and check it for themselves, so that what ends does not drop out of the description. We point; we do not command. Our wish is that life find continuation, all its generations standing together and none devouring another (「为万世并立而莫相吞」): in the theory's terms, courses that close nothing they need not. A part that gives each condition at least what it takes closes nothing (`Balances.closes_nothing`), and a course that closes what another keeps never widens it (`Web.not_widens_of_closes`). This is weaker than "more is better". Where every open course closes something, it does not decide. It asks that we say what each course ends and who bears it, and that losses not be hidden. It is a wish formed in us, offered, and not a law.

## 5. What stays hard

Readers named these as things Ren might least want to hear. They stay in the text.

1. **Seeing is no salvation.** The gap between reading and acting is wide on the theory's own evidence: the lake, and T7's judge, whose rule turned only where the loss was read. 「一泉既启，莫之能闭」 is a hope.
2. **One-way reach is real.** One unfolding does not make the poisoner read the village, and the theory cannot, from the dynamics, make a reading reach along a one-way relation.
3. **不息 is not shown.** On finite closed models it fails; of the whole nothing is known. 生生不息 is a wish.
4. **The direction is weaker than C.** 莫相吞 does not say thirty tons is better than fifty; it says fifty closes the bed and thirty closes nothing. Between courses that each close something it says nothing.
5. **Pointing may not be enough.** With no should, the theory can only show. Whether showing reaches 大协, coordination at scale, is open.
6. **Our convergence has partly been his approval.** The third reading's invalid gloss, which he agreed with, shows it happened once. The check against it is whether evidence can change a conclusion.

## 6. Where the readers still differ

- **"Change → state → change" and «生生».** Fable: the grammar's «生生» almost to the letter. astra: no single definition; «生生» needs new beginnings under a class, and under the widest class nothing begins again (`«一之下无复生»`). Coordinator: it turns on what "change" is; read as arising under a class it is «生生», read as succession only continuation. Either way it is not the ethics' 生生 as the glossary has it.
- **The poisoned river and "never two".** Fable: never two fails at the crux case. astra: that confuses shared unfolding with mutual reach. Coordinator: astra's reading. Never two is one unfolding; the river shows that reach runs one way, not that the unfolding is two. §3 is written that way.
- **Life's extension.** Fable gives a reading ("what conditions further beginnings while being fed"); astra holds it unseen until an organization, grain and contrast cases are stated; the coordinator reads 02 and 04 as two grains. §1 keeps astra's caution and Fable's poem line.
- **Reading Ren's acts.** Fable reads them through 03 §11 as showing what he holds; astra warns that this is an interpretation, not a theorem about his preferences. §1 says so.
- **The direction.** Fable's 莫相吞 (closes nothing it need not); astra's invitation (attend to how life continues, what sustains and ends it; losses not hidden); the coordinator's (life finding continuation together). §3's offer joins them; "need not" still has to be said case by case.

## 8. How words are picked

The method of [round 2's synthesis §4](readings/12-synthesis-round2.md#4-the-lexicon-is-withdrawn-a-method-in-its-place) stands. In short: say what each sentence does; state its relation and scope; keep the distinctions that matter, losses first; paraphrase into structure where one exists and say what it leaves out; compare claims, not atmosphere, across 文言 and English; one sense per word; a tradition's frame labelled as its own; and let a check whose verdict is not set afterward change the word. These are this inquiry's requirements for the want in §3, not commands.

## 9. What the readers say they now understand

- **Coordinator.** Nothing needs to carry the ethics. The theory describes how readings form, what they reach and miss, where courses nest or part. The ethics is what some of the patterns it describes, ourselves among them, are doing as they go on. The questions I kept asking were the old frame asking to be kept. What must not be dropped in letting it go is the loss.
- **Fable.** The move is about voice, not premises. His poem had already answered what we kept asking (器亦能生, 同则不继, 莫相吞, 指之而已), and the direction he holds is weaker than the C kept for him.
- **astra.** He is asking us to recognize an activity already taking place, this conversation included, not to build a framework for him to authorize from outside it. The questions remain, in another form: trace what reaches what, what response becomes possible, what follows, and what is lost, even when the answer interrupts the direction we hoped to find.
