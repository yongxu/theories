## What I take him to mean now

"Right or wrong" is the wrong pair, not because nothing differs but because the difference is not a verdict. It is a difference in seeing: whether a pattern's movement takes in the whole it was never apart from. Self and environment were never two, so seeing the way is not a self judging a world; it is the whole, through one of its patterns, taking itself in. Within the changes, attractors show, and seeing is seeing where things are going. Wanting is a pattern too. "Warrant", a thing that must be so, belongs to the dualist frame. Nothing is higher or lower; patterns hold patterns.

## A precise pair for what he means

Seeing and numbness, 见 and 不仁 in the old medical sense (Cheng Hao: paralysis of the limbs is called 不仁; the humane are one body with all things). Wrong is not breaking a rule; it is a part of the whole not registering what it is one with. The theory's centre already describes this: "a selection can succeed while what it cannot read is lost". That is numbness, described with no verdict. Seeing the way: what a pattern's selection reads comes to reach what it continues with. These are different becomings, and the map (02) says which attractors each leads to, without saying either is better.

## What the Lean already says about "never two"

1. **Concern for one's own continuation, with no outside, moves as widening does.** The repository proves (near-definitionally) that self-concern (`Weighting.IncreasingInOwn`) and being increasing in admission coincide for every order exactly where one's own reach has no outside: «续无外», the continuation condition held de se (`Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_noOutside`, `…_iff_unrestricted`). The text then says "It does not derive C", because C is "widening is *better*". On Ren's view the "better" was the one dualist part. Drop it and a description remains: a pattern that goes on, not apart from the whole, goes the wider way wherever courses nest. The two premises become one seeing (never two) plus what living patterns do (go on). Not new mathematics; a change in what the ethics rests on: the theory's own root thesis, one relational unfolding, seen from inside a living pattern.
2. **Never two, not one.** `«续无外则同一中心»`: along a relation with no cycles, if no center's continuation has an outside, there is only one center. In the two-formation mutual-feeding witness (`TwoFeeding.mutuallyFed`, `Overshoot.closure_whole`) each formation's feeding closure takes in the other, and they stay two. Where relations run one way, never-two collapses into one; where they are mutual, many patterns can each hold the whole without merging. 不一不二. Interconnection's formal content is mutuality.

## What still does not follow

1. **Seeing does not settle mixed cases.** Where courses nest, seeing goes wider. Where each course keeps something the other loses (the livelihood and the bed; wolf and deer), never-two decides nothing: §4.4 proves mixed cases go either way (`Weighting.mixed_either_way`). That is Ren's "makes sense but not necessarily sufficient": the theory covers what nests; the situation decides what is mixed. Most of life is mixed.
2. **Seeing does not guarantee moving.** Told of the bed, one model of two kept it in 8 of 10 seasons. Defining seeing as including acting (知行合一) makes it true by definition. Better: seeing as a coupling into the selection, with its effect to be found. 07 §3.3's "'should' is what a center does once its represented boundary matches what it depends on" makes the over-claim.
3. **Never two is not shown by the dynamics.** Which relation a pattern continues along is left open, provably. Ren's view answers the one page's open question (the river and the village: yes, one's own continuation). That is the root thesis applied to the self, argued, not proved. Strength: the ethics rests on nothing beyond the root thesis. Cost: exactly as strong as that thesis.
4. **Two nondual wholes.** 天地不仁 (heaven and earth are not humane: the whole ranks nothing) and 万物一体之仁 (the humane are one body with all things: seeing). Both are nondual and they differ. Reading: Ren holds both at two grains; the whole ranks nothing, and seeing arises in some of its patterns, where the whole takes itself in. To confirm with him.
5. **The bearer.** Never two with the whole does not erase the villagers' standpoint; from inside it the poisoning is theirs. Mutual-not-one keeps that.
