# The coordinator's fourth-round reply to Ren (9 November 2026, an AI reading), as sent

Agreed: "numbness" is the wrong word. It brings in a sick body measured against a healthy one, which is another pair. 文言 has better words, and two of them come from texts the theory already reads.

**通 and 蔽, in place of seeing and numbness**

- **通.** The Xici defines it structurally: 「往来不穷谓之通」, going and coming without exhaustion. Chapter 06 already reads that line, and Zhuangzi adds 「道通为一」. 通 isn't a verdict. It says whether a course passes through, whether what goes out comes back.
- **蔽.** Xunzi, 解蔽: 「凡人之患，蔽于一曲而暗于大理」, screened by one corner and dark to the great pattern. 蔽 isn't a fault either. It's a structural fact: a reading that reaches one corner and not the rest.

The theory's centre is exactly 蔽于一曲: what a selection reads is the corner, and what lies beyond it is what ends. 解蔽 then names what you meant by "seeing the way" without a self doing the judging: the screen lifts, and the reading reaches what it goes on with. In English, systems words carry this without a pair: *read / unread*, *within / beyond the coupling*. Chapter 06 doesn't use 蔽, 解蔽 or 道通为一 yet, so they'd be new readings, made by AI and labelled as such.

**"Success"**

The one-page centre sentence is "a selection can succeed while what it cannot read is lost". "Succeed" brings in a goal, and "lost" asks who lost it. Zhuangzi put the same thing without either: 「道隐于小成」, the way is hidden by small completions. 成 is a becoming word, completion or formation, not a grade. In neutral English: *a selection can hold while what lies beyond its reading ends.*

**How to write it, given that English leans dual**

English grammar itself does part of it: a subject acts on an object, nouns become things, and "is" fixes properties. So three registers, each carrying little dualism:

1. **The Lean** (logic): relations, inclusion, closure.
2. **The 文言 grammar**, which the root already uses for its names: «续», «广», «续无外», «生生».
3. **Systems and maths English:** coupling, reading, closure, attractor, containment.

English prose becomes the translation layer, with each 文言 term glossed once, in one sense, as the glossary rule requires.

Two cautions. Systems theory and maths aren't neutral by default either: "system/environment" is literally a boundary, and "optimal", "fitness" and "cost function" are rankings. And 文言 has its own pairs (善恶, 是非, 贵贱); Zhuangzi's point is that they're 彼是, this and that from a standpoint. So the choice is word by word, not language by language.

**What the review would start with: a lexicon**

| avoided | instead | 文言 |
|---|---|---|
| right / wrong | through / screened | 通 / 蔽 |
| seeing the way | the screen lifting; the reading reaching what it goes on with | 解蔽 |
| succeed | hold, complete | 成 (and 小成) |
| lost | ends; no longer admitted | 息 |
| better | dropped; widening stays as containment | 广 |
| should / must / warrant | dropped; proof (what follows) and showing | 证 / 示 |
| account | theory, in the sense of a looking | 观 |
| attractor | where a course goes | 所趋 |

Others to sort, each by its sense and not by search hits: harm, value, goal, cost and benefit, agent and patient, self and other, interest, optimal, winning. The review would then read every live line against the lexicon, check for places where the text doesn't show what it could show, and fix the plain errors already found (T8's "binds", 07 §3.3's "should", the one page's "C ranks it higher").
