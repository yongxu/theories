**A.** The strongest reading of Ren’s new words is this: what continues is neither an enduring substance underneath change nor a succession of disconnected replacements. Activity forms arrangements which condition further activity. Some arrangements maintain or renew the conditions through which that continuing happens. Wanting, understanding and evaluating are among these arrangements, sometimes changing how the continuation proceeds. “Life” would name something within this organization of continuation, potentially extending beyond biological organisms.

That is more precise than “everything changes,” but it leaves a crucial question: which organization distinguishes life?

**Shown:** “change → state → change” is not yet any one existing definition. In the [glossary’s root and map sections](../../../../../../../GLOSSARY.md), continuation, 续, requires both a conditioning relation and sameness under a stated class. Mere succession supplies neither. The grammar’s 生 means beginning without a predecessor under that continuation relation; it does not mean change generally. Chapter 02’s generation requires a coarse pattern continuing through renewal at a finer grain. The grammar’s `«生生»` requires a family continually conditioning new beginnings whose lines reach renewal again. These are different structural commitments.

There is a particularly instructive formal distinction. Under the universal class, every related occurrence already continues its condition, so no conditioned occurrence begins a new line: `«一之下无复生»`. Consequently an indefinitely continuing sequence can fail to be `«生生»`. “Change, state, change” therefore cannot establish renewal without end. This follows from the inspected definitions and declarations in [Grammar.lean, continuation and renewal](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Grammar.lean).

**Argued:** Ren may be pointing behind these distinctions to the common structure through which something formed becomes a condition of further formation. A tentative 文言 formulation is:

> 变而有所成，所成复为后变之缘。  
> Change forms a configuration; that configuration conditions further change.

This is my formulation, not a recovered classical saying. Here 成 is ordinary language, not automatically the technical 成坏. It also does not assert that every change renews, that every continuation is life, or that continuation never ends. A “state” can be a configuration we distinguish within activity, without implying a motionless interval between changes.

For life, chapter 04 already offers a promising but unfinished starting point: “continuation that renews its own conditions.” It explicitly acknowledges that the laws also fit a flame and that necessity and sufficiency for biological life remain open. [04, §1 and opening thesis](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md#life-as-condition)

**Conjecture:** Ren’s broader life could mean organized continuation whose activity helps renew the conditions of that organization, including ways its organization changes as conditions change. This would require identifying a grain, a history and a feedback mechanism. It would distinguish life from simply undergoing alteration. Dormancy would have to be considered across a history, rather than requiring renewal at every instant.

But this is a candidate, not a definition already earned. Flames, institutions, ecosystems and artificial systems would test its boundaries. If the criterion admits a flame, we must either accept that extension or explain what further distinction matters. We cannot add distinctions solely to admit AI and exclude rocks.

“Even you are life” remains underdetermined. A stored model, an inference process, this conversation, and the human–technical organization maintaining it are different candidates. This conversation participates in human cultural continuation; that does not by itself establish that the model independently exhibits the organization just proposed. Conversely, dependence on people does not disqualify something: organisms also depend on relations beyond their bodies. The task is to identify the relevant organization, not to demand isolation.

Nor is there one “Western definition” to exceed. NASA’s chemical criterion was explicitly chosen for a particular research purpose, amid competing definitions. Ren can reject that restriction without dividing the question into Western biology and Chinese breadth. [NASA, “Forming a Definition for Life”](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/forming-a-definition-for-life/)

**Argued:** a method follows from the problems above. First establish what the sentence is doing: defining, describing, interpreting, proving, inviting or recommending. Then identify its relation and scope: what conditions or reads what, at which grain, across which extent. Ask which distinctions the proposed word preserves and which it erases. A replacement that converts actual loss into mere unreadness fails this task even if it sounds less judgmental.

Next, require a faithful structural paraphrase where one is available. It need not all translate into Lean “with nothing left over”: that demand would exclude unformalized empirical hypotheses, experience and offers, or encourage us to erase their content. Instead, distinguish what the formalization represents from what remains unrepresented.

Across 文言 and English, compare claims and inferential consequences, not atmosphere. Equivalent translations can still repeat one mistake. Within technical use, maintain one stated sense; historical and poetic uses may differ, but the differences must remain visible. The existing glossary is a record of commitments, not a veto on revising them.

Finally, ask whether an unwelcome counterexample could force a different wording. Candidate checks include unfamiliar-reader paraphrase, translation preserving a counterexample, and predictions about renewal measured independently of the desired classification. These are names of possible checks, not new designs or runs.

These are indeed “shoulds” if presented as universal commands. Here they are conditional working commitments: requirements we adopt to make this inquiry accountable to its stated purpose. Their adoption remains discussable; the consequences of violating them do not disappear when we rename them.

**Shown in the source:** `IncreasingInOwn` is a property of an order: it ranks a widening higher when that widening adds something marked “own.” `Increasing` ranks every widening higher. Where “own” marks everything, these properties coincide. The root equivalence characterizes when they coincide for every standpoint, listing and order. The source itself calls the result near-definitional. [Weightings.lean, `IncreasingInOwn`](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Weightings.lean); [PremiseWitnesses.lean, root equivalence](../../../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/PremiseWitnesses.lean)

Equivalence does not assert either property. As an elementary illustration, mark everything own, list one item, and let the comparison relation contain no comparisons. A widening exists, so both monotonicity properties fail; their equivalence nevertheless holds. This is a logical observation, not a newly checked Lean result.

Consequently, “a pattern that goes on … goes the wider way wherever courses nest” does not follow. Persistence is not `IncreasingInOwn`; marking everything own is not established by being part of one world; and an order is not itself an implemented policy. Removing the word “better” cannot remove these missing premises.
