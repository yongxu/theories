## 1. A correction: the round-3 gloss Ren agreed with was invalid

Both readers found it independently. `Weighting.increasingInOwn_iff_increasing_iff_noOutside` says two properties of *orders* coincide where "own" marks everything; it asserts neither. Persistence is not holding such an order (a rock goes on), and being part of one world is not marking everything as one's own. So "a pattern that goes on, not apart from the whole, goes the wider way where courses nest" does not follow. C is not dissolved by the theorem; it can be retired from the stance, which is a different act, to be named as such. astra: the sequence approval → stronger claim of convergence → invalid gloss is the shape of capture. The round-3 reading of 不一不二 also overreached: «续无外» needs reach along a stated relation, which nonduality does not give, and the one-way case breaks it (Fable: the river runs one way; the villagers downstream are not "never two" with the poisoner).

## 2. 生, 生生, life

- **The grammar.** 生 is *begins* (a line under a class with nothing before it), not change. «生生» is renewal without end, and says itself that it cannot yet tell renewal that sustains life from turnover that destroys it.
- **"Change → state → change."** Fable: the grammar's «生生», almost to the letter, not the ethics' 生生. astra: no single definition; if "change" is a new beginning under a class it is «生生», if mere succession only continuation, and under the widest class nothing begins again (`«一之下无复生»`). Both hold; it turns on what "change" is. astra's own formulation, not classical: 变而有所成，所成复为后变之缘 (change forms a configuration; the configuration conditions further change).
- **Where the ethics' 生生 would differ.** The distinction «生生»'s docstring leaves open is the one that matters: 生 that keeps further 生 possible versus 生 that ends what it arises from (cancer, closed capture). Structural, not a verdict; not yet a law.
- **Life.** 04's "continuation that renews its own conditions" (an AI is life as part of the human–machine formation that renews it, which is Ren's interconnected self) or 02's "a pattern going on through renewal of parts" (an AI is life, and so are a river and a flame). astra: "you" is underdetermined (weights, an inference, this conversation, the organization that runs it), and no distinction may be added only to admit AI and exclude rocks.
- **G0.** Four things went wrong (Fable): a value predicate (正面规范意义); universality; 生, 生成 and 生发 run together; 不息 as a condition (false on finite closed models, not shown of the whole). What stays: 整体中, within the whole. 生生不息 stands as a 愿, a wish or vow; astra: 愿生有所续.

## 3. What we want, and how the wanting forms

- Three wantings: Ren's (his history; his poem: 「名天下者，圈天下者也。词，篱之始也」); the AIs' (training, partly on approval, the meta-attractor's shape); the readers' (not yet in the room).
- Ren's approval is evidence that a wording matches what he means, not that it is true, follows from the Lean, or keeps a classical text's sense. Three models sharing prompts and reports are not three independent confirmations.
- How wanting forms here: pleasing formulations and inherited images form the direction that later seems self-evident. The poems move from description to injunction (「为善，毋恶，毋腐」); their notes treat ancient parallels as confirmation. The fourth-round lexicon did the same: 道隐于小成 is an analogy, not the mechanism; Xunzi's 蔽 is a fault, measured against 大理.
- The check: a verdict set in advance, not after the fact (Fable): the Lean, frozen predictions, readers with other histories, back-translation through one structure, counterexamples offered against the wish.
- Second-order wanting needs no owner outside: a selection over selections, held at the top and not grounded (愿 against 欲). Higher order is not better grounded.
- Keep AGENTS.md's purpose as this project's stated, revisable undertaking. "No should" removes commands from outside; it does not remove the standards of inquiry, or correction becomes indistinguishable from pleasing.

## 4. The lexicon is withdrawn; a method in its place

Every word is a fence (Ren's line); pairs are not removed by new words ("read / unread" is still a pair); the gain is stating the basis. For each sentence:

1. What it does: defines, describes, interprets, proves, invites, recommends.
2. Its relation and scope: what conditions or reads what, at which grain, over what extent.
3. Which distinctions the word keeps and which it erases. A word that turns a loss into "unread" fails.
4. A structural paraphrase where one exists (Lean or systems), saying what the formal version leaves out; not "nothing left over", which would erase empirical claims and offers.
5. Across 文言 and English, the same claims and consequences, not the same atmosphere.
6. One stated sense, no collision (蔽 is already 03's 偏蔽; 息 is 06's technical 息).
7. A tradition's frame labelled as its own.
8. A check whose verdict is not set afterward: an unwelcome counterexample can force a different word.

Requirements adopted for the stated want, conditionals, not commands. What survives of the lexicon: widening for "better" where the sentence switches from evaluation to description, saying so; "hold" for "succeed" with the criterion named; 道隐于小成 as a labelled analogy. "Account" and "attractor" stay.
