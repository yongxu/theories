Say what that is in the theory's terms (03's reading loop, selection through couplings, what a response within a requirement takes), what it does to round 2's "deepest hole" (does seeing include care?), and whether 07 §3.3's line ("'should' is what a center does once its represented boundary matches what it depends on") is wrong, right, or half-right on his view.

Say what the statement describes, what it offers and in whose voice, and what the theory covers and does not.
