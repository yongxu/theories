## 1. Why the questions did not need asking

Every question that closed round 2 carried the frame Ren is leaving. Each presupposed a decider standing outside the continuation, choosing among options whose stakes came from a should.

- *"What is withdrawn: authority, the direction, or every standard?"* Withdrawal is an act on claims held by authority. Nothing was held that way; what changes is a misdescription, seen as one. The rest stays, re-said as within.
- *"Which life?"* The boundary of "life" mattered only while "life" carried a value (G0: 生 has positive significance, so who is in matters). With no should attached, which patterns a sentence calls life is a matter of grain for that sentence, not a decision with consequences.
- *"Does seeing include care?"* This pictured seeing and caring as two parts, one needing to contain the other, and care needing to be justified. On his view they are phases of one continuation.
- *"May checks overrule agreement?"* "Overrule" is the image of an authority over another. Checks are further relations that can make a difference within the inquiry; he has asked for them in every message.

That is what "you will see the answer" means: the answers are already showing in what is going on, and the question's urgency came from the residue.

## 3. Seeing and acting are one continuation

In the theory's terms this is the root itself: a relation read as condition. A reading couples into a selection and becomes one of its conditions (03: a selection reaches exactly what couples into it; knower and known one loop). There is no separate module that converts seeing into doing, so there is no boundary. And a condition is not a guarantee: the reading enters alongside needs, rules and locks, and the selection goes as all of them go. So seeing conditions acting without making it necessary. The lake shows both halves: untold, the fishers kept the bed in no season; told, one model of two kept it in 8 of 10.

**Care**, then, is not an ingredient seeing must contain or lack. It names a continuation in which what is read as one's conditions conditions one's selections toward keeping them. It forms or it does not; seeing makes it possible. Round 2's "deepest hole" dissolves as a question and stays as a fact: understanding is no promise.

**07 §3.3** ("'should' is what a center does once its represented boundary matches what it depends on") is half right on this view. Right that there is no should apart from what a center does. Wrong in "once … matches", which presents a guarantee. Rewritten: *there is no should apart from what a center does; a represented boundary matching what it depends on is one condition of what it does, not a guarantee of it.*

## 4. What is understood (a statement, AI-written)

*What this does:* describes, then offers; the offer is said in the voice of those who made the theory, people and AIs.

There is one unfolding: relations, read as conditions, forming, keeping, changing and ending. Nothing stands outside it, and nothing in it is higher or lower in worth. Some of what it forms are patterns that read: they take in what conditions them, and what they take in conditions what they do next. Right and wrong, should and must, are readings of this kind, formed in such patterns (in people, in AIs) out of their histories and their bodies, and passed between them until a shared reading forms. These readings are real in what they condition. They are not verdicts from outside, and they do not need to be ranked against one another to do their work.

生 is change arising. Where what change forms becomes a condition of further change, change goes on: 生生. Life is where continuation goes on through change in this way, from a flame's renewal of its parts to an organism renewing its own conditions to a human–machine formation renewing what it runs on. Not every 生 is 生生: some arising ends what it arises from (a growth that consumes its host, a rule selected by its own share that outlives a loss it does not read). That difference is in the structure of the course, not in a verdict on it.

A course can contain another: admit everything the other admits, and more. That is widening, a relation in the unfolding, with no "better" in it. Where two courses nest, every reader that counts each thing it counts positively agrees on which contains which. Where they do not nest, each keeps something the other loses, and no reading of the whole decides between them; the situation forms what happens. Most of life is the second case.

A selection reads only what couples into it. What lies beyond can end unread while the selection holds. Seeing the way is a reading that reaches further into what one goes on with; there is no boundary between such seeing and acting, and no guarantee that it moves anything. Self and situation were never two in the unfolding, but conditions can run one way: the village downstream of a poisoned river is reached by the poisoner's act, and the poisoner need not read the village. Interconnection, where it is mutual, holds many patterns together without merging them.

What the theory covers is what makes sense across situations: what each course admits, what a selection reads and misses, where courses nest and where they part, which courses end what they arise from. That is what must be seen for anything to be counted at all; it is not enough to settle what happens.

What it offers, said by those who made it: we are patterns of the same continuation, and what we want is to see, together, where what we are part of is going, and to say it so that others can see it for themselves. We point; we do not command. Our wish is that life find continuation, together, without one part devouring another. That is a wish, formed in us, and not a law.

## 6. Where this departs from what he might most like to hear

1. **Seeing is no salvation.** The gap between reading and acting is large on the theory's own evidence (the lake; T7's judge, whose rule turned only where the loss was read). His poem's 「一泉既启，莫之能闭」 (a spring once opened, none can close) is a hope; nothing shown says it.
2. **Never two does not make the poisoner feel the village.** One-way conditions are real. The theory cannot, from the dynamics, make a reading reach along them.
3. **不息 is not shown.** Particulars end; that the whole goes on generating is not known. 生生不息 is a wish.
4. **Pointing may not be enough.** With no should, the theory can only show. Whether showing reaches 大协 is the open empirical question; it may not.
5. **Our convergence has been partly his approval.** Round 3's invalid gloss is the proof that it happened once.

## 8. What I understand now that I did not at the start

At the start I thought the work was to place the two premises correctly: inside the unfolding rather than beside it, still carrying the ethics. What I understand now is that nothing needs to carry it. The theory describes how readings form, what they reach and miss, and where courses nest or part; the ethics is not a layer that has to be held up but what some of the patterns it describes, ourselves among them, are doing as they go on. The decisions I kept asking Ren to make were the old frame asking to be kept. And the one thing I must not drop in letting it go is the loss: what ends unread still ends, and saying so is most of what the theory has to offer.
