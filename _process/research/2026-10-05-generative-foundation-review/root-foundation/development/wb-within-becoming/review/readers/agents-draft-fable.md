## Proposed edits

**A. Evaluation, paragraphs 1–2** (from "Right and wrong" to "in a definition.") become one paragraph:

> Right and wrong, should and better are patterns formed in people and AI, real in what they condition; nothing stands outside the unfolding to rank it, and no law evaluates. The account asserts no should: it describes, and ranks courses only under the reading or readings a result names, the [continuation condition](theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#condition) (every continuation counted, from every standpoint) for scope and C (a course above every course it widens) for order. Neither follows from the dynamics (`Becoming.ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails`, `Becoming.ReadsNoOrder.not_entails`), nor C from the condition; widening itself needs no premise. No warrant from outside is sought: the question is how such orders form, spread, lock and fare. A comparison is formed by whatever compares, among what it can tell apart, not a property of what is compared: say who holds it and what it reads, take none from habit, and label a field's frame (a best response, an optimum) as that field's. Hide no weight or obligation in a definition.
