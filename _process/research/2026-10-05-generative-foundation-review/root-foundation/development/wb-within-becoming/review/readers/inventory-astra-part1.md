The formal homes often contain precisely the distinction their summaries lose: all possible selections versus one actual selection; an available policy versus conduct; a witness versus a universal requirement; an independent predicate versus a dynamically represented practice. These are substantive corrections, not a change of philosophical vocabulary. The statistical-bound inference in A3 and the recruitment necessity in A10 are especially clear internal failures.

The ethical revision is more selective. A conditional theorem about C’s order can remain exactly that. “Warrant” need not mean an authority outside becoming, and “must establish the hypotheses” is not an ethical command. What changes is presenting one two-premise appraisal as the account’s entry point for evaluation generally. Conversely, removing evaluative language must not turn the dweller’s ending into “nothing lost,” or make binary admission stand for every cost.
