**The question.** OPENING's body says that a collective made of lives that each end goes on only as new lives keep arising, and that one consuming them consumes what it goes on through. Its note `collective` marked the first clause proved (`Web.Whole.needs_arising`) and the second not, because `Web.Whole` relates a whole to its parts only by coincidence of activity and carries no draw; joint excess (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`) is stated on flows, with no whole. TH1's revised proposal 8 ("where it draws on them beyond what they renew, it ends what it goes on through") rested its second clause on reading the joint-excess law with the collective as the drawer ([significance review](BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#proposals-for-opening-revised)). The question was whether the two laws join on one world, and in exactly what form.

**What was built** (`Becoming.Wholes`, pure core, every axiom report empty; it now imports `Becoming.Continuation`, both at layer L1, with no cycle).

- *Without arising, ending parts end together.* `Web.Listed.end_without_arising`: parts each of whose activity ends (`Web.Ends`), the active ones listed at each step (`Web.Listed`), none arising from step `N` on, are all inactive from a later step. A part active after `N` was not inactive at `N` (`active_back`, since nothing arose), so it is among the finitely many listed at `N`, which all end by one step (`Ends.all`). `Web.Whole.ends_without_arising`: so their whole ends. `Covered.needs_arising` is now its corollary (a cover from `T` meets an active part at `T + M`, against the parts' ending by `M`), and its direct proof goes (rule 9). Constructively the new law says more than `needs_arising`'s contrapositive: not only that the whole does not persist, but that from some step on it is never active.
- *The join.* `Web.Whole.family_excess_ends`: with a flow `L` on the same web, balance, only active formations giving (`Acting`) and activity needing support (`Dependent`); a family `ds` whose renewal is gated on it (whatever gives to a member depends on some member at a need of at least `m`, as in `Flow.family_excess_ends`); the whole's parts arising only where they then depend on some member at such a need (a part arising across step `t` requires some `e ∈ ds` at `t + 1` with `m ≤ need`); `n` steps of excess of the family's uptake over its replenishment from `T`, from a stock below `n + m`. Then no part arises from `T + n` on, and the whole ends. The proof: a part arising at `t ≥ T + n` would be active at `t + 1`, a step from `T + n` on, depending on a member of a family whose stock is below `m` there (`Flow.family_excess_ends`); so none arises, and `Whole.ends_without_arising` ends the whole. `Web.Whole.joint_excess_ends` is the family of one condition, through `Flow.gate_single`.
- *Witnesses.* `WholeExceeding.drawn`: a lineage of `Generations`' members on one condition standing at two, one, then nothing; each member depends on the condition at need one at its first active step only and gives it one there; the lineage takes two at steps zero and one. Every premise holds, the lineage is active at steps zero to two, and, derived through the law, no member arises from step two on and the lineage ends: the second member, arisen while the condition stood at its need, lives out its span; the third would be first active at step two, where the condition stands at nothing. `WholeWithin.kept`: the lineage taking one, within what its members give, the condition standing at two; every premise but the excess holds, the lineage is active at every step and a member arises at every step. `WholeUnending.persists`: a whole of one part that never ends, the whole taking the condition's one unit at step zero; every premise but ending parts holds, the excess included, and the whole is active at every step. `WholeUngated.persists`: `Generations`' lineage drawing the condition down while no member depends on it; every premise but the arising's dependence holds, the excess included, and the lineage is active at every step while a member arises at every step.

**What it says, and what it does not.** The draw is joint: the uptake counts every taker, so the theorem holds whoever draws, and the whole need not be a taker at all. That is the joint-difference-making result read for a whole ([^meta]'s "joint consumption has no single consumer"): the collective's own draw within what is renewed proves nothing (`Pair.own_not_joint`), and the law does not say that the collective is the one that consumes. The parts need not depend on the family once arisen: only their arising does, so lives already living may go on, and the whole ends once they have ended, at a step after `T + n` that the listing at `T + n` fixes, not at `T + n`. The gate on renewal is the joint-excess law's: renewal given only by what depends on the family; where the family is renewed from beyond, with no such dependence, the law does not apply (`Coupled.alone_ungated` read for a patch alone). The finite listing is the census premise of `Whole.needs_arising`; that it is needed for this law is not shown, since `Countless` has no flow. What the law forbids: a whole of finitely many parts at each step, each ending, whose parts arise only where the drawn family stands at their need, lasting through a run of joint excess on that family longer than its stock stands above the need.

*Reading* [AI reading, D5, D45]. OPENING's sentence reads as this law with three readings named: a collective as the whole of its lives (`Web.Whole`, coincidence of activity), the lives' renewal as the family whose level new lives arise through (in the witness, a condition each new life depends on at its first step and gives to), and consuming as the joint draw exceeding that renewal. "Consumes them" in Ren's sentence is the lives; the law reads it as drawing on what they renew, since a life is a formation and not an amount, and a draw on a formation is not represented. Under these readings the second clause of proposal 8 holds as a theorem, and "cancer, turned around" stays a figure: a growth whose draw on what renews its own members ends the arising it lives through.

**Self-review.** The join is a composition: the arising premise turns `Flow.family_excess_ends` into "no part arises from `T + n` on", and the new law below `needs_arising` turns that into the whole's ending. Its content is (a) the law below `needs_arising`, which says how a whole of ending listed parts ends where generation stops, not only that it cannot persist; and (b) the premises under which OPENING's clause is a theorem, each named, three of them shown needed by witnesses. The premise carrying the reading is the arising's dependence on the drawn family; where new lives arise through something else, the law says nothing about them (`WholeUngated`). The witnesses' activity is stipulated, as in `Becoming.Continuation`; the general theorem is where the ending is derived, and `drawn` derives it through the law.
