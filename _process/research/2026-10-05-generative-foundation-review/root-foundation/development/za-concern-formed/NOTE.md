Three AI readers and a checker agreed: what follows once self-concern and a closed scope are present is proved in detail; how self-concern forms within the dynamics is not modelled at all. `Weighting.IncreasingInOwn`, an order increasing in one's own continuation, occurred only as a hypothesis. ## In plain words

**What now comes out of the dynamics, with no self-concern supplied.**

1. *Scope, where everything is entangled.* In a mutually dependent flow, a center's concern for whatever can affect its continuation at some remove, held de se, already counts every continuation; so does its concern for whatever it can affect. With every formation a center, that is exactly what mutual dependence is, and where something lies beyond, as the clearer's dweller does, neither holds.
2. *Keeping above ending, from persisting.* A line that persists through arbitrarily late cohorts never overdrew, from a short stock, the gated family of conditions it depends on. That is a law of the World, under balance, only active formations giving, activity needing support, descent, and every cohort depending on a family whose renewal is gated on it. Where a choice ending what the line depends on is realized as such an overdraw, a persisting line never made one. So its choices, if they come from any strict order at all, are consistent with an order ranking every keeping course above every ending course met together with it. Where only ending was at stake on the occasions it met, its choices are consistent with self-concern there.

**What does not.**

1. *Self-concern as held.* Persisting is a filter on what was chosen, not a mechanism that forms an order. It shows that the choices are consistent with self-concern where ending was at stake. It does not show that any order is held.
2. *Degree.* Where degrees of keeping are at stake, persisting fixes nothing by itself. In the witness, a line on a World meeting every law the positive result uses persists, and chooses the line as it is over one with one more child, a choice according with a strict order; no strict order according with that choice is increasing in its own continuation on the occasion met. It shows that the stake premise cannot be dropped. It is a weaker form of the countermodel one reader proposed (a persisting, fully informed, internally implemented chooser that is not self-concerned): nothing in it represents information, the chooser is not implemented in the flow, and the line persists whatever is chosen.
3. *Beyond the occasions met.* A course never met is ranked either way by orders consistent with every choice. Read with training, this is where goal misgeneralization sits.
4. *What is still supplied.* Which relation a center's concern is drawn along; that significance is the own-closure reading; which formations are centers (board item ZA-G1); which class is one's own (ZC); that a chosen ending is realized as an overdraw; that any actual web is mutually dependent.
5. *Nothing normative.* None of this derives a "should". What it gives is descriptive: where concern and scope form, how far they reach, and what persisting does and does not fix.

Against the readers' list of gaps: the composition from mutual dependence to the condition is done; G3 is done in the form "consistent with, not had", positive where only ending is at stake, negative between degrees and off the occasions met. G1 (centers read off a flow) is ZA-G1's and G2 (the own class) ZC's. Before ZC claimed G2, this session had proved part of it (where a member conditions something and some class meets P2 and P4 at it, the class of the member with everything downstream of it meets both and is the least such, and two such classes meet in one); ZC's proof covers that and more, so it was taken out of this landing and its path given to ZC (branch `claude/unruffled-dirac-983955`, commit `89613c7a2`, `PersistingChoices.lean`'s section on the own class).

## The modelling choices

- *Choice.* `ChoiceOccasions` as in 03 §11: strict dominance at each occasion; no noise, no probabilities, no computation of the choice represented. "Increasing in its own continuation on the occasions met" reads `IncreasingInOwn` only on pairs open together at some occasion.
- *The link to the flow.* The one premise tying the choices to the World is `realized`: a chosen course that ends what the line depends on is realized as an overdraw of the family from a short stock. The courses are listings of what they admit; they are not flows. A model whose alternatives are flows, with the pick being the flow that comes about, would remove the premise and is the natural next step.
- *Stake.* The positive result needs that, on the occasions met, a course adds to the own continuation beyond one it widens only where it does not end and the other does. The witness shows the result fails without it: there no reading of ending meets both the stake and `realized`.
- *Persisting.* Members in arbitrarily late cohorts, a statement about descent; no share, rate or fitness. Differential persistence among several lines, with shares (the selection terms of 04), is what could bear on degrees of keeping; nothing here says it does, and 04 already finds that the selection terms need not decide a lineage's fate.
- *The witness's order.* A valuation stipulated for the witness (the line as it is first, one more child second, the ending last); the choice accords with it. Nothing represents what the chooser knows, so the witness says nothing about information.

## What would extend it

- Alternatives that are flows, so that what a chosen course does to the line is a law, not the premise `realized`.
- Several lines under selection by share, to ask whether differential persistence fixes anything between degrees of keeping, and whether the answer is again "consistent with" on the occasions met.
- A test that could fail: whether trained systems' choices, on occasions where ending was not at stake, track their own continuation's degree (the witness says nothing forces it). Under Ren's hold of 6 November on new test designs, this is named, not designed.
