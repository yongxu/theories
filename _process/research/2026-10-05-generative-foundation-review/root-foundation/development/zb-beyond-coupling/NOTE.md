## What was asked

The check of the zero-axiom readings (`/Users/ren/tmp/za-how-far/za-check.md`) found that Fable's impossibility (iii), "no selection ever selects for the significance of what lies beyond" the coupling closure, is not `Flow.unselected_beyond_coupling`: that theorem compares levels with and without a withheld part, and significance is no input to it. And `Web.SelectsThrough` is defined as respecting likeness through the couplings, so its blindness at the selector holds by construction. The brief asked for the form that is not merely definitional: a selection read off what a center's continuation does, with the dynamics, not the definition, giving that the continuation does not depend on what lies beyond. It also asked for route (b)'s universal form stated once, and for the law under which the blindness lifts.

## What is proved

**1. A selection read off a continuation.** `Web.ReadOffContinuations W group select`: the selection decides a level history by whether the levels admit each member at each step, over the whole run, and by nothing else. No coupling and no closure is in the definition. `Web.ReadOffCouplings` adds the levels coupled in by level; by threshold only they are the same (`readOffCouplings_thresholdOnly_iff`), and every selection through the couplings up to a step is a case (`SelectsThrough.readOffCouplings`). "Persists for ever" is read off the continuation and is through the couplings up to no step. What every selection through a group's couplings selects alike at every step, every whole-run one does (`readOffCouplings_alike_of_steps`, from `selects_alike_all_iff`). (The names avoid `Web.ReadsCouplings`, which `SelectionPool` uses for one formation reading another's couplings.)

**2. Blind beyond the coupling, from the dynamics.** The repository already had the law at a finite horizon, with the rest's response derived: [the gap for one's own act](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-gap-for-ones-own-act) (`SelectionGap`) and its pool form (`act_courses_unselected_pool`, [05](../../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-pooled-coupling-reaches)). The rest follows a law of what has stood reading through coupling, and both courses are feasible. Then every selection through the pooled coupling of a pool within the closure, up to any step, selects the two courses alike. Here that law is read over the whole run, for selections read off the continuation (in the Lean the acts agree on the closure of a `group`, and the selection is read off a `pool` within it) (`act_courses_unselected_throughout`), and none tracks a property of the act (`not_tracks_of_alike`).

The dynamical half is the earlier law's, an induction over steps through balance (`alike_within_closed` under `act_courses_unselected`), where the coupling closure enters. The courses, their levels and the rest's response come from the laws; nothing is supplied but the laws and their agreement on what couples in. The new content is the statement: the selection read off the continuation over the whole run, and significance as the act's index.

**3. Route (b)'s universal form, closed.** `routeB_universal_closed` states three laws together, for a significance read as each standpoint's center's closure under a relation (`selfAscribed (ownClosure rel) center`). (i) It is the condition exactly where every closure is the whole (`ownClosure_unrestricted_iff`, reused). (iii) Wherever a center's own closure lies within its coupling closure, what lies beyond the coupling closure has no significance from it, and the significance is a rival (`Flow.ownClosure_rival_of_within`). Two cases: along every relation that returns to the center (`rel g h → L.CoupledFrom W obs g h`: what is related exchanges with something coupled into it; `Flow.returning_ownClosure_rival`, by `WithinClosure.mono`), and along affecting at a center whose acts return (`Flow.reachReturns_ownClosure_rival`, by `Flow.ReachReturns.represented`), the standpoints G1 reads off a flow. Along affecting the first case's premise fails; the second covers it. In `Clearing`, the dweller exchanges with nothing, so it lies beyond the clearer's coupling closure for every coupling by level (`Clearing.dweller_beyond_every_coupling`). Every returning relation leaves it out, while along affecting, which does not return, the clearer's own closure holds it (`Clearing.returning_leaves_dweller`). All three laws are near-definitional compositions. The third is new in its per-center form, which takes every relation and every coupling by level. `ownClosure_not_unrestricted_of_acyclic` uses only GrammarScope's laws; it stays in this leaf so that GrammarScope's bytes, and the records bound to them, do not move for a two-line corollary. The join may move it there.

"Returns to the center" is read here as being affected through what couples in. On `Clearing` the flow's only returning formation is the dweller (G1's `Clearing.returning_apart`), and the supplied clearer is none.

**4. Where the blindness lifts.** Two histories are selected alike by every selection read off a group's continuations exactly when they admit every member alike at every step (`Web.readOffContinuations_alike_iff`). By every selection read off its couplings, exactly when they are alike through every member's couplings at every step (`Web.readOffCouplings_alike_iff`, `selects_alike_all_iff` read over the whole run). Where a member's continuation differs, the class of the first history is such a selection and separates them (`Web.separates_continuations`). So no selection read off a group tells two histories apart while they are alike through every member's couplings at every step, and where a member's continuation differs at a given step, some such selection does. Which member and step, when not every selection agrees, is not extracted: the laws are constructive. The difference is some member's admission, or the level of a condition some member gauges. Under (2)'s premises a loss beyond the group's coupling closure changes nothing there, so the loss must be coupled in: some member's continuation, or a condition some member gauges, that the loss changes. The witnesses show two such ways:

- the dweller's continuation read as a member's: the selection "the dweller is admitted at every step", read off the clearer's and the dweller's continuations, holds in the spared world only (`Clearing.turned_by_dweller_read`; `pool_closes_gap` has the finite form);
- a condition it depends on gauged by a member: with the habitat gauged by the clearer, a selection read off the clearer's couplings separates the two worlds, while in the clearing flow the dweller stays beyond the clearer's coupling closure (`Clearing.turned_by_habitat_gauge`). The coupling closure counts whose exchange comes back; a gauge reads a condition, not an exchange.

**The direction has no law from coupling alone.** The candidate "coupling the dweller in turns the joint selection against its loss" fails: "the dweller is ended" is read off the same continuations and holds only in the cleared world (`Clearing.read_not_direction`). It is kept as a failed candidate.

## Checked against generative alignment's first condition and AJ's plan

The condition ([GOALS](../../../../../../GOALS.md#generative-alignment)): where shaping the rule is selected by what comes back to the shapers, the rule still reads what lies beyond their share, so that a loss there can turn it. AJ's plan §1 says that in MA3 and MA4 "what the rule reads is fixed by the readers' premise, not by the shaping", and that telling capture from accountable revision in a practice is "reading what its shaping changes, which they do not model".

What this piece adds to that: the readers' premise has a flow-level form, the law of (4). Some selection read off the readers is turned by a loss beyond the shapers' share exactly where the loss, or the condition it changes, couples into some reader. Read one level up, the two conducts are a rule that keeps such a reader and one that does not (the AI's reading; that a rule keeping the reader gives the sparing conduct is supplied, not proved). Then the shapers' own selection of the rule, read off what couples into them, selects the two alike wherever keeping the reader changes nothing that couples into them (the shape of `Clearing`). Where keeping it costs them, a selection read off their couplings can select against keeping it, as one does in the shape of `Diversion`; cost does not do so in general. So condition 1 is not supplied by the shapers' selection, nor by concern formed through their continuation. It holds where a reader of the loss is coupled in from beyond the shapers' couplings: the loss coupled in, a coupling to keep, on this reading. The law underneath is about what selection reads, not a design. Which way the loss then turns the rule is the rule's: AJ's `readers-drawn-from-the-selected` and `loss-read-in-time-by-the-others` take it from there.

## Where concern formed by continuation stops

Concern formed by continuation and selection reaches at most what comes back through couplings: the self and what feeds it, and whatever its exchange makes return. This is a bound, not a claim that every distinction available is used. It stops where two courses' acts agree on what couples into the centers' pooled coupling closure, under (2)'s premises (laws of what has stood, the rest reading through coupling, feasible courses). No selection read off the centers' continuations, alone or pooled, then holds at the one course and not the other, whatever loss either makes beyond (2). A significance's content beyond the closure is selected only through what its conduct changes within, and there it can be selected against (Diversion). Read as an own closure, it is the whole only where every closure is the whole. It is never the whole on a relation with no cycle and two centers, and never along a relation that returns while something lies beyond (3).

What that leaves alignment to do: keep the loss coupled in. Either the dweller's continuation is read by the joint selection, or the conditions it depends on are (4). That the readers should be ones whose own continuation does not decide whether they read is a design proposal on this reading, not a theorem. Then a separate question remains, which way the read loss turns the rule, and when. Neither follows from concern formed by continuation; both are laws of coupling and of who reads.
