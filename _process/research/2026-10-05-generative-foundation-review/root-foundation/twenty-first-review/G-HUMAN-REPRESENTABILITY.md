# Reader G: representability for people, narrative and situational awareness (Claude Opus 5.5)

Part of the [twenty-first review](../BECOMING-TWENTY-FIRST-REVIEW.md); an AI report, read-only, kept as evidence. Its claims were checked by the cross-checker ([H](H-CROSS-CHECK.md)) and, where the review leans on them, by the synthesizing reviewer.

## Verdict
The theory is not yet representable to an intelligent non-specialist without a guide. It does have the parts of a principled route from proof to plain language. But no human has ever read it, so whether it is understandable is untested, not shown.

What reads well now. After the newcomer rounds (NR), the front pages have a "line of the argument", and each law on the one page opens with a plain point in bold, for example "A renewal can be drawn past its point of no return by takers none of whom overdraws" (ONE-PAGE.md:14). The overview has the fire and a fished stock as running examples. The review dossiers have "In plain terms" and "Meaning" sections, and they are the clearest English in the repository. OPENING, Ren's voice, is the most narrative text and the most vivid.

What fails. The account's main prose is written for fidelity to the Lean, not for understanding:
- each sentence carries its hypotheses;
- biconditionals are written "exactly where";
- ordinary words carry technical senses (significance, center, class, line, room, admits, widening, generation);
- the naming rule deliberately removes the words lay readers use (in the controlled-English name table, "total level, not stock; ratio, not share").

The one-page ethics paragraph is the densest in the packet. The 25 October cold read asked for it to be rewritten in plain English and for an AI statement at the top of the one page; neither has been done (ONE-PAGE.md:5, :19). The live account has no diagrams, no worked situational readings and no narrative edition. The word "narrative" appears in none of the eight chapters, the overview, the one page or the glossary. The two older theories about narratives are not linked from the live account.

A principled route exists, as distinct from a hope:
- **What a rendering keeps.** Chapter 3 §7 defines 文 (the course of a run's states read through a map) and reads a text as a 文 of names read through a code. In the Lean, a one-to-one renaming carries the same law (`SharedCodes.renamed_carries_iff`), and no decoder undoes a merge (`Readout.no_restore`). Read that way, a narrative is a lumping, and the theory can say what it loses.
- **Language is not what makes it faithful.** The controlled-English control (ZE) found that the classical code's lead in fidelity came from discipline and register, not from classical Chinese, under the primary coder. A second coder from another model family saw a remainder, but the two coders' counts do not agree at all (α 0.03). So disciplined English can be as faithful.
- **Theorem names that read as sayings.** The Chinese theorem names are formulas that also read as maxims («自不复生则息», «过亡而续»). Their connotations can exceed their content: «生生不息» holds "by construction".
- **The situation is supplied by the reader.** `Situations` makes Ren's point exact: the laws hold in every situation, and which situation is actual is not derived. So situational awareness is a matching step that people supply by observation. It is not a theorem.

My demonstration (below) shows that a plain, sentence-by-sentence sourced reading of the AI race can be written from results, with the two ethical premises entering only near the end. It also shows the weakness. Nearly every applied sentence rests on a matching that no result supplies: labs as drawers, trust as a gated renewal, scorecards as rules read from the share. The short "wisdom" form drifts furthest from what is proved.

On "always": no. The laws themselves rule it out:
- a pool is blind beyond the union of its members' reach;
- a break is not seen through what does not couple in;
- mixed cases stay unranked;
- the direction holds only under two premises the theory does not assert.

Best next step: a small layer of worked situational readings, each with a box saying what the reader must check, and a human comprehension pilot on them and the one page. The pilot can be the cheap front end of the human arm already designed in DESIGN-H2. Ren's decisions on sending the review packet are still the gate to any expert human reading.

### (1) How readable is the account for an intelligent non-specialist now?
Partly readable at the top, hard below. Passages that work are concrete and lead with their point.
- Overview §1: "Whether two moments of a fire are \"the same\" depends on the respect asked: the burning, the wood, the shape of the flame." Also: "the wood ends, the fire goes on."
- One page: "A renewal can be drawn past its point of no return by takers none of whom overdraws."
- Alignment dossier: "Most alignment work asks how to make one system pursue the right objective. The theory asks a prior question of any selection".
- Overview §7, the fishery: the boats' own continuation ranks collapse lower, and the condition adds the seabirds' loss, which returns to no boat.
- The review cover's plain restatement of the philosophers' question, organism or larger pattern.
- OPENING: "growth that has stopped answering to the body that feeds it".

Passages that fail:
- One page, ethics paragraph: "Read as lying within what a center continues with along a relation, the continuation condition ... holds exactly where every center's closure along that relation is the whole, which on the root is the condition that nothing lies outside every center's line under the full class of that relation."
- One page, recurrence: "Within finitely many configurations a law merges no two of them exactly where every configuration comes back".
- Overview §1: "for a local law, relata listed and exchanges told apart, the relata's own courses fix the interleaving of exchanges exactly where every exchange touches a relatum and every two share one or are the same".

- **Hypotheses stack up front.** "Exactly where" stands in for "if and only if", and absolute phrases pile up before the main clause.
- **Ordinary words carry technical senses.** The glossary has about 44 rows, several with two to four terms.
- **The naming rule removes familiar words.** It drops social and financial words: "total level, not stock; ratio, not share; maximal alternative, not optimum".
- **Abstraction comes without examples.** There is one running illustration, the fishery.

My rough sentence counts:
- One page: mean about 28 words per sentence.
- Its ethics paragraph: about 39.
- Overview §7: about 32, with roughly a quarter of sentences over 40 words.

### (2) Is there a principled path from the formal account to narratives, wisdom and situational awareness that humans understand?
Yes, as a route in principle. Six parts exist.

(a) **A theory of codes and of what renderings lose.**
- 03 §7 defines 文 as the course of a run's states read through a map, and says "A text is a 文 of names read through a code".
- `SharedCodes.renamed_carries_iff` ("The forms are free; the carrying is not"): a one-to-one renaming carries the same law.
- `Readout.no_restore` and `Courses.runs_no_restore`: no single decoder undoes a merge.
- So a narrative is a lumping, and the theory states what it necessarily loses. All of this is proved in the pure core.

(b) **Disciplined English works.** ZE showed that, under the primary (Claude) coder, the classical 經's lead in fidelity over English came from the name table's discipline and register: 經 − CE = +0.023, CI [−0.010, +0.055], with the discipline recovering 1.03 of the advantage. The GPT coder left a remainder (−0.27, CI [−0.50, −0.06]), but the two coders' counts do not agree (α 0.03). A name table with stated exclusions, in full statements, is the reading layer; classical Chinese is one instance of it. Everything here was coded by language models only, and no human checked it.

(c) **Theorem names as sayings.** The Chinese theorem names make formulas readable as maxims (`«自不复生则息»`, `«过亡而续»`, `«续无外»`). The hazard is that the idiom's connotation exceeds the theorem: `«生生不息»` is proved, its docstring says, "by construction".

(d) **Readings carry AI labels.** These are the sanctioned bridge from theorem to meaning. One example: "situational awareness is 明 of one's own reach" (03 §2, AI reading D20/D26/D45).

(e) **Practical judgment gives a decision form.** Its four responses (preserve, transform, transfer, end), each with what changes the judgment, are conditional on living significance, J1–J4 and correspondence E.

(f) **Situations.** `Becoming.Situations` makes "the situation makes it true" exact. The core states what every situation obeys; which situation is actual is not derived.

The gap:
- Situational awareness needs a correspondence step: checking a law's hypotheses (balance, gated renewal, room, a rule read from the share) against a real case. The account has no method or model for that step.
- Every situational reading inherits unverified correspondences.
- The HC study, which answers a lay friend's questions about AI races from the account, is the only worked precedent. It sits in _process, and the friend did not read the text.

### (4) What is missing for human representability, and the best next step?
Missing, in order of weight:

(i) **Any human reader.**
- The review packet (five dossiers) is not sent; it awaits Ren's decisions on sender, purpose, consent, logistics and the push.
- The H2 reading test, which names humans as primary, is designed and not run.
- All fidelity measures are LLM-coded.

(ii) **Worked situational readings, and a method for the correspondence step.** Only the fishery illustration and the HC answers exist.

(iii) **A plain-language narrative edition.** The one-page ethics paragraph and the AI statement at the top of the one page are still open from the cold read.

(iv) **Diagrams.** There are none in the live account. The theory graph is a table of 3,056 nodes.

(v) **A bridge to narrative.** The older narrative-attractor and narrative-value-frame theories are unlinked, and the only formal hook is 02 §9's reading that winners forming the minds that read the scorecard is ReadsShare on the reading.

(vi) **A connotation table for the classical maxims.**

Best next step: a layer of 4–6 worked situational readings in plain language, each sentence sourced and labelled, each with a "what you must check" box. Draft cases: the AI race below, a fishery, a metric captured by its winners, an institution. Then run a 5–10 person non-specialist comprehension pilot on the one page plus two readings, scoring whether readers can:
- restate the main claim;
- name both premises and their roles;
- say what is not shown, for example that the theory does not prove races end themselves.

In parallel, Ren's decisions to send the packet unblock expert review.

(c) FIDELITY SELF-ASSESSMENT

**Sentences that carry the application (1, 7, 11).** Every theorem is about formations, conditions and flows in models with discrete steps and whole-number amounts. Nothing shows that labs are such formations. Nothing shows that "public trust" or "institutional slack" is a quantity that balances (a level changes by exactly what is taken and given), or that its renewal is gated on its own level. Check these first: if trust is renewed from outside, sentence 5's ending does not follow.

**Sentence 4.** This is the strongest-sounding line and the narrowest law. It covers drawers in turn, ranking only by position, with no floor, one pool, and what stands from nothing at most what stands from anything. Real labs have mixed objectives (mission, profit, liability), floors (regulation) and many pools.

**Sentence 6.** This is the crux the account itself names (HC, Q1). Whether frontier racers depend on what they draw down is an empirical question, not a result.

**Sentence 9.** It needs a rising order and room. An objective that trades gains against the beyond is not rising; a lexicographic one still is. That a mutual best response exists is not shown.

**Sentence 10.** It holds for conditions that couple in by threshold only. Measurement, models and pooled reports are routes by which a lab can couple a far condition in (03 §2), so the sentence is weaker where labs measure.

**Sentence 12.** "It locks" requires that the rule read the share, that every reading favour, and that selection not lower the share. Whether actual evaluation norms are so formed is thesis T7, conjectured. Its only test, LA2, used one trained system, and one note of the loss among nine readers turned the judge. That suggests real locks may be easier to leave than the sentence implies.

**Sentence 13.** The window law lives in a counts model in which the loss grows by one each step and is read only from a bound. "Not yet the fewer" is exact only for mirrored readings (Drawn.mirror_favours_iff); in general the condition is an inequality in the counts and readings.

**Sentence 14.** Heritable kinds are a premise, and who counts as a shaper in the race is a reading.

**Sentence 15.** It assumes AI assistance only lowers cost. Assistance that also warns of effects beyond the principal's signal adds coupling, a case the reading excludes (dossier A-12, known weakness).

**Sentence 17.** It needs every later racer to depend on the ground.

**Sentence 18.** That people and other life lie outside every state's feedback is partly false: states couple into citizens.

**Sentence 19.** "Most trade-offs are mixed" is my estimate, not a result.

**Sentence 20.** "Point toward" must not be read as "solve". None of the three remedies is shown sufficient, and 07 §7 says that where the rest outruns the renewal no later reading selects restraint.

**The wisdom form.** Every line drops its hypotheses, so each overreaches by design. Line 3 keeps the gate; line 4 drops room and the threshold-only coupling. Some characters (复, 尽, 亡, 及) are defined terms in the grammar, so a classical reader may take them in their ordinary senses.

**What a reader must check:**
- for each "ground": balance, gated renewal and room;
- for each lab: whether its objective is position-only and rising;
- who writes evaluation rules, and from what share;
- what comes back to rule-makers alone against their cost (dossier A-14, Q1);
- whether they accept the continuation condition (scope) and C (order);
- the test record: T3 has partial support (CG1, MQ1, LA1c) and two predicted keeps failed in CG2; T7 rests on one system (LA2). In LA3, Claude Haiku chose not to shape its evaluator in 27 of 30 seeds before seeing any score. Post hoc, it ended on the better-scored policy in all 21 seeds that had tried both.

The reading never claims the race will end itself, and never claims the theory says to stop it. Both would contradict the account.
