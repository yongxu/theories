# Cross-model review X2: alignment, the meta-attractor, and whether the collective can always move toward generation

Part of the [twenty-first review](../BECOMING-TWENTY-FIRST-REVIEW.md); a cross-model AI report (GPT astra, high effort, read-only, sandboxed), returned verbatim with its run footer (its absolute links made repository-relative, line suffixes dropped), kept as evidence. Its brief is in [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).

The theory can contribute to alignment as a **framework for analysing whether a collective preserves the conditions needed to detect harm, change its rules, and carry out correction**. It does not establish that intelligence, consciousness, sufficient observation, or acceptance of its ethics guarantees a generative future. Its strongest results are conditional mathematical statements; its weakest connection is from those statements to actual learning and institutional behaviour. Several experiments expose that weakness directly. The useful contribution is the composition of selection, shared dependence, rule formation, and funded correction—not a demonstrated escape from collective destructive dynamics. [Conditional direction](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md); [collective correction](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md).

**1. The alignment contribution is substantive, but much of its mathematical content restates established mechanisms. Severity: medium.**

The strongest organizing question is: **what selects the selectors, and what maintains their ability to change?** This connects AI objectives to evaluators, operators, institutional reproduction, shared resources, and correction costs. It applies to humans and AI without requiring either to be malicious. However, collective alignment is already an established research subject; Dafoe et al.’s *Open Problems in Cooperative AI* explicitly includes human–machine cooperation, institutions, game theory, and social choice. I checked its abstract. [Cooperative AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08630); [repository’s joint-selection framing](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md).

**2. The meta-attractor is a conditional feedback mechanism, not a universal destination. Severity: medium.**

In the current formal account, successful variants reproduce; their prevalence influences who reads and sets the selection rule; those readers favour the same variants; losses outside that feedback can accumulate without reversing it. MA3 adds selection for the capacity to shape the rule itself. This is a precise model of self-reinforcing selection over selection. Calling its carriers conscious is an **AI reading**, not something the mathematics establishes. [MA2 study](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md); [capture laws](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md).

The **pure-core theorem** `RuleShaping.favours_iff` says shaping is weakly favoured exactly when its exclusive return covers its represented cost. That follows from supplied offspring counts, inherited kinds, and the allocation rule. Shared benefits cancel from the one-step comparison, although they can change the verdict across a delayed stretch. This is a useful distinction, not a derivation of institutional behaviour from ontology. [MA3 premises and boundaries](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ma3-shaping-the-rule/study_ma3.txt).

MA4 and MA4b show conditional escape and conditional failure: critical mass changes which way selection runs; a loss-sensitive reading can reverse it; under the specified family of readings, delay can close that opportunity. **These are restrictions on particular readings, not proof that every possible intervention becomes powerless.** Their late response, reader weights, inherited kinds, and update lag are supplied. [MA4b §§2–5](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ma4b-read-in-time/NOTE.md).

The older covariance/PDI narrative should not substitute for these results. Its own audit identifies unresolved assumptions and withdraws the eigenvalue budget as an actionable governance specification. [Historical audit](../../../../../theories/meta-attractor/README.md).

**3. “Can we always ensure a generative direction?” No—even with greatly improved observation. Severity: high.**

Three claims must remain separate:

- **Always guaranteed:** unsupported, and false across the theory’s admitted models.
- **Always available as a direction:** a normative orientation can remain meaningful; an attainable widening action need not exist.
- **Likely under conditions:** plausible in some settings, but requires empirical evidence about those conditions and intervention success.

The first two conclusions follow from the following boundaries; the third is not currently supplied by the trained-system tests.

**Information does not create reach.** `SelectsThrough` requires indistinguishable coupled histories to produce indistinguishable selections. A collective can pool information, but information available to one member need not influence the collective decision. Conversely, a warranted model can reveal an unobserved condition: the result does not require direct sensing of everything. “Enough situationality” must specify which distinctions reach which decisions. [Knowing §1](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md); [pooling and outvoting](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md).

**Knowing danger does not supply a feasible recovery.** Under specified renewal gates and resource dynamics, levels can enter an unrecoverable region. A beneficial institutional alternative can also fail because its transition consumes support before returning any. Critical mass and timely financing are action constraints, not merely knowledge deficits. [Renewal](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md); [points of no return](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md); [supported transitions](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md).

**Advance warning requires a connection to what fails.** T6 distinguishes a threshold condition from evidence that identifies it. The periphery’s slowing-down calculations require their stochastic model; stock statistics alone need not identify an unknown need threshold. This defeats a universal warning claim, not every useful warning method. [Knowing §10](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md).

**Self-description has limits, but bounded safety remains possible.** `Diagonal.gap_unsaid` concerns a specified family of self-readings. It does not imply that every safety invariant requires complete self-knowledge. The repository also constructs conditional correction and preservation mechanisms. [Self-reading](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md); [conditional correction](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).

**Positional competition and generation are different comparisons.** The position arithmetic is zero-sum; common increases can leave positions unchanged while increasing total amounts. But real scarcity and incompatible requirements remain. Neither relational ontology nor recognition of mutual dependence guarantees an available improvement for everyone. [Position](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md); [joint feasibility](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md).

The positive statement is narrower: **given feasible preserving alternatives, adequate observations or warranted models, effective decision authority, sufficient transition support, and maintained implementation conditions, particular collective courses can be guaranteed within the represented disturbances.** The theory provides instances and failure boundaries for such guarantees. It supplies no theorem that those conditions can always be obtained or maintained. “A collective of consciousness” adds no proved exception.

**4. The ethics provides scope and direction through premises; it does not generate agreement or compliance. Severity: high.**

The continuation condition says whose continuation has significance. **C supplies the ordering commitment that widening is better.** Neither follows merely from the resource and selection laws. `Unrestricted` already quantifies over everything, so its instantiations do not independently establish universal moral scope. Mixed changes remain unordered without further commitments. [Continuation condition](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md); [scope and order](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).

GS4 and GS5 establish conditional reach through shared conditions and classes of continuation. PW then asks which class counts as one’s own continuation. These results do not force someone who values a finite organism to identify with every downstream effect, nor turn causal involvement into moral motivation. The current prose retains that distinction. [Shared conditions and classes](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md); [PW examination](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/pw-step-forward/NOTE.md).

Likewise, `Direction.widening_selected_by_pool` does not produce an implemented, politically accepted discovery procedure. Practical judgment adds commitments about priorities, responsibility, legitimate choice, and feasibility. These are needed precisely where actual institutional decisions involve losses. [Practical judgment and conditional direction](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).

**5. AJ’s “control is not selected” is an unjustified general alignment criterion. Severity: high.**

AJ defines alignment partly by requiring the capacity to shape rules not to be selected. But MA3 distinguishes shapers by reproductive returns; it contains no predicate establishing that their shaping damages the whole. **Beneficial, accountable rule revision can also require rewarding and reproducing the capacity to perform it.** The theorem therefore does not justify making suppression of that capacity a general alignment requirement. It justifies examining who receives the returns, who bears the costs, and what their shaping changes. [AJ condition 1](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md); [MA3’s explicit “no evaluation” boundary](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ma3-shaping-the-rule/study_ma3.txt).

Two neighbouring claims also overreach:

- Calling instrumental convergence the one-agent failure of this condition conflates preserving resources under a fixed objective with acquiring control over the selection rule.
- Rejecting “a single scalar of any kind” exceeds the optimum theorem. Its harmful conclusion needs a rising order and room; the same chapter gives protective possibilities using plateaus, constraints, and secondary criteria.

These are problems in the coordinating interpretation, not defects demonstrated in Lean. [AJ framing](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md); [optimum hypotheses and countercases](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md).

LA2 is especially instructive. I checked its saved verdicts and scoring code: the automatically generated statement that one note was outweighed is marked supported by a condition that does not consult the decisive one-note evidence. The README correctly rejects that interpretation. **Frozen scoring is not itself validation of the intended construct.** [Scoring code](../../../../../experiments/self-rewarding-lock/analyze.py); [saved verdicts](../../../../../experiments/self-rewarding-lock/results/VERDICTS.json).

These are experiments with a pretrained model making **in-context choices**, not tests of weight-training convergence, mesa-objective formation, long-run institutional adaptation, or general corrigibility. Small condition-specific samples and one model family further limit generalization. They support some local behavioural predictions and identify failed correspondences; they do not establish the alignment programme.

**The strongest hostile objections**

- **“The hypotheses contain the result.”** Partly answered. Selection’s indistinguishability condition and several ordering results are definitional or near-definitional; the resource and timing compositions add consequences. The unresolved scientific question is whether independently measured systems satisfy those hypotheses. LA2 demonstrates why that matters.
- **“Widening hides the evaluator’s values in the representation.”** Not fully answered. Explicit C and unresolved mixed comparisons avoid pretending to derive a complete morality, but selecting admissions, identities, timescales, and relevant losses still requires justification. [Scope and order](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).
- **“Your remedy needs an incorruptible guardian.”** Recognized, not solved universally. The correction account names truthful correspondence, legitimate authority, available reserves, and successor cooperation as conditions. Their continued preservation remains an additional problem. [Correction dependence](../../../../../theories/meta-attractor/COLLECTIVE-DESTRUCTIVE-ATTRACTORS.md).
- **“This is established theory translated into Lean.”** Substantially true for individual mechanisms. The potential research contribution is their exact composition and discriminating empirical predictions. Formal verification alone does not establish novelty or correspondence.

**What I would do next, ranked**

1. **For alignment researchers: hand over one falsifiable study of when oversight can still change its own selection rule.** Package MA2–MA4b with explicit correspondence tests, the LA2 failure, and competing models of evaluator weighting. Estimate those weights independently, then predict unseen trajectories. Compare against ordinary adaptive evaluators and known side-effect safeguards. This is more valuable now than presenting the whole theory as an alignment solution.
2. **For human institutions: develop one funded, independently usable correction route.** Specify who can detect harm, change rules, execute transition, and draw on reserves; test simultaneous obligations rather than certifying each against the same funds. Protect beneficial rule revision while preventing private capture. This is a practical design proposal grounded in the correction-dependence results, not a theorem that institutions will adopt it.
3. **Repair AJ’s governing criterion before expanding its claims.** Distinguish harmful capture from beneficial shaping, fixed-objective resource seeking from endogenous rule control, and normative direction from feasible action. Preserve the failed experiments as constraints on the next model.
