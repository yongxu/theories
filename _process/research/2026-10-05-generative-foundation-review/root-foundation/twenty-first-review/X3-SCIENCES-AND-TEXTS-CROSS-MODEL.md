# Cross-model review X3: how the sciences and the wisdom traditions map onto the theory

Part of the [twenty-first review](../BECOMING-TWENTY-FIRST-REVIEW.md); a cross-model AI report (GPT astra, high effort, read-only, sandboxed), returned verbatim with its run footer (its absolute links made repository-relative, line suffixes dropped), kept as evidence. Its brief is in [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md).

The repository currently offers a **formal synthesis of selected scientific structures and an explicitly interpretive reconstruction of classical texts**, rather than a theory containing the sciences in full. UE0’s verdict needs one correction: it reaches farther than **some isolated mathematical formulations after adding premises**, not farther than every framework it discusses. I found no established new physical or biological prediction. The strongest contributions are precise conditional connections and counterexamples that limit the theory’s own ambitions—especially the conflict between widening future possibilities and preserving continuation. The detailed chapters generally acknowledge these limits; the programme’s language of “containment” remains stronger than several bridges warrant. [UE plan §1–§3](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue-unified-explanation/PLAN.md), [UE0, Summary](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue0-third-parts/AUDIT.md), [CausalGrowth.lean:1051](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/CausalGrowth.lean).

This was a read-only source review. “Core theorem” below means a theorem present in the pure-core source, with its stated hypotheses; I did not independently compile it or reproduce its axiom report. A logical proof does not verify its scientific correspondence. The repository itself makes that distinction in its [premise card](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md).

1. **Thermodynamics: restatement with substantial physical debt. Severity: high.**

   **First law:** the bridge is a conditional translation, not a derivation of conservation. `balances_iff_unproduced` and `balances_of_conserved` require local accounting and a correspondence between real physical quantities and counted levels. Total conservation alone is correctly shown insufficient. These are **periphery theorems**, reporting `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. Their scope is accurately stated in [01 §8.4](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md).

   **Second law:** `Flow.DissipatesOn` explicitly requires each participating taker to return at least one whole unit less than it takes. The stock bound then follows by summation. This is stronger than merely nonnegative entropy production. The physical bridge additionally assumes positive production for the relevant exchanges and whole-unit available work; it also requires no entropy production inside stores for the stated balance correspondence. These are consequential modelling premises, not deductions from “becoming.” [Dissipation.lean:240](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Dissipation.lean), [`Balances.takers_within`:404](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Dissipation.lean), [01 §8.4](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md).

   **Landauer and Bennett:** classification: **restatement of finite information-accounting structures; debt for physical thermodynamics**. `dissipatesOn_iff_conserved` makes dissipation equivalent to conservation after adding a positively funded record. It does not establish that actual erasure supplies that record cost. The distinction matters: Sagawa explicitly allows logically irreversible computation to be thermodynamically reversible in the quasistatic limit. I checked his abstract, not the complete derivation. [Entropy.lean:815](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Entropy.lean), [Sagawa, *Thermodynamic and Logical Reversibilities Revisited*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.1886).

   **Prigogine’s dissipative structures:** classification: **restatement of a necessary resource condition; debt for structure formation**. Showing that sustained activity requires supply under dissipation does not derive an instability, select a spatial pattern, or predict its amplitude. The repository’s own audit calls this a known exclusion under a stronger least-loss premise. Its halving counterexample correctly prevents finite-time rest from being inferred from strict loss alone. [UE0 §2.11](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue0-third-parts/AUDIT.md), [`Halving.approaches_without_reaching`](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/LeastLoss.lean).

2. **Poincaré recurrence and quantum mechanics: exact restricted mathematics, not physical containment. Severity: medium.**

   **Poincaré recurrence:** **restatement**, with the measure-theoretic theorem remaining a debt. `Law.lossless_iff_returns` assumes a list containing every configuration and a deterministic self-map. It proves the finite permutation result. That is not the full recurrence theorem for finite-measure, measure-preserving dynamics, whose return is generally approximate and almost everywhere. The source declaration and prose agree on the finite scope. [Recurrence.lean:689](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Recurrence.lean).

   `Irreversibility.same_change_different_return` supplies a useful but elementary warning: the same observed transition can belong to laws with different return possibilities. It is not a thermodynamic arrow theorem. [Irreversibility.lean:40](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Irreversibility.lean).

   The requested quantum spot-check gives the same pattern. `Square.no_context_free_values` genuinely rules out a global Boolean assignment compatible with the supplied Mermin–Peres observables. Integer matrices suffice for this witness. It does not derive quantum dynamics or the Born rule. This is a **core realization of a known contextuality result**, not quantum theory contained. [Quantum.lean:464](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Quantum.lean).

3. **Free-energy principle and active inference: partial restatement, still substantial debt. Severity: high.**

   UE1’s actual bridge is a simplified chooser, not the free-energy principle’s stochastic dynamics. `ChoiceCountCapture.selectsThrough`, `counted_locks`, and `counted_outlives_loss` formalize observation-limited selection and reinforcement of executed choices. The note acknowledges that its contrasting posterior-counting rule is not the cited implementation’s update rule. Its claim to a new exclusion was withdrawn. [UE1 §3–§4](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue1-fep-bridge/NOTE.md).

   UE2 adds **core counting identities**: restriction as conditioning, cross-multiplied Bayes, and weighted exit bounds. Reciprocal probability preserves the ordering of pointwise surprisal; it does not replace logarithms in entropy or variational free energy. The real-valued variational bound is described as a **periphery draft without a receipt**, and the exit bound contains no recognition density. These are material debts, not cosmetic differences. [UE2 §4–§6](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue2-conditional-weights/NOTE.md).

   UE1b provides an important correction: homeostatic preferences need not drive unlimited accumulation. `Optimum.optima_iff` characterizes possible optima at an attainable set point; `counted_after_most` shows that a secondary criterion can preserve the unobserved remainder there. Drain below the set point additionally needs the room condition. These declarations support the revised prose, not an unrestricted claim that all optimization drains everything beyond observation. [SetPointOptimum.lean:340](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SetPointOptimum.lean), [:400](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SetPointOptimum.lean), [:502](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SetPointOptimum.lean).

4. **Constructor theory and causal sets: the representations are useful, but the scientific frameworks are not contained. Severity: high.**

   **Constructor theory:** classify as **restricted restatement plus debt**. `Tasks.Delivers` says that from each legitimate presentation **some reachable endpoint** completes the task and restores the performer’s region. It does not generally require a uniform procedure, guaranteed completion along every possible run, or a completion signal. UE12 openly acknowledges that an endlessly counting process therefore “performs” too much. [Tasks.lean:205](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Tasks.lean), [UE12, Law 1](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue12-tasks-and-what-performs-them/NOTE.md).

   `Tasks.Performs.serial`, `Catalysis.performs`, and `Catalysis.transpose_not_possible` establish composition and catalytic examples within that reading. They do not supply constructor theory’s arbitrary-accuracy and repeatability limits. Its approximation requirement is independently explicit in Marletto’s *Constructor Theory of Probability*, whose online text I checked. [UE12, Laws 2–4](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue12-tasks-and-what-performs-them/NOTE.md), [Marletto](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2015.0883).

   **Causal sets:** classify as **kinematic restatement plus physical debt**. `ordered_reach_links_iff` reconstructs precedence from links for naturally labelled, decided orders; that covers less than all locally finite orders. `birth_ordered` and `birth_holds` implement sequential growth’s kinematics. They do not establish a probability law for growth or a continuum spacetime correspondence. [UE13, Laws 1–2](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue13-causal-sets/NOTE.md).

   UE14–UE15 are stronger as **internal unification**: presence laws transfer to finite World reorderings through a supplied footprint. `precedes_iff_every_reordering` and `persists_of_present_reordering` are instance connections, not analogies. But the footprint remains supplied, and the results do not select a physical present. [UE15 §2–§3](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue15-reorderings-as-growth/NOTE.md).

5. **Population genetics: restricted containment when joined to resource laws; otherwise restatement. Severity: medium.**

   The distinctions among the biological results are mostly accurate:

   - **Price:** `price` in `Evolution.lean` is a total-accounting identity, not yet the covariance selection term. Later cohort results supply that decomposition. The module explicitly warns against confusing weighted parental totals with selection. [Evolution.lean:117](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Evolution.lean).
   - **Fisher:** `Reproduction.Breeding.response` assumes exact transmission, a supplied combination, and the normal equations. It does not establish that those breeding values exist in an actual genetic system. **Restatement under explicit assumptions.** [Reproduction.lean:1161](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Reproduction.lean).
   - **Hamilton:** `SelectionLevels.rises_iff_returns` is an exact cross-multiplied selection criterion under equal group sizes and additive own/groupmate effects. Synergy and general demographic structure remain outside it. **Restricted restatement.** [SelectionLevels.lean:914](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SelectionLevels.lean).
   - **Multilevel selection:** `levels_sized` is total covariance with size weights. `GroupEnding.Flow.coupled_end_together` adds resource-mediated joint extinction. **Restricted containment:** the added flow and threshold assumptions forbid continued reproduction that covariance bookkeeping alone leaves open. [04 §5](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md).
   - **Drift:** `Drift.fixation` proves finite weighted bounds supporting the neutral fixation-probability reading. `Replacement.fixation` concerns the count-changing Moran chain. **Restatement**, not a derivation of which biological sampling process occurs. [Drift.lean:616](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Drift.lean), [04 §6](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md).

   The strongest resource connection I inspected, `response_sq_within_inflow`, additionally assumes inherited offspring number, exact trait transmission, per-member stock bounds, balance, and nondecreasing cohort size. It bounds inflow into the carrying lineages—not external inflow into the whole. This is a legitimate conditional connection, but too specialized to advertise as a general energetic law of evolutionary speed. [SelectionPace.lean:899](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SelectionPace.lean).

6. **Cybernetics and systems thinking: mostly restatement; archetype coverage is uneven. Severity: medium.**

   **Ashby and homeostasis:** `Regulation.no_narrower_band`, `relay_threshold`, and `Flow.withinGap_iff_between` give precise discrete control results about delay, disturbance, overshoot, and settling. These are **restatements of restricted control structures**. A band-width bound does not by itself constitute a general instance theorem for Ashby’s information-theoretic requisite-variety law. [02 §8](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md).

   **Stocks and flows:** balance is accounting. **Feedback loops:** `gap_polynomial_iff_no_loop` concerns worst-case propagation under supplied stationary strength bounds; it is not a prediction that every actual loop grows exponentially. The text correctly distinguishes the bound from an individual system’s behaviour and identifies whole-number artifacts in `LoopReturn.nothing_between`. [02 §7](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md).

   For the requested archetypes:

   | Archetype | Classification |
   |---|---|
   | Tragedy of the commons | **Restricted containment:** `Flow.family_excess_ends` adds irreversible ending under gated renewal and thresholds. Against resource ecology including depensation, it is a restatement. |
   | Success to the successful | **Restatement:** counted-choice lock-in supplies one reinforcing mechanism; it is not every version of the archetype. |
   | Limits to growth | **Restatement/debt:** resource and renewal bounds are present; a general empirically identified growth-limiting dynamics is not. |
   | Escalation | **Analogy/debt:** positive-feedback bounds and positional competition supply ingredients; I did not find a dedicated equivalence theorem for the archetype in the inspected material. |

   These classifications follow the specific mechanisms, not similarity of vocabulary. [UE0, rows 7, 10, 15–16](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue0-third-parts/AUDIT.md), [UE1 §4](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue1-fep-bridge/NOTE.md). I did not independently check Meadows’ texts.

7. **UE0’s novelty verdict is broadly right, but its first half overgeneralizes. Severity: high.**

   The defensible statement is: **some joined models exclude behaviours their isolated accounting components leave unspecified; no inspected result establishes an exclusion new to the combined sciences.** This follows from the hypotheses themselves, not merely from UE0’s self-assessment. UE6’s three searches reinforce it, but a bounded search cannot prove universal absence of novelty. [UE6, Verdict](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue6-beyond-the-sciences/VERDICT.md).

   My strongest candidate for a valuable constraint is **internal**: `CausalGrowth.no_order_contains_both`. Widening the available stems favours the antichain; widening continuation favours the chain. No strict transitive order can contain both rankings. This directly limits any claim that “more possibilities” already supplies one unambiguous direction of 生生. It is an exact core theorem, but neither a new physical prediction nor established mathematical novelty. [CausalGrowth.lean:1051](../../../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/CausalGrowth.lean).

   UE7’s openness and upkeep results do not resolve that ordering problem: they constrain what maintaining widening requires, without comparing two courses. Empirical novelty remains possible, but UE6 reports mixed frozen-test outcomes, including failures against the locked-side claim. Those are **reported test results**, not independently reproduced evidence here. [08 §8](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md), [UE6, Verdict](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue6-beyond-the-sciences/VERDICT.md).

8. **The classical reconstruction is disciplined interpretation, not demonstrated historical identity. Severity: high.**

   The terms have distinct standings:

   | Term | Meaning here | Standing |
   |---|---|---|
   | 文 | A course of values read through a map; sameness means matching future courses. | Formal definition; identifying it with classical 文 is an AI reading. |
   | 理 | Lumping that respects the relevant structure and evolution; the coarsest value-preserving stable relation is especially important. | Exact mathematical construction; historical identification is interpretation. |
   | 道 | The level of inquiry into the whole unfolding; expressly not identical to one spine term. | Philosophical reading, not theorem. |
   | 生生 | Life in the whole and continuing generation; the grammar also formalizes renewal through repeated arising. | Formal properties plus an interpretive connection; ethical scope and order require separate premises. |

   `Law.core_is_sameFuture` and `Law.coarsest_iff_core` make the 文–理 connection exact **inside the chosen definitions**. They do not establish what an ancient author meant. [03 §6–§7](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md), [06 §8–§9](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md).

   The reported corpus is **4,116 units**, not necessarily independent sentences: 2,997 plus 1,119. Four AI classifiers supplied the readings; no human review is reported. The table retains 88 divergence units and 540 counter-reading units. That is evidence of documentation effort, not a validated accuracy rate. [06 §2](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md).

   Specific corrections increase confidence in the scholarship’s discipline: Xunzi’s individual identity is not reduced to location; Mohist similarity is not automatically transitive; Wang Bi’s root–branch hierarchy is not assimilated to symmetric mutual arising; and 生生 in the Changes is distinguished from excessive life-seeking in the *Laozi*. Nevertheless, `«化之二说同形»` proves equivalence of two reconstructed definitions, not equivalence of Mohist and Xunzian thought. [06 §2–§3](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md).

   **Buddhist dependent origination:** the condition relation is a formal reading, not an equivalence theorem for 缘起. The chapter correctly records that its response to impermanence and continuity resembles the opponent’s position criticized in the *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*. `«因灭而续»` supplies a class-relative model; it does not settle Nāgārjuna’s argument. UE14–UE15 answer selected presence questions within particular models, not the whole doctrinal dispute. [06 §11](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md).

   **道法自然 and 无为:** the reconstruction reads these through unfolding and criticism of excessive control. It correctly refuses to infer that nonintervention is always beneficial. This is an argued AI interpretation, not a control theorem establishing Daoist doctrine. **Whitehead:** the repository records a resemblance between continuation relative to a class and enduring objects, explicitly not a proved correspondence. I did not independently reread *Process and Reality*; stronger claims about prehension, concrescence, or subjective aim would exceed this review. [06 §9](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), [continuation record, literature comparison](../../../../../theories/becoming/results/records/continuation-relative-to-a-class.md).

**The strongest hostile objections**

- **Physicist:** “Your resource variable and loss floor do the physical work. Where are measured quantities, constitutive equations, and a prediction?” **Partly answered:** the correspondence premises and halving counterexample are explicit. **Unanswered:** broad physical identification and predictive superiority. [01 §8.4](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/01-ROOT.md).
- **Biologist:** “Accounting for supplied offspring counts does not explain reproduction, inheritance, fitness, or ecological rates.” **Acknowledged, not solved:** the inheritance and demographic assumptions remain supplied; the resource connections add conditional constraints. [04, “What this chapter does not show”](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/04-LIFE.md).
- **Neuroscientist:** “Your chooser is not my active-inference model, and observability is not an explanation of cognition.” **Acknowledged:** UE1’s implementation mismatch and UE2’s absent recognition density block full containment. [UE1 §4](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue1-fep-bridge/NOTE.md), [UE2 §4](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue2-conditional-weights/NOTE.md).
- **Sinologist:** “You selected relational passages, translated them into your formal vocabulary, then proved consequences of your translation.” **Methodologically answered in part:** the chapter labels readings and preserves objections. **Historically unanswered:** independent assessment of selection, translation, context, and excluded meanings. [06, “未示”](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md).

**What I would do next, ranked**

1. **Choose one application and validate its correspondence prospectively.** Fix measurements, thresholds, losses, and forecast before seeing outcomes; compare against the domain’s existing model. This addresses the actual gap between conditional theorem and scientific explanation. [08, empirical correspondence](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md).
2. **Make each containment claim carry a precise coverage boundary.** Prioritize constructor delivery/completion and active inference’s actual update equations; both currently formalize weaker substitutes. [UE12, Law 1](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue12-tasks-and-what-performs-them/NOTE.md), [UE1 §4](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue1-fep-bridge/NOTE.md).
3. **Commission independent sinological review of a stratified sample**, especially disputed Buddhist passages, commentator-dependent readings, and allegedly exact definitions. The current classification count cannot substitute for this. [06 §2 and §11](../../../../../theories/becoming/chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md).
4. **Use the causal-growth counterexample to sharpen the ethical question.** State what additional comparison could reconcile opening possibilities with preserving continuation, without concealing it in “widening.” The continuation condition fixes scope; C supplies order; neither alone resolves mixed cases. [UE13 §1](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue13-causal-sets/NOTE.md).
