# Brief for the overview

Board item RA, 23 October 2026. The overview is the second of the four layers ([spine](SPINE.md) §1): `theories/becoming/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md`, the whole argument of the theory in one account, for a careful reader who does not read Lean, at most 10,000 words (aim 7,000–9,000). Above it stands the one page (`theories/becoming/ONE-PAGE.md`, written first, the same claims in 900 words); below it, the eight chapters, which state every result once.

## What it must do

- Say the theory as one argument: each part follows from or feeds the next, in the order of the chapters.
- State every claim at the standing the chapters give it, in words: "proved for every relation and class", "proved on the World model, under balance and dissipation", "proved with Mathlib", "under the continuation condition and C", "a reading", "a thesis, with what would refute it", "open". Never stronger. Where a sentence summarizes a law, it keeps the law's hypotheses in plain words or says "under conditions the chapter states".
- Link every paragraph to the chapter section that states its results (`chapters/0N-NAME.md#anchor`), and the central results to their record anchors there (`#r-<id>`).
- Explain mechanisms, not names: few Lean names (at most one in a paragraph, where it helps a reader search), no record ids in prose, no chains of identifiers.
- Keep the ethics' rule: two premises with two roles, neither asserted, wherever the ethics is summarized.
- Name no person and no AI as author. Ren's words appear only where a coined term is studied (生生), with its source; the opening statement in Ren's voice is [OPENING](../../../../theories/becoming/OPENING.md), linked, not quoted.
