## DF3 迁移的八项记录：复读与两项提议 / The eight records DF3 migrated: read, and two proposals — 28 October 2026

工作板 DF3（研究第 NNN 节）。本链所记的八项记录由 DF3 迁移到 schema 2；GC 把它们的 10 个来源留给 DF3。每个来源都对照链接它的条目（C01、M20、M24、M25、F30）复读。8 个来源相合，记入。另有 2 个暂不记入，所附提议 A4、A5 接在 GC 的 A1–A3 之后，按维护规则不改条目：M20 陈述部分纠正的“当且仅当”时未写 0 ≤ β ≤ 3/4 的范围；陈述两端分类时未写其所需的判定。这是作者复读，不是独立验收。

Board item DF3 (study section NNN). DF3 migrated eight records this chain fingerprints to schema 2, and GC left their ten sources to DF3 ([section above](#gc-chain-review-28-october-2026)). Each source was read at its landed bytes, against the entries that link it.

**Recorded, 8.** The entries hold as these sources now state them:

- **C01 → derived-history-law-exactness.json.** C01 says transitions are constructed from admitted histories and their summary, and that the actual history language and its warrant remain supplied. The migrated record states the law on the admitted histories of a prefix-closed language. It now also says that every process's feasible words are realized by the law of its histories (`HistoryLanguages.realizes_feasible`), endpoints and branches not kept, which C01 does not deny.
- **M20 → market-supported-finite-transition.md.** M20's "supported finite correction now reaches the original protection region from every positive renewal seed", with "no uniform pulse duration" in its scope, is the record's law: each positive state has its own horizon N.
- **M24 → pending-task-context-applicability.md.** M24 states nothing the migration narrowed. The record now reads the review law for a pending endpoint, as the Lean takes it.
- **M25 and F30 → recoverable-mediated-change.md, and its JSON.** "At nine units, recovery exhausts while activity keeps running and ordinary shutdown is blocked" is the record's exhaustion counterexample, and the stop during recovery is reserved-shutdown-during-recovery's. The JSON is linked by no entry.
- **Linked by no entry:** finite-joining-tree-lifting.json, growth-constrained-fragmentation.json and classification-robust-generative-comparison.json, read for their claims and recorded.

**Held, 2, for two proposals.** M20's text would need to change. It is not edited here, and each source stays pending until M20 is revised or Ren declines the proposal.

- **A4. The range of the partial-correction boundary** (M20; held: market-partial-correction-boundary.md). M20 says "all positive renewal states in the original zero-error model reach protection in finite time iff beta<=5/12". `MarketInstances.lands_iff_margin` and `PartialRegimeTransition.exact_universal_landing_boundary` prove it for 0 ≤ β ≤ 3/4. Proposed: "for 0 ≤ beta ≤ 3/4, all positive renewal states … iff beta ≤ 5/12".
- **A5. The decisions the extreme classifications take** (M20; held: classification-robust-generative-comparison.md). M20 says "strict agreement at the all-secondary and all-basic endpoints covers every mixed assignment". `Lexical.classified_extremes_iff_of_decided` proves it where it is decided whether the alternative below holds each fresh content, and, for each classification, whether it holds every fresh content classified basic. The criterion over every type is exactly excluded middle (the record's sixth law). Proposed: "where the realizations compared are decided, strict agreement at the all-secondary and all-basic endpoints covers every mixed assignment".

Both bear on M20 beside A1, which restates its G0 wording, so they can be applied with A1.

This is author reading, not independent acceptance, and proves no new claim.
