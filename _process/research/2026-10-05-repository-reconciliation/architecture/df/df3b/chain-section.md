- **A6. The excluded middle some joining and granularity laws take** (C30 and F22, whose text is the same). Held: finite-granularity-question-recovery.json, incidence-common-refinement.json, multivalued-joining-common-structure.json, residual-continuation-correspondence.json and retained-reference-joining-recovery.json. The migrated records state where these laws need excluded middle: matching histories through finite branching and complete answer agreement, and roots agreeing on every incidence question, where local finiteness is also needed. Where descriptions are alike through their roles, or a kernel is a bisimulation of the joining, the laws hold with no premise. Proposed for C30 and F22 (the zh follows each):
  - "Receiving-side finite branching and translated-answer agreement match histories with different native lengths" becomes "Under excluded middle, receiving-side finite branching and translated-answer agreement match histories with different native lengths" (在排中律下，接收侧有限分支及翻译问答一致迫出不同原生步数的有限历史匹配).
  - "Across different fragment carriers, incidence agreement yields a common refinement with one-position lifting and a unique join-preserving factor commuting with given projections" becomes "Across different fragment carriers, roots alike through their incidence roles yield, with no finiteness or decision, a common refinement with one-position lifting and a unique join-preserving factor commuting with given projections; roots agreeing on every incidence question do so under excluded middle with local finiteness".
  - "finite outcome fibers and richer branch questions force a common joining relation; each code result lifts from the originally specified inputs" becomes "under excluded middle, finite outcome fibers and richer branch questions force a common joining relation, and each code result lifts from the originally specified inputs; where the descriptions' one kernel is a bisimulation of the joining, both hold with no premise".
  - "common syntax and complete native-answer agreement match partial histories with residuals retained" becomes "common syntax and alike starts match partial histories with residuals retained, as does complete native-answer agreement under excluded middle with receiving-side finite branching".
  - The scope sentences gain excluded middle. "Fixed finite expansion, complete question agreement, excluded middle and actual correspondence remain explicit." "Incidence refinement uses connectivity, and from roots agreeing on incidence questions also local finiteness and excluded middle; reference repair uses equal initial slot counts with alike starts, or complete answer agreement with finite fixed-pair outcomes and excluded middle."
  
  The Chinese of each is in the chain reader's report.
- **A7. The decided equality the copying laws take** (C14). Held: formed-copying-correction.json, formed-copying-registration.json, inherited-structural-copying.json and recurrent-chain-assembly.json. C14 says the copying machines are instances of general copying laws "for every alphabet and law of draws". The laws compare tokens decidedly, and inspection never increases defects exactly where, wherever both have a token there, the reference agrees with the template. Proposed: "for every alphabet with decidable equality and every law of draws", and "exactly when, wherever both have a token there, the reference agrees with the template" (zh 对每个相等可判定的字母表与每个汲取律成立的一般复制律；当且仅当参考与模板在该位都有记号时二者一致).

A6 and A7 change only conditions the Lean already takes, so they can be applied with A1–A5 in one revision of the chain.

This is author reading, not independent acceptance, and proves no new claim.
