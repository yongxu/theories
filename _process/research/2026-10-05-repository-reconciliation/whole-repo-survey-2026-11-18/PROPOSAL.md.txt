## His thought, and what the survey can say of it

On 18 November he said that maybe a lot of the repository is garbage, to be revised, compressed or removed, and that he did not know the best approach.

- *Against it.* Where the meaning is, almost nothing is a copy: word-for-word overlap between chapters, records and the Lean's comments is low, 3 to 19% by the survey's measures. Nearly everything large is bound to a record, a tool or a link.
- *For it.* One AI-drafted paper is kept in eleven old editions, about 3.5 million words. Eleven live files name paths in them, the lineage page and its data among these, which name a handful of their files; nothing was found that reads the rest. Half the one page lies in long runs shared with the overview.
- *Not tested.* Whether what is written is wrong or overtaken. Only the twelve front pages and sampled sections of the eight chapters were judged for that. So this proposal does not answer "garbage" in that sense.

The filing says that the checks counted 71 errors of fact in the first round's reports, and where the checks list them.
3. **A trial on twenty theorems.** The twenty headline theorems the survey read, by two models on the same twenty. Their verdicts are filed as data, in the form row DJ's study already uses where a telling was set against the Lean: what the text says, what the Lean says, the evidence, whether it must be mended, and a wording. Nothing is edited in the trial. The twenty sit in 19 Lean files, and about a hundred records are bound to those files by whole-file hash (91 or 102, by which path is counted; two readers counted differently), so every mend has a cost that is said to Ren before any is made. The trial is judged by a reader who has only the proposed wording: do they recover the theorem's premises and, where a premise can fail, say of a case that fails it that the theorem does not apply. By one model's count nine of the twenty have no premise.
4. **Leaves the old paper editions out of plain search.** One line in `.ignore` for `drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/history/` (972 files). Nothing is moved, every link to them still resolves, and a search that names the folder still finds them. Whether they leave the working tree is a later question: each file's bytes are in git, and the lineage page's render checks that the files it names exist.

No Lean is built for these. Each landing runs its focused checks, and the full gate where the workflow page asks it.
