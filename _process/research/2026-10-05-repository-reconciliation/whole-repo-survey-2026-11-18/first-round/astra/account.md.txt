## What a continuing session needs from here

**Inferred** from `AGENTS.md`, the chapter links and the sampled chains. A session needs the one page for orientation, the relevant chapter for the precise claim, its Lean declaration for the premises, and the experiment or textual source for the proposed application. The lake illustrates the last distinction. With talk, one model kept the bed in 8/10 seasons under the added instruction against 3/10 with the community goal alone. Three of four frozen predictions failed for the other model. The account keeps that failure visible and the experiment keeps its evidence. A routine proof change has no evident need for six complete dossiers or a blind coding sheet. **Not looked at:** session access logs. I cannot say a file is never opened.
