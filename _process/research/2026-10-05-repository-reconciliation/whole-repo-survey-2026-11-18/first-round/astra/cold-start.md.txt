## 1. What this work is, and what is wanted

This is an attempt to draw one map on which a person, a society, or an AI could locate something it is doing and ask three questions. What keeps this going? Who or what is changed by it? Which of those changes can still turn what happens next?

Alignment gives the map its first practical purpose. Ren wants humans and AI to be able to change how they choose together while there is still time to protect what they depend on. He also wants to understand how gaining control over the way choices are made can itself become the winning move. The wider purpose is coordination. Different traditions and sciences have seen parts of these dynamics. A shared account would let people see where their claims meet, check a proof for themselves, and bring a case that makes the account change. The work wants to remain answerable to that last person, too. ## 2. The idea as I understand it

Anything that lasts is being made again through other activity, and what it does changes the conditions for somebody or something to go on. What sustains a course can fall away without affecting how its next step is chosen, and several people can also see a shared supply falling and still each have reason to take from it. What a body, firm, or training run holds to helps determine which differences it registers and which of those can change what it does. An account drawn from those differences can come to fit how people act when many use it together, even as something it leaves out is exhausted. The question is where a course renews what it or others need, where it spends that down, and whether the loss can still reach and change the rule of choice before recovery becomes impossible. - **Proved on a model close to the central idea.** In the “line on a stock” model, units need, take, and leave offspring. Two courses starting with the same units have the same succession of units for as long as their levels meet the same needs, even on different stocks. There is also a result about how long that likeness can last. Start with a nonempty population whose needs are all met, give every unit a positive need, bound the stock by a ceiling, allow no renewal from nothing, and suppose each unit’s offspring **together** take more than their parent. Some need is unmet within the ceiling plus one steps. Until that first failure, descent looks just as it would if every need were met. The model supplies the needs, draws, and offspring.
