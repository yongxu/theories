## What repeats

Group 20 alone has 2,402 paths and 1,337 blobs. Across the entire tracked tree, 4,454 source files occur exactly once by blob identity. These figures measure identical bytes, not intellectual novelty or Git pack savings.

## The commands behind the counts

**Counted** from the repository root. `git ls-files -z -- sources` supplied the paths. I grouped them by first directory, summed `os.stat(path).st_size`, and ran `wc -w` in batches on the `.md` paths. Chinese text without spaces makes word counts a poor proxy for reading time. `git ls-files --stage -z` supplied blob identities across the tree. `git ls-tree -r --name-only 0cfb9c567 -- sources` supplied historical private counts without opening files. Small read-only scripts counted speaker headings, timestamped blocks and normalized Markdown link targets. The date sweep used `rg --no-ignore-dot -il` on explicit tracked Markdown paths.
