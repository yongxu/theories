# lean-structure: the check

*AI-written (Claude Opus 5.5, the checking reader), 18 November 2026. Each claim below was tested against the repository itself, not against the two reports. Nothing was changed. No build, lake, test, gate, render or checker ran; `check_becoming.py` was read as text and its lists were parsed with `ast`. The machine's load average was 96 to 125 throughout, so every command was a light read. `LEAN` = `experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean`.*

## How the check was done

- I parsed `FOUNDATION`, `CORE`, `INSTANCES`, `APPLICATIONS`, `BRIDGE`, `PERIPHERY_JOINS`, `SUPERSEDED` and `SCOPE` out of `LEAN/becoming/check_becoming.py` with `ast.literal_eval`. I rebuilt `module_index` and `closure` by the checker's own rules: the `ARCHIVE` regex, lookup by file name outside `becoming/`, and import lines with comments stripped. From that I computed the import graph. The script is in my scratchpad and only reads.
- I hashed every `source_paths` entry of `LEAN/becoming/becoming-verification.json` and compared it with `source_sha256`. I did the same for every live `*verification*.json` that has `source_paths`.
- I read `git ls-files -s -- LEAN` (89,064 rows) for blob ids, and `git cat-file --batch-check='%(objectsize)'` for logical bytes.
- I read code in these places: `gate.py` `receipt_check` (lines 897–924), the gate's `STEPS` and test-input map (lines 170–290), and the checker's assertions (lines 4970–5005) and `passed_receipts` (line 4617).
- I counted links with `rg -l` over `theories/becoming`, the root `*.md` and `theories/becoming/results`.
- I took 5 `LEAN/history` files, one every 2,400 rows, and compared each with its blob at the old path (`git rev-list -1 HEAD -- old`, then `git rev-parse c^:old`).

## What a decision can rest on now

The core is 409 modules, fixed by Python lists and their import closure. The 16 November receipt binds every byte of it: 27,894 declarations, 20,352 theorems, no axioms. Its imports form one component. The core run depends on the periphery in three ways: a periphery `check.py`, three foundation files in periphery folders, and passed periphery receipts for those files. 92 percent of `LEAN`'s bytes are run folders and history; 33,501 saved `.lean` copies hold 6,468 contents; `LEAN/history` repeats git (5 of 5 sampled). 75 superseded modules are kept, and the checker hashes them but does not prove their equivalence. 21 periphery receipts and the fallible-inquiry receipt are stale on the core, and the Mathlib periphery needs a project outside the repository.
