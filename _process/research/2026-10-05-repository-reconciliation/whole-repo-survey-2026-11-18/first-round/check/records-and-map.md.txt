"Confirmed" below means I ran the count or opened the file myself. My scripts read JSON and `git ls-files`; none imports a repository tool. ## In short

Both reports are mostly right in their numbers. The astra report had no error of fact that I could find in fifteen statements checked, six of them recomputed to the exact figure; it is narrower than the Claude report and leaves out three things a decision needs (the pins that point outward from the records, how little of the records the atlas covers, and what links in). The Claude report sees more and has eight errors of fact, most of them small; two of them touch its candidates (which generated files nothing links to, and what `atlas.py` reads).

## Errors of fact

**Claude report: 8.** (1) 711 and 595 for 709 and 597. (2) "543 records" for 543 versions of 284 records. (3) `atlas.py` "reads" the mapping files. (4) "No page links into" the four large generated files. (5) 449 files for 765. (6) 4,421 laws "each" listing declarations (9 do not). (7) `GENERATIVITY-CHAIN.md` counted as hand-kept and unique. (8) "Names and hashes only", against the term-level checks on 24 items and one boundary field. The worst is (4): the report's first candidate calls these four files "the plainest cases" for leaving the working tree on the ground that nothing links to them, and the two html pages are where `LEDGER.md`, `LAWS.md`, `CROSSWALK.md` and `MAP.md` send a reader.

**astra report: 0 found** in the fifteen statements checked. Its weakness is what it leaves out, not what it says: no outward pins, no count of what the atlas leaves uncovered, no inbound links, and its candidates are marked inferred. Its overlap figure (12.8%) I reproduce as 12.1%; I take that as method, not error.

## What neither report says, found while checking

- The one live theorem record with no chapter home is `a-line-on-a-stock`. The dynamic is in the atlas and has a card, and is not on the sheet, in the passages, the paths or the cases. So the newest part of the direction is carried by the record and the atlas and by no telling a reader meets first.
- The generated manifest lists two record pages that do not exist. I did not find out how the gate treats them.
- `GENERATIVITY-CHAIN.md` is rendered and is outside the manifest, so "in the manifest" and "machine output" are not the same set: at least this page and `shared-laws.json` (1.9 MB) are derived and unlisted.

## What a decision can rest on now

The area is 1,306 tracked files and 104.7 MB. 709 files (69.8 MB) are rendered pages and data the manifest hashes; the 567 record files, six atlas entry files and seven map files are kept by hand and have no byte copy in the tracked tree. Records pin 1,746 outside paths by hash, and about 9,300 link occurrences point in, so moving either side is a tool change. The tie from a sentence to its theorem is a name, a hash and a reader's judgment; no tool reads the sentence. The map is mostly unjoined by its own data (82 of 185 dynamics alone; 249 of 567 records listed), and the two theorems the direction page names are in no map store.

## What it cannot yet rest on

- That any generated file is unlinked and so free to leave: the one claim of that kind was refuted for all four files named.
- Whether the site build could render the pages in place of the tree holding them: `tools/publishing/build_site.py` was read by nobody.
- Whether record, atlas or card sentences say what their theorems prove: no reader set one against the Lean, and I did not either.
- Whether the Lean's own dependency structure would join the dynamics better than the hand-kept links: nobody computed it; it needs the checker's cache.
- Whether the premise rows still match the Lean's binders: they were derived on 10 and 11 November and I found no tool that reads the binders again; `shared-laws.json` is already 25 laws behind the records.
- "A session never opens" the record pages, the registers or the crosswalk: no reader looked at what sessions open.
- The git weight of the area, and inbound links from history folders (the 9,315 leaves them out).
- How the gate treats the two manifest entries with no file.
