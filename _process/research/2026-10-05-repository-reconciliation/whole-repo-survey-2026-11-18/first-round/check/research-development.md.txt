## Errors of fact

**Claude report: 4.**

1. *The reason `xr-exchange-root` was superseded was "not found elsewhere".* Refuted. `development/one-unfolding-root/history/PLAN-v8.md:286` gives the same reason in Chinese: the earlier work started from exchange, and exchange gives the two ends first. Also `du-deep-unification-review/raw/reviews-and-attacks.json` cites the plan's §2 and §4 and says XR is superseded. The sweep looked only for the English phrasing. This is the worst error, because it was offered as evidence that failures are held only here.
2. *"8 [studies are cited] only by themselves (e.g. `xr-exchange-root`, `ks-says-less-than-proved`, `r52-joint-draw`)".* Wrong. `git grep -F` finds `ks-says-less-than-proved` and `r52-joint-draw` in no tracked file, their own included. `xr-exchange-root` is named by two files of another study (`du-deep-unification-review/raw/`). The script counted studies with no citation other than their own and printed them as "only by themselves". Zero-citation studies (`ev5b`, `ks`, `r47`, `r52`, `xl1`) are a different finding.
3. *"`../history/root-foundation/development` (253 folders, 4,362 files there)".* The 253 is right. The files there number 2,203. The 4,362 is the whole of `history/root-foundation`.
4. *"the 61 live links that point at mirror pages" (36 records, 25 account).* Understated. There are 25 account links. Record JSON holds no mirror links. The generated record pages (`results/records/*.md`, in the generated manifest) hold 2,053 links to mirror pages, of which 36 carry an anchor. So the risk is in the renderer rather than in hand links, which the candidate's "a renderer … would have to change" already says.

Partly, not counted: the 212 Lean copies are "byte copies of package modules" only in the sense that they copy past versions. Only 32 match the current package; 178 match only receipt snapshots in `becoming-checks`. Also partly: the logs row's 1,967 against 2,073 by suffix (the classifier is stated as rough). Also partly: "logs: what reads it: not found". `gate.py` lists `development/y1-changes/**` as an input glob of `test_classical_verifiers`, and that folder holds 111 log files, so moving them changes a gate input even if nothing parses them.

Omissions that matter: the insert-to-ledger repetition (93.8% of insert lines are in the log), the experiment's hash-pinned dependency on `reason-action-evidence`, and that the 50 agentdojo-identical files are empty.
