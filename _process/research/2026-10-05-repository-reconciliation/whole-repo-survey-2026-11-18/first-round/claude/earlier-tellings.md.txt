# earlier-tellings: what is there
*AI-written (Claude Opus 5.5), 18 November 2026. A survey; nothing was changed.*

## In three sentences
The area holds about 6.1 million words of earlier tellings: one AI-drafted conference paper kept in twelve near-identical editions (4.0M words, 1,122 files), the sixteen September theory folders (0.73M), the account's own retired chapters in `theories/becoming/history` (1.05M), and media-heavy `worldbuilding/` and `output/` (234 MB tracked between them). Almost none of it is read on the reading path, but two parts are load-bearing in a way the "trace that can leave" view does not expect: 292 of 567 records have their only hashed *statement* source in `theories/becoming/history`, and the one page defines the meta-attractor by linking `theories/meta-attractor/README.md`. The IASEAI folder, by contrast, has 2 live Markdown links in and no record reference, and its bulk is successive editions plus pages assembled mechanically from `src/`.

## What repeats
- **Exact copies are few.** Of 1,122 IASEAI files, 191 share a blob with another tracked file, only 4 outside the folder (e.g. `history/v3/No-Viable-Race-v3.1.pdf` = `output/pdf/No-Viable-Race-IASEAI27-v3.1-draft.pdf`); `output/` has 13 cross-area duplicates, branches 4 (e.g. `evolving-community-swarms/context/2026-09-05-conversation.md` = a file in `sources/15-…`), becoming/history 4. Counted by blob over `git ls-files -s`.
- **Near copies are the bulk.** Successive IASEAI editions differ little: v7.2→v7.3 THEORY.md changed 41 of 1,406 lines, SUPPLEMENT 92 of 1,599, DISCUSSION 27 of 670; v7.1→v7.2 similar (diff counted). Within each edition from v6.1 the root THEORY/SUPPLEMENT/DISCUSSION/TESTS are assembled outputs of `src/` ("root Markdown files are assembled outputs", v7.3 README), so each edition holds its text about twice. Inferred: the distinct prose of the 4.0M words is of the order of a few hundred thousand words; not measured.
- **Everything moved into a `history/` folder is also in git** at its old path (MANIFEST blob fields, 40 of 40 sampled across two manifests). The working-tree copy exists because of the 17 October layout rule, not because git lacks it.
- The branch folders retell `sources/`; the crosswalk (`results/atlas/CROSSWALK.md`) retells the branch folders against the account (838 entries).

## What depends on it
- `tools/research/registry.py` (lines ~1015–1043): every record reference must exist and carry a sha256 checked against the file; `canonical_source` must be one of them. 312 records reference `theories/becoming/history`, 292 of them with *only* history pages as statement sources (277 references to the old `THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md`); 11 records name a history page as canonical source. Moving these files breaks the registry check unless the references are rebound.
- `tools/research/lineage.py` (line ~133 and `evidence_errors`) checks at every render that 220 lineage paths exist and their quotes are present; 45 are branch-folder paths, 6 becoming/history, 7 drafts. It already accepts a `commit` field and reads the file at that commit, so a path could leave the tree if pinned.
- `paths.py` classes everything outside a `history` component as `live`: v7.3, all of `drafts/*` top levels, the branch folders, worldbuilding, output. Their links are checked by the gate's `links` check (`moves.py check_links`, referrers live and generated).
- `gate.py` lists `drafts/**`, `output/**`, `worldbuilding/**` as areas; `test_classical_verifiers` reads `theories/becoming/history/YI-ZHUAN-AND-BECOMING.md`; `lean_catalogue.py` classes `drafts/` Lean (3 files) as `historical-draft`.
- Generated pages in the area: `theories/README.md`, `LINEAGE.md`, `LINEAGE.zh.md` (atlas.py, lineage.py), 6 in becoming/history (INDEX, the 8-October ARGUMENTS, SESSION-RESULTS, THEORY-MAP snapshots; last changed 20 October), 4 in drafts, 1 in output.

- **becoming/history: stays for now.** It cannot leave until 292 records' statement references are rebound to live chapters or to a pinned commit; the registry requires the file to exist with a matching hash. This is a tool change plus a record pass, not a move.
- **Branch folders: stay.** Live definitions point into them; 45 lineage paths and 8 records check them. Could be shortened to their READMEs only if the crosswalk is accepted as their reading; cannot tell.
- **worldbuilding/, output/: could leave the tree or stay; cannot tell.** They are a separate project and machine output; output's review-packet PDFs are what readers received (tagged commits exist, so regenerable, but who is sent what is Ren's).

## Against the two views
- Against "garbage to remove": the largest mass (IASEAI) is not wrong text but kept editions; and the smaller becoming/history is not dead: about half the records' statement sources are there, so the records point at retired tellings as what they state. That is a joining problem, not garbage.
- Against "trace can leave without loss": becoming/history and the meta-attractor folder are trace by label but load-bearing by tool and by link. And the one place found holding the IASEAI pilot's failed prediction is the draft folder, so "keep failures" is met here only by keeping a draft.
- For the coordinating session's view: at least 3.5M of the area's 6.1M words are byte-for-byte history already in git, and the IASEAI paper's text is AI-drafted, held twice per edition, twelve times over.

## The commands behind the counts
- Files and words: `git ls-files <d> | wc -l`; `git ls-files -z <d> | grep -z '\.md$' | xargs -0 cat | wc -w`, per folder and per IASEAI edition.
- Tracked bytes: `git ls-files -z <d> | xargs -0 stat -f %z`; disk: `du -sh`; ignored: `git status --short --ignored`.
- Distinct blobs and duplicates: `git ls-files -s` grouped by blob (python, scratch).
- Edition diffs: `diff a b | grep -c '^[<>]'`.
- Record references: python over `theories/becoming/results/records/*.json`, fields `references[].path/role/sha256`, `canonical_source`.
- Inbound links: python over `git ls-files '*.md'`, relative `](…)` targets resolved, referrers outside history/development/sources.
- Manifests: `git hash-object` on 20 sampled `new_path`s per manifest vs recorded `blob`; `git cat-file -e`.
- Tools read as text: `registry.py` 1012–1050, `lineage.py` 125–140 and `evidence_errors`, `paths.py` 26–110, `moves.py check_links`, `gate.py` 55–75, 180–200, 250; `.ignore` whole.
