# entry-and-direction: what is there
*AI-written (Claude Fable 5.2), 18 November 2026. A survey; nothing was changed.*

## In three sentences

The area is small (48 files, about 133,000 words, 0.5% of the repository's Markdown) and it is of two dates that do not point at each other: four hand-kept pages carry the direction as understood from 14 to 18 November (`AGENTS.md`, `_process/DIRECTION.md`, the plan, the board), and every other entry page was last changed in content on or before 13 November and tells an earlier aim, some of it as "current first priority". The state of the work (proved, assumed, failed, next) is written in at least eight places and no one of them holds it. The trouble here is currency and interleaving, not mass: trace is written inside the live pages a session must read, and the only machinery that holds the fronts to anything holds them to one sentence of `AGENTS.md`, not to the Lean.

## What repeats

- **The README is mostly the opening.** 255 of its 346 sentences, about 9,600 of 13,700 words, are also in `theories/becoming/OPENING.md` once link targets are dropped (counted by script).
- **The aim sentence** ("…shaping how they select does not become what they select for") is on 16 live pages (counted). `navigation.py` lines 785–790 hold it as a Python string, and `test_uses_pages.py` and `test_featured_results.py` read it out of `AGENTS.md` and hold the fronts to its words.
- **The idea itself repeats nowhere.** "made again and again" and "run down unseen" occur in `AGENTS.md`, `DIRECTION.md` and the `dj` study only (counted).
- **Kept instruction copies.** All 22 `AGENTS-before-*` files in the fourteen `*-instructions` folders, and both kept `RESULT-WORKFLOW` copies, are byte-identical to a past committed version (blob hashes compared; 24 files, 38,483 words; thirteen folders belong to research-history).

## Candidates

- **`AGENTS.md`, `DIRECTION.md`, the plan: stay.** The present understanding is nowhere else. Risk as they are: `DIRECTION.md` opens with a 676-word head note of provenance, and two of its paragraphs are 866 and 768 words; a reader meets trace before the idea. The provenance could be said once in a record the page points to. No tool blocks that.
- **The board: could be said once and pointed to.** The clauses of "The way now" repeat `DIRECTION.md`'s closing note and `AGENTS.md`; the October target is history. Risk: the gate refuses a board that loses Queue clauses or shrinks by a fifth unless `GATE_BOARD_REWRITE` is set, and landings are in flight. First: a decision on what the board is for.
- **Goals and the pages rendered from them: cannot tell without Ren.** 15 of 16 scopes are `unmarked`, none changed after 13 November, and "Theory of Becoming — current first priority" is still the title the 10 November review asked him about (`ag-the-map/FOR-REN.md`, II.5, which says "first" was an AI's addition). Until scopes are marked, `GOALS`, `RESEARCH` and the README give October's target in the present tense.
- **Generated pages: stay as outputs.** The question is their sources. If prose is to follow the Lean, the hand-kept first screen and the two tests that pin it to `AGENTS.md` change first.
- **Kept instruction copies: could leave the working tree** (22 of 22 are in git). First: links from `AGENTS.md` and `RESEARCH-DIRECTIVES.md` and about 100 mentions in live files would need to name a commit. The gain is 38,000 words.
- **`RESEARCH-DIRECTIVES.md`: cannot tell.** Newest entry 13 November; its own index calls it incomplete. An AI reading stands beside each quote, which `AGENTS.md` now warns against, yet it is the only curated list of what he asked for.
- **`LOST-ARTIFACTS.md`: stays.** It is the only inventory of texts that exist outside the repository.

## Against the two views

- Against "much is garbage": nothing here is unused. Every file is generated, feeds a generator, is parsed by the gate, or is linked from a page a session reads. What is wrong is that the pages are of two dates.
- Against "trace and machine output could leave the reading path": here trace cannot leave by moving folders, because it is written inside the live pages (the direction page's head note, the board's clauses, the register's readings). And the 43% that is machine output is the public reading path itself.
- Against "prose is judged against the Lean, not against other prose": on the fronts the machinery does the opposite. Tests hold pages to `AGENTS.md`'s sentence and a render string copies it. Only `DIRECTION.md` and the plan were set against the Lean, by AI readers; by their own head notes no person has checked either. Twenty Lean names sampled from the two pages all resolve to declarations in the live tree (by name; statements not read).
- For Ren's view in part: about 9,600 words of the README and all 38,000 words of kept copies are exact repeats.

## The commands behind the counts

- Sizes and dates: `wc -w -l -c`; a Python script over the files and the `files` keys of `_process/architecture/generated-manifest.json`; `git log -1 --format='%ad %h' -- <file>`; `grep -c '1[4-8] November' <file>`; `awk` word counts per line of the board; `git log --since=2026-11-11 --format=%h [-- board] | wc -l`.
- Links: `rg -n --no-ignore-dot '(_process/|\.\./|\()DIRECTION\.md' --glob '!tmp/**'`.
- Repeats: `rg -l -F '<phrase>' --glob '!tmp/**' --glob '*.md' | wc -l`; README against `OPENING.md` by a script that splits sentences after dropping link targets and note markers.
- Kept copies: in bash, `git rev-parse HEAD:<copy>` against `git rev-parse <commit>:AGENTS.md` over `git log --format=%H -- AGENTS.md`.
- Readers: `rg -n` for each page name over `tools/`, `.github`, `Makefile`, `_process/research-config.json`.
- Lean names: `rg -l -w '(theorem|def|lemma|abbrev|structure|namespace|inductive) +…<name>'` over the Lean root, check copies and history excluded.
