The Lean that carries the two paragraphs of `AGENTS.md` is small: six modules written or reworked since 13 November (4% of the lines) and some forty October modules whose pieces were found only when readers went looking on 16 to 18 November. What ties the direction's sentences to theorems is not in the Lean and not on the direction page but in five read reports in `DJ/`, and one proof it names exists only outside git.

**The two paragraphs, sentence by sentence** (my grades; "in part" names what is handed in).

*The idea.*
1. *Whatever lasts is made again out of other goings-on.* In part: `Whole.parts_unceasing` (`Wholes:164`), `Flow.fed_from_beyond` (`Continuation:180`), `PatternPassing`. Heads read, statements not.
2. *Rests on and makes a difference need not be the same; the gap.* In part: `ClosedCapture.unread_course` (186) with `lock_outlives_loss` (307, read); `Reading.requirement_beyond_course`; turning in time below.
3. *Draining in full view.* Stated in one model: `CoupledDrawers.gated_threshold` (774, read), first conjunct, two or more drawers in turn on a gated renewal.
4. *Holding to something; registering some differences.* In part: `ReadingLoop.sensed_alike_iff` (442, read), `alike_of_kept`. What is held to is a parameter.
5. *An account, taken for the whole, says more.* In part: `PartialReadout.silent` (133), `RelativePosition.rise_needs_fall` (167, read), `change_balances`. No statement has the drawing or anyone taking it.
6. *Many act on it; what they do can come to fit; it need not; fitting is not holding.* In part, each of one rule, one line of cohorts or one loop: `RuleCapture.favouring_locks`, `SharedCodes.omitted_rest` (1240, read), `ReadingLoop.Loop.rests_of_unrefuted` (read), `ReflexivePrediction.Negating.none_right`.

*The alignment paragraph.*
1. *A selection is one dynamic among others, some of which sustain it.* None found as a statement; nearest `PoolFromCourse`, `ReaderRebuilding` (titles).
2. *Courses that differ in nothing that makes a difference are selected alike.* **Whole**, twice: `Web.selects_alike_iff` (`Selection:191`), whose docstring says it "states what the definition builds in"; and `LineOnAStock.ways_alike` (547, read), with needs and offspring given.
3. *What sustains can be used up while nothing in the selection differs.* In part, close: `LineOnAStock.need_unmet` (1011), `falls_to_unmet` (1057, both read). Given: offspring together take more (`grows`), a ceiling, offspring need no more.
4. *A rule set again from its share, drawing more, goes on until what sustains it gives out, unless read early enough.* In part, two theorems: `TurningInTime.locked_ends_rising` (1440, read) with `count_rises`; the "unless" is `locked_turns_in_time_on_window_iff` (1633). Given: the use rises with each step (`more`), the level makes no difference while the need is met.
5. *The design we want.* Wanted; no Lean.
6. *Instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor.* Docstrings only: "meta-attractor" in 6 modules (9 hits), "instrumental" in 3 (5 hits), each under "Readings (the AI's)". `ReadingsPersist` says its `cleared_removes_its_readers` is "the meta-attractor's shape … not the meta-attractor itself". The only declarations with "attractor" in a name are the hexagrams' (`HexagramLaws`, 10).
7. *A value fixed once.* In part: `RuleCapture.perturbation_absorbed` (read), `Pace.never_reads_loses`.
