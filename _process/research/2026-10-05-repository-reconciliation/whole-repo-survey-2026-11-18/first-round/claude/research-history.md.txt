# research-history: what is there
*AI-written (Claude Opus 5.5), 18 November 2026. A survey; nothing was changed.*

## In three sentences

This area is about 18.6 million words in 6,870 tracked files (6.6 million of them in Markdown). Almost all of it is trace: the October packets, spent landing kits, retired studies, old record texts and instruction copies, plus machine output (check logs, move receipts). Every moved file I checked is byte-identical to what its manifest names, and nothing here is the only copy in git. It cannot simply leave the tree, though: about 1,970 of the 4,363 files in the generative-foundation history are still linked from live pages and record JSON, the tools read the manifests and the record snapshots, and two parts of the area are not retired at all.

## What repeats

- **Byte-for-byte with git.** All 4,363 GFR-history manifest entries and all 473 `_process/history` manifest entries hash to the manifest's sha256. Their git blob equals the manifest's `blob` (counted). The 24 instruction copies are earlier blobs of `AGENTS.md`/`RESULT-WORKFLOW.md`. Two of them, `2026-11-09-instructions/AGENTS-before-landing-wording.md` and `…-before-readers-corrections.md`, are the same blob as each other.
- **In the working tree.** 235 GFR-history files (≥500 B, 5.1 MB) and 329 `_process/history` files (11.4 MB) have an identical copy outside their folder. Most of those copies are Lean saved copies under `becoming-checks/` (224 and 275 files). 34 `_process/history` files are also in `sources/22-research-reconciliation-2026-10-05/files`, and 16 are in `sources/20-…/root-of-wisdom/…/source-snapshots`. Of 251 Lean files in GFR history, 120 are also saved check copies, 51 are identical to a live module and 78 have no other copy in the tree.
- **Record snapshots.** `architecture/history` holds 3.18 M words of earlier record JSON. The git log already holds the same bytes at the record path (6 of 6 sampled).
- **Earlier surveys.** The review of 16 and 17 Oct (`whole-repo-review-2026-10-16/`) planned the moves that made this area. `RESTRUCTURE-REPORT.md` says they ran as batches H1 to H7 and cut the live files from 64,547 to 19,298. The receipts confirm it: H1 to H7 are timestamped 16 Oct 19:39 to 19:54 UTC, and H5 alone moved 3,328 files and H7 12,772. The 19 Oct review reported public history holding private material. I did not find a 9 to 10 Nov or 14 Nov survey that treats this area: `ag-the-map/` mentions `_process/history/` only as the home of old instructions, and the "26.6M words" count was not found in tracked text except this survey's AREAS.md.

## What depends on it

- Tools: `research-config.json` names `history_manifest`, `generated_manifest`, `record_snapshots` (`_process/architecture/history`) and `move_receipts`. `navigation.py` resolves snapshots by hash. `moves.py` writes and checks MANIFEST/INDEX and receipts. `indexes.py` lists `_process/history` on the research index. The gate's `test_schema2` reads `_process/architecture/history/**` and `moves/*.json`.
- Links: 628 live files reference 2,054 distinct GFR-history paths, 1,967 of them manifest entries (about 45%). 327 of the 628 are under `theories/becoming/results`. 721 live files reference 582 snapshot paths. The record JSONs cite GFR-history sections by anchor (e.g. `ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION-INTEGRATION.md#3-exact-mathematical-scope`), which is citation pinned to a section. The canonical chapters 04-LIFE and 08-METHOD link into GFR history. (A script over live md/json/py/tsv, excluding history, sources and tmp; relative links resolved; a reader should treat ±5% as the regex's margin.)

## What a continuing session needs from here

It needs the latest instruction copy (linked from AGENTS.md), the board log only when trimming the Queue line, the manifests and receipts through the tools, and the snapshots only through records. Following a record's `progress` source lands in a GFR packet. It never needs `worktree-salvage`, the spent kits in `development/`, `repository-reconciliation/architecture/df|mv|gt`, the September consolidation, or the 5 Oct retired studies. That is inferred from the kinds and from the link counts above: the salvage and kits have no live readers that I found.

## Candidates

- **worktree-salvage, spent kits (`development/`, `repository-reconciliation/architecture/df|mv`):** these could leave the tree. Evidence: no tool reads them, they are git blobs, and they are machine output or applied scripts. Risk: the 78 Lean files with no other tree copy are attempts someone may want to diff, and some development folders hold failed attempts. A salvage ref and an index line would have to name them first, and the live links into `development/` (part of the 2,054) would need repointing.
- **GFR top-level packets:** cannot tell yet. They are trace, but records cite them by section as the provenance of results. Moving them needs `repoint.py` or a record rebind, and a decision whether a record may cite a commit-pinned URL instead of a path.
- **architecture/history snapshots:** these could be said once (git already holds them). That needs `navigation.py`, `text_versions` and `test_schema2` changed first. Risk: the gate goes red.
- **Instruction copies:** stay (small, linked, AGENTS.md promises them). Their identity with git means they could equally be pointers to commits.
- **Board log:** stays. Its name and folder should say it is live. Risk now: a reader takes it for retired.
- **Small 5 Oct live studies:** cannot tell. They are dormant since 17 Oct and linked from experiments. Whether their CLAIMS are carried by Lean was not checked.
- **reading-notes-inputs and the Ceroni files:** these are not a shape decision. They are Ren's (see below).

## Against the two views

- Against "garbage to remove": nearly nothing here is garbage in the sense of unread. About 45% of GFR history is cited from live results and records, and the counterreviews and COUNTEREXAMPLES are failures AGENTS.md keeps. Removal without rewriting would break records, not just clutter.
- Against "trace can leave without loss": the area is not all retired. The board log is appended daily, `generated-manifest.json` is live render state, and the snapshot store is read by the gate. The rule "retired material moves byte for byte" has also been followed exactly (0 mismatches in 4,836 entries). So the layout rule works as a ledger, but it keeps every byte twice, once in the tree and once in git, and the reading path still runs into it through record citations.
- Against both: prose here is not judged against the Lean either way. The packets are October tellings that the chapters say were superseded, yet records still name them as where a result's scope is stated.

## The commands behind the counts

- `git ls-files <dir> | wc -l`; `git ls-files -z <dir> | xargs -0 cat | wc -w` (and the same filtered to `\.md$`).
- A Python read of each `MANIFEST.json`: it checks sha256 of each `new_path`, compares `blob` with `git ls-files -s`, and lists tracked files not in the manifest.
- A Python duplicate scan over `git ls-files -s` blob ids (files ≥500 B, copies outside the folder).
- `git log --find-object=<blob> -- AGENTS.md _process/RESULT-WORKFLOW.md` for each instruction copy, and the same against `theories/becoming/results/records/<name>` for 6 random snapshots (seed 1).
- `rg -l --no-ignore-dot` for path mentions; `grep -n` in `tools/research/*.py` and `_process/research-config.json`; a Python link scan over live files.
- `git log --format=%h -- _process/history/workboard | wc -l`; `git check-ignore sources/12-claude-ai-chats`; `git branch -r --contains fceb4f2bb`.
