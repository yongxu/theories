# sources: what is there
*AI-written (Claude Opus 5.5), 18 November 2026. A survey; nothing was changed.*

## In three sentences
`sources/` is 6,538 tracked files (1,557 MiB, about a quarter of the repository's tracked bytes) and 4.88 million Markdown words, but Ren's own words are a small part of it. The words are mostly AI-written: Codex session workspaces of 4 to 5 October (1.57M), Ren's earlier projects that he built with AI agents (2.28M), and agent reports inside the "prompts" export (0.57M). The bytes are mostly imported data that experiments read. What a continuing session needs from here is narrow: the directive records of group 15 (Ren's words, timestamped and verbatim, with AI context labelled), the data the experiments and verifiers read, and the classical texts. The protection "never edited, never moved" covers all of it equally, Ren's words and AI snapshots alike.

Of them, 1,081 are in group 20, whose sessions saved `snapshots/` of one another (287 files under `snapshots/` or `source-snapshots/` hold 571,240 words), and 43 are in civilization-thought. Counted.
- Outside `sources/`, 137 non-empty files have byte-identical copies, almost all under `experiments/history/` and `_process/history/` (from groups 20 and 22). Four group-15 records are copied in `theories/evolving-community-swarms/context/`. Counted.
- Older versions of `SOURCES.md` are saved as "before" copies in `_process/` (four files of 220–315 KB). Counted by name; not compared.

## What depends on it

- **Links.** Markdown outside `sources/` holds 6,844 links into it. 3,999 are from history, frozen or `-BEFORE-` paths. The other 2,845 come from 809 files (`_process` 1,630, `theories` 946, `GOALS.md` 60, `README.md` 4), 2,319 of them to group 15. 276 carry a `#anchor`, so headings inside the records are pinned by citations. Counted by regex; a few links may be missed.
- **Tools.** `tools/research/paths.py` classes everything under `sources/` as "source: never edited, never moved". `moves.py` excludes it from rewrites. `indexes.py` writes `sources/README.md` and `_process/DIRECTIVE-INDEX.md` from the directories, and those directories are all it reads; it does not read `SOURCES.md`. `gate.py` warns when a directory has no tracked files, and `test_classical_verifiers` watches `sources/zhouyi-yizhuan/**`, `chinese-classical-texts/**` and `civilization-thought-v1.0/**`. `build_site.py` refuses any `sources/` path, so the public site carries none of it. Read.
- **Experiments.** 112 non-history `.py` files under `experiments/` name `sources/` paths. Counted.
- **Manifests.** JSON files outside `sources/` that name `sources/` paths: `_process` 1,329, `theories` 267, `experiments` 217. Counted by file, not by line; most are under history folders (inferred from the link split above).
- **GROUPS.json** is named in `_process/REPOSITORY-LAYOUT.md` (line 49) and was planned by the 16 October review, together with `MANIFEST.tsv`. Neither exists in the tree, and `git log --all` finds neither in history.

## What a continuing session needs from here

It needs the group-15 records, above all the two November files, reached through `_process/DIRECTIVE-INDEX.md`. It needs the evidence groups that experiments read, the classical texts the verifiers and crosswalk read, and `SOURCES.md`'s dated import notes for provenance. Inferred from what links and tools read: it should never need group 20's `work/` trees, group 14's agent reports, or the `snapshots/` copies.

Finding "what Ren said about X" is the weak point (read). The index lists each record by file and by the title of its first message. Later messages are appended to an existing file under a time stamp, sometimes inside a section whose heading names an earlier day. The hand-kept register `RESEARCH-DIRECTIVES.md` stops at 13 November and mentions neither November direction file (0 matches). `SOURCES.md` has 3 lines dated November and was last changed on 11 November. Today a reader finds his words of 14–17 November through the private AI memory or by reading those two long files. That goes against item 5 of the brief (the repository alone should be enough).

## Candidates

- **Group 15–17 (directive records): stays.** These are Ren's words, with 2,319 live links and 276 pinned anchors. What would help is a finding aid, not a move: index entries per dated message, or a register brought up to date. Risk of changing the records: broken anchors. First: the index generator in `indexes.py`.
- **Groups E, G, H, and F's data: stays.** Experiments and verifiers read them by path, and freeze manifests hash them. Before anything moved, every script path and manifest would have to change. Leaving them in place costs nothing on the reading path, because they are data.
- **Group 20 (and 19, 21, 25, 26): could leave the working tree.** Evidence: 1.57M words, 1,081 internal copies, 22 non-history citers, and the site already refuses it. Risk: the 22 citers (the atlas crosswalk, the selective-handoff experiment and its fixtures) and the history receipts that hash these paths would break. First: repoint or freeze those 22, and relax `paths.py`'s "never moved" rule for this group. Countermodels and failed runs are kept here (307 group-20 `.md` files mention countermodels, counterexamples or failed tests). Git keeps them either way, but how they are reached would change.
- **Group 14: cannot tell.** Splitting Ren's prompts from the 86% that is agent reports would be a new reading edition, which the layout rule allows only labelled and registered, with the original kept. The 16 October review recorded an OpenRouter key here. The credential check allowlists it as revoked, and the pattern still matches 4 lines in 3 files (counted; key not printed).
- **Groups 00–11 and F's prose: could be said once and pointed to**, as earlier tellings, if a page said what each project was and where it stands beside the Lean. They are not repeated byte for byte elsewhere, so they stay as originals.
- **`SOURCES.md`: cannot tell.** It is a hand-kept manifest that no tool reads and that has stopped growing. It is unique as provenance prose, and partly superseded by the generated README and the per-group PROVENANCE files.
- **The layout rule's GROUPS.json: should be said as it is.** The line names a file that was never made.

## Against the two views

Against "much is garbage": the bytes are mostly data that experiments read and that manifests hash, and the group-15 records are carefully made (time stamps, typed or spoken, AI context labelled). Neither is garbage. Against "most is trace and machine output that can leave without loss": the 2.28M words of groups 00–11 are neither. They are Ren's earlier work, the only copy, and an earlier Lean repo (07's `ssbx-repo`, whose README admits about 150 `sorry`s, a failure kept). Group 14 is mostly agent output but holds Ren's prompts inside it. Supporting the second view: group 20 is AI trace, about a third of it repeated. One finding fits neither view: the area's own protection treats AI snapshots and Ren's words alike ("source", never moved). That is why 1.5M AI words sit beside his words, outside the history convention.

## The commands behind the counts

- `git -c core.quotepath=off ls-files -z sources`, summed per group and per kind with `os.path.getsize` and `len(text.split())` for `.md` (a scratch script).
- `git ls-files -s -z`, grouping paths by blob hash, for the copies inside and outside `sources/`.
- `re.finditer(r'<task-notification>.*?</task-notification>', re.S)` over group 14, for the 86%.
- Lines starting with `>`, over groups 15 to 17, for the blockquote shares.
- `rg -o --no-ignore-dot --with-filename -g '*.md' -g '!sources/**' -g '!tmp/**' -g '!work/**' 'sources/[^)#\s]+(#[^)\s]*)?\)' .`, split by `/history/`, `/frozen/` and `-BEFORE-`, for the links.
- `rg -l --no-ignore-dot 'sources/' -g '*.py' experiments`, for the experiment scripts.
- `rg -c --no-ignore-dot 'sources/2[0-5]-' -g '!sources/**' -g '!tmp/**' .`, for the 617 lines.
- `git log --since=2026-10-17 --diff-filter=M|D --name-only -- sources`, for the changes since 17 October.
- `git log --all -- sources/GROUPS.json`, empty.
- `git ls-tree -r origin/main -- sources/14-claude-code-prompts` and `git log origin/main -- sources/12-claude-ai-chats`, for what is on the remote.
- `find <private dir> -type f | wc -l` and `du -sh`, for the private folders.
- `git ls-files -z | xargs -0 stat -f%z`, for the repository total (5,922 MB).
