**Inferred from the three sources.** The common idea is that what a process produces can come to shape how it meets the world next. The older power account asks who gets to write the next scorecard. ## What the declarations establish

**Read whole** — the following four `ClosedCapture` theorem statements and the definitions of `Unread`, `Accrues` and `Locked`.

- `courses_alike_iff_unread`. Fix the share and rule and vary what lies beyond. Equality of the next share and the rule read under every such variation is equivalent to equality of share and rule at every time, from every pair of matching starts, along every common reading schedule. This states precisely what the omitted difference cannot turn. [ClosedCapture.lean](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ClosedCapture.lean:202)
- `lock_outlives_loss`. The share update and rule reading omit the loss by construction. A favouring rule never lowers the share, a no-lower share gives a no-lower reading across prior rules, and favouring is upward closed. Initially the rule favours and its rereading is at least as high. **Assume also** that every step adds at least one to the loss. Then at time `t` the loss is at least its start plus `t`, the course is locked, and share and rule are each at least their starting values. The increment may be the same whatever anyone does. Calling it destruction compounded *by holding to an account* adds a causal claim. [Lock definition](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/RuleCapture.lean:160) · [Loss law](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ClosedCapture.lean:307)
- `reading_absorbed`. Start jointly locked. Assume selection raises under favouring, favouring is upward closed, the reader is formed monotonically from share, and the rule reading is monotone in reader and share. Replace the reader state arbitrarily and the rule with one that still favours. After any reading step the joint lock holds again, with rule and reader at least their original values. The function forming the reader is supplied. [Reader update and theorem](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ClosedCapture.lean:412)
- `turned_by_loss`. Suppose loss accrues and every reading at loss at least `N` returns a non-favouring rule. If step `r` reads and initial loss plus `r` reaches `N`, the rule at `r+1` does not favour. This is one sufficient turn. The empirical question is whether a particular reader responds that way. [Turn theorem](/Users/ren/theories/experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/ClosedCapture.lean:291)
