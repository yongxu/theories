## What a theorem would have to state for the 18 November picture

My reading, not found stated anywhere. Four things, as outcomes of one course and not as premises:

1. **An account that can be false.** In `ClosedCapture` the reading `Q` says nothing of what lies beyond, so nothing is "taken for the whole". The model needs a claim, read off the registered part alone, that is false at some configuration the course reaches. `ReadingLoop` has readings as sets of admitted worlds, with the remark that being kept is not being informative.
2. **A holder formed within it.** What stands, and what it holds to, must be generated from the account, not handed in as `favours` or a target. Every held-to in the modules I opened is a parameter (`Seeking`, `Capture`, `ClosedCapture`; the direction page says the same of about seventy modules).
3. **Holding.** The holder's own revising keeps the account: the region is closed under its update though false of the world. Pieces exist apart: `reading_absorbed`; `fixed_of_closedFrom` (07 §4.14, chapter's telling); `LumpedSeeking.seeking_keeps_order`.
4. **Compounding.** Acting from the account draws on what it does not register, no less as the holder's share rises, and what would revise it goes with what is drawn. Pieces: `PoolFromCourse.window_order_kept` and the removal of readers (02 §10); `ReadingsPersist.cleared_removes_its_readers` (a built course, which lines read supplied); `ReadingsSpread.Commons` (built, acts assigned by design).

The conclusion would be: the registered never differs from a course in which nothing is drawn, the unregistered falls past its bound, and nothing within the holder's reach turns it. That anyone is in such a course no theorem gives (his 14:53; `AGENTS.md` rule 2).
