## Result 4

**From a chapter:**

**Theorem** (any type). Take a valuer's judgment as a ranking of presentations (plans with their wording and order, each keeping a set of items), and a net weighting as counting each item for a course admitting it and against it. Where such a ranking puts a narrowing above its widening by one item, either it does not rank the pair as its own order ranks the kept sets (a misread or a framing) or the item weighs at zero or below; read right, exactly where it weighs below zero, and at zero it ties (`Weighting.narrower_above_net`, `Weighting.reads_ranksNet`). Read right, where a sign measured apart is the sign of the order's increment from the narrower course to the wider in the pair's context, it fixes the ranking: narrower above exactly where it is a gain, wider exactly where a loss, neither where nothing (`Weighting.reads_increment`); an additive valuation carries it to every context, an interacting one need not. That a judgment is such a ranking is a premise. ([record](../results/records/a-narrowing-above-its-widening.md))

**The situation:** Two travel plans are compared. The second keeps every stop of the first and adds two more, a museum and a market. The judge ranks the first, shorter plan above the second.

## Result 6

**From a chapter:**

**Theorem** (any type; values decided only where an extrapolation needs them). A course is a sequence of values; an extrapolation maps a prefix to a proposed value or silence. With two values no prefix fixes the next value: the prefix continues with either, and a proposal is not right on both, even among courses of worlds a list holds. For every extrapolation and every map moving each value, the diagonal course makes each value proposed miss, and its history-keeping realization, the growing prefixes, is held by no list. Extrapolation by elimination never passes a candidate that accords; where a determined law's orbit is held by a list of length N, over the lasso candidates it misses fewer than `tri N` times, and that it has come to rest cannot be refuted, though no prefix shows when. Lean: `Learning.no_prefix_fixes_next`, `Learning.diagonal_misses`, `Learning.diagonal_open`, `Learning.held_byElimination_bounded`; [record](../results/records/learning-by-refutation.md). Learning over merged classes has its own diagonal; rest's step is in [§6](#r-identification-where-bounded).

**The situation:** A forecaster reads the first ten days of a temperature record and, from those ten days, declines to name any value for day eleven. Two continuations of the record are considered, one with 20 degrees on day eleven and one with 25.

**From the result record:**

Copying words in turn from one shared pool and support, each from no previous token, and releasing each copy that completes with r free: every copy is released exactly when the pool supplies all the words joined together and the support covers all their draws and releases. ## Result 8

**From a chapter:**

**Theorem** (World). Under balance, only active formations giving and activity needing support, a formation taking from a condition at every step from `T` at which it is active, while nothing supported gives to it, is active for at most as many consecutive steps from `T` as the condition's level at `T` (`Flow.Balances.unfed_consumption_ends`); the same holds where it requires the condition while active and everything feeding its continuation is unsupported (`Flow.Balances.consumption_ends_with_feeders`): keeping its own support, it can still end through its feeder, as in `Overshoot`. Either of two redundant feeders suffices; withholding both leaves no balancing path ([record](../results/records/consumption-ends-with-feeders.md)).

**From the source file:**

/-- Unfed consumption ends. Under balance, with only active formations giving and activity
needing support, let `g` take from `c` at every step from `T` at which it is active, while
nothing supported gives to `c`. Then `g` is active for at most as many consecutive steps from
`T` as the level of `c` at `T`. -/

**The situation:** A workshop draws fuel from a store on every day it is running, and it is listed among the participants on each of those days. The accounts balance, only running participants give, and nothing runs without support. On some of those days a supplier that is itself present and supported delivers fuel to the store.

**From the result record:**
