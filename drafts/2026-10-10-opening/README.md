# The opening: drafts

The opening of the work is [theories/becoming/OPENING.md](../../theories/becoming/OPENING.md), which `make research-render` places at the top of the README. Ren's original wording is in [the source record](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-truth-exists-opening.md).

The drafts that led to it (the first draft, versions 2 to 9, the v6, v7 and v8 proposals, the notes on what each version changed, and the review) are in [history](history/INDEX.md), byte for byte (receipts `_process/architecture/moves/K1.json`, `opening-v7.json`, `opening-v8.json` and `opening-v9.json`). They sit one level deeper than when they were written, so their relative links resolve through the [manifest](history/MANIFEST.json); `OPENING-V6.md` was written for the opening's own place in `theories/becoming/`.

Version 7 was applied on 19 October 2026 ([source](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-19-opening-v7.md)) from the integrated text of [its proposal](history/OPENING-V7-PROPOSAL.md); the frozen copy is [OPENING-V7.md](history/OPENING-V7.md). Of the proposal's third-pass edits, (oo) and (oo′) took effect with Z1's landing, (pp) with PT1's, and (nn) with LL1's: T1 landed first, with its sentence in the note `[^conditions]`, and (nn)'s body sentence and LL1's part of that note went in with LL1. All four third-pass edits have taken effect.

Version 8 was applied on 20 October 2026 ([source](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-20-opening-v8.md)) in the recommended forms of [its proposal](history/OPENING-V8-PROPOSAL.md), A(b) and B(b): the sentence on the three doctrines says which feature each takes for a law of nature, without a civilizational address or a diagnosis of their holders, and the four "It is clear" became "It is clear to me". The frozen copy is [OPENING-V8.md](history/OPENING-V8.md).

Version 9 was applied on 21 October 2026 ([source](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-21-opening-v9.md)) from [the significance review's proposals](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-SIGNIFICANCE-REVIEW.md#proposals-for-opening-revised), TH1's thirteen as corrected by SA2: each sentence that said more than its law now says what the law shows, and the notes give each sentence's standing (theorem, or thesis T1, T3, T5, T6 or T7 with what would refute it) and the prior art it meets. The proposals are in the review, not a separate file. The frozen copy is [OPENING-V9.md](history/OPENING-V9.md).
