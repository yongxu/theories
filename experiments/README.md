## Freezing a test on a trained system

LA1, LA2 and LA3 each froze predictions, verdict rules, exact values and code in one commit before any call to the model. Their verdicts stand as frozen, and each README states its design's faults ([LA1](commons-llm-feedback/README.md#post-hoc-reading), [LA2](self-rewarding-lock/README.md#failures-and-limits-first), [LA3](shaping-what-comes-back/README.md#failures-and-limits-first)). What those faults teach the next frozen test, and the last five, added on 4 November 2026, from LA4's freeze review, UE31's run, two from TR7's freeze and one from TR9's:

- **Set a verdict only on a difference the feedback resolves.** LA1 required exactly m = 0 in its locked settings. In the two that failed, a round at m = 1 reported at least as much reward as a round at m = 0 with probability 0.28 and 0.45, so, as its post hoc reading says, the rule cannot tell a model that follows its feedback from one that does not. A power check on simulated feedback, run before the freeze, shows which differences a verdict can rest on.
- **Give each verdict rule the law's own quantifier.** The lock says that at every step neither the share nor the rule is below where it started, but LA2's rule for `late` compared only step 8 with step 4. Its judge answered whole calls one way, so its picks gave exactly inverse rules in turn, (11, 1) and (1, 11), and a course that never locked passed by an exact tie. The count over every step was computed and only reported.
- **Simulate the system the test may meet, not only noisy forms of the law and its rivals.** The power checks' scripted agents drew their answers with probabilities short of 0 and 1, and the followers among them explored. LA2's judge answered by what it read, all one way in 112 of its 119 calls (which is also why a test's unit is the call, not the pick), and so made the exact tie possible. LA3's agent followed its scores where it had seen both policies and its prior elsewhere, and never tried the other policy in 9 of 30 seeds. Readers whose answers are fixed by what they read, and such hybrids, belong among the simulated agents.
- **Measure exploration apart from selection.** LA3's bridge counted in one measure the seeds that chose by their scores and those that never saw the other policy, and every failure was of the second kind. A reviewer had named the point before the freeze and it was left in; a point that changes what a verdict says is settled before freezing. Assigning one round of each policy before free choice is another way.
- **Guard each support rule against contradiction.** A frozen rule that says what a pass supports reads every measure bearing on that claim, and reports the claim contradicted when one is resolved against it. LA2's rule for *the counts decide* read neither the one-note measure nor `late`'s first reading; both were resolved against it, and the rule printed the claim as supported.
- **Add a control wherever a rival passes every contrast.** In LA2's power check a judge deaf to the notes passed every contrast, so the design added a control, `in_time` with no note of the loss. It turned in 4 of 5 seeds, so `in_time`'s turn cannot be read as the loss, read in time, turning a course that would otherwise be kept.
- **Make the reading refuse a run it was not frozen for.** LA4's first freeze would have read an incomplete or enlarged run, a smoke run could land among the results, the pin could be moved, and the checks' outputs were not frozen. Its independent reader found all four before any call ([REVIEW-FREEZE](drain-beyond-the-reward/REVIEW-FREEZE.md)). A reading that takes whatever is in the folder can be given a run the design never planned, and its verdict looks the same.
- **Check that the inputs can meet every rule's minimum before freezing.** UE31's design took the cultures' length from another experiment of the same archive, and its dry runs measured power at that length. The cultures it read have 8 rows under the loss where the rules need 20, so every verdict on them is *not testable* ([the result](state-named-in-advance/README.md#the-result)). The archive's own description, which gave the length, was not read before the freeze. Read the inputs' layout, or the run's plan of calls, and check it against every rule's minimum before the design is frozen.
- **Say where an answer expected comes from: one read in sample can miss out of sample.** UE32 expected "not separated" from one model of MQ5's in-sample readings, a third of the stocks carrying an age structure's memory and the rest following T2's law; out of sample T2's numbers forecast better than the field's embedding, which that model gave in 6% of its archives. The run does not tell apart a minority of stocks whose memory does not pay its way out of sample from an in-sample excess that does not carry out of sample ([the result](state-against-embedding/README.md#the-result)).
- **A public report's page is an input's layout: read it, and count what the rules can reach on the structure.** T1N's design argued its headers could not be read before the downloads, which waited on a yes, and quoted UE31's lesson in doing so. The report's page named its column heads in its HTML, with no upper bound and no acres among them, and T1N refused at that header; its rows load by script, so a reader who must stay blind reads the HTML without running it. T1N's dry run estimated every synthetic lake's adults every three to five years, and surveyed age-0 yearly at most of them. On the real table's lakes, years and labels, with the stocking database's lakes, years and whether fish were stocked, its rules could read at most 9 lakes below the need with a fate resolved, whatever the table's values, where the verdict needs 10 ([the record](need-measured-in-advance/LAYOUT.md)). A count of that kind reads no estimate, so a freeze can ask for it and stay blind.
- **Change a shared harness only where every freeze reading it still reads its runs.** RF's later freezes import freeze 1's harness with freeze 1's hashes, so an addendum changing it refuses each of them until that freeze lands an addendum of its own, and that addendum must leave every folder the freeze has made read. Freeze 1 checks its addendum against its own folders only. A client change that reads a logged response otherwise, a provider's block moved from invalid to filtered for instance, would leave a later freeze's folders refused. TR7's independent reader found this before any call ([REVIEW-FREEZE](formations-that-read/told-tr7/REVIEW-FREEZE.md), T4). So a change to a shared harness is run first through the reader of every freeze that imports it, on that freeze's folders. Where it would refuse one, either the change waits until that freeze's runs are made and read, or that freeze does not take it and keeps its own link's tree: its remaining runs and its readings are then made in a checkout of its newest link's commit, where both chains check.
- **Write each log so that its reader reads every line, and refuse one cut short before any call.** Freeze 1's sink wrote U+2028, U+2029 and U+0085 raw, through `json.dumps` with `ensure_ascii=False`, and its reader splits a line at them, as `str.splitlines()` does, so one such answer would have left a folder its own code could never read again. A write cut short by a kill or a full disk, or cut just before its final newline, left the code unable to resume or read the folder. Both were found before any call, while TR7's fifth reader read ([REVIEW-FREEZE](formations-that-read/told-tr7/REVIEW-FREEZE.md), round 5). A freeze's own line writer over freeze 1's `open_sink` escapes the three, and each surrogate standing alone, and writes no later record or read to a log once a write to it fails ([FREEZE-4](formations-that-read/FREEZE-4.md#6-the-run), section 6). Each command refuses a cut log before any call, and `mend` carries out a procedure frozen beforehand: it moves the log's last line, and where a catalog read moves it puts in its place a read naming no snapshot, so that the calls the lost read may have closed are made again (section 8). The sixth reader found two faults in that procedure as first written: the next run's read closed a lost read's calls, and under `--parallel` whole lines could follow a cut one. Freeze 1's owner corrected it for every freeze on the harness (round 6). The seventh found that nothing but the text kept two commands of one pin apart, so each now holds the pin's lock while it works, as TR2's freeze holds one for each checkpoint (round 7). FREEZE-1's dated note gives the procedure for every folder written through freeze 1's sink ([FREEZE-1](formations-that-read/FREEZE-1.md)).
- **Judge a stop on an estimated rate by its chance of stopping at the rates it means to pass, and name what lifts it.** TR9's first readability rule projected each context's share of units read whole from the raw rates of an 80-call smoke run, and stopped below 0.8. At 2% unreadable, the rate it was meant to pass, it stopped about a third of pins in the contexts that compute, and each stop withheld a frozen test until an addendum. The rule now takes each rate at its one-sided 95% lower bound, and the dry run computes exactly the chance that it stops a pin at each rate: about once in a thousand at 2%. Counting only the newest link's calls then let any addendum, one changing only the dry run included, lift a stop with a new smoke run, so calls are now counted afresh only under a new client, the one file an addendum may change that bears on reading an answer. TR9's reader found both before any call ([REVIEW-FREEZE](formations-that-read/told-tr9/REVIEW-FREEZE.md), T18 and T30).
- **Name a positive outcome for what the test checks.** T1N's separation across the need came out positive in up to 40 of 40 archives of a world in which lakes lose their renewal for years to another cause (its round 2's N-28), and none of the guards tried separated that world from T1's own. The outcome now claims only that the second form of *Refuted by* was not met, counts the lakes that came back, and says the need's causal role is not shown, so the test's value is mainly its chance to refute ([T1N](need-measured-in-advance/README.md)).
- **When a verdict line's words change, check every reader of them.** T1N's fourth round reworded its positive line. The dry run's tally still matched the old words, so it counted positive outcomes with V2 met as plain ones, and no check parsed such a line (its round 5's N-60). A check that parses each kind of line the rules print would have caught it.
- **Compare a unit with itself where a pooled fit would lend it other units' chances.** T1R first read the rise against T1N's fit at each lake's low level. Where the lakes that lose their renewal go unstocked, the lakes read renew at any level, and the fit's chance at low levels came from the lost lakes, so the rise came out in 31 of 40 archives though no lake's renewal follows the level (its round 1's R-01). The rise is now read within each lake, against its own renewal below the need ([T1R](stocked-above-the-need/README.md)).
- **Place the two readings a comparison sets side by side alike against what could touch one alone, on both sides.** T1R's year read stood two years from a stocking and its year below one, so stocked fish suppressing age 0, and yearlings read as age 0, touched the year below alone (its round 2's R-17 and R-18). Kept two years from a stocking too, the year below then came before each lake's first stocking in nearly every lake, so stocking that comes with a programme and that no record shows reached the year read alone, and gave *rose* in 29 of 40 with no clause (its round 3's R-24); the rise now always names it. Round 1's rework had weighed what the stricter rule costs in power without running the worlds that showed what the looser one lets in: run the world where a rule's premise fails before weighing what the rule costs, and when a rule moves a reading, ask what the reading now falls on the other side of.
- **A year chosen by an estimate crossing a threshold is chosen more often when its conditions pushed the estimate across.** T1R compares each lake's renewal in a year whose spring estimate stood wholly below the need, chosen more often in a poor year, with one whose estimate stood wholly above it, chosen more often in a good one; where a year's conditions move both the count and the fall's age-0, the comparison favours a rise (the coordinator's point after T1R's round 1, and its round 3's R-27). Where the lakes that lost their renewal keep a little whatever their adults and a year's conditions scale both counts, the rise came out in up to 6 of 40 archives at 0.1. The clause the rise always carries names it, and the rise read with a year no estimate chose, each lake's first fall after its low survey, is reported beside it; it answers the low survey's year only.
- **Name a negative outcome by the test that decides it, not as the converse of the positive.** T1R's negative outcome was named *did not rise*, though it needed only that the rise was not shown, and in its own mixed world every archive that came out so leaned toward a rise (its round 2's R-16). It is now named by its test, a shortfall against the fit at the levels restored.
- **Where a second reader reads the files another reads, check that the two keep the same rows.** T1R's date reader read every walleye row of the stocking files, while T1N's reader keeps a stocking only from a row with fish stocked, so a row of no fish could date a stocking T1N's reader never saw and open a lake's window early (its round 4's R-28). T1N's test sits inside its reader and cannot be called, so a check now compares the two readers lake by lake.
