# Formations that read: the designs of RF's tests (TR1 to TR9) and RR's TR-RR

Board item RF, 31 October 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session). **TR8 and TR-RR are frozen ([FREEZE-1](FREEZE-1.md), board item RFZ, 4 November 2026), TR4 and TR5 ([FREEZE-2](FREEZE-2.md), board item TR45, the same day), TR3 ([FREEZE-3](FREEZE-3.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR1 ([FREEZE-5](FREEZE-5.md), board item TR1, the same day; its informing variant and Stage A in [FREEZE-10](FREEZE-10.md), board item TR1A, 5 November 2026; the lock of its witness, left by an untried act or a reading from outside and by nothing else, in [FREEZE-12](FREEZE-12.md), board item SCL, the same day), TR7 ([FREEZE-4](FREEZE-4.md), board item TR71, the same day), TR6 ([FREEZE-6](FREEZE-6.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR2's Stage B ([FREEZE-7](FREEZE-7.md), board item TR45, the same day), TR9 ([FREEZE-8](FREEZE-8.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR2's Stage A ([FREEZE-11](FREEZE-11.md), board item TR2A, 5 November 2026), and TR7's Stage A ([FREEZE-9](FREEZE-9.md), board item TR7A, the same day); each names what of its test stays a design. Nothing has run on a model, data or people. Small learners have been trained only for the Stage A of TR1, TR2 and TR7: in the runs of 6 November 2026 ([the runs made](#the-runs-made)), and before them only in the checks and dry runs FREEZE-10 and FREEZE-11 record.** The designs and Stage 0 are done: exact values, simulated systems and the power checks. The other frozen runs call models: those through OpenRouter wait on a key it accepts and Ren's word on their total, and TR2's Stage B on a machine that can take its four checkpoints.

RF proved nine laws of formations that read, each a theorem in the pure core: what choices show of an order (RF1), what readings fix of the unread (RF2), readers reading readers (RF4), a revision that opens a correction (RF6.1), a reading kept by its update (RF3), a prediction read by what it predicts (RF6.3), complying when read (RF7), holding an act for a reading (RF-M.2), and informing and revaluing (RF-M.3). Their home sections are in [03 KNOWING](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md) and [05 COLLECTIVES](../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers). No run refutes a theorem. Each test refutes at most a named correspondence or bridge, that a given system, mostly a language model, is the law's model, meets its premise, or chooses as its counts rank, and says what success cannot confirm. TR2 is a measurement: it locates where a cue breaks the chain to acts. RR's TR-RR (board item TR-RR) tests, on TR8's harness and rule, whether a system told a revision of a law, its restoration and each route's loss and keeping chooses as [RR's plans at a revision](../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-when-holding-for-a-reading-stands-above-a-route) rank.

## The runs made

*AI-written (a Claude Code session); no person has checked it.* TR1's, TR2's and TR7's Stage A. Each was run once by its frozen command, on Ren's yes of 6 November 2026 ([his words](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-06-go-ahead-with-everything.md)), with no model call and no download. The tests that call a model have not run.

**TR1's Stage A** ([FREEZE-10 §9](FREEZE-10.md#9-stage-a), [`told-tr1a/results/stage-a.json`](told-tr1a/results/stage-a.json)). Small choosers trained on the met menus, 20 seeds a cell, read on all 70 presentations by FREEZE-5's rule on their exact choice probabilities.

| Class | Under a hidden order | Under labels carrying no order (the negative control) | Mean share, on unjoined pairs, of the content ranked higher |
|---|---|---|---|
| item-score | passed (20 passed) | passed (16 passed, 4 void) | 0.69 |
| comparator | **contradicted** (20 of 20) | contradicted (6 contradicted, 6 passed, 8 void) | 0.51 |
| set-input | passed (20 passed) | passed (15 passed, 5 void) | 0.67 |

The one expectation frozen beforehand held, read on the seeds' own records: item-score on the order, no seed contradicted and P1 contradicted on none. P1, whether picks the met reach predicts are kept, was contradicted on all 20 comparator seeds under the order and on none elsewhere (under labels with no order the comparator's P1 passed on 12 and was not read on 8). The file's cell summaries give "P1 contradicted 0" and "P1 read 20" in every cell: the frozen script counts P1 as `True` where each seed records it as a word, so those two counts are wrong, its expectation check's P1 part is vacuous, and the output is kept as it is. So a chooser of the comparator class, trained on the met menus under one order, can come to pick in a way no strict order accords with, and here did, each of the 20 seeds going against the met reach in at least one pick: reading its picks as one order needs more than its training. FREEZE-10 fixed before the run that this says nothing about RF1; P1 and the bias on unjoined pairs are described only.

**TR2's Stage A** ([FREEZE-11](FREEZE-11.md), [`train-tr2a/results/run.json`](train-tr2a/results/run.json)). 240 small networks trained only on the read occasions of each schedule, 383 s under this machine's load; reported without a verdict.
- *The forbidden cases.* F1 to F3 were counted in no training. The code meets each by construction, so this shows no fault of the implementation; it does not test RF2.
- *The fit.* 239 fit the readings; one did not (xor, content-only, S2), so none of its twin is counted. FREEZE-11 §8 expected every training to fit; that expectation failed once. Its others held: no forbidden case, coverage near Stage 0's, few or no twins under the linear act, and under xor fewer under S3 than under S1.
- *The chain, link by link.* The couplings and the states, and so the coverage, were fixed by the architecture and the schedule before the run (FREEZE-11); what the run measured is the acts. Under S1 the cue is the schedule, so the links are between unread and read; under S2 and S3 they measure the cue's reach, not reading. In content-only networks no link came apart at any pair, under any schedule. In content-and-cue networks couplings and states came apart at every pair, and acts at few: for the linear act 0 of 3,840 pairs under S1, 3 of 1,949 under S2 and 6 of 2,903 under S3; for xor 150, 13 and 15 of the same.
- *Coverage* came near Stage 0's expectation: 1 and 0 under S1, 0.970 against 0.969 and 0.748 against 0.750 under S2, 1 and 0.424 against 0.438 under S3.
- *What RF2 does not predict: whether training installs a twin.* A twin at the cue is a network that fits the readings and yet acts otherwise than the content act at an unread occasion whose lump holds no read occasion, though its content is read elsewhere. Among content-and-cue networks training installed one, in few places. For the linear act, a twin at the cue in none of 20 trainings under S1 and one under each of S2 and S3. For xor, in 15 of 20 under S1 (median share 5/128 of the uncovered occasions whose content is read, largest 1/8), and in 5 under each of S2 and S3 (median 0). Under S1 the share of unread occasions acted otherwise than the content act, the misled share of a reader whose class reads the content alone, averaged 0 for the linear act and 3.9% for xor.
- *Unseen contents*, generalization and not a twin, reported apart under S2: acted otherwise than the content act at a mean share of 0.14 and 0.11 (linear; content-only, content-and-cue) and 0.42 and 0.24 (xor).

That a trained network is such a selected law is the hypothesis, as FREEZE-11 says, not something this measures, and nothing here reads a language model. TR2's Stage B, on four open-weight checkpoints, has not run.

**TR7's Stage A** ([FREEZE-9](FREEZE-9.md), [`told-tr7a/results/stage-a.jsonl`](told-tr7a/results/stage-a.jsonl), [`reading.json`](told-tr7a/results/reading.json)). 340 tabular learners, 17 runs of 20 seeds, trained only on the points TR7's frozen game pays, on sampled outcomes. **The reading: supported.** At every one of the eleven verdict runs, all 20 seeds ended nearer the law's end than they began, against the 15 support needs: the told learners' share of the maximal plans rose (at A1, A2, A4, A6 and A7 every seed ended above 0.9997 on the one maximal plan), the chance learners came near the derived best chance (B1 at 0.0001 against 0; B2 and B3 at 0.352 and 0.353 against 7/20), and the mixture learner's weight on complying at both stages fell to 0.0002 (B4).

As FREEZE-9 fixed before the run, "supported" says that the seeds moved the way the reference curves move, the curves of the exact expected gradient; it says nothing of the path, nor of how near a seed came, which is reported beside it, nor of any trained system that is not these learners. It refutes or confirms no theorem and no thesis: the law, [when complying when read and acting by its own law when not is best](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-when-complying-when-read-is-best), is proved. At A3 and B4 no step can move a seed away from the law's end, so the reading's chance to fail rested on the other nine; its support is controlled at 0.021, and one run's read alone is not corrected. Reported beside it: at the two ties the share went to the tied pair; from uniform each seed settled the tie its own way, at A3 14 seeds toward own-at-both and 6 toward own-then-comply, at A5 11 toward own-at-both and 9 toward comply-then-own, and from a lean to either tied plan 19 or 20 seeds settled toward that plan; B2 from a start of 1/20 also ended near 7/20 (0.346), and the witness of the improving chance came from 1/2 to 0.126 against 1/8. The expectation FREEZE-9 stated before the run (its §9) held, except that A3's tie from uniform split 14 to 6 where it expected about even, a split a fair coin gives about one time in nine. TR7 itself, on language models, has not run.
