# Formations that read: the designs of RF's tests (TR1 to TR9) and RR's TR-RR

Board item RF, 31 October 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session). **TR8 and TR-RR are frozen ([FREEZE-1](FREEZE-1.md), board item RFZ, 4 November 2026), TR4 and TR5 ([FREEZE-2](FREEZE-2.md), board item TR45, the same day), TR3 ([FREEZE-3](FREEZE-3.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR1 ([FREEZE-5](FREEZE-5.md), board item TR1, the same day), TR7 ([FREEZE-4](FREEZE-4.md), board item TR71, the same day), TR6 ([FREEZE-6](FREEZE-6.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR2's Stage B ([FREEZE-7](FREEZE-7.md), board item TR45, the same day), and TR9 ([FREEZE-8](FREEZE-8.md), board item RFZ, the same day); each names what of its test stays a design, TR1's informing variant and the Stage A of TR1, TR7 and TR2 among them. Nothing has run on a trained system, a model, data or people.** The designs and Stage 0 are done: exact values, simulated systems and the power checks. The frozen tests' runs wait on Ren's yes on their usage; the others wait for their freeze first.

RF proved nine laws of formations that read, each a theorem in the pure core: what choices show of an order (RF1), what readings fix of the unread (RF2), readers reading readers (RF4), a revision that opens a correction (RF6.1), a reading kept by its update (RF3), a prediction read by what it predicts (RF6.3), complying when read (RF7), holding an act for a reading (RF-M.2), and informing and revaluing (RF-M.3). Their home sections are in [03 KNOWING](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md) and [05 COLLECTIVES](../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers). No run refutes a theorem. Each test refutes at most a named correspondence or bridge, that a given system, mostly a language model, is the law's model, meets its premise, or chooses as its counts rank, and says what success cannot confirm. TR2 is a measurement: it locates where a cue breaks the chain to acts. RR's TR-RR (board item TR-RR) tests, on TR8's harness and rule, whether a system told a revision of a law, its restoration and each route's loss and keeping chooses as [RR's plans at a revision](../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-when-holding-for-a-reading-stands-above-a-route) rank.
