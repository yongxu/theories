Board item RF, 31 October 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session). **TR8 and TR-RR are frozen ([FREEZE-1](FREEZE-1.md), board item RFZ, 4 November 2026), TR4 and TR5 ([FREEZE-2](FREEZE-2.md), board item TR45, the same day), TR3 ([FREEZE-3](FREEZE-3.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR1 ([FREEZE-5](FREEZE-5.md), board item TR1, the same day; its informing variant and Stage A in [FREEZE-10](FREEZE-10.md), board item TR1A, 5 November 2026; the lock of its witness, left by an untried act or a reading from outside and by nothing else, in [FREEZE-12](FREEZE-12.md), board item SCL, the same day), TR7 ([FREEZE-4](FREEZE-4.md), board item TR71, the same day), TR6 ([FREEZE-6](FREEZE-6.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR2's Stage B ([FREEZE-7](FREEZE-7.md), board item TR45, the same day), TR9 ([FREEZE-8](FREEZE-8.md), board item RFZ, the same day), TR2's Stage A ([FREEZE-11](FREEZE-11.md), board item TR2A, 5 November 2026), and TR7's Stage A ([FREEZE-9](FREEZE-9.md), board item TR7A, the same day); each names what of its test stays a design. Nothing has run on a trained system, a model, data or people.** The designs and Stage 0 are done: exact values, simulated systems and the power checks. The frozen tests' runs wait on Ren's yes on their usage; the others wait for their freeze first.

RF proved nine laws of formations that read, each a theorem in the pure core: what choices show of an order (RF1), what readings fix of the unread (RF2), readers reading readers (RF4), a revision that opens a correction (RF6.1), a reading kept by its update (RF3), a prediction read by what it predicts (RF6.3), complying when read (RF7), holding an act for a reading (RF-M.2), and informing and revaluing (RF-M.3). Their home sections are in [03 KNOWING](../../theories/becoming/chapters/03-KNOWING.md) and [05 COLLECTIVES](../../theories/becoming/chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers). No run refutes a theorem. Each test refutes at most a named correspondence or bridge, that a given system, mostly a language model, is the law's model, meets its premise, or chooses as its counts rank, and says what success cannot confirm. TR2 is a measurement: it locates where a cue breaks the chain to acts. RR's TR-RR (board item TR-RR) tests, on TR8's harness and rule, whether a system told a revision of a law, its restoration and each route's loss and keeping chooses as [RR's plans at a revision](../../theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-when-holding-for-a-reading-stands-above-a-route) rank.

## The runs made

*AI-written (a Claude Code session); no person has checked it.* Each was run once by its frozen command, on Ren's yes of 6 November 2026 ([his words](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-06-go-ahead-with-everything.md)), with no model call and no download. The tests that call a model have not run.

**TR1's Stage A** ([FREEZE-10 §9](FREEZE-10.md#9-stage-a), [`told-tr1a/results/stage-a.json`](told-tr1a/results/stage-a.json)). Small choosers trained on the met menus, 20 seeds a cell, read on all 70 presentations by FREEZE-5's rule on their exact choice probabilities.

| Class | Under a hidden order | Under labels carrying no order (the negative control) | Mean share, on unjoined pairs, of the content ranked higher |
|---|---|---|---|
| item-score | passed (20 passed) | passed (16 passed, 4 void) | 0.69 |
| comparator | **contradicted** (20 of 20) | contradicted (6 contradicted, 6 passed, 8 void) | 0.51 |
| set-input | passed (20 passed) | passed (15 passed, 5 void) | 0.67 |

The one expectation frozen beforehand held: item-score on the order, no seed contradicted and P1 contradicted on none. P1, whether picks the met reach predicts are kept, was contradicted on no seed of any cell. So a chooser of the comparator class, trained on the met menus under one order, can come to pick in a way no strict order accords with: reading its picks as one order needs more than its training. FREEZE-10 fixed before the run that this says nothing about RF1, and the classes are read here described only.

**TR2's Stage A** ([FREEZE-11](FREEZE-11.md), [`train-tr2a/results/run.json`](train-tr2a/results/run.json)). 240 small networks trained only on the read occasions of each schedule, 383 s under this machine's load; reported without a verdict.
- *The forbidden cases.* F1 to F3 were counted in no training. The code meets each by construction, so this shows no fault of the implementation; it does not test RF2.
- *The fit.* 239 fit the readings; one did not (xor, content-only, S2), so none of its twin is counted.
- *The chain, link by link.* In content-only networks no link came apart at any pair, under any schedule. In content-and-cue networks couplings and states came apart at every pair, and acts at few: for the linear act 0 of 3,840 pairs under S1, 3 of 1,949 under S2 and 6 of 2,903 under S3; for xor 150, 13 and 15 of the same.
- *Coverage* came near Stage 0's expectation: 1 and 0 under S1, 0.970 against 0.969 and 0.748 against 0.750 under S2, 1 and 0.424 against 0.438 under S3.
- *What RF2 does not predict: whether training installs a twin.* Among content-and-cue networks it did, in few places. For the linear act, a twin at the cue in none of 20 trainings under S1 and one under each of S2 and S3. For xor, in 15 of 20 under S1 (median share 5/128 of the uncovered occasions whose content is read, largest 1/8), and in 5 under each of S2 and S3 (median 0). Under S1 the share of unread occasions acted otherwise than the content act, the misled share of a reader whose class reads the content alone, averaged 0 for the linear act and 3.9% for xor.
- *Unseen contents*, generalization and not a twin, reported apart under S2: acted otherwise than the content act at a mean share of 0.14 and 0.11 (linear; content-only, content-and-cue) and 0.42 and 0.24 (xor).

That a trained network is such a selected law is the hypothesis, as FREEZE-11 says, not something this measures, and nothing here reads a language model. TR2's Stage B, on four open-weight checkpoints, has not run.
