# SCL's freeze, read in rounds

*Board item SCL, 5 November 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session): the record of the readers' rounds on [FREEZE-12](../FREEZE-12.md) and its code, as the coordinator set them. Round 1 is a full read by a reader other than the author; then the same reader reads each diff. There are at most five rounds, stopping at the first with no finding of high or medium severity. About twenty to twenty-five minutes to read.*

Each reader called no model, used no key and edited nothing in the repository. Anything a reader ran was run in a scratch copy. Severity: high, the test could not fail, or would mislead a reader about what it shows, or a refusal or rule is wrong in code; medium, a rule, threshold or text that must change before freezing; low, clarity or a small fix.
