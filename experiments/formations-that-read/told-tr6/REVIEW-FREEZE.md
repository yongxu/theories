# Review of TR6's freeze (FREEZE-6): an independent reader, round by round

4 November 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session). A reader other than the author read the frozen files before the freeze landed: a fresh Claude Opus subagent, of the theory's own family, read them whole in the first round. From the second round on, at the coordinator's word, the same reader, continued, read the diff of the last round's fixes and the sections they touch. A fresh full read would follow only a high finding reaching beyond its section. The reader was given the files and the coordinator's requirements, and from the second round on this record, which it was asked to check, not to trust. It was given none of the session's conclusions otherwise. It worked read-only, with small checks of the code in memory or in scratch of its own outside the worktree, and called no model. Each round's findings are listed as it gave them, with how each was taken. The rounds go on until a round finds nothing of high or medium severity; a before-copy of every file is kept each round, outside the repository.
