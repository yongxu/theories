4 November 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session). From the second round on, at the coordinator's word, the same reader, continued, read the diff of the last round's fixes and the sections they touch. The rounds go on until a round finds nothing of high or medium severity; a before-copy of every file is kept each round, outside the repository.
