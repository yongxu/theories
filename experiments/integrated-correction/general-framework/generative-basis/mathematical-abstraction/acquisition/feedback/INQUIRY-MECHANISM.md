# Acquired completion changes whether a remaining question is pursued

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation under the [nondual method](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/history/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). Named connection: **an actual consequence → acquired knowledge of local completion → changed continuation of inquiry**. The question concerns one specified productive-use episode. It does not establish learned ultimate purposes, a new planning theorem, or completion of the unified foundation.

**Status:** the [prospective protocol](INQUIRY-PROTOCOL.md), [additive implementation](inquiry.py) and [first run of fifteen fixed cases](inquiry-results.json) realize the local connection described below, accepted by [independent reconstruction and review](../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/INQUIRY-COMPLETION-REVIEW.md). The [independent receipt](inquiry-independent-check.json) checks all fifteen cases, 321 extension events, 1,396 acquisition/first-use event occurrences and fourteen unique source bindings. Repeated shared prefixes are not independent empirical observations. No producer or price changes followed the first run. The [preceding refinement study](REFINEMENT-MECHANISM.md) remains unchanged.

The original all-word sensory learning contract is preserved; it is not replaced with next-reading prediction or a smaller acquisition task.

## A compact record and its exact scope

The supplied installation forms three outcome slots alongside H and sixteen controller cells. Each slot is unknown, zero or one. The phase index is computed from H and the actual issued word; it is not read from hidden native phase. The new contact supplies the consequence, and a charged write stores it. A receipt that cannot be acquired or written does not become a stored result.

For the declared stationary Boolean readiness family, the table has a simple factorized meaning. Write `T[q]=?` for an unknown slot and define compatibility with the acquired table alone by

`R(T) = {r in {0,1}^3 : T[q] is known implies r[q] = T[q]}`.

This is a mathematical interpretation of the table. The controller does not enumerate eight maps or obtain their true member from the auditor. Under faithful binding, stable phase correspondence and truthful contact, each committed slot update preserves the actual readiness map in `R(T)` and intersects the previously compatible family with the acquired coordinate value. An unknown slot remains unconstrained.

`R(T)` describes table-only information, not indistinguishability under every possible reader of the full process. Fuel changes, operation denials or other accessible conditions might support an additional inference under suitable premises and an implemented reader. This controller does not convert those signals into an outcome-slot value. Its table can therefore remain unknown without establishing that the outcome is unknowable through every available relation.

The two-map row `[0,1,?]` is sufficient for the specified episode because the successful inquiry has itself delivered the required effect. It is not sufficient to answer phase two's outcome, predict the entire application map, or certify an indefinitely repeatable support process. The remaining question is unnecessary to complete this episode; it may matter to another undertaking.

Actual fulfillment, its acquired indication and a retained record must remain distinct. The implementation's external `actual_episode_fulfilled` report inspects actual transfers. The controller instead reads its own table. A false positive can therefore produce a reported completion without the required effect; a false negative can leave the controller searching after the effect has occurred. Neither the table nor a final retention flag establishes the contact premises by itself.

## How the record changes the next operation

After each successful slot write, the controller expends the declared work to read the table for a positive. If it finds one, the ordinary completion reader ends this episode. Otherwise it begins a unary walk through H, constructs the shortest reaching word incrementally, and selects the first unknown phase. It inspects known slots and extends the current word with explicit work and workspace charges. It does not reconstruct a full candidate family or repeatedly simulate every word prefix.

After the faithful first zero, the selected word is `a`, reaching learned state one. A positive acquired result stops the search with phase two still unknown. A negative result leads to a new search and `aa`, reaching the only remaining untested phase. A later actual arrival can fund subsequent contact and retention, but cannot fund the commands that produced it retrospectively. The native operation, receipt acquisition and slot update occur separately.

The rule is an ordinary finite feedback procedure. Its implementing transition law, shortest-word ordering, memory recipe and one-extraction completion condition are supplied. The acquired knowledge changes the operative decision to continue; the study does not show that the learner formed or revised its criterion of sufficiency. A fixed implementing law does not erase the demonstrated dependence on acquired outcomes, but that dependence does not explain the law's own formation.

The controller does not inspect hidden source stock. Native denial and actual fuel charges constrain its execution; the external whole-history account establishes whether the resulting trajectory was feasible. This is different from an internally computed certificate that enough stock remains for every branch.

## Relation to established sufficiency and inquiry results

The [existing finite contingent-policy argument](../../../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/INQUIRY-SUFFICIENCY-MATH.md#4-a-finite-contingent-continuation-criterion) already gives the conditional `W_n` characterization. It explicitly includes inquiry's changes to actual conditions and permits an inquiry to fulfill its undertaking. It also requires a separate implementation argument. The current construction supplies one finite realized connection; it neither proves that characterization again nor implements a general `W_n` solver.

No policy can guarantee a positive arrival over the full admitted family after the first failure: `000` has no productive phase. The saved runs succeed in all three productive worlds `001,010,011` under the specified support, stable correspondence and faithful contacts. This is a conditional finite-case result that preserves `000` as failure. The controller does not exclude `000` or count a nonproductive halt as completion to manufacture an unconditional guarantee.

There is also no universally best query order supplied by the evidence alone. Testing phase one first avoids a wasted packet in `010`; testing phase two first would avoid one in `001`. The shortest-word order is a declared local recipe, not a learned probability distribution or unique optimal policy. The [attainable-questions analysis](../../../../../../../theories/becoming/history/ATTAINABLE-MATHEMATICAL-QUESTIONS.md) already preserves conflicts between information and actual opportunities. The present case must not be reported as a general proof that additional information is harmful.

## Realized continuation and resource boundary

In the primary `010` history, the phase-one extraction is funded before its ten-unit arrival, leaving `107057/65536` fuel at that point. The receipt and slot write then produce `[0,1,?]`. The charged completion read stops further inquiry, and final retention succeeds. The extension expends `54623/16384`, ending with fuel `178253/16384` and one ten-unit source packet. Peak storage is 33 cells; retained storage is 26. Its exact full-history account is:

`145 = 4 preparation + 1 old adapter + 67 old operation + 10 old waste + 117909/4096 acquisition/first use + 10 first-use waste + 54623/16384 extension + 178253/16384 final fuel + 10 remaining source`.

This actual arrival belongs to the inquiry itself. It does not occur after a new completion judgment has somehow caused an earlier success. The acquired judgment changes what follows: no phase-two inquiry is issued, and the remaining packet is preserved. No further extraction is executed after stopping, and the procedure does not establish indefinite renewal.

The `001` history first records the failed phase-one attempt, then constructs and executes `aa`. After funding that final extraction, before its arrival, only `591/8192` fuel remains. The actual ten-unit arrival enables its receipt, slot write, completion reading and final retention. The result is `[0,0,1]`, final fuel `76335/8192`, and no remaining source. Both the conditional choice of another inquiry and its execution occur in the same history, without an incoming grant.

The blocked `000` history shares this latter Account prefix through all 28 events ending at the final extraction. It likewise has `591/8192` fuel and source zero, but receives no incoming support. Its next receipt would require `2052/8192`; the operation is denied. Final retention also fails. The table remains `[0,0,?]`, not `[0,0,0]`. The last negative is not acquired through the receipt or written to the table, although the audit knows it. The denial itself could support another implemented inference; no such reader is installed here. The entire search has physically consumed its opportunity without achieving or reporting completion.

## Causal contrast and preserved failures

The disconnected-reader comparison retains the same records and expends the same completion-reading work, but suppresses that read's effect on stopping. In `010`, it proceeds to the still-unknown phase two after a real phase-one arrival. That operation acquires more information while consuming the final packet without another arrival. The extension expends `41573/8192`, ending with fuel `74865/8192`, source zero and the full table `[0,1,0]`. In `011`, the same continued inquiry produces an additional ten-unit arrival, ending at fuel `156785/8192` with `[0,1,1]`. Both outcomes were fixed comparisons in the prospective protocol. The ordinary stopping rule is appropriate to the stated one-extraction episode; it is not uniformly superior for every possible continuation purpose.

The primary and disconnected `010` arms have identical first eighteen Account events, through the positive slot write and charged completion read. Their acquired table and actual incoming support also agree. The subsequent difference concerns whether acquired completion controls further inquiry, rather than whether one arm had a better sensor model, different earlier funding or a missing outcome record.

The compact implementation also changes representation and work envelopes relative to the older refinement controller. Its different feasibility cannot be attributed solely to one changed decision rule. The matched reader interventions within this implementation establish the narrower causal connection while holding those other conditions fixed.

The ancestry controls preserve the support tradeoff. Exposure eight cannot fund installation despite having more source. Exposure nine with the old novel first word acquires the first zero but cannot fund the next native reset or final retention. Exposure ten under `010` funds a successful phase-one continuation and retention, using its last packet. Under `001`, it expends that packet at phase one and records zero; the subsequently selected phase-two operation is denied for lack of source, although table retention succeeds. More fuel is not another packet.

A lost first contact leaves all three slots unknown, with the installed blank table retained. A false-positive first contact reports completion immediately despite no productive arrival. A false-positive phase-one contact under `001` likewise stops with `[0,1,?]` while the actual episode remains unfulfilled. A false-negative phase-one contact under `010` produces the opposite mismatch: the actual arrival occurs, but the stored zero causes further inquiry, whose phase-two failure leaves a fully retained `[0,0,0]`. The controller reports all outcomes recorded without reported completion, although the episode was actually fulfilled. These are preserved failures of correspondence, not successful diagnoses.

The unresolved explanatory relation remains the formation and warranted revision of the undertaking, its completion interpretation, the inquiry recipe and its contact assumptions. This construction concerns acquired knowledge governing a supplied local episode. It does not turn an analyst's success score into a learned reward, establish legitimate experimental risk, or derive ethical concern from a ten-unit return.
