This is a direct conditional consequence of the family and acquired record. The record does not establish that `aa` will succeed. The ensuing test can itself accomplish the productive effect; inquiry and use need not be separate events. A successful faithful receipt then leaves complement as the sole map in this family. Failure would instead leave constant zero. Neither conclusion extends automatically beyond the family.

The original model is not relabeled to `001`, and its all-word certificate is not weakened to the observed examples. No hidden phase, inversion flag, raw target table or auditor's waste record may select the new word. It must be generated from the retained model, acquired consequence record and supplied candidate machinery. The first failed packet and any new expenditures remain consumed.

The retained failed application makes that relation untenable within the admitted family. Subsequent inquiry must discriminate an application relation on which the remaining explanations disagree; repeating an experiment whose arrival prediction they already share does not resolve it.

The continuation's purpose of productive use can remain stable through this change. What changes is the evidence and relation required to support the next application. This is a bounded acquired revision of applicability, not proof that every undertaking or value was learned. The fixed encompassing implementation can realize the changing criterion without another controller being introduced above every revision.
