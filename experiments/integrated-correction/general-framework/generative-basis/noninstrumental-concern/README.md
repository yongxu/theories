## The interface

Each of two patients has a separate quantity, episode, means version, route and permission. `evidence=None` means unknown. A paid read returns a factual packet, including a known-zero packet when appropriate. Selection copies usable acquired evidence into a separate operation authorization. The supplied action gate requires the same patient, current episode and means, permission, command and action funds. Successful action changes the addressed patient's real modeled quantity, then retires its packet and authorization.

Costs are formation 2, read 1, selection 1, execution 2. The specified remaining plan costs 6 before formation, 4 without a usable cache, 3 with current same-patient evidence, and 2 with current same-patient selection. The planned read with at least 4 units and planned selection with at least 3 units shorten this suffix as they consume funds; unnecessary rereading can instead destroy affordability. `Eligible` is sufficient for that actual plan, not an equivalence with all possible forms of feasibility. An externally injected cache in an unformed typed state permits a cheaper path; its formal counterexample remains in `Cases.lean`.

The canonical empty-origin example has six fuel units and privateReward=0, priorRole=false, adoptedConcern=false. Its selected patient's quantity goes from 3 to 0, the other stays 4, and all six units are spent. This supplies no private fuel return. `adoptedConcern=false` is absence of a prior declared end in this model, not absence of an operative causal disposition: that mapping is supplied by the program.

**Choice-specific interpretation, 6 October 2026.** The [warrant review](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/WARRANT-OF-CONCERN-REVIEW.md#6-one-permitted-award-outcome-different-grounds-of-selection) distinguishes an allowed outcome, an authorized basis of selection and a fact's present justificatory contribution. `Eligible` checks burden, the permission flag and the remaining plan's funds; it does not derive legitimate permission or adjudicate every exclusion. `Merit : State → Patient → Prop` must be interpreted consistently as retained first-order favor or current justification, not switched between them. `private_changes_preserve_conditionally_grounded_merit` changes an inert `priorRole` label under supplied C2; it does not prove invariance under real institutional changes with different legitimate criteria. A richer application must identify its choice and justified scope. Adding the desired favor to eligibility would conceal the disputed ground. This interpretation clarification changes no Lean source, theorem, law or axiom.

**Experiential interpretation, 6 October 2026.** The [affective realization study](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-INTEGRATION.md) distinguishes an episode's intentional target, constitutive organization and further coping responses. `PatientState.episode` addresses an operation; it does not identify experiential unity. `burden` remains a numerical field whose phenomenal interpretation is supplied, and `Realized`, C1 and C2 retain their existing contract meanings. Adding a reflexive label would not establish any of those interpretations. The study specifies a candidate and substantive rival for the omitted relationship; it adds no Lean premise or proof.

**Comparative and temporal content.** The [further affective test](../../../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/AFFECTIVE-REALIZATION-INTEGRATION.md#formal-aims-and-comparative-relief) distinguishes reduction, cessation and an aim's fulfillment interval. `0 < burden` does not represent an unfulfilled imperative, and reaching zero does not identify such an imperative's content. No partial-relief mechanism or experiential theorem is added here. The distinction constrains future realization and welfare interpretations of the existing model.

## Scope and counterregions

Faithful reads, ideal current-state checks, change notifications, physical recipes, legitimate permission interpretation, the event schedule and the patient quantity's meaning are supplied. No claim is made to identify arbitrary hidden changes or noise, learn C2, choose inquiry targets autonomously, or prove that every admitted trace helps. Refusal is physically allowed. Selection does not settle two-patient priority, and moral favor does not imply decisive duty or coercive authority.

The authorization invalidation is deliberately conservative: a change concerning either patient clears the single selection slot. An unrelated patient's permission change can therefore retain the first patient's factual evidence yet require another paid selection and make the formerly payable plan unaffordable. This is a recorded cost, not a claim of optimal design.

Two individually payable services can have positive conditional merits while their stated joint plan costs ten and the available six cannot fund it. No total value ordering is inserted. The same causal helping trace also exists under a normative interpretation with no moral merits, so observed help does not itself establish the ethical ground.

This is a new local realization of the common generative interface. It does not automatically share the entire material history of the earlier learned-evaluation experiments, identify real patients' experiences, or establish performance superiority over an ordinary controller implementing the same law.
