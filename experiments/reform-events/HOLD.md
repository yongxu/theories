# T7F held: T7's field test of reform events, attempted and not frozen as designed

*Board item T7F, 5 November 2026. AI-written (a Claude Code session); no person's review of it is recorded. A holding limit, dated, with its reason: it holds so far and lapses when a design of this test is frozen. Nothing here was frozen, downloaded or sent to a model. About four to five minutes to read.*

## What was attempted

[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) designs a field test of [T7](../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#t7), the thesis that a measure read from what it selects outlives its refutation. It has a frame fixed by features documented before any refutation (the rankings an inventory reviewed, and China's provincial GDP growth targets), a [codebook](CODEBOOK.md) that coders from families that neither wrote nor read the theory would apply, a verdict rule, the drift bound of the drawn model (`DrawnReplacement.lost_above`), two positive controls, and a dry run on planted cases.

Readers who did not write it read it in eleven rounds, all recorded in [REVIEW-FREEZE.md](REVIEW-FREEZE.md):

- five rounds, then a sixth that the coordinator approved;
- a hold after the sixth, which still found a high finding, and a simplification of where the faults came from (the packets the coders read, and absence coding);
- five fresh rounds by a new reader.

Every round found at least one finding of medium severity or higher. Apart from the hold's simplification, each fix added rules, and the dry run grew from 59 checks to 234. By the coordinator's rule the rounds stop here. Fresh round 5's fixes were not read by a reader, and the design is not frozen.

## Why it could not be frozen as designed

1. **Absence.** T7's *Refuted by* needs a coding fixed in advance that finds no new reader outside the ranked share, no change in what the ranking reads and no loss entering the selectors' share. Those are findings of absence. A coder can report what the documents read show, but not what the record lacks. The rules for coding absence, and for what the coders read, needed further rules at every round, and each round found faults in them.
2. **Drift.** The *Refuted by*'s drift condition needs the selectors' number and their share's distance from the critical mass, read by the drawn model's premises: one member drawn at a time, constant readings and cost. No reading of it was made on any record, since nothing was downloaded. The readings this design tried, from voting thresholds and from turnover, were faulted by its readers. The last one needs a body's every replacement over ten years, listed by the kind of who entered and who left, which few public records are expected to hold (from memory, not checked); in simulation it seldom reads "rare" even where the lock holds. So the drift condition was out of reach of this attempt. That holds so far, from 5 November 2026, and lapses when a reading of the condition on a kept record passes a reader round.
3. **Controls.** A refutation counts only where the positive controls show their known exits. The controls guard the absence findings of reason 1, and they are coded by the same rules, so they share its faults.

## What remains possible

A smaller test, which anyone may claim; it is not started:

- one frame;
- whole records only, with no packets to assemble; a case whose record is too large to show whole is left out of the test by a rule fixed before any record is read (a larger cap is Ren's to give);
- three codes: kept, dropped by its selectors, and reformed in its reading alone, with the exits (a new reader, a new reading, a loss entering the share) still coded on the whole record after a reform;
- the two positive controls kept, each having to show its known exit;
- no drift reading.

Its third case and its controls still rest on absence findings, as reason 1 says. Whole records are what would make those findings "not in the record" rather than "not shown in what was read". But the first round (T7F-R1-1) found realistic records too large to fit whole under the present cap, so whether any case fits is open until the smaller test's cap is fixed and its records are counted.

Where its records fit whole, it could meet T7's third forbidden case; meeting it would count against that case, as LA2 counts against the fourth. On the first case it could report drops by the selectors with no exit found, but without the drift reading it cannot tell them from drift alone, so it cannot meet the *Refuted by*. So it is a "not yet", not a ban: a field test of the third case where records fit whole, and of the *Refuted by* once a reading of its drift condition on a kept record passes a reader round.
