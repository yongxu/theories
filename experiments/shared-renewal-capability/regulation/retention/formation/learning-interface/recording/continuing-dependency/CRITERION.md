# Actual consequences change the operative completion criterion

6 October 2026. AI-authored continuation of the [existing material dependency study](README.md), under the project's [nondual method](../../../../../../../../theories/becoming/history/NONDUAL-METHOD.md). The [prospective protocol](CRITERION-PROTOCOL.md), [executable extension](criterion.py) and [saved result](criterion-results.json) address one named connection: **an already completed component encounters a further obstruction → acquired restoration evidence forms a more specific continuation requirement → the new requirement changes both reconstruction and completion of the protocol**.

The result is conditional completion-criterion acquisition. The continuing maintenance undertaking remains supplied. The construction does not derive an ultimate purpose, ethical standing, a uniquely necessary physical law or universal favorable direction. It also does not join this material history to the separate Evaluation/Succession or InquiryRevision histories. Its actual ancestor is the original material formation history already used in this study.

## Why this is a distinct advance

Prior work already establishes consequence-sensitive learning, revision of inquiry, acquisition of a finer application distinction and stopping inquiry on acquired fulfillment of a fixed component. The original material law also already contains repair that changes its own applicability. The present extension does not rename those achievements.

Here the initial operative completion criterion checks that the admitted A-count replacement is complete and the protocol can return. It initially contains no learned repair-support channel. Actual later damage prevents capture despite that completed count. A coupled repair encounter and restored capture then provide acquired evidence through which a particular support requirement becomes part of the criterion. The initial count completion remains a true recorded historical event.

The updated criterion is used twice: to choose between available reconstruction plans, and to decide whether a consuming handoff can occur. The latter distinction is isolated by a forced-route control. Thus the criterion is not only an analyst's description, a new recommendation label or a better ranking of unchanged completion outputs.

## The acquired content changes an actual choice

A fixed continuation loses the local unit and269 common units, encounters another free-A wear, and opens the next episode at a charge of one common unit. The decision begins at common4. Reading and selecting consumes one unit, leaving3.

The selector accounts for its future criterion reading and the handoff, each consuming one common unit. With the faithful learned common requirement, bulk copying cannot preserve enough common support for both operations and the later repair. The local route can. The learned criterion therefore qualifies the local route.

The matched clamped consumer retains the same acquired record, activated criterion, physical decision boundary and reading expenditure. They do not have the same full endpoint: one retains common3/source214, the other common2/source215. That differentiated relation is the point of the comparison.

No global score or future benefit is credited to the learner. Actual native effects and the acquired channel determine the selected plan within the declared two-plan family. The copy recipes and current-state access are supplied; the construction does not learn those recipes or implement an unrestricted planner.

## Completion itself has operative consequences

In the primary branch, the final reading leaves common2. The revised criterion closes the reconstruction protocol while reserving one unit for handoff and one for the specified later repair. Handoff consumes one, leaving1. Actual damage then occurs and native repair consumes the retained unit. Both handoff and the later repair execute. Common support is then zero: no further charged observation or handoff is thereby available. This is finite completion, not an indefinite renewal or inquiry result.

The clamped branch reaches common1 after the same final reading. Its count-only reader closes. The actual handoff consumes the last unit. After the same damage, repair is disabled, and no original or added event replenishes common support.

A forced-bulk control preserves the faithful learned criterion but intervenes on plan selection. It reaches exactly the clamped branch's reconstruction state, including its record, acquired criterion and resource ledger. The final reader has the same charge. The learned criterion now refuses closure at common1, because the unit cannot support both handoff and repair. Handoff is disabled by the completion gate even though its own one-unit expenditure is affordable. The unspent unit subsequently enables actual repair after the imposed damage.

This control isolates an effect of the revised completion criterion beyond plan choice: the changed completion gate changes whether a real consuming operation occurs and consequently changes later repair capacity. It does not declare the forced branch completely successful. Its A reconstruction and later repair occur, but its handoff remains uncompleted and its protocol remains open. The native law does not permit a refill that would erase that unresolved condition.

Damage is externally imposed in the fixed test. The completion gate does not control its occurrence. Native repair remains governed by the original physical guard; the gate affects its support through the preceding handoff.

## What remains supplied and what would defeat the result

The continuation scope and disturbance schedule, native physical law, finite support-consumption family, contact/read/write/assembly recipes, their tariffs, copy-plan family and forward budget composition remain supplied. They belong to this local explanation and are not thereby external to generative reality. Their origins and their ethical significance remain further explanatory questions.

The blocked/restored observations occur with an active resident, an empty local store at the attempt, and available source. They do not establish that intactness alone is globally sufficient for capture. The learned support channel does not prove an arbitrary future law has no fallback, failure or additional prerequisite. The broader maintenance undertaking is independently admitted; acquired evidence changes its concrete operative completion content rather than creating that direction from nothing.

An invalid native step, hidden resource reset, double-counted memory part, receipt supplied independently of its bound repair, or failure of the matched-record/physical-state comparisons would defeat the executed connection. A successful original-law path replenishing common support would defeat its retained irreversibility claim. A real fallback or context change would narrow the three-channel identification; the separate countermodel exhibits that possibility instead of defining it away.

For ethics, more material reconstruction or available repair is not itself a proof of good. The example supplies a concrete generative connection that a fuller account of purpose correction can use: activity encounters that its present completion description omits a continuing dependency, acquires a more specific condition, and lets that acquired condition change later activity without rewriting the earlier completed event.
