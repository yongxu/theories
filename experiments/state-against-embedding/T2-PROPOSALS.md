Proposals for T2's owner, documents only: nothing here changes [02 §5](../../theories/becoming/chapters/02-MAP.md#t2), [08's row](../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md) or [S-15](../../theories/becoming/review/SCIENCES.md#s-15) until that owner takes it. They come from UE31 and UE31b, which read T2 in the flour beetle, and from the sciences dossier's question 4. **Proposal 3 taken on 6 November 2026,** on the run it waited for ([UE31b's result](../state-named-in-advance/README.md#ue31bs-result), [study §509](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#509-t2-in-the-flour-beetles-cultures-by-cell-and-its-wording-narrowed-ue31bs-run)). **Taken on 4 November 2026:** proposals 1, 2 and 4 are in 02 §5, 08's row and S-15 ([study §505](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#505-t2s-statement-the-level-alone-at-the-regimes-step-end-the-horizons-grain-and-the-lags-place-t2s)); proposal 3 waits on a frozen run that fails the clause. The study also names what the level alone is set against, the three numbers, so of the readings listed under proposal 1 only MQ5's report read the item; MQ3's F2 and MQ4's F5 read other claims. Each gives the argument, the text proposed and what it changes in what T2 forbids. About fifteen minutes to read.

T2, as 02 §5 states it: "The state of a renewable condition under a draw is its level, its recent giving and one lag; with the draw they fix the next giving as far as anything measured does, the regime's step and the frozen rest its two ends." It forbids "a fourth variable removing a tenth of the residual variance in the median series; the level alone forecasting as well at five years; the lag's coefficients changing sign between series".

## 1. The second forbidden item at the regime's-step end (S-15's question 4)

**The tension.** T2's law of the giving, in the three-number form MQ5 read, is γ_t = φ γ_{t−1} + (1 − φ) g(E_t) + λ (E_{t−1} − E_t) ([MQ5's predictions](../margin-measured/PREDICTIONS-2026-10-21-MQ5.md), the state with the lag). At φ = 1 and λ = 0 it is the frozen rest, which holds the giving as it was. At φ = 0 and λ = 0 it is the regime's step, whose next giving is g(E_t), a function of the level alone; at φ = 0 with λ ≠ 0 the giving still reads the lag, λ (E_{t−1} − E_t) = λ (H_t − γ_{t−1}), so the step needs both. T2's sentence names the two as its law's ends. At the regime's-step end the level alone fixes the next giving, so it forecasts as well at one step and at five. So the second forbidden item, as worded, is met by T2's own law at one of the ends T2 names: a series sitting there would count against T2 although T2's law holds of it. The sciences dossier reads MQ6's yeast at a constant dilution as such a series, the level alone the whole state, and asks whether the item should exclude that end and what would mark a series as near it ([S-15](../../theories/becoming/review/SCIENCES.md#s-15), question 4).

**What would mark a series as near that end.** Not a failure to detect the recent giving. Detection is limited by power: in MQ5's frozen dry runs its S1 found the recent giving in only 67 of 145 stocks generated by T2's own law in the spawning-biomass reading, and in 47 of the 69 stocks of its run. An item that lapsed wherever the recent giving went undetected would reach only the series long or clean enough to detect it, and would lapse most where the data are poorest. The mark must be shown, not inferred from silence: a series is at the regime's-step end when the recent giving and the lag together are shown to add little beyond the level one step ahead, by an upper bound on what they add. The bound can borrow T2's own measure, the first item's tenth of the residual variance, so that no new value is chosen for the purpose.

**Proposed.** Text for the *Forbids*:

> the level alone forecasting as well at five steps, in a series not shown to sit at the regime's-step end (where the recent giving and the lag together remove less than a tenth of the level's residual variance one step ahead, at the upper bound of a test fixed before the data)

**What it changes.** The item forbids less, and only in series shown to sit at the end: a series too short or noisy for the showing is still within the item. In MQ6's constant dilution the showing would have to be made before the item could exclude it; it was not measured there.

It changes no verdict read so far. The fish-stock tests read the level alone at five years three times, on stocks near the frozen-rest end (φ = 0.96, chosen on other stocks, also fitting MQ4's best):
- **MQ3's F2:** the level alone (the regime's step, RM) at five steps against the frozen rest and momentum on direction (0.62 against 0.68 and 0.61) and against the frozen rest and persistence on error (median |log error| 0.30 against 0.19 and 0.19); it failed.
- **MQ4's F5:** RM against the level with the recent giving (LG) at five years. Pooled, the level alone did worse: median |log error| 0.270 against 0.148, direction 0.633 against 0.678. Per stock, LG was better in 43 of 76 (p = 0.30), so stock by stock the two were not told apart.
- **MQ5, reported, not a verdict:** RM against T2's three numbers (LGD) at five years, 0.392 against 0.194, direction 0.467 against 0.618.

None was a series shown to sit at the end, so none changes under the proposal. In the flour beetle, UE31's and UE31b's V4 read the item at five census steps on every culture or cell, in its strong form: V4 fails T2 only where the level alone forecasts *better* on both of its criteria (UE31 §6), not merely as well. Under the proposal V4 would first need the showing, cell by cell, which their rules do not make; that is a change to a frozen rule, so it would hold for designs frozen after it, not for UE31b.

## 2. "At five years" read at the grain the three numbers are read

**The tension.** T2 claims its three numbers for every renewable condition under a draw, but its second item gives its horizon in years, the fisheries' step. UE31 and UE31b read it at five census steps of the beetle, and S-15 reads it at the culture's own grain; neither is what the words say. A record's grain is its recorder's choice, so "five steps of the record" alone would let the horizon move with the recorder.

**Proposed.** "at five steps of the grain at which the three numbers are read, fixed before the data (five years in the fisheries)". With proposal 1, the item reads: "the level alone forecasting as well at five steps of the grain at which the three numbers are read, fixed before the data (five years in the fisheries), in a series not shown to sit at the regime's-step end".

**What it changes.** Nothing tested: every test so far read it this way, with the grain fixed before its data (UE31 §4 fixed the census, and reports every other census without a verdict). It makes the item say so.

## 3. "As far as anything measured does", once a frozen test fails it

**The tension.** UE31 expects this clause to fail in the beetle, where the pupae counted now are the next recruits, and UE31b expects the same of the cells: W fails in every dry-run archive where the pupae are the recruits to be ([UE31b §1](../state-named-in-advance/cells/DESIGN.md)). 02 §5 already names what would remain: "as far as the level's own history does". Neither run has read a culture under a set loss: UE31's were too short and UE31b waits on Ren's yes.

**Proposed.** Keep the clause until a frozen test's run fails it. When one does, record the failure in 02 §5 and 08's row, in the domain where it happened, and narrow the clause in 02's own words, with the failure kept beside it:

> with the draw they fix the next giving as far as the level's own history does (as far as anything measured does failed in the flour beetle, where a stage before recruitment is counted: [the run])

UE31 had proposed "as far as anything in the condition's own course does". 02's words came later and say the same; this proposal uses 02's.

**What it changes.** The narrowed clause forbids less: a measured variable outside the level's history, such as a stage before recruitment, may then carry more without counting against T2. The first forbidden item, "a fourth variable removing a tenth of the residual variance", would then need the same reach: "a fourth variable from the level's own history". The failure stays on record as a loss of the broader claim, not a narrowing chosen in advance. The narrowed clause still forbids something UE32 can read: a delay embedding of the escapement and the giving is more of the level's own history (with the draw), so one that forecasts better would count against the narrowed clause and against T2's count of three, not only against the broader wording.

## 4. Where the one lag sits

**The tension.** T2 says "one lag" without saying where. MQ5 read the escapement one year back, named for every stock before its data from a generic reading of age structure ("recruits from last year's spawners, as an age structure gives"), not from each stock's own renewal. UE31 put the lag two censuses back in the beetle, the adults whose eggs are now the pupae, the next census's recruits, named before any value was read. A lag whose place could be chosen after seeing the data would leave "one lag" forbidding little.

**Proposed.** "one lag, its place named before the data". The condition's renewal is one source for the place; the tests so far named it so: one year back in the fish stocks tested, from a generic reading of age structure, and two censuses back in the flour beetle, from its life cycle.

**Why not "at the delay of renewal".** UE31 had proposed that wording. MQ5's level is the spawning biomass, so its delay of renewal is the age at maturity, which in most of the species MQ5 read is longer than one or two years (this is general knowledge of the species, not a measurement of MQ5's stocks). Under that wording MQ5's one-year lag would not be T2's lag in those stocks, and MQ5's pass would need restating. MQ5 itself does not separate what its lag column reads: by the balance it equals the giving reading the current draw, and its verdicts do not tell it from the other order of draw and giving or from compensation ([MQ5's predictions](../margin-measured/PREDICTIONS-2026-10-21-MQ5.md)); 02 adds that the lag "may be the assessment's own". Naming the rule, "before the data", keeps MQ5's test T2's and still forbids choosing the place afterwards; it does not claim that one year is each stock's renewal.

**What it changes.** It forbids more: a lag chosen after the data is no longer T2's. UE32's test names T2's state in the fish stocks as MQ5 did, one year back ([DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)).
