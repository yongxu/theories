## 1. What is tested, and the answer expected

UE31 ([design](../DESIGN.md), [result](../README.md#the-result)) asks whether T2's three numbers do as well as the flour beetle's life stages, the field's state, on cultures whose adult loss is set by hand. On its primary group it decided nothing. The archive's adult-loss experiment (its Data Set 2) holds 40 cultures: two strains, five set losses and four replicates. Each has 13 censuses under the loss, which gives 8 rows of data, against the 20 that UE31's rules need to fit a culture alone.

UE31b asks UE31's questions of the same 40 cultures with one change. The four replicates of one strain at one set loss are read together under one law: one *cell*, ten cells in all. A cell has 36 rows (§3).
- The questions, the models, the statistics, the bounds and the way the verdicts combine are UE31's, imported unchanged from [`state_rules.py`](../state_rules.py). Where UE31 has a culture, UE31b has a cell.
- The out-of-sample comparisons leave one culture out of its cell (§4).
- Nothing else differs (§2).

**The answer expected, stated before the run** (from the dry runs, §6):

In the field's worlds, the eight in which the field's model holds (lpa to lpa-offband, §6), at this shape:
- **The next step: separated, the rival's state.** W fails in every archive where the pupae are the recruits to be, T2's own law included. The reading is "separated on the next step: the rival's state" in 99.2% to 99.6% of archives under the four three-stage worlds whose replicates are alike (lpa, lpa-two, lpa-recalled, lpa-env), 98.7% under lpa-offband, 96.5% under lpaa, and 90.2% under lpa-jars, whose replicates differ. In the rest V2 fails.
- **The course: three numbers suffice.** H1 holds in 96% to 99.9% of archives under the three-stage worlds, and in 83% under lpaa, whose new adults are an age class. This could fail: where the life cycle is longer (lpa-long), H1 fails in 94% of archives.
- **The sign: not separated.** It cannot go to the rival, by counting (§5), and at the field's densities it did not go to T2 either. In the seven field worlds other than lpa-offband the rival predicts the negative sign in at most five cells in every archive where the sign was read, so where T2's majority sign is positive too few cells differ to compare (§5). Where the majority came out negative, the cells compared are the sparse ones, which the rival predicts positive: of the 232 archives of those seven worlds in which the sign was read so, none went to T2. Where the turning point lies outside the band (lpa-offband), the rival predicts the negative sign in six cells, and the sign went falsely to T2 in 2 of 1,000 archives. Under T2's own law, as near the field's densities as its law at MQ5's rates comes (t2-field), it went to T2 in 22% of archives, and in 58% of those where the rival predicts the negative sign in six cells or more.
- **V5: not testable,** in 72% to 99% of archives in every field world but lpa-long.
- **V4: not decided, or T2 holds** (in 7% to 43%). It never fails T2 in the field's worlds.
- **The recording check** (§7) let through every archive recorded as the protocol says with count errors (lpa-miscount, at most 33 rows of 360 above), and stopped every archive recorded in the counted form (lpa-counted, lpa-counted-recalled). In the field's worlds no row's recruits exceed the pupae, by construction.

So the reading expected is the one UE31 expected: "separated on the next step: the rival's state; three numbers suffice on the course", with the sign not separated. It would be the first reading of T2's three numbers on cultures under a draw. It would say three things:
- T2's wording, "as far as anything measured does", fails to the pupae and the adults, a class of fewer variables. That is 08 §9's defeater, met in this domain.
- T2's three numbers, with the lag at the delay, suffice on the adults' own course, where the field's state ties them. At this shape that rests on H1, a prediction the field's model makes the same way, and on V5 not failing, which it does in 1% to 16% of the archives of every field world but lpa-long, and in 48% under lpa-long, where H1 fails in 94% (§8).
- Their one contrary prediction is not decided by this archive.

Two outcomes would say more:
- **The course needs more than three numbers** (H1 fails). T2's universal clause then fails in this domain even narrowed to the course. The cause could be a longer life cycle, which the field's three-stage model would miss too (UE31 §10); the chance events in the stages no adult count holds, which make the three adult numbers less than the whole state; or T2's law form, polynomial in the level, against the field's exponential terms, whose misfit the transient from the onset exposes over a wider range of densities (§6, §10). The first two are T2's state falling short at this grain; the last is its law's form.
- **The sign separates for T2.** T2's one contrary prediction would be borne out against the field's model at constants from other analyses (§8), in a comparison that could not have gone the field's way at this shape (§5). With T2's majority sign positive it can happen only where the rival predicts the negative sign in six cells or more: if the archive's cells at 0.50 to 0.96 are denser than the field's model makes them (§5), or if the turning point lies outside the band, where the field's model gave it falsely in 2 of 1,000 archives (lpa-offband). In the seven other field worlds it never gave it.

## 3. The cell

**Why the cell.** T2 claims a state, the same three numbers for every renewable condition under a draw. It does not claim that its law's coefficients are the same between conditions, nor between jars of one condition. The four replicates of one strain at one loss share the strain, the loss, the medium and the incubator. One law for the four is an assumption, made for both states alike, so that each cell has rows enough to read.
- **Not pooled across strains.** The strains (RR and SS, the archive's README) may differ in their parameters. One law for both would count that difference against both states, as a misspecified form. Pooled per loss, there would also be 5 units, fewer than the 6 a verdict needs.
- **Not pooled across losses.** The draw enters both states' laws, and the field's turning point (§5) moves the lag's sign with density, which the loss sets.

**What pooling assumes, and what it costs.** One law for the four replicates. A difference between replicates that neither law's coefficients carry, a jar's own fecundity or larval survival, is not neutral between the states. It shows in the jar's pupae, which the rival's state reads and T2's adult lags do not. So such differences move W toward the rival, can push V2 toward failing, and lower V4's power. The dry runs measure this with a world whose cultures differ in four parameters (lpa-jars, §6). The excess's chance level treats rows as independent, as in UE31: rows of different cultures are independent, and rows within a culture are serially dependent, as there.

**Rows.** A culture's rows are t = 3 to 11, with t + 1 in its run: 9 rows, 36 a cell.
- At t = 3 every lag the rules read, E_{t−3} in H1 included, is the escapement at a census under the loss.
- UE31 began at t = 4 to drop the start's transient, from a start of counts the experimenter chose. Here t = 0 is 12 weeks after the start, at the census at which the loss is first set, and the stages then are the culture's own. The transient from the onset is part of each culture's course under the draw, which T2's state claims to fix.
- In a pilot of 60 archives, starting at t = 4 took about half of V4's power and added power to no verdict (§6).

**Scale.** A cell's counts are divided by its cultures' largest adult count. Every column of every model is a monomial in the counts and the giving is a count, so a common scale changes no fit and no excess. The scale only sets the forecasts' floor, 1% of it (UE31 §5).

**Size.** A cell needs at least 24 rows, twice the columns of the largest model. A culture is read through its last census with any animal, as in UE31. Every culture had animals at its last census (UE31's result), so each cell has 36 rows.

## 4. Out of sample, across replicates

UE31's forecasts trained on a culture's first 60% of rows and tested on the rest. At 9 rows a culture that leaves no five-step forecast. Here each culture is left out of its cell in turn:
- T2's lean law (`T2F`), the level alone (`M1`) and the rival's state (`RB`) are fitted on the cell's other three cultures (27 rows), at their own scale;
- the culture left out is forecast from every origin with two censuses behind it and five ahead, t = 2 to 7, its level iterated five steps with the set loss known (V4);
- its next giving is predicted on each of its rows (V3, reported).

The cell's errors are those of its four cultures. V4's two criteria are UE31's: the pooled median |log error|, and a sign test over the units, here the cells. A cell's errors enter the sign test as one unit, their mean, since any two of its cultures' forecasts share two of their three training cultures.

Leaving one out is neutral for V4, since both laws it compares read only the adults. Where replicates differ, it favours the rival's state on V3, which reads the pupae; V3 is reported only.

## 5. The sign at this shape

The two rules are UE31's (its §3, §6). T2's rule is one sign for every series, the majority of the fitted signs, here over the cells. The rival's rule gives each cell its sign from its counts and the field's constants, where both sets of constants agree. The two are compared on the cells where they differ, and the comparison needs at least 6 of them.

**At this shape the comparison is one-sided.**
- The set loss sets the density. The rival predicts a negative sign in the dense cells, at the low losses, and a positive sign in the sparse cells, at the high losses.
- **Toward the rival, never.** This is exact, by counting. Where the two rules differ, the rival is right only in a cell whose fitted sign is the one T2's rule did not take; at a 5–5 split, ties going to the positive sign, that is the five negative cells. So the rival can be right in at most five cells, and a decision at p < 0.05 needs at least six right: 6 of 6, 7 of 7, 8 of 8, 8 of 9 or 9 of 10 of the cells where the rules differ. So the sign cannot go to the rival here, whatever the data.
- **The cause is UE31's rule for T2's sign,** the majority of the fitted signs, which UE31 already notes favours T2 (its §6 and §12). With six differing cells needed and only ten units, that leniency decides the direction. In UE31's own dry runs, at the archive's likely size, the sign went to the rival in up to 21.7% of groups (its §1). UE31b keeps the rule unchanged and states the cost: here the sign cannot tell against T2.
- **Toward T2, sometimes.** With one sign everywhere, as T2's law has it, the rules differ in every cell that the rival predicts negative. Where at least six are, the comparison can go to T2. In the dense T2 worlds the rival predicts the negative sign in a median 10 cells, and the sign goes to T2 in 42% to 76% of archives. Under t2-field the median is 5 cells, and the sign goes to T2 in 22%.
- **Where it can decide.** With T2's majority sign positive, the rules differ in the cells the rival predicts negative, so the sign can go to T2 only where those are six or more. Over the T2 worlds (`sign_by_rival_negative` in the JSON) it went to T2 in none of the archives with four or five such cells; in 58% of t2-field's archives with six or more (222 of 382); and in 42% to 76% of the dense worlds' archives, where they are eight to ten. In the seven field worlds other than lpa-offband the rival predicts the negative sign in at most five cells: four in every archive read under lpa, lpa-env, lpaa and lpa-long, three under lpa-two, two under lpa-recalled, four or five under lpa-jars. There, with T2's majority sign positive, too few cells differ to compare. The sign is read only where the majority came out negative, on the cells the rival predicts positive, the sparse ones, and could then go to T2 only if nearly all of those were fitted negative (6 of 6, 7 of 7, 8 of 8, 8 of 9 or 9 of 10), against the positive sign the field's model gives them (next bullet). **So at the field's densities the sign decided neither way in any archive, and with T2's majority sign positive it cannot.** It would go to T2 if the archive's cells from 0.50 on were denser than the field's model makes them, dense enough for the rival to predict the negative sign there, as T2's law at MQ5's rates makes them (§6).
- **How often T2 gets it falsely.** The frozen label "the rival's turning point does not split the cultures" records fewer than six cells where the rules differ, not that the turning point leaves the cells on one side. After the gate it is the reading in 45% (lpaa) to 99% (lpa-env) of the archives of the field's worlds other than lpa-long, where H1's failure stops the sign, and lpa-offband. The sign was read with at least six cells differing in 232 archives of the seven field worlds other than lpa-offband (lpa 27, lpa-two 40, lpa-recalled 13, lpa-env 9, lpaa 132, lpa-long 0, lpa-jars 11), each with T2's majority sign negative, since the rival predicts the negative sign in at most five, and none went to T2. Under lpa-offband, where the turning point lies outside the band (in lpa-jars it varies culture to culture about the band's lower edge), the rival predicts the negative sign in six cells, the sign was read with at least six differing in 924 of 1,000 archives, and went to T2 in 2.
- So at this shape the sign can decide only for T2, and only where the rival predicts the negative sign in at least six cells. A result for T2 would be T2's one contrary prediction borne out (UE31 §10), with the qualifications of §8.
- V5, T2's own item on the sign, reads the signs of the cells where the lag is detected (UE31 §6). Few cells' lags are detected at this shape, so it is not testable in 72% or more of archives in every field world but lpa-long.

## 6. Dry runs

Each draw is one synthetic archive of the adult-loss experiment's shape: 2 strains × 5 set losses × 4 replicates, 40 cultures ([`dry_runs_cells.py`](dry_runs_cells.py), seed 2026110302, 1,000 draws a generator).
- Each culture starts at 250 larvae, 5 pupae and 100 adults, Dennis et al. 2001's start, since Data Set 2's own start is not read.
- It runs five census steps with the adults' natural loss (weeks 0 to 10).
- The loss is then set from the census at week 12 on. The adults returned there are the recruits since week 10 plus the set share of the adults returned at week 10.
- The rules read the 13 censuses from week 12 to week 36.
- Under T2's own law the escapement is the adults' natural share, 1 − 0.007629 a step (Dennis et al. 2001's estimate), until week 10's adults, and the set share from them on, so that week 12's adults are the first returned under the loss, as in the LPA worlds.

Beside the draws, one archive per generator with 30 replicates a cell shows where each rule goes when power is ample.

**The generators.** UE31's (its §8), at this shape, with seven added:
- **lpa:** the demographic LPA model at Dennis et al. 2001's estimates, both strains.
- **lpa-two (added):** strain 1 at the 2001 estimates, strain 2 at the recalled set (UE31 §3, of uncertain source). These are two strains of different parameters, as the archive's two may be.
- **lpa-recalled:** both strains at the recalled set.
- **lpa-env:** lpa with the egg rate varying lognormally, sd 0.3, from step to step.
- **lpaa:** adults that matured in the last step lay and eat at half the rate.
- **lpa-long:** the feeding larvae take two census steps, so the course needs four numbers (H1's power).
- **lpa-jars (added):** lpa with each culture's egg rate and three cannibalism rates (b, c_el, c_ea, c_pa) varying lognormally, sd 0.2, about the 2001 estimates: replicates that differ in what neither law carries (§3).
- **lpa-offband (added):** lpa with c_ea halved, so that the turning point, about 153 adults, lies outside the rival's band of about 76 to 91.
- **t2-stage-\*, t2-copy-\*:** T2's law with the lag at the delay, the pupae either the recruits to be or a noisy copy of the mean, at MQ5's rates (φ, λ) = (0.96, 0.55) and at (0.9, 0.3) and (0.5, 0.4) (UE31 §8). In these worlds every cell is dense: T2's level law, 30 E e^(−E/50), puts the adults' equilibrium between about 280 and 450 at every loss, above the field's turning point.
- **t2-field-mq5 (added):** T2's law at MQ5's rates with the pupae the recruits to be, as near the field's densities as that law comes. At each loss the level law's constant is set, by bisection on calibration batches, so that T2's cultures hold on average lpa's mean adults over the censuses the rival's sign reads, t = 1 to 9 ([`results/dry-runs-cells.json`](results/dry-runs-cells.json), `t2_field_mean_adults`). At the two lowest losses it does so. At 0.50, 0.73 and 0.96 no constant does: the constant stays at the search's floor, K = 1, where the level law gives almost nothing, and T2's law at these rates answers the onset's fall with giving through its lag's term and carries it on. There T2's cultures hold a mean of 96, 93 and 92 adults against lpa's 88, 71 and 46. So its cells from 0.50 on are denser than lpa's, and the rival predicts the negative sign in some of them (§5), which under lpa it does at none of these losses.
- **Three recording worlds (added),** for the check of §7: **lpa-counted**, lpa with the adults recorded as counted before the manipulation from week 14 on, not as returned; **lpa-counted-recalled**, the same at the recalled set, which of the two parameter sets tried puts fewer rows above the pupae in the counted form; and **lpa-miscount**, lpa recorded as the protocol says but with count errors: the survivors' set share rounded up, 5% of the pupae missed at each census, and 0.2% of the larvae maturing within a step without being counted as pupae. The check stops every archive of the two counted worlds, so their verdicts are not read; lpa-miscount's are, and the table shows them.

## 7. The measured error and the recording

**The measured error.** As in UE31 §7, reported and not a verdict: each cell's floor is the mean binomial variance of recruitment given the pupae over its rows, and each state's in-sample residual is reported as a multiple of it.

**The recording, checked before any verdict is read.** UE31 left two choices open because they touched only cultures too short to read (../LAYOUT.md, the recording; ../README.md). Here they are read.
- **Which census's adults.** The reading takes each census's adults as those returned to the culture after the manipulation. That form sets every value read from t = 1 on. The archive's README does not say whether a count is that one or the one counted before the manipulation. Under the counted form the recruits read would often exceed the pupae, which a recruit cannot; under the returned form they can exceed them only through errors in the stage counts or the protocol's rounding. A probe puts the two apart ([`probe_threshold.py`](probe_threshold.py), [`results/probe-threshold.out`](results/probe-threshold.out), 200 archives each). A recording as the protocol says, with count errors up to a tenth of the pupae missed, or a twentieth missed with the survivors' share rounded up and some larvae maturing uncounted, gives at most 40 rows above of 360. The counted form gives at least 57 at the recalled set and 144 at the 2001 estimates. The dry runs carry one world of each kind (lpa-miscount, lpa-counted-recalled, lpa-counted; §6). So the check is frozen with a consequence at one eighth, between them: **where the recruits exceed the pupae by more than half an animal in more than 12.5% of the cells' rows (46 or more of 360), the reading is "the reading's form or the stage counts disagree with the protocol", and no verdict is read** ([`cell_rules.py`](cell_rules.py), `PROTOCOL_SHARE`). The threshold lies 6 rows above the worst departure probed and 11 below the counted form's least, so count errors larger than those probed would stop the reading too. Half an animal allows for rounding to the nearest; a protocol that rounds up adds rows, which lpa-miscount includes. When the check stops the reading, the run writes only the check, the labels and the counts of cells, and withholds every cell's numbers and statistics, so that a design for the recording found could still be made blind ([`run_cells.py`](run_cells.py), `read`).
- **Which of week 12's entries.** The README says the two week-12 entries "are pre- and post-treatment"; UE31's reader keeps the later one in the sheet's order. The entry read sets t = 0 alone: E_0 enters H1's furthest lag in one row of nine a culture, V4's first origin and the cell's scale, and no other rule reads it. Where the adults' natural loss is below the set share, the treatment removes adults; where it exceeds the set share, the share can be met only by adding adults, and the entry after the treatment holds more. From 0.27 on the set loss exceeds both natural-loss estimates the design uses, 0.007629 a step (Dennis et al. 2001) and the recalled set's 0.1108; 0.04 lies between them. Frozen: **from 0.27 on, in each culture, the entry with fewer adults is read as the one after the treatment, the later where they hold the same; at 0.04, each culture's entry at the position, earlier or later in the sheet, that its strain's cultures from 0.27 on mostly show, the later on a tie** ([`run_cells.py`](run_cells.py), `week12`). Which was read, how the losses from 0.27 on show the sheet's order, in how many cultures at 0.04 the entry read holds fewer adults, and how many cultures hold the same larvae and pupae in both entries are reported.

## 8. What each outcome means

UE31 §10's tables are fixed and hold here, read with a cell where they have a culture: the next step from W and V2, the course from H1, the sign beside them, and V5 and V4 as T2's own items. What the unit changes:

- **The labels.** The run prints UE31's frozen labels, which say "cultures". Here each means cells.
- **W, V2 and H1** are read on ten laws, each fitted to four replicates. A difference between replicates that neither law carries is not neutral (§3): it moves W toward the rival and can push V2 toward failing.
- **The sign** can decide only for T2 at this shape (§5). *Not separated: the rival's turning point does not split the cultures* is the expected reading in the field's worlds. The frozen label records fewer than six cells where the two rules differ, not where the turning point lies (§5). It says that the archive does not decide T2's one contrary prediction, not that T2's sign holds.
- **T2 narrowed to the condition's own course.** UE31's rule reads it as holding only if H1 holds, neither V5 nor V4 fails, and the sign does not separate for the rival (UE31 §6). At this shape the sign cannot go to the rival (§5), and V4 never failed T2 in the field's worlds (§6). V5 fails in 1% to 16% of the field's archives where H1 mostly holds (every field world but lpa-long), and is not testable in most of the rest. So a reading that T2 narrowed holds would rest on H1, a prediction the field's model makes the same way, and on V5 not failing, mostly by not being testable. It is not T2 passing its sign test or its own items.
- **V5** reads cells, not cultures. A split of the detected cells' signs is T2's third forbidden item met in this domain, as in UE31.
- **V4** reads forecasts of a culture from its cell-mates' law. A culture's own difference from its cell-mates counts against both laws alike.

**Recorded now, so that no outcome can be reread afterwards** (UE31 §10's, all of which stand):
- **A failure counts.** A culture under a set adult loss is a renewable condition under a draw, and T2 claims its three numbers for every one. A failure here counts against that clause in this domain. It does not defeat T2 in the fisheries, nor the reading 08 §9 names.
- **W tests T2's wording as it stands at this freeze**, "as far as anything measured does". UE32 carries a proposal to narrow it to "as far as anything in the condition's own course does", which H1 reads. If the wording is narrowed before the run, W still reports the old wording's fate, and the narrowed claim is read from H1.
- **H1 holding** would be a prediction that could fail and did not: under lpa-long it fails in most archives (§6). But the field's model makes the same prediction at this life cycle, so it shows nothing the field does not (UE31 §10).
- **H1 failing** can come from a longer life cycle, from the chance events in the stages no adult count holds, or from T2's law form, polynomial in the level, against the field's exponential terms, which the transient from the onset exposes over a wider range of densities (§6, §10). The first two are T2's state falling short; the last is its law's form. Under lpa itself H1 fails in some archives (§6), and lpaa's age class adds to its failures there.
- **A sign for T2** would be the one place where T2 named in advance would do better than the field's model here (UE31 §10): against the rival at constants from other analyses, in a comparison that could not have gone the rival's way at this shape (§5), and which the field's model got falsely in 2 of 1,000 archives when its turning point lay outside the band, and never in the seven other field worlds. With T2's majority sign positive it can come only where the rival predicts the negative sign in six cells or more, which in those seven worlds it never does (§5). With these qualifications stated, it would change 08 §8's verdicts for this domain.

## 10. Limits

UE31 §12's limits all apply. Beyond them:
- **One law a cell.** Four replicates share it. A difference between them that neither law carries leans toward the rival's state (§3, §6).
- **What a further lag picks up.** Under lpa two sources make the further lag carry something, and the probe of §6 sizes them only roughly. One is T2's law form, polynomial in the level against the field's exponential terms, which alone fires the gate at 30 replicates a cell and shows most in the transient from the onset, since the rows begin three censuses after the loss is set and at the highest loss the adults are still falling then. - **The sign is one-sided at this shape** (§5).
- **V4 reads across replicates.** Each forecast uses a law fitted to three other cultures.
- **The rival's constants.** They come from other analyses: the 2001 estimates of another experiment's RR cultures, and the recalled set of uncertain source. Data Set 2's two strains may differ from both. A world whose turning point lies outside the band (lpa-offband, §6) gives the cost of that.
- **Ten units.** V5 is not testable in most archives of every field world but lpa-long, and V4 decides in at most 43% of the field's archives. The sign cannot go to the rival (§5), and in the field's worlds other than lpa-offband it went to T2 in no archive.

- **The start.** The dry runs start every culture at Dennis et al. 2001's counts; Data Set 2's start is not read. Only the state at the onset depends on it.
