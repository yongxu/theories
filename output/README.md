## output/pdf/review-packet/

The reader edition of the review packet, edition 5: thirteen PDFs (README and README.zh; the one page in English and Chinese; the overview, The Theory of Becoming, in English and Chinese; the dossiers on classical texts, Buddhist thought, logic, philosophy, alignment and the sciences; the re-coding codebook), the re-coding sheet as `SHEET.xlsx` and `SHEET.tsv`, and `MANIFEST.txt` with the sha256 of each file. It was built by `tools/publishing/build_review_packet.py --release` from commit 3395e82ee (committed 2026-11-07, tag `review-packet-5`). Its sources are `theories/becoming/review/`, `theories/becoming/ONE-PAGE*.md` and `theories/becoming/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING*.md`.

Each edition is a frozen reader edition: it does not change after release, and the live text in `theories/becoming/` moves on past it. The board's "Which edition to send" line in `_process/BECOMING-WORKBOARD.md` names edition 5 as the current one. Whom it goes to, and when, is not decided here.
