## Operational interpretation

The instruction authorizes parallel deep investigation and makes **universal normative grounding and candidate metaethical invariants** the repository's first research priority. (Revised on 30 October 2026: neither of the two goals then revised pursues a warrant binding every possible mind as a target, and both keep it as an open question the work does not wait on, [source](2026-10-30-goal-practical-judgment-for-life-approved.md); on 8 November Ren said warrant is "like saying a thing must be so", from a dualist framing, [source](2026-11-08-within-becoming-no-should.md).) The concrete priority is to justify, delimit or revise the transition from whole generative relationships to ethical significance and practical constraints, including constraints that can survive learning and self-modification. It is not an instruction to presume a favorable theorem.

The existing nondual method, whole-grounding orientation, plural differentiated lives, conditional completion, counterexamples and stronger unmet no-assumptions objective remain. A candidate concerning correction is a bounded first test, not a new prerequisite for every good, a complete definition of ethics, or an agency threshold for moral standing.

Mechanism, valuation-development, formalization, empirical and alignment work may continue as supporting studies when they identify the precise premise, counterexample, correspondence or implementation requirement they supply to this first priority. Existing scheduled or requested work is not silently canceled. Another successful dynamical example, proof count or literature survey alone does not discharge normative warrant.
