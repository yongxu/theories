## Context (AI-authored)

- **文理生生.** A research project Ren built with Codex on 21–22 October 2026, separately from the ZH work: a plan for "the minimum conceptual grammar required to understand any system that comes into existence, persists, changes, and passes away" founded on 文; a typed concept grammar (propositional dynamic logic over finite sorted models) with a dictionary of 144 characters and 245 senses, each with sources; twelve constructions and thirteen micro-models; an eight-character typed rule graph; 八卦 dynamics; audits; a relational atlas website (v2.2). Its own audit leaves coverage of the lexicon, gains in understanding and minimality unestablished.
- **The AI's reading of the comparison** (six readings in `development/zhonghua/agents/wenli/`). On the characters whose senses can be compared, the two projects agree for about three quarters, including the same refusals (道, 气, 性, 仁 not names); on the 八卦 they share the encoding and several laws. The formal core of 文理生生 is a modern logic with characters bound by hand, so both projects arrived at two layers: a formal core and a classical reading layer. The AI's answer: the line of 文 that takes 文 as the pattern of differing things intermixed (物相杂，故曰文), composes characters as operations, and knows by reconstructing an internally generative structure through repeated reading is the best expression of the core's language and epistemology, because it is isomorphic to what the core proves (laws read off the unfolding; learning through a lumping that carries the law, with its exact condition and its failure). Whether it is the best of all possible expressions is the project's own hypothesis H2, a test still to run.
