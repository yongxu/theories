## What the work brings to the gap (AI-authored, kept as a note)

What the work adds to each of them, and where it fails, is what that use shows.

## How the AI reads it (AI-authored)

The objective is a reading of the work by its own law. A selection reaches only what couples into its selector. The work's selectors (the Lean checker, the completion checks, AI reviews) take in no alignment researcher, so no check run inside the work can show that it serves alignment ([08 §12](../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#the-work-by-its-own-laws)).

"Taken well" therefore means that alignment researchers come to couple into the work: they can read it in an hour, check each claim against its proof or test, rerun the tests, and change the account by what they find. It does not mean approval. Approval read as a score would be a share the work selects itself by, which is the capture the work describes. MA3 adds the same point from the other side: what comes back to the work alone (credit) would select for shaping the reception, and what is shared (proofs, tests, credit given to neighbours) would not.
