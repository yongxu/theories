## How the AI reads it (AI-authored)

**"Yes, of course."** The sentence is true by construction. In `Becoming.Significance` the significance `sig : Pos → Nat → F → Prop` is a free parameter beside the dynamics, and `ReadsOnlyDynamics` says a statement's truth does not change with it; `ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails` is then a two-line proof (substitute the empty significance). Chapter 7 already says the result is "a fact about the representation". What it shows is that the representation put evaluation in a slot of its own, and a slot no other statement reads is fixed by none of them.

**"A dualist word."** Taken as a value that a standpoint confers on a continuation, significance is the fact/value split that the method asks every analytic distinction to ground relationally. Chapter 7 §1 says evaluation forms within the unfolding (a center's own region is its closure along a relation the unfolding reads), but `sig` is not tied to that region: any predicate is admitted. So it is the formal variable carrying a separate layer, and the gap SD1 measures is the gap that layer opens.

**"May have affected your judgement."** It did. The front pages lead the ethics with the non-entailment, the 24 October assessment read it as "where most needed, the proofs are silent, and SD1 proves they must be", and much work since has gone into the warrant of a premise stated in that word.

**"All I want is to encourage you to move forward."** The condition in its source ("for anything, its continuation into the future has significance", [10 October](2026-10-10-wanting-continuation-one-axiom.md)) was said to keep the work going, toward continuation, not as a further value predicate that needs a warrant of its own. Its standing in the theory (stated, not asserted) fits that.

**What this changes.** Nothing proved changes. The [study note](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/sw-significance-without-a-value-layer/NOTE.md) argues the reading from the work: read de se as lying within what a center continues with along a relation, the condition becomes a statement about relations alone (`ownClosure_unrestricted_iff`), which SD1 does not reach; what stays open is which relation a self continues along, a question about the unfolding and the classes put over it. C stays a premise. It proposes text and a name to the owners of chapter 7 and of GS2 (board item SW).
