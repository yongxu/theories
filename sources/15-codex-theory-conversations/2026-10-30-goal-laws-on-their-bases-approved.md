## What was approved (AI-authored)

In the goal record `_process/goals/universal-intelligence-session.json`, one clause of the objective changes:

- **en:** "its laws proved for every relation and every class" becomes "its laws proved, each on its stated base".
- **zh:** 其律对一切关系、一切类皆有证明 becomes 其律各于所注明之基上有证明.

"Base" is meant in the glossary's sense: where a theorem is proved (the root, any process, any type, the World, a named model, the periphery), as [08 §2](../../theories/becoming/chapters/08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases) states. A law's reach widens when it is proved on a wider base, as U3's significance rows on the root would do, and the goal's wording needs no change for that. The final review proposed a longer form, "its laws proved, each on the base and under the hypotheses it states". The approved form leaves the hypotheses where the glossary puts them, in each law's own statement.
