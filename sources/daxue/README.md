# 《大學》: Chinese Wikisource revisions

Retrieved on 11 November 2026 through the MediaWiki API of zh.wikisource.org, for TM's reading of 《大學》 onto the map. Each page is kept at the revision named in the [provenance](PROVENANCE.json), with the SHA-256 of its bytes and of its wikitext. The texts are pre-modern and in the public domain; Wikisource's punctuation and markup are CC BY-SA 4.0. This is a punctuated electronic edition, not a scan, and it is not collated with a printed edition.

- `liji-daxue.json`: 《禮記·大學》, the old text, as one continuous chapter.
- `daxue-zhangju.json`: 朱熹 《大學章句》: the preface's opening and the ten 傳 in his order, with his 補傳 to the fifth; this page does not hold the 經 chapter.
