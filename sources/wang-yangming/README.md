# 王陽明: 《傳習錄》 and 《大學問》, Chinese Wikisource revisions

Retrieved on 10 November 2026 through the MediaWiki API of zh.wikisource.org, for TM's reading of 王陽明 onto the map. Each page is kept at the revision named in the [provenance](PROVENANCE.json), with the SHA-256 of its bytes and of its wikitext. The texts are pre-modern and in the public domain; Wikisource's punctuation and markup are CC BY-SA 4.0. This is a punctuated electronic edition, not a scan, and it is not collated with a printed edition.

- `chuanxilu-shang.json`, `chuanxilu-zhong.json`, `chuanxilu-xia.json`: 《傳習錄》 卷上, 卷中 (with the letters, among them 答顧東橋書 and its 拔本塞源論) and 卷下.
