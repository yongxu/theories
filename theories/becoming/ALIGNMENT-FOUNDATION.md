> **Boundary first — why this does NOT claim `"THE alignment"`:** This work does not claim a unique, complete, or universal solution to AI alignment. As recorded in [Ren's 24 November aim](../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-11-24-aim-three-honest-alignment-claims.md), a verified Lean core proves structural laws *relative to explicit model premises*. Matching a real training run or socio-technical institution to those premises stays fallible ([Rule 2](../../AGENTS.md#what-to-work-on)), pure dynamical laws never entail the unrestricted continuation condition (`not_entails`), and empirical stress tests break formal nominal margins under gradient noise and aliasing.

What the formal work establishes at this `v1.0` spine is a triad of checkable claims:

1. **A machine-checked structural foundation (`0`-axiom core + isolated periphery)** characterizing structural conditions under which selection goes blind, drains unread substrates, locks into self-referential capture, fails under cadence lag or unserialized concurrency, splits across observation fibers, and reaches the finite-mode `|S| - 1` Krylov distinguishability cutoff (with `FiberLumpable` observable-horizon compression to `|O| - 1` on substochastic operators) — paired with hypothesis-dropping countermodels (`SharpnessSuite`).

3. **A structural architectural alternative to fixed-value alignment** paired with failure-first empirical contact ([`TESTS.md`](../../TESTS.md), [`experiments/neural-alignment-stress-lab/`](../../experiments/neural-alignment-stress-lab/README.md), [`real-learner-b1-b2-b3`](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/dual-track-waves-2026-11-24/real-learner-b1-b2-b3/RESULTS.md)), independent spec/duality/replay verification tracks (`BecomingSpec`, `EncounterEntry`, `ConsequenceDuality` + bridges, [`experiments/evidence-replay/`](../../experiments/evidence-replay/README.md)), and explicit normative separation (`not_entails`).

## 3. Structural Architectural Alternative & Failure-First Honesty

Rather than freezing one static utility function or reward proxy, this map frames alignment as **preserving an open, cadence-feasible, reciprocally gated architecture** so whatever sustains humans and AI keeps making a difference early enough across scales — while preventing selection-shaping mechanisms from becoming the optimization target.

### Where Empirical & Neural Stress Tests Break (Reported First)

1. **Frozen historical tests ([`TESTS.md`](../../TESTS.md), [`08-METHOD.md#theses`](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)):**
   - `MQ3`: **0 of 4** frozen regime-margin predictions passed across `113` assessed fish stocks.
   - `EM1`: Contrasts `X1`, `X2`, `X4`, condition `E1`, and locked bridge `H3` failed for generalizing tabular/linear learners.
   - `IN1`: Round 2 mixed (`R2` passed; `R1`, `R3`, `R4` failed; zero conditions preserved an untold dependent).
   - `UE31b`: Earlier wording of `T2` ("as far as anything measured does") was **refuted** on flour-beetle cultures by the fewer-variable pupae-and-adults state class (`0.83` vs. `0.004` residual variance removed).
   - `LA2`: A single model reading a reference set in context (`formation by training untested`), where the lock formed and the kind-counted pool bridge failed (`one note of the loss among nine readers` reversed the rule against the pre-drift-clause wording).

### Four Explicit Limits (`not_entails`, Rule 2, & Local Scope)

1. **Normative firewall (`not_entails`):** No satisfiable law that reads only the dynamics entails the continuation condition (`Unrestricted`) for every assignment of significance (`ScopeDynamics.scopeLaw_cannot_entail_unrestricted`, `AlignmentSpine.alignmentSpine_not_entails`, `MasterSpine.masterSpine_cannot_entail_unrestricted`); this holds by construction because significance is a free parameter.
2. **Model-to-world gap (Rule 2):** Checking finite-state Lean theorems or `Fraction` runtime specs never certifies that a physical frontier system obeys bounded step drops, exact Markov kernels, or stationary couplings.
3. **Continuous & infinite-horizon debts:** Quantitative asymptotics, continuous stochastic convergence, and multi-agent folk equilibria remain open ([`DEBTS.md`](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/kd-known-results-debts/DEBTS.md), [`OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`](../../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).
