*The title asks it in the account's terms. A collective is part of the world it would move, so the title asks of the collective's course; and the map's generation allows loss, so it asks of widening what can form ("A generative direction", read here, below).*

Labels: **Theorem** with its model in brackets (*any type*, *any process*, *World*: webs and flows with natural-number amounts and discrete steps, *counts*: two kinds in whole-number counts with no noise, *witness*: one constructed model, showing that something can happen); **premise** for the two readings the ranking results use; **empirical** for what only a measurement settles; **Reading** [AI reading] for an interpretation, open to revision.

## The answer in brief

**No guarantee.** The theory's own laws, each on its stated model, show why (§3): a selection reaches only what couples into it, how far off a break is does not couple in through a threshold, no finite record certifies renewal, points of no return are exact, and where the rest outruns a renewal at a need of one, no weight on a reading of the condition from the rest's end on selects one member's restraint. What the theory does give is exact, and narrower:

- **a criterion**, widening, which every weighting positive on the listed items ranks higher, and which is silent where a comparison is mixed (§1); it is the question's generative direction only under the two readings ("A generative direction", read here, below);
- **exits** from the loops that lock a collective, each holding under stated hypotheses, several with the step at which its window closes (§2);
- **a precise meaning for "enough situationality"**: six conditions, of which observation is only the first (§6);
- **one law on keeping the future open**: along a course that makes only the moves of one fixed process from some step on, the possible under that process does not keep widening (§7).

The three senses of "always" come apart:

| Sense | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Always guaranteed | **No** | the laws of §3 |
| Always available as a direction | **As a criterion only** | widening is always defined and agreed by every positive weighting where it applies, but a mixed comparison is decided both ways, and the hard cases are mixed; a widening move need not exist past a point of no return, after a window closes, or where the rest outruns the renewal, and in a finite balanced world no course keeps widening by name, though one can within kinds |
| Achievable under stated conditions | **Yes, for named exits** | §2, each under its hypotheses, none shown to hold of an actual collective |

**"A generative direction", read here.** The ethics compares courses by *widening* what can form: one course widens another when it admits everything the other admits and at some step more ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-as-what-every-positive-weighting-ranks)). Whose losses count is the continuation condition's (scope); that widening is better is C's (order); the theory asserts neither ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). What the account's makers offer for life, a way of seeing what each course draws on, ends and carries on, and who bears it, every continuation's losses still counted, is thesis T8, argued, binding no one; 生生 is the continuation of life ([07 §8](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)). The map's generation (生成, [02](chapters/02-MAP.md#branches)) allows loss: on a course's moments, under premises of deciding, keeping widening is generation that loses nothing ([07 §2](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-keeping-widening-is-generation-losing-nothing)), and on a witness, the kind relay, every supported occurrence is generation at two orders of grain along passing while what it can form by name only returns ([07 §2](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-keeping-widening-needs)). So this page reads the question's generative direction as widening, under the two readings, and says where each course draws beyond a renewal.

## 4. What needs a premise

- **Scope: the continuation condition.** A loss beyond every selector's closure, a loss that returns to no one, is counted under it, and particular scopes can count particular unreturned losses without meeting it ([07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-approaches-under-the-selection-laws)). Without the step of invariance, the condition's content beyond the self's presupposition ([07 §4.2](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#step)), and under P1–P4 of [07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path) (acting presupposes, held de se, that one's own continuation has significance; the self is a line on the root under a class constant up to the net's end at oneself; the conditions one affects feed other lines; everything one conditions is alike to oneself), concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of what one conditions, past one's own end and across lines fed by conditions one affects ([07 §4.5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-reach-through-a-shared-condition)); that is a law, not only a reading of witnesses. What that path does not reach, other scopes can count, the step of invariance among them: each center counting from another's standpoint beyond its part as a condition; what affects one's conditions without being fed by them, counted as more than a cause; present beings beyond every closure; and whatever no chain joins, among it future beings in no one's line.
- **Order: C.** Calling a widening course better is C's. The condition by itself orders nothing.
- **Mixed comparisons** are left unranked by both. The practical comparison 07 proposes among them uses J1–J4 (complete comparison with retained losses, priority of basic conditions, bounded responsibility, legitimate feasible choice), further premises; constraints, undertakings and other held orders can also decide. A measure for mixed parts is open.
- Neither reading is asserted here, and by §3's last two rows neither can come from the dynamics alone; no warrant from outside is sought.

## 6. What "enough situationality" would have to mean

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. Read in the theory's terms, enough of the situation, for a given collective and condition, is all of the following at once:

1. **Coupling.** The drawn level and the need of what depends on it both couple into a selecting pool, by level and not by threshold ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-runaway-distance-uncoupled), [05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-pooled-coupling-reaches)).
2. **Response.** The pool's members respond. Coupling bounds what an act responds to and fixes nothing within the bound ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#does-not-show)); a difference beyond responsive reach stays what it was (`Flow.stays_beyond_response`, [home](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-where-two-courses-rejoin)).
3. **Time.** It happens before the regime's point of no return, and before the share reaches the step from which the late reading favours the shapers (§2, §3).
4. **Resolution.** The feedback separates the differences that matter. LA1's locked side failed where its feedback barely separated the best choice from the next; its frozen follow-up, with the feedback resolved and the best choice assigned (two changes it does not tell apart), passed every verdict ([T3](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3)).
5. **Pace.** A slow rule reads the fast level over a long enough stretch: given two values, a stretch of `L` steps fixes every pair of held-rule courses with at most `N` states exactly when `L ≥ 2N − 1` (`Pace.pace_law`, [home](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-how-fast-a-rule-may-change)).
6. **Structure.** The rest does not outrun the renewal. Where it does, a score reading the condition only from the rest's end scores every act of a member alike, so no weight on it selects restraint, and only a change of the rest's exchange helps (§3).

Observation is the first condition, necessary and not sufficient: seeing is not valuing, and coupling is not compliance ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#does-not-show)). And all six together still rank nothing: calling the widening course better is C's, and counting the losses that return to no one is the continuation condition's.

## 7. Keeping the future open

**Theorem** (any process; witnesses; [home](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-where-the-possible-grows)).
- Along a course of a process, nothing is added to the whole possible under it: what is possible from the next configuration is possible from the present one (`Run.possible_of_next`). A tower of rules with no top adds nothing to its whole possible either.
- Where the possible under a process `Q` keeps widening along a course, from every step some bounded stretch is not made of moves of `Q` throughout (`Run.departs_of_keepsWidening`); where every step from some step on is a move of `Q`, it does not keep widening (`Run.not_keepsWidening_of_within`).
- The possible with a part held grows across a step only where the step changes the part (`Run.changes_of_heldGrows`), and keeps widening only where the part changes without end (`Run.changes_of_heldKeepsWidening`); endless change of the part does not suffice (`Oscillating.converse_fails`).
- On a witness, with the rule raised at every other step, the possible with the rule held keeps widening, every formation stays possible, and renewal without end holds at every even moment (`«生生»`, `RaisedEveryOther.renews_without_end`; the grammar's renewal without end, not the ethics' 生生, [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md)), so moment `0` does not cease (`«生生不息»`, `RaisedEveryOther.both`): widening relative to what is held and renewal without end can hold together.

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. Read for collectives: a fixed rule, institution, constitution or AI policy whose moves a course makes from some step on does not keep the possible under it widening. Keeping it widening needs the rule held to change without end, and its changing without end is not enough. Strict continuing widening relative to a held rule is then a property of how rules keep changing, not of any rule chosen once; keeping what is possible open does not require endless change. This fits the alignment of the joint selection being stated as conditions on how its rules are read and revised, and not as a final state ([AJ plan §1](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md#1-the-goal)). Nothing here says which changes keep it widening, or that any actual collective can make them.

## 8. Zero-sum competition

Starting from relations removes the frame of a fixed sum divided among independent owners. It does not remove rivalry, and both are theorems:
- **Joint effects.** A difference parts make only together is made by no part alone, and in the witness Pool the relational reading still counts the first drawer as consuming (`Pool.difference_only_together`, [home](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-joint-difference-making)); a part's own widening widens the whole exactly within others' margins (§1); self-concern, a premise weaker than C, ranks a coupled ending below (§1).
- **Real conflict.** Positions' rises total their falls, so no change raises every part's position and a rise needs a fall (`RelativePosition.change_balances`, `RelativePosition.rise_needs_fall`, [home](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-relative-position-is-zero-sum)); mixed comparisons stay unranked (§1).
