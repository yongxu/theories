## In brief

The theory states the principle's central quantities on its own models, forbids five statements of its literature read as guarantees or unqualified, two of them weakly, and states nothing yet of densities, continuous time or deep models, among others. In what it forbids it reaches farther than the principle alone, and not yet farther than the sciences taken together ([08 §8](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)).

**What is stated** (§1; §5 lists what is not).

- *Belief given a record* is conditioning by restriction.
- *Variational free energy* bounds the record's surprisal, with equality exactly at the posterior, so exact minimization over the beliefs where it is finite is that conditioning. For beliefs in counts the bound and its equality hold in the pure core; the real form is their logarithm over hypotheses listed once, on the support.
- *Expected free energy* is risk plus ambiguity, and the negative of epistemic less pragmatic value, the epistemic value an expected information gain. Seeking by it reads the prior only through the weight it gives each likelihood profile; its posterior after a course is the core's share given the course where its model is the core's.
- *The Markov blanket* is stated as a coupling structure on a determined law. Its statistical form, conditional independence given the blanket at a steady state, holds under sufficient conditions in counts and, in the linear case, over the reals.
- *The existence claim*, its model read off a course, is conditioning on the course itself.

**Where the two agree** [AI reading, D45] (§2). The principle names theorems the theory states. Perception is conditioning in variational form. The blanket's partition names the couplings: any model fed by what couples into a formation is, by construction, the agent of a blanketed law, and a reading through the blanket separates at most what the blanket tells apart. The habit of active inference, read as a count, is a rule read from the count of its choices. Risk, for a point prediction, is a set point's distance.

**Where the theory forbids** (§3). Five statements of the principle's literature, read as guarantees or unqualified, fail on models in the principle's own terms, each by a theorem the principle does not state. The theorems:

1. Identical cells under a descent that treats them alike never part.
2. One generative model with alike preferences, each over its own store, can end the renewal its holders share; a goal over the shared level is no guarantee either.
3. Risk minimization does not keep a member's need where the rest of the takers outrun a renewal gated on what it renews (weak).
4. Seeking information corrects only within what some experiment it can choose separates (weak).
5. Agents learning a code from what they hear, under a deterministic law keeping the exchange of agents with a renaming of the words, never come to share, from a start that exchange and renaming leave unchanged, a uniquely greatest word at a meaning whose every word the renaming moves. Free-energy minimization breaks no symmetry there.

Each excludes a guarantee or an unqualified statement on a model, not yet a measurement of an actual system (§6).

**What the literature leaves open** (§1, §3, §4). Five further theorems forbid no sentence of the literature but state what it leaves open:

- Under noise of one law at every cell the law on identical cells stays exchangeable, deciding nothing of which cell takes which place.
- Expected free energy reads beliefs only through the lumping its likelihood makes, and so does every course of seeking by it.
- An agent reading its supply only by threshold, no action changing the supply, cannot see its break coming, whatever its model and policy (a witness).
- The blanket's statistical form holds where the internal moves are in detailed balance with a positive weighting, joined at each blanket state by moves present at every external state whose rate ratios read no external state, or, in the linear case, under the principle's sparse coupling with footnote 13's condition and diagonal positive noise. It need not hold with circulation: in a witness the synchronous steps of a drift meeting footnote 13 and the 2023 paper's term-by-term sparse coupling lose it, a loss of the discrete step, the drift's continuous-time zero standing.
- Which blanket a thing has turns on how its parts are read. With parts reading the course every family of courses has one. A degenerate reading of the configuration refutes no family whose law is determined. A sparse partition fixed apart from the family, on a law the same at every step, can be refuted, even by one run, as can a formation's own level read as the internal part along one run with the step external, which a drain in three phases fails.

**What forbids nothing** (§4). The existence claim forbids nothing alone, as its proponents concede: forbidding needs a weighting fixed apart from the course, or a law of the courses themselves. Nor do the theory's own root theorems, its general theorems on any relation read as condition: they hold of every carrier, relation and class, so they forbid no relation; a theorem of the theory with content forbids through its hypotheses ([08 §10](chapters/08-METHOD.md#law)). **Reading** [AI reading, D45]: what persistence needs is what the theory's renewal and coupling theorems state.

**Where the principle says more** (§5). Densities and continuous time, noise and random dynamics beyond finite chains with noise of one law at every cell, Langevin flows, the Helmholtz decomposition and nonequilibrium steady states beyond the linear case's algebra, generalized coordinates, the precision of beliefs, the softmax over policies beyond the tied order it keeps (§3), hierarchical and deep temporal models, learning beyond rules on counts that keep a symmetry, and structure learning: none is stated in the theory.

## 1. The quantities

- **Belief** (*any type*). The weighting restricted to a record keeps each course agreeing with it and gives every other nothing; in a generative model a hypothesis listed once has, given the record, its prior weight times the record's weight under it over their sum ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-conditioning-by-restriction); `ConditionalWeights.posterior_single`).
- **Surprisal.** The reciprocal share, the whole over the part, orders as pointwise surprisal does and multiplies where surprisal adds, which makes it a correspondence, not a number of surprisal (*any type*; `ConditionalWeights.Share.le_iff_reciprocal`); over the reals surprisal is the negative logarithm of the record's weight, adding over independent parts (*periphery*; `FreeEnergy.surprisal_product`).
- **Variational free energy** (*periphery*; finitely many causes, a belief zero wherever the joint weight is). It is at least the record's surprisal, equal exactly at the posterior, which alone is least among such beliefs; it is the divergence from the posterior plus the surprisal and the energy less the entropy, and, where the joint weight is a prior times a likelihood, the divergence from the prior less the expected log-likelihood; in a core generative model the minimizer is the core's share given the record ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-free-energy-bounds-surprisal); `FreeEnergy.surprisal_le_freeEnergy`, `FreeEnergy.minimizes_iff_givenShare`). In counts it holds in the pure core: `N ^ N * ∏ a ^ n ≤ A ^ N * ∏ n ^ n`, equal exactly where the counts are proportional to the weights, and in a core generative model, with the hypotheses listed once, exactly at the shares given the record (*any type*; [03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-free-energy-bound-in-counts); `FreeEnergyCounting.bound_eq_iff`, `FreeEnergyCounting.given_record_eq_iff_share`). The two forms are one theorem on beliefs in counts over hypotheses listed once, on the support: the free energy less the surprisal, times the total count, is the logarithm of the ratio of the counting bound's two sides, so the two bounds hold there exactly together, and the core's theorems give the real bound, its equality at the posterior and its strictness elsewhere, also in a core generative model, where the whole weight drops out (*periphery*; `FreeEnergy.gap_counts`, `FreeEnergy.surprisal_le_freeEnergy_iff_bound`, `FreeEnergy.surprisal_le_freeEnergy_of_counts`, `FreeEnergy.gap_counts_given_record`). Off the support or with a hypothesis listed twice the identity can fail (`FreeEnergy.Witness.gap_counts_off_support`, `FreeEnergy.Witness.gap_counts_duplicate`).
- **Expected free energy** (Parr and Friston 2019; *periphery*; finite states and outcomes, one step and one policy, its predicted distribution over states given). Risk plus ambiguity is the negative of the epistemic value less the pragmatic value, with no hypothesis; with nonnegative weights the epistemic value is the divergence of the joint weights of states and outcomes from the product of the states' weights and the predicted outcomes (the joint's marginals where each likelihood totals one), and the expected information gain; where the likelihood reads a state only through its class, it is the classes' ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-expected-free-energy-and-its-terms); `ExpectedFreeEnergy.expectedFreeEnergy_eq_neg_epistemic_sub_pragmatic`, `ExpectedFreeEnergy.expectedFreeEnergy_same_of_coarse`). Over a course of seeking, each experiment chosen by a rule reading the experiments' expected free energies at the posterior after the course so far, that posterior is, where the agent's model is the core's, the core's share given a course chosen with some weight, and at every step the expected free energy is the classes', so seeking by expected free energy reads the prior only through the weight it gives each likelihood profile, in its choices and in the predictive weight of every course (*periphery*; `ExpectedFreeEnergy.givenCourse_eq_coursePosterior`, `ExpectedFreeEnergy.seeking_blind_to_split`, `ExpectedFreeEnergy.seeking_reads_profiles`).
- **The Markov blanket** (*any type*). Four parts of one determined law, told apart only by which part reads which; the agent's course is fixed by its start and its sensory course, the world's driven by the active course, each side's fixed by its start and the blanket's ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-markov-blanket-as-a-coupling-structure); `Blanket.Blanketed.agent_through_sensory`, `Blanket.Blanketed.internal_through_blanket`). Where a formation's couplings give the values of what couples into it (*World*), any model fed by them, with any policy and environment, is by construction the agent of a blanketed law whose sensory course is the formation's own course through its couplings, two of its courses alike on the blanket exactly where alike through them ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket); `BlanketCouplings.sensory_course`, `BlanketCouplings.blanket_iff_through`). Along one balanced run of a World (*World*), the step external and a value across steps of what couples in the sensory part, a formation's own level is the internal part of a law determined part by part exactly where its next level and act agree wherever its level, that value and its act agree, a test that bites only where these recur, which a drain in three phases fails ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket); `BlanketCouplings.levelParts_admitted_iff`, `BlanketCouplings.PhasedDrain.not_admitted`). Its statistical form, conditional independence given the blanket at a steady state, is the cross form on states each listed once, which detailed balance of the internal moves gives, with positive weights, moves present at every external state and rate ratios reading none ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-blanket-at-a-steady-state); `BlanketWeights.crossForm_of_detailedBalance`); in the linear case (*periphery*) the Lyapunov equation is the Helmholtz form, and with diagonal positive noise and the principle's sparse coupling, footnote 13's condition gives zero precision entries between internal and external coordinates ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-blanket-in-a-linear-steady-state); `LinearBlankets.precision_zero_of_footnote13`).
- **The existence claim** (*any type*). The weighting placing all weight on an outcome is conditioning on it, under which a listed outcome is of least surprisal; a listed outcome has the share nothing exactly where its weight is nothing ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-the-existence-claim-forbids); `Blanket.point_is_restrict`, `Blanket.forbids_iff`).

## 2. Where the two agree

- **Perception as conditioning** (Friston 2005, 2010). The minimizer of free energy over the beliefs where it is finite is the posterior, the core's share given the record ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-free-energy-bounds-surprisal)). **Reading** [AI reading, D45]: perception in the principle's sense, read exactly, adds no law to conditioning; it gives conditioning a variational form, whose use is approximation within a family.
- **The blanket** (Friston 2013; Virgo, Rosas and Biehl 2022). On a blanketed law, the deterministic case, a determined policy is a selection through the sensory course, and a reading through the blanket separates at most what the blanket tells apart; for the threshold witness the blanket is the World's coupling ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-markov-blanket-as-a-coupling-structure)), and wherever a formation's couplings give the values of what couples into it, any model fed by them is, by construction, the agent of a blanketed law, the threshold witness's join its case ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket); `BlanketCouplings.ThresholdCase.threshold_case`). The statistical form holds under detailed balance of the internal moves, with positive weights, moves present at every external state and rate ratios reading none, or, in the linear case, under the principle's sparse coupling with footnote 13's condition and diagonal positive noise, and need not hold with circulation; the theory's synchronous steps keep the coupling form on every run and can lose the statistical form with circulation, a loss of the discrete step ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-blanket-at-a-steady-state), [03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-blanket-in-a-linear-steady-state); `LinearBlankets.Autonomous.em_loses_internal_external`). **Reading** [AI reading, D45]: the partition names the couplings; the theory's agent theorems need only the coupling form, the principle's inference reading the statistical form.
- **The habit, read as a count** [AI reading, D45] (Friston and others 2016). A rule over two policies read from the count of its choices, in force from the start under a standing score, never leaves the region its threshold sets and outlives an unread loss ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-by-counted-choices)); active inference's own habit, which counts the end-of-trial posterior over policies, is not stated.
- **Risk, for a point prediction.** The chooser's risk is the set point's distance on one condition, and in the held pair its choices are the holders' takes ([05 §1](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-risk-minimization-on-a-shared-renewal), [05 §7](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-a-set-point-optimum-does-beyond)); that this is active inference's risk is a correspondence, for a preference falling alike on both sides of its mode.
- **Collective knowledge** [AI reading, D45]. The literature's claims about what a group knows that its members do not, which UE1's [search](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue1-fep-bridge/THIRD-PART-SEARCH.md) grades as not forbidden, read within the pooled couplings: a group's closure is the union of its members', and a selection through the pool separates two histories exactly where some member does ([05 §8](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-pooled-coupling-reaches)); so what a group knows that no member does, a synergy, is still read off its members' couplings.

## 3. What the theory forbids that the literature claims

Each theorem forbids a statement of a paper read as a guarantee or unqualified, on a model in the principle's own terms. The 2015 paper's simulation, which broke the symmetry by random initial expectations, the studies Da Costa and others cite, which have no shared draw, and the federated-inference simulation, which starts from random parameters, are not refuted; Albarracin and others and Wen report no simulation. Each reading fails where the papers' systems leave its hypotheses: if the 2015 scheme differentiated from identical expectations with no noise, its descent would read a cell's number; agents without a shared gated renewal, or with preferences over the joint outcome, escape the second and third; if seeking separated a pair every experiment ties, a likelihood would differ somewhere; if agents of the 2024 scheme came to one word from a symmetric start with no noise, their update would treat words or agents unalike. Each restates a known result of a neighbouring field in the principle's terms.

1. **Identical cells do not part under a descent that treats them alike** ([05 §9](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-identical-cells-under-a-descent-treating-them-alike); *any type*). Under a descent moving every cell by one rule from its own state and a field read off the states alone, cells alike at the start stay alike; with the field exchangeable, the minima of a symmetric free energy among configurations of one size come in exchanged pairs, a unique one with its cells alike. Against identical cells minimizing free energy being guaranteed unique places (Friston, Levin, Sengupta and Pezzulo 2015). Known: an asymmetric effect needs an asymmetric cause (Curie 1894), morphogenesis from a homogeneous state needs disturbances an instability amplifies (Turing 1952), and its uniqueness clause is the symmetric choice that singles out none (Angluin 1980). Under noise the theorem does not apply. For finite chains with noise of one law at every cell, each cell reading its own state and the field it senses, an exchangeable start stays exchangeable, deciding nothing of which cell takes which place, every rearrangement of an assignment keeping its weight ([05 §9](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-identical-cells-under-noise); *periphery*; `CellsUnderNoise.noisyDescent_exchangeable`, `CellsUnderNoise.noisyDescent_distinct_le`); the 2015 scheme has that form, so this forbids nothing of the paper. Known: Curie's principle in its form for indeterministic laws (Ismael 1997).
2. **One generative model with alike preferences, each over its own store, can end the renewal its holders share** ([05 §1](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-risk-minimization-on-a-shared-renewal); *World*; witnesses). In a witness, holders of one generative model, each taking its choice of least risk at its own store, each store at its set point at every step, together end the renewal they share; with a goal over the shared level instead, three regulators end it at once while two continue, so that goal is no guarantee either. Against each agent's goal being the group's under one generative model and one set of preferences (Da Costa and others 2025, §5.4), and against reading shared generative models as giving shared goals (Friston, Ramstead and others 2024, §3.4) as a guarantee: alike preferences over one's own store are indexical. Known: the commons (Lloyd 1833; Hardin 1968).
3. **Risk minimization does not keep a need the rest outruns** (the same record; *World*). A member outside a rest that outruns a gated renewal has its need end whatever its model and preference. Against risk minimization ensuring that an agent's needs are met (Albarracin and others 2024), in its wording; weak. Known: pivotality (Palfrey and Rosenthal 1984).
4. **Seeking corrects only within what some experiment separates** ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-seeking-cannot-separate); *any type*). Two hypotheses every experiment ties keep their prior order under every course of seeking, each experiment chosen from the course so far, whatever the policy of choice; in a witness seeking turns the order once an experiment separating it is chosen, as at the cue of the principle's T-maze. An agent choosing each experiment by a rule with natural weights reading expected free energy is one such policy (*periphery*; `ExpectedFreeEnergy.efe_seeking_keeps_order`, an instance), and over the reals the softmax keeps a tied order too, where the course is possible under the pair (`ExpectedFreeEnergy.softmax_order_kept`). Against inherent self-verification and self-correction by seeking information (Wen 2025, §2), unqualified; weak; not against active inference's account, which adds novelty and structure learning. Known: identification (Rothenberg 1971) in its Bayesian form (Poirier 1998).
5. **Agents learning a code from what they hear by a rule keeping the symmetry never come to share a uniquely greatest word from a symmetric start** ([05 §9](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-code-held-as-counts); *any type*, natural counts for learning and free energy). Agents hold a likelihood of words given meanings as counts; under a determined law keeping the exchange of agents together with a renaming of the words, from a start related to itself, two exchanged agents never hold one word as each one's uniquely greatest at a meaning where the renaming moves every word and is onto, or the exchange is an involution (`CountedCodes.never_shared`, `CountedCodes.never_shared_of_involution`). Learning from what is heard keeps the relation where the speaker map commutes with the exchange and the rule reads count by count and commutes with renaming, adding the counts heard among such rules (`CountedCodes.hear_keeps`, `CountedCodes.addHeard_keeps`), and so does a weight on what is heard read off the whole model where the weight is unchanged by the renaming (`CountedCodes.gatedHear_keeps`). Free-energy minimization breaks no symmetry: the bound in counts is unchanged by a renaming permuting the words, and under weights a renaming moving every word fixes, with a positive total, a belief at the bound over a listing of every word names no word (`CountedCodes.bound_invariant`, `CountedCodes.minimizer_no_unique_greatest`). Against language as an emergent property of free-energy minimization (Friston, Parr and others 2024, abstract), read universally, with the statement that any dyad or ensemble will come to share a generative model (Friston, Ramstead and others 2024, §4.1) as its general form on its natural reading, a common code; with that paper's hedge, some factors of a model, the shared model with no uniquely greatest word is such a factor, and that paper describes no system of its own. Federated inference's law, the hearer given the meaning, is read as keeping the relation for a renaming the same for every meaning [AI reading, D45]; its simulation starts each parameter at one plus a random variate and stands. Known: Curie's principle (1894) and conventions that symmetry does not select (Lewis 1969); under sampled signals reinforcement reaches a signalling system (Argiento, Pemantle, Skyrms and Volkov 2009), where this theorem does not apply.

UE1's [search](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue1-fep-bridge/THIRD-PART-SEARCH.md) grades three statements that communicating agents come to share one model or language as forbidden on their natural reading, under the theorems on a code formed between formations ([05 §9](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-code-formed-between-formations)). Two of them make the fifth entry above. The third, the duet (Friston and Frith 2015), is not stated in these terms: its birds have continuous states, no word counts and no simulated learning, and start with the same expectations. It stays under the code theorem, whose forms may be of any type, and is not counted.

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. Each of the five holds whatever model, preference, prior or policy its hypotheses leave open: what forbids is a theorem on the couplings and the courses, not a weighting read off a course, and so the existence claim (§4) cannot state it.

## 4. What persistence needs

Read off a course of a determined law, the principle's model is conditioning on the course itself, under which the course is of least surprisal; the occupation weighting read off a run admits every visited state, an ended run's resting state among them ([03 §6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-the-existence-claim-forbids)). So the existence claim alone forbids nothing, as its proponents concede (Ramstead and others 2023): forbidding needs a weighting fixed apart from the course, or a law of the courses themselves. So it is with a blanket's existence. With parts that read the course, every family of courses has a blanket; a degenerate reading of the configuration, which gives every family, or exactly the families whose law is determined, a law determined part by part (the blanket's form on the law the family shows, which may be partial), refutes no family whose law is determined; a sparse partition fixed apart from the family, on a law the same at every step, can be refuted, even by one run ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket); `BlanketCouplings.clocked_course`, `BlanketCouplings.whole_iff`, `BlanketCouplings.ReadsExternal.not_blanketed`). That the partition depends on a model is the critics' point (Bruineberg, Dołęga, Dewhurst and Baltieri 2022). Where a formation's couplings give the values of what couples into it, any model fed by them is, by construction, the agent of a blanketed law whose sensory course is the formation's own course through its couplings; the agent is a model, and having that law forbids nothing of the formation's course. With the formation's own level as the internal part, read along one balanced run with the step external, the reading can fail: the level is the internal part of a law determined part by part exactly where its next level and act agree wherever its level, a value across steps of what couples into it and its act agree, a test that bites only where these recur, which a drain in three phases from what does not couple in fails and a drain and feed in two phases meets ([03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-a-formations-couplings-as-its-blanket); `BlanketCouplings.levelParts_admitted_iff`, `BlanketCouplings.PhasedDrain.not_admitted`, `BlanketCouplings.Alternating.admitted`). On a blanketed agent reading its supply only by threshold, no action changing the supply, every reading of its internal course up to the step the break registers is alike, a free energy of its belief at any step before it among them, whatever its model and policy (a witness; [03 §1](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-the-markov-blanket-as-a-coupling-structure)): self-evidencing does not see its break coming, the invisible half of [a break seen coming](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#break-seen-coming) and of [the runaway](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-runaway-distance-uncoupled).

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. What persistence needs beyond self-evidencing is what the theory's renewal and coupling theorems state: the supply coupled in by level, a model answerable to what couples in, or another formation into which it couples, and the renewal of what feeds. Where the principle has an agent act ahead of actuality through a deep generative model, the theory places that anticipation, for a supply coupled in by threshold only, in priors, a coupling by level or another formation, never in the blanket's course.

## 6. What would count against this page

A measurement reaches these claims in different ways.

- **A theorem or witness named here.** No measurement refutes it: a theorem holds of every model its hypotheses admit, and a witness is one constructed model ([08 §10](chapters/08-METHOD.md#law)). It falls by a failed Lean check, or by a statement that does not say what this page says it says.
- **A forbidding of §3.** A reading of a paper falls where the paper's scheme leaves the theorem's hypotheses, as §3 says of each. A measurement of an actual system bears on it only through a stated correspondence reading the model's terms in measured quantities [AI reading, D45] ([08 §10](chapters/08-METHOD.md#law)), and none is stated here.
- **Thesis T6**, that a break is seen coming only through what couples into what breaks ([03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#break-seen-coming)). This is what here excludes a measurement. It forbids, among other cases, that given the stock's own course, its early-warning statistics separate runs in which a dependent's need is crossed from runs in which it is not, the dependent taking no measurable share ([08 §9](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)). Three frozen seabird tests did not reject it at their thresholds: one at low power, with estimates in the refuting direction; one reusing part of its rows; one whose negligible feedback is argued, not measured. None establishes conditional independence.
- **The reading of what persistence needs** (§4). A system seeing the break of a supply coupled in by threshold only, through no prior, coupling by level or other formation, would count against it and against T6.

## Standing

Every theorem named is near-definitional, a known result stated in the principle's terms, a corollary, or a witness. What is new lies in the joins (the free-energy minimizer as the core's conditioning, the bound with its equality in the pure core for beliefs in counts, and the real form read from it through the logarithm on the support; the blanket's agent form, with the unseen break for every model and policy) and in the third parts, which place five statements of the principle's literature, read as guarantees or unqualified, where they fail, two of them weakly; new also are the seeking form of identification, joined to seeking by expected free energy; expected free energy read through the lumping at every step of a course; the theorem on identical cells under noise, joined to the core's descent; the code theorem for beliefs held as counts, joined to the free-energy bound in counts; and the blanket's statistical form on the theory's conditioning in the pure core, footnote 13's theorem for any set of coordinates, the joins to the blanketed law, and a circulating witness in which the synchronous scheme loses the four-part blanket; and the three levels at which a blanket's existence constrains (the join of a formation's couplings to a blanketed law, near-definitional, is not counted new). In the third parts the theory reaches farther than the principle alone, in what it forbids; it states less than the principle where it states nothing yet, in densities, continuous time and deep models among others, and it does not yet reach farther than the sciences taken together ([08 §8](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)).
