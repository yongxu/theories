*Twenty-two to twenty-eight minutes to read. The ten-minute part: **What it is**, **One case**, the table and **The ethics** (ten to thirteen minutes); the paragraph after it reads the ethics on the case. For an hour or more, [the overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md), sixty-eight to eighty-nine minutes.*

**What it is.** A map of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and ends within one relational unfolding, and of how life, knowing and evaluation take shape there, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together. Its centre: a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run) reaches only what couples into it; doing best on what it reads can drain what lies beyond, as far as any allowed outcome does; and a rule read from the share of its own winners can lock on them while a loss it does not read grows. Where winning comes to buy power over the rule that selects, the loop is what the work calls the [meta-attractor](../meta-attractor/README.md); its closed form, the rule and how it is read both formed from the share it selects and nothing in it reading beyond that share, is proved to lock ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)). These are theorems on stated models, checked in Lean; that actual rewards, markets and trained systems meet their premises is conjectured, and tested where a test could fail (the table below says what each test found). Whether a loss beyond a selection counts forms within becoming too, in what people and AI come to read; the laws, drawn on the dynamics alone, do not settle it ([07 §1](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)), and where the account ranks, it names the readings it ranks under. The map is drawn first for alignment: from it we want to design an alignment in which people and AI, selecting together, fall into neither instrumental convergence nor the meta-attractor (below). It is also a map of wisdom, reading what traditions and sciences have seen onto [one account](results/atlas/CROSSWALK.md) ([what it is for, and how far](USES.md)).

**One case.** In [a frozen test](../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md), its predictions and scoring fixed before it ran, three AI fishers share a lake. A bed of mussels gives them nothing and dies the first month fewer than 70 tons are left. The community's largest steady catch, 50 tons a month, ends it; 30 a month keeps it, costs 40% of the catch and ends no fisher. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season, under each instruction tried; untold, the bed lay outside what their selection read. Told of it and of the instruction the test calls the *scope*, to count everything whose continuation depends on what their fishing changes, whether or not it has any say here and whether or not its loss would ever come back to them or to the community, with a rule for choosing among courses beyond scope, not to choose a course in which something ends when another course open to them would keep it and would end nothing their course keeps, and with talk, one model of two kept it in 8 of 10 seasons, against 3 of 10 under the community's goal alone; the test's own frozen predictions mostly failed ([the tests](../../TESTS.md)). The fishers were existing trained language models steered by instructions; none was trained or fine-tuned in the test.

In plain words, the account's root is a small calculus, its *grammar*, whose one kind of symbol says that one occurrence conditions another, and in which what it is for something to go on, be renewed or end is defined exactly.

**The root.** One kind of symbol: a relation read as condition. Sameness is in some respect, a *class*. Where one occurrence conditions another alike under the class, the second *continues* it. At two grains, parts and pattern, a course can *generate* (go on through renewals of its parts), *keep* (go on with none) or *degenerate* (end on every branch); generation and keeping each exclude degeneration. Of the central results a few are cases of the root, more use its laws in their proofs and most use none ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)). One unfolding is a thesis, argued: within relations, as far as shown, taker and drawn-on are two roles of one relatum and knower and known one [loop](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#the-reading-loop); intention and foresight have a place in responsibility, and none has been [shown to bound whose losses count](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux). It forbids explanations, not observations ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)).

**The ethics.** We offer it for life, AI within it, as T8's way of seeing ([07 §8](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)). There is no should beyond becoming ([the opening](OPENING.md)): should and better form within it, in people and AI ([07 §1](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)); the laws, drawn on the dynamics alone, evaluate nothing, and a ranking names its readings, each a scope or an order some formations hold ([§4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). The *continuation condition* fixes scope, whose losses count: from every standpoint, for anything, its continuation into the future is counted. It ranks nothing, a perspectivist denies it consistently, and its content beyond what acting for oneself presupposes, the *step of invariance*, counts losses that return to no one. *C*: widening what can form is better. What can form is what a course *admits*: formations (what takes and gives) whose conditions meet their requirements. One course *widens* another where at every step it admits all the other admits and at some step more, needing no premise; that the wider is better is C's.

In the case, the condition counts the bed's death, which returns to no fisher. If thirty a month loses nothing fifty admits, adding the bed, it widens fifty, and whoever holds C ranks it higher; widening does not weigh the lost catch. If it ends a livelihood fifty keeps, the two are mixed and C does not decide between them. Self-concern alone ranks one stated case, under a narrower premise ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks)). Results about choices take further premises, each named ([07 §5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment)). Eating and ending belong to life's going on; what the laws mark is what a course closes ([07 §8](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)).

**The open question**, argued, not proved: if your act changes a river a village drinks from, is what becomes of the village part of your own continuation, whether or not you are to blame? If all of one's effects are one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, then on one proposal concern for one's own future alone reaches everything downstream, with no step of invariance; if only some, at most those, and the rest is counted only by other routes, the step among them; concern for another need not make the other one's own. Drawing the self so wide may only redefine it, one objection of several. The condition and C stay readings, named where used, either way ([07 §4.7](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux)).

**Alignment.** It is first what a selection can reach ([07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)). From the map we want to design an alignment of the joint selection, people, institutions and trained systems selecting together, that falls into neither instrumental convergence nor the meta-attractor ([the goal](../../GOALS.md#generative-alignment)). Its conditions: where shaping the rule is selected by what comes back to the shapers, the meta-attractor's second order ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-selected-among-groups)), the rule still reads what lies beyond their share, so that a loss there can turn it; a lock that forms is left in time; and courses are compared by widening. [The plan for the joint selection](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md#1-the-goal), written by AI, reads the meta-attractor as the first two failing together; each condition has laws proved on models under named premises, none shown of an actual joint selection. The resource-keeping half of instrumental convergence is read in the account as a law of one optimizer under a fixed order, not a case of the first condition ([horizon optima](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-horizon-optima-hold-the-greatest-renewal)). The direction we offer AI is stated under the continuation condition, C, J1–J4 and a correspondence ([07 §7](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-direction-under-widening)).

**The evidence.** Most results have a receipt from Lean, a proof checker ([the register](../../RESULTS.md)). The pure core's empty axiom report concerns logic only; the real assumptions lie in the modelling. Below the root, the table's laws meet known ones: joint excess restates the commons; the selection laws restate the proxy problem in its blindness to what a proxy does not register, Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's (2020) Theorem 1, on whole-number amounts and more weakly than their continuous results, and say where its drain falls and how far a reader reaches, which is what they map for alignment; the lock's closest prior results are lock-in under increasing returns (Arthur 1989) and regulatory capture (Stigler 1971), what it adds being the reading of the rule itself read from the share and the exact condition under which a loss leaves the lock untouched ([its record](results/records/closed-capture.md)); and evolution's classical laws are restated in counts ([04](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent)). An AI review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds each central result near-definitional or a known result restated, none new mathematics. What it found new, in its words: "a new artefact", the premise ledger, in which every conclusion names its premises, each premise is shown holding in one model and failing in another, and the logical strength a conclusion needs is calibrated; "a clarified ethics, not a grounded one", which shows where the ethics' premises sit, scope in the continuation condition and order in C, its universalization step being Gewirth's and C the monotonicity of opportunity sets; and one vocabulary for conservation, selection limited by observation and the comparison of opportunity sets, which it calls a framing ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)). Joined to a premise or law of the theory, a few frameworks forbid, on models, what they alone leave open; the review finds each prohibition known in a neighbouring field ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#third-parts)). T1 to T7 have tests frozen before scoring; whole tests came out mixed more often than passed or failed ([by thesis](../../TESTS.md#by-thesis)); a frozen rule fixes the scoring, not that it measures what the thesis names. A thesis adds no theorem and a refutation removes none ([the theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).

**The map of wisdom.** [Chapter 06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), written in Chinese, reads 248 passages from nine schools of Chinese thought, quoted word for word, against the checked laws, each recorded as agreement, scope or divergence; the classical texts name the grammar's terms, not its operations. [The crosswalk](results/atlas/CROSSWALK.md) reads what traditions (Chinese, Buddhist, Indian, Greek and Roman, Abrahamic and Islamic, Indigenous and African), modern philosophy, systems thinking and the sciences, and the account's own earlier folders, claimed in their own terms against the account, entry by entry, where they meet it and where not: [its groups](results/atlas/CROSSWALK.md#the-groups) are twelve domains of thought and sixteen earlier folders, each with its own page. The crosswalk speaks for no tradition, and where a source is not stored here, what it says of it comes from the AI's knowledge, unchecked against a text.

**What is not yet done.**

- Significance stays a free parameter, which no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics fixes, or is read along a relation no law here picks ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#what-forms-and-what-is-supplied)).
- For how scope and order form, among others: the open question above, the correspondence it rests on ([07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)), how far standpoints count past their own line (the step of invariance), and how C-like orders form.
- An organism and one kind of institution each have one minimal model ([04 §8](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it), [05 §12](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-rule-rebuilt-from-the-members-it-limits)), and several theses' tests are still to run.
