# The Theory of Becoming in one page

[中文](ONE-PAGE.zh.md) · [The overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The eight chapters](chapters/README.md) · [The theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md)

*About ten minutes to read.*

**What it is.** An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life, knowing and evaluation form within it, without assuming that becoming is good. It was made with AI, the Lean proofs included. The proofs are checked by machine, everything else only by further AI review; no person has yet reviewed it ([the review packet](review/README.md)). Each claim states its [standing](chapters/08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases): proved on a named base, a witness (one constructed case), a reading [AI reading], a thesis or a premise.

**The line of the argument** [AI reading]. Anything goes on only as continuation, and a pattern outlasts its parts by renewal. Takers each within what is renewed can together end it and what depends on it. A selection (a knower, a training run) reaches only what couples into it, and what ends a renewal can lie beyond that. So whose losses count turns on how far what each continues with reaches, which the dynamics leave open, and calling the wider course better is a further premise.

**The root.** It has one kind of symbol: a relation read as condition (`x` is among what `y` comes from). Sameness is always in some respect, a *class*. Where one occurrence conditions another and the two are alike under the class, the second *continues* the first. Read at two grains, fine for the parts and coarse for the pattern, a course can *generate* (go on through renewals of its parts), *keep* (go on with none) or *degenerate* (end on every branch), and generation never overlaps degeneration. These are theorems for every relation and class ([01](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class), [02](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains)). That this grammar explains how things go on is argued, not proved; that every becoming can be represented in it is open.

**What the laws say.** Most laws are proved on models of the root (webs of conditions with whole-number amounts and discrete steps), each under hypotheses its home section states.

- *Change and return.* Over finitely many configurations, a determined law that merges none brings every one back ([01](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-recurrence-without-premise)).
- *What ends a pattern.* Takers each within what a renewal gives can together exceed it; where the renewal depends on what it renews, enough such steps from a low enough level end it and what depends on it ([05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)).
- *Knowing.* A selection reaches exactly what couples into its selector. Near-definitional: its force lies in the premise that a selector selects through just those couplings ([03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)).
- *Life and evolution.* Price's equation, Fisher's theorem in its exact case, Hamilton's rule and drift hold in counting form ([04](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent)).
- *Optimization.* Where there is room, every optimum of an order rising with what couples in drains what lies beyond as far as any eligible outcome does. That trained systems drain so is thesis T3, conjectured ([05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in)).

**The ethics.** No law above evaluates: none says a loss is a harm or restraint better. The ethics adds two premises with two roles, and the theory asserts neither; each is a hypothesis wherever used, its warrant open ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)).

- *Scope*, whose losses count: the *continuation condition*, that from every standpoint, for anything, its continuation into the future has significance. Its content beyond what acting for oneself presupposes, the *step of invariance*, counts losses that return to no one; by itself it ranks nothing.
- *Order*, which course is better: *C*, that widening what can form is better. One course *widens* another where at every step it admits all the other admits and at some step more. That order needs no premise; calling it better is C, and mixed cases, something gained and something lost, stay unranked.

In a fished stock, the boats' own continuation ranks a collapse that ends them below keeping the stock, with no C; the condition adds losses that return to no boat, such as the seabirds'; ranking courses where the boats are not at stake needs C. With significance a free parameter, no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the condition, by construction ([07 §1](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)). Under both premises the ethics argues one direction, thesis T8: for life, the way is 生生, life in the whole and its continuing generation (生发, not the map's generation) [AI reading, D1], compared by widening; no verdict binding every possible mind is claimed ([07 §8](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)). Choices need further premises, named; alignment is first what a selection can reach ([07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)).

**The evidence.** Each registered result has a record of its premises, evidence and limits, most with a Lean receipt (counts in [the register](../../RESULTS.md)). Each source declaration in the Lean package's pure core has an empty axiom report. That concerns logic only: the real assumptions lie in the modelling choices (discrete steps, whole-number amounts). An AI review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds none of the central results new mathematics; the work adds a ledger of premises, each shown holding in one model and failing in another, and an ethics with scope and order apart ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)). Eight theses about actual systems name what refutes them; their tests were frozen before scoring, and some passed and some failed (T1 passed in three settings and failed in four; T5's first test failed every frozen rule). A frozen rule fixes how a verdict is scored, not that it measures what the thesis names ([the theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).

**The classical texts.** The grammar's terms are named from classical Chinese texts, not its operations; the readings are AI's, checked by no person, and divergences are kept ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), in Chinese).

**What is not yet done.**

- Most laws are proved on the models, not yet restated on the grammar; each central declaration has its standing on the root's edge, with its reason ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)). <!-- Refresh at OU's pass after §八 step 5: say that each conditional law is written as an exact condition with a counterexample where it fails, or what is left of that. -->
- The root and the ethics are one account only in part: held de se (each center of itself), the condition is a statement on the root, met by some nets and failed by others: nothing lies outside every center's line under the one of that relation (`«续无外»`); along a net with no cycle it holds only with one center ([07 §4.5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root)); but the free significance is not lifted onto the root.
- A warrant for the condition comes neither from the dynamics' laws alone nor from logic alone. Still to be argued: that one's own line is a regularity of its own whose class holds one's effects (P2 and P4 of [07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)), which would carry concern for one's continuation past one's end, with P3 a correspondence not shown; the step of invariance, for what that does not reach; and a reason for C.
- An organism and an institution have no model yet, and several theses' tests are still to run.
