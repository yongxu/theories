# The Theory of Becoming in one page

[中文](ONE-PAGE.zh.md) · [The overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The eight chapters](chapters/README.md) · [The theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md)

*About twelve minutes to read.*

**What it is.** An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life and knowing take shape within it, and how far evaluation does, without assuming that becoming is good. Made with AI, the Lean proofs included; the proofs are checked by machine, everything else only by further AI review, and no person has yet reviewed it ([the review packet](review/README.md)).

**The root.** One kind of symbol: a relation read as condition. Sameness is in some respect, a *class*. Where one occurrence conditions another alike under the class, the second *continues* it. At two grains a course can *generate* (go on through renewals of its parts), *keep* (go on with none) or *degenerate* (end on every branch).

**The whole at a glance.** Its centre [AI reading]: a selection can succeed while what it cannot read is lost, and the laws do not say whether that loss counts.

**The ethics.** No law evaluates: none says a loss is a harm or restraint better. The ethics adds two premises, each a hypothesis wherever used ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). The *continuation condition*: from every standpoint, for anything, its continuation into the future has significance. It ranks nothing; its content beyond what acting for oneself presupposes, the *step of invariance*, counts losses that return to no one. *C*: widening what can form is better. One course *widens* another where at every step it admits all the other admits and at some step more. C gives order; a ranking needs it or a premise in its place. C leaves mixed cases, something gained and something lost, unranked.

Read on a fished stock where every later boat depends on it, a law of the World model says: every order increasing in the boats' own continuation, a narrower premise than C, ranks a course admitting only the boats, so ending with them, below every course agreeing with it until then and adding to that continuation ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks)). The condition adds the seabirds' losses. Choices take further premises, each named ([07 §5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment)); alignment is first what a selection can reach ([07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)).

**The open question** [AI reading], argued and not proved: if your act changes a river a village drinks from, is what becomes of the village part of your own continuation, whether or not you are to blame? If all of one's effects are one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, then on one proposal concern for one's own future alone reaches everything downstream, with no step of invariance; if only some, at most those, and the rest needs the step. Drawing the self so wide may only redefine it, one objection of several, and whatever no chain joins still needs the step. The laws stand either way, and the condition and C stay premises ([07 §4.7](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux)).

**The evidence.** Each result has a record of its premises, evidence and limits, most with a Lean receipt ([the register](../../RESULTS.md)). The pure core's empty axiom report concerns logic only; the real assumptions lie in the modelling. An AI review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds none of the central results new mathematics; what survives, none of it a discovery, is a ledger of premises, each shown holding in one model and failing in another, and an ethics with scope and order apart ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)). T1 to T7 have tests frozen before scoring, some passed and some failed; a frozen rule fixes how a verdict is scored, not that it measures what the thesis names. A failure moves a thesis and removes no theorem ([the theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).

**The classical texts.** They name the grammar's terms, not its operations; the readings are AI's, checked by no person ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), in Chinese).

**What is not yet done.**

- Which relation, if any, significance is read along: no law here picks one. Otherwise significance is a free parameter, which by construction no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics can fix ([what forms and what is supplied](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#what-forms-and-what-is-supplied)).
- Still to be argued for a warrant, among others: the open question above; that the actual unfolding joins lines through what one conditions, a correspondence not shown ([07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)); the step of invariance, for what these do not reach; and a reason for C.
- An institution has no model yet, an organism one minimal model, read as such [AI reading] ([04 §8](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it)), and several theses' tests are still to run.
