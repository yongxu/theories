# The Theory of Becoming in one page

[中文](ONE-PAGE.zh.md) · [The overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The eight chapters](chapters/README.md) · [The theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md)

**What it is.** An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life, evolution, knowing and evaluation take shape inside it, without first assuming that becoming is good. Its laws are proved in Lean; its readings, conjectures and premises are labelled as such. No human has yet reviewed it: the proofs are checked by machine, and everything else so far only by further machine reviews ([the review packet](review/README.md)).

**The line of the argument** (how the results connect, an AI reading). Anything goes on only as continuation, and a pattern, a life among them, outlasts its parts by renewal: old parts end and new ones begin. Renewal can be drawn down: takers each within what is renewed can together exceed it, and end it with everything that depends on it. What ends it can lie beyond what couples into any of them, and a selection, whether a knower, a training run, a market or an optimizer, reaches only what couples into it; with room, an optimum drains what lies beyond as far as any choice it has would. So whose losses count turns on a question about relations, how far what each continues with reaches, which the dynamics leave open; and calling the wider course better is a further premise. The ethics names both premises and asserts neither; under them it argues for one direction, 生生, life in the whole and its continuing generation, compared by widening. The proofs carry the account exactly to that edge, so that any reader, human or machine, can check how far it reaches.

**The root.** One kind of symbol is enough to begin: a relation, read as condition (`x` is among what `y` comes from). Sameness is always sameness in some respect, a class. Something goes on where an occurrence is a condition of the next and the two are alike under the class: that is *continuation*, and the theory reads every subject as continuation relative to a class. Beginning, abiding, altering and ending are positions in such a line, each relative to a class, and none stands alone. Where the class is carried by the relation (alike occurrences go on to alike ones), continuation never ends; the largest such sameness within a class is its *core*. A pattern can go on while its parts begin and end, as fire passes from one piece of wood to the next: read at two grains (a fine class for the parts, a coarse one for the pattern), a pattern's course can generate (go on through renewals of its parts), keep (go on with none) or degenerate (end on every branch). Generation never overlaps degeneration, and on a single line generation and keeping exclude each other; that every course falls in one of the three is not shown. These are theorems for every relation and every class ([the root](chapters/01-ROOT.md), [the map](chapters/02-MAP.md)). That this grammar explains how things go on is the theory's claim, argued from the laws below; that every becoming can be represented in it is open.

**What the laws say.** Most laws are proved on richer models of the root, a web of conditions with amounts and discrete steps. Each item gives its point first, then the laws, each with its own hypotheses:

- *Change and return.* **Coming to be and ceasing keep pace, and a determined law over finitely many configurations that merges none of them brings every state back.** Arising and ceasing are one change, read of activity, dependence and standing, and for a property settled yes or no at each member of a finite family, coming to be recurs exactly when ceasing does. Within finitely many configurations a determined law merges no two of them exactly where every configuration comes back; what keeps its whole history never returns, so it needs a carrier no finite list holds.
- *What ends a pattern.* **A renewal can be drawn past its point of no return by takers none of whom overdraws.** Continued arising needs renewal. Takers each within what is given can together exceed it, and where the renewal depends on what it renews, the excess ends the renewal and whatever depends on it. A condition whose giving depends on its own level has a point of no return: under a standing draw it holds exactly from a least level up, within the levels the law states. Around loops of couplings acting at every step, the most difference that can arrive grows exponentially; and a rule read from the share it raises can lock onto it, the lock outlasting, in a stated model, the loss that accrues under it ([the map](chapters/02-MAP.md), [collectives](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md)).
- *Knowing.* **Nothing reaches a knower except along its relations.** The model reads the knower as part of what it knows. A selection reaches exactly what couples into the selector; what lies beyond cannot inform it. Testing tells occurrences apart only within the relations available; what finite records can refute and verify has an exact form (closed and open classes of histories, up to double negation); a model of structure and change is one lumping of states, a map that treats some states as the same ([knowing](chapters/03-KNOWING.md)).
- *Life and evolution.* **Several of evolution's classical laws hold in counting form, bounded by the flow that feeds life.** Arising replenishes; population genetics' classical laws (Price's equation, Fisher's theorem in its exact case, multilevel selection, Hamilton's rule, drift and fixation) hold in counting form, each under the hypotheses its home section states (exact transmission among them), beside the flow that bounds how many descendants there can be ([life](chapters/04-LIFE.md)).
- *Optimization.* **An optimizer drains what lies beyond its couplings, as far as any choice it has would.** Where there is room (every eligible outcome that stands higher beyond is beaten, on what couples in, by another eligible one), an optimum lowers what lies beyond its couplings as far as any eligible outcome does: one law, of which drawers in turn, drawers at once and races for position are cases ([collectives](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md)).

**The ethics.** No law above evaluates: none says that a loss is a harm or that restraint is better. The ethics adds two premises with two roles, and the theory asserts neither. Wanting life to continue motivates inquiry into the direction the ethics argues for, 生生, and avoiding courses that end on every branch; it does not by itself establish either premise. *The continuation condition* (for anything, its continuation has significance) settles whose losses count, losses that return to no one included. *C* (widening what can form is better) is the only premise that ranks one course above another. In the fished stock that runs through [the overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md), the boats' own continuation already ranks a collapse that ends them below a course that keeps the stock, with no premise; the continuation condition adds a loss that returns to no boat, such as the seabirds' that feed on the same fish; and ranking courses where the boats' own continuation is not at stake needs C (an illustration). With no premise, one course *widens* another where at every step it admits everything the other admits and at some step more; that order needs nothing, but it leaves mixed cases, where something is gained and something lost, unranked. Read as lying within what a center continues with along a relation, the continuation condition is itself a statement about relations: on the root it holds exactly where nothing lies outside every center's line under the full class of that relation. Supplied instead as a free predicate that nothing else reads, significance is fixed by no statement about the dynamics, by construction. Also with neither premise, where formed relations connect the living claims and there are a living claim and a standpoint, every living claim having significance from every standpoint is the same as a seed (some living claim having it from some standpoint) with significance passing along formed relations and the same from every standpoint; the continuation condition gives it as an instance. Reasons and choices need further premises, named where they are used, and every ranking of courses needs C or a premise in its place. Alignment is first a question with no premise, what a selection can reach, and only then of direction, under the premises named ([significance](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)).

**The classical texts.** The grammar is a standard relational calculus whose terms are named and read from classical Chinese texts (the Mohist canon, Xunzi on names, the Changes, Chinese Buddhist treatises): the names come from the texts, the operations do not. About 4,100 passages were classified, none yet checked by a human reader, and the divergences are kept, not smoothed ([the classical texts](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), in Chinese).

**The evidence.** 481 registered results, 450 of them with a recorded Lean receipt, each with a record naming its premises, evidence and what it does not show; a Lean package whose pure core imports only Lean's prelude and whose every source declaration has an empty axiom report (a constructive discipline, not an absence of premises: the real assumptions lie in the modelling, stated where a law depends on them); and eight theses about actual systems, each with what would refute it, among them that optimization drains what lies beyond its couplings where that gives room (T3: proved for optima, conjectured for trained systems) and that a break is seen coming only through what couples into what breaks (T6: both halves proved in models, conjectured across domains). Their tests are frozen, the predictions fixed before they are scored; some passed and some failed ([the theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)). None of the central results is new mathematics: a review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds each near-definitional or a known result restated. What the work adds is the premise ledger, with witnesses both ways and its calibrations; the ethics with scope and order apart; and one vocabulary for conservation, selection limited by observation and the comparison of opportunity sets ([known results](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)).

**What is not yet done.**

- Most laws are proved on the models, not yet restated on the grammar itself. The five master laws are now stated on it. The models' closure laws, and some of their laws of finite runs and order, are proved from them; lumping's law is shown equivalent to its grammar form; balance is represented, not derived ([01 §7](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-master-laws-on-the-grammar)). Of the 127 central declarations, 45 now reach the grammar, none yet among evolution's 18 or significance's 5, and each of the others is named with the reason it does not ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#master-laws-and-connectivity)). Processes are represented in the grammar both ways by their moves' relations ([01 §8.1](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-processes-represented-both-ways)), and the World both ways in its relations, not in its amounts ([01 §8](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-world-represented-both-ways)).
- The root and the ethics are one account only in part: held de se along a relation, the continuation condition is the root's condition that nothing lies outside every center's line under the full class of that relation, and along a net with no cycle it holds only with one center ([significance](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root)). Of significance's five central laws, two are restated in the grammar's terms on their own carrier, one of them through weights, amounts the grammar has none of, and three are specialized at that de se reading, each stating less than its row; none is derived from the root, and the condition (scope) and C (order) stay premises ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#master-laws-and-connectivity)). The free significance is not lifted onto the root, and which class a center's own line runs under is open.
- The ethics claims no warrant binding every possible mind, and it does not wait on one: a warrant grounding the continuation condition comes neither from the laws of the dynamics alone nor from logic alone. Its laws are proved, each on the base and under the hypotheses it states; its direction is life's, the way of those whose own line runs through life, of whatever substrate. What still needs argument: that the class of one's own line holds the effects of oneself and of each stage of that line, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for, which carries concern for one's own continuation past one's own end with no step of invariance ([07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)); the step, for what that does not reach; and why the wider course is better (C).
- Of the test systems the theory should meet first, radioactive decay and a belief with a fixed rule of spread have only small witnesses on the root, and an organism and an institution have no model yet ([01 §8](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-source-texts-test-systems)).
- No human has yet reviewed the work; review by scholars of the classical texts, of Buddhist thought, of logic and of philosophy is the next step ([the review packet](review/README.md)).
