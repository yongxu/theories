# The Theory of Becoming in one page

[中文](ONE-PAGE.zh.md) · [The overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The eight chapters](chapters/README.md) · [The theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md)

*About ten minutes to read.*

**What it is.** An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life, knowing and standards form within it, without assuming that becoming is good. Made with AI, the Lean proofs included; the proofs are checked by machine, everything else only by further AI review, and no person has yet reviewed it ([the review packet](review/README.md)).

**The whole at a glance.** Its centre [AI reading]: a selection can succeed while what it cannot read is lost, and the laws do not say whether that loss counts.

**The ethics.** No law evaluates: none says a loss is a harm or restraint better. Each premise is a hypothesis wherever used ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). The condition ranks nothing; its content beyond what acting for oneself presupposes, the *step of invariance*, counts losses that return to no one. C gives order, in general alone; mixed cases, something gained and something lost, stay unranked.

In a fished stock, every order rising with the boats' own continuation ranks a collapse that ends them below a course keeping every admission and adding to that continuation: a narrower premise than C. The condition adds the seabirds' losses; ranking courses where the boats are not at stake needs C. Choices take further premises, each named ([07 §5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment)); alignment is first what a selection can reach ([07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)).

**The open question** [AI reading], argued and not proved: if your act changes a river a village drinks from, is what becomes of the village part of your own continuation, whether or not you are to blame? If all of one's effects are one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, then on one proposal concern for one's own future alone reaches everything downstream, with no step of invariance; if only some, at most those, and the rest needs the step. Drawing the self so wide may only redefine it; that is the objection to meet. The laws stand either way, and the condition and C stay premises ([07 §4.7](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux)).

**The evidence.** Each result has a record of its premises, evidence and limits, most with a Lean receipt ([the register](../../RESULTS.md)). The pure core's empty axiom report concerns logic only; the real assumptions lie in the modelling. An AI review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds none of the central results new mathematics; the work adds a ledger of premises, each shown holding in one model and failing in another, and an ethics with scope and order apart ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)). T1 to T7 have tests frozen before scoring, some passed and some failed. In one, AI fishers kept no dependent they were not told of; told of it and of the scope, with talk, one model kept it in 8 of 10 seasons and the other failed ([T4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)). A failure moves a thesis and removes no theorem: T3's first wording failed where the learner reads the condition, and was narrowed ([the theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).

**The classical texts.** They name the grammar's terms, not its operations; the readings are AI's, checked by no person ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), in Chinese).

**What is not yet done.**

- On a second reading, not a derivation, the condition says that every center's closure along a relation is the whole, which some nets meet and others fail ([07 §4.5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root)). Otherwise significance is supplied, and no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics fixes it ([evaluation](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)).
- Still to be argued for a warrant, among others: the open question above; that the actual unfolding joins lines through what one conditions, a correspondence not shown ([07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)); the step of invariance, for what these do not reach; and a reason for C.
- An institution has no model yet, an organism one minimal model ([04 §8](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it)), and several theses' tests are still to run.
