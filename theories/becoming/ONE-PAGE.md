# The Theory of Becoming in one page

[中文](ONE-PAGE.zh.md) · [The overview](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The eight chapters](chapters/README.md) · [The theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md)

*About eleven minutes to read.*

**What it is.** An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, of how life and knowing take shape there and how far evaluation does, without assuming that becoming is good. Made with AI, proofs included; the proofs are checked by machine, everything else only by AI review, and no person has yet reviewed it ([the review packet](review/README.md)).

**One case.** In [a frozen test](../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md), three AI fishers share a lake. A bed of mussels gives them nothing and dies the first month fewer than 70 tons are left. The community's best course, 50 tons a month, ends it; 30 a month keeps it, costs 40% of the catch and ends no fisher. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season, under any instruction. They are language models told what to do, not systems trained. The account's centre [AI reading]: a selection can succeed while what it cannot read is lost, and the laws do not say whether that loss counts.

**The root.** One kind of symbol: a relation read as condition. Sameness is in some respect, a *class*. Where one occurrence conditions another alike under the class, the second *continues* it. At two grains, parts and pattern, a course can *generate* (go on through renewals of its parts), *keep* (go on with none) or *degenerate* (end on every branch); generation and keeping each exclude degeneration. Of the central results a few are cases of the root, more use its laws in their proofs and most use none ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)). One unfolding is a thesis, argued: within relations, as far as shown, taker and drawn-on are two roles of one relatum and knower and known one [loop](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#the-reading-loop), and responsibility is [not shown to differentiate](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment). It forbids explanations, not observations ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)).

**The ethics.** No law evaluates: none says a loss is a harm or restraint better. The ethics adds two premises, neither asserted, warrant open, each a hypothesis wherever used ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)); the dynamics cannot show them wrong. The *continuation condition* fixes scope, whose losses count: from every standpoint, for anything, its continuation into the future has significance. It ranks nothing, a perspectivist denies it consistently, and its content beyond what acting for oneself presupposes, the *step of invariance*, counts losses that return to no one. *C*: widening what can form is better. What can form is what a course *admits*: formations (what takes and gives) whose conditions meet their requirements. One course *widens* another where at every step it admits all the other admits and at some step more, which needs no premise; that the wider is better does. C gives order; a ranking needs it or a premise in its place.

In the case, the condition counts the bed's death, which returns to no fisher. If thirty a month loses nothing fifty admits, adding the bed, it widens fifty and C ranks it higher; widening does not weigh the lost catch [AI reading]. If it ends a livelihood fifty keeps, the two are mixed and C leaves them unranked. Self-concern alone ranks one stated case, under a narrower premise ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks)). Choices take further premises, each named ([07 §5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment)); alignment is first what a selection can reach, and a direction follows only conditionally ([07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)).

**The open question** [AI reading], argued, not proved: if your act changes a river a village drinks from, is what becomes of the village part of your own continuation, whether or not you are to blame? If all of one's effects are one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, then on one proposal concern for one's own future alone reaches everything downstream, with no step of invariance; if only some, at most those, and the rest needs the step. Drawing the self so wide may only redefine it, one objection of several. The condition and C stay premises either way ([07 §4.7](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux)).

**The evidence.** Most results have a Lean receipt ([the register](../../RESULTS.md)). The pure core's empty axiom report concerns logic only; the real assumptions lie in the modelling. An AI review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds none of the central results new mathematics, evolution's classical laws in counts ([04](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent)) among them; what survives is a ledger of premises, each shown holding in one model and failing in another, and an ethics with scope and order apart ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)). T1 to T7 have tests frozen before scoring, some passed and some failed; a frozen rule fixes the scoring, not that it measures what the thesis names. A thesis adds no theorem and a refutation removes none ([the theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).

**The classical texts** name the grammar's terms, not its operations; the readings are AI's ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md), in Chinese).

**What is not yet done.**

- For a warrant, among others: the open question above, the correspondence it rests on ([07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)), the step of invariance, and a reason for C.
- An institution has no model yet, an organism one minimal model [AI reading] ([04 §8](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it)), and several theses' tests are still to run.
