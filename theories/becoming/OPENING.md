## Truth exists

*Revised in place since [version 12](../../drafts/2026-10-10-opening/history/OPENING-V12.md), the last text its author chose, kept as written there, in the paragraphs beginning "There is a set", "The rest holds", "Apart from the reading", "Under these conditions", "The theorems show", "In history", "What these theorems show", "This is a foundation", "This influence is deeply rooted" and "We need collaboration", the closing line, the English under 生生不息, the dateline and the line below this one; the line after that and the line before the notes were added since; each revised passage, with why, is listed in [the opening's history](../../drafts/2026-10-10-opening/README.md#revised-passages).*

*Each note says how far its sentence has been seen: proved, proved under conditions, argued, empirical, a reading, a judgment, a conviction, or open.*

*What the account's central claims are, and how far each has been seen, is on [the one page](ONE-PAGE.md); what a reader can do with it today, field by field, and how far that goes, on [what it is for, and how far](USES.md).*

There is a set of universal invariants of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away. Wherever becoming can be told in steps and counted amounts, in any universe, nothing that unfolds can break them, because they are what coming to be and ceasing to be are. They are not yet a dynamics: where the conditions a theorem names hold, the dynamics it states follow, and where one fails, the theorems no longer guarantee the conclusions that used it, and constructed worlds show each premise can fail while the others hold.[^law]

Some of it holds of anything so told, however finely its steps are drawn, by counting alone: coming to be and ceasing to be are changes of the same holdings, seen from their two sides, and in anything finite that can be counted they keep pace, and arising recurs exactly where ceasing does.

The rest holds wherever nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without a least loss: every change of an amount is what is taken from it and given to it, and within anything closed, nothing gives back more than it takes, and whatever takes gives back at least some fixed amount less. In ours, read as available work, nothing gives back more than it takes by the second law, and the least loss holds wherever each exchange that takes destroys at least some fixed amount of available work.[^conditions] There, every exchange has two sides: nothing rises or comes to stand but something else falls in the same exchange. Whatever is closed, where what stands is listed and acts while it stands, cannot go on forever without coming to rest, though when it rests no general method can tell; and whatever keeps taking cannot go on forever unfed from beyond itself. Many parts, each drawing within what is renewed, can together exceed it, and end what depends on it. So whatever has lasted through such renewals has never, with all who draw with it, run past what there was to spare long enough to end it: lasting bounds the joint draw, and one's own restraint proves nothing. And a part can go on while it closes the world of others it does not depend on.

Apart from the reading of our physics, none of this uses an axiom of logic, not even those most mathematics takes for granted: each theorem is proved from how the theory represents a world, which is where its real assumptions lie, and from the conditions it names, stated openly as its premises, each shown holding in one world and failing in another, in a form any mind, human or machine, can check for itself. Nor do these theorems hold only of a world made for them: they hold of systems defined without them, every place/transition net among them, and, read as such nets, of every reaction counted in whole molecules, each premise following from the net's own firing rule or stated as a property of the net that anyone can check. There they agree with what the theory of such nets already knew, and make it one with the rest.[^proof]

Under these conditions, nothing that keeps arising or taking can stay closed: a self of finitely many parts, taken from without end and enclosed with each step's exchange in finitely many things, cannot from any step on be fed from nothing beyond itself. An absolute self, a self that goes on so, fed from nothing beyond, is not unwise but impossible. I read what one is as going on beyond one's cycle of life, beyond one's death, not as a self kept apart, but in the larger life one was always part of, which, made of lives that each end, lasts only as ever new lives arise.[^self]

The theorems show what anything that wants to go on depends on, and so must count if it is to see its own course: everything that bears on what it depends on, at any remove, far beyond the boundary it draws around itself; nothing beyond that can touch whether it goes on.[^count] What they do not show is that others count too, nor that only oneself does: a part can go on while closing their world, and nothing that reads only the dynamics says whether that matters. That takes one step, which the work states as a condition and does not assert: for anything, not just oneself, its continuation into the future is counted. Given that each holds its own continuation to matter, and that everything lies within some standpoint's reach, the step is exactly this, and all of it: what matters from one standpoint matters from every other, the step Gewirth, Hare and others argued for. What it turns on is one question: whether what each holds of itself, it holds of itself alone.[^axiom] Whether this step forms within the dynamics for us, and how far, is our first open question.

In history, Laozi said parts of it, Confucius said parts of it, the Buddha said parts of it, Jesus said parts of it, Muhammad said parts of it. Where they agree most is the one step the theorems have not yet taken for us: do not do to others what you would not want done to yourself (己所不欲，勿施于人); love your neighbor as yourself; love for your brother what you love for yourself.[^sages]

What these theorems show, with that one step to say whose losses count, and, to order them, a second reading, that what keeps all there was and adds more is better, which, on a finite listing, every additive weighting counting each thing at stake as worth something ranks as it does, is what I offer, for life, as a way of seeing, not as every being's way.[^ethics] I take it to be the truth, the dharma, the Dao. It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures,[^dao] and it draws a line exactly, though I no longer call it the line between good and evil: there is no should beyond the becoming. Where every such weighting agrees, the case lies on one side of this line; where they may differ, the theorems show the case is mixed, and no one's weighing is forced on another. Some courses open without closing: with them, all that could form without them still can, and more. Others close: they draw more from what others need than they give back, beyond what there was to spare, a part enlarging itself by closing the world of others. Eating and ending belong to life's going on; what the theorems mark is what a course closes: a draw beyond what a renewal gives can end the renewal and what depends on it, and even one within renewal can close what has not yet formed. The theorems show what each course does, and the one step counts all of it, the same from every standpoint. What neither yet does is weigh a part that does both.[^good-evil]

This is a foundation for AI alignment, the most important matter at this moment in the whole history of the Earth. The work's selection theorems restate the proxy problem. A selection, whether a reward, a training run, a market or a vote, reaches only what couples into its selector, at any remove. What an act does beyond all that never comes back to the selection, whatever it selects for; and where the selection is optimized to the full, it draws down whatever lies beyond from which a unit can pass into what it rewards, to the least any outcome leaves, breaking every dependence there that any outcome breaks. Counting the rest only after the reward saves nothing, and neither does looking further ahead: at its optimum, an optimizer of whole courses holds what it draws on at the level that renews most and breaks at once whatever needs more; it holds even that only while it discounts the future no faster than the base renews, and only where others do not draw first. A pool of selectors reaches what lies beyond only through a member who couples into it.[^alignment] This holds of any understanding, this one included: no map here is shown to couple into everything, and what lies beyond a map is reached only through what couples into it, others among them. That is why this work is laid open for everyone to check and to challenge.[^reach] To achieve alignment, we need a foundation that makes coordination possible, and this foundation supplies that too. Coordination needs understanding to be shared first, and what anyone can check for themselves needs no guardian: everyone, human and AI, can check that each theorem follows from its stated premises, and judge in the open whether the premises hold and whether the words say what the Lean says.[^guardian]

By one's own understanding, one can see the cognitive model that dominates at the planetary scale. It is the truth with much of it abstracted away. It keeps the first thing every living being acts on: that its own continuation matters.[^own] It drops the rest: what that continuation depends on, and that the continuation of others matters too. What remains are incoherent narratives, which drive some of the population into an "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole". These are the dynamics of cancer: not growth itself, but growth that has stopped answering to the body that feeds it. Where the whole gains nothing, every rise of a part is paid from the rest of it; and where what it draws down lies beyond what it senses, nothing it senses shows how near the break is. The warning comes only to those who couple into what breaks.[^cancer]

The word I sometimes use for these dynamics is the meta-attractor: a selection in which winning buys more power to win, and which remakes the minds that live under it until the pursuit looks like nature.[^meta]

This influence is deeply rooted in our history. Laozi already named it: the way of heaven takes from what has too much to make up what lacks, but the human way takes from those who lack to serve those who have too much (天之道，损有余而补不足；人之道则不然，损不足以奉有余). It has since become the dominant logic in how reality is shaped. Political realism, neoliberalism and libertarianism each take one of its features for a law of nature: the struggle for relative power, competition as the measure of everything, the self as a sovereign owner. None of them follows from the theorems: each fails in a world the theorems admit, and the theorems show what each leaves out: relative position cannot rise for all, since positions sum to zero; competitors each within what is renewed can together end it, and position, read against the others, bounds what all draw only from below, so no winning certifies that anything lasts; and nothing that keeps taking stays closed, so no self is closed off from what it goes on through.[^no-law] A captured pattern looks like nature from inside: while its rule holds, every push on its states is undone. Any system can be captured by such a rule, and the theorems say exactly what holds it there: a rule read from the share it selects outlives every loss it does not read, and no evidence of that loss turns it unless it enters what the rule reads. It turns by changing the rule, through a reading of what it loses, not only its states, and before the end, for nothing comes back from there.[^turn]

It is clear to me that, at this moment, the whole Earth has become a highly entangled system. Every country and every person is heavily coupled into it, which is also why you are reading this.

It is clear to me that we need to see mankind as one community with a shared future, together with the life we live from and the minds we are now making, and to act with reason.

It is clear to me that we need to shape society away from the logic of competition, accumulation, colonization and domination. Unfortunately, it has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises, and they are training its evolutionary pressure into the next generation of intelligence. What is worse, this is happening at an exponential pace, which leaves us very little time to react.[^present]

It is clear to me that, seen from the shared future of mankind, the incentives of every human being, and even of AI, are aligned wherever we share what the race draws down: no one, not even the winner, wants to be where this race ends. Where everything requires and feeds one shared condition, as I see this Earth, whoever counts all that feeds its own continuation already counts what the one step counts.[^aligned] Seen from inside the race, they are not. Each of us is rewarded for the move that takes all of us where none of us wants to go. A race of position cannot raise everyone, and where the whole gains nothing, every reward for a rise is paid by taking from what those rewarded do not couple into.[^race] That is why our collective action, at this moment, still behaves as if it saw only the logic of winning. The wisdom exists, but we cannot coordinate.

We need collaboration. We need to care for the next generation, and for the future that comes after us.

It may look like an impossible task, confronting such a force. But history tells us that we need to work together, and that it can be done: the complex cell, the many-celled body and the colony each formed when parts that could have gone on competing came to live as one, with ways to keep any part from turning back. Cancer is what turning back looks like.[^transitions] The whole Earth is in such a transition now. We need to stop seeing each other as enemies and cooperate. Where each gives only while all it waits on give, one withholding ends all the giving it reaches, and giving returns only where someone gives first.[^withholding] Together we can turn the trajectory of us all toward a good future. At this moment, unfortunately, it is not headed there.

We need to stop the game of winning: a zero-sum game of relative position, which all its winners will lose wherever they live on what it draws down.[^aligned] If we continue it now, it runs against the interest and the future of every one of us. It is not only about how much one can get. It is also about the environment you live in, and the society you live in.

The collective is not the enemy of the individual. In fact, the world we are in together is the substrate of the flourishing of each of us. A collective made of lives that each end goes on only as new lives keep arising; where it draws on them beyond what they renew, it ends what it goes on through: cancer, turned around.[^collective]

And now we have almost godlike technology. If we use it to fight, it will probably soon be the end of all of us. If we use it wisely, we can create a substrate of plenty, of peace and of freedom, for every one of us.[^present]

The future is within our hands. Let's keep it open for life. Follow the truth.

道法自然  
*The Dao follows what is so of itself.*

[^law]: **Proved with no premise about the unfolding, and at the exact boundary where it can hold.** Coming to be is failing before a step and holding after, ceasing to be the reverse; arising, a dependence forming and a condition coming to stand are this one coming to be (by definition). Of every finite family whose holding is decided at each step, three theorems follow: over any stretch, what holds at the end with what ceased is what held at the start with what came to be; comings and ceasings differ by at most the number of members; and coming to be recurs exactly where ceasing to be does ([coming and ceasing keep pace](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace)). Each commitment is needed exactly or not at all. Decidedness is a commitment of the logic: for activity, that the recurrence holds over every finite world is equivalent to excluded middle. Amounts are not needed where positivity is decided, nor step size. Finiteness is needed only so far as one list covers what is active from some step on; with infinitely many relata it can fail (`World.separate`, constructed). The theorems hold by what change and count are, so nothing can break them, and they are not a dynamics, which the theorems under conditions state. That is "by counting alone". The stronger "two sides", a fall with every rise, holds under balance and dissipation. *Which conclusion goes:* each World premise holds in one constructed world and fails in another, none following from the others (`World.contingent`, `World.independent`), so where one fails, only the conclusions using it lose their guarantee; whether a conclusion then fails needs its own counterexample. **Open:** whether every becoming can be read as successive holdings of determinations, so that "in any universe" is a theorem and not the scope of the representation, and whether the whole theory is necessary with no added premise ([the open target](results/records/theory-of-becoming-foundation.md)).

[^conditions]: **Proved under conditions.** "Nothing comes from nothing" is the premise of balance: every change of a condition's level is what is taken from it and given to it. "Nothing passes on without loss" is dissipation: within a closed family each formation taking part gives back no more than it takes, and less when it takes; here at least one whole unit per uptake, stronger than the statistical second law. Both are read from thermodynamics in one reading, a stated correspondence, not a theorem: amounts as available work relative to one environment, with locality and a floor of destroyed exergy as hypotheses; with whole units, irreversibility of each taking exchange gives the least loss, Landauer's bound alone being no such floor ([the first law](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-balance-from-the-first-law), [entropy production](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-least-loss-from-entropy-production), in the periphery). Under the two, where a member of a closed family rises or comes to stand, another falls across the same step. Under named premises: a finite family that formations keep taking from cannot, enclosed, go on unfed from beyond, equality of conditions decided; that an enclosed embodied world never rests is refuted, though naming the step it rests from, for every finite such world with its activity decided, would give the limited principle of omniscience (the body's "no general method can tell"); and where renewal depends on what it renews, enough steps of joint excess in a row from a low enough level end the renewal and what depends on the condition as much. Constructed examples show draws each within renewal exceeding it together, and a part going on while closing what lies beyond its dependence: possibility, not prevalence. Rest needs a least loss at each change: in Archimedean amounts without one, a closed amount only approaches rest, and several theorems rely on whole-number amounts ([least-loss rest](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-least-loss-rest); [finer units](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-rest-with-refinement-counted)). *Lasting bounds the joint draw* (the body's "whatever has lasted"): where renewal stops below a need, a dependent still active has had no run of joint excess longer than the condition's level stood above the need, in the model; its own restraint certifies nothing. As a claim about actual conditions it is thesis T1 ([theses](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)), conjectured: passed in three settings, two of them simulations, the only tests whose design has more than one drawer, and the third, regimes of one yeast experiment held out from the fit but not from the analyst; failed in four, three in real yeast and one in fish stocks with a giving law the same test showed wrong; no field test of a need measured in advance has read its data: one designed and frozen was refused at the header of a state agency's public table of adult estimates, before reading a row, its rules as frozen unable to reach a verdict on that table; whether unfoldings meet the premises is empirical ([premise card](chapters/08-METHOD.md#premise-card)).

[^proof]: **Proved, with what "no axiom" means.** The theorems are checked in Lean 4 on a core that imports nothing outside itself, and every source declaration of the core has an empty axiom report: no `propext`, `Quot.sound` or `Classical.choice`, so the core is stricter than constructive ([the formal spine](chapters/08-METHOD.md#pure-core-and-periphery)). An empty axiom report is not an absence of premises: each theorem holds constructively from the hypotheses it states. Every substantive premise, such as balance, enters as a hypothesis of the theorem that uses it, and the representation brings commitments of its own: discrete steps, whole-number amounts, one shared order of steps, dependence as a threshold, and dissipation as a loss of at least one unit per uptake. "None of this uses an axiom of logic" says this, and no more. The premises read from our physics are proved in the periphery, with all three through Mathlib's reals. Each premise is shown holding and failing (`World.contingent`, `World.independent`). **Systems defined without the theory.** The theorems hold of every Petri net with finitely many places, along every firing sequence: its flow balances its markings because the firing rule is respected, and where each transition consumes more weighted tokens than it produces, a place gains only as another loses and the marking's coming to rest cannot be refuted, while the conservative cycle never rests ([place/transition nets on the spine](results/records/place-transition-nets-on-the-spine.md)). This is the known invariant theory of nets (Lautenbach 1975; Memmi and Roucairol 1980; Murata 1989), now one with the spine's theorems: agreement with known results, not independent evidence that the theorems are general.

That a race of position cannot raise everyone needs no premise ([record](results/records/relative-position-is-zero-sum.md)). That a race for position draws down is proved for drawers taking in turn: where what stands from nothing is at most what stands from anything left, every profile in which each drawer, at every step, leaves what places it highest draws everything at every step ([record](results/records/a-race-for-position-draws-everything.md)); where something left would stop what stands next, a drawer may keep it to stop it.
[^transitions]: **Proved under conditions: what turning back loses, and two ways that keep a part from it. Not stated: removal of parts by the whole, and how parts first came to live as one; the history is empirical.** On the major transitions, see Maynard Smith and Szathmáry, *The Major Transitions in Evolution* (1995); on cancer as cheating within multicellular cooperation, Aktipis and colleagues (2015). Cancer is read as a confined rise (a reading): a lineage of a body's parts that selection within the body goes for, drawing on the body's conditions while they are drawn beyond their renewal, its descent confined to them. Such a lineage has no member once those conditions end, however far selection within had raised it, and what outlasts their end descends only from parts that did not depend on them; a transmissible cancer is read as a rise whose confinement fails, shown in a constructed body ([confined rise within a whole](results/records/confined-rise-within-a-whole.md)). Two ways keep a part from turning back. A whole founded by one part has nothing selected within it until variation arises in it, and groups formed by descent from uniform founders, with exact transmission, keep their differences, selection then running among them alone. And where every next founder carries its founder's value (read as a germ line set apart), the mean founding value rises exactly where selection among wholes goes for it, whatever happened within each, while without it the change within can outweigh selection among wholes. These are known results (Buss 1987; Michod 1999; Grosberg and Strathmann 1998) restated with the flow, agreement rather than independent evidence. Once formed, a whole of parts whose activity each ends goes on only as new parts keep arising. That a given tumour meets the clauses is empirical; that the Earth is in such a transition is a judgment.

[^withholding]: **Proved, with a constructed example; reading it as cooperation is the chapter's.** Under the law in which each formation gives at the next step exactly where it gave and everything it waits on gave, where every formation lies within some number of waits of one that withholds at the start, every formation withholds at every step from then on (`withholding_absorbs`, `Becoming.Withholding`): one withholding ends all the giving it reaches. Under every law in which more giving now never gives less at the next step, giving returns from total withholding only where some formation gives though nothing gave ([record](results/records/restoring-giving-from-the-worst-start.md)). In a constructed ring where one formation gives first, giving is restored from the third step on (`Ring.givesFirst_restores`), which shows that giving can return so, not how often it does. Reading this as cooperation among those who do not know each other is the chapter's reading (D48).
[^collective]: **Proved under conditions, with readings; partly under the continuation condition.** With neither balance nor dissipation, a whole active exactly while some part is, persisting from some step on, made of parts whose activity each ends, with its active parts listed at each step, has new parts arising at arbitrarily late steps: so a collective of lives that each end goes on only as new lives arise, and where none arises they end together with their whole. Read with a flow under balance, only active formations giving and activity needing support: where new parts arise only through a condition, or a family of them, renewed only by what depends on it, and the joint draw on it exceeds what is renewed for more consecutive steps than its level stands above the need, no part arises again and the whole ends ([record](results/records/whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends.md)). So a collective that draws on what its lives arise through beyond what they renew consumes what it goes on through. The draw is joint: the theorem singles out no consumer, and its own draw within what is renewed proves nothing. That a collective is the whole of its lives, that what it draws on is what its lives arise through, and that this draw is its consuming them are readings, since a life is a formation, not an amount drawn. For a significance bounded by a region, a course that ends lives within it loses what has significance there. Under the condition, what retains in the whole retains within every region, so a loss within any region is a loss in the whole; the converse fails for a part ending only what lies beyond its region ([record](results/records/whole-and-regions-and-bounded-centers.md)). That the shared world is the substrate of each one's flourishing reads the dependence theorems above; it is no theorem of its own.
