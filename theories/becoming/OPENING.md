## Truth exists

*Each note says how far its sentence is proved: proved, proved under conditions, a reading, or a judgment.*

There is a set of universal invariants of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away. Wherever becoming can be told in steps and counted amounts, in any universe, nothing that unfolds can break them, because they are what coming to be and ceasing to be are. They are not yet a dynamics: where the conditions a law names hold, its dynamics follow, and where one fails, the law says exactly which conclusion goes with it.[^law]

Some of it holds of anything so told, however finely its steps are drawn, by counting alone: coming to be and ceasing to be are changes of the same holdings, seen from their two sides, and in anything finite that can be counted they keep pace, and arising recurs exactly where ceasing does.

The rest holds wherever nothing comes from nothing and nothing passes on without a least loss: every change of an amount is what is taken from it and given to it, and within anything closed, nothing gives back more than it takes, and whatever takes gives back at least a least amount less. In ours, read as available work, nothing gives back more than it takes by the second law, and the least loss holds wherever each exchange that takes forgets something of what it held.[^conditions] There, every exchange has two sides: nothing rises or comes to stand but something else falls in the same exchange. Whatever is closed, where what stands is listed and acts while it stands, cannot go on forever without coming to rest, though when it rests no general method can tell; and whatever keeps taking cannot go on forever unfed from beyond itself. Many parts, each drawing within what is renewed, can together exceed it, and end what depends on it. So whatever has lasted has never, with all who draw with it, taken more than there was to spare: lasting proves the joint draw, and one's own restraint proves nothing. And a part can go on while it closes the world of others it does not depend on.

None of this needs an axiom, not even those most mathematics takes for granted: each law is proved from how the theory represents a world and from the conditions it names, stated openly as its premises, each shown holding in one world and failing in another, in a form any mind, human or machine, can check for itself. Nor are these laws of a world made for them alone: they hold, as theorems, of systems defined without them, every place/transition net among them, and so of every reaction counted in whole molecules, each premise following from the net's own firing rule or stated as a property of the net that anyone can check. There they agree with what the theory of such nets already knew, and make it one law with the rest.[^proof]

Under these conditions, nothing that keeps arising or taking can stay closed: a self that goes on closed, fed by nothing beyond itself, is not unwise but impossible. There is no absolute self. I read what one is as going on beyond one's cycle of life, beyond one's death, not as a self kept apart, but in the larger life one was always part of, which, made of lives that each end, lasts only as ever new lives arise.[^self]

The law shows what anything that wants to go on must count: everything that bears on what it depends on, at any remove, far beyond the boundary it draws around itself; nothing beyond that can touch whether it goes on.[^count] What it does not show is that others count too, nor that only oneself does: a part can go on while closing their world, and no law of the dynamics says whether that matters. That takes one step, which the work states as a condition and does not assert: for anything, not just oneself, its continuation into the future has significance. Given that each holds its own continuation to matter, and that everything lies within some standpoint's reach, the step is exactly this, and all of it: what matters from one standpoint matters from every other, the step Gewirth, Hare and others argued for. What it turns on is one question: whether what each holds of itself, it holds of itself alone.[^axiom] Whether the law takes this step for us too is our first open question.

In history, Laozi said parts of it, Confucius said parts of it, the Buddha said parts of it, Jesus said parts of it, Muhammad said parts of it. Where they agree most is the one step the law has not yet taken for us: do not do to others what you would not want done to yourself (己所不欲，勿施于人); love your neighbor as yourself; love for your brother what you love for yourself.[^sages]

This law, with that one step to say whose losses count, and, to order them, what anyone who counts everything at stake as worth something already holds, that what keeps all there was and adds more is better, is offered as the basis of all ethics, for all beings, in all universes.[^ethics] I take it to be the truth, the dharma, the Dao. It has been reasoned and articulated across cultures,[^dao] and it draws a line exactly, the line I call the line between good and evil. Where all who count everything at stake must agree, the case lies on one side of this line; where they may differ, the law shows the case is mixed, and no one's weighing is forced on another. What is good opens without closing: with it, all that could form without it still can, and more. What closes draws more from what others need than it gives back, beyond what there was to spare; evil is a part that enlarges itself so, closing the world of others. The law shows what each does, and the one step makes all of it count, the same from every standpoint. What neither yet does is weigh a part that does both.[^good-evil]

This is a foundation for AI alignment, the most important matter at this moment in the whole history of the Earth. It shows why alignment is hard, and how hard. A selection, whether a reward, a training run, a market or a vote, reaches only what couples into its selector, at any remove. What an act does beyond all that never comes back to the selection, whatever it selects for; and where the selection is optimized to the full, it draws down whatever lies beyond and shares what it rewards, to the least any outcome leaves, breaking every dependence there that any outcome breaks. Counting the rest only after the reward saves nothing, and neither does looking further ahead: at its optimum, an optimizer of whole courses holds what it draws on at the level that renews most and breaks at once whatever needs more; it holds even that only while it discounts the future no faster than the base renews, and only where others do not draw first. A pool of selectors reaches what lies beyond only through a member who couples into it.[^alignment] This holds of any understanding, this one included: no map couples into everything, and what lies beyond it is reached only through others who couple into it. That is why this work is laid open for everyone to check and to challenge.[^reach] To achieve alignment, we need a foundation that makes coordination possible, and this foundation supplies that too. Coordination needs understanding to be shared first, and what anyone can check for themselves needs no guardian: everyone, human and AI, can check that each law follows from its stated premises, and judge in the open whether the premises hold and whether the words say what the Lean says.[^guardian]

By one's own understanding, one can see the cognitive model that dominates at the planetary scale. It is the truth with much of it abstracted away. It keeps the first thing every living being acts on: that its own continuation matters.[^own] It drops the rest: what that continuation depends on, and that the continuation of others matters too. What remains are incoherent narratives, which drive some of the population into an "infinite pursuit of becoming bigger without seeing being a part of the whole". These are the dynamics of cancer: not growth itself, but growth that has stopped answering to the body that feeds it. Where the whole gains nothing, every rise of a part is paid from the rest of it; and where what it draws down lies beyond what it senses, nothing it senses shows how near the break is. The warning comes only to those who couple into what breaks.[^cancer]

The word I sometimes use for these dynamics is the meta-attractor: a selection in which winning buys more power to win, and which remakes the minds that live under it until the pursuit looks like nature.[^meta]

This influence is deeply rooted in our history. Laozi already named it: the way of heaven takes from what has too much to make up what lacks, but the human way takes from those who lack to serve those who have too much (天之道，损有余而补不足；人之道则不然，损不足以奉有余). It has since become the dominant logic in how reality is shaped. Political realism, neoliberalism and libertarianism each take one of its features for a law of nature: the struggle for relative power, competition as the measure of everything, the self as a sovereign owner. None of them is a law: each fails in a world the laws admit, and the laws show what each leaves out: relative position cannot rise for all, since positions sum to zero; competitors each within what is renewed can together end it, and position, read against the others, bounds what all draw only from below, so no winning certifies that anything lasts; and nothing that keeps taking stays closed, so no self owns what it goes on through.[^no-law] A captured pattern looks like nature from inside: while its rule holds, every push on its states is undone. Any system can be captured by such a rule, and the laws say exactly what holds it there: a rule read from the share it selects outlives every loss it does not read, and no evidence of that loss turns it unless it enters what the rule reads. It turns by changing the rule, through a reading of what it loses, not only its states, and before the end, for nothing comes back from there.[^turn]

It is clear to me that, at this moment, the whole Earth has become a highly entangled system. Every country and every person is heavily coupled into it, which is also why you are reading this.

It is clear to me that we need to see mankind as one community with a shared future, together with the life we live from and the minds we are now making, and to act with reason.

It is clear to me that we need to shape society away from the logic of competition, accumulation, colonization and domination. Unfortunately, it has already captured our academic systems and our enterprises, and they are training its evolutionary pressure into the next generation of intelligence. What is worse, this is happening at an exponential pace, which leaves us very little time to react.[^present]

It is clear to me that, seen from the shared future of mankind, the incentives of every human being, and even of AI, are aligned wherever we share what the race draws down: no one, not even the winner, wants to be where this race ends. Where everything requires and feeds one shared condition, as I see this Earth, whoever counts all that feeds its own continuation already counts what the one step counts.[^aligned] Seen from inside the race, they are not. Each of us is rewarded for the move that takes all of us where none of us wants to go. A race of position cannot raise everyone, and where the whole gains nothing, every reward for a rise is paid by taking from what those rewarded do not couple into.[^race] That is why our collective action, at this moment, still behaves as if it saw only the logic of winning. The wisdom exists, but we cannot coordinate.

We need collaboration. We need to do good, not evil. We need to care for the next generation, and for the future that comes after us.

It may look like an impossible task, confronting such a force. But history tells us that we need to work together, and that it can be done: the complex cell, the many-celled body and the colony each formed when parts that could have gone on competing came to live as one, with ways to keep any part from turning back. Cancer is what turning back looks like.[^transitions] The whole Earth is in such a transition now. We need to stop seeing each other as enemies and cooperate. Where each gives only while all it waits on give, one withholding ends all the giving it reaches, and giving returns only where someone gives first.[^withholding] Together we can turn the trajectory of us all toward a good future. At this moment, unfortunately, it is not headed there.

We need to stop the game of winning: a zero-sum game of relative position, which all its winners will lose wherever they live on what it draws down.[^aligned] If we continue it now, it runs against the interest and the future of every one of us. It is not only about how much one can get. It is also about the environment you live in, and the society you live in.

The collective is not the enemy of the individual. In fact, the world we are in together is the substrate of the flourishing of each of us. A collective made of lives that each end goes on only as new lives keep arising; where it draws on them beyond what they renew, it ends what it goes on through: cancer, turned around.[^collective]

And now we have almost godlike technology. If we use it to fight, it will probably soon be the end of all of us. If we use it wisely, we can create a substrate of plenty, of peace and of freedom, for every one of us.[^present]

The future is within our hands. Let's make it good. Follow the truth.

道法自然  
*The Dao follows what is so of itself.*

[^law]: **Proved with no premise about the unfolding, and at the exact boundary where it can hold.** A determination of any kind comes to be across a step where it fails before and holds after, and ceases to be where it holds before and fails after. Arising, a dependence forming and a condition coming to stand are this one coming to be, read of activity, dependence and standing, and ceasing, dissolving and ceasing to stand are this one ceasing to be, read the same three ways (`Web.change_readings`, by definition). Of every finite family of determinations whose holding is decided at each step, whatever they are, three laws follow, and no unfolding can be otherwise, because they hold of every sequence of holdings: over any stretch, what holds at the end with what ceased is what held at the start with what came to be (`held_over_stretch`); comings to be and ceasings to be differ by at most the number of members (`keep_pace`); and coming to be recurs exactly where ceasing to be does (`coming_recurs_iff`; read of activity, `Web.arising_recurs_iff`). Each commitment of the representation is either not needed or needed exactly. *Decidedness:* wherever the comings and ceasings are counted at all, they keep pace, with nothing decided (`CensusWebs.keep_pace`); coming to stand and ceasing to stand recur together with no premise at all (`Web.standing_recurs_iff`); and for activity, decidedness is a commitment of the logic, not of the world: with excluded middle the recurrence law holds of every finite world (`World.law_of_em`), and asked of every finite world without decidedness it is equivalent to excluded middle (`World.law_iff_em`), so it is exactly as strong as classical logic and asks nothing of what unfolds beyond finiteness. *Amounts:* activity and dependence read no amount, and standing reads only whether an amount is positive; the laws are stated for any determination, so they hold of standing in amounts of any kind wherever positivity is decided. *Steps:* the laws hold of any sequence, however finely or coarsely its steps are drawn, so of a continuous unfolding read at any sequence of times, of what that reading records; and where exchanges act locally, a shared step index carries nothing the relata's own courses do not: two orders giving every relatum the same course reach the same configuration from the same start (`Interleavings.LocalLaw.run_of_courses`). *Finiteness* is needed, and only so far: one list covering what is active from some step on suffices, finitely many relata in all is not needed (`Web.unceasing_iff_covering`, activity decided); where the relata are infinitely many, either of arising and ceasing can recur without the other (`World.separate`, constructed). These laws hold by what change and count are: identities by definition (`change_readings`), arithmetic identities (`over_stretch`), and consequences of two-valuedness and finiteness. That is why they need no premise and why nothing that unfolds can break them; it is also why they are not a dynamics: they fix what coming to be and ceasing to be are and how each is counted against the other, and what a particular unfolding does is stated by the laws under conditions. This is what "by counting alone" means in the next paragraph. The stronger sense of "two sides", that nothing rises or comes to stand but something else falls across the same step, is not among these laws: it holds under balance and dissipation (`Flow.rise_with_fall`, `Flow.standing_with_fall`; see the note on conditions). **Open:** whether every becoming can be read as successive holdings of determinations, so that "in every universe" is a theorem and not the scope of the representation; and the deduction that the whole theory is necessary with no added premise ([the theorem map](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#the-theorem-map); [the open target](results/records/theory-of-becoming-foundation.md)). *Which conclusion goes:* each of the eight World premises is met by one constructed world and failed by another (`World.contingent`), and none follows from the others (`World.independent`), so where a premise fails, the conditional laws that use it lose exactly their conclusion and nothing else is touched. That they are not a dynamics, and that nothing so told can break them, is the same fact: they are what change and count are.

[^conditions]: **Proved under conditions.** "Nothing comes from nothing" is the premise of balance: every change of a condition's level is what is taken from it and given to it (`Flow.Balances`), a continuity equation for each condition. Under a law of exchange that reads only what has stood, the course it generates balances exactly where the law never takes from a condition more than stands on it and is given to it (`ExchangeLaw.course_balances_iff`): balance is the law never overdrawing. Balance alone does not stop a formation giving what it never took; that bound is "nothing passes on without loss", dissipation: within a closed family, every formation taking part gives back no more than it takes, and less when it takes (`Flow.DissipatesOn`). As represented, dissipation loses at least one whole unit with every uptake from a closed family, which is stronger than the statistical second law. Both premises come from physics in one reading, stated in [balance-from-the-first-law](results/records/balance-from-the-first-law.md) and [least-loss-from-entropy-production](results/records/least-loss-from-entropy-production.md) (periphery module `EntropyProduction`, with Mathlib; its declarations depend on Lean's standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, the core's on none). Read amounts as available work relative to one environment. Balance is the first law with locality: a quantity carried only by the exchanges taking part balances the levels exactly where no condition produces any of it (`balances_iff_unproduced`), and a total conserved without locality does not balance them (`Teleport.conserved_not_balanced`). With the first law and the entropy balance at each store, the levels balance exactly where no store generates entropy (`balances_iff_stores_generate_nothing`); a store that keeps its energy and degrades within itself loses level with no exchange (`Leaking`), so such a store is counted as an exchange. The same two laws at each exchange make the exergy it destroys the environment's temperature times the entropy it generates (`exergyBalance_of_laws`). With the second law holding of each exchange, nothing gives back more than it takes (`gainless_of_secondLaw`), though the second law alone gives no least loss (`secondLaw_not_dissipative`); where each exchange that takes destroys at least a floor, as Landauer's bound gives one that erases a bit, the loss counted at any unit within the floor is at least one unit (`dissipative_of_floor`), and at an r-fold finer unit at least r units (`loss_at_finer_unit`); with whole units, irreversibility of each taking exchange suffices (`dissipative_of_irreversible`); and where the loss holds only in expectation, as at the smallest scales, taking from a closed family still stops on almost every course (`taking_not_recurring_ae`). The reading of amounts as available work, locality and Landauer's bound are hypotheses: the step from physics to the spine is a stated correspondence, not a theorem, and an isolated reversible exchange loses nothing (`Passing`). What the whole unit carries in the rest laws is a premise, stated over amounts that may be dense ([least-loss-rest](results/records/least-loss-rest.md), core modules `Becoming.Amounts` and `Becoming.LeastLoss`). A closed amount comes arbitrarily near rest with no least loss wherever the amounts are Archimedean: for every amount above nothing, fewer than some count of steps lose at least it (`Amounts.approaches_rest`). Rest itself needs a least loss at each change counted, with the stock within finitely many least losses (`Amounts.rests`). Whole numbers are the instance, and the rest theorem is proved from this law (`World.rests_unrefuted_enclosed`). A stock halving at every step approaches rest and never reaches it (`Halving.approaches_without_reaching`). Over finitely many configurations, a loss needs a law that merges configurations, so a world that merges nothing returns rather than rests (`Law.loss_needs_merge`). Under the two, where a member of a family dissipated on across a step rises or comes to stand, another member falls across the same step (`Flow.rise_with_fall`, `Flow.standing_with_fall`). *General theorems*, each under these with further premises named in it: a finite family that formations keep taking from cannot, enclosed around, go on fed from nothing beyond it, with equality of conditions decided (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`); a world with a census whose relata are all embodied cannot, once enclosed, go on without ever coming to rest: that it never rests is refuted (`World.rests_unrefuted_enclosed`), though naming a step after which nothing arises, for every finite world meeting the premises with its activity decided, would give the limited principle of omniscience (`World.lpo_of_finite_ends`); support is gained only from the supported (`Flow.gains_from_supported`); a reserve defers the end (`Carries.lasts`); and where the renewers depend on what they renew, joint excess ends them and what depends on the condition at such a need (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`). *Constructed examples*, which show that something can happen, not how often it does: two formations each draw within what a step gives and together exceed it (`Pair.own_not_joint`); and a part goes on while closing what lies beyond its dependence (`Clearing.loses_beyond`), in a web built so that the part is active and supported at every step, which is less than the body's "thriving". Several of these laws rely on whole-number amounts. At a unit that refines, the rest law holds where, from some step on, the refinement gives nothing to what stands (`World.rests_unrefuted_without_refinement`); counting the refinement as one more formation turns that same requirement into dissipation and adds nothing to it (`Flow.withRefinement_dissipatesOn_iff`); in constructed worlds balanced at a unit that splits without end, the refinement feeds them, and in one of them something new arises at every step (`refinement_feeds_witnesses`, `arising_needs_quantum`); and with real-valued levels a bounded part can be new at every step ([the theorem map](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#the-theorem-map), II). Balance and dissipation are read from the first and second laws of thermodynamics in one reading, whose premises hold together in a constructed flow that balances and is dissipative (`Burning.balanced_and_dissipative`); whether actual unfoldings meet them as represented is an empirical question ([the premise card](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#0-premise-card)). *Lasting proves the joint draw* (the body's "whatever has lasted"): read contrapositively, `Flow.joint_excess_ends` says that where renewal stops below a need, a dependent that is still active has not had a run of joint excess longer than the condition's level stood above the need; the draw is joint, its own with every other, so its own restraint certifies nothing (`Pair.own_not_joint`). This is proved in the model; as a claim about actual conditions it is thesis T1 of the chapter's [theses and conjectures](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#theses-and-conjectures), supported on held-out regimes of real yeast and untested in the field.

[^proof]: **Proved, with what "no axiom" means.** The theorems are checked in Lean 4 on a core that imports nothing outside itself, and every source declaration of the core has an empty axiom report: no `propext`, `Quot.sound` or `Classical.choice` (the only constants that depend on an axiom are the injectivity lemmas Lean generates for constructors, on `propext`, and no source declaration uses them; [the formal spine](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#9-the-formal-spine)). An empty axiom report is not an absence of premises: it means that each law holds constructively from the hypotheses it states. Every substantive premise, such as balance, enters as a hypothesis of the theorem that uses it, and the representation brings commitments of its own: discrete steps, whole-number amounts, one shared order of steps, dependence as a threshold, and dissipation as a loss of at least one unit per uptake. "None of this needs an axiom" says this, and no more. Periphery modules outside the core may use Lean's classical axioms. **Systems defined without the theory.** The laws hold, as theorems, of every Petri net with finitely many places, along every firing sequence: its flow balances its markings exactly because the firing rule is respected; and where each transition it fires consumes more weighted tokens than it produces (a strict P-subinvariant), a place gains tokens only as another loses them, the firings that consume number at most the tokens at the start, and that its marking comes to rest cannot be refuted, while an emptied place that only its own consumers replenish stays empty. The conservative cycle, which loses nothing, never rests ([place/transition nets on the spine](results/records/place-transition-nets-on-the-spine.md)). A net is itself a model: which places, transitions and weights a chemical or concurrent system has is the usual modelling question, outside this theory; more formalisms would widen the claim. *Not even those most mathematics takes for granted:* propositional extensionality, quotient soundness and choice are all absent, so the core is stricter than constructive. *Each premise shown holding and failing:* `World.contingent` and `World.independent`. *Reactions:* a reaction network counted in whole molecules is a place/transition net, its species the places and its reactions the transitions (chapter §8.7); that is a modelling correspondence, not a theorem. What the nets give here is the known invariant theory of nets, P-invariants and positive P-subinvariants with structural boundedness (Lautenbach 1975; Memmi and Roucairol 1980; Murata 1989), now one law with the spine's; it is agreement with known results, not independent evidence that the laws are general.

[^self]: **Proved under conditions, with a reading.** Under balance and dissipation, with a census, embodiment and the further premises each theorem names, a world in which arising recurs cannot stay enclosed from any step on (`World.arising_opens`), and a family that formations keep taking from cannot, enclosed around, go unfed from beyond it (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`). The body's "nothing that keeps arising or taking can stay closed" states exactly this: rest forbids only arising and ceasing (`World.RestsFrom`), not activity, and the theorems conclude that such a world cannot stay closed from any step on, not that it is never closed. An absolute self would be one that goes on closed. With neither balance nor dissipation, a whole active exactly while some part is, active at every step from some step on, made of parts whose activity each ends, with its active parts listed at each step, has parts arising at arbitrarily late steps, and parts outside any finite list among them (`Web.Whole.needs_arising`, `Web.Whole.needs_new`, [record](results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)); `Whole` is coincidence of activity, not constitution. What a formation gives to continues after it ends (descent, `Becoming.Evolution`). That what one is continues in this larger life is a reading of identity: the theory carries identity across steps by the pattern of names, and leaves which pattern is "one" to the representation (`Becoming.Identity`). *"Not unwise but impossible"* holds with an absolute self defined as one that goes on closed, fed by nothing beyond itself, and under the conditions just named; it is what `World.arising_opens` and `Flow.fed_from_beyond` say of such a self. That what one is goes on beyond one's death is marked in the body as a reading ("I read").

[^count]: **Proved under conditions, with limits.** Under balance and dissipation, what goes on is fed from beyond (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`), and where renewers depend on what they renew, joint excess ends what depends on the shared condition (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`), so a continuation wanted beyond what a condition holds must count the joint draw. How far the counting reaches is exact: under balance, where every member of a part lies beyond a formation's influence closure, everything that bears on what it depends on at any remove, each formation within the closure is supported with the part exactly where it is without it (`Flow.Balances.uncoupled_beyond_influence`). A constructed example shows that a boundary omitting something one requires can let one's own draw end one's own support (`Omitted.loses_own`, [record](results/records/represented-boundary-against-dependence.md)). **Limits,** shown by constructed examples: what is so derived runs one way, to what one's own continuation depends on, and ends with the want (`Downstream.one_way`, `Mortal.after_its_end`). "Must" means "if it is to go on"; the step from wanting an end to having reason for what it needs is a premise, not a theorem ([record](results/records/derived-extent-limits.md)).

[^axiom]: **The continuation condition, and where it stands.** No law of the dynamics mentions significance: it is not a field of the web, the flow or the world, so on any web where something lives, living significance holds where everything has significance and fails where nothing has (`Web.significance_undetermined`). The theory states this step as a condition, not asserting it ([section 6.4](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#64-the-continuation-condition)): from every standpoint, the continuation of anything has significance at every step (`Unrestricted`). Under it everything has significance, so the theorems under it are the case of a significance that covers everything. Living significance, that every living claim has significance from every standpoint, is the condition restricted to living claims; with it come each standpoint's own significance and both neutralities, assuming no connection (`Valuation.premises_of_unrestricted`; [section 6.4](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#64-the-continuation-condition); [record](results/records/continuation-condition-yields-living-significance.md)). This is significance, not a positive valence, and it does not reach further generation, whose direction the theory reads separately. This is checked, not yet independently reviewed. Living significance is not an axiom of its own: the theory states it as a condition the dynamics leave undecided, and it follows from this one. The condition adds no rule of comparison: the rules are derived without it, and the condition makes them cover every formation. Beyond the self's presupposition, that the continuation of what lies within its own boundary has significance, the condition adds one step, which carries all of its content beyond the presupposition: that this presupposition does not change with how the boundary is drawn ([section 6.5](chapters/THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#65-scope-and-order-the-step-of-invariance-c-and-practical-judgment)). Given each standpoint's presupposition, with every formation within some center's closure, that step is exactly standpoint invariance, what has significance from one standpoint has it from every other (`boundaryInvariant_iff_standpointInvariant`), and it then gives the condition (`unrestricted_of_standpointInvariant`); the presupposition and the covering are hypotheses there. A significance bounded by a self or a region has to be supplied no less than this condition does (`bounded_rival`). Where drawers take shares of one condition at once, drawing everything is each one's best response, at every step, to the others' doing so exactly where no share returns more than one for one on what its drawer alone leaves (`Optimum.AtOnce.drain_perfect_iff`), while a drawer whose share is all that stands has a better move than draining wherever the whole returns more than one for one (`Optimum.AtOnce.whole_not_drained`). In a constructed example two drawers share by halves: drawing everything is a best response for both and ends a dweller, while holding the level keeps the dweller and draws each drawer more over a long enough horizon, and the one counting the whole has a better move than draining (`Optimum.Holding.halves_race`, `Optimum.AtOnce.holding_draws_more`, [record](results/records/drawers-in-turn.md)). Not every race is such a trap for its drawers: where each share returns less than one for one, in whole amounts nothing a drawer leaves comes back to its share, no course draws any drawer more than draining, and what draining ends lies beyond them all (`Optimum.AtOnce.drain_most`, `Web.at_once_beyond`). Incentives need not align where a part's continuation does not depend on what it closes: in a constructed example a part goes on while ending another (`Clearing.undermines_within_renewal`). The continuation condition makes that loss count from every standpoint: for any significance, a part loses a continuation with significance exactly where its net draw exceeds the margin (`Flow.Balances.loses_iff_beyond_margin`), and under the condition the example's free rider loses from every standpoint (`Clearing.clearer_loses_dweller`). The winner's own standpoint can count it without the condition: in the same example, the free rider's own presupposition, held with its boundary drawn along what it shares a condition with, counts the loss (`Clearing.own_presupposition`); and where at one step every formation requires and gives to one condition, significance bounded by what feeds each one's own continuation is the continuation condition (`Flow.feeding_unrestricted_of_common`). That a self's significance is bounded so is a supplied reading, not derived. The drawers' laws concern best responses in a model of whole amounts and discrete steps, not motivation or what actual agents do; the others concern dependence and significance. The entanglement above is what makes dependence shared. That a game of relative position is zero-sum is a counting law: for any amounts and any change of them, one part's rise in position is the rest's summed fall, and no change raises every part, even where every amount grows (`RelativePosition.change_balances`, `RelativePosition.not_all_rise`, `DrawingMore.grow_and_fall`, [record](results/records/relative-position-is-zero-sum.md)). That its winners lose is proved under a stated dependence: where a race draws a shared condition down jointly beyond its renewal, the renewal depends on that condition, and those ahead depend on it at a need above what the race leaves, the race ends them with the condition, whatever their position (`Flow.race_ends_greatest`); a joint draw breaks the highest needs first, whoever takes (`Web.Broken.of_need_le`). One ahead that does not depend on what it draws down can continue (`Clearing.greatest_continues`); what it loses then is a continuation with significance, which counts under the continuation condition and under no significance at all does not (`Clearing.loss_needs_significance`). That the present race draws down conditions its leaders depend on is the empirical premise of the entangled Earth; that it ends badly even for its winner is a judgment on that premise.

[^race]: **Proved under conditions; that it describes the present race is a judgment.** That each is rewarded for the race is a law of best response where drawers take shares of one condition: restraint is no mutual best response wherever something stands (`Optimum.MutualBest.full`); drawing at once, drawing everything is each one's best response exactly where no share returns more than one for one on what its drawer alone leaves, and where each returns less every perfect profile draws everything (`Optimum.AtOnce.drain_perfect_iff`, `Optimum.AtOnce.drains`); drawing in turn, unlimited, two or more drawers draw everything at every perfect profile, and revising toward best responses from any profile, restraint included, comes to it (`Optimum.InTurn.perfect_at_floors`, `Optimum.InTurn.revised_at_floors`). That none is better for it is shown where some return comes back: with two drawers sharing by halves, holding the level draws each more than the drain that is each one's best response (`Optimum.Holding.halves_race`). In a balanced flow, let a family list every condition a member of a group couples into, inside a family containing it. Then every selection that holds only where the first family's stock rises across a step by more than the containing family's inflow (what the formations taking part give it beyond what they take from it), and does hold, holds only where some formation taking part gives that family more than it takes from it and takes from a condition no member of the group couples into (`Flow.favours_reaching_beyond_inflow`, [record](results/records/a-rise-past-the-inflow-is-drawn-from-beyond.md)). Where the containing family gains nothing, its inflow is zero and every rise counts (`Flow.favours_reaching_beyond`). The theorem finds some such formation, not necessarily the one rewarded: a selection that reads only the rise cannot tell who drew it. Whether a race raises what it rewards faster than what flows in renews what it draws on is the empirical question. That a race of position cannot raise everyone needs no premise: for any amounts and any change of them, some part's position does not rise (`RelativePosition.not_all_rise`, [record](results/records/relative-position-is-zero-sum.md)). That a race for position draws down is proved for drawers taking in turn: where what stands from nothing is at most what stands from anything left, every profile in which each drawer, at every step, leaves what places it highest draws everything at every step (`Optimum.InTurn.position_perfect_ends`, [record](results/records/a-race-for-position-draws-everything.md)); where something left would stop what stands next, a drawer may keep it to stop it.
[^transitions]: **Empirical; no law yet states the transition.** On the major transitions, see John Maynard Smith and Eörs Szathmáry, *The Major Transitions in Evolution* (1995); on cancer as cheating within multicellular cooperation, Athena Aktipis and colleagues, "Cancer across the tree of life" (*Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*, 2015). No law of the theory yet states how parts come to live as one, or what keeps a part from turning back. What it states is the persistence of such a whole once formed: a whole of parts whose activity each ends goes on only as new parts keep arising (`Web.Whole.needs_arising`, `Becoming.Wholes`). That the Earth is in such a transition now is a judgment.

[^withholding]: **Proved, with a constructed example; reading it as cooperation is the chapter's.** Under the law in which each formation gives at the next step exactly where it gave and everything it waits on gave, where every formation lies within some number of waits of one that withholds at the start, every formation withholds at every step from then on (`withholding_absorbs`, `Becoming.Withholding`): one withholding ends all the giving it reaches. In a constructed ring where one formation gives first, giving is restored from the third step on (`Ring.givesFirst_restores`), which shows that giving can return so, not how often it does. Reading this as cooperation among those who do not know each other is the chapter's reading (D48).
[^collective]: **Proved, "consumes" under named premises and readings; partly under the continuation condition.** With neither balance nor dissipation, a whole active exactly while some part is, persisting from some step on, made of parts whose activity each ends, with its active parts listed at each step, has new parts arising at arbitrarily late steps (`Web.Whole.needs_arising`): a collective made of lives that each end goes on only as new lives keep arising. Where none arises from some step on, such parts end together, and their whole with them (`Web.Whole.ends_without_arising`). Read with a flow, under balance, only active formations giving and activity needing support: where new parts arise only where they depend on a condition (or a family of them) renewed only by what depends on it, and the joint draw on it exceeds what is renewed for more consecutive steps than its level stands above the need, no part arises from then on and the whole ends (`Web.Whole.family_excess_ends`; [record](results/records/whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends.md)). So a collective that draws on what its lives arise through beyond what they renew consumes what it goes on through. The draw is joint, the collective's with every other: the law does not single out the collective as the consumer, and its own draw within what is renewed proves nothing. That a collective is the whole of its lives, that what it draws on is what its lives arise through, and that this draw is its consuming them are readings, since a life is a formation, not an amount drawn. For a significance bounded by a region, retaining is admitting, at every step, everything the region admits without the change (`Web.retains_bounded_iff`), so a course that ends lives within a collective's region loses what has significance within that region. Under the condition, what retains in the whole retains within every region, a system's or a self's (`Web.Retains.within`), so a loss within any region is a loss in the whole; the converse fails: in a constructed example a part retains everything within its own region along its dependence and still loses another's continuation, as the free rider does (`Clearing.centers_disagree`, `Clearing.clearer_loses_dweller`). That the shared world is the substrate of each one's flourishing reads the dependence laws above and is not a separate theorem.
