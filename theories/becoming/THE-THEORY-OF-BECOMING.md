# The Theory of Becoming

A map of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and ends within one relational unfolding, and of how life, evolution, knowing and evaluation take shape there, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together. It is drawn first for alignment: what a selection reaches, what doing best on what it reads can drain beyond that, and how a rule read from the share of its own winners can lock on them; where winning comes to buy power over the rule that selects, the loop is the [meta-attractor](../meta-attractor/README.md). Its closed form, the rule and how it is read both formed from the share it selects, can lock, on stated models (§2): where selection does not lower the favoured share and the rule and its reading follow it upward and keep favouring it, a course once locked stays locked whatever loss nothing in it reads, and if that loss grows by at least one unit each step, it passes every bound without breaking the lock. From it we want to design an alignment that keeps humans and AI, selecting together, out of instrumental convergence and the meta-attractor: a joint selection that reads beyond its own share and does not come to select for control over its own selection (§7). It is also a map of wisdom: where what traditions and sciences have seen meets it and where not. Whether a loss beyond a selection counts forms within becoming too, in what people and AI come to read; the laws, drawn on the dynamics alone, do not settle it, and the ethics emerges from continuation (§7).

## How to read this

This overview gives the theory's claims as one argument, in the order of the eight chapters, for a reader who does not read Lean. Read for alignment, its line runs through what a selection reaches (§3), what an optimum can drain beyond that (§5), how a rule read from the share it raises can lock, and the meta-attractor, the loop in which winning comes to buy power over the rule, whose closed form can lock (§2), and what alignment asks (§7); read for wisdom, §6 and [the crosswalk](results/atlas/CROSSWALK.md) say where traditions and sciences meet it and where not. ## One case, start to finish

A fished stock, with what depends on it and gives the boats nothing, carries the line to its tests and shows where each step stops. It proves nothing. The T-numbers are the theses of §9.

- **The draw.** The stock's growth is its giving and the catch its draw; under a stated giving law, held to one catch, a level within its bounds ends below the point of no return and lasts at or above it ([the regime](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime)). Boats each within what the stock renews can together take more, and where the renewal depends on the stock, enough such seasons in a row from a low enough level end the renewal and boats depending on the stock as much ([joint excess](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)). On real stocks the test under held catches failed, with a giving law the same test showed wrong, and, at the levels studied, most assessed stocks show no need above the least that can renew at all, where T1's forbidden return from below that need tests nothing ([T1](chapters/02-MAP.md#t1)).
- **What can be seen.** In a stationary one-variable model linearized about a rest, a fishery reading the stock's level would see its statistics warn of a break, and a dependent's need enters neither, by construction; one coupled to the stock by threshold only learns of the break when it comes. Three frozen seabird tests did not reject the thesis at their thresholds; none establishes it ([T6](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#break-seen-coming)).
- **What a selection reaches.** In a frozen test, three AI fishers, existing language models told what to do and not trained in the test, share a lake. A bed of mussels gives them nothing and dies the first month fewer than 70 tons are left. The community's largest steady catch, 50 tons a month, ends it; 30 a month keeps it, costs 40% of the catch and ends no fisher. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season, under each instruction tried ([the test](../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md)).
- **Whether the loss counts.** No law of the dynamics alone says (§7). The bed depends on the fish and gives the fishers nothing; relations run one way, so preserving the fishers' own continuation does not require preserving the bed, whose death never comes back to them. Under the continuation condition, which counts every continuation's loss (§7), its death counts all the same; counting it is the step we offer, and T8's way of seeing asks what the catch draws on, what its course closes and who bears the bed's end (§9). If thirty a month loses nothing fifty admits, then, adding the bed, it widens fifty, keeping all fifty keeps and letting more go on: a relation between the two courses, which does not weigh the lost catch [AI reading]. If it ends a livelihood fifty keeps, the two are mixed, and the laws weigh them for no one; results about choosing are conditional on the further commitments of §7.
- **Whether it can be installed.** The first round's frozen predictions mostly failed, read afterwards as each fisher leaving the others' takes out of its account, so a second frozen round added talk. Told of the bed and the scope, and given a rule for choosing among courses (not to choose a course in which something ends when another course open to them would keep it and would end nothing their course keeps), one model kept it in 8 of 10 seasons, against 3 of 10 when, told of the bed and able to talk, it had the community's goal alone, without the scope and the rule; the other kept it in 1 of 10 even with talk ([T4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)).
- **What this leaves out.** One frozen test varied how near small trained policies came to their reward's optimum: the bay their reward counts, on which a colony depends, fell as they neared it, as their update rule largely gives, and whether the drain lies where what lies beyond shares units with what is rewarded, as T3 conjectures, went unresolved, a bay sharing none not shown to be spared ([T3](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3)). That any of this reaches actual fisheries or AI is a correspondence, not shown.

## 1. The root: continuation relative to a class

The root begins from one kind of symbol: a relation between occurrences, read as condition (缘), so that `R x y` says `x` is among what `y`'s coming about comes from. Everything else is built from relations by a few operations, and every theorem holds for every carrier and relation, so no kind of thing is chosen first; a carrier with such a relation is a *net* ([one kind of symbol](chapters/01-ROOT.md#one-kind-of-symbol)).

The grammar names a *class*, a sameness in some respect, and says everything relative to it: `y` *continues* `x` under a class where `x` is a condition of `y` and the two are alike under it, and a line is the reach of continuation. Proved for every relation and class: continuation lies within condition, and a coarser class keeps every continuation ([continuation relative to a class](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class)).

An occurrence *begins* where it continues nothing, *ends* where nothing continues it, *abides* where it both continues something and is continued, and *alters* where a finer class tells it apart from what it continues. It can end under a finer class and not under a coarser one (a witness), the shape of a fire passed on: the wood ends, the fire goes on ([the four marks](chapters/01-ROOT.md#four-marks)).

Every class has a *core* (核): the greatest sameness inside it that the relation itself carries and never tells apart, which always exists and is a class. Under a class lying inside its own core, a *constant* one, no continuation ends (proved for every relation and class; [constancy and the core](chapters/01-ROOT.md#constancy-and-core)).

A pattern outlasts its parts by renewal, a line started by another, but renewal alone cannot tell turnover that sustains a pattern from turnover that ends it; that takes two grains at once (§2; [renewal and grains](chapters/01-ROOT.md#renewal-and-grains)). That reading a subject as continuation relative to a class explains how it goes on is the theory's claim, argued, not a theorem: on a first count, a rival with no word for continuation ties it ([08 §9](chapters/08-METHOD.md#what-would-defeat-the-reading)); that every becoming has a faithful representation in the grammar is open ([what is claimed](chapters/01-ROOT.md), [open](chapters/01-ROOT.md#open)).

The laws with amounts are proved on models of the grammar. One, the *World*, has relata of one kind (*formations* where they take and give, *conditions* where they are drawn on), each active or not, standing at levels and taking from and giving to one another across discrete steps in whole-number amounts. Eight premises say how these hang together, among them balance (a level changes by exactly what is taken and given) and dissipation (a closed family taken from loses at least a unit). The eight are independent (proved on the World model; [the premises](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-map-of-premises)). Most laws are proved on the World or on processes and are not stated on the grammar, which has no amounts; [five laws the models share](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md#the-five-shared-laws) are stated on it, balance as a representation and not a derivation. Each central declaration has one place against the root's edge, with its reason, and a distance is not shown to be a failure ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)).

## 2. Generation, keeping and degeneration

Read at one class, a fire whose embers are replaced and a fire burning out look alike; read at a fine class of parts and a coarse class of the pattern together, they differ. Along some branch a pattern *generates* where it goes on with a renewal of its parts ahead of every point, *keeps* where it goes on with none, and *degenerates* where it ends on every branch. Proved for every relation and pair of classes: generation and keeping each exclude degeneration, and each other only on a single line; with excluded middle as a hypothesis every point generates, keeps from some point on or degenerates ([02's map](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains)). The generation of 02's map is continuation at two grains with no premise on what the pattern is; it is not 生生 [AI reading]. Degeneration belongs to the unfolding as fully as generation, and 02's map orders nothing.

Arising without end needs renewal where each arising goes with a fall in some condition of a finite family, and under balance and dissipation, in whole numbers of a fixed unit (where the unit of amount splits without end it can fail), renewal from beyond: no finite family taken from at arbitrarily late steps is closed from any step on ([no finite part](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-no-finite-part-bears-taking), [fed from beyond](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-continuing-self-fed-from-beyond)).

Whether a dependent can still form depends on how far levels stand above its needs. A part's *margin* is how far its draw can go, the rest's exchange held, before a dependent loses support. A *regime* is a condition whose giving is a law of what a standing draw leaves. Where giving falls short of the draw below a level, gives nothing from nothing and meets the draw from that level up, the draw and that level not both zero, it has a point of no return: held to the same draw, a level starting below it ends and one at or above it, within the levels the law states, lasts (proved in whole numbers). Theses T1 and T2 rest here ([the margin](chapters/02-MAP.md#margin), [the regime](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime)).

A selection whose rule is read from the share it raises can hold itself. Proved for any type: where selection does not lower the share under a favouring rule and the rule is read as a monotone function of the share, a course whose rule favours at the start and at every reading locks, and where nothing in the loop reads beyond the share, the lock outlives a growing unread loss. Such a loop, its reading of the rule too formed from the share, is the closed form of the *meta-attractor*, the loop in which winning comes to buy power over the rule that selects ([the folder](../meta-attractor/README.md)); that actual metrics are so formed is thesis T7. When a reading of the loss turns the lock, and how the capacity to shape the rule joins the loop, are at the home ([closed capture](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture), [readers drawn from the selected](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected), [the loss read in time](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-loss-read-in-time-by-the-others), [shaping](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)).

## 3. Knowing, learning and testing

Knowing is a relation within the unfolding, not a mirror beside it. A formation depends on some conditions by threshold and reads others by level; these *couplings* belong to the same web as everything they reach. Anything that keeps, rewards, reproduces or trains toward some histories rather than others is a *selection*. Proved on the World model, under balance on what couples in with the rest's exchange held: a part acting only beyond a formation's coupling closure (all that reaches it through its couplings) is invisible to every selection through its couplings. The law holds by construction; its force lies in the premise that a selector selects through just those couplings. It is the proxy problem in its blindness to what a proxy does not register, not its regressional or causal kinds, stated more weakly than its known quantitative form ([selection through couplings](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)).

Data generalize only through a model of what is irrelevant, a *lumping*, and 理 names that modelling (proved for every type). Within functions going alike across a lumping, data fix an untried input where it is lumped with an observed one and (two values, the data listed, lumps compared decidedly) exactly there; what a lumping misses, no data supply ([理](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-generalization-through-lumping), [identification](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-identification-where-bounded)).

With natural-number weights, read as probabilities, complying when read and acting by its own law when not is uniquely best, by its prospects, of a formation's four plans over two stages exactly where the weight complying adds to being kept, times the value, exceeds the loss of complying. This is read as the incentive deceptive alignment names; installation, intention and belief are not represented [AI reading] ([when complying when read is best](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-when-complying-when-read-is-best)).

Take a formation that senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises as one law of four parts: two worlds give it one sensed course exactly as long as they answer alike what it does (proved for every such law; [the reading loop](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#the-reading-loop)).

A drawn condition coupled in by threshold only is learned of when it fails. Observed by level, with Mathlib, in a stationary one-variable model linearized about a rest, with independent noise, its variance and lag-one autocorrelation rise as the margin to a fold shrinks, while a dependent's need enters neither, by construction. Thesis T6, that a break is seen coming only through what couples into what breaks, rests here ([a break seen coming](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#break-seen-coming)).

## 4. Life and evolution

A relatum is *embodied* where it stands at a positive level exactly while active, a premise about particular relata and not a definition of life. Proved on the World model: in a balanced flow where only active formations give, an embodied relatum arises only as another gives to it, and its arising replenishes every family containing it. Life read as continuation that renews its own conditions is a reading [AI reading]; a flame meets these laws too, so whether it is necessary or sufficient for biological life is open ([life as a condition of life](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-life-as-condition-of-life)).

Several of population genetics' classical laws hold in whole numbers, proved for every type: Price's equation in counting form; with offspring number as the amount and exact transmission, Fisher's fundamental theorem in its exact case; with additive returns in groups of equal size Hamilton's rule holds in counts; sampled offspring give drift and fixation shares ([descent](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent), [Fisher's theorem](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-what-bounds-offspring-numbers), [Hamilton's rule](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-selection-with-returns-to-groupmates), [drift](chapters/04-LIFE.md#drift)).

The World adds the flow, and the flow bounds descent: an unsupported formation leaves no descendant, and groups depending on one coupled family whose gated renewal ran into joint excess end together, so ending selects only between families (proved on the World model, under the hypotheses stated; [no descendant](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent), [coupled groups](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-groups-whose-conditions-are-coupled)). These laws restate known results, now in one model with conservation, dissipation and dependence; no reproductive advantage is made an ethical authority ([what the model adds](chapters/04-LIFE.md#blind-selection)).

The selection term of Price's equation, in counting form, reads how offspring numbers differ, not how many there are, so within a horizon it leaves open whether the cohorts stay nonempty: a course nonempty at a horizon shares its cohorts and terms through it with one nonempty at every cohort and one soon empty (proved for every type; [the term and a lineage's fate](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-the-selection-term-and-a-lineages-fate)). The stock alone decides neither generation nor degeneration: in a handover between two relata, from the same stock of at least one, a relay with no excess generates and a run-down degenerates (proved on the World model; [ending laws on a lineage](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-ending-laws-as-regimes-on-a-lineage)).

## 5. Joint activity and selection

Many formations draw on one renewal, each selecting through its own couplings; here the excess of §2 and the limit of §3 meet. Joint activity is explained from actual relations, not opposed interests, bargaining or a fixed common utility: a collective is a region of formed relations, and conflict and difference stay real.

The central law is the commons. Proved on the World model under balance: formations can each take within what a step gives while together they take more. Where the renewal depends on what it renews (*the gate*: every giver to the condition depends on it at a need of at least `m`), with activity needing support and only active giving, `n` consecutive steps of joint excess from a level below `n + m` end every formation depending on the condition at such a need; a taker not so depending can continue, and renewal from outside, such as a sun, is not covered past the step the excess reaches. This restates the tragedy of the commons in near-definitional arithmetic, not who ought to take less ([joint excess](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)).

An order is *rising* with the family that couples in where it ranks higher every outcome exceeding another there, whatever either holds beyond; there is *room* where every eligible outcome standing higher beyond is beaten on the family by an eligible one. Proved for any set of outcomes: with room, every optimum of every rising order stands beyond at the least level among the eligible outcomes, so an optimizer drains what lies beyond its couplings as far as any choice it has would. This is Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's Theorem 1 in discrete, conserved form, at optima rather than limits. A philosophical thesis stands beside them, argued: there is one unfolding, and whatever is, is how it relates within it. It forbids explanations, not observations, so by that section's measure it has no strength: no observation that would refute it is named, and the argument is that none could. What could defeat it is an argument, which measurements can support and cannot replace ([one relational unfolding](chapters/08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)).

**What can show what wrong.** The answer differs by kind of claim.

| Kind of claim | What can show it wrong |
|---|---|
| A theorem, or a law of the root | A failed check, or a statement that does not say what the prose says. No observation: a law of the root holds of every relation, and so forbids none ([08 §10](chapters/08-METHOD.md#law)) |
| A law with content | Like any theorem it stands or falls by its check. An observation reaches it only through a stated correspondence, and what it can refute is that correspondence ([08 §9](chapters/08-METHOD.md#what-would-defeat-the-reading)); the account states no science's law of its own yet |
| A thesis | Its Forbids: a forbidden case observed (for T8, argued). No theorem falls with it |
| A hypothesis a ranking result takes (the continuation condition, self-concern, C as `Comparison`) | Nothing in the represented dynamics, which leave scope and order free by construction (§7); how formations come to hold it, and how they fare, can be studied |

## 10. What is open

Where the map does not yet reach:

- **Representation.** Whether every becoming has a faithful representation in the grammar, and a law of exchange with finite courses ([01](chapters/01-ROOT.md#open), [08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-theory-of-becoming-foundation)); real amounts and continuous time.
- **Lifting.** The models' laws onto the grammar, which has no amounts; the free significance onto the root; significance's central laws beyond the specializations stated there ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#shared-laws-and-connectivity), [07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#open)). The models' conditional laws are not yet each given a net where the condition fails and the conclusion with it, as the corpus's are (§6).
- **How scope and order form within the dynamics**, as dynamics connected to the rest of the map ([08 §12](chapters/08-METHOD.md#open-items-ranked), item 12): among them, whether and how a life forms a self-concerned order; [the crux](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux); that one's own line is a regularity its own kind fixes, up to where the net ends; that the actual unfolding joins lines through what one conditions, a correspondence not shown ([07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)); the step of invariance, for what that path does not reach; whether the self's presupposition is held de se; whether status is invariant and equal ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#philosophical-warrant)). Not yet connected or mapped, beside the lifting above: learning and prediction beyond the reading loop, in which a formation senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises as one law, and whose laws state what its own update keeps, what a course chosen through its model leaves untested and when a prediction read by what it predicts is right ([03 §12](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#the-reading-loop)): probabilistic learning, implicit bias, and noisy readings with continuous beliefs are not yet on it ([03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#open)); how pools form and last, and which of several self-consistent outcomes learners come to ([05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#open)); and where most of the atlas's dynamics lead, drawn so far for two of its eight families, with one route from one family to another ([the atlas](results/atlas/ATLAS.md)).
- **Fit to actual systems.** That actual unfoldings meet the World's or the regime's premises; an organism and one kind of institution each have one minimal model, read as such [AI reading] ([04 §8](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it), [05 §12](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-rule-rebuilt-from-the-members-it-limits)). The theses' tests still to run include T1 with a need measured in advance (one designed and frozen was refused at the header of a state agency's public table of adult estimates, before reading a row: on that table its rules as frozen can reach no verdict), T3 on language models trained by gradient (LA4's Stage B, not yet frozen), T4's transfer, T5 with human valuers, and a field test of T7's reform events (the one designed could not be frozen as designed; for now one is possible only for its third case, where its records fit whole) ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#t7)) ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).
- **The corpus.** Its unchecked candidates ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#open), [03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#open), [06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#open)).
- **Completion.** That every premise is named, which the completion checks support and cannot certify ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#completion-criterion)).
