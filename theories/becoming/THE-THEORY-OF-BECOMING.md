# The Theory of Becoming

An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life, evolution, knowing and evaluation take shape inside it, without first assuming that becoming is good.

## How to read this

This overview says the theory's claims as one argument, in the order of the eight chapters, for a reader who does not read Lean. [The chapters](chapters/01-ROOT.md) state every result once, with its hypotheses, its Lean names and its record, and [the theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md) shows how concepts, results, modules and sections connect. The theory's opening statement is [OPENING](OPENING.md). A paragraph opens with its point in plain words; the sentences after it state each law with the hypotheses that change what holds, or say that the chapter states them, and nothing here is stronger than its home.

Each claim carries one standing ([the labels](chapters/08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases)):

## The line of the argument

How the results connect, read as one line [AI reading]. Anything goes on only as continuation relative to a class, and a pattern outlasts its parts by renewal (§1). At two grains a course generates, keeps or degenerates, and a renewal can be drawn down: takers each within what is renewed can together end it and what depends on it (§2, §5). Whether anything sees this coming depends on what couples into it: a selection, whether a knower, a training run, a market or an optimizer, reaches only what couples into its selector (§3), and with room an optimum drains what lies beyond as far as any choice it has would (§5). Life is where continuation renews its own conditions, and descent selects through the same flow (§4). So whose losses count turns on how far what each continues with reaches, which the dynamics leave open, and calling the wider course better is a further premise; the ethics names both and asserts neither (§7). The classical texts are where the grammar's terms come from (§6), and the method says what each standing means and where proof stops (§8). The proofs carry the account exactly to the edge where the dynamics stop deciding, so that any reader, human or machine, can check how far it reaches. A fished stock runs through §§2, 3, 5 and 7 as an illustration; it proves nothing, and the frozen tests of T2 are on fish stocks.

## 1. The root: continuation relative to a class

The root begins from one kind of symbol: a relation between occurrences, read as condition (缘), so that `R x y` says `x` is among what `y`'s coming about comes from. Everything else is built from relations by a few operations, and every theorem holds for every carrier and relation, so no kind of thing is chosen first. Precedence is the reach of the condition relation, and every condition strictly precedes what it conditions exactly where the net has no cycle (proved for every relation); that moments and positions are told apart at all rests on equality on the carrier, a commitment of each model ([one kind of symbol](chapters/01-ROOT.md#one-kind-of-symbol)).

Whether two moments of a fire are "the same" depends on the respect asked: the burning, the wood, the shape of the flame. The grammar names a *class*, a sameness in some respect, and says everything relative to it. `y` *continues* `x` under a class where `x` is a condition of `y` and the two are alike under the class; a line is the reach of continuation. Proved for every relation and class: continuation lies within condition, a line stays within its class, and a coarser class keeps every continuation. Every single condition is a continuation under some class, so lying within condition is necessary for continuation and not in general sufficient (under the full class the two coincide); and where one occurrence is continued by two that go no further, sameness read along lines is not transitive (a witness; [continuation relative to a class](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class)).

Beginning, abiding, altering and ending are positions in a line, each relative to a class, and none stands alone. An occurrence either continues something or not, and is continued or not; the answers fix its position relative to the class. It *begins* where it continues nothing, *ends* where nothing continues it, *abides* where both hold, and *alters* where a finer class tells it apart from what it continues. No mark stands alone: where nothing conditions itself, an occurrence with a condition and a consequence abides under the full class and both begins and ends under identity (proved for every relation). An occurrence can end under a finer class and not under a coarser one (a witness), which is the shape of a fire passed on: the wood ends, the fire goes on. That every occurrence in every model begins or has a predecessor is exactly excluded middle, a calibration ([the four marks](chapters/01-ROOT.md#four-marks)).

Every class has a core: the greatest sameness inside it that the relation itself carries and never tells apart. Some classes are carried by the relation: whatever one member conditions, any member alike to it conditions something alike. Under such a *constant* class no continuation ends, so a class under which some line ends is never constant. The root theorem, proved for every relation and class: inside every class lies a greatest symmetric relation that the relation carries in this way and never tells apart, the *core* (核); it always exists, is itself a class, and a class is constant exactly where it lies inside its own core. On a determined law read through a map, the core is sameness of future courses through the map, which knowing calls 文同, the 文 being the course itself (§3). Calling the core "what continues of its own" is a reading that asserts no causal independence and no mechanism of self-maintenance [AI reading] ([constancy and the core](chapters/01-ROOT.md#constancy-and-core)).

A flame of three moments shows what the core is not. Under "burning or not" each moment continues the one before, yet under that class's core none continues another, since alike moments have unlike futures (a witness). So the core alone is not the criterion of continuation, and self-maintenance read as a constant class belongs to lines that never end; the witness derives no combustion and does not decide which description carries a flame's regularity ([fire and fuel](chapters/01-ROOT.md#fire-and-fuel)).

A pattern outlasts its parts by renewal, but renewal alone cannot tell turnover that sustains a pattern from turnover that ends it. *Renewal* is a line started by another: `x` conditions an occurrence that begins a line which goes on. Read at a coarser grain, through the conditions between different classes, a pattern continues while its parts begin and end, and renewal without end never ceases (proved for every relation). But renewal cannot tell turnover that sustains a pattern from turnover that ends it: a short-lived pattern can renew and still end. That takes two grains at once, the map of §2. In the grammar 生生 names only renewal without end; the theory reads it as life in the whole and its continuing generation, which asks more [AI reading] ([renewal and grains](chapters/01-ROOT.md#renewal-and-grains)).

That reading a subject as continuation relative to a class *explains* how it goes on is the theory's claim, argued from the laws on the models, not a theorem of the grammar; that every becoming has a faithful representation in the grammar is open (processes' is shown both ways by their moves' relations, [01](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-processes-represented-both-ways), and the World's both ways for its relations, failing on its amounts, [01](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-world-represented-both-ways)), and a law written in the grammar's words is stated with its model until it is lifted (§8; [what the grammar derives](chapters/01-ROOT.md#expressible-and-derived)).

The laws with amounts are proved on models of the grammar; the richest, the World, rests on eight premises, each of which can fail while the others hold. A model gives the grammar a carrier, a relation read as condition and its classes, and carries what they do not fix; a process, configurations with a step, is the simplest. The *World* is richer: relata of one kind, each active or not, standing at levels and taking from and giving to one another across discrete steps of one shared index, in whole-number amounts; formations and conditions are two roles of one relatum. Eight premises say how these hang together: activity needing support, balance (a level changes by exactly what is taken and given), only active relata giving, only active relata taking, dissipation (a closed family taken from loses at least a unit), embodiment, a census, and bounded transmission. Proved on the World model, the eight are independent, which measures the freedom of separately chosen fields, not contingency in becoming. The World meets the grammar by instance theorems with empty axiom reports: a formation arises or ceases exactly where its occurrence begins or ends under the class "alike in activity" ([the models](chapters/01-ROOT.md#models), [the premises](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-map-of-premises), [the World in the grammar](chapters/01-ROOT.md#instances)).

The premises need not be chosen separately. Read them off one exchange, with activity as taking part, drawing as taking, requirement as response, and the rest of the whole counted as one more relatum. Then four tying premises hold by the reading; a census holds where a named family closes each step, and in every finite world, with participation listed without repetition; and dissipation, embodiment and bounded transmission are states of the law of exchange (proved on the World model). Balance is relocated, not removed: nothing coming from nothing becomes the feasibility of the law. In the periphery, a locally conserved quantity such as energy balances the levels; conservation of the total alone does not ([balance from thermodynamics' first law](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-balance-from-the-first-law)). That these determinations are internal relations of one exchange is a reading that reaches no necessity beyond the World so read [AI reading] ([read off one exchange](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-premises-read-off-the-exchange), [what internal means](chapters/01-ROOT.md#what-internal-means)).

Some laws hold on every model of their kind: coming to be keeps pace with ceasing, and within finitely many configurations a determined law that merges nothing brings every one back. Arising and ceasing, a dependence forming and dissolving, and a condition coming to stand and ceasing to stand are one change, a determination coming to hold or ceasing to hold across a step, read of activity, dependence and standing. An amount at the end of a stretch with all it fell is the amount at the start with all it rose, and for a decided determination over a finite family coming to be recurs exactly when ceasing does (proved on the World model). A configuration is how it relates, and separating two parts loses exactly the relation. Within finitely many listed configurations a determined law merges nothing exactly where every configuration returns, and becoming that keeps its whole history never returns, so it needs a carrier no list holds (proved for every type; [keeping pace](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace), [how it relates](chapters/01-ROOT.md#how-it-relates), [recurrence](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-recurrence-without-premise)). Proved on the World model: for a local law, relata listed and exchanges told apart, the relata's own courses fix the interleaving of exchanges exactly where every exchange touches a relatum and every two share one or are the same; a renaming of relata, one to one at each step, preserves the five readings checked that follow a formation (renewing and consuming in the run, a dependence's tenure, undermining and unceasing arising), while who continues whom carries identity ([the step index](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-step-index-from-own-courses), [identity](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-identity-across-steps)).

What can feed a formation, what can affect it and what it can affect are regions of different reach, and the region that can feed it lies within the one that can affect it. A region is *closed* under a relation where whatever a member bears it to is a member; a formation's *closure* is the least closed region holding it. On the World closures differ by relation: being fed, another giving to a condition the formation then requires (the *feeding closure*); being affected, another exchanging with a condition it depends on (the *influence closure*); affecting (the *reach closure*); and being affected through what couples in (the *coupling closure*). Proved on the World model, the feeding closure lies within the influence closure, and influence and reach within the region sharing conditions ([levels of closure](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-levels-of-closure)).

## 2. Generation, keeping and degeneration

Read at one class, a fire whose embers are replaced and a fire burning out look alike: both renew at the grain of their fuel. Read at a fine class of parts and a coarse class of the pattern together, they differ. A *carrying renewal* is a step at which the pattern continues under the coarse class while a fine line begins. Along some branch a pattern *generates* where it goes on with a carrying renewal ahead of every point, and *keeps* where it goes on with none; it *degenerates* where it ends on every branch. Proved for every relation and pair of classes: going on along some branch excludes ending on every branch, and generation and keeping each exclude degeneration; but generation and keeping can share a point along different branches (a witness), and exclude each other only on a single line. With excluded middle as a hypothesis, every point lies in one of three strong forms, which exclude one another. Whether the three some-branch regions cover every point is open ([the map](chapters/02-MAP.md#branches), [its laws](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains)).

The map's generation is continuation at two grains, with no premise on what the pattern is. It is not 生生, it never overlaps degeneration, and no law yet relates it to widening what can form [AI reading]. Degeneration belongs to the unfolding as fully as generation, and the map orders nothing ([the map on the World](chapters/02-MAP.md#map-on-the-world)).

Under a law some configurations come round again; what does not is drawn into a region it never leaves. For every determined law, with no finiteness or decision, a configuration is drawn into an absorbing region exactly where it does not return (proved for every type; near-definitional). A *juncture* for `s` is a configuration with one step after which `s` can still be had and one after which it cannot. Where courses branch, every course that loses a returning `s` passes a juncture, up to double negation. With every configuration listed, steps decided and `s` returning, at a configuration that is no juncture no step can be refuted to keep `s`: a choice matters only at a juncture (proved for every process; [attraction](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-attraction-is-not-returning), [junctures](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-every-loss-passes-a-juncture), [before and after](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-reaching-before-and-after-a-juncture)).

Whether a formation renews or consumes what it draws on belongs to the whole run, and two drawers each within renewal can together consume it. A formation *renews* in a run where every condition it depends or draws on meets every dependence on it at every step; a condition is *consumed* where some dependence on it stays below its need from then on. The two classes exclude each other without exhausting the cases, and the class belongs to the whole run: from one configuration one run renews and another consumes (a witness). Where renewal is gated by the support of what it renews, two drawers each within renewal can together consume it (proved on the World model; [renewal and consumption](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-relational-renewal-classification)). A formation beyond a feeding closure gives nothing to a condition a member then requires, and, proved on the World model under balance, only active giving and activity needing support, a formation taking from a condition it requires while everything feeding it is unsupported stays active for at most as many steps as the condition's level: keeping its own support, it can end through its feeders ([the feeding closure](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-feeding-closure), [ending with its feeders](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-consumption-ends-with-feeders)).

Arising without end needs renewal, and under dissipation renewal from beyond. Proved on the World model under balance and dissipation: no finite family without repetition that is taken from at arbitrarily late steps is closed from any step on, and, enclosed and with equality of conditions decided, it cannot from some step on be fed from nothing beyond it. With amounts that can shrink geometrically, this fails ([fed from beyond](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-continuing-self-fed-from-beyond)).

A closed amount that keeps losing need not stop: a stock halving at every step never rests. Rest needs a least loss at each change, in Archimedean amounts such as whole numbers, or the amount read off finitely many configurations: an amount not rising, read off configurations a list holds, falls strictly at most as often as the list is long (proved for every type). Within finitely many configurations a loss needs a law that merges, and a part's merge is kept by the rest: Landauer's count with Bennett's history, with no energy read. The rest theorem, proved on the World model: with censuses of what is active and what stands, arising embodied, the flow balanced and dissipative, and every step from some step on enclosed around what stands, that the world comes to rest cannot be refuted. Each premise is needed, openness is necessary for arising without end and not sufficient, and naming the step after which nothing arises gives LPO ([rest](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-least-loss-rest), [the merge](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-merge-of-a-part-kept-by-the-rest), [the rest theorem](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-continued-change-needs-openness)).

Whether a dependent can still form depends on how far levels stand above its needs, and two distances are kept apart. A part's *margin* reads its draw with the rest's exchange held: in a balanced flow a formation is supported exactly where its need with all the part has taken stays within the level without the part with all the part has given (proved on the World model). A *regime* is a condition whose giving is a law of what a standing draw leaves, and it has a point of no return. Proved for any giving law in whole numbers: where giving falls short of the draw below a level `y`, gives nothing from nothing and meets the draw from `y` up to a top the step keeps, a level up to that top ends exactly where it starts below the draw plus `y` (that sum positive), its point of no return. A fished stock is the case to keep in mind: its growth is its giving and the catch its draw, so, held to the same catch, a stock starting below that point ends and one starting at or above it, within the levels the law states, lasts (an illustration). Under hypotheses the chapter states, two draws each survivable alone can end a condition together. Theses T1 and T2 rest here ([the margin](chapters/02-MAP.md#margin), [the regime](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime)).

A change at one condition reaches another only through a later exchange that reads it. Proved on the World model, under hypotheses the chapter states: where the rest follows one law of exchange, the counterfactual of an act is the course that law generates, not a second world. The *carried amount* is the most difference that can arrive, a worst case over every law with the given coupling strengths, and a condition's gap is bounded by a polynomial, uniformly over such laws, exactly where no loop of couplings lies upstream of it. Where a stock couples into a formation by threshold only, how far off its break is does not couple in ([the counterfactual](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-counterfactual-is-the-course), [how it grows](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-how-a-carried-difference-grows), [the break](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-runaway-distance-uncoupled)).

A level held from both sides settles only where the response does not overshoot, a rule reading late needs a band wider the later it reads, and a reserve defers an end without escaping it (each proved under hypotheses the chapter states; [regulation](chapters/02-MAP.md#regulation)).

A selection whose rule is read from the share it raises can hold itself. Proved for any type: where selection does not lower the share under a favouring rule and the rule is read as a monotone function of the share, with the start's rule and every rule read favouring, the course locks, and where the reading favours at every share only a change of the law, of the reading or of the selection leaves. Where nothing in the loop reads what lies beyond the share, and each step adds a unit beyond, the lock outlives a growing unread coordinate (a loss by reading) that passes every bound. A loop whose rule and reading of the rule are both formed from the share it selects, reading nothing beyond it, is the *meta-attractor*; that actual metrics are so formed is thesis T7 ([capture through the rule](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-through-the-rule), [closed capture](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)). Where the rule's readers are drawn from those it selects, its being read from the share is proved exactly where each kind reads at least as favourably for its own kind. Where only those it disfavours read the loss, the counts when they read it decide for good: the same reading turns the lock when it comes in time, and leaves it in place once the selection has left those readers no more than its carriers ([readers drawn from the selected](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)). Where the capacity to shape the rule is carried from parent to offspring and the rule after shaping is read by readers so drawn, the shapers among them reading at least as much coming back to the shapers as the others do, the capacity's selection and the rule's lock are one loop on either side of a critical mass that who reads and the cost locate exactly: above it the capacity rises and the rule stays locked in its favour, below it the capacity falls and the rule stays locked against it, read from the share alone either way ([what comes back, read by readers drawn from the selected](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)). Where the others among those readers read a loss once it reaches a bound, a reading while their late reading, at the share one step on, still favours them turns the course for good, and a reading once it would favour the shapers leaves the lock in place; with the rule read at both steps at most one step lies between, from which the course starts a new round, and from a start favouring the shapers strictly a deadline, quadratic in the start's counts, bounds the window ([the loss read in time](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-loss-read-in-time-by-the-others)).

## 3. Knowing, learning and testing

What ends a renewal can lie where none of its takers looks (§2); what can reach a formation at all is the question here. Knowing is a relation within the unfolding, not a mirror beside it. A formation depends on some conditions by threshold and reads others by level; these *couplings* are relations of the same web as everything they reach. Anything that keeps, rewards, reproduces or trains toward some histories rather than others is a *selection*, and it goes through a formation's couplings where histories alike through them are selected alike. Proved on the World model with no premise: two histories are selected alike by every selection through a formation's couplings exactly when they are alike through them. With the rest of the exchange fixed and balance on what couples in, a part acting only beyond the coupling closure is invisible to every such selection. The law holds by construction; its force lies in the premise that a selector selects through just those couplings. It is the proxy problem, stated more weakly than its known quantitative form ([selection through couplings](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)).

Seeking rests only where its target can be met at rest. Under a determined law a course comes to rest with its target met exactly where it reaches a state the law leaves unchanged that meets it; "more than now", met at every step, needs an open world, and "above all others" never rests for two (proved for every type). The theory reads 无明 (not-seeing) as seeking locked onto a target that cannot be met at rest; the position every formation selects from, which reaches only what couples in, grounds it and is not it. 明 (clearness) is coupling joined to a selection, growing through measurement, models and pools [AI reading] ([seeking](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-seeking-boundary), [明](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#observation)).

A difference goes where couplings carry it and nowhere else: for every law reading into each condition a schedule of listed levels, two balanced courses with the same formations present, the part acting alike and the rest following the law, apart at the start only in one stock, stay alike at every condition not downstream of it (proved on the World model). To observe is to stand at the end of such a route ([observation is a route](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-observation-is-a-route)).

To test is to tell apart, and nobody stands outside the net to do it. Proved for every relation: the core lies in every finite stage of telling apart, so what some stage tells apart is not in the core. A room in balance with its surroundings and one held warm by a thermostat share a core class of "warm or not" until the window's opening is in the net (a witness). On the World model a claim is excluded only through a difference in what couples in. Proved for every process: what one's own act would have done otherwise is fixed only where the configuration returns, and a projection carries a determined law exactly where configurations alike under it go by the same move to configurations alike under it ([testing within the net](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-testing-within-the-net), [experiment](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-experiment-needs-return), [laws along a run](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-projection-carrying-a-law)).

What finite records can settle has an exact form: the verifiable classes of histories are exactly the open ones, and recurrence can be neither verified nor refuted. A process's histories are read off its finite stretches: each is a nonempty collection of stretches holding every beginning of a member and extending any two members by a third, and a run is the collection it passes through. With no premise the open classes of histories are exactly the verifiable ones. A refutable reading holds at every run adhering to it up to double negation, and outright where it is stable; that every refutable class is closed, asked of every process, is exactly double-negation elimination. Recurrence is neither verifiable nor refutable, and under Markov's principle no method verifies it even in the limit, while settling is verified in the limit. Each classical step here is a calibration: it would decide what the rest of a course does. Induction is resolved into laws, not certified [AI reading] ([refutation and verification](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#refutation), [what a stretch verifies](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-a-finite-stretch-verifies)).

Data generalize only through a model of what is irrelevant: they fix an untried input where the model lumps it with an observed one, and only there. A model of what is irrelevant is a *lumping*, a map sending configurations that count as the same to one value. 理, the modelling of the laws of structure and change, is one notion, because a model of change is a lumping the step goes alike across, which is a model of structure (proved for every type). Within a class of functions that go alike across a lumping, data fix the value at an untried input where it is lumped with an observed one, and (two values, data listed, lumps compared decidedly) only there; the class of all functions is free at every untried input: what a lumping misses, no data supply. Two courses with at most `N` states that agree for `2N − 1` steps agree everywhere, after Fine and Wilf; given the bound, induction is deduction from that step, and not given it the least-bound extrapolation is right from the same step, so the bound buys silence, not speed. Under a determined law, in a world a list holds, a feature is learnable through a map exactly where configurations with the same future through it have features eventually agreeing: observability, not a procedure ([理](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-generalization-through-lumping), [identification](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-identification-where-bounded), [learnable](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-learnable-through-a-map)).

These laws are about one object: the course of a run's states through a map, named 文 [AI reading]. Proved on the grammar for a determined law and a map, with the step read as condition and the map's values as a class: two states have the same course exactly where they lie in one class of the value class's core ([文 in the grammar](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-wen-in-the-grammar)). Where a law reads finitely many conditions, what it is sensitive to is its least coupling (proved on the World model; [sensitivity](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-sensitivity-is-the-least-coupling)).

Readings have limits. Proved for every type: what contexts show glues into one context-free reading exactly where they agree where they meet; where each context requires a parity of its listed claims, every context-free assignment meets at most `m − 1` of `m` contradicting contexts, with Specker's boxes, CHSH and the Mermin–Peres square as cases. Where a map moves every value, no family of readings indexed by what it reads says its diagonal gap, and completing one step by step never returns: Lawvere's fixed point as an unfolding ([context](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-claims-shown-in-context), [gluing](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-gluing-under-parity), [readings within what they read](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-readings-within-what-they-read)).

What a selection reading a formation's acts fixes of it is what those acts show. Proved on the root's grammar: strict choices come from some strict order exactly where nothing they show returns, and then the positive comparisons every such order shares are exactly the reach of what they show, none forced beyond it. Proved for every type: where a formation's lumping tells an occasion it is not read at from every read one, being in that lump decided, a selection through the readings that selects a law reading through the lumping also selects one taking any value there. A formation that senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises is one law, its internal part reading what goes on through what it senses. ## 7. Significance, ethics and alignment

Sections 1–5 say how things go; this section says what that leaves open and what the ethics adds. Positions and standards form within the one unfolding, and none outside it owns value. ## 8. Method

The theory is built from zero premises first and states each premise it uses where it uses it, all gathered in one premise card. Distinctions such as fact and value are drawn along relations, with what they leave out. Nonduality is a requirement of method; it proves neither the ontology nor an ethical necessity ([the start](chapters/08-METHOD.md#beginning-within-the-one-unfolding), [the premise card](chapters/08-METHOD.md#premise-card)).

The Lean package's pure core imports only Lean's prelude, and every source declaration in it has an empty axiom report (no propositional extensionality, quotient soundness or classical choice). That is stricter than constructive, and it concerns logic only. The pure core proves that propositional extensionality holds exactly where every reading of propositions is lumped by interderivability; that transport along it does not compute is a kernel observation on one value, not a theorem, and it is why the pure core states ↔ where it would need =. A law the pure core cannot yet prove is proved in the periphery, its axioms reported ([the pure core](chapters/08-METHOD.md#pure-core-and-periphery), [why not propext](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-proposition-identity)).

The real assumptions lie in the modelling: discrete steps on one index, whole-number amounts, activity as a proposition, one kind of relatum, the naming of relata, requirements as thresholds. Their cost is measured with Mathlib over the reals: a balanced continuous course can stand still at every whole instant and change between them, and no reading into whole numbers keeps an amount falling at every step. Decidedness turns out local: a count exists exactly where its property is decided at each member ([commitments](chapters/08-METHOD.md#modelling-commitments), [their cost](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-what-discrete-steps-and-whole-amounts-represent), [counts](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-counts-exist-where-decided)).

Where a law needs a classical principle, the need is itself a finding about the unfolding. A calibration states a classical principle as a hypothesis or proves the law equivalent to it, and says what the need says about the unfolding. The canonical case is LPO, deciding whether a history changes again: a run in a listed world with no undoable change makes fewer changes than there are configurations, but the step of its last change is unbounded, and that every such run settles is exactly LPO ([calibrations](chapters/08-METHOD.md#calibrations), [the last change](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-the-last-change-of-a-run)).

Writing a law in the grammar's words does not prove it there; lifting takes a dependency audit, a restatement with only what the proof uses, and a representation theorem, and most modules are not yet on the grammar, as [the theory graph](results/THEORY-GRAPH.md) shows ([expressible is not derived](chapters/08-METHOD.md#expressible-is-not-derived)). None of the central results is new mathematics: a review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds each near-definitional or a known result restated, from finite Poincaré recurrence to the is–ought gap. What survives is the premise ledger, with witnesses both ways and its calibrations; the ethics with scope and order apart; and one vocabulary for conservation, selection limited by observation and the comparison of opportunity sets ([known results](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)).
