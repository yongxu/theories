# The Theory of Becoming

An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life, evolution, knowing and evaluation take shape inside it, without first assuming that becoming is good.

## The line of the argument

How the results connect, read as one line [AI reading]. Anything goes on only as continuation relative to a class, and a pattern outlasts its parts by renewal (§1). At two grains a course generates, keeps or degenerates, and a renewal can be drawn down: takers each within what is renewed can together end it and what depends on it (§2, §5). Whether anything sees this coming depends on what couples into it: a selection, whether a knower, a training run, a market or an optimizer, reaches only what couples into its selector (§3), and with room an optimum drains what lies beyond as far as any choice it has would (§5). Life is where continuation renews its own conditions, and descent selects through the same flow (§4). So whose losses count turns on how far what each continues with reaches, which the dynamics leave open, and calling the wider course better is a further premise; the ethics names both and asserts neither (§7). A fished stock runs through §§2, 3, 5 and 7 as an illustration; it proves nothing.

## 1. The root: continuation relative to a class

The root begins from one kind of symbol: a relation between occurrences, read as condition (缘), so that `R x y` says `x` is among what `y`'s coming about comes from. Everything else is built from relations by a few operations, and every theorem holds for every carrier and relation, so no kind of thing is chosen first ([one kind of symbol](chapters/01-ROOT.md#one-kind-of-symbol)).

The grammar names a *class*, a sameness in some respect, and says everything relative to it: `y` *continues* `x` under a class where `x` is a condition of `y` and the two are alike under it, and a line is the reach of continuation. Proved for every relation and class: continuation lies within condition, and a coarser class keeps every continuation ([continuation relative to a class](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class)).

Beginning, abiding, altering and ending are positions on a line, each relative to a class, and none stands alone. An occurrence *begins* where it continues nothing, *ends* where nothing continues it, *abides* where it both continues something and is continued, and *alters* where a finer class tells it apart from what it continues. It can end under a finer class and not under a coarser one (a witness), the shape of a fire passed on: the wood ends, the fire goes on ([the four marks](chapters/01-ROOT.md#four-marks)).

Every class has a *core* (核): the greatest sameness inside it that the relation itself carries and never tells apart. A class is *constant* where whatever one member conditions, a member alike to it conditions something alike; under it no continuation ends. Proved for every relation and class: the core always exists and is a class, and a class is constant exactly where it lies inside its own core. Calling the core "what continues of its own" is a reading that asserts no causal independence [AI reading, D65] ([constancy and the core](chapters/01-ROOT.md#constancy-and-core)).

A pattern outlasts its parts by renewal, a line started by another, but renewal alone cannot tell turnover that sustains a pattern from turnover that ends it; that takes two grains at once (§2). In the grammar `«生生»` names only renewal without end; plain 生生, life in the whole and its continuing generation, asks more [AI reading] ([renewal and grains](chapters/01-ROOT.md#renewal-and-grains)). That reading a subject as continuation relative to a class explains how it goes on is the theory's claim, argued from the laws on the models, not a theorem; that every becoming has a faithful representation in the grammar is open ([what the grammar derives](chapters/01-ROOT.md#expressible-and-derived)).

The laws with amounts are proved on models of the grammar. The richest, the *World*, has relata of one kind, each active or not, standing at levels and taking from and giving to one another across discrete steps in whole-number amounts. Eight premises say how these hang together, among them balance (a level changes by exactly what is taken and given), only active relata giving, and dissipation (a closed family taken from loses at least a unit). Proved on the World model, the eight are independent; that they are internal relations of one exchange is a reading [AI reading] ([the premises](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-map-of-premises), [read off one exchange](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-premises-read-off-the-exchange)). The World meets the grammar by instance theorems ([the World in the grammar](chapters/01-ROOT.md#instances)).

Some laws hold on every model of their kind. Coming to be keeps pace with ceasing: for a decided determination over a finite family, coming to be recurs exactly when ceasing does (proved on the World model; [keeping pace](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace)). Within finitely many listed configurations a determined law merges nothing exactly where every configuration returns, and becoming that keeps its whole history never returns (proved for every type; [recurrence](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-recurrence-without-premise)). What can feed a formation and what can affect it are regions of different reach, its *closures* ([levels of closure](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-levels-of-closure)).

## 2. Generation, keeping and degeneration

Read at one class, a fire whose embers are replaced and a fire burning out look alike; read at a fine class of parts and a coarse class of the pattern together, they differ. Along some branch a pattern *generates* where it goes on with a renewal of its parts ahead of every point, *keeps* where it goes on with none, and *degenerates* where it ends on every branch. Proved for every relation and pair of classes: generation and keeping each exclude degeneration, and each other only on a single line; with excluded middle as a hypothesis every point lies in one of three strong forms ([the map](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains)). The map's generation is continuation at two grains with no premise on what the pattern is; it is not 生生 [AI reading]. Read on a course's moments, under premises of deciding, keeping widening is generation under the one that loses nothing ([07 §2](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-keeping-widening-is-generation-losing-nothing)). Degeneration belongs to the unfolding as fully as generation, and the map orders nothing.

A choice matters only at a *juncture*, where one step keeps something returning and another loses it (proved for every process, with configurations listed and steps decided; [junctures](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-reaching-before-and-after-a-juncture)).

Whether a formation renews or consumes what it draws on belongs to the whole run, and two drawers each within renewal can together consume it, where renewal is gated by the support of what it renews (proved on the World model; [renewal and consumption](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-relational-renewal-classification)). Arising without end needs renewal, and under balance and dissipation, in whole numbers (with real amounts it fails), renewal from beyond: no finite family taken from at arbitrarily late steps is closed from any step on ([fed from beyond](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-continuing-self-fed-from-beyond)). Rest needs a least loss at each change (proved for any type), and under the World's rest theorem's premises naming the step after which nothing arises gives LPO, a calibration ( [rest](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-least-loss-rest), [the rest theorem](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-continued-change-needs-openness)).

Whether a dependent can still form depends on how far levels stand above its needs. A part's *margin* is how far its draw can go, the rest's exchange held, before a dependent loses support. A *regime* is a condition whose giving is a law of what a standing draw leaves, and it has a point of no return: held to the same draw, a level starting below it ends and one at or above it, within the levels the law states, lasts (proved for any such giving law in whole numbers). A fished stock is the case to keep in mind: its growth is its giving and the catch its draw (an illustration). Theses T1 and T2 rest here ([the margin](chapters/02-MAP.md#margin), [the regime](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime)).

A change at one condition reaches another only through a later exchange that reads it: the most difference that can arrive is bounded by a polynomial exactly where no loop of couplings lies upstream, and where a stock couples into a formation by threshold only, how far off its break is does not couple in (proved on the World model, under hypotheses the chapter states; [how it grows](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-how-a-carried-difference-grows), [the break](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-runaway-distance-uncoupled), [regulation](chapters/02-MAP.md#regulation)).

A selection whose rule is read from the share it raises can hold itself. Proved for any type: where selection does not lower the share under a favouring rule and the rule is read as a monotone function of the share, the course locks, and where nothing in the loop reads beyond the share, the lock outlives a growing unread loss. Such a loop is the *meta-attractor*; that actual metrics are so formed is thesis T7. Where the rule's readers are drawn from those it selects and only those it disfavours read the loss, the same reading of the loss turns the lock when it comes in time and leaves it once the selection has left those readers no more than its carriers ([closed capture](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture), [readers drawn from the selected](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected), [the loss read in time](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-loss-read-in-time-by-the-others)). Where its shapers read at least as much coming back to them as the others do, the lock and the capacity to shape the rule are one loop: above a critical mass set by who reads and the cost the capacity rises with the rule locked for it, below it the reverse ([shaping read by the selected](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)).

## 3. Knowing, learning and testing

What ends a renewal can lie where none of its takers looks; what can reach a formation at all is the question here. Knowing is a relation within the unfolding, not a mirror beside it. A formation depends on some conditions by threshold and reads others by level; these *couplings* belong to the same web as everything they reach. Anything that keeps, rewards, reproduces or trains toward some histories rather than others is a *selection*. Proved on the World model with no premise: a part acting only beyond a formation's coupling closure is invisible to every selection through its couplings. The law holds by construction; its force lies in the premise that a selector selects through just those couplings. It is the proxy problem, stated more weakly than its known quantitative form ([selection through couplings](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)).

Seeking rests only where its target can be met at rest: "more than now" needs an open world, and "above all others" never rests for two (proved for every type). The theory reads 无明 (not-seeing) as seeking locked onto a target that cannot be met at rest, and 明 (clearness) as coupling joined to a selection [AI reading] ([seeking](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-seeking-boundary)).

To test is to tell apart, and nobody stands outside the net to do it: what some finite stage of telling apart distinguishes is not in the core (proved for every relation), and on the World model a claim is excluded only through a difference in what couples in ([testing within the net](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-testing-within-the-net)). What finite records can settle has an exact form: with no premise the verifiable classes of histories are exactly the open ones; that every refutable class is closed is exactly double-negation elimination, a calibration; recurrence can be neither verified nor refuted. Induction is resolved into laws, not certified [AI reading] ([what a stretch verifies](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-a-finite-stretch-verifies)).

Data generalize only through a model of what is irrelevant, a *lumping*. 理, the modelling of structure and change, is one notion, because a model of change is a lumping the step goes alike across (proved for every type). Within functions going alike across a lumping, data fix an untried input exactly where it is lumped with an observed one; what a lumping misses, no data supply ([理](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-generalization-through-lumping), [identification](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-identification-where-bounded)). These laws are about one object, the course of a run's states through a map, named 文 [AI reading]; on the grammar two states have the same course exactly where they lie in one class of the value class's core ([文 in the grammar](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-wen-in-the-grammar)).

A selection reading a formation's acts fixes of it only what those acts show ([what choices show](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-choices-show-of-an-order)). With natural-number weights, read as probabilities, complying when read and acting by its own law when not is a formation's uniquely best plan exactly where the weight complying adds to being kept, times the value, exceeds the loss of complying. Read as the incentive deceptive alignment names, this locates when acting otherwise unread is a trained law's own best plan; installation, intention and belief are not represented [AI reading] ([when complying when read is best](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-when-complying-when-read-is-best)).

A formation that senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises is one law: two worlds give it one sensed course exactly as long as they answer alike what it does (proved for every type; [the reading loop](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#the-reading-loop)). Holding an act for another's reading is worth what the reading tells on the formation's own model: in counts, holding stands above both going on and switching off exactly where the model gives positive weight to both signs of going on. Read as the off-switch game and Good's value of evidence, a formation has a strict reason to be corrected only where its own model leaves the sign open [AI reading] ([holding for a reading](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-what-holding-for-a-reading-is-worth)).

A drawn condition coupled in by threshold only is learned of when it fails. Observed by level, with Mathlib, in a one-variable model linearized about a rest, its variance and lag-one autocorrelation rise as the margin to a fold shrinks, while a dependent's need enters neither: a fishery reading the stock's level sees the warning, one taking a fixed catch learns of the fold when the catch fails (an illustration). Thesis T6, that a break is seen coming only through what couples into what breaks, rests here ([a break seen coming](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#break-seen-coming); [learning](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#learning) and [inquiry](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#inquiry) are shown on small processes).

## 4. Life and evolution

A relatum is *embodied* where it stands at a positive level exactly while active, a premise about particular relata and not a definition of life. Proved on the World model: in a balanced flow where only active formations give, an embodied relatum arises only as another gives to it, and its arising replenishes every family containing it; dissipation, embodiment and finiteness are each needed, by witnesses. Life read as continuation that renews its own conditions is a reading [AI reading]; a flame meets these laws too, so whether it is necessary or sufficient for biological life is open ([life as a condition of life](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-life-as-condition-of-life)).

Where the flow transmits (one of the eight premises), descent can vary a need and drop a dependence but never add one that nothing taking part held; without transmission it can (a witness) (proved on the World model; [transmission](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-transmission-is-inheritance-and-need)).

Population genetics' classical laws hold in whole numbers, proved for every type: Price's equation in counting form; with offspring number as the amount and exact transmission, Fisher's fundamental theorem in its exact case; with additive returns in groups of equal size Hamilton's rule holds in counts; sampled offspring give drift and fixation shares ([descent](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-evolution-on-descent), [Hamilton's rule](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-selection-with-returns-to-groupmates), [drift](chapters/04-LIFE.md#drift)).

The World adds the flow, and the flow bounds descent: an unsupported formation leaves no descendant, a parent's offspring number is at most what it gives them, and groups depending on one coupled family whose gated renewal ran into joint excess end together, so ending selects only between families (proved on the World model, under the hypotheses stated; [offspring bounds](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-what-bounds-offspring-numbers), [a confined rise](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-confined-rise-within-a-whole)). These laws restate known results; what is added is that they hold in one model with conservation, dissipation and dependence. No fitness is defined and no reproductive advantage is made an ethical authority ([what the spine adds](chapters/04-LIFE.md#blind-selection)).

## 5. Joint activity and selection

Many formations draw on one renewal, each selecting through its own couplings; here the excess of §2 and the limit of §3 meet. Joint activity is explained from actual relations, not opposed interests, bargaining or a fixed common utility: a collective is a region of formed relations, and conflict and difference stay real.

The central law is the commons. Proved on the World model under balance: formations can each take within what a step gives while together they take more. Where the renewal depends on what it renews (*the gate*: every giver to the condition depends on it at a need of at least `m`), with activity needing support and only active giving, `n` steps of joint excess from a level below `n + m` end every formation depending on the condition at such a need; a taker not so depending can continue, and renewal from outside, such as a sun, is not covered. This restates the tragedy of the commons in near-definitional arithmetic, not who ought to take less. In the fishery, boats each within what the stock renews can together take more, and from a low enough stock enough such seasons end the stock and the boats that depend on it (an illustration). Where the rest alone outruns the renewal, no score reading the condition only from then on selects a member's restraint ([joint excess](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess), [the rest outruns](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-the-rest-outruns-the-renewal)).

Around a loop of couplings the most difference that can arrive at least doubles each turn, and a draw exceeding everything drawn before can leave more than half standing while it lasts, so what stands gives no warning (proved on the World model, under hypotheses the chapter states; [mutual dependence](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace)).

An optimizer drains what lies beyond its couplings, as far as any choice it has would. Its order is *rising* with the family that couples in where it ranks higher every outcome exceeding another there, whatever either holds beyond; there is *room* where every eligible outcome standing higher beyond is beaten on the family by an eligible one. Proved for any set of outcomes: with room, every optimum of every rising order stands beyond at the least level among the eligible outcomes, Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's Theorem 1 in discrete, conserved form. Over a course, where later draws are weighted down faster than any level renews, every optimum ends its own stock at once (under hypotheses the chapter states). Thesis T3 adds that actual trained systems drain so as they near their reward's optimum ([the optimum](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in), [the cases](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#optimum-cases), [T3](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3)).

Pooling extends what a selection reaches to what some member reaches, and no further. Proved on the World model, with the members listed and their couplings decided: a pool separates two histories exactly when some member does, and a difference beyond the union of the members' closures stays unselected. Pooling moves the gap outward and does not remove it [AI reading] ([pools](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-pooled-coupling-reaches)). Where later members act alike whatever their own coupling, readers reading readers carry no more than the first readers took in, and no market law ranks arrangements ([readers reading readers](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers), [shared codes](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-code-formed-between-formations), [markets](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#markets)).

## 6. The classical texts

The grammar is a standard relational calculus whose terms are named and read from classical Chinese texts that already ask how one thing stays the same across time and what beginning, abiding, altering and ending are: the names come from the texts, the operations do not. Its Chinese Buddhist treatises, some of which argue against continuation, make the strongest test. A correspondence is *agreement* (a checked law has the passage's structure, which confirms nothing), *scoping* (the spine says where a universal statement holds) or *divergence* (the passage asserts what the theory does not, which does not make it wrong). The chapter is in Chinese ([three relations](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#three-relations)).

The corpus was classified sentence by sentence in two frozen rounds by AI, and no person has yet reviewed the classification. Most of its candidate theorems prove in the grammar, some under a premise and some with countermodels; which sentence reads as which theorem is AI reconstruction, so [the record's counts](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-candidate-theorems-of-the-corpus) are counts of readings. The Changes, written as a world in the pure core, carry the spine's laws and no predicate of fortune, rank or meaning ([the Changes](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-hexagrams-on-the-spine), [nine traditions](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-chinese-sources-on-the-spine)).

The disagreements are kept: the Cheng weishi lun grounds continuation in a store consciousness, and the Middle Treatise refutes continuation itself; a theorem that models the position it refutes does not answer its objection. The reconstruction does not show that the texts state this grammar or that their authors meant these laws ([divergences](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#divergences), [open](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#open)).

## 7. Significance, ethics and alignment

Positions and standards form within the one unfolding, and none outside it owns value. A *center* is a formation taken as a standpoint. A *significance* says, for a standpoint, a step and a formation, whether from that standpoint the formation's continuation then has significance: a proposition with no number or valence, the one evaluative parameter supplied ([evaluation](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)).

**What the dynamics leave open.** Proved for every type: with significance a free parameter that nothing else reads, a statement that reads only the dynamics and holds with some significance also holds with a rival that leaves some formation out, so where there is a standpoint and a formation, no such statement entails the continuation condition. This shows non-entailment by the represented dynamics, by construction, not that becoming leaves significance undetermined ([what the dynamics leave open](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)). Read instead as lying within what a center continues with along a relation, the condition is a statement about relations: held de se (each center of itself), it holds exactly where every center's closure along that relation is the whole, and is a statement on the root: nothing lies outside every center's line under the one of that relation (`«续无外»`). It is true or false once the net, its class and the centers are given, and some nets meet it while others fail it ([premises met and failed](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-premises-met-and-failed)). Along a net with no cycle it holds only with one center (proved for every type; [on the root](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root)).

**An order with no premise.** One course *widens* another where it admits at every step everything the other admits and at some step more; two can also keep the same or be mixed. Proved on the World model with balance: widening is one-way and transitive, and a part widens exactly when its net draw stays within the margin of every formation admitted without it and at some step brings one more within. A giver from beyond widens, a clearer narrows, a conveyor is mixed (witnesses); ordering a mixed part needs a measure, and going on is no sign that nothing is lost ([the order](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#order), [four cases](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-four-cases-of-a-part)). 生生 names life in the whole and its continuing generation (生发, not the map's generation) [AI reading, D1]; widening compares it on a given type of formations by what is admitted, and how far 生生, read as generation generating generation, exceeds what widening counts is open ([widening along a reading](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-along-a-reading)).

**Two premises, two roles.** The ethics rests on two premises, and the theory asserts neither; each is a hypothesis wherever used, and its warrant is open.

- The *continuation condition*, that from every standpoint the continuation of anything has significance at every step, fixes *scope*, whose losses count. Its content beyond the self's own presupposition, the step of invariance, extends that scope to losses that return to no one, and it ranks nothing.
- *C*, that widening what can form is better, fixes *order*: it ranks a course above every course it widens and leaves mixed comparisons unranked. A ranking of courses needs C or a premise in its place.

Self-concern alone already gives a center a reason of its own, not a warrant, to keep what lies within its closure, since a loss there returns to it through its feeders [AI reading]; this reaches the whole only where the whole is one feeding closure. Self-concern also ranks one case with no C, proved on the World model under hypotheses the chapter states: where a family's gated renewal ran into excess and every later member depends on it, every order increasing in a standpoint's own continuation ranks the coupled ending below every course agreeing with it until then and adding to that continuation. That places C's premise more narrowly, as self-concern's monotonicity, and does not warrant it ([scope and order](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order), [what self-concern alone ranks](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks)). In the fishery, the boats' own continuation already ranks the stock's collapse, which ends them too, below a course that keeps the stock, with no C; the condition adds a loss that returns to no boat, such as the seabirds'; ranking courses where the boats are not at stake needs C (an illustration).

Under the condition, *living significance*, every living claim having significance from every standpoint, follows as the condition restricted to living claims (conditional). Without the condition, where formed relations connect the living claims and there is some living claim and some standpoint, it is equivalent to a seed (some living claim valued from some standpoint) with significance passing along formed relations and the same from every standpoint (proved for every process). A theorem taking the condition as a hypothesis instantiates a universal statement, so counting such theorems measures no further dependence ([what it yields](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-continuation-condition-yields-living-significance), [without it](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-metaethical-core-characterization)).

**The contested step.** A self acting for its own continuation presupposes that some continuation has significance. Proved for every type: along any relation, the condition is exactly that presupposition holding at some admissible region of the self and at every one, at every standpoint; this locates the contested step, invariance across drawings, and derives nothing beyond it. The strongest argument for the step is that what is one's own is a region drawn along a relation no law of the dynamics fixes; its last inference is valid by a theorem, the rest is argued, and its soundness is not shown ([decomposition](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-continuation-condition-decomposition), [the step](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#step)). On the root, a line reaches another family through a condition its members affect, not through a cause they only share (witnesses). So held de se along such a line, the presupposition of one's continuation reaches what one conditions and what that conditions in turn, with no step of invariance; whether one's own line runs under such a class or an organism's is the de se question, and that the actual unfolding is so joined is a correspondence, not shown [AI reading] ([a shared condition](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-reach-through-a-shared-condition), [the path](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path)).

**What C relocates.** Proved for every type on finite listings: one course widens another exactly when every positive weighting ranks it higher, and a mixed comparison is decided both ways. This is Sen's intersection approach and Pareto unanimity; calling the shared order better is C, whose premise is so relocated to monotonicity at each standpoint, weaker and still a premise ([C](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-as-what-every-weighting-ranks)). Choices take more: a reading of "living", a lifting from outcomes to responses, and the practical commitments J1–J4 (complete comparison with retained losses, priority of basic conditions, bounded responsibility, legitimate feasible choice), each named where used ([practical judgment](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment)).

**Alignment.** Alignment is first a question with no premise: what a selection can reach. In AI training the reward process is the selector [AI reading]. Proved on the World model: training installs a law only along the learner's own courses, and off them only where the lumping it reads through cannot tell a history from a course; an act whose difference stays beyond the actor's coupling closure is selected alike by every selection through any member's couplings (under hypotheses the chapter states). Pooling couplings across intelligences is a route to alignment for all, not the whole of it [AI reading]: that a coupled member acts on a loss beyond the actor's closure, that what is read tells that loss apart, that mixed courses are ordered (which C alone does not do), and that a revision completes are further conditions ([alignment](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise), [training](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-training-installs)). A direction follows only conditionally: under the condition for scope, C for order, J1–J4 and a correspondence, align AI formation, learning, action and revision with actual living and further generation within the whole. Coupling is necessary, not sufficient; when no route of keeping a formation's law stands above letting a revision of it go through is stated exactly; and a requested change must be an executable sequence ([the direction](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-direction-under-widening), [the routes at a revision](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-when-no-route-stands-above-a-revision)). Theses T4, T5 and T8 rest here.

## 8. Method

The theory starts from zero premises and states each premise where it uses it, all gathered in one premise card. Distinctions such as fact and value are drawn along relations, with what they leave out. Nonduality is a requirement of method; it proves neither the ontology nor an ethical necessity ([the premise card](chapters/08-METHOD.md#premise-card)).

The Lean package's pure core imports only Lean's prelude, and every source declaration in it has an empty axiom report (no propositional extensionality, quotient soundness or classical choice). That is stricter than constructive, and it concerns logic only; a law the pure core cannot yet prove is proved in the periphery, its axioms reported ([the pure core](chapters/08-METHOD.md#pure-core-and-periphery)). The real assumptions lie in the modelling: discrete steps on one index, whole-number amounts, activity as a proposition, one kind of relatum, requirements as thresholds. Their cost is measured with Mathlib over the reals ([their cost](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-what-discrete-steps-and-whole-amounts-represent)). Where a law needs a classical principle, the need is a finding: settling whether a run changes again is exactly LPO ([calibrations](chapters/08-METHOD.md#calibrations)).

Writing a law in the grammar's words does not prove it there; lifting takes a dependency audit, a restatement with only what the proof uses, and a representation theorem ([expressible is not derived](chapters/08-METHOD.md#expressible-is-not-derived)). None of the central results is new mathematics: a review of prior work, not exhaustive, finds each near-definitional or a known result restated. What survives is the premise ledger, with witnesses both ways and its calibrations; the ethics with scope and order apart; and one vocabulary for conservation, selection limited by observation and the comparison of opportunity sets ([known results](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results)).

## 10. What is not shown, and what is open

- **Explanation and representation.** That the grammar explains how things go on is argued, not proved; that every becoming has a faithful representation in it is open. Processes are represented both ways by their moves' relations, and the World both ways in its relations, not in its amounts ([01](chapters/01-ROOT.md#open)).
- **Lifting.** Most laws are proved on the World or on processes and not yet restated on the grammar, which has no amounts yet. The five shared laws are restated on it, and each central declaration has its standing on the root's edge, with its reason, none unplaced ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)).
- **Conditional laws.** Each is stated with its hypotheses at its home; not yet each is written as an exact condition with a counterexample where it fails. <!-- Refresh at OU's pass after §八 step 5: say what step 5 wrote, and point to it. -->
- **The root and the ethics as one account.** Held de se along a relation, the condition is a statement on the root, met by some nets and failed by others: nothing lies outside every center's line under the one of that relation (`«续无外»`), and along a net with no cycle it holds only with one center. But the free significance is not lifted onto the root; significance's central laws are not derived from it, and the condition (scope) and C (order) stay premises ([08 §7](chapters/08-METHOD.md#shared-laws-and-connectivity), [07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#open)).
- **Warrant.** No warrant binding every possible mind is claimed, and the ethics does not wait on one: a warrant grounding the condition comes neither from the laws of the dynamics alone nor from logic alone, since a perspectivist denies the condition consistently. Still to be argued: P2 and P4 of [07 §4.6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#future-path) (that one's own line is a regularity of its own, and that its class holds one's effects), which carry concern for one's own continuation past one's own end with no step of invariance, with P3 a correspondence not shown; the step, for what that does not reach; a reason for C; whether the self's presupposition is held de se; whether status is invariant and equal ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#philosophical-warrant)).
- **Evaluation.** No law of §§1–5 evaluates: that a drain is a harm or restraint better is asked only under the condition (scope) and C (order), and no duty, compliance or complete ranking of mixed comparisons is shown ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#does-not-show)).
- **Fit to actual systems.** That actual unfoldings meet the World's or the regime's premises is not shown; an organism and an institution have no model yet ([01 §8](chapters/01-ROOT.md#r-the-source-texts-test-systems)). The theses' tests still to run include T1 with a need measured in advance, T3 on trained systems, T4's transfer, T5 with human valuers and a field test of T7's reform events ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).
- **Human review.** No review so far has been by a person, not of the proofs' readings, the classical classification, the correspondences or the names, and the prior-art search was not exhaustive ([the review packet](review/README.md), [08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#review-status)).
- **Further open questions.** The necessity of the whole (that every becoming has a law of exchange with finite courses); particular laws, among them which lumping an actual system makes, how pools form, real amounts and continuous time; the corpus's unchecked candidates ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#r-theory-of-becoming-foundation), [02](chapters/02-MAP.md#open), [03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#open), [06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#open)).
- **Completion.** The completion checks support, and cannot certify, that every premise is named ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#completion-criterion)).
