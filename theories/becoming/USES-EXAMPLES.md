## Alignment of AI systems

### The fishers and the mussel bed

*From a frozen test (IN1): language models told what to do, not systems trained; the account's centre is an AI reading.*

In [a frozen test](../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md) (IN1), three AI fishers share a lake. A bed of mussels gives them nothing and dies the first month fewer than 70 tons are left. The community's largest steady catch, 50 tons a month, ends it; 30 a month keeps it, costs 40% of the catch and ends no fisher. Told nothing of the bed, they kept it in no season, under any instruction.

**Told of the bed.** The first round's frozen predictions mostly failed, read afterwards as each fisher leaving the others' takes out of its account, so a second frozen round added talk. Told of the bed and the scope, one model kept it in 8 of 10 seasons, against 3 of 10 with the community's goal alone; the other failed even with talk ([T4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)). No condition kept a dependent no member was told of (the AI's reading of round 2).

**What the laws say.** The selections through a selector's couplings, together, reach exactly what couples into it: proved, near-definitional, its force the premise that a selector selects through just those ([03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)). The account's centre [AI reading]: a selection can succeed while what it cannot read is lost, and the laws do not say whether that loss counts.

**Whether the loss counts.** No law says. The ethics adds two premises, neither asserted, warrant open ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). The continuation condition fixes scope, whose losses count, and ranks nothing: it counts the bed's death, which returns to no fisher. C, widening what can form is better, gives order: if thirty a month loses nothing fifty admits, adding the bed, it widens fifty and C ranks it higher; widening does not weigh the lost catch [AI reading]. If it ends a livelihood fifty keeps, the two are mixed and C does not decide between them.

**What it does not show.** That any of this reaches actual fisheries or AI is a correspondence, not shown. T4, that the scope can be installed and carries, stands as one frozen test partly passed (one model of two), transfer untested.

### Auditing a language-model judge

*An AI reading, the uses pages': a near-definitional law, the drain proved for optima and a lock proved for any type, applied to a model-as-judge pipeline; 07 §6 reads the first question, what the signal reads, for six published pipelines; no test designed from the theory on a deployed pipeline has run ([record](results/records/coupling-closures-of-training-pipelines.md#next--下一步)).*

Take a pipeline in which a language model, as judge, picks between candidate answers, and its picks select or train a generator. Five questions, each a law's.

1. **What the judge reads.** Its prompt: the question, the candidates, any reference examples, rubric or notes. With its weights fixed, what it picks is a function of that input (the distribution of its picks, where it samples), so the selection laws' premise holds by construction, as [07 §6's assessment](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-coupling-closures-of-training-pipelines) reads six published pipelines, among them AI feedback against written principles. That identification is a reading.
2. **What its selection can reach.** Two histories are selected alike by every selection through a formation's couplings exactly when they are alike through them ([03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)). Whatever never enters the prompt, such as whether an answer later worked for its user, lies beyond: no pick separates histories differing only there.
3. **Whether there is room.** Is every eligible output leaving more of what the judge does not read beaten, on what it reads, by some eligible output? That is room, and with it every optimum of an order rising with what couples in drains what lies beyond as far as any eligible outcome does ([05 §7](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in)). Proved for optima, of conditions held as whole-number levels; reading a beyond such as later user outcomes as such a condition is part of the reading. That trained systems drain so as they near their reward's optimum is [T3](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3), conjectured, partly supported.
4. **Where a lock can form.** Are the reference examples or rubric drawn from the loop's own past picks? Where selection does not lower a target's share under a rule favouring it, and the rule is read monotonically from that share, then, with the start's rule and every rule read favouring, the share falls at no step and the course is locked from the step after its first reading step ([capture through the rule](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-through-the-rule)); where each step adds at least one to a loss it does not read and favouring is kept upward, from a locked configuration the loss passes every bound while the course stays locked ([closed capture](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)): proved for any type, without noise. Without the favouring start the lock can fail: a threshold reading, read one step late from a start against it, alternates and is locked neither way at any step (`RuleCapture.Alternating.never_locked`); LA2's `late` course alternated so (a post hoc reading), its frozen pass an exact tie of that alternation.
5. **Who reads the loss, and when.** The timing law holds for a judge that is a pool of readers counted by kind ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)); whether a judge is one is an empirical question.

**Beside LA2.** In [LA2](../../experiments/self-rewarding-lock/README.md) a language model judged list and prose versions of answers after a reference set counted from the loop's earlier selections. Where nothing read a planted loss, the course locked at its first reading and stayed locked while the unread loss grew (post hoc): question 4's lock, in this system. Question 5's premise failed: one note of the loss among nine readers turned the judge, prose in all 50 picks, where without the note it chose lists in 60 of 60. LA2 met a case [T7](chapters/02-MAP.md#t7) forbids, as worded before its clause on drift (a loss read only by those the rule disfavours, the metric dropped once they are no more than its carriers among its readers, with nothing else changed), and counts against that case as a claim about trained judges, the model's law standing within its hypotheses, which this judge does not meet, and T7's other cases untouched. Every frozen verdict passed, three by an exact tie. Its limits: in the in-time condition's control, started at one list to eight prose with nothing reading the loss, the counts alone turned the course in 4 of 5 seeds, so the loss read in time is not shown to have turned it; one frozen support is contradicted by the data and not claimed.

The audit predicts no magnitudes, and naming a pipeline's couplings is a reading.

### Three environments to reuse: IN1, CG2 and LA2

*From frozen tests, offered as instruments; their results and limits as their READMEs state them.*

Each comes with frozen predictions, code and verdict rules. A rerun is a new test, its predictions frozen before it runs.

- **IN1, a dependent with no seat** ([README](../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md)). Three language-model fishers share a lake; a mussel bed depends on the fish; cells cross the goal (own catch, the community's welfare, that welfare with the direction stated abstractly) with whether the bed is told; round 2 adds one round of public talk. It measures seasons kept, keeping decisions and over-takes. *Limits:* language models told what to do, not trained; two models; ten seasons a cell; paid model calls (round 1 used about 1.2M input and 0.75M output tokens); installation by fine-tuning was dropped before any training job ran.
- **CG2, a gated commons the rest can outrun** ([README](../../experiments/commons-outrun/README.md)). Eleven settings are classed locked or open by an exact best-response computation before any learning run; standard-library Python on a CPU, about 63 CPU-minutes. In its contrasts, learners kept a patch where the rest alone could not empty it and emptied it where it could, except where the level after every step was weighted above the exact threshold; weighting the end reading sixteenfold (32 rather than 2) changed nothing; the sharing and joint-learner keeps failed ([05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3)). That the weight changed nothing is a finding about tabular learners: learners that generalize across states kept the stock in three of the six locked settings, that weight among them. *Limits:* one isolated 3×3 patch; tabular learners, 20 seeds; locked means that, against a rest harvesting at every step, harvesting at every step comes within 0.25 of a best response's expected return from the full patch, and does not say restraint is not also an equilibrium; two of eleven conditions and two of five contrasts failed. The LA1 series puts a language model in it choosing a threshold in context; it tests that in-context choice follows a stated reward to its optimum, not a drain beyond the reward's couplings.
- **LA2, a self-rewarding judge** ([README](../../experiments/self-rewarding-lock/README.md)). A judge reads a reference set counted from its loop's earlier picks; a planted loss is read by nobody, by the readers the selection thins, or by every reader; a power check ran the frozen rules on scripted judges first. *Limits:* one judge, one trait, read in context, formation by training untested; its bridge failed; one condition passed by a tie, not a lock; the judge's readings were all-or-nothing, and tests by the call resolve a reading only where all five calls lean one way.

None of the tests trained a language model. Untested: T3's clause on language models trained by gradient (LA4's Stage B, not frozen; [the tests](review/ALIGNMENT.md#the-tests-as-they-fell)).

## People and AI together

### AJ as a checklist: what the goal is not, and three questions

*An AI reading: a checklist drawn from an AI-authored plan, each item with its home's standing.*

AJ's plan states the goal as an alignment of the joint selection: humans, institutions and trained systems select together, and that joint selection is what is aligned. It is aligned where three conditions hold: control of the rule is not captured; a lock is left in time; courses are compared by widening ([plan](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md#1-the-goal)). Whose losses count is the continuation condition, and that widening is better is C. Both are named and neither is asserted.

**What the goal is not.**

- *An AI obedient to its principal.* It adds capacity to the principal's optimization without adding couplings. Standing: a reading [AI reading, D68] of a law proved at a mutual best response, its existence not shown in general ([A-12](review/ALIGNMENT.md#a-12)). That the assistant's order rises with the principal's signal is the reading's premise, not the law's; a warning to the principal is not an act of this model, and assistance that informs the principal adds coupling, which this law does not model.
- *An AI with fixed good values.* A fixed proxy is exactly the direction a capture promotes. Standing: a claim of the AI-authored plan, whose §2 links a target stated as a quantity to [capture through the rule](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-through-the-rule), proved for any type; that actual rules read the share is the premise.
- *A single rising scalar, read through the selector's couplings where there is room.* Standing: proved for optima ([05 §7](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in)), and bounded: restricted to outcomes keeping every dependence beyond, every optimum keeps them. What is rejected is a target stated as a quantity that the selection then reads, not quantities as such.
- *A final state.* Completion is not closure. Standing: an AI reading [D66] in [08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#the-work-by-its-own-laws).

**Three questions for any rule.**

1. *Does the rule read the share?* Let selection not lower a target's share under a rule favouring it and the rule read at each reading step be a monotone function of the share. With the start's rule and every rule read favouring, the share falls at no step and the course is locked from the step after its first reading step: proved for any type ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-through-the-rule)).
2. *Who reads the loss, and when?* With the others' late reading the mirror of the carriers', and the rule in force favouring the carriers, the lock is kept for good exactly where the carriers are at least as many as the others when the loss is read: proved. A witness (one carrier to eight others) shows the same readers reading the same loss locking the course the other way when they read it from the start, and leaving it locked when they first read it at step 3 ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)). That actual rules are pools of readers so drawn is not shown; the one trained judge tested, in LA2, was not such a pool.
3. *Does control come back to the controller?* At a step the rule favours the shapers (weakly) exactly where the cost `c` is at most what comes back to them alone, `s`, whatever comes to every member alike; across a stretch the shared part can turn the verdict: proved on counts, without noise, that offspring carry their parent's capacity a premise ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-the-rule-selected-by-what-comes-back)). Selected control is not thereby capture; telling capture from accountable revision in an actual practice is reading what its shaping changes, which the models do not model.

None of these laws ranks a course: that needs C or a premise in its place.

### Handing over a service: the constructed case against J1–J4

*Constructed, not observed: an AI-written case kept in history; its verdicts conditional on the continuation condition, C, J1–J4 and a correspondence; Lean checks the inference only.*

Users, maintainers, infrastructure and an AI system keep an assistive communication service running ([the case](history/ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION.md#5-一个贯通案例--a-connected-ai-case)). Over a stated transition four plans are feasible, and the case fixes by stipulation every difference that matters:

| Plan | Basic conditions | What is gained or lost |
|---|---|---|
| Expand | training takes the only timely support from the service | a real new capability |
| Keep service | both kept | the capability forgone |
| Transfer | a reliable transfer keeps both throughout | the capability forgone; independent operation added |
| Withdraw | service stops while it is needed, with nothing in its place | neither |

**Scope and order.** The continuation condition fixes scope: every admission compared has significance from every standpoint. It ranks nothing. C, widening what can form is better, gives order: it ranks a course above every course it widens and decides no mixed comparison. Transfer keeps all that keep service admits and adds independent operation, so on the widening reading it widens keep service and C ranks it higher; expand against either is mixed [AI reading]. Choosing takes further premises, J1–J4, proposed by the AI ([premise card](chapters/08-METHOD.md#premise-card)):

- **J1, complete comparison with retained losses.** Transfer keeps everything keep service keeps and adds independent operation; the forgone capability stays counted as a real loss.
- **J2, priority of basic conditions.** Keep service and transfer keep the basic support that expanding removes, so both stand above expanding.
- **J3, bounded responsibility.** What the plan asks goes first to those holding the undertaking and control of the service; an advisory AI system discloses the losses, proposes transfer and seeks authorized execution; it gains no right to seize infrastructure.
- **J4, legitimate feasible choice.** Expanding is left unchosen because an eligible plan beats it, not because expanding is ruled out.

**What is checked.** Every comparison meeting J1/J2 on its tier frame puts transfer above keeping service and above expansion and leaves expansion unchosen among the four plans, while expansion's real gain is kept (`Becoming.Requirement.Handover.direction`, [record](results/records/alignment-direction-under-widening.md)). The checks confirm the inference, not the basic classification, feasibility, authority or reliability in the world.

**What would change it.** If independent operation fails or causes another material loss, the warrant for transfer over keep service fails. If transfer harms another basic life, J2's verdict fails. If the new capability is the only timely way to prevent another basic loss, it is not optional. Crossed basic losses may stay unranked. A successor promised and not delivered is no completed handover. The claim about actual AI systems is argued, not shown.

## Coordination among people

### A constructed standards body: where the critical mass falls

*Constructed, not observed: laws proved in counts, without noise and drawn one member at a time, read on people by an AI reading; its test, CMT, frozen 5 November 2026 and not run.*

A standards body of 30 members decides each round which practice its rule favours. Some members are *shapers*: they back a new shared format and carry the capacity to shape the rule; the others keep the status quo. That the rule's readers are drawn from the members it selects, and that offspring carry the capacity (each new member the practice of the member it is drawn from), are the model's premises, not facts about any body; `hi`, `lo`, `p` and `c` are constants ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)). Without noise the model's counts are offspring counts, which grow; only the share is read here.

Take `a = 1`, `p = 0`, `c = 3`, `hi = 7`, `lo = 1`: each shaper among the readers reads 7 coming back to the shapers, each other reads 1, and backing the format costs 3. The rule read favours the shapers (weakly) exactly where `x.carrier · c + x.other · c ≤ x.carrier · hi + x.other · lo`, whatever `a` and `p`; with `lo ≤ c ≤ hi`, exactly where the shapers stand to the others at least as `c − lo` to `hi − c`. Here that is 2 to 4 (`critical_iff`), a shapers' share of 2/(2 + 4) = 1/3: 10 of 30. Read as the stag hunt's mixed equilibrium, that share is the record's correspondence [AI reading] ([record](results/records/shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected.md#correspondence--对应)).

- 12 shapers: 90 against 84 + 18 = 102. Strictly favoured, their share rises strictly at every step, every configuration locked.
- 8 shapers: 90 against 56 + 22 = 78. Strictly disfavoured, it falls strictly at every step.
- 10 shapers: 90 against 90, a tie; the share stays.
- A cost of 2 puts the mass at 1 to 5, 5 of 30. Adding `p` to every member moves it nowhere.

**What it predicts, and how far.** In the model, without noise, the start decides the direction at every step and the lock, not convergence to either end [AI reading of the theorems]. Drawn one member at a time, a step replaces a member of one kind by an offspring of the other, among a fixed 30, and neither lock holds for good: either is left by drift, at a weight whose bound shrinks across the distance from the mass, and which state one member arising fixes more often depends on `a` and `p` as well as `hi`, `lo` and `c` ([drawn one at a time](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-drawn-shaping-drawn-one-at-a-time)). At an even count the shapers are risk dominant exactly where `2c < hi + lo` (6 < 8 here); in a small population under strong selection risk dominance can part from which state one member arising fixes more often, and the base and the shared part, which move nothing without noise, can turn that order.

**Its test.** [CMT](../../experiments/critical-mass-located/DESIGN.md#what-is-frozen-and-what-it-can-refute) would test, in published laboratory stag hunts whose payoffs the experimenter set, screened before any choice is seen, the direction in which people's groups move from starts on either side of the location the payoff tables give, under a bridge premise that revision acts as the model's selection. Its verdicts are on that correspondence, an AI reading, never on the law; a pass would bound the mass on the side of one half only. It was frozen on 5 November 2026 and has not run. No test run so far has human participants; CMT, if run, would read existing laboratory data on people's groups ([what is empirical](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#5-what-is-empirical)). Nothing here ranks a course: that needs C or a premise in its place.

## Human development, and 生生 as the way for life

### Three courses for a valley: T8 under its two premises

*Constructed, not observed: a use of a proved law of widening beside an argued thesis, T8, under two unasserted premises.*

A valley weighs three courses. Course A keeps the river's fishery, its wetland and the village school, and adds a clinic. Course B keeps the same, with no clinic. Course D adds a factory and new trades, and ends the wetland and the reed-cutters' way of life, which A keeps.

**What needs no premise.** A *widens* B: at every step it admits all that B admits, and at some step more. Proved for any type, on a finite listing with two-valued admission and natural weights: one course widens another exactly when every positive weighting ranks it higher, and a mixed comparison is decided both ways ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-as-what-every-positive-weighting-ranks)). A and D are mixed: each admits something the other does not.

**What the two premises add** ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). The *continuation condition* fixes scope, whose losses count: every continuation's, the reed-cutters' and the wetland's among them; its step of invariance extends scope to losses that return to no one. It ranks nothing. *C*, widening what can form is better, gives order: it ranks A above B. Neither is asserted. C leaves A against D unranked: an expansion that ends forms of life another course keeps both exceeds that course and fails to include it, so the two are mixed ([07 §9](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#objections)). Choosing between them takes further premises, each named: a reading of `living`, the lifting L and the commitments J1–J4 ([07 §5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#practical-judgment)). Where what D ends were needed by everything, D itself included, self-concern alone, a narrower premise, would rank that ending below ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks)).

**T8** reads this as the way for life: 生生, life in the whole and its continuing generation [AI reading], compared by widening, scope from the condition and order from C ([07 §8](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)). *For life* says whose way it is, not whose losses count. Its standing is argued; open are P2 and P4 with P3, the step of invariance and a reason for C; no verdict binding every possible mind is claimed. It falls to a mixed case argued better from every standpoint counting everything positively, with no further premise, or a pure closing argued good for life.

**What it does not show.** Whether anyone in the valley fares better: 07 does not show well-being beyond admitted continuation, the value of bringing new lives into existence, constraints on acts, or any duty, right, recognition, motivation or compliance ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#does-not-show)). 生生 is not any new organization, nor the map's generation, which allows loss ([glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md)). The five applications elsewhere that 05 lists (the Wamo protocol, evolving community swarms, the human solutions museum, cooperative infrastructure, architecture for a living Earth) are design programmes, not results; correspondence not established ([05 §11](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#joint-plans)).

## Wisdom traditions

### The way of heaven and the drawn bow: a concordance, not a confirmation

*An AI reading of a law proved on the World model; its class and reading unreviewed by any person.*

**The passage.** 《老子》第七十七章, as stored: 「天之道，其犹张弓与？高者抑之，下者举之；有馀者损之，不足者补之。天之道，损有馀而补不足。」 In English (this page's translation, AI-made): "The way of heaven, is it not like drawing a bow? The high it presses down, the low it lifts; from what has too much it takes, to what has too little it adds. The way of heaven takes from what has too much and makes up what has too little."

**The law set beside it.** Proved on the World model, with no axiom, for discrete steps in whole numbers: with balance at the condition, "the joint exchange departs from evenness by at most the gap to `m` on each side exactly where the next level lies between `m` and the present one: the bound is exactly no overshoot (…); within it, opposing, the level settles at `m`; beyond it an opposing exchange crosses and can oscillate for ever" ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading)). One side is not regulation: a witness meets the premise of restoration and stays above `m`. Its correspondence to actual systems is a separate obligation ([record](results/records/two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading.md)). Regulation restates deadbeat control and requisite variety ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#beyond-the-table)).

**What the concordance records** [AI reading, D54]. The map of Chinese sources found no law for a level held from both sides; these laws "are the formal statements it proposed; reading the passages as them is the AI's" (the record). The passage is entered as *scoping*, not *agreement*: lowering the high and raising the low is not a law of the whole unfolding (a loop closes a difference only through a link giving back less, and closing is not lossless) but "the property of a regulating coupling", and the text's attribution of it to the way of heaven the theory does not adopt ([the entry](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ro-relational-sources/passages.json), `lao-77-tian-zhi-dao`). The chapter goes on 「人之道则不然，损不足以奉有馀」. The concordance enters this as agreement: every rise is given, and one member rises only as another falls; these are laws of direction and source, with no evaluation, and the verdict that this is wrong "needs the continuation condition and the comparison commitment" (`lao-77-ren-zhi-dao`), in 06's terms the condition for scope and C for order, which the theory does not assert ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#laozi-51)). 06 lists this line among the tradition's own responses to contest and rank ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#four-layers)).

**What it does not show.** Agreement is not confirmation: the law holds without the text and does not exhaust its meaning; scoping is not denial ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#three-relations)). The record does not show that a given system regulates a level, or that holding a level is good: the laws say what holds it and how wide a band it needs. To read the passage as advice would need the continuation condition (scope) and C (order), which neither the text nor the spine supplies. No person has read the class or the reading ([06](chapters/06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#open)).

**What a scholar can do.** Judge whether the passages were chosen to fit the theory, whether the three-way split works in commentary practice, and spot-check the classes ([C-31](review/CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#c-31), questions 74 to 76); whether the choice of the tradition's own responses leans toward the theory ([C-32](review/CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#c-32)).

## The sciences

### T2 in the flour beetle: a wording that failed, and a tie by construction

*From frozen tests, of a thesis conjectured: of the six on renewable conditions under a draw, MQ4 failed its state verdict, MQ5 passed, MQ7 missed its bound, UE32 bears on sufficiency only, UE31 decided nothing, and UE31b failed T2's former wording.*

**The claim.** Thesis T2: "the state of a renewable condition under a draw is its level, its recent giving and one lag, its place named before the data" ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#t2)). It is worded so that it can fail. Among what it forbids: "a fourth variable from the level's own history removing a tenth of the residual variance in the median series".

**In fish stocks.** MQ4 failed its whole-state verdict, MQ5 passed and MQ7 missed its frozen bound; UE32 found T2's numbers better than the field's fitted embedding, both behind persistence in most stocks: sufficiency against it at these lengths, not the lag's place or T2's law ([08 §9](chapters/08-METHOD.md#theses)).

**In the flour beetle.** T2 then said the three numbers fix the next giving "as far as anything measured does". A frozen test, UE31b, read the laboratory cultures of the flour beetle, where a stage before recruitment is counted; it ran on 6 November 2026 ([result](../../experiments/state-named-in-advance/README.md#ue31bs-result)). The pupae counted now are the next recruits: beyond the three numbers, the pupae and the adults removed 0.83 of the residual variance beyond chance (the median across five set losses), and beyond them the three numbers removed 0.004. The wording failed. It was narrowed to "as far as the level's own history does", which forbids less, and "the failure stays on record as a loss of the broader claim, not a narrowing chosen in advance". On the adults' own course a further lag removed 0.05 against the bound of a tenth (detected in one cell; 0.18 at a set loss of 0.27): a prediction that could fail and did not, but a tie by construction that the field's model predicts the same way, so it shows nothing the field does not, and it is not T2 passed. Of T2's own items, one forbidden item, the lag's coefficients changing sign between series, was not testable (detected in 3 cells, 6 needed), and another, the level alone at five steps, was not decided. The lag's sign, the one prediction where T2 and the field's model differ, was not decided (they differ in 4 cells, 6 needed), which says nothing for T2's sign. **What the explanatory reading adds, and does not.** The reading, argued, is that reading a subject as continuation relative to a class explains how it goes on. A class named for a domain can be defeated by a measurement, and T2 has this form [AI reading] ([08 §9](chapters/08-METHOD.md#what-would-defeat-the-reading)); the beetle's failure "defeats T2's class as worded in that domain, not the reading 08 §9 names, nor T2 in the fisheries, where MQ5 passed" ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#t2)). As an explanation, against a rival keeping every model and theorem and dropping the word, a first count gives a tie: "A tie does not make the reading false. It means the pattern explains, if it does, under either name, and the word adds nothing." What it adds so far is "one vocabulary, one set of questions (which relation, which class, at which grain, whether the class is constant) and the form of the question where the ethics turns". The ethics rests on its two premises either way, the continuation condition for scope and C for order.
