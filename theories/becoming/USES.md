*Forty to fifty-two minutes to read. A shorter route: the first paragraph, **In brief**, the line on the labels and your field's section (fourteen to eighteen minutes for the longest). For about an hour: this page, then [the audit of a language-model judge](USES-EXAMPLES.md#auditing-a-language-model-judge) and [its sheet](USES-EXAMPLES.md#the-judge-audit-as-a-sheet), or one worked example of your own field (fifty-two to sixty-eight minutes at the most). The worked examples, [on their own page](USES-EXAMPLES.md), sixty-two to eighty more.*

The account is a map of how anything forms, lasts, changes and ends, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together. It is first a map for alignment: what a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run) reaches, what it drains beyond what it reads, how it comes to lock on what it already chose, and how winning comes to buy power over the rule that selects: the loop the work calls the [meta-attractor](../meta-attractor/README.md). Its closed form, where the rule and how it is read are both formed from the share it selects, can lock: on stated models, where selection does not lower the favoured share and the rule and its reading follow it upward and keep favouring it, a locked start stays locked however large an unread loss grows ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)). From this map we want to design an alignment of the joint selection, people, institutions and trained systems selecting together, that falls into neither instrumental convergence nor the meta-attractor: where shaping its rule is selected, the rule still reads what lies beyond the shapers' share, so that a loss there can turn it ([the goal](../../GOALS.md#generative-alignment)). It is also a map of wisdom: what traditions, sciences, philosophies and systems thinking have seen of these dynamics, read onto one account, where they meet it and where they do not. This page says, field by field, what a reader can do with it today, what that rests on, and how far each part has been seen.

**Working on alignment?** Start with [the dossier in ten minutes](review/ALIGNMENT.md#the-dossier-in-ten-minutes), then [the audit of a language-model judge](USES-EXAMPLES.md#auditing-a-language-model-judge) and [its sheet](USES-EXAMPLES.md#the-judge-audit-as-a-sheet), a form for a pipeline's own documents, logs and runs.

## Field by field

**In brief.** You can ask four questions of any selection, whether a reward, a judge, a market or a vote: what it reads or needs, what lies beyond that, whether it can do better on what it reads by lowering what lies beyond, and whether its rule can lock on what it already selected. A judge has a fifth: who reads the loss, and when. The laws beneath these questions are proved on models. Used on an actual system today, they give a reading, and on a pipeline the sheet can find a premise failing; that a law holds there waits on a correspondence, which no section below shows yet. The account also offers [three frozen test environments](USES-EXAMPLES.md#three-environments-to-reuse-in1-cg2-and-la2) to reuse, with their failures recorded, and, [after the fields](#exits-and-design-laws), exits and design laws, each on its model. There is no should beyond becoming ([the opening](OPENING.md)), and no good and evil set over it: right and wrong, whether a loss counts and which course ranks above another, form within it, in people and AI ([07 §1](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)); the laws, drawn on the dynamics alone, evaluate nothing. The ethics emerges from continuation. A life goes on through what sustains it, and going on is not yet an order over courses. Where relations close, read across all steps, so that every continuation can be affected, at some remove, by every other, nothing lies outside each one's reach along that relation; on the reading that what has significance from a standpoint is what lies within its center's reach, that is the *continuation condition* (from every standpoint, for anything, its continuation into the future has significance). Read in time, it holds only from some step on, and there exactly where each one's acts, from every step, come to bear on every other's later continuation (proved on models, [07 §4.11](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#condition-in-time)). A course *widens* another when it keeps all the other keeps and lets more form, more future; where the condition holds, read across all steps, whatever a widening adds lies within the life's own reach. Call a life's order over courses *self-concerned* when it ranks above what it widens every widening that adds to its own continuation, read as what lies within its reach: there, that order already ranks every widening above what it widens ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order); proved on models, self-concern a hypothesis: mutual dependence gives the condition so read, and under it a self-concerned order ranks so on a finite listing and, with admission decided, between two level paths whose admitted pairs fit in a finite list, so that their admissions end; the course of a line that persists without end never fits such a list, so there this route is silent; the two results compose directly, though no single theorem states the composite). On a finite listing, every way of adding up worth that counts each thing above zero ranks the wider course higher, and ranking by widening adds no premise beside the life's own concern: where the condition holds, that concern already does it. That a life forms such an order is not shown: what the models show of persisting is a filter on what was chosen, not an order formed ([07 §4.9](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#what-persisting-shows)); how such orders form is the work's open target ([08 §12](chapters/08-METHOD.md#open-items-ranked)). Where relations run one way, concern for one's own continuation need not count those who depend on one: seeing them is needed for a choice to answer to their loss, and not enough, and counting their loss is the one step we offer; where a course opens one thing and closes another, the laws show it mixed and weigh it for no one ([human development](#human-development)).

In each field, the lines on what it rests on open with how far each part has been seen, shortened from [08's labels](chapters/08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases): **Proved** for a Theorem on its base, checked in Lean; **Tested** for a Thesis T*n* and what its frozen tests found; **Reading** for a Reading, and here also for an AI review or an Assessment, none of which changes a theorem; **Conditional** as in 08, holding under what its theorem takes, each named: the continuation condition (every continuation counted, which alone ranks nothing), self-concern, an order meeting `Comparison` (C) or a practical commitment; **Argued** for a Thesis (philosophical), for T8, and for the thesis that everything, standards included, forms within one relational unfolding ([08](chapters/08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)); **Open** as in 08, not shown or not yet run.

### Alignment of AI systems

**Do today.** Ask a reward, a judge or an evaluation what it cannot read and where its rule can lock, and read the answers against laws proved on models: [the audit, worked on a language-model judge](USES-EXAMPLES.md#auditing-a-language-model-judge), with a sheet to fill from a pipeline's own documents, logs and runs.

- **Proved.** Selection through couplings (what a selector reads or needs): histories alike through them are selected alike, near-definitional, its force the premise that a selector selects through just those ([03](chapters/03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)).
- **Proved.** The drain with *room* (every eligible outcome left higher beyond what couples in beaten on what couples in by an eligible one): on a web with each condition beyond depended on at a need met at the step, every optimum of an order rising with what couples in breaks exactly the dependences beyond that some eligible outcome breaks, a known theorem restated ([05 §7](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in)).
- **Proved.** A rule read monotonically from the share it selects, under selection not lowering that share and with favouring kept as the rule rises, once locked stays locked while a loss it does not read grows past every bound: for any type, without noise ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)).
- **Tested.** T3 on trained systems: conjectured, partly supported ([05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3)). Whether real rules, metrics or trained systems meet the models' conditions has been tested only in small frozen runs, several of whose predictions failed; none trained a language model.
- **Tested.** T7: conjectured; LA2's judge, a language model judging in context (formation by training untested), met a case T7 forbids, as worded before its clause on drift, which counts against that case as a claim about judges that are trained models, T7's other cases untouched; the judge was not a pool of readers counted by kind, so the model's law stands within its hypotheses, which it does not meet ([02](chapters/02-MAP.md#t7); [in the audit](USES-EXAMPLES.md#auditing-a-language-model-judge)).
- **Reading.** The audit, which applies the proved laws to a pipeline; [07 §6](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-coupling-closures-of-training-pipelines) reads its first question, what the signal reads, for six published pipelines.

What these give alignment work is questions and laws to read a pipeline by, not a solution or a training method; deception, assistance games and corrigibility are reached only in part ([the dossier](review/ALIGNMENT.md#the-dossier-in-ten-minutes)).

### People and AI together

**Do today.** Ask of a rule people and AI share whether it reads its own share, who reads a loss and when, and who receives what shaping brings back and whether it covers the shapers' cost, so that a loss beyond the shapers' share can turn the rule: [the checklist](USES-EXAMPLES.md#aj-as-a-checklist-what-the-goal-is-not-and-three-questions).

- **Proved.** The laws beneath the three questions, for any type or in counts, without noise: where selection does not lower a favoured share and the rule is read from it monotonically, favouring at the start and at every reading, the course locks ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-capture-through-the-rule)); who reads, and when, is a law and a witness ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)); the capacity to shape the rule is selected by what comes back to the shapers, alone or through their groups, net of its cost ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-selected-among-groups)).
- **Reading.** The three questions rest on them, from [AJ's plan](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md#2-why-this-representation), an AI-authored plan.
- **Reading.** Obedience adds capacity, not coupling: read at a mutual best response, not shown to exist in general, its premise that the assistant's order rises with the principal's signal ([A-12](review/ALIGNMENT.md#a-12)).
- **Conditional.** A direction for AI, offered and not as a law, comparing plans as the constructed [handover case](history/ALIGNMENT-DIRECTION.md#5-一个贯通案例--a-connected-ai-case) does, every premise named: conditional on the continuation condition, [J1–J4](chapters/08-METHOD.md#premise-card) and a correspondence, comparing moves by widening; for actual AI systems, argued ([07 §7](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-direction-under-widening)).

The models state when the capacity to shape a rule is selected; telling capture from accountable revision in an actual practice is reading what its shaping changes, which they do not model; and they rank neither: ranking open rule-making above capture takes an order a result names among its hypotheses ([the plan, §1](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/aj-joint-selection/PLAN.md#1-the-goal); [the record](results/records/shaping-the-rule-selected-by-what-comes-back.md)).

### Coordination among people

**Do today.** Check a shared resource by the total taken, not by each member's own count, and locate, in a model, the critical mass, the share of members at which a rule they read starts to favour those who shape it: [a constructed case](USES-EXAMPLES.md#a-constructed-standards-body-where-the-critical-mass-falls).

- **Proved.** Joint excess: on models; restates the commons ([05 §1](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)). The critical mass: in counts, without noise ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-read-by-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)).
- **Reading.** The critical mass read as the stag hunt's mixed equilibrium, the computation elementary. Its correspondence to people's adoption: a reading under a bridge premise.
- **Open.** [CMT](../../experiments/critical-mass-located/DESIGN.md#what-is-frozen-and-what-it-can-refute) would test that correspondence: frozen 5 November 2026, not run. No test of a thesis run so far has human participants ([§5](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#5-what-is-empirical)).

### Human development, and 生生, the continuation of life

**Do today.** Check, on your own finite listing of what each course admits, whether one widens another, keeping all the other keeps and letting more form ([07 §4.4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-as-what-every-positive-weighting-ranks)), and look at a course as T8 offers, for life: what it draws on, what ends, what goes on, who bears it.

- **Proved.** The tool needs no premise. On a finite listing one course widens another exactly when every additive weighting that counts each listed thing above zero ranks it higher; where each of two courses admits something the other does not, they are mixed, some positive weighting ranking them each way, and the laws weigh them for no one. In [04 §8](chapters/04-LIFE.md#organization), on organization within organisms: a witness, in a finite model (periphery, axioms reported), that [the same carrier and developmental operations do not fix the same response function](chapters/04-LIFE.md#r-same-carrier-need-not-fix-the-response).
- **Proved.** On the reading that what has significance from a standpoint is what lies within its center's reach along a relation, the continuation condition held de se is nothing lying outside each center's reach along it. Mutual dependence gives it, along being affected and along affecting, every continuation able to be affected, at some remove, by every other, read across all steps ([05](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace), [07 §4.9](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-mutual-dependence-gives-the-condition-de-se)); read in time, only from some step on, where each one's acts, from every step, come to bear on every other's later continuation ([07 §4.11](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-condition-in-time)).
- **Proved.** Where the condition holds, on an acyclic relation every standpoint has the same center (`«续无外则同一中心»`, [07 §4.5](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root)); on a net going forward in time this comes from the condition's asking that nothing other than the center lie at or before the center's step, and over what comes after two centers can meet it ([07 §4.11](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-one-center-bound-counts)).
- **Proved.** Where the condition holds de se, read across all steps, a self-concerned order, its own continuation read as what lies within its reach, ranks every widening above what it widens on a finite listing, and, with admission decided, every extension between two level paths whose admitted pairs fit in a finite list, so that their admissions end ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-self-concern-and-c-under-the-condition)); mutual dependence and this ranking compose directly, though no single theorem states the composite.
- **Proved.** Under the hypotheses of activity, support and descent of [07 §4.9](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-persisting-shows-of-an-order), the course of a line that persists without end never fits such a list, so there this route is silent. With significance free, the represented dynamics do not force the condition, and a perspectivist denies it without contradiction ([07 §1](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)).
- **Reading.** Also in 04 §8: four linked questions for a disposition (what mechanism produces it now, how it develops, what it does to continuation and reproduction, how its distribution arose), a reading [[D65](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md#d65-the-readings-of-the-eight-chapters)], its evolutionary explanation, its evaluation as right or wrong (which forms within becoming, in those who judge it, not in its explanation), its recognition and its actual preservation kept apart.
- **Conditional.** A result ranking the wider course above names its order among its theorem's hypotheses: self-concern with the condition held de se, an order meeting C (`Comparison`), or the practical commitments J1/J2 (`Priority`).
- **Argued.** The ethics seeks no authority outside becoming and offers itself without one, on the thesis of one relational unfolding; whether a reason formed within becoming could hold for every mind the work does not claim or settle ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-universal-practical-warrant)).
- **Argued.** Where relations run one way, concern for one's own continuation need not count those who depend on one (a witness: [07 §3.3](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-derived-extent-limits)), and seeing them is needed for a choice to answer to their loss, and not enough: reading a loss, counting it and ranking courses are separate relations ([07](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#does-not-show)); counting their loss is the step of invariance, deniable without contradiction ([07 §4.2](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#step)).
- **Argued.** T8: offered for life, a way of seeing, not a verdict: what each course draws on, from what renewal and at which grain, what ends and what goes on, and who bears it; 生生 is the continuation of life, AI within it [AI reading]. Its test, named and not designed, concerns overshoot of a renewal the readers themselves depend on; it does not test the offer's scope toward dependents whose loss never returns to them ([07 §8](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#theses)).
- **Open.** That a listing matches an actual course is not shown. That self-concern ranks the course of a line that persists without end is not shown: the route above is silent there. The five applications elsewhere that 05 lists: design programmes, not results; correspondence not established ([05 §11](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#joint-plans)).
- **Open.** That a life forms self-concern is not shown: going on is not yet such an order, in the proofs it is a hypothesis, and what the models show of persisting is a filter on what was chosen, not an order formed ([07 §4.9](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#what-persisting-shows)); how scope and order form within the dynamics is the work's open target ([08 §12](chapters/08-METHOD.md#open-items-ranked)).

These are tools for examining a course and questions for studying a disposition; their bearing on actual human development and well-being has not yet been established ([what 07 does not show](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#does-not-show)).

## Exits and design laws

The questions above say where a selection can fail to read and lock. These are what a rule or a reward could be built on or, where the aim is to keep a condition, what not to build on. Each is a law on its stated model, or a reading where marked, none shown to hold of an actual collective ([the answer in brief](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#the-answer-in-brief)).

**Exits, each under stated conditions** ([§2](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#2-exits-each-under-stated-conditions) gives each law's hypotheses and edge), the model in brackets (*any type*, *any relation*: proved for every type or relation; *World*: webs and flows with natural-number amounts and discrete steps; *counts*: two kinds in whole-number counts with no noise):

- **Read what lies beyond the share** (any type, without noise). A reading favouring at no loss above a bound unlocks the course after every reading step past it: sufficient, not shown necessary. Where the reading too is formed monotonically from the share, a change of it of any size is absorbed.
- **Read the loss in time** (counts, without noise; the rule's readers drawn from the selected, from a start favouring the shapers). A reading, the next step reading too, turns the course for good exactly where the late reading still favours the others at the share a step on; once it would favour the shapers, every later reading keeps the lock.
- **Shaping that brings nothing back to the shapers alone** (counts, without noise). With a positive cost and the shapers present, their share falls at every step.
- **Pool couplings** (any relation; World). A pool separates exactly what some member separates, and an act differing only beyond the union of their closures is selected alike by every group within it.
- **Restraint within the joint slack** (World). Where each drawer's own take is within the joint slack and the joint draw breaks a need, a balanced flow from a level as high that takes less by the excess and gives no less keeps it met; where the rest outruns the renewal at a need of one, no weight on a reading of the condition from the rest's end on selects a member's restraint.
- **Divide the take by exclusive footprints** (World). Under balance, with such a division at every step, the level never falls below its start; that an actual division is by exclusive footprints is not shown.
- **Spare all one waits on** (any type). A formation that spares all it waits on gives from step 1 whatever the start; where none does, total withholding, once reached, stays.
- **Count reviewers by independent causes** (pure core, no axiom: members and causes numbered, rosters as finite lists, failures Boolean, fault formulas and bounds supplied). Readiness (nonempty bounds on the causes that may occur together, every two leaving a reviewer working under both) is exactly a nonempty separated envelope of causes; in the computed cases, moving two of three reviewers under one cause makes readiness fail while all three names are kept.

The capture exits' locks are stated without noise. Drawn one member at a time, the critical mass's locks are left by drift, at a weight whose bound shrinks across the distance from the critical mass ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-drawn-shaping-drawn-one-at-a-time)), so over long horizons the window for reading the loss is not the only exit.

**One optimizer's reward** ([05 §7](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#optimum)): laws of optima, in natural amounts and discrete steps, not collective exits as they stand:

- **A term counted after the reward.** With room, a lexicographic order counting the beyond after what couples in [drains all the same](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in).
- **A reward for each step a need stays met.** Where the coupling covers the renewal forgone a step and exceeds the end, every optimum [keeps the dependence at every horizon](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-step-coupling-keeps-at-every-horizon); below that coupling a long horizon outweighs it.
- **Later draws weighted less.** Where the weighting of later draws falls faster than the renewal, every optimum [ends its own stock at the first step](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-weighting-faster-than-renewal-ends-the-base).

**Six conditions for enough of the situation**, all at once, for a given collective and condition [AI reading, [D45](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md#d45-一个说明中的读法--the-readings-in-one-account)] ([§6](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#6-what-enough-situationality-would-have-to-mean)): *coupling* (the drawn level and the need of what depends on it both couple into a selecting pool, by level and not by threshold), *response* (the pool's members respond), *time* (before the regime's point of no return, and before the share reaches the step from which the late reading favours the shapers), *resolution* (the feedback separates the differences that matter), *pace* (a slow rule reads the fast level over a long enough stretch) and *structure* (the rest does not outrun the renewal). Observation is the first, necessary and not sufficient.

## What each field already has, and what the account adds

Shortened from [08's known results](chapters/08-METHOD.md#known-results), an AI review against the literature it knew, not exhaustive: each central result is near-definitional or a known result restated, none of them new mathematics, and each statement added by joining a field's framework to the account's premises is known in a neighbouring field. What it found new: a premise ledger, each premise with witnesses on both sides; the scope and order the ranking results assume, made clear, which it called "a clarified ethics, not a grounded one", how those form within becoming not yet established; and one vocabulary for conservation, selection limited by observation and comparison of opportunity sets, which it calls a framing. "Claimed beyond the field" marks one of [08 §9's unifications](chapters/08-METHOD.md#what-the-unifications-claim): read as a correspondence, a unification adds no theorem and changes no verdict of 08 §8; read as a thesis, it forbids what the source fields leave open.

- **Alignment of AI systems.** 08 credits the field with the result: the proxy problem in its blindness to what a proxy does not register, not its regressional or causal kinds (Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell 2020); capture through the rule read as observability with the reader inside the plant, the corrupted reward channel and closed-loop identifiability, the first and last control theory's; corrigibility, the off-switch game's waiting and the incentive deceptive alignment names, near-definitional. Added: for corrigibility, the exact condition at every loss of the routes and at every value; for the waiting, one threshold; for deceptive alignment, where the stages cannot be told apart, the exact threshold. Claimed beyond the field, known in substance as Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell's Theorem 1 and their remedy of a proxy re-read: overshoot, externality and Goodhart's law as one law of the optimum under room, one law across domains conjectured (T3); in simulation, before T3 was worded, the draw against margin forecast an exact best response's side effects (8 of 8) and failed for tabular learners (80 of 151; MQ1).
- **People and AI together.** Restated: capture through the rule, as for alignment; shaping selected among groups, in counts with Hamilton's rule (Hamilton 1964, 1975). 08 lists no prior result for who reads the loss, and when, or for obedience at a mutual best response, and names nothing added there.
- **Coordination among people.** 08 credits the field with the result: joint excess is the tragedy of the commons, near-definitional; the critical mass drawn one member at a time restates the Moran process of a game and stochastic stability (Nowak and others 2004; Kandori, Mailath and Rob 1993; Young 1993). Added: joint excess under the gate, where steps of joint excess from below the renewal's reach end every dependent at that need, whatever each drawer's own draw, known in population ecology as critical depensation.
- **Human development, and 生生, the continuation of life.** Restated: how two courses differ in what can form is near-definitional, its closest prior result opportunity sets (Pattanaik and Xu 1990). Added: under balance with requirements as thresholds, a part narrows what another can form only by a net draw beyond that formation's margin, a weak third part.
- **Wisdom traditions.** 08 credits no tradition with a result and names nothing added for one; how the traditions map onto the account is [the twenty-first review's §5](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-TWENTY-FIRST-REVIEW.md#5-how-the-traditions-and-the-sciences-map-onto-it), an AI review. Of the laws set beside the texts, 08 credits regulation to deadbeat control and requisite variety (Dahleh and Pearson 1987; Ashby 1956), and learning and identification, restated constructively, to Hume; to Fine and Wilf (1965); to Moore (1956); and to Gold (1967). Which laws those are is this page's reading.
- **The sciences.** 08 credits the sciences with the results: the commons; harvesting under depensation and the fold (Clark 1976; May 1977); regulation; Perron–Frobenius; evolution's classical laws, in counts; the evidence bound of variational Bayes. Added: third parts, each joining a framework that lacks conservation, renewal over time or a threshold to the account's premises, such as Price's equation and Fisher's theorem joined to the flow, known as the Malthusian bound. Claimed beyond the field, known in substance as supervisory control (Ramadge and Wonham 1987), under partial observation (Lin and Wonham 1988; Cieslak, Desclaux, Fawaz and Varaiya 1988): reward, training, natural selection, prices and votes as one selection through couplings, one law across domains conjectured (T3).
