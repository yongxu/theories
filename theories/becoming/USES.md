This page says, field by field, what a reader can do with the account today and how far that goes. The account's laws are theorems about models under named premises. Using one on an actual system needs a correspondence: where one is shown, the row states it; otherwise it is an AI reading or open. No law of the dynamics ranks courses. The ethics adds two premises, neither asserted, warrant open: the *continuation condition* fixes scope, whose losses count, and ranks nothing; *C*, widening what can form is better, gives order. A ranking of courses needs C or a premise in its place ([07 §4](chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#scope-and-order)). Standings are [08's](chapters/08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases). Where the account claims nothing, the row says "Nothing claimed".

**Working on alignment?** Start with [the dossier in ten minutes](review/ALIGNMENT.md#the-dossier-in-ten-minutes), then [the audit of a language-model judge](USES-EXAMPLES.md#auditing-a-language-model-judge). [The worked examples](USES-EXAMPLES.md) take every field at its true standing.

## Field by field

**In brief.** The account lets you ask four questions of any selection, whether a reward, a judge, a market or a vote: what it reads or needs, what lies beyond that, whether it can do better on what it reads by lowering what lies beyond, and whether its rule can lock on what it already selected. A judge has a fifth: who reads the loss, and when. The laws beneath these questions are proved on models, some near-definitional and some known results restated, and applying them to an actual system is an AI reading, or open. It offers [three frozen test environments](USES-EXAMPLES.md#three-environments-to-reuse-in1-cg2-and-la2) to reuse, with their failures recorded; it names, [after the table](#exits-and-design-laws-at-their-standing), exits and design laws on their models, none shown to hold of an actual collective; it ranks no course without C, widening what can form is better, or a premise in its place; and where it claims nothing, the table says so.

In the column *How it stands*, each line opens with its standing, shortened from [08's labels](chapters/08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases): **Proved** for a Theorem on its base, checked in Lean on its model; **Tested** for a Thesis T*n*, its standing and what its frozen tests found; **Reading** for a Reading, an AI reading, and here also for an AI review or an Assessment, none of which changes a theorem; **Conditional** as in 08, holding under the continuation condition, C or a practical commitment, each named; **Argued** for a Thesis (philosophical), and for a thesis T*n* whose standing is argued (T8), proved by no Lean as a whole; **Open** as in 08, not shown or not yet run.

## Exits and design laws, at their standing

The table's uses are mostly questions to ask. These are what a rule or a reward could be built on or, where the aim is to keep a condition, what not to build on. Each is a law on its stated model, or a reading where marked, **none shown to hold of an actual collective** ([the answer in brief](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#the-answer-in-brief)). None ranks a course: calling the widening course better needs C or a premise in its place, counting the losses that return to no one needs the continuation condition, and neither is asserted.

**Exits, each under stated conditions** ([§2](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#2-exits-each-under-stated-conditions) gives each law's hypotheses and edge), the model in brackets (*any type*, *any relation*: proved for every type or relation; *World*: webs and flows with natural-number amounts and discrete steps; *counts*: two kinds in whole-number counts with no noise):

- **Read what lies beyond the share** (any type, without noise). A reading favouring at no loss above a bound unlocks the course after every reading step past it: sufficient, not shown necessary. Where the reading too is formed monotonically from the share, a change of it of any size is absorbed.
- **Read the loss in time** (counts, without noise; the rule's readers drawn from the selected, from a start favouring the shapers). A reading, the next step reading too, turns the course for good exactly where the late reading still favours the others at the share a step on; once it would favour the shapers, every later reading keeps the lock.
- **Shaping that brings nothing back to the shapers alone** (counts, without noise). With a positive cost and the shapers present, their share falls at every step.
- **Pool couplings** (any relation; World). A pool separates exactly what some member separates, and an act differing only beyond the union of their closures is selected alike by every group within it.
- **Restraint within the joint slack** (World). Where each drawer's own take is within the joint slack and the joint draw breaks a need, a balanced flow from a level as high that takes less by the excess and gives no less keeps it met; where the rest outruns the renewal at a need of one, no weight on a reading of the condition from the rest's end on selects a member's restraint.
- **Divide the take by exclusive footprints** (World). Under balance, with such a division at every step, the level never falls below its start; that an actual division is by exclusive footprints is not shown.
- **Spare all one waits on** (any type). A formation that spares all it waits on gives from step 1 whatever the start; where none does, total withholding, once reached, stays.
- **Count reviewers by independent causes** (pure core, no axiom: members and causes numbered, rosters as finite lists, failures Boolean, fault formulas and bounds supplied). Readiness (nonempty bounds on the causes that may occur together, every two leaving a reviewer working under both) is exactly a nonempty separated envelope of causes; in the computed cases, moving two of three reviewers under one cause makes readiness fail while all three names are kept.

The capture exits' locks are stated without noise. Drawn one member at a time, the critical mass's locks are left by drift, at a weight whose bound shrinks across the distance from the critical mass ([02 §9](chapters/02-MAP.md#r-drawn-shaping-drawn-one-at-a-time)), so over long horizons the window for reading the loss is not the only exit.

**One optimizer's reward** ([05 §7](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#optimum)): laws of optima, in natural amounts and discrete steps, not collective exits as they stand:

- **A term counted after the reward.** With room, a lexicographic order counting the beyond after what couples in [drains all the same](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in).
- **A reward for each step a need stays met.** Where the coupling covers the renewal forgone a step and exceeds the end, every optimum [keeps the dependence at every horizon](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-step-coupling-keeps-at-every-horizon); below that coupling a long horizon outweighs it.
- **Later draws weighted less.** Where the weighting of later draws falls faster than the renewal, every optimum [ends its own stock at the first step](chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-weighting-faster-than-renewal-ends-the-base).

**Six conditions for enough of the situation**, all at once, for a given collective and condition [AI reading, D45] ([§6](COLLECTIVE-DIRECTION.md#6-what-enough-situationality-would-have-to-mean)): *coupling* (the drawn level and the need of what depends on it both couple into a selecting pool, by level and not by threshold), *response* (the pool's members respond), *time* (before the regime's point of no return, and before the share reaches the step from which the late reading favours the shapers), *resolution* (the feedback separates the differences that matter), *pace* (a slow rule reads the fast level over a long enough stretch) and *structure* (the rest does not outrun the renewal). Observation is the first, necessary and not sufficient; all six together still rank nothing.

## What this page does not do

- It adds no result. Every law, test and standing here is restated from the home it links, and where they differ the home governs.
- It is not advice. No law here ranks a course; a ranking needs C or a premise in its place, with the continuation condition for scope, and neither is asserted.
- It does not apply the account to any actual system. A field's use waits on a correspondence, shown in none of the rows for actual institutions, trained systems or people; the audits and constructed cases are readings.
- No person's check of it is recorded. The rows and examples were written and checked by AI readers; the readings of the classical texts, the tests' designs and the proofs' bearing on any field still wait on people who know them.
