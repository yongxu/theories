**Thesis.** Whatever goes on is conditioned by what came before it and stays alike to it in some respect. The chapter takes one relation, read as condition (缘, *yuán*), and one class, read as sameness (类, *lèi*), and shows what their meet, continuation (续, *xù*), carries: beginning, abiding, altering and ending are positions relative to a class, none standing alone; under a class constant for the relation no continuation ends; the core (核, *hé*) of a class, the greatest symmetric relation within it that the relation never tells apart, always exists; renewal is a line started by another, and at a coarser grain a pattern continues while its parts begin and end. Processes and the World model are models of this grammar. Claimed: these laws, for every carrier, relation and class or on a named model; that the reading explains how things go on is argued from them, not proved. Open: that every becoming has a faithful representation in the grammar; processes' is shown both ways by their moves' relations (§8.1), and the World's both ways for its relations, failing on its amounts (§8.6).

**Within the whole map.** The account is one map, for alignment and of wisdom ([glossary](../../../GLOSSARY.md#the-map)), and this chapter gives its common terms, the grammar of relation, condition, class and continuation. In them a selection's lock, from a locked start and under its premises, is a line under the class of being locked or not that never ends, each of its steps altering the loss it does not read ([02 §9](02-MAP.md#r-rules-that-change-themselves-in-the-grammar)), and five groups of definitions from eight classical texts, written in them, hold for every carrier and relation ([06](06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-classical-definitions-in-the-grammar)).

**Where this chapter sits.** Its grammar, [`Becoming.Grammar`][Grammar], imports no other module, and the chapter hands its grammar, classes and models to all the others: first to [the map](02-MAP.md), which reads renewal at two grains as generation, keeping and degeneration, and to [knowing](03-KNOWING.md), which takes the core and likeness into testing within the net. The chapters are numbered for reading, not in an order of proof: about half of this chapter's results are proved with results whose homes are later chapters, most often the map's ([the dependencies](../../../DEPENDENCIES.md)). Every later chapter reads its subject as continuation relative to some class: which relation, which class, at which grain, constant or not. Of the central results a few are cases of the root, more use its laws in their proofs and most use none ([08 §7](08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)).

**Bases.** **Theorem** (root): every carrier, relation and class, in [`Becoming.Grammar`][Grammar] or a grammar module; (any process): every process, read in the grammar by its moves' relations ([§8.1](#r-processes-represented-both-ways)), its statement in the process's vocabulary; (any type): every type, not yet in the grammar's vocabulary; (the steps): every determination of the natural-number steps; (World): the World model, its premises named; (periphery): with Mathlib, its axioms reported; a witness is one constructed example, labelled as one. A **Calibration** needs an omniscience principle and says what the need says about the unfolding; a **Reading** changes no theorem ([08](08-METHOD.md)). Every pure-core declaration named here has an empty axiom report, which concerns logic only; the real assumptions lie in the modelling commitments ([§10](#modelling-commitments)). Where the chapter touches evaluation (§8.3, §9, §9.9), evaluation forms within the unfolding, and a result that ranks courses states what its theorem takes as hypotheses: the continuation condition (whose losses count), self-concern, or C (`Comparison`, a requirement on an order: with every claim counted as living, that it rank each course above every course it widens, widening being keeping all another course keeps and letting more form; in general, above every course it extends by a claim counted as living) ([07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)).

**What is claimed.** The grammar is a relational calculus: its general theorems hold of every carrier, relation and class, and its witnesses are single constructed models. That reading a subject as continuation relative to a class *explains* how it goes on is the theory's claim, argued, not a theorem of the grammar: as a language it forbids nothing, and as an explanation, on a first count, a rival with no word for continuation ties it ([08 §9](08-METHOD.md#what-would-defeat-the-reading)). Three things are kept apart: one kind of relational expression, which the grammar gives; processes and the World model read in the grammar, processes both ways by their moves' relations (§8.1) and the World by instance theorems and a representation theorem both ways for its relations, not for its amounts (§8.6); and a faithful representation of every becoming, and of the whole theory, in the grammar, which is open ([Open](#open)).

**Not assumed.** That becoming is good; inevitable improvement or universal growth; a fixed common utility; independent owners of value; that every change is generation; a definite global causal order at every interface (the laws of one step, totals, counts and recurrence use only the order of change, rates being relative to the reading's unit, §9.6). Degeneration, ending and mixed change belong to the same unfolding and are not renamed generation.

<a id="one-kind-of-symbol"></a>
## 1. One kind of symbol

The grammar has one kind of symbol, a relation between occurrences, and builds everything else from relations by a few operations; every theorem is stated for every carrier and relation, so no frame or kind of thing is chosen before the relations. Nor is a time: its order is the reach of the condition relation, a strict order where that relation has no cycle (below). Time and the four positions (§3), whose statements use no equality, tell two occurrences alike in every relation apart only by equality on the carrier, a commitment of the model: a stationary process has two moments alike in every relation, which nothing but that equality tells apart.

**Definition.** A relation (际, *jì*) on a carrier `α` is a two-place predicate, `«际» α := α → α → Prop`, with these operations:

| Name | Reading |
|---|---|
| 而 | composition: first `R`, then `S` (`R;S`) |
| 逆, 合, 非 | converse (`R˘`), meet, complement |
| 则, 谓 | inclusion; pointwise equivalence, the form of a definition |
| 一, 静 | the full relation ("the one"); identity, the change that stays in place |
| 及 | reach: the transitive closure, each witness a finite chain |
| 无环 | without cycles: nothing reaches itself; a condition a net may meet, not a claim that it does (below) |

The carrier is a parameter of the statements, not a claim that its elements exist apart from their relations: a theorem says nothing of an element beyond how it relates. Equality on the carrier is a commitment of the model, and the statements that use it (identity as a class, the two-block class of a single condition, fission, the flame) say so; the merge uses none. Every result is pointwise, so no extensionality enters, and the grammar imports only Lean's prelude.

**Definition** (the main reading). `R x y` is read as 缘: `x` is a condition of `y`, among what `y`'s coming about comes from. Conditions may be many-to-many, mutual and simultaneous. Each model declares how its net is read: in one history an occurrence's successors are its co-occurring effects; in a net of possibilities they are alternatives, with a conflict relation beside the net. Constancy and the core (§4) are the same formulas under both; a test by what would happen "after the window opens" has sense only in a net of possibilities ([03](03-KNOWING.md#r-testing-within-the-net)).

**Theorem** (root). Without cycles, reach is a strict order: irreflexive, transitive and asymmetric (`«无环则及为序»`). Where two occurrences condition each other, they are co-present and reach is no longer a strict order (`«相由则俱有»`); co-presence (俱有, mutual reach) is symmetric, transitive and reflexive on what it relates, one cluster inside which precedence is not defined (`«俱有为一念»`, [GrammarCandidates][GrammarCandidates]). That every condition strictly precedes what it conditions holds exactly where the net has no cycle (`«缘先果后当且仅当无环»`, [06](06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-candidate-theorems-of-the-corpus)).

Without cycles is a condition a net may meet, not a claim that the unfolding meets it: the laws that use it take it as a hypothesis. Whether a net with feedback meets it depends on what the model takes as an occurrence. Where each occurrence is dated, one per step, feedback makes no cycle: in a heated room the temperature at one step is a condition of the thermostat's reading at the next, the reading of the heating, and the heating of the temperature later, each another occurrence. What comes round is the class: the later temperature continues the earlier under the class of this room's temperature, so a feedback loop is a line returning to a class, not an occurrence reaching itself. The loops of [the map](02-MAP.md#r-how-a-carried-difference-grows), where a loop upstream and only a loop makes a difference run away, are of this kind: couplings between conditions across steps, on the World model. Where a model takes states as its occurrences, as the warm room that goes on as it is in [knowing](03-KNOWING.md#r-testing-within-the-net) does, or two occurrences condition each other with no step between them, the net has a cycle, and the laws above read it as co-presence: one cluster within which precedence is not defined, not something reaching back into its own past.

<a id="r-precedence-and-its-links"></a>
**Theorem** (root; orders on the numbers, and witnesses). *Precedence and its links.* The links of a relation, causal set theory's term, are the pairs it relates with nothing it relates between (`CausalGrowth.links`). A link of the reach `«及» R` is a step of `R`, since a chain of two or more steps has a point between (`CausalGrowth.link_of_reach`), so every net whose reach is a relation contains that relation's links, on any carrier (`CausalGrowth.links_least`). Where each element is preceded only by smaller labels, a natural labelling of the numbers, and precedence is decidable, every pair is reached by links, with no finiteness (`CausalGrowth.reach_links`, `CausalGrowth.nat_reach_links`), and with transitivity precedence is exactly the reach of its links (`CausalGrowth.reach_links_iff`): on the finite orders causal-set growth produces ([07 §2](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-can-be-born-next); `CausalGrowth.ordered_reach_links_iff`) the links are the least net whose reach is precedence. Deciding what lies between two elements suffices (`CausalGrowth.reach_links_of_decided`) and cannot be dropped: that every pair of every causal set is reached by its links is excluded middle (`CausalGrowth.reach_links_all_iff_em`). Where an interval is infinite the links can fail: on `ω + 1`, the numbers with a top above them all, `0` precedes the top and no chain of links reaches it (`OmegaPlusOne.not_reach_top`). Each law is near-definitional, a witness or a calibration ([record](../results/records/precedence-and-its-links.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. Time as the reach of the condition relation (above) holds with the links as the conditions on naturally labelled orders with decided precedence, the finite orders causal set theory grows among them, and there the links are the least conditions it needs; telling a direct condition from a longer chain is deciding whether anything lies between. Where an interval is infinite, precedence is still the reach of a condition relation, itself, since a transitive relation is its own reach, but it need not be the reach of its links ([study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue13-causal-sets/NOTE.md)).

<a id="r-the-present-on-causal-set-growth"></a>
**Theorem** (causal-set growth, and witnesses). *The present on causal-set growth.* The grammar has no relation of presence in time ([Open](#open)); causal-set growth ([07 §2](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-can-be-born-next)) is a model in which one can be read and the question asked exactly. Its labels, the order of births, are taken to carry no physical meaning (discrete general covariance, Rideout and Sorkin 2000), a premise of the model. The renumberings of a causal set keeping precedence, its natural labellings (`Presence.NaturalLabelling`), are exactly the orders of birth of the courses from the empty set growing a configuration of its kind (`Presence.natural_iff_course`), and births of unrelated elements commute, and only they (`Presence.rearranges_iff_natural`, an instance of the core's theorem of Mazurkiewicz). What a course has born by a stage is a stem, and every stem with decided membership is what some course bears first (`Presence.born_stem`, `Presence.stem_born_first`). With precedence decided, an element is born before another on every course exactly where it precedes it (`Presence.prec_iff_every`), so what is born no later than an element on every course is its past with itself, the largest relation every course respects (`Presence.past_every_iff`, `Presence.past_largest`); with precedence weakly decided, it is born before it on some course exactly where the two differ and the other does not precede it (`Presence.before_some_iff`), so no order of birth of unrelated elements is common to all courses (`Presence.either_order`). Read the present at a stage as the maximal elements of what a course has born, where the grammar's ending holds on what is born (`Presence.MaximalBorn`, `Presence.maximalBorn_iff_ends`). It is an antichain (`Presence.maximalBorn_unrelated`), and with precedence weakly decided every antichain given by a test is one (`Presence.antichain_present`); two elements are present together on some course exactly where neither precedes the other, precedence weakly decided, and on every course exactly where they have the same future, precedence decided (`Presence.together_some_iff`, `Presence.together_every_iff`), which is not co-presence, mutual reach. A condition is present at the birth of what it conditions where nothing it precedes is born before (`Presence.PresentAt`): on some course exactly where it is a link below it, precedence weakly decided (`Presence.present_some_iff_link`), and on every course exactly where that is the one element it is linked to, with no decision (`Presence.present_every_iff_only_link`). Asked of every causal set, each calibrated decision is exactly its principle: excluded middle for the decided forms of born before, present together and the least element above on every course, weak excluded middle for the laws on some course (`Presence.every_iff_em`, `Presence.past_every_iff_em`, `Presence.together_every_iff_em`, `Presence.present_least_iff_em`, `Presence.either_order_iff_weakly`, `Presence.present_some_iff_weakly`). On three elements, `0` below `1` and `2`, `0` is present at `2`'s birth on one course and not on another, and the present after two births is exactly `1` on one course and exactly `2` on another (`Presence.Vee.present_swap`, `Presence.Vee.not_present_identity`, `Presence.Vee.maximal_after_two_identity`, `Presence.Vee.maximal_after_two_swap`). On Wüthrich and Callender's four elements their relation, kept by every automorphism, is not what every course has born by a birth, and on those elements it is the past with the pair present together on every course (`Presence.Diamond.kept_not_agreed`, `Presence.Diamond.pastOrPair_iff_together`); elsewhere that join need not be transitive (`Presence.SameFuture.pastOrTogether_not_trans`). Each main law is also stated on the courses of the growth themselves, the runs of its process from the empty set (`Presence.every_course_iff`, `Presence.prec_iff_every_course`, `Presence.present_some_course`, `Presence.present_every_course_only_link`). Each law is near-definitional, a lemma, a witness or a calibration ([record](../results/records/the-present-on-causal-set-growth.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. In this model the open question on presence in time has an answer for each reading of presence. Read as being in the configuration, a condition once born is still there when what it conditions is born, on every course, since the growth holds its past; read as Dowker's present, the birth, or as a maximal antichain of the configuration, it is not, whatever the course. Read as being among the maximal elements of what has been born, the answer varies with the course, between two bounds that do not: present on some course exactly at a link, on every course exactly at the one link. Whatever every course keeps, or some course has, is defined from precedence, since the natural labellings are (`Presence.natural_congr`); a relation of presence read on one course, a foliation, is a choice of course. So, given covariance, a premise of the model, the grammar's condition relation defines every relation of presence the growth keeps, and its lack of a primitive relation of presence in time loses no covariant content here. On the growth the continuation condition's scope ([07 §3](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#condition)) is the same on every course where a thing's future is read as what it conditions, under any class given on the elements, and a choice of course where it is read as what comes after a present stage, or under a class given on the order of births; this bears on scope, not on order, which stays with the requirement on an order a ranking result takes, self-concern or C ([study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/ue14-the-present/NOTE.md)).

The corpus's own definitions (化, 成坏, 同, 复, 通) are written in this grammar in [the classical chapter](06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-classical-definitions-in-the-grammar).

<a id="class-and-continuation"></a>
## 2. Class and continuation

Whether two moments of fire are "the same" depends on the sameness asked: the same burning, wood or shape of flame. The grammar does not choose: it names the class and says everything relative to it.

**Definition.** A class (类) is a reflexive, symmetric and transitive relation (`«类»`); the full relation is one (`«一为类»`). **Reading** [AI reading, D69]. The class is plural. The 墨经 names four samenesses (重, 體, 合, 類), each a class once the whole, place or respect it relies on is fixed; 類同, 「有以同，類同也」 ([《墨经·经说上》][Mojing]), need not be transitive until its respect is fixed (`«同于不必传递»`, a witness; [06, §二](06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#corpus)).

**Definition.** Continuation (续): `y` continues `x` under the class `E` where `x` is a condition of `y` and the two are alike under `E`, `«续» R E := R ⊓ E`; the criterion is `E` and no other choice enters. A line (相续) is the reach of continuation, and a family (同族) its equivalence closure, every occurrence joined to another by continuation read either way. The line from `x` never ends (不穷) where every occurrence it reaches has a successor; that an endless chain then exists is a further step, which takes dependent choice.

<a id="r-continuation-relative-to-a-class"></a>
**Theorem** (root). For every carrier and relation, continuation is contained in 缘 and every line runs along the reach of 缘 (`«续含于缘»`, `«相续含于及»`). Under the full class continuation is 缘 itself (`«一之续»`), and under a coarser class every continuation remains one (`«粗化»`). A line under a class stays within it, its ends alike (`«线内同类»`), and the family is a class (`«同族为类»`). These are the opening laws of [continuation relative to a class](../results/records/continuation-relative-to-a-class.md); its further laws follow in §§3–6 and its instances on the World model in [§8.6](#instances). Its only hypotheses: each class an equivalence, and equality on the carrier where identity serves as a class.

Continuation is a relation, not an identity. Three theorems (root) show what that leaves open:

- *Every single condition is a continuation under some class*, the class with two blocks, its two ends and the rest (`«任一缘可为续»`): containment in 缘 is necessary only, and to keep the conditions that maintain something a sub-relation of 缘 is named.
- *A merge*: two conditions of one occurrence, each alike to that occurrence under the class, are both continued by it (`«蜡烛之合»`), as one candle's heat is among the conditions of another's first flame.
- *Fission* (a witness): where one occurrence is continued by two that go no further, sameness read along lines either way is not transitive (`«分叉»`). The family gathers the branches; it is coarser than identity.

Continuation is not defined through the core (§5): its criterion is the class, and the core is asked of a line.

<a id="four-marks"></a>
## 3. The four marks

An occurrence in a line either continues something or not, and is either continued or not. Those two questions fix its position, and the answers depend on the class.

**Definition.** Under a class `E`, an occurrence begins (生) where it continues nothing, ends (亡) where nothing continues it, and abides (住) where something precedes and something follows it. `y` alters (异) from `x` where it continues `x` under `E` while another class `F` (in use, a finer one) tells the two apart. An occurrence with neither predecessor nor successor is a flash: it begins and ends.

**Theorem** (root). Beginning and abiding exclude each other, and so do abiding and ending (`«生住不并»`, `«住亡不并»`). Under the full class an occurrence begins exactly where nothing conditions it (`«一之生»`).

**Theorem** (root). *No mark stands by itself.* In a net where nothing conditions itself, an occurrence with a condition and a consequence abides under the one and, under identity, both begins and ends (`«四相无自立»`): one occurrence holds different positions at once, each relative to a class. **Theorem** (World). A formation arising across one step and ceasing across the next is, at the step between, abiding under the one and beginning and ending under identity and under activity (`Web.flash_positions`).

**Theorem** (root). *Coarsening.* What ends under a coarser class ends under the finer, and what abides under the finer abides under the coarser (`«亡于粗则亡于细»`, `«住于细则住于粗»`); what begins under the coarser begins under the finer (`«生于粗则生于细»`), and a beginning under the finer continued under the coarser is an alteration (`«生于细而续于粗则异»`). The converse fails (a witness): an occurrence can end under the finer class and not end under the coarser (`«亡于细者不必亡于粗»`). This is the shape of a fire passed on: the wood ends, the fire goes on.

**Theorem** (root). *A change between the ends has a place.* For a transitive relation `K` (a class is one), where every step of continuation either keeps `K` or carries a mark `P`, a line whose ends `K` tells apart (`K` fails from its start to its end) has a step carrying the mark, placed on the line between them (`«两端分则中有标»`).

**Calibration** (root). That every occurrence, in every model, either begins or has a predecessor is exactly excluded middle (`«四相之定当且仅当排中»`): whether an occurrence has a predecessor under a class, and so begins, is decided only where going on is. The marks are not a partition even where this is decided: a flash begins and ends, and alteration relates two occurrences under two classes. On the steps, what holds and ceases at no later step holds on wherever each later step's position is decided (`persists_of_positions`).

<a id="r-negation-change-and-stable-cases"></a>
**Theorem** (the steps). For every determination `p`, what ceases to be is the coming to be of its negation, and what comes to be is the ceasing of its negation, with no premise (`CeasesToBe.not_comes`); read back, from the negation's change to the determination's, it holds wherever the determination is stable (double negation eliminable) where it is read (`ComesToBe.of_not`). **Theorem** (any type). A conclusion following both from a proposition and from its failure holds wherever the conclusion is stable, the proposition left undecided (`Decided.cases_stable`); that negations and equations of numbers are stable is a fact of the logic, not a theorem here. **Theorem** (World). A formation ceasing is the beginning, at the next step, of a line of inactivity (`Web.ceases_iff_inactive_sheng`; [record](../results/records/negation-change-and-stable-cases.md)).

<a id="constancy-and-core"></a>
## 4. Constancy and the core

Some classes are carried by the relation: whatever one member becomes, a member alike to it becomes something alike. Others are not: occurrences alike now go to unlike futures, and the line has a regularity finer than its class.

**Definition.** `E` is constant under `R` (有常) where `E˘;R ⊆ R;E˘`: where `u` is alike to `u'` and `u` conditions `v`, `u'` conditions some `v'` alike to `v` (`«有常之逐点»`).

**Theorem** (root). *Constancy never lets a continuation end.* Under a relation `E` constant for `R`, symmetric or not, whatever continues something is itself continued (`«有常则续不尽»`). So under a constant class a line never ends after its first step (`«有常则相续不尽»`), and a class under which some line ends is not constant (`«有亡之续则不有常»`). Whether a line that ends has its own regularity cannot be asked as whether its class is constant: it never is.

**Definition.** The core (核) of `E` under `R` relates two occurrences where some symmetric relation inside `E` that is constant under `R` relates them.

**Theorem** (root). *The root theorem.* The core lies inside `E`, is constant and symmetric, and contains every symmetric constant relation inside `E`: it is the greatest one (`«根»`). The core of a class is a class (`«核为类»`), and a finer class has a finer core (`«核之单调»`). A class is constant exactly where it lies inside its own core (`«类有常当且仅当含于核»`). So the core is the greatest matching relation within the class, and it always exists.

**Reading** [AI reading, D65]. The core is what continues of its own, the regularity a line carries by itself. The reading interprets indistinguishability under matched conditioning; it asserts no causal independence of an occurrence and no mechanism of self-maintenance.

**Theorem** (root). *Closure.* For a class `E`, constancy is exactly that `R` read through the class adds nothing, `E;R;E ⊆ R;E`: what an occurrence's class conditions is fixed by the occurrence's own conditions, without looking at the rest of its class (`«闭合当且仅当有常»`). The relation `E;R;E` keeps the conditions inside a class; the coarse net of §6 drops them. For a class this is bisimulation equivalence, with the lumpability of Markov chains and computational closure as analogues not proved here.

**Definition.** The core read both ways (位核) relates two occurrences where some symmetric relation inside `E`, constant under `R` and under its converse, relates them: nothing that conditions them, and nothing they condition, tells them apart.

**Theorem** (root). *Beginning, abiding and ending are read off the two-way core.* The two-way core is the greatest symmetric relation inside `E` constant both ways (`«位根»`); it lies inside the core (`«位核含于核»`) and inside the core of the converse net (`«位核含于逆核»`). For a class, ending is not told apart by the core (`«亡于核下不变»`) and, the mirror, beginning not by the core of the converse net (`«生于逆核下不变»`), so neither by the two-way core (`«生于位核下不变»`): what shares a position under the two-way core shares beginning and ending (`«位同则生亡同»`), and abiding too (`«位同则住同»`). The forward core can tell beginnings apart, and the core of the converse net relates pairs that neither the core nor the two-way core relates ([06](06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-conditional-laws-of-the-corpus)). Altering is a relation between two occurrences with a finer class, not a mark of one occurrence, and is not read off the core. A position is fixed by all its relations, before as well as after.

**Theorem** (root; with a witness). *The core and 理.* Let `E` be a class. Where the step's successor is fixed (`«定后»`), the core of `E` is a class inside `E` that the step goes alike across (`«定后则类之核同行»`; going alike, `«同行»`, is [§7](#r-shared-laws-on-the-grammar)'s): a 理 in [knowing's](03-KNOWING.md#li) sense, read relationally. Where every occurrence has a successor, every class inside `E` that the step goes alike across lies inside the core (`«遍后则其内同行之类含于核»`), so with both the core is the coarsest such class, the 理 of `E` (`«定后遍后则核为其内最粗同行之类»`). Off a fixed successor the core need not go alike: where one occurrence steps to two, the identity is a class whose core relates that occurrence to itself and not its two successors to each other, so the step does not go alike across it (`«后不定则核可不同行»`).

<a id="r-a-regularity-kept-and-changed"></a>
**Theorem** (root). *A kept regularity can always be kept, and a line ends only by a step that leaves it.* From a step continuing under the core of `E`, every occurrence its line under the core reaches has a successor under the core (`«守核则不穷»`), and a line along which the core is kept leaves its last occurrence continued under `E` (`«守核之线不亡»`). A step into an end under `E` leaves the core, for every relation `E` (`«亡前核变»`), and so does the last step of every line under `E` that ends (`«有亡之线末步离核»`); where `E` is a class, the two ends of such a line lie in different classes of the core (`«有亡之线两端核异»`). These are corollaries of the root theorem and of constancy never letting a continuation end; what they add is where the core is left, at the last step, found with no decision. So what goes on for a while and then ends is a class that does not hold by itself, whose line passes from one class of the core to another before it ends, while the core always admits continuation ([`Becoming.GrammarRegularity`][GrammarRegularity]; [record](../results/records/a-regularity-kept-and-changed.md)). In a branching net a successor under `E` can leave the core where another keeps it; along a determined law the next step keeps it. Where a longer line first leaves its start's class of the core is placed by `«两端分则中有标»` only where the core is decided at each step.

**Theorem** (root; a determined law). Along a determined law's step a line under any relation never ends exactly where every step of the orbit continues under it (`Law.unending_iff_steps`), and under a class such a line keeps every configuration of the orbit alike with its start (`Law.unending_alike`).

On the models the core is the familiar invariant. Along the steps, the core of "alike in `p`" relates two steps exactly where `p` agrees at every pair of steps equally far after them (`core_iff_same_course`). For a determined law read through a map, likeness by bisimulation is the core of the value class (`Law.alike_iff_core`), the course through the map that is [knowing's 文](03-KNOWING.md#r-wen-in-the-grammar); the coarsest relation keeping the values that the law keeps is the root theorem read on a law ([knowing](03-KNOWING.md#r-the-split-that-makes-sense)). Finite stages approximate the core from above, and what a stage tells apart is not in it ([03](03-KNOWING.md#r-testing-within-the-net)).

<a id="fire-and-fuel"></a>
## 5. Fire and fuel

A flame of three moments ends in ash. Under "burning or not" each moment continues the one before, but constancy asks alike moments to have alike futures, and these do not: the first has two moments of burning left, the second one, the third none. A finer description fixes the course. Which description carries a flame's regularity one flame does not decide; two flames side by side decide it against one alternative.

**Theorem** (root; witness). In the flame `f₀ → f₁ → f₂ → ash`, four elements whose equality is the model's, the moments continue one another under "burning or not", and under that class's core no occurrence continues another, so read through the core the flame would not continue at all (`«以核为判据则火无续»`).

**Definition.** Holding by itself (自持) is a constant class; mutual need (相须): the joint class of `E₁` and `E₂` is constant and neither alone is; leaning-on (因于): the joint class and `E₂` are constant and `E₁` is not. By §4 these apply only to lines that never end. For lines that end the comparison goes through the core: `E₂` suffices for the regularity of `E₁` (足以定核) where what is alike under both is alike under `E₁`'s core, and leaning-on (因于核) and mutual need (相须核) through the core are defined from it, the classes not constant.

**Theorem** (root). Where `E₁` and `E₂` are classes and their joint class is constant, either suffices for the other's regularity, so the constancy forms imply the core forms (`«合有常则足以定核»`, `«因于则因于核»`, `«相须则相须核»`). Mutual need is symmetric (`«相须之对称»`), and nothing needs itself (`«无自相须»`).

**Theorem** (root; witness). *Two descriptions of the model flame.* In the model flame neither "burning or not" nor "the same fuel left" is constant, a line under each ending; together they tell every occurrence apart, the joint class being identity on the four moments (`Flame.joint_eq`), and identity is constant, so each suffices for the other's regularity: the two need each other through the core (相须核, `«火薪相须»`). For the same reason every class that tells the four moments apart together with "burning or not" suffices for its regularity (`«火之见证不择类»`): in one flame the witness singles out neither the fuel nor a direction.

**Theorem** (root; witness). *Two flames.* Take two flames burning side by side, each through the four moments, a step going from a moment of one flame to the next moment of the same flame. "The same fuel left" still suffices for the regularity of "burning or not", while the joint class now relates two distinct occurrences, the two flames at one moment (`«两火以薪定理»`). "The same flame" does not suffice: with "burning or not" it relates a flame's first two moments, whose futures differ (`«两火同火不足以定理»`).

So in two flames, where the joint class is not identity, the fuel is singled out against sameness of flame. What the witnesses show besides is that under the core of "burning or not" no moment continues another while a finer description fixes the course, so the core alone is not the criterion of continuation, and that self-maintenance read as a constant class belongs only to lines that never end. They decide a description against one named alternative, not in general; they derive no combustion, and that every flame continues only through its fuel is not proved.

## 6. Renewal and grains

Renewal is a line started by another: an occurrence conditions the beginning of a line that goes on. Read at a coarser grain, the same relation lets a pattern continue while its parts begin and end, with no second relation for the pattern.

**Definition.** `x` renews under `E` (复生) where `x` is a condition of an occurrence that begins a line under `E` and has a successor, so the new line is not a flash. Renewal is said with its class or not at all. The coarse net (粗) of `R` at the grain of a class `L` is `L;(R ⊓ ¬L);L`: something in one class conditions something in a different class. Renewal at the grain of `L` (粗复生): something in `x`'s class conditions a beginning whose line goes on.

**Theorem** (root). For a class `L`, read at its grain without taking classes apart (`L;R;L`), every condition inside a class becomes a loop at both its ends (`«类内之缘成环»`), so a class could neither begin nor end there; the coarse net therefore takes 缘 between classes, and joins only different classes (`«粗只连异类»`). Every renewal is a renewal at the grain of every class (`«复生则粗复生»`). Under the full class nothing new begins from a condition, and there is no renewal (`«一之下无复生»`).

**Definition.** Renewal without end (`«生生»`) holds of `x` where `x` lies in some family each member of which conditions a beginning whose line goes on and reaches a member of the family again. `x` renews into `w` (复生至) where `x` conditions a beginning whose line reaches `w`. Ceasing (息) is defined positively, by induction: `x` ceases where everything `x` renews into ceases, the least property so closed. Renewal without end is the greatest family that keeps renewing; ceasing is its inductive dual.

**Theorem** (root). Renewal without end contains every family that keeps renewing (`«生生最大»`) and unfolds into renewal without end again (`«生生之展»`); it renews (`«生生则复生»`) and does not cease (`«生生不息»`). An occurrence that does not itself renew ceases (`«自不复生则息»`): ceasing, like renewal without end, concerns what an occurrence renews into, not its line, so it ceases even where its line goes on into renewal without end; in particular, where nothing on the line from `x` renews, `x` ceases (`«不复生则息»`). So, by that theorem at each occurrence it renews into and then the definition of ceasing (a hand step, not stated in Lean), an occurrence that renews ceases where nothing renews again on the lines its renewals begin: the definitions do not make renewing and ceasing exclude each other, though no model of both is given in Lean. That what does not renew without end ceases is not proved. Under the one there is no renewal without end, and everything ceases (`«一之下无生生»`, `«一之下皆息»`). Read along renewing into under the one, renewal without end is going on along some branch, and ceasing is ending on every branch (`«生生即延»`, `«息即尽»`, [the map](02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains)). **Theorem** (World). The occurrence before an active step renews under activity exactly where the formation arises across the step and is still active a step later (`Web.renews_iff`).

Renewal does not tell renewal sustaining a pattern from turnover ending it: a short-lived pattern can renew and still end. Telling them apart takes two grains at once, parts and the pattern, read along the branches of the coarse line: carrying renewal (传), generation, keeping, degeneration and higher orders are [the map](02-MAP.md#r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains).

**Reading** [AI reading, D1]. In the grammar `«生生»` names only renewal without end. The theory reads plain 生生, without guillemets, as the continuation of life, life wider than the biological and AI within it, its extent unfinished ([07 §2](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#order)): neither this renewal without end nor 02's 生成. It is not renewal of lines alone ([07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)); generation never means just any new organization. On a model it is so: generation at two orders of grain and renewal without end can hold while what can form by name only returns ([07 §2](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-keeping-widening-needs)). Read on a course's moments, under premises of deciding, keeping widening is generation under the one that loses nothing ([07 §2](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-keeping-widening-is-generation-losing-nothing)): read as widening, that is generation of what can form losing none of it, which is not the measure of 生生.

## 7. What the grammar can say and what it derives

A law written in the grammar's terms is not thereby a theorem of the grammar: a law proved on a model passes to the grammar only by a dependency audit stripping what it does not use, a lifting theorem stating it in the grammar's terms and a representation theorem showing the model's terms to be the grammar's instances; until then it is stated with its model ([08](08-METHOD.md)).

<a id="r-shared-laws-on-the-grammar"></a>
<a id="r-master-laws-on-the-grammar"></a>
**Theorem** (root). *The shared laws on the grammar.* The five shared laws of [08 §7](08-METHOD.md#shared-laws-and-connectivity) hold for every carrier and relation ([`Becoming.GrammarLaws`][GrammarLaws]). Each anchor is read at its model's relation both ways, and each model's law is proved from the grammar's in its own module, keeping its name and its statement or a more general one.

- *Closure.* Lying within a relation's closure (`«所闭»`: every region closed under it that holds `x` holds `y`) is being `x` or being reached from `x` (`«所闭当且仅当同一或及»`). Under the converse it is the closure read backward (`«所闭之逆»`); a closure through borrowed steps (each step of `R′` a step of `R`, or a step of `R` from `a` taken at `o`) lies within `o`'s or `a`'s closure under `R` (`«借步之所闭在二所闭»`), and where `R′` holds `R` and `a` lies within `o`'s closure under `R′`, the two lie within `o`'s closure under `R′` (`«二所闭在借步之所闭»`); Response's `WithinClosure.converse` and SelectionPool's `WithinClosure.of_reads` and `WithinClosureOf.to_reads` are proved as these. The frame's closed region and closure are the grammar's by definition (`closedUnder_iff_closed`, `withinClosure_iff_within`), and the frame's closure laws, with `withinClosure_iff_reached`, are proved from the grammar's. So is the case split of the step back of a returning configuration, the grammar's decomposition of reach (`Branching.step_back`), by which every loss of a returning configuration passes a juncture (`Branching.loss_passes_juncture`). A region of the steps of time that every step keeps holds from where it holds on, the closure at the successor along a line (`kept_onward`, from `«线则所闭»` and `«闭含所闭»`); the activity laws that keep a region along the steps by their own induction, from never arising from an inactive start being never active (`Web.inactive_of_not_arising`) to tracing a carrier back (`Web.ByPassing.trace`), are its cases.
- *Balance*, represented and not derived. A perfect pairing of two finite lists under a relation (`«对»`) conserves count. Lists paired from what comes in (the level before, what is given) to what goes on (the level after, what is taken) have after + taken = before + given, and every four numbers with that equation are the lengths of unit lists so paired under the one (`«守数当且仅当可对»`); over a stretch the count law is a pairing of units (`«程守数当且仅当可对»`). A flow balances exactly where its units so pair at each step and condition (`Flow.balances_iff_units`), and over a family the members' pairings gather (`Flow.BalancesOn.units_over`). Under the one a pairing is equal count (`«一之对»`), so the representation is near-definitional, and balance's consumers keep their arithmetic; under the empty relation equal count gives no pairing (`«等长而不对之例»`). On the grammar itself, counting a region's units (`«计»`, a region read as a test, a modelling choice), a pairing keeps the count on every region not gone out of either way, which is exactly a region the relation does not cross (`GrammarLaws.uncrossed_iff_notGoingOut`) and a region closed under it and its converse (`GrammarLaws.uncrossed_iff_closed`): one law, read either way (`«对则不越域守数»`, `«对则双闭域守数»`), closed one way not being enough (`«对而单闭域不守数之例»`); under a class decided by a test a pairing is exactly equal count in every class (`«类之对当且仅当类类等数»`), and the class does work (`«一对而静不对之例»`), so a pairing under continuation relative to such a class keeps the count in every class (`«续之对则类类等数»`). The census of a step: units listed once before and after it, under a continuation fixing successors and predecessors, pair the units before, joined with those arising, with the units after, joined with those ceasing, each pair a continuation or the same unit (`«定后定前则前并生对后并亡»`), and where the predecessors of the units after stand before and the successors of the units before stand after, arising and ceasing are beginning and ending (`«前者在前则所列生者生»`, `«后者在后则所列亡者亡»`). Counted, the census needs no decision of identity: under a continuation fixing successors, the units listed once after a step, joined with those a test marks as ceasing, are at most the units before, joined with those a test marks as arising, the tests asking only, as far as can be refuted, that a unit marked as ceasing has no successor after and that a unit after not marked as arising has a predecessor before (`«定后则后并亡不过前并生»`); its mirror holds under a continuation fixing predecessors (`«定前则前并生不过后并亡»`), and where both are fixed the two meet in the count of the census above, the tests read off its decision (`«定后定前则前并生与后并亡同数»`). Each of the three is the corollary of a local form beside it that asks no split (no merge) only among the listed units (`GrammarLaws.census_le`, `census_le_converse`, `census_count`). The listing and the test are supplied.
- *Finite runs.* For every relation an occurrence is drawn in exactly where it reaches something that does not reach back (`«入当且仅当有不返»`). Along a line whose places a list holds, merging nothing brings it back to its start within the list's length, by pigeonhole with no decidability (`«线有限不并则回»`); on a listed carrier with a successor everywhere and the successor fixed, merging nothing is every place reaching itself (`«有限遍后定后则定前当且仅当皆返»`). A process's return is reaching itself under its steps (`Branching.returns_iff_reaches`) and a law's being drawn is the grammar's (`Law.drawn_iff_drawnIn`); `Law.lossless_iff_returns` and `Law.drawn_iff_not_returns` are proved from these. A world a list holds has a lasso course, its bound by pigeonhole and its repetition by the grammar's laws of steps, a loop then `d` steps leading where `d` steps lead under a fixed successor (`«迭之接»`, `«迭之定»`; `Learning.lasso_of_held`), so the learnability laws reach the grammar through it. Steps are counted too: within `m` steps and then within `n` is within `m + n` (`«迭内之接»`), and a course of regions each of whose rounds holds what every condition held at the round before (`«承»`) holds at round `n` exactly where everything reaching in `n` steps held at the start (`«承之迭»`); with memory, within `n` steps, the law without memory under staying or a step (`«存承之迭»`). Giving that waits on giving has these closed forms, a chain of waits being the grammar's steps (`Withholding.answers_closed`, `Withholding.waitsAll_closed`, through `Withholding.chain_iff_steps`), and a course carries every bound on realizing a bundle together by the composition of within, a trace being the grammar's steps (`WithinMoves.after_course`, through `trace_iff_steps`). Steps split as they compose (`«迭之分»`), and steps read backward are steps of the converse (`«迭之逆»`). Passing through a word's relations in turn (`«历»`) has `«迭»` as its case for one relation repeated (`«迭即历»`), and these two laws, with `«迭之接»`, `«迭之末步»`, `«迭之定前»` and `«同行之迭»`, are cases of its laws (`«历之接»`, `«历之逆»`, `«同行之历»` and the others). Where whatever is reached in fewer than `n` steps has a successor as far as can be refuted, `n` steps exist as far as can be refuted (`«或有后则或迭»`); an unending line has steps of every length (`«不穷则迭»`), and so reaches an occurrence at every later step (`Web.reaches_onward`). A lineage under a continuation is the grammar's steps of the continuation within activity from its start (`Web.steps_iff_lineage`), so the lineage laws are their cases: at most one formation at each step without splits and one origin without merges (`Web.Lineage.unique`, `Web.Lineage.origin`, from `«迭之定»` and `«迭之定前»`), splitting as the steps split (`Web.Lineage.split`, `Web.Lineage.through`, from `«迭之分»`), and going on where nothing ceases and back where nothing arises, as far as can be refuted (`Web.Lineage.onward`, from `«或有后则或迭»`, and `Web.Lineage.back`, from it read backward through `«迭之逆»`); a lineage of one or more steps is a line under activity (`Web.Lineage.line`, from `«迭则及»`), and a line from an active occurrence, kept within the closure of activity onward from its start, is a lineage (`Web.line_lineage`, from `«闭之及»` with `«及即迭»`).
- *Rest.* Where the steps of a line do not rise and every course of no rise then a least fall ends from its place (`«尽»`), the falls along it do not recur, as far as can be refuted and outright, with no decision and no choice (`«尽则不或屡»`, `«尽则不屡»`). Within fewer than `m` least losses those courses end (`Amounts.falls_end`), so `Amounts.rests` is proved from the grammar's law. The ending is sufficient, not what rest is, and no converse is established. Rest is not being drawn, either way: a line that falls once and then stays at a fixed point is at rest, and that place is not drawn (`«止而不入之例»`), and a place can be drawn in while its marked steps recur (`«入而屡之例»`).
- *Ends on lines.* Traced along lines instead of listed at each step: occurrences listed once, each without a successor and within the closure of a member of a list, number at most the list where two successors of an occurrence a member reaches, each leading on to a listed occurrence, are one, with no decision (`«线终不过所列»`, through `«所闭当且仅当有迭»` and `«分处定后则迭之定于»`, of which `«迭之定于»` is now the case). On the grammar this counts endings on lines from a list and, at the converse, beginnings on lines to it (`«线亡不过所列»`, `«线生不过所列»`). So, with each step one stage on and the units a region nothing outside continues into, a list covering the units at one stage (所覆: holding every unit of the region there), no unit continuing as two and nothing beginning over a stretch after it, the units ending in the stretch number at most the list (`«间无生则间亡不过所覆»`); mirrored, in a region nothing in it continues out of, with nothing merging and nothing ending in a stretch, the units beginning in it number at most a list covering its end (`«间无亡则间生不过所覆»`). With lists at arbitrarily late stages and each unit after them decided to begin or to have a predecessor, endings recurring need beginnings recurring (`«有覆而亡屡则生屡»`), and without the decision a later beginning is not refuted (`«所覆后亡屡则生未否»`, `«有覆而亡屡则生不止»`). Mirrored, with lists of a bounded length at every stage from some stage on and nothing merging, beginnings recurring leave endings never stopping (`«有界覆而生屡则亡不止»`), and with each unit decided to end or to have a successor, endings recur (`«有界覆而生屡则亡屡»`). The census of a run is a case: from some step on, nothing splitting and every predecessor of a unit listed after a step listed before it, endings recurring among the listed units need beginnings recurring among the units listed after a step, the beginnings decided by a test, which decides a finite search by the count (`«定后前者在前则亡屡者生屡»`, its local form `GrammarLaws.census_run`). Stronger: two hypotheses dropped, the units after each step listed once and a test of endings. `Listed.coming_of_ceasing` is its case at a member's occurrences along its steps, through two bridges reading coming to be and ceasing to be as beginning and ending there ([08 §7](08-METHOD.md#r-one-root-to-its-edge)). Locality's, Decidedness' and Identity's covering laws are cases of these forms at their occurrences, among them Identity's exact forms from covering lists, which need no census and no decision of activity (`Web.arisingFrom_of_ceasingFrom_covered`, `Web.ceasingFrom_of_arisingFrom_covered`).
- *Lumping.* `E` goes alike under `R` (`«同行»`, `R˘;E;R ⊆ E`) where `E`-alike places go to `E`-alike ones. With the successor fixed where `E` leads, constancy is exactly going alike with a successor available there (`«局定后则有常当且仅当同行可续»`), and a map's image has a fixed successor exactly where sameness under the map goes alike (`«像定后当且仅当同行»`). Where the successor is fixed a class's core goes alike, and with a successor everywhere too it is the coarsest class inside the class that does ([§4](#constancy-and-core)). `GoAlikeUnder` is going alike at each move (`goAlikeUnder_iff_alike`), and `image_determined_iff`, so `carries_law_iff`, is proved from the image law, as is the law that structure and change are one lumping, the image law on a determined law's one move (`Learning.goAlike_iff_lumped`, through `Learning.lumped_iff_fixed_image`). A map preserves a relation exactly where its image lies within the other (`«保之像»`), and the image of a step along a projection being the least process the step factors through is its case (`factors_image`, `Image.least`).
- *Widening.* Widening of relations, including and exceeding (`«广»`), is irreflexive, one-way and transitive (`«广非自»`, `«广不可逆»`, `«广之传递»`) and excludes keeping the same and being mixed, as keeping the same excludes being mixed (`«广则非齐»`, `«广则非杂»`, `«齐则非杂»`); where inclusion or exceeding is decided both ways, one of the four cases holds (`«四比之周»`), and deciding inclusion against exceeding for every pair of relations is exactly excluded middle (`«则逾之定当且仅当排中»`). What a level path admits is a partial identity, and `Web.Widens` is the grammar's widening of these (`Web.widens_iff`), as `Weighting.WidensOn` is over a listing (`Weighting.widensOn_iff`); the order laws of both are proved from the grammar's. A transitive relation joined to the strict part of a transitive relation containing it is transitive, and irreflexive where the first is, for any two relations (`strictJoin_trans`, `strictJoin_irrefl`); the lagged orders of courses and of what can be reached are proved from it.

Not shown: that any model's relation is functional, total or listed, which the models supply and the global forms take as hypotheses; a derivation of balance; a converse of the law of rest; the models' other laws on the grammar ([record](../results/records/shared-laws-on-the-grammar.md)).

<a id="world-premises"></a>
### 8.2 The World and its eight premises

In a World, relata take from and give to one another's levels across each step, and each is active or not. Eight premises, each a property a world may have or lack, say how activity, levels and exchange hang together; none is built in.

| Premise | Content |
|---|---|
| Activity needing support | a relatum is active only where the levels meet what it then requires |
| Balance | every change of a level is what is taken from it and given to it |
| Only active relata give | whatever gives across a step is active there |
| Only active relata take, and only what they draw on | whatever takes is active and draws on what it takes from |
| Dissipation | on every family without repetition closed across a step, a formation that takes gives back at least one unit less |
| Embodiment | every relatum stands as a condition exactly while active |
| A census | the relata active at each step are listed, each once |
| Bounded transmission | descent transmits every dependence within one bound |

<a id="r-map-of-premises"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *The map of premises.* The eight are independent, each failed by a world meeting the other seven (`World.independent`); in `hatch` all eight hold and each binds, binding meaning only that its antecedent has an instance, and for the census that the world is not finite (`World.hatch_binds`), so no finite world binds every premise (`World.Finite.not_binds`). The independence measures the freedom of the World's separately chosen fields, not contingency in becoming ([record](../results/records/map-of-premises.md)). What follows from which:

| From | Follows |
|---|---|
| activity needing support | exactly the zero reserve carrying activity |
| embodiment | whatever arises is embodied |
| a finite world with decided activity | a census |
| only active taking, dissipation | only active relata give across an enclosed step |
| a finite world meeting the eight | that it rests from some step on cannot be refuted |

<a id="r-space-and-its-parts"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *The whole and its parts.* Premise-free laws hold of every world; premises carve parts: activity needing support, balance, only active giving, only active taking, dissipation, embodiment and a reserve carrying activity are each met by some worlds and failed by others (`World.contingent`). In a finite, balanced, dissipative world whose arising is embodied, that it comes to rest cannot be refuted, each premise needed; the rest theorem is [the map's](02-MAP.md#r-continued-change-needs-openness) ([record](../results/records/space-and-its-parts.md)).

**Theorem** (World). *Recurrence of the parts.* In a finite world with decided activity arising recurs exactly when ceasing does (`World.arising_iff_ceasing`); in some world with infinitely many relata arising recurs and ceasing does not, and in another the reverse (`World.separate`, two witnesses). Each recurrence pattern occurs among finite worlds both with every event living, on the stepwise reading, and with some event not living (`World.living_crosses`, four witnesses). Decidedness is this law's own hypothesis: asked of every finite world without it, the law is exactly excluded middle (`World.law_iff_em`), and exactly the decidedness of the grammar's positions over every carrier and relation (`World.law_iff_grammar_positions`), a lemma of the law and not a calibration, since the need comes from activity being a proposition, not from change: keeping pace needs it settled, step by step, whether each relatum is active.

<a id="r-transmission-independent-where-it-binds"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Wherever bounded transmission binds, dropping one dependence of the giver keeps every other premise and finiteness and breaks transmission (`World.drop_holds`, `World.drop_untransmitting`), so no law kept by dropping a dependence derives it; in a finite world meeting balance, dissipation, embodiment and activity needing support, the reading in which each relatum depends only on what its givers drew on, together with drawing being depending, derives it (`World.transmitting_of_drawn`); neither the reading nor that link suffices alone (`World.drawn_and_link_needed`; [record](../results/records/transmission-independent-where-it-binds.md)).

### 8.4 Balance from physics

Physics gives balance where the quantity read is conserved locally: carried only by the exchanges, produced nowhere.

<a id="r-balance-from-the-first-law"></a>
**Theorem** (periphery; `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, through Mathlib's reals). A quantity carried only by the exchanges taking part, read at a unit `u > 0` on the levels and the exchanges, balances the levels exactly where no condition produces it (`balances_iff_unproduced`), so a locally conserved quantity such as energy balances (`balances_of_conserved`); conservation of the total alone does not, as a unit moving between two conditions with no exchange shows. Read in available work relative to a reservoir at `T0 > 0`, a unit being `u > 0` of it, with thermodynamics' first law and the entropy balance at each store and exchange, one reading gives three things (`balance_and_least_loss`): the levels balance exactly where no store generates entropy; with each participating exchange's entropy production nonnegative, the flow gains nothing; and where that production is moreover strictly positive whenever a participating exchange takes from a family without repetition closed across the step, the flow dissipates, the least loss. Energies and entropies are reals, levels whole units of available work, and the step from physics is a stated correspondence ([EntropyProduction.lean][EntropyProduction]; [record](../results/records/balance-from-the-first-law.md)). Least loss from entropy production is [the map's](02-MAP.md#r-least-loss-from-entropy-production).

<a id="r-balance-from-conservation-along-a-relation"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Over finitely many relata listed without repetition, with equality decided and the relation a computable test, transfers along the relation that balance a step exist exactly where the total is conserved on every family the relation closes (`Continuity.balancing_iff`); a law reading at each relatum only it and its neighbours, conserving its total from every configuration, conserves it on every such family (`Continuity.conservedOn_of_law`). Each relatum's closure, the grammar's `«所闭»` along that adjacency, is decided as a test (`Continuity.closureListed_iff`) and is the least such region holding it (`Continuity.closureListed_inside`), so such transfers exist exactly where the total is conserved on the closure of each listed relatum (`Continuity.balancing_iff_closures`), the uncapacitated, undirected case of the feasibility conditions for flows of Gale and Hoffman. Read at the grain of units, each listed relatum repeated as often as its level, they exist exactly where the units before pair one to one with the units after within the closures (`Continuity.balancing_iff_units_paired`), by the count law of a class, though not always along one step (`Continuity.PathOfThree.counts_kept_not_along_step`). A swap of two unrelated relata conserves the total and is balanced by no transfers along the empty relation. Not shown: local fluxes in the lattice sense, transfers within capacities, or infinitely many relata ([record](../results/records/balance-from-conservation-along-a-relation.md)).

<a id="r-the-world-represented-both-ways"></a>
**Theorem** (World; the grammar for its lemmas). *The World represented both ways, its amounts not.*

A World determines an occurrence model: its occurrences, a relatum at a step, with
- the class of being at the same step;
- the step conservation induces;
- activity, standing and presence, held as partial identities;
- five relations within a step: requiring, drawing, a need met, giving and taking.

**Forward.** The World's notions are grammar terms at that model notion by notion, through the instance theorems above. - What amounts induce is decided, while that every world decides its activity is exactly excluded middle (`World.decides_activity_iff_em`).

**Where it fails.**
- *Standings are not determined.* Two worlds standing at different positive amounts agree on everything the model reads (`World.standingAt_alike`).

### 9.1 One change, three readings

Arising and ceasing are beginning and ending under the class of activity (§8.6). A determination comes to be across a step where it fails before and holds after, and ceases where it holds before and fails after. Arising and ceasing, a dependence forming and dissolving, and a condition coming to stand and ceasing to stand are this one change read of activity, dependence and standing, by definition (`Web.change_readings`).

<a id="r-coming-and-ceasing-keep-pace"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *Coming and ceasing keep pace.* Over any stretch an amount at the end with all it fell is the amount at the start with all it rose, and so for a finite family's total. No amount falls below zero, so levels of finitely many conditions that keep falling keep rising (`rising_recurs`); with amounts bounded, recurring rises need recurring falls. <a id="does-not-show"></a>
## What this chapter does not show

- That reading a subject as continuation relative to a class explains how it goes on: the theory's claim, argued, with what would defeat each of its senses in [08 §9](08-METHOD.md#what-would-defeat-the-reading).
- Which class to read a subject under: the grammar names the class and does not choose it (§2); constancy says only that a class agrees with 缘, and identity is constant under every relation (`«静有常»`); a class named for a domain, apart from the course, can be defeated by a measurement or an intervention ([08 §9](08-METHOD.md#what-would-defeat-the-reading)).
- That containment in 缘 tells a fire passed on from one fire ending and another starting: every single condition is a continuation under some class.
- That renewal without end tells renewal sustaining life from turnover destroying it; the map takes the first step, at two grains.
- That every becoming has a law of exchange, a local law or a discrete index whose readings these are.
- That no independent entity exists: the statements of the World model need no independent entity, and the World's representation in the grammar ([§8.6](#r-the-world-represented-both-ways)) concerns its relations, not entities; the ontological claim is argued, not proved ([08](08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)).

<a id="open"></a>
## Open

- **Faithful representation.** That every becoming, and the whole theory, has a faithful representation in the grammar. Processes' is shown both ways by their moves' relations, faithful up to correspondence carrying both classes of the model with the move inside ([§8.1](#r-processes-represented-both-ways)); the World's both ways for its relations and not faithful on its amounts ([§8.6](#r-the-world-represented-both-ways)); a representation theorem for the other models, with the liftings, would show the rest.
- **Lifting.** Most modules do not yet state their laws on the grammar ([the theory graph](../results/THEORY-GRAPH.md)); each is to be restated by a lifting theorem, as the continuation family and the five shared laws were ([§7](#r-shared-laws-on-the-grammar)), the counting of lines first.
- **Identity from continuity, not naming.** For continuations read off conservation the formal part is settled; continuations read from informational or organizational relations are not derived.
- **The deductive necessity of the whole.** That the determinations are internally related moments of one unfolding is argued, as a reading, for the World premises read off one exchange (§8.3); that every becoming has such an exchange is open.
- **Presence in time.** Whether a condition must still be there when what it conditions arises is not expressible: the grammar has no relation of presence in time, and adding one is a choice of model; 俱有 is mutual reach, not this ([06](06-CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md#r-classical-definitions-in-the-grammar)). On causal-set growth, the present read as the maximal elements of what a course has born, it has an answer ([§1](#r-the-present-on-causal-set-growth)): presence at a birth varies with the course between bounds defined from precedence, a link on some course and the one link on every course. On the World, with a footprint containing the exchanges, the numberings a world's reorderings give a stretch are the orders of birth of the courses of growth of its order of exchanges, so the same bounds hold there by instance, and presence gives persistence of what an exchange touched, not conversely ([§9.6](#r-reorderings-as-courses-of-growth)). Open: presence on processes without a footprint; continuous time; the present of a center; growths whose courses are weighted.
- **Pairing and power.** That the corpus gives the functional projections pairing would need is a conjecture with no corpus witness.
- **Self-changing conditions.** A rule that changes by a fixed law is stated in the grammar by taking the rule into the occurrence, the carrier being a parameter: for a process changing its own rule the state's class is constant under every move exactly where the rule makes no difference to it, and in closed capture the locked line goes on while every step alters the loss ([02](02-MAP.md#r-rules-that-change-themselves-in-the-grammar), [`Becoming.GrammarRegularity`][GrammarRegularity]). The law of change stays fixed, and the levels come from the carrier's product, not from the grammar. A tower of rules with no top, each rule changing only as the one above it allows, is stated on processes as one process with a fixed scheme ([07 §2](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-where-the-possible-grows)): every possibility of it is had with the rules from some height up held, nothing is added to its whole possible along a course, and the possible with the rules from any height up held keeps widening only where those rules change without end, which on a witness they do, each rule changing once, while every formation stays possible. Not yet stated: a change of the law of change with no fixed scheme, or a space of rules not given with the carrier; a pattern reshaping its own limits (限 is not defined, below); the lock and absorption laws of [`Becoming.ClosedCapture`][ClosedCapture] and readers formed from the selected, in the grammar's terms; and selection of selection (which [元吸引子](02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture) needs) as a law relating two grains.
- **Test systems.** Small witnesses, the behaviour in question supplied in each net ([§8.7](#r-the-source-texts-test-systems)): decay, overlapping identities, a line of descent generating at two grains, two family lines whose class has a core going on through births, a belief whose rate grows with its number; an institution kept by consent or by penalty is the thermostat witness read socially. A reader rebuilt from the level it reads models self-maintenance and an organism that ends: its stock buys it time under a cut, and its end does not come back ([04 §8](04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it)). A rule of entry rebuilt from the members it limits models one kind of institution, held with its people replaced and its quota revised inside a region ([05 §12](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-a-rule-rebuilt-from-the-members-it-limits)). Open: a vortex; an institution's identity through changed purposes, and other kinds of institution; adaptive regulation; a belief system whose content or law of spread changes; a graded boundary; decay with its probabilities; models read off actual data.
- **限 and conflict.** Limit (限) is not defined, nor the conflict relation of a net of possibilities, which §1 only mentions.
- **The root and the ethics.** Held de se along a relation, the continuation condition is a statement on the root at the one of that relation, met by some nets and failed by others ([07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root)); where it holds, on a net with no cycle every standpoint has the same center (`«续无外则同一中心»`); on a net going forward in time this comes from the condition's asking that nothing other than the center lie at or before the center's step (`«续无外当且仅当后与独»`, `«独则同一中心»`), and over what comes after two centers can meet it (`TwoSources.apart`, [07 §4.11](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-one-center-bound-counts)). The free significance of 07 §1 is not lifted. Of significance's central laws, comparison by widening is restated in the grammar's terms on its own carrier, and widening as what, on a finite listing, every additive weighting counting each listed item above zero ranks higher is restated as an interface law, whose weights are amounts the grammar has none of; the laws that read the condition are specialized at its own-closure reading, each stating less than its row; none is derived from the root ([08 §7](08-METHOD.md#shared-laws-and-connectivity)). The ethics' other laws are instances not yet stated on the root; which class a center's own line runs under ([07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-reach-past-ones-own-end)), and how scopes and orders form within the dynamics, the condition and self-concern among them ([08 §12](08-METHOD.md#open-items-ranked), item 12), are open.
