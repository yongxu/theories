# Generation, keeping and degeneration

<a id="the-map"></a>

**Thesis.** A pattern goes on while the occurrences carrying it begin and end: a flame through its fuel, an organism through its cells, a practice through its members. Read at a fine and a coarse class together, the grammar defines, for every relation, where a pattern *generates*, *keeps* or *degenerates*, and proves which of these exclude each other; witnesses inhabit each region, and with excluded middle every point lies in one of three strong forms (§1). On the models, exact laws say what ends a pattern or keeps it going: attraction, renewal and consumption, rest, the margin of a regime, closures, carried differences and loops, regulation and reserves, and capture through a rule (§§2–9). *Claimed*: that these laws explain how a subject goes on, argued from the laws on the models, not a theorem. *Open*: coverage by the three some-branch regions, and whether every becoming has a faithful representation in the grammar.

**Where this chapter sits.** It takes from [the root](01-ROOT.md) its grammar of continuation and its bases: models, processes and the World. It hands couplings and carried differences (§7) to [knowing](03-KNOWING.md); renewal, consumption and rest (§§3–4) to [life](04-LIFE.md); and the margin (§5) to [collectives](05-COLLECTIVES.md) and [significance](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).

<a id="how-to-read"></a>
**Bases.** Relation (际), condition (缘), class (类), continuation (续), the four marks (生 住 异 亡), constancy (有常) and the core (核) are as [01](01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class) defines them. Labels of standing and bases are [08's](08-METHOD.md#standings-and-bases): §1's grammar is on the root, for every carrier and relation; the other laws are on any type, any process, the World, the regime or a named witness, and a periphery label names its axioms. The World premises used here are among [01's eight](01-ROOT.md#world-premises), each met by some worlds, failed by others: balance (`Flow.Balances`), only active formations giving (`Flow.Acting`), activity needing support (`Web.Dependent`), dissipation (`Flow.Dissipative`), embodiment (`Web.Embodied`) and a census (`Web.Census`, `Web.StandingCensus`). Two hypotheses of particular laws are not among them: *the first law* (`Flow.Gainless`: a closed family without repetition gets back at most what is taken from it) and *enclosure* (`Flow.Around`: some finite family without repetition containing a given family is closed across the step; `Flow.Enclosed` where no family is given). Amounts are natural numbers unless a law says otherwise. *Cannot be refuted* is a double negation, naming no witness; *does not recur*: not at arbitrarily late steps. Most World laws also hold locally, each premise asked only where used; the global form is given. The map orders nothing. Whether a course supports, enriches or damages 生生 (life in the whole and its continuing generation) is asked in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md), where the ethics rests on two premises with two roles, neither asserted and neither used here: the continuation condition for scope (whose losses count) and C for order (that widening is better).

<a id="branches"></a>
## 1. The map on branches

Read at one class, turnover that sustains a pattern and turnover that ends it look alike: a fire whose embers are replaced and a fire burning out both renew at the grain of their fuel. Read at a fine class `E` of the parts and a coarse class `L` of the pattern together, they differ.

**Definition.** 传 (chuán, carrying renewal): `«传» R L E x y` holds where the pattern continues from `x` to `y` under `L` and a fine line begins at `y` under `E` (火传, fire passed on). 延 (yán, goes on): `x` lies in a family each member of which is continued within it (coinductive); 尽 (jìn, ends): everything continuing `x` ends (inductive). *Generation* (生成, shēngchéng): along some branch (such a family) the pattern goes on and from every member a carrying renewal between members lies ahead; *keeping* (守, shǒu): along some branch it goes on with no carrying renewal between members; *degeneration* (退化, tuìhuà) is 尽. Three *strong forms* read the whole line from `x`: 全生成, every point the line reaches has a continuation (不穷) and from each some carrying renewal can be reached; 全守, 不穷 and from some point it reaches no carrying renewal can be reached, renewals before that point allowed; 有终 (yǒuzhōng, reaches an end), some point the line reaches ends, which is not degeneration.

<a id="r-generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains"></a>
**Theorem** (root). For every carrier, relation and pair of classes (`Becoming.GrammarMap`; [record](../results/records/generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains.md)):

**Open.** Coverage by the three regions: 守 forbids renewal throughout its family where 全守 allows an earlier one, so a line that renews once and then goes on still is 全守 and, at its start, appears to be none of 生成, 守, 退化 (a hand check, not in the Lean).

**Calibration.** That every point of every model goes on along some branch or ends on every branch is exactly excluded middle (`«延尽之定当且仅当排中»`), and so is that every coarse line of every model is without end (不穷) or reaches an end (有终) (`«不穷有终之定当且仅当排中»`, a dichotomy of 不穷 against 有终, not of 全生成 against 有终), and so is placing every point of every model in one of 全生成, 全守 and 有终 (`«全三域之定当且仅当排中»`): the boundary between going on and ending is where a line's going on is not decided, and a law placing a point in a region supplies that decision.

**Theorem** (World). Read the World with occurrences `(t, f)` as carrier, a continuation's step as 缘, activity as the coarse class and activity with one formation as the fine (`Becoming.MapInstances`). From an active occurrence, a carrying renewal is a continuation's step into an active occurrence whose formation did not carry on as itself (`Web.carries_iff`); under a pattern's passing continuation, a passing to a carrier arising across the step (`Web.passing_carries_iff`). So, under the passing continuation, a line from an active carrier in the strong form 全生成 has carriers arising at arbitrarily late steps (`Web.generation_arises_late`); under any continuation its carrying renewals lie beyond every step (`Web.generation_renews_late`). Read by names alone nothing is 全生成, and every line with no end anywhere is 全守 (`Web.itself_not_generation`, `Web.itself_keeps`). These instance the strong forms; the some-branch regions are not read on the World ([record](../results/records/generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains.md)).

**Theorem** (World). From an active occurrence, a line along which nothing it reaches at some step `N`, at or after its start, is active is not without end (not 不穷, `Web.not_endless_of_gone`); where it is decided at each point the line reaches whether that point has a continuation, it reaches an end (有终, `Web.degenerates_of_gone`, whose name says "degenerates" for 有终, not 尽). In two witnesses every carrier renews, the shifts being 全生成 and the relay reaching an end (有终) (`Map.renewal_undecided`): renewal of relations implies none of the strong forms ([record](../results/records/generation-and-degeneration-at-two-grains.md)).

<a id="map-on-the-world"></a>
Where later laws meet the map, under their own premises, a line from an active occurrence is not 不穷: where activity needs support, one that later holds a dependence on a condition kept below that need (§3; `Web.consumed_held_not_endless`); under [05's](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends) premises, one depending on a family drawn beyond its renewal ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends); `Web.family_excess_not_endless`); under passing with brief carriers, one whose passing has stopped ([04](04-LIFE.md#r-a-pattern-lasts-through-passing); `Web.stopped_not_endless`). §§4–9 are not read as regions.

**Reading** [AI reading, D65]. The map's 生成 is continuation at two grains, a pattern going on through carrying renewal, with no premise on what the pattern is; it is not 生生 and never overlaps degeneration. Widening what can form ([07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-four-cases-of-a-part)) is another notion, read at a part (`Web.Widens`); no law relates the two. Degeneration and dissolution belong to the unfolding as fully as generation: a loss needs no successor to have its place on the map. Where generation, keeping, transformation and degeneration overlap in one activity, the map places the overlap along different branches (生守相杂) or at different pairs of classes.

## 2. Return and attraction

Under a law some configurations come round again; what does not is drawn into a region it never leaves, the unfolding narrowing its own future.

<a id="r-attraction-is-not-returning"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). For every determined law `next : S → S`, with no finiteness or decidability, a configuration is drawn into some absorbing region exactly where it does not return (`Law.drawn_iff_not_returns`), and once drawn never comes back. What can be had `n` steps on is absorbing, and every possibility lost by step `n` is drawn into it (`Law.reachable_absorbing`). The main law is near-definitional, not a classical attractor ([record](../results/records/attraction-is-not-returning.md)).

**Theorem** (any type; any process). Of two configurations merged, not both return (`Law.Merges.not_both_return`), so a law that merges draws something, up to double negation (`Law.Merges.draws`); within finitely many listed configurations with equality decided, the law merges nothing exactly where nothing is drawn, while an open world separates the two (counting on draws 0 and merges nothing). For processes, absorbing under a law is invariant under its process, and a run in an invariant region stays locked in (`Run.locked_in`).

<a id="r-basins-of-a-determined-law"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Under any determined law, courses *meeting* (at one configuration at some steps) is an equivalence whose classes are basins; a course reaches every returning configuration of its basin, and within `m` listed configurations every course enters what returns before step `m` (`Law.enters_returning_within`) and each basin holds one cycle (`Law.basin_one_cycle`; [record](../results/records/basins-of-a-determined-law.md)).

<a id="r-returning-where-the-course-branches"></a>
**Theorem** (any process). Where courses branch, what returns can be had at every step, and every configuration returns exactly where each region the process never leaves keeps every possibility of its own (`Branching.returns_iff_keepsWithin`). Keeping every possibility no longer gives returning (`Transit.keeps_not_returns`, three configurations). Merging nothing gives it along a run whose configurations a list holds, the run taken as given: building one needs a choice at each step ([record](../results/records/returning-where-the-course-branches.md)).

<a id="r-necessarily-drawn-is-not-returning"></a>
**Theorem** (any process). Where courses branch, a configuration is *necessarily drawn* into a region where every run from it enters, *possibly drawn* where some reachable configuration lies in it. Every process necessarily draws exactly what does not return (`Branching.necessarilyDrawn_iff_not_returns`), a returning configuration having a run round to it for ever, built constructively; possibly drawn is losing oneself on some course, allowing return on another (`OneWay`). Runs are infinite, so a dead end, having no run, is necessarily drawn vacuously: the law quantifies over runs and does not say a course entering the region exists. With configurations of a state and a self-keeping rule on which alone the next depends, a perturbed state is drawn back at once (`Law.perturbation_drawnInto`) ([record](../results/records/necessarily-drawn-is-not-returning.md)).

**Definition.** 机 (jī, juncture) for `s`: a configuration with one step after which `s` can still be had and one after which it cannot.

<a id="r-every-loss-passes-a-juncture"></a>
**Theorem** (any process). Where `s` returns, every course from `s` to where `s` cannot be had again passes, up to double negation, a juncture at which it takes its losing step (`Branching.loss_passes_juncture`). Keeping only the steps after which `s` can still be had keeps `s`, holds every sub-process keeping `s` on its course, and removes exactly the losing steps; a process keeping `s` has no juncture for `s` on its course (`Branching.no_juncture_of_keeps`), however many options it has (witness `Many`) ([record](../results/records/every-loss-passes-a-juncture.md)).

**Calibration.** Where a list holds the configurations and steps are decided, the juncture is found by search; finding the juncture of every loss of every process gives LPO (`Branching.lpo_of_junctures_found`): to find where a loss happened is to decide whether `s` can still be had.

<a id="r-a-source-before-a-juncture"></a>
**Theorem** (World, with a process; before `D` both courses balanced with the same formations present, a common start, and every formation the source has not reached responding through its couplings; the reader at one configuration `x` in both courses supplied). A reader the source has not reached by `D`, whose step at `x` is chosen from what it reads through its couplings up to `D`, takes the same step in both courses (`TimedResponse.same_step_unreached`). Where the step chosen in one course keeps `s`, and the step chosen in the other leads somewhere and loses `s` wherever it leads, `x` is a juncture for `s` and the source has, not not, reached the reader by `D` (`TimedResponse.turned_at_juncture`): to turn a reader at a juncture, a source must reach it before. Finding the reaching outright needs reaching decided ([record](../results/records/a-source-before-a-juncture.md)).

<a id="r-reaching-before-and-after-a-juncture"></a>
**Theorem** (any process). With every configuration listed, steps decided and `s` returning, what can have `s` has it within the count of configurations (`Branching.reach_within_listed`); at a configuration with a step keeping `s` that is no juncture, no step can be refuted to keep it: a choice matters only at a juncture (`Branching.keeps_before_juncture`; [record](../results/records/reaching-before-and-after-a-juncture.md)).

<a id="renewal"></a>
## 3. Renewal and consumption

Drawing on a condition can leave every dependence on it met, or one unmet for good. Which a run shows is visible neither in one configuration nor in the drawer's own course: it belongs to the whole run and all that draws on the condition.

<a id="runs"></a>
### 3.1 Renewal and consumption belong to runs

**Definition.** A formation *renews* in a run where every condition it depends or draws on meets, at every step, every dependence on it (`RenewingIn`). A condition is *consumed* where, for some dependence holding on it at a step, its level stays below that dependence's need at every later step (`Consumed`; it need not have been met at that step, which *lost* adds: `Lost`), and a formation *consumes* in a run where it draws at some step on a consumed condition (`ConsumingIn`). It does not say the drawing made the deficit.

<a id="r-relational-renewal-classification"></a>
**Theorem** (World). The two classes exclude each other and do not exhaust the cases (a seasonal condition is neither). Loss for good is a law of every balanced flow: where whatever gives to a condition depends on it at a need of at least `m` and is supported where it gives, a level below `m` stays below (`Flow.stays_below_at`; `Flow.stays_below` where only active formations give and activity needs support), and where activity needs support every formation depending at such a need is inactive at each such step (`Flow.ends_below_at`), so from then on (`Flow.ends_below`). In the support-gated family, drawing beyond renewal ends the renewal for good, and two drawers each within renewal can together consume it (`RelationalRenewal.Law.jointly_consuming`); forming new organization at every step, or the drawer's own course, leaves the class undecided ([record](../results/records/relational-renewal-classification.md)).

<a id="r-becoming-renewal-reading"></a>
**Theorem** (any process). Every run of every process, read through observables of conditions, is a web the classification applies to; a consumer and what it consumes both take part in the drawn condition; a natural-number observable never rising from some step on does not fall at arbitrarily late steps (`falls_not_recurring`). **Theorem** (the renewable process; witness): the class belongs to the run: from one configuration, the focal formation active at every step, one run renews and another consumes the condition the sustaining formation depends on, and the sustaining formation ends (`Renewable.class_belongs_to_the_run`). Its use for significance is in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md) ([record](../results/records/becoming-renewal-reading.md)).

<a id="r-becoming-dependence-dynamics"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Each step keeps, breaks, forms or dissolves each dependence. Of a dependence holding at one step and not at a later one, it cannot be that no step between dissolves it (`ceases_at_a_step`, constructive, naming no step; found where the dependence is decided at each step). Where activity needs support, three outcomes of a loss are constrained, not exhaustive or exclusive: continuing unweakened means restored, still active after a loss without return means transformed, and fixed dependence means ended (`Web.consumed_ends_when_fixed`); in `Crossing` all three follow one break ([record](../results/records/becoming-dependence-dynamics.md)).

<a id="r-becoming-return-grades"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Return grades renewal: within `p` steps, at some later step, never. The grades are ordered; consumption is exactly a dependence that never returns (`Web.consumed_iff`), which restates the definition, and a condition whose dependences all return is not consumed (`Web.Restored.not_consumed`). A seasonal condition is renewed within two steps, not one; in `Slowing` recovery slows without bound, always returning with no cycle bounding it ([record](../results/records/becoming-return-grades.md); the stepwise reading is in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-becoming-stepwise-reading)).

<a id="r-becoming-finite-horizon"></a>
**Theorem** (the renewable process; the support-gated law with feedback). For every horizon `h`, two runs agree before `h` and then one renews and one consumes, so a reading fixed by `h` steps that includes every renewing run includes a consuming one (`Renewable.finite_reading_includes_consumption`); likewise with feedback in the support-gated law. The force lies in "every renewing run", which overdraws before `h`. Finite stretches in general are in [03](03-KNOWING.md) ([record](../results/records/becoming-finite-horizon.md)).

<a id="r-substitution-preserves-consumption"></a>
**Theorem** (World). On every web, a consumer stays consuming under every change of its own dependence from a step on exactly where its loss is witnessed by a dependence the change leaves, another's or its own before that step (`Web.consumingIn_kept_iff`). The support-gated law with a substitutable condition is an instance: where the focal formation draws beyond renewal from the start (both needs positive, the focal need within the sustaining one and met at the start), it stays active at every step by switching, it is still consuming, and the sustaining formation ends (`persists_consuming`). Substitution keeps the focal formation active without undoing its consumption; whole-run reading only ([record](../results/records/substitution-preserves-consumption.md)).

<a id="r-supported-renewal-recovery"></a>
**Theorem** (World). In every balanced flow a step of joint net giving raises a level by at least one, and where giving jointly exceeds taking at each step at which the level is not below its start, `m − level` steps bring it to at least `m` (`Flow.restores`). In the support-gated law a supported route of contribution restores the sustaining activity, with both unsupported the level stays, and two histories with the same later inputs recover or do not. Recovery does not guarantee maintenance ([record](../results/records/supported-renewal-recovery.md)).

<a id="r-renewing-consuming-formation"></a>
**Theorem** (periphery; `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`). Under a supplied wear law with no recovery, formation within renewal continues and keeps its capacity; formation persistently beyond the current rate has a bounded total, ceases and loses capacity, and what shares its conditions ceases with it, also where two parts each within renewal jointly exceed it. Consuming formation can form more at first, so a one-step count ranks it first; past that bound every continuation within renewal exceeds every one beyond. The wear law carries much of this; what a count of every realized formation event cannot separate is in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md) (`RenewingFormation`; [record](../results/records/renewing-consuming-formation.md)).

<a id="r-represented-boundary-against-dependence"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Over a region covering what a formation takes part in, renewal over the region is renewal at the step; over one covering what it requires, requirements not tightening, it keeps its own support (`Web.RenewingOver.keeps_own`) and can still undermine another (`Clearing`); omitting a required condition, its own draw can lose its own support (`Omitted.loses_own`). How a center represents the region is unmodelled ([record](../results/records/represented-boundary-against-dependence.md)).

<a id="generation-needs-renewal"></a>
### 3.2 Continued arising needs renewal, from beyond

Arising takes from conditions; unrenewed, a finite stock runs down and arising stops. Arising without end needs renewal, under dissipation from beyond any finite family.

**Definition.** A formation *arises* across a step where it is inactive before and active after; arising is *unceasing* where something arises at arbitrarily late steps (`Web.Arises`, `Web.Unceasing`). Arising is activity starting, not §1's generation; the records `unceasing-generation-needs-renewal` and `whole-persists-through-generation` are laws of arising that keep "generation" only in their names. That arising of an embodied relatum replenishes is [04's](04-LIFE.md#r-generation-is-replenishment).

<a id="r-unceasing-generation-needs-renewal"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Where each arising lowers some condition of a finite family wherever none rises (a supplied premise), unceasing arising leaves the family still from no step on, and where every rise is an active renewer's, renewing stops from no step on (`Web.Unceasing.activeRenewer_recurs`). Under balance, uptake at arbitrarily late steps needs replenishment at arbitrarily late steps. Where a quantity never rises and every arising lowers it, arising is not unceasing; a closed cycle sustains it, and a tide renews with nothing arising: unceasing arising is strictly stronger than recurring renewal ([record](../results/records/unceasing-generation-needs-renewal.md)).

<a id="r-more-needs-renewers"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Read *more* as: for every number, from some step on at least that many formations take at every step. Under balance, more without end needs replenishment with no eventual bound and, each formation giving at most a fixed amount, renewers with no eventual bound, though they can be absent at infinitely many steps (`Flow.More.replenishment_unbounded`, `Flow.More.renewers_unbounded`). Under a census, with activity decided, the present listing each formation once and only active formations taking, it forces unceasing arising (`Flow.More.unceasing`, through `Web.unceasing_of_growth`); without these it does not. The least take of one whole unit is needed: with takes that halve, takers more without end are balanced by one unit given a step (`Halving.takes_halve`, [§4](#r-least-loss-rest)) ([record](../results/records/more-needs-renewers.md)).

<a id="r-no-finite-part-bears-taking"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Under balance and dissipation, no finite family without repetition containing a family taken from at arbitrarily late steps is closed from any step on (`Flow.Dissipative.opens`); enclosed around, it keeps drawing in from beyond; if finitely many conditions are all there is, taking does not recur. Maintenance is such taking. Where exchange reaches infinitely many conditions dissipation is vacuous and a renewer can give while taking nothing ([record](../results/records/no-finite-part-bears-taking.md)).

<a id="r-continuing-self-fed-from-beyond"></a>
**Theorem** (World). A family is *fed from beyond* across a step where a formation takes from outside it and gives to a member. Under balance and dissipation, with equality of conditions decided, a finite family without repetition, enclosed around from some step on and taken from or rising at arbitrarily late steps, cannot from any step on be fed from nothing beyond it (`Flow.fed_from_beyond`); fed from beyond is then exactly not inward. No source need recur (`Lone`); no step of feeding is named. It needs natural-number amounts: with real amounts losses can shrink geometrically, and it fails ([record](../results/records/continuing-self-fed-from-beyond.md)).

<a id="r-far-conveying-runs-the-family-down"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Balanced and dissipated on across `N` steps each conveying from a member, a family's stock falls by at least `N`: from a stock `S` at most `S` stages are conveyed (`Flow.conveyed_loses`; [record](../results/records/far-conveying-runs-the-family-down.md)).

<a id="r-arising-needs-change"></a>
**Theorem** (World). A formation active exactly where supported, at a step and the next, does not arise across the step where every condition it required before is still required, its needs stay and every level stays (`Web.not_arises_of_unchanged`): arising needs a change in what is read. A disappearing requirement can admit it with nothing else changed ([record](../results/records/arising-needs-change.md)).

That support is gained only from the supported, and that an end with nothing supported absorbs, is [07's](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-support-from-the-supported).

<a id="r-support-passed-on"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Under balance, only active formations giving and activity needing support, with requirements never loosening, no formation supported at `K + 1` consecutive steps and support gained at arbitrarily late steps, it cannot be refuted that, for every `T` and `N`, support is gained after `N` through the giving of a supported formation that itself gained support after `T` (`Flow.support_passes_on`): support runs as a chain of gains, each giver a newer gainer ([record](../results/records/support-passed-on.md)).

<a id="wholes"></a>
### 3.3 Wholes of parts

A *whole of parts* is coincidence of activity, not constitution: a formation is a whole of some parts where it is active exactly while some part is (`Web.Whole`). Parts are supplied with each application; this is not [08's](08-METHOD.md) whole, everything in the one unfolding.

<a id="r-whole-persists-through-generation"></a>
**Theorem** (World). A whole arises only as a part arises. While the whole stays active from some step on: where parts are brief (none active at two steps `d` apart), no `d` steps pass without one arising; and where every part ends and the active ones are listed, that ever new parts arise cannot be refuted (`Whole.arises_within`, `Covered.needs_new`). A part that never ends (`Immortal`), or infinitely many active at once (`Countless`), lets a whole persist with nothing arising ([record](../results/records/whole-persists-through-generation.md)).

<a id="r-a-whole-carried-by-lasting-parts"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Conversely, where from `T` on a part arises within every `d` steps and lasts `d` steps, the whole is active from `T + d` on (`Web.Whole.persists_from`; [record](../results/records/a-whole-carried-by-lasting-parts.md)).

<a id="r-a-whole-of-every-part"></a>
**Theorem** (World). A whole of every part ends once one part ends, with no listing (`Web.AllWhole.ends_of_part`). Between it and the whole of some part lies the grade, the count of listed parts active, existing where their activity is decided, changing only as a part arises or ceases (`Web.Grade`); a whole of at least `j` parts arises and ceases only through a part, and is *diminished* where some parts are active and not all (`Web.diminished_iff`): of three rafters with one taken, two stand and the house does not ([record](../results/records/a-whole-of-every-part.md)).

A whole whose parts arise only through a family drawn beyond its renewal ends ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends)), and a pattern outlasting its carriers lasts only through passing ([04](04-LIFE.md#r-a-pattern-lasts-through-passing)).

## 4. Rest

A closed amount that keeps losing need not stop: a stock halving at every step never rests. Rest needs a least loss at each change, or the amount read off finitely many configurations. Under either, enclosure excludes endless change; openness is necessary for arising without end, not sufficient.

<a id="r-least-loss-rest"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World for the instance). Over any amounts (a cancellative preordered commutative monoid in which nothing (zero) is least, possibly dense): where an amount does not rise from `T` on, falls by at least `ε` at each step at which `P` holds, and `m` copies of `ε` are not within its value at `T`, steps at which `P` holds do not recur (`Amounts.rests`, also in unrefuted form). In Archimedean amounts a least loss above nothing suffices; with none a closed amount still approaches rest. Whole numbers are the instance through which the World's rest theorem is proved. Each premise is needed: halving stocks in dyadic and layered amounts never rest. Over finitely many configurations a loss above nothing needs a law that merges (`Law.loss_needs_merge`): rest belongs to descriptions that lump and dissipate ([record](../results/records/least-loss-rest.md)).

<a id="r-rest-from-a-finite-reading"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Read off configurations a list holds, an amount not rising from `T` on falls strictly at most as often as the list is long, over any amounts, with no least loss, Archimedean property, totality or decision (`Amounts.rests_of_finite_reading`); so along any process's run whose configurations a list holds, such falls come to rest. Recurring falls need a reading no list holds (`Halving.stock_unlisted`) ([record](../results/records/rest-from-a-finite-reading.md)).

<a id="r-merge-of-a-part-kept-by-the-rest"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; a law that merges nothing). Where a part and a rest together tell configurations apart, `k` configurations no two alike, their parts held by one list and their rests by another, number at most the product of the lists' lengths (`Law.distinct_within`), and so at every step of a law that merges nothing: narrowing the part to one configuration spreads the rest over at least `k` (`Law.Lossless.narrowing_spreads`). Where a law merges nothing and a part reads one value `p` after every step, if some configuration's part is not `p`, no list holds every configuration (`Law.Lossless.reset_unlisted`): the configuration type has unbounded capacity, which is not information gathered along one run; **Theorem** (any type; witness). In `Reset`, a one-bit part set to false at every step with the rest the history of what it held, the law merges nothing while the part merges, and no list holds the rest of any run (`Reset.kept_by_rest`). This is Landauer's (1961) count with Bennett's (1973) history; no energy, temperature or probability is read, so kT ln 2 is not derived ([record](../results/records/merge-of-a-part-kept-by-the-rest.md)).

**Theorem** (any type). Within finitely many configurations a law merges nothing exactly where every configuration returns ([01](01-ROOT.md#r-recurrence-without-premise)); a loss needs a merge and a part's merge is kept by the rest (above); a kept history never returns (`Law.withHistory_never_returns`), so no list holds a kept history's course (`Law.withHistory_unlisted`): a recorder of bounded size does not keep its whole history and go on.

**Reading** [AI reading, D65]. Irreversibility in computation, heat, extinction and forgetting is one merge of configurations, read through these laws; the laws are about a map on a type and say nothing of computation, heat or extinction, whose content lies only in finding the merge. As a thesis of unification this forbids little, so the laws are what is proved; Landauer's bound is measured (Bérut et al. 2012).

**Reading** [AI reading, D65]. Learners, archives and genomes are such recorders.

<a id="r-continued-change-needs-openness"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *The rest theorem.* In a world with censuses of what is active and of what stands, arising embodied, flow balanced and dissipative, and every step from some step on enclosed around what stands, rest from some step on cannot be refuted; neither arising nor ceasing recurs (`World.comes_to_rest_unrefuted`). Each premise is needed, by a witness without it; the world need not be finite, but finitely many stand and act at each step. Read the other way, with a census of what is active, every relatum embodied and flow balanced and dissipative, arising without end leaves no step from which every step is enclosed (`World.arising_opens`); with only arising embodied, a world can be enclosed at every step and arise without end (`World.rest_needs_standing`). The eight premises decide neither enclosure nor openness (`World.openness_contingent`), and openness with them does not give arising without end: a holder giving back at every step the unit it takes from each ground relatum meets the eight with no step enclosed, nothing arising or ceasing (`World.holding_world`); in `hatch`, every premise binding, rest cannot be refuted though the world is not finite (`World.hatch_rests_unrefuted`) ([record](../results/records/continued-change-needs-openness.md)).

The theorem says rest cannot be refuted, not when it comes: naming the step after which nothing arises gives LPO, [01's calibration](01-ROOT.md#counting) (`World.lpo_of_finite_ends`).

<a id="r-rest-theorem-in-any-amounts"></a>
**Theorem** (World). In any amounts, a family balanced (no amount made) and dissipated on (whatever gives back all it takes takes nothing) has a total that falls strictly at every change of a level, with no least loss (`World.InAmounts.change_lowers`); so with a census of the active relata, every relatum embodied, flow balanced and dissipative and every step from `T` on enclosed (*the enclosed premises*), where the census's total is read off configurations a list holds, rest from some step on cannot be refuted (`World.InAmounts.rests_unrefuted_enclosed`). **Theorem** (World; witness): along an amount that does not rise and falls strictly at every step, a standing passed between two relata meets the enclosed premises and never rests (`Pass.endless_keeps_from_rest`) ([record](../results/records/rest-theorem-in-any-amounts.md)).

<a id="r-rest-exactly-where-falls-stop"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Over any amounts, a family balanced and dissipated on stops changing, as far as can be refuted, exactly where its total's strict falls stop (`World.InAmounts.change_stops_iff`), and under the enclosed premises a world whose census total stops falling rests, unrefuted (`World.InAmounts.rests_unrefuted_enclosed_of_stops`). Rest over every world in given amounts follows from no endless fall, which a least fall in Archimedean amounts gives (`Amounts.worldsRest_of_noEndlessFall`), and excludes an amount falling at every step (`Amounts.not_endlessFall_of_worldsRest`), so dyadic and layered amounts each carry a world that never rests. The two coincide classically, with dependent choice; constructively this is not shown ([record](../results/records/rest-exactly-where-falls-stop.md)).

<a id="r-rest-by-balance"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). A count balanced across each step of a stretch and losing at least one more than it gains at each falls by at least the stretch's length (`Amounts.falls_by_stretch`); where marking is decided, some step within the count's value is unmarked, found by search; two balanced counts, the first starting within the second, compare at the end by their exchanges (`Amounts.le_by_exchange`). In denser amounts falling at every step need not end ([record](../results/records/rest-by-balance.md)).

<a id="r-least-loss-from-entropy-production"></a>

<a id="r-entropy-history-and-record"></a>

With no premise a family's history of exchanges never shrinks (`Flow.history_mono`); under balance an exchange with its reverse restores every level while the history counts both (`Flow.Balances.reverse_restores`). ## 5. Draw and margin

Whether a dependent can still form depends on how far levels stand above its needs. Two distances are kept apart: the margin against which a part's draw is read, the rest's exchange held as it was, and a regime's point of no return, its own giving responding to its level.

**Definition.** A *draw* on a condition is what is taken from it across a step, *giving* what is given to it. A part's *withheld levels* start where the flow starts and change by what it takes and gives with the part's exchange withheld (`Flow.WithheldLevels`; in natural numbers they need not exist; the laws below assume them). The *margin* of a part at a formation is how far the withheld levels at `t` meet its need: in a balanced flow the formation is supported at `t` exactly where, on every condition it requires, its need with all the part has taken from the condition before `t` is within the withheld level with all the part has given it before `t` (`Flow.Balances.supported_iff_within_margin`). That a part closes what could form only by a net draw beyond this margin, and opens only by a net give, is [07's](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-closing-needs-net-draw). A *regime* is a condition whose giving `g` is a law of what a standing draw `H` leaves: the next level is `(x − H) + g(x − H)`, the draw taken first, as much as there is (`RegimeMargin.step`).

**Assessment** (a simulated Allee yeast; assessed fish stocks). On measured data the margin with the rest's exchanges held is an identity, so a measurement tests the margin of a regime. In assessed fish stocks the frozen-rest margin over the net draw ranks the risk of a break but does not time it (§277: a third of those it put within five years fell, 47 of 149): the giving moves toward the draw and the draw follows the stock ([study §253](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#253-the-margin-as-a-measured-quantity-mq1), [§272](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#272-the-margin-of-a-regime-in-the-field-mq3), [§277](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#277-a-state-of-level-and-recent-giving-in-the-field-mq4)).

<a id="r-a-need-with-a-cap"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Where a need has a cap, levels keep a formation in its band where each required condition stands between need and cap; with the withheld levels and balance at the required conditions before `t`, exactly where the need with the part's cumulative take is at most the withheld level with its cumulative give, which is at most the cap with its cumulative take (`Flow.fitsBand_iff_within_margins_at`; [record](../results/records/a-need-with-a-cap.md)).

<a id="r-margin-of-a-regime"></a>
**Theorem** (the regime; any giving law, whole numbers). For one condition under a standing draw `H`: where giving falls short of the draw below `y`, gives nothing from nothing, meets it from `y` up to a top `hi` the step keeps, and `0 < H + y`, a level up to `hi` ends exactly where it starts below `H + y` (`RegimeMargin.ends_iff_below`): the point of no return is exact. Where giving stops below a need and the draw is positive, the point lies at least the draw above the need (`RegimeMargin.root_above_need`), and, `y` the least level meeting the draw, more than that exactly where giving at the need falls short of the draw (`RegimeMargin.beyond_draw_iff`); giving lowered by the regime raises it further ([record](../results/records/margin-of-a-regime.md)).

**Theorem** (the regime). Draws add, harms do not: with joint giving subadditive above the need and no greater than under either draw, the point's joint rise is at least the sum of the rises less one (`RegimeMargin.harms_superadditive`); two draws each held alone can end the condition together. The time to break lies in a band. They locate an end, ordering nothing ([record](../results/records/margin-of-a-regime.md)).

<a id="r-threshold-in-the-state-plane"></a>
**Theorem** (any fate over whole-number levels; the regime for its cases). *One law of exact thresholds.* Where a fate is down-closed in the level and read off an exact check, the least level up to a bound from which the check fails is exact (`RegimeMargin.leading_exact`); where one fate implies another, the implying fate's threshold is no higher (`RegimeMargin.leading_falls`). Every threshold below is its case. For giving that adjusts, reading what is left and the recent giving, rising in both and keeping a band up to `hi`, the runs from a level and a recent giving up to `hi` that rest below the draw are, for each such recent giving, those from levels below a least level, decided at step `3 hi + 3`, which does not rise as the recent giving rises (`RegimeMargin.Adjusted.falls_iff_below`); under the regime's own step it is `H + y`. Unless giving rises with what is left, no level separates the fates (`Notch`) ([record](../results/records/threshold-in-the-state-plane.md)).

**Thesis T1.** *Persistence certifies the joint draw.* Whatever persists through many renewal cycles of a condition it depends on, renewal stopping below a need, has had the joint draw on it, its own with every other drawer's, kept within the regime's margin (its point of no return, not the withheld-levels margin). *In the model*: that point is exact and, the draw positive, lies at least the draw above a need where giving stops below it ([above](#r-margin-of-a-regime)), and joint excess over a gated renewal ends it whatever each drawer's own draw ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)). *Forbids*: a condition below a need measured in advance returning with nothing given from beyond; a dependent active while its condition stood below its need. *Refuted by*: a stock driven below a need measured beforehand returning with no measured giving from beyond, or fates across a predicted point separated no better than by a null. *Evidence*: passed on held-out regimes of real yeast (93–100% each side, §266), a simulated Allee yeast (§253) and a commons with gated regrowth (§240; as the scope requires, failing for a patch read alone that a neighbour reseeded, holding for the two as one). Failed: the sharp need at the instrument's floor (§253); survival above the predicted need after a salt shock (§266, its first prediction: 7 of 8 died; the converse, not claimed: a shock changes the regime and its need); fish stocks under held catches, with a giving law the same test showed wrong (§272); the time to break past the fold (§314). Depensation is rarely detected in assessed fish stocks (Myers et al. 1995), so most lie outside the scope rather than against it; the need is measured apart from the fate it predicts, so the scope is not chosen afterwards. No field test of a need measured in advance has run ([study §240](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#240-a-commons-with-gated-renewal-the-first-falsifiable-test-on-learning-agents-cg1), [§253](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#253-the-margin-as-a-measured-quantity-mq1), [§266](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#266-the-margins-equation-of-state-against-held-out-regimes-mq2), [§272](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#272-the-margin-of-a-regime-in-the-field-mq3), [§314](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#314-the-margins-time-to-break-past-the-fold-in-the-lab-mq6)).

<a id="t2"></a>
**Thesis T2.** *The state of a renewable condition under a draw* is its level, its recent giving and one lag; with the draw they fix the next giving as far as anything measured does, the regime's step and the frozen rest its two ends. *Forbids*: a fourth variable removing a tenth of the residual variance in the median series; the level alone forecasting as well at five years; the lag's coefficients changing sign between series. *Evidence*: on 76 fish stocks recent giving added beyond the level, but the pair was not the whole state (§277, that verdict failed); on 69 more, with the lag nothing further was needed (§280, passed; the lag may be the assessment's own, the gain damping); the fates either side of the curve in the plane missed their frozen bound (§316: 0.39 from 5 stocks, of the predicted sign, lower bound 0.00). The proved laws reach the field's state only on the low side of its level law, below the peak, where the point of no return lies (§280, post hoc). In yeast past its fold (§314), residuals at constant dilution carry nothing day to day (lag-1 −0.14, passed); under a rising draw giving falls below the curve about three days before the break (post hoc). ## 6. Closures within the map

A continuation goes on only while what it requires is given.

<a id="r-feeding-closure"></a>
**Theorem** (World; no premise). A formation's continuation is *fed* by another at a step where the other takes part and gives to a condition the first then requires. What a continuation depends on is its *feeding closure*, the least region holding it closed under feeding (`Flow.fedClosed_within`); a formation beyond it gives nothing at any step to a condition a member then requires (`Flow.beyond_gives_nothing`). The closure is of formations, its links at any steps in any order. Feeding across steps, earlier giving carried, gives a closure containing the same-step one, strictly in `Provision`; amounts carry no mark of origin ([record](../results/records/feeding-closure.md)). Levels of closure and the links a closure needs are in [01](01-ROOT.md#r-levels-of-closure) ([links](01-ROOT.md#r-which-links-a-closure-needs)); what the derived extent does not reach, in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-derived-extent-limits).

The laws below say what ends a formation through that closure. Keeping conditions together, keeping a part not being renewal ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-conditions-kept-together)), what taking all excludes ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-taking-all-excludes)) and where a rise in a family is drawn from ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-raising-what-couples-in-draws-from-beyond)) are collective laws.

<a id="r-consumption-ends-with-feeders"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Either of two redundant feeders suffices; withholding both leaves no balancing path ([record](../results/records/consumption-ends-with-feeders.md)).

<a id="r-growth-constrained-fragmentation"></a>

<a id="r-where-a-rising-take-ends"></a>

Where renewal depends on what it renews (only active formations giving, activity needing support, every giver requiring the condition at a need of at least `m`) and the seeker stays active while the level is at least `m`, from a step within the window on every formation depending on the condition at such a need is inactive (`Flow.seeking_exhausts`). ## 7. Carried differences and loops

A change at one condition reaches another only through a later exchange that reads it. Where the rest follows a law, the course without an act is the course that law generates, and the most difference that can arrive grows by orders of response: through a loop of the couplings that go on it can outgrow every polynomial; with none a finite order is exact.

<a id="r-counterfactual-is-the-course"></a>
**Theorem** (World; balance; the law of exchange supplied). Where the rest follows one law of exchange reading what has stood, two balanced flows with the same formations present, starting alike, in which the part acts alike, have the same levels and exchange at every step (`counterfactual_fixed`): the counterfactual of an act is the course the law generates (`counterfactual_is_course`, for an act that itself reads what has stood and whose course is feasible). The generated flow balances exactly where the course is feasible (`ExchangeLaw.course_balances_iff`), and where every formation follows the law it is the course (`ExchangeLaw.course_unique`); a determined law steps beyond what a run shows exactly at the moves where it steps at all and the run never takes (`Run.beyond_shown_iff`). Where the law reads through the couplings, the comparison of two courses is decided within the responsive reach, neither the second flow nor the response supplied; with finitely many conditions and their equality decided, that coupling is the law's sensitivity. An infeasible act has no course ([record](../results/records/counterfactual-is-the-course.md)).

<a id="r-where-two-courses-rejoin"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Two courses, with and without an act, balanced at a condition in both, change from alike to apart there, or back, only across a step exchanging with it differently (`Flow.exchange_apart_of_change`); where the difference closes over a stretch, what the two exchanged with it then makes up the starting gap exactly (`Flow.gap_made_up`). With the same formations present, where the rest follows a law reading through its couplings and the part acts alike with the condition after the act, a difference closes only through a formation outside the part that the courses' couplings tell apart (`Flow.closes_through_coupling_apart`). With the same formations present, both courses balanced at the condition and at every condition into which a formation outside the part's responsive reach couples, those conditions alike at the start, and every formation outside the part exchanging alike at a step where the courses are alike through its couplings up to it (`RespondsThroughCoupling`), where no formation within the responsive reach takes from or gives to the condition in either course from step `s` on, the courses are alike there at a later step exactly where they are alike at `s` (`Flow.stays_beyond_response`): a difference neither opens nor closes. Coupling a difference apart does not make a formation close it; the course without the act compares, it is no standard ([record](../results/records/where-two-courses-rejoin.md)).

**Definition.** The *gap* of two amounts is what either has beyond the other. An exchange couples to a level with *strength* at most `k` where histories differing in that level alone change its take and give by at most `k` times the gap (`ExchangeLaw.RespondsWithin`). The *carried amount* is the course of the carrying law from the starting gaps, each formation outside the part giving what its strengths make of the levels so far (`carried`). A *loop* is a chain of couplings back to its start. Under *the standard bounds* the part gives no gap after the start, the starting gaps are at most `D`, and the strengths into each condition sum to at most `M` a step.

<a id="r-differences-below-threshold"></a>
**Theorem** (World). With balance on what couples into a formation and the exchange around it following its couplings (supplied), a difference in a stock coupled in by threshold only stays exactly as it was until the two webs' support diverges (`Flow.subthreshold_alike`); coupling it in by level lifts it (`Gauged`), and others responding to the stock break the premise (`Attracting`) ([record](../results/records/differences-below-threshold.md)).

<a id="r-how-much-difference-arrives"></a>
**Theorem** (World). In two balanced flows the difference of levels changes across a step by exactly the difference exchanged (`levels_step_exact`); with the same formations present, a condition's gap after a step is at most its gap before plus the gaps of what each formation exchanges with it (`gap_step`), a bound only, as exchanged differences can cancel. With every condition listed once: over every law of what has stood whose exchanges outside the part couple with strengths at most `k`, and every two balanced flows of it with the given formations present, the part's exchange differing by at most `σ` and the starts by at most `δ`, a condition's gap at `T` is at most `a` exactly where the carried amount there is at most `a` (`most_difference_arrives`). The carried amount is the most difference that can arrive, a worst case over laws, not a prediction for one pair; it is positive exactly along a route in time of positive strengths, and a held difference arrives without bound with no loop. Strengths are supplied bounds; a square law has none ([record](../results/records/how-much-difference-arrives.md)).

<a id="r-two-act-worlds-carry-the-difference"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Under one law of what has stood with strengths at most `k` outside the part, two feasible acts of the part generate two balanced worlds, both runs of the law of exchange (`TwoActs.balanced`); with every condition listed once and equality of conditions decided, the bound above holds of them and is attained (`most_difference_worlds`). The polynomial law below holds of them too (`gap_polynomial_iff_no_loop_worlds`), under its own hypotheses: the part's exchange gap after the start bounded by zero, one fixed list of formations present at every step, stationary strengths, and every condition listed once; for each bound on the starting gaps, one polynomial bounds the gap at `c` uniformly over every law with these strengths and every two feasible acts of it (`TwoActs`), exactly where no loop lies upstream of `c` ([record](../results/records/two-act-worlds-carry-the-difference.md)).

<a id="r-how-a-carried-difference-grows"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Order `j` of the carried amount is what routes of at most `j` responses carry, exact up to step `j`, and everywhere where no chain of `j + 1` couplings ends; with every condition listed and every coupling holding at every step, a finite order is exact from every starting gap exactly where no loop lies upstream (`finite_order_iff_no_loop`). Under the standard bounds the carried amount is at most `D(1 + M)^t`, attained by a self-loop (`carried_le_geomBound`). With stationary strengths, the same formations at every step, each condition listed once and the part acting alike in both flows, the gap at `c` is bounded by a polynomial in the steps, uniformly over every law with these strengths and its balanced flows and from every bound on the starting gaps, exactly where no loop lies upstream of `c` (`gap_polynomial_iff_no_loop`): a worst case, since a law exchanging nothing meets any such bound ([record](../results/records/how-a-carried-difference-grows.md)).

<a id="r-what-a-cut-loses"></a>
**Theorem** (World). For every schedule of couplings a cut at order `j` loses exactly what `j + 1` responses carry (`carried_eq_carriedWithin_add`); order `j` is exact from every start exactly where no chain of `j + 1` couplings in time order arrives; and where no loop of the couplings from step `N` on lies upstream, under the standard bounds the carried amount is at most `D` times a polynomial of degree `N` plus the number of conditions: faster growth goes only through a loop of the couplings that go on (`carried_le_polynomial_from`) ([record](../results/records/what-a-cut-loses.md)).

<a id="r-orders-as-a-sum-over-histories"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Traced back in time, the carried amount is a sum over routes of responses and sources; at horizon `j + 1` this sum over histories is order `j` (`routeSum_eq_carriedWithin`): a cut is a truncation; for one condition the orders are binomial sums of `(1 + M)^n` ([record](../results/records/orders-as-a-sum-over-histories.md)).

<a id="r-sums-over-histories-by-support-horizon-and-order"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). For every weighting with the laws of weights: one vanishing off a possibility sums only where it reaches; the transfer splits at every horizon; where alternatives commute, exact order `i` sums the courses with exactly `i` responses (`Weights.exactly_eq_courses`); what is carried depends only on the steps between. Grouping by order needs commuting alternatives (`HistorySums.grouping_needs_commuting`). Counting weights do not cancel: no interference ([record](../results/records/sums-over-histories-by-support-horizon-and-order.md)).

<a id="r-how-loops-form"></a>
**Theorem** (World). The loops formed by step `T + 1` are those formed by `T` and those through a coupling acting at `T` that closes a chain (`Closing.forms_by_closing`). Up to `T` the carried amount reads only couplings acting before `T`, so before a loop forms growth is polynomial and the amounts do not show whether one will (`Closing.Onset`). With strengths read from the levels so far, which loops form and when is read off the unfolding ([record](../results/records/how-loops-form.md)).

<a id="r-loops-within-what-a-law-reads"></a>
**Theorem** (World). An unread level couples with strength zero: loops lie within what a law reads (`not_onLoop_unread`). For every law reading a stock only below a threshold (`ReadsBelow`), two balanced courses of it with the same formations present, the part acting alike, apart at the start only in that stock, the stock standing at or above its threshold in the first at every step before `T`, are alike elsewhere up to the break, the stock higher by the raise: the distance to the break is carried nowhere (`break_carried_nowhere`). **Theorem** (World; witness): in the constructed draw (`Thresholds.Draw`), while the stock stands above the threshold the draw gives as its standing law, coupled to the stock with strength zero and with no loop through it, while every bound on the strengths of the draw itself puts the stock on a loop of one (`Thresholds.Draw.forms_at_break`) ([record](../results/records/loops-within-what-a-law-reads.md)).

<a id="r-whether-a-loop-closes-a-difference"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World for the ring read on flows). A difference around a loop is the course of a return map. Where every link gives on at least what it receives the difference never falls, so a loop closes a difference only through a link giving back less; a map below the identity off zero closes within `d` turns, exactly, and merges. With whole-number factors a loop closes at once, keeps, or exceeds every bound, never closing gradually (`LoopReturn.nothing_between`). A self-loop of strength `M` has factor `1 + M`; read on balanced flows (World; witness), on a ring of two or more conditions with one positive strength on every link and a positive starting gap, one plus the product of strengths is strictly below a turn (`LoopReturn.Ring.bound_strict`) ([record](../results/records/whether-a-loop-closes-a-difference.md)).

<a id="r-how-often-couplings-act"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Under the standard bounds the most difference that can arrive is at most `D·(1 + M)^a`, `a` counting the steps at which anything couples (nothing coupling at the others), attained by a self-loop (`carried_le_count`). Each return at least doubles what reached it, so frequent returns outgrow every polynomial, while a self-loop coupling at the sparse steps 0, 1, 3, 7, … recurs yet stays polynomial (`Frequency.Sparse.polynomial`) ([record](../results/records/how-often-couplings-act.md)). Around a loop coupling at every step the pace is exponential both ways, and under a draw exceeding everything drawn before what stands gives no warning: these laws are [05's](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace).

**Calibration.** That every schedule settles, each coupling recurring or stopping, is exactly the dichotomy `RecursOrStops`, which gives LPO (`settled_iff_recursOrStops`): settling is deciding whether a coupling acts again. That it is Σ⁰₂ excluded middle is cited, not proved.

<a id="r-a-coupling-saturated-by-what-it-has-carried"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Where strengths read off what has passed through a coupling are zero once `A` has passed and `A` has passed by `N`, the coupling stops by `N` and a finite order is exact (`Saturation.stops`); a non-rising response stops exactly where it reaches zero; floored at one it recurs. Settling at a known step without that zero needs LPO, not stated here ([record](../results/records/a-coupling-saturated-by-what-it-has-carried.md)).

<a id="r-repeated-disturbance"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Under the standard bounds, except that the part's gaps are at most `E` at `d` disturbed steps and none at the others, and with `a` as above, the carried amount is at most `(D + E d)(1 + M)^a` (`carried_le_disturbed`): each disturbance adds what later couplings multiply ([record](../results/records/repeated-disturbance.md)).

<a id="r-runaway-distance-uncoupled"></a>
**Theorem** (World). For every policy through a formation's couplings, the rest of the exchange fixed and balance on what couples in, starts differing only in a stock coupled in by threshold only stay alike through the couplings while the first supports the formation; one that has used the larger stock has already lost support (`Flow.policy_uses_after_failure`). Where that stock is the only requirement that may fail up to a horizon, some raise of it is alike through the couplings and supports the formation to the horizon (`Web.break_not_bounded`): how far off the break is does not couple in. Coupled by level, every raise passes at once ([record](../results/records/runaway-distance-uncoupled.md)). Critical slowing down before a condition's own end is in [03](03-KNOWING.md#r-slowing-down-before-an-own-end).

**Reading** [AI reading, D25, D39, D43, D45]. An act has two counterfactuals, the rest fixed and the rest responding; neither is a second world: each is the law's step beyond its history. The orders are structurally a sum over histories, a cut a Neumann remainder. In the constructed draw the blindness before the break and the loop's forming are one event seen from two sides.

## 8. Regulation and reserves

A level held from both sides settles only where the response does not overshoot; a rule reading late needs room; a reserve defers an end without escaping it. Counted work in protocols is in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-pending-task-context-applicability).

<a id="r-two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading"></a>
**Theorem** (World). With balance at the condition, the joint exchange departs from evenness by at most the gap to `m` on each side exactly where the next level lies between `m` and the present one: the bound is exactly no overshoot (`Flow.withinGap_iff_between`); within it, opposing, the level settles at `m`; beyond it an opposing exchange crosses and can oscillate for ever ([record](../results/records/two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading.md)).

**Theorem** (any type; rules over whole numbers). A rule reading `ℓ` steps late keeps no band `[a, b]` with `0 < a` narrower than `2(ℓ + 1)d` against rests moving the level by at most `d` a step, attained by the late return (`Regulation.no_narrower_band`). **Theorem** (World; balance at the condition). With `a ≤ b`, `0 < a`, `0 < M` and the floor `(ℓ + 1)M ≤ b + 1`, every balanced run of a relay reading `ℓ` steps late, from every start below `a` held for its first `ℓ + 1` steps, settles in the band `[a, b]` exactly where `a + (ℓ + 1)M ≤ b + 1` (`Regulation.relay_threshold`; [record](../results/records/two-sided-regulation-and-late-reading.md)).

<a id="r-regulation-from-one-side-and-locally"></a>
**Theorem** (World). With balance, where the joint exchange gives no more than it takes below `m`, a level below `m` never returns to it, gated renewal being the case (`Flow.never_returns_below`). Where at each condition of a family the joint exchange stays within the gap to its own level and opposes departure from it at every step from `T`, the family's stock is the total of those levels from the step by which every condition has settled, nothing reading the total (`Flow.stock_settles`); opposition alone permits overshoot ([record](../results/records/regulation-from-one-side-and-locally.md); tolerance: [05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-tolerance-monotone-across-laws)).

<a id="r-how-fast-a-rule-may-change"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). A two-level course, the rule changing only at reading steps, is a run of one law; a rule that never reads is unreached; a fast difference reaches the rule only at a reading step (`Pace.rule_until_reading`). Given two values, a stretch of `L` steps fixes every pair of held-rule courses with at most `N` states exactly when `L ≥ 2N − 1` (`Pace.pace_law`) ([record](../results/records/how-fast-a-rule-may-change.md)).

<a id="r-reserve-defers-ending"></a>
**Theorem** (World). A reserve does not rise while its formation is unsupported and falls where it is active then; the zero reserve is exactly activity needing support; a held condition can be one (a fox burning its fat). With its activity decided at each step, a formation unsupported from some step on is active over any later stretch at most as often as its reserve (`Web.Carries.lasts`). Where the reserve decays, dormancy is bounded, and along a chain of embodied links, each requiring the one before at a positive need from `T` on with the first ended from `T`, coextinction runs down the chain link by link (`Web.Carries.chain_ends`); without decay each link ends given excluded middle, with no bound ([record](../results/records/reserve-defers-ending.md)).

**Calibration.** Without decay, a last active step for every never-supported formation with a reserve gives LPO (`Web.lpo_of_ending`): naming when a deferred end comes is deciding whether a run is active again.

<a id="r-dormancy-over-a-stretch"></a>
**Theorem** (World). Bounded dormancy needs its premises only over the stretch (`Web.ends_within_on`). With an ending made final as a separate premise (after the stretch, inactive with no reserve, the formation neither arises nor regains one), support returning after the reserve is spent finds no dependent (`Web.late_return_finds_none`); exhaustion alone does not give it. A reserve spanning the gap bridges it ([record](../results/records/dormancy-over-a-stretch.md)).

## 9. Capture

A selection rewarding a target raises its carriers' share; where the rule of selection is read from the share it selects, the loop holds itself; where every level of the loop reads only the share, a loss it does not read leaves it locked however large; where its readers are drawn from those it selects, who reads decides whether, and when, a reading of the loss turns it; and where the capacity to shape the rule is carried from parent to offspring, what selects it at a step is the part of what it gives that comes back to the shapers alone, net of its cost. That a target needing ever more cannot rest, and that "above all others" never rests for two, is [03's](03-KNOWING.md#r-seeking-boundary), with the boundary of enough under undetermined laws ([03](03-KNOWING.md#r-the-boundary-of-enough-under-undetermined-laws)). Markets as such a regime are in [05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#markets).

<a id="r-target-capture"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Where target carriers each leave at least `a` offspring and other parents at most `b < a`, both kinds present, the carriers' offspring stand to the others' strictly more than the carriers to the others (`share_rises`), read as shares where offspring carry the target (a premise). Where the next configuration depends on the rule, not the state, a perturbed state is re-absorbed in one step (`Law.perturbation_reabsorbed`). **Theorem** (World): where what stands is listed and runs down at every step from `T` on, descent does not recur (`Flow.descent_exhausted`) ([record](../results/records/target-capture.md)).

<a id="r-capture-through-the-rule"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). On a two-level course, let selection not lower a target's share under a rule favouring it (`Raises`) and the rule read at each reading step be a monotone function of the share (`ReadsShare`). With the start's rule and every rule read favouring, the share falls at no step and the course is locked from the step after its first reading step (`RuleCapture.favouring_locks`). Locked, neither share nor rule falls; a perturbation keeping the rule's direction is absorbed at the next reading step; where the reading favours at every share every course locks from its second reading step, so only a change of the law, of how the rule reads or of the selection leaves. That actual rules read the share is the premise. **Theorem** (any type; witness): a perturbation reversing the rule's direction, held past the threshold, is not absorbed: from the step after it neither share nor rule rises, and the rule after the first reading step is below the one before (`RuleCapture.Reversed.not_absorbed`). The lock belongs to how the rule reads, not the share it favours ([record](../results/records/capture-through-the-rule.md)).

<a id="r-two-rules-lock-together"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Two rules each read monotonically from one share are read so together, and where selection under both favouring does not lower the share and both favour at every share, from a start whose two rules favour the pair locks from the step after the first reading step (`RuleCapture.pair_locks`; [record](../results/records/two-rules-lock-together.md)).

<a id="r-a-reading-against-the-share-does-not-ratchet"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Where the rule is read against the share, selection under favouring rules does not lower it, and the rules along the course favour at every step, a later reading's rule is not above an earlier one's (`RuleCapture.against_not_rising`): the rise the lock rests on does not form ([record](../results/records/a-reading-against-the-share-does-not-ratchet.md)).

**Definition.** 元吸引子 (yuán xīyǐnzǐ, meta-attractor): a loop of selection whose rule and whose reading of the rule are both formed from the share it selects, nothing in it reading what lies beyond the share.

<a id="r-closed-capture"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). With the fast configuration the share and what lies beyond it, where neither the next share nor the rule read reads the beyond (`Unread`), courses alike in share and rule stay alike, exactly then along every schedule (`ClosedCapture.courses_alike_iff_unread`). Where each step adds at least one beyond, selection does not lower the share under a favouring rule, the rule is read monotonically from the share and favouring is kept upward, from a locked configuration the loss passes every bound while the course stays locked and neither share nor rule falls below the start (`ClosedCapture.lock_outlives_loss`); a reading favouring at no loss above a bound unlocks the course at the step after every reading step past it (`ClosedCapture.turned_by_loss`): a sufficient way out. Where the reading too is formed monotonically from the share, a change of the reading of any size is absorbed at the next reading step ([record](../results/records/closed-capture.md)).

<a id="r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). *Readers drawn from the selected.* Read the rule as the pool of its readers' readings, each carrier among the readers reading it as `u` and each other as `v`, the readers counted as the population counts them (`ClosedCapture.Drawn.pool`). The rule read is then a monotone function of the share exactly where `v` is not above `u` (`Drawn.pool_reads_iff`), so within the pool `ReadsShare` is one comparison of two readings. Where `u` is not above `v` the pool reads against the share (`Drawn.pool_against`); where who reads is itself a monotone function of the share, it still reads the share (`Drawn.pool_through`). Against a mirrored reading the counts decide: the pool favours the carriers exactly where they are at least as many as the others (`Drawn.mirror_favours_iff`, `Drawn.mirror_disfavours_iff`). Now let the others alone read the loss, once it reaches a bound (`Thinned.lossPool`). At a reading step at which the rule in force and the rule read favour alike, the course is locked in that direction for good (`ClosedCapture.agreeing_stays`). With the others' late reading the mirror of the carriers', and the rule in force favouring the carriers, the lock is kept for good exactly where the carriers are at least as many as the others when the loss is read (`Thinned.kept_iff`); with the rule in force favouring the others, the course is turned for good exactly where the others are at least as many (`Thinned.turned_iff`). **Theorem** (witness): one carrier to eight others under a rule favouring the carriers four to one, the loss rising by one a step. The same readers reading the same loss lock the course the other way when they read it from the start, and leave it locked for good when they first read it at step 3 (`Thinned.inTime_turned`, `Thinned.late_kept`), though the late reading reads beyond the share (`Thinned.late_reads_loss`). The premise is moved to who reads, not removed: that actual rules are pools of readers so drawn, each kind reading at least as favourably for its own kind, is not shown ([record](../results/records/readers-drawn-from-the-selected.md)).

<a id="r-shaping-the-rule-selected-by-what-comes-back"></a>
**Theorem** (counts). *The capacity to shape the rule, selected by what comes back to it.* On `RuleCapture`'s counts let the carriers be shapers: shaping gives `s` to the shapers alone and `p` to every member alike and costs each shaper `c`, so that each shaper leaves `a + s + p` offspring and each other `a + c + p`, `a` positive (`RuleShaping.shaped`). At a step the rule favours the shapers (weakly) exactly where `c ≤ s`, whatever `p` (`RuleShaping.favours_iff`, `RuleShaping.favours_alike`); a larger `p` pulls the rule toward even, not higher where `c ≤ s` and not lower where `s ≤ c` (`RuleShaping.shared_lowers`, `RuleShaping.shared_raises`), and a smaller cost raises it (`RuleShaping.lower_c_raises`). With nothing coming back and a cost, the shapers' share falls strictly at every step, the shapers present (`RuleShaping.nothing_back_falls`); with no cost to them alone and nothing coming back, selection leaves the share where it is (`RuleShaping.neutral_steady`). Selection by rules in turn is selection by their product (`RuleShaping.select_thenBy`, `RuleShaping.orbit_held`), so across `m` steps before anything comes back and `n` steps with it, the shapers present, their share does not fall exactly where `(a + c + p)^m (a + c + p)^n ≤ (a + p)^m (a + s + p)^n`, and rises exactly where the inequality is strict (`RuleShaping.stretch_iff`, `RuleShaping.stretch_lt_iff`); only how many steps of each kind matters. One more step before anything comes back never helps the shapers, and one more step with it never hurts them where it covers the cost (`RuleShaping.wait_not_more`, `RuleShaping.back_longer_not_less`). On the reading of what comes back from the shapers' share at each reading step (`RuleShaping.byBack`), the rule read reads the share exactly where what comes back is monotone in it (`RuleShaping.byBack_reads_iff`), so there `ReadsShare` is a statement about what comes back; monotone and covering the cost at every share, the loop locks (`RuleShaping.covering_locks`). At a threshold, what comes back `hi` where the shapers are at least as many as the others and `lo` where they are fewer, with `lo < c < hi`, the course tips: from a start above, the rule in force stays the one read above and the shapers' share rises strictly at every step; from a start below it falls strictly at every step (`RuleShaping.threshold_rises`, `RuleShaping.threshold_falls`); with `lo ≤ c ≤ hi` neither side is left (`RuleShaping.threshold_from_above`, `RuleShaping.threshold_from_below`). That offspring carry their parent's capacity, and that what comes back is read from the share, are premises; `s` and `p` are constants of the rule. **Witnesses**: with `s + p = 5` to each shaper and a cost of one, the rule favours the shapers where two of the five come back to them alone and disfavours them where none does (`RuleShaping.Split`); with `a = 1`, `c = 1`, `s = 2`, two steps before and three with what comes back lower the shapers' share with nothing shared and raise it with one to every member, so across a stretch the shared part can turn the verdict (`RuleShaping.SharedFlips`); with `a = 2`, `c = 1`, `s = 2`, one step before and two with it raise the share, two and two or one and one lower it, and after five steps before, seven with it lower it and eight raise it (`RuleShaping.Timing`) ([record](../results/records/shaping-the-rule-selected-by-what-comes-back.md)).

<a id="r-capture-by-counted-choices"></a>
**Theorem** (World for the selection laws; counts for the count laws). *A chooser reading what couples in, its rule read from the count of its choices.* The choice made from a fold of the prefix of what couples into a formation, whatever the fold and whatever the choice, is a selection through the formation's couplings up to each step (`ChoiceCountCapture.selectsThrough`), so a part acting only beyond the coupling closure does not change it, under the hypotheses of the blindness law (`ChoiceCountCapture.unchosen_beyond_coupling`); choosers sharing their folds select through the pooled couplings (`ChoiceCountCapture.pooled_selectsThrough`, `ChoiceCountCapture.pooled_unchosen_beyond`). This part is near-definitional. On `RuleCapture`'s counts let a chooser over two policies take the first where its rule weighs it at least as much as the second, that policy's count rising by one (`ChoiceCountCapture.choose`), the rule read from the counts weighted by a standing score, `κc` for the first policy and `κo` for the second (`ChoiceCountCapture.weighted`): choosing by a favouring rule raises the share it reads, and the reading is monotone in the share (`ChoiceCountCapture.choose_raises`, `ChoiceCountCapture.weighted_reads`). Under a standing score, with the rule in force read from the counts at the start, along every schedule of reading the threshold `κo · other ≤ κc · carrier` divides the counts into two regions the course never leaves: at or above it the first policy is taken at every step and neither the share nor the rule falls, also where the score favours the second policy (`κc < κo`); below it the second policy is taken at every step (`ChoiceCountCapture.counted_locks`, `ChoiceCountCapture.counted_chooses`, `ChoiceCountCapture.counted_locks_down`, `ChoiceCountCapture.counted_chooses_down`). Joined to a coordinate the loop does not read and that rises at every step, the locked course outlives every bound on it (`ChoiceCountCapture.counted_outlives_loss`). Counted instead from the posterior at the time of choosing, the counts scaled with the posterior's weights added, a score favouring the second policy lowers the first policy's share strictly at every step (`ChoiceCountCapture.posterior_count_falls`, `ChoiceCountCapture.posterior_course_falls`, from `RuleShaping.falls_strictly`). **Theorem** (witnesses): three choices to one keep the first policy against a score weighing the second twice as much, and from one to one the score decides for good; one count moved, three to two, locks the other way; a rule an older score left in force carries counts above the threshold the other way, and a change of score read one step late leaves the old lock in place where read at once it would turn; counted from the posterior, three to one falls at the first step while the weighted rule still favours the first policy, by a tie, and favours the second from the second step (`ChoiceCountCapture.Witness`; [record](../results/records/capture-by-counted-choices.md)).

<a id="r-rules-that-change-themselves-in-the-grammar"></a>
**Theorem** (root; on processes and the two-level step). *Rules that change themselves, read in the grammar.* A move of a process is a relation, `s` conditioning `t` by the move (`moveRel`): where every move is possible, a process going alike by a map has the map's class constant under every move (`constant_of_goAlike`), and where the process is determined the converse holds (`goAlike_of_constant`). For a state going on under a rule that itself goes on, the state's class is constant under every move, and is its own core under every move, exactly where the rule makes no difference to the state (`adaptive_constant_iff`, `adaptive_core_iff`); where two rules take one state by one move to different states, the core of the state's class under that move tells the two configurations apart (`adaptive_core_apart`): the rule is read in the state's regularity. In closed capture the class of share, rule and step count is constant under the two-level step along every schedule exactly where nothing beyond the share is read (`ClosedCapture.constant_iff_unread`). Under the premises of `lock_outlives_loss`, from a locked start the line under the class of being locked or not never ends and each of its steps alters the loss (`ClosedCapture.lock_line`): the course stays locked while the coordinate it does not read changes at every step. Along a two-level step whose reading favours at no loss past a bound, with a reading step past the bound, the line under favouring or not from a favouring start is not unending (`ClosedCapture.loss_ends_line`), one sufficient way out, as in T7. In the witness, read from the share alone the line under being locked never ends, and read from the loss the line under favouring ends at the first step (`ClosedCapture.Beyond.closed_line`, `ClosedCapture.Beyond.open_line_ends`). This is capture through the rule; the reading formed from the share (`both_reads`) and the lock's own laws are used or left as they are, not restated in the grammar ([record](../results/records/rules-that-change-themselves-in-the-grammar.md)).

**Thesis T7.** *A measure read from what it selects outlives its refutation*: a metric whose rule and reading are formed from the share it selects is kept by its selectors however large a loss it does not read grows, and is dropped only through a change in what the loop reads (a reader outside the share, a loss entering it, a new reading), not on evidence alone. *In the model* only invariance under what the loop does not read, and the lock outliving a growing unread coordinate, are proved; a reading of the loss is one sufficient way out, not shown the only one. With the rule the pool of readers drawn from those it selects, its being read from the share is proved exactly where each kind reads at least as favourably for its own kind; a loss read only by those it disfavours, against a rule in force favouring the carriers, then keeps the lock for good exactly where the carriers are at least as many when it is read ([readers drawn from the selected](#r-readers-drawn-from-the-selected)). Evidence, refutation and institutions are unformalized, and the unread coordinate is a loss by reading. That actual metrics are so formed is conjectured. *Forbids*: such a metric dropped by its selectors on evidence alone; a reform of the reading alone outlasting the next reading; a metric whose loss is read only by those it disfavours, dropped once the selection has left them no more than its carriers among its readers, with nothing else changed. *Refuted by*: a metric whose rule and reading were documented in advance as set by those it ranks, dropped by those selectors while a coding fixed in advance finds no new reader outside the ranked share, no change in what the ranking reads and no loss entering their share. *Evidence*: none frozen; a test needs a coding of reform events fixed before the cases are read.
