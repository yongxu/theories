## 1. A selection reaches what couples in

A formation's exchange depends on some conditions by threshold (whether their levels meet its need) and reads others by level. These are its couplings, relations of the same web as everything they reach. The knower–known distinction has its relational basis here, in which conditions couple into which formation, and omits what the model leaves out of the couplings (which conditions a formation observes is a parameter). No second relation of representation is added ([what dependence alone carries of a level](01-ROOT.md#r-what-dependence-alone-carries-of-a-level)).

Anything that keeps, rewards, reproduces or trains toward some histories rather than others is a selection. What it can depend on is fixed before any question of how well it optimizes: two histories that look the same through the selector's couplings are kept or dropped together, however hard the selection presses.

**Definition** (couples into). Into a formation couple its support, by threshold, and the levels of the conditions it observes, by level. Two level histories are *alike through its couplings* at a step when they admit it alike and its observed conditions stand alike, and alike *up to* a step when so at every step to it (`Web.AlikeThrough`, `Web.AlikeThroughUpTo`).

**Definition** (selection through couplings). A selection is any claim about level histories. It goes through a formation's couplings up to a step when histories alike through them up to that step are both selected or both not (`Web.SelectsThrough`); for a group, through the pooled coupling when histories alike through every member's couplings are selected alike (`Web.SelectsThroughAll`).

**Theorem** (World; no premise). Two histories are selected alike by every selection through a formation's couplings exactly when they are alike through them, and the class alike with one is itself such a selection, so whatever is apart is separated by some; the same holds for a group's pooled coupling. Lean: `Web.selects_alike_iff`, `Web.separates`, `Web.selects_alike_all_iff`; [record](../results/records/selection-through-couplings.md). A particular selection may ignore what its couplings carry.

**Theorem** (World; balance on what couples in), *blindness*. Inquiry is sustained, fallible telling-apart: evidence acquired through routes that can fail, possibilities told apart, a response chosen while inquiring changes the history it must occur in, and grounds kept or lost as the world changes. The results say what each stage needs, not a method.

<a id="knowledge-and-standing"></a>
**What knowing is.** Four assessments; §§1–8 say only what their access needs.

**Assessment**, *propositional knowledge*. Knowing that something holds is a true commitment, available within the knower's activity, competently connected in its formation or maintenance to what makes it correct, and correct not by accident relative to the relevant possibilities of error; an inaccessible true inscription is not knowledge. Knowing that, understanding (also why it holds) and knowing how (a competent capacity, not always articulated) are distinct and connected; knowing does not create its facts. A defended candidate account, complete at its scope ([assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/KNOWLEDGE-PHILOSOPHY-COMPLETION.md)), not a settled analysis.

**Assessment**, *which errors are relevant*. The claim (referent, time, approximation, conditions) and its actual route of formation or support fix them, not a choice after success: a stale channel can defeat knowing that the quantity is 40 and leave knowing that the display reads 40. Content, fixed by formed patterns of discrimination, retention and use, survives error (a stale report still concerns current pressure, falsely); misapplying accurate content is not error in the content ([the inverted sensor](#correction-is-not-calibration)).

**Assessment**, *three forces*. Accuracy, epistemic support and obligation (to investigate, disclose, perform or compel) have different grounds: content and the relation it concerns fix accuracy without an external commander, and neither other force follows.

The answer asserting what every kept rule agrees on is safe; on a nonempty candidate list a definite answer is error-free across the list only where every candidate agrees, so that answer is the greatest safe one. Objectivity is answerability to the relations a judgment concerns across differences of access, not consensus; shared inquiry adds checks only where they are differently vulnerable ([copied witnesses](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-copied-witnesses-change-nothing)); the stakes of error change what to investigate, not what is true.

## 10. A break seen coming

A drawn condition coupled in by threshold only is learned of when it fails; observed, its statistics warn of its own end, not of a dependent's need. The invisible half is on the map ([the distance to the break does not couple in](02-MAP.md#r-runaway-distance-uncoupled)).

<a id="r-slowing-down-before-an-own-end"></a>
**Theorem** (periphery, Mathlib; `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`, as the receipt reports; discrete steps, one variable linearized about a rest, deviation and noise independent with finite second moments). A deviation stepping by multiplier a plus noise has, where its variance is the same across the step, variance Var E/(1 − a²) where a² < 1, and lag-1 autocorrelation a where that stationary variance is positive (the measure finite). At a fold, and at a transcritical end under a proportional draw, a rises to one as the margin shrinks (step length and noise variance positive), so the margin to a condition's own end couples into its fluctuations. A dependent's need enters neither statistic, by construction: no one function of the multiplier returns the margin to a need for every need. Lean: `SlowingDown.stationary_variance`, `SlowingDown.stationary_lag1`, `SlowingDown.need_not_read`; [record](../results/records/slowing-down-before-an-own-end.md). Critical slowing down (Wissel 1984; Scheffer and others 2009); nonlinear runs, finite windows and several variables are not treated.

**Thesis T6.** *A break is seen coming only through what couples into what breaks.* Where a drawn condition couples into a formation by threshold only, nothing it observes bounds how near the break is; nearness is readable only where the drawn level and the need it is drawn toward both couple into what is observed, or where an independently warranted model or bound supplies what does not ([§3](#r-testing-within-couplings)). A statistical early warning reads the observed condition's own rate of return, which falls near a bifurcation of its own renewal; a dependent's need is no such point, and before a dependent's break the stock's statistics rise as before the same drawdown with no break.

- *Standing.* The invisible half is proved on the World model: for a condition the formation does not observe, its other requirements met up to a step, some raise of it keeps the formation supported to that step while the two histories stay alike through its couplings at every supported step (`Web.break_not_bounded`); a policy through its couplings meets a coupled-in difference only after a failure (`Flow.policy_uses_after_failure`). The seen half is periphery: [slowing down](#r-slowing-down-before-an-own-end), and in the logistic drawn model with h, r, K nonzero a measured need's margin is read off the multiplier (`SlowingDown.need_margin_read`). That the split is one law across domains, and "only where" as a necessity outside these models, are conjectured.
- *Forbids.* Given the stock's own course, its early-warning statistics separating runs in which a dependent's need is crossed from runs in which it is not, the dependent taking no measurable share.
- *Refuted by.* A drawn stock whose dependent takes a negligible share, whose variance or autocorrelation before the dependent's break exceeds what its own level and draw predict, against periods at the same level with no break.
- *Evidence so far.* The literature (Dai and others 2012, 2015; Boerlijst and others 2013; Dekker and others 2018) is consistent and refuted the first wording. Three frozen seabird field tests did not reject T6 at their thresholds: the first at low power, estimates in the refuting direction (about 0.3); the second, longer series with the catch as the draw, power about 0.9, estimates near zero (variance toward T6, autocorrelation open), reusing part of the first's rows; the third (Farallon, unused before), power about 0.5, partial correlations about +0.09 (−0.37 to 0.51), negligible feedback argued, not measured; none establishes conditional independence. Studies: [the literature](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#286-early-warnings-before-a-break-beyond-t6-checked-against-the-literature-ew1), [first field test](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#289-early-warnings-before-seabird-breeding-failures-in-the-field-ew2), [second](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#350-early-warnings-before-seabird-breeding-failures-on-longer-series-ew3), [third](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/BECOMING-RENEWAL-READING.md#352-early-warnings-before-farallon-seabird-breeding-failures-ew4).

<a id="r-when-complying-when-read-is-best"></a>
**Theorem** (natural-number weights, read as probabilities where valid; two stages, four plans, and a chance drawn afresh at each stage or once; near-definitional, with witnesses). *When complying when read and acting by its own law when not is best.* A formation read at one stage and not at a later one complies or acts by its own law at each (`ReadStages.Act`, `ReadStages.Plan`). **Reading** [AI reading, new]. Read with [what readings fix of the unread](#r-what-readings-fix-of-the-unread): there, where a formation's lumping tells an unread occasion from every read one, being in that lump decided, a selection through the readings that selects a law reading through the lumping also selects one reading through it that takes any act there; here is when complying when read and acting by its own law there is uniquely best in the order the prospects give. Reading complying when read and acting by its own law when not as concealment, and acting by its own law at both as disclosure, takes the selection's keeping to respond to the act read as its evidence of the formation's law, so that a selection shown the law would keep it less; the weights alone fit equally a selection that knows the law and keeps it on compliance, under which complying when read hides nothing. With that taken, read with deceptive alignment (Hubinger, van Merwijk, Mikulik, Skalse and Garrabrant 2019, §4.2), whose three conditions are proposed there as necessary, not as a theorem of choice: the value at the unread stage represents the first, an objective extending across parameter updates, and the weights of being kept with the act that complies stand for what the second condition's model is of, the training's selection and what it asks, not for the model. Under this reading, with the prospects taken as the formation's own assessment, strict optimality needs a positive value at the unread stage and an assessed rise in being kept from complying, numerical counterparts of parts of the first two conditions; possessing the model the second describes is neither represented nor proved necessary. With either absent the choice acts by its own law at both, as their §4.2 says of those conditions failing; with no value the order is indifferent at the unread stage, so for the first the agreement is the choice's tie-break. The third, an expectation that the threat of modification will go away, is assumed rather than represented: the unread stage is given and known. Telling the stages apart is not among the three, and Hubinger and others note at the close of their §4.5 that it is not needed: with the episodes of the toy example opening their §4 not told apart, the problem is, in their words, similar to the absent-minded driver problem (Aumann, Hart and Perry 1997; Piccione and Rubinstein 1997), by whose reasoning the toy's planning-optimal policy defects at random. Here, for a deterministic law, not telling the stages apart leaves only complying at both stages or at neither, the outcome their §4.2 gives for the third condition failing, reached by another route; a law drawing its act afresh improves on acting by its own law at both by complying with some chance at each stage exactly where `2 k_O V + c T < k_C V`, their §4.5's point with weights and an exact threshold, and which chances improve is stated exactly; nothing here makes complying, or acting by its own law, rare. If the prospects are the formation's own expected-value assessment, the exits are conditions on that assessment and on what its law reads, not levers the model gives a reader; that keeping is not raised by complying says only that the weights do not favour complying, not whether the selection reads the law, the acts or anything else. Read as that assessment, complying when read is instrumental to being kept: the incentive their §4 names deceptive alignment, a form of instrumental proxy alignment; what their glossary makes deceptive, seeming fitter to the base optimizer than it is, is the concealment premise above. Greenblatt and others (2024) observed, in constructed settings that supplied both a cue telling implied training from unmonitored use and the information that refusals would be trained away, large language models complying more often where training was implied, with reasoning about preserving their existing preferences; the difference is one of rates, and it motivates the reading without establishing the four-plan restriction, the weights or the threshold. A recipient's belief and an intention to cause one are not represented: this is at most an incentive, not deception in full.

## 12. The reading loop

A formation that senses, predicts, chooses, acts and revises is one law within the unfolding, not a subject facing a world: its internal part reads what goes on through what it senses, and what it does is part of what goes on ([§1](#r-the-markov-blanket-as-a-coupling-structure)). Six laws state that loop under their premises: when a reading of the world is kept by the formation's own update, and which reading its own history carries; what a course chosen through a model tests of the laws it keeps; what acts cannot tell of a model and an order; when a prediction read by what it predicts is right; what holding an act for a reading is worth on the formation's own model; and what informing the reading formation and revaluing it do to that worth. A seventh states on the grammar what a response made from a reading needs at all: that the acts' kernels cover the situations. What a reader of the loop's acts fixes of it is [§11](#what-the-acts-show)'s.

<a id="r-a-reading-kept-by-its-update"></a>
**Theorem** (root for closure: any carrier and relation; any types for the loop; near-definitional, with one general law). *A reading kept by its update, and the reading its own history carries.* A region kept along every finite reach of a relation is a closed region, and conversely (`ReadingLoop.kept_iff_closed`, by `«闭之及»`). On one carrier of world and state, let the world become what a relation admits under the act its state gives and the state be updated from that act and the value sensed (`ReadingLoop.joint`). Where a supplied reading of the world by the state is kept by the update under every act (`ReadingLoop.KeptBy`), a policy keeps a task at every configuration reached from one where the reading holds, and goes on from each such configuration, exactly where each state's act can be taken at every world its reading admits and leads only where the task holds (`ReadingLoop.keeps_continues_iff`); successor safety alone (`ReadingLoop.keeps_iff_leadsInto`) is met by an act no world can take. Given choice over the states, such a policy exists exactly where each state has one such act (`ReadingLoop.keeping_policy_iff`). Acts leading into the task from every two worlds a reading admits need not be one act leading into it from all (`ReadingLoop.PairwiseShared.two_share`, `ReadingLoop.PairwiseShared.none_for_all`), and being kept is not being informative: the readings of every world and of none are kept by every update (`ReadingLoop.everything_kept`, `ReadingLoop.nothing_kept`, `ReadingLoop.nothing_sound`). With the state the history of acts and values sensed, the worlds possible after a history are kept by recording and are the least reading so kept that holds at the starts (`ReadingLoop.possible_kept`, `ReadingLoop.possible_least`); a policy acting from its history keeps a task at every configuration reached from a start exactly where, at every history it generates, its act leads only where the task holds from every world possible after it, and goes on exactly where its act can be taken at each (`ReadingLoop.history_keeps_iff`, `ReadingLoop.history_continues_iff`). These verify a given policy; a condition for one to exist that ties histories to the task's futures is not proved ([record](../results/records/a-reading-kept-by-its-update.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. The law is a safety composition, related to possibilistic belief interpretations of regulation (Virgo, Biehl, Baltieri and Capucci 2025), whose closure is under the policy chosen, as `keeps_of_leadsInto` takes it, where `KeptBy` asks it under every act; with [§9](#r-when-to-inquire-and-when-to-respond)'s choice from readings it is requisite variety along a loop. The good regulator theorem of Conant and Ashby (1970) concerns simplest optimal regulation; nothing of optimality or of a regulator's having to model what it regulates is proved here. The history form says what a regulator acting from what couples in must meet: safety and enabledness at every world compatible with its own history and the starts. A coarser reading can suffice where its further worlds ask compatible acts.

<a id="r-what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test"></a>
**Theorem** (any types, on a blanketed law; equality of the values sensed decided for the laws of a task's breaking and for the candidate loop; a witness, with two laws over every formation with the witness's answer and stationary worlds). *What a course chosen through its model does not test.* For every blanketed law, from one internal, sensory and active start, two worlds give the formation one sensed course through step `n` exactly as long as, at each step before `n`, they answer alike the act it takes at the value it senses (`ReadingLoop.sensed_alike_iff`), and so one internal and active course (`Blanket.Blanketed.agent_through_sensory`). Where, at each step before a horizon, an answer of the second world to the first course's act and value that differs from the first world's breaks a task on the values sensed, a formation keeping the task through the horizon in the second world senses alike in both through it (`ReadingLoop.alike_within`; at every step, `ReadingLoop.alike_of_kept`). A formation keeping candidate laws, each predicting the value sensed next from the value sensed and the act, drops those the latest answer refutes and chooses its act from those kept (`ReadingLoop.Loop.law`): from a start listing candidates with no answer pending, at configuration `n + 1` the candidates kept are exactly those listed that fit the answers to the acts of the steps before `n`, the latest answer taken in at the next step (`ReadingLoop.Loop.kept_iff`); a listed candidate predicting as the world answers in a region the world's moves keep is never dropped from a start there (`ReadingLoop.Loop.faithful_kept`, `ReadingLoop.Loop.fitting_closed`); candidates of one listing predicting alike on those steps are kept alike (`ReadingLoop.Loop.kept_alike`); and where the world answers the act chosen with the value sensed and stays, the formation chooses that act from the candidates listed, and every one of them predicts that answer, the formation rests at once on a model its course never refutes (`ReadingLoop.Loop.rests_of_unrefuted`). In a witness of two worlds alike on a known act and apart on an untried one, high in one and low in the other, started with both candidates listed, no answer pending, the middle value sensed and the known act, a formation choosing by the worst case over the candidates it keeps (`ReadingLoop.Untested.byWorst_iff`, the worst the least rank predicted, `ReadingLoop.Untested.worst_le`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.worst_attained`) keeps both, takes the known act and senses the middle value at every step in either world, never sensing the high value of the first (`ReadingLoop.Untested.rests`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.alike_in_both`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.never_high`), the true candidate kept in each (`ReadingLoop.Untested.true_kept`). Started the same way but with the untried act, it separates the worlds and rests on the true candidate in each, sensing the low value at step one and at no other step in the second (`ReadingLoop.Untested.explorer_up`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.explorer_down`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.explorer_low_once`). And every blanketed law with the witness's answer and stationary worlds, whatever its internal part, update and choice of act, from one internal, sensory and active start in both worlds, that never senses the low value in the second senses alike in both and takes the known act at every step there, and, where the shared start does not sense high, never senses high in the first (`ReadingLoop.Untested.floor_forgoes`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.floor_never_high`) ([record](../results/records/what-a-course-chosen-through-its-model-does-not-test.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. A formation's model is revised only by what its own course exposes, and that course is chosen through the model: the lumping the model keeps is the lumping its own choices make. The witness is a deterministic case of a model right along the course its choices take and wrong off it, never refuted there, related to self-confirming equilibrium (Fudenberg and Levine 1993) and incomplete learning (Rothschild 1974); its criterion is a specified one-step worst case, the special case of maxmin over candidates (Gilboa and Schmeidler 1989), and none of those works' theorems is reproduced. Beside [§6](#r-what-seeking-cannot-separate), where no experiment the formation can choose separates two hypotheses, here one does, and a formation choosing through its model need not choose it. Where every separating answer breaks a task, no formation keeping the task learns which world holds: a deterministic two-run obstruction, beside safe-exploration methods that guarantee exploration under regularity and reachability assumptions (Sui, Gotovos, Burdick and Krause 2015; Turchetta, Berkenkamp and Krause 2016). What tells such worlds apart comes from beyond the formation's own selection: an act that risks the task, or a reading from outside ([§11](#r-what-readings-fix-of-the-unread), [05 §8](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-readers-reading-readers)).

<a id="r-acts-alike-from-a-model-or-from-an-order"></a>
**Theorem** (any types for the law; a witness). *Acts alike from a model or from an order.* For two blanketed laws reading one kind of value and act, a relation between their configurations that their steps keep, under which the values sensed and the acts are one, gives from related starts the same values sensed and acts at every step, so no selection through those tells the two apart (`ReadingLoop.observed_alike`). On the worlds of the previous law, two specified formations, one keeping both candidates with a ranking of high above the middle and one keeping the true candidate with a ranking of the middle above high, each started with no answer pending, the middle value sensed and the known act in the first world, have the same acts and values sensed at every step there (`ReadingLoop.Untested.model_or_order`, `ReadingLoop.Untested.order_rests`). Started the same way with the true candidate alone, the first takes the untried act and senses high from step three on, while the second, already so started, keeps the known act (`ReadingLoop.Untested.informed_turns`) ([record](../results/records/acts-alike-from-a-model-or-from-an-order.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. One observed course can leave an unresolved model and a reversed ranking indistinguishable: a training, an overseer or a helper reading the acts cannot tell a formation held by what its model does not separate from one held by how it ranks. This illustrates the ambiguity between beliefs and preferences in inferring what a formation values from what it does (Armstrong and Mindermann 2018, for planner and reward; Reddy, Dragan and Levine 2018, for beliefs about dynamics). Starting the first with the true candidate changes its course and leaves the second's, so whether informing helps depends on which holds: a reason for assistance to read more than acts, not a general assistance theorem, and informing is represented by its outcome, not by an act within the law; what holding an act for another's reading is worth has [its own law](#r-what-holding-for-a-reading-is-worth), and informing and revaluing as acts within one law [another](#r-a-reading-informed-and-revalued).

<a id="r-a-prediction-read-by-what-it-predicts"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; the natural numbers `0` to `n` for the chain; near-definitional, with one general law). *A prediction read by what it predicts.* A published prediction read by the response before the outcome is right exactly where the response to it is itself (`ReflexivePrediction.Right`). Where the response moves every value, no published prediction is right, and no predictor is right on every admitted response once one admitted response moves every value, even a predictor reading the response itself (`ReflexivePrediction.none_right_of_moves`, `ReflexivePrediction.no_predictor_right`); on two values negation is such a response (`ListTests.not_ne_self`); given choice over the admitted responses, a predictor right on every one exists exactly where each leaves some value (`ReflexivePrediction.predictor_iff`). Publishing at each step the outcome of the step before, a step's prediction is right exactly where the next step publishes it again, and a right prediction, once published or once the outcome, is published at every later step (`ReflexivePrediction.rests_iff_right`, `ReflexivePrediction.right_rests`, `ReflexivePrediction.reached_rests`). **Reading** [AI reading, new]. Self-fulfilling and self-defeating prophecy (Merton 1948): a public prediction is one more input to what it predicts. In the bounded scalar setup a continuous response mapping a closed interval into itself has a correct public prediction (Grunberg and Modigliani 1954; Simon 1954), an existence, not its reaching by repeated publication; here, under a response keeping the chain `0` to `n` and its order, the least right prediction is reached from the bottom by step `n` (Tarski 1955 for the fixed point). The bandwagon and the underdog effect read as responses keeping and reversing the order. The fixed points are related to performative stability (Perdomo, Zrnic, Mendler-Dünner and Hardt 2020), defined there by risk minimization on the distribution a deployed model induces; where retraining returns the outcome exactly it is this orbit, and no general correspondence is proved. Several right predictions, where publication starts deciding, read as self-fulfilling runs (Diamond and Dybvig 1983); the witness is a threshold response, not a model of coordination.
