# Life and evolution

**Thesis.** Read with one kind of relatum, a formation can also stand as a condition, and arising replenishes: in a balanced flow in which only active formations give, an embodied relatum arises only as another gives to it, and in a balanced flow its arising replenishes every family containing it. Evolution is read as change along descent through transmission, variation, selection and drift. Claimed, as theorems on their bases: these laws of arising; several classical laws of evolution in counting form (Price's equation, Fisher's theorem in the exact case and with given breeding values, neutral and Moran fixation, mutation–selection balance), with the flow beside them, so that offspring number is bounded by what is given and taken; and selection blind beyond its couplings. Open: that continuation renewing its own conditions (the reading below) is necessary or sufficient for biological life (a flame meets the same laws), and the restatement of these laws on the grammar.

**Where this chapter sits.** It takes continuation and its marks from [01](01-ROOT.md), renewal, consumption and rest from [the map](02-MAP.md), and the reach of a selection from [03](03-KNOWING.md); it hands group selection and what a rise draws from beyond to [05](05-COLLECTIVES.md), and every verdict on lineages to [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).

**Bases.** Labels and bases are [08](08-METHOD.md)'s. The laws are proved on the World model (webs and flows: natural-number amounts, discrete steps, Prop-valued activity), on finite lists of any type, for any process, or in the periphery with their axioms named; a few are in pre-spine modules, labelled so, one with an empty axiom report and the rest periphery. Of the chapter's 18 central declarations, the [boundary record](../results/records/one-root-to-its-edge.md) places:

- one on the root under a stated hypothesis: `GroupEnding.Flow.all_end`, as `RootForms.outside_leaves_no_offspring`, each hypothesis needed;
- four as counting, below the grammar: Price's covariance, the levels partition, the levels partition read for a rise and the drift bound;
- one at a represented model: `Web.transmits_at_iff`;
- five with balance in their statements;
- seven at amounts.

The law of passing is read on the grammar, in part ([below](#r-a-pattern-lasts-through-passing)). None of the 18 is derived as a case of grammar laws. The one under a stated hypothesis, those with balance in their statements and those at amounts are proved with the World premises they take as hypotheses (balance, only active relata give, activity needing support, dissipation, bounded transmission, in [08](08-METHOD.md#premise-card)'s wording), and no one of those premises follows from the others (`World.independent`, `World.none_follows`). A pure-core law's empty axiom report concerns logic only; its assumptions lie in these modelling choices and in the hypotheses its statement names: balance, only active formations giving, activity needing support (`Dependent`), dissipation, transmission, descent and exact transmission. Offspring number is everywhere a weight and a reading, never a measure of worth: no law here says that an amount, a lineage or a group is better. Where evaluation enters, it rests on two premises, neither asserted: the continuation condition for scope and the comparison C for order, both in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).

<a id="life-as-condition"></a>
## 1. Life as a condition of life

**Reading** [AI reading, D65]. *Life and lineage.* Life is continuation that renews its own conditions, at the grain of formations and the conditions they depend on; read through the [root](01-ROOT.md), a life is a line of continuation relative to a class, and a lineage is such lines joined by descent. The chapter argues this reading from the laws below; it is not one of them.

**What happens.** In the World a relatum can stand as a condition at some level, which others depend on and draw on, and can be active as a formation, which depends, draws, takes and gives. A flame is both. Read with one kind of relatum, the roles meet: a life feeds a life.

<a id="r-life-as-condition-of-life"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *Formations and conditions are two roles of one relatum; an embodied relatum is a condition, and it arises through another's giving.*

- *The older two-kind models are cases.* Every web and flow with formations and conditions apart is one with a single kind of relatum, in which a formation stands as a condition at level zero that nothing touches and a condition is never active; the embedding keeps support, dependence, renewal, consumption, the living events, arising, balance and dissipation (`Becoming.Web.roles`, `Becoming.Flow.roles`). What the one-kind reading adds is that condition *x* and formation *x* are one relatum, which the shared index supplies and the laws do not check: relabelling the conditions onto them keeps whether activity needs support and which events are living (`Becoming.Web.relabel_dependent`, `Becoming.Web.relabel_alive`).
- **Definition.** A relatum is **embodied** when it stands as a condition at a positive level exactly while it is active, and **bears on itself** when, while active, it depends on its own level at a positive need (`Becoming.Web.Embodied`, `Becoming.Web.SelfBearing`). Both are premises about particular relata, not definitions of life: remains stand after activity ends, and water stands without being active. Embodied relata are read as living formations, whose living is their standing; this is not the mark 生 (begins) of [01](01-ROOT.md), and what can form is not limited to them: dormant formations and held conditions count too [AI reading, D4].
- *Co-ending.* Where activity needs support (the premise `Dependent`, which leaves out buffers, reserves and lags), a formation depending on an embodied relatum at a positive need is active only while that relatum is, and activity passes along every chain of such dependence: a relatum not active at a step leaves inactive there every formation whose chain of such dependence reaches it (`Becoming.Web.coextinct`). Lagged ending is [a reserve defers the end](02-MAP.md#r-reserve-defers-ending).
- *Arising is given.* In a balanced flow in which only active formations give, an embodied relatum arises only as another active formation gives to it (`Becoming.Web.Embodied.arises_given`). The giver need not be embodied or depend on anything; only where every giver is embodied, a supplied premise, is it embodied too, and even then it need not be a life.
- *Drawing at an ending.* Where activity needs support, drawing on a self-bearing embodied relatum across the step at which it ends breaks its own dependence, whatever ends it, so on the stepwise reading the drawing event is not living (`Becoming.Web.Embodied.ending_breaks`). This verdict reads co-occurrence, not causation, and is no valuation of the drawer [AI reading, D6].
- *Mutual renewal.* A bee and a flower each depending on itself and on the other renew each other and every event is living; where each depends only on the other, both are embodied and still neither co-participation nor interaction relates them, so embodiment does not enter the valuations (`Becoming.Mutualism.apart_embodied`). What valuations do with this is [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md).
- **Theorem** (World). *Stated at the model.* At the occurrence model of the one-kind reading, a web's support at an occurrence is an inclusion: what the occurrence requires within its step lies within the needs met there. The partial identity of one occurrence is added to WRT2's relations (`Becoming.ModelStatements.roles_supported_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.supported_iff`).
- **Theorem** (World). *Stated at the model.* At the same model, dependence is requiring from the active occurrences lying within the needs met: WRT2's relations within a step alone (`Becoming.ModelStatements.roles_dependent_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.dependent_iff`).
- **Theorem** (World). *Stated at the model.* At the same model, a living event is an active occurrence from which no drawing reaches a condition whose dependence is undermined across the step, the undermining reading the dependence's history along the names (`Becoming.ModelStatements.roles_alive_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.living_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.underminingAt_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.undermined`).

No axiom; balance, only active formations giving and activity needing support where named. [Record](../results/records/life-as-condition-of-life.md); [Roles.lean][roles], [LabelledRoles.lean][labelledroles] (co-ending), [Correspondence.lean][correspondence] (mutual renewal).

<a id="r-generation-is-replenishment"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *Arising replenishes.* (The record's title, "generation is replenishment", uses "generation" in the older sense of arising.) Read with one kind of relatum, in a balanced flow an embodied relatum is given at least the standing it arises with, since before it arises it is not active and so stands at zero (`Becoming.Web.Embodied.given_of_arises`); so its arising replenishes every family containing it, whatever is taken (`Becoming.Web.Embodied.replenishes`, with balance, `Balances`, as a hypothesis; balance on the family across the step suffices, `Becoming.Web.Embodied.replenishes_at`). Under balance and dissipation, a finite family without repetition in which embodied members arise at arbitrarily late steps is not closed from any step on, and, enclosed around from some step on, it is not the case that from some step on every formation giving to it takes only from within it (`Becoming.Web.embodiedArising_opens`, `Becoming.Web.embodiedArising_draws_in`): negative forms, which name no step of exchange. Under balance and dissipation, if finitely many relata, listed without repetition, are all there is, embodied relata do not arise at arbitrarily late steps (`Becoming.Web.embodiedArising_not_recurring`). The requirement follows standing, not activity: a seed is given its standing while dormant and arises later. Dissipation, embodiment and finiteness are each needed (witnesses): without dissipation two sides handing one standing back and forth arise without end in a closed world (`Shuttle`), and cohorts giving without taking are not dissipative (`Brood`); without embodiment a relatum arises without end in a dissipative world of one (`Flicker`); with a fresh fuel at every step, infinitely many relata, the sides arise without end under dissipation (`Throughput`). No axiom. [Record](../results/records/generation-is-replenishment.md); [Roles.lean][roles].

The map has the neighbouring laws of arising: [more needs renewers](02-MAP.md#r-more-needs-renewers), and arising that never ceases needs renewal ([unceasing generation needs renewal](02-MAP.md#r-unceasing-generation-needs-renewal), a title in the older sense). Among finite worlds, living crosses arising and ceasing: each combination occurs ([the root](01-ROOT.md#r-space-and-its-parts)).

<a id="living-on-and-descent"></a>
**Living on and descent.** Once condition and formation are one relatum, two relations relate relata directly: a formation lives on what it draws on and depends on, and a relatum descends from what gave it its standing. Both are adopted as formed relations of continuation, through which succession is read, not as direct valuations [AI reading, D9].

<a id="r-living-on-and-descent"></a>
- **Definition.** A formation **lives on** a relatum at a step where it draws on it and depends on it at a positive need (`Becoming.Web.LivesOn`).
- **Definition.** A relatum **descends** from a formation across a step where the formation takes part in the step, gives to the relatum, and the relatum comes to stand across it (`Becoming.Flow.DescendsAt`). Descent follows coming to stand, not arising: a seed descends from the parent that provisioned it while dormant, and waking from dormancy descends from nothing; descent counts give entries, not net flow.
- **Theorem** (World). *What the two relations connect.* Under `Dependent` what an active formation lives on stands then, and is active if embodied (`Becoming.Web.LivesOn.stands`, `Becoming.Web.LivesOn.active`); in a witness, living on connects the bee and the flower that depend only on each other, which neither co-participation nor interaction connects (`Becoming.Mutualism.apart_sustenance_connected`). In a balanced flow in which only active formations give, an embodied relatum that arises descends from another formation active then (`Becoming.Web.Embodied.descends`). Both readings depend on which condition is which relatum. [Record](../results/records/living-on-and-descent.md); [Descent.lean][descent].
- **Theorem** (World). *Stated at the model.* At the occurrence model, relational neutrality for the sustenance valuation is significance closed under the formed relation into living occurrences («闭»), the relation interacting or living on either way. The row is one step of that closure along living on, read as drawing with a dependence at a positive need (`Becoming.ModelStatements.living_on_reaches_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.sustenance_neutral_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.neutral_transmits_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.livesOn_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.interacts_iff`).
- **Theorem** (World). *Stated at the model.* The same along descent: the descent valuation's relation read on occurrences as interacting or descent either way, descent being the giver present and giving within the step and the receiver coming to stand across it, between relata at any steps (`Becoming.ModelStatements.descent_reaches_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.descent_neutral_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.descends_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.descentStep`).

<a id="r-conveyance-crosses-the-boundary"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *Conveyance crosses the boundary.* Two events at a common step are related where something is conveyed between their relata, a formed relation beside interaction, living on and descent [AI reading, D12]. A region's boundary is a cut where no formed relation leaves it. Under balance and dissipation, with no repetition, decided equality, enclosure around from some step on and recurring taking, a finite family's boundary is from no step on left uncrossed, so its region is no cut (`Becoming.Flow.boundary_crossed`, `Becoming.Flow.region_not_a_cut`). A cut is the root's not going out at a valuation's relation, by definition ([01 §9.9](01-ROOT.md#r-not-going-out-of-a-region)), so a region closed under the relation is a cut, and a cut is closed where membership is stable (`Becoming.Flow.cut_iff_notGoingOut`, `Becoming.Flow.cut_of_closedUnder`, `Becoming.Flow.closedUnder_of_cut`); conveyance's relation is symmetric, so along it nothing goes out of a region exactly where nothing comes in (`Becoming.Flow.conveyance_symm`, `Becoming.Flow.cut_iff_notComingIn`). Read on a self's boundaries along a relation ([07 §4.1](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-presupposition-scopes)), the family's region is no boundary of any event's self along conveyance, and not every member's event has its closure along conveyance within the family (`Becoming.Flow.region_no_boundary`, `Becoming.Flow.closures_not_within_region`); in the clearing the resource's region is no boundary along conveyance nor along living on (`Becoming.Clearing.resource_no_boundary`). What conveyance adds, in the embedding of the older two-kind model, is a relation between two condition-only relata (relata that take no part), in the direction of inflow, which interaction, living on and descent never give between such relata (`Becoming.Flow.roles_conditions_unrelated`); in a general one-kind web a relatum standing as a condition can also be active, and those relations can then relate it. [Record](../results/records/conveyance-crosses-the-boundary.md); [Standpoints.lean][standpoints], [ConveyanceBoundaries.lean][conveyance-boundaries].

<a id="passing-and-transmission"></a>
## 2. Passing and transmission

**What happens.** A pattern whose carriers are brief lasts only while carriers keep beginning, and under a premise of passing only while carriers pass it on. Descent is how a carrier begins from another. What the descendant then depends on is either inherited from its giver or formed anew at the descent, and the laws below say exactly which.

<a id="r-a-pattern-lasts-through-passing"></a>
**Theorem** (World; any web, its activity read as carrying). *A pattern outlasting its carriers lasts through passing.* Let a pattern be present at every step from *T* on (some formation carries it) and its carriers brief for *d* (none carries it at two steps *d* apart). Then from every step *S* from *T* on, it is not the case that no carrier begins across any of the *d* steps from *S* (`Becoming.Web.begins_within`): a double-negation form that, with activity undecided, names no carrier. Under the **passing premise** (`Web.ByPassing`: whatever carries the pattern after a step carried it before or was given it across the step by a carrier), every beginning is a passing (`Web.ByPassing.begins_passed`), the pattern is passed across one of every *d* consecutive steps from *T* on (`Web.ByPassing.passes_within`), a step with no carrier is followed by none (`Web.ByPassing.empty_stays`), and where no carrier passes it from *T* on, nothing carries it from *T* + *d* on (`Web.ByPassing.ends_within`); `PatternRelay` attains the bounds (witness). On the grammar (occurrences, pairs of a step and a formation, as the grammar's carrier; a step of continuation a formation going on or one it gives the pattern to; the pattern's class alike in carrying, and the finer carrier class also of one formation), the passing premise says exactly that every occurrence carrying the pattern after a step has a predecessor (有前) under the pattern's class (`Web.byPassing_iff_hasPred`); a carrier begins (生) and ends (亡) under the carrier class exactly where it arises and ceases, an alteration (异) of a carrying occurrence is a passing, and a passing to another formation is an alteration; and that every beginning is a passing, that the pattern is passed across one of every *d* consecutive steps from *T* on, and that where no carrier passes it from *T* on nothing carries it from *T* + *d* on are derived from grammar laws (`Web.ByPassing.begins_passed`, `Web.ByPassing.passes_within`, `Web.ByPassing.ends_within`). The passing premise is a hypothesis, supplied as transmission is below. Which formations pass, when and to which, whether a pattern changes as it is passed, and which patterns are worth passing are not said. No axiom. [Record](../results/records/a-pattern-lasts-through-passing.md); [PatternPassing.lean][patternpassing].

<a id="r-transmission-is-inheritance-and-need"></a>
**Definition.** A dependence on condition *c* is **inherited** at a descent where the descendant's dependence at its first standing step was held by the giver at the giving step; the flow **transmits within δ** where every such dependence is inherited and the descendant's need exceeds the giver's by at most δ.

**Theorem** (World). *Transmission is inheritance with a bounded need.* At one descent, transmission is exactly inheritance with the need within δ (`Becoming.Web.transmits_at_iff`), and where the descendant held no dependence on *c* before, inheritance is exactly the law of forming: a dependence forming as a relatum comes to stand is one its giver held (`Becoming.Web.inheritsAt_iff_forming`). With balance at the relatum coming to stand and inheritance at its descents, where nothing taking part depends on *c*, nothing coming to stand depends on *c* after the step (`Becoming.Flow.no_new_dependence`): transmission lets descent vary a need and drop a dependence, never add one that nothing taking part held. The bounds of §3 need it only from some supported giver, which is strictly weaker. Witness (`Becoming.TwoGivers.twofold`): in a balanced flow where only active relata give and activity needs support, a child given its standing by two givers, one depending on the pool and one not, depends on the pool while transmission fails for every δ.

**Calibration.** A dependence the descendant forms at the descent, or one it already held and keeps, may each be known to come from the giver; the two cases combine into inheritance where it is decided whether the descendant already held the dependence, without that only irrefutably, and that they combine at every descent of every flow is exactly excluded middle (`Becoming.split_iff_em`, module `Becoming.Transmission`). What the need says: where inheritance is read off these two cases, it waits on deciding which case the descendant is in; inheritance known another way, from the giver's holding, needs no such decision.

Transmission is a premise wherever it enters, read as a law of how dependence forms at one descent [AI reading, D31]; its independence where it binds is in [the root](01-ROOT.md#r-transmission-independent-where-it-binds). [Record](../results/records/transmission-is-inheritance-and-need.md); [Transmission.lean][transmission].

- **Theorem** (World). *Stated at the model.* At the occurrence model, transmission at a descent is two inclusions. What the descendant requires of a condition at the step after the giving step, read from the giver and carried back one step along the names, the giver requires; and the pair, where both require it, lies within the need within δ, read from amounts with an addition (`Becoming.ModelStatements.transmits_at_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.transmits_at_one_kind`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.inheritsAt_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.needsWithinAt_iff`).

<a id="law-of-forming"></a>
**The law of forming.** Inheritance excludes forming anew, and where the giver's dependence is stable the two are complementary; which happens is read off what the giver held and drew on.

<a id="r-forming-anew-necessary-and-sufficient"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *Forming anew, necessary and sufficient.* A dependence **forms anew** at a descent where the descendant depends on what its giver did not hold (`Becoming.Web.FormsAnewAt`). Forming anew excludes inheritance, and where the giver's dependence is **stable** (its double negation gives it, as wherever it is decided; stability here is a logical property of the proposition, not persistence in time), its exclusion gives inheritance (`Becoming.Web.inheritsAt_iff_not_formsAnew`). Where depending and drawing coincide at the giver, a dependence forms anew exactly where the descendant depends on what its giver did not draw on (`Becoming.Web.formsAnew_iff_undrawn`). With no premise, inheritance gives the drawing exactly where the giver draws on what it holds and passes on (`Becoming.Web.drawn_necessary_iff`); where drawing is depending, the reading of drawing is inheritance with the givers drawing what they pass on (`Becoming.Flow.dependsOnDrawn_iff`); and where also depending is drawing at the givers and their dependences are stable, inheritance, the reading of drawing and no descendant depending on what its giver did not draw on are one (`Becoming.Flow.inherits_iff_none_undrawn`). It does not say that depending and drawing coincide in any actual descent. [Record](../results/records/forming-anew-necessary-and-sufficient.md).

<a id="r-dependence-formed-from-drawing"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *A dependence formed from drawing.* Where depending and drawing part at the giver, the giver's holding decides inheritance against forming anew, in four cases: it held the condition and drew on it, held it only, drew on it only, or neither (`Becoming.Web.four_cases`, decidability only for the split). Under the reading of drawing at the descent, a hypothesis, the case of neither is excluded, so a dependence forms anew exactly where the giver drew on what it did not depend on (`Becoming.Web.formsAnew_iff_drawn_unheld`): a dependence formed by descent is a giver's drawing become its descendant's dependence. Witness: a parent drawing on food it does not depend on, its child coming to depend on it. The law says where a new dependence comes from, not why. [Record](../results/records/dependence-formed-from-drawing.md); [Transmission.lean][transmission].

<a id="along-a-lineage"></a>
**Along a lineage**, each member descending from the one before, two laws carry what descent passes.

<a id="r-need-drift-along-a-lineage"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *Need drift along a lineage.* With the dependence on *c* transmitted within δ, each member descending, depending on *c* at its first standing step and its need not rising until it gives in turn, the need after *i* + 1 descents is at most the founder's plus (*i* + 1)δ, and, the founder supported where it gives, at most the condition's level then plus (*i* + 1)δ (`Becoming.Flow.transmitted_along`, `Becoming.Flow.transmitted_along_supported`). That actual inheritance drifts by at most a fixed δ is not shown. [Record](../results/records/need-drift-along-a-lineage.md); [Evolution.lean][evolution].

<a id="r-a-goal-along-a-lineage"></a>
**Theorem** (World). *A goal along a lineage.* For any predicate of steps and formations: along a lineage, from a founder carrying the goal at its giving step, with the goal passed at each descent and each member keeping it until it gives, every member carries it from its first standing step (`Becoming.Flow.goal_along`); and passing across every descent from a carrier is equivalent to passing down every lineage whose members keep it from a carrying founder (`Becoming.Flow.goal_descends_iff`). Nothing in descent makes the passing hold. [Record](../results/records/a-goal-along-a-lineage.md).

<a id="copying"></a>
**Copying.** Before descent there is copying: a unit drawing from a stock and becoming several.

<a id="r-copying-b-fold"></a>
**Theorem** (copying on counts: every *b*, *d*, *n*, *k*). *Copying b-fold.* Where every unit draws *d* from one stock and becomes *b*, *n* rounds from *k* units run exactly where the stock covers *d* times *k* + *bk* + … + *b*<sup>*n*−1</sup>*k*, ending with *k* · *b*<sup>*n*</sup> units and the rest of the stock (`Becoming.Copying.copying_iff`); doubling is the case *b* = 2. Witness: one teaching ten, five rounds from one unit run from a stock of 11,111 and end with 100,000 units and none left (`Becoming.Copying.tenfold`). Whole numbers, the same draw each round; that teaching or reproduction copies at a fixed factor is not shown. [Record](../results/records/copying-b-fold.md); [Copying.lean][copying].

<a id="r-stronger-arrangement-formed-by-reconfiguration"></a>
**Theorem** (periphery, `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`, except `reconstruction_counts` with `propext` only and the witnesses with none; pre-spine module; witness for the unavailability). *A stronger arrangement formed by reconfiguration.* In a declared material history, an initially absent stronger arrangement forms by reconfiguring an existing one, using support and keeping all its carriers; its actual output fixes how many daughters the maximal faithful reconstruction rebuilds (`GeneratedSelectionRecurrence.reconstruction_counts`); those daughters operate, and later reconfiguration can erode the arrangement again. ## 3. Descent

**What happens.** Read any amount on parents and offspring. The offspring's total differs from the parents' for two reasons only: parents with more offspring count more, and offspring differ from their parents. Price's equation divides the change so. On the spine it is an identity of counts; what the World adds, where only active formations give and activity needs support, is that only supported formations give, so selection meets support. Here and below a **cohort** of descent is a listed family, each cohort listed as the offspring of the last (in Lean, `Becoming.SelectionTerm.Generations`).

<a id="r-evolution-on-descent"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World for support and transmission). *Evolution on descent.*

- *Price's equation in counting form.* Over any finite family of parents, each with a finite list of offspring, and any natural-number amounts, the offspring's total, with what transmission lowered, is each parent's amount counted once per offspring, with what transmission raised (`Becoming.price`); lowered and raised are truncated differences. No premise. The weighted total is not the selection term proper: where every parent has *k* offspring it is *k* times the parents' total (`Becoming.selected_uniform`).
- *Only the supported leave descendants.* Where only active formations give and activity needs support, a formation unsupported at a step leaves no descendant across it (`Becoming.Flow.unsupported_childless`), and the parents' needs on a condition, weighted by their offspring, are at most its level so counted, where each pair is related by descent across one step and every parent depends on the condition there (`Becoming.Flow.selected_le`).
- *What is transmitted was selected.* Where the flow transmits a dependence within δ, a descendant's need is at most the condition's level at the giving step plus δ (`Becoming.Flow.transmitted_supported`); with δ zero the need was met then; with δ one, in a witness, it exceeds the level at every step (`Becoming.Slack.slack`). In a balanced flow, what is taken and given meanwhile enters what can be transmitted later.
- *One unfolding* (witness). A parent depending on a pool at need two takes from it and gives to two descendants; the one inheriting its need is unsupported from its first standing step, since the parent's taking lowered the pool, while the one needing less is supported and takes in turn: transmission, variation, selection and the feedback of activity on selection in one unfolding (`Becoming.Drawdown.drawdown`).

## 4. Reproduction

**What happens.** Read the amount as offspring number itself. No pair is then discordant, and selection on offspring number is its own spread: Fisher's fundamental theorem in its exact-transmission case. The flow adds what classical theory leaves to another model: each offspring is given at least one unit, so offspring number is bounded by what the parent gives and takes, and within a dissipating family by its stock.

<a id="r-what-bounds-offspring-numbers"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World for the bounds). *What bounds offspring numbers, and selection on offspring number itself.*

- With no premise, the number of members times the total of the squared weights is the square of the total weight with the squared differences over pairs, equal exactly where the weights are uniform (`Becoming.Reproduction.variation`, `Becoming.Reproduction.concord_self_zero_iff`).
- Over cohorts read by offspring number, with exact transmission (each offspring leaving as many as its parent) the mean offspring number rises across a step exactly where offspring numbers vary and is kept exactly where they are uniform (`Becoming.Reproduction.Generations.ByNumber.rises_exact_iff`, `Generations.ByNumber.kept_exact_iff`): Fisher's theorem in counts, exact case.
- A parent's offspring number is at most what it gives them (`Becoming.Reproduction.Flow.born_le_given`); its offspring distinct and standing within a family dissipated on, with one more where there is any, at most what it takes from the family (`Flow.number_within_take`); and across a step at which a family is balanced and dissipated on, distinct offspring standing within it, with its takers, number at most its stock (`Flow.born_within_stock`).
- Over cohorts of descent within a family balanced and dissipated on at each step, a lineage reproduces at most the starting stock many steps (`Flow.reproducing_within`) and then ends (`Becoming.Reproduction.Flow.lineage_ends`), so read by offspring number a lineage that reproduced at its first step within such a family has a step at which transmission lowered offspring number by more than selection and what transmission raised (`Flow.closed_lineage_lowered`). Witness: without dissipation a lossless relay keeps a lineage going within a family of stock one (`Becoming.Reproduction.Relay.relay`): dissipation is needed.

**Theorem** (any type). *The breeding value.* The counted covariance is linear in the amount. **Definition.** A **breeding value**, scaled by a common denominator *d*, is a two-sided combination of carried amounts: at every member, it with one combination is a constant with another, each combination with natural coefficients (`Becoming.Reproduction.Breeding.Combination`), so that, divided by *d*, its coefficients are rationals of either sign with no subtraction written. ## 5. Groups

**What happens.** List a cohort as groups. The selection term divides into selection within each group and among the groups. An amount selected against within every group can rise in the whole only where the groups differ in it and those with more leave more. Whether that among-group part lasts is decided by how the next groups are formed: kept by descent from uniform groups, used up in one step by mixing. On the World, a group can end through the ending of the conditions its members depend on, and coupled conditions end their groups together.

<a id="r-selection-within-and-among-groups-of-any-sizes"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). *Selection within and among groups of any sizes: the general law.* With *n* a positive common multiple of the sizes and each group's own term scaled by *n* over its size, *n*<sup>2</sup> times the whole's selection term balances *n* times the whole's size times the groups' own terms with the term of the groups' means read member by member (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.levels_sized`): the law of total covariance with size weights. So the whole selects for the amount exactly where the means' concordance with the scaled within-group concordances exceeds the same for the discordances (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_sized`). Groups alike in mean leave selection within alone, so with exact transmission a cohort listed so raises its mean exactly as selection within goes: how the next cohort is listed fixes the next step's among-group part. Ending can use the among-group part up: two keeping groups beside a group leaving nothing raise holding back, their offspring form groups alike in it, and at the next step it falls below its start (`Becoming.GroupEnding.Exhausted.next_step`). <a id="r-selection-with-returns-to-groupmates"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). *Selection with returns to groupmates: Hamilton's rule.* Between the two ends lie groups founded mixed. Suppose groups of equal size *m* and offspring numbers additive in own and groupmates' amounts: each unit a member keeps gives *b* to every other member of its group and costs the keeper *c*. In counts with nothing subtracted, each member's offspring number *w* with *b* + *c* per unit of its own amount *a* is a constant *B* with *b* per unit its group holds: *w* + (*b* + *c*)*a* = *B* + *b* · (the group's total of *a*). Selection within groups goes against keeping by *b* + *c* times the groups' own spreads; among groups it goes for keeping exactly where *b* + *c* times the spread among groups falls short of *m* times *b* times it, so where the groups differ, exactly where *b*(*m* − 1) exceeds *c* (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.among_for_iff`); and the whole selects for keeping exactly where *b* + *c* times the whole's spread falls short of *mb* times the spread among groups (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_returns`). With the assortment the share of the spread among groups, this is Hamilton's rule, in counts with nothing subtracted. Its general additive form needs no groups: on any list, where each member's weight with *k* per unit of its own amount is a base with *b* per unit of a reading, the whole selects for the amount exactly where *b* times the reading's covariation with it exceeds *k* times its spread (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_reading`): over the rationals, where the amount varies, where *b* times the regression of the reading on the amount exceeds *k*. So, with a member's group total as the reading and *k* = *b* + *c*, the whole's verdict through that covariation, though not through the spread among groups, holds for groups of any sizes. Groups uniform in the amount and differing in it select for it exactly under that same inequality (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_returns_of_uniform`); the same returns give either verdict by how groups are founded, and assortment below one can lose where assortment one would win (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.Partly.below_one`). The rule is a case of a law with something kept: where each unit a member keeps costs it *K*, brings *s* back to it alone and gives *q* to every member of its group, its own included, selection within the groups goes by *s* against *K* alone, what is shared coming to a group's members alike (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.within_kept`), and the whole selects for keeping exactly where *K* times the whole's spread falls short of *s* times it with *mq* times the spread among groups (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_kept`, in spreads): read over the rationals where the amount varies, with the assortment *ρ*, exactly where *K* < *s* + *mqρ*. What comes back alone counts at every assortment, what is shared within the group only through it; returns to groupmates are the case *s* = 0, *K* = *b* + *c*, *q* = *b*, derived from it. For groups of any sizes, with *n* a common multiple of their sizes, the law is stated through a reading of the group: where what each unit kept shares reaches every member of its group as *q* per unit of that reading, selection within each group goes by *s* against *K* as before, among the groups' means, read member by member, by *s* against *K* with *nq* times the reading's covariation with them, and the whole selects for keeping exactly where *K* times *n* times its spread, with *q* times the reading's discordance, member by member, with the groups' means, falls short of *s* times *n* times the spread with *q* times their concordance (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_shared_sized`). Which reading is a modelling choice. Each member gaining *q* for every unit its group holds, as above, the verdict goes through the covariance, member by member, of a member's group total with its group's mean (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_kept_sized`): with equal sizes the size times the spread among groups, with unequal sizes no function of it. So no form in the spread among groups gives that verdict ([below](#r-unequal-groups-not-decided-by-the-spread-among-groups)). What each unit kept gives its group divided among the members, the verdict goes through the size-weighted spread of the group means: over the rationals, *K* < *s* + *Qρ*, with *Q* what a unit gives its group and *ρ* the share of the variance lying among the means (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.rises_iff_divided_sized`). The capacity to shape a rule, a group's shared part what its shapers give, is a case too, in groups of any sizes each member gaining *q* for each shaper in its group ([02 §9](02-MAP.md#r-shaping-selected-among-groups)). Exact transmission for the step law. Synergy is not stated; nor which sharing actual groups have, which is a premise; the split weighted by size is [the general law](#r-selection-within-and-among-groups-of-any-sizes). [Record](../results/records/selection-with-returns-to-groupmates.md).

<a id="r-unequal-groups-not-decided-by-the-spread-among-groups"></a>
**Theorem** (counts). *Groups of unequal size not decided by the spread among groups.* Two keepers and two others, in groups of three and one: the keepers together, with one other, beside the last member alone, or one keeper alone beside the other keeper with the two others. Both listings have the same spread of keeping over the whole and the same spread of the group means, each member read as its group's mean. Let each member gain two for every unit its group keeps, at a cost of one to the keeper and nothing kept: with the keepers together the whole selects for keeping, and with one in each group, where every member's group total is the same, it selects against it (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.Sized.either_verdict`). So where each member gains for every unit its group holds, no form in those two spreads gives the verdict at unequal sizes; it goes through the covariance of a member's group total with its group's mean ([above](#r-selection-with-returns-to-groupmates)). Where what each unit kept gives its group is divided among the members instead, the two listings give one verdict whatever the offspring numbers meeting the same returns (`Becoming.SelectionLevels.Sized.divided_one_verdict`). The modelling choices are the returns' additive form and which sharing holds, one step with the groups listed as given and their sizes fixed over it, and the spread among groups read by the means weighted by size ([record](../results/records/unequal-groups-not-decided-by-the-spread-among-groups.md)).

<a id="r-confined-rise-within-a-whole"></a>
**Theorem** (World; any type for the founding laws). *A confined rise within a whole, and what a founding carries.* Within one whole the two levels meet.

- Where a family of conditions ran into excess under the coupled gate, every member of a later cohort of descent is the offspring of a member that depended on no condition of the family at the gate's need (`Becoming.Founding.Flow.survivors_independent`).
- **Definition.** A **rise** is a lineage marked by an amount that selection within goes for before the end, taking from the family during its excess, the family's renewal gated and in excess; it is **confined** where every later member depends on the family. A confined rise has no member after the end, whatever selection within had raised it to (`Becoming.Founding.Flow.ConfinedRise.ends`). In the witness `Body`, of two lineages meeting every clause of a rise, the confined one ends and the one whose descendant depends on a condition outside the body still has a member a cohort after every part depending on the body has ended.
- In cohorts founded uniform, exact transmission keeps every member at the founding value, so selection within at a step needs transmission that raised or lowered the amount at some step before.
- Over wholes each founded by one part, where every next founder carries its founder's value (`Sequestered`, a hypothesis stating an outcome, not a mechanism), the mean founding value rises exactly where selection among wholes goes for it, whatever happened within (`Becoming.Founding.Nested.raises_iff_among`); without it, in the witness `Germline`, a variant selected for within lowers the mean founding value from a half to none although selection among wholes goes for holding back (`Becoming.Founding.Germline.unsequestered_falls`).

Hypotheses: balance, only active formations giving, activity needing support, descent, the coupled gate and *n* steps of excess from below *n* + *m*; confinement for the lineage law; one founder per whole for the founding laws. These restate known results on cancer and the germ line (Buss 1987; Michod 1999; Michod and Roze 2001; Aktipis et al. 2015) with the flow; that a given tumour meets the clauses, or a germ line is sequestered, is empirical. ## 6. Drift, fixation and mutation

**What happens.** Where the next cohort is drawn from the listed offspring instead of being them, its share varies around the listed share: a step of selection can be undone by the draw, and over cohorts of drawing, the weight of the histories still mixed is bounded by a share falling geometrically, and the share of the histories fixing the first kind lies within that bound of a share the laws give. Counting weighs samplings and histories by the ways of drawing them; it does not say which happens and supposes no chance of the unfolding. Mutation is transmission that is not exact. <a id="blind-selection"></a>
## 7. Selection blind beyond its couplings

Counts of offspring see only what the selection couples into: a [selection through couplings](03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings) is blind beyond their closure, and over cohorts of descent, where more offspring go only to formations that raise a family, the share of members reaching beyond it is never selected against ([what a rise draws from beyond](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-a-rise-draws-from-beyond), with its hypotheses and its slack law; what optimizing drawers do beyond their couplings is also [05](05-COLLECTIVES.md)).

**What the spine adds, and what it does not.** The laws of §3–§6 restate known results: Price (1970, 1972), Fisher (1930) in its exact case and with breeding values, the multilevel division, Hamilton (1964, 1975), the Wright–Fisher and Moran fixation probabilities, mutation–selection balance and the error threshold, the Malthusian bound; their verdicts against prior work are in [08 §8](08-METHOD.md#known-results). What the spine adds is that they hold in one model with conservation, dissipation and dependence: offspring numbers are bounded by what the flow gives and takes, under the stated descent and resource hypotheses, though the offspring lists themselves stay supplied; a lineage closed in a dissipating family ends; selection among groups can be fixed by the ending of conditions, and groups on one coupled family supply none among them; the selected's verdict meets what they draw from shared conditions. It adds no new population-genetic prediction. Fitness is not defined, and no reproductive advantage is ethical authority.

<a id="organization"></a>
## 8. Organization within organisms

**What happens.** Evolution names particular mechanisms (transmission, reconstruction, variation, selection, drift, development), each explained through its own relations; it is not every change, and sampling, movement and development cannot be relabelled selection. Formation before there is inheritance is a question upstream of differential transmission, and the forming of an organized individual is not explained by changing the partition of a population already supplied, which is all the group laws of §5 read. An organism and its supporting relations are distinguished within one history, not posited as separate substances. Where an organization changes, the change enters at a gated step (values decided); it is kept along every ungated course exactly where every ungated step keeps it. The root states this as [every origin passes the gate](01-ROOT.md#r-every-origin-passes-the-gate): a **gate** is a decided mark on moves, and where the ungated steps preserve or reflect a property, a course that changes it passes a gated move. The laws below use it, first in general, then in models of living and social organization.

<a id="r-organizational-rule-inheritance"></a>
**Theorem** (any process). *Formation and inheritance of organizational rule differences.* For every process, gate on moves and quantity, the quantity is kept along every ungated course exactly when every ungated step keeps it (`Becoming.Gated.kept_along_iff`). If every ungated step keeps the quantity and its values have decidable equality, a course that changes it splits into a prefix keeping it, a gated step that itself changes it, and the rest (`Becoming.Gated.change_located`). [Record](../results/records/organizational-rule-inheritance.md); [Gated.lean][gated].

**Theorem** (any process; one declared finite protocol, an instance). Of its moves (review, enrollment, proposal, adoption, integration and handoff of a rule), only adoption is gated, so every ungated step keeps the rule (`Becoming.RuleHandoff.rule_kept`): every non-adopting history inherits the rule (`Becoming.RuleHandoff.rule_inherited`), and every change is located at an adoption step (`Becoming.RuleHandoff.rule_change_located`) that uses the recorded proposal (`Becoming.RuleHandoff.adoption_uses_recorded_proposal`). Supplied: the seed rule, its recognition and copying, proposals, participation, a fixed amendment procedure and the adoption threshold; no clock or consensus process is simulated ([RuleHandoff.lean][rulehandoff]). In the record's fuller construction, reviewed work and recognition form mandates, an actual proposal changes the later filtering rule through the existing authorization procedure, and branching or handoff carries the rule while continuing participation decides its execution. A copied rule with no one continuing to take part cannot operate, and with two participants technical decisions can go on while revision of the governance itself fails: continuing to operate is not still being able to change how one is organized. Which proposals a procedure admits and how procedures are themselves kept or dropped are distinct questions; how tasks and tests change is open ([OrganizationalRuleInheritance.lean][orgrule]).

**Theorem** (periphery, `propext`, `Quot.sound`; instance of the gate laws). *Where an organizing pool came from.* In a living-organization model of recipes and reactions, take a reaction's entry into a pool (a recipe that outputs a pool member while taking none in) as the gate; every other step reflects the pool's presence. With equality of species and membership in the pool decided, a pool present at the end was present at the start, or an entry step occurs, by the pure core's `Gated.origin`, which decides nothing about the pool's presence beyond the recipe lists (`Becoming.LivingOrganizationCorrespondence.trace_pool_origin_by_core`), and the pool is present right after that entry (`entry_then_present`); where every admitted recipe is closed, an empty pool stays empty (`empty_pool_preserved_by_core`): in that model an organizing pool does not arise without an entry. Closure is a checked property of the admitted recipes, not a law of chemistry, and no correspondence to chemistry is shown ([LivingOrganizationCorrespondence.lean][livingorg]).

<a id="r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it"></a>
**Theorem** (root; a family of models). *The stock buys time where the rebuild draws on it.*
- **The rule.** One rule makes a family of nets. Each holds a level in whole units and a reader, a list of units by age. Each step draws a unit, the reader's units read in turn while the supply is on (each taking one while the level is below its set level), every unit ages, and a spent unit is rebuilt from the rule's source. The members are a given reader, which does not age, and readers rebuilt from the supply, from the level, or not at all. The supply is chosen at every step, so every sequence of on and off is a course.
- **Kept while supplied.** Where the rebuild draws on the level, at least one unit wanted and a set level of at least one, a present reader is present after a supplied step from every level (`«供则读者不绝»`).
- **Under a cut.** The reader rebuilt from the level is present after `k + 1` steps from every level of at least `2 (k + 1)` (`«断而有储则读者存»`). A reader rebuilt from the supply, and one never rebuilt, are gone after their span from every level (`«断则取于供者尽»`, `«不复建者尽»`).
- **Its end.** Where it ends, the level is empty and nothing returns it on any branch (`«读者绝则无储»`, `«取于储者绝而不复»`). A reader rebuilt from the supply returns with the supply (`«取于供者复»`).
- **The family law.** Over the four members, the stock buys time under a cut, with an end from an empty stock, exactly where the rebuild draws on the level (`«以储买时当且仅当取于储»`). It holds at a span of one too, so the rebuild's source separates the members, not the turnover.
- **Lean.** [Record](../results/records/the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it.md); [ReaderRebuilding.lean][readerrebuilding].
- **Not proved:** that each further `s + 1` units buy `s` steps for one unit is decided at spans two and three for small levels only (`«一元二期之时»`, `«一元三期之时»`). Ending from every stock, a horizon never decreasing in the stock and any rate for more units are not proved.

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. The member rebuilt from the level is the family's model of an organism, and a minimal one: one constraint, with turnover, regenerated from the flow it regulates, below closure of constraints, autopoiesis and Rosen's closure, each of which asks more. "Upward" and "downward" name different relational paths within one unfolding, and an intervention on a whole needs an admissible realization: one cannot hold every constituting relation fixed while changing the whole they constitute ([study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/RELATIONS-ACROSS-SCALES-INTEGRATION.md), [proof correspondence](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/RELATIONS-ACROSS-SCALES-PROOF.md)).

<a id="r-equal-totals-can-hide-what-a-capacity-carries"></a>
**Theorem** (periphery, its imports reaching Mathlib; pre-spine module; witness; the laws stated here with empty axiom reports). *Equal totals can hide what a capacity carries.* Two histories from one state that acquire information at different times end with equal material, current support, spent work, route and context, yet differ in retained effect and withdrawal; and equal whole-organ burden can conceal different regional signals. A description must keep the relations through which a capacity is realized. The transfer, retention law, costs and timing are supplied; cell differentiation, tissue formation and ecological regeneration are not derived ([record](../results/records/equal-totals-can-hide-what-a-capacity-carries.md); [study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/ORGANIZED-SCALES-INTEGRATION.md); [OrganizedScaleLoop.lean][organizedscale]).

**Reading** [AI reading, D65]. *Development: four linked questions.* An inherited organization does not carry an adult psychology apart from development. For a disposition, ask what mechanism produces it now, how it develops, what it does to continuation and reproduction, and how its distribution arose. A present benefit does not establish origin; selection, drift, constraint, byproduct and cultural transmission must be told apart where they are live alternatives. The same mature structure does not by itself identify its formative history, so a candidate reproducing adult behaviour while leaving its developmental and historical origin arbitrary does not meet the burden of evolutionary psychology, and an established developmental or computational theory supplying the same mechanism keeps its credit. An evolutionary explanation of a disposition, its normative warrant, its recognition and its actual preservation are different achievements ([study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/COGNITION-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-INTEGRATION.md)).

<a id="r-same-carrier-need-not-fix-the-response"></a>
**Theorem** (periphery, `propext`, and `Quot.sound` in two declarations; pre-spine module; witness). *The same carrier need not fix the same response.* In a finite model, two histories with the same carrier, native state and support, differing only in whether developmental sampling came before or after an earlier repair, form different connections: one later repairs and reconstructs, the other does not unless a supplied assisted repair comes first (`supplied_assistance_can_rescue_development`), so the same carrier and developmental operations do not fix the same response function (`DevelopedResponse.same_carrier_different_developmental_record`, `equal_cost_development_reconstructs_different_gates`, `same_assay_enacts_developed_difference`, each with `propext` only; `Quot.sound` enters only the two energy laws, `enabled_energy_balance` and `runnable_energy_balance`). Primary studies bearing on these links are in [§9](#empirical) ([record](../results/records/same-carrier-need-not-fix-the-response.md); [DevelopedResponse.lean][developed]).

Required organization under the comparison, a conditional result on building shared capacity, is stated in [05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-organizational-formation-from-generative-comparison).

<a id="does-not-show"></a>
## What this chapter does not show

- Why a descendant comes to depend on what its giver drew on: the law of forming says where a new dependence comes from, not why.
- How parts first come to live as one, wholes founded by several parts, removal of parts by a whole's rule, or how groups and couplings form beyond the bonded model of §5.
- That fixation alone is causal proof, or that laboratory protocol changes establish endogenous evolution of the selector.
- That embodiment, descent, transmission or confinement holds of an actual organism: each is a premise or a correspondence duty.
- That any amount, lineage, group or level of selection is better.
- Fitness, rational amounts beyond a common denominator, or a new population-genetic prediction.

## Open

- Mixing beyond the next listing, assortment changing over cohorts, synergy, groups each counted once.
- A solution of the normal equations as a two-sided combination with natural coefficients.
- Offspring numbers tied to what a formation gives, so the share laws' premise follows from the flow.
