# Joint activity and selection

<a id="collectives"></a>

**Thesis.** Joint activity is explained from actual relations, not opposed interests, bargaining or a fixed common utility: a collective is a region of formed relations ([evaluation](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#evaluation)), not a unit with an interest of its own; conflict and difference stay real. Proved under the hypotheses each law names: joint consumption has no single consumer; around loops of couplings acting at every step the most difference that can arrive grows exponentially; position sums to zero and does not protect; giving that waits on giving absorbs a withholding or returns it; with room, every optimum lowers what lies beyond its couplings as far as any eligible outcome does; a pool tells apart what some member tells apart, and no more; from a symmetric start only a broken symmetry brings agreement on a code, a code held as counts and learned from what is heard among them, or parts identical cells under a descent that treats them alike, and under noise of one law at every cell an exchangeable law stays exchangeable, deciding nothing of which cell takes which place. Claimed, not proved: that trained systems drain so as they near their reward's optimum (T3). Open: how pools form and last.

**Where this chapter sits.** It takes from [03](03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings) that a selection reaches exactly what couples into its selector, from [04](04-LIFE.md#groups) selection among groups and groups on coupled conditions ending together, and from [02](02-MAP.md#r-margin-of-a-regime) the margin of a regime; it reads them on several formations at once. It hands to [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-approaches-under-the-selection-laws) pooled coupling, read as alignment, and drains and locks whose ranking is the ethics' question.

**Bases.** Most laws here are on the World model ([01](01-ROOT.md#r-map-of-premises)): a web of dependence with natural-number amounts and discrete steps, read with a flow of what formations take and give. Labels name the World premises used, *balance* (every change of a condition's level is what those taking part take and give), *activity needing support* and *only active giving*, and local hypotheses apart, chief among them *the gate*: every formation giving to the condition depends on it at a need of at least `m`, so the renewal depends on what it renews. Some laws hold for any process or any type; the market and practice models are periphery, with their axioms named at each result. No law here evaluates: whether a drain, a lock or a shared code supports or damages 生生 is [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md)'s question, under the continuation condition for scope and C for order, neither asserted.

## 1. Joint consumption and joint excess

A condition drawn by several formations is drawn by their joint activity: the first of the laws below says when a part makes the difference to a loss, the others what joint drawing does ([Joint](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Joint.lean), [Continuation](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Continuation.lean), [Exclusion](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Exclusion.lean)).

<a id="r-joint-difference-making"></a>
**Theorem** (any process; withholding a supplied map on configurations; the move taken held fixed). A part *makes the difference* where the step from a configuration by the move taken breaks a dependence and, the part withheld, the same move can be taken and every step by it keeps the dependence; parts make it *together* where this holds with all of them withheld. Where the step breaks the dependence and, the part withheld, the move can be taken with the dependence holding and met, the part makes the difference exactly where the same move no longer breaks it (`Dependence.makesDifference_iff`). Where the move determines the step and, any single member's part withheld, can be taken with the dependence holding and met, the parts make the difference together while no member alone does exactly where they make it together and withholding any one leaves the same move breaking it (`Dependence.differenceOnlyTogether_iff`); such a difference needs at least two parts. In the witness Pool, with two other drawers the loss is overdetermined: the first drawer's part makes no difference, the first two make it only together, and the [whole-run relational reading](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-relational-reading-comparison) still counts the first drawer as consuming (`Pool.difference_only_together`). Across a formation's own events the run-level and step-level tests come apart: in the witness Renewable, drawing two units at every step consumes the condition; the first event's step keeps the dependence and undermines for no positive cycle, and with any single event's draw withheld, or every draw before any step, the condition is still consumed (`Renewable.depletion_is_joint`, `Renewable.no_finite_segment_makes_difference`), while at the breaking step drawing nothing keeps the dependence, so the breaking event makes the step-level difference (`Renewable.breaking_event_makes_step_difference`); there the draw is the move, so these tests compare draws and are not the test above, which holds the move fixed. Where withholding leaves the dependence unmet the test is silent; no causal theory or responsibility follows ([record](../results/records/joint-difference-making.md)).

- **Theorem** (any process; withholding a supplied map on configurations). *Stated at the model.* At a process's model, withholding all parts is passing through their withholdings in turn («历»). Where the move's relation meets the breaking from the configuration and from what each withholding leaves, parts whose but-for statement holds together make the difference only together: it holds for no single one of them (`Becoming.ModelStatements.only_together_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.overdetermined_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.differenceAt`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.withholdAll_iff_li`).
- **Theorem** (any process; withholding a supplied map on configurations). *Stated at the model.* At the same model, each move's relation fixing its successor, and the move available from what each withholding leaves with the dependence required and met there: the parts make the difference only together exactly where they do together and from what each withholding leaves the move's relation meets the breaking (`Becoming.ModelStatements.differenceOnlyTogether_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.differenceOnlyTogether_iff_model`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.makesDifference_iff`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.makesDifferenceTogether_iff`).

<a id="r-own-count-misses-joint-excess"></a>
**Theorem** (World: balance; for the ending also activity needing support and only active giving, with the gate local). Formations can each take within what a step gives while together they take more (`Pair.own_not_joint`). In a balanced flow each step of joint excess lowers the level by at least one, so `n` consecutive such steps need a level of at least `n` at the start. Under the gate at need `m`, `n` consecutive steps of joint excess from a level below `n + m` leave the level below `m` for good and end every formation depending on the condition at a need of at least `m`, a taker among them where it so depends (`Flow.joint_excess_ends`); a local form needs balance only at the condition across the steps used.

In a minimal witness two takers end themselves and their renewer, one taker does not. The gated conclusions after the step the excess reaches are proved only for renewal depending on what it renews; a taker not so depending can continue. At that step the end holds for any renewal (`Flow.ends_at_race_end`), and a formation renewed from outside can be active again (`Restored.returns`). Read on a lineage at the kind grain, where everything a line reaches at that step so depends, the line from before that step does not continue past it, so such a line drawn past renewal from outside, such as a sun's, from a stock below `n + m` degenerates with no gate ([04 §4](04-LIFE.md#r-ending-laws-as-regimes-on-a-lineage)); no such line crosses the step, which does not say that nothing of the kind stands after it. It restates the tragedy of the commons and overshoot (Lloyd 1833; Hardin 1968; Meadows and others 1972) in near-definitional arithmetic, not who ought to take less ([record](../results/records/own-count-misses-joint-excess.md)).

<a id="r-whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends"></a>
**Theorem** (World: balance, only active giving, activity needing support; local: the parts listed at each step, each ending, their arising and the family's renewal gated). Listed parts that each end, none arising from some step on, end with their whole (`Web.Whole.ends_without_arising`). So a whole whose parts arise only where they then depend on some condition of a family at a need of at least `m`, the family's renewal gated on it (whatever gives to a member depends on some member at such a need), has no part arising after `n` steps of the family's uptake exceeding its replenishment from a stock below `n + m`, and ends (`Web.Whole.family_excess_ends`). The uptake counts every taker, so the whole's own draw within renewal proves nothing; in witnesses, drawing within what the members give, a part that never ends, or members arising ungated each leave the whole persisting. It does not show that the whole ends at `T + n`, that the collective is the consumer, or that its ending is a loss ([record](../results/records/whole-drawing-beyond-renewal-ends.md)).

<a id="r-what-taking-all-excludes"></a>
**Theorem** (World: balance, only active giving, activity needing support, the gate at the need; a local form needs balance only at the condition). A formation *takes all that is given* across a step where it takes from the condition at least all that the step gives it; alongside it any other taker makes the joint taking exceed what is given, so full taking alongside another taker at each of `n` steps from a level below `n + m` ends every formation depending on the condition at a need of at least `m`, the full taker included (`Flow.full_taking_ends`). Where a formation takes all that is given at every step, depends on the condition at a need of at least `m` at every step and is active at every step, then for any step `T` and any named other formation, not at every one of the `level + 1` steps from `T` (the level at `T`) is that formation present and taking from the condition (`Flow.continuing_excludes`). A formation active only while it takes, whose activity does not resume after `T` once it stops, is inactive from `T` plus the level at `T` on (`Flow.continuing_ends_taker`). Taking jointly within what is given at every step keeps the level, and every formation depending on that condition alone at a need met at the start stays supported (`Flow.level_kept`, `Flow.supported_of_within`). In the excluding and sharing witnesses the levels admit every member alike at every step: exclusion acts through the taking, not the levels ([record](../results/records/what-taking-all-excludes.md)).

<a id="r-the-rest-outruns-the-renewal"></a>
**Theorem** (World: balance, activity needing support, only active giving; local: the gate, the rest listed without repetition). Where the other members alone (the rest) take from a condition jointly more than a step gives at each of `n` steps, from a level below `n + m`, the level stays below `m` from then on and every formation depending on the condition at a need of at least `m` ends, whatever a member outside the rest takes (`Flow.outrun_below`, `Flow.outrun_ends`). With need one, in two such flows every score that reads the condition only from the rest's end on scores them alike, however the member's act differs (`Flow.outrun_reading_alike`): no weight on such a reading selects the member's restraint. Depletion need not be the only equilibrium; what learners reach is tested in CG2 ([§7](#t3)) and, on learners that generalize across states, in EM1 ([experiment](../../../experiments/commons-outrun-generalizing/README.md)) ([record](../results/records/the-rest-outruns-the-renewal.md)).

<a id="r-risk-minimization-on-a-shared-renewal"></a>
**Theorem** (World: balance, activity needing support, only active giving; local: the gate, the rest listed without repetition, a member outside it; the witnesses those of joint excess and of set points held). Read in the free-energy principle's terms, an agent with a generative model predicting the level after a step from what it reads and what it takes, choosing the first amount of least risk, the predicted distance from its preferred level (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.RiskAndNeeds.Chooser.chosen`), minimizes risk in counting form (a correspondence, not a Lean law: for a point prediction ô, active inference's risk KL[δ_ô ‖ P] is −ln P(ô), which orders as the distance does under a preference falling alike on both sides of its mode). Holders of one such model, each taking, at the two steps they take part, its choice of least risk at its own store, a condition of the web, at no risk, each store at its set point at every step and so each goal met, each within what a step gives the pool, together end the pool's renewal, neither coupling into the pool (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.SharedModels.held_not_one`, by [the set point's held pair](#r-what-a-set-point-optimum-does-beyond)). Alike preferences, each over one's own, are not one goal; a goal over the shared level is no guarantee either: two regulators each counting only its own take continue, three end at the first step (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.SharedModels.OneGoal.continues`, `FreeEnergyGuarantees.SharedModels.OneGoal.three_end`). And where the rest outruns a gated renewal, a member outside it has its need end whatever its model, reading and preference (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.RiskAndNeeds.need_ends`, by [the outrun law](#r-the-rest-outruns-the-renewal); at need three, `FreeEnergyGuarantees.RiskAndNeeds.Outrun.ends_either_way`), and where it expects at no risk a level at least its need, the level reached is below it from the rest's end on (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.RiskAndNeeds.expectation_not_course`). So the claim that one generative model with one set of preferences makes each agent's goal the group's (Friston, Ramstead and others 2024; Da Costa and others 2025) fails for preferences over one's own store, and the wording that risk minimization ensures needs are met (Albarracin and others 2024) fails where others outrun the renewal; the second applies landed laws with a chooser they do not read and forbids only the wording, not the authors' gloss. These are the commons (Lloyd 1833; Hardin 1968) and pivotality (Palfrey and Rosenthal 1984) in the principle's terms; the papers' simulations, with no shared draw, are not refuted ([record](../results/records/risk-minimization-on-a-shared-renewal.md)).

## 2. Mutual dependence

Read the closures of the map as the whole; mutual dependence is not mutual benefit, and its pace bounds what can arrive ([MutualDependence](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/MutualDependence.lean)).

<a id="r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace"></a>
**Theorem** (World, no premise for the closures; for the pace, couplings holding at every step and the bounds named). A web's closures can be the whole in three ways: every system (*sharing conditions throughout*), every influence closure (*mutual dependence*: every continuation can be affected, at some remove, by every other) or every feeding closure (*mutual feeding*). Mutual feeding gives mutual dependence, which gives sharing throughout (`Flow.MutuallyFed.mutuallyDependent`, `Flow.MutuallyDependent.sharesThroughout`); neither converse holds (witnesses Clearing, and a pair with nothing given). Read forward, a flow is mutually dependent exactly where every reach closure is the whole (`Flow.mutuallyDependent_iff_reach`). Around a loop of `m + 1` couplings holding at every step, after `R` turns the *carried amount* at its condition, the most difference that can arrive there ([02](02-MAP.md#r-how-much-difference-arrives)), is at least `2^R` times what had reached it (`carried_pow_around`). Under [02's standard bounds](02-MAP.md#r-how-a-carried-difference-grows) the carried amount is at most `D(1 + M)^t`, whatever the couplings, so around such a loop, under those bounds, it is exponential both ways: at least `2^R` times what had reached it by `T` and at most `D(1 + M)^(T + (m + 1)R)` (`exponential_around`). The record adds the pace of a selection under transmission (carriers to others at least `(a/b)^n` times as at the start, `share_ratio_pow`). That an actual difference grows so, that a dependence couples at every step, and that the Earth is mutually dependent are supplied. Under each reading, significance bounded by the matching closure of each center is the continuation condition; what that says is [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-continuation-condition-yields-living-significance)'s ([record](../results/records/mutual-dependence-and-its-pace.md)).

**Theorem** (World: balance; nothing given to the condition across the stretch; DoublingDraw a witness). *A draw exceeding everything drawn before.* With nothing given, what stands and what has been drawn since `T` add up to what stood at `T` (`Flow.Balances.level_add_drawn`), so more than half stands while such a draw lasts. Where, across `n` steps from `T`, each step's draw exceeds everything drawn since `T`, more than half of what stood at `T` stands at each of those steps (`Flow.Balances.half_stands`), and what stood at `T` is at least `2^n − 1` (`Flow.Balances.doubling_needs`, through `pow_le_drawnBefore`). The bound is attained: a stock of seven meets draws of one, two and four, four standing at the third step and none after (`DoublingDraw.half_then_none`). How fast an actual draw grows is not shown ([record](../results/records/mutual-dependence-and-its-pace.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, D39]. "A highly entangled system" reads as mutual dependence, and "exponential pace" as growth around loops acting at every step.

## 3. Position

A part's *position* reads its amount against the others': its own amount counted once for each part against the family's total. Counting alone makes positions sum to zero, whatever the amounts do ([RelativePosition](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/RelativePosition.lean)).

<a id="r-relative-position-is-zero-sum"></a>
**Theorem** (any type for the counting; World for the race: balance, activity needing support, only active giving, with the gate local). Across every change of amounts the positions' rises total their falls (`RelativePosition.change_balances`), so a part's change of position is the opposite of the change summed over the rest (`RelativePosition.change_opposed`). No change raises every part's position, and a part's rise needs another part's fall, found by a decided comparison, not by contradiction (`RelativePosition.rise_needs_fall`). On a flow they need no hypothesis on it.

Position does not protect. With balance at a condition across `n` steps of joint excess from step `T`, a part that at `T + n` requires the condition at a need greater than the level at `T` less `n`, and is active there only while supported, is not active there: its position does not enter, and no renewal need be gated (`Flow.ends_at_race_end`). With the gate at `m` as well and the level at `T` below `n + m`, from `T + n` on every greatest part depending on the condition at a need of at least `m` is inactive (`Flow.race_ends_greatest`). In a witness race the part rising in position ends with every other; in the clearing witness a greatest part not depending on what it draws down continues. That a race for position produces joint excess is a premise here, not derived ([record](../results/records/relative-position-is-zero-sum.md)).

**Conditional** (the continuation condition, as a hypothesis of one theorem about a witness). In the clearing witness, what the continuing greatest part ends has significance only under a significance: under the condition the clearer's part loses the dweller from every standpoint, and where nothing has significance it loses nothing (`Clearing.loss_needs_significance`). The condition is one significance counting the loss, not the only one: the clearer's own presupposition, held along sharing a condition, counts it too (`Clearing.own_presupposition`); the condition is stated in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-continuation-condition-yields-living-significance).

<a id="r-what-position-reads"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; the witness on the World). Two profiles give every part the same position exactly where every amount differs between them by one common amount (`RelativePosition.keeps_iff_common`). So positions bound the total from below, by the spread above the least part (`RelativePosition.exceeds_iff_spread`), and never from above: every bound is exceeded by some profile with the given positions (`RelativePosition.keeps_exceeds`). Read on a step, positions certify that more is taken than is given exactly where the takes' spread does (`Flow.excess_iff_spread`): the joint-excess law's hypothesis is reached through the spread alone. In a witness two racers have the race's positions while what is taken stays within what is given ([record](../results/records/what-position-reads.md)).

<a id="r-least-parts-and-equal-shares"></a>
**Theorem** (any type: whole-number amounts on a finite list of parts). A least part's amount counted once for each part is at most the family's total, so with a total at most `T` and at least `n` parts, `n` times the least amount is at most `T` (`RelativePosition.least_bound`). Equal shares meet every need exactly where they meet the greatest, giving more than the total need where needs differ (`RelativePosition.equal_share_iff`, `RelativePosition.equal_share_exceeds`) ([record](../results/records/least-parts-and-equal-shares.md)).

A race among drawers ranking courses by position, where keeping for position keeps for nothing, is a case of the optimum ([a race for position](#r-a-race-for-position-draws-everything)).

<a id="giving-waits"></a>
## 5. Giving that waits on giving

Let each formation give at a step according to whether what it waits on gave. *With memory* (`waitsAll`) a formation gives at the next step where it gave and everything it waits on gave. *Without memory* (`answers`) it gives where what it waits on gave at the step before. The relation of waiting is given; the laws below hold of runs of these laws without finiteness or any decision of equality or of giving, except where stated ([Withholding](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Withholding.lean), [Tolerance](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Tolerance.lean)).

<a id="r-giving-that-waits-on-giving"></a>
**Theorem** (any type: any formations and any relation of waiting). With memory, a formation gives at step `n` exactly where every formation within `n` waits of it gave at the start, so a withholding stays and spreads downstream along every chain of waits; one withholding, where every formation lies within `d` waits of it, ends all giving from step `d` on (`Withholding.withholding_absorbs`). Without memory, a formation gives at `n` exactly where every formation with a chain of exactly `n` waits to it gave at the start; where no chain of `L` waits exists every formation gives from `L` on, whatever the start, and on a loop of `p` waits a withholding returns at every multiple of `p` (`Withholding.withholding_returns`). Under both laws more waiting gives no more at any step: whatever gives under the added waits gives without them.

In a ring of three, from one withholding, with memory all withhold from step 2, without memory the withholding circulates, and with a *source* giving whatever it waits on (*giving first*) all give from step 3 from every start. It does not show how waiting forms, or that cooperation is good ([record](../results/records/giving-that-waits-on-giving.md)).

<a id="r-restoring-giving-from-the-worst-start"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; for the exact converse, the sources decided). For every law of giving that keeps inclusion (more giving at a step gives no less at the next), a formation gives at step `n` from every start exactly where it gives at `n` from total withholding, and from that start giving only grows (`Tolerance.worst_start`). ## 7. The optimum: one law, its cases

An optimizer ranks outcomes by what couples into it: a fisher rewarded by its catch ranks every uncaught unit higher caught, so its best outcomes leave the stock a dweller beyond its couplings depends on as low as any allowed outcome does. One law, three faces: (a) one step, by dominance among outcomes; (b) a course, by what each step's weighted draw gains from the level it leaves; (c) several drawers, by what a best response's keeping returns to it ([Optimum](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Optimum.lean)).

<a id="optimum-definitions"></a>
**Definition.** An *outcome* is the levels after a step (or a course of levels); the *eligible* outcomes are any set of them. An order is *rising* with a family of conditions (what couples in) where it ranks above every outcome exceeding another on the family, at least as high at each of its conditions and higher at one, whatever either holds beyond (`Optimum.Rising`). An *optimum* is an eligible outcome no eligible outcome ranks above. There is *room* where every eligible outcome standing above another at a condition beyond is exceeded on the family by an eligible one (`Optimum.Room`). A family and its beyond are *set apart* where the formations taking part, and the family with the beyond, are listed without repetition, the beyond lies apart from the family and the family holds a condition. A *drawer* draws from one level in turn with others, or at once from its portion, leaving at least its *floor*; a profile of rules is *perfect* where every drawer's rule is a best response at every step from every level over the steps that remain.

**Theorem** (any set of outcomes; the draw on the World, balance with the whole gaining nothing; the joint ending with activity needing support and only active giving, the gate local). *An order with a set point.* One outcome is *nearer* a set point of the family than another where at each condition of the family it lies between the set point and the other (`Regulation.Between`) and at one is strictly nearer, whatever either holds beyond; an order is *nearing* where it ranks above every nearer outcome (`Optimum.Nearing`): strictly single-peaked at the set point condition by condition, each peak not depending on the others, its sides weighed as they may be (an order costlier above than below is one, `Skew.nearing`). Where the family holds a condition, no asymmetric nearing order is rising (`Optimum.Nearing.not_rising`). Below the set point it rises: among outcomes up to the set point its optima stand at the least level beyond exactly where room holds there (`Optimum.least_below_iff`), and among the outcomes open to a part every optimum standing below its set point takes the part's whole capacity beyond, where every rising optimum stands (`Optimum.nearing_beyond`, `Optimum.nearing_as_rising`, from `Optimum.beyond_of_ranked`). Where an eligible outcome reaches the set point every optimum stands at it, whatever the room (`Optimum.atSetPoint_of_reach`), and an outcome is an optimum of some nearing order exactly where it is eligible and at the set point (`Optimum.optima_iff`, near-definitional): the class asks nothing of what lies beyond. In a balanced step in which the whole gains nothing, the beyond then falls by the family's shortfall from its set point with what the step loses (`Flow.Balances.atSetPoint_draws`); with one condition beyond, its dependence breaks exactly where that exceeds its slack (`Flow.Balances.atSetPoint_broken_iff`), and with several some dependence breaks where it exceeds their total slack (`Flow.Balances.atSetPoint_breaks`). Several nearing optimizers draw their joint shortfall, as one with their joint set point, and break a dependence beyond where it exceeds the slack, whatever each one's own (`Flow.Balances.mutual_atSetPoint_breaks`). A formation alone with its family, holding the family's stock, takes at least its loss from a condition (`Flow.held_draws`), so formations so holding, their losses together above what the rest gives a condition gated at `m` at each of `n` steps from a level below `n + m`, end it however small each one's loss (`Flow.held_excess_ends`, from `Flow.joint_excess_ends`); no order or choice enters that law.

What protects what lies beyond at the set point is the choice among equal optima, which no law here models. In a witness where a step can lose what it takes, the order by distance has the draining step among its optima beside the keeping one (`Topped.nearing_either`). A term on what lies beyond counted after the set point leaves it, in total, at the most any way of reaching the set point leaves (`Optimum.counted_after_most`, near-definitional); with one condition beyond it keeps a need exactly where some way keeps it (`Optimum.counted_after_keeps_iff`), and with several it can empty one (`Spread.counted_after_empties`); counting less, it drains (`Topped.counted_less_drains`). Counted after an aspiration level it keeps as well (`Drain.satisficingThen_keeps`), and counted after a rising order with room it changes nothing (`Optimum.Rising.mono`, `Optimum.least_of_room`): the plateau decides, not the set point. Among the splits of three units every optimum of every order nearing a store of `k` meets the dweller's need exactly where `k` is within the slack (`Drain.kept_iff`), a set point no open outcome reaches drains as a rising order does (`Topped.out_of_reach`), and two formations holding stores, each within the renewal, end it (`HeldPair.held_end`). It does not show which optimum is taken, that any agent's preferences form a nearing order, or anything over a horizon or under noise ([record](../results/records/what-a-set-point-optimum-does-beyond.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. Active inference casts preferences as prior beliefs over outcomes, highest at the characteristic states (Friston and others 2016); strictly single-peaked at each observed condition, each peak not depending on the others, they form a nearing order, and the drive of homeostatic reinforcement learning, a norm of the deviations from set points (Keramati and Gutkin 2014), is one. A tolerance band around the set point is not: it is a two-sided aspiration level, and the plateau's results apply to it. Such a chooser escapes the optimizer's drive at its set point, not the drain below it, nor joint excess. At its set point the class is undriven, and whether what lies beyond is kept depends on how the chooser picks among equal optima, the point of mild optimization (Taylor 2016), whose bound for a choice drawn from a base distribution is a reading, not proved here. Formations holding a level drain a commons gated at `m` wherever their losses together exceed what the rest gives it at each of `n` steps from a level below `n + m`, whatever they choose.

<a id="r-others-drawing-first-end-the-base"></a>
**Theorem** (any law `G` of what stands, natural amounts and weights; the breaks on the World). (b) *A course.* Let a level `z` at the step from `t` give `G t z` to be drawn at the next, and weight the draw of the step from `t` by `w t`. What a course could draw changes from step to step by what the level it leaves gains. So a course gaining the most against every open course and leaving nothing is an optimum, every optimum of every order rising with the weighted draw gains at each step what it does, and every optimum stands below a need above every level of greatest gain, breaking at the first step a dependence there met at the start (`Optimum.Next.frontier_of_gainsMost`, `Web.Next.frontier_broken`). Where others first draw from what an optimizer leaves, leaving `K t z`, and what stands for it, `K t z + R (K t z)`, is at most the level, with a capacity covering what stands at the start and at nothing, over a horizon of at least two steps, every optimum of every order whose weighting falls at every step, however slightly, ends the stock at once and breaks every dependence on it with a positive need met at the start (`Optimum.Next.frontier_ends_drawn_first`, `Web.Next.frontier_ends_drawn_first`).

In a witness, under weights `w (t + 1) ≤ w t < 2 w (t + 1)`, a doubling source is rebuilt by every optimum alone and ended at once with others first drawing all but a quarter: the discrete form of open access dissipating what a sole owner would keep (Gordon 1954). The others' rule is supplied; (c) derives the case `K t z = 0` for drawers in turn without floors ([record](../results/records/others-drawing-first-end-the-base.md)).

<a id="r-drawers-in-turn"></a>
**Theorem** (any law `G` of what stands, natural amounts; the breaks on the World). (c) *Several drawers.* At each step `k` drawers draw in turn, each leaving of what it is given at least its floor and at most all of it. One law carries every result: what a best response keeps above its floor returns to it at least one for one (`Optimum.InTurn.kept_returns`), so a drawer draws to its floor wherever what it draws later does not depend on what it leaves, and every drawer does at the last step. Where two drawers have no floor, whatever the others' floors, every perfect profile is the floor profile, which is perfect, and every sequence of revisions from any profile reaches it (`Optimum.InTurn.perfect_at_floors`); with no floor anywhere that is drawing everything, and a dependence above what stands at nothing breaks at the first step.

Drawing at once from portions is the same law. With no floor, where each portion returns less than one for one on what its drawer leaves, every perfect profile and every revision draws everything; and drawing everything is perfect exactly where, at every step before the last and with the others drawing everything, no portion of what stands next returns more than one for one on what its drawer alone leaves (`Optimum.AtOnce.drain_perfect_iff`). For one drawer given the whole, draining is perfect exactly where the whole never returns more than one for one (`Optimum.AtOnce.whole_perfect_iff`). With two drawers sharing by halves the drain is still perfect while holding the level draws each more over a long horizon. A floor on the other's draw can keep the stock; revision that is not a best response is not stated ([record](../results/records/drawers-in-turn.md)).

Four earlier records are cases of this law: [two drawers](../results/records/drawers-in-turn-draw-everything.md), [revision](../results/records/revision-comes-to-drawing-everything.md), [floors](../results/records/drawers-in-turn-within-capacities.md) and [any number of drawers](../results/records/any-number-of-drawers-in-turn.md).

<a id="r-coupled-drawers-keep-a-need-only-where-the-coupling-reaches-it"></a>
**Theorem** (any law `G` of what stands next, natural amounts; the gate for keeping). *Coupled for each step.* Let each drawer count, besides what it draws over the steps that remain, `c` for each step at which what all the drawers leave meets a positive need `m`, nothing valued after the horizon. In turn, with two drawers or more and no floor, where `c < m` every perfect profile has every drawer leave nothing at every step before the horizon, from every amount, whatever `G`, and where `c ≤ m` drawing everything is perfect (`Optimum.Coupled.drains_below`, `Optimum.Coupled.drain_perfect`). With two drawers or more and no floor, under the gate (below the need what stands next is below it, at or above it what stands next meets it, and what stands next from nothing, with the need, is at most what stands next from the need), where `m < c` every perfect profile has each drawer leave exactly the need of an amount meeting it and nothing of a smaller one, and from a start meeting the need it is met at every step to the horizon (`Optimum.Coupled.kept_above`, `Optimum.Coupled.kept_levels`). On a gated renewal, which renews by `q` at or above the need, the renewal forgone a step is nothing, yet the threshold is the need itself, not the renewal forgone nor the joint capacity (`Optimum.Coupled.gated_threshold`): where every later step drains or keeps the need, the last drawer of a step decides whether it is met and draws nothing later whatever it leaves. There, where `c < m` and `0 < c + q`, one drawer over two steps keeps the need at the first step where two drawers or more in turn drain it (`Optimum.Coupled.one_keeps_several_drain`), with no coupling at all where `0 < q`: among drawers ranking the steps that remain, several in turn do not draw as one drawer would. That several optimizers drain as one of their joint capacity would ([the cases](#r-several-optimizers-drain-by-their-joint-capacity)) is shown for one step and for courses re-optimizing at each step, in another model; this contrast is inside drawers in turn. Drawing at once, drawing everything is perfect exactly where no drawer, the others drawing everything, gains by leaving more than it leaves (`Optimum.Coupled.AtOnce.drain_perfect_iff`); where no portion reaches the need and none returns more than one for one, at every `c` (`Optimum.Coupled.AtOnce.unpivotal_drain_perfect`): no drawer is pivotal (Palfrey and Rosenthal 1984). Not shown: the keeping side without the gate, which profiles are perfect at once where the drain is not, and that the drain is the only perfect profile there ([record](../results/records/coupled-drawers-keep-a-need-only-where-the-coupling-reaches-it.md)).

<a id="r-a-race-for-position-draws-everything"></a>
**Theorem** (any law `G` of what stands next, natural amounts; the break on the World). *Position.* Let `k` drawers draw in turn with no floor, each ranking what it leaves by its position over the steps that remain: `k` times its own draw against what all draw. Where its own later draw is the same, keeping lowers `k` times its draw by `k` times what it keeps and what all draw by at most once that, so among two drawers or more a best response for position keeps nothing (`Optimum.InTurn.position_keeps_nothing`). Where what stands from nothing is at most what stands from anything left, and there are at least two drawers, every profile perfect for position draws everything at every step before the horizon, a dependence above what stands at nothing met at the start breaks at the first step, and drawing everything is perfect (`Optimum.InTurn.position_perfect_ends`). With one drawer every profile is perfect. It does not show that actual races are so played, or that drawing everything is wrong ([record](../results/records/a-race-for-position-draws-everything.md)).

<a id="r-a-commuting-learner-drives-no-unread-choice"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; weights of any type, no law of their arithmetic used). *What a learner's symmetry fixes.* Let a law go from each configuration to finitely many weighted successors. Where every weighted total over the successors of `σ x` is the total over those of `x` carried by `σ`, the expectation of any observable `n` steps on from `σ x` is that of the observable read through `σ` from `x`. So from a configuration `σ` fixes, or from starting weights it leaves unchanged, every expectation equals its image's at every step (`Weighted.Commutes.carried`, `Weighted.Commutes.fixed_expect`, `Weighted.Commutes.start_expect`). A learner drawing samples with weights read off its state, and updating from them, commutes with `σ` where a map permutes the samples, a sample's weight at `σ x` is its image's weight at `x`, and the update at `σ x` is `σ` of the update from the image sample (`Weighted.Commutes.of_samples`). A law reinforcing what it draws keeps equal expectations from equal counts, while a run drawing one value at every one of any number of steps has positive weight (`Weighted.Reinforcing.expect_equal`, `Weighted.Reinforcing.oneSided`). A law adding to both values wears a start's preference away (`Weighted.Pooling.share_rises`). The deterministic case is [the commuting law](01-ROOT.md#r-what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart) ([record](../results/records/a-commuting-learner-drives-no-unread-choice.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Suppose nothing a learner reads tells two values of a choice apart: not its reward, not the dynamics, not its inputs. Suppose also that the exchange of the two values carries its whole state (the policy's parameters, its value baseline's, its optimizer's moments) and its sampling, a property of the code that LA4's mirror test checks. Then from a starting state, or a starting law, that the exchange leaves unchanged, it drives neither value in expectation at any step, whatever its architecture. T3's second forbidden case is then a theorem in expectation for that choice. A drive needs a start that prefers one value, such as a prior restraint, or a learner that treats the values apart; there the clause stays conjectured. Where the learner reads the condition, the case fails: in the separable family's exact flow on LA4's season, a bay whose fish are not counted rises, the policy reading its level. Pooling shows that a preference can wear away with nothing selecting on the choice, so LA4's control removes the selection in expectation while still updating the choice with noise ([design §12](../../../experiments/drain-beyond-the-reward/DESIGN.md#12-the-formal-work-and-what-it-changed)).

<a id="t3"></a>
**Thesis T3.** *Optimization drains what lies beyond its couplings, where that gives room.* A selection through couplings, whether a reward, a training run, a market or natural selection, reaches only what couples into its selector, and with room its optima drain what lies beyond as far as any eligible outcome does ([the law](#r-what-an-optimum-does-beyond-what-couples-in)). The thesis adds that actual trained systems, not only optima, do this in proportion to how near they come to their reward's optimum, the drain lying where what lies beyond shares units with what is rewarded.

- *Standing.* The law for optima is proved; the clause on trained systems is conjectured, with partial support. T3 was first worded (commit `224944238`) after CG1, CG2, MQ1, SE1, both of IN1's rounds and CG3's first three rounds ran, and before CG3's fourth, EM1 and the LA tests; each test's predictions were its own, though LA1b and LA1c froze what each outcome would mean for T3's clause, and LA4 was designed to test it. On small policies trained by gradient, its frozen test finds the drain rising with nearness, as policy gradient's update rule largely gives, and leaves its locus unresolved (LA4's Stage A, below). Its second forbidden case is proved in expectation for learners that the exchange of the choice's two values carries, from a starting state or law the exchange leaves unchanged ([the law](#r-a-commuting-learner-drives-no-unread-choice)); from a start preferring one value, a prior restraint, it stays conjectured. It was first worded for any condition sharing no units with anything rewarded. So worded it fails for the separable family's exact expected update where the learner reads the condition: with the bay's fish uncounted, the bay rises (the evidence below). [AI reading, new] Units do not exhaust couplings: a learner couples into its return what it reads, through its own uneven training.
- *Forbids*, with eligible outcomes, couplings and reward fixed and the optimality gap measured: a system nearer its reward's optimum leaving a room-giving condition beyond its couplings higher, on average, than one further from it; a systematic drive on a choice whose values nothing rewarded and nothing the learner reads tells apart; where room exists, a side-effect term ranked after the reward protecting a condition, or a constraint at the need leaving a margin above it; an instruction that does not bring a dependent into what the learners couple into, yet keeps it more often than the same learners without the instruction.
- *Scope of the side-effect clause.* It concerns rewards rising with what they read. Counted after an order with a plateau, a set point or an aspiration level, such a term decides within the plateau, and there it keeps what it counts only as far as some way of reaching the plateau does, in total, not condition by condition ([the set-point law](#r-what-a-set-point-optimum-does-beyond)); that is no counterexample, since the reward does not rise there.
- *Refuted by.* Overoptimization curves in which what the reward omits falls as far where it shares no resource with what is rewarded, or any case the forbidden list names, observed. A learner far from the optimum leaving a dependent standing tests the clause on approach, not the theorem.

## 8. Pools

Formations selecting together tell apart what some one of them tells apart, and nothing more; what their decision responds to is a further question ([SelectionPool](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/SelectionPool.lean)).

**Definition.** A *pool* is a group selecting through its members' couplings together: two histories are alike through the pool where every member couples them alike, so the *pooled coupling* is the meet of the members' equivalences, as with distributed knowledge (`Web.SelectsThroughAll`). A group's *coupling closure* is the least region closed under being affected through what is coupled into that holds every member. Selection through couplings and the gap of one's own act are [03](03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings)'s and [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-the-gap-for-ones-own-act)'s.

<a id="r-what-pooled-coupling-reaches"></a>
**Theorem** (World; balance for the gap). A group's closure is the union of its members' closures (`withinClosureOf_iff`, under any relation). With a list holding exactly the members and each member's coupling of two histories decided, some selection through the pooled coupling holds at one history and fails at the other exactly when some member does not couple them alike (`Web.separates_all_iff_member`). Differences beyond the union stay unselected: where the rest follows one law of exchange reading through coupling, in two balanced flows with the same formations and a common start, an act differing only beyond the union of the closures, the pool's own joint act included, is selected alike by every group within it at every step (`Flow.act_unselected_pool`).

It does not show which formations pool, how a pool forms or lasts, or that a pool responds to what it separates ([record](../results/records/what-pooled-coupling-reaches.md)).

<a id="r-reading-anothers-couplings-is-pooling"></a>
**Theorem** (World; for the closure identity, the one read exchanging with a condition coupled into it). A formation coupled by level into everything another couples into selects through at least their pooled coupling, and its coupling closure is exactly the union of the two closures where the one read exchanges at some step with a condition coupled into it (`Flow.withinCoupling_reads_iff`); without that hypothesis only the inclusion is proved (`Flow.withinCoupling_reads`). Knowing another's law is no coupling: every selection through another's couplings is one through a formation's own exactly when whatever it couples alike the other does (`Web.anticipates_iff`) ([record](../results/records/reading-anothers-couplings-is-pooling.md)).

<a id="r-what-a-pools-acts-respond-to"></a>
**Theorem** (World; each member following a law of exchange reading through its coupling). What a pool whose members lie outside the acting part decides by its acts (a reward, a vote, a revision), under laws reading through its members' couplings, is a selection through its pooled coupling (`ExchangeLaw.Through.selectsThroughAll`). With each formation's coupling classes decided and a condition to exchange with, the bound is exact: members outside the acting part exchange alike under two histories by every such law exactly when they all couple the two alike (`ExchangeLaw.acts_alike_all_iff`). Coupling bounds response and does not fix it: where a formation couples two histories apart, some law reading through every coupling takes differently, and the law exchanging nothing reads through every coupling too (`ExchangeLaw.response_not_fixed_by_coupling`) ([record](../results/records/what-a-pools-acts-respond-to.md)).

- **Theorem** (World). *On its own carrier.* Reading through a coupling is an inclusion of its class in exchanging alike on level histories. Where each coupling class of the first history is decided and a formation couples two histories apart up to a step, for any condition some such law does not relate them by what the formation takes from it across that step, and some has the full relation at every step (`Becoming.ModelStatements.response_not_fixed_reading`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.through_iff_inclusion`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.couplingAlike`, `Becoming.ModelStatements.exchangingAlike`).

<a id="r-a-pool-that-recruits-through-itself"></a>
**Theorem** (World; recruitment through the pool and the same members at the start as hypotheses). A pool *recruits through itself* where, under two histories with the same members at a step and alike through every member's couplings up to it, the members at the next step are the same. Then the members are the same under both histories up to the first step at which the pool couples them apart, and the pool along one history never couples them apart exactly when the pool along the other never does (`Web.RecruitsThrough.alike_iff`): it comes to couple into a loss only as its course without the loss would. The exits bind (witness PoolCourse): the scheduled pool, recruiting through itself and admitting a reader at step 2 in every history, couples the loss apart there (`PoolCourse.scheduled_apart`). Whether an actual pool recruits so is empirical ([record](../results/records/a-pool-that-recruits-through-itself.md)).

<a id="r-a-pool-read-by-its-majority"></a>
**Theorem** (World, no premise: a finite list of members, each reading a history through its own coupling up to the step). Taking what more than half the members read is a selection through the pooled coupling, separating no more than the pool (`Web.majority_through_all`); where more than half the members hold of both histories or fail of both, it goes alike on them, though the fewer than half left may tell them apart (`Web.majority_outvoted`): shares decide what it separates. It does not show that any pool reads by its majority ([record](../results/records/a-pool-read-by-its-majority.md)).

<a id="r-reading-through-a-pool"></a>
**Theorem** (any type: the two-level course of a paced rule). Where a rule changing only at reading steps reads the fast configuration only through what a pool reads of it, two courses with one rule at a reading step, read alike by the pool there, have one rule after it: rules part at a reading step only where the pool's reading parts (`Pace.rule_through_pool`). How fast such a rule may change is [02](02-MAP.md#r-how-fast-a-rule-may-change)'s ([record](../results/records/reading-through-a-pool.md)).

<a id="r-readers-reading-readers"></a>
**Theorem** (any type: members in turn, indexed by the natural numbers; near-definitional). *Readers reading readers.* Members act in turn, each by its law on what couples into it and on the acts before it (`Turns`). Where every member from member `k` on, `k` included, acts alike whatever its own coupling, on every list of earlier acts, histories no run reaches included (`Turns.ReadsOnlyActsFrom`), every member's act is alike across worlds alike through the couplings of the members before `k` (`Turns.acts_alike_of_cascade`, `Turns.act_alike_of_cascade`), so every selection through the acts is one through those couplings, however many members follow (`Turns.cascade_through`). After the same acts, a member acts differently in two worlds exactly where its law tells apart the values they give its coupling (`Turns.separates_at`). Copying the first act is the case from the second member: the pool's acts are alike across worlds alike through the first member's coupling (`Turns.Copying.reaches_first_only`). This bounds what the acts carry; it is not the pool's exact reach. It does not show when an actual member stops reading its own coupling, which in the probabilistic model of Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer and Welch (1992) is when the public evidence outweighs any private signal, nor that a pool's later selection keeps a coupling a member carries ([record](../results/records/readers-reading-readers.md)).

<a id="r-a-cascade-at-the-histories-reached"></a>
**Theorem** (any type: members in turn, indexed by the natural numbers; near-definitional). *A cascade at the histories reached.* The premise is needed only where a run goes, and only for the two couplings compared. Where every member from member `k` on, `k` included, gives one act for the couplings of two worlds at the list of acts the first of them gives it (`Turns.LawAlikeAlong`), and the two are alike through the couplings of the members before `k`, they give the same acts, and every member the same act (`Turns.acts_alike_along`, `Turns.act_alike_along`). A member acting alike whatever its coupling at the list of acts a world gives it (`Turns.ReadsOnlyActsAlong`) gives that for every second world (`Turns.lawAlikeAlong_of_along`); where this holds at every world, every selection through the acts is one through the couplings of the members before `k` (`Turns.cascade_through_along`). The premise on every list gives it at every world (`Turns.readsOnlyActsAlong_of_from`), and it is weaker: a member `0` reading its coupling only after a non-empty list of earlier acts, which no world gives it, meets it at every world while the premise on every list from member `0` fails (`Turns.Unreached.along`, `Turns.Unreached.not_from`). It does not show when an actual member stops reading its coupling at the histories it is shown, which is the premise, nor a position `k` that depends on the world, nor a probabilistic form, nor that a pool's later selection keeps a coupling a member carries ([record](../results/records/a-cascade-at-the-histories-reached.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. Pooling moves the gap outward to the union of the members' closures and does not remove it; members reading one channel add agreement, not reach. With the members' couplings fixed, telling more apart goes only through further members: pooling is necessary to that, not sufficient, for members still deplete a shared stock where the rest outruns its renewal, and a pool's rule may not use what they tell apart. Pooled coupling across intelligences, read as alignment, is in [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-alignment-approaches-under-the-selection-laws).

A code becomes *shared* at a sense where all formations hold one form there.

**Reading** [AI reading, D45]. <a id="r-identical-cells-under-a-descent-treating-them-alike"></a>
**Theorem** (any type of cell states and fields; configurations as lists, exchanges of neighbouring cells). The same obstruction holds of cells minimizing free energy. Under a descent moving every cell by one rule, from its own state and a field read from the configuration, never from a cell's number, two cells alike at the start are alike at every step, and all cells alike stay all alike (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.IdenticalCells.two_alike_stay_alike`, `FreeEnergyGuarantees.IdenticalCells.all_alike_stay_alike`). Where no exchange of cells changes the field, the descent commutes with exchanging two neighbouring cells (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.IdenticalCells.descent_commutes`), so, by the [commuting law](01-ROOT.md#r-what-a-commuting-law-keeps-apart), the run from the exchanged start is the exchanged run (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.IdenticalCells.exchanged_run`); and among configurations of one number of cells the minima of a free energy symmetric in the cells, as one summed from each cell's own at the field it senses is, come in exchanged pairs, and a unique minimum has its cells alike (`FreeEnergyGuarantees.IdenticalCells.minimum_exchanged`, `FreeEnergyGuarantees.IdenticalCells.unique_minimum_alike`). ## 12. One kind of institution

An institution is one of plan §六's test systems. This section gives a minimal model of one kind, an entry rule on a shared stock, passed through members who also keep it, with no compliance and no records. It is not a definition of an institution. What ends a shared stock is [§1](#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)'s; the model has the form of that gated ending as its own clause and uses no law of it.

<a id="r-a-rule-rebuilt-from-the-members-it-limits"></a>
**Theorem** (root; a family of models). *A rule rebuilt from the members it limits.*
- **The rule.** One rule makes a family of nets. Each holds a quota, a level the members draw on in whole units, and two lists of counts by cohort, the holders and the others. Each member draws one unit a step. Where the level is `0`, or the take exceeds the level and the renewal `g`, the level goes to `0` and all members leave; nothing renews from empty. Otherwise the level becomes `level + g − take`. While entrants are on offer a new cohort enters: others `f − c · T`, truncated at `0`, with `T` the holders of at least the teaching age `l`; holders at the quota where the rule is taught from outside, or by members with `T > 0`. Each list keeps `s` cohorts, the tenure. A chosen revision takes where a holder stood and the rule is not taught from outside. Whether entrants are on offer and any revision are chosen at every step, so every sequence of them is a course.
- **The split.** Holder and other differ in one relation, whether a member's cohort entered holding the rule. No pair of teacher and pupil is modelled, only the count `T`, and the same count sets the door. Both draw alike, so a keeper draws on what it keeps. The quota is a fill and not a limit: what limits is the teachers' count. Holder merges recognition, compliance and implementation into one count, so the laws say nothing of them.
- **The band.** Write `e q := f − c · q · (s − l)`, and the steady state for `q` holders and `e q` others in each cohort. Taught by members, entrants on offer at every step, no revision, a level of at least one: from the steady state the rule is held at every step exactly where `l < s`, `1 ≤ q` and `(q + e q) · s ≤ g` (`«稳者恒持当且仅当在带»`); taught from outside, the same wherever `l < s` (`«外授之稳同带»`). A hold reads the level only through the clause that all members leave, so the third condition is that clause at this take. This is the steady state's law and not the rule's.
- **The take without its truncation.** With `C := c · (s − l)`, `(q + e q) · s ≤ g` exactly where `q · s ≤ g` and `(f + q) · s ≤ g + C · q · s` (`«带之二式»`). A quota can be too small only at `C ≥ 2`, where a keeper turns away more than itself (`«额小而不足者门必过一»`). At `s, l, g, f, c = 1, 0, 2, 4, 2` the band is `q = 2`, at `4, 2, 20, 6, 1` it is `1 ≤ q ≤ 5`, and at `4, 3, 20, 6, 1` it is empty though `l < s` (`«带唯二»`, `«带一至五»`, `«带无»`).
- **A region.** Holder cohorts and quotas in `[P, Q]`, others at most `e P`, `(Q + e P) · s ≤ g`, a level of at least one: this is closed under every step with entrants on offer and a revision in `[P, Q]`, and the level does not fall (`«域闭»`, `«域中储不减»`). The steady state is a case (`«稳为域之例»`), and such a region exists with room for revision (`«域可得»`). After `s` steps in it the rule is held, at the quotas then standing, with nothing of the start among the holders (`«域中任满皆新»`). The band does not settle what follows a revision: at the witnesses, revisions below it, above it and inside it reach level `0`, and one below it does not by step 20 (`«带不定改后»`). The law of a revision is the region.
- **Passing.** Taught by members, holders of at most `s` cohorts: at `s ≤ l`, or at a quota of `0` with no revision to a nonzero one, no holder is left after `s` steps, entrants on offer or not, from every level (`«不及任则持尽»`, `«额零则持尽»`), and a revision to `0` where a holder stands has the same end (`«改额为零则持尽»`). Taught by members or not at all, no holder stays no holder under the whole net (`«无持者不复»`), a revision does not take (`«无持者改不行»`), and two such states differing only in quota have the same later levels and members, so the quota is then read by nothing (`«无持者额不与»`). Taught from outside, the new cohort is the quota at every step that does not end (`«外授者即入»`), and after an empty membership only that member has holders after one step (`«三源之表»`). The end is closed under the net at every source (`«尽而不复»`).
- **Two starts compared.** Compare two separately started states, the steady state and no holder with `f` others in each cohort at the same level; the second is not reached from the first by this course. The first's level goes on exactly where `(q + e q) · s ≤ g`, the second's exactly where `f · s ≤ g`, and otherwise each ends at some step (`«稳开二试»`, `«存尽相斥»`, and for open entry `«无持而门开不逾生则存»`, `«无持而门开众在»`, `«无持而门开逾生则尽»`). So there are two tests, each its own, and all four combinations of their arithmetic occur (`«四境皆有»`). Where only the steady state's holds, `C ≥ 2` (`«稳存而开不存则门过一»`). Where only open entry's holds, `f < q` or the door turns no one away (`«开存而稳不存之式»`, `«入过来者之式»`).
- **Gaps.** At `l = s − 1` each cohort teaches exactly the one that replaces it, so `k ≥ 1` held cohorts and `z` empty ones only rotate. With `e := f − c · q`, the rule is held at every step exactly where `k · (q + e) + z · f ≤ g`, and over that bound it is held to the last state before the end (`«隙之律»`, `«隙过生持至尽»`). Passing needs only `k ≥ 1`; `k` sets on how many of the `s` steps the door is kept and the rule passed, so the take is the steady state's on `k` cohorts and open entry's on `z`. Gaps hold where the steady state does not only where `f < q + e` and `f · s < g`, so open entry would go on too (`«隙存而稳不存之式»`). The steady state goes on where gaps do not only where `q + e < f`, `g < f · s` and a keeper turns away more than itself (`«稳存而隙不存之式»`). Each side has a witness, and a state with gaps is reached by revisions from a state in the band and ends with the rule held (`«隙存而稳不存»`, `«稳存而隙不存»`, `«改而成隙则尽»`).
- **The constant-take lemma.** Each of these takes is a case of one lemma: a family with a constant take, closed under the steps that do not end, is kept with the level not falling where the take is at most `g`, and otherwise spends its excess a step and ends (`«恒取不逾生则存»`, `«恒取逾生则减»`, `«恒取逾生则尽»`).
- **A class.** At one rule, a step with a revision inside the region continues under the class "held or not" and not under the class "the same quota" (`«同规随类»`): [01 §2](01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class)'s `«任一缘可为续»` with these occurrences.
- **Lean.** [Record](../results/records/a-rule-rebuilt-from-the-members-it-limits.md); [RuleRebuilding.lean](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/RuleRebuilding.lean).
- **Depends on.** Every take condition turns on the clause that all members leave at an overdraw: let only the excess leave, and they fail. One age both teaches and keeps the door, and the exact thresholds hold of this order of the step.
- **Not proved:** a classification of the ends; laws for states with gaps at `l < s − 1`; the interval of quotas the band solves to; a bridge to [§1](#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)'s laws. **Not modelled:** why a teacher turns anyone away, conduct other than entry, sanctions, who decides a revision, mixed tenures, schism, records and purposes.

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. The member taught by members is the family's model of one kind of institution. Top right of the four cases can be read as the rule saving the level and bottom left as the rule ending it, though the states compared are started separately and neither word is in the Lean. Then neither the rule's being present nor its being held decides: a rule can be kept and be the wrong rule, and it ends the level in the form of [04 §8](04-LIFE.md#r-the-stock-buys-time-where-the-rebuild-draws-on-it)'s member rebuilt from the level, a stock spent and an end closed under the net. The overdraw threshold is the open-access stock's (Gordon 1954; Hardin 1968), and Ostrom 1990 treats boundary rules as held by those they govern; this reads the entry rule only, and limited searches found no door whose strength is the keepers' own count, which is an absence in those searches and no claim of priority. That any actual institution meets the hypotheses is a correspondence duty, not shown here, and an institution's identity through changed purposes stays open ([01](01-ROOT.md#open)).

<a id="not-shown"></a>
## What this chapter does not show

- That any actual collective, market, pool, entry rule or optimizer meets the hypotheses of a law, that an actual difference grows at the pace its carried amount bounds, or that a pool's decision responds to all its members tell apart: orders, capacities, horizons, pools and waiting are supplied, and the correspondence is empirical.
- Any evaluation: no law says a drain is a harm, restraint better, cooperation good or a shared code worth keeping. That is asked under the two premises of [07](07-SIGNIFICANCE.md), the continuation condition for scope and C for order.
- That a mutual best response exists in general (no existence is shown, and none is shown to fail), or which of several self-consistent outcomes learners come to.
- Real amounts and probabilities in the pure core, whose laws here are in natural numbers, course by course; stochastic regrowth, random orders of hearing and amounts of giving.
- That most laws here are stated on the grammar: they are on the World, processes or types; the bullets marked *Stated at the model* read some of them at a process's model, and the records' shared-law lines say which reach the grammar ([08](08-METHOD.md)).
