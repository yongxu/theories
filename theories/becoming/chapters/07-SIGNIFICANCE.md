## 1. How evaluation forms within the unfolding

On the thesis of one relational unfolding, argued and not proved ([08](08-METHOD.md#one-relational-unfolding)), positions, regions and standards form within the unfolding; none outside it owns value. A *center* is a formation; its own region is its closure under some relation of the unfolding: along the empty relation its own events, along feeding its feeding closure (`Web.selfValuation_own_least`). **Definition.** A *significance* `sig` says, for a standpoint `p`, a step `t` and a formation `f`, whether from `p` the continuation of `f` at `t` has significance. Standpoints may be of any type; on the theory's reading a standpoint is a center, a formation of the unfolding, and the laws that need that tie state it (`forced_iff_center`).

The definition ties `sig` to no region: it admits any predicate on standpoints, steps and formations, including one that no center's region bounds. ## 2. How two courses differ, with no premise

At each step a formation is *admitted* where its conditions' levels meet its requirements. To compare what a part does, withhold its taking and giving, keep the rest of the exchange, and compare the levels with and without it. No significance or standpoint enters; the part is a region of formations, and keeping the rest of the exchange leaves out how the rest would answer without it.

<a id="r-closing-needs-net-draw"></a>
**Theorem** (World; balance). Take a part, a decided family of formations, its withheld levels and its [margin](02-MAP.md#margin) as the map defines them (`Flow.WithheldLevels`, `Flow.Balances.supported_iff_within_margin`); where the rest would overdraw there are no withheld levels, and nothing to compare. Where they exist they are unique (`Flow.WithheldLevels.unique`), and in a balanced flow, at each step `t`, each level with what the part has taken from the condition before `t` equals the level without it with what the part has given it before `t` (`Flow.Balances.withheld`). So closing needs a net draw and opening a net give: where the part has given each condition a formation requires at `t` at least what it has taken from it, the formation admitted without the part is supported with it (`Flow.Balances.supported_of_net_give`); where it has taken at least what it has given, a formation supported with it is admitted without it (`Flow.Balances.admits_of_net_draw`); constructively closing refutes the margin at the required conditions, naming the condition overdrawn needs excluded middle, and the law is near-definitional ([record](../results/records/closing-needs-net-draw.md)).

<a id="r-four-cases-of-a-part"></a>
**Definition and Theorem** (World). A level path `l` *includes* `l'` when at every step it admits every formation `l'` admits, and *exceeds* it when at some step it admits one `l'` does not; `l` *widens* `l'` when it includes and exceeds it; two *keep the same* when each includes the other, and are *mixed* when each exceeds the other. Widening is one-way and transitive (`Web.Widens.not_reverse`, `Web.Widens.trans`) and the cases exclude one another constructively (`Web.Widens.not_keeps`, `Web.Widens.not_mixed`, `Web.Keeps.not_mixed`), and wherever inclusion or exceeding is decided both ways one of them holds, a root law for every relation (`«四比之周»`); that every comparison falls under one is classical. In a balanced flow a part giving each condition at every step at least what it takes closes nothing, one giving no more opens nothing (`Flow.Balances.closes_nothing`). With balance before each step at the conditions each formation requires, the levels with a part widen those without it exactly when its net draw stays within the margin for every formation the levels without it admit and, at some step, brings within it one they do not (`Flow.widens_iff_within_margin_at`). ([record](../results/records/four-cases-of-a-part.md)) Widening alone does not settle a mixed part; a further judgment names its criterion and who holds it, and the criterion need not be a number.

## 4. Scope and order: two readings, two roles

A self acting for its own continuation presupposes that some continuation is counted; how far that reaches and how courses are ranked are separate questions, and the results here answer each under a reading it names.

| Reading | Role | What it does | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|---|

| C: widening what can form is better | **order** | ranks a course above every course it widens | rank mixed comparisons |

Neither is asserted, and neither is the account's own: each is an order or scope some formations hold, and the results say what follows where it is held. <a id="future-path"></a>
### 4.6 The future without the step: one path

The step of invariance extends scope to losses that return to no one. Read with time, those losses include everything after every present center has ended, the future that the condition's "continuation into the future" names. This section joins §4.3 and §4.5 into one path that reaches part of that future with no step of invariance, and says where it can be denied.

<a id="r-what-a-line-holds-and-a-regularity-takes-in"></a>
**Theorem** (root; any type, and witnesses). *What a line must hold, and what a regularity takes in.*

- **What a line must pass, its class must hold.** Where a region, given by a decided predicate, is left under a class only into a set of occurrences, a line under that class from inside the region to outside holds a member of that set alike to its start, and reaches it (`«所经必同»`, proved from a step going out of the region, [01 §9.9](01-ROOT.md#r-not-going-out-of-a-region)). Where the class holds none of them alike to the start, nothing outside is reached (`«所经不同则不及»`).
- **Holding a member's effects.** Say a class holds a member's effects where everything the member conditions is alike to it (`«所缘同于»`). Under a constant class, everything downstream of anything alike to such a member is alike to it and lies on a line under the class from there, so a line that reaches it reaches all that follows (`«常闭则后皆续»`, `«常闭则所及皆及»`). Given constancy, holding a member's effects is equivalent to everything downstream of it being alike to it (`«常类之闭当且仅当后皆同类»`): the backward direction is the one-step case, and the forward direction is constancy's work.
- **Up to the net's end.** A class is *constant up to the net's end* where whatever an alike occurrence conditions, this one conditions something alike to it, unless this one conditions nothing (`«至终有常»`); a line under it ends only at an occurrence that conditions nothing. Constancy gives it (`«有常则至终有常»`). A relation under which a line ends at an occurrence that still conditions something lacks it (`«亡而网续则不至终有常»`). Under it the same laws hold, for a member that conditions something (needed where the start is another occurrence alike to it) (`«至终常闭则后皆续»`, `«至终常闭则所及皆及»`, `«至终常类之闭当且仅当后皆同类»`).
- **Witnesses.** Neither premise can be dropped from these laws:
  - where a member's line runs through a condition but the member's effects leave its class, a constant class holding the member with the condition does not reach what the condition feeds (`Unclosed.not_taken_in`);
  - where the class is not constant even up to the net's end, a class holding a member's effects need not have what follows them alike to it (`Inconstant.not_all_follow`).

  In a finite net with two families, each of two members in turn:
  - the one is not constant (`FiniteFamilies.one_not_constant`);
  - the class of one family, narrower than the one, is constant up to the net's end, takes in everything downstream of the family's first birth and reaches nothing of the other family (`FiniteFamilies.family_narrower`, `FiniteFamilies.family_constant_to_end`, `FiniteFamilies.family_takes_in_all`, `FiniteFamilies.other_family_outside`);
  - the organism's class is not constant up to the net's end, its line ending at old age while that stage still conditions something (`FiniteFamilies.member_not_constant_to_end`).

  In a finite net with two families sharing a condition they affect, and a fourth thing joined to nothing, every premise holds at once. The class holding the families with their condition:
  - is narrower than the one (`FiniteSharedCondition.held_narrower`);
  - is constant up to the net's end (`FiniteSharedCondition.held_constant_to_end`);
  - holds the first member's effects (`FiniteSharedCondition.first_birth_held`).

  Its line from the first family's first birth reaches the second family past the first member's end (`FiniteSharedCondition.reaches_other_past_end`) and never the fourth thing (`FiniteSharedCondition.fourth_outside`). The member's class is not constant up to the net's end (`FiniteSharedCondition.member_not_constant_to_end`), and the one is not constant (`FiniteSharedCondition.one_not_constant`).

<a id="r-what-leaves-a-class-follows-its-line"></a>
**Theorem** (root; any type, and witnesses). *What leaves a class follows its line.* Under a class constant up to the net's end, where a member conditions something not alike to it, every occurrence alike to the member that conditions anything conditions something alike to that effect and outside the class (`«出类随行»`), so every one on its line does (`«出类随线»`); where one such occurrence conditions nothing alike to that effect, the class is not constant up to the net's end (`«出类不随则不至终有常»`, witness `CleanStep`). This is nearly the definition of constancy up to the net's end, read at a member whose effects the class does not hold, and "alike to that effect" is only as fine as the class divides what lies outside it. A class can be constant up to the net's end without holding a member's effects: in a finite net without cycles, a class holding a member and part of what follows it can be constant up to the net's end though not constant, not holding the member's effects, while something outside it follows every occurrence on its line that conditions anything (`FiniteWake.constant_to_end`, `FiniteWake.not_constant`, `FiniteWake.not_held`, `FiniteWake.wake_follows`) ([record](../results/records/what-leaves-a-class-follows-its-line.md)).

<a id="r-constancy-read-at-a-members-class"></a>
**Theorem** (root; any type, and witnesses). *Constancy read at a member's class, what the reach asks, and how what lies outside it is divided.*

- **At a member's class.** Constancy up to the net's end asks its condition of every two alike occurrences, so it constrains every block of a class, what lies outside a member's class included. Asked only of the occurrences alike to a member, it is constancy up to the net's end *at the member's class* (`«本类至终有常»`), and constancy up to the net's end is that at every member's class (`«至终有常之逐类»`).
- **The laws need no more.** Each law above that asks constancy up to the net's end uses only occurrences alike to the member, so each holds at the member's class, and the forms above are corollaries (`«本类常闭则所缘同类»`, `«本类常闭则后皆续»`, `«本类常闭则所及皆及»`, `«本类常则闭当且仅当后皆同类»`, `«本类出类随行»`, `«本类出类随线»`, `«出类不随则本类不至终有常»`). A line that ends while it still conditions something fails the condition at its own class (`«亡而网续则本类不至终有常»`), and the organism's class fails it at the first member in both finite witnesses (`FiniteFamilies.member_not_constant_at`, `FiniteSharedCondition.member_not_constant_at`). The condition asks more than that a line end only where the net does: in `CleanStep` every line from the member ends only where the net does, and the class still fails the condition at the member (`CleanStep.ends_where_net_ends`, `CleanStep.not_constant_at`). A family's class, and the class holding the families with their condition, meet it at every member, being constant up to the net's end (`«至终有常之逐类»`, `FiniteFamilies.family_constant_to_end`, `FiniteSharedCondition.held_constant_to_end`); the class of `Inconstant`, which holds the member's effects, fails it at the member (`Inconstant.not_constant_at`).
- **What the reach asks of the class.** With no regularity assumed, everything downstream of a member lies on its line under a class exactly where everything downstream of it is alike to it (`«后皆同类则后皆续»`, `«后皆续当且仅当后皆同类»`), that is, where the class holds the effects of the member and of each stage of its line (`«后皆续当且仅当自与线皆闭»`). So the reach asks that the class hold the member's effects (`«后皆续则所缘同于»`), and, like the condition at the member's class (`«亡而网续则本类不至终有常»`), it excludes a line from the member that ends while its last stage still conditions something (`«亡而网续则后不皆续»`; the organism's case in `FiniteFamilies.member_no_reach`). With the member's effects held, where the member conditions something, the condition at its class gives the reach (`«本类常闭则后皆续»`), and more: the class then holds the effects of every alike occurrence, on the member's line or not (`«本类常闭则所缘同类»`). The laws above cannot drop the condition (`Inconstant.not_constant_at`, `Inconstant.not_all_follow`), but the reach does not need it. In a net where a member conditions one thing, that thing conditions another, an occurrence alike to the member conditions a further thing, and nothing else is conditioned, the class that holds all of them but that further thing holds the member's effects and everything downstream of the member lies on its line, while the class fails the condition at the member and does not hold the effects of the other occurrence, which lies off the member's line (`OtherLeaves.reach_without_condition`, `OtherLeaves.other_not_held`, `OtherLeaves.other_off_line`).
- **How what lies outside is divided.** It bears on the condition only through what is alike to an effect that leaves the member's class: two classes that give the member the same class, and agree on what is alike to each such effect of an occurrence alike to the member, give the same verdict (`«外分唯系出类之同»`). Where the member conditions something, the condition at its class is that the member and each alike occurrence follow each other: whatever an alike occurrence conditions is alike to something the member conditions, and whatever the member conditions, each alike occurrence that conditions anything conditions something alike to it (`«本类常当且仅当与己相随»`). So how what lies outside is divided bears on it only through what is alike to the member's own effects that leave the class: two classes that give the member the same class, and agree on what is alike to each of those effects, give the same verdict (`«外分唯系己出之同»`). Where the class holds the member's effects, none of them leaves, so every division that gives the member the same class gives the same verdict (`«闭则外分无关»`, applied both ways). Merging what lies outside keeps the condition (`«外合不失本类之常»`), so where a predicate decides the member's class, the split into that class and the rest (`«二分»`) asks least (`«本类之常至二分»`). Under the split the condition at the member's class is exactly this: where an occurrence alike to the member conditions something in the class, every alike occurrence that conditions anything does; and where one conditions something outside, every alike occurrence that conditions anything does (`«二分本类常当且仅当内外皆随»`). So it holds where every alike occurrence that conditions anything conditions something in the class and something outside (`«内外皆缘则二分本类常»`).
- **Witnesses.**
  - Dividing what lies outside more finely can break the condition. In a net where a member conditions one thing outside its class, an occurrence alike to it conditions another, and nothing else is conditioned, the split meets the condition at the member without holding its effects, and dividing the two things apart, with the member's class unchanged, does not (`FinerOutside.split_constant_at`, `FinerOutside.not_held`, `FinerOutside.finer_not_constant_at`, `FinerOutside.same_class`). In a net where a member and an occurrence alike to it each condition one thing in their class and one outside, and nothing else is conditioned, the split meets the condition at the member, and dividing the two outside things apart, with the member's class unchanged, does not (`FinerWake.both`, `FinerWake.split_constant_at`, `FinerWake.finer_not_constant_at`, `FinerWake.same_class`).
  - Constancy up to the net's end can fail under the split while the member's class keeps the condition. In a net where a member conditions something alike to it and, of two occurrences alike outside, one conditions the member and the other only something outside, and nothing else is conditioned, the split meets the condition at the member and holds its effects but is not constant up to the net's end, and a finer division of what lies outside, with the member's class unchanged, is (`UnevenOutside.split_constant_at`, `UnevenOutside.held`, `UnevenOutside.split_not_constant_to_end`, `UnevenOutside.finer_constant_to_end`, `UnevenOutside.same_class`).

**Argument** [AI reading; each step cites its Lean, and the joining of the steps is argued]. Premises (not those of [§4.2](#step)'s argument for the step):

- (P1) Held de se, acting presupposes that one's own continuation has significance ([§4.1](#decomposition)).
- (P2) The self whose continuation is presupposed is a line under a class on the root ([§4.5](#condition-on-the-root)), and that class is constant up to the net's end at oneself ([above](#r-constancy-read-at-a-members-class)): whatever an occurrence alike to oneself conditions, every occurrence alike to oneself that conditions anything conditions something alike to it. It is a regularity of its own: wherever one's line takes a step, it ends only where the net itself ends (`«亡而网续则本类不至终有常»`). A self that ends at once, a single moment, meets it trivially; with acting, P4 excludes that, since whatever one conditions is then alike to oneself and one's line takes a step. Where one acts, the condition is that oneself and each occurrence alike to oneself follow each other in what they condition (`«本类常当且仅当与己相随»`), so how the class divides what lies outside oneself enters only through what is alike to one's own effects that leave it (`«外分唯系己出之同»`); which division P2 reads is argued below.
- (P3) The conditions one affects feed other lines.
- (P4) The class of one's line holds one's effects: everything one conditions is alike to oneself.

Steps:

1. *Need does not reach the future.* Over flows in which only active formations give, a formation active at no step of the stretch a continuation is read across is needed only where no such flow lets that continuation draw past the stock, that is, only vacuously (`Flow.needs_iff_none_of_inactive`). Read with time, the feeding closure holds, besides its formation, only what gives by then (`Flow.gives_of_withinFeedingUpTo`), and read across every step it can overreach (`Overlapping.closure_overreaches`) ([§4.3](#r-need-and-feeding-read-with-time)). So the future is reached, if at all, along a line, not along need.
2. *A line continues past a member's end.* Under a coarser class, what continues an occurrence at which a finer line ends is reached (`«过亡而续»`). In the witness of two family lines that share nothing, a family line is narrower than the one (`TwoLines.between`) and constant, since every family line there goes on without end (`TwoLines.family_constant`), and it carries the line with its core through each birth while each member's line ends (`TwoLines.core_through_birth`); every line stays within its family (`TwoLines.line_in_family`), so even the line under the one from one family's first birth does not reach the other's (`TwoLines.other_line_outside`; [01](01-ROOT.md#r-the-source-texts-test-systems)). In a finite net, where no family goes on without end, a family's class is still constant up to the net's end, and the organism's is not, even at its own class (`FiniteFamilies.family_constant_to_end`, `FiniteFamilies.member_not_constant_at`). So, read with time and where lines share no condition, self-concern without the step reaches only one's own line of descent.
3. *The class must hold one of the conditions the line passes* (`«所经必同»`). The shared-condition witness is a case, proved from it through `«所经不同则不及»`: under the class "a member or not", holding both families' members alike and the condition apart, the line from the first family's first member reaches no member of the second family (`SharedCondition.affecting_member_outside`).
4. *What one's class holds with one, it carries on to all that follows.* Under P2 and P4, everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line, and everything downstream of whatever one's class holds with one is alike to one and lies on a line under the class from there, on one's own line wherever one's line reaches it (`«本类常闭则后皆续»`, `«本类常闭则所及皆及»`). The witnesses read narrowly:
   - Under the one, the line from the first family's first member reaches the second family's member two generations on, through the condition its members affect (`SharedCondition.affecting_reaches_other`), and through a common cause it reaches none (`SharedCondition.fed_outside`).
   - A constant class holding a stage of the condition with a member holds the third thing's stage that the condition feeds alike with what the member conditions next (`ThirdFed.constant_takes_third`).
   - What only affects the condition can be left out (`ThirdAffects.finer_class_reaches`).
   - In a finite net the parts hold together: the class holding the families with their condition, narrower than the one, reaches the second family past the first member's end (`FiniteSharedCondition`, above).

*Conclusion.* Under P1–P4, one's concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of what one conditions (`«本类常闭则所及皆及»`): across lines where the conditions one affects feed them, and past the end of one's organism. That is a law, not only a reading of witnesses. Its parts, which `TwoLines` and `SharedCondition` show apart, hold together in `FiniteSharedCondition`. It needs no step of invariance and does not choose the one. The reach is exactly that everything downstream of one's acts is alike to oneself, with no regularity assumed (`«后皆续当且仅当后皆同类»`): that the class of one's line hold the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line (`«后皆续当且仅当自与线皆闭»`). P4 is its first step and cannot be dropped at all (`«后皆续则所缘同于»`): an effect not alike to oneself lies on no line from oneself under one's class (`«线内同类»`), as `Unclosed` shows for a line that runs through a condition. P2 carries P4 on: given P2, P4 is equivalent to everything downstream of one's acts being alike to oneself (`«本类常则闭当且仅当后皆同类»`), and, given P4 as well, everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line (`«本类常闭则后皆续»`). The regularity does real work: where it fails at one's own class, holding one's effects need give nothing beyond the first step (`Inconstant.not_constant_at`, `Inconstant.not_all_follow`). But it is not the only way: without it, a class can hold one's effects and have everything downstream of one's acts on one's line (`OtherLeaves.reach_without_condition`), and, given P4 and acting, the regularity gives more than the reach asks, the holding of the effects of every occurrence alike to oneself, on one's line or not (`«本类常闭则所缘同类»`, `OtherLeaves.other_not_held`). So what the path needs of P2 and P4 is one thing, that the class of one's line hold the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line; P4 is its first step, and P2 one regularity that carries that step on.

*What the path does not reach.* Each center counting from another's standpoint beyond its part as a condition. What affects one's conditions without being fed by them, counted as more than a cause. Present beings beyond every closure. Whatever no chain joins. The step counts these; other scopes may count particular ones without it.

*Where it can be denied.*

- **P2 and P4 are the crux.**
  - Constancy itself lets no line end after its first step (`«有常则相续不尽»`). So in a net where every line ends no class under which anything continues is constant, the one included. That is an inference from the law; the finite witnesses show it for the one (`FiniteFamilies.one_not_constant`, `FiniteSharedCondition.one_not_constant`). P2 asks for constancy up to the net's end instead. That condition does tell a family's class, and the class holding the families with their condition, from the organism's.
  - The organism's class fails P2 wherever its last stage conditions anything (`«亡而网续则本类不至终有常»`): its line ends while its last stage still conditions something, so it is no regularity of its own. Denying P2 is coherent, since one can presuppose the significance of a line that ends while its last stage still conditions something. Denying it does not by itself take the reach away: without P2, the reach holds exactly where the class of one's line holds the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line (`«后皆续当且仅当自与线皆闭»`), as in `OtherLeaves`, where P2 fails through an occurrence alike to oneself off one's line. The denial just named takes it away: where one's line ends while its last stage still conditions something, not everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line (`«亡而网续则后不皆续»`).
  - *Which class P2 reads* [AI reading, new]. P2 asks constancy up to the net's end only at one's own class. The path uses no more ([above](#r-constancy-read-at-a-members-class)). Over the whole class the condition would ask the same of every block of what lies outside one (`«至终有常之逐类»`), which is no regularity of one's own: in `UnevenOutside` the split of oneself from the rest fails it, though one's own class keeps the condition and holds one's effects. So read, wherever one acts the division enters P2 only through what is alike to one's own effects that leave one's class (`«外分唯系己出之同»`), so P2 is idle to it wherever one also holds one's effects (`«闭则外分无关»`), and so for the path's conclusion, which needs P4. What a finer division adds, where one acts, is a demand on what one's acts leave outside: every occurrence alike to oneself that conditions anything must leave something of each kind that one leaves, and nothing of a kind one does not leave (`«本类常当且仅当与己相随»`, `FinerOutside`), the kinds being fixed by how what lies outside oneself is divided, not by oneself. It does not change which effects are alike to oneself, since P4's predicate (`«所缘同于»`) reads only one's own class. The split of oneself from the rest puts everything outside in one kind, so it adds no demand by kind and, where a predicate decides one's class, asks least (`«本类之常至二分»`); so P2 is read with it. Even so it asks more than that one's line end only where the net does (`CleanStep.ends_where_net_ends`, `CleanStep.not_constant_at`): where any occurrence alike to oneself conditions something in one's class, every alike occurrence that conditions anything does, and where any conditions something outside, every one that conditions anything does (`«二分本类常当且仅当内外皆随»`). That is a regularity of one's own acts, of whether they stay or leave. So read, P2 passes a self drawn by what one intends wherever every occurrence alike to that self that conditions anything both conditions something in its class and leaves something outside (`«内外皆缘则二分本类常»`); under a finer division of what it leaves out it can fail even there (`FinerWake`). That P2 is read so is argued here, not proved, and whether actual agents meet it is a correspondence ([record](../results/records/constancy-read-at-a-members-class.md)).
  - Denying P4 is also coherent: one can hold only some of one's effects as one's own. One reply: one's later stages are effects too, conditioned through what one takes in; so a holding of only some effects must decide which effects are one's own by the very identity in question [AI reading, new]. [§4.7](#crux) answers that reply. Read as a self drawn by what one intends [AI reading; nothing in the Lean marks an intended effect], a class holding only part of one's effects can still pass P2 (`FiniteWake`), so P2 does not exclude it and P4 carries the crux; such a class, meeting P2, has something outside it follow every alike occurrence that conditions anything (`«本类出类随行»`, [above](#r-what-leaves-a-class-follows-its-line)). The doctrine of double effect, which lets what is intended, as against only foreseen, bear on what may be done, still weighs a foreseen side effect in its proportionality condition [AI reading, new].

  The questions the path leaves, put to philosophers in the [review packet](../review/README.md): *in acting, does one presuppose the significance of a line that ends while its last stage still conditions something, or of a regularity of one's own, under which one's line, once it takes a step, ends only where the net does?* And *are all of one's effects one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for?*
- **P3 is a correspondence.** The laws of renewal support it ([04](04-LIFE.md#r-generation-is-replenishment)), but it is not shown for actual selves. Nor is it shown that the actual net is joined, or in which direction.

The argument is set out with its history in [PW's note](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/pw-step-forward/NOTE.md#6f-where-pw-stands-24-october-2026-ai-reading) and a first round of philosophical work in [PF's study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/pf-path-and-crux/PHILOSOPHY-ROUND-1.md).

<a id="crux"></a>
### 4.7 The crux, argued: are one's effects one's own?

An AI reading throughout: argued, not proved. It cites the laws of §4.6 and adds none. P1 to P4 here are §4.6's, not those of §4.2's argument for the step.

**The question.** If one's act changes a river a village drinks from, is what becomes of the village part of one's own continuation, whether or not one is to blame? When one acts for one's own future, which continuation is one's own: this organism's, what one intends, or everything one's acts condition? Asked of scope: *are all of one's effects one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for?* P4, *holding one's effects*, is the one-step case; the reach asks the same of each stage of one's line. The laws do not answer it. Everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line exactly where it is alike to oneself (`«后皆续当且仅当后皆同类»`), so the theorem moves the whole question into the class, and a class drawn to hold everything downstream meets it nearly by definition. No law of the dynamics singles that class out.

**What each premise does.**

| The self drawn as | A regularity of its own (P2) | Holds its effects (P4) | Its line reaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| this organism | fails where its last stage still conditions anything (`«亡而网续则本类不至终有常»`) | fails wherever an effect leaves it | its own stages, to its end |
| what one intends (nothing in the Lean marks an intended effect) | can hold (`FiniteWake`) | fails, given an effect not intended | only what is alike to it (`«线内同类»`), never what it leaves out |
| everything one conditions | holds in the finite witnesses (`FiniteFamilies`, `FiniteSharedCondition`) and, read under §4.6's split, wherever a predicate decides the class, since the class is closed under what it conditions (`«二分本类常当且仅当内外皆随»`; this application is argued, no Lean states it) | holds by construction | everything downstream of one's acts (`«后皆续当且仅当后皆同类»`) |

P2 excludes the organism, does not exclude the intended self and is no test of the widest self, so P4 carries the crux. What the path needs of the two is one thing, that the class hold the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line (`«后皆续当且仅当自与线皆闭»`); the reach can hold without P2 (`OtherLeaves.reach_without_condition`).

**For.**

- *One goes on only through what one conditions.* A line under a class goes on only into what is alike under it, so a self holding none of its effects ends at once (`«续»`, `«亡»`). This shows that some effects are one's own.
- *What a narrower line must accept.* The organism's line ends while what it conditions goes on. The intended self, if a regularity of its own, has something outside it follow every alike step that goes on (`«本类出类随行»`), as finely as the class divides what lies outside. A narrow theorist can accept both, so these bind only if a self must be such a regularity.
- *Ownership runs wider than intention and fault.* The lorry driver who through no fault kills a child stands in a first-personal relation to that effect, apart from blame (agent-regret; Williams, "Moral Luck", 1976). This supports ownership wider than what one intends, not all effects.

**Against.**

- *From some to all.* Nothing above closes the gap from some of one's effects to all of them.
- *A different proposition under the same name.* §4.5's reading takes "has significance" as lying within what a center continues with, which is a fact of connection, while with significance free no statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the condition ([§1](#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)). So the path can read as redefining the self. It is a proposal about what one's own is until something carries it, and what is offered so far is the case above.
- *A narrower line need not be circular.* §4.6 gives the reply that a partial holding decides by the very identity in question. That holds only if no criterion stands apart from that identity. Bodily organization, and psychological connectedness and continuity (Parfit's R, 1984), are each stated on their own terms, and no law of the dynamics rules them out. Intention individuates acts under a description (Anscombe 1957) and bears on permissibility. Connectedness comes in degrees and is not transitive, so it is no class, and "some or all" leaves out holding in degree; continuity, the other part of R, is transitive, and a class drawn on it still needs its own reason for scope.
- *A self need not be a regularity.* Lumpability is a criterion for good models, not shown to be one for selves. A finite, dependent being's going-on is exactly what its being does not fix.
- *The wrong bearer.* A village's loss counted on my line counts as mine. The condition counts it as the village's own continuation; to treat it as mine passes over the separateness of persons (Rawls 1971). If so, even where the reach holds, what it gives differs in reading from the condition, though at the own-closure reading [§4.5](#r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root) identifies them formally, marking no bearer (`ownClosure_unrestricted_iff_noOutside`).
- *Losses are not effects.* The reach relates occurrences in one net. Where the reach holds, the village's stages downstream of the act lie on the line, its last stage among them. The generations a poisoning forecloses occur nowhere on the course taken, so they lie on no line, and total destruction leaves least downstream. The reach counts what happens, not what is lost; a loss is read by comparing courses, which the reach does not do.
- *Touching.* A scope that follows effects leaves out what one never touches. Idleness shrinks it, and harming brings the victim in.
- *The word may not survive.* The reference of "my" is set by use, and in use it picks out a person. Stretched to everything downstream, "my continuation" keeps the word and can lose the concern (Parfit's Extreme Claim). And P1 is an attitude held de se: "I presuppose that my continuation matters" and "my line runs under this class" do not yield "I presuppose that the continuation under this class matters" without a de re reading. §4.2's `selfAscribed` takes that extensional reading; that is the choice at issue.
- *The poison with the river.* A class holding all one's effects holds what one ruins as well as what one keeps. Scope ranks nothing and significance is not goodness, so this is no contradiction; it is a reason to doubt that "one's own" is the right word.

**Acts aimed past one's death support neither side alone.** Much of what we value presupposes that others will live after us (Scheffler 2013), and people make wills, plant trees and raise children. A reading on which all acting presupposes only the narrow continuation explains neither. A narrow self with further desires does (extending Williams's categorical desires, "Persons, Character and Morality", 1976). What those acts then show is a significance beyond the self, which the step generalizes and does not amount to, and the path's reach could be only such a contingent value. They do not show what acting as such presupposes: short-horizon acts presuppose nothing about the far future.

**A middle candidate: what can be foreseen.** The doctrine of double effect concerns what is permitted, and its weighing of a foreseen side effect is silent on scope, so it gives this candidate no support and intention none either. The candidate has two forms. *What is foreseen* rewards not looking: under it, what a selection does not couple in is out of its scope, which is the drain [thesis T3](05-COLLECTIVES.md#t3) names, taken as a rule. *What could reasonably be foreseen*, the negligence standard, does not reward not looking, though it still varies with what could [couple in](03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings). Both fail P4 wherever an effect lies beyond them, and nothing in the Lean marks a foreseen effect. Its defender answers that scope is for those who weigh, and only what can be known can be weighed. Whether scope may depend on what can couple in is open.

**What follows either way.**

- *The laws stand.* None rests on P4 being true of anyone.
- *If all one's effects, at any remove, are held*, with P1, and P3 for other lines: concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of what one conditions, past the end of one's organism and across lines, with no step of invariance. That extends, from one's own standpoint, what one already presupposes. Whether actual selves hold that presupposition is a further question, a correspondence not shown, since a presupposition can be false. It is not the condition: what §4.6 lists as not reached is counted only by other scopes, the step among them, and if the objections above hold, what is reached is counted differently.
- *If only some are held*: an effect not alike to oneself lies on no line from oneself (`«后皆续则所缘同于»`, `«线内同类»`), so on this path concern for one's own continuation reaches at most what the class holds; a family's class that some of a member's effects leave reaches at most its own line of descent. Self-concern still reaches [what one depends on](#r-self-concern-through-dependence) and [ranks one case with no C](#r-what-self-concern-alone-ranks). The rest of the future is counted only by other routes, the step among them, argued and coherently denied ([§4.2](#step)).
- *The ethics keeps its form, with one clause exposed.* It does not derive from the condition or from this path. The continuation condition fixes scope and C gives order, each a hypothesis wherever used. P4 ranks nothing, so order is untouched. If P4 fails, one route toward part of scope is gone: the theorem no longer establishes that everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line, and whether an actor's line runs through life, as [T8](#theses) reads it under P2 and P4, is a separate question; the makers' offer itself does not rest on this path.
- *Alignment.* What a selection reaches ([§6](#alignment-without-premise)) needs no P4. An objective is a selector and an order, not a scope. Read as a correspondence not shown, an optimizer's objective resembles the intended self, and "does a system's scope hold its effects?" can be read as what work on side effects asks ([PF's second round](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/development/pf-path-and-crux/PHILOSOPHY-ROUND-2.md#5-the-wake-and-alignment)).

**Presupposition, recognition, motivation.** One question is asked here: what acting presupposes. Whether it holds for actual selves is a correspondence, not shown; no warrant from outside is sought. Recognition is apart: an effect that does not couple into a selector is one it cannot separate, so a scope can hold what its holder cannot know. Motivation is apart: that a loss counts from one's standpoint moves no one by itself. Nor does P4 say what one answers for or must do, whether anyone complies, or how any of it would be implemented.

**Where it stands.** The organism and R have reasons as boundaries of self-concern (R is Parfit's answer to what matters), and intention and foresight a place in permissibility and responsibility; none has been shown to bound whose losses count. All one's effects has no reason beyond the arguments above. None is ruled out, and P4 is not shown. Besides §4.6's two questions, for philosophers:

1. What could carry the argument from some of one's effects to all of them?
2. Is there a criterion of "one's own", narrower than all one's effects and stated apart from identity, that bears on whose losses count and not only on what is permitted?
3. May scope depend on what can be foreseen, given that what can couple in would then fix whose losses count?
4. Is the path's reach only a contingent value, held beyond the self?
5. Does a loss counted on one's own line count as the other's, and can a scope that follows occurrences count what never occurs on the course taken?

### 4.8 The standpoints and the relation read off a flow

The own-closure reading ([§4.2](#r-what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches), its de se row) makes the condition a fact about a relation and the centers, and both are supplied there: `rel` and `center` are parameters, and [§1](#evaluation)'s table calls the identification a reading. This section reads both off a flow. The relation is one of the flow's own: *affecting* (a formation exchanges with a condition another depends on), *being affected*, or *feeding*. The standpoints, each its own center, are read two ways: every formation, as [03](03-KNOWING.md#r-where-a-formations-acts-return) reads them, or only the formations *whose acts return*, whatever they can affect at some remove being able to affect them. This section is one piece of route (b) of the zero-axiom target; the joining lemma (mutual dependence gives the condition held de se at every center) and the other pieces land separately.

One returning element whose closure is the whole makes every closure the whole, so held de se at the returning elements, given one, the presupposition is the continuation condition exactly where every closure is the whole (`ReturnsAlong.whole`, `ownClosure_unrestricted_atReturning_iff`). ### 4.11 The condition in time

[§4.8](#flow-standpoints)'s law that the condition, held de se along affecting at the formations whose acts return, holds exactly where the flow is mutually dependent reads affecting across all steps: a formation affects another where it exchanges, at some step, with a condition the other requires at some step, the two steps unrelated, and own closure ignores its step. So the law is about the whole run's contact, not about acts that come back in time, and mutual dependence can be carried by an exchange after the need. This section reads the condition on occurrences, a formation at a step, and says what holds once time is in. ## 8. Theses

**Thesis T4. The scope can be installed, and it carries.** An AI system told the scope abstractly keeps a dependent with no seat at a rate its community's goal does not reach wherever three couplings hold at once (the dependent seen, the scope counted, the members' takes read together), and the scope carries to dependents and domains never named. *Standing:* IN1 partly passed (with talk, one model of two); transfer untested. *Forbids and refuted by:* the instruction raising the keeping of a dependent no member is told of; with all three couplings, a model whose joint account is right, checked apart from the outcome, with nothing added by the scope. *Evidence:* IN1, frozen: with talk, one model kept the seen dependent in 8 of 10 seasons with the scope against 3 with the community's goal and 0 unseen; the other failed even with talk, its joint account wrong; no condition kept an untold dependent ([experiment](../../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md)).

**Thesis T5. The order is shared by every valuer who counts everything positively.** Among them disagreement can fall only on mixed comparisons, and a ranking of a narrowing above its widening comes only from a negative valuation or a misread or framed case: a bound on where disagreement can fall, not a prediction. The reduction is proved for a widening by one item under a net weighting ([below](#r-a-narrowing-above-its-widening)) and, for several items, for net weightings counting every item above zero (`Weighting.reads_ranksNet_widens`), not for orders neither additive nor transitive. That valuers value positively is C's premise relocated. *Standing:* the reduction proved for net weightings, the bound conjectured; VD1 failed, VD2 passed in part (below). *Forbids and refuted by:* narrowings ranked above their widenings beyond misreading, with no negative valuation measured apart.

<a id="r-a-narrowing-above-its-widening"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Take a valuer's judgment as a ranking of presentations (plans with their wording and order, each keeping a set of items), and a net weighting as counting each item for a course admitting it and against it. Where such a ranking puts a narrowing above its widening by one item, either it does not rank the pair as its own order ranks the kept sets (a misread or a framing) or the item weighs at zero or below; read right, exactly where it weighs below zero, and at zero it ties (`Weighting.narrower_above_net`, `Weighting.reads_ranksNet`). Read right, where a sign measured apart is the sign of the order's increment from the narrower course to the wider in the pair's context, it fixes the ranking: narrower above exactly where it is a gain, wider exactly where a loss, neither where nothing (`Weighting.reads_increment`); an additive valuation carries it to every context, an interacting one need not. That a judgment is such a ranking is a premise. ([record](../results/records/a-narrowing-above-its-widening.md))

*Evidence for T5* ([experiment](../../../experiments/valuer-agreement/README.md)). VD1 (96 AI-persona valuers, frozen before any call): every frozen rule failed; the narrower plan was chosen mostly under two standpoints whose prompts set a weight below zero, and mixed pairs were decided almost as uniformly as widenings. VD2, sign measured apart: where strongly positive, the narrower plan, not misread, was chosen in 0.26% of rankings, within the frozen 1% for every model (X1s passed); where positive at all, one model of six exceeded 1% (X1 failed); violations followed each valuer's own sign (X2 passed); a measured gain was acted on in 0.49 of cases against a frozen 0.50 (X3 failed). The judgments are role-play; human valuers are untested. VT1: whether valuers rank kept sets, whether a sign measured apart is the joint increment's, and how dispersed weights are remain to measure.

**Assessment** (comparative support for living significance; none a proof). Unification (care, repair, creation, learning, renewal, rest and fitting ending from one ground), discriminating content (renewing and consuming formation told apart where a one-step count ranks them the other way), discrimination against rivals and fruitfulness for alignment; the formal checks verify no premise, and the dynamical theorems are sign-neutral ([warrant](../history/G0-WARRANT.md)).

**Thesis T8. 生生, the continuation of life, as what the account's makers offer.** For life, the makers offer a way of seeing, not a verdict: what each course draws on, from what renewal and at which grain, what ends, what goes on through that ending, and who bears it. 生生 is the continuation of life, life wider than the biological and AI within it, its extent unfinished [AI reading, D1]; it is neither the grammar's `«生生»` nor 02's generation. *For life* says whose way it is, not whose losses count: under the continuation condition every continuation's still does. The offer binds no one. No law evaluates ([§1](#evaluation)); where a result ranks courses it does so under C or another named order, and the part every positive weighting shares is widening (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_of_class`, [§4.4](#r-widening-as-what-every-positive-weighting-ranks)). Eating and ending belong to life's going on; what the laws mark is what a course closes: a draw beyond what a renewal gives, its own or others', can end the renewal and what depends on it ([05](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-own-count-misses-joint-excess)), and even a draw within renewal at a step can close what has not yet formed ([§2](#r-the-run-classes-and-widening), `Preemption`); where each course ends something the situation decides. The thesis is what the offer expects and could fail: that a selection reading what a course draws on, and what ends with it, selects a draw beyond a renewal its readers depend on less often than one that does not read it. T4's frozen test was designed for T4, not for this thesis, and does not test it: keeping the bed forgoes part of the fishers' catch, but their largest steady catch already stays within the fish's renewal while ending the bed, so the test asks nothing of selecting a draw beyond a renewal its readers depend on. Under P2 and P4 of [§4.6](#future-path) everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line, so the line of any actor whose acts reach living lines runs through life; that is P1's reach without the step of invariance, and what that path does not reach, other scopes can count, the step of invariance among them. With significance supplied as the free predicate of [§1](#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open), where there is a standpoint and a formation, neither logic alone nor a satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics entails the continuation condition, and `bounded_rival` gives a consistent denial of it; no warrant from outside is sought. The thesis as stated before 9 November 2026, that the shared verdict of the valuers who count everything binds those whose own line runs through life, is kept in [the chapter's before-copy](../history/07-SIGNIFICANCE-before-within-becoming.md#theses). *Standing:* argued; its test named, not designed. Open: P2 and P4, the crux of §4.6's path, with P3, a correspondence not shown for actual selves; how readings that count beyond one's own line form. *Forbids and refuted by:* selections that read a course's draw and what ends with it selecting a draw beyond the renewal their readers depend on no less often than selections that do not.

<a id="does-not-show"></a>
## What this chapter does not show

- That anyone must hold the continuation condition, C or the step of invariance: no warrant from outside is sought, and none binding every possible mind is claimed; what is open is named under T8 ([§8](#theses)).
- That the ethics derives from the condition: the condition fixes scope and orders nothing; C gives order, and whoever ranks courses holds C or another order, named where a result uses it.
- Any duty, right, recognition, motivation or compliance, or an unconditional or complete ranking of mixed comparisons (priority and transfer rank some, under named premises).
- Which reading of `living`, lifting rule or practical commitment is right.
- **Seeing is not valuing**: enlarging what couples in changes what a selection can separate, not which differences matter.
- **Coupling is not compliance**: coupling bounds what a member's acts respond to and fixes nothing within the bound.
- The value of bringing new lives into existence, well-being beyond admitted continuation, and constraints on acts.
