# Significance, ethics and alignment

**Thesis.** Dynamics do not entail significance: where there is a standpoint and a formation, no statement reading only the dynamics and holding of some dynamics entails the continuation condition. With no premise, widening what can form is an order, and alignment is first what a selection reaches. The ethics rests on two premises with two roles, neither asserted: the continuation condition fixes scope (whose losses count), its content beyond the self's presupposition being the step of invariance; C fixes order (that widening is better), the order every positive weighting shares. Claimed: these laws; living significance as a theorem under the condition; reasons, choices and a direction for alignment under further named premises. Open: the warrant of the condition, of C and of the step; whether the self's presupposition is held de se and, on the root, which class a center's own line runs under and which occurrences are centers ([§4.5](#condition-on-the-root)); and the condition on the root beyond one instance.

**Where this chapter sits.** It takes the [margin](02-MAP.md#margin) of a part from the map, the [reach of a selection](03-KNOWING.md#r-selection-through-couplings) from knowing, and [pools](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-what-pooled-coupling-reaches) and [mutual dependence](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace) from collectives; the verdicts the [map](02-MAP.md), [life](04-LIFE.md#along-a-lineage) and [collectives](05-COLLECTIVES.md) leave open, on courses, lineages, drains and locks, are asked here. It hands its theses T4, T5 and T8 to the method's [table](08-METHOD.md#theses).

**Bases.** The laws are on the World model (webs and flows: natural-number amounts, discrete steps, Prop-valued activity), for any process or for any type, each label naming its base ([01](01-ROOT.md#models)) and, where a law uses it, balance (every change of a level is what is taken and given); periphery results and instances name their axioms. Evaluation enters through the ethics' two premises, each a hypothesis where used and neither asserted: the continuation condition for scope, C for order. Further premises, hypotheses where used: **A**, access with positive content secured from one's own standpoint gives a contributory reason; **D**, every position with access to a response securing new living content has a contributory reason for it, whatever its endorsement; **E**, the response realizes, in every admitted continuation, living content absent before; **L**, a lifting from outcomes to responses with several outcomes; **J1–J4**, complete comparison with retained losses, priority of basic conditions, bounded responsibility, legitimate feasible choice.

## 3. The continuation condition

The dynamics leave open whether anything is left out of significance ([§1](#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)); the continuation condition, not asserted, is the premise that nothing is, a hypothesis in 3.2, 3.4 and the conditional parts of 3.3.

**Definition.** The *continuation condition* `Unrestricted sig`: from every standpoint, the continuation of anything has significance at every step; in words, for anything, its continuation into the future has significance ([source of the wording](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-10-wanting-continuation-one-axiom.md)). It is boundary neutrality applied once: `Unrestricted sig` is `Neutral sig`, and `Neutral rule` is `∀ i, rule i`.

*Instantiation, said once.* Under the condition `sig` holds of every standpoint, step and formation, so a theorem taking it as a hypothesis instantiates a universal statement; counting such theorems measures no further dependence.

**Reading** [AI reading, D16]. The formation index reads "anything", a continuation at a step "its continuation into the future", and the standpoint index "has significance" with no center privileged; significance is neither graded nor necessarily positive. Retaining reads "as long as not harm", the source's phrase, and adding "creating more significance".

With no condition, "should" is what a center does once its represented boundary matches what it depends on, and since a loss returns to a center through its feeders, this gives, without warranting, a reason of its own to retain what lies within its closure (route (b) [AI reading, D17]).

Route (b) uses four laws: what a continuation depends on is its [feeding closure](02-MAP.md#r-feeding-closure); feedback carries at most the [influence closure](03-KNOWING.md#r-feedback-and-reach); the feeding, influence and reach closures and the system are [levels of one closure](01-ROOT.md#r-levels-of-closure); and where every center's closure is the whole, as under [mutual dependence](05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace), significance bounded by each center's closure is the condition.

<a id="decomposition"></a>
### 4.1 The condition decomposed

Along a relation of the unfolding, a self's *boundary* is a region holding its center and closed under the relation, the closure the least, the whole the greatest. The *self's presupposition*, drawn at a region, is that from `p` the continuation of everything within it has significance (`SignificantWithin`). It is *sub-true* if it holds as the boundary is drawn somewhere, *super-true* if however drawn, and *boundary-invariant* if, holding as drawn at one admissible region, it holds at every other.

<a id="r-continuation-condition-decomposition"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World instances). Wherever the whole is admissible, invariance at `p` is exactly the conditional from the presupposition to the condition at `p` (`boundaryInvariant_iff_conditional`), and given the presupposition sub-true it is the condition at `p` (`boundaryInvariant_iff_all`). Along any relation, the condition is exactly the presupposition sub-true and invariant at every standpoint (`unrestricted_iff_presupposed_along`): logically A ⇔ P ∧ (P → A), given A → P, which locates the contested step and derives nothing beyond it. With *covering* (every formation within some center's closure) and the presupposition sub-true at every standpoint, standpoint-invariance gives the condition (`unrestricted_of_standpointInvariant`) and is equivalent to invariance across drawings at every standpoint (`boundaryInvariant_iff_standpointInvariant`); without the presupposition everywhere they come apart (witness `OneCounts.forms_apart`). In `Clearing` each premise is needed. ([record](../results/records/continuation-condition-decomposition.md), [Boundaries.lean](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Boundaries.lean))

Admitting every closed region holding the center as a drawing is a substantive choice.

<a id="r-presupposition-scopes"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World instances). Along a relation, the presupposition sub-true is significance over the closure, and super-true it is the condition: route (b) and the condition are one presupposition with "however the boundary is drawn" at narrow and at wide scope (`presupposition_scopes`), route (b)'s significance being the least making every presupposition sub-true (`bounded_closure_least`). ### 4.2 The step of invariance

The step has two forms: *across drawings*, at one standpoint, given the presupposition, invariance is the condition from that standpoint alone; *across standpoints*, what has significance from one standpoint has it from every other. They coincide only under covering and the presupposition everywhere.

<a id="r-what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches"></a>
**Theorem** (any type; World witnesses). What each premise forces, with nothing asserted:

| Premises | What they force to have significance | Lean |
|---|---|---|
| each standpoint's presupposition about its own continuation, with standpoint-invariance | exactly the centers' continuation; the condition only where every formation is a center | `forced_iff_center`, `unrestricted_atCenters_iff` |
| one standpoint's presupposition along a relation, alone | exactly what lies within its closure | `forced_iff_within` |

| held de se (each standpoint ascribes the property to its own center) | each center's closure, from it: invariant exactly where the closures agree, the condition exactly where each is the whole | `ownClosure_standpointInvariant_iff`, `ownClosure_unrestricted_iff` |
| held de dicto (one proposition from every standpoint), with the step | the union of the centers' closures | `forced_iff_within_some` |

The de se and de dicto readings agree exactly where whatever lies within one center's closure lies within every center's (`selfAscribed_iff_withinSome`). Witness: with the clearer the only center, its part retains at the centers and not under the condition (`Clearing.centers_retain_yet_loses`), the scopes parting at the dweller, no center. ([record](../results/records/what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches.md), [Weightings.lean](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/Weightings.lean))

*Prior forms.* Across standpoints the step is Gewirth's (1978), with agents as centers (`forced_iff_center`); across drawings, the reductionist step of Parfit (1984) and Siderits (2003).

*The strongest argument for the step* runs across drawings. (P1) Acting for a continuation presupposes that some continuation has significance. (P2) What is "one's own" is a region drawn along a relation of the unfolding, and no law of the dynamics fixes which. (P3) A presupposition whose truth depends on a choice nothing privileges is relative to it or invariant across it. (P4) What acting presupposes is that continuation has significance, not continuation under a description of its own drawing. So the presupposition is invariant, and held at every standpoint is the condition. This last step's validity is a theorem (`unrestricted_iff_presupposed_along`); the step from P2–P4 to invariance is argued, not proved, and soundness is not shown: P2 holds in the representation only because the relation is supplied, and a relation the facts privilege (the organism's life) denies it; P4, Parfit's thesis that identity is not what matters, is coherently denied.

*The strongest case against* reads the presupposition as de se, held essentially indexically, so "however the self is drawn" takes narrow scope and reaches only the closure. Its grounds are set out in [the step of invariance §4](../history/THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE.md#4-the-strongest-case-against-the-step). Against it: a motive held de se can presuppose de dicto that the outcome matters, and privileging a narrower relation needs an argument the dynamics do not supply, Parfit's R coming in degrees and the closures nesting (`Flow.withinInfluence_of_withinFeeding`). A privileged relation is costly, not refuted; a perspectivist holding significance relative all the way down denies the condition consistently (`bounded_rival`). The open question: **is the self's presupposition held de se of a self drawn along a privileged relation narrower than being related at all?** If so, the table's de se row applies; if not, with the step, its de dicto row. ### 4.5 The condition on the root

The root reads every subject as [continuation relative to a class](01-ROOT.md#r-continuation-relative-to-a-class): an occurrence continues another where it is conditioned by it and alike to it under a class, and a line's regularity is its class's [core](01-ROOT.md#constancy-and-core).

<a id="r-the-continuation-condition-on-the-root"></a>
**Theorem** (root; any type) **and Conditional** (the root's condition at the one, the own-closure reading). Given a net `R`, a class `E` and each standpoint's center, the condition *along the net* is that every occurrence is reached on every center's line under `E` (`«续无外»`), and *in families* that every occurrence lies in every center's family under `E` (`«族无外»`). The first gives the second (`«续无外则族无外»`), and either at a class gives it at every coarser class (`«续无外之粗化»`, `«族无外之粗化»`). Lying within a closure along a relation is being reached on a line under the one of that relation (`withinClosure_iff_reached`). So the condition held de se along a relation, `Unrestricted (selfAscribed (ownClosure rel) center)` ([§4.2](#r-what-the-step-across-standpoints-reaches)), is exactly the root's condition at the one of that relation taken as the net (`ownClosure_unrestricted_iff_noOutside`). Each theorem of this chapter taking the condition as a hypothesis instantiates at that significance; one instance is proved: on every reading where a claim's significance from a standpoint is that its formation lies within that standpoint's center's closure, living significance follows, with S0 at every standpoint, both neutralities and, wherever something lives and there is a standpoint, a seed (`premises_of_noOutside`) ([`Becoming.GrammarScope`](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/Becoming/GrammarScope.lean); [record](../results/records/the-continuation-condition-on-the-root.md)). Read so, the condition has no significance parameter: it is true or false once the net, the class and the centers are given. Along a net with no cycle it holds only with one center, which nothing conditions (`«续无外则同一中心»`, `«续无外则中心无缘»`); in families at the one it asks only that the net be joined. The relations a self is drawn along in §4.1–4.3 (feeding, influence) run from what is fed or affected to what feeds or affects it, the converse of 缘, so read along them "ahead" runs to conditions, not into the future. The free significance of §1 is not lifted onto the root.

<a id="r-reach-past-ones-own-end"></a>
**Theorem** (root; witnesses). On a line under a class `L` from a center, what continues under `L` an occurrence at which the line under a relation `O` ends is reached on the line under `L`, and lies on no line under `O` from that occurrence (`«过亡而续»`); the case read is a class `O` finer than `L`, such as an organism's. The core of a class, fixed by the net once the class is given, continues something exactly where some line under it goes on without end (`«核有续当且仅当有不穷»`, by GS1's [`«守理则不穷»`](01-ROOT.md#r-a-regularity-kept-and-changed)), so where no line under the core goes on without end, the core continues nothing (`«无不穷则核无续»`). Two lines of descent, each with the one as its class of descent, show both. In endless descent, each member young, then old, then the parent of the next, every occurrence conditions a successor, so the one is constant, its core is the whole and continues through each birth while the member's line ends at old age (`LineOfDescent.Endless.core_through_birth`); with the first birth as the only center the condition holds under the one, and under the member's class it fails (`LineOfDescent.Endless.descent_no_outside`, `LineOfDescent.Endless.member_outside`). In descent that ends, two members with the second childless, no line under any relation goes on without end, so no class's core continues anything, while the one reaches the second member past the first's end (`LineOfDescent.Last.core_no_continuation`, `LineOfDescent.Last.descent_past_end`). Neither model has a class narrower than the one and wider than the member's ([record](../results/records/reach-past-ones-own-end.md)).

**Reading** [AI reading, new]. Held de se of a line under a coarser class than one's organism, the presupposition of one's own continuation reaches successors past one's own end with no step of invariance; so the case against the step, held de se, becomes in part a question of classes: under an organism's class nothing of the self follows its end, under a coarser one the line can go on through new members. **Thesis T4. The scope can be installed, and it carries.** An AI system told the scope abstractly keeps a dependent with no seat at a rate its community's goal does not reach wherever three couplings hold at once (the dependent seen, the scope counted, the members' takes read together), and the scope carries to dependents and domains never named. *Standing:* IN1 partly passed (with talk, one model of two); transfer untested. *Forbids and refuted by:* the instruction raising the keeping of a dependent no member is told of; with all three couplings, a model whose joint account is right, checked apart from the outcome, with nothing added by the scope. *Evidence:* IN1, frozen: with talk, one model kept the seen dependent in 8 of 10 seasons with the scope against 3 with the community's goal and 0 unseen; the other failed even with talk, its joint account wrong; no condition kept an untold dependent ([experiment](../../../experiments/direction-installation/README.md)).

**Thesis T5. The order is shared by every valuer who counts everything positively.** Among them disagreement can fall only on mixed comparisons, and a ranking of a narrowing above its widening comes only from a negative valuation or a misread or framed case: a bound on where disagreement can fall, not a prediction. The reduction is proved for a widening by one item under a net weighting ([below](#r-a-narrowing-above-its-widening)) and, for several items, for net weightings counting every item above zero (`Weighting.reads_ranksNet_widens`), not for orders neither additive nor transitive. That valuers value positively is C's premise relocated. *Standing:* the reduction proved for net weightings, the bound conjectured; VD1 failed, VD2 passed in part (below). *Forbids and refuted by:* narrowings ranked above their widenings beyond misreading, with no negative valuation measured apart.

<a id="r-a-narrowing-above-its-widening"></a>
**Theorem** (any type). Take a valuer's judgment as a ranking of presentations (plans with their wording and order, each keeping a set of items), and a net weighting as counting each item for a course admitting it and against it. Where such a ranking puts a narrowing above its widening by one item, either it does not rank the pair as its own order ranks the kept sets (a misread or a framing) or the item weighs at zero or below; read right, exactly where it weighs below zero, and at zero it ties (`Weighting.narrower_above_net`, `Weighting.reads_ranksNet`). Read right, where a sign measured apart is the sign of the order's increment from the narrower course to the wider in the pair's context, it fixes the ranking: narrower above exactly where it is a gain, wider exactly where a loss, neither where nothing (`Weighting.reads_increment`); an additive valuation carries it to every context, an interacting one need not. That a judgment is such a ranking is a premise. ([record](../results/records/a-narrowing-above-its-widening.md))

*Evidence for T5* ([experiment](../../../experiments/valuer-agreement/README.md)). VD1 (96 AI-persona valuers, frozen before any call): every frozen rule failed; the narrower plan was chosen mostly under two standpoints whose prompts set a weight below zero, and mixed pairs were decided almost as uniformly as widenings. VD2, sign measured apart: where strongly positive, the narrower plan, not misread, was chosen in 0.26% of rankings, within the frozen 1% for every model (X1s passed); where positive at all, one model of six exceeded 1% (X1 failed); violations followed each valuer's own sign (X2 passed); a measured gain was acted on in 0.49 of cases against a frozen 0.50 (X3 failed). The judgments are role-play; human valuers are untested. VT1: whether valuers rank kept sets, whether a sign measured apart is the joint increment's, and how dispersed weights are remain to measure.

**Assessment** (comparative support for living significance; none a proof). Unification (care, repair, creation, learning, renewal, rest and fitting ending from one ground), discriminating content (renewing and consuming formation told apart where a one-step count ranks them the other way), discrimination against rivals and fruitfulness for alignment; the formal checks verify no premise, and the dynamical theorems are sign-neutral ([warrant](../history/G0-WARRANT.md)).

**Thesis T8. 生生 as the direction.** The good is 生生, life in the whole and its continuing generation, compared by widening what can form, counted for everything (scope: the continuation condition) and ranked by what every valuer who counts everything shares (order: C). Beneath it is the finite theorem of [§4.4](#r-widening-as-what-every-weighting-ranks) (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_of_class`); that these are the valuers who count everything, and that their shared verdict binds, is the thesis. *Standing:* argued; both warrants are open. *Forbids and refuted by:* a mixed case argued better from every standpoint counting everything positively, with no further premise; a good that is a pure closing.

<a id="does-not-show"></a>
## What this chapter does not show

- That the continuation condition, C or the step of invariance is warranted.
- That the ethics derives from the condition: the condition fixes scope and orders nothing; C alone ranks.
- Any duty, right, recognition, motivation or compliance, or an unconditional or complete ranking of mixed comparisons (priority and transfer rank some, under named premises).
- Which reading of `living`, lifting rule or practical commitment is right.
- **Seeing is not valuing**: enlarging what couples in changes what a selection can separate, not which differences matter.
- **Coupling is not compliance**: coupling bounds what a member's acts respond to and fixes nothing within the bound.
- The value of bringing new lives into existence, well-being beyond admitted continuation, and constraints on acts.
