**核心候选：生成活动形成可延续和可再形成的组织；组织的差异通过实际传递参与后续活动，并改变变异、发育、共同生活与下一轮选择的条件。演化本身的组织方式可以在这同一展开中改变。**

**Central candidate:** generative activity forms organizations capable of continuing and being reconstructed. Differences in those organizations enter subsequent activity through actual transmission and alter the conditions of variation, development, shared living and later selection. The organization of evolution itself can change within this unfolding.

This is a proposed connected explanation, not a new universal theorem. Its content must be supplied by mechanisms, correspondences and evidence. Biological evolution retains its particular inheritance, population and temporal meanings; development, learning, cultural transmission and institutional replacement require their own accounts. Mere change does not establish an evolutionary explanation. Formation before there is inheritance is an upstream question, not already differential transmission.

## Current theoretical focus

**Selection on the spine.** The laws of selection are now stated on [the Becoming spine](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#24-evolution-and-transmission), in the same model as conservation, dissipation and comparison, each Lean-checked with an empty axiom report:

- **Descent and Price's equation.** A descendant is given to by a parent that takes part, and comes to stand; over descent, Price's equation holds in counting form ([evolution-on-descent](../results/records/evolution-on-descent.md)).
- **The selection term proper.** Concordance less discordance of offspring number and amount over ordered pairs is the covariance with its denominators cleared; a moved mean over generations has a step of selection proper or of transmission, found by deciding each; a reading highest where activity is supported is never selected against ([selection-term-over-generations](../results/records/selection-term-over-generations.md)).
- **Selection on offspring number itself, and what bounds it.** Read on itself, offspring number has no discordant pair, so selection on it is its spread over pairs; with exact transmission the mean offspring number rises exactly where offspring numbers vary, the counting form of Fisher's fundamental theorem in that case. Offspring numbers are amounts the flow fixes: at most what a parent gives and takes. A lineage within a family balanced and dissipated on ends within its stock, so its transmission must lower offspring number by more than selection raises it; a lossless relay escapes, so dissipation is needed ([what-bounds-offspring-numbers](../results/records/what-bounds-offspring-numbers.md)). Any carried amount moves with offspring number at most as far as their spreads allow: the squared covariance is at most the product of the variances, so the part of the variance in offspring number an amount carries, Fisher's additive part, is at most the whole, and all of it where the amount is offspring number itself ([additive-part-of-offspring-variance](../results/records/additive-part-of-offspring-variance.md)).
- **Selection within groups and among them.** For a family listed as groups of any sizes the selection term divides exactly into the groups' own terms and the term of the groups' means, the multilevel form of Price's equation (with equal sizes, the term among groups read by their totals); an amount selected against within every group is selected for in the whole only where selection among groups goes for it, and the same within-group counts give either verdict ([selection-within-and-among-groups](../results/records/selection-within-and-among-groups.md)). On the base, a group whose own condition ran into joint excess under gated renewal leaves no offspring, so where the groups with less of an amount are such groups nothing is selected against it among groups; one condition shared by every group ends every group and supplies no selection among them ([selection-among-groups-by-ending](../results/records/selection-among-groups-by-ending.md)). Groups whose conditions are coupled, renewed across one family of conditions read as one, end together after the family's excess and are not selected among; the excess can show only on the family, a patch renewed by its neighbour showing none read alone ([groups-whose-conditions-are-coupled](../results/records/groups-whose-conditions-are-coupled.md)). Selection among groups acts only while the groups differ: a generation listed as groups alike in mean selects by selection within them alone, and ending among groups can leave the surviving groups alike, so that what it raised falls back at the next step ([selection-within-and-among-groups-of-any-sizes](../results/records/selection-within-and-among-groups-of-any-sizes.md)). Groups formed by descent from groups uniform in an amount keep their differences: with exact transmission each lineage keeps the value it was founded at, nothing is selected within the lineages, and where the lower lineages end the mean never falls back, so whether selection among groups accumulates is fixed by how the next groups are formed ([groups-formed-by-descent](../results/records/groups-formed-by-descent.md)). Between the two ends, where each unit a member keeps gives to every member of its group and takes from the keeper, selection within goes against keeping and selection among groups for it, and the whole selects for it exactly where the share of the spread among groups, times the group size and the return, exceeds the return with what keeping takes: Hamilton's rule with the assortment that share, so the same returns give either verdict by how the groups are founded ([selection-with-returns-to-groupmates](../results/records/selection-with-returns-to-groupmates.md)).
- **Offspring sampled rather than listed.** Where the next generation is drawn from the listed offspring rather than being them, the sampled share is the listed share on average and its spread is exact; the samplings that undo a step's selection, a rise by ending among groups included, weigh at most a share falling as one over the sample size for a fixed gap, and at least the samplings that lose the favoured kind outright, so drift makes undoing selection rare in large samples and never impossible ([drift-offspring-sampled](../results/records/drift-offspring-sampled.md)). Counting weighs the samplings; it does not say which happens. Over many generations, each sampled from the last, drift keeps the count on average and gives no direction, but it ends variation: the histories still varied vanish geometrically, and each kind is fixed with exactly its starting share in the limit, the neutral fixation probability ([fixation-by-drift](../results/records/fixation-by-drift.md)).
- **What selection sees and what a rise draws from.** Selection through a selector's couplings is blind beyond their closure ([selection-through-couplings](../results/records/selection-through-couplings.md)). Where a whole gains nothing, a rise draws from beyond it, past the slack of the dependences there one breaks unseen by a selection rewarding the rise, and over generations whose offspring go to raisers the share reaching beyond is never selected against ([what-a-rise-draws-from-beyond](../results/records/what-a-rise-draws-from-beyond.md)).

These are known results restated in counting form (Price 1970 and 1972, Fisher 1930 in its exact-transmission case, Hamilton 1975, the Malthusian bound), as the canonical chapter's [relation to known results](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#relation-to-known-results) says of the selection term. What the spine adds is that they are stated with the flow: offspring numbers are bounded by what is given and taken, and a selection's verdict meets what the selected draw from conditions they share. Fitness is not defined; offspring number is a weight and a reading, and no reproductive advantage is ethical authority.

The two-method task, its tests, seed qualification and the available participants stay supplied; how tasks and tests themselves change, and broader comparative strength, are open. ## One unfolding and differentiated evolutionary roles

The starting orientation is [activity forming and transforming the conditions of further activity](NONDUAL-METHOD.md). A living organization, its supporting relations and its effects on later activity are differentiated within that history. Their analytical separation allows questions about ancestry, development, resource access and population composition; it does not posit independent organism and environment substances.

An evolutionary explanation must identify what is reconstructed, what changes, how descendants arise and how their consequences affect later continuation. Selection describes one part of that account. Variation, stochastic sampling, movement, changed transmission and developmental reconstruction cannot all be relabeled selection. The emergence of an organized individual also cannot be assumed to have been explained merely by changing a partition of an already supplied population.

The proposed advantage is a connected explanation of **organization formation → inherited change → changed continuation → changed conditions of evolution**, including the later emergence of learning and evaluation. Some links already have conditional proofs and empirical correspondences. A general account recovering the relevant established theories and earning a comparative advantage remains to be completed.

### Direction for demonstrating a comparative contribution
