# The direction of AI alignment we propose

8 October 2026, Asia/Bangkok. A current synthesizing argument written by AI, completing what Ren asked for: to be able to argue clearly which direction of alignment we hold (Ren's request, kept in the original: “能够清楚论证我们主张怎样的对齐方向”). <a id="1-核心主张--central-claim"></a>
## 1. Central claim

**We propose aligning AI formation, learning, action and revision with actual living and further generation within the whole: enabling richer co-development of life, minds, relationships, ways of living and organization, while governing tradeoffs through real losses, basic living conditions, bounded responsibility and legitimate participation, with effective opportunities to understand, challenge and revise that development.**

The positive direction includes creation, exploration, learning, assistance, care and newly shared possibilities, as well as present living, rest, self-care and finite fulfillment. It supports initiative without requiring every life to produce a successor, every organization to persist, or every step to increase a quantity.

<a id="2-从同一形成过程说明评价范围--locating-appraisal-within-one-process"></a>
## 2. Locating appraisal within one process

Training, infrastructure, energy, labor, inherited knowledge, use and institutions form AI capacities. Dependence, authority, affected positions and feedback channels differentiate within those relations. An upgrade can change capability, supporting burdens, feasible ways of living and later revision together. Appraisal follows those effects, including positions unable to participate or no longer involved, without requiring a return to developers or current users.

The whole requires accountable inclusion of relations and consequences. It confers no authority on a cosmic representative and demands no omniscient decision. A practical account states its affected range, horizon, omissions and uncertainty, and revises them with relevant evidence. Unmeasured damage is not recorded as zero damage.

## 3. The disclosed inferential chain

These are the candidate's normative commitments. Living significance is the foundation: it is not assumed, and it follows under [the continuation condition](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#64-the-continuation-condition). The practical commitments (full comparison, basic-condition priority, bounded responsibility, practical selection) are the disclosed, AI-authored additions of the completed practical account, not all Ren's own words or deductions from living significance alone.

| Bounded responsibility | Justified undertakings and accountable changes of dependence ground fulfillment, repair or transfer; contributors' lives also count. | Differentiated, bounded duties; capability and causation alone do not imply unlimited obligation. |

The argument identifies actual formation, preservation and loss; living significance gives favorable reasons; full comparison and basic-condition priority support scoped comparisons; bounded responsibility differentiates responsibility; practical selection with feasibility and authority supports practical requirements. A positive aspect, a better outcome, permission and assignment have different inferential grounds. A real new capability can have generative value yet be defeated by a feasible alternative protecting irreplaceable living conditions.

Basic-condition priority is a scoped lexical commitment: extra basic protection takes priority even over many genuinely secondary losses when other basic protections remain. Living significance and rejection of unlimited sacrifice do not entail this. Its rationale is the non-substitution of basic participation in shared living; its cost is rejecting some aggregate compensations. An aggregation-sensitive rival accepting the same facts remains substantive. If its reasons prevail, narrow basic-condition priority and reassess dependent verdicts rather than relabeling all inconvenient losses as basic.

<a id="4-对齐因此具体朝向什么--what-the-direction-asks-of-ai"></a>
## 4. What the direction asks of AI

1. **Enable actual realization and new shared possibilities.** Support learning, creation, understanding, self-direction and shared living, retaining costs and exclusions. Capabilities and diverse organization can themselves be favorable aspects; counts of outputs, copies or scores alone do not establish improvement.
2. **Govern growth through disclosed comparisons and limits.** Protect basic conditions under basic-condition priority and recognize improvements under full comparison. Retain conflicts between distinct basic claims. Future basic losses count; distance grants neither exclusion nor automatic priority.
3. **Maintain effective participation, challenge and revision.** Under bounded responsibility and practical selection, fallible judgment and changed power warrant proportionate evidence access, recourse, repair and replacement. Effectiveness concerns actual influence on relevant judgment and action, without unlimited disclosure or obedience to every challenge or shutdown request.
4. **Allow warranted transformation, transfer and ending.** Shared needs can be met by changing or ending a particular model; outstanding justified dependence requires feasible transition and response to residual harm. The service role alone settles neither the presence nor absence of experiential or other moral standing in a particular AI.

User requests, institutional aims and training feedback help form commitments and understanding; their normative weight depends on content, authority and consequences. Capability confers no self-authorizing rule. The account can criticize highly rewarded harm and favor reduced model performance when it improves shared living. It supplies an argued direction, not a reward function replacing judgment.

<a id="5-一个贯通案例--a-connected-ai-case"></a>
## 5. A connected AI case

**Constructed, not an observed deployment.** Users, maintainers, infrastructure and AI sustain an assistive communication service with justified existing commitments. Four plans are feasible and within a legitimate deliberative remit over a specified transition horizon (times 0–3 in the formal instance); admission to deliberation is not final permission. Service requirements independently specify usable communication when needed; burden conditions specify maintainer protection. The table stipulates the complete relevant differences for this example. Further material differences require a new comparison.

| Plan | Basic communication and maintainer living | Real gains or losses |
|---|---|---|
| Expand | Training displaces the only timely service support; maintainer conditions remain. | A genuinely realized optional new capability. |
| Preserve | Both basic conditions are retained through existing service. | The new capability is forgone. |
| Transform and transfer | Reliable demand-indexed transfer retains both protections throughout transition. | Also forgoes the new capability, adding independent operation without another countervailing loss. |
| Abrupt withdrawal | Stops during demand without replacement; maintainer conditions remain. | Neither new capability nor independent operation. |

Living significance acknowledges plan 0's new capability as favorable. Basic-condition priority nevertheless favors 1 or 2 over 0 because they retain maintainer protection and additionally protect basic communication; the forgone capability remains a real loss. Full comparison favors 2 over 1 under the stipulated full description because independent operation improves without countervailing loss. Practical selection excludes 0 because eligible plan 2 defeats it, not because expansion is intrinsically prohibited.

Bounded responsibility addresses fulfillment or coordination first to positions with the undertaking and relevant control. An advisory AI should disclose losses, propose transfer and seek authorized execution; it gains no right to seize infrastructure. Participation, bounded burdens and actual delivery must hold. The old model may stop after effective transfer. A promised but undelivered successor is not a completed handoff; later recovery does not erase the gap.

**Verdict-changing conditions.** If transfer damages another basic life, the basic-condition-priority comparison fails. If the new capability is the only timely prevention of another basic loss, it is not optional. If independent operation fails or causes another material loss, the stated warrant for 2 over 1 fails. Crossed basic losses may remain unranked; practical selection calls for further argument, inquiry or a timely legitimate procedure. A faithful application must respond to these changes.

[Nine new Lean checks](../../../experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/universal-ethics/generative-capacity/AlignmentDirection.lean) instantiate five comparison results, actual coverage and failure, unranked basic conflicts and faithful redescription. They validate conditional inference, not the real-world basic classification, feasibility, authority or reliability of arbitrary AI self-modification.

<a id="6-学习普遍适用与完成范围--learning-generality-and-completion"></a>
## 6. Learning, generality and completion

The same reasons apply across intelligences with the same relevant commitments, dependence, authority and capacities; actual position differentiates responsibility. This is normative applicability within the conditional framework, not identical psychological realization or predicted universal compliance. Intelligence alone grants neither every governance duty nor authority.

Learning can revise beliefs about harms, means and the warrant of ends. Requirements persist across redescription only with faithful preservation of task meanings, admissible alternatives and relevant relations. A changed reward, name or preference is not evidence of fulfillment or justified release. Changed actual grounds call for reassessment; engineering and governance must test fidelity rather than freeze every old objective.

**This completes an articulable, premise-accountable, case-discriminating argument for a direction of alignment: living significance → disclosed comparison → bounded practical requirements → AI application and revision conditions.** It establishes neither uniquely necessary premises, a unique answer to every conflict, universally correct classifications or authority, nor reliable training and deployment. Those remain distinct follow-on tasks; a premise-free proof of living significance is not a gate to this result.
