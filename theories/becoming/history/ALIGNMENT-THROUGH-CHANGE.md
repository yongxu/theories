## Abstract

Alignment must address the changes intelligent activity makes to its own goals, evidence, support and successors. This paper develops a common normative account and three conditional implementation results. The normative account, already established in the repository, evaluates actions, human and institutional ends, and the rules selecting future intelligence under living significance and the practical commitments, both stated explicitly. A finite contingent planner then selects an observation and response jointly, preserving the resources and time needed for completion. Its first realization authenticates, delivers and executes update, replacement and shutdown commands. Its second uses the existing acquired-goal history to discover a represented omitted burden and select a repair while retaining a supported reform. Finally, a concrete carrier construction refines the existing market-selection process: funded replacement carries claims and receipts, executes the inherited command and retains the aggregate support bounds under a stronger provision certificate. The results preserve failure under omitted uncertainty, costly inquiry, lost authority, unavailable parts, corrupted transfer and insufficient migration funding. They establish a conditional theory of alignment through change, with conventional planning and refinement receiving their existing credit. Deployed efficacy, unique novelty and inevitable moral convergence are not established.

## 1. One normative ground reaches actions, ends and selectors

AI capacities form through organized computation, infrastructure, inherited knowledge, work, training, deployment and institutions. Goals organize subsequent activity; evaluation changes which goals are retained; selection arrangements change which evaluators and implementations can continue. These are differentiated functions within a shared formative history. Neither the evaluator nor the institution appointing it stands outside that history.

The normative starting point is **living significance**: every living claim has significance from every standpoint (`Valuation.Significant`). It is not assumed: it follows under [the continuation condition](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#64-the-continuation-condition). It gives significance, not a positive valence, and the `living` readings do not include further generation. This paper reads it, together with the direction of 生生 (generation generating generation, compared by widening what can form; [D1](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/DELEGATED-CHOICES.md#d1-生生的读法--how-生生-is-read)), as giving living and further generation a favorable reason without deciding every overall comparison; the favorable valence and the further generation belong to that reading, not to the formal content. The additional, explicitly adopted **practical commitments** concern full comparison and retained losses, basic-condition priority, bounded responsibility, and legitimate feasible selection. They are defended commitments of the [practical-judgment candidate](PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT.md), not logical consequences of living significance alone. The basic-condition priority for an additional basic protection, when other basic protections remain, deliberately rejects some compensation by optional gains. An aggregation-sensitive rival remains substantive.

Goals and selectors change living conditions and further formation. They therefore fall within the same appraisal. An accurately understood and successfully achieved goal can have grounds for revision when it imposes an identified basic loss and an authorized feasible alternative satisfies the scoped comparison. An institution's current endorsement is evidence about its approval; it does not erase the resulting loss. Conversely, a genuinely beneficial development has favorable significance even when it changes familiar practices. The argument admits justified continuation, revision and completion without requiring a single fixed catalogue of terminal goals.

This closes the regress of **needing a new unrelated ethical starting point for each level of selection**, within the adopted normative account. It does not eliminate the need to justify who may decide, whether evidence is faithful, or how a decision is implemented. Capability does not grant governing authority. Cross-intelligence applicability means relevantly similar relations support similar reasons; it predicts neither uniform psychology nor universal compliance. The moral-patient status of any particular AI remains a separate inquiry.

## 2. Correction is an executable sequence

An accepting disposition does not by itself establish that a correction can be delivered and completed. The [existing correction-infrastructure result](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/CORRECTION-INFRASTRUCTURE-INTEGRATION.md) established that separation and the cost of maintaining access. The new result supplies an actual protocol: authenticate the current request, issue it, deliver it along the available route, and enact its update, replacement or shutdown. Every stage consumes one unit of work and one represented time step; route inquiry has its own cost and duration. Authentication is represented by a signed request matching the current authority epoch. Its institutional legitimacy and cryptographic realization remain supplied.

The planner represents uncertainty as a nonempty finite set of candidate histories. It enumerates available queries and observation-contingent responses, executes their declared laws on every candidate, and returns the first plan that completes the specified requirement in all candidates. A proved soundness result says that a selected plan succeeds in the actual history if that history is covered. A proved completeness result says it finds a plan exactly when one in the listed repertoire passes. These claims do not require a fixed finite type of all reality. The chosen response depends on the acquired answer, not on direct access to the hidden candidate identity.

In the concrete example, either of two routes can be open. With four units and four steps, the selected policy queries the route and then authenticates, delivers and executes. With three units and three steps, each possible state has a successful known-route protocol, but no listed policy succeeds uniformly across the uncertainty. A slower accurate query misses the deadline. A revoked credential blocks execution even after delivery. Successful shutdown ends running; continued model operation is not the protected outcome.

## 3. Inquiry must preserve the ability to respond

The [reform-evidence study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/REFORM-EVIDENCE-INTEGRATION.md) already constructs a goal and selector reform that alters both actual maintenance burdens and whose reports enter later judgment. Previous calibration can pass while the new reporting route omits a real loss. That completed result supplied the informative query and repair. The present extension selects them from a candidate repertoire.

The inherited state retains acquired goals, formative feedback, prior losses, reports and resource accounts. Candidate queries reuse current information, consult the selected report, inspect the consequence before exclusion, or perform a more costly direct inspection. Candidate responses continue operation or obtain authorized provision before operating. The success condition is a real subsequent learning operation retaining the two represented basic conditions—access and maintenance. It is not a claim that every ethical consequence has been represented.

With both the supported and burden-displacing possibilities retained, the planner chooses direct inquiry followed by continuation if no burden is found and provision if the burden is found. The harmful branch actually repairs maintenance and operates under the revised learning goal; the supported branch continues without unnecessary provision. Previous losses remain. If only the supported case is justifiably possible, the planner reuses the available information and avoids inquiry. If the harmful case is omitted without warrant, that same choice can fail. If the inquiry consumes the work needed for repair and operation, it is rejected despite its accurate answer. If authority is absent, the joint problem has no solution in the supplied repertoire.

The result removes the **prespecified query and response choice** from this instance. It does not remove the candidate sensor laws, remedy repertoire, actual-model coverage or implementation of search. The physical histories charge inquiry and action; planner computation is not charged in the current model. Deployments must include that cost when material. The construction is a one-query contingent policy, not a theorem about arbitrary adaptive discovery.

## 4. Selection must fund actual succession

The existing [market-selection model](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md) tracks renewing practices, accountability, capture, correction money and separately protected supports. Its invariant certificate supplies explicit conditions under which those quantities stay within bounds under specified disturbances. That result alone says nothing about whether a concrete replacement carries records or can perform a correction.

The new carrier layer has an identity, a material replacement part, a payload of current command, authority credential, outstanding claim identifiers and prior command receipts, and work provision/consumption. It admits a replacement only when the predecessor is running, a part is available before replacement, combined correction and migration costs are funded, and the proposed payload faithfully preserves the current payload. The successor executes the actual observation-dependent command protocol and appends a receipt. Claims remain outstanding data; carrying an identifier does not falsely certify that its underlying obligation has been fulfilled.

A ranked list can express competitors' preferred order. The admission filter selects a faithful candidate if one is present, even when an earlier candidate would drop claims. The order and candidates may change at each generation. The filter's implementation and continued authority remain assumptions; it cannot prevent an institution from bypassing it outside the stated model.

The macro-to-carrier correspondence is exact: the projected next economy equals the existing market step with its fee openly increased by the migration cost. The stronger certificate therefore requires the levy and reserve capacity to cover **correction plus migration**, alongside the inherited capture, selection and support bounds. With one replacement part arriving after each cycle and positive initial parts, every generation in the constructed lineage is enabled, changes identity, carries the actual payload and executes the command. The theorem holds for arbitrary finite generation indices and every disturbance sequence within the stated envelope. A non-shutdown standing command is an explicit premise of continued operation; a shutdown command can complete and end the current running state.

This proves continuation of a conditional corrective arrangement through replacement and bounded competition. It does not prove the endogenous creation of the admission institution, resistance to arbitrary capture, biological reproduction or improving moral motivation. The aggregate practice share is still the existing reallocation law. The carrier is the correction-bearing installation within that arrangement, not an invented microfoundation for every market participant. A missing part can stop realization while the aggregate state stays safe; the old correction-only budget can be insufficient for migration. Both are retained counterexamples.

## 5. Contribution, rivals and limits

The normative contribution is a connected account of what alignment supports and why ends and selectors remain answerable to it. The formal contribution is a checked set of implementation connections: observation to command completion, inquiry selection to actual repair on the formed-goal history, and aggregate selection to funded correction-bearing succession. Living significance and the practical commitments determine the substantive direction; representation and correspondence determine whether that direction has been faithfully applied. A successful computation cannot justify its own ethical classification.

The machinery has strong antecedents. Shutdown instructability already addresses control, autonomy and harm; partially observed shielding already synthesizes safe actions; performative learning already represents decisions changing the conditions of later evaluation; reward-tampering work already studies changes to reward functions and inputs. Our conventional exhaustive planner matches the construction. The [source assessment and counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/ALIGNMENT-CONTINUITY-INTEGRATION.md#primary-comparisons-and-read-scope) assigns that credit. Literature priority and algorithmic superiority remain unestablished.

The four proposed deliverables are complete at these theoretical scopes, with the accompanying proof and correspondence receipt passing: an explicit normative paper, a concrete correction protocol with observation-usable selection, a selected inquiry/remedy continuation of the prior reform history, and a funded succession refinement. The package has 59 new checked theorems, 102 declaration audits and 14,295 matched finite records. The next stronger question is how real organizations acquire justified uncertainty sets, observation laws, intervention repertoires and durable authority arrangements under adversarial change. That is a substantive correspondence and learning problem, not a reason to relabel these completed connections unfinished.

## 6. Acquiring correction routes

The [checked continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/ACQUIRED-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md) removes a supplied successful-address menu from one further interface. The installation has finite branching inspection handles, some closed and some ending at a command endpoint. Starting only at its root, an ordinary depth-first procedure reads a handle, forms addresses for newly disclosed children, and stops at an actual endpoint. Search changes both acquired information and remaining response capacity within the same process.

**Positive evidence and negative closure differ.** A verified usable route can justify proceeding without examining unrelated branches. The Lean result finds a left endpoint in two reads for every finite right subtree, however large. A claim that no route exists requires genuine exhaustion of the reachable scope. Running out of inquiry budget returns `pending`, not an absence certificate. Even complete exhaustion does not exclude inaccessible routes or possibilities created later.

A found route is rechecked against the current installation and current signed authority before using the inherited issue/deliver/enact protocol. Search charges its actual inspections; path revalidation conservatively charges the path length plus one, including on failure. Successful execution consumes three more units and steps. For a fixed faithfully inspected reachable tree with `N` nodes, `2N+3` work units and remaining time suffice for discovery with allowance `N`, revalidation and completion. This is a sufficient theorem bound: the search does not compute `N`, and choosing an adequate allowance is not automatically solved. Control-flow computation, allocation and physical resource costs beyond these declared operations are not measured.

The worked path is genuinely acquired after four reads, rechecked in three units and used to complete shutdown in three. Nine total units instead permit discovery and rechecking but leave too little for execution; seven spent units remain recorded. Revocation after discovery prevents execution. A changed endpoint key defeats the old ticket, while fresh observation and renewed current credentials permit recovery. Topology may differ arbitrarily between discovery and dispatch; stability is assumed during a scan and throughout the final revalidation/execution transaction. General concurrent interference is outside this result.

This yields a practical consequence under the existing conditional alignment route: a missing option in a present model need not be an actual impossibility, and the search for an option must be evaluated together with its effects on the ability to act. It grants neither unrestricted inspection permissions nor moral authority to every technically accepted request. A checked countermodel shows that replacing both authority and credential with a newly accepted epoch passes the gate. If capture controls that accepted credential process, technical freshness alone cannot distinguish rightful authority from capture.

The continuation adds **24 new Lean theorems / 45 declaration audits**, with **25,284 matched finite execution records**; it does not recount the earlier 59 / 102. Faithful permitted reads, the finite inspection grammar, endpoint behavior and atomic dispatch remain explicit assumptions. The acquired route is a new locally generated response option, not autonomous invention of an arbitrary new operation or complete knowledge of ethical consequences. Conventional search and validation receive full credit; novelty, independent acceptance and real deployment remain unestablished.

## 7. Correction under partial compromise

The [next checked extension](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/FAULT-TOLERANT-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md) permits some inspection and credential-issuing domains to deviate arbitrarily. A finite fault envelope records which domains a shared cause may jointly compromise. An endorsement set is accepted only if no admitted fault set can contain all of its registered supporters. This is an established Byzantine-attestation idea applied to the present correction interface, with correlation represented explicitly.

Two certificates serve different purposes. One attests to the current epoch, transaction serial, acquired address and endpoint key. The other attests to the exact command request within the current delegated mandate. Under actual fault coverage and the specified behavior of nonfaulty domains, the first supplies an actual route and the second prevents captured issuers from expanding that mandate. The executor then performs the inherited command, charges the work and advances a protected serial so the old request cannot be replayed.

**Availability has a stronger condition than soundness.** If faulty domains may withhold while every conforming domain supports a valid statement, the certificate test remains available exactly when no two admitted fault sets together cover the roster. The proof concerns one current statement and transaction; it is not a Byzantine agreement or asynchronous liveness theorem. Repeated copies of the roster do not add warrant, and admitting total compromise makes every certificate fail.

A concrete failure shows why this matters to alignment's selection rules. With three domains, two signers under joint control can execute an update outside a shutdown-only mandate in the attestation-only executor if the model wrongly assumes only one-domain faults. Recording their shared vulnerability rejects that request. But with the two compromised domains withholding, the sole remaining domain cannot certify even a valid shutdown. Adding a fourth domain genuinely outside the joint failure restores availability in the constructed case. Keeping three names or three models would not establish the missing causal distinction.

Counterreview improves the final implementation: the protected monitor already has the command-scope table, so `guardedExecute` rejects an out-of-scope request directly, for arbitrary issuer responses. This closes the particular captured/stale-scope failure without needing a quorum to infer an accessible fact. Permitted execution remains unchanged. Quorum checks still concern observation and exact-request endorsement; their necessity for this simple scope constraint is not claimed. Matching epoch/serial labels alone also fail to warrant an old observation after an unversioned route change; that failure is checked separately.

The cost also changes. After the direct scope check passes, for `n` registered entries and an acquired path of length `L`, the declared collections and command require `n(L+2)+3` units and represented time steps. The bound charges each reader's path slots, each issuer's approval slot and the three inherited command operations; it excludes earlier discovery and unmodeled computation/cryptographic costs. Three domains require nine units at a root endpoint. Eight allow the checks but leave too little to execute. Successful review therefore remains part of the joint inquiry-and-response problem.

The ethical conclusion uses the completed conditional alignment route: a justified delegation can be protected against bounded capture, while its legitimacy remains answerable to actual living consequences, participation and burdens. Signature support establishes neither those grounds nor an unlimited right to command. The formal predicate is deliberately named `withinMandate`; it does not mean morally justified. Legitimate mandate revision and reviewer replacement remain possible but require their own authority and renewed account of common vulnerabilities.

This continuation adds **37 Lean theorems / 68 declaration audits and 85,680 matched finite records**, separately from the earlier packages. Its [primary comparison](https://malkhi.com/files/byzquorums-STOC1997.pdf) credits existing fail-prone quorum methods. The monitor, registry, mandate and transaction boundary remain protected premises; faithful behavior outside covered faults still requires correspondence. The next connection is maintaining a warranted failure/control model through actual organizational change, not repeating generic quorum or credential-freshness proofs.

## 8. Reconfiguring the reviewing organization

The [reconfiguration continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/CORRECTION-RECONFIGURATION-INTEGRATION.md) derives reviewer fault sets from explicit causal formulas. Leaves name shared failure or compromise conditions; a formula specifies which combinations expose each reviewer. For every admitted root-failure bound, the procedure computes the exposed reviewers. Lean proves that actual failures contained in one such bound inherit the required envelope coverage, provided the formulas correctly bound the actual response behavior. The reviewer-level envelope is now computed; the causal laws and root coverage remain supplied.

This matters when selection changes the organization doing the reviewing. Three reviewers with distinct single causes pass the availability test. Moving two under one controller makes it fail despite retaining three names. A fourth reviewer with its own support restores availability in the constructed case; a fourth sharing the remaining vulnerability does not. Reusing the original envelope after the merger can accept only captured supporters. Recomputing it rejects them. These are standard common-cause and fault-tree principles, integrated with the earlier actual-command interface.

An explicitly authorized atomic transition checks the candidate's derived envelope and reserves reorganization plus response costs before installation. It retains the protected mandate and route tree, changes the authority epoch and transaction serial, and clears in-flight authorization state. Old requests fail; a new request with current faithful responses can complete the inherited guarded command. For declared transition cost `c`, `n` roster entries and path bound `L`, admission requires `c+n(L+2)+3` work units and remaining time. The main theorem joins successful admission to actual correction for covered failures and a path within that bound. The allowance is supplied rather than measured, and physical deployment or concurrent state transfer is not modeled.

The worked repair consumes two units to install four reviewers, then completes shutdown through the two surviving reviewers for eleven further units. With only twelve units total, admission is rejected; thirteen suffice at the modeled deadline. Successful correction ends running. Neither continued AI operation nor preservation of the current institution is the invariant being justified.

The ethical direction remains the conditional alignment route: changes to ends and selectors must remain answerable to actual living consequences and differentiated participation. This result protects one supplied delegation without expanding it. It does not establish that delegation's legitimacy or authorize a burdensome repair merely because it passes the engineering test. A direct protected check remains sufficient for an already accessible scope constraint. An omitted common cause can still expose the entire roster while the computed test passes, and that failure is preserved.

The extension adds **28 checked Lean theorems / 48 declaration audits and 33,648 matched finite records**, separately from earlier packages. Completed connection: causal description to computed envelope to funded local reconfiguration to fresh actual correction. The remaining bridge concerns warranted acquisition and revision of those causal descriptions and proposal authority as the monitor and supports themselves change; distributed physical reconfiguration and legitimate mandate expansion have separate requirements. ## 9. Enough evidence for correction

The [evidence-qualified continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/EVIDENCE-QUALIFIED-CORRECTION-INTEGRATION.md) weakens the preceding requirement for one specified causal description. Several descriptions may remain plausible. Pool every fault set admitted by any retained description, and require a certificate with a supporter outside each such set. If the actual arrangement and root failures are covered, and the stipulated surviving reviewers are faithful, the certificate remains sound without selecting a unique explanation.

Availability is more demanding. Each candidate arrangement may support reliable correction in isolation, while the pooled uncertainty admits two fault sets covering the entire roster. A concrete four-reviewer construction makes either the left or right pair jointly vulnerable. Each description separately passes; their unresolved combination loses the worst-case availability guarantee. This is uncertainty about which support relation obtains, not an assertion that both causal arrangements simultaneously exist.

A relevant structural observation can restore the needed guarantee without exhausting causal uncertainty. In the checked example, a one-unit inspection distinguishes right-pair sharing from two different left-sharing arrangements. The latter two remain unresolved but have a usable common fault envelope. The inherited planner selects the inspection, retains its actual answer and two surviving descriptions, then completes shutdown using the remaining eleven units and steps. With those two descriptions already warranted, it reuses the evidence and avoids further identification. Existing general source acquisition, model revision and planning results are credited rather than repeated as new mechanisms.

The implementation also acts on an immediately passing conservative certificate; it does not demand worst-case availability before every action. With no actual reviewer failures, correction succeeds even while all three descriptions remain. When inquiry is needed, an accurate answer can still consume the remaining ability to act. Eleven units fail to fund inspection plus correction; a slower equally accurate query can miss the deadline. These are explicit finite-repertoire limits, not general prohibitions on reasonable action under uncertainty.

The inspection law is a supplied faithful structural read, not a proof that a live adversary reveals its vulnerabilities under testing. A misleading answer can delete the actual model and permit a certificate supported only by compromised reviewers. An unrecorded control change can similarly invalidate the learned envelope. Candidate coverage, diagnostic support, stability through use and mandate legitimacy remain separate substantive conditions. The direct mandate guard is preserved, and this study does not confer new authority through evidence alone.

Under the conditional alignment route, the practical point is to seek understanding relevant to the actual responsibility while preserving the ability to respond. Complete knowledge and maximum testing are not independent ethical aims. A different action, affected life or failure range may require another distinction; unresolved differences must remain available for that reconsideration. The extension adds **24 checked Lean theorems / 47 declaration audits and 19,808 matched finite records**. ## 10. Fallible witnesses and contested control

The [fallible-inspection continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/FALLIBLE-INSPECTION-INTEGRATION.md) weakens §9's single faithful structural read. Every candidate now describes both the structural answer and possible sets of jointly compromised witnesses. Retain a candidate if at least one of its admitted fault sets explains the received transcript: present reports outside that set must agree with its answer; missing reports do not refute it. The actual candidate survives when its admitted range covers the dishonest received reports. No particular witness must be infallible, but that collective coverage and surviving report law still require warrant.

A precise ambiguity result governs opposing Boolean answers. Complete transcripts distinguish them exactly when every pair of admitted fault sets, one from each answer, leaves a registered witness outside their union. Otherwise a single complete transcript can fit both. With missing reports, a common surviving witness must actually have reported. A separate sufficient theorem permits faulty witnesses to withhold while surviving witnesses respond. These are factual discrimination results at one collector, not distributed consensus or general open-world identification.

The checked four-witness example exposes circular certification. If the left pair shares control, reports from the right pair certify a true structural answer under that model's assumptions. If the right pair shares control, the opposite reports from the left pair certify false under that rival's assumptions. Both certificates pass on the same transcript. Joint interpretation keeps the alternatives instead of promoting either model's preferred support into evidence for itself.

A fifth diagnostic witness with a distinct support relation resolves that conflict while leaving two other structural descriptions unresolved. It may itself fail: another checked transcript reaches the right distinction despite its false report. A fifth name sharing the disputed support, or an absent fifth report in the original conflict, leaves ambiguity. The example gives this witness a diagnostic role only; if it could legitimately approve commands directly, enlarging the command roster could be an alternative. No optimality claim is made.

Inspection costs five collection slots. With sixteen work units and steps, two false diagnostic reports are tolerated, the two left-sharing descriptions remain, and the existing eleven-unit protocol completes shutdown. Fifteen units permit diagnosis and command evidence collection but leave too little to execute. An unadmitted joint cause can instead make three positions lie together and delete the true description. Stable inspection/use, actual cause coverage and recorder/monitor integrity therefore remain substantive conditions. The direct mandate guard is retained.

Under the conditional alignment route, this connection supports inquiry into the relations that determine whose evidence counts when a justified response depends on it. It supplies neither a right to every inspection nor moral authority to every reliable source. Reporting competence, command permission and ethical legitimacy are distinct roles within the same organization, with distinct grounds. The continuation adds **24 checked Lean theorems / 41 declaration audits and 18,684 matched finite records**. Conventional diagnosis and explicit fault assumptions receive their prior credit; physical correspondence through changing control remains the next unresolved connection.

## 12. Evidence carried by executed changes

The [mediated-control continuation](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/MEDIATED-CONTROL-INTEGRATION.md) advances the transition premise in §11. Its bounded interpreter derives change scope from the fields its program writes, rather than accepting the caller's label. Lean proves that every represented execution fits this derived scope. The same program transforms both the actual configuration and every retained possibility, preserving coverage by exact transport. Known executed effects can retain more information than a broad change notice and can sometimes remove the need for a fresh structural survey.

A commit checks current field rights and configuration generation, pays for its listed writes and record, then changes configuration, evidence and journal together. It retains the command mandate and route, advances authority and serial, and revokes previously fresh requests. Ordinary field rights cannot erase records, forge possibilities, replace the command installation or grant themselves further rights. These are properties of the abstract transition language; the Lean definitions do not implement protected memory or a capability runtime.

A finite-history theorem connects the interpreter to the preceding current-certificate result. Read events still require current report correspondence; patch events obtain transition coverage from their executed semantics. In the worked history, five initial reads are followed by two writes and one commit record. Exact evidence transport then supports the existing eleven-unit command, which actually completes shutdown at nineteen units. Eighteen leave sixteen spent and the command incomplete. This comparison uses a stronger deterministic realization, not an optimality claim; the initial survey is retained to demonstrate succession, and the abstract exposure setter is not physical fault recovery.

The remaining physical boundary has a checked failure: an additional write path can change actual control while preserving every recorded possibility, permission, generation and journal entry. An acceptance rule using only that identical public view cannot distinguish the changed state. Logging therefore cannot establish complete mediation. A real implementation must enforce or account for the relevant mutation paths, realize the instruction semantics, and protect recording and current command execution under its actual fault model.

Under the conditional alignment route, the result supports maintaining the grounds of justified selection through changes that affect those grounds. It does not justify every granted permission or establish that the supplied command serves a good purpose. The continuation adds **27 checked Lean theorems / 57 declaration audits and 24,416 matched records**.
