**Central candidate.** Cognitive activity forms when differentiated organization makes consequential relationships available in discrimination, skilled response, anticipation and, in some cases, representation and reflection. ## 2. What the higher orders actually explain

Begin with activity whose effects change later activity. Some relations recur sufficiently to distinguish an organized range of access and response. Within that history, changing a connection can change which differences are available, how long their effects persist, or which subsequent activity they influence. A body, learned model and shared practice are distinguished by these organized relationships and their histories, not installed as independent starting worlds. The [nondual method](NONDUAL-METHOD.md) governs the decomposition.

Ordinary regulation changes an ongoing condition through a response. Learning changes a disposition or relation involved in responding. Meta-learning changes how such modifications occur: which encounters are selected, what is retained, which errors trigger revision, or how a new procedure is constructed. Reflective inquiry can additionally make some of these formative relationships available as content. Evolutionary and institutional changes can alter which learning organizations form and continue. The same change may participate in several descriptions.

This classification is functional and relational. A slower timescale is not sufficient to establish a higher-order mechanism, and a meta-level need not occupy a separate physical component. Conversely, writing one encompassing transition function does not explain how its effective learning relations arise. The explanation has to identify the changed organization and the actual route through which the change affects later activity.

The [retrospective composition](../results/records/retrospective-source-binding.md) now gives another concrete instance. Learning about how reports were produced changes the alternatives an earlier retained naming episode supports. The earlier reports remain unchanged; their present inferential role changes. This is a higher-order change in evidence use, connected to actual interpretation and response. Whether that new source assessment applies to the earlier event is a further historical relationship to establish, not a benefit guaranteed by higher order alone.

The [existing dynamics audit](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/SYSTEMS-HIGHER-DYNAMICS.md) credits updater formation, method revision, acquired distinctions, recurrent revision and changing shared conditions. The [current evolution account](THE-THEORY-OF-EVOLUTION.md#the-next-theoretical-connection) now also credits acquired calibration and reference renewal. None should be reopened as wholly absent.

### Consequences can select the organization that learns

[Ren's recursive-selection proposal](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-09-learning-recursive-selection.md) clarifies the general connection. Effects of activity change which operative relationships are formed, maintained, reconstructed or replaced; these relationships then organize further activity, including how later learning occurs. Formation, retention and use therefore already have a common causal account. Their particular psychological and historical realizations still require identification.

Backpropagation computes how internal connections affect a specified loss; an optimizer uses those sensitivities to change the connections. Differential reproduction changes which inherited organization recurs. Institutional evaluation can alter participation, practice, authority and opportunities to learn. These mechanisms can realize a common consequence-to-organization relationship without being the same algorithm. Learned optimizers supply an actual example of changing a way of learning; their outer task distribution and criterion remain supplied. The [primary-source assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/LEARNING-SELECTION-SOURCES.md) separates these implementations and their evidence.

Consequences for an organized whole can affect the continuation of its constituents without measuring a separate reward for each part. Their connection runs through actual updating, support, development and reconstruction. The learning mechanism can itself participate in what is retained. Parts can be maintained through what they jointly enable, including supported, resting or terminal roles. **Absolute continuation, increasing relative frequency and renewal of wider conditions are different outcomes.** No one of them defines the others.

The positive evolutionary direction is correspondingly concrete: renewing activity can favor the organization responsible when its consequences enter actual maintenance and successor formation, and reconstruction preserves the relevant capacity. The arrangement tying contribution to continuation can itself vary and recur. This supports selection across multiple interdependent scales; it needs neither an isolated generative self nor a fixed tree of independent selectors.

Longer duration alone supplies no missing feedback. Early transmission, changed association, misleading evaluation, failed reconstruction and support supplied elsewhere can alter which organization continues. Unconditional eventual convergence to generative alignment is not established by the general connection. The [independent counterreview](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/LEARNING-SELECTION-COUNTERREVIEW.md) preserves the affirmative route and limits that inference without claiming an all-history impossibility theorem.

This gives cognition a route of formation and continuation, while its content still depends on what the retained relations distinguish, predict and enable. A successful proxy does not redefine a mistaken representation into truth. The [integrated argument](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/COGNITION-LEARNING-EVOLUTION-INTEGRATION.md#8-learning-and-recursive-selection-the-connection-being-completed) connects the existing content/use results to this selection account and distinguishes general explanation from a complete empirical genealogy.

## 3. From organization to cognitive activity

The constructive explanation has several linked transformations.

**Differentiated access.** A dependency can affect continuation without being detected. A formed route makes some changes available to later activity through contact, signal propagation, retained effects or testimony. Access remains selective: the activity establishing a measurement can itself change the measured relation, and exclusion from a reporting route can conceal a real consequence. This connects attention to what encounters and records can occur; it does not yet explain every attentional phenomenon.

**Skilled coordination.** Retained organization can guide action without an explicit sentence, a detached inner observer or a reflective theory. The corresponding claim concerns an acquired capacity and the conditions of exercise. Accurate performance can depend on bodily and social supports that a narrow neural description omits. Habit and flexible skill require different explanations when consequences change.

**Representational content.** In cases where a distinction stands for a relation across encounters, acquisition, differential use and calibration constrain what it concerns. Content can remain the same when a report becomes false. A question, imagined possibility and assertion can concern the same condition while taking different roles in judgment. The [knowledge account](KNOWLEDGE.md) has developed these distinctions and the mechanisms of selective revision and acquired source dependence; this chapter reuses them.

**Understanding and inference.** A relationship becomes available for more than one trained output when the organization supports its implications, challenges and changed applications. Whether someone can revise a source's credibility, preserve the claim's referent, or separate contrary evidence from a changed desire supplies different evidence about that organization. This is the current affirmative account of understanding through differentiated answerability. Its universal sufficiency is not settled by a finite task battery.

**Combination and imagination.** Acquired components can participate in new organized combinations. An expression can be evaluated without its described action being performed, and the resulting anticipated trace can later guide a choice. The distinction between representing and enacting must be implemented by the actual access and execution paths. The new result below supplies a restricted compositional connection; richer counterfactual alternatives require an acquired model of the relevant dependencies and the limits of transfer.

## 5. Learning content, acquiring motives and changing ends

A learned relation can have several uses. It can guide a prediction, support an explanation, help obtain a wanted outcome, or disclose why a previously wanted outcome should be questioned. These roles depend on its actual connections to activity. A neutral relation can be acquired before it becomes motivationally significant; changing a motive can change action while some learned content remains correct.

The new compositional model contains only a minimal example of that distinction: the same acquired interpreter can support different choices under two supplied preferences. It does not form those preferences. Their formation and reflective revision are supplied by the existing [evaluative](../history/EVALUATIVE-PLASTICITY.md), [goal-formation](../history/GOAL-FORMATION-AND-REVISION.md) and [ethical-learning](ETHICAL-LEARNING.md) studies at their own scopes. No physical composition of every module is claimed merely by linking them.

A fuller affective explanation must connect bodily regulation, anticipation, salience, felt quality and social history where they actually occur. It must preserve cases of wanting something inaccurately represented, accurately recognizing a reason without acting, and care that persists without private return. Neither a reward variable nor retention success exhausts these questions.

Ethical justification follows the conditional alignment route, which is stated explicitly. The generative account can explain how a motive develops and how consequences alter it; that history does not independently certify the motive. There is no requirement to restart an axiom-free proof of living significance to continue this cognitive investigation.

## 6. Development and evolutionary psychology

An inherited organization does not carry a complete adult psychology independently of development. Its causal contribution may be a particular response, a biased range of attention, a sensitive developmental period, an available learning operation, or a way of obtaining later support. Which contribution is present must be established. Equally, calling something learned does not explain the formation of its learning architecture.

The proposed connection is that transmitted organization changes how subsequent encounters reconstruct cognitive and motivational capacities. Those capacities change later encounters, relationships, care and transmission. Their effects can then alter which organizations recur. The roles of inheritance, development and social conditions are identified through these pathways within the common history, with their actual differences retained.

For a psychological disposition, ask four linked questions: what mechanism produces it now; how it develops; what effects it has on continuation and reproduction; and how its historical distribution arose. A present benefit does not establish its historical origin, and an evolutionary history does not reveal every current motive. Selection, drift, developmental constraint, byproducts and cultural transmission must be distinguished where they are live alternatives. The [evolutionary-organization account](../history/EVOLUTIONARY-ORGANIZATION.md) supplies mechanisms for changing evolutionary organization, not a license to assign one adaptive story to every behavior.

This supports several concrete explanatory possibilities. Selection can favor a capacity for flexible acquisition without specifying every later belief. A reliably recurring disposition can be reconstructed through care or social practice. The same learning organization can give different outcomes after different histories. An acquired practice can modify the conditions under which its successors learn. These are conditional routes to investigate, not a claim that all psychological traits take one route.

The [Liangzhi evidence assessment](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/LIANGZHI-EVOLUTION-SOURCES.md) already preserves developmental and transmission examples with important limitations. Their experiments must not be spliced into a single observed genealogy. The complete psychological explanation still requires an identified chain joining the relevant inherited/developmental contribution to acquired discrimination, motivation, action and subsequent transmission in the claimed domain.

The [historical extension](../results/records/historical-source-binding.md) uses dated acquired controls to constrain possible production histories, then interprets a retained report using routes still possible **at its earlier date**.
