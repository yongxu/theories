# Keeping markets in generative regimes

6 October 2026. AI-authored development of [Ren's proposal](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-markets-and-generative-basins.md), within the continuing [generative foundation](../history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md). This is a conditional framework for market governance. Its application to a particular market requires identified dynamics, affected parties and feasible interventions.

> **Status:** live: statement source of the market records, cited by §4 of [the canonical chapter](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md). Its dated round note of October 2026 is kept verbatim in [its rounds history](../history/MARKETS-AND-GENERATIVE-REGIMES-rounds-2026-10-20.md).

A market coordinates exchange and selects among practices through prices, revenue, financing, survival and expansion. Those practices also change the conditions on which future exchange and life depend. The proposed guidance is therefore: **arrange market selection so that successful activity renews those conditions, while preserving affected people's ability to discover damage and change the arrangement.** This connects economic performance to the reproduction of human capacities, shared supports and accountable institutions.

## What makes a market generative

A generative market regime would support useful production and discovery while renewing or replacing the conditions of further worthwhile activity: material and ecological supports, people's health and skills, reliable knowledge, shared infrastructure, and effective agency. Which conditions matter, whose lives are affected, and which losses are unacceptable must be specified. A larger financial total cannot establish these properties by itself.

Generation in the foundation is broader than favorable regeneration. Harmful institutions also generate successors and can be durable. Here, “generative market” names a favorable regime relative to explicit, justified commitments about affected beings and their futures. Those commitments extend beyond whatever supports profitable exchange. A person retains standing even when the market no longer needs their labor or purchasing power. The [ethical foundation](../history/ETHICAL-FOUNDATION.md) and [formation study](../history/FORMATION.md) distinguish that standing from bargaining leverage.

Markets can contain mixed regimes at different scales. A firm may develop useful technology while depleting workers; a region may renew its own resources by shifting damage elsewhere. Some enterprises and products should disappear as better possibilities develop. Maintaining generativity does not require preserving every incumbent, every transaction, or growth in every output. It requires identifying which continuations and transformations are warranted and keeping them feasible.

## The connection from dependence to market selection

The named connection is **organizational dependence → consequences becoming operationally significant → selection of practices → revision of the mechanisms of selection**. The market application tests whether consequences for shared supports actually reach the mechanisms that decide which practices persist.

Consider a hypothetical service market. Two providers deliver an equally useful service today. One pays to maintain equipment and train successors; the other can defer those costs or shift them to workers and the public. If buyers cannot distinguish future reliability, financiers reward only present margins, and neither contracts nor other institutions make the deferred costs consequential, the second strategy can expand faster. The first provider's virtue does not change that selection mechanism. Conversely, reliable information, usable long-term commitments, maintenance obligations or shared provision could change the comparison. Each route needs an actual carrier, funding and enforcement.

This is conditional. Narrow optimization need not win: customers may recognize quality, workers may leave, reputation may matter, and future costs may return to the firm. The task is to identify where these feedbacks work, where they fail and who remains outside them. The analyst's assessment of renewal is not itself an economic reward.

Akerlof supplies a concrete antecedent: when quality cannot be distinguished, those producing it may fail to receive its return, allowing poorer offerings to displace better ones. He also notes that institutions counteracting this problem can concentrate power. [The Market for Lemons, 1970](https://pims.math.ca/files/Akerlof1970.pdf).

A second loop connects this application to the [meta-attractor theory](../../meta-attractor/README.md). Successful firms or coalitions may gain influence over contracts, standards, information access and public rules. Where that influence weakens restraints on the strategy that produced the success, present advantage changes future selection. Where it instead funds trustworthy knowledge, accessible infrastructure and accountable renewal, success can strengthen the conditions of further contribution. Neither direction follows from success alone.

The decisive governance question consequently concerns both what wins under today's rules and **what today's winners can do to tomorrow's rules**. Regulators, cooperatives and public providers face the same question about their own authority. Assigning an institution the name “guardian” does not explain why it will remain answerable.

## Practical guidance and its tests

The following are design directions to assess in context. Their combination matters: evidence without action can be ceremonial, obligations without funding can exclude smaller participants, and protection without accountability can entrench power.

| Condition to preserve | Practical direction | Evidence that the direction is working |
|---|---|---|
| Renewal of indispensable supports | Account for maintenance, skill formation, care, infrastructure and ecological replacement where relevant; protect non-substitutable limits directly | Actual support conditions remain adequate through the transition, including for people and places outside the transaction |
| A viable route for useful contribution | Make consequential quality and deferred costs affect procurement, contracts, liability, finance or other legitimate selection mechanisms | Practices that maintain the specified supports can obtain resources and continue without silently receiving an analyst's reward |
| Effective refusal and alternatives | Reduce coercive dependence through appropriate basic provision, bargaining capacity, portability and access to alternative providers or forms of provision | People can decline harmful terms and use alternatives in practice, within their time and resource constraints |
| Independent learning and timely correction | Support trustworthy evidence, protected challenge, competent investigation and usable remedies | A consequential error can be detected and corrected before the relevant loss becomes irreversible; the correction leaves the next review affordable |
| Accountable changes to market rules | Constrain exclusive control of standards, evidence and rule revision; give affected parties meaningful routes to contest changes | Economic success cannot automatically buy exemption from obligations or shut every effective route of challenge |
| Diversity and adaptation | Preserve feasible experimentation, entry, replacement and public or cooperative alternatives where appropriate | A failed practice can be replaced without destroying essential provision; a successful practice remains open to challenge |

Prices can carry some consequences, but their usefulness depends on information, power, enforcement and what is exchangeable. Some losses require limits, rights or direct provision. Conversely, restricting activity can destroy useful renewal capacity or serve incumbent interests. The framework evaluates an intervention through its actual consequences and revision conditions, including those of the intervention itself.

## Making the basin claim precise

In a specified dynamical model, an attractor basin consists of states whose trajectories approach the relevant attractor. A viability region instead concerns states from which admissible action can maintain specified constraints. A desirable region is not automatically an attractor; a recoverable situation is not automatically one the actual institutions will repair. The [governance assessment](../history/ALIGNMENT-AND-GOVERNANCE.md#the-exact-necessity-claim) gives a finite viability formulation under explicit information and action assumptions.

For a market model, the state must include relevant supports, participants, resources, information and installed rules. Rule revision and the resources that make it possible belong inside the evolving state. One must state the shocks, horizons and policies being considered, then demonstrate attraction, continued viability or recovery at that scope. There may be several favorable and harmful regimes, mixed trajectories, or no stable attractor.

The foundation supplies a useful necessary accounting condition for a represented support stock. If renewal is \(R(t)\), loss is \(L(t)\), initial stock is \(C_0\), and the justified floor is \(C_{\min}\), then remaining above the floor requires

\[
C_0+\int_0^t [R(s)-L(s)]\,ds\ge C_{\min}
\qquad\text{for every relevant }t.
\]

This is the existing [prefix renewal condition](../history/THEORY.md#4-from-becoming-to-maintained-regeneration), not a new market theorem. Eventual gains cannot pay an earlier irrecoverable loss. A transition toward a better incentive structure must therefore preserve enough time and usable resources to reach it. Different supports retain their own units and substitution conditions; this expression does not turn agency, money and ecological function into one interchangeable stock.

“Keeping the market in the generative basin” now has a conditional mathematical realization in [the coupled model](MARKET-DYNAMICS-MATH.md#an-actual-attracting-set). Its protected and recovery regions are certified subsets of a basin, alongside a region ending in collapse. Applying that result to an actual market still requires identified laws, parameters and support conditions; it does not establish a complete empirical basin boundary.

## What this application adds to the research

The framework joins three obligations that should be examined in one continuing history: shared supports must be renewed, that renewal must become consequential for actual selection, and the institutions carrying that consequence must survive resource pressure and rule capture. Existing work already demonstrates scoped separations between shared benefit and private retention, between correct evidence and effective action, and between one successful correction and repeated funded correction. See the [unified theory](../history/THEORY.md), [joint formation](../history/JOINT-FORMATION.md) and [reason protection](../history/REASON-PROTECTION.md). Their formal results are interfaces and counterexamples, not empirical proofs about markets.

The proposed contribution must be compared with established accounts of externalities, information, institutional power and collective action. The [review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-REVIEW.md) records primary comparators and the scope of the internal assessment. Common vocabulary or agreement with existing economics does not establish a distinctive predictive advantage.

Ostrom's institutional framework already connects outcomes to changing rules and material conditions, and examines monitoring, participant rule revision and conflict resolution. It is a strong comparator for this proposal's account of correction and differentiated centers. Its findings concern contextual institutional arrangements rather than an automatic tendency of markets to regenerate. [Beyond Markets and States, 2010](https://web.pdx.edu/~nwallace/EHP/OstromPolyGov.pdf).

The next discriminating study should choose one bounded market with measurable renewal costs and a real route for revising its rules. It should trace maintenance or harm into evidence, decisions, resource allocation and subsequent rule changes. Outcomes should include support renewal, harms by affected group, entry and exit, time to remedy, the cost of correction, and who controls later revisions. An aggregate performance score must not conceal any of these outcomes.

Compare matched interventions with equal initial resources and information: an existing remedy for the identified incentive problem, and that remedy with an explicitly funded, accountable revision mechanism. Include the additional mechanism's costs and test changed dependence or incumbent power. If the mechanism only works because an evaluator pays for “generativity,” the intended derivation has been supplied rather than explained. If an established institutional model explains the same findings, the result may support the mechanism while narrowing the claim of theoretical novelty.

A damaging counterexample would be continued support renewal accompanied by domination of dispensable people, accurate evidence that cannot change allocation, or successful correction that exhausts its own successor. These would require revising the claimed sufficiency of the proposed guidance. They remain compatible with the wider foundation's obligation to explain destructive regimes.

This application identifies a testable institutional interface. It leaves local production laws, measurement, legitimate authority, evaluative commitments and the formation of concern explicit. The continuing foundational task is to explain how shared consequences acquire and retain warranted practical significance, including when narrow private advantage favors ignoring them.

Whether dissent can be raised and sustained, whether alternatives can continue to be provided, and who controls the resources that correction needs, all belong to the activity being aligned.

The affirmative direction follows the completed conditional alignment route: support different lives and their further generation, and appraise how the execution of an aim changes the shared conditions. ### Degeneration boundaries and transitions

2026-10-08. [Ren explicitly asked](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-08-generative-alignment-priority.md#degeneration-boundaries-and-phase-transitions): not all market evolution is degeneration, and one should find the boundary of degeneration, possible phase transitions, and the scope of application of the law of the meta-attractor. [This round's argument, proof, correspondence with reality and counter-review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#degeneration-boundary-and-transition) limit the foregoing research in this direction.

**The degenerative meta-attractor is a law candidate with a boundary of application: an activity that erodes shared generative conditions gains a selective advantage, and its consequences rebuild the conditions that select such activity; within a specified state domain and continuing conditions, the existing correction does not suffice to reverse this continuation.** The returning relation explains self-maintenance, while actual consequences and the conditional alignment route explain degeneration; the two must each obtain their ground. High profit, concentration, a single exit or dissatisfaction do not by themselves complete the judgment. The general meta-attractor mechanism may equally maintain shared support and well-grounded correction.

Three kinds of boundary need to be separated: ethical comparison, applicability of the mechanism, and the retention/departure of a state domain. A change can cause real loss without forming a meta-attractor; a recursive organization can sustain benign creation; the same set of selection laws can also admit two state domains. "Meta-attractor" cannot be used to deny effective reform that has actually occurred, nor can the withdrawal of one practice be taken directly as the disappearance of the whole returning relation.

This round actually proved an exact boundary within the original market model. Let the strength of correction that is continuously authorized and paid for be \(\alpha\), the strength of erosion by influence \(\beta\), the upper bound on the influence target \(\lambda\), and the private selection threshold \(\theta\); define

\[
M=\alpha(1-\theta)-\beta\lambda\theta.
\]

Under the physical domain and renewal conditions stipulated by the model:

- For all \(\theta\le q\le1,\ 0\le w\le\lambda\), the selection threshold obtains protection at the next step if and only if \(M\ge0\); to cover an upper bound of adverse error \(\varepsilon\ge0\), if and only if \(M\ge\varepsilon\).
- The strictly low-selection rectangle certificate specified in the original mathematical study exists if and only if \(M<0\); its continuing execution projection stays in that region at any natural-number time, and the positive share of maintenance practice strictly falls under a positive selection rate.
- The negative-margin case at the original parameters still has a distinct benign protected region. So going beyond the parameter bound means that this degenerate region can appear, and it cannot be inferred that all histories necessarily enter it.

The [19 new Lean theorems / 27 declaration audits](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/boundary-verification.json) have actually passed, with the 39 imported-theorem audits listed separately. The [model scope](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/README.md#checked-regime-boundary) keeps the premises of current-period payment, authorization, actual support, correct correspondence, \(w\le\lambda\) and fixed parameters. It is not a necessary and sufficient condition for all forms of degeneration, nor a measured critical share of a platform.

Possible transitions should be studied separately: **a parameter change makes a region lose its condition of retention; a shock or cumulative change brings the state into another retainable region; a failure of funds, authority or support makes the original law of correction no longer executed; the speed of change makes correction come too late to be completed.** These routes may also in reverse promote recovery. A change in the existence of a region, a state's departure, and a strict mathematical bifurcation cannot be called by the same name as one proof of a phase transition.

The new practical meaning for alignment is to check how the execution of an aim changes the margin of correction and the future domain of selection. A stronger executing power, if it erodes the capacity for well-grounded revision, may enlarge the conditions under which degeneration is sustainable; repair should fall on the actual returning relations, substitute support and authority, and not only pursue replacing the organization or breaking the stability. Loss and the cost of reform are still compared by the existing practical principles.

This round completed the conditional boundary described and the proof within the domain, and does not reopen the normative direction. What should follow is to identify how real returning relations change with institutions, and to obtain the conditions of transition under a complete state domain, actual support and rates of change; from the cases that have occurred one cannot directly claim that the calibration of all parameters or the classification of stability is complete.

English: Ethical loss, reproduced selection and dynamical persistence have different boundaries. The existing paid-correction law now has an exact rational margin for uniform floor protection and for existence of the specified low-selection certificates. An all-time invariant projection is checked, while a distinct favorable region remains possible at the same parameters. Parameter change, state displacement, execution failure and excessive rate of change are different candidate transition routes. This is a conditional law with explicit correspondence, not inevitable degeneration, a full basin theorem or an empirically identified phase transition.

### Local attraction and actual support

2026-10-08. The [follow-up study and counter-review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#attraction-and-support-coupling) have carried the retention of the above regions to actual local attraction, and then connected it back to the original complete run and the support relations. The newly added [38 Lean theorems / 52 declaration audits](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/attraction-verification.json) pass; 58 imported theorems / 66 audits are listed separately and not counted again as new results.

On the parameter path of the original example that changes only the erosion strength \(\beta\), all physical equilibria have been classified. The maintenance end \(H=(1,1,0)\), the depletion end \(L_\beta=(0,1/(1+4\beta),1)\), and the interior point \(S_\beta=(1-5/(12\beta),3/8,5/(12\beta))\) that enters the physical domain only for \(\beta\ge5/12\), have definite relations of joining. At the section \(\beta=1/2\), **both end points have geometrically shrinking neighbourhoods; the interior point has arbitrarily small perturbations that eventually leave in finite time.** The proof of this round covers every positive rational precision and all later times, going beyond finite simulation and a mere invariant region.

This local two-attractor structure must still be understood within the scope of its representation. After the budget and the essential support are connected back to the same activity history, the proof of this round projects stepwise in agreement with the original law of operation, and obtains different actual outcomes:

- A high-end neighbourhood plus sufficient initial support satisfies the old protection certificate, and the actual run does not trigger the failure guard.
- A low-end neighbourhood, even when money is available at every step to pay for the specified correction, exhausts the essential support of the original model in finite time and triggers an actual stop.

So **a selection organization that can absorb perturbations back into itself may also continue to erode the shared generative conditions on which it depends.** Its attractiveness does not by itself prove that the whole is sustainable, still less that it is ethically legitimate. Support is not understood here as an external object independent of the activity; what the three-coordinate projection provisionally omits is precisely the support relations in the same activity history.

The kinds of correction should therefore be further distinguished: a state improvement that still remains in the low-end attracting neighbourhood will be drawn back by the original relations; changing the returning law may change that neighbourhood itself; a participating capacity already lost further needs real entry and rebuilding. The original share law has no mechanism of entry, and a zero share cannot regenerate by changing the erosion parameter alone. This is a representational limit of the local model, and cannot be taken as a proof of irreversibility in reality.

The direction of alignment obtains a new requirement of timing here: according to the existing practical principles, appraise whether correction can change subsequent selection, and also, before it is complete, who still has support, who bears the loss, and whether a feasible continuation exists. Attractiveness, efficiency, growth and "resilience" cannot replace this comparison.

The local three-state structure and the support connection are complete; the [branch diagram of equilibria](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/figures/regime-fixed-states.png) merely marks the proved scope. The complete three-dimensional basin of attraction, extension to the real domain, the type of stability/strict bifurcation for all parameters, and real-world thresholds are not complete. The next connection is how supported entry and sustained correction can cross the boundary of the state domain before actual support fails; the question of rate should be placed in this actual timing, reusing the completed boundary and attraction proofs.

English: Two endpoint neighborhoods now have checked local attraction in the original rational operating projection, while the interior state admits arbitrarily small perturbations with finite escape. Coupling the original essential support reveals finite guarded failure on the low side and certificate-backed nonfailure on the high side. Reproducing a selection organization can undermine the wider conditions of continuation. The next connection is supported entry and sustained change before those conditions fail, not another proof of the completed local mechanism.

### A supported finite transition

2026-10-08. [This round's argument and counter-review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/foundation-assessment/MARKET-INTERNET-DEGENERATION.md#supported-finite-transition) closed one concrete connection of the sustained correction above: **so long as the maintenance activity still has positive margin, actually changing the selection relation can bring the state into the original protected region in finite time; support must run through this transition.** [27 new Lean theorems / 35 audits](../../../experiments/market-generativity/formal/transition-verification.json) pass, with 96 inherited theorems / 118 audits listed separately.

In this example, the erosion parameter β is changed from 1/2 to 0 for a finite stage. The maintenance share x, the efficacy of responsibility q and the cumulative influence w all continue to evolve by their original relations and are not reset. For every rational physical initial state x>0, there is a finite N such that it enters the existing protected region x≥3/4, q≥1/2, w≤1/4. This includes initial states with positive share in the original low attracting neighbourhood. Afterwards β=1/2 is restored, and the original certificate can go on protecting; this round does not need first to find the complete three-dimensional separating surface.

The extra correction consumes at each step κ≥0 represented units of support, and the original monetary correction budget is still executed as pay first, receive income after. Given the above N, a conservative sufficient reserve that has been proved is

\[
C_0\ge K_N=(15+10\kappa)(10/9)^N-10(1+\kappa).
\]

The actual guard of the same complete state allows all N steps, and, when the extra correction is withdrawn, hands the remaining support directly to the original protection law, after which no guard failure occurs. This bound does not draw in advance on the gains produced by maintenance, and therefore does not claim to be cost-minimal; the initial support is given in the local model, and how to obtain it still needs a real-world account. The [result record](../results/records/market-supported-finite-transition.md) makes explicit the [original protection result](../results/records/market-protected-continuation.md) reused and its formal connection.

**Three boundaries are kept at once.** At x=0, the original share law will not produce new maintainers by changing β alone; a real entry must be provided otherwise. For any preset duration N, there is a positive but sufficiently small initial maintenance share that has still not entered the protected region at expiry, so no uniformly sufficient finite duration of intervention exists.
