## Begin within the one unfolding

Understand the core (named the generative core when this method was written) and the tree as differentiated articulations of one unfolding. The core is articulated through the formation, interaction and transformation investigated in the branches. The branches can disclose limits in our current articulation of the core and require its revision. A diagram with a root and descendants is an explanatory aid; it does not establish a finished independent foundation with externally attached applications.

The working starting point is activity forming and changing the conditions of further activity. Its effects can persist or change, becoming conditions of what follows. Within this unfolding, relatively enduring organizations can form; some develop ways of sensing, remembering, acting and evaluating. Investigate how these distinctions arise, how their consequences remain connected, and how those consequences can change the organization of further activity. Knowing, modeling, formalizing and criticizing also occur within this unfolding; their adequacy is part of the inquiry.

Here **activity** includes change and ongoing interaction without presupposing an agent, intention or purpose. **Conditions** are the relations and circumstances that enable or constrain what follows; they need not be quantities that can be stored or exchanged. **Formation** concerns the forming and transformation of those conditions, generative and degenerative alike. **Generative** is narrower: it names a change that widens what can form, and generation and degeneration do not overlap, so "generative" never means any new organization ([the canonical chapter](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md), glossary). Whether a particular development sustains life, enables learning or causes harm requires further explanation.

Differentiation is real work for the account to explain. Nonduality does not erase differences in experience, history, access, control, consent or consequences. Nor does a shared process imply one cosmic subject, one aggregate interest or an administrator entitled to speak for everything. The [existing core reconstruction](../history/DEEPER-GENERATIVE-ROOT.md#10-nondual-core-and-derived-positions) supplies a compatible architectural entry; the [generated-self account](../history/GENERATED-SELF-AND-ETHICS.md) investigates particular formed perspectives.

The [earlier nondual integration](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/NONDUAL-CORE-INTEGRATION.md) already records that the original generative core did not contain primitive Actor, Patient or Owner fields, and develops conservative role descriptions and conditional formation of changing effective ranges. This method does not claim to have removed a dualist axiom from that core or to have introduced nonduality for the first time. Its addition is project-wide practice and reciprocal core/tree articulation.

**Goodness beyond return to an individual.** [Ren's correction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-goodness-without-return-to-self.md) directs the ethical inquiry toward actual good and the conditions through which it generates further good. Do not make return to an initiating individual, subgroup or enlarged self the foundation's test. Shared private stakes can explain one practical route, but insulation from those stakes does not erase goodness elsewhere. Follow effects into the lives and conditions they actually change, including after the initiator ceases participating. Test propagation through actual benefits, burdens and opportunities; neither mere reproduction nor a promised future payoff establishes ethical direction. This restores the existing [relational account](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/RELATIONAL-GOODNESS-ARGUMENT.md), without claiming inevitable favorable dynamics or completed universal necessity.

**Whole-process ethical grounding.** [Ren's next-goal correction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-whole-process-ethical-goal.md) makes the whole unfolding the grounding question. Treat subjects, objects, interests and evaluative standards as differentiated formations within it. A controller's benefit can explain selection without establishing whole goodness or an automatically valid counterclaim. Preserve actual differences and conflicts; the whole is neither a larger owner nor a scalar total. Distinguish consequence inclusion, joint feasibility and the substantive ethical appraisal of the resulting configuration. The [current reconstruction](../history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#the-whole-as-the-grounding-question) develops this direction without declaring the ethical derivation complete.

## Keep foundational language at the right level

### Formation, participation and responsibility

[Ren's terminology correction, 7 October 2026](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-generative-language-and-formation.md), requires naming the relations previously compressed into “authorship.” Begin with **the formation and transformation of the conditions of further activity / 后续活动条件的形成与改变**. The activities, their capacities and their differentiated positions form within that unfolding. An independent originating author is not a generative primitive.

For the support comparison, name **the formation and transformation of dependence / 依存关系的形成与改变**: which activities sustain support, displace alternatives, invite reliance or change possibilities of continuation. Joint formation can include unequal contributions, unequal control and different consequences. It implies neither an isolated sole cause nor equal responsibility for everything.

When the question concerns effective choice, use **participation in decisions**, **joint participation**, or **self-direction**, identifying the actual capacity and its formation. These descriptions do not themselves confer authority, permission or a right. When the question concerns responsibility, explain why the identified relationship warrants that responsibility, its scope and its possible discharge. Historical contribution alone does not prove responsibility; absence of that contribution alone does not prove absence of every reason to respond.

**先说明什么关系怎样形成、怎样持续起作用，再说明其伦理意义。** Avoid letting one term silently mean causal contribution, practical influence and justified responsibility at once. This separates explanatory questions within one process; it does not place facts and values in different realities.

Apply the same discipline to other shorthand. In a local mechanism, name the resource consumed or required, the operation and the specified amount. “Resource-dependent” in a summary refers to that declared local requirement, not a universal positive cost or a definition of learning. Retain money and payment for actual economic relations. Use actor, recipient or patient for a stated differentiated role in a particular history, explaining how it forms; none names a fundamentally independent or necessarily passive kind. Documentary authorship, source quotations, attributed terminology and stable code identifiers retain their ordinary provenance roles.

**Whole as unit of grounding, renewed 6 October 2026.** [Ren repeats that the inquiry concerns the whole](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-whole-as-grounding-unit.md). Do not make independent intrinsic value for each selected relationship a mandatory gate to whole-process appraisal. Investigate differentiated lives, purposes, conditions and actual realizations through the continuation they form together. A representation that merely moves an analytical boundary while preserving that history must preserve its whole appraisal. This methodological constraint does not itself establish the proposed ethical ordering; the [current candidate](../history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#the-whole-is-the-unit-of-grounding) states the substantive identification to investigate.

**The whole includes genuine self-good, clarified 7 October 2026.** [Ren explicitly includes doing good to oneself](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-self-good-within-the-whole.md). Self-care need not produce a separate outward benefit, receive public approval or justify itself by later productivity before it counts within the whole. This does not reinstall independently owned private values as the foundation. Distinguish actual ethical inclusion from overall comparisons involving other effects and relationships, without using that distinction to erase the acting life's good. The [integrated account](../history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#self-care-is-a-realization-within-the-whole) states the substantive scope.

**Future-of-the-whole criterion.** [Ren's later clarification](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-future-whole-generativity-criterion.md) directs the ethical comparison toward generation or degeneration of the future of the whole, expressly correcting a reference to the entity. Emotional valence and entity preservation do not independently set this criterion. Investigate the changed shared conditions and actual realizations of continuations; preserve differentiated effects without making an affective C1 derivation the foundation's mandatory first step. More activity, options or reproduction is not already a warranted ethical identification. The [canonical revision](../history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#the-future-of-the-whole-is-the-criterion) retains earlier local results and the stronger unfinished goal.

[Ren's terminology correction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-nondual-core-tree-continuation.md#subsequent-wording-correction-6-october-2026) rejects “付费记录” as too market-like and insufficiently basic. Begin with activity forming, maintaining or changing the conditions of subsequent activity; some effects continue and participate in later activity. This does not yet posit a separate recorder, a representation or a subject remembering an object. Explain the specific relations that constitute retention, memory or a record where those descriptions apply.

Use “记录的形成与更新” / “record formation and updating” when describing the formed learner. Its selected write rule consumes one fuel unit; that is a local mechanism detail, not the meaning of recording. Retention may involve maintenance, decay or transformation, whose conditions must be explained in the relevant process. Do not promote payment, a fixed resource budget or a universally positive cost into a generative primitive. Existing names such as `paid`, `rent` and `lease` remain traceable local-model vocabulary; their presence in source code does not establish market organization at the foundation.

[Ren further questions “recipient, money, pay”](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-foundational-vocabulary-correction.md) in the foundational account. Begin with activity and its changing relations and effects, rather than a preassigned giver and passive receiver or an exchange between already constituted owners. Explain how acting and undergoing differentiate within particular histories; the same organized activity can participate in both. Preserve real differences in experience and consequence when those distinctions are warranted. “Participant” or “affected process” can be useful local descriptions, but replacing the word alone does not explain the formation of its referent.

Money, payment, debt, refund and scalar accounting belong to particular realizations. Where a model only specifies depletion or restoration of a quantity, name that operation and quantity without implying monetary exchange. More generally, describe the conditions that activity maintains, changes, consumes, enables or interrupts. Do not silently assume that all conditions are owned, transferable, additive, scarce or commensurable. The foundation must earn any more specific account through derivation or evidence; this language correction does not itself establish such an account.

[Ren's instruction to revise the wording](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-06-generative-language-revision.md) is applied through the following descriptive choices. These are AI-authored formulations, not quotations or a new list of foundational entities.

| What the account is explaining | Wording appropriate to that explanation |
|---|---|
| How something changes what follows | **Activity and its consequences / 活动及其后果**; identify the relations through which the effects continue. |
| What enables or constrains continuation | **Conditions of further activity / 后续活动的条件**; name a material stock, access relation or other specific condition only where identified. |
| How relatively enduring patterns form | **Formation and transformation of organization / 组织的形成与转化**; explain recurrence, maintenance and changing boundaries. |
| Which part acts or undergoes an effect | Describe the **differentiated relations / 分化的关系** within the history; use actor, recipient or patient for a particular established role. |
| How earlier effects participate in later activity | Begin with **continuing effects / 持续起作用的后果**; use retention, memory or record formation when the relevant mechanism is specified. |
| How a specified support quantity changes | Name **depletion, replenishment or restoration / 消耗、补充或恢复**, with the actual quantity and operation. Use payment for an actual payment. |
| How continued activity changes its own organization | Describe **maintenance, repair, learning or revision / 维持、修复、学习或修正** according to the mechanism demonstrated. None follows from persistence alone. |

Keep process, theory and model distinct: the process unfolds; the theory seeks to explain it; a mathematical model represents selected relations, and an internally formed model can itself participate in activity. A description can use one lens without making that lens the whole foundation. State what it preserves and what it leaves unresolved.

## Make analytical separations accountable

**Whole continuation, not selected persistence, 7 October 2026.** [Ren corrects the use of a persistence example](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-07-whole-continuation-counterexample-scope.md): the question is whether ending an activity can improve the whole continuation. An activity's remaining realizable after another becomes impossible does not answer that question. The existing `severing` model contains no primitive independent subjects; the overreach lies in using its narrow result to assess a richer whole-quality claim. Retain the theorem and assess the omitted relationships before classifying the example as an ethical counterexample. The [canonical comparison](../history/UNIFIED-ETHICAL-GROUNDING.md#ending-an-activity-and-the-quality-of-the-whole-continuation) permits constructive endings without assuming their warrant.

Before treating two terms as independently supplied primitives, reconstruct the relationship the proposal attributes to them. This applies to core/tree, subject/object, actor/patient, observer/world, knowing/acting and experience/significance. Separate mathematical types, local resource accounts or distinct investigative questions do not by themselves assert fundamental separation. Conversely, common notation does not establish a constitutive connection.

For a decomposition, explain what it preserves, what it omits, why it is appropriate to the question and how the differentiated terms remain related in actual activity. Do not begin with a neutral object plus an external meaning-giver or evaluator when their formation and relationship are precisely what the theory proposes to explain. Study the whole relevant configuration before isolating its components.

The term "supplied" is local to an explanation. A law or boundary supplied to a particular model need not be external to reality. Its provenance remains a research question; identify whether the limitation lies in this model, in a missing connected explanation, or in the proposed foundation itself. Likewise, a definition can attempt to articulate a constitutive structure, an interpretation can concern a generated representation's actual relationship to its conditions, and a proof system can be investigated as an organized practice within the account. None of these descriptions alone settles their adequacy.

Do not require occurrence to be derived from an initially inert distinction space merely because that was an earlier formal starting point. Preserve the existing underdetermination results at their declared scope and assess the positive starting point on its own stated content. Under the [current conditional-universality target](../history/GENERATIVE-GROUNDING.md#minimal-grounding-and-universal-scope), independently specified law properties support claims about all admitted histories. A uniquely necessary ontology is a separate question. Neither moving content into definitions nor calling it immanent discharges an explanatory dependency.

## Test constitutive connections substantively

Ren endorses challenging a proposed connection by preserving the relevant experiential or formative condition while contesting its alleged significance, or by showing that the proposed identification misdescribes cases. Apply this beyond the suffering example to the relationships investigated throughout the tree.

1. State the constitutive claim and its scope before testing. Specify the phenomenon through independently assessable features; do not include the desired conclusion in its description.
2. Reconstruct the strongest alternative account of those features. Explain what it preserves and where its account of the relationship differs.
3. Distinguish a change in the actual condition from a change in its report, label, proxy or detached evaluation. A relevant counterexample must satisfy the claim's stated conditions; it need not duplicate every fact in the universe.
4. Explain the relevance of a formal countermodel. Freely toggling a predicate can establish non-entailment from a weak premise set. It does not establish that the fuller proposed structure permits that toggle. Equally, declaring a constitutive identity does not establish that it is true.
5. Specify what would narrow, revise or defeat the claim. Do not expand "full condition" after each objection or make an alleged local cost undetectable by every possible challenge.

For example, whether felt aversiveness constitutes an adverse aspect of undergoing is a different question from whether a particular response is warranted overall. Investigate their relationships within the situation, preserving actual effects, permissions and competing consequences. Neither a subject's endorsement nor an analyst's score should silently supply the conclusion.

## Use proofs, evidence and philosophical comparison within this method

Formal proofs check particular relations under an explicit representation. Empirical investigation tests realization, correspondence and competing explanations. Philosophical inquiry can investigate constitutive identities, explanatory adequacy and the formation of the very distinctions in use. Assess what each method actually establishes and whether it faithfully preserves the target. No cultural origin automatically validates or disqualifies a method.

A branch report or foundational criticism should state the configuration being explained, the status of its differentiations, the scope preserved by the selected model, and a substantive condition of revision. When identifying a gap, say whether it is an omitted relation in the formalization, an unresolved explanation within the reconstructed account, or evidence against a specified claim. These are methodological distinctions within the inquiry, not separate ontological realms.

## Worked continuation: knowledge, action and support in one history

The [existing acquired-perspective history](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/NONDUAL-TREE-MECHANISM.md) provides a concrete application. Its actual work event produces a receipt that participates in both the resource update and a retained, action-tagged input for later writing. The record update changes the retained score, available fuel, phase and future command together; the selected local rule consumes one fuel unit. The subsequent work acts through that command and the current circumstances; actual success replenishes support. These are dependent aspects of one established history, not three already complete ontological modules joined by an outside meaning-giver.

The order matters. In the corrective suffix, a failure receipt changes the next command while preserving which earlier action actually failed. A later successful work event supplies the replenishment. Treating these as one simultaneous success would erase the learning history. Different analyses may select records, resources or future possibilities, but they must retain the relations relevant to their claims. The prior studies already established this local connection; this continuation does not invent an earlier denial in order to claim a new discovery.

This also lets the tree constrain the core's articulation. Event-word feasibility alone omits which action was issued and what endpoint it produced; the existing outcome-sensitive interface retains those differences. Branch findings identify what a proposed abstraction must preserve. They do not establish a complete world description merely because that interface suffices for the selected question.

For concern, apply the same discipline to actual undergoing, transformation and responsive activity. A report can disappear while its target persists; a change in understanding can also genuinely change the experience. The latter cannot be dismissed as a mere label just because it is described on the cognitive side. Identify what actually changed before judging whether the case supports relief, masking or another relation. The [genealogy and proposed tests](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/NONDUAL-TREE-GENEALOGY.md) return to Ren's original feedback formulations and distinguish them from AI elaboration and inherited classical comparisons.

The next concrete join remains **formed, reorganizable effective ranges → acquired distinctions of participation and consequence → changes in those ranges' continuation**. The previous range construction and this material learner do not yet constitute one physical history. That is a specific composition obligation; it is different from a general claim that knowing and activity occupy separate realms. [Independent review](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/NONDUAL-TREE-REVIEW.md) preserves that distinction and the remaining substantive grounding work.
