## 1. Premises and their roles

Living significance recognizes living and further generation within the whole as basically favorable. Current life, rest, self-care and finite fulfillment count without first serving later production. Biological/ecological, mental/relational and broader organizational ranges remain distinguishable. The following commitments make living significance practically more determinate; **they are proposed additional normative commitments, not logical consequences of the bare sentence of living significance or newly attributed instructions from Ren.**

| Item | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Living significance | Living and further generation have positive significance | Follows under the continuation condition (canonical chapter §6.4); not assumed |
| Full comparison | Genuine improvement without countervailing loss has comparative favor; restoration does not erase history | Explicit comparative commitment, continuing C |
| Priority of basic conditions | Preserving other basic claims, restoring a threatened basic condition takes priority over optional enrichment; optional enrichment cannot license imposed basic deprivation | Additional scoped comparison commitment, not living significance alone |
| Bounded responsibility | Justified undertakings and accountable changes of dependence ground fulfillment, repair or bounded transition duties; control and capacity constrain their form and cumulative burden | Additional responsibility commitment, not a causal definition |
| Practical selection | Choose authorized, jointly feasible, undefeated plans; use a justified, bounded procedure for unresolved alternatives when a timely choice is needed | Explicit selection, permission and allocation commitment |
| Correspondence | Descriptions of life, loss, basic conditions, feasibility, authority, evidence and discharge are faithful | Factual and normative application obligations |
| Formal consequences | Conditional implications for comparison, finite alternatives, collective requirements, allocation, transfer and discharge | Kernel-checked implications, not warrant for the premises |

These are analytical distinctions within one unfolding. Training, support and coordination form capacities; actual effects differentiate affected positions; inquiry and rules participate in changing later conditions. The formal model takes these formed roles as local parameters. It tests their stated relations without purporting to reconstruct their entire genesis.

## 2. A substantive mixed comparison

Basic-condition priority treats the conditions of living and further formation as realizations within living significance, not merely consumable inputs to extra output. It rejects granting optional enrichment an unlimited claim over those conditions. This supports a restricted priority for restoration, while retaining losses that remain below the independently defended protection boundary. This is an argument for adopting basic-condition priority, not a proof that every view compatible with living significance must adopt it.

**Classify basic conditions before choosing the winner.** Identify threatened life, serious impairment, minimally effective participation/self-care, or a critical ecological continuation, together with intensity, duration, reversibility, alternatives and dependencies. Institutional persistence and added complexity do not qualify merely by being described as essential. Unique farewells, coercion, exclusion and cumulative exhaustion cannot be silently classified as optional. Operational thresholds require independent calibration; no universal exchange rate is supplied here.

Compare two layers of the whole history: indexed basic claims protected, then other realizations, enrichments and losses. Within the scope of basic-condition priority, retaining every protection of B and restoring another favors A despite disclosed secondary losses. With equivalent protections, apply full comparison. Different sacrificed basic claims, or crossed secondary gains, can remain incomparable. A protection predicate must preserve its independently specified content; material degrees of impairment require a richer description. This is a sufficient partial ordering, not a complete scalar ranking.

**Exact strength: a scoped lexical commitment.** Where the independent classification holds, this candidate gives basic protection strict priority over the secondary layer: restoration wins even against very many genuinely secondary losses, provided all other basic protections remain unchanged. Rejecting unlimited sacrifice alone does **not** entail this stronger claim. This commitment is adopted explicitly instead of concealing it in the meaning of “basic.” Secondary losses still supply reasons for mitigation, sharing and repair and can determine comparisons at equivalent protection. The commitment denies that accumulation of such realization purchases the loss of another basic living condition.

The affirmative defense is non-substitution: the basic layer concerns an irreplaceable standing for a particular form of living to continue, while secondary expansion occurs with such standing retained elsewhere. Protecting that participation in the whole is an explicit interpretation of the shared direction. A strong rival can accept these relations and still favor sufficiently extensive, genuinely below-boundary losses over one basic restoration. We have not shown that rival inconsistent. Basic-condition priority is a contestable ethical commitment under living significance, with the stated cost of rejecting cross-layer compensation. We cannot evade the rival by reclassifying its losses after the fact. If further judgment favors it, retract or narrow basic-condition priority and reassess dependent verdicts; the separate full-comparison, allocation, handoff, discharge and general finite-menu results can survive. The completed candidate openly bears this commitment; it is not the unique necessary answer of every theory that accepts living significance.

**Mixed anchor.** A reschedulable ordinary meeting conflicts with the last timely repair opportunity. Repair actually restores basic conditions of living and shared activity but sacrifices that meeting; holding it loses those conditions. With feasible, authorized, bounded repair and no other basic deprivation, basic-condition priority favors repair and practical selection excludes the defeated alternative. The missed meeting remains a loss. A final farewell, serious displaced harm, or an empty repaired facility changes the premises. The sufficient comparison is stronger than the earlier defeasible K because it explicitly adds basic-condition priority.

**Sacrifice boundary.** Fixed optional enrichment cannot demand arbitrary imposed deterioration. Once burdens undermine basic living conditions, the earlier priority argument fails and a protective alternative may defeat it. This is a substantive limit, not a numerical bound derived from living significance. Competing basic losses require the conflict procedure below. Below-boundary losses still require comparison and fair allocation; being below a ceiling is not itself permission.

### 2.1 Why support acquires a reason: continuing together when needed

13 October 2026. An application argument written by AI; it reuses the conditional alignment route and the existing dynamics proofs and adds no axiom, mathematical theorem or independent result. See [the original study §85](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ETHICAL-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md#timely-continuation-and-support).

This section reuses the completed conditional alignment route. In the current structure living significance follows under [the continuation condition](THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md#64-the-continuation-condition); it is not assumed, and no second axiom is added.

Begin with activities changing the conditions of subsequent activity, including the support needed by the activities that maintain those conditions. New support can form and old support can fail. A life, its needs, its supporting activities and positions capable of changing them are differentiated within that unfolding. Support matters through this actual relation of formation, without first assigning separate values to independent owners.

**Affirmative argument.** Living significance gives living and further generation positive significance. Where their realization depends on common support, maintaining or forming effective support participates in making that realization actual. The favorable reason therefore extends to support, repair or an adequate replacement. This explains a concrete practical reason within the conditional alignment route. Causation supplies its actual connection; a causal proposition alone establishes neither normative significance nor permission for every means. Damage caused by the supporting arrangement enters the same judgment.

The reason concerns **actual continuation**, not the perpetual survival of a particular condition, institution or role. Where several routes can sustain it, compare their whole effects rather than preserve every old route. A replacement that is not yet available, or supports only the changing formation while abandoning the living relations previously supported together, does not establish adequate succession. Forming stronger common capacities can be the better response; size or concentration alone settles nothing.

Apply them by stating the following in turn. This is the corresponding requirement for “preserving”, “restoring” and “substituting”, not a checklist that adds points to a proposal.

For application, establish the following content. These are correspondence requirements for preservation, recovery and replacement, not a scoring checklist.

| Content | What must be established |
|---|---|
| What continues | Actual living, rest, self-care, formation or finite fulfillment, with what is retained, changed and lost; organizational survival is not a substitute. |
| When support is needed | When need arises, tolerable interruptions and actual reserves; timing comes from the changing relations, not a deadline selected to choose a winner. |
| Continuation through transition | How support persists until recovery; a gap without demand can be harmless, while later recovery does not erase loss during actual need. |
| Joint realization and replacement | Whether relevant living relations can continue together, what supports the replacement and whether interruption is displaced elsewhere; separate possible routes do not establish joint feasibility. |

Existing checks support these distinctions. [Return grades](../results/records/becoming-return-grades.md) supplies a course that always recovers without any uniform recovery period; it does not show that each recovery misses an actual need, whose timing must still be established. [Consumption under substitution](../results/records/substitution-preserves-consumption.md) supplies a course where the focal formation remains active after changing its dependence while the sustainer permanently loses support. It excludes certifying common preservation by focal persistence, not all substitution. The two witnesses retain their original model scopes and are not a joint proof of a complete transition.

**How comparison follows.** Full comparison applies where a route retains the other route's relevant living realizations and adds capacity for further formation, with all relevant transition losses included and no countervailing loss. Basic-condition priority applies where repair protects an additional basic living condition, retains all other basic protections and sacrifices only genuinely optional enrichment. Where each route loses different basic living relations, dependence alone still supplies no exchange rate. An actual need can defeat a claim that later restoration is equivalent; urgency alone does not establish overriding priority.

The alignment direction can therefore be stated concretely: **support living and further generation within the whole by forming, sustaining and improving the actual relations through which lives can continue together, recover, transform and reach fitting completion; revise ends and actions through their real gains and losses.** This supports creating capacities and changing inherited forms. Present life need not earn its standing through future production: rest and finite fulfillment remain within living significance. Nor should one living realization be counted repeatedly merely because several supporting relations contribute to it.

<a id="3-四种回应及其转变条件--four-responses"></a>
## 3. Four responses

| Response | Grounds | What changes the judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Preserve | Existing relations sustain living; alteration lacks sufficient advantage or causes avoidable loss | Failure, domination, unsustainable burdens or a better real alternative |
| Transform | The arrangement fails or damages shared conditions; feasible change restores living or improves realization with equivalent protections | Mere output growth, displaced deprivation, failed restoration or a less damaging alternative |
| Transfer | Need remains warranted, exit is justified, and an accepted successor has capacity, information and support in time for demand | Nominal succession, vacancy, overload, imposed service or a gap during actual need |
| End | Fulfillment, justified release, loss of the target's warrant or unjustifiable continuation burdens, with appropriate response to residual losses and dependence | Mere renaming, deleted records, changed preference or hidden outstanding undertakings |

Responses can combine: preserve living through institutional transformation and transfer that ends an old role, or permit finite fulfillment with no productive successor. Generativity requires neither every organization to persist nor every good life to produce more life. Necessary conditions explain an already warranted continuation; they do not make it perpetual. When continuation is impossible, feasible responsibilities concern honest acknowledgment, relief, appropriate closure and remaining repair. Failure to save is not fulfillment of saving.

A no-demand gap may permit withdrawal before replacement; unsupported actual demand defeats a claim of complete transfer. Successful transfer can discharge an old service duty while leaving responsibility for earlier harm intact. Permission to exit and successful continuation are distinct judgments.

## 4. Allocating responsibilities

Bounded responsibility is grounded in reliable coordination and the inclusion of contributors' own lives. Inviting justified reliance, displacing alternatives or undertaking a role changes others' actual possibilities. Automatically externalizing the resulting burden omits those consequences from the same generative relation. Unlimited liability for every causal connection would instead erase contributors and their other relations. Responsibilities therefore specify fulfillment, repair, coordination or transition, with capacity limits and discharge conditions.

| Relational ground | Presumptive responsibility | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Justified undertaking or shared arrangement | Fulfill within scope, give timely notice and arrange appropriate succession | Reliance does not validate an unjust undertaking; fulfillment, release or justified renegotiation can end it |
| Accountable creation of harm/dependence or removal of alternatives | Stop aggravation, repair or support transition, account for the change | Causation is not automatically fault; manufactured dependency does not authorize continued control |
| Control of critical shared conditions | Avoid arbitrary obstruction, maintain conditions or enable justified alternatives, explain restrictions | Control gives no self-authorizing sovereignty; title does not ensure capacity |
| Timely, effective, bounded emergency capacity | Assist or summon support when serious harm is preventable without comparable harm and no timely alternative exists | An explicit bounded-responsibility rescue commitment, not capability alone or unlimited sacrifice |
| Benefit with capacity for shared provision | Contribute to maintenance, repair or institutional reconstruction under a justified shared arrangement | Benefit alone sets no debt amount; unsolicited benefits cannot manufacture arbitrary debt |
| Affected participation and relevant knowledge | Standing to be heard, challenge and participate in relevant decisions | No duty of total disclosure or unlimited veto over others |

Identify the collective task, then test one jointly feasible allocation: time, support, skills, existing duties, self-care and actual consent. Separate per-task feasibility is insufficient. Undertakers have presumptive fulfillment/coordination responsibilities; accountable contributors have presumptive repair responsibilities; capable others may have temporary emergency responsibilities without inheriting perpetual service. Those managing shared provision must organize fair burdens. Immediate response need not await a final account of fault; later repair and redistribution remain due where justified.

**A collective task need not bind every individual.** Alternative legitimate allocations preserve latitude. A particular contribution is indispensable only when every admissible allocation requires it. Once an authorized, feasible and fair allocation is settled, its participants must fulfill assigned undertakings or promptly seek justified revision; prior alternatives do not erase a settled commitment. If all allocations overload someone, revise goals, timing, support or allocation and acknowledge unmet claims. An empty plan set cannot generate unlimited duties by vacuous logic.

## 5. Conflict and uncertainty

A general account must address cases where comparison does not yield a unique answer. Incomparability is neither equality nor unrestricted permission. Practical selection adds the following procedural commitments; it does not supply unrestricted coercive authority.

1. **Form whole feasible plans**, including combinations and timely inquiry. Inaction and delay have consequences; state the decision window and omitted scope.
2. **Establish constraints independently.** Include authority, protection, self-care and cumulative burden. If jointly impossible, acknowledge conflicts, urgency and reducible infringements; seek support or rescheduling. Emergency authority remains limited to necessary scope.
3. **Exclude sufficiently defeated plans.** Use full comparison and basic-condition priority with fixed meanings. Distinct basic sacrifices are not resolved by counting output or complexity. Severity, urgency, preventability and undertakings may support further disclosed argument; they are not unexamined winner labels.
4. **If unresolved and time permits, investigate decision-relevant uncertainty**, including the inquiry's costs and delays. A smaller belief set is not better if it excludes actuality.
5. **When a decision is due, use existing legitimate authority or establish a shared procedure.** Allow affected participation and challenge, disclose reasons and conflicts, prefer corrigible action without concentrating grave irreversible burdens on excluded positions. Rotation or a public lottery is permissible for otherwise unresolved fairly allocable chances; it does not equate unequal harms. Urgent protective decisions require narrow scope and subsequent review, not permanent control.
6. **Retain residual responsibilities and reopening conditions.** A permissible choice leaves real losses; specify monitoring, repair, further support and evidence that would reopen judgment.

The account gives definite kinds of practical conclusion: sufficient priority, procedural latitude among alternatives, warranted inquiry, a provisional urgent decision, or acknowledged joint infeasibility. It does not promise a unique verdict in every real case. Lean verifies an undefeated alternative for any finite nonempty menu under the stated strict transitive comparison. Infinite improvement chains may lack a final optimum. Formal existence does not certify authorization or correspondence.

## 6. Changed purposes, learning and discharge

Ends and their revision form within the same history. Correcting means may preserve an end; informed refusal may warrant ending intervention; a superseded end may remove a future production requirement. New preferences or names do not erase justified reliance, undertakings or actual harm. Conversely, coercive roles, invalid promises and unjustified targets do not gain permanence from their past existence.

Discharge requires actual fulfillment, effective transfer, justified release/revision or appropriate closure. Release needs standing and warranted procedure; it is not an arbitrary flag. Fulfilled service no longer has the same outstanding ground, while earlier unrepaired harm can support a distinct responsibility. Ending demand cannot be overridden by an injunction to keep producing forever; finite fulfillment and terminal peace can themselves realize living within living significance.

Faithful redescription preserves requirements when admissible alternatives, task meanings and relevant relations are preserved. Actual changes in feasibility, dependence, permission or ends require reassessment. Lean checks the former conditional result, not the fidelity of arbitrary learning or self-modification. Normative validity, motivation and compliance remain distinct across intelligences.

<a id="7-有内容的反对及边界--substantive-rivals-and-limits"></a>
## 7. Substantive rivals and limits

**Unlimited continuation priority.** A rival can acknowledge present losses while giving recurrence priority over every finite sacrifice. Living significance alone does not refute it. Basic-condition priority explicitly rejects that priority for optional continuation; future basic losses must instead be included among basic claims and not erased because they are distant.

**Rigid thresholds.** Living differences do not become valuable only at a cutoff. Thresholds can be manipulated. Preserve continuous differences and uncertainty, classify independently and allow challenge. An unsupported classification cannot authorize the strong verdict of basic-condition priority. This is an application limit, not a license to reclassify counterexamples until a favored answer wins.

**Productive domination.** Coercion can genuinely create organization; those gains are not redefined out of 生. Basic-condition priority, bounded responsibility and practical selection separately assess deprivation, participation and authority. Favorable effects do not confer unlimited permission; limited protective intervention requires its own grounds rather than categorical approval or rejection of “control.”

**Irreplaceable care.** Dependence and the contributor's life both remain real. Acknowledge conflict, seek shared support, revise scope and retain losses. Neither unlimited service nor automatic discharge follows. Principles constrain process, burden and repair without inventing a lossless outcome.

Repeated concealment of more serious losses under a “basic” classification defeats that application and can require revision of the principle. Persistent exclusion or burden concentration defeats procedural legitimacy. Output without the claimed restored living defeats correspondence. No exclusive necessity of this vocabulary is asserted.
