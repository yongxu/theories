Judgment forms within the same unfolding as capacities, understanding and affected living, and participates in changing their further conditions. The required result must explain why these actual relations have practical force and how that force differentiates comparison, preservation, transformation, transfer, ending and responsibilities. Formation of a judgment and preservation of its effects do not by themselves establish its warrant.

Formal background, domain and actual correspondence remain visible dependencies. Neither separate representations nor a failed local entailment establishes a fundamental fact/value dualism.

## 3. New test of basic-condition priority

The restricted test keeps one history description: one protection status and a count of specified secondary realizations. The count is not universal welfare. One rule gives protection lexical priority; another gives it a positive finite weight while favoring each secondary realization. Both retain the described outcomes and can be transitive and positive in each coordinate. The two rules are openly specified rival interpretations, not evidence that either is ethically correct.

**New checked result:** every finite sample admits a positive finite weight agreeing with the lexical rule on every pair in that sample, yet reversing a comparison outside it. If the maximum secondary count is M, weight M+1 agrees on the bounded domain; a case with M+2 secondary realizations exposes the disagreement. No fixed finite weight agrees globally. The quantifier order matters.

Thus finite agreement, coherent ordering and retained descriptions do not identify the unrestricted priority of basic-condition priority. This does not disprove it, make empirical evidence irrelevant or refute a fuller relational constitution omitted from the model. A successful derivation must identify the substantive relation excluding finite compensation. It cannot simply appeal to favorable samples or retrospectively reclassify the rival's secondary losses.

## 4. Responsibility and symmetry

In a history with exactly symmetric relevant dependence, capacity, undertakings, control and burdens, task need and feasibility do not uniquely distinguish one position. A swap-invariant assignment cannot select exactly one of two roles. Actual undertakings, earlier rotations, another relevant asymmetry or a warranted selection procedure can differentiate the result. Their existence and normative force remain to be established.

This identifies what could ground differentiated responsibility without denying relational ethics. A collective requirement that one of the two contribute may be appropriate; a universal account need not name a unique individual. The open question concerns the warrant of that requirement and of the deciding procedure.

## 11. Constructing normative differentiation within whole activity

**This direction permits a substantive construction.** The first target is how one activity forms and retains differences between what occurs, what is expected, what counts as correct or fitting, and what remains insufficiently determined. Those distinctions can enter knowing, feeling, shared life and possible action. The construction concerns the formation of normative structure; its existence and the justification of every standard are not identified.

<a id="一段相连活动--one-connected-episode"></a>
### One connected episode

Consider a shared measurement activity. Contact, visible marks, participants' discrimination and memory, retained records and later checks together form a comparison that can remain available. Movement, marking, attention and understanding are distinguishable related activities in this history. Their neural realization belongs within it; this conceptual case does not claim to have measured that realization.

| Development within the activity | Distinction that can form | What does not follow merely from it |
|---|---|---|
| A mark is connected to an actual comparison, retaining its referent and time | What this record concerns | That someone understands the record |
| The comparison becomes available to further judgment, anticipation, checking or suspension | What is judged and what it rests on | Present action or compulsory compliance |
| The same report can be accepted with adequate support or withheld when its supporting check is missing | Being accidentally correct differs from having grounds to take it as correct | That every cognition must articulate reasons |
| Content and its conditions remain during inactivity | Understanding or an evaluative standard not currently enacted | That inactivity is failure or permanent loss |
| A changed reference, object or condition prompts reassessment of the old record's scope | Past correctness, present staleness and original error | That any disagreement defeats the earlier judgment |

The construction requires actual comparison, retention, content formation and mutual understanding. It builds on the existing accounts of cognitive uptake and relations across scales rather than deriving all those capacities anew from noncognitive material. The measurement convention and physical correspondence are disclosed inputs. Selecting a reference does not automatically justify every standard. See [cognitive uptake in the knowledge account](KNOWLEDGE.md#how-content-becomes-cognitively-available) and [relations across scales](RELATIONS-ACROSS-SCALES.md).

<a id="规范分化究竟加在哪里--what-makes-the-differentiation-normative"></a>
### What makes the differentiation normative

The key candidate is a capacity to treat a difference as a basis for assessing judgment while retaining the distinction between actual acceptance and warranted acceptance. Repetition can describe what happened without supporting the measurement claim. Present inactivity need not erase acquired grounds. A basis is not an additional object hidden in the brain: it concerns relations among the judgment, reference, record, acquisition and relevant counterevidence.

Successful regulation alone does not establish that a standard is apprehended as a standard; recitation alone does not establish understanding. Conversely, lack of verbal articulation does not settle their absence. Candidate evidence concerns transfer preserving content, sensitivity to changed support, appropriate suspension and specific error correction. No single output or proposed test is a sufficient algorithm for detecting consciousness or normativity.

<a id="两条可能接续--two-possible-continuations"></a>
### Two possible continuations

**Calibration:** when the reference changes, the activity re-establishes correspondence. An old record can remain true of the past while a new comparison receives present support. Records, memory, words and relationships change selectively.

**Closure:** the reference changes, relevant checking is blocked, and repetition is treated as evidence of reliability. Transmission can strengthen while the capacity to establish present applicability weakens. If the claim still concerns the current comparison, retention does not supply its missing basis. If content or purpose has changed, that change must be described rather than concealed by unchanged words.

Both continuations belong within the same generative account. The specified degeneration concerns a capacity for grounded discrimination of current conditions. It does not mean every aspect of the whole becomes worse: checking can be costly, and accurate judgment can serve harmful ends. Ethical comparison must retain those relationships.

<a id="正面说明所理解的不是一串指令--positive-account-more-than-a-string-of-instructions"></a>
### Positive account: more than a string of instructions

**The candidate account treats understanding a standard as a formed, contentful relational capacity:** identifying what it concerns, what supports it, and which changes constitute error or loss of support, with those distinctions available to retention, transfer, questioning or revision. Articulating reasons and enacting a revision need not occur every time. Silence, incapacity to act and deliberate noncompliance are not thereby failures of understanding.

A possible formation route begins with distinctions acquired through perception and shared activity. Retention, anticipation, others' responses and later outcomes make content reidentifiable across encounters. Discrepancies can then differentiate a false report, a stale record, an unreliable source, a changed question and understood but unfulfilled direction. Earlier acceptance and its basis can themselves become available for comparison. Reflexive assessment develops through that return to earlier activity; an additional inner judge already understanding every rule is unnecessary.

This builds on the parallel content-and-reasons account, not a mandatory developmental sequence. Conforming to a standard, recognizing it, using it as a reason and possessing a warranted standard can fail separately. A field named “standard” proves none of them. The account is a defended functional candidate, not an algorithm establishing consciousness or every form of normative understanding. See the [content-and-reasons account](KNOWLEDGE.md#how-reasons-and-standards-become-available).

<a id="根据为何可以约束评估--why-a-ground-can-constrain-assessment"></a>
### Why a ground can constrain assessment

For a claim that a present quantity lies within a stated range, the relation it concerns makes it accurate or inaccurate. Confidence, sanctions and repetition do not alone change that relation. A competing criterion that certifies the same false content as accurate while claiming to retain the original question misassesses that content. Switching to a wish, hypothesis or another time scope changes the assessment being undertaken.

This is the positive constitutive argument: **determinate content and its actual relations constrain accuracy without authorization by an external commander.** Truth-directed motivation is not presumed in everyone. A deliberate falsehood remains false even when its speaker prefers it. The identification of assertion and preserved content still requires the formation/use account; erroneous judgments are not defined out of existence.

But three kinds of force cannot be merged:

| Question | Ground and scope |
|---|---|
| Is the judgment accurate? | Its content and the relation it concerns, independently of how strongly it is accepted. |
| Is acceptance epistemically supported? | Acquisition, competence, relevant alternatives and undefeated objections; real correspondence and a defensible fallible scope matter. |
| Must one investigate, disclose, perform or compel? | Actual lives, losses, responsibilities, authority and comparison require further grounds; the existing conditional alignment route discloses its ethical commitments. |

The first argument alone does not establish the other two. A standard can correctly assess an activity whose continuation lacks justification; a well-supported judgment may appropriately remain undisclosed. These are different relationships within the same activity, not separate realities of fact and value.

<a id="lean-推进了什么--what-the-new-lean-result-establishes"></a>
### What the new Lean result establishes

The model begins with an explicit candidate-rule repertoire. Supported acquisition supplies question–result records; revision removes candidates conflicting with them. The answer procedure reads retained candidates, not the actual law used to prove correspondence. For every finite evidence sequence, the retained rules are exactly the original rules fitting all acquired records, and every eliminated original candidate has a specific counterexample in those records.

The stronger result constrains **every** procedure meeting the stated guarantee: on a nonempty candidate range, a definite answer can be given without error across that range only when all candidates agree. If a further requirement asks for every definite answer compatible with that guarantee, the resulting answer function is unique. That additional informativeness requirement is explicit, not a universal ethical duty to maximize information.

## 20. Normative differentiation within one activity

First explain how living continues through interaction, dependence and environmental change, and how some changes form obstruction, feeling, recognition, joint attempts and repair. Ends form from these activities and in turn organize attention, means and what follows; an end can also stop actual damage from entering choice. Actor and patient are relational positions differentiated here, and knowing and evaluating are activities within it. One cannot first take a list of facts abstracted from relations as complete reality and then demand that value be added to it.

**Affirmative candidate: a norm can be one activity's warranted discernment, organization and revision of the unfolding of its living; a reason is the expression, in judgment and response, of an actual relation within it that can be explained.** This is a constitutive explanation that needs defense. It does not call every actual regulation or internal target legitimate, nor does it require that a reason hold only once expressed. Expression can omit, distort or not yet have formed; a damaged relation not yet understood still actually exists. How the full relation supports the corresponding judgment must still be worked out item by item.

A concrete entry point is the completion of repair. When activity enables obstructed living to participate, rest, interact or learn again, the manner and intensity of care, and even whether it continues, may change accordingly. When a repair ends and the formerly obstructed living continues, this cannot be judged degeneration because the repairing organization shrinks. Conversely, maintaining a removable dependence to preserve the carer's position does not realize the same repair, even if it makes the organization more stable. This reuses the [existing completed study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/history/root-foundation/WHOLE-PROCESS-REALIZATION-ARGUMENT.md#5-valuable-response-does-not-warrant-creating-its-occasion) and does not record the distinction as a new finding.

The point of this entry is: **how living continues can change an activity's ends and its fitting conditions of ending; an already formed end cannot monopolize the explanation of the whole activity.** Under what conditions a repairing relation holds, what authority it has and what burdens it leaves are explained within the same history. Where continuing care is actually needed, an abrupt exit leaves real damage; “ending” is not automatically right either.

The test must also separate three cases. First, a description counts only the persistence of the caring organization and omits the obstructed living: an omission in the representation. Second, the activity actually suppresses feedback, obstructs exit or maintains dependence: a problem in the organization of relations, which completing the description alone cannot remove. Third, the organizers know the relevant consequences of the case accurately and still choose to keep the harmful arrangement, without necessarily suffering any returning loss themselves: the genuinely difficult normative rival. The third cannot be said to be necessarily ignorant or necessarily self-destructive, or to have already accepted a common good because it belongs to the one whole.

The monist correction therefore both withdraws the presupposition that an independent fact and value must be bridged, and raises what the affirmative explanation must do: repair, fulfillment, closure and damage must be explained within the actual unfolding, not by attaching labels to a neutral picture. **Complete description, accurate understanding and normative validity remain analytically distinguishable; their distinction does not establish three independent worlds.** The corresponding grounding is complete only when the explanation that completes the relations actually supports the judgment claimed.

What is now complete is the reconstruction of the starting point and its concrete test. The candidate must still give a substantive whole assessment of an arrangement that maintains harm with accurate knowledge, and state the scope of differentiated responses within it; it cannot merely say “it belongs to the whole”, nor require that every local persistence be maintained forever. The positive unfolding of 生生, rest, completion and fitting endings belong to the same scope of inquiry. The universal zero-substantive-axiom target remains; the completed conditional alignment route and the formal bridge to the Becoming spine stand.

**A further concrete judgment.** Boundaries can protect the unfolding of living: rest reduces present participation, privacy limits access, and coordinated work may need continuing support. Study §22.6 compares how the same restriction changes after learning and alternatives form: keeping support while a real need remains, adjusting cooperation to new capacities, or blocking change to maintain exclusive control are different continuations. Historical necessity cannot by itself warrant every later restriction; claiming that an alternative exists does not mean the alternative is actually supported. The existence of a boundary does not decide the matter; one must explain what it lets unfold and what it obstructs in actual relations. The comparison does not assume that every increase in options is good, nor does it prove that every removable restriction should be removed.

<a id="同一个理论中的选择规范与修订--selection-normativity-and-revision-in-one-theory"></a>
### Selection, normativity and revision in one theory

Following [Ren's further correction](../../../sources/15-codex-theory-conversations/2026-10-13-one-theory-normativity-meta-attractor.md), this section's case of “informed maintenance of harm” should return to the existing organizational explanation of the same theory. Relations form activity, and the consequences of activity in turn change the formation and selection of later organization; ends, the use of evidence and evaluative standards also form, persist and change within this. [Meta-attractor](../../meta-attractor/GENERATIVE-REGIMES.md) names the organization, within this unfolding, whose consequences take part in reproducing the conditions that select it and that can continue across carriers. It is not an independent world of mechanisms added to the theory of becoming or of norms.

In destructive cases, local success can strengthen the conditions that select ends of the same kind, so that damage, though known, cannot change the actual direction. “I act only on these ends” then becomes an actual organization to be explained and tested in its history of formation, not a final boundary of reasons needing no further explanation. Not all ends are treated here as captured; one must identify the actual relations in which consequences, selection, transmission and correction are restricted. Informed harm by itself is not enough to show that a meta-attracting organization has formed.

The existing [target-capture result](../results/records/target-capture.md) gives the mechanism scope of differential continuation, target transmission and, under specified conditions, the reabsorption of state-only changes; [selection and registration revision](../results/records/selection-under-registration-revision.md) and [the consequence-revised reference plan](../results/records/consequence-revised-reference-plan.md) explain, under their own conditions, how actual distinctions and revisions enter later response. They are existing explanatory inputs of this theory, not new proofs of this round, and they do not by themselves prove that every organization falls into or escapes destructive attraction.

This corrects the role the previous section gave the counterexample: informed harm tests the mistaken inference “monism necessarily leads to goodwill or compliance” and tests how correction fails; **its actual persistence does not by itself refute the validity of normative grounds.** What remains to be explained is why the organization of the same activity is deflected in this way, how it acts on actual living, and which revisions are warranted and realizable. The production of activity, the validity of judgment and the enactment of change have different evidential requirements, but all are one theory's account of the same unfolding.

## 21. Learning, capture and revision of ends

13 October 2026. The detailed argument and counterreview are in [§23 of the study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md#selective-correction-and-formed-ends). It continues the history of formation of the same supporting activity: shared work forms organization, ends and evaluation, and the consequences of activity in turn change its knowledge, capacities and conditions of selection. Learning cannot first be treated as an independent good and then asked how to fit it into the organization.

**A harmful arrangement can contain genuine learning.** An organization may understand its situation ever more accurately and improve its means, while treating people's no longer needing it as a failure to be corrected. If such local success in turn reproduces the conditions that select ends of the same kind, learning can take part in maintaining a meta-attracting organization. It need not reject all feedback, nor need everyone be deceived. This conceptual case is not yet an empirical judgment about any actual organization.

The existing results on [selection through registration](../results/records/selection-through-couplings.md), [registration revision](../results/records/selection-under-registration-revision.md) and [reference-plan revision](../results/records/consequence-revised-reference-plan.md) each keep their exact scope: being able to distinguish is not actually selecting accordingly, and a method's success on a given task does not automatically show that the end is legitimate. The existing mechanisms enter this explanation directly; they are not proved again or rewritten as ethical proofs.

The affirmative direction can therefore ask more concretely: **when an activity realizes the living it claims to support, can that result change its tasks, conditions of selection and conditions of ending?** Learning, recovery and new capacities to participate can make a formerly reasonable intervention need to change. If an end only ever allows its means to change, and never lets the actual realization of living change the end itself, one must test what that restriction is preserving. The revision may be continued support, transformation, handing over or ending; no one form is necessarily right in advance.

**After the test in the next section, this question is limited to a local diagnostic.** Real changes in tasks, selection and conditions of ending can still reproduce a harmful organization; this question alone cannot certify normative direction or escape from capture.

This credits the [existing argument on support and succession](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ETHICAL-LEARNING-INTEGRATION.md#53-the-affirmative-argument-and-its-acquired-content): continuation of the work can go together with the end of the present controller, and normative application still keeps the conditional alignment route and its factual premises. Care still genuinely needed, alternatives not yet supported, and real losses caused by transformation can all change the judgment. An end that openly pursues control need not involve an instrumental contradiction; criticizing it requires stating the actual relations and normative grounds. An end formed through selection is not thereby wrong; continued winning likewise does not by itself prove legitimacy. Neither a genealogy of ends nor successful learning by itself completes universal grounding.

<a id="fulfillment-test-counterreview"></a>

<a id="对这项检验的修正--correction-of-the-proposed-test"></a>
### Correction of the proposed test

As Ren asked, [§23.5 of the study](../../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/ZERO-AXIOM-PRACTICAL-JUDGMENT-INTEGRATION.md#fulfillment-test-counterreview) tests this question with five conceptual continuations. The strongest contrast: an organization genuinely helps its intended group, then ends the old project and changes its selection and tasks, yet keeps extending control through new dependencies. All three aspects really change, and the meta-attracting organization can still continue. Testing only whether change is possible cannot certify the direction of the change; the five cases narrow the original question to a local diagnostic.

“Claims to support” cannot delimit the living actually affected either; “when it has realized” cannot exclude damage, failure and mistaken ends before completion; and an arrangement that continues to meet real needs is not unfitting merely because it has not reorganized. A text that permits revision does not mean that a feasible path actually exists. The original question can still help identify cases in which completion is excluded from the organization's judgment, but it cannot serve as a universal criterion.

The revised version is: **how do the living relations an activity actually forms, sustains or damages enter the grounds of its ends, its selection, and its continuing, changing or ending? Do these grounds operate in concrete feasible responses?** The actual changes, the reasons for or against the arrangement, and the feasible responses and their consequences must be stated separately; these are analytic aspects of the same activity. “Warranted” still needs substantive explanation and cannot count as done by being written into a definition. The new version is a more adequate research question, not yet a universal normative grounding.
