*AI-written taxonomy over [entries/premises.json](entries/premises.json) and the live result records (AGENTS.md Rule 2 & Rule 5). A premise family groups hypotheses and representation commitments by the structural condition a proof assumes; it is an index for checking fit and reading across [the premise ledger](PREMISES.md) and [what does not join](JOINS.md), never a new theorem.*

The formal corpus separates two complementary premise views:

1. **Formal Premise Ledger ([entries/premises.json](entries/premises.json), [PREMISES.md](PREMISES.md)):** **1,728** extracted premise entries (`1,702` distinct English labels, `1,723` distinct formalized statements across `1,724` stated entries + `4` awaiting home re-saying). Of these, **1,711** are bound in `dynamics.json` (`1,691` law hypotheses plus extracted domain commitments) across the **204** premise-extracted dynamics of the prior live atlas, and **17** are K3 / Chapter 08 card standing entries (`k3-*`, `card-*`). Statement-level premise extraction remains pending for the **16** newly added Waves U3–U6 and Frontier 1–3 dynamics in [entries/dynamics.json](entries/dynamics.json), which omit the `"premises"` key so they are not misreported as taking no premises.
2. **Live Record Hypothesis Wordings (`records/*.json`):** **1,340** distinct English hypothesis wordings across **1,368** hypothesis entries in the **590** live result records (`status` neither `superseded` nor `withdrawn`), including the **16** newly linked records whose statement-level ledger extraction is pending.

Each ledger entry in `entries/premises.json` (`1,728 / 1,728`) and each distinct live-record hypothesis wording (`1,340 / 1,340`) maps uniquely to one of the **12 structural families** below; across the atlas as a whole, law-by-law premise extraction covers `204` of `220` dynamics (`16` pending).
