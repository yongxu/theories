## The dossier in ten minutes

**The question.** When you act for your own future, is the self whose going-on you take to matter your organism, which ends at death, or a larger pattern your acts keep going, such as a family line, a practice or a community? If the larger pattern, concern for your own continuation reaches part of what comes after you without a further step, under premises none of which is shown ([P-20](#p-20)). If the organism, that concern stops at your death, and reaching those after you needs the *step of invariance*, for which chapter 7 sets out the strongest argument and the strongest case against ([P-10](#p-10), [P-11](#p-11)).

**The ethics, from continuation** ([P-04](#p-04)). No should stands beyond becoming, and no good and evil are set over it: right and wrong, should and better form within it, in people and AI, real in what they condition, and no law evaluates ([P-02](#p-02)). The ethics emerges from continuation. A life goes on through what sustains it, and going on is not yet an order over courses: a stone goes on and ranks nothing. Call a life's order over courses *self-concerned* when it ranks above what it widens every widening that adds to its own continuation, read as what lies within its reach (*self-concern*). That a life forms such an order is not shown: in the proofs it is a hypothesis, derived from no dynamics, selection or persistence, and what the models show of persisting is a filter on what was chosen, not an order formed ([P-16](#p-16)).
- *How far concern reaches*, whose losses count: the continuation condition, that from every standpoint, for anything, its continuation into the future has significance ([P-05](#p-05)). By itself it ranks nothing. Read on relations, with significance from a standpoint taken as what lies within its center's reach along a chosen relation, its closure (a reading, [P-03](#p-03)), and held *de se*, it says that nothing lies outside each center's reach along that relation, `«续无外»` ([P-17](#p-17) gives what it asks of centers).
- *How courses rank*: *widening*, one course keeping all another keeps and letting more form, that is, admitting at every step all the other admits and at some step more, is, on a finite listing, exactly what every additive weighting that counts each listed item above zero ranks higher ([P-07](#p-07)). Where the condition so holds and a life's own continuation is read as what lies within its center's reach, whatever a widening adds lies within that reach, so self-concern ranks every widening above what it widens on a finite listing and, with admission decided, every extension between two level paths whose admitted pairs fit in a finite list, so that their admissions end; under 07 §4.9's hypotheses of activity, support and descent, where a line persists without end, the course actually taken never fits such a list, so there this route is silent (item 3 below). There ranking by widening adds no premise beside the life's own concern: where the condition holds, that concern already does it. *C* (`Comparison`) names the requirement that an order rank each course above every course it widens, every claim counted as living ([P-07](#p-07) gives its general form); a result taking it says so. Where a course opens one thing and closes another, the laws show it mixed and weigh it for no one.

Read along being affected, the condition holds where relations close, read across all steps: mutual dependence, every continuation able to be affected, at some remove, by every other, gives it near-definitionally, for significance bounded by each center's reach ([05](../chapters/05-COLLECTIVES.md#r-mutual-dependence-and-its-pace)); that result and the ranking between level paths compose directly, though no single theorem states the composite ([P-16](#p-16)). Read in time, it cannot hold over every step, since nothing reaches back; read from some step on, it holds exactly where, from any formation at any step, every formation is reached at some later step, through exchanges and each one's going on ([P-16](#p-16)). Where relations run one way, concern for one's own continuation need not count those who depend on one: nothing in it requires their losses to count ([P-13](#p-13)). Seeing them is needed there for a choice to answer to their loss, and not enough, and counting their loss is the one step we offer, the step of invariance, argued ([P-10](#p-10)) and deniable without contradiction ([P-11](#p-11)), beside T8's way of seeing, offered for life ([P-26](#p-26)). The ethics seeks no authority outside becoming and offers itself without one, argued on the thesis of one relational unfolding ([07](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-universal-practical-warrant)). That neither the represented dynamics nor logic alone force the condition is proved: with significance a free parameter, where there is a standpoint and a formation, no satisfiable statement reading only the represented dynamics entails it, by construction ([P-01](#p-01)), and a perspectivist denies it without contradiction ([P-11](#p-11)). Whether a reason formed within becoming could hold for every mind, the work does not claim or settle; how scope and order form within becoming is its open target ([08 §12](../chapters/08-METHOD.md#open-items-ranked), item 12). A center's couplings limit what its selection can respond to, the gap alignment works on ([P-25](#p-25)), and reading a loss, counting it and ranking courses by it are separate relations: seeing more is needed for a selection to answer to a loss, and not enough. Results on choices need further premises, each named ([P-21](#p-21)).

**What is proved**, in Lean, each on its stated base: any type; the root (every carrier, relation and class of the theory's grammar); or the World (natural-number amounts, discrete steps, each formation active or not). A *witness*, one constructed model, shows what can happen, not what does.
1. (Any type.) The condition is exactly the self's presupposition (that the continuation of everything within the self has significance) at every standpoint, held as the self is drawn somewhere, which alone is significance over the self's closure (route (b)), and invariant across drawings, which is the step. This decomposition Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "locates the contested step and derives nothing beyond it" ([P-08](#p-08), [P-09](#p-09)).
2. (Any type.) On a finite listing with two-valued admission, one course widens another exactly when every additive weighting that counts each listed item above zero ranks it higher ([P-07](#p-07)).
3. (World.) Without the condition, a formation's concern for its own continuation reaches what it depends on (an AI reading), not, in a witness, a dweller whose habitat it clears; where only active formations give, need reaches nothing active only later. Yet, under stated premises, a standpoint preferring more of its own future ranks a course that overdraws a condition every later generation needs, keeping nothing beyond, below one that agrees with it until then and keeps more of that future, with no C ([P-13](#p-13) to [P-16](#p-16)). Where the condition held de se holds, a self-concerned order, its own continuation read as what lies within its center's reach, is increasing in admission on any listing, so it ranks every widening above what it widens on a finite listing and, with admission decided, every extension between two level paths whose admitted (step, formation) pairs fit in a finite list, so that both admit nothing from some step on, as C asks there, with self-concern and the condition as hypotheses, the condition used only at the life's own standpoint. Under 07 §4.9's activity, support and descent hypotheses, where a line persists without end, the course actually taken never fits such a list, so there this route is silent ([P-16](#p-16)).

**The open question** ([P-11](#p-11), sharpened in [P-20](#p-20)). Chapter 7 §4.6 joins results on need and root laws ([P-15](#p-15), [P-17](#p-17) to [P-19](#p-19)) into one path, an AI reading whose steps each cite their Lean and whose joining is argued; a finite witness shows P2 to P4 holding together. The premises, not those of the step's argument ([P-10](#p-10)):
- P1, contained in the condition: held de se, acting presupposes that one's own continuation has significance.
- P2: the self is a *line* under a *class* (a way of counting occurrences as alike) with a regularity of its own, constancy up to the net's end at oneself, under which one's line, once it takes a step, ends only where the net does, though the regularity asks more.
- P3, a correspondence not shown for actual selves: the conditions one affects feed other lines.
- P4: the class of one's line holds one's effects: everything one conditions is alike to oneself.

Under P1 to P4, by a root law, one's concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of one's acts, past one's organism's end and across lines, with no step of invariance. P1 to P4 rank nothing; self-concern does, and on a listing of what lies downstream a self-concerned order is increasing in admission ([07 §4.4](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-would-give-c)). P2 and P4 are the crux, and denying either is coherent; the case for and against is set out in [07 §4.7](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#crux). The chapter asks: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "in acting, does one presuppose the significance of a line that ends while its last stage still conditions something, or of a regularity of one's own"? And Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "are all of one's effects one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for?"

What the path does not reach, such as present beings beyond every closure, is counted only by other routes, the step among them, which is argued for, its soundness not shown ([P-10](#p-10)), and coherently denied ([P-11](#p-11)).

**Theses and tests** ([P-26](#p-26)). Of its two frozen tests, with AI personas as valuers, the first failed every pre-registered rule and the second some. Human valuers are untested.

**What is not claimed.** That the ethics is proved, or that a life forms a self-concerned order. That self-concern ranks every widening where a listed item is not its own (some self-concerned order then does not), or ranks the course actually taken where a line persists without end, which, under 07 §4.9's hypotheses of activity, support and descent, never fits a finite list. Constraints on acts, the value of new lives, or well-being beyond admitted continuation. That P2 and P4 hold, or that P3 holds of actual selves.

## Terms

Lean paths are relative to `experiments/integrated-correction/general-framework/generative-basis/lean/becoming/`. Lean blocks give statements exactly, proofs cut at `:=`, definitions whole; they are given for the central items only, and the rest are named. No Lean is needed to review: each item's **Meaning** says in words what is stated.

- *Formation*: a pattern that goes on; its *continuation*: its going on from step to step. A *course* is one way things can go; a course *admits* a formation at a step where that formation can go on there, its conditions meeting its requirements. *Standpoint*: a position from which significance is held; *center*: the formation at a standpoint. *Standard*: what something is read against, so that it can be met or missed.
- *Closure* of a center along a relation: everything reached from it by following the relation step by step. Along feeding it is what feeds the center, directly or through others; along affecting, what affects it. These relations run from the fed to what feeds it.
- *Net*: a relation of conditioning among occurrences. *Class*: an equivalence relation, a way of counting occurrences as alike. A class is *constant* when whatever an alike occurrence conditions, this one conditions something alike.
- *De se*: held of oneself as oneself, each standpoint ascribing a property to its own center; *de dicto*: one proposition, the same from every standpoint. *Sub-true*, *super-true*: true at some admissible boundary of the self (some place the self is drawn), and at every one.
- *The step of invariance*: the continuation condition's content beyond the self's presupposition (P-10 to P-12).
- Labels. *(any type)*, *(any process)*: a theorem for every type, or every process. *World*: the model of webs and flows (natural-number amounts, discrete steps) on which most laws of the ethics are proved. *Root*: a theorem for every carrier, relation and class of the theory's grammar. *Witness*: one constructed model, showing that something can happen, not that it does. *Conditional*: a theorem taking a premise as a hypothesis. *Reading* [AI reading, Dn]: an interpretation registered with its argument in the decision register; *new* means not yet numbered.
- Hypotheses a result may take, each named where it is used, as a proof states its hypotheses: self-concern (a life's order over courses is self-concerned when it ranks above what it widens every widening that adds to its own continuation, read as what lies within its reach; P-16), except where P-09, P-14, the titles of Part 4 and P-13 and P-18's first question, like [07 §4.3](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-self-concern-through-dependence), say "self-concern" held de se: there it names significance held de se over one's own closure, a scope, the one such an order's marking reads; the continuation condition, whose losses count (P-05); `Comparison`, named C, the requirement that an order rank each course above every course it extends by a claim counted as living (with every claim counted, above every course it widens; P-07); and further practical premises A, D, E, L and J1–J4 (P-21), of which J1/J2, the priority of basic conditions, give C wherever it is decided whether an extension adds a basic claim (`Priority.comparison`). Widening itself is no hypothesis: on a finite listing it is what every additive weighting that counts each listed item above zero ranks higher.
- The grammar's names that occur: `«续»` continuation (conditioned by and alike under a class), `«相续»` a line (continuation's reach), `«所及»` reached on a line, `«亡»` ends, `«不穷»` goes on without end, `«核»` the core of a class (the regularity a line carries by itself), `«一»` the one (the class under which everything is alike), `«续无外»` nothing outside continuing: everything reached on every center's line.

### P-01 What the dynamics leave open

```lean
def ReadsOnlyDynamics {D : Type} (law : D → (Pos → Nat → F → Prop) → Prop) : Prop :=
  ∀ d sig sig', law d sig → law d sig'

theorem ReadsOnlyDynamics.not_entails {D : Type} {law : D → (Pos → Nat → F → Prop) → Prop}

```

File: `Becoming/Significance.lean`. **Meaning.** Significance enters the representation as a free parameter: for a standpoint, a step and a formation, a proposition saying whether that continuation has significance from there. A statement *reads only the dynamics* when its truth does not change if the significance is changed. Such a statement, if it entails the continuation condition (P-05), holds of no dynamics at all, wherever there is a standpoint and a formation. So no fact about how things go, as represented, settles what has significance. **How far seen, and use.** Theorem (any type) ([07 §1](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-the-dynamics-leave-open)). The chapter limits what it shows: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "That every World law reads only the dynamics is a fact about the representation: the result shows non-entailment by the represented dynamics, not that becoming leaves significance undetermined or needs the condition." Since 24 October it is stated of the free parameter only: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "The theorem below concerns the untied parameter." Where a result ranks, its theorem states the hypotheses it takes: the continuation condition, self-concern, `Comparison` (C) or J1/J2 (P-21); this theorem concerns the condition's free form only and does not show that it is needed. This theorem is the limit in chapter 7's sorting of what is shown, read and supplied ([P-02](#p-02)).

### P-03 Significance as lying within a center's reach


### P-04 How far concern reaches, and how it ranks courses

**Meaning.** The ethics emerges from continuation. Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "A life goes on through what sustains it, and going on is not yet an order over courses. Call a life's order over courses self-concerned when it ranks above what it widens every widening that adds to its own continuation, read as what lies within its reach (self-concern, `Weighting.IncreasingInOwn`). A life's reach, along being affected, holds everything that can affect its continuation at some remove: what sustains it and what draws on the same conditions alike. ### P-07 Widening, what every positive weighting ranks higher

```lean
theorem widens_iff_every_positive {items : List X} {a b : X → Bool} :
    WidensOn items a b ↔ ∀ w, Positive items w → weighed items w b < weighed items w a
```

File: `Becoming/Weightings.lean` (namespace `Weighting`). **Meaning.** A course *widens* another when at every step it admits every formation the other admits, and at some step one more: widening, keeping all another course keeps and letting more form. *C* names a requirement on an order (`Comparison`, with a parameter `living`): that it rank each course above every course it extends, keeping every claim the other realizes and realizing some claim counted as living that the other does not; with every claim counted as living, as between level paths (`Web.paths_extension_iff_widens`), that it rank each course above every course it widens. With no premise, the laws give how two courses differ in what they admit: one widens the other, they admit the same, or they are mixed (World; the cases exclude one another constructively, one of them holds wherever inclusion or exceeding is decided both ways, a root law for every relation, and that every comparison falls under one is classical). Widening is one-way and transitive (`Web.Widens.not_reverse`, `Web.Widens.trans`), a strict partial order in the mathematical sense, and says which admits more: more and less future, not good and evil. The theorem shows, on a finite listing with two-valued admission, that widening is exactly what every *positive weighting* (each item counted with a natural-number weight above zero, a course weighed by adding the weights of what it admits) ranks higher. The same holds for any class of orders from the positive weightings up to every order ranking each widening higher, priority to the worse off and lexical priority included (`Weighting.widens_iff_every_of_class`): a class that need not be additive shares widening only where every order in it already ranks each widening higher. **How far seen, and use.** Theorem (any type) ([07 §4.4](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-widening-as-what-every-positive-weighting-ranks)): Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "on a finite listing, widening is exactly what every additive weighting that counts each listed thing above zero ranks higher, and what every order increasing in admission shares", and Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "nothing here shows that a valuer counting each thing above zero, but not additively, ranks every widening higher". So widening itself is no hypothesis; a result ranking by it states the hypotheses its theorem takes, among them `Comparison` (C); the priority of basic conditions, J1/J2, which gives C wherever it is decided whether an extension adds a basic claim (`Priority.comparison`, P-21); or self-concern, which where the condition holds de se ranks every widening above on a finite listing and, with admission decided, between two level paths whose admitted pairs fit in a finite list, so that their admissions end (P-16): Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "An order increasing in admission is monotone at its standpoint; where the condition holds de se, self-concern is such an order". Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "On a listing's frame, every claim counted, an order is increasing in admission exactly when it meets C (`Comparison`), so monotonicity at the standpoint is weaker only in scope, a listing and not all of what can form". Since 6 November 2026 the chapter states [what would give an order meeting C](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-would-give-c): Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "no premise is both strictly weaker than C at a datum and, with such help holding there, enough for C at that datum" (`ReadsNoOrder.not_strictly_weaker`). One route is Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "calling better what no chooser does worse from and some does better from", and Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "the chooser route is Foster's effective freedom with every preference admitted, on a list with refusal an item in every menu, and the range's law Nehring and Puppe's condition at every listed item". Its menu witness, the prisoner's dilemma read as a menu (`Weighting.Restraint`), is a theorem on the listing's frame and stands there against that route and against reading C as "more options are better"; its bearing on courses of what can form, and so on C, stands until the full account is shown to remove it. The chapter credits Sen's intersection approach and Pareto dominance; the study of the step adds Quote [theories/becoming/history/THE-STEP-OF-INVARIANCE.md]: "inclusion with strict monotonicity on opportunity sets (Pattanaik and Xu 1990)". Widening decides no mixed comparison, each course admitting something the other does not: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Widening alone does not settle a mixed part; a further judgment names its criterion and who holds it, and the criterion need not be a number." Under the condition, Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Under the condition every narrowing loses something with significance."

**Known weaknesses.**
- Among what the chapter does not show: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "The value of bringing new lives into existence, well-being beyond admitted continuation, and constraints on acts."
- [this dossier, AI] Adding a life that takes nothing from others is a widening, so an order meeting C (`Comparison`) ranks it above the course without it. That is stronger than the first step of Parfit's mere addition paradox (1984, Part Four), which says only that such an addition is not worse. The chapter's reading on creation, Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "further living formation can count with no existing beneficiary, inventing no unlimited duty to create", concerns duty, not ranking.

**Questions.**
1. Is dominance over what can form a fair account of ranking by more future, which the chapter keeps apart from good and evil, and are the credits above (Sen 1970, Pareto dominance, and the study's credit for ranking opportunity sets) accurate?
2. An order ranking by widening ranks adding any life that takes nothing from others above not adding it. What does the theory then owe on the procreation asymmetry and on the Repugnant Conclusion?
3. Is it a strength or a gap that widening ranks no mixed comparison?

### P-08 The condition decomposed

### P-20 The open question, sharpened: a line that ends while it still conditions something, or a regularity of one's own

- Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "(P1) Held de se, acting presupposes that one's own continuation has significance". This is narrower than §4.2's P1 (P-10), which says only that some continuation has significance.
- (P2) The self whose continuation is presupposed is a line under a class on the root, and that class is constant up to the net's end at oneself: whatever an occurrence alike to oneself conditions, every occurrence alike to oneself that conditions anything conditions something alike to it. Where one acts, the condition is that oneself and each occurrence alike to oneself follow each other in what they condition: whatever an occurrence alike to oneself conditions is alike to something one conditions, and whatever one conditions, each alike occurrence that conditions anything conditions something alike to it (`«本类常当且仅当与己相随»`, a root theorem). Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "It is a regularity of its own: wherever one's line takes a step, it ends only where the net itself ends" (`«亡而网续则本类不至终有常»`, a root theorem); the condition asks more than that (`CleanStep.ends_where_net_ends`, `CleanStep.not_constant_at`), and a single moment meets it trivially, which P4 excludes for one who acts.
- Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "(P3) The conditions one affects feed other lines."
- Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "(P4) The class of one's line holds one's effects: everything one conditions is alike to oneself."

Its steps:
1. Need does not reach the future: over flows in which only active formations give, a formation active at no step of the stretch is needed only vacuously, and the feeding closure read across every step can overreach (P-15); Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "So the future is reached, if at all, along a line, not along need."
2. A line continues past a member's end under a coarser class (`«过亡而续»`, a root theorem). In the witness of two family lines that share nothing, a family's class is narrower than the one and constant, and carries the line through each birth while each member's line ends (`TwoLines.between`, `TwoLines.family_constant`, `TwoLines.core_through_birth`); in a finite net, where no family goes on without end, a family's class is still constant up to the net's end, and the organism's is not, even at its own class (`FiniteFamilies.family_constant_to_end`, `FiniteFamilies.member_not_constant_at`). Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "So, read with time and where lines share no condition, self-concern without the step reaches only one's own line of descent."
3. The class must hold one of the conditions the line passes (`«所经必同»`, a root theorem); the shared-condition witness is a case.
4. What one's class holds with one, it carries on to all that follows: under P2 and P4, everything downstream of one's acts lies on one's line (`«本类常闭则后皆续»`, `«本类常闭则所及皆及»`, root theorems at one's own class). In a finite net the parts hold together: the class holding two families with the condition they affect, narrower than the one, reaches the second family past the first member's end (`FiniteSharedCondition.held_narrower`, `FiniteSharedCondition.reaches_other_past_end`).

The conclusion: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Under P1–P4, one's concern for one's own continuation reaches everything downstream of what one conditions", across lines and past the end of one's organism; Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "That is a law, not only a reading of witnesses." The reach is exactly that everything downstream of one's acts is alike to oneself, with no regularity assumed: that the class of one's line hold the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line (`«后皆续当且仅当后皆同类»`, `«后皆续当且仅当自与线皆闭»`, root theorems). P4 is its first step and cannot be dropped (`«后皆续则所缘同于»`, `Unclosed.not_taken_in`); given the regularity of P2, holding one's effects is equivalent to the reach (`«本类常则闭当且仅当后皆同类»`), and without the regularity holding one's effects need give nothing beyond the first step (`Inconstant.not_all_follow`, `Inconstant.not_constant_at`). But the regularity is not needed: a class can meet the path's need without it, and, given P4 and acting, the regularity gives more, the holding of the effects of every occurrence alike to oneself, on one's line or not (`OtherLeaves.reach_without_condition`, `OtherLeaves.other_not_held`, `«本类常闭则所缘同类»`). Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "So what the path needs of P2 and P4 is one thing, that the class of one's line hold the effects of oneself and of each stage of one's line; P4 is its first step, and P2 one regularity that carries that step on." The chapter puts two questions to philosophers: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "in acting, does one presuppose the significance of a line that ends while its last stage still conditions something, or of a regularity of one's own, under which one's line, once it takes a step, ends only where the net does?" and Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "are all of one's effects one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, not of what one answers for?" **How far seen, and use.** Root theorems with witnesses ([07 §4.6](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-what-a-line-holds-and-a-regularity-takes-in)), and an argument joining them, an AI reading: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "each step cites its Lean, and the joining of the steps is argued". **Questions.**
1. 2. Are all of one's effects one's own, in the sense of whose losses count, while what one answers for stays narrower? Is that separation of scope from responsibility defensible?
3. Is constancy up to the net's end at one's own class a fair reading of a regularity of one's own? 8. Is there a criterion of "one's own", narrower than all one's effects and stated apart from identity, that bears on whose losses count and not only on what is permitted?
9. May scope depend on what can be foreseen, given that what can couple in would then fix whose losses count? Is the negligence standard its strongest form?
10. If one values what comes after as not oneself, is the path's reach only a contingent value, held beyond the self?
11. Does a loss counted on one's own line count as the other's (Rawls's separateness of persons), and can a scope that follows occurrences count what a harm forecloses, which occurs nowhere on the course taken?

### P-23 The disclosed comparison commitments

**Meaning.** Beyond ranking by widening the chapter discloses commitments for comparing basic losses: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "additional and revisable, neither theorems nor second conditions." Each with its content and, after the dash, the chapter's entry for its formal content:
- BC1, equal rescue: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "among equivalent basic losses with reliable rescue and no distinguishing burden or chance entitlement, prefer the plan preserving more lives"; none new.
- BC2, gravity one to one: a reason toward preventing the clearly graver avoidable basic loss, weighed with chances and other reasons; none new.
- LC, joint loss: each basic loss of one plan matched by a distinct loss of the other at least as grave, the other having more or graver, gives a reason for the first; a two-tier diagnostic.
- LR, local persistence: with a sufficient ground for the equivalent greater rescue, a slight worsening of one basic loss of an independently specified kind can leave its priority in place; checked consequences.
- CP, initial entry: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "in finite shared basic rescue, a real request with no sufficient ground to exclude it keeps a positive chance where a timely shared lottery is feasible"; checked consequences.
- NP, near parity: close to equality, a slight difference in exposure earns no fixed extra priority; a conditional interface.
- FR, finite responsiveness: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "FR proposes that among independently specified same-kind recoverable basic losses, where support, burdens and history persist with scale, some finite scale of joint lesser protection acquires sufficient ground over a fixed graver loss." Its formal content is strict loss matching: making strict matching sufficient for strict priority contains C, not conversely (`Frame.SufficientMatching.comparison`); Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "the commitment is consistent only where matching is asymmetric, and needs finiteness"; with content read as a class, claim equality decided and each configuration's losses listed without repetition, a strict match exists exactly when the better loses no more in each class and fewer in total (`Frame.strictLossMatch_iff_counts`). Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "No exchange rate or global ranking follows."
- RP, repair: an actor answerable for an identifiable uncompensated deprivation of another's independently warranted resources or capabilities has an actor-addressed reason for appropriate repair; none new.

A periphery theorem adds that finite agreement does not identify priority. **How far seen, and use.** Disclosed commitments, with the formal content each entry states ([07 §5](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#r-finite-aggregation-within-restorable-basic-losses)).

**Known weaknesses.**
- [this dossier, AI] The commitments answer questions much discussed in the literature on aggregation and numbers, which they do not cite: whether numbers count (Taurek 1977), lotteries and claims (Kamm 1993; Scanlon 1998), and partial aggregation (Voorhoeve 2014).

**Questions.**
1. Are these the right commitments, and how do they stand to positions on numbers and aggregation (Taurek 1977; Kamm 1993; Scanlon 1998; Voorhoeve 2014)?
2. Is it coherent to hold them as disclosed and revisable commitments, neither theorems nor hypotheses of the kind a ranking result takes, such as the continuation condition?

### P-25 Alignment first as what a selection reaches

**Meaning.** On the thesis of one relational unfolding, argued and not proved ([P-02](#p-02)), a person, an AI system and a training process are formations within the unfolding. Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Alignment is first what each one's selections reach and how far that can extend, not a bargain among separate owners of value." A selection that goes through a formation's couplings is bounded by them, and all such selections together tell apart exactly what couples into the selector. In the chapter's assessment of six published approaches, each selects through what its deciders read and, with no ethical premise, is blind beyond the union of its deciders' closures (`Flow.unselected_beyond_pool`). Under the condition a loss beyond every rater's closure still counts, so an approach coupled to preferences is incomplete for that scope whatever its raters value. **How far seen, and use.** Theorems (World) and an assessment ([07 §6](../chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md#alignment-without-premise)). **Readings.** [AI reading, D65], of a theorem in the same section (World; balance; part and region decided): where a part's activity widens what can form within its own region against the levels without its exchange, the whole widens exactly when its net draw stays within the margin for every formation outside the region the levels without it admit (`Flow.Balances.widens_iff_others_within`). Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Read as when keeping options open disempowers others, this locates, for outcomes reached by thresholds on conserved amounts and against the levels without the part, the boundary that power-seeking results (Turner and others 2021) leave open; impact measures read the actor's own attainable set, while the boundary lies in the others' margins." The impact measures are those such as attainable utility preservation (Turner, Hadfield-Menell and Tadepalli 2020) and relative reachability (Krakovna and others 2019), whose inaction baseline is the levels without the part ([record](../results/records/own-widening-within-the-margin.md)). The chapter scopes it: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "It does not answer the power-seeking question in that question's terms, since it formalizes no reward, average optimal value (POWER) or comparison between two of the part's actions, and it predicts no behaviour".

**Known weaknesses.**
- Among what the chapter does not show: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Seeing more is needed for a selection to answer to a loss, and not enough" and Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Coupling is not compliance". Of the first: Quote [theories/becoming/chapters/07-SIGNIFICANCE.md]: "Reading a loss, counting it and ranking courses by it are separate relations, so a narrower ranking or a disagreement is not only a failure of reach."

**Questions.**
1. Is it right to frame alignment first as reach, before any question of values?
2. Does it follow that an approach coupled to preferences is incomplete for the scope the condition fixes, whatever its raters value?
