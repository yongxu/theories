*This cover takes twelve to sixteen minutes to read; each dossier gives its own times in its opening section.*

This packet asks people who know the fields to judge what a machine cannot: whether the readings are faithful, whether the claims are put at their right strength, and what is missing. It comes in parts, each for readers of one field ([below](#what-is-asked)); any one part can be done without the others, and any item within a dossier. The theory was written with AI, the Lean proofs included; the proofs are checked by machine, in Lean, and everything else, the readings of the classical texts included, by further AI reviews; the person who directs the work has read its important parts, not every word, and no independent human review of it is recorded yet. The covering letter gives who sends it, why, and by when a reply would help.

## What the theory is

A map of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and ends within one relational unfolding, and of how life, knowing and evaluation take shape there, drawn so that people and AI can see the way together. First, a map for alignment: the differences that make a difference to a selection (a reward, a judge, a market, a vote, a training run), how what sustains it can run down while that makes no difference to it, and how it can go on the same way until that gives out, or turn early enough. Where what a selection raises comes to set the selection, the loop is what the work calls the [meta-attractor](../../meta-attractor/README.md); its closed form, where what is selected next and the rule (how the selection selects at a step) as each step sets it again follow from the share and the rule alone, how the rule is set again being formed from the share too, can lock: on stated models, where selection does not lower the favoured share, a share not lower sets again a rule not lower, and a rule not lower than a favouring one still favours, a locked start stays locked however large a loss grows that makes no difference to either ([02 §9](../chapters/02-MAP.md#r-closed-capture)); at its second order, also on stated models, the capacity to shape the rule is selected by what comes back to the shapers, alone or through the groups they are in ([02 §9](../chapters/02-MAP.md#r-shaping-selected-among-groups)). From this map we [want to design](../../../GOALS.md#generative-alignment) an alignment in which what sustains humans and AI keeps making a difference, early enough, to how they select together, and in which shaping how they select does not become what they select for. Read on the map, it asks two things. Where shaping the rule (how they select) rises by what comes back to those who shape it, alone or through the groups they are in, something other than their share still makes a difference to the rule, so that a loss that makes no difference to their share can turn it. And a lock, once formed, gives way early enough: something other than the share comes to make a difference to the rule before the loss passes its point of no return. Where both fail, the meta-attractor has closed and holds. Each has laws proved on models under named premises; none is shown of an actual joint selection. It is also a map of wisdom: what traditions, sciences and philosophies have seen of these dynamics, read onto one account, where they meet it and where they do not ([the crosswalk](../results/atlas/CROSSWALK.md)). Its root is a small relational grammar whose terms are named and read from classical Chinese texts: one relation read as condition (缘), one class read as sameness (类), and continuation (续) as their meet. Its operations are those of Tarski's calculus of relations, with four departures ([the logic dossier's L-01](LOGIC.md#l-01)). The names come from the texts; the operations do not, and the classical-texts and logic dossiers say how much of each reading is the texts' and how much the theory's. [The one page](../ONE-PAGE.md) says the whole in thirty to forty minutes; [the overview](../THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) gives the argument with every result linked to its chapter.

Each dossier opens by naming the items to read **if you have one hour**: those only a reader of that field can judge. Questions marked optional are open research problems, or spot checks of a linked table, not judgments asked of you.

**If you will re-code, do the re-coding first,** before reading any dossier or chapter: the dossiers quote some sampled sentences with the AI's class beside them. The codebook is all you need for it.

## How to respond

Each dossier ends with a response form. For each item give one verdict: **accept** (the reading or claim stands as written), **accept with changes** (it stands once rewritten; say how), **reject** (it does not stand; say why) or **cannot judge**, and any comment. Questions are numbered, so a plain email reply works, for example "L-08: accept with changes. Q21: …" or "C-08：可而须改。问 19：……". The re-coding is answered by the filled sheet. Return them to whoever sent you the packet.

The work is kept in a public repository, so a review kept with the work is published there, and AI systems read reviews when they revise the work. Whether you are named or quoted is your choice; the covering letter gives the terms.

## What happens with a review

Every finding is answered in the place it concerns: a reading rejected is revised or withdrawn in its chapter, and in the decision register where the reading has an entry there; a claim put too strongly or too weakly is restated; a missing credit is given. The re-coding's agreement is computed by [`agreement.py`](recoding/agreement.py) and read as [`FREEZE.md`](recoding/FREEZE.md) fixed before coding began; until then the classification's counts stand only as the AI's.
