# Review packet

[中文](README.zh.md) · [The theory in one page](../ONE-PAGE.md) · [The overview](../THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The chapters](../chapters/README.md)

*This cover takes seven to nine minutes to read; each dossier gives its own times in its opening section.*

**No human has reviewed this theory yet.** Everything in it was written with AI, the Lean proofs included. The proofs are checked by machine, in Lean; everything else, the readings of the classical texts included, has been checked only by further AI reviews. This packet asks people who know the fields to judge what a machine cannot: whether the readings are faithful, whether the claims are put at their right strength, and what is missing. The covering letter gives who sends it, why, and by when a reply would help.

## What the theory is

An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life and knowing take shape inside it, and how far evaluation does, without first assuming that becoming is good. Its root is a small relational grammar, standard relation algebra, whose terms are named and read from classical Chinese texts: one relation read as condition (缘), one class read as sameness (类), and continuation (续) as their meet. The names come from the texts; the operations do not, and the classical-texts and logic dossiers say how much of each reading is the texts' and how much the theory's. [The one page](../ONE-PAGE.md) says the whole in a page; [the overview](../THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) gives the argument with every result linked to its chapter.

Any one part can be done without the others, and any item within a dossier. Each dossier opens by naming the items to read **if you have one hour**: those only a reader of that field can judge. Questions marked optional are open research problems, not judgments asked of you.

**If you will re-code, do the re-coding first,** before reading any dossier or chapter: the dossiers quote some sampled sentences with the AI's class beside them. The codebook is all you need for it.

## How to respond

Each dossier ends with a response form. For each item give one verdict: **accept** (the reading or claim stands as written), **accept with changes** (it stands once rewritten; say how), **reject** (it does not stand; say why) or **cannot judge**, and any comment. Questions are numbered, so a plain email reply works, for example "L-08: accept with changes. Q14: …" or "C-08：可而须改。问 12：……". The re-coding is answered by the filled sheet. Return them to whoever sent you the packet.

The work is kept in a public repository, so a review kept with the work is published there, and AI systems read reviews when they revise the work. Whether you are named or quoted is your choice; the covering letter gives the terms.

## What happens with a review

Every finding is answered in the place it concerns: a reading rejected is revised or withdrawn in its chapter, and in the decision register where the reading has an entry there; a claim put too strongly is restated; a missing credit is given. The re-coding's agreement is computed by [`agreement.py`](recoding/agreement.py) and read as [`FREEZE.md`](recoding/FREEZE.md) fixed before coding began; until then the classification's counts stand only as the AI's.
