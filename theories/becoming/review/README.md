# Review packet

[中文](README.zh.md) · [The theory in one page](../ONE-PAGE.md) · [The overview](../THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) · [The chapters](../chapters/README.md)

**No human has reviewed this theory yet.** Its proofs are checked by machine, in Lean; everything else, the readings of the classical texts included, was made with AI and checked only by further AI reviews. This packet asks people who know the fields to judge what a machine cannot: whether the readings are faithful, whether the claims are put at their right strength, and what is missing.

## What the theory is

An account of how anything comes to be, lasts, changes and passes away within one relational unfolding, and of how life, knowing and evaluation take shape inside it, without first assuming that becoming is good. Its root is a small grammar, read from classical Chinese texts: one relation read as condition (缘), one class read as sameness (类), and continuation (续) as their meet. [The one page](../ONE-PAGE.md) says the whole in a page; [the overview](../THE-THEORY-OF-BECOMING.md) gives the argument with every result linked to its chapter.

## What is asked

| Part | For | Language | What it asks | Time, roughly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Classical texts](CLASSICAL-TEXTS.md) | scholars of classical Chinese texts | Chinese | whether the readings of the 墨经, 荀子·正名, 系辞, 老子 with 王弼, 庄子, the Changes and the passages of nine traditions are defensible; whether the classes and correspondences fit | half a day |
| [Buddhist thought](BUDDHIST-THOUGHT.md) | scholars of Buddhist thought | Chinese | whether the readings of the 中论, 成唯识论, 大乘起信论, 金师子章 and 华严五教章, and of 无明 and the four marks, respect the texts' own arguments | half a day |
| [Logic](LOGIC.md) | logicians | English | whether the grammar's theorems, the calibrations and the claims about representation are stated at their exact strength, and what is restated without credit | half a day |
| [Re-coding](recoding/CODEBOOK.md) | readers of classical Chinese, coding on their own | Chinese | classify 237 sampled sentences by the codebook, blind to the AI's classes, so that agreement can be measured ([how the sample was drawn](recoding/FREEZE.md)) | four to five hours |

Any one part can be done without the others. **If you will re-code, do the re-coding first,** before reading any dossier or chapter: the dossiers quote some sampled sentences with the AI's class beside them.

## How to respond

Each dossier ends with a response form: for each item, one of accept, accept with changes, reject or cannot judge, and a comment. The re-coding is answered by the filled sheet. Return them to whoever sent you the packet. Reviews are kept with the work; whether a reviewer is named is the reviewer's choice.

## What happens with a review

Every finding is answered in the place it concerns: a reading rejected is revised or withdrawn in its chapter and in the decision register, a claim put too strongly is restated, a missing credit is given. The re-coding's agreement is computed by [`agreement.py`](recoding/agreement.py) and read as [`FREEZE.md`](recoding/FREEZE.md) fixed before coding began; until then the classification's counts stand only as the AI's.
