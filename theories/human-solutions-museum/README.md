# The Human Solutions Museum (人类解决问题的方法博物馆 / 文明方法博物馆)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Civilization is not a sequence of clever objects; it is an accumulated stock of *methods* by which people converted real problems into working mechanisms. That stock is mostly unreadable today, because we archive inventions as results — what the thing is, who made it, when it appeared — rather than as problem-solving processes. The consequence is not ignorance but repetition: creators, organizers, policy designers and founders keep re-deriving structures humanity already worked out, and keep failing in the same two ways — asserting a value without designing a mechanism that can land it, or shipping a mechanism without understanding the problem it lands in. The corrective is an index change. Re-archive every invention, tangible and intangible alike, as a seven-part dossier — the real problem, how people at the time misread it, the key insight that redefined it, the mechanism it became, how it diffused and against what resistance, what it solved, what new problems it created — and add an eighth part that is not history at all: the method a present-day actor can borrow. Then organize the whole collection by human problem rather than by chronology, country or discipline, and link exhibits by *problem structure* rather than by subject, so that a person arriving with a problem can travel through structurally similar problems and leave with a transferable method. Stated as one sentence, which is how Ren states it: 用户带着一个问题进入，沿着人类历史上的相似问题旅行，最后带走一种可迁移的方法 — "the user arrives with a problem, travels through structurally similar problems in human history, and leaves with a transferable method." Three refusals hold the thing together: it will not sanctify inventions (every dossier must show the harm it produced), it will not be an encyclopedia (the exit of every exhibit is a method, not a fact), and it will not launder unreviewed prototype copy as authoritative history (the boundary is machine-enforced: a public build against unreviewed content must fail). Its governance is an open loop with a threshold — 开放提名、公共讨论、策展审核、持续修订 — and its deepest users are not visitors but people who intend to change something.

**Status.** Two things live under this name and they are at very different stages. The *research question* — "which systems solved this, and what do the exceptions have in common?" — is Ren's own, dated, in his own hand, and runs from 2024-11 to at least 2026-05: multicellular organisms, hunter-gatherer bands, Confucianism as social technology, Talmud/Halakha as decentralized coordination, the 奏折 memorial as a zero-trust messaging protocol, and a comparative sweep of governance protocols from the Han dynasty down to group scale. The *museum project* is a six-document specification cluster dated 2026-07-05, AI-drafted from Ren's direction, which converts that question into a product: a concept document, a web-experience spec, a content-asset spec, an audit, a 1,126-line engineering spec, and a short agent working-rules file. The concept document `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` is essentially publishable today as an essay, needing only a note that it describes a direction rather than a live museum. Everything downstream of it is specification: the data file, schema, validator and any built page are described in detail and are **not present in this corpus**, so every field-level claim below is a claim about what the documents specify, not a verified reading of code. By the project's own rule nothing here may yet be published as fact — zero of the ten seed exhibits meet the stated two-reliable-source floor. Verdict: strong draft, one document publishable, the empirical half unbuilt, and the research question that motivated it has not yet been folded back into the museum's own collection.

## The argument

### What is actually broken

The complaint the museum answers is not that people are ignorant of history. It is that history is stored in the wrong shape. 今天很多社会运动、公共倡议、产品创新和发明创造都在重复造轮子 — "today a great many social movements, public campaigns, product innovations and inventions are reinventing the wheel." The wheel-reinvention is not laziness; it is a retrieval failure caused by an indexing decision. Encyclopedias, museums and textbooks index inventions by *what they are* and *when they appeared*. That index answers the question "what was the printing press?" It does not answer the question a person with a problem actually has, which is "has anything with this shape been solved before, and by what move?"

From that one indexing failure the concept document derives three specific failures in the field. First: 有些人提出了很好的价值主张，但没有设计出可以落地的机制 — people put forward excellent value claims and design no mechanism that can land. Second, its mirror: 有些人做出了新的工具，却没有真正理解自己面对的社会问题 — people build new tools without really understanding the social problem they face. Third, the one that binds the other two: 有些人以为自己在解决新问题，但其实人类历史上已经多次遇到过类似结构的问题，并且曾经发展出不同层次的解决方法 — people believe they face a new problem when humanity has repeatedly met problems of *similar structure* and developed solutions at several different levels.

These three are not a list of complaints; they are the argument for the format. The first failure needs the mechanism module. The second needs the problem and misreading modules. The third needs the index. If you accept the diagnosis, the dossier and the star map follow from it rather than being design taste.

### The shape of a civilizational advance

The museum's substantive claim — the one that makes it a theory rather than a content format — is that a civilizational advance has a canonical shape with seven stages, and that the stages are separable and separately interrogable.

A *real problem* exists in some historical, social, bodily, technical or institutional setting. People hold a *misreading* of it: they moralize it, privatize it, suppress it, ignore it, or take it as fate. Someone produces an *insight* that redefines the problem rather than answering the old version of it. That insight is converted into a *mechanism* with a concrete substrate — 材料、制度、教育、标准化、传播网络、法律保护、社会运动、商业化 (materials, institutions, education, standardization, diffusion networks, legal protection, social movements, commercialization). The mechanism *diffuses*, through a force that must be named rather than assumed: state promotion, market, grassroots transmission, or crisis-forced adoption. It *solves* something, changing behaviours, relationships, institutions, risks or capabilities. And it *creates new problems* — side effects, misuse, controversy, new inequalities.

Each stage is doing work that no other stage does. Drop the misreading module and you get a story in which the answer was simply found, which teaches nothing, because the reader's own problem is currently misread and they cannot see how. Drop the mechanism module and you get inspiration. Drop the diffusion module and you get the fallacy the document names explicitly: 一个好想法不等于一个有效改变 — a good idea is not an effective change. Drop the new-problems module and you get hagiography, and hagiography is not merely distasteful here, it is *useless*, because the whole point of the archive is that a borrower must know the cost of the method they are borrowing.

The load-bearing stage is the third. 真正推动文明前进的，往往不是技术本身，而是一个新的洞察 — what truly pushes civilization forward is often not the technology itself but a new insight. And the insights that count are of a particular kind: they redefine the problem. 很多伟大发明的价值，不只在于给出了答案，也在于重新定义了问题. Two archetypes get named, and they are worth taking as the two named entries in a much longer list rather than as a taxonomy: some grand, unregulatable social problems must be converted into tools ordinary people can use autonomously; some problems treated as personal failure actually require a public institution to carry them. The first archetype produces the condom, eyeglasses, paper. The second produces public health systems, libraries, accessible design.

### The eighth module is not history

The seventh stage ends the historical account. The dossier has an eighth part, and the document is unambiguous that it is 本馆最重要的部分 — the most important part of the museum: 今天可以借走的方法, the method you can take today. 每个展品最后都应提炼出对今天有用的方法，而不是只留下历史知识 — every exhibit must end in a method useful today, not merely leave historical knowledge behind.

The worked case is the condom, and it is worth following because it shows what the format extracts that an encyclopedia does not. An encyclopedia gives material history and dates. The museum reads the condom as a response to a *bundle* of problems — the tight binding of sex to pregnancy, disease risk, women's bodily autonomy, power asymmetry in intimacy, family structure and fertility choice, and 公共卫生系统难以进入的私人场景, the private scenes public-health systems cannot enter. Its civilizational significance is then a single move: it converted a complex problem spanning body, desire, disease, shame, intimacy and public health into 一个低成本、可携带、可复制、可个人自主使用的工具 — a low-cost, portable, replicable, individually self-usable tool.

And the borrowable method is stated as a conditional design rule, not a slogan:

> 如果一个问题发生在高度私人、羞耻、分散、难以监管的场景里，那么解决方案必须足够低成本、私密、可自主使用，并且不要求人先改变成理想中的样子。
>
> If a problem occurs in highly private, shameful, dispersed, hard-to-regulate settings, then the solution must be low-cost enough, private enough, autonomously usable, and must not require that people first change into their ideal selves.

The generalization the document draws from it is the museum's clearest value stance: 好的文明工具，往往不是要求人先变得更高尚，而是在真实的人性、真实的处境、真实的限制中，提供一个更好的选择 — good civilizational tools usually do not ask people to become nobler first; they offer a better option within real human nature, real situations, real constraints. This is the same anti-perfectionism that runs through the rest of Ren's corpus: design for the humans that exist, not the humans a doctrine would prefer.

### Why the index is the theory

For a stock of methods to be usable, there must be a way to route a present problem to structurally analogous past ones. The museum's answer is to refuse both conventional indices. It is organized 按"人类问题" — by human problem — not by timeline, country, discipline or technology class. The concept document proposes twelve thematic halls; the web spec narrows v0.1 to eight galleries: 记忆、传播、身体、信任、协作、发声、照护、公平 (memory, transmission, body, trust, cooperation, voice, care, fairness). One exhibit can sit in several galleries at once, and the data is required to distinguish `primaryGalleryId` from `galleryAppearances` so that routing, filtering and breadcrumbs do not fight each other.

The edges matter more than the nodes. Galleries and exhibits are linked by *method relations*, an explicit seven-item vocabulary: 低成本复制 (low-cost replication), 私人场景接口 (private-scene interface), 公共制度承接 (public-institution catchment), 专家能力普及 (expert-capability popularization), 陌生人协作 (stranger cooperation), 风险转移 (risk transfer), 权利重新定义 (rights redefinition). This is the actual analogy vocabulary of the project, and it is what makes the trail from the condom to vaccines to anonymous help hotlines to accessible design to public-health systems a single path rather than a category error: 这些推荐不是因为它们属于同一学科，而是因为它们处理了相似的问题结构 — the recommendations exist not because the items share a discipline but because they handle a similar problem structure.

The user-facing loop closes on one decision that carries more of the thesis than any other: 用户可以收藏的不是展品，而是方法 — what the user can save is not exhibits but methods. Favouriting stores `method.id` and nothing else. If you could save exhibits, you would have built a wishlist of interesting historical objects, which is exactly the artifact the project exists to refuse. The intended felt result is de-isolation, written in the user's own voice: 原来我现在面对的问题，并不是孤立的。人类以前也在类似结构里挣扎过。我可以从历史里借一种方法，而不是从零开始乱撞.

### Four refusals, and why they are engineered rather than declared

The specification cluster spends more words on failure prevention than on features, because the project's characteristic failure is epistemic, not technical. Four guards are installed, and in each case the guard is built into the data model or the build, not stated as an editorial intention.

*Anti-sanctification.* The new-problems module is mandatory. Ambivalent inventions are explicitly admissible — 一些发明可能既推动文明，又带来新的风险、伤害或争议。它们仍然可以入馆，但必须被完整呈现 — precisely on condition that both halves are shown. 本馆不应把发明神圣化，而要展示它们的复杂性.

*Anti-sloganization.* Methods must read as borrowable action principles rather than keywords mechanically extracted from exhibit titles, and the method library — not the exhibit list — is the primary entity of its own page. Structurally, every method reserves four fields it does not yet use: `steps`, `antiPatterns`, `notApplicableWhen`, `failureSignals`. They may be empty arrays but must not be removed from the schema, because 它们是避免方法库口号化的长期接口 — they are the long-term interface that keeps the method library from turning into slogans. A companion rule guards against over-abstraction: 一个核心方法最好由多个不同领域展品支撑 — a core method should be supported by exhibits from several different domains.

*Anti-authority-laundering.* Content without sources, review and visual licensing may be used only as prototype copy and 不应以正式事实档案口吻公开 — must never be published in the voice of a formal factual archive. This is enforced rather than promised. `contentStatus` splits into `prototypeReady` and `publicationReady`; setting `VITE_CONTENT_MODE=public` while `publicationReady !== true` must *fail the build*, and the spec explicitly rules out the two softer failures — emitting an empty site, or emitting a formal-looking site carrying unreviewed content. Public mode admits an exhibit only when `publicationReady === true`, facts and sensitivity are both approved, at least two reliable sources exist (`primary`/`secondary`/`reference`; `uncertain` does not count), and any displayed key visual carries `src`, `alt`, `credit`, `license`. The wording rules follow the same logic: 可以说"原型内容""种子展品""待补来源"；不要说"权威档案""已考据""正式馆藏".

*Anti-fake-governance.* The nomination form must not simulate a submission pipeline — no server, no stored submission, no review promise, local draft only, with the UI stating plainly that it is a thinking-tool form; and the living-museum page must not imply a review backend already exists. This is unusually honest for a project that wants to look like an institution, and it costs the project its most distinctive claim in v0.1 (see the open questions).

The engineering discipline is not a separate concern from the theory, and reading it as boilerplate misses the argument. `data/museum-content.json` is declared a *content contract* and the single source of content truth; hardcoding exhibit titles, gallery mappings, method copy, form fields or similarity links in components is a red line. The layer boundaries are strict — `content/` is the only layer allowed to read the JSON and may not touch DOM or localStorage; `domain/` holds museum logic and may not read JSON, DOM or localStorage; components may not read JSON, read or write localStorage, resolve routes, or hardcode business copy. The reason is epistemic: a museum whose claims live in scattered component strings cannot be reviewed, sourced, corrected, contested or de-accessioned. A knowledge institution that cannot revise itself is not a living museum; it is a snapshot with a masthead. The eight dossier modules are likewise not a template but a machine-checked contract — the canonical section keys `problem, misreading, insight, mechanism, diffusion, solved, new-problems, method`, with `normalizeContent` required to backfill them and the validator required to block any exhibit missing one. The dossier is enforced at the level where it cannot quietly erode.

### The older question underneath

The 2026-07 cluster reads as though it began as a product idea. It did not. The question it operationalizes is one Ren had been drilling for twenty months, in his own hand, and the record of that is in the chat corpus rather than in the museum documents.

On 2024-11-03 he states the Ouroboros dynamic: power-capture efficiency compounds, single-value optimizers out-compete balanced agents, winners acquire discourse power and become the template, and the system erodes its own foundations. The next day, 2024-11-04, he does something methodologically distinct from either diagnosing or moralizing. He asks: "multicellular organism, if treated as a system, successfully eliminated this pattern, can you explain why and how" — and then immediately: "can you think of other systems that also prevented this pattern?" and "elaborate on Common Patterns Across These Systems." That is a three-step research method stated in sequence: generalize the failure dynamic, enumerate the exceptions, then extract what the exceptions share. It is exactly the museum's method with a different collection.

The same question recurs for eighteen months and each recurrence adds a candidate. 2025-02-03: "In the past many societies has its own value system to prevent selfishness to get compounded beyond systems regulatory control, so they last longer, or even keep a equilibrium. This is the root cause we need equality" — historical societies read as regulatory systems, and equality reframed from a moral demand into a stability requirement. 2025-03-16: instantiate a value-carrier and let it look at the present ("if Jesus is alive, how would he think about today's world") — a value system treated as an instrument you can point at a situation. 2026-03-28: 我也认为中国的传统叙事，例如孔子，老子，墨子和其它，在不被moloch吞噬的方面有非常明显的结构性优势 — classical Chinese narratives credited with *structural* resistance, not moral superiority. 2026-04-12, and this is the museum question asked in one line: 儒家…是一个非常优秀的认知框架，或者说，social technology followed immediately by 还有其他同样优秀的social technology吗 — "are there other social technologies of the same calibre?" The answer he brings forward that day is Talmud/Halakha, endorsed in the strongest available terms: 从工程角度看这可能是人类历史上最成功的去中心化协调系统 — from an engineering perspective possibly the most successful decentralized coordination system in human history, with dissent preserved, micro-behaviour normed, and no territory or army. 2026-04-13, the search extended and the reading level specified: "Too surface, I want to know what cognitive dynamics, thought dynamics are at play collectively", and then "can you find a culture that is still great, people living in has high harmony, cooperative and openness."

The clearest single instance of the method being *performed* rather than described is 2026-05-02. Ren asks how a 奏折 (Qing memorial to the throne) is written, and when offered a literary answer replies: 对 格式化的协议 — "yes, a formatted protocol." Then: 对 我想要整个协议-整个官僚体系的机制都有什么 — "yes, I want the whole protocol, every mechanism of the entire bureaucratic system." He endorses the reading that the memorial system achieved, 在一个低带宽、高延迟、零信任的环境里，用极少的物理 metadata（匣子+钥匙+换行规则）+ 高度结构化的语言层，实现了准确、私密、可审计的双向通信 — accurate, private, auditable two-way communication in a low-bandwidth, high-latency, zero-trust environment, using minimal physical metadata plus a highly structured language layer. Then he runs the comparative sweep in seven moves: 那汉朝呢 → 那今天的中国呢 → 再用当代美国对比一下 → 那古印度呢 → 那其他的呢？比如先秦或者北美/东南亚 → 这些都是大规模社会，之前中小规模社会，直至团体呢. Han, contemporary China, contemporary United States, ancient India, pre-Qin, North America, Southeast Asia, then down the scale ladder to medium, small and group-sized societies. That is a museum wing, indexed by problem (how does a large polity get accurate information upward under adversarial incentives?), collected across civilizations, read as mechanism rather than as heritage, and executed nine weeks before the museum documents were written.

There is one more piece, from 2026-04-11, which matters because it is Ren pre-empting the sharpest criticism of his own collection: "just realized i might be to bounded by chinese and american perspective, what does other cultures behave on self-others/individualism-collectivism/object-relation spectrum?" He caught the provincialism risk in his own research before the museum was specified. Nothing in the specification carries that correction forward.

### The gap between the two collections

Put the two collections side by side and something is visibly missing. The research question, 2024–2026, collects *coordination systems that resisted a runaway dynamic*: multicellular organisms, hunter-gatherer bands, Confucian 修身齐家治国平天下, Talmud/Halakha, the Han and Qing memorial protocols, cultures not organized by collective trauma. The museum, 2026-07, collects *inventions that converted a problem into a usable mechanism*: writing, paper, printing, the condom, vaccines, libraries, eyeglasses, the scientific method, accessible design, open-source collaboration.

Not one of the social technologies from the research thread appears in any gallery, method or seed list. The overlap is nearly zero. Two readings are available and the sources do not choose between them.

The first reading: the museum is a *deliberate narrowing*. Inventions are tractable — datable, sourceable, visually representable, and safe to write about. Confucianism and Halakha are none of these; a dossier on either would be contested on its first sentence, and the project's own sensitivity rules would bite hardest exactly there. On this reading the seed collection is a scaffold and the institutional expansion list the audit demands (法律、合同、货币、医院、心理治疗、学校、工会、人权、公共卫生制度、污水处理) is the bridge back toward the research question, deliberately taken one step at a time.

The second reading: the museum is a *drift*. The original question was about what stops a system from eating itself. That question has a specific shape — it is about feedback, regulation, and selection pressure over long horizons. The museum's question is about problem-to-mechanism conversion, which is a different and gentler shape. The reframing insight, the private-scene interface, the low-cost replication — these are conversion moves, not regulation moves. Nothing in the eight-module dossier asks "what kept this from being captured?" or "what happened when it was?", and no gallery is named for it. The concept document's twelve halls do include 人类如何降低暴力 (how humans reduced violence), which is the closest the museum comes to the original question — and that hall is the single item that survives nowhere in the later documents: not as a gallery, not as a method, not as a seed exhibit, not on the expansion list.

The second reading is better supported by the texts, because the loss of the violence hall is documented and unexplained, and because the museum's own governance vocabulary contains no state for a mechanism that worked and was destroyed. The first reading is better supported by what would be sensible to do. Both should be held: the practical narrowing is real, and so is the fact that the narrowing dropped the part of the question that originally motivated it.

### The counterexamples the sources raise, and how they are answered

*"This is a demo dressed up as a knowledge system."* Raised directly and in the project's own voice by the audit: 审计前的内容资产更接近静态 demo 文案，不足以支撑长期活博物馆. The answer is the data contract — schema, validator, publication states, evidence table, sensitivity fields, search fields — so that content can be checked by machine and upgraded without a rewrite. The answer is real but partial: it hardens the container while the contents remain demo copy, which relocates the risk rather than removing it.

*"`mvpReady` will be read as 'ready to publish.'"* Named as a bottleneck and answered by splitting the flag into `prototypeReady` and `publicationReady`, and by stating explicitly that `published` — a per-exhibit workflow state — does not equal site-level `publicationReady`. This is the cleanest fix in the cluster.

*"The collection is a set of pretty examples."* Raised twice, in the content document and the audit, in Ren's own register: the ten seeds are 不是长期馆藏的平衡状态 and skew to knowledge diffusion, body tools and digital collaboration — 漂亮例子. The answer is a required expansion into institutional cases, chosen precisely because they are the exhibits where mechanism, diffusion and cost are messiest. The answer is specified and undelivered.

*"Methods will drift into slogans."* Raised as a P1 priority and answered structurally by the four reserved fields plus the multi-domain support rule. Every one of the fourteen v0.1 methods currently ships with those fields empty, so the guard exists and has never been exercised.

*"Scope will slide from 'prototype complete' to 'the whole product vision.'"* Raised as a bottleneck and answered by an explicit v0.1 exclusion list, a seven-phase plan with completion criteria, and an adversarial review checklist. The answer is undercut by the same document, which adds seven phases, four validation layers, three viewport reviews and an axe pass for a project with no built page.

*"Structural similarity is a soft claim."* Answered only partially — every `similarStructures` entry must carry `relationType`, `dimension` and `confidence`, checked by the validator, and future AI-generated relations may only be candidates, must include a `reason`, may not be shown publicly when low-confidence, and must be written back into the data by a human. But the space of `dimension` values is never defined anywhere, and the seven method relations that would be the natural vocabulary appear in one prose paragraph of one document and are not carried into any data specification. The museum's central promise is that your problem has a structural ancestor; that promise currently rests on curator intuition.

**Positioning and diagnosis**

17. The project is not an "invention encyclopedia" (发明大全) nor an encyclopedic website displaying great inventions; it is 一座收录人类曾经如何解决真实问题的活博物馆 — "a living museum collecting how humanity once solved real problems." [value stance] — `11-human-solutions-museum/人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` §一
18. The selection question is not how novel an invention was but whether it 推动了人类人文文明往前一步 — moved humanistic civilization one step forward. [value stance] — same, §一
19. Therefore the essence of the museum is 一个"文明方法库" — a civilizational method library, not a collection. [design direction] — same, §一
20. Many social movements, public campaigns, product innovations and inventions today are 重复造轮子 — reinventing the wheel. [observation] — same, §二
21. Failure mode A: people propose good value claims but design no mechanism that can land (提出了很好的价值主张，但没有设计出可以落地的机制). [observation] — same, §二
22. Failure mode B: people build new tools without actually understanding the social problem they face. [observation] — same, §二
23. Failure mode C: people believe they face a new problem when humanity has repeatedly met problems of *similar structure* and developed solutions at several different levels. [mechanism] — same, §二
24. The corrective is to reorganize historical experience so people see not only 结果 (results) but 问题、洞察、机制、扩散、代价和可迁移方法 (problem, insight, mechanism, diffusion, cost, transferable method). [design direction] — same, §二
25. The intervention point is *before* action: help people think more clearly before inventing something, pushing a social change, designing an institution, or proposing a new idea. [design direction] — same, §二

**What counts as an invention**

26. Tangible and intangible creations are collected on equal terms; the intangible list — 文字、学校、图书馆、法律、货币、民主制度、科学方法、心理治疗、版权、公共卫生制度、合作社、开源协作、无障碍设计、人权观念 — are 同样是文明发明, equally civilizational inventions. [value stance] — same, §三
27. Intangible inventions count because they changed how humans organize society, preserve knowledge, build trust, express pain, coordinate action and distribute rights. [mechanism] — same, §三
28. Tangible objects are admitted not because they are objects but because 它们把某类人类问题转化成了更可处理、更可传播、更可使用的形式 — they converted a class of human problem into a more tractable, transmissible, usable form. [mechanism] — same, §三
29. Five admission criteria: does it answer a real and important human problem; does it change the basic way humans handle that problem; does it expand 表达、选择、尊严、自由、安全、协作或理解能力 (expression, choice, dignity, freedom, safety, cooperation, understanding); does it create a method later people can transfer, reuse or reflect on; did it have durable influence on later institutions, tools, ideas or social structures. [design direction] — same, §四
30. Admission is explicitly not restricted to wholly positive inventions: ambivalent ones may enter, but must be presented completely — both what they solved and what new problems they made. [value stance] — same, §四
31. Being first and being famous are both explicitly rejected as admission criteria. [value stance] — same, §四

**Exhibit form: the dossier**

32. Exhibits are displayed as 问题解决档案 (problem-solving dossiers), never as 物品说明书 (object spec sheets). [design direction] — same, §五
33. Reframing thesis: the value of many great inventions lies not only in giving an answer but in 重新定义了问题 — redefining the problem; so the museum must record how the problem was previously misunderstood, suppressed, ignored, or moralized. [mechanism] — same, §五.2
34. What pushes civilization forward is usually not the technology itself but a new insight (真正推动文明前进的，往往不是技术本身，而是一个新的洞察). [mechanism] — same, §五.3
35. Two named insight archetypes: some grand social problems must be converted into tools ordinary people can use autonomously; some problems treated as personal failures actually require public institutions to carry them. [mechanism] — same, §五.3
36. The museum foregrounds mechanism rather than story, and names the possible mechanism substrates: 材料、制度、教育、标准化、传播网络、法律保护、社会运动、商业化. [mechanism] — same, §五.4 (the web spec's variant list adds 工具、标准、组织、协议、市场)
37. 一个好想法不等于一个有效改变 — a good idea is not an effective change; diffusion must be interrogated separately, including resistance and whether spread came from the state, the market, grassroots transmission, or was forced by a crisis. [mechanism] — same, §五.5; `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §五.5
38. Any civilizational advance may have side effects; the museum must not sanctify inventions but show their complexity. [value stance] — same, §五.6
39. The transferable-method module is 本馆最重要的部分 — the most important part; each exhibit must end in a method useful today, not merely historical knowledge. [design direction] — same, §五.7
40. Exhibit titles must carry explanatory power rather than only a name — 避孕套：把宏大的公共卫生问题，变成一个人可以随身携带的选择. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §五
41. The web spec fixes the dossier at eight modules: 它遇到的难题 / 当时人们误以为问题是什么 / 关键洞察 / 它把问题变成了什么机制 / 它如何扩散 / 它解决了什么 / 它制造了什么新问题 / 今天可以借走的方法. [design direction] — same, §五
42. Those eight become machine-enforced canonical section keys — `problem, misreading, insight, mechanism, diffusion, solved, new-problems, method` — with `normalizeContent` required to backfill them and the validator required to block any exhibit missing one. [design direction] — `11-human-solutions-museum/engineering-implementation-spec.md` §4.2
43. The problem stated in a dossier must be concrete: 问题应尽量具体，而不是抽象口号 — as specific as possible, not an abstract slogan. [design direction] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §五.1
44. The misreading module must show the *old problem frame* and why that frame constrained the available solutions — not merely that people were wrong. [design direction] — same, §五.2

**The anchor case: the condom**

45. The condom is treated not as an object invention but as a response to a bundle of problems: the tight binding of sex to pregnancy, STI risk, women's bodily autonomy, power asymmetry in intimacy, family structure and fertility choice, and 公共卫生系统难以进入的私人场景. [observation] — `人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` §六
46. Its civilizational significance is the conversion move: it turned a complex problem spanning body, desire, disease, shame, intimacy and public health into 一个低成本、可携带、可复制、可个人自主使用的工具. [mechanism] — same, §六
47. The transferable method is stated as a conditional design rule: 如果一个问题发生在高度私人、羞耻、分散、难以监管的场景里，那么解决方案必须足够低成本、私密、可自主使用，并且不要求人先改变成理想中的样子. [conditional] — same, §六; `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §五.8, §十
48. General value stance derived from it: good civilizational tools usually do not demand that people first become more noble; they offer a better option 在真实的人性、真实的处境、真实的限制中. [value stance] — same, §六
49. Two failure diagnoses restated through the case: many social movements fail because they only offer value and no mechanism; many inventions fail because they only optimize the product and do not understand the real setting the problem occurs in. [observation] — same, §六
50. The condom is designated the v0.1 气质锚点 (temperament anchor) and the quality baseline for information density and visual treatment; `condom` must reach anchor quality before v0.1 is done. [design direction] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §十; `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §5.3, §12

**Information architecture**

51. The museum is organized by 人类问题 (human problems), not by timeline, country, discipline or technology class; the structure is a 问题星图 / 文明地图 (problem star map / civilization map). [design direction] — `人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` §七; `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §四
52. The concept document proposes twelve thematic halls: 人类如何保存记忆、传播知识、降低暴力、扩大信任、组织大规模协作、保护身体自主权、让弱者也能发声、把羞耻问题变成公共问题、把道德诉求变成制度流程、治疗痛苦、承认差异、把专家能力封装成普通人可用的工具. [design direction] — `人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` §七
53. The web spec narrows v0.1 to eight galleries: 记忆、传播、身体、信任、协作、发声、照护、公平. [design direction] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §四
54. Each exhibit is simultaneously a historical record and a method case (每个展品都既是历史档案，也是方法案例). [design direction] — `人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` §七
55. One exhibit may appear in several problem galleries; the data must distinguish `primaryGalleryId` from `galleryAppearances` to keep routing, filtering and breadcrumb semantics coherent. [design direction] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §四
56. Galleries are linked to each other by *method relations* rather than by category — 低成本复制、私人场景接口、公共制度承接、专家能力普及、陌生人协作、风险转移、权利重新定义 — so users see deep structure instead of isolated inventions. [design direction] — same, §六.1
57. Adjacency between galleries is not authored as data in v0.1 but derived in the domain layer: shared method first, shared exhibit second, an exhibit's `similarStructures` reaching another gallery third; an explicit `adjacentGalleryIds` field is added only if that derivation proves unstable. [design direction] — `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §5.2
58. Cross-exhibit recommendations are made on *problem structure*, not discipline — the condom links to vaccines, anonymous help hotlines, accessible design and public-health systems 不是因为它们属于同一学科，而是因为它们处理了相似的问题结构. [mechanism] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §六.3
59. Similarity links must be typed, not loose: every `similarStructures` entry needs `relationType`, `dimension` and `confidence`, checked by the validator. [design direction] — `content-assets-production.md`; `content-engineering-audit.md`

**The interaction thesis and the site**

60. The site must not be an ordinary "online museum homepage" nor an "inventions list" but 一件可交互的文明思考工具 — an interactive civilizational thinking tool. [design direction] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §一
61. The core experience in one sentence: 用户带着一个问题进入，沿着人类历史上的相似问题旅行，最后带走一种可迁移的方法. [design direction] — same, §一
62. The intended felt result is de-isolation: 原来我现在面对的问题，并不是孤立的。人类以前也在类似结构里挣扎过。我可以从历史里借一种方法，而不是从零开始乱撞. [value stance] — same, §二
63. The axis is 问题到方法 (problem → method), explicitly not 发明到知识点 (invention → factoid). [design direction] — same, §二
64. The homepage's first line must be 你现在想解决什么问题？ — never a "welcome to the museum" opener; marketing-style heroes, long project blurbs and passive-visitor entrances are forbidden. [design direction] — same, §三; `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §5.1
65. The user is framed as 带着问题进入博物馆的人 — someone entering carrying a problem — not a passive visitor. [value stance] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §三
66. Each homepage entry prompt must lead to a problem gallery, never to a conventional category page. [design direction] — same, §三
67. What users collect in the 文明工具箱 (civilizational toolbox) is *methods*, not exhibits, because the intent is that they leave with thinking tools rather than historical knowledge; engineering red line: favorites store only `method.id`. [design direction] — same, §六.4; `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §5.5, §15
68. Reverse search — entering from one's own problem description, e.g. 我想解决一个羞耻、隐私、难以公开求助的问题 — is realized in v0.1 only by preset entry prompts and `entryPrompts`, with free-text, semantic and AI-recommended search explicitly deferred. 69. Methods must be written as sentences that can guide action today, not as slogans — 把集体问题降到个人可操作层面、把稀缺知识变成低成本复制媒介、把道德呼吁变成制度流程、把隐形痛苦变成可命名问题、把专家能力封装成普通人可用工具. [design direction] — `可交互网页-最终执行预期.md` §六.2
70. The nomination form is itself a thinking tool: it must demand problem, common misreading, key insight, mechanism, observable impact, side effects and transferable method rather than name + blurb. [design direction] — same, §六.6
71. Ten seed exhibits carry the v0.1 collection, each with a one-line core reading: 文字 (把记忆从身体里取出来), 纸 (让知识变得轻、便宜、可复制), 印刷术 (把少数人的知识变成公共基础设施), 避孕套 (把私人风险变成个人可操作工具), 疫苗 (用小风险训练身体与社会的免疫能力), 图书馆 (把知识从私人财产变成公共空间), 眼镜 (用一个小工具延长人的能力), 科学方法 (让争论不只依靠权威，而依靠可验证过程), 无障碍设计 (把"个体缺陷"重新定义为"环境设计问题"), 开源协作 (让陌生人围绕共享协议共同创造). [design direction] — same, §九

73. The method library is a first-class object, not a by-product: the methods page's primary entity is `methods`, not exhibits, and methods must read as borrowable action principles rather than keywords mechanically extracted from exhibit titles. [design direction] — `content-assets-production.md`; `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §5.4

**Living museum and governance**

79. The museum is not finished once: it must be open, continuously updated, nominable and discussable — 任何人都可以自荐或推荐某个发明、制度、方法、观念或社会创造. [design direction] — `人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md` §八
80. 但开放不等于无门槛 — openness does not mean no threshold; every candidate must answer the six dossier questions to be considered. [design direction] — same, §八
81. The governance loop is 开放提名、公共讨论、策展审核、持续修订, producing something that is neither a rigid authority nor an arbitrary submission platform but 一个不断生长的文明知识系统. [design direction] — same, §八
82. The museum's surface audience is the general public and its deep audience is creators and actors: 发明者、创业者、社会运动者、公共政策设计者、教育者、研究者、公益组织、想要改变某个现实问题的人. [design direction] — same, §九
83. The public-facing purpose is stated separately and is not the same as the actor-facing one: ordinary readers should understand 文明不是自然发生的，而是一代代人解决问题的结果 — civilization did not happen naturally; it is the result of generation after generation solving problems. [value stance] — same, §九
84. The actor's four arrival questions are given in the actor's own voice: 我面对的问题属于哪一类？人类过去处理过类似结构的问题吗？当时成功或失败的关键机制是什么？我今天能不能借用、改造或避免这些方法？ — note that both *failure* and *avoidance* are explicitly in scope. [design direction] — same, §九
85. Publication is a state machine, not a boolean: `proposed → drafted → fact-checking → sensitivity-review → published → revised`. [design direction] — `content-assets-production.md`
86. Content status is split so prototype completeness cannot be mistaken for publication readiness: `contentStatus.prototypeReady: true`, `contentStatus.publicationReady: false`; and `published` as a per-exhibit workflow state does not equal site-level `publicationReady`. [design direction] — `content-assets-production.md`; `content-engineering-audit.md`; `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §1.1.1
87. Content without sources, review and visual licensing may be used only as prototype copy and must never be published in the voice of a formal factual archive. [value stance] — `content-assets-production.md`; `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §4.3
88. The prototype/public boundary is machine-enforced: `VITE_CONTENT_MODE=public` with `contentStatus.publicationReady !== true` must *fail the build*, rather than emit an empty site or a formal-looking site carrying unreviewed content. [design direction] — `engineering-implementation-spec.md` §1.1.1
89. `public` mode admits an exhibit only if `publicationReady === true`, `reviewStatus.facts === "approved"`, `reviewStatus.sensitivity === "approved"`, at least two reliable sources exist, and any displayed key visual has `src`, `alt`, `credit`, `license`; reliable means `primary`/`secondary`/`reference`, and `uncertain` sources do not count toward the threshold. [design direction] — same
90. Prototype wording rules: may say 原型内容 / 种子展品 / 待补来源; must not say 权威档案 / 已考据 / 正式馆藏; sensitive exhibits must use restrained public-discussion language (克制的公共讨论语言). [value stance] — same, §4.3
91. The nomination form must not simulate a real submission pipeline: no server, no review promise, local draft only, with the UI stating that it is a thinking-tool form; likewise the living-museum page must not imply a review backend already exists. [value stance] — same, §5.6–5.7
92. Sources must live in a top-level `sources` table referenced by `evidence.sourceIds`, 不要把来源散落在正文里 — never scattered inside prose. [design direction] — `content-assets-production.md`
93. Before public release each exhibit needs key historical nodes, reliable sources, points of dispute, over-generalizations to avoid, and citable further reading. [design direction] — same
94. The admission criteria must eventually be rewritten as executable curation rules: real problem / changed the basic handling / expanded capability / yields a transferable method / fully presents side effects and disputes. [design direction] — same

**Audit findings and self-correction**

95. Audit verdict: the direction across the four core documents holds — the project is a civilizational method library organized around 问题、误解、洞察、机制、扩散、副作用、可迁移方法 — but the pre-audit content assets were closer to static demo copy than to a long-lived living museum. [observation, AI review] — `content-engineering-audit.md`
96. Named pre-audit bottlenecks: no machine-executable data contract; no source/review/sensitivity/publication status; `mvpReady` easily misread as publication-ready; unstable routing, detail structure, gallery membership, similarity and search fields; MVP scope sliding from "prototype complete" to "the complete product vision"; a collection structure still too narrow. [observation] — same
97. Hardening applied: `schemas/museum-content.schema.json`, `scripts/validate-content.mjs`, a `validate:content` npm script, governance fields (`publicationPolicy`, `sources`, `publicationStatus`, `publicationReady`, `reviewStatus`, `evidence`, `timeline`, `assets`, `sensitivity`, `search`), and `schemaVersion: 0.2.0`. [observation] — same
98. The seed collection is biased and must be corrected: the current ten validate the product structure but are 不是长期馆藏的平衡状态, over-weighted toward knowledge diffusion, body tools and digital collaboration — 漂亮例子 — so institutional exhibits (法律、合同、货币、医院、心理治疗、学校、工会、人权、公共卫生制度、污水处理) must be added. [observation][design direction] — `content-assets-production.md`; `content-engineering-audit.md`

## Genealogy

### Prehistory: the research question (2024-11 → 2026-05)

The museum's motivating question is two years older than the museum, and it is in Ren's own hand throughout.

**2024-11-03/05 — the failure dynamic.** The Ouroboros work states the pattern the question is asked against: power-capture efficiency compounds, single-value optimizers eliminate balanced agents, winners acquire discourse power and rewrite the rules, the system erodes its own foundations, and it collapses under its own optimization pressure.

**2024-11-04 — the turn to exceptions.** Within a day of stating the pattern, Ren asks the question that becomes the museum: which systems solved this? The first named case is the multicellular organism, and the follow-up sequence is a method, not a curiosity — generalize the dynamic, enumerate exceptions, extract common patterns across exceptions. This is the museum's operation applied to coordination systems rather than to inventions.

**2025-02/03 — regulatory value systems.** The exceptions acquire a mechanism: past societies held value systems that stopped selfishness compounding beyond regulatory control, which is why they lasted, and that — not moral preference — is why equality is needed. What this stage has that later stages lose is the explicit framing of a *tradition as a regulator*.

**2026-03 — structural advantage, and its degenerate twin.** Classical Chinese narratives are credited with structural resistance to Moloch (2026-03-28), and in the same month the corrective is stated: high points and degenerate modes coexist; Confucian logic also became the value of whoever held power (2026-03-18). The anti-sanctification rule that the museum will later impose on inventions is here first, applied to a philosophical tradition.

**2026-04 — the museum question, asked in one line.** 儒家…是一个非常优秀的认知框架，或者说，social technology, immediately followed by 还有其他同样优秀的social technology吗 (2026-04-12). The answer brought forward that day is Talmud/Halakha as possibly the most successful decentralized coordination system in history. Two days earlier Ren had already flagged the collection's own bias: bounded by Chinese and American perspectives (2026-04-11). Two days later the reading level is specified — collective cognitive dynamics, not customs — and the search is extended to living cultures (2026-04-13).

**2026-05-02 — the method performed.** The 奏折 conversation is the clearest single instance: a historical artifact read as a formatted protocol, then the whole bureaucratic system demanded as the object of study, then a comparative sweep across six civilizational settings and down the scale ladder from large polities to groups. Problem-indexed, mechanism-read, cross-civilizational, and executed entirely in his own questions.

### The specification cluster (all files dated 2026-07-05)

**A caveat that shapes everything below.** All six files carry the same modification date and came from one directory (`~/Desktop/human-solutions-museum docs`, per `sources/SOURCES.md`). There is no external chronology. The layering below is reconstructed from *internal* evidence: documents that explicitly supersede others, documents that describe a prior state that no longer exists, and paragraphs clearly inserted into an earlier document after a later one was written.

**Layer 0 — the working-rules layer (`agent.md`).** The only file that looks backward at a process failure rather than forward at a product: "Do not repeat the mistake of turning an exploratory answer into implementation without confirmation." It also fixes the stage — the concept documents exist, the web does not. It is earliest in effect and continuously applicable.

**Layer 1 — the concept (`人类解决问题的方法博物馆-方向定稿.md`).** The theoretical core; everything else is downstream. ## What was lost along the way

1. **The research question itself.** The largest loss in the theory, and it happens between the chats and the specification rather than inside the specification. Nothing in any of the six documents carries "which systems solved the runaway dynamic, and what do the exceptions share?" No gallery, method or seed exhibit corresponds to a social technology in Ren's sense. Confucianism, Talmud/Halakha, the 奏折 protocol stack, hunter-gatherer bands and multicellular regulation — the entire 2024–2026 candidate list — are absent. The museum inherits the *format* of the question (problem → mechanism → transferable pattern) and drops its *subject matter*.
2. **人类如何降低暴力 (how humans reduced violence).** One of the twelve thematic halls in 方向定稿 §七, and the one that comes closest to the original research question. It survives nowhere: not as a gallery, not as a method, not as a seed exhibit, not on the institutional expansion list. This is the single largest silent loss inside the cluster.
3. **Four more of the twelve halls.** Also dropped as galleries: 人类如何把道德诉求变成制度流程, 人类如何承认差异, 人类如何把专家能力封装成普通人可用的工具, 人类如何把羞耻问题变成公共问题. Two survive demoted into methods (把道德呼吁变成制度流程; 把专家能力封装成普通人可用工具) and one into a homepage entry prompt (如何把私人痛苦变成公共议题). 承认差异 survives only obliquely, inside the 公平 gallery.
4. **Tangible exhibits named then dropped.** 方向定稿 §三 names 显微镜, 乐器, 摄影机, 个人电脑, 手机, 太阳能板, 轮椅. Only 轮椅 reappears (as a 身体 candidate) and 摄影 (as a 发声 candidate). Musical instruments and solar panels vanish entirely, losing respectively the expressive/aesthetic dimension of civilizational invention and the ecological/energy dimension.
5. **Intangible exhibits named then dropped.** 版权 (copyright) appears in the concept document's intangible list and in no later list at all; 民主制度 survives only obliquely as 选票 among 公平 candidates. Copyright's loss is notable because it is one of the few named inventions whose "new problems created" module would be sharply contested — exactly the kind of exhibit the anti-sanctification stance calls for.
6. **The seven method relations as a controlled vocabulary.** Named once, in 可交互网页 §六.1. The later data specifications require `relationType`, `dimension` and `confidence` on similarity links but never re-list this vocabulary, so the enumeration survives only in prose in one document and is at risk of being re-invented ad hoc in the data.
7. **Failed mechanisms and methods to avoid.** 方向定稿 §九 gives the actor's arrival questions in the actor's own voice, and two words in them are load-bearing: 当时成功或**失败**的关键机制是什么？ and 我今天能不能借用、改造或**避免**这些方法？ The concept document explicitly wants failed mechanisms and methods marked "do not reuse." No later document gives either a place in the dossier or in the data model. `newProblems` is not the same thing: it records the cost of a solution that worked, not a solution that did not.
8. **The full list of naming candidates.** Seven candidates in 方向定稿 §十一 narrow to a three-way naming policy; 人类发明与社会问题博物馆, 活的文明博物馆 and 人类问题解决史 drop out with no recorded reason.
9. **The public-facing register.** 方向定稿 §九 insists the museum must remain readable by 普通公众 so that they understand 文明不是自然发生的，而是一代代人解决问题的结果. Every later document is written for the builder; nothing downstream specifies how the general-public reading level is preserved, and the anti-marketing, anti-welcome rules push in the opposite direction.

## Open questions and critiques

### Raised inside the corpus

1. **The content assets were demo copy.** 审计前的内容资产更接近静态 demo 文案，不足以支撑长期活博物馆. Partially answered by the data contract; the substantive content debt remains untouched.
2. **No machine-executable data contract, and no source, review, sensitivity or publication status.** Both recorded as fixed in the hardening pass — but the fix is described, not verifiable here, because the files do not exist in this corpus.
3. **`mvpReady` invites misreading as publication-ready.** Fixed by the split flags. The cleanest resolution in the cluster.
4. **Routing, detail structure, gallery membership, similarity and search fields were unstable.** Fixed by `slug` / `routePath` / `primaryGalleryId` / `galleryAppearances` / `sections`.
5. **Scope creep from "prototype complete" to "full product vision."** Mitigated by the exclusion list, phase plan and adversarial review — while the same document expands scope considerably for a project with no built page.
6. **The collection is too narrow and skews to pretty examples.** Flagged twice, unresolved. The institutional exhibits (法律、合同、货币、医院、心理治疗、学校、工会、人权、公共卫生制度、污水处理) remain unwritten.
7. **Methods risk becoming slogans.** Flagged as P1. The structural remedy is specified and every one of the fourteen methods currently ships with the four fields empty.
8. **Over-abstraction from a single case.** 一个核心方法最好由多个不同领域展品支撑 — yet the anchor exhibit is one case and the flagship method is derived from it alone.
9. **The two-source floor is met by nothing.** 正式公开前，每个展品至少需要两个可靠来源. Currently zero exhibits meet it, so the entire site is prototype-only by its own rule.
10. **Provincialism, in Ren's own voice.** "just realized i might be to bounded by chinese and american perspective" (2026-04-11). Raised in the corpus about the research programme and never carried into the museum's sourcing plan.

### Found by reading

11. **No theory of structural similarity.** The whole retrieval mechanism rests on "structurally similar problems," yet nothing defines what a problem *structure* is or enumerates the `dimension` values a similarity link may take. The seven method relations are the only candidate vocabulary and were not carried into the data specifications. Without this, similarity is curator intuition and the museum's central promise — that your problem has a structural ancestor — is unfalsifiable.
12. **The research question is missing from the museum it produced.** No social technology in Ren's own sense appears in any gallery, method or seed exhibit; the one hall that came closest (人类如何降低暴力) was dropped without comment. Two readings are available — deliberate narrowing toward tractable cases, or drift away from the motivating question — and the second is better supported by the documents, the first by what would be sensible practice. Either way, the museum's own collection does not currently contain the exhibits that made Ren want a museum.
13. **No falsification or failure test for a transferable method.** Methods are extracted post hoc from successes. There is no way to record that a borrowed method was tried and failed, no feedback path from a user's outcome back into the dossier, and — per "What was lost" item 7 — the concept document's explicit interest in failed mechanisms and methods-to-avoid has no home in the data model. A method library with no failure record is a survivorship machine.
14. **Survivorship and Whig-history risk at the collection level.** The museum indexes what worked and diffused. Problems humanity never solved, solutions that were suppressed, and mechanisms that worked and were then destroyed are structurally invisible. The new-problems module mitigates triumphalism about individual exhibits but not the selection bias of the collection as a whole. A 未解决 (unsolved) or 失败的解决方案 (failed solutions) wing would be the direct fix, and would also be the natural home for the coordination systems that regulated selfishness and were then overrun — which is precisely the research question's material.
15. **The anchor method reads, unamended, as a dark-pattern licence.** 解决方案…不要求人先改变成理想中的样子 is a genuine insight about designing for real humans. Stated as a general transferable method with no boundary conditions, it also exactly describes engagement-optimized products that meet people where they are and keep them there. The museum has anti-sanctification guards for *exhibits* but no ethics guard on *method transfer*, and the `antiPatterns` field where this would live is empty for every method.
16. **Curatorial legitimacy is undefined.** 策展审核 appears in every governance statement, but no document says who curates, how curators are selected, how a dispute between nominator and curator resolves, whether decisions are appealable, or whether an admitted exhibit can be de-accessioned when later found wrong. The workflow ends at `revised`; there is no `withdrawn` or `contested` state. For a project whose whole claim is that it can be corrected, this is the load-bearing omission.
17. **The living museum has no discussion surface.** 公共讨论 is one of the four governance verbs, yet v0.1 ships no comments, no forum, no visible nomination queue, and the nomination form deliberately does not submit anywhere. The most distinctive governance claim is the least implemented, and the engineering spec explicitly forbids anything that would imply otherwise — honest, but leaving the model entirely on paper.
18. **Language scope contradicts the object.** The site is specified `html lang="zh-CN"`, multi-language is excluded from v0.1, and the object of study is human civilization. The UI risk is small; the sourcing risk is not. A Chinese-only sourcing and review pipeline will silently narrow which civilizations' solutions get archived — the exact failure Ren named in his own voice a year earlier.
19. **Sensitivity handling is asserted, not specified.** `sensitivity` carries 等级、类别和措辞提醒 and sensitive exhibits must use 克制的公共讨论语言, but no document defines the levels, the categories, or who adjudicates. For a collection whose anchor exhibit is the condom and whose expansion list includes psychotherapy and human rights, this is load-bearing.
20. **The museum's own content licence is unspecified.** Image credit and licence are required for exhibit visuals, and nominators must declare copyright, but nothing states the licence of the museum's own dossiers and methods. For a project whose thesis is that methods should be borrowable, an explicit open licence is a substantive design decision, not a legal footnote.
21. **Preset entry prompts partly contradict the arrival thesis.** 用户带着一个问题进入 is the core promise, but v0.1 admits only preset problem entrances and forbids free text. A user whose problem does not match one of roughly seven prompts cannot in fact arrive with their problem. The deferral is defensible for v0.1; the tension should be named rather than left as a scope note.
22. **Engineering weight versus content weight.** Roughly 25 KB of the cluster's 66 KB is engineering specification and about 4 KB is audit, against a single worked exhibit example appearing three times in overlapping form. The most likely failure mode of the project as written is a beautifully validated, well-tested, accessible shell around nine one-line exhibits — precisely the risk the audit named as demo copy, displaced from the content layer to the app layer.
23. **The museum never uses its own method on itself.** The obvious self-application — "the online museum / open knowledge commons" as an exhibit, with its own problem, misreading, insight, mechanism, diffusion, new problems and transferable method (Wikipedia already appears as a 协作 candidate) — is never taken up. Nor does any of the six documents cite any of Ren's other frameworks. Doing either would both test the format and connect this cluster to the rest of the corpus.
24. **The museum has no account of why the archive was mis-shaped in the first place.** The diagnosis says we store results rather than processes, and treats that as an oversight. It may not be. Storing results is what institutions with reputations to protect, patents to defend and heroes to celebrate are incentivized to store; the misreading module is the part a national museum cannot write. If the archival failure has a cause, the cause is a candidate exhibit — and the museum's own governance would be its transferable method.
25. **Unverifiable artifacts.** All field-level, schema-level and validator-level claims describe files not present in this corpus. Treat `data/museum-content.json`, the schema, the validator, `schemaVersion 0.2.0` and the ten seed exhibits as *specified*, not as *confirmed to exist*.
