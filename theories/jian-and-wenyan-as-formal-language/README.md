**The theory in one paragraph.** Classical Chinese — 文言, especially its pre-Qin form — is not a decorative vehicle for a formal system; its grammar already is one. Content words (实词) are polymorphic primitives whose noun / verb / adjective mode is dispatched by *position* rather than carried in morphology, so 文言 does structural typing where Indo-European languages do nominal typing. Function words (虚词) carry relation-kind and no semantic content, so they are typed edges written directly rather than encoded. The "type" of a passage is never declared; it emerges from composition the way a Hindley–Milner type is inferred, and what a trained reader calls 文气 or 章法 *is* that inference running. Ren's own compressed statement of the generative principle is that the grammar-bearing single characters (虚字) are mostly formal-logic operators and the concept characters (实字) are constructible from one root plus those operators — so a small kernel of particles (his nine: 者 之 也 而 即 非 凡 令 是) is STLC-complete, and everything else is library. If that is right, the whole pre-Qin formal inheritance — 墨家's 同/异/类/故/法, 名家's type-token discriminations, 易传's 几, 五行's feedback ring, 医家/兵家's control vocabulary, 庄子's self-reference — is not a set of proto-sciences awaiting Western completion but a single lineage that was cut off, whose native operational vocabulary can be recovered and shown structurally equivalent to type theory, cybernetics, calculus, complex systems, ecology, statistics, information theory and the three modern mathematical foundations. The project therefore describes itself as **名实之辨在数字时代的延伸** — the extension of the name-and-reality debate into the digital age, explicitly not a Greek ontology of being qua being — and as *recovery, not invention*. On the ontology side one primitive suffices (**間**, later **系**, finally **一**), whose definitional content is that it contains itself; time is folded inside it as a periodic anchoring rather than laid alongside it; and two duals — 位⟷际 (structural, i.e. Yoneda: a locus is exhausted by its relations) and 化⟷理 (dynamic: a trajectory and its attractor are one phenomenon under two views) — span the working vocabulary, with everything else derivable but not reduced. Two hard commitments run through every layer: **反诛心**, that no metric may adjudicate anyone on inferred inner states, so only speech (言) and act (行) enter measurement; and **一切形式皆指月之指**, that every formalism is a finger pointing at the moon and is judged by 功用 rather than 真值. And one argument, made only in conversation and never written into the drafts, joins the language claim to the ethics: a monist language is a *communication attractor* — where many parties must understand each other in an environment of 和 rather than 争, symbols are selected toward concepts traceable to a common root, because cooperation reasons while contention only wins — which makes 道 something more than a construct and makes 大同 a mechanism rather than a utopia.

## The argument

### Where it starts: a word for a thing that is both a place and a group

The program does not begin with classical Chinese. It begins with an engineering problem. Through April 2026 Ren was trying to name the central object of an agent substrate, and asked: "is there a word of abstraction that describes both space and group?" (`chats/2026-04-23`, via voice notes hb-02 #249). The candidates in the actor-model literature all fail in the same way: *session* names a protocol but not a place, *space* names a place but not a protocol, *context* names neither. On 2026-05-01 he answered his own question — "session也是space，可能这个名字有点迷惑，文言文的间或许是一个更好的名字，发生的地方，有点类似于spacetime" — and renamed SST to 間 (`chats/2026-05-01_sst可不可以作为pi_calculus设计`).

What matters is why that renaming was not cosmetic. 間 is simultaneously spatial and temporal, container and content, structure and process, and Chinese has carried that for two thousand years in ordinary compounds — 人间, 之间, 瞬间, 间或, 间不容发. The 間 essays make the strong version of the observation: "这不是中文词义的模糊，这是「间」所指的那个 reality 本来就在这些区分之先" — Western languages split the thing into space and time, container and content, structure and process, then labour to sew it back together; 間 never needed the seam (`dl/间.md`). If you accept that, then choosing 間 is *recognition, not invention*: not creating a new concept but noticing that a concept Chinese has always used happens to be the category a relational reality actually requires.

That is a claim about one character. Within a day it generalised into a claim about the language. On 2026-05-01 Ren opened the second thread with: "文言文，特别是先秦版本，每一个表达都是一种对过程的编码，每一个字-都有独特且明确的所指结构，动态或拓扑" — every expression in pre-Qin 文言 encodes a process, and every character has a unique, definite referential structure that is dynamic or topological (`chats/2026-05-01_古文字的编码结构与动态所指`). Told the claim was too strong, he did not weaken it; he restricted its domain and *added* a requirement: "不说所有，至少一个子集字是这样的，并且它们complete." Not all characters — a subset, and that subset is complete. That move, restriction-plus-completeness rather than hedging, is the shape of the whole program.

### The load-bearing novelty: grammar is the surface syntax, not a style

The claim the project itself marks as "此部最 novel" (`jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III`) has three parts, and each is needed.

**First, content words are polymorphic primitives dispatched by position.** 王天下 puts 王 in verb position and it means to-king-ify (a process, 化); 天下王 puts it in noun position and it means the king (a locus, 位); 王道 puts it adjectivally and it means kingly (a pattern, 理). 君君臣臣 sets both modes of one character adjacent: the first character of each pair is the locus, the second is the process. This is not sloppy grammar. It is that the language "把 化 直接 build 进 grammar primitives" — the process/entity distinction is not lexicalised, so it does not have to be reconciled later. Indo-European languages take the opposite option: declension, conjugation, gender and case put the syntactic role *inside the word form*, so the word carries its own type. That is nominal typing; 文言 is structural typing. The project insists this is "categorical 差别，不是 stylistic 差别" — the Go/TypeScript-versus-Java/Haskell argument was staged by two language families millennia before either language existed.

**Second, function words are typed edges.** 之 is a genitive edge; 於/于 a locative or comparative edge; 以 instrumental or causal; 而 a polarized conjunction; 則 a conditional; 也 a terminal predication; 者 a nominalizer; 乎 interrogative-locative; 矣 perfective; 焉 locative-pronominal. Each carries relation-*kind* and essentially no semantic content, which is exactly what an edge in a typed graph is. In the earlier architecture the point was made ontologically — 虚词 are reducible to 际 patterns and are retained only as syntax sugar, since eliminating them is possible in principle but explodes expression length ten- to a hundred-fold (`jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v0.7.md §V`). Classical function words, on that reading, are "sophisticated linguistic technology": common relational patterns compressed into single characters.

**Third, the type of a passage is inferred, not declared.** Nothing in a 文言 sentence announces its own type. A trained reader converges on the contextually coherent reading, and that convergence is what the tradition calls 文气 or 章法. The claim is that this is structurally Hindley–Milner inference — the reader is *doing* type inference, not appreciating a style. And Ren had already required, on the first day, that the disambiguation be rule-governed rather than merely pragmatic: "这些都可以在上下文去歧义，并且消除歧义有形式规则."

Put together: 实词 = polymorphic primitives typed structurally; 虚词 = typed edges; 理 = inferred type. The engineering corollary is drawn explicitly — if this is right, an expression layer native to a relational substrate should look syntactically more like 文言 than like Python: primitives typed by position, a small set of particle connectives, minimal explicit inflection. "文言文 也许不只是巧合 relational —— 它是几千年用户测试出来的、最接近 JIAN primitive 的自然语言 surface syntax" (`jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.6`).

### Why the compression claim is made, and how far it is allowed to go

From the grammar claim the project draws an information-theoretic one: 文言 is near-globally-optimal for the dense expression of relational and processual phenomena. Three arguments support it. Source-encoding match: if reality is relational-processual, then a relational-processual encoding is minimal, because no nominal-to-structural translation overhead is paid. Compression density: 道德经's five thousand characters need roughly ten times the length in modern English and still lose ontological nuance. Primitive polymorphism: each character is effectively a functor liftable into different syntactic contexts, which is minimal description length.

The claim is then bounded three times in the same section, which is the discipline that makes it worth taking seriously (`jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.5`; `jian-repo/WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §I.3`). The compression is *trained-receiver-conditioned* — it offloads the dictionary onto the reader's prior, so it is a conditional optimum, not an unconditional one. It is *demonstrably lossy for procedural specification*: mathematical proof, engineering spec, legal contract. And the historical evidence for that lossiness is internal to the tradition — Ming–Qing 考证学 had to invent philological conventions, and modern Chinese had to import mass bisyllabic technical vocabulary. Finally, optimality is relative to a loss function, so the claim is narrowed to "near-optimal *for high-density expression of relational phenomena*". The section also refuses the tempting overreach outright: "Ontology grammar alignment 不证 ontology truth；只证 文言 适合 articulate 此 ontology."

### The λ-calculus test, and the point at which the analogy stops being an analogy

Ren's own validation method was to run the correspondence backwards: "我们反向测试一下：用文言文算子表达Lambda calculus算子" — express λ-calculus in 文言 operators, and admit any character into the kernel that can express something accurately (`chats/2026-05-01_古文字的编码结构与动态所指`). The result, which he pasted back with "我又想了想，我觉得这才是底层": 之 / 其 / 所 are variables (之 the purest, being both object pronoun and genitive; 所 binding from the passive direction); 者 is the λ-binder, so 学而时习之者 corresponds strictly to `λx. (learn x ∧ practice x)`, tightening an open expression into a closed predicate; juxtaposition plus 之 is application.

Then the part that matters more. 古文 carries three things untyped λ does not. A *time grain*: 而 is sequential composition with time, not ∘ — 学而时习之 is ordered and non-commutative, and where pure λ-reduction is equivalence, 古文 reduction is process. An *aspect*: 也 is a settling marker, "the configuration has become a fact", and 矣 is perfective — ontological status markers with no λ counterpart. And an *effect*: 令 is a genuine monadic bind, a `let` with world-changing implication. So 古文 ≈ λ-calculus + temporal composition + aspect monad + effect.

And then the deepest point, which is Ren's and appears nowhere in the written drafts: λ-calculus presupposes the separation of syntax from semantics — terms denote, environments evaluate. 古文 never made that separation. 名 is not reference; 名 *is* structure. 正名 is not correcting a description but adjusting a structure. 心即理 pushed to the limit means terms are the things, 算 即是 现象. Curry–Howard–Lambek is a deep *discovery* in the West and a *default* in this tradition. Hence: "古文不是给 λ-calc 加 time，是从一开始就在 Being-time 里运算，从来没把 syntax 抽离出来。λ-calc 是后来的 abstraction；古文是没做这个 abstraction 之前的状态" (`chats/2026-05-04_求导求积与其他势的处理方法`). That is the argument that choosing wenyan particles as the kernel is principled rather than aesthetic — which is exactly the charge the project most needs to answer.

The corresponding division of labour is stated in one line: **"Lean 是 framework verifier / 古文 是 framework 的 *本身*"** — Lean verifies the framework; 古文 *is* the framework. And the methodological axiom that follows: "我认为Chinese already is the framework，因此…我们先找是不是已经有" — when a concept is needed, search whether the language already has it before coining one.

### One primitive, and why the number is one

The ontology side of the program runs on a single move repeated at three scales. Any relational ontology with several primitives exposes circular dependency: 是, 际, 化, 理 each presuppose the others, which proves they are *perspectives on one thing rather than independent primitives* (`jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v0.3.md §I`, restated `v0.9 §I.3`). So there is one.

Which one changed twice, and each change is instructive. **v0.3–v0.8: 間.** Chosen over Process/Event ("still presupposes somewhere it happens"), over Resonance/感 (too dynamic), over Locus/位 (too static) and over Field, because "只有间不偏向任何一极" — only 間 leans to neither pole. The Japanese *ma* aesthetic is invoked as the deepest grasp of it: the 間 between two people in a tea room is at once space, time and their present relation, and "空间-时间-关系不是三件事，是同一个 間 之 三种言说." 涉 was carried alongside as its verb-aspect — 一相二称, one phenomenon in two grammatical guises, since classical Chinese does not presuppose the noun/verb split. **v0.9: 系.** More explicitly relational (it is the stem of 关系), connects to systems theory (系统论), needs no noun-verb pair, one character. Its definitional content is 系含系 — a system's being what it is *is* that it contains systems: `凡系即系系`. This differs from ZFC, where ∈ is a primitive relation that is not itself a set; 文言 builds the ∈-like relation into the primitive. Formally it is the same structure as W-types, the cumulative hierarchy V_α, and the least fixed point μX. F(X). **2026-05-04, in conversation: 一.** "我由思考了一下，根位非事，而是一也" — the root is not 事 but 一 — and then the identification that closes the circuit: "系统，因果互化，不就是我们找的那个一？一即是系统，一切可表达之物皆来自系统."

Two consequences follow from the recursion being *definitional* rather than operational. First, the framework can refuse creation-verbs (造 / 生 / 元) without losing generativity: ontological recursion is not made, it is what it is — which is also why 生生不息's generative cosmology, not an ex-nihilo creation cosmology, is the background. Second, time need not be a second primitive. Inside a 系 there are many processes; one is conventionally designated the standard — a sundial, an atomic oscillation, a heartbeat — and the others measure their pace against it. That designated process *is* 时. Time is therefore a periodic anchoring internal to the primitive: a coinductive stream and a Lamport clock, 庄子's 时机, not Newton's t. This single move is load-bearing, because it is what allows *two* duals rather than three to span the ontology.

### Two duals, and the argument that two is minimum-complete

系含系 plus internal 时 yields four names. **位** is a stable site inside 系 with no intrinsic essence: "凡位之是其位，由其际之凡然定" — a locus is what it is entirely by the totality of its relations. That sentence is the Yoneda lemma written in 文言, and the text refuses to soften it: "这不是隐喻——是同一结构." **际** is the relation between loci, of any arity, and is what 虚词 instantiate at the grammatical level. **化** is 系 evolving along 时. **理** is 系's time-invariant.

The two duals are 位⟷际 (structural) and 化⟷理 (dynamic). The second is the subtler one: 化 and 理 are not process versus structure but *one phenomenon under two views* — a trajectory and its attractor, a dynamical system and its phase portrait, a reaction and its rate equation. The Chinese word 化学 already encodes it, being literally the discipline of the patterns of process. Curry–Howard then falls out directly: `term : type` in type theory *is* `化 : 理` in 文言, a trajectory belonging to an invariant pattern.

The argument that this pair-of-pairs is minimum-complete rather than redundant is structural: the four are mutually defining but not mutually reducible, because (位/际) has no time and (化/理) has no structural site. Only both pairs, plus 时 folded inside 系, span a complete process-structure ontology. The text is honest that this interdefinition is "slightly circular", and says what breaks the circle: the well-foundedness at the bottom of 系含系 — W-type induction, regularity, no infinite ∈-descent — plus case-by-case verification of every cross-domain mapping (`jian-repo/WENYAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §I.2` 限度).

### Idioms are library, not core — and the confession about 八象

Layer 1 plus Layer 2 alone is a Peano-minimum: technically sufficient, engineering-painful, since 修身 would have to expand to 化(位X, 自X, 时T) every time it is said. So Layer 3 supplies idioms that are **可还原但不被还原** — reducible but not reduced — which is exactly the core / standard-library / syntactic-sugar architecture of a programming language. 感 = 化 propagating along 际 (the ontology of 仁, grounded in 易传's 感而遂通天下之故). 修 = 化 applied to self (the ontology of 智; 修身齐家治国平天下 is its recursive base case iterating outward). 约 = 理 with a time bound locked by the act of promising (the ontology of 义; note the self-reference — a commitment constraining future 化 is itself a present 化). 势 = d化/d时, whose *shape* is 理(化), so it is simultaneously differential and structural, and 孙子's 势 — 转圆石于千仞之山者，势也 — corresponds to the modern gradient natively rather than metaphorically. 命 ≡ 类 ≡ 理 under a propositional stance. 数 as a von Neumann construction over 系, never a primitive, with classical warrant from 左传's 物生而后有象，象而后有滋，滋而后有数. And 心 / 性 / 气 admitted as descriptive idioms but barred from adjudication.

The most valuable single moment in the whole series is the demotion of 八象. From v0.6 to v0.9, eight aspects (是 际 感 化 修 约 势 理) were Tier 1, and v0.7 justified the number eight by building a graded architecture 太极 → 两仪 → 四象 → 八象 explicitly isomorphic to the 周易 doubling sequence, saying the isomorphism was "不是巧合". v1.0 reverses it: the eight are four Layer-2 primitives plus four idioms; "基数 8 来自 2³，不来自存在论必然"; and treating them as Tier 1 was "*脚手架借用了周易倍增结构* 的痕迹" — a trace of having borrowed a scaffold. What makes this credible is that v0.7 and v0.8 had already conceded, in their own appendices, that the strict-minimal independent set was four and that eight was chosen for idiomatic foregrounding and engineering centrality. The project caught itself, and the §0.3 avoidance list of the final version now *refuses* the 太极→八卦 doubling scaffold along with 阴阳-as-primitive, post-medieval terminology (体用不二, 理一分殊, 法界缘起), creation verbs, the ambition of a single universal formal system, and 诛心.

### The domain sweep, and what it is evidence of

The payoff layer is a tour of twelve domains, each shown to instantiate the same two duals in native Chinese operational vocabulary, with the claim that *no domain requires a new Layer-2 primitive*. 墨家 supplies the minimal logical vocabulary: 体/兼 (mereology — 体，分于兼也), 同/异 (同，异而俱处于之 — sameness as an alignment relation among distinct co-located items, which is a modern equivalence relation), 类, 故 (cause *and* proof at once), 法 (法，所若而然也 — a rule reliably mapping antecedent to consequent). 同异交得 — sameness and difference mutually derive — is the ground of information-as-distinction, and 墨经's 小故/大故 is necessary-versus-sufficient condition stated flatly. 名家 is a native type-token theory: 白马非马 is not sophistry but the claim that as *types* 白马 ⊊ 马, which is subtyping; 离坚白 yields a bundle theory of substance that directly supports the structuralist reading of 位. 庄子 is the tradition's own self-aware terminus and the framework's interface to its own limits: 言无言 is the Liar/Russell/Gödel family, 得意忘言 is native tool-theory, 齐物论's 彼/是 is frame-relativity, and the engineering consequence drawn is a design rule — always leave an *unclassified* space outside every metric, type and pattern.

Then: Martin-Löf type theory expressed natively (Π as 凡 X : 类_A 者 之 Y(X), Σ as 有…而…, Id as 同 with 自同 as refl, and the observation that 故 carries cause and proof and program-inhabitant in one word, which is a more native articulation of Curry–Howard than English's lexical split). Cybernetics as almost entirely native vocabulary (反 应 调 衡 御 制 节 治 顺 逆 导 守 持 谐 振 稳), with pre-Qin instances in three domains — 孙子's 能因敌变化而取胜者，谓之神 as closed-loop adaptive control, 黄帝内经's 谨察阴阳所在而调之，以平为期 as homeostatic control with target-equals-balance, 韩非's 因/势/威 as a three-stage political loop. Calculus (渐 微 积 累 导 率 速 差 极 趋 势 连), with 祖暅原理 — 缘幂势既同，则积不容异 — as Cavalieri's principle roughly a millennium early. Complex systems (涌 自然 凝 散 临 机 生 克 群 吸 排 混 序 几), where 自然 read literally as *self-so* corresponds directly to self-organization. Ecology with Lotka–Volterra written out. Probability and statistics, with an explicit homonym warning that 信 as confidence and 信 as word-deed alignment must never be conflated. Information theory, where 墨经's 异 is Shannon's bit. And 五行 read strictly as a five-node cybernetic graph: 生 a Hamiltonian cycle of positive edges, 克 a Hamiltonian skip-cycle of negative ones, every node with in-degree two and out-degree two, one of each sign — a mass-balanced, cyclically symmetric negative-feedback ring with internal positive coupling, i.e. a stable oscillatory regime, with 中医's 补/泻/清/温/下 as interventions on topological neighbours.

The distinctive engineering signature extracted from all this is **几工程**: observe the spectrum of 势, detect attractors that are small in magnitude but *rising*, and intervene before the manifest threshold — which is early-warning-signal detection plus pre-emptive intervention, and which 中医's 治未病 states directly, long before the modern literature. The contrast drawn is with the Western monitoring model: something breaks, an alert fires, someone fixes it.

Two disciplines keep this from becoming triumphalism, unevenly. The 五行 section carries an unusually strong fence — not five substances, not a Chinese periodic table, not a universal classification, and any claim of the form "X belongs to 木/火/土/金/水" is idiomatic rather than ontological. And the verification claim is stated with its caveats attached: some cells admit several reasonable mappings, the cross-domain correspondence is structural alignment rather than rigorous bijection, and monism remains a *stipulated position*, not a proof.

### Mathematics as a facet, not a tribunal

The placement of the mathematics part parallel to the domain part, rather than above it, is itself the claim: mathematics is one facet of 文言, not the court that judges it. What follows is a demonstration rather than a foundation. All eight ZFC axioms are written in 文言 (外延 as 同体则同兼, power set as 凡兼，立其体之兼, foundation as the absence of an infinite ∈-chain), with the cumulative hierarchy read as the native articulation of 系含系. MLTT is written at axiomatic depth, with W-types as the type-theoretic form of 系含系 and ℕ ≡ W(Bool, [True ↦ Unit, False ↦ Empty]). Category theory is written with 位 as object, 际 as morphism, 化 as functor, 化之化 as natural transformation, limits and colimits named, adjoints as 互为伴, and Yoneda as the sentence already quoted. The three foundations are then argued to be mutually equivalent modulo caveats — via Lawvere's ETCS, Voevodsky's univalence written as 凡类 A, B. (A 同 B) 同 (A 等价 B), and Curry–Howard–Lambek — and the claim is that *one* structure generates all three, which is offered as the deepest evidence for the monism. Number is constructed at every level, ℕ ⊊ ℤ ⊊ ℚ ⊊ ℝ ⊊ ℂ, with "没有任何阶段 introduces 新的 ontological primitive"; Ren's own one-line version, and the single phrase the drafts attribute to him verbatim in this area, is "数是一个集合的关系."

The completeness claim is then bounded carefully: expressive equivalence is not proof completeness, it holds only modulo encoding choices, it does not cover all of mathematics, and mathematical *practice* may be less efficient in 文言 than in modern notation. That bounding is a reversal — v0.8 and v0.9 had asserted that 八象 was a complete formal foundation with "internal limits: 0".

### The ethical constraint is inside the mechanism, not bolted on

诚 in its full Confucian sense is the coherence of five modalities: what is said (T), what is done (E), the body (B), one's relational standing (R), and inner inclination (M) — equivalently, prediction error across modalities going to zero. But only T and E are third-party observable. Inferring the other three in order to judge someone is **诛心**, which the Confucian, Legalist and Mohist traditions jointly condemned, and which the framework therefore declares *ill-formed*: "凡 *推断之以判定他者内心* 之 epistemic move 在本框架中作 ill-formed 处理."

The engineering consequence is a set of five invariants (`jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §IV.5.8`). The metric is computed on T + E only. It is graded, never Boolean — the system never outputs "P is not sincere", only "P's alignment over window W = x". Observation sovereignty rests with the observed party, who decides which speech and acts are public, because compulsory full disclosure is institutionalised 诛心. B/R/M signals may enter the system only with consent and never enter the judgement; they serve medicine, cultivation support and relationship-building. And high alignment is not correctness: a person can be highly consistent in maintaining a false belief, so alignment is *coherence*, not truth — which Confucian usage itself separates, since 诚 plus 智 gives 明.

Two further moves keep it from becoming a surveillance instrument. Loss of alignment is not failure: plan changes on new information, mid-learning lag between word and act, conflicting commitments, and the process of 修 itself all legitimately produce temporary misalignment. So the sanctioned response to low alignment is *inquiry, not verdict* — "我注意到你之前承诺的与现在的行动之间有距离，是否需要重新约定？" rather than "你失信了", the latter being precisely the 诛心-style judgement. The slogan: **信/诚 是基础设施，不是道德裁判**. And 信/诚 is an asymptotic limit never reached, since full word-deed consistency would require all future acts to match all past words; what is system-visible is not the level but whether the long-run trajectory is slowly rising, and that rise is the observable sign of 修.

The same restriction reappears as a constraint on the vocabulary: 心 and 性 are permitted for self-description and barred from adjudication, because using 心 as a metric *is* 诛心; 气 is kept out of the foundations entirely on account of its accumulated medieval ambiguity.

### The reader is under the same discipline as the system

The epistemic stance is the mirror of the ethical one, and is the most finished writing in the corpus. Reading is 做工夫, not 学知识, and internalisation has four phases. **学**: the symbols enter the ear; you can restate the definitions, but 势 still means only its dictionary sense. **见**: the language becomes an organ of perception; you do not recall the word 势, you *see the meeting as 势*. **化**: reaction rhythm is remodelled — latency, attention direction and the source of discomfort all change; 庖丁's 以神遇而不以目视 is the paradigm. **忘**: the language becomes transparent, 得鱼忘筌; an old doctor does not recall 阴阳 and then judge the pulse, he directly sees an imbalance. The phases are non-linear and domain-local: one may be at 化 in one domain and 学 in another, stress can knock an already-化 reaction back to 学, and 见 and 化 mutually presuppose each other, which is 重重无尽 showing up in practice. "四层是 phase, 不是 milestone." The book explicitly cannot deliver 化 or 忘 — those require 事上磨练 in real relationships, projects, failures and corrections — and offers only 学之材料 and 见之触发.

Then the trap. The subjective experience of "I have seen / I have been changed" is *internally indistinguishable* between the genuine article and the counterfeit: self-intoxication, jargon covering a blind spot, ignorance mistaken for depth. Subjective certainty has no introspective channel for calibration against objective attainment. This is a structural problem, not a moral failing. 王阳明's 致良知 is offered as the answer — not vague moral intuition but something extremely acute at the point a specific thought arises, experienced as a discomfort you cannot lie past: 尔那一点良知，是尔自家底准则…更瞒他一些不得. Ren's own gloss, in conversation: "良知是在具体情境里的一种极其敏锐的、会让你不舒服的东西…所以致良知的工夫,很大程度上是学会不蒙蔽自己."

And the one stable marker: **对自己未看见的敏感 在加深** — deepening sensitivity to what one has *not* seen. The genuine article increasingly discovers that it misread a situation, that it used a beautiful word in place of "I don't know what happens next", that it was enjoying a listener's reaction and that the enjoyment is itself the doorway to self-deception. The counterfeit's trajectory runs the other way: I see deeper, others don't understand, my insight is scarce. Direction is diagnostic, not level. 反自欺 is then the exact mirror of 指月之指: §0.4 admits the limits of outward reference (language cannot exhaust the real), §0.6 the limits of inward reference (introspection cannot exhaust oneself), and the two must be accepted together. The author is placed under his own constraint: read with 诛文不诛心 vigilance, check the text's own coherence between what it says and what it does, and do not trust the author's self-assessment.

### The second track: 間 unfolded downward into shipped code

Parallel to the foundation notes, the same month, a six-document descent runs from metaphysics to reference implementations and holds one discipline throughout. The chain formula, built up incrementally and stated at the end: 理立则气有方，气行则式有据，式定则构有形，构成则仪有节，仪行则器有用，器用则道行于世.

《间》 and its revision 《理：间》 state the ontology: things manifest in 間, and 間 is constituted in the relations among things — "这一句话是这套体系的全部；其余的展开，只是把这句话所已经包含的，逐层让它显出来." A 間 is not a container of actors; an ordinary actor is a 間 whose relational field is minimal and whose reflexivity is weak, so 間, 事 and 機 are three faces of one reality rather than three entities. There is no actor outside a 間 — an actor in no 間 does not exist independently, it does not exist, because existence is relational, exactly as there is no position outside spacetime. There is no single-actor 間, because relation needs two ends, which internalises Heidegger's Mitsein: co-being is the default and solitude is derived. Boundary, visibility and protocol are not attributes but emergent expressions of internal relational state, so the permission system *is* the relation system under another view — 修改权限就是修改关系 — which pushes the object-capability insight one step deeper: ocap says access control is the reference structure, 間 says the reference structure is one view of a deeper relational ground. All 間 are gathered in a cosmic 間 that is not a super-container but the coherence pattern by which any 間 is a 間, which is **理**; hence unity at the level of 理, plurality at the level of 事, hence no global registry, no master coordinator, no universal naming system.

《气》 is the correspondence discipline, and is the single most portable artefact in the corpus: a twenty-row table giving, for each concept, both what it shows up as in code and what it must not be confused with, plus a seven-question self-check. Its concrete tests are what make it usable — if your code can create an empty jian and then add actors to it, that is container thinking and the ontology is already violated, because a 間 is spawned at the moment its first internal interaction is about to occur and its birth and that interaction are the same event. Four things must be **derived, never stored**: caveat, visibility, protocol legitimacy, and jian boundary. The general rule for reification is that an individuated entity with its own boundary, agency and history may be reified, a mode or pattern of ongoing activity may not, and *in the grey zone the default is not to reify*, because a reification error is nearly impossible to repair later. Explicitly non-reifiable: the actor-side 心, relations themselves, 理, the cosmic 間, and substantive identity. Events are the source of truth and state is a cache, so event sourcing is an ontological commitment rather than an architectural preference chosen for audit and replay. There is no privileged "current": any moment is one observational cross-section, causal ordering is reliable, linear time ordering is not. Autopoietic health has three named pathologies — 凝固 (frozen), 耗散 (activity without coherence), 自闭 (zero coupling) — and monitoring should measure these rather than message rate and latency. Say *spawn*, not *create*, because create implies a maker and spawn implies activity generating activity. Priority order is fixed: **paradigm > structure > parameter**; if an optimization requires a paradigm violation, abandon the optimization.

And then the move that matters most for the whole corpus: 《气》 refuses to totalize its own paradigm. The highest leverage point is *transcending* paradigm, so 間 must not become another totalizing attractor — "在某些场景（比如纯 computational tasks、no inter-actor coordination）substance ontology 可能更 fit——这种时候不要 force jian."

《式》 turns this into a process calculus. No existing calculus meets the seven requirements at once (π-calculus makes names ontologically prior to processes; Agha's actor calculus lacks native nesting; Distributed π's locations are syntactic wrappers; Mobile Ambients move ambients rather than have them message; join calculus does not match emergent boundaries; ocap vats fall back to π when formalised). So a configuration is a parallel composition of actors, jians, in-flight messages and events with name binding, and *contains no global state component* — the no-god-view commitment is refused at the level of syntax, and every reduction is local. Messages do not disappear on receipt; they reify into events and enter history, each carrying its own prehension set. Prehension makes history a **DAG, not a timeline**: any linear ordering is a topological sort, there is no canonical linearization, and two observers can disagree about which of two events came first and both be right — the formal counterpart of relativity of simultaneity. Because prehension carries content, a decision's justification is reconstructible without any central audit log. Capability is not a primitive but a forwarder actor: attenuation is a filter in its behaviour and revocation is terminating it. `caveat`, `visible`, `legitimate` and `alive` are predicates over configurations, never syntactic constructs, and the formal test is stated — a property is stored iff it appears in the syntax, derived iff it is a function over the configuration. Liveness requires internal activity, self-production and boundary maintenance in a recency window, and cannot be simulated by a stored flag because the predicate's type signature forces derivation. Perspectival semantics makes the restriction strict: an observer's view is *always* a proper subset, reductions invisible to them leave their view identical though the world changed, and bisimulation is relativized and does not transfer across observers — "这是更 cumbersome 的——但 cumbersomeness 是诚实的." The eight invariants form a mutually reinforcing web in which violating one cascades into the others, which is what one expects if they are facets of a single commitment rather than independent rules.

《构》 lays out five layers and names the four conventional layers that deliberately do not exist: no global state layer, no central control layer, no central registry, no global monitoring. 《仪》 makes the wire protocol *ritual rather than mechanism* — bridge establishment and dissolution are ceremonial, references are path-based and perspectival and are "claim plus proof, not address", event ids are content hashes so two disconnected parties know they mean the same event, and a revoked fetch capability leaves the prehension standing as a historical fact that is no longer traversable, because historical knowledge of a relation is more fundamental than ongoing access. 《器》 commits to *multiple* reference implementations rather than one, and names eight pitfalls that look like reasonable engineering and break the paradigm — stored caveat config, a global registry, synchronous cross-jian calls, a centralized event log "even if just for this deployment", capability as data rather than actor, a stored authoritative current state, bypassing the boundary for performance ("『Trusted』不是 ontological category——everyone is in some jian"), and treating a jian as a namespace.

### The third track: 文 and the algebra that wants to be the substrate

The same month, the project is renamed from 間 to **文**, and a second formal line runs in the opposite direction — from requirements to a structure. Ask what any universal formal substrate must have and you get seven properties: minimum non-trivial structure gives $\mathbb{F}_2$; composition gives direct sum; relations give three bilinear layers; scale gives a squaring tower; self-reference gives Hom-as-content; temporality gives a four-modality carrier; atomic operations give eight. What emerges is the family $R_N = \mathbb{F}_2^N$, and it coincides layer by layer with the 易经 cosmological sequence: 无极 = $R_0$, 太极 = $R_1$, 四象 = $R_2$, 八卦 = $R_3$, spacetime = $R_4$, 六十四卦 = $R_6$, 字/文 = $R_8$, 万物 = $R_\infty$.

The feedback that makes this self-supporting is Hom-as-content: operations on the family are themselves members of it, so nothing external is needed to talk about it. That is the precise sense given to 道法自然, and the exact respect in which ZFC (needing first-order logic), type theory (needing a kernel) and category theory (needing a metalanguage) are said to fall short. The recursion terminates where it names itself: below $R_8$ there is not enough structure to carry a symbol system, at $R_8$ = 256 there is, so $R_8$ is the level at which the substrate can articulate itself — and the project takes its name, 文, from that level.

On top sits the information result, which is the most defensible mathematics in the corpus. Information conservation has two senses: linear (non-degeneracy of the pairing) and block-typed (component-wise recovery with no cross-block communication). Only *even* $N$ has both, because "half an $R_2$" — a lone bit with no dual axis — has no typed identity. Hence odd ranks are IR-internal and never surface, which corrects the earlier picture of a nine-level tower: only $R_0, R_2, R_4, R_6, R_8$ are interfaces. And 64 and 256 stop being mysterious: 64 = $V_4^3$ and 256 = $V_4^4$, so the basic unit of measure is the three Klein coordinates, not the six lines. The $V_4$ note contributes the sharpest single result: of the Klein four-group's elements only $\{e, 错综\}$ is a structure-preserving subgroup, since 错 and 综 are each anti-homomorphisms by De Morgan while their composite is a homomorphism — so 错综 should be promoted to a primary concept rather than derived, and it is the algebraic form of the contrapositive law. An independent argument from the state level (which functionals survive a group action: exactly the annihilator, with preserved dimension $8 - \dim H$) lands on the same subgroup, and that double evidence is the kind a referee actually wants.

The programming-language claim follows from the algebra rather than being added to it: because the tower's even layers decompose canonically, whether an operation conserves or dissipates information is readable off its type. `split`/`merge` conserve as isomorphisms; projections lose a factor with an explicit fibre size; lifts are marked dangerous; and ⊠ — gate-closed — is a first-class 256→1 collapse that is irreversible, distinct from Kleene's *undefined* and Belnap's *both*, and modelling real hardware failure modes (leakage, erasure, gauge mismatch) that every existing quantum language treats as an error. The design philosophy is named honestly: **结构化贫困**, structural poverty — deliberately restricting what is expressible in order to obtain properties by construction, the same trade Rust made with ownership. No-cloning falls out structurally, since a duplication map cannot be an isomorphism when the codomain carries twice the information. And the rescue invariant is 格物先于诚意 pushed into the type system: inner-layer work on a collapsed cell fails at compile time.

Then the corpus criticises itself, in the document that is the strongest signal of its health. The v0.8 revision notes state the problem in one sentence: **claim strength exceeded proof strength**. What had been shown was that R-Family can express many formal structures, corresponds deeply to several traditions, and is a candidate universal substrate; what had been claimed was that all formal systems *must* be so expressed and that "formal" *means* R-Family-articulable. The reformulation keeps the ambition and fixes the calibration — "R-Family is the minimal universal normal form of formal articulation", where minimal means not the smallest alphabet but the smallest closed generative grammar, and where competing substrates satisfying the same closure conditions are *equivalent* rather than excluded. Claim Z is split into a Z-Principle (a methodological injunction: to formalize is to seek an R-Family articulation) and a Z-Conjecture (a stateable, falsifiable mathematical claim). Specific errors are itemised — Hom is a canonical representation after a basis choice, not a literal equality; direct sum is the minimal additive composition, not the only natural one; $R_4$ carries a ring structure only once multiplication is chosen; $R_{2n}$ is the Pauli *label* space, not the Hilbert space; Gödel and the halting problem are about unbounded self-reference inside a finite symbol system, not about needing an uncountable $R_\infty$. A high-risk vocabulary substitution table replaces *is* with *can be articulated as* and *forced* with *motivated*. And the closing line is changed from "the foundation is complete; the lineage closes" to **其语法已显，其工程始行** — the grammar has been articulated; the programme begins. The document's own summary: **定理已立，证责已明**.

### The bridge nobody wrote down: why a monist language would be selected

The written drafts justify the language claim structurally. In conversation, on 2026-05-05, Ren gave it a *causal history*, and this argument appears in none of the drafts.

Posit an ideal 古文 in which every character is constructible from the same one concept plus formal-logic operators — that is what 一元 means, 一生万物. Pre-Qin 古文 is an *approximation* of that ideal, and refined it can serve as the skeleton of a true text. Why would such an approximation exist at all? Because it is an attractor of communication: where many groups with different conceptual sets must understand one another, the symbols that survive are those whose concepts are logically traceable to a common root, since those have the strongest consensus property and are the maximally robust convergence direction of a language. Wherever environments are complex enough and intelligences must communicate and cooperate, that attractor emerges.

He then corrected his own premise twice, which is what makes the argument worth keeping. First: "先秦的我的表达有误 - 我不知道具体多早，egalitarian也是我的假设." Second, and substantively: the condition is not egalitarianism but **和 rather than 争** — "一旦争，则'我对'，我大于理；合作才讲理，斗则讲赢." Under contention the selection pressure runs on winning, and the self outranks the reasoning; only under cooperation does the pressure run on being understood. The consequence for the metaphysics is stated flatly, against postmodernism: 道 is of course also a construct, but it is not *only* a construct — "道非其他，它存在于心外." The consequence for the politics is that 大同 stops being a utopia and becomes a mechanism: a direction in which language and existence automatically manifest toward 理, toward 善, toward 生生不息. And the consequence for evolution is the mirror argument: lineages that optimise for 斗 go extinct when the environment shifts, because 斗-directed traits are not 生 and have low adaptability.

One more clarification, which prevents the whole thing from reading as monoculture: "我说的一元当然不是基数，是所有概念回归同一root，很多宗教也是，只是回归了神，回归了空，回归了斗，回归了灭." Monism is not about cardinality; it is about *which* root everything returns to, and religions are monist too — they just return to god, to emptiness, to struggle, to extinction. Hence the formulation that closes the loop: **一生万物即是真理，乃多元** — one generating the ten thousand things is the truth, and it is pluralism. Monism at the root *is* plurality in the world.

### The counterexamples the sources raise, and their answers

*"Near-global-optimum is overreach."* Raised by the project against itself. Answer: three caveats are attached rather than a defence mounted — trained-receiver conditioning, demonstrated procedural lossiness with historical evidence, and loss-function dependence — and the claim is narrowed accordingly. This is the right shape of answer, but it leaves the claim asserted rather than measured; see the open questions.

*"The 八象 are just the eight trigrams smuggled in."* Raised in v1.0 §IV.10. Answer: conceded and confessed. 8 = 2³, from the doubling scaffold, not from ontological necessity; the eight are demoted from foundation to vocabulary, and only four Layer-2 names are minimum-complete.

*"Layer 2's four concepts define each other circularly."* Answer: the circle is grounded by well-foundedness at the bottom of 系含系, and the cross-domain mappings must be checked case by case rather than taken as proof.

*"Cross-domain isomorphism is 六经注我 — reading your doctrine into everything."* Answer: partially conceded. The mappings are argued to be native, but the text admits several cells admit multiple reasonable mappings, that the alignment is structural rather than bijective, and that monism is stipulated rather than proved.

*"Does grammar-alignment prove the ontology true?"* Answer: explicitly no. Grammar alignment shows only that 文言 is suited to articulating this ontology.

*"A coherence metric is a surveillance instrument."* Answer: the five invariants, graded output, observation sovereignty, consent-gating of the unobservable modalities, the coherence≠truth separation, and inquiry-not-verdict.

*"How would anyone know they have internalised this rather than learned the jargon?"* Answer: they cannot know by introspection. The only stable marker is whether sensitivity to one's own unseen is increasing, and direction rather than level is diagnostic. The author is placed under the same test — which also means the framework's central quality question is unanswerable in the present, a cost the text accepts.

*"Does 五行 commit you to a cosmology?"* Answer: an explicit fence keeps the graph and refuses the cosmology, declaring every "X belongs to 木/火/土/金/水" claim idiomatic.

*"Is this cultural chauvinism?"* Answer, from v0.3 onward: no view from nowhere is claimed, the framework is offered as an extension of one tradition, co-authors from other backgrounds are asked for, and the misreading is expected and accepted as a cost. Ren's own version is blunter and older: "这不是文化复兴。如果有更准的语言形式，我们就用那个。古文 happens to be 现在能找到的最准" and "间是本体精确性选择，不是文化身份."

*"Continuous mathematics is not natively $\mathbb{F}_2$."* Answer: encoding is being confused with articulation; reals live in the inverse limit, which is Cantor space, and IEEE-754's misbehaviour is a property of one encoding.

*"Neural networks are not R-Family."* Answer: networks are an implementation technique; the cognitive functions computed are computable and therefore articulable — the training method is engineering, not foundation.

*"Qualia are not formal."* Answer: conceded and welcomed. The claim is delimited to formality; whether phenomenal experience is formally describable is a different question. (Note the tension: v0.8 of the earlier line had argued the opposite, that under 心物不二 qualia *are* 涉 and therefore inside.)

*"Other substrates might be equivalent."* Answer: fully conceded — anything satisfying all seven properties *is* the same structure under isomorphism, and "R-Family" is only the name for it.

*"You have not proven completeness."* Answer: v0.7 said foundational claims are demonstrated by articulation rather than proved; v0.8 disagrees with v0.7 and demands the theorem be stated with its nine proof obligations visible. The later answer is the better one.

### B. The grammar claim — 文言 as the surface syntax of the ontology

17. **文言 is not stylistically suited as a reasoning vehicle — it is the native surface syntax of the (位⟷际, 化⟷理) layer.** The project marks this as its most novel part. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III`

18. **实词 are polymorphic primitives dispatched by position**: 王天下 (verb mode = 化), 天下王 (noun mode = 位), 王道 (adjectival = 理); 君君臣臣 puts 位 and 化 of one character adjacent. Functional polymorphism is not grammatical looseness — it builds 化 directly into the grammar primitives. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.1`; `WENYAN §I.3`

19. **虚词 are typed relational connectives** carrying relation-type and no semantic content: 之 (genitive edge), 於/于 (locative/comparative), 以 (instrumental/causal), 而 (polarized conjunctive), 則 (conditional), 也 (terminal predication), 者 (nominalizer), 乎 (interrogative/locative), 矣 (perfective), 焉 (locative-pronominal). This is 际 instantiated at the grammatical layer. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.2`; `WENYAN §I.3, §II.2`

20. **理 emerges from composition, it is not declared.** 文气 and 章法 are emergent pattern, structurally isomorphic to Hindley–Milner type inference; a trained reader recognising 文气 *is* doing type inference. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.3`

21. **Indo-European languages are nominal typing; 文言 is structural typing.** IE encodes syntactic role in morphology so the word carries its own type; 文言 leaves the type to positional dispatch. This is a categorical difference, not a stylistic one — the Go/TypeScript-versus-Java/Haskell debate staged by two language families millennia earlier. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.4`

22. **虚词 are ontologically reducible to 际 patterns and retained only as syntax sugar**; full elimination is possible in principle but explodes expression length 10–100×. Classical function words are sophisticated linguistic technology encoding common relational patterns as single characters. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v0.7.md §V`; `v0.8 §VIII`

23. **文言 is information-theoretically near-globally-optimal for the dense expression of relational/processual phenomena**, on three grounds: source-encoding match, compression density (道德经's 5,000 characters need ~10× the length in modern English and still lose nuance), and primitive polymorphism as minimal description length. [conditional] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.5`

That optimality is **explicitly conditioned**: it is trained-receiver-dependent; it is lossy for procedural specification, with Ming–Qing 考证学 conventions and modern bisyllabic technical vocabulary as the historical evidence; and it depends on the loss function chosen. [conditional] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §III.5`; `WENYAN §I.3`

### C. The operator kernel and the λ-calculus correspondence

40. **The kernel must be a pure formal kernel and simultaneously complete** — purity and completeness demanded together, not traded off; and minimality is found *through* the attempt at a completeness proof, which is itself the search procedure. [design direction] — `chats/2026-05-01_古文字的编码结构与动态所指`

41. **Kernel-first release doctrine**: implement the kernel first, then extend continuously; the minimal kernel can describe everything but is not convenient; the minimal kernel is already the MVP. Hence a batch-1 / batch-2 / batch-3 release order. [design direction] — same

42. **Compound characters must be split into operator components** — 不如 is two operators, 不 → negation and 如 → structural/relational similarity — and many characters treated as "synonymous variants" are in fact not quite the same. [mechanism] — same

43. **Validation is by reverse test**: express λ-calculus operators *in* 文言 operators, and admit any character into the kernel that expresses something accurately. [mechanism] — same

### D. Layer 1 — the single primitive

59. **一切是间。間 是唯一原语。間 即 happening, 即 connection, 即 function evaluation.** The founding sentence, unchanged v0.3→v0.8. [mechanism] — `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v0.3.md §I`

## What was lost along the way

Ren's standing complaint about lossy consolidation applies to his own series more sharply than to anything else in the corpus. The rewrite pattern — v0.3 reorganise → v0.6 radical simplification → v0.8 ground-up rewrite → v0.9 primitive replacement → v1.0 reframe → WENYAN re-titling — deletes something real at every step.

1. **間 as the primitive, with its whole justification.** The comparison of candidate words, the 只有间不偏向任何一极 argument, the 門中見日 etymology, and the *ma* tea-room image where space, time and relation are three sayings of one 間. Gone at v0.9. A comparison table survives; the aesthetic argument does not.
2. **涉 and the 一相二称 doctrine** — one phenomenon in noun-aspect and verb-aspect, with the argument that classical Chinese does not presuppose the noun/verb split. Gone at v0.9. Ironically the *same* insight returns at JIAN v1.0 §III.1 as 实词 polymorphism, but the 間/涉 formulation of it is lost, and with it Ren's own gloss that 涉 is input → change → output, i.e. a function.
3. **The 124/146-character core vocabulary in eighteen or nineteen semantic categories,** with its careful sense distinctions (力/能/权; 有/无/在/存; 源 vs 始; 归 vs 还; 中 vs 内 vs 子; 前后 vs 来往; 思/虑/知/觉/悟/省/见/观/念/明/迷/忘; 言/默 with 默契). From v0.6 it is compressed into a derivation table, and by v0.9 it survives only as a promised appendix — "138-字 derivation `[carry from v0.9 Appendix C]`" — whose content is never re-articulated in three successive documents. **This is the single largest silent loss in the series.**
4. **The 心性情志(欲) inner-subject vocabulary as a six-character indivisible set** — 这六字一起，有则全有，无则全无 — reduced to a restricted 心/性 idiom barred from engineering judgement, with 情/意/志/欲 dropped entirely.
5. **体/用/本/末.** A core pair from v0.3 to v0.8 (protocol vs instance, character vs behaviour, interface vs implementation as one thing at two levels) and the backbone of the Eastern-engineering interface design. v1.0 §0.3 explicitly avoids 体用不二 as a post-medieval term colliding with the framework's primitives — defensible, but it deletes a working engineering concept without replacing it. Note that Ren himself had conceded the point in the other direction: "你对体用的frame要比我好，但是这两个字，本身我感觉即是多义."
6. **Homoiconicity: code = data = log = documentation = spec.** Fully argued in v0.3 §V and extended in v0.8 §VII.4 with five readings of one expression. Absent from both v1.0 documents — even though Ren states it in his own voice in April: "这个语言本身，就是'编程' 也是数据，数据流可以直接以此表示."
7. **The whole engineering apparatus of v0.3–v0.8**: the identity system (Ed25519/DID, composite IDs, calendar IDs, spoofing defence by signature), append-only storage, query-as-happening, relative time arithmetic, the parsable BNF subset of 古文, the disambiguation rules, the S-expression fallback, the four-phase Clojure prototype path, and the stack recommendations. WENYAN defers all of it to a document that does not exist.
8. **Layer-3 detection algorithms** — density/KDE-based 势 detection with persistence, strength and duration verification; local-Lyapunov 机 detection; 轨 construction with velocity and short-term prediction; discontinuity-based 节 detection; and the counter-势 intervention protocol in which the system detects and proposes but **humans decide what is unhealthy**. That last point is a governance commitment, and it vanishes with the algorithms.
9. **The failure model as ontology**: 失/塞/误/复 as ordinary happenings, no try/catch, no happy-path/error-path split, and 复 = 修 之 反失 — recovery is cultivation reversed — with 无咎不是无失，是有失能复. Survives to v0.8; gone from v1.0 and WENYAN.
10. **是之生灭 — the full life cycle**: sponsorship-based birth, identity by history-continuity rather than manifest similarity, four kinds of death, the awakening case of a long-dormant identity reactivating, and the post-death regime in which history stays quotable, memorials happen, and an unfulfilled 志 can be inherited by a successor's new 约. Entirely absent from v1.0 and WENYAN.
11. **The Byzantine and conflict scenarios**: multi-witness verification, cross-identity cross-checking, 信 as a tracked quantity moving 0.7 → 0.3, suspected-dishonest marking, the explanation-and-audit path back to restored trust, concurrent-edit merge, and federation. Note the tension: v0.5 tracks a numeric trust score attached to a person, which v1.0's invariants would substantially constrain, and the later framework never revisits the earlier scenario to say what replaces it.

13. **The graded architecture 太极→两仪→四象→八象 as an *evaluation procedure*** for candidate new aspects — is this a missing fundamental dimension, in which case restructure to sixteen, or is it derivable, in which case Tier 2? The renunciation of the scaffold is principled, but the procedure for evaluating proposed additions goes with it and nothing replaces it.

## Open questions and critiques

Citation abbreviations as in the Claims section: **`jian-repo/`** = `08-jian-wen-language/jian-repo/`; **`dl/`** = `08-jian-wen-language/downloads-2026-05/`; **`chats/`** = `12-claude-ai-chats/`.

### Raised inside the corpus

1. **Minimality was never proved, only graded.** v0.6 flags it as its own limit 1; v0.7 answers with the 太极→两仪→四象→八象 doubling scaffold, which makes eight a level in a differentiation sequence rather than a count; and then v0.7 附录 F and v0.8 §III.4 concede in their own appendices that 修/约/势/理 are specialisations of 化 plus multiplicity, that the strict-minimal set is four, and that eight was chosen for idiomatic foregrounding and engineering centrality. v1.0 §IV.10 accepts the concession and demotes 八象 — **基数 8 来自 2³，不来自存在论必然** (*the cardinality eight comes from 2³, not from ontological necessity*). The concession is three versions older than the correction. (`jian-repo/…v0.6.md`, `…v0.7.md 附录 F`, `…v0.8.md §III.4`, `…v1.0.md §IV.10`)
2. **Derivations are not unique.** v0.6 flags it as limit 2; v0.7 reframes non-uniqueness as *perspective* and adds a canonical-form rule (fewest hops, tie-break by foregrounded aspect, document the alternatives); v0.8 resolves it by polysemy splitting, one derivation per *sense*. The reframe is coherent, and it means the framework cannot claim a unique decomposition of its own vocabulary. (`jian-repo/…v0.6.md`, `…v0.7.md`, `…v0.8.md`)
3. **Verification is empirical, not exhaustive** (`jian-repo/…v0.7.md §VI.7`): common usages were checked and edge cases may have been missed; the derivations require a reader who has already internalised the framework; and 心/性/情/志 carry millennia of cultural weight that an ontological derivation strips.
4. **Quantity words resist purely compositional derivation** (`jian-repo/…v0.7.md §VI.5`): 计/量/序/比/平 need an operational rather than a structural definition, and v0.8 papers over the gap with 数 ⊂ 际.
5. **Contemporary domains were never stress-tested** (`jian-repo/…v0.7.md §XIV.6`): the verification sample is the framework's own earlier vocabulary, so bioengineering, AI training and quantum-physics concepts may break it.
6. **The near-optimality claim is bounded three times in the same section** (`jian-repo/…v1.0.md §III.5`; `WENYAN §I.3`): trained-receiver conditioning, demonstrated lossiness for procedural specification with the Ming–Qing 考证学 evidence attached, and loss-function dependence. Bounding it is the right move, and it leaves the claim asserted rather than measured.
7. **Grammar alignment does not prove the ontology true** (`WENYAN §I.3 限度`): **Ontology grammar alignment 不证 ontology truth；只证 文言 适合 articulate 此 ontology** (*grammar–ontology alignment does not prove the ontology true; it proves only that 文言 is suited to articulating it*). An unusually clean piece of self-limitation.
8. **Monism is a stipulated position, not a proof** (`WENYAN §I.5`, `§II.13 限度`): a different choice of lens could yield different mappings, and the cross-domain correspondence is structural alignment rather than rigorous bijection.

### Found by reading

33. **The three tracks disagree about the four roots, and it is not a naming variation.** `dl/jian-ontology-v7.md` / `v7.1.md` make 事 the sole ground with 四本 = 物 / 動 / 間 / **心**; `dl/wen-substrate (1).md`, `dl/wen-formal (1).md` and `dl/wen-algebra (4).md` put 物 / 動 / 間 / **事** in the $R_4$ Mian matrix and 心 does not appear at all. Either 事 is the ground *or* it is one aspect among four; it cannot be both. And if 心 is genuinely irreducible (法则 1, 法则 17), a sixteen-cell phenomenology matrix without it cannot be complete. This is the disagreement `MAP.md` names when it calls the theory "split across three disagreeing tracks", and nothing downstream of either track is safe until it is settled.
34. **The two tracks also disagree about the 征 set.** R-Family: 幾 / 勢 / 機 / **時** (limit, momentum, event, halt). v7: 幾 / 勢 / 機 plus 萌 / 绪 / 韵, with 時 nowhere in the list — 艮 / fixpoint maps to 時 in R-Family and to nothing in v7. So the 4×4 Mian matrix and the 六显 × 六征 structure are not one object under two names, which is how they are read.
35. **The atomic bases differ, and neither document acknowledges the other's.** R-Family's atoms are the eight trigrams read as ⊤ / ⊥ / rewrite / compose plus momentum / fixpoint / limit / event; v7's algebraic bottom is the commutative semiring 合 / 和 / 寂 / 空 / 无.
36. **心 is simultaneously non-reifiable and a first-class type.** 《气》 §3 says the actor-side 心 must have no first-class API, no data structure and no capability — **试图 represent 心，就是把它从 immediacy 中拉出来** (*to try to represent 心 is to pull it out of immediacy*). `dl/jian-ontology-v7.md` makes 心 an ontic root with a reading operator 觉, an invariant 明, three manifestations, three dynamic marks, two internal operators and a canonical inhabitant 吾. 法则 17 (every projection out of 心 is lossy) is a weaker claim than "no representation at all" and does not resolve the conflict. Any implementation of this theory has to pick a side, and no document picks one.
37. **The 間 → 文 rename is undocumented on the 間 side.** `dl/wen-handoff.md §0` states flatly **已更名为「文」——不要再用 Jian 这个旧名** (*renamed to 文; do not use the old name Jian*), yet `dl/jian-ontology-v7.1.md`, a later and more developed document, is titled 「間/Jian 本体框架 v7.1」 throughout. Either the rename applies only to the programming-language track, or v7.1 predates it, or it did not hold. Nothing says which, and the canonical document above inherits the ambiguity in its own title.
38. **The anti-totalization commitment and Claim Z contradict each other, and nobody notices the ethical half.** 《气》 §8 insists the 間 paradigm must not be treated as a universal solution — which is the corpus's own highest value, refusing totalizing attractors. Claim Z says formality *is* R-Family articulability by definition. `dl/r_family_v_0_8_revision_notes.md` repairs the epistemic half of this (the claim was over-stated); the ethical half is untouched. A project whose stated ethics is anti-totalization produced a totalizing claim about the space of formal systems.
39. **The anti-registry commitment sits oddly with a universal substrate.** 《间》 refuses a global registry, a master coordinator, a universal naming system and any god view; wen-substrate proposes one universal formal substrate into which everything formal translates. These are compatible in principle — a substrate is not a registry — but nothing argues the compatibility, and the two documents' rhetoric pulls hard in opposite directions.
40. **The Chinese-character thesis is not load-bearing in the R-Family core.** `dl/wen-algebra (4).md` Rule 10 and `dl/wen-formal (1).md` §0.2 exclude all traditional terminology from the core type system and freeze the surface as `o`/`x` bit strings, with characters returning only as opt-in application-layer aliases. That is good epistemic hygiene, and it quietly removes the project's distinctive claim — that Chinese characters are the right carriers — from the part of the system actually being built. Both documents' own open questions keep circling back to it.
41. **The project's only concrete ethical safeguard is orphaned in a deprecated file.** The complete statement of 反诛心 — the five modal classes, the T+E restriction, the five I-信 invariants, the misalignment-legitimacy list, the inquiry-not-verdict protocol, the 五德 table — exists only in `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §IV.5`, which WENYAN v1.0 marks deprecated; WENYAN §I.4.5 keeps the concept and defers the invariants to a document that does not exist.
42. **B/R/M is defined two different ways.** `jian-repo/JIAN_FOUNDATION_NOTE_v1.0.md §IV.5.4` defines the three unobservable modalities as 所体 (body signals), 所处 (relational standing) and 所向 (inner inclination); `WENYAN §I.4.5` redefines them as belief / reasoning / motivation. These are different partitions of the unobservable with different consent implications — body signals are physically measurable and can be consent-gated, beliefs are not measurable at all — and the difference must be reconciled before anything is implemented.
43. **The historical-priority numbers are inconsistent.** `jian-repo/…v0.9.md 附录 F.6` says 五行 predates Wiener by ~3000 years; `WENYAN §II.12` says ~2300. Both are stated flatly. Similar round numbers ("~2300 年" before Shannon, "~2500 年" before Scheffer, "~1100 年" before Cavalieri) appear without derivation, and they will be the first thing a hostile reader attacks.
44. **Several classical citations should be verified before publication.** 「命也者，承天而出」 attributed to 中庸 一 (`WENYAN §I.4.6`) does not match 中庸's opening, which is 天命之谓性; the 庄子·秋水 passage quoted at `WENYAN §I.1` to support 时机 does not in fact concern timeliness; 「读文以气为主」 attributed to 韩愈《答李翊书》 is a paraphrase rather than a quotation; and the attributions to 王阳明《拔本塞源论》, 韩非子, 吕氏春秋·知度 and 韩非子·八说 all warrant checking. Since the project's entire warrant is philological recovery, one mis-citation is disproportionately damaging.
45. **The 修身 example lost its metric and then its content.** v0.5/v0.6 carried rich narrative cultivation cases; `jian-repo/…v1.0.md §VII.5` converts them into an alignment trajectory (0.62 → 0.81 over six months) hedged with the inquiry-not-verdict rule; `WENYAN §IV.5` reduces the entry to 修身（qualitative，无 alignment metric）. The project now has no worked example of its own central practice.
46. **The 五行 fence may not hold.** `WENYAN §II.12` keeps the feedback graph and disclaims the cosmology, and the 中医 subsection §II.12.7 immediately reasons "if X's 生 is too strong, add more of the node that 克 X" — which requires substantive assignments of things to the five nodes, exactly the "X belongs to 木/火/土/金/水" move the section forbids two pages later.
47. **v0.5's trust-score tracking contradicts the v1.0 invariants, and nothing revisits it.** `jian-repo/…v0.5.md §12.5, §14.13` track a person's 信 as a number falling 0.7 → 0.3 and mark them `suspected_dishonest`. Under I-信-2 (never output "P is not sincere") and I-信-3 (observation sovereignty) that scenario is disallowed — but no later version says what replaces it, and the reputation-Gini health indicator at `…v1.0.md §VI.10` raises the same question again.
48. **Structural typing is asserted as a categorical difference where the evidence supports a strong tendency.** Classical Chinese does carry some morphological and phonological role-marking (使动/意动 usage, 破读 / 四声别义, the 者/所 nominalisers themselves), and Indo-European languages permit substantial zero-derivation (English *water*, *table*, *fast*). The claim would be stronger stated as a gradient with quantitative support than as a categorical opposition — and the gradient version is enough to carry the grammar argument.
49. **The information-theoretic argument is asserted and never computed.** "MDL 约趋近 Kolmogorov 复杂度下界" is supported by a table of qualitative high/medium/low density ratings. A corpus experiment — compressed-length comparison of the same content in 文言, modern Chinese and English, or a held-out prediction test — is both possible and absent, and it would convert the project's most distinctive claim from assertion into evidence.
50. **No prototype ever appears, so the project's own criterion has never been applied to itself.** Every version from v0.3 to v0.9 lists a Clojure prototype as the next step; WENYAN v1.0 reclassifies the whole engineering layer as future work. The framework's stated test of a formalism is 功用 — use — and it has never been run on the framework.
51. **"Recovery, not invention" plus priority claims invites the retrojection charge.** Reading 墨经's 故 as Curry–Howard, 五行 as a feedback graph and 白马非马 as subtyping are modern reconstructions; the sections' own 限度 notes acknowledge textual obscurity and interpretive dispute, but the headline sentences ("这不是隐喻——是同一结构", "predates Wiener by 2300 years") do not carry the hedge. Recovery framing plus priority claims is precisely the combination that draws the accusation the project most wants to avoid.
52. **The relationship between the two written clusters is undeclared.** Nothing in `jian-repo/` says how it relates to `dl/` — whether jian-ontology v7.x precedes or postdates v0.9, and whether the wen-* algebra is the formal core the foundation notes keep promising. The two lines were written in the same month and neither cites the other.
53. **The mathematical foundation of the programming-language claim is missing from the archive.** `dl/wen-handoff.md`, `dl/wen-layered.md` and `dl/wen-info-paradigm.md` all cite a `wen-formal.md` with §1 爻空间, §2.1 偶倍分解定理, §3 道-集/道-依赖族, §4 位翻转, §5 闭包定理, §6 稳定层, §7 四值模态, §9 塌缩不可逆, §10 文-代数原子 and appendices A–B. The file present under that name (`dl/wen-formal (1).md`) is the $R_0$–$R_8$ layer spec, which is a different document. The cited one is not in the corpus, and the whole information-conservation section above rests on it. **This is the theory's highest-priority retrieval task.**
54. **Two different documents are called `v4-foundation.md`.** The one present (`dl/v4-foundation (1).md`, v0.2, 2026-05-12) is the Klein four-group note; `dl/wen-formal (1).md` says it was renamed *from* `v4-foundation.md`, and `dl/wen-algebra (4).md §11.3` lists `v4-foundation.md` as the "$R_0$–$R_8$ 单层 spec, v0.5". The distance between the corpus's confidence and its executed verification is large, and `dl/r_family_v_0_8_revision_notes.md` is the only document that registers it.

There is no end-to-end program, no derived-not-stored caveat implemented, no bridge negotiated, no hexagram orbit counted, and no social or cultivational example after v0.9 replaced them with physical-science demonstrations.
57. **The load-bearing counts do not agree across the record.** The written sources give **9** (Ren's own wenyan-operator kernel, `dl/wen-handoff.md §0`, confirmed as pre-existing at `dl/jian-ontology-v7.1.md §−1.1`) and **15** (Tier −1 = Ren's nine plus six added by the draft); the 三本 architecture attached to `chats/2026-05-04_求导求积与其他势的处理方法` has Tier 1 at exactly **14** characters, and one synthesis pass concluded that a fourteen-glyph core "does not appear anywhere". It appears in the conversation, not in the drafts — which is the general pattern, not an isolated slip.

Against 《间》's engagement with 王阳明, 华严, Whitehead and Bateson this is thin, and 「协而不同」 is not the standard phrase — 论语's is 和而不同, which v7 uses correctly.

60. **The qualia reversal is a straight contradiction that no document mentions.** v0.8 argued that under 心物不二 qualia *are* 涉 — happenings, not descriptions of them — and therefore fall inside the framework; the R-Family line concedes qualia as outside scope and welcomes the concession. The drafts are the weaker half of the theory.
62. **The project has not met its own standard, and Ren said so first.** On 2026-05-04 his verdict on a draft was **太蛇头虎尾了法则也很糊弄，完全不完整** — *snake's head, tiger's tail; the rules are perfunctory; completely incomplete*. Every track is strong at the head and thin at the tail, and the standard for judging that is already in Ren's own words.

63. **The 止戈为武 example (added 2026-10-02).** Ren's spoken illustration of the inter-tribal selection history is the 左传 gloss on 武. On most paleographic readings it is a moral reading of the graph, not its etymology. Any public use of the selection history should drop it as evidence, or present it as the tradition's self-reading, which supports the culture of 和 but not the history of the script. *2026-10-04.* The example recurs in the Soi Si Lom recording [22:14], introduced as 「中国字的构成」, and the community document 《从小康到大同》 v0.3 §三 states it as the original meaning ("武的本义，是止息兵戈"). Every use should carry the caution, which costs one clause; the document's line should read as the tradition's self-understanding (for example 《左传》以「止戈为武」解「武」).

64. **心理 read as 心 and 理 (added 2026-10-04).** The talk applies to a modern word the method the corpus uses for 止戈为武 and for 仁 read as 人 + 二 (`relational-self-and-datong`): it reads 心理 as 心 and 理 in one dynamic [00:39]. As with 武, this is a constructive reading. It is evidence of how Ren reads the language, not of the word's history; 心理 in the sense of psychology came into modern Chinese with the translation term 心理学, probably coined in Japan in the 1870s (西周) and adopted in Chinese around 1900, which should be checked before publication. The theory should keep a list of its constructive glosses and mark each one, so that none is later cited as etymology.

65. **Western categories for this ontology (added 2026-10-04).** At [22:46] Ren says "ontology" and "epistemology" cannot precisely capture what is here. Yet this theory, and his own speech of 2026-10-02 ("use the the pre-Qin ontology as the basis"), call the 間 work an ontology. The asymmetric-scrutiny rule applies to analytic categories as well as political ones. A version of the theory addressed to readers of 文言 should either say what the Western category loses or use 間's own terms.
