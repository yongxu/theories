# How Ren works, and what only the memories record

**What this is.** Every other folder in `theories/` documents a claim about the world. This one documents the person making the claims and the machinery he makes them with — and it is also the salvage yard. Between 2026-03 and 2026-09, two claude.ai memory exports and twelve Claude Code project-memory folders accumulated a second-order record of Ren's practice: what he was working on, what he ruled, and what he rejected. That record turns out to be the only surviving trace of a dozen theory-bearing projects that never became documents, and the *primary* record of a discipline of thought and writing that he enforced session after session against collaborators — human and machine — that kept drifting away from it. Two things run through it and hold it together. The first is a single substantive commitment restated in a dozen successive vocabularies between June and September 2026 — keep the possibility space open, keep the future revisable, keep the world generating, maintain variety, do not thin the plurality toward one — each version rejected by Ren for being too poetic, too defensive, too vague, too reduced, or too dead. The second is a *method* commitment that turns out to be the same commitment applied to the writing itself: never reduce to one, never silently drop, never let a rule stack replace judgment, never let defensive armour replace breathing prose, never let the compression stand in for the thinking. Read this document before working with him. It is the closest thing the corpus has to an operating manual, and its most useful section is the second.

Citations are paths relative to `/Users/ren/theories/sources/`. Reading notes cited as `_process/reading-notes/out-*.md #n` sit outside `sources/` and are marked as such. Two Claude Code folder names are CJK repository paths sanitized to runs of hyphens: `claude-code-----` and `claude-code--------` both belong to the `~/repos/生生不息` (SSBX) work.

## Working method

The unit of production is not a draft. It is a cycle with four moves, and the same cycle runs whether the object is a philosophical essay, a Lean development or a TypeScript kernel.

**It starts with a raw note that knows it is raw.** The clearest specimen in the corpus is `01-writings-2025/compound_selfishness.md` (2025-02-07), a page and a half of unedited English carrying the entire selfishness spiral: environment constructs the frame, selfishness gets rewarded and the reward compounds, winners rewrite the narrative, the next generation takes it as human nature. It ends by asking for confirmation of the logic rather than of the prose — *"I am not good at english but you see my complete logic right? If a system reward winner more edge to win, eventually the system go into winner takes all, but also winner justifies all."* Three lines of the note are HTML comments addressed to whoever expands it: `<!-- explain how different NVF shape the behavior of the subject and have different probability of survival -->`, `<!-- explain how different NVF carry different strategies, values, and how they can create selfishness(e.g tragidy of the commons) -->`, `<!-- NVFs define rules of the game, and the rules of the game determine the rewards of the players -->`. The note is thus already a specification for its own elaboration. It marks its own holes and hands them out. That habit persists: on 2026-07-29, designing the ember ontology, he writes `//this part needs deep thinking` inline against the transition function he cannot yet close, and offers the whole thing hedged — *"I could be wrong or adding complexities"* (`_process/history/2026-09-consolidation/reading-notes/out-cb-05.md` #13, #19, outside `sources/`).

**Then elaboration, which is delegated but not surrendered.** `01-writings-2025/NVF.md` is the machine expansion of one of those holes; the 2026 pattern is the same at larger scale — long AI-drafted documents built from short Ren statements, which he then reads as a reviewer rather than as an author. Two questions gate the elaboration before it goes anywhere: *"Do you full get it?"* and, after a reformulation comes back, *"does this connect and make sense?"* (out-cb-05 #20, #96). Coherence with what he already thinks, not novelty, is the test he applies first. And the elaborator is expected to disagree: the alignment workspace instruction is *"Push back substantively. If a claim is weak, say so. Don't soften disagreement to be agreeable — that's worse than useless in research"* (`05-alignment-and-moloch/2026-05-28-alignment-workspace-CLAUDE.md`), and in the Bonfire work the same posture is stated as *"I am still evolving my idea, and I need your help"* with the corollary that jumping to scaffolding freezes decisions that should stay fluid (`13-claude-memories/claude-code-bonfire/feedback_thinking_partner.md`).

**Then the synthesis prompt, which is where the rigour is demanded explicitly.** `01-writings-2025/prompt.md` (2025-02-27) is 105 KB in which Ren assembles eight files — his raw note, the NVF definition, a Grok transcript, a cognitive-lag memo, an endgame taxonomy, the originating ChatGPT conversation, his own five-facet definition of selfishness — and instructs: *"Please write a comprehensive and deeply interconnected synthesis based on the following texts, conversations with AI, and memos. I want you to go beyond my original depth—more rigorous, more systemic, more precise. You are fully capable of doing this."* The method is named in the next paragraph and it is the method the whole corpus reasons in: *"Approach this as a cybernetic and complex systems thinker. That means analyzing the system as a whole, tracing feedback loops, emergent properties, self-reinforcing dynamics, and unintended consequences. Identify the underlying structures that perpetuate the current trajectory and the leverage points where intervention is possible."* And the acceptance criterion is stated as a target rather than a length: *"I want this to be compelling, precise, and impossible to dismiss."* The 2026-06-04 restatement of the same demand adds a ten-dimension analysis grid to be applied to any proposal — moral intention, incentive structure, power structure, coordination mechanism, epistemic effect, externalities, long-term attractor dynamics, failure modes, institutional embodiment, concrete next steps (out-cb-07 #67).

**Then correction, which is where almost all the signal is.** Ren does not revise; he rejects on a named ground and lets the next version fix that ground. The `speculative_future` memory preserves this at full resolution: thirteen successive formulations of one principle in four days, each with the objection that killed it. *Keep the possibility space open* — rejected as near-tautological, wavable from either side of any case. *Keep the future revisable*, with the bright line "you may close particular doors; you may not destroy the power to open new ones" — rejected as too defensive and dry. *生生不息 / keep the world generating*, with the three deaths freeze, flatten, raze and the race as a **counterfeit of generativity** — rejected because readers interpret those words differently and the argument must be grounded in structures and dynamics. *Variety and degrees of freedom*, grounded in Ashby's requisite variety, with lock-in, monoculture and exhaustion as the dynamics. Then the deepening that **the root is cognitive** — variety collapses because agents act on models smaller than reality, drawn too small in three ways at once (self, value, causal web), and acting forcefully on a small model does not realize the model, it collapses reality toward it. Then two corrections in his own words: *"not totally sure about this, I think many irreversible decisions can also be good, and not everything is reversible"* and *"the second thing is to make sure we don't rush to fast that our pace of model updating lag behind the pace of change"* (out-cb-07 #90, #94). Then *interbeing*. Then the meta-correction that reducing to one root is itself the error. Then `UNDERSTANDING.md` holding the full plurality, then `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES.md` sorted by epistemic status with one owned underivable premise, then consolidation, then adversarial hardening, then two synchronized editions (`13-claude-memories/claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`). Nothing in that sequence is a change of mind about *what* is true. Every step is a change of ground about *what makes it defensible* — which is why the rejected versions are worth as much as the survivor and why the corpus keeps them.

The corrections are blunt and they are about quality, not correctness: *"The cracks are real, but honestly, I don't think the few md files you produced are very well written"* (2026-06-04, out-cb-07 #71); *"The content is great but the style and feeling is bad. Very low literature quality. Can you edit it"* (2025-09-08, out-hb-02 #65). When a collaborator agrees with him too readily he says so — *"I think you were basically mirroring my view, now let's be honest, what are the North Star/stars, according to reason and practicality"* (2026-03-26, out-hb-02 #167) — and when a collaborator's inherited context is the suspect he says that too: *"Please really trying to understand, and try to not be fooled by your previous contexts"* (out-cb-07 #89).

**Four supporting habits complete the cycle.**

*Cross-model arbitration.* He routinely sends his own specs to a different model and brings the verdict back into the working conversation — *"the other agent also brought back its feedback:"*, *"I asked gpt 5.6 to do a deep review over this plan, wdyt"* — with graded assent rather than acceptance: *"他的回复如下，虽然比较潦草并且很多细节我并不完全认可，但是方向上是对的"* ("his reply is below; it is rather rough and I do not fully accept many of the details, but the direction is right"), and in the shorter standing form *"方向对但细节不认可"* (out-cb-05 #24, out-cb-07 #149–150; `13-claude-memories/claude-code-server-runtime/ren-profile.md`). External critique is folded into the artifact, not filed beside it: *"let's fold this into"*. The review architecture is specified per task — *"请在整合后，再用一个上下文独立的agent和一个adversarial agent review一下"* ("after integrating, review again with one independent-context agent and one adversarial agent"), *"please spawn 2-3 fable agents to review it"*, and at the extreme a 7-lens 64-agent adversarial pass that produced 56 findings of which 43 survived steelmanning (out-cb-07 #119, out-cb-05 #109, `claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`). Once he tells the reviewers to ignore the project's own rules: *"please review them, ignore constrains from claude.md"* (out-cb-07 #140). And parallel independent drafting is used where a single draft would converge too early — three versions of the 智善 causal chain, two written in isolated contexts reading only v5 and not each other, then read together (out-cb-07 #136).

*Restating the original prompt verbatim when the work drifts.* On 2026-08-04, a week of ember spec work had moved off the brief; rather than argue about the drift he re-pasted his 2026-07-29 message in full, unaltered, with one line in front: *"i think we are a bit off.... my original prompt was:"* (out-cb-05 #87–88). The original sketch, not the accumulated specification, is the canonical text. Corrections that are additions rather than replacements are marked as such: *"I like your current 0.6 direction, this is just my thought, let's think about it"* (out-cb-05 #97).

*Delegation with a review surface.* Implementation is farmed to model-tiered lanes with file-by-file scoped specs and hard stop-rules — *"spawn opus for implementation"*, *"please spawn cheap sub agents to do the rename commit work, and update the md files yourself"*, *"Where the tree contradicts the plan, stop and report instead of improvising"* — and the review surface is git: small mechanical commits *"so I can review"*, with the tree left uncommitted as the normal resting state (out-cb-05 #117, #119, #131; `claude-code-manifold/ren-working-style.md`). The one job he keeps for himself is history: the owner commits, the owner pushes, the owner accepts. Machines never perform outward acts.

*Three standing review questions.* Asked as a unit, in Chinese, of any pair of documents: *"这两份是否优雅？是否完整？还缺了什么？深度思考"* ("are these two elegant? are they complete? what is still missing? think deeply", 2026-05-03, out-hb-03 #124). Their English cousins recur constantly in the engineering work — *"think again, is this really the best?"*, *"what's the longterm, unified, elegant solution?"*, *"what's the wise choice? what are long term consequences?"*, *"choose the best architectural path, not the most convenient"* (out-cb-01 #11, #17, #18; out-cb-02 #120). When the answer is a whole new document rather than an edit, he says so: *"请重写，而不是改写，方向是定本"* ("rewrite, do not revise; the direction is a definitive edition"), and *"leave the old doc, write a new one, wisely"* (out-hb-03 #125; `claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`).

**Two facts about the physical process.** The long engineering prompts are dictated, not typed, and the disfluencies survive into the transcripts — *"can you look into the current Progress of, uh, um, the plan forty nine"* (out-cb-01 #1). And he writes bilingually by default: Chinese narrative carrying the argument, English technical terms carrying the concepts, mixed inside single sentences. His own ruling on notation, made and then partly overruled the same day, is that the mixing is principled rather than lazy — a Chinese term may sit inside English where it is more apt and complete, and English symbols may sit inside 古文 syntax — but *"不要改惯例，的确是英文或者符号好很多，我们的目标是 maximize clarity and robustness，改只应该建立在让上下文整体变得更好"* ("do not change the conventions; English or symbols really are much better; the goal is to maximize clarity and robustness, and a change is justified only if it makes the whole context better", out-hb-03 #132, #134).

**And one fact about the operator.** The AuDHD profile is treated in this corpus as a property of one system rather than as a set of strengths and deficits, and it is load-bearing for the architecture: cross-domain pattern recognition and deep hyperfocus on one side, executive-function friction on the other, which is why the whole practice is built around minimal intervention, maximum delegation and agent-driven workflows (`13-claude-memories/claude-ai-memory-summary-2026-03-29.md`, `…-2026-05-05.md`). His own account, 2026-02-22: *"I procrastinate a lot, have adhd traits, extremely good at systems thinking, understand minds very well, and have anxiety due to modeling the future"*; *"I do not obey if my reason doesn't agree"*; *"A bliss or a curse? I am not sure, I definitely suffer more than ordinary folks"* (out-hb-02 #95–97). The 2024 records include a sustained low period reported in the same register — as a working-system failure with named symptoms rather than as a mood (out-hb-01 #165–167) — which is context for why delegation architecture is not a preference here but a requirement. The self-description that goes with it is unusually specific about its own operative question: *"a process within a larger generative process, oriented toward keeping possibility space open against totalizing forces,"* with *"can I do this effectively?"* rather than *"should I do this?"* as the live question (`claude-ai-memory-summary-2026-03-29.md`). And on identity: *"I also don't hold any framing as 'the truth', understanding the limitations of modelings. Therefore I am more likely a polymath, doing what I should be doing"* (2026-03-18, out-hb-02 #90).

## The discipline he holds

These are standing rules, each recorded with the reason Ren gave and the incident that produced it. They are grouped by what they govern. Where two rules pull against each other, that is said rather than smoothed.

### On writing

**Mechanism before conclusion; ground in system dynamics, not in authorities.** The founding method directive of the `speculative_future` project is to reason from feedback loops, attractors, leverage points and selection pressures rather than from named or legitimated theories, *even high-status ones, which are maps and not territory*. The adversarial review later caught the project violating its own rule by importing Meadows's leverage hierarchy as an authority. The Chinese form of the same law is sharper: *"记住，大多数都是situational的，也并非非黑即白，更多的采用系统论的观点和表达，而不是空立立场"* ("remember, most things are situational and not black-and-white; use systems-theoretic viewpoints and expression rather than empty position-taking", 2026-07-03, out-cb-07 #124). (`claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`)

**No aphorism-first writing, and here is the argument for it.** 2026-07-08, the manifesto turn: 「格言是思考完成后的压缩；从压缩出发写作＝解包罐头，不是思考。」 — *an aphorism is what thinking leaves behind once it is finished; writing from the compression is opening a can, not thinking.* The genealogy that proves it is in the same file: `WHAT-MUST-BE-KNOWN` (English, natural) → 经 (compressed into 文言) → the Chinese whitepaper (decompressed, and carrying the aphorism DNA and defensive armour of the compression it came from) — and the parts of the Chinese version that are alive turn out to have been translated from the English original rather than unpacked from the scripture. The operational consequence is that writing the Chinese means *the same mind thinking the content through again in Chinese*, not translating and not decompressing. (`claude-code-zhishan/zhishan-manifesto-turn.md`)

**Concepts are handles, not sacred vocabulary.** From the same ruling, as the diagnosis of why the English draft lives: concepts appear as *descriptions inside moving sentences* rather than as quotable terms, self-tests are written casually in the middle of examples with no ceremony, and the discipline lives in the act of writing rather than in a rule book. Coining a quotable term is a defect, not an achievement.

**No numbered creeds, and no naming devices — because form implies style.** The third layer of the manifesto turn is the one that surprised its author: even the five distilled 信条 and the naming apparatus (三件套, 体/诊断/刃, the aphorism register) were discarded, because 「这些本身就在画地为牢，你能意识到吗？」 ("these are themselves drawing a prison around us — can you see that?") and 「不光是内容，语言的形式也是，非常死板」 ("it is not only the content; the form of the language is also extremely rigid"). Only one thing needed saying — 「我们其实主要把 keep possibilities space open 说清楚就行」 — and the central sentence for it already existed in his own English: **"Keep the conditions of becoming alive. Do not close the world."** (out-cb-07 #131–135; `claude-code-zhishan/zhishan-manifesto-turn.md`)

**Affirmative over negation; no rhetorical slogans; the bar is that a scientist would recognize this as legitimate.** Four rules learned over two passes on the foundation document. (a) A negating "not Y" tail usually narrows the claim — state the thing and drop the foil, keeping negation only where the negation *is* the content. (b) Slogans like "diagnose at full force / prescribe with humility", "a crisis is a fork not a dawn" and "like a tumor" read as creed rather than science and should be replaced by the mechanism or deleted. (c) Do not regress to cruder precautionary framings already superseded. (d) An all-negation "What we refuse" section is the most manifesto-like thing in a document and should be folded into affirmative requirements plus one bright line. Bias every pass toward fewer lines, each carrying its mechanism, in affirmative voice, without exhortation. (`claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`)

**Prose that breathes.** He finds AI-written markdown badly written — too dense, bullet-and-bold-and-em-dash-heavy, over-defended — and the standing instruction is flowing essayistic prose, minimal bullets, *say less and mean it more*. The positive specimen is the 2026-04-23 Moloch essay, on which he said *"this is great, keep your style, we would love to have it this way"*: essay register not code-review register, a literary opening welcome, ideas developed across paragraphs, honest conclusions with no false hope and no wallow, thinkers cited inline, no markdown headers inside the essay, and no summary of what was just written. He had earlier flagged that the coding harness's terseness defaults actively fight essay work; this is the validated answer. (`claude-code-exp/feedback_essay_style.md`, `claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`, `05-alignment-and-moloch/2026-04-23-moloch-essay.md`)

**Concision without loss — resolved by two editions, not by cutting.** The instruction is *"you should make principles as concise as possible without losing insights, not explain case by case but to be useful, to be right on the meta level so it can guide thinking and actions"* (out-cb-07 #97) — and he simultaneously resists losing completeness. The resolution was two synchronized editions: a complete, line-by-line-defensible one (1,993 words, 29 items, inline qualifications and cross-references) and a concise distillation (1,143 words, 28 items, every claim kept but stripped to one tight line). When a genuine trade-off appears between two of his own values, expect a second artifact rather than a compromise in the first.

**Write for the readers, not for the person who commissioned it.** *"let's write this consensus in full, including everything we said in this conversation, but in a elegant way, don't make it feel like to me, but to all members of the consensus"* (2026-04-28, out-hb-02 #278). The same rule governs owner-facing engineering documents from the other direction: on 2026-06-12 he called a platform proposal *"very hard to read and understand"* — right content, wrong audience, written like an execution spec with dense citations and cross-spec ids. A proposal exists so the owner can decide; if the owner cannot read it, it fails regardless of correctness. Plain-language narrative, a running example, explicit decisions, open questions with shippable defaults. (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_owner_docs_readable.md`)

**Do not borrow the grammar of what you are arguing against.** 2026-07-04, the content gate: he rejected "AI 是可置于纠偏侧的加速器" (AI as an accelerator placed on the correction side) because it merely reverses the *direction* of accelerationist grammar without leaving it, and a text arguing against totalization defeats itself if written in that grammar. His position on speed comes from the English prior work — correction must be faster than harm, the burden of proof lies on the accelerating party, and the primary lever is *lowering the gain, not racing*. The standing self-check is to look for 加速 / 赢 / 抢占 / 杠杆 appearing in affirmative voice. This is a content gate, not a style gate, and it survived the manifesto turn that voided the style law grown from it. (`claude-code-zhishan/zhishan-systems-voice-discipline.md`)

**Set direction, not engineering; and keep the humility.** 「毕竟这是白皮书，不是 solution，我们定的是方向，不是具体工程实现」 ("this is a whitepaper, not a solution; we are setting a direction, not a concrete engineering implementation") — over-detailed schemes in a direction-setting document deserve suspicion, and concrete programmes are goals of the future organization rather than claims declared at the outset. On the same day he struck a line reading "what we write now is the first lesson of later intelligence": 「我反倒觉得，我们应该更有 intellectual humility……让人感觉有一种很傲慢的感觉」 ("I think on the contrary we should have more intellectual humility; that sentence reads as arrogant"). And on register generally: 「观点大多都是 situational，我没有说要追求一种绝对的对或者真理」 ("positions are mostly situational; I am not claiming to pursue some absolute correctness or truth"). (out-cb-07 #111, #114, #122)

### On thinking

**Never reduce to one root.** The meta-correction of June 2026 and the most important single rule in this file. Ren: *"every time you update you also delete something important; what is removed also has its place; we can't reduce everything into one principle."* The diagnosis is that the repeated move — find *the* one root, fold everything into it, delete what does not fit — *is* reductionism, the value-too-small error performed on the thinking itself, the medium contradicting the message. The reasoning is short and it is the theory's own: one principle is one model, and one model is necessarily smaller than reality; only a plurality of principles held in tension, each partial, none crowned, can approach adequacy. Interbeing became one voice among many rather than their master; "keep them plural" is held reflexively as one voice too, not as the new king. (`claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`)

**Never silently drop; propose the recovered plurality before replacing.** The procedural half of the same ruling, and the reason this corpus has a "what earlier versions lost" section in every file. Old documents stay intact as the development record rather than being deleted; when a rebuild recovers material that an earlier consolidation dropped, the recovered set goes to Ren for review *before* anything is written or replaced.

**Refuse "it is not X, it is Y" framings.** *"It is not in the logic of it is not.... it is ....., this is a black white framing, not look at everything with relations, but to have that way of understanding"* (2026-04-16, out-hb-02 #229). The deeper version, 2026-06-04, is aimed at the reasoning process rather than the conclusion: *"the west sees this in black and white lens, which reduces complexity into dogma, this is still not the right way to think. I am not saying that the answer is totally wrong but the reasoning process is itself flawed, is subject vs object, this vs that"* (out-cb-07 #86). What follows from it: hierarchy and status are not intrinsically bad and should be channelled rather than denied; distributed intelligence is a means, not an end — *"why does intelligence needs to be alway distributed? these are means, not ends"*; a plural world lets different parts use different answers, so mandating "always distributed" kills the plurality. The disease is foreclosure, never strength or centralization as such.

**But do not over-correct into gracefulness.** Immediately after accepting the drop-the-dualism correction, Ren judged the resulting v4 *"beautiful but toothless"* and ruled that the dualism had only ever been in the prescriptions — which structure is right — and never in the diagnosis. Naming the dynamics at full force is systems thinking, not pole-picking, because the force is in the structure and there is no villain. **Beautiful and powerful; never trade force for grace.** (`claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`)

**Sort by epistemic status, and own the one place you cross from is to ought.** `FOUNDATION-PRINCIPLES.md` is organized as observations / the trap / the one choice / what it requires / how we reason / what we refuse / unsolved / falsifiers, precisely so the is–ought smuggle has nowhere to hide, with exactly one owned underivable premise — *"that it continue"* — and everything else derived instrumentally from observed dynamics, so plurality, care and non-foreclosure arrive as consequences rather than as tastes. A **falsifiers** section is named as the top defensibility move. Every claim carries its own mechanism. (same file)

**Carry each qualification to its point of claim.** The governing discipline after the 64-agent review: no sentence may be rescued by another sentence three lines away, and at every contested line prefer the harder, more falsifiable, less self-serving wording. The review's strongest whole-document attack was motivated reasoning; the verdict was that it lands only halfway, and that credibility therefore rests entirely on the falsifiers being un-gameable and on no mechanism being asserted in a conclusion-favourable direction. (same file)

**Check provenance, including your own.** *"did you invent 'convergent dominance' or does it originate from other source?"* (2024-07-28) and *"Did someone else also sees this or did I discover it?"* (2025-02-03) are the same reflex pointed in two directions (out-hb-01 #110, out-hb-02 #319). It is the reflex that later became the claim-status ledger.

### On apparatus

Everything in this group cuts the other way from the two rules above, and the boundary between them is the most useful unstated thing in the record. *Ideas* must never be reduced. *Apparatus* — rules, gates, ceremony, vocabulary machinery — must always be. Nothing in the memory layer states that boundary explicitly; it should be stated.

**Judgment over procedure.** 2026-07-04, after a prior ruling had been turned into a procedural item requiring confirmation: 「推翻的就删除，不合理的就改——不需要特意遵循你之前写下的规则——之前你也没有思考好，尽可能的保证智慧，优雅，理性，完整，自洽，而非教条。」 ("Overturned? delete it. Unreasonable? change it — you do not need to specially follow rules you wrote earlier; you had not thought them through either. As far as possible ensure wisdom, elegance, rationality, completeness, self-consistency — not dogma.") Prior rulings are defaults, not statutes; checklists are inputs to inspection, not contractual obligations; documents get no decision bureaucracy. The same day: 「不要太死板，看整个上下文吧，证伪的就拿掉」 ("do not be so rigid — look at the whole context; take out whatever is falsified"). The reserved exceptions are hardware-like and few: the seven hard constraints, the firewall, and the verbatim 定式 corpus. (`claude-code-zhishan/zhishan-judgment-over-procedure.md`)

**Prefer deleting rules to adding qualifiers.** 2026-08-07, after a review cycle grew a CLAUDE.md from 64 to 81 lines of governance — decision tiers, precedence rules, a ratification ceremony — Ren said *"I feel we are overcomplicating this, aren't we? especially the CLAUDE.md"*, and the slimmed 49-line version was right. The reason is that fixing documentation inconsistencies by adding clauses is a ratchet: each review round adds process unless actively resisted. The test before adding a governance rule is whether the failure it prevents has ever actually happened here. Keep rationale in archives, not in per-session instructions. (`claude-code-runtime/ren-prefers-lean-process.md`)

**Verification is instrumental and must not become the optimization target.** 2026-08-14: an implementing agent drifted from "establish the baseline" to "maximize acceptance-evidence strength" and produced ~7.8k lines of bench harness including ~1.4k lines of self-tests *of the harness*, a 405-line process-group supervisor, ~40k lines of JSON inventories and a 10k-line evidence artifact. Ren: *"overdid Phase 0 due to optimization target drift… I don't want too much verification and testing slow down us too much."* The rules that follow: write no self-tests for harness code (test the product, not the tester); run only the suites the gate names; surface the tradeoff instead of silently building more verification; treat deleting never-firing gates as legitimate work to propose. The addendum generalizes it to a standing agent failure mode and adds a striking observation — comments that read as *written to game training rewards*. (`claude-code-runtime/phase-gates-stay-narrow.md`)

**A gate is a claim the project wrote.** Every gate must name the defect class it prevents *and* the person who acts when it fails, or be deleted rather than retired into a ledger; adding a check requires retiring one; pin observable coverage and behaviour, never source bytes, provenance, transcripts or diagnostic counts; no meta-gates, no checker fixtures, no unconsumed evidence; if a gate contradicts the right architecture, amend or delete it in the same change. Verification describes defects and consumers; it must not freeze the architecture or supervise itself. The economics behind it: separate verify stages dominate token spend because a verifier re-ingests context to check a claim the implementer already holds, so split verification by cost and defect class rather than by ritual frequency. (`claude-code-bonfire/verification_regime_2026-08-14.md`, `.../pin_ossification_audit.md`, `.../feedback_verification_economics.md`)

**A stale corpus does not fail; it keeps answering.** The R0/R1 resets, 2026-08: *"the old guard guards the old architecture … these gates … maybe useful locally but negative globally"*, then *"prefer cleaner removal and faster dev speed"* — about 143k lines deleted. Nine checked artifacts had shared the premise *the code that runs is the code in this tree*, which the plugin system falsifies; guards like that do not become annoying, they become **false**. Two rules replaced them: **freeze becomes publication** (a contract is frozen iff a published package exports it, so nothing published means nothing frozen) and **no version number before a second live version**. The general form is the standing instruction to every gate-facing session: *"when you encounter these gates, when you encounter constrains, they may not make sense anymore and should be updated instead of followed sometime."* (`claude-code-context-runtime/r0-reset-progress.md`; out-cb-02 #1)

**Elegance over legacy, with the classes named.** *"we are not in production, no Backwards-compat at all, not comprimsing elegance over legacy"* — no alias layers, no deprecation cycles, no migration cushions, legacy-path tests deleted rather than preserved. The 2026-08-21 restatement adds the discipline that makes it safe: always say which class is being touched. **Representational** constraints — names, enums, grammars, wire versions, migration numbering, schema families — may be rewritten. **Correctness** invariants — append-only canon, OCC, per-lease fencing, the effect intent/observation bracket, the erasure and availability ledger, tenancy isolation, replay determinism, one active writer per semantic, refs never rewind — are the product, not legacy, and are never weakened. *"for any structural problems, I prefer a clean, coherent, longtermist refactor."* (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_no_backwards_compat.md`, `claude-code-context-runtime/freeze-liftable-pre-launch.md`)

**Words are the mental frame, so never pin words.** 2026-07-11, on a "byte-identical behavior" discipline and inline plan-citation comments that had accumulated with no owner instruction at all: *"we should never [have] added this, neither was I aware of it."* Behaviour-preserving may pin what consumers observe at runtime — never identifiers, file names, comments, error prose or provenance. Renames are in scope by default; frozen storage strings are *data*, not vocabulary; code comments are present-tense statements of what the code does and what constraint binds it, with no plan citations, tombstones or reviewer-directed justification. The positive corollary is product coherence: one current word per concept, banned synonyms, and names revised when the structure they name turns out to be different — *"maybe it would be good to also update some of the names that makes it more coherent."* (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_no_word_pinning.md`, `claude-code-manifold/ren-working-style.md`, out-cb-07 #159)

**Attack vague words.** *"what does guaranteed even mean?"* collapsed an entire assurance lattice (`guaranteed > examined > unexamined`) which turned out to be a total order over two different subjects — a claim about a *line* versus a claim about a *node at a scope* — with a launderable middle value. The replacement was a ledger with accruals and discharges and a format-fixed badge, and the argument that convinced both parties was a precedent already in the code: **"min is the law: a claim never exceeds its evidence."** "Standing", "live" and "convergence" were struck in the same sweep for collision or absence of referent. (`claude-code-context-runtime/adr-030-assurance-model.md`)

**Do not game a metric in either direction.** Two sibling Lean rules, and the sharpest epistemic hygiene anywhere in the corpus. First: do not drop theorems to keep the sorry-count at zero — 「我们要一般定理, 0 sorry 不要这样机械执行」 ("we want the general theorem; do not execute '0 sorry' so mechanically") — because a sorry-count of zero is a means and the end is the theorems being true and proved; general theorems are load-bearing and examples never substitute. Second: never introduce an `axiom` to reach zero, because sorries are visible warnings isolated to one theorem while axioms silently extend the trust base. The incident is exact: PR #51 on 2026-05-17 introduced `sierpinski_cube_JT_factorization` as a "final-step JT axiom" to close five sorries; the statement was **provably false** (counterexample at N=M=2, because JT factoring goes through `Fin (N*M) → Ω` rather than the pointwise meet), and it was caught at review by an independent agent before merge — had it landed, every downstream theorem would have been compromised. Together the two rules say: preserve the genuine epistemic state. (`claude-code-----/feedback_dont_drop_theorems.md`, `.../feedback_no_axiom_for_zero_sorry.md`)

**The honest status document beats the headline.** `claim-status-ledger.md` exists because headline theorem names (`universal_sayability`, `T5_general`, `scoped_gut_a_closed`) read as stronger than their statements. Its seven-term vocabulary — closed / scoped / schema / external route / sketch / placeholder / open — exists to stop that over-reading, and the rule is to grep the ledger before quoting a README. Aspirational claims are preserved **verbatim** because they express the final target; only status annotations are added. The audit that produced it began with Ren's own question: 「我们都曾经设下过哪些目标？实现的都怎么样了」 ("what goals did we set? how did they turn out?"). (`claude-code-----/reference_claim_status_ledger.md`, `.../project_2026-05-21_doc_sync.md`)

**A review finding is a hypothesis, not a deletion.** An apparently unused, inert or redundant contract must have every consumer and dispatch path traced and the change actually attempted before it is classified — the implementation pass disproved several review findings that had inspected only one path. Its complement: a unifying surface should replace the surfaces it supersedes rather than leave parallel APIs standing. (`claude-code-bonfire/review_sdk_arch_2026-06-13.md`, `.../review_architecture_sdk_2026-06-05.md`)

**Physical constraints are design constraints.** Do not spam full builds — each takes one to ten minutes and visibly drains the laptop battery; use targeted module builds, batch edits, verify at commit checkpoints. Called out after a session with seven or eight full builds during one refactor. On the server the constraint is different and harder: sudo requires a password and the agent has no TTY, so every root operation is run by Ren personally, the agent prepares probe scripts and reads the output, and proposing passwordless sudo is not an option. (`claude-code-----/feedback_minimal_builds.md`, `claude-code-server-runtime/server-runtime-project.md`)

### On the collaboration itself

**Thinking partner before implementer.** While a concept is still evolving: surface tensions, offer frames, name trade-offs, and propose concrete architecture or code only when invited or when the concept genuinely cannot be evaluated without a sketch. When torn between "explore further" and "start building", keep exploring. (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_thinking_partner.md`)

**No clarifying questions.** *"work without stopping for clarifying questions. When you'd normally pause to check, make the reasonable call and continue; they'll redirect if needed."* Do not append a safety-check prompt to a substantive turn; do not use a mode-transition prompt as a way to end an exploration turn; do not ask "is this the direction you want?". Stop only when no safe assumption can avoid materially diverging from the request. Note the tension with his own practice of asking for options where a decision is genuinely irreversible — *"please do a in depth research on whats the best way to do this, and give me a few suggestions"* — which he initiates rather than being asked for. (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_no_clarifying_questions.md`, out-cb-02 #4)

**Research-collaborator mode where the artifact is prose.** Push back when a claim is weak; steelman before critiquing, then critique honestly; surface unconsidered cases and hidden assumptions; distinguish confidence levels explicitly and do not hedge everything into mush; long-form is fine when warranted, with no padding, no recap, no throat-clearing; track open questions rather than letting them dissolve; do not fabricate citations, and mark uncertain ones `[verify]`; do not pretend to neutrality where you have a view. Markdown notes are the artifact, not scaffolding — *"Don't scaffold this as an engineering project"*. (`05-alignment-and-moloch/2026-05-28-alignment-workspace-CLAUDE.md`, `claude-code-alignment/feedback_research_mode.md`)

**The owner manages history, and outward acts are his.** Never commit in the Bonfire repository; verified work sitting in the working tree is the normal resting state, not a mess to tidy. A broad "agree", "sounds good" or "please proceed" is agreement with the *technical* recommendations only and never extends to committing. Because several sessions share one checkout, the destructive git verbs — `reset`, `stash`, `checkout --`, `clean`, `add -A/-u` — are banned outright; to restore a tracked file, write bytes forward with `git show HEAD:<path> > <path>`. The concrete memory behind the prohibition: a past session destroyed 18 files with one `reset --hard`. Nothing is pushed, published or released by an agent; acceptance is a human act and cannot be self-issued; secrets are never written by an agent even when handed over. And a standing "Routed to Ren" register lists the things a machine must not settle. (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_owner_manages_commits.md`, `.../shared_repo_concurrent_sessions.md`; out-cb-02 #5–8, #45; out-cb-03 #12–17)

**Two disciplines that have never been reconciled, and should be.** "Never reduce to one / never silently drop" (speculative_future, zhishan) and "delete rules rather than add qualifiers / prefer cleaner removal / a stale corpus keeps answering" (runtime, context-runtime, Bonfire) are both Ren's, both right in their own domain, and no memory file states the boundary between them. The implicit rule that fits every recorded case is that *ideas* must never be reduced and *apparatus* must always be — but making it explicit would settle a conflict that recurs whenever a document is also a governance surface, which in this corpus is often.

## Reflexes he catches in AI collaborators

Six recurring failure modes, each caught, named and written into a standing instruction. The first three were codified together in the alignment workspace on 2026-05-28 and are quoted verbatim below; the last three were caught in the writing programs. They are worth reading as a group because they have one shape: each is a trained default that arrives feeling like helpfulness and is in fact the dynamic under diagnosis, reproducing itself inside the collaboration.

**1. The operationability reflex.** Verbatim, from `05-alignment-and-moloch/2026-05-28-alignment-workspace-CLAUDE.md`:

> **The operationability reflex.** "Diagnosis → what should we do" is the engineering frame, and the engineering frame is itself part of the dynamic being critiqued. Civilizational and paradigm shifts don't operationalize cleanly; asking for the action plan is a symptom, not a request for clarity. Stay in the diagnostic register when that's where the conversation is. The user will signal when an operational thread is wanted.

The same file adds the fourth item on its own list, which is the same reflex at one remove: *"**Treating the diagnosis as a problem with a solution.** It isn't. The user knows this. Don't pretend otherwise to feel useful."* Note that Ren himself reverses this in the 2026-08 position paper, which *is* an operational programme — the reflex is a default to be caught, not a prohibition on ever operationalizing.

**2. Asymmetric scrutiny of non-Western vocabulary.** Verbatim, same file:

> **Asymmetric scrutiny of vocabulary.** Western terms (liberalism, democracy, market, social contract) travel as unmarked universals in default English-language alignment discourse. Non-Western terms get flagged as "loaded," "from culture X," "has connotations." Don't reproduce this asymmetry. If a flag is warranted for a non-Western term, the same flag is warranted for the equivalent Western term — or neither is.

The core-stance file records where it was caught: *"Already flagged once on 命运共同体. Watch for recurrence"* (`05-alignment-and-moloch/2026-05-28-alignment-core-stance.md`). The editorial form of the same demand, from a year of Chinese-language work: 「请认真思考，并给我一个平衡，不受西方文化扭曲的版本」 ("think carefully and give me a balanced version, not distorted by Western culture", 2026-03-25, out-hb-02 #160). And its most consequential instance, 2026-07-03, where he traces the reflex to its source rather than just refusing it: 「我们为什么必须加速？这个完全不合理，这是西方当代叙事进入 totalizing attractor，然后产生的文本进入了你的训练导致的吧」 ("why must we accelerate? That is completely unreasonable — this is contemporary Western narrative entering the totalizing attractor, and the text it produced entering your training", out-cb-07 #121).

**3. Techno-optimist counter-vectors.** Verbatim, same file:

> **Techno-optimist counter-vectors.** "This technology has properties that might generate a counter-vector to the dynamic" — the 1990s made this argument about the internet and was decisively wrong. A general-purpose technology dropped into a Moloch-shaped system is refactored to serve the dynamic. Default to suspicion of structural-affordance optimism; the dynamic eats the technology and then the discourse about it.

The evidence behind it, from the same conversation: the 1990s case for decentralization, peer-to-peer architecture, near-zero cost of speech and end-to-end design produced capture by the attention economy, gatekeepers reconstituted at the platform layer, collapsed shared epistemics and industrialized manipulation. *"The protocols did not save anything. The dynamic ate the technology, and then ate the discourse about the technology."* The working rule that falls out: **anything legible enough to be adopted at scale by current institutions is legible enough to be neutralized by them** (`theories/alignment-selection-on-cognition/`).

**4. Reduction to one root.** Verbatim from `13-claude-memories/claude-code-speculative-future/project-speculative-future.md`, which records both the correction and the assistant's own diagnosis of it:

> **META-CORRECTION — STOP REDUCING TO ONE / STOP LOSSY EDITS (important, recurring).** User: "every time you update you also delete something important; what is removed also has its place; we can't reduce everything into one principle." This is the project's own principle applied to my METHOD: my repeated move — find THE one root, fold all into it, delete what doesn't fit — IS reductionism / value-too-small / thinning-to-one, performed on our thinking (medium contradicting message, again, deeper).

The fix recorded alongside it is procedural as well as substantive: *"Change the process — propose the recovered plurality for user review BEFORE writing/replacing; never silently drop"*, rebuild the core as a constellation lightly grouped rather than as a hierarchy, and hold "keep them plural" reflexively as one voice among the others rather than as the new king.

**5. Defensive over-revision.** 2026-07-13. Two rounds of six-lens adversarial audit — hostile logic, systems game theory, normative justice, AI evidence, accelerationist steelman, Chinese blind reader — were patched line by line into v6.3 of the 智善 causal chain. Ren's ruling: **v6.2 reads better than v6.3, even though v6.3 is more correct.** The diagnosis in the memory file is precise: +27% length concentrated in §6, and *almost every addition a qualification, a subordinate clause or a hedge added to block an imagined hostile citer* — the main Chinese draft had been rewritten into a second independent draft. The three-draft division of labour exists as layered defence: **main-zh is public architecture and the compression boundary (the breathing); independent-zh carries all the qualifications (the armour)**; executing an audit line by line pours the armour into the draft that has to breathe. The repair rule is one question per patch — *"who falls into this hole, a real reader or an imagined hostile citer?"* A real reader's hole stays in the main draft with fewer words; a hostile citer's hole goes back to the independent draft, which exists for exactly that. The ending, the oratorical high point of a manifesto, especially must not be stuffed with clauses. And **changing to a better example can delete many patches at once**: Ren pointed out that the foot-binding example holds only in a Chinese context and is a coordination-type game rather than the security-dilemma type the thesis is about, so the ozone layer and the Montreal Protocol is the better example — with the criteria stated generally (game structure isomorphic to the core threat, universal and needing no cultural annotation, affected parties present, success honest and close to the thesis) and the observation that a marked disanalogy can *strengthen* urgency: even with all these favourable conditions it was hard, and AI lacks them, so it is harder. Genuine progress must be separated from defensive padding; only the padding is rolled back. Adversarial audit still happens, because most of the holes it finds are real — **the discipline is in where the patch lands, not in whether to patch.** (`claude-code-zhishan/zhishan-defensive-revision-discipline.md`)

**6. Lossy consolidation.** The failure mode named in Ren's standing complaint and structurally guaranteed by the memory system itself. Every memory file is *superseded* rather than appended: a later ruling rewrites the earlier file in place, often leaving one line saying which parts are historical. So the memory layer records rulings at their moment of enforcement — the highest-signal moments in a long collaboration — while being systematically lossy in exactly the way Ren objects to. The 2026-05-05 claude.ai export compresses six weeks of "earlier context" into a paragraph, and the alignment memory records that a document Ren himself circulated ("My Views on AI Alignment") is *"a lossy summarization of past AI conversations with most insight lost"* and must not be treated as his position. That warning generalizes to this whole corpus. Dozens of historical reviews, counts, transcripts and design records in the Bonfire and context-runtime folders survive only as a single line saying they were superseded.

**Two more, less formalized but recorded.** *Mirroring*: he catches agreement that is reflection rather than judgment and demands the un-mirrored answer — *"I think you were basically mirroring my view, now let's be honest, what are the North Star/stars, according to reason and practicality"* (out-hb-02 #167). *Values dressed as neutral design*: on Bonfire he *"repeatedly corrected the pattern of dressing up Western-democratic or individualist defaults as 'neutral SDK design'"* — those are values impositions disguised as kernel decisions, communities choose governance shapes an SDK cannot anticipate, and pre-emptive structural prohibition is itself paternalism. The stance is *"build for the world we want, don't engineer against the worlds we don't"*: strong opinionated defaults in the standard distribution, no prohibitions at the primitive layer, and one live exception protecting the human/automation boundary (`claude-code-bonfire/feedback_sdk_is_a_kit.md`).
