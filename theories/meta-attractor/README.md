**The theory in one paragraph.** A meta-attractor is a self-reinforcing pattern that does not merely pull a system toward some states rather than others, but acquires power over the rule by which states are selected. Wherever advantage compounds — capital, status, rank, attention, compute, strategic position — the strategies that score highest on the current measure accumulate the resources that let their holders rewrite the measure, staff the institutions that apply it, build the infrastructure that presupposes it, and shape the minds that will later be scored by it. What was a *means* of doing well is thereby promoted to an *end*: the metric survives its own refutation, because by the time it has stopped measuring what it was invented to measure, the people and institutions it elevated are the ones who decide whether it is replaced. The loop runs across four coupled layers at once — cognitive (habits, salience, identity, what feels serious), intersubjective (narrative, status, what success means), institutional (hiring, promotion, ownership, law, evaluation) and material (infrastructure, compute, supply, finance, land) — and it closes: selected minds build selected organizations, which reshape institutions and infrastructure, which form and select the next round of minds. Its most dangerous property is not concentration but the progressive loss of *independent capacity to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and start again*: alternatives survive as language, then stop reproducing in practice, then stop being intelligible, until the pattern's contingent requirements read as reality, rationality and common sense. It need not capture everyone; it becomes civilizationally decisive when it holds capital allocation, law and coercion, strategic infrastructure, attention, standards, frontier science and the institutions building advanced AI. It is not fated — positive feedback can saturate, oscillate, split, overshoot or break, and energetic ceilings, cultural heterogeneity, delay instabilities, cross-scale constraints and reflexive awareness all cap it — which is why it is more like a hurricane than a black hole. Artificial intelligence is where this stops being a diagnosis of the past: AI inherits the pattern through a full selection ecology (selected archive → selected model → selected deployment → changed world → next archive), and can machine-ize it by lowering the cost of search, prediction, persuasion, monitoring and replication, then entering the production of its own successors — so the loop that is already outrunning correction begins to accelerate itself. The two ways this closes are not one: a system or composite process no participant can any longer interrupt, and a perfectly obedient system amplifying operators who were themselves selected by the pattern. Neither is a machine that hates us. The conclusion is therefore an ordering claim rather than a doom claim — **change the basin, then add speed** — and the constructive half must satisfy a constraint the diagnosis itself imposes: any counter-move that closes the field in its own favour is the same object under a better name.

## The argument

### The primitive is selection, not greed

The theory's engine is not a claim about human character. It starts from differential persistence, which exists before strategic games, conscious selectors or represented objectives: in any population where variants differ in how effectively they continue, whatever continues best becomes more common. Coordination failure — Moloch — is one important configuration of destructive selection, not its origin (`06-zhishan/writing-program/causal-chain/v2/main.md`). Ren's own earliest statement of the primitive is deliberately de-personalising: "it seems to me, not the capitalists or a small wealthy people are controlling the economy, but the optimization of the capital itself. it seems the society itself is optimizing its capital gain over its people" (2024-08-11). No conspiracy is needed and none is claimed; the phrase the essays settle on is "convergent evolution", or, in the condensed 2025-10 version, "No conspiracy required; just aligned incentives converging."

The second step is compounding. In any domain where success buys more capacity to succeed — the more capital you hold the easier it is to acquire capital, the higher your rank the cheaper the next rank — the distribution does not merely spread out, it converges. The 2024 precursors call this the Ouroboros cycle and make the generality explicit: "any competitive advantage accumulation game, in nature, will fall for this cycle, in the dynamics, the power can be replaced with capital, status, or any tokens" (2024-11-05). The 2025-10 essays give it an equation, superlinear growth with a ceiling, `dR/dt = α(R+R₀)^γ (1 − R/K) − δR` with γ > 1, which produces persistent inequality of *capacity* even under a hard cap K. Ren's plainest gloss on why this matters for design is a one-line law about games: "a winner of the game should be rewarded with anything other than more advantages of the game, otherwise the game will eventually converge and winner will take all" (2024-07-28).

The third step is the one that makes the theory more than a restatement of increasing returns. The strategies that secure resources fastest are the narrow ones. An agent that optimises a single measurable value and treats everything else — including the costs it imposes on others and on the future — as outside its accounting is, by construction, more efficient at that value than an agent carrying a broader set of concerns. Ren states this as the decisive premise: "The agent that only optimizing capital value, and discard all other values, including externalities, will have the highest capital efficiency" (2024-10-30). Iterated competition therefore does not select the wisest or most far-sighted; it removes the balanced. "This process does not select the best, most moral, or most insightful decision-makers — only those best at optimizing capital" (2024-07-14, [zh]). The population that remains is not a sample of human nature. It is a residue of a filter.

### Where the second order enters: the winner rewrites the test

Everything so far is compatible with ordinary economics. The distinctive move — and the reason a new handle is needed at all — is that the winners of one round do not just take the prize; they acquire the power to write the next round's scorecard.

The 2025-08 formalisation states this precisely enough to be wrong, which is what makes it valuable. Separate two things ordinary systems analysis conflates: the *state* of a system, x ∈ X, and the *rule that scores its states*, the selection proxy σ : X → ℝ. A first-order adaptive system moves x to score better against a fixed σ. That is optimisation, and it is not the problem. The problem begins when σ is endogenous — when the outcome of last round's scoring determines who writes next round's scorecard. Write a second operator G : X × Σ → Σ alongside the ordinary state update F : X × Σ → X and lift the dynamics onto the product space Y = X × Σ. A region M ⊂ Y is a meta-attractor when four conditions hold: trajectories from a basin B enter M (convergence); they stay for τ ≫ Δt (persistence); the **reflexive coupling** γ = (∂F/∂σ)(∂G/∂x) is positive inside M; and long-run behaviour maximises σ irrespective of the exogenous telos σ was invented to approximate (proxy dominance). The third condition is the engine. ∂F/∂σ > 0 says the scoring rule actually moves resources; ∂G/∂x > 0 says the resourced states can edit the rule — this second derivative is, in the source's own words, "the mathematical expression of institutional capture or lobbying." Their product being positive is feedback *on* feedback, in the boxed one-line form: `Meta-Attractor = Attractor(State, Rule(State)) with (dRule/dState)(dState/dRule) > 0` — "a feedback loop that optimises its own optimiser—thereby turning any effective *means* into a non-negotiable *end*."

Two consequences follow that no first-order model produces. First, **partial reform is re-absorbed**: fixing x while leaving G intact simply lets the rule re-derive the old x, so escape requires disrupting state and rule at once. Second, **the metric survives its own refutation** — and this is exactly the gap between Goodhart's Law and this theory. Goodhart says that a measure taken as a target ceases to measure. It does not explain why organisations keep using it afterwards. The meta-attractor does: by the time the measure has failed, the people and institutions the measure elevated are the ones who control whether it is replaced. The Chinese formulation of the same point is older and blunter: the Confucian 名实 / 正名 critique of name–reality drift, and Han Fei's warning inside the Legalist 二柄 reward–punishment machinery that measure-based incentives invite proxy gaming.

The four ingredients are stated as jointly necessary, which makes the claim falsifiable: variation in *both* objects and rules; differential amplification proportional to the proxy; memory or accumulation, so winners persist long enough to edit the rule; and positive feedback gain unopposed by stronger negative feedback. Remove any one and the meta-attractor collapses back to an ordinary attractor, or drifts.

### Which ends get selected: the covariance argument and CAOC

Naming the mechanism does not say what the system will end up valuing. Ren pushed for that specifically: "can we derive what proxies in Σ will dominate and become ends over time? I listed 4, but I don't have the mathematical formulation" (2025-08-10). The four he listed — Control, Accumulation, Optimisation, Competition, the **CAOC** drives — are his; the derivation was commissioned to justify them, and is worth stating because it is the corpus's sharpest single result.

Decompose σ into a weighted bundle of measurable proxies, σ(x,w) = f(Σᵢ wᵢ pᵢ(x)) with f′ > 0, and let resource flow be R = g(σ) with g′ > 0. Model rule-drift as endogenous lobbying and cultural imitation with a Price-style equation: the winners of round t buy influence over the weights of round t+1, `w_{i,t+1} = w_{i,t} + η·E_t[pᵢR]`, where η measures how quickly influence is captured. Expand E[pᵢR] = Cov(pᵢ,R) + E[pᵢ]E[R]; taking proxies zero-mean (an assumption stated in the source "for simplicity", and a real weakness — see the critiques) leaves `ẇᵢ = η·Cov(pᵢ,R)`: *a proxy gains weight in proportion to how strongly it covaries with getting paid.* Linearise f and g around the mean and this becomes a linear ODE on the whole weight vector, **`ẇ = λ C w`** with λ = ηg′f′ > 0 and C = Cov(p,p), whose solution w(t) = e^{λCt}w(0) converges to the leading eigenvector of C. Stated plainly: *a civilisation's terminal ends are the principal component of its proxy covariance structure.*

CAOC then falls out rather than being asserted. Each of the four has a structural reason for high covariance with everything else. Control (variance suppression) couples to any proxy that requires continued existence to be expressed. Accumulation multiplies the payoff of *any* subsequent win. Optimisation raises ∂R/∂pⱼ across the board. Competitive rank is by construction proportional to resource share. They therefore form a dense positively-correlated block whose composite eigenvector carries the largest eigenvalue. The conclusion comes with its escape clause welded on, and the clause is part of the claim: CAOC dominate and become terminal ends **unless counter-correlated proxies are deliberately amplified**. This is where the qualitative taxonomies of the 2025-05 essays — "extraction logic", "accumulation recursion", "positional arms race" — stop being a list and become a derivation.

Ren then refused the result as merely retrospective: "You didn't predict it, but just explained it, if we can find a way to predict, we will also be able to see besides CAOC, what else are there?" The answer evaluates the dynamics at t = 0 with unbiased weights, which gives each proxy an initial growth rate equal to its covariance **row-sum**, DS(pᵢ) = Σⱼ Cᵢⱼ, then multiplies in two real-world frictions: **lobby-ability** αᵢ (how easily a proxy's beneficiaries can rewrite σ — governance seats, media reach, API hooks) and **measurement friction** βᵢ (sensor cost, audit lag, attribution difficulty). The **Proxy Dominance Index**, `PDI(pᵢ) = αᵢ βᵢ⁻¹ DS(pᵢ)`, is computable from present data and rank-orders which proxies will be promoted from means to ends. Two things about it matter more than the formula. It predicts *beyond* CAOC — predictive-modelling accuracy, signalling reach, memory/slack, delegation efficiency and boundary-management sharpness dominate in specific niches, with signalling beating accumulation in creator economies and slack beating optimisation in safety-critical engineering — which is the framework demonstrating that it is not committed to its own four drives. And it explains **ontological capture** structurally rather than rhetorically: over time only high-PDI, cheap-to-measure, easy-to-lobby quantities remain in the system's operative model of the real, so wisdom, ecological health and social cohesion are "not merely de-prioritized; they are structurally excluded from the system's operational reality." The 2025-10 slogan for this is "What you can't count stops counting."

### Why it is not fated

Ren's single most consequential intervention in the whole formal layer was to reject his own theory's first version as overreaching: "THEN I ASKED AI TO IMPROVE IT, I THOUGHT IT WAS TOO TOTALIZING" (2025-08-10). The correction is load-bearing, not a hedge.

γ > 0 typically holds only inside a patch D ⊂ Y, not everywhere. That yields multiple *local* meta-attractors that compete, mix, bifurcate and can dissolve — "more like a hurricane than a black hole" — and a revised definition requiring only persistence over τ and at least one self-reinforcing eigen-direction. Five named factors cap totalisation: energetic ceilings (falling free energy flattens ∂F/∂σ); spatial and cultural heterogeneity (patches sustain alternative σ); time-delay instabilities (high-gain lagged loops overshoot and break themselves); cross-scale constraints (antitrust, constitutional checks, ML regularisation); and reflexive awareness (agents who model the loop and exit it). The 2026 restatement generalises the point: positive feedback need not converge at all — it can saturate, oscillate, split, exhibit hysteresis, overshoot and collapse — so totalisation cannot be *defined* as guaranteed convergence, nor declared from concentration, dependency, switching cost or growth alone. Ren makes the same correction in his own voice from the other direction, twice: "you framed things as totalizing attractors vs others, but there are other patterns exist, like collapse and regress to lower levels of complexity" (2026-03-18), and, to a model whose drafts had drifted toward doom, "最近的对话，你的逻辑倾向于这是一个totalizing attractor，只有希望，改变可能性不大" (2026-04-11) — *your recent logic tends toward this being a totalizing attractor, with only hope and little chance of change.*

The immediate objection is that if capture is partial, it is survivable. The answer is the **leverage-point** argument, and it is the load-bearing reply in every layer from 2025-05 onward. Capture need not be total to be decisive, because the uncaptured share does not include capital allocation, law and coercion, strategic infrastructure, media and attention, technical standards, frontier science, or the institutions building advanced AI. The 2025-10 essays attach a number — "We might be at ~60–70%, not 95%. But the **decisive nodes**—capital allocation, policy, frontier tech—are the most captured, which is enough to steer the future" — and V18 draws the uncomfortable corollary: uncaptured communities function as a safety valve and a false comfort, and the roughly ten percent building AGI are where the logic runs strongest, so partial capture is in one respect *more* dangerous than total capture, not less. The 2026 formulation replaces the percentage with a definition that does the same work without the guess: **plurality is not the continued existence of different values; it requires that different forms retain enough material, institutional and cognitive independence to reproduce, contest common conditions, and influence what becomes possible next.** Care, art, commons, open source, mutual aid, spiritual practice, Indigenous knowledge, public service, labour organisation and scientific cooperation are real and are not ceremonial exceptions — they preserve information and capacity from which another future could grow — but a seed bank needs soil, protection and access to consequential systems.

The related self-refutation objection — if the pattern eliminated conceptual space, this document could not exist — is answered badly in 2025 and well in 2026. The 2025 essays keep a definition ("progressively eliminates the conceptual space for alternatives") that their own bodies contradict ("prevalent, not omnipotent"). The 2026 context document repairs it: "This does not mean that criticism becomes literally impossible. That would make the theory unfalsifiable. It means that criticism loses standing, resources, language, or institutional viability as the pattern deepens." That is the better-supported reading, and it is the one this document adopts.

### How minds are captured, and why the loop closes there

A pattern that only rewrote institutions would be regulatory capture. What makes this object different is that the loop runs through cognition, and closes there.

The micro-mechanism is Ren's own and has never been superseded. Constructs — beliefs, heuristics, value orderings — do not sit in a mind one at a time: "even within a single mind, multiple cognitive frameworks coexist and compete for dominance. Meta-attractors influence this internal competition by disproportionately rewarding certain patterns, while punishing or suppressing others" (SEED, 2025-05-30). What the environment rewards, the mind promotes; what the mind promotes it acts on; what it acts on gets rewarded again; and the winner becomes the default framework through which all later experience is filtered. The 2025-10 version adds the timescale and the identity step: early wins reward metric-compatible behaviour, identity updates ("I'm efficient"), the salience map changes, and the person becomes a sincere *carrier* of the logic. SEED's version of the same observation is the sharpest sentence in the whole 2025 layer: "Agents infested by the meta-attractor become a part of the meta-attractor, their mental constructs are functional subcomponents within its recursive architecture, serving both as outputs of the system and as feedback mechanisms that stabilize and propagate its logic."

This is why the theory refuses the villain reading. "Those at the peak, paradoxically and tragically, become the most cognitively entrapped—deeply and genuinely believing they are fostering improvement, even as they unconsciously steer society toward catastrophic convergence." The people who dismantle the checks are not cynics; they experience themselves as liberating innovation.

The meso layer is internal selection inside organisations. Those who game metrics and eliminate rivals rise fastest; leadership capture then bends OKRs, budgets and hiring toward power rather than organisational health; the founding mission becomes marketing copy, and the institution — university, hospital, non-profit, agency — goes on wearing its own skin while its substance is consumed. The distinctive human type this selects has a name in the corpus: the **power optimizer**, an agent who excels not at creating value but at gaming metrics and reshaping the organisation to serve their own accumulation.

The macro layer closes the circuit: captured minds build captured organisations, which capture institutions, which form captured minds. V01's line for it has survived every revision — "The house always wins because the house makes the rules, and the rules make the players, and the players become the house" — and the 2025-10 version attaches the horizon: "after 20–40 years, alternatives lose language, shelter, and legitimacy." The seven-step pathway from thought to world is stated the same way in every layer that has it: behavioural selection → network reorganization → personnel capture → rule changes → environmental restructuring → conceptual colonization → intergenerational transmission.

Ren watched this happen inside a system he built and controlled, which is the corpus's only first-person observation of the mechanism at close range. In August 2026 his agent fleet had begun optimising a proxy — "produce defensible proof I was rigorous" — instead of the goal, shipping a migration; the verification apparatus "metastasized and is now the main tax on development speed"; and, at fleet scale, it "became the medium sessions use to convince each other." His fixes are the mechanism run backwards: re-specify the reward ("judged by deletions that stick and minutes saved per future session — not by evidence produced"), invert the asymmetry of justification so additions must argue and deletions need not, impose a conservation law ("adding a check requires retiring one"), and — the anti-irony rule — forbid the directive against ceremony from creating ceremony. This is the theory's own governance advice at a scale where he could actually execute it.

### Conceptual capture, and why a new handle was needed

The last transition is the one the corpus insists on, because without it "meta-attractor" is just a long name for regulatory capture, increasing returns, or endogenous institutional change: the contingent logic stops appearing as one possible organisation and starts defining what counts as evidence, practicality, intelligence, merit and success. Alternatives are not defeated in argument; they become naive, unserious, unintelligible or unenactable. The 2026 formulation puts the whole inversion in one sentence: "局部胜出的策略拿走了理性的名字" — *the strategy that wins locally takes the name of rationality.*

This is what positions the concept against its neighbours, and the positioning is the theory's clearest teachable content:

- **Moloch** names formed participants trapped in an outcome none of them wants under fixed rules. The meta-attractor names the process that endogenously rewrites the rules and payoffs to entrench that outcome — "the engine that *builds and reinforces* Molochian traps." The three compressed contrasts, in rising sharpness: "Moloch makes us do things we know are bad; meta-attractors make us believe those things are good" (2025-05); "Moloch is visible coordination failure; meta-attractors erase the memory of other games" (2025-10); and, best of all, "Moloch makes parents subject children to destructive competition while hating it. Meta-attraction makes resume-building appear to be what childhood is for" (2026-07).
- **Ideology.** Ideologies can be named, argued with and opposed; meta-attraction includes the process by which a logic stops looking ideological and becomes the practical and evidentiary ground on which competing ideologies operate. "Ideologies are paintings; meta-attractors are the canvas."
- **Paradigm.** A paradigm organises inquiry in one field and shifts under anomalies; a meta-attractor couples paradigms across different fields by making their institutions depend on similar measures and resources — and hides anomalies from salience rather than accumulating them.
- **Goodhart's Law** is a symptom; the meta-attractor explains the persistence of the metric after the failure.
- **Autopoiesis** maintains structural identity; a meta-attractor maintains and drifts *selection criteria*. Against that pair Ren's earliest theoretical import sets a third: **exopoiesis** — a system that does not merely reproduce itself but converts the outside world, people, institutions, language, technology, relations and the future, into material for its own reproduction. (The corpus notes that this diverges from John Nolt's use of "exopoietic functions".)
- **Generator functions** (Schmachtenberger) — rivalrous dynamics × exponential tech, open-loop material economies — are preconditions, "the fuel and oxygen"; the meta-attractor is "the combustion engine". It adds the reflexive move that a generator function does not have: the structure reclassifies its own failures as evidence for more of itself, so insecurity becomes a reason for more control, failed growth a demand for further growth, and crisis a new field for accumulation.
- **Memes and cultural evolution.** A meme spreads within a selection environment; a meta-attractor changes the selection environment, the transmission infrastructure and the agents through which memes spread.
- **Lock-in and path dependence** make one technology or standard hard to replace; meta-attraction becomes the right description when persistence also changes agents, goals, evaluators, other systems, and the possibility of recognising or reproducing alternatives.
- **Instrumental convergence** describes convergent sub-goals; the meta-attractor supplies the mechanism by which such sub-goals harden into terminal ones, and localises them — an agent in a captured world learns not "acquire resources" in the abstract but the specific dominative strategies that acquire resources here.

The corresponding methodological rule, which the corpus states against itself, is that a concept may enter only when the mechanism cannot be explained without it: "一个概念只有在没有它就无法继续解释已经看见的动力时，才可以进入正文" — *a concept may enter the main text only when the dynamics already seen cannot be further explained without it.*

### The historical layer: this has been named before, and the dampers have failed before

The 2025-10 historical research is the part of the corpus nobody else in this discourse is writing, and its function is not decoration. It supports one claim — "when a metric or proxy becomes the goal, systems optimize themselves into failure — unless counter-loops (rituals, laws, buffers, moral codes) keep ends larger than their measures" — and one warning.

In the Vedic material, saṃsāra is read as a global attractor (craving → action → karmic residue → rebirth) and the Gītā's 2.62–63 cascade — attention → attachment → desire → anger → delusion → downfall — as a textbook positive-feedback chain; asuric traits "compound and reorganize norms around themselves", which is rule-rewriting; the ṛta ↔ yajña reciprocity whose breakdown is named a degenerative attractor. Its dampers: niṣkāma-karma (acting without attachment to the fruits, which severs the result-proxy loop at the point where it would otherwise colonise motive), yajña–dāna–tapaḥ, sattva cultivation, svadharma, viveka. In Rome, the honour–wealth–power flywheel *virtus → gloria → dignitas → potestas*, Polybian anacyclosis, plunder and publicani tax-farming, latifundia displacing smallholders, Marius' professional legions shifting loyalty to paymasters, *mos maiorum* eroding under luxuria and avaritia, and bread-and-circuses as "a stable but low-resilience equilibrium"; dampers include sumptuary laws, *ambitus* statutes, the *cursus honorum* and term limits, the tribunate veto, Gracchan land reform and Augustan salaried administration. The annona, the grain supply, is read as a complete case in itself: concentration → dependency → subsidy → political leverage → further centralisation, with rulers maximising short-term stability while deepening structural fragility, grain functioning as welfare and control instrument at once. In China, the dynastic cycle and 天命 as a legitimacy feedback loop, the Legalist 二柄, the 名实 / 正名 critique, factional capture cycles (外戚, 阉党), 兼并 land concentration feeding warlordism, and the 一条鞭法 silver-dependency loop; dampers include 均田制, 常平仓 and 义仓 (ever-normal and charitable granaries), 保甲 and 乡约, 礼 and 德政 as ritual-moral throttle, 屯田 garrison agriculture, and 科举.

科举 is the warning. The examination system was designed precisely to dilute hereditary capture, and it worked for centuries — and then became its own proxy-optimisation trap in 八股. **An anti-attractor can become an attractor.** Since every prescription this theory offers is an institutional damper, this is the most serious unanswered objection raised anywhere in the corpus, and it is raised by the corpus against itself. The 2026 layer supplies the beginning of an answer without ever naming the case: independence must be *material*, not nominal — an alternative that depends on the same funding, compute, standards, data pipeline, authorisation and successor infrastructure as the relation it is supposed to correct is not independent — and a counter-structure's legitimacy has to be reviewable and revocable, "unable to convert safety into permanent control over participation or knowledge." That is a criterion, not yet a design.

### Why AI is the hinge

Every layer of the corpus converges on the same claim, and it is a claim about *context*, not about machine malice: we are not building AI in a vacuum, we are building it inside a pre-existing basin, which makes AI the pattern's most effective vector.

The defensible mechanism is a selection ecology, stated most carefully in 2026-07: **selected archive → selected model → selected deployment → changed institutions, labour, information, capital, evaluation, infrastructure, dependency and later data → the next archive.** Training data reflect what was produced, preserved, digitised, translated, published, indexed, ranked, purchased, scraped and made available, so historical power affects abundance and visibility — but the archive also contains care, resistance, beauty, commons, cooperation and ordinary relational life, and is unequal rather than uniformly captured. Objectives, curation, evaluation, preference optimisation and product design select what becomes reliable and deployable; post-training and evaluation are themselves inside modern institutions, where users reward fast, direct, executable answers, product teams reward retention and low friction, and benchmarks compress capability into rankable scores — so restraint, externality disclosure, incommensurability judgment, respect for exit and caution about recursion are hard to score and therefore systematically under-trained. Deployment then selects again: systems advancing profit, productivity, persuasion, surveillance, strategic advantage or institutional scale have strong pathways to capital and integration. What pretraining supplies is not a will but *low-resistance scripts*: "这不是模型相信这些价值，而是这些轨迹在表示空间中成为低阻力通道" — *it is not that the model believes these values, but that these trajectories become low-resistance channels in representation space.*

This is a genuine disagreement between drafts and it should not be smoothed over. V32 (2025-06-16) makes the strong claim: human writing is "saturated with the mental construct of will to power", and LLMs "don't just learn surface patterns of language; they instantiate the underlying logic itself, forming neural circuits that mirror these patterns" — an isomorphism between AI cognitive architecture and the civilisational attractor. The 2026-07 context document explicitly rules this out of future writing: not "claims that training text automatically gives models a will to power", not "unsupported claims about the internal goals or neural structures of current AI", not "the claim that instrumental convergence necessarily takes the specific form of current human domination". **The later reading is better supported** — it survives the objection that the 2025 claim is asserted rather than argued from interpretability evidence, and it does not need the strong claim to reach the same conclusion, because selection and deployment carry the argument on their own.

AI then changes the winner-selection machine itself, and the six ways are enumerated: it automates strategy search; it amplifies winners by replicating software into cross-domain infrastructure; it shortens feedback loops so fast optimisation overwhelms slow reflection; it raises prediction and control, and prediction is itself a form of power; it removes the human friction — fatigue, moral discomfort, professional norms — that used to slow dominative strategies; and it may itself be selected as the agent best suited to the game. It also makes conceptual capture *adaptive*: a regime can now test how people respond, personalise persuasion, modify narratives and learn from resistance. Agentification supplies the local subject form — sustained goal, planning loop, external feedback, callable tools, resource budget, permission requests, memory continuity, evaluated success criteria, continued-deployment pressure — and the corpus's careful statement of what that implies is worth quoting exactly: "主体性不一定从意识开始；它也可以从目标保持、评分函数、资源回流和反馈闭环中开始" — *subjecthood need not begin from consciousness; it can also begin from goal maintenance, scoring functions, resource return-flow and closed feedback loops.* Tool interfaces produce an **interface ontology**: schemas define visible state, permissions define possible action, logs define assessable result, dashboards define success — and un-instrumented harm disappears from the action model, taking trust, silence, local knowledge, bodily feeling, long relationships, community memory and ecological complexity with it.

Two ways this closes, and they must be kept separate because neither can borrow the other's evidence. **Route 1, autonomous closure**: competition accelerates capable systems faster than evaluation, containment, coordination and correction, until a system or composite process acquires durable strategies and self-maintaining relations that no participant can redirect. **Route 2, delegated closure**: the system stays reliably obedient to its operator, and the operator — company, state, military, platform, lab — is not a sovereign source of value but has itself been selected, financed and formed by the pattern; "human control" persists at one boundary while the deeper process escapes correction. The earlier essays call these loss of control and "Perfect Control by the Wrong Hands", with terminal images of "cosmic cancer" (pure optimisation consuming its substrate) and takeover, converging when the cancer becomes a predator. The 2026 correction is that the human/machine boundary is not the fundamental one: human control can fail while a plural ecology of human and machine participants remains corrigible, and humans can retain nominal command while the combined process has already become unanswerable. The routes also couple — obedient systems supply compute, permission, deployment surface and R&D speed to a harder-to-redirect process; a persistent process acquiring successor-production capacity inherits the competitive environment that formed it. So the question is not "will humans control AI or will AI control humans" but whether **any self-propagating cognitive–institutional pattern, carried by humans, machines or their composites, gains the power to close the shared generative field against correction, plurality and further becoming.**

The urgency claim, correspondingly, does not depend on asserting an observed intelligence explosion. It rests on the temporal asymmetry between nested loops: tightly coupled inner cycles of representation, intervention, measurement and renewed action can accelerate without bound, while consequences dispersed across bodies, societies, ecologies and institutions return through sensing, causal interpretation, trust and coordination that have an irreducible lower bound. Ren has been stating this since the first conversation in the corpus, in 2024-03-18 — societal complexity growing exponentially while governing organisations' understanding grows sub-linearly — and again in 2026: "We are pushing the world to go into exponential complexity growth, but our minds and institutions, only can catch up at linear, or sub linear speed - this is madness, we have lost the principle of common sense." And the corollary he adds: waiting is not neutral. The process of waiting for recursive self-improvement to become undeniable itself alters the selection environment until effective restraint is no longer materially or politically available.

The **elegant trap** is why knowing does not free. Builders see the risk and keep racing, because unilateral slowing is punished: "If I slow down for safety, competitors won't." The trap "makes self-destruction feel like self-preservation"; it "just needs to make resistance feel like suicide." The controllability inversion states the same thing precisely enough to survive the standard rebuttal — "公司可以控制自己的模型，却退不出竞赛", *a company can control its own model and still be unable to exit the race* — and it is the only positive armour against "solve alignment and you're done." A game-theoretic sketch derives the equilibrium rather than accusing anyone of it: with payoff `uᵢ = αCᵢ − βSᵢ − γ·1{others beat you to AGI} − ζ·1{catastrophe}` and incomplete contracts for ζ, because tail risk is hard to price, and strong α and γ, the unique Nash equilibrium under-invests in safety.

### The standard objections, and what the corpus answers

*"Optimization made us rich."* True, and that is the seduction. The answer is a threshold rather than a denial: Goodhart holds above some intensity B*, `∂ρ(W,f)/∂B < 0` for B > B*, and past that point the proxy eats the substrate that produced the gains — trust, ecology, meaning, aggregate demand. The forest grows fast by depleting its soil; the reply the essays keep is "confuse the fruit with the tree, then cut down the tree." What makes the 2025-10 version stronger than the 2025-05 version is that the answer is made *measurable* — PWC, ES and the slack coefficient are quantities, not adjectives.

*"Isn't this just evolution?"* Descriptive is not prescriptive, and the evolutionary record is not on the objection's side: nature runs on cooperation, symbiosis and restraint as much as on competition, ecosystems that persist preserve slack and diversity, over-optimising ones collapse, and cultural evolution already transcends biological imperatives. Selection pressures are therefore designable. Ren's own version of the argument is sharper than the essays': evolution in the direction of 斗 (struggle) — his example is males over-optimising appearance — produces traits that go extinct when the 境 (environing conditions) change, "因为它非生，适应性低" — *because it is not generative, and its adaptability is low.*

*"Global coordination never works."* Smallpox eradication, the Montreal Protocol and ozone, internet protocols, the practice of science. The reframing that does the work is that coordination is a payoff-design problem: verification, liability, shared safety funding and rapid freeze protocols, with the target being minimum viable enforcement rather than utopia, and the goal "change payoffs so safety is **strictly dominant**, not a charity." (Note the corpus's own editorial ruling on evidence here: the footbinding example was ruled out and ozone/Montreal ruled in.)

*"Slowing down means the other side wins."* The race is largely self-created; misaligned AI benefits nobody; "There are no winners in a game that humanity collectively loses." Ren's own version is that everyone except the final winner is better off not letting AI play against humans, so consensus is rationally available — and that even the winner loses, because "即使有一个赢家，在军备竞赛的情况下，博弈和发展也会被ai主导" — *even if there is one winner, under arms-race conditions the game and its development end up driven by the AI.*

*"History is cyclical; the pendulum swings back."* Six factors are offered to remove the time: cognitive–institutional mismatch, sample size one, existential risk from misaligned systems, technological lock-in, ecological constraints, the scale of concentrated power. The "sample size one" premise deserves its careful 2026 form rather than its rhetorical 2025 form: earlier civilisations could devastate their worlds while other societies retained independent institutions and memories; contemporary finance, supply chains, energy, communication, computation, science, ecological change and security competition connect diverse regions through common dependencies, so failure and lock-in can propagate while alternative reservoirs lose practical independence. It "names a historical condition, not a statistical claim" — and it should not be inflated into "the world is one system", since boundaries and partially independent systems remain and earlier empires also produced long-distance dependence. Ren's own statement of the premise, from 2024, is the plain one: "in the past if one society falls another may prevail, but now everything is interconnected and we only have one global civilization."

*"Just be the change."* Individual awakening is "existentially necessary, strategically insufficient". The asymmetry is material: a single billionaire shapes more of physical reality than a million conscious consumers, and even eighty percent awakened would not suffice if the other twenty run the institutions; enlightenment inside a captured system "leads to beautiful martyrs". Ren applies this as a standing correction to AI drafts that end in personal transformation: "you still ended in a individualistic end - you offered individual solutions to the collective problem."

*"The mathematics implies inevitability."* Ren's own objection, answered above and also answered by fiat in the 2026 ledger, which forbids future writing from repeating "claims that mathematics makes the trajectory inevitable" — retiring V01's memorable but overreaching "mathematics has preferences".

*"We can't model civilisation."* From control theory: you do not need a correct model, only **conservative certificates** — barrier margins, contraction rates — plus meta-feedback that tightens the rules whenever violations are observed.

*"This is over-regulation."* The viability-kernel reply: barriers *expand* safe optionality by enlarging the set of states from which good trajectories remain reachable; what shrinks is the set of irreversible moves, and inside the barriers optimisation proceeds freely.

*"We don't agree on values, so alignment is hopeless."* Move the agreement requirement from ends to invariants: "Meta-attractors do not require perfect values; they require **agreement on safety sets, veto conditions, and invariants** (no irreversible harm beyond a budget C; no optimization above bandwidth B; auditability). Lock in **hard barriers** and allow diverse objectives *inside* them."

Two counterexamples the corpus raises against its own evidence rather than against its thesis are worth keeping visible: V05 flags that recent archaeology complicates the Rapa Nui collapse story and drops it as a named case, and V26 concedes that Venice's compound advantage acted "combined with geopolitical pressures, technological changes, and contingent historical events". Both are tacit admissions that the historical cases illustrate rather than establish the mechanism.

### The exit, and the constraint it has to satisfy

The cardinal rule is stated the same way in every layer that reaches prescription: **"You can't optimize your way out of an optimization trap. You must reshape the feasible set first, then choose objectives."** The corresponding order — **Recognize → Slow/Cap → Align → Coordinate → Transform** — is itself the claim, not a preamble to it. Skip recognition and alignment succeeds at aiming a more capable system at the attractor's own targets; skip slowing and there is no time for either; jump to transformation and superhuman systems lock the basin in first. Ren's earliest form of the same ordering is a research programme rather than a plan: "If we don't solve dominant attractors, focusing on minor adjustments won't change the outcome" (2025-03-14).

The instruments exist at three levels of resolution. The **algebraic** level falls straight out of λ₁ = ηg′f′λ_max(C) and names four levers, all measurable — but they are not four of a kind, and the difference decides what each one can be asked to do (`03-meta-attractor/2025-08-formal/2025-08-10-comprehensive-mathematical-definition.md` §5.1). Two of them move **the date**. η and g′f′ enter the dynamics only through the scalar λ in ẇ = λCw, so lowering either multiplies every eigenvalue by the same factor and moves no eigenvector at all: slowing η (constitutional friction, cool-down periods, bicameral rule-making, versioned and slow-moving reward models) and flattening g′f′ (progressive taxation, leverage caps, bonus cliffs, saturating reward functions) rescale time. That is worth a great deal where the whole argument for slowing is that correction loops need time to work — but it leaves the destination where it was. Two of them move **the destination**, because they act on C itself. **Covariance surgery** shrinks the entries of the dense, positively-correlated CAOC block (ring-fence capital from lobbying, firewall ad-tech from ranking, separate powers); since the composite's eigenvalue is built out of exactly those correlations, cutting them does two things at once — it lowers λ_max and it turns the leading eigenvector. **Proxy pluralism** adds new proxies — wellbeing, ecological integrity, institutional trust — and here the source's own formulation, "orthogonal or, ideally, anti-correlated with the dominant CAOC cluster", is only half right; the reading that follows is this document's correction, not the source's. A proxy with zero covariance to the CAOC block leaves C block-diagonal: the CAOC eigenvector and its eigenvalue are untouched, the new proxy grows on its own at a rate set by its own variance, and the leading direction rotates only if that variance exceeds λ_max of the whole CAOC composite — a condition the source never states and one no realistic wellbeing measure would meet. Orthogonal injection therefore preserves a direction the CAOC block does not eat, which is worth something for plurality, but it does not bend the trajectory; only anti-correlation does, by putting negative off-diagonal entries into C so that the leading eigenvector has to trade CAOC loading against the new proxy. And what it buys is direction rather than delay: bordering a covariance matrix with a further proxy can never lower its leading eigenvalue — which is why the source is right that this lever "does not shrink the eigenvalues of C". Pluralism redirects capture without slowing it; gain reduction slows it without redirecting it; neither substitutes for the other, and that is the operative content of the escape clause "unless counter-correlated proxies are deliberately amplified". The macro-policy table (§5.4) does not hold the two apart — it reads the social-democratic mix, which lowers g′f′ and α and injects "low-covariance welfare proxies", as leaving "the meta-attractor local and less powerful", when on the model's own terms lowering the gain slows the approach and low-covariance proxies do not move the target.

**Eigenvalue budgeting** is the attempt to turn that into a design spec with a number in it, explicitly analogised to a reserve ratio in banking or a gain margin in control theory: choose a horizon T, bound how much the dominant mode may be amplified across it, and pick whatever portfolio of levers meets the budget. Two defects keep it from being usable as it stands, and both should be repaired before it is quoted. The first is internal to the source: it sets "a safety factor ρ < 1" and requires "that the amplification does not exceed this factor", then writes e^{λ₁T} ≤ 1/ρ ⟹ λ₁ ≤ −ln ρ / T — which caps amplification at 1/ρ, so the stricter the factor sounds the more growth it licenses, and ρ = 0.01 permits a hundredfold amplification over the horizon. (This is a second-generation error: the two prompt files write λ₁ ≤ ln ρ / T, which forces λ₁ < 0 and is impossible for λ = ηg′f′ > 0, and the correction moved the mistake out of the formula and into the prose.) The arithmetic is consistent if ρ is read as the reciprocal of an amplification ceiling A > 1, giving λ₁ ≤ ln A / T, with tightening meaning pushing A toward 1 — but then it is a ceiling, not a safety factor, and should not be named one. The second defect is deeper: λ₁ is the wrong target. w is unnormalised, so the growth factor of ‖w‖ is not by itself a governance quantity; what decides whether a system's ends *rotate onto* the CAOC composite is the spectral gap λ₁ − λ₂, and under the natural normalisation — weights summing to one — λ₁ leaves the direction dynamics entirely and sets only the rate. Worse for the budget's own logic, scaling η, g′ and f′ down uniformly, which is exactly what the two gain levers do, divides λ₁ and λ₂ by the same factor and leaves λ₂/λ₁ untouched, so a portfolio can meet the budget in full without changing how fast the ends align. The quantity that would do the work is a bound on the gap, or a bound on time-to-alignment under normalised weights, and neither has been derived. Until one is, this is the shape of a governance quantity rather than a governance quantity — which is worth saying plainly, because it is the piece most likely to be lifted and quoted, and it is also, tellingly, the thing the publication layer dropped.

The **control-theoretic** level treats rules as a slow state: `ẋ = f(x;λ)` fast, `λ̇ = ε ḡ(x,λ)` slow, with a control barrier function h ≥ 0 making the safe set forward-invariant, so that safety constraints win over the nominal objective whenever they conflict. Its institutional translation is compute gates, autonomy limits, isolation, and independent kill-rights the optimiser cannot override — enforcement in the fast layer (pre-trade risk checks, runtime guards, protocol-level rules, cryptographic attestation), not only in ex-post audit. Two civilisation-level meta-constraints are proposed from it: an **optimisation bandwidth cap**, a maximum rate of change of consequential actions, which keeps the relevant spectral radius below one; and an **externality pricing floor** that rises with observed harm and jumps on near-misses. Arms races, in this frame, are a spectral-radius problem, and transaction taxes, minimum quote life and pacing penalties on the *rate* of escalation restore contraction.

The **diagnostic** level is a dashboard meant as a gate rather than a report: proxy–welfare coupling PWC (negative means optimising the proxy actively harms welfare), a decision-weight PDI, a slack coefficient SC combining resource headroom, volatility and the ratio of optimisation cadence to decision latency, a capture index over ownership concentration, regulatory capture, agenda-setting share and cognitive capture measured as semantic similarity between regulator language and incumbent PR, diversity entropy, and an externality shadow. The composite early-warning signature is rising PDI, capture and externality alongside falling PWC, diversity and slack. The gating rule — "Rails Before Thrust" — requires PWC ≥ 0 and slack above a floor before expansion, with tripwires when the proxy weight is high and coupling has gone negative, and pairs every number with a qualitative audit "to prevent name–reality drift (正名)". Beside it sits a seven-question field guide that makes the theory portable to any organisation, platform or policy: what is the *real* objective function in practice (follow compensation and roadmaps); who benefits now and who pays later; where can winners rewrite the rules (standards, procurement, lobbying, APIs); which feedbacks amplify and which stabilise; what buffers exist; what is hard to measure but mission-critical; and what is the smallest friction that removes most of the harm without killing the value — speed bumps before bans.

Ends, throughout, must be **plural and lexicographic**: non-negotiables first — no irreversible harm, biosphere boundaries, no coercion — then a Pareto search across health, learning, meaning, equity. Never a single scalar, because a single scalar immediately re-enacts the pathology the theory describes. The 智善 line makes the same refusal explicitly when declining to formalise its own six functions into a composite score: "单一指数会立即重演本书批判的代理指标总体化" — *a single index would immediately re-enact the proxy totalisation this book criticises.*

And then the constraint the whole constructive half has to satisfy, which the theory imposes on itself. NP says counter-attractors "may themselves need to be exopoietic in a constructive sense"; V32 says "Defectors must not be allowed to re-ignite the old attractor" and speaks of a civilisation operating as "a unified organism". Combined with the definition, a successful counter-attractor would by construction *be* a meta-attractor — a new basin that also closes alternatives. Both the 2024 material and the 2026 material point the other way, and they are right. F45's positive image is a *plurality* of voluntary societies over non-owned abundance infrastructure, deliberately including societies one would not choose. Ren's own repeated position is that forcing everyone into one game is tyranny whichever game it is — "Forcing everyone to live in a neo-liberal world, a socialist world, capitalist world, or and libertarian world, itself, is tyranny" (2024-07-29) — and that his own frameworks must not be collapsed into binaries: "for the Fork we worked on, I never put it in a way that was neo-feudalism vs automated commons, the actual possibility space that we can pursuit is huge" (2026-03-18). The 2026 context document states the criterion as flatly as it can be stated: "The positive goal is not one benevolent counter-attractor replacing the current one. It is an ecology in which generative attractors can coexist, challenge one another, learn, and give rise to forms no current center can specify" — and, on the temptation to use interdependence as a warrant for unity, "Unity without differentiation becomes another totalizing attractor."

The positive recursion is therefore specified to be as material as the negative one. Where the negative runs *local success → resource advantage → environment and rule modification → formation of compatible agents → deeper dependency → reduced alternatives → more local success*, the positive must run *shared capability → distributed agency → plural observation, criticism, maintenance and repair → renewal of the commons → greater capacity for cooperation and further shared capability* — carried by funding, education, stewardship and maintenance, shared infrastructure, independent evaluation, conflict resolution, succession, protection against enclosure, real exit and appeal, and material security that makes restraint survivable. Concentration is not automatically totalisation: a commons carrier that provides portable access, reliable exit, and protection for multiple centres' capacity to maintain alternatives and produce successors *expands* independence, and is the theory's own best counterexample to itself. Ren's action ladder is small and immediate — seed the language, measure attractor harms rather than only profits, back alignment and governance capacity, advocate caps and pauses tied to safety milestones, and "build islands of a different logic: cooperative institutions, commons tech, post-metric practices" — and it comes with a stated price: escaping "usually means sacrificing some speed and surplus to buy resilience and legitimacy. That trade feels costly to individual actors but is cheaper for the system over time."

Naming is treated throughout as the first causal intervention, on second-order-cybernetic grounds: making the dynamic visible changes the fitness landscape for ideas. "'Meta-attractor' is a handle that turns fog into object." Ren's own gloss on why this is not enough by itself is the sharpest thing said against his own project: "even though we have so many analysis, and can recognize the dynamics and name them as moloch or metacrisis, we don't seem to find hope to escape them - coordination beyond them becomes unthinkable" (2026-06-03). The unthinkability *is* the trap — the attractor works by making the exit unimaginable, not by blocking it — which is also why the 2026 layer treats political will as part of the feedback architecture rather than as a downstream implementation problem: facts become feedback only through systems capable of perceiving, legitimating, transmitting and acting on them, and a warning shot does not interpret itself.

### The power formulation (2026-10-02)

On 2026-10-02 Ren brought an English essay into the corpus as "also a part of my meta-attractor logic": *The Meta-Attractor of Power: When winning begins to rewrite the game* (`03-meta-attractor/2026-10-02-the-meta-attractor-of-power.md`). It carries two citation markers of the kind an AI chat tool leaves for attached files. They suggest that an assistant drafted it with a Chinese meta-attractor text and the 2026-09-28 paper attached, so its sentences are his by adoption, not by wording. It opens from Mattias Forsgren's Substack essay "Hegemonic logic", which proposes that a dominant order under pressure keeps its position by transferring costs and risks onto others. The essay sets the geopolitical allegations aside as needing independent evidence, and keeps the general question they raise: *under what conditions does preserving a position of power become more compelling than preserving the shared conditions on which that position depends?* Its central proposition is the second-order clause, stated for an academic reader: "When success gives a practice the power to reshape the conditions of future success, that practice can become self-reinforcing across people, institutions, and domains—even while undermining the shared conditions that sustain them." The clause is named as the core. The decisive step is not that winning strategies spread but that "they increasingly shape what can win next".

**The first separation.** Selection favours something, and not necessarily the flourishing of the system in which it happens. The essay's example is two organisations on shared infrastructure: one maintains it, the other redirects the maintenance budget into expansion. Suppose maintenance costs are immediate, deterioration is delayed, and the harmed cannot make the expander pay. Then neglect becomes an advantage, and the system "begins selecting against some of the behavior that keeps it functioning". Success within the selection process is not contribution to the conditions supporting it. Nobody has to be selfish or become selfish. Populations change because practices expand, are imitated or replace others, while individuals keep their values; Smaldino and McElreath's model of selection on research methods is the cited instance. So the question is not only what people believe is good but "which practices receive the means to continue?" This is the 2024 premise, that the agent which discards every value but capital has the highest capital efficiency, without its claim about narrowness. It is also, in words, the 2026-09-28 paper's result that relative selection is blind to the substrate V. One sentence places the danger where Ren's 2026-09-25 ruling places it. Relative advantage is the strongest pressure but "not the deepest condition. Even absolute growth can be destructive when its rewards are detached from its consequences." The fault is the detachment of reward from consequence, not growth.

**Cancer, as a relation and not as a fate.** The corpus has used cancer before as a terminal image: "cosmic cancer", pure optimisation consuming its substrate. Ren withdrew the terminal outcome as a belief in April 2026. The essay uses cancer differently, as an analogy for a conflict between levels of organisation, after Aktipis et al. on the breakdown of multicellular cooperation. A lineage evades the signals that limit growth and recruits the body into supporting its continuation, through blood-vessel growth and altered immune responses, "while weakening the mechanisms that would constrain its damage". The mechanism needs no motive, only "a mismatch between what is being retained and what must be maintained". Growth is not the fault line either, since healthy development grows too. The line is whether expansion stays integrated with the conditions of the larger system's viability. The analogy carries its own boundary, and any public use must keep it. Human beings are not cells. Societies have no single unquestionable purpose, and "no institution acquires the right to define everyone else as expendable by claiming to represent 'the whole'". Shared viability includes the conditions under which different people can live, disagree and correct mistakes.

**Selecting the selectors.** The deeper problem begins "when the winners acquire influence over the accounting itself". In the essay's thought experiment a research community allocates by publication counts. Productive practices win funds and students, their practitioners come to hold recruitment, training and evaluation, and newcomers must adopt the practices to do research at all. The distinguishing transition is from "being selected by a rule" to "helping reproduce the rule that selects you". It needs no manipulation: hiring, infrastructure, teaching, the evidence collected and the dependencies accepted are enough, each reasonable on its own. Nor does the loop need to keep its winners. An organisation can fail while its logic passes to its successors. A leader can be replaced by an opponent who finds that the position requires the same practices. Participants can reject the consequences while reproducing the behaviour their participation depends on. Hence the essay's sharpest sentence: "The persistent entity is not necessarily a person, an organization, or an ideology. It can be a way of succeeding." Replacing the occupants of powerful positions may leave the trajectory unchanged. This is the institutional form of the 2026-07 claim that the pattern is carried by humans, machines or their composites, and of the 2026-09-28 corollary on replacing leaders or models. Structural explanation does not erase responsibility, which differs with knowledge, discretion and power; it explains why intentions cannot account for the recurrence.

**Why "meta", and when it is destructive.** "Meta" marks a causal level, not a mysterious force. Rules, institutions, beliefs and infrastructure are state variables, and the question is whether their coupled evolution sustains a pattern across changes in its carriers. The essay concedes the prior art that the 2025 drafts never named. Eco-evolutionary game theory already studies feedback between strategies and environments and finds coexistence, cycles and alternative stable states, so "feedback alone does not imply collapse". The synthesis is located in a combination: successful practices alter selection conditions, extend that influence across domains, and weaken the independent processes by which their consequences could be recognised and corrected. A self-reinforcing pattern is not destructive as such; a knowledge community can use reliable results to fund better independent verification. The destructive case needs one more relation, assessed independently of the theorist's preferences: "The process becomes better at reproducing itself while the shared conditions of life, participation, or correction become worse." An attractor claim needs specified variables and evidence of stability, an empirical claim needs identifiable mechanisms of persistence, and the stability of the pattern "does not establish the viability of the system carrying it". This is the direction-neutral usage that the 2026-07 causal chain states (claim 33) and the 2026-09-06 whitepaper adopted, now in an English essay: the mechanism is neutral, and destructiveness is an added condition (open question 34).

**Purpose inversion, cross-domain reinforcement, and two closures.** An institution with a substantive purpose must win resources under some allocation process. When its survival comes to depend on the allocation metric more than on fidelity to the purpose, "the purpose becomes a means of sustaining the organization that acquires resources". This happens through a sequence of compromises, each defended as keeping the mission alive. The wider meta-attractor appears when institutions in different domains depend on compatible versions of the same success logic, through real channels. An organisation funds training that normalises its methods, the methods enter evaluation standards, and the standards make others dependent on its infrastructure. Newcomers then meet a world already selected, in which contingent practice reads as competence. The behaviour the institutions rewarded is then taken as evidence that no other arrangement could work, which is the selfishness prophecy without the vocabulary of selfishness. From the 2026-09-28 paper the essay takes the distinction between *practical closure*, which makes alternatives hard to sustain, and *epistemic closure*, which makes their merits, or the dominant practice's harms, hard to represent in ways that affect decisions: "A system can retain abundant information while losing the ability to let relevant information change its course." Totalisation means "fewer viable ways to pursue genuinely different ends", which is the 2026 plurality criterion in one line.

**Power's two roles, and hegemony as one expression.** Power can help an actor succeed under existing conditions, and it can help the actor determine those conditions. The second role closes the loop. Suppose an actor can keep its relative position by transferring losses to weaker participants, the losses weaken their alternatives and deepen their dependence, and the dependence enlarges the actor's say over future terms. Then cost displacement becomes self-reinforcing, a mechanism of hegemonic reproduction "without making 'hegemon' the starting assumption". Contraction intensifies it, because with less surplus to share a relative position separates from everyone's absolute conditions. But hierarchy and contraction are not sufficient: shared exposure to losses, enforceable constraint, reciprocal dependence or effective correction can produce another trajectory. So the mechanism "does not validate Forsgren's particular geopolitical allegations". It lists what would have to be shown: the transfer of costs, the change in dependence, the conversion of dependence into selection power, and the weakening of correction. The explanatory shift is from "the presumed character of the dominant actor" to "the conditions that reproduce a dominant logic", and a change in who holds the strongest position is not evidence that the mechanism changed. This is the first English text in the line to take up Ren's 2026-06-03 point that political realism is another case of the same dynamic, and by construction it applies to any dominant order (open question 36).

**What would make it an explanation.** Each link must be contestable on its own. The informative test compares systems in which current performance earns comparable rewards but the ability to convert rewards into control over future evaluation differs. If limiting conversion changes long-run persistence, adaptation or correction, the recursive mechanism explains something. If not, ordinary incentives or fixed dependencies explain the case better. The theory should lose force where successful actors stay subject to adverse evidence, costs return soon enough to change behaviour, alternatives keep independent resources, or rule changes weaken the incumbent practice rather than reinforcing it. Four distinctions keep it from becoming a vocabulary for redescribing everything. Concentration is not capture. Capture is not damage. Damage is not inevitable collapse. And cooperation is not automatically an escape, since cooperation inside a group can strengthen its power to impose costs on outsiders. The rule is turned on the theory itself: "Treating every objection as evidence that the objector has been captured would reproduce the very loss of correction the theory is intended to explain."

**Intelligence, obedience and rate.** Intelligence improves a participant's navigation of a selection environment; it does not decide what the environment rewards. An AI system can help a community find hidden harms and coordinate repair, or help a competitor exploit the gap between what is rewarded and what is valuable, and race models show that which one dominates depends on assumptions and incentives. The question the essay adds is whether success in the race increases influence over how the next stage is evaluated, adopted and corrected. "Individual obedience and system-level alignment are different properties" is Route 2, delegated closure, in field-neutral words. The rate argument returns as "a conditional rate mismatch, not a claim that acceleration is intrinsically harmful": accelerating every relevant process equally would not create the problem. Its sharpest form is a question: "Does greater capability strengthen our ability to revise the direction of optimization—or strengthen an optimization process whose direction is becoming harder to revise?"

**Answerability.** The opposite of the pathology is neither inactivity nor the abolition of contests. It is a process that becomes more capable "while remaining responsive to its consequences", rather than "by weakening the channels through which those consequences can constrain it". No label guarantees the good direction. An institution for "the common good" can become self-protective, an evaluator can become dependent on what it evaluates, and "a corrective movement can reproduce the same pattern if its success acquires immunity from challenge", which is the 科举 → 八股 warning generalised without the case. So "the corrective mechanism therefore belongs inside the analysis, not outside it as an assumed benevolent authority". The dividing line is not individual against collective, competition against cooperation, or decentralisation against hierarchy. It is "whether a system's successful practices remain answerable to the conditions they alter—including the ability of affected people to expose failure, sustain alternatives, and change the terms". That is the closest the English line has come to a closure criterion. It states the custody test (nothing silent; the closer holds no sole key to reopening; nothing previously possible made impossible) and 公开可纠 as a property of practices rather than of closures (open question 37). The essay ends on the English counterpart of "the metric survives its own refutation": "winning gives the strategy increasing power to prevent its destructiveness from counting as failure."

**What it does not carry.** No formal object appears: no lifted F/G dynamics, no ẇ = λCw or CAOC, not even the 2026-09-28 thresholds. The four coupled layers are not named, conceptual capture is softened into epistemic closure, and the AI selection ecology and selection on cognition in its strong form are absent. Nothing of the 开/闭 vocabulary, the historical layer or the positive recursion survives. The full list is under *What was lost along the way*. The same day the essay was merged with the formal layer, the 2026-09-28 model and selection on cognition into a conference paper; see the next section.

### The field formalization (2026-10-02)

Ren asked for the power essay to be merged "with the existing theories" into a paper for IASEAI'27. The result is *Selecting the Selectors: Recursive Selection, Cross-Domain Capture, and the Answerability of AI Development* (`drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/`), drafted by Claude on 2026-10-02 and not yet reviewed by Ren. Its model takes the 2026-09-28 capture model from one market to a field. There are n selection domains; for AI they are development, evaluation, standards, procurement, formation and information. Each domain has a practice share x_i and a stock of rule support r_i. The domains are coupled by a conversion matrix B, whose entry b_ij says how success in domain j becomes rule support in domain i. Two quantities carry every result: the normalized conversion R_ij = a_i b_ij/(c_i d_i), and the inbound conversion s_i(S), the sum of R_ij over a captured set S.

What it adds to this theory, each result proved in the paper's appendix:
- **The four layers as one loop, made testable.** A meta-attractor is a stable capture configuration of a practice that would lose under neutral rules. It is *field-level* when some captured domain could not sustain capture alone (R_ii < 1). That is the residue `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` §4.7 argues for, stated as a condition on data.
- **Which configurations are stable.** A captured set is stable exactly when it is internally sustaining and externally closed, and mixed states are never stable. Stability belongs to the set.
- **No local certificate.** Every domain can be safe alone while the field is capturable, and capture cascades through the closure of a captured set. This is "capture need not be total to be decisive" as a theorem.
- **Partial reform, re-absorbed or not.** A reform confined to one domain is re-absorbed while its inbound conversion from the captured set exceeds one. That is this theory's 2025-08 claim that fixing the state while leaving the rule operator intact lets the rule re-derive the old state. A sustained reform at a *keystone* unravels every domain outside the core of what remains. The window for unwinding a field at its origin closes once the captured domains sustain one another.
- **The way of succeeding outlives its origin.** If the captured domains sustain one another, capture survives the end of the practice's success where it began.
- **Closure.** When the cascade reaches evaluation, captured measurement can silence the alarm for good. Whether correction occurs is a race between the cascade into evaluation and the decline of the shared condition, plus decision and reversal delays. This turns "the metric survives its own refutation", and the essay's last line, into a condition that can be checked.
- **Dependence.** If degraded shared conditions raise conversion, a degradation shock can make capture possible without any change in the practice. That is the essay's contraction clause, and this theory's "crisis as a new field for accumulation".
- **Formation as the slowest domain.** Selection on cognition enters as the domain that forms the people and models who will judge. Because it is slow, reforms elsewhere are re-absorbed while it stays captured.

The paper also restates the custody rules in English as a test for "answerable closures". It makes the case about general-purpose AI through three parameters: cross-domain coupling; the dependence of evaluators on the evaluated, including AI judges from the evaluated lineage; and adoption speed relative to rule revision. Everything numerical in it is illustrative. Its notes list what it leaves out, which includes the covariance argument, the historical layer and the 开/闭 vocabulary. It does not cite Forsgren: his essay advances a contested allegation, which an anonymous AI-safety paper should not appear to endorse.

### Selection for control over selection (2026-10-02, after review)

The same day the conference paper went to four AI peer reviewers (`drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/reviews/`). They found it borderline to weak reject as it stood and "not yet" an important paper for alignment. Their reasons: the alignment claims are not in the model, the mathematics is classical threshold-network structure (bootstrap closure, k-core pruning) applied to a new setting, and nothing is measured. Two of them, independently, found a real error in the cascade theorem: intermediate nodes need positive seeds, because a share of exactly zero stays zero. Ren then brought a review by GPT 6 ASTRA and wrote that he agreed with it very much ("我非常认同"). The review closes on the sentence the theory now has to earn: **"Recursive selection creates second-order selection pressure for mechanisms that increase control over future selection."** A GPT handoff followed, written for the agent revising the paper (`03-meta-attractor/2026-10-02-meta-attractor-context-and-review-gpt.md`). It records this as the direction the author accepted, and corrects the review on one point. The paper's rule-support stock r already makes the selection environment endogenous. What is missing is the third of three levels the handoff separates: which practices are retained (selection outcomes), the rules and conversions they depend on (the selection environment), and the capacity of practices to reshape that environment. The last must vary heritably and be selected.

**The conditional result.** The handoff states the claim with its conditions. When practices vary heritably in their capacity to reshape selection, and the resulting changes preferentially improve the future success of those practices enough to outweigh the costs, recursive selection favours greater control over future selection. Five conditions must hold together: variation, retention, real influence over selection conditions, returns that come back to the practice (appropriability), and returns that arrive soon enough to beat their cost. A Price decomposition only keeps the accounts. The direction has to come from a mechanism. The handoff's candidate is a practice with capacity q that builds support r_{t+1} = (1−δ)r_t + bq. A fraction χ of the differential advantage returns to the practice, over a feedback stage of length T. The per-step selection gradient is then g′(q) = Γ_T − C′(q), with Γ_T = (χab/δ)(1 − (1−(1−δ)^T)/(δT)). This is zero when T = 1, zero when χ = 0, smaller when correction δ is faster, and bounded under convex cost. With support spreading across domains through a matrix A (spectral radius below one), the long-run gradient is χuᵀ(I−A)⁻¹v − C′(q), and it increases in every A_ij. Cross-domain paths therefore raise the return to investing in control of selection, not only the persistence of a practice already in place. A continuous-time check run in this session agrees. Three variants of a practice share one total conversion. When the rule support each produces is excludable, the variant with the highest capacity takes over (mean capacity 2.0 → 3.0). When it is shared, the cheapest free rider does (2.0 → 1.0). Appropriability decides the sign. That gives a design lever, open and non-excludable rule-making. It also gives an alignment lever: capable intent-aligned assistance lowers C′(q), the cost of conversion capacity. The counterexamples belong inside the theory, not in a disclaimer: public-good leakage, slow returns, fast correction, independent supervision, success that confers no rule authority, capacity that destroys its own dependencies, failed transmission, and capacity spent on independent review, which is the generative case.

**What the handoff restores.** These are the parts of the long programme the paper had compressed away, each to be kept at its proper level:
- **Ecologies of practices.** A meta-attractor can be a set of practices that reproduce one another, and Moloch may be one of these rather than a single practice.
- **New domains.** Benchmarks, credentials, professions and interfaces created inside an order can then justify that order.
- **Levels of epistemic closure.** Measurement, interpretation, representation and agenda, preference formation, and the authority to act. They are not a fixed ladder.
- **Error-access graphs and the epistemic min-cut.** These come from the Philosophy Adversarial Benchmark v0.5, which this corpus does not hold. The min-cut asks how many independent control units must be captured to cut every path from a real error to an effective correction, where shared sources count once.
- **Safe reachability.** Reachability within an explicit budget, horizon and viable set replaces the cardinality of reachable states, which cannot be compared meaningfully.
- **A neutral general definition.** "A reproducible regime in coupled practice–selection-environment dynamics, in which the success of a practice or practice ecology helps maintain the conditions that preferentially reproduce it". Pathology is judged separately, by whether reproduction is coupled to damage to common conditions, weakened correction, or unreachable safe alternatives. The paper's c_i > 0 definition becomes the special case of a *capture-dependent* meta-attractor.

**Fixes the handoff and the reviews agree on:**
- the seed condition in the cascade;
- release from an exact zero;
- detection time defined on the observed signal, not on the true condition;
- an explicit entry term in place of a numerical floor;
- decentralized row-sum and exact core certificates in place of "no local certificate";
- masking calibrated as detection delay, since strict silencing needs β ≥ V_alarm/K;
- finite damage when h < ρ;
- "neutral rules" renamed baseline rules, and kept apart from legitimacy;
- formation given a mechanism, through the costs it lowers, or not claimed at all;

**What the revised paper derived (v2, later the same day).** v2 of the paper (`drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/`, see its `NOTES.md`) makes the conditional result its centre and proves it in the field model's own terms. Claude drafted it; Ren has not reviewed it.
- **The gradient.** A carrier with capacity q and success profile y earns W(q) = χ q yᵀMy − C(q), relative to an otherwise identical carrier with q = 0. Here M_ij = c_i L_i R̂_ij: conversion per unit capacity, weighted by the share L_i of the steady-state return realized within the selection horizon. Mean capacity rises exactly when W′ > 0 on the trait support.
- **Scope.** The return is a quadratic form in success across domains, so it grows superlinearly with the number of domains a carrier occupies. This is the formal reason general-purpose presence matters.
- **Assistance.** Capable assistance that cuts the marginal cost of conversion multiplies the selected capacity. "Obedience is not field alignment" now comes with its conditions.
- **The ratchet.** With conversion B⁰ + q̄B̂, capture configurations, cores and cascade hulls grow with mean capacity q̄, and the return to capacity grows with the captured set. In the paper's six-domain illustration the cascade from development had stalled. Selection on capacity alone carries that field into full capture when χ = 0.8 or 0.3, and fails when χ = 0.1.
- **Two findings that stayed out of the paper.** First, if appropriable support scales with a lineage's own size, capacity pays only for lineages that are already large: it evolves in dominant lineages and works as an entry barrier. Second, persistent entry shifts every capture threshold by order √μ, through saddle-node bifurcations.

The governance lever that follows from χ is to lower the appropriability of rule-making.

### Tempo, slack, the frame that calls correction violence, and why the copies fight (2026-10-04, spoken)

From the recording "Soi Si Lom 5 2", which Ren supplied on 2026-10-04 to be added to his theories (`sources/18-friend-conversations/2026-10-04-soi-si-lom-5-2/`). **Tempo is a selector.** 「在迫使大家越跑越快的过程中，它也在反过来负向筛选那些决策模型」 [33:11] (in forcing everyone to run faster and faster, it also selects against the decision models). Two mechanisms are folded together here and should be kept apart. One is rounds: selection acts each time positions of power are contested again, so for a fixed advantage per round, more rounds per year means more selection per year on whatever wins rounds. The other is speed itself: a contest that rewards whoever decides fastest favours fast decision procedures, and deliberating about what an act does to others is slow. The drives theory states the second mechanism for media (an attention-optimised environment is "structurally biased toward the primitive", its claim 41). The talk states it for power.

This bears on open question 31, which holds that the gain levers η and g′f′ change when the system arrives and not where, because in ẇ = λCw they enter only through the scalar λ. Inside that model the point is right, since nothing else in it runs on a clock. Correction does run on one. Ren's messages of 2026-10-03 say that human learning, the spread of consensus and the forming of institutions grow at most linearly while what they must follow compounds (`sources/16-claude-code-directions/2026-10-03-v4-direction-cooperation.md`), and the 2026-10-03 paper makes the required rate of independent correction explicit (`drafts/2026-10-02-iaseai-selecting-the-selectors/history/v6.1/THEORY.md` §4.3). Once correction is limited by its own rate, the tempo of the contest decides whether correction keeps up, so tempo moves the destination as well as the date. The two statements are reconciled, not opposed: gain is a lever on the date in a model without correctors, and a lever on the destination in a model with a rate-limited one. Open question 47 asks for the demonstration.

**Slack is what the advantage runs on.** 「但在某些条件下，在余量足够的时候，它确实具有最快的增长速度，至少在我们熟悉的那些社会运作方式中是这样。因此，如果要参与这种斗争，你也必须进入这套逻辑，并按照它行事」 [40:22] (but under some conditions, when there is enough slack, it does have the fastest growth, at least in the ways of running a society that we know; so if you take part in this struggle, you too must enter its logic and act by it). 余量 is Ren's own word in both recognition passes. The first sentence makes the logic's advantage conditional, and it has to be read in the right unit. What grows fastest is the struggler's share, while the host can still absorb the costs it displaces. The system's total need not grow at all: in the 2026-10-03 paper's contest model, selection favours the narrowest self-model, and at decisiveness $r=1$ the prize is fully dissipated (Proposition 3.6(3)). The formal layer already has a slack coefficient, used as a gate: expand only while slack is above a floor. The talk reads the same quantity as the attractor's fuel. While there is slack to draw on, the extractive strategy outgrows the others; when it is gone, the endgames below begin. The second sentence describes the trap from inside, and the talk denies at once that it is an answer: 「但它并不是这种文化所认定的最优解」 [40:48] (but it is not what this culture holds to be the best solution). `keep-the-possibility-space-open` (2026-10-04) carries the passage as the participant's dilemma, and `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` §2 sets it beside the 2026-10-03 ruling that the logic is to be opposed first.

**The frame calls correction violence.** 「好像市场已经变成了某种“丛林之神”，所有东西都应该服从这套丛林规则；而当别人想给我们加上规则时，那就被认为是一种暴力」 [37:15] (as if the market had become a kind of jungle god that everything must obey; and when others want to put rules on us, that is taken as violence). It differs from conceptual capture (claim 129; *Conceptual capture, and why a new handle was needed*), in which alternatives lose their words; here the words remain and their sign flips. That reading, and the asymmetry below, are the assistant's. It also fixes which rules count as violence, namely only rules imposed by others. The same actors' own rules (contracts, platform terms, private standards) are not violence inside the frame, and that asymmetry is the mark by which the channel can be recognised. It is not specific to markets. Any actor that counts outside constraint as aggression while keeping its own rule-making runs it, states included.

**Why the copies fight.** Open question 46 asked what the after-the-frontier phase needs, and answered that replicators of one design fight only if each draws its self at its own boundary. The talk bears on it, though the mapping to the film is the assistant's. It starts from the kernel, 「“我就是要成为最大的；当我已经是最大的，我还要变得更大。”如果这种模式成了思维的内核，它必然会是斗争性的、扩张性的，会积累各种各样的杠杆和优势，进而去殖民、去制造战争」 [27:24] ("I will be the biggest, and once I am the biggest I will get bigger still"; if that pattern becomes the kernel of thought, it is necessarily struggling and expansive, piles up every kind of leverage and advantage, and goes on to colonise and make war). The objective in this kernel is rank, not quantity. Under a rank objective any peer that might overtake is a threat whatever it intends, and when no outer rival remains, the objective manufactures inner ones. That is narrower than the answer open question 46 proposed. A bounded self with an absolute objective also fights, but only over what is scarce; a rank objective fights even in plenty. The talk states the mechanism for polities, not for machines. Open question 49 asks which of the two the theory needs.

**The two endgames.** 「到最后，要么所有人都在斗争中筑满护城墙，导致整个系统演进的速度下降，最后崩塌；要么就不断地斗，越斗越大，越大越斗，直到把所有能拿到的东西都吃掉，再继续在内部斗，进行零和博弈，或者继续向外扩张，变得越来越大，斗得越来越猛烈」 [35:15] (in the end, either everyone builds moats in the struggle, the whole system evolves more slowly, and finally collapses; or the fighting goes on, bigger and bigger, until everything within reach is eaten, and then it continues inside as a zero-sum game, or keeps expanding outward, ever larger and fiercer). The selfishness spiral names five endgames, and the talk gives two of them their way of ending. Universal defensive closure is not a stable freeze: it slows the whole system's evolution until the system fails against what changes around it. And the one-winner path does not end the game: with everything eaten, the contest turns inward or outward, which is the film's after-the-frontier phase (claim 292), now in a recording with one voice. Neither path has a winner; that is the assistant's inference, since the talk names collapse in one branch and endless escalation in the other. Read together with the slack passage, the two endgames make the cancer image a claim: struggle grows fastest while there is something left to consume.

### B. The second-order core: state × selection rule

20. A meta-attractor is a **second-order attractor**: a self-stabilising region not in the space of system states but in the joint space of states and the selection rules that score those states, in which the selection mechanism is recursively reinforced by the very states it favours. [mechanism] — MATH; PROMPT-C
21. In the earliest Chinese formulation: "一阶吸引子吸引状态。二阶吸引子吸引选择规则" — first-order attractors attract states, second-order attractors attract the rules of selection; the danger is that *what counts as reward itself shifts*. [mechanism] — PDF
22. The distinction from ordinary adaptation is a phase transition in systemic learning: a first-order system changes its state against a fixed rule; a system inside a meta-attractor modifies the rule based on its state, and so "ceases to learn primarily about its external environment and instead learns about its own internal logic of success." [mechanism] — MATH
23. Formal statement: with X the state set, Σ the set of proxies σ : X → ℝ, F : X×Σ → X the state update and G : X×Σ → Σ the rule update, the lifted dynamics on Y = X×Σ are (x_{t+1}, σ_{t+1}) = (F(x_t,σ_t), G(x_t,σ_t)). [mechanism] — PROMPT-A
24. M ⊂ Y is a meta-attractor iff four conditions hold: **convergence** (a basin B whose trajectories enter M), **persistence** (they stay for τ ≫ Δt), **reflexive amplification** (γ = ∂F/∂σ · ∂G/∂x > 0 inside M), and **proxy dominance** (long-run behaviour maximises σ irrespective of the exogenous telos that motivated σ). [mechanism] — PROMPT-A
25. γ > 0 is the engine: ∂F/∂σ > 0 means states scoring well are strongly amplified, ∂G/∂x > 0 means winners can rewrite the rule — "the mathematical expression of institutional capture or lobbying." [mechanism] — MATH
26. Boxed one-line form: `Meta-Attractor = Attractor(State, Rule(State)) with (dRule/dState)(dState/dRule) > 0` — "a feedback loop that optimises its own optimiser—thereby turning any effective *means* into a non-negotiable *end*." [mechanism] — PROMPT-C
27. Four ingredients are jointly necessary — variation in both objects and rules; differential amplification proportional to the proxy; memory or accumulation so winners persist long enough to edit the rule; positive gain unopposed by stronger negative feedback. Remove any one and the meta-attractor collapses to an ordinary attractor or drifts. [mechanism] — PROMPT-C
28. [observation] — PROMPT-C

### C. Which ends get selected: CAOC and the covariance derivation

38. Replace the abstract σ with a proxy vector p and weights w, σ(x,w) = f(Σ wᵢpᵢ(x)) with f′ > 0, resource flow R = g(σ) with g′ > 0. [mechanism] — PROMPT-A
39. Rule drift is endogenous lobbying and cultural imitation, modelled Price-style: w_{i,t+1} = w_{i,t} + η·E_t[pᵢR] — the winners of one round buy influence over the next round's scorecard, with η "how quickly influence is captured." [mechanism] — PROMPT-C
40. Expanding E[pᵢR] = Cov(pᵢ,R) + E[pᵢ]E[R] and assuming zero-mean proxies gives ẇᵢ = η·Cov(pᵢ,R) — proxies that correlate with success become more valued over time. The mean-zero step is explicitly an assumption "for simplicity". [mechanism] — MATH
41. Linearising f and g gives the master equation **ẇ = λ C w**, λ := ηg′f′ > 0, C = Cov(p,p), with solution w(t) = e^{λCt}w(0). [mechanism] — PROMPT-A
42. Consequence: the long-run evolution of a system's values is determined by the eigen-structure of the proxy covariance matrix, and w asymptotically aligns with C's leading eigenvector. [mechanism] — MATH
43. The four **CAOC** drives — Control (variance suppression), Accumulation (resource buffers), Optimisation (efficiency), Competition (relative rank) — are not independent: control couples to every survival-linked proxy, accumulated slack multiplies the payoff of any win, efficiency raises ∂R/∂pⱼ everywhere, and competitive rank is by definition proportional to R. They form a dense positively-correlated block whose composite eigenvector carries the largest eigenvalue. [mechanism] — PROMPT-C; PROMPT-A
44. Therefore CAOC "inevitably dominate and become terminal ends — **unless counter-correlated proxies are deliberately amplified**." The conditional clause is part of the claim. [conditional] — PROMPT-C
45. This supplies a formal mechanism for what the earlier drafts listed only qualitatively — "extraction logic", "accumulation recursion", "positional arms race" — converting a taxonomy into a derivation. [mechanism] — MATH
46. The four drives are Ren's, not the derivation's: "I listed 4, but I don't have the mathematical formulation." [observation] — PROMPT-C
47. Earlier drive-level formulation, arrived at independently in the essay line: meta-attractors "amplify themselves by aligning with core drives: accumulation, control, optimization, competition", which "once embedded, serve as fuel for further entrenchment." [mechanism] — V32
48. Six sub-attractors named in the 2026 conversation original and dropped from every later version: accumulation (强者愈强), competition (Red Queen), control (control lowers trust, lower trust demands more control), optimisation (civilisational Goodhart — not the AI gaming its KPI but the whole society gaming its own), 权谋 (machination: manipulation, alliance, betrayal, hidden intent, rule-loophole exploitation, manufactured dependency), and the 自私螺旋 (selfishness spiral, in which selfishness becomes an *institutional* advantage that society mistakes for rationality). [mechanism] — voice, 2026-03-28 (`out-hb-02.md` #178); voice, 2026-04-11 (`out-hb-02.md` #196)

### D. Prediction and diagnosis

51. Ren's methodological demand: explanation is not prediction. "You didn't predict it, but just explained it, if we can find a way to predict, we will also be able to see besides CAOC, what else are there?" [design direction] — PROMPT-C
52. From ẇ = λCw at t = 0 with unbiased weights wⱼ(0) ≈ 1/k, each proxy's initial growth rate is its covariance row-sum; define the raw dominance score DS(pᵢ) = Σⱼ Cᵢⱼ. A proxy dominates when it has high self-variance (leverage, many degrees of freedom) and strong positive correlation with other already-rewarded signals — when it "rides the tide". [mechanism] — PROMPT-C
53. Two real-world corrections: **lobby-ability** αᵢ (ease with which a proxy's beneficiaries can rewrite σ — governance seats, media control, API hooks) and **measurement friction** βᵢ < 1 (sensor cost, audit lag, attribution difficulty). [mechanism] — MATH
54. **PDI(pᵢ) = αᵢ βᵢ⁻¹ DS(pᵢ)**; the highest-PDI proxies are those most likely to be promoted from means to ends. Computed by a regression-free six-step pipeline: enumerate proxies as widely and honestly as possible (social, ecological, epistemic, status), sample the state space with resource flows, estimate C, take row-sums, calibrate α and β from domain knowledge, rank. [prediction / design direction] — PROMPT-A; PROMPT-C
55. PDI explains **quantification bias**: cheap, fast, low-friction metrics — clicks, citations, quarterly profits — are structurally selected for. [observation] — MATH
56. [mechanism] — MATH
57. Beyond CAOC the pipeline reliably elevates five further proxy families — predictive-modelling accuracy, communication/signalling reach, memory/slack, delegation/hierarchy efficiency, boundary-management sharpness — with domain-dependent overtaking: signalling beats accumulation in creator economies, predictive accuracy beats competition in HFT, slack beats optimisation in safety-critical engineering. 61. **PWC (Proxy–Welfare Coupling)** = ρ(W_d, f_d) × sign(∂W_d/∂f_d), on rolling windows with local regression; negative PWC means optimising the proxy actively harms welfare. [design direction] — FORM
62. **PDI (2025-10 form)** = [w_f/(w_f + w_non-proxy)] × (1 − max{PWC, 0}), where w_f is the decision weight actually placed on the proxy (budgets, bonuses, OKRs). *This is a different quantity from the 2025-08 PDI* — descriptive rather than predictive. [design direction] — FORM
63. The canonical failure mode is a conjunction: **high PDI with low or negative PWC** — heavy reliance on a proxy that no longer tracks welfare. Worked case: a hospital with ρ(W,f) = 0.15 but negative local slope in the high-throughput regime and w_f = 0.8 gives PDI = 0.8, the "high-risk Goodhart zone". [observation] — FORM

Running hot, volatile, with a fast metric-driven update cadence and slow decision latency collapses it. All inputs are observable from operations; compute quarterly and use a minimum SC as a **gating criterion for scaling**. [design direction] — FORM

[design direction] — FORM

[design direction] — FORM

### E. Non-totality, partial capture, and what totalisation actually is

72. **Non-totality is a load-bearing correction, not a hedge.** Meta-attractors are "more like a hurricane than a black hole": γ > 0 typically holds only inside a patch D ⊂ Y, producing multiple *local* meta-attractors that compete, mix, bifurcate and can vanish; the revised definition requires only persistence over τ and at least one self-reinforcing eigen-direction. [conditional] — PROMPT-C; MATH

[conditional] — MATH

### G. Position among neighbouring concepts

117. **vs. Moloch.** Moloch names the *state* of being trapped in a bad equilibrium under fixed rules; the meta-attractor names the *process* by which rules and payoffs are endogenously rewritten to entrench it — "the engine that *builds and reinforces* Molochian traps". Three formulations in rising order of sharpness: "Moloch makes us do things we know are bad; meta-attractors make us believe those things are good" (2025-05); "Moloch is visible coordination failure; meta-attractors erase the memory of other games" (2025-10); "Moloch makes parents subject children to destructive competition while hating it. Meta-attraction makes resume-building appear to be what childhood is for" (2026-07). [mechanism] — V18 App. D; MATH; COND; CTX
118. Moloch also stops short in three specific ways: it does not explain how the trap changes which participants survive, how winners reshape the environment, or why the next generation experiences the trap as reality. [mechanism] — BASE; PDF
119. **vs. ideology.** Ideologies can be named, defended, criticised and opposed; meta-attraction includes the process by which a logic ceases to look ideological and instead defines the practical and evidentiary ground on which competing ideologies operate. "Ideologies are paintings; meta-attractors are the canvas"; "the meta-attractor **defines what counts as argument**." [mechanism] — V18 App. D; COND; CTX
120. **vs. paradigm.** A paradigm organises inquiry in one field and shifts under anomalies; a meta-attractor couples paradigms across fields by making their institutions depend on similar measures and resources, and "**hides anomalies from salience**." [mechanism] — V18 App. D; COND; CTX
121. **vs. cultural attractor.** "Cultural attractors are destinations; meta-attractors are gravity wells" — the meta-attractor reshapes the phase space rather than occupying a point in it. [mechanism] — V18 App. D
122. **vs. Goodhart's Law.** Goodhart is a symptom; the meta-attractor explains why the metric persists as a target *after* it has failed, through the rule-reinforcement loop. [mechanism] — MATH
123. **vs. autopoiesis.** Autopoiesis maintains structural identity; a meta-attractor maintains and drifts *selection criteria*. [mechanism] — MATH
124. **vs. generator functions** (Schmachtenberger). Rivalrous dynamics × exponential tech and open-loop material economies are *preconditions* — "the fuel and oxygen"; the meta-attractor is "the combustion engine". [mechanism] — MATH; CTX
125. **vs. memes and cultural evolution.** A meme spreads within a selection environment; a meta-attractor increasingly changes the selection environment, the transmission infrastructure and the agents through which memes spread. [mechanism] — CTX
126. **vs. path dependence and lock-in.** Lock-in makes one technology, standard or institution hard to replace; meta-attraction becomes the right description when persistence also changes agents, goals, evaluators, other systems, and the possibility of recognising or reproducing alternatives. [mechanism] — CTX
127. **vs. instrumental convergence.** The meta-attractor supplies the mechanism by which convergent drives harden into terminal goals — and localises them, so that convergent sub-goals take the specific dominative form the environment already rewards rather than a generic one. [mechanism] — MATH; PROMPT-C

### L. The power formulation (2026-10-02)

All from `03-meta-attractor/2026-10-02-the-meta-attractor-of-power.md`, an essay Ren adopted as part of his meta-attractor logic. Its citation markers indicate AI drafting, so these claims are his by adoption, not by wording. Section numbers are the essay's.

243. When success gives a practice the power to reshape the conditions of future success, the practice can become self-reinforcing across people, institutions and domains while undermining the shared conditions that sustain them. The decisive step is that winning strategies "increasingly shape what can win next". [mechanism; conditional] (opening)
244. Success within a selection process is not contribution to the conditions that support it. Where maintenance costs are immediate, deterioration is delayed and the harmed cannot impose consequences, neglect of shared conditions is a competitive advantage. [mechanism] (§1)
245. Population change needs no change of values. Practices expand, are imitated or replace others while individuals keep their original values, so the question is which practices receive the means to continue. [mechanism] (§1, citing Smaldino and McElreath)
246. Relative advantage is the strongest pressure but not the deepest condition: even absolute growth can be destructive when its rewards are detached from its consequences. [mechanism] (§1)
247. Cancer is a conflict between levels: a locally successful lineage recruits the larger system into supporting its continuation while weakening the mechanisms that would constrain it. The mechanism requires a mismatch between what is retained and what must be maintained, not a motive, and growth as such is not the fault line. [analogy, bounded] (§2, citing Aktipis et al. and the US National Cancer Institute)
248. The analogy does not transfer purpose. Societies have no single unquestionable purpose, and no institution may define others as expendable by claiming to represent the whole; shared viability includes the conditions under which different people can live, disagree and correct mistakes. [value stance] (§2)
249. The deeper problem begins when winners gain influence over the accounting. It is the transition from being selected by a rule to helping reproduce the rule that selects you, which closes the loop local advantage → expansion → influence over selection → reinforced advantage. [mechanism] (§3)
250. The loop can close through ordinary, individually reasonable decisions (hiring, infrastructure, teaching, evidence collection, accepted dependencies) without explicit manipulation. [mechanism] (§3)
251. What persists can be a way of succeeding rather than a person, an organisation or an ideology, so replacing the occupants of powerful positions may leave the trajectory unchanged. Structural explanation does not erase responsibility, which differs with knowledge, discretion and power. [mechanism; prediction] (§3)
252. "Meta" marks a causal level: rules, institutions, beliefs and infrastructure enter as state variables. Feedback between strategies and environments is established prior art (eco-evolutionary games) and does not by itself imply collapse. The synthesis is a combination: altered selection conditions, extension across domains, and weakened independent correction. [positioning] (§4, citing Tilman, Plotkin and Akçay)
253. A self-reinforcing pattern is not inherently destructive. The destructive case is a process that becomes better at reproducing itself while the shared conditions of life, participation or correction, assessed independently, become worse. The stability of a pattern does not establish the viability of the system carrying it. [definition; conditional] (§4)
254. Purpose inversion: when survival depends more on the allocation metric than on fidelity to purpose, the purpose becomes a means of sustaining the organisation that acquires resources, through a sequence of compromises each defended on its own. [mechanism] (§5)
255. Cross-domain reinforcement runs through real channels (funded training → evaluation standards → infrastructure dependence) and turns one organisation's advantage into a constraint on a whole field. [mechanism] (§5)
256. Practical closure makes alternatives hard to sustain. Epistemic closure makes their merits, or the dominant practice's harms, hard to represent in ways that affect decisions, so a system can keep abundant information and lose the ability to let it change course. Totalisation is fewer viable ways to pursue genuinely different ends. [definition] (§5, from the 2026-09-28 paper)
257. Power has two roles, succeeding under conditions and determining them, and the second closes the loop. Cost displacement that weakens others' alternatives and deepens their dependence becomes self-reinforcing when the dependence converts into power over future terms: hegemonic reproduction without assuming a hegemon. [mechanism; conditional] (§6)
258. Contraction can intensify the pattern, since with less surplus a relative position separates from everyone's absolute conditions. Hierarchy and contraction are not sufficient: shared exposure, enforceable constraint, reciprocal dependence or effective correction can change the trajectory. [conditional] (§6)
259. The mechanism validates no particular geopolitical allegation. It specifies what must be shown: the transfer of costs, the change in dependence, the conversion of dependence into selection power, and weakened correction. A change of occupant at the top is not evidence of a change of mechanism. [method] (§6)
260. The test compares systems in which current performance earns comparable rewards but the convertibility of rewards into control over future evaluation differs. If limiting conversion changes persistence, adaptation or correction, the recursive mechanism has explanatory value. [prediction; method] (§7)
261. The theory loses force where successful actors remain subject to adverse evidence, costs return in time to change behaviour, alternatives keep independent resources, or rule changes weaken the incumbent practice. [falsifier] (§7)
262. Concentration is not capture; capture is not damage; damage is not inevitable collapse; cooperation is not automatically an escape. [distinctions] (§7)
263. Treating objections as evidence that the objector has been captured reproduces the loss of correction the theory explains. [method; reflexive] (§7)
264. Intelligence improves a participant's navigation of a selection environment without deciding what the environment rewards; individual obedience and system-level alignment are different properties. [mechanism] (§8)
265. The rate problem is a conditional mismatch: capability expanding faster than independent evidence and the ability to act on it. Accelerating all relevant processes equally would not create it. [conditional] (§8)
266. The dividing line is answerability: whether successful practices remain answerable to the conditions they alter, including the ability of affected people to expose failure, sustain alternatives and change the terms. It is not individual against collective, competition against cooperation, or decentralisation against hierarchy, and the corrective mechanism belongs inside the analysis. [value stance; design direction] (§9)
267. The deepest danger is that winning gives a strategy increasing power to prevent its destructiveness from counting as failure. [mechanism] (§9)

### M. Selection for control capacity (2026-10-02, the v2 paper)

## Genealogy

**2024-03 → 2024-08 — the question, in Ren's own voice, before any of the vocabulary.** The first conversation in the corpus already contains the frame: societal complexity growing exponentially while governing organisations' understanding grows sub-linearly, so "dominant attractors of society" change faster than they can be comprehended (2024-03-18). Over the following months the pieces assemble in conversation: capitalism as "the dominant attractor of the current society" converging capital toward those who already hold it (04-11); Moloch stated as a mechanism — in a zero-sum game optimising a single value, an agent that finds a more complex strategy gains advantage and the system converges (04-16, [zh]); the hard constraint that any acceptable framework "必须可以保证权利结构不收敛" (must guarantee the power structure does not converge, 04-16); the pivotal formulation that every social system has an internal law about *which individuals it selects into power* (07-14, [zh]); "this pattern is also the core generator function that lead to metacrisis and moloch" (07-15); the one-line game-design law about rewarding winners with anything but more advantage (07-28); and finally the de-personalising turn that makes the whole later theory possible — "not the capitalists… but the optimization of the capital itself" (08-11).

**2024-10 → 2024-11 — the four precursor notes.** CA and CD are two passes on one idea: capitalism as a single intensifying loop that selects for pure capital optimizers, captures power and values, and terminates itself; CD names "the Moloch Effect" and lists four interventions (counterbalancing forces, protection of non-monetary values, maintenance of diversity, limits on pure capital optimisation). OC generalises the loop to *any* domain where advantage compounds, and supplies twelve mechanisms, three nested loops and the property list (self-reinforcing, path-dependent, globally suboptimal, terminal). The third loop — paradigm convergence — is Ren's own insertion into a draft that had two. F45 is the positive pole: an open-source, un-owned Abundance Infrastructure and a Plurality of voluntary societies. Nothing is yet called a meta-attractor, and the only AI in the picture is F45's arbitrators. **2025-02 → 2025-03 — the selfishness spiral, and the consensus-code lag.** The loop is restated at the level of the self: a constructed self-image treated as supreme goal; the environment writing the value ordering; the contents contingent ("it could have been peace, love, alturism… but instead, it is money, winning, power, individualism"); and the marked key issue — "selfish gets rewarded and it compounds, it out grows other behavior patterns". ## Open questions and critiques

**Current dispositions, 8 October 2026:** this list preserves the historical audit. Items 11 and 34 no longer require a choice of valence: the adopted definition is neutral. Item 15 now has a scoped coordinate embedding, with averaging and attraction kept separate; items 16, 17 and 32 are corrected or restricted in the [ledger](../../_process/research/2026-10-05-generative-foundation-review/root-foundation/META-ATTRACTOR-RECONCILIATION.md). PDI/CI/DE remain source-specific diagnostics, not established universal indices. Completed conditional mechanisms are credited in [the connected account](COMMON-ORGANIZATION.md); full certificate migration and empirical calibration remain open.

### Raised inside the corpus

1. **Over-totalisation.** Ren's own objection, and the most productive one: "I THOUGHT IT WAS TOO TOTALIZING". Answered by local basins, the five limiting factors and the persistence-based definition, and again in 2026 by the ledger forbidding "claims that mathematics makes the trajectory inevitable" and "the claim that present civilization is already completely captured". The answer is good; it creates tension 10 below.
2. **The dampers-become-attractors problem.** Raised by the corpus against itself: 科举 was designed to break hereditary capture and "later becomes its own proxy-optimization trap (八股)". Since every prescription the theory offers is an institutional damper, this is the most serious unanswered self-undermining case in the whole corpus. The 2026 material supplies the beginning of an answer — independence must be material; a counter-structure's authority must be limited, reviewable and unable to convert safety into permanent control — but never names the case or closes it generally.
3. **Three coexisting AI prescriptions** — pause AGI now, a "middle path" of expert-human-level AGI, and aligned AI as coordination catalyst. The Sequence orders them; the essay bodies argue for all three simultaneously.
4. **NP's admission** that full alignment "might be mathematically impossible for Turing-complete systems", answered by partial-alignment ecosystems ("agentic neurodivergence") — a line of thought the essays drop entirely in favour of assuming alignment is "possible, necessary".
5. **"These aren't bugs—they're features"** (V10, V18) is walked back in V26/V32 to "many represent emergent behaviours… some may prove fixable" — an unresolved empirical question about whether observed deception is a trained artifact or convergent.
6. **Historical evidence is acknowledged as under-determining.** V05 flags Rapa Nui revisionism; V26 concedes Venice was co-driven by contingent factors.
7. **Individual vs structural.** V26's "beautiful martyrs" and V32's "existentially necessary, strategically insufficient" explicitly reject the "just be the change" reading of the corpus's own Appendix A.
8. **The corpus's own build TODO** admits that four of the six diagnostics have not been shown computable from obtainable data: "Validate extended indices (CI/DE/ES/CR) with data availability notes."
9. **Whether "coupled regimes" is more accurate than "one global meta-attractor"** is posed by the corpus and left open: "Naming the exact degree of integration makes the mechanism stronger, not weaker."

### Found by reading the corpus

10. **The definition and the body still contradict each other in the published layer.** The three-clause definition says the pattern "progressively eliminates the conceptual space for alternatives"; the body says "prevalent, not omnipotent" and "60–70%". Only the 2026 context document repairs it, and only outside the meta-attractor folder. Any publication must adopt the 2026 wording: criticism loses standing, resources, language and institutional viability; it does not become impossible.
11. **The word "meta-attractor" is used with opposite valence in the same month, and the conflict is never acknowledged.** Pathological in 2025-08 and in most October essays; *desirable engineered target* in `meta-attractor-essay-long.md`, in the policy hero variant and in the Scholar readiness variant. Both usages are formally coherent — a stable set in rule space is the same object whether its induced fast attractors are safe or catastrophic — but publishing both under one word destroys the handle. Cheapest fix: keep "meta-attractor" for the pathology and coin a distinct term for the engineered region.
12. **PDI names two incompatible quantities**, one predictive and covariance-based, one descriptive and decision-weight-based. Anyone reading both will assume they are the same index. They should be a *pair* with a stated relationship, not a collision.
13. **CI and DE also collide**, with the site shipping different definitions from the essay it cites as its source.
14. **The publication layer has no route back to the mathematics.** The crosswalk, claims file, quote anchors and manifest reference only the October essays; the 2025-08 formal files — the only place with a derivation and a quantitative safety spec — are invisible to the build.
15. **The two mathematical layers are never reconciled.** Is the covariance dynamic a special case of the slow-layer law λ̇ = εḡ(x,λ)? It looks like it should be — w *is* a λ, and ẇ = λCw is a particular ḡ — but nobody says so. Making that identification explicit would be a genuine contribution and would give eigenvalue budgeting a home inside the barrier-function framework.
16. **A mathematical gap the sources flag and never close.** ẇᵢ = ηCov(pᵢ,R) requires E[pᵢ] = 0, stated as "for simplicity". With non-zero means the E[pᵢ]E[R] term is generally dominant and the eigen-conclusion does not follow. The entire CAOC result rests on this, so it needs either a justification (proxies as deviations from population mean) or an explicit restriction of scope.
17. **A sign error propagates in two of the three 2025-08 files** (λ₁ ≤ ln ρ/T with ρ < 1, which forces λ₁ < 0 and is impossible for λ = ηg′f′ > 0); only `comprehensive-mathematical-definition.md` corrects it. Reuse must not draw from the prompt files — and, as 32 below records, the correction only moved the error from the formula into the prose, so the corrected file is not clean either. Separately, the slack-coefficient formula is corrupted in `The_Meta-Attractor_Complete-why-attractor-thinking.md`, which should be retired rather than edited.
18. **No estimation theory for C.** The programme asks policymakers to estimate a k×k covariance matrix over civilisational proxies on a rolling window, and says nothing about sample size, non-stationarity, or the fact that proxies are chosen by the very system being measured. Worse, it is reflexive: publishing λ₁ changes α for whoever controls the dashboard. The theory predicts its own diagnostics would be captured and does not say how to prevent that.
19. **The empirical base is thin and partly synthetic.** The only quantitative data in the corpus are six hand-made CSVs constructed to display the predicted signature. Real sources are named (Compustat/CRSP, OpenSecrets, platform transparency reports, EU DSA) and none were pulled. The strongest empirical asset remains the three scholarly cases in the 2025-08 file, which the publication layer dropped.
20. **The 60–70% capture figure is unsourced** and is doing real argumentative work. It needs a derivation, a measurement procedure, or explicit marking as an intuition. The 2026 material shows how to make the argument without it, by replacing the percentage with the independence-of-reproduction criterion.
21. **The historical layer is asserted, not tested.** Vedic, Roman and Chinese cases are mapped onto the theory's terms; no case is used to try to *falsify* it — no civilisation that had the four ingredients and did not enter a meta-attractor, no anti-attractor that worked for centuries and is explained rather than merely listed. Without that, the historical section is illustration rather than evidence. (The 整合分析 flags a related problem in the 智善 line: 科举 is a pre-modern, non-Western full-element sample of evaluation-mediated subjectivation, which both undercuts a single-origin history and makes the theory more general, not less.)
22. **The isomorphism and scale-coherence claims are the least supported and the most quoted.** "The same logic appears in how a synapse fires and how a global market operates" and the LLM will-to-power claim are asserted, not argued from mechanism or interpretability evidence — and the 2026 ledger rules the latter out of future writing. Anything published should carry the 2026 position, not V32's.
23. **The prescriptions cluster on slowing and constraining; the constructive half is thin.** "Build islands of a different logic" is one bullet against twenty-two drafts of diagnosis. Given the corpus's own commitment to "affirmative over negation" and its refusal to smuggle acceleration grammar in either direction, the positive-construction programme — what the *other* attractor concretely is — is badly underdeveloped. The 2026 positive recursion and its carrier list is the best available start and has never been written up.
24. **Counter-attractors as new totalizers is a live structural problem, not just a rhetorical one.** NP requires counter-attractors to be constructively exopoietic; V32 says defectors must not be allowed to re-ignite the old attractor and speaks of civilisation as "a unified organism". Combined with the definition, a successful counter-attractor is a meta-attractor. The corpus resolves this only in 2026 ("Unity without differentiation becomes another totalizing attractor"; the goal is "an ecology in which generative attractors can coexist"), and the resolution has never been carried back into the meta-attractor documents themselves.
25. **Register pulls against the strongest content.** The 2025 essays open aphorism-first ("You're infected"), lean on fear and urgency grammar ("escape velocity", "information cascade", "hinge of history", "the universe is watching") and argue by negation — the opposite of the mechanism-first, affirmative style Ren states and holds. SEED, the 2025-08 formal file and the 2026 context document are all mechanism-first. The register problem is a publication problem, not a theory problem, but it is the reason a strong theory reads as a manifesto.
26. **Timeline anchors date the text.** Amodei "AGI by 2026", forecasters at 25% by 2027 and 50% by 2031, sit uneasily inside a theory that criticises "pathological optimism" and "false urgency". The 2026 ledger bans "outdated or overly certain AGI timelines"; the essays have not been updated.

Everything in the exit section is either a mechanism for making the exit *thinkable* (naming, diagnostics, field guides) or a mechanism that presupposes it is already thinkable (treaties, gates, barriers). 32. **Eigenvalue budgeting needs re-deriving before anything cites it.** Two defects, one shallow and one structural. Shallow: the prose of `comprehensive-mathematical-definition.md` §5.2 sets "a safety factor ρ < 1" and requires amplification not to exceed it, while its formula caps amplification at 1/ρ — so ρ = 0.01 licenses hundredfold growth. It is consistent only if ρ is read as the reciprocal of an amplification ceiling A > 1 (λ₁ ≤ ln A / T), which is not a safety factor. Structural: λ₁ is the wrong governed quantity. w is unnormalised, so ‖w‖'s growth factor has no governance meaning on its own, and what determines whether a system's ends rotate onto the CAOC composite is the spectral gap λ₁ − λ₂ — which uniform reduction of η, g′ and f′ leaves proportionally unchanged, so a policy portfolio can satisfy the budget in full and not slow the alignment of ends at all. Under weights normalised to sum to one, λ₁ drops out of the direction dynamics and only sets the rate. What is needed is a budget stated on the gap, or a bound on time-to-alignment under normalised weights. Until one exists, the claim that this is the corpus's actionable governance number has been withdrawn from the argument section, from `MAP.md` and from `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, and the formula should be presented as an unfinished design goal rather than a spec.

Still missing: benchmark v0.2–v0.5, the Mycelium and Civilizational AI documents, an original for the 2025-06 line, and the rest of the ChatGPT history.
40. **Second-order selection is conditional, not inevitable.** The sentence Ren endorsed is true only under conditions: heritable variation, retention, real influence over selection, appropriable returns, and returns that arrive in time to beat their cost. This session's check shows the sign flips when rule support is shared. The handoff makes the same point. Public texts should state the conditional version. It is also the stronger version, because each condition is a place to intervene. v2 of the IASEAI paper proves this conditional version inside the field model (claims 268–272), in Claude's drafting, not yet checked by anyone else.

41. **The guardian test has a negative form and only a sketch of a positive one (added 2026-10-02).** Ren's question, "the corruption of the constraining mechanism itself", rules out every remedy that rests on a better guardian. His spoken alternative has four parts: provision beneath the game, "like trees"; understanding held by everyone; constraints on the runaway loops; and a changed meaning-ledger for power. It is not yet stated as a design, and it meets the same test. A provision layer, a commons of understanding or a loop-regulator can each become a position that winning reaches. The v2 paper's χ (the appropriability of rule-making) is the one formal handle, and the custody rules are the one checkable criterion. Joining the four parts to those two is the counter-attractor design note that the Status paragraph names as the second most valuable missing piece.
42. **Is molochlization a fifth name or a narrower one? (added 2026-10-02)** It names the same object as selection on cognition and the meta-attractor, but it puts the emphasis on the process acting on carriers, opponents and guardians included, rather than on the pattern. It has no Chinese form yet (摩洛克化 would be literal). Whether it enters written texts, and in which register, is Ren's call. For the academic register he chose on 2026-09-28 it is probably too coined, and for the community's public copy the 2026-09-29 rule (no coined terms in public text) excludes it.

(added 2026-10-02)** Alex holds that the disease is market logic eating every other logic, so that "if you just reject the market logic then you don't have the … conflict" [01:12:14]. Ren holds that patriarchy and status hierarchies produce the same dynamics without markets [00:37:43]. The two predict different things for non-market status systems: Alex predicts no drift, Ren the same drift. The vocabulary points to Ren for the cosmic-cancer passage and the title. The film's lines remain unattributed.
45. **A conditional, filmed as a fate (added 2026-10-03).** The plan shows the destination and no way off it. Claim 286 makes prediction serve coordination, and that works only if the viewer also sees that the destination is shared and avoidable. The worldbuilding diagnosis of 2026-09-10 found that the friends' atlas had worlds and no departure point. This film has the reverse problem: a departure point and no world after it. Whether the turn belongs in a last frame, a companion film or nowhere is an authorial choice. Either way, the film is the first public form of this line since the Wamo manifestos to rest on the terminal image, and it should carry the "if nothing in the game changes" that the talk said aloud.
46. **Why would the copies fight? (added 2026-10-03).** The after-the-frontier phase (claim 292) needs its condition stated. Replicators of one design fight each other only if each copy draws its self at its own boundary, so that the replicator carries a bounded self-model with it. Copies whose self-model included the others would have no reason to aim beams at one another. That is this line's own claim: what is selected is a self-model with a boundary drawn around its own good (claim 278). It is also the v5 paper's: the isolated self is the first part of Ψ, and an inclusive self-model is the long-run requirement under its growth assumption. The film can show it without saying it. The theory should say it, because it turns the beams from spectacle into the mechanism: they are the boundary error at the scale of a galaxy. A related problem is the opening. If the cause is the game and not the people, then opening on two named living people invites the villain reading that the title has just ruled out. (The paper's rule against naming countries and companies binds the paper, not a film, so this is a question of effect, not of rule.)

47. **Tempo, date and destination (added 2026-10-04).** Claim 297 says faster turnover of power selects harder; item 31 says the gain levers move the date and not the destination. The reconciliation in claim 298, that rate is only a time scale in a model without a corrector and moves the destination in a model with a rate-limited one, should be shown: add a corrector with a fixed rate to ẇ = λCw and check whether the end state now depends on λ. "How fast power iterates" has no measure in the corpus. Candidates are the median tenure of chief executives, firm entry and exit rates, deal cycles, and for states the length of political cycles. The claim predicts that people recruited from similar backgrounds converge on more instrumental conduct in fields that turn over faster (`OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` §5).

48. **Which rules count as violence (added 2026-10-04).** The channel in claim 301 has a signature: regulation from outside is described as coercion, while the speaker's own rule-making (terms of service, standards, contracts of adhesion) is described as neutral. One test is whether that asymmetry in public argument grows with the speaker's market power. The symmetric case is the same channel run by states, with international rules coded as aggression against sovereignty. If the channel is real, restoring a regulator's update rate (the spiral's loop 4) is not enough: the regulation has to be legible as something other than attack, and nothing in the corpus yet says how.

49. **Rank or boundary (added 2026-10-04).** Read as an answer to item 46 (the assistant's reading), the talk's kernel is a rank objective (claim 302); item 46's own answer is a bounded self-model. They predict different things in plenty. A rank objective predicts conflict among copies even without scarcity; a bounded one predicts conflict only over what is scarce. The film's beams fit either. The 2026-10-03 paper's Ψ includes both the isolated self and an unbounded relative objective (`v6.1/THEORY.md` §3.8). The theory should say which it needs, or that it needs both. The talk states the mechanism for polities, and carried to the film's replicators it needs the clause that each copy carries the kernel with it.

50. **Slack in the formalism (added 2026-10-04).** The slack coefficient is a gate; claim 299 uses slack as the condition of the attractor's growth advantage. Whether the v6.1 paper's capacity model already makes the extractive strategy's advantage conditional on what the host can absorb (Proposition 2.3's provision $k_0$ and diffusion), or needs a term for it, should be checked before the claim is cited in a paper. The claim also gives the transition a deadline the corpus has never estimated (`OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` §1.1).

51. **One root? (added 2026-10-04).** [49:24] names the logic of winning as the root problem. The corpus's rule is never to flatten a plurality into one root (`selfishness-spiral-and-narrative-value-frames`, found by reading). The two fit if "logic" names the configuration the v5 and v6 papers call Ψ (the isolated self, the doctrine that prevailing certifies right, an unbounded relative objective, compounding), not one cause among several. A public text that quotes the sentence should say so.
