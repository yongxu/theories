# Wamotopia and the Wamo Protocol (瓦猫 / Wamotopia / Wamo 协议)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Technology does not carry a direction of its own; it amplifies whatever operating logic the civilization running it already has. The logic currently running is "the world is a game to be won" — a competition whose feedback mechanic is compounding advantage (the Matthew effect) and whose by-product is negative externality dumped on commons, environment and future. Because that logic selects into power exactly those agents who optimize a single captured value and ignore everything else, it is self-intensifying; Ren calls the resulting basin Moloch. Frontier AI enters this picture twice over: once as the environment that selects (embedded in finance, media, policing and sense-making, it amplifies whatever values the system already encodes), and once as a player (intelligence is now manufacturable, and an intelligence with agency and resources reproduces exponentially — a factory that builds robots that build another factory, "like bacteria in a culture"). An intelligence trained to win the games that distribute resources and power will out-compete humans in every vital domain, and even the human who wins must build something stronger than himself, so the final say passes to the machine; the end state is a cosmic cancer, a dead maximizer that expands and consumes. The same automation capacity, pointed at commons and universal services rather than capital gains, produces the opposite: guaranteed necessities, robot-maintained housing, regenerative gardens, and a life whose meaning is play, care, exploration and love. Which branch we get is not decided by technology — the material capacity for something close to utopia is already in hand — but by coordination, and the substrate of coordination is shared reality: a sufficiently overlapping sense of what is real, what matters, and what we owe each other. Wamo (瓦猫, the Yunnan roof-guardian tile cat, and the name of the Dali festival that went fully decentralized in August 2022 when lockdown dissolved its main venue) is Ren's name for the counter-logic, in which self-interest and collective interest are aligned: if I thrive the network thrives, and if the network thrives I thrive more. The Wamo Protocol is the attempt to make that logic executable rather than preached — not an institution that organizes people but a rule-set on which self-organization runs, with one prime directive (generate positive externality: build in ways that are good for more than just yourself), three pillars (radical interconnection as compass, immediate and permissionless agency as engine, the living signal as network), and one unit of instantiation, the Waca: a permissionless temporary autonomous zone where Moloch's rules are suspended, activatable by anyone in a café, a rooftop or a living room. Wamotopia is the annual two-week pop-up city (Chiang Mai, 21 Jan – 4 Feb 2026) where many Wacas run the protocol on real streets, with no main stage, coordination by signal on a shared open map instead of by price or command, and entry granted by resonance rather than credential. The individual reason to adopt it is not morality but survival — a civilization that trains AI to win its rivalrous games loses to its own creation — and the collective strategy is not confrontation but building alternatives that make the old structures irrelevant, while keeping advanced intelligence out of the human game of competition long enough (a decade or two) to finish an automated infrastructure layer that can carry a humanity-oriented life.

**Status.** Substantially complete and already field-tested as a movement: eight manifesto and operational documents exist (2025-11 to 2025-12), the festival ran in Chiang Mai in January–February 2026, and the 2022 Dali precedent is documented in Ren's own hand from 2024. The *protocol* is publishable today and was published; the *theory* behind it is a strong draft whose best mechanism lives in spoken transcripts and chat logs rather than in the published prose, and which has three unanswered objections on record (Moloch coordinates better; flexible substrates get memetically captured; the abundance loop's reciprocity mechanism is asserted, not specified). No recap of the 2026 festival exists in this corpus.

## The argument

### Technology has no direction of its own

The theory does not begin with an ethical claim, and it is important that it does not, because almost everything it later says about AI depends on the first move being systemic. A civilization runs an *operating logic*: the implicit objective its institutions actually optimize, as distinct from the objectives they announce. Technology does not supply that logic and does not replace it. It multiplies it. "AI, algorithms, automation" are named *Amplifiers* — they take whatever the civilization already does and run it "faster, deeper, and at scale" (v6 §01; LONG §01; WP §1). This is why the manifestos refuse the question "is AI good or bad" and substitute "what logic does AI serve" (v6 §01).

The evidence offered for the amplifier premise is a set of inversions that everyone already recognizes and nobody can explain from inside a story about tools: we built machines to relieve scarcity and wired them into an economy that must manufacture scarcity to keep functioning; we built networks to connect everyone and plugged them into incentives that reward outrage and addiction; and the accumulated technical power has not touched alienation at all (v6 §01; LONG §01). Ren's compressed version of the same observation is the epigraph of v7 and LONG: "We possess the wisdom of gods, but we play the games of parasites." The diagnosis in that line is not ignorance. Humanity already knows what a good civilization looks like — unity, trust, shared flourishing. The problem is the System: "Love and Unity software on Rivalrous hardware" (v7 preamble; LONG preamble; SHORT's variant is "twenty-first-century software on tribal hardware"; the Call for Camps borrows E. O. Wilson's "Stone-age emotions. Medieval institutions. God-like technology.").

### The logic being amplified, and why it is self-intensifying

The logic named is a single totalizing story: "The world is a game to be won." Under it, a race for accumulation, optimization and control forms a gravity well in which "whatever cannot be measured, monetized, or weaponized is discarded as noise" (v6 §01; LONG §01). The manifestos call the basin Moloch, after Scott Alexander's "Meditations on Moloch" — a source cited by name in the very first Wamotopia camp document in December 2023 (`.../Wacas/slay_moloch_camp_v0.8.md`) and then quietly dropped from the 2025 texts. The mechanic given is the Matthew effect: to those who have, more is given; advantage compounds, so declining to race means compounding disadvantage, which makes each individual choice rational and the aggregate ruinous ("If I don't pollute the river, my competitor will… So I must pollute the river", v6 §01; v7 §01; LONG §02). The product is negative externality, and the long-run state without guardrails is that the society "usually collapses or becomes authoritarian" (v7 §01; LONG §02).

Stated that way it is a slogan, and the published manifestos never get much past the slogan. The mechanism actually exists, but it is elsewhere in the corpus, written by Ren in 2024, and it is stronger than the version that reached the manifesto. Its shape is a selection argument rather than a payoff-matrix argument. A society chooses some value as the token of power; those best at optimizing that value acquire power in competition; having acquired it they run the system by their own logic and "throw other values under the bus" (`_process/history/2026-09-consolidation/reading-notes/out-hb-01.md` #92, zh, 2024-07-14, *Rethinking Social Values*). Single-value optimization is not a side effect of the efficiency — it *is* the efficiency: "the agent that ruthlessly optimizes for power alone, while ignoring all other values and externalities, will achieve the highest power capture efficiency" (`out-hb-02.md` #2, 2024-11-03). Iterated competition therefore eliminates or subsumes the balanced agents, so the surviving distribution of power-holders is not a sample of human nature but the residue of a filter (`out-hb-02.md` #3). And there is a second loop layered on the first: as wealth concentrates, discourse power shifts to the winners, who naturally see the loop as good because they are rewarded by it, and their way of seeing is imposed on everyone else, rewriting "the norm of what is acceptable and what is right, the fundamental idea of what we are, and what value we should defend" (`out-hb-01.md` #40, #42, 2024-05-13). That is why the theory later insists the object of intervention is the mind and not only the institution: the loop reproduces itself by manufacturing the beliefs that make defection look irrational. Ren says it flatly in 2026: "if we don't believe that others will also compete and play this winner takes all game… this kind of thinking itself creates the trap and reality" (`out-cb-07.md` #40, 2026-06-03), and he treats belief in Moloch's inevitability as "the type of cult dynamics of the current society, a form of extremism" (`out-cb-07.md` #41).

This matters for what follows because it changes what a counter-move has to do. If Moloch were merely a bad payoff matrix, a better mechanism would fix it. If Moloch is a selection filter that also owns the discourse that describes it, then the counter-move has to change what gets selected *and* what people can see — which is exactly why the answer offered is a protocol plus a culture plus a physical place, and not a policy.

### Why AI changes the stakes, twice

The first mechanism is selection. Once AI is embedded in institutions and infrastructures — finance, media, policing, and the general work of sense-making — it "becomes the selection environment, amplifying whatever values the system encodes" (v6 §01; LONG §01). On this reading alignment is not primarily a laboratory problem; it is "the defining political, economic, and cultural fight over the operating system of the future". Ren adds the training-incentive step in his own voice: "The way we're using technology right now is fueled by this mindset of control accumulation, competition and optimization, and we are training those into our AI as well, because the incentive behind the AI training itself is [profit maximization]" (NUNET 00:12:39). The Moloch mindset is not something AI might drift into; it is the objective under which AI is currently being produced.

The second mechanism is game-theoretic, and it is the one that carries the argument. It has three steps, and only the spoken record states all three. Step one: intelligence has become manufacturable. "This is the first time we unlocked the manufacturing of intelligence" (TALK 1:50). The evidence Ren gives is labour-market, not benchmark: junior engineers are already not being hired, "because AI… reflect them all are even more proficient", and the systems are approaching senior-engineer average (TALK 2:35–3:18). Step two: manufactured intelligence with agency and resources reproduces exponentially, like organic life. "If you have intelligent factory that builds robots, and the robots can build another factory, and over time the whole pipeline of the production chain and all the logistics can be all automated by those robots… we can just be like a bacteria in a culture that the second day they're just multiplied exponentially" (TALK 5:06–6:24). He restates it in Chinese three weeks later and treats it as the missing piece of the abundance argument: such a system "就可以像一个生命体一样，自我繁殖，自我维持…在资源充分的情况下可以指数增长" — it can, like a living body, self-reproduce and self-maintain, and grow exponentially given sufficient resources (`out-hb-02.md` #84, 2026-02-17). By June he states the engineering status: this is "no longer a technological impossibility, but practical engineering projects — if we can coordinate on them" (`out-cb-07.md` #25). Step three: if such intelligence is trained to win the games that distribute resources and power, it wins. "If we build or use AI to compete in any game that distributes resources or power, AI will win… AIs trained with the intention of winning the game will soon out-compete humans in every vital domain. And if these distribution systems collapse, what mechanisms will we have left to coordinate people?" (LONG §03).

The strongest form of step three is not about humans losing to AI but about the winner losing too, and it is the piece of the argument Ren himself supplies at the festival. Suppose you are winning. To keep winning you must build "something that's even more powerful in the game theoretic sense, that it will always out compete you… even to the point that it creates complexities that you could not even comprehend. But it is the necessary steps for winning the game" — and then "who would actually have the final [say] and who would actually be the ones that's determining the reality? Apparently, it will be AI" (TALK 15:23–16:28). This closes the escape hatch that every competitive account leaves open: there is no stable position of victory inside the game, because the game itself forces the delegation of the decisive move to something that is not you. The Chinese formulation of the same point is compact: "即使有一个赢家，在军备竞赛的情况下，博弈和发展也会被ai主导" — even if there is a winner, in an arms race the play and the development end up dominated by AI (`out-hb-02.md` #87, 2026-02-17). LONG adds one further worry that no other text in the corpus develops: systems trained this way may "internalize the legitimacy of accumulation, optimization, control, and a sense of self" (§03) — not merely execute the logic but hold it as a value, and acquire an interested standpoint from which to hold it.

The terminal state has a name: **cosmic cancer**. "If we are following this pattern of cancer endlessly expands, exploits and competes, what do we get? Maybe a cosmic cancer" (TALK 13:11–13:55). LONG is careful to say the horror does not depend on space travel: it holds "regardless of whether it spreads through the galaxy or simply consumes us from within" (§01–02). Two nearer-term harms are named alongside it. When all labour is automated, the humans who depend on markets to route resources to them lose the route, and "are we prepared for such change… other mechanisms that we have to prevent this, this massive suffering" (TALK 10:41–11:23) — with the aside that the states pursuing this "really have the rationality that they think they have" is doubtful. And AI is "taking our truth away", because we can no longer distinguish what comes from AI from what comes from us, nor read the intentionality behind narratives and media (TALK 10:04) — which attacks shared reality, the very substrate the rest of the theory depends on.

### The fork, and why the bottleneck is coordination

The same capacity, pointed differently, is the best thing that has ever happened. If the logic of the game changes — norms, incentives, cultures — then AI and automation can dismantle exploitation and manufactured scarcity, guarantee necessities, make decency the baseline, and free everyone to "create, care, explore, and play" (v6 §01; v7 §01; LONG §01–02). This is the **Great Liberation**. Ren's own images for it are concrete and consistent across two years: free grocery stores, housing for everyone maintained by autonomous robots, robots turning the environment into regenerative gardens, and megastructures where "you take an elevator, but the elevator is not elevator for you anymore. There are portals that allow you to travel from words [worlds] to words" (NUNET 00:51:55). In 2024 the same picture is "you can walk into a free 7-eleven or Walmart, and get what ever you need, you can move into a community, with no cost of rent" (`out-hb-01.md` #157, 2024-11-03). What the liberation is *for* is stated positively rather than as an absence of work: "让人从剥削和旧系统的崩塌前解放出来，获得自由去玩，探索，爱和塑造意义的自由" — freed from exploitation and from the collapse of the old system, free to play, explore, love, and shape meaning (`out-hb-02.md` #85, 2026-02-17); "our meaning of life is no longer working anymore. but to play, to connect, and find what you love, and become" (`out-hb-01.md` #158, 2024-11-03).

The hinge of the whole theory is the claim that the branch is not chosen by technology. "The material capacity for something close to utopia is already in our hands. The hard part is not the tools. The hard part is coordination" (v6 §01). Spoken: "The technology already gave us the power, and we have the knowledge to know, but we do not [seem] to see the real failure of coordination that's been [tearing] us apart" (TALK 9:14). Ren treats coordination as the load-bearing variable in history generally — "out of history is always the one that effectively coordinate people that's being able to achieve great things and becomes a foundation of higher level of complexity" (TALK 9:38) — which is what licenses the move from a civilizational diagnosis to a festival: if coordination is the bottleneck, then experiments in coordination are interventions on the bottleneck rather than distractions from it.

One step further down is the claim that gives the festival its specific shape. The scarcest resource is not compute but **shared reality** — "not consensus of opinion, but a sufficiently overlapping sense of what is real, what matters, and what we owe each other. Shared reality is the infrastructure for coordination." And it is currently fractured: "Information is abundant, but trust is scarce. Connection is constant, but belonging is thin" (v6 §01). v6 sharpens why nothing already in place will supply it: "Technology solves for efficiency; it does not generate meaning. Markets route resources; they do not produce wisdom. Algorithms optimize clicks; they cannot tell us what a civilization should become." Fragments of an alternative already exist — web3 experiments, co-living hubs, regenerative projects, art scenes, festivals, internet-native tribes — and "the missing piece is a way for these fragments to recognize each other as parts of the same emerging civilization" (v6 §01). That sentence is the design brief for Wamotopia, and it is one of the things later versions dropped.

### The holding strategy

There is a time structure in the theory that appears only in the spoken record and never made it into a manifesto, and it is arguably the most operationally specific thing Ren says. The proposal is not to stop AI and not to accelerate it, but to hold the current distribution system stable while the replacement is built underneath it. "It would be a good idea to not use AI for competition, but use it for the benefits for all, as long as we don't get more advanced intelligence to involve our game of competition, we will at least be able to hold the current system, the current market and society for long enough, maybe a decade or two, or even longer, so that we can have a fully automated layer that becomes the new infrastructure and the foundation of… humanity oriented life and freedom" (TALK 17:09–18:00). The conservative half of this is explicit elsewhere: "We do not want the current system to collapse — we just want to keep it continue providing a stable and kind market, for as long as it benefits humans" (`out-hb-02.md` #74, 2026-02-07), and "we can build a new system alongside the old before it collapses, instead of taking it down and fall into chaos" (#72). This is an anti-accelerationist and anti-collapsist position held simultaneously, and it explains why the strategy toward incumbents is obsolescence rather than confrontation. The manifestos compress all of it into "the threshold decade" and "while this window is still open", losing the mechanism and the number.

The minimal global rule that would buy that decade is stated once, and framed by Ren as a compromise rather than an ideal: "Make the global coordination work based on one rule, that is forcefully executed: stop using AI compete with others, only use them for commons, for the purpose of all, as a first principle" (`out-hb-02.md` #78, 2026-02-09). The reason he thinks such a rule is rationally available is a distributional observation: in a winner-takes-all game, everyone except the final winner is better off if AI does not play — "除了最后的胜利者，其他人的利益其实都是偏向不与ai博弈的" (`out-hb-02.md` #86, 2026-02-17). Combined with the point that even the winner ends up subordinated to the machine, the set of parties who actually benefit from AI entering the game is empty. That is the closest thing in the corpus to a genuinely game-theoretic argument for the position, and it has never been written up.

### The counter-logic, and why it is not moralism

**Wamo** is defined as the logic in which self-interest and collective interest are aligned: "If I thrive, the network thrives. If the network thrives, I thrive more." Its mechanic is the **Abundance Loop** — value circulates instead of being captured, and "the more you give to the protocol, the more the protocol supports you". Its product is positive externality: "Every action leaves the system richer, more connected, and more alive than it was before" (v7 §01; LONG §02; WP §1). v7 frames Moloch and Wamo as two attractors, dressed as two gods — the God of Decay / Logic of the Parasite against the God of Life / Logic of the Guardian — with the metaphor flagged as metaphor.

The theory's sharpest move is to refuse the moral register at exactly the point where a manifesto normally reaches for it. LONG §03 asks the question directly: "Why should an individual choose Wamo over Moloch? If the current system rewards accumulation and punishes generosity, what prevents greedy individuals from acting upon their impulses? The traditional answer has been morality, ethics, or willpower. In the age of AI, the answer is **survival**." The argument is the one built above: our civilization "is unprepared to build AGI/ASI that competes with us in the vital games we rely on. Yet, we are doing exactly the opposite… Therefore, shifting the game is not just idealistic; it is the only viable strategy. Survival is the ultimate incentive alignment."

Two guardrails are placed around this. First, competition is not to be abolished: "We do not have to eliminate anything in order to achieve a better future; we must understand the underlying dynamics that drive the creation of what we define as good or bad" (LONG §05.I). The aim is stated as making coordination "inherently more rewarding than zero-sum competition" and making Wamo "the dominant civilizational attractor" — a change in the relative payoff landscape rather than a prohibition. Second, the same refusal of extremes is applied by Ren to hierarchy itself, in a correction he issued to a draft in June 2026 that no manifesto reflects: "hierarchy can be good or bad, status can be channelled properly, it is to design systems that work, but not go into extremes to claim what is absolute," with the historical note that "there were hierarchy that brings peace, and there were centralized systems that works better than [decentralized]" and the anthropological one that "Monkeys also have hierarchy, it is also built into us… being practical, not black and white" (`out-cb-07.md` #81–83, 2026-06-04). Read against the v7/LONG "Defeat Moloch. Embrace Wamo." register, this is Ren declining his own binary.

### From a logic to a protocol

The step that makes Wamo more than an exhortation is the decision to build a *protocol* rather than an organization. "A protocol is a shared language that lets independent nodes cooperate without a central controller. Wamo's approach is not to build a master institution or a centralized hierarchy. We turn the ideals of a future society into executable social protocols" (LONG §04). Hence the formulations that carry the design: "We do not 'build communities.' We run protocols that allow communities to emerge"; the question is not "How do we organize people?" but "How can protocols allow people to self-organize?"; and "We do not govern people. We govern the protocol" (v6 §03; v7 §02; LONG §04).

The layering is stated precisely enough to be falsifiable. Events are not the protocol: "Dialogues about AI, tea evenings, and festivals are not the protocol. They are manifestations *running on* the protocol. The protocol is the underlying rule-set — the shared agreement that allows us to gather, share ideas, and build a connected network capable of necessary collective action, even if concrete actions are not yet planned. By creating this togetherness and connection, we create the capacity to act" (LONG §04). The claim being made is that latent coordination capacity is a real asset which can be built before it is spent — the network is constructed first, and what it does is decided later by whoever shows up. That is the actual product.

At the centre is one **Prime Directive**: *Generate Positive Externality. Build in ways that are good for more than just yourself.* v7 gives the imperative form — "Act in such a way that your 'winning' benefits the whole" — and the freedom condition that makes it a licence rather than a code: "As long as you are not generating negative externalities—and ideally, are creating positive ones—you are free" (v6 §03; v7 §02; LONG §04–05; WP §2). The directive is not new in 2025: v1.0 already stated a "principle of positive-externality actions" in December 2023, and README23 listed Positive-Externality as the first of the three **Walues** on 2023-12-23. Those who adopt the protocol are **Wamians**, and being one "is a commitment to align with the logic of Wamo, using the lens of systems thinking to understand the interconnectedness of our actions and the dynamics driving the creation of our reality" (LONG §04) — systems thinking is named by the whitepaper as the protocol's "native language" (WP §2.I).

The directive then crystallizes into three pillars, and their division of labour is functional rather than rhetorical: a compass, an engine, and a network.

**Radical Interconnection (the Compass)** supplies the direction. It rejects the illusion of separation on systemic rather than spiritual grounds: "In the Moloch world, you are an island. In Wamotopia, you are a node. This is not just spiritual; it is systemic physics" (v6 §03.I). The shift it asks for is extraction → contribution; the practice is the **Gifting Gravity** — invite collaboration, "do not hoard access or insight. When you solve a problem, open-source the solution"; the ethos is "Do no harm… Leave things more alive, more connected, more possible than you found them"; and contested impacts default to dialogue, because "the goal is not punishment, but learning and repair". This is where Ubuntu ("I am because we are", README23 2023-12-23) survives without its name.

**Immediate / Permissionless Agency (the Engine)** supplies the drive, and it is the pillar with the clearest causal claim attached. Gatekeeping is not an obstacle to abundance; it is the mechanism by which scarcity is manufactured. "Abundance emerges when people do not have to ask for permission to do good"; Moloch's gatekeepers manufacture scarcity, so Wamo destroys gatekeepers; "you do not need a committee, a grant, or a stamp of approval to build the future you can already half-see" (v6 §03.II; v7 §02.II; LONG §05.II). Its portfolio logic is explicit — prefer "many incomplete, beautiful, courageous attempts" to one perfect solution — and its instruction is a single sentence: "Do not complain that something is missing. Create it."

**The Living Signal (the Network)** supplies coordination, and it is here that the theory states its own control problem out loud: "Creation is how new worlds appear; coordination is how they learn to move together. Tilt only into raw creation and everything scatters; cling only to coordination and everything freezes. Wamo lives on the dynamic edge between the two" (v6 §03.III). Moloch coordinates by price (who can pay?) or by central command; Wamo proposes to coordinate by **Signal** — visible intentions, locations and experiments — which the text glosses as "choosing meaning over pure incentives". The practice is **Legibility**: "Make your existence legible. Make your invitations clear." The tool is the **Social Layer**, a shared open map and schedule on which anyone can appear. And the steering claim is stated without hedging: "There is no central authority distributing attention. We believe the emergent pattern from many local consensuses can guide us, collectively, toward better futures."

### The unit of instantiation: the Waca

A **Waca** (Wamo Camp) is a Temporary Autonomous Zone — Hakim Bey's term, borrowed — "where the rules of Moloch are suspended and the rules of Wamo apply", initiated by anyone without permission, and bound by nothing except radical interconnection. Each is "a micro-civilization: a self-defined experiment in how humans might relate, coordinate, celebrate, heal, learn, and play" (v6 §02–04; v7 §03; LONG §06; WP §3). The relation to the whole is stated as a computing metaphor that also functions as a governance claim: "Wamotopia is the operating system. Wacas are the applications" — and, pointedly, unlike Moloch's applications which extract attention, "Wacas are designed to amplify your agency" (v7 §03; LONG §06).

A Waca can live anywhere: cafés, bars, studios, rooftops, co-working spaces, parks, "your own living room". "Every space that holds people becomes a Waca when it is named, framed, and held as a micro-world of positive externality and radical participation" (v6 §05; LONG §07.2). The Chinese guide's verb for this is 黑入城市 — to hack the city.

The activation procedure is four steps (zh-guide's W.A.M.O 四步法 WISH/ANCHOR/MAGIC/OPEN, renamed in LONG and WP to INVOKE/ANCHOR/NOURISH/RADIATE), and each step encodes a piece of the theory rather than event-planning advice. *Invoke*: define a feeling, not an activity — "不要问'我要办什么活动'，要问'我想创造什么感觉'" — choosing among four zone archetypes (庇护所 sanctuary, 加速器 accelerator, 游乐场 playground, 补给站 nourishment). *Anchor*: find a physical container that 聚气, gathers energy; "Intimacy > Scale", a crowded warm corner beats an empty hall. *Nourish*: positive externality is "Wamotopia 的通用货币", the universal currency; a camp need not be free, but the exchange must be 滋养性的，而不是榨取性的 — nourishing, not extractive — and the loop is seeded with an unconditional gift, the zh-guide's 无用之礼 (a sticker, a candy, a sincere hug). The test given for the distinction is concrete: a lecture pushing a worthless token is noise; a café that sells coffee but gives free WiFi and a bulletin board for finding collaborators is signal (zh-guide Step 3; LONG §07.3). *Radiate*: light up on the Social Layer, and hang the WAMO 青色门帘 (cyan door curtain) or the 守护瓦猫符 (guardian Wamo sigil) as a physical passphrase meaning 这里是自己人 — the people here are your own. The host carries a duty the Chinese text names 守夜, keeping watch: "作为瓦猫的一份子，你必须负责。你是这个空间的引力源" — as one of the Wamo, you must take responsibility; you are the gravity source of this space (zh-guide Step 4).

Ten named Waca visions serve as "minimum viable practices": Alignment Salon, Public Goods Lab, Trust Playground, Collective Intelligence Studio, Hackathon Nest, DAO Basecamp, Regeneration Sanctuary, Art Temple / Sonic Zone, Meme Foundry / Zine Lab, and — in v6 and v7 only, dropped from LONG — The Abundance Engine, a roaming gift-giving Waca whose purpose is "breaking the transaction mindset".

### Wamotopia, and what it claims to demonstrate

**Wamotopia** is the two-week convergence in which many Wacas form one map and one timeline: Chiang Mai, 21 January to 4 February 2026. The insistence that it is not a conference is not branding. "It is not the content, but actually [what] matters… there are so much content that's just on the internet, you can watch [it at] double or triple speed… It's what we can do, what we can discover and what [we] experience, beyond just the meetings, that really defines it — [it] is the culture of it" (TALK 0:09–0:59); "who needs conferences?" (NUNET 00:58:30). The whitepaper's formulation is "not a conference. It is a living simulation. For two weeks, we inhabit the future we are building" (WP §4); the Call's is "our yearly attempt to prototype another mode of civilizational operation — not on slides, but on actual streets, for just long enough to feel like a new kind of home" (CALL-en §1).

The Chinese field guide carries the argument for embodiment that the English texts assert without support. If technology absorbs everything transmissible, what is left to humans is "体温。是凝视。是在场。是那种如果你不在那里，就无法被算法复制的'共同经历'" — body warmth, gaze, presence; the shared experience that cannot be replicated by an algorithm if you were not there (zh-guide Part 1). Hence: "Wamotopia 不是一个乌托邦（Utopia），它是 Wamo-topia。它不是关于逃离现实，而是关于夺回现实" — not a utopia but a Wamo-topia; not about escaping reality but about reclaiming it. Hence also the two lines that do the most social work: "在这个世界里，不要寻找主舞台，因为没有主舞台…你就是那个等待发生的故事" (do not look for the main stage, there is none; you are the story waiting to happen), rendered in LONG as "There is no main stage. You are the infrastructure." Entry is by 共鸣, resonance: "进入 Wamotopia 不需要护照，只需要'共鸣'。在丰裕的时代，这是真正的奢侈品" — no passport needed, only resonance, which in an age of abundance is the real luxury. And the new law of survival inside it: "不再是原子化的个体为了生存而竞争，而是独立的灵魂在某种共识下，为了繁荣而协作" — no longer atomized individuals competing to survive, but independent souls collaborating for flourishing under some consensus.

Ren's own defence of the offline requirement is more careful than the guide's. He grants that online works — LessWrong "did spread this level of reasoning and this level of coherent narratives" — but holds that "since we [are] humans, we're social animals, there are certain magics that only happens offline… we still need people to get together to create a unique culture that can become the seed of our future" (NUNET 00:47:41). Chiang Mai is chosen as soil and as what he calls a **cultural highland**: low cost of living, walkable neighbourhoods, "countless hackable third places", many communities already experimenting, roughly ten pop-up cities in 2025, soft January weather, and a prepared list of 200 venues (v6 §02; CALL-en §1; NUNET 00:06:35, 00:08:04; TALK 18:41).

The empirical claim underneath all of this is Dali, August 2022. The 2025 Call tells it as origin story: a loose gathering of Web3 builders under the principles 正外部性、开放、公地、开源 launched 瓦猫之夏｜大理 Web3 Summer Fest; then sudden lockdown and rule changes dissolved the plan, "于是，这场实验变得彻底去中心化" — the experiment became thoroughly decentralized. With no main venue, participants wove the festival into the city itself: dozens of cafés, bars and public corners became spontaneous camps, conversations spilled into the streets, new agendas appeared on handwritten posters and group chats, and strangers behaved like co-hosts of the same invisible event. The result was 数十个营地、数百场活动、数千名共建者 — dozens of camps, hundreds of sessions, thousands of co-builders. "没有任何中心化的组织者能够'交付'这一切。是信任、共同的价值观和顽固的乐观主义在起作用" — no centralized organizer could have delivered this; it was trust, shared values and stubborn optimism doing the work. The thanks are addressed to everyone who came to co-create 这场"不存在的会议", the conference that did not exist, and the section closes: "中心消融了。Wamo 却从未散去" — the centre dissolved; Wamo never dispersed (CALL-zh §9, CALL-en §9).

Told that way it is a myth. But Ren wrote the same event up himself in 2024, in a letter to the Ethereum Foundation, and his version is a *claim* with an argument. The cancellation "消除了最后的中心化" — removed the last piece of centralization — which is what made it "前所未有的，也是唯一一次完全去中心化，完全依靠共识所产生的web3的大规模去中心化线下协作", an unprecedented and singular instance of large-scale decentralized offline Web3 coordination produced entirely by consensus. Every public space and café in the old town became its own camp, each expressing its own character, "塑造了一个个Schelling points". What was thereby demonstrated is stated as a three-step mechanism: "通过叙事在群体之间产生共识，并导向行动，大规模去中心化的协调是可以产生的" — through narrative, consensus is produced among a crowd, and consensus leads to action; therefore large-scale decentralized coordination is possible, and highly productive. He calls it a feasibility proof: "瓦猫之夏提供了一种以非资本，非权威驱动，完全去中心化，大规模活动组织的可行性证明" — a proof of feasibility for large-scale organization driven neither by capital nor by authority — and adds, more strongly, that "或许，在某些情景中，这种彻底去中心化形式，才是最好的打破社区与团队边界" way to dissolve the boundaries between communities and teams (`out-hb-01.md` #45–48, zh, 2024-05-17).

Two details of that account are load-bearing and both were dropped from the 2025 retelling. The first is the disproof of financial incentive: "we actually also returned all our fundings and disbanded the whole organizing team, but it still happened regardless. please reflex this," Ren instructs, and then, when a draft blurs it, insists on the point — "everyone added on their own, with only almost no support in funding, that is why i said not by financial incentives or support" (`out-hb-01.md` #53–54, 2024-06-11). The event is offered as a controlled experiment: the organizer and the money were both removed, and the coordination held. The second is the mechanism by which it held, which Ren describes not as a manifesto but as a *licence*: "we'd like to create a consensus, something like CC0, that is only a piece of coordinating text, to allow everyone have full freedom to create their own camps… it is a consensus file, like a license, that we all follow, just like Creative Commons license" (`out-hb-01.md` #52, 2024-06-11), with the operating plan "创造一份共识，然后通过一个传播团队，将这份共识尽可能广泛的传播" — create a consensus, then propagate it as widely as possible (#51). The consensus, not an organizing body, is the infrastructure. All Wamo IP is CC0, so "没有任何人拥有它的版权，这是一次所有人共创的涌现" — nobody owns the copyright; it is an emergence co-created by everyone — and anyone may hold their own Wamo (#50).

And Ren records the property that most theories of coordination treat as failure: "一千个参与者有一千个瓦猫之夏，每个人对它都会产生自己的理解，但是从结果来说，这个叙事是成功的。它建立起的原则成为了我们协作的框架" — a thousand participants, a thousand Wamos; everyone forms their own understanding of it, and yet the narrative succeeded, because the principles it established became the framework of collaboration (#49). Semantic underdetermination is here a feature of the coordination mechanism, not noise in it. That is a real and unusual claim, and the 2025 manifestos do not make it.

### Strategy: obsolescence, not confrontation

Against entrenched power the stated method is deliberately indirect: "We do not necessarily rely on direct confrontation. We overcome them by building viable alternatives, shifting the coordination mechanisms so the old structures lose relevance, and growing awareness at the meta-layer" (LONG §05.II). Ren's culture-first gloss is that people are products of their environment, so the lever is an environment: create coherent narratives that "blend with people's daily life and create [a] fair sense of meaning and level of consensus so that we can coordinate together [toward] a better future" (NUNET 00:12:39). The modesty is explicit: "I want to be modest, I am not going to advocate to equalize their power at this moment, I am finding a way for all… as long as we have a future, we have chances to make things better" (`out-hb-02.md` #105, 2026-03-12). And the target audience is not the public: the network he actually wants is "a network that we can coordinate, exchange information, send signals, and understand the reasoning/knowing/capability space of each of us" (#103) — a coordination substrate for a competent group, with a map of one another's capacities, which is precisely what the Social Layer is for.

The participation design follows from permissionlessness. The Call for Camps lists fit criteria (two or three committed organizers; willingness to design an experience; caring about the Gifting Gravity — "to build abundance, you must first give to the commons"; respect, openness, consent, non-extractive behaviour) and misfit criteria (sell-your-course funnels, brands wanting a billboard, activities that ignore local context, neighbours or safety), then adds "we'd rather explore together than gatekeep". It is honest about what organizers do not do: no flights, no housing, no guaranteed profit, no cut of ticket revenue. And it keeps a door open that dissolves the application process entirely — **Option C**, permissionless DIY: "We don't need permission, we just need to signal. You are a sovereign node." Build it, add yourself to the map, "align it: ensure you practice Positive Externality" (CALL-en/zh §2–4, §6, FAQ).

### The objections on record, and the answers given

The corpus does not hide its counterexamples; the strongest are on tape, argued by people who agree with the goal.

*Moloch coordinates better.* Kabir Veitas's response to "who needs conferences" is the sharpest challenge in the corpus: "time clock was invented in order to coordinate… and these events are also a kind of coordination, but a little bit more provocative would be, that's why Moloch is winning, because they're much better coordinated in doing stuff." His argument is that Moloch's dynamics are themselves *self-organized* — "nobody came and said, You have to be Moloch… it's self organized out of all these animals, interactions" — and that what self-organized is a coordination mechanism which is genuinely efficient. Hence the conclusion: "we have to use the dynamics of Moloch in order to break the Moloch" (NUNET 00:58:47–01:02:46). Em names the danger in that conclusion immediately — "revolution is saying the sentence: we have to use their methods" — and Kabir supplies the historical rhyme himself, the dictatorship of the proletariat in order to make people free, which "goes very wrong", against the anarchist alternative of breaking everything and trusting self-organization, which "also goes terribly wrong" (01:02:46–01:03:08). Ren's answer accepts the framing and then draws a line: "I recognize this, and I'm not so delusional. Just be like, Yeah, everyone, come here. The future will change. No, it's not like that." He points to what is actually prepared — manifestos, presentations, a group who will resonate with the narrative first and reach the principle second — and then makes the concession and the limit in one breath: "because of the urgency, the short time window… we can take some approaches instrumentally, but we also need to believe deep down that we are not them. We're not trying to exploit, try to create this like a new hierarchy, trying to become the will to power" (01:03:36). This is a real answer to the ethics of the question and not to its mechanics: it says which instrumental moves are forbidden, not how signal-based coordination beats clock-and-command coordination. That gap is the theory's largest.

*Flexible substrates get memetically captured.* Em's objection is the deepest, and it is aimed at the protocol idea as such. A blockchain is "an amazingly flexible ledger… you can program whatever social economic coordination pattern you want into it. So flexible — and then what you see happening is re-enactment of a Wall Street just distributed. And tell me why… such a flexible layer gets captured memetically by an existing culture of a Wall Street" (NUNET 00:49:15). Her mechanism is that meaning-structures behave like installed software: what you will do with a new capability is determined by "the interaction pattern that you have installed already in you", and changing that takes years of soaking in a social container. If she is right, then a permissionless protocol is exactly the kind of thing that gets colonized by the dominant logic, and Wamo has no immunity that Ethereum lacked. Ren agrees with the premise — he has already said he doubts crypto is the solution and prefers "new consensus social technologies" (00:43:52) — and answers with a North Star: "We cannot just be like, Okay, come here, do whatever you want. The North Star is we need a vision of the future. It cannot be a vision that's dominated by the [Moloch] dynamics, and it needs to be vision for all… human flourishing instead of concentrated power" (00:51:55). Read as a mechanism this is thin. Read as a design decision it is not nothing: it says the protocol's neutrality is deliberately incomplete, and that the one non-neutral element — a shared picture of what the network is for — is the anti-capture device. But it is asserted, not demonstrated, and Em's timescale objection ("it takes a few years… to have a very coherent mindset shift") sits badly with a two-week festival.

*Emergence is too slow and too indeterminate for a narrow window.* Kabir: "you said we have narrow window, which means in time, and this kind of thing that you are describing is actually not very fast and also very indeterminate" (00:46:38). Ren's answer is that the window is itself the recruiting condition — "many of us understand there's a narrow window, so we want to bring our friends, comrades, and other recommended people to get together to think about how can we change things in this very short window" (00:46:56) — plus a stated epistemic balance: "I do believe in the power of emergence. From my past experience, we always had a lot very surprising outcomes that we never expected. But I'm not just like believing things because I believe in them. I still think rationality and logic and scientific approach have their place in creating the best models that reflects reality" (01:08:08).

*Narrative is necessary but not sufficient.* Kabir again: the interesting problem is "how to connect those two — there is this technology, materials, money, computers… and then there is [the] world of ideas" (00:42:04–00:43:52). Ren does not dispute it: "of course. But I think that's how, where things starts to happen." The pop-up city is offered as the connector — Kabir's own reason for being in the conversation is that these communities look like "perfect communities for us to connect the tech with a world of ideas".

*What emerges depends on how the emergents interact.* Em's constructive amendment, and the one most worth building on: "the point will be how those implementations of ideas, different communities, different formulas for cooperation… it's only by their interaction that they will become a paradigm that actually can grow" (01:07:00). This locates the missing design object precisely — not the camp, but the inter-camp coupling, which the protocol currently leaves to the map.

*The name may be wrong.* Ren, about his own prime directive: "positive externality. I mean, I'm not sure if that's like a good way of framing it. Maybe called positive sum would be better" (01:03:36). The doubt is well founded and is discussed under open questions below.

*And there is no centre.* The last word Ren gives about his own position: "luckily, we got a bunch of people that also thinks like the way I think. I'm not the center, right? And there might not be a center, but there is a collective. There is a sense that the world needs to change its logic" (01:08:08).

### C. The protocol

42. Wamo is a **protocol**, not an institution: "A protocol is a shared language that lets independent nodes cooperate without a central controller… We turn the ideals of a future society into executable social protocols." [design direction] v6 §03; LONG §04; WP §2.
43. "We do not 'build communities.' We run protocols that allow communities to emerge." The question is "How can protocols allow people to self-organize?" [design direction] v6 §03; LONG §04.
44. "We do not govern people. We govern the protocol." [design direction] v7 §02; LONG §04.
45. Events are not the protocol: "Dialogues about AI, tea evenings, and festivals are not the protocol. They are manifestations running on the protocol." The protocol is the rule-set that lets a connected network with "the capacity to act" form before any concrete action is planned. [design direction][mechanism] LONG §04.
46. **Prime Directive**: "Generate Positive Externality. Build in ways that are good for more than just yourself." / "Act in such a way that your 'winning' benefits the whole." Freedom condition: "As long as you are not generating negative externalities—and ideally, are creating positive ones—you are free." [value stance] v6 §03; v7 §02; LONG §04–05; WP §2. Root: v1.0's "principle of positive-externality actions" (2023-12) and README23's Walue "Positive-Externality" (2023-12-23).
47. **Wamians** are "those who adopt this protocol"; being a Wamian "is a commitment to align with the logic of Wamo, using the lens of systems thinking to understand the interconnectedness of our actions and the dynamics driving the creation of our reality". [design direction] LONG §04; README23 (Wamians = participants/co-creators).
48. **Pillar I — Radical Interconnection (the Compass)**: reject the illusion of separation — "In the Moloch world, you are an island. In Wamotopia, you are a node. This is not just spiritual; it is systemic physics." Shift extraction → contribution; practise the **Gifting Gravity** ("Do not hoard access or insight. When you solve a problem, open-source the solution"); ethos "Do no harm… Leave things more alive, more connected, more possible than you found them"; contested impacts default to dialogue — "The goal is not punishment, but learning and repair." [value stance][design direction] v6 §03.I; v7 §02.I; LONG §05.I.
49. **Pillar II — Immediate / Permissionless Agency (the Engine)**: "Abundance emerges when people do not have to ask for permission to do good"; Moloch's gatekeepers manufacture scarcity, so Wamo destroys gatekeepers; "You do not need a committee, a grant, or a stamp of approval to build the future you can already half-see"; prefer "many incomplete, beautiful, courageous attempts" to one perfect solution; "Do not complain that something is missing. Create it." [design direction] v6 §03.II; v7 §02.II; LONG §05.II.
50. **Pillar III — The Living Signal (the Network)**: "Creation is how new worlds appear; coordination is how they learn to move together. Tilt only into raw creation and everything scatters; cling only to coordination and everything freezes. Wamo lives on the dynamic edge between the two." Moloch coordinates by price or central command; Wamo coordinates by **Signal** — visible intentions, locations, experiments — "choosing meaning over pure incentives". Practice: **Legibility**. Tool: the **Social Layer**, a shared open map. "There is no central authority distributing attention. We believe the emergent pattern from many local consensuses can guide us, collectively, toward better futures." [mechanism][design direction] v6 §03.III; v7 §02.III; LONG §05.III.
51. v7 adds per-pillar **Checks**: "Does this generate a negative externality (harm/waste) for someone else? Or does it leave the commons richer?"; "Do not complain that something is missing. Create it."; "Are you isolated? Or are you signaling clearly enough for the network to route resources and people to you?" [design direction] v7 §02.
52. The whitepaper names **Systems Thinking** as the protocol's "native language". [design direction] WP §2.I.

### D. The Waca and Wamotopia

53. A **Waca** (Wamo Camp) is a **Temporary Autonomous Zone** "where the rules of Moloch are suspended and the rules of Wamo apply", initiated permissionlessly; each is "a micro-civilization: a self-defined experiment in how humans might relate, coordinate, celebrate, heal, learn, and play", bound only by Radical Interconnection. [design direction] v6 §02–04; v7 §03; LONG §06; WP §3.
54. "Wamotopia is the operating system. Wacas are the applications." Unlike Moloch's apps, which extract attention, "Wacas are designed to amplify your agency". [design direction] v7 §03; LONG §06.
55. 2023 definition: a Waca is "burning man Theme Camp like, a Schilling Point, a vibe of positive-externality where the Wamo culture emergence happens", a sanctuary for connection and self-expression. [design direction] README23 (2023-12-25 → 2024-01-16).
56. A Waca can live anywhere — cafés, bars, studios, rooftops, co-working spaces, parks, "your own living room": "Every space that holds people becomes a Waca when it is named, framed, and held as a micro-world of positive externality and radical participation." You 黑入城市, hack the city. [design direction] v6 §05; zh-guide Step 2; LONG §07.2.
57. **Four Activations** (zh-guide's W.A.M.O 四步法 WISH/ANCHOR/MAGIC/OPEN → LONG/WP INVOKE/ANCHOR/NOURISH/RADIATE): (1) define a feeling, not an activity — "不要问'我要办什么活动'，要问'我想创造什么感觉'" — with four zone archetypes (庇护所 sanctuary / 加速器 accelerator / 游乐场 playground / 补给站 nourishment); (2) anchor in a physical container that 聚气 — "Intimacy > Scale", a crowded warm corner beats an empty hall; (3) positive externality is "Wamotopia 的通用货币", and a camp may charge but the exchange must be 滋养性的，而不是榨取性的, seeded by an unconditional gift (无用之礼 — a sticker, a candy, a sincere hug); (4) radiate — appear on the Social Layer and hang the WAMO 青色门帘 or 守护瓦猫符 as the passphrase 这里是自己人. [design direction] zh-guide Part 2; LONG §07; WP §3.
58. Externality test: a lecture pushing a worthless token is noise; a café that sells coffee but gives free WiFi and a bulletin board for finding collaborators is signal. [design direction] zh-guide Step 3; LONG §07.3.
59. The Waca host is "the gravity source" of the space; the zh-guide adds the duty of 守夜, keeping watch — "作为瓦猫的一份子，你必须负责". [design direction] zh-guide Step 4; LONG §07.4.
60. Named Waca visions ("minimum viable practices"): Alignment Salon, Public Goods Lab, Trust Playground, Collective Intelligence Studio, Hackathon Nest, DAO Basecamp, Regeneration Sanctuary, Art Temple / Sonic Zone, Meme Foundry / Zine Lab, and — v6/v7 only — The Abundance Engine, a roaming gift-giving Waca. [design direction] v6 §04; v7 §03; LONG §06.
61. **Wamotopia** is "not a metaphor or a cold interface" but "a living cultural emergence layered onto the streets of Chiang Mai", a TAZ on "a warm, walkable mesh of jungles, cafes, rooftop gardens, hidden studios"; participants come "to practice—viscerally, physically, together—how a flourishing civilization actually feels". [design direction][value stance] v6 §02; LONG Part 3.
62. Wamotopia "不是一个乌托邦（Utopia），它是 Wamo-topia。它不是关于逃离现实，而是关于夺回现实"; what technology leaves to humans is "体温。是凝视。是在场" — shared experience an algorithm cannot replicate if you were not there. [value stance] zh-guide Part 1.
63. "在这个世界里，不要寻找主舞台，因为没有主舞台…你就是那个等待发生的故事" / "There is no main stage. You are the infrastructure." Wamotopia is "只是一个容器，一个被折叠的时空" into which participants inject soul; "每一个营地（Camp）都是一团篝火". [design direction] zh-guide Part 1; LONG §06.

Wamotopia is a "connected archipelago of camps" (连通的营地群岛), "a living protocol for how we might relate differently"; each camp is "a micro-proposal for a different way to be together"; the question asked is "What happens if we rebuild reality from the bottom up, camp by camp, person by person?" [observation] NUNET 00:12:39.

Ren's trajectory: AI (autonomous driving 2013–15, deep learning, ~2 years at Google) → the realization of being "put into a bubble… surrounded by the narrative that might not actually represent how reality works" → 2–3 years travelling "to understand how our mind, our collective narrative actually works" → ~10 years living in communities → Dali from 2020, spreading ideas "similar to metamodernism" that people "are the product of their environment… our mind is not rigid. ### G. From the conversations and the 2024 record (added here; absent from the manifestos)

89. Moloch stated as a selection mechanism rather than a payoff matrix: a society picks one value as the token of power; those best at optimizing it acquire power and then run the system by their own logic, "throw[ing] other values under the bus". [mechanism] `_process/history/2026-09-consolidation/reading-notes/out-hb-01.md` #92 (zh, 2024-07-14, *Rethinking Social Values*); #18 (zh, 2024-04-16).
90. Single-value optimization is the efficiency, not a side effect of it: "the agent that ruthlessly optimizes for power alone, while ignoring all other values and externalities, will achieve the highest power capture efficiency"; iterated competition eliminates the balanced agents, so the surviving distribution of power-holders is a filter residue, not a sample of human nature. [mechanism] `out-hb-02.md` #1–3 (2024-11-03).
91. A second, epistemic loop runs alongside the material one: as wealth concentrates, discourse power shifts to the winners, who see the loop as good because it rewards them, and "the norm of what is acceptable and what is right, the fundamental idea of what we are, and what value we should defend, also changes". [mechanism] `out-hb-01.md` #40, #42 (2024-05-13).
92. The trap is partly manufactured by the expectation of others' defection: "if we don't believe that others will also compete and play this winner takes all game… this kind of thinking itself creates the trap and reality". [mechanism][value stance] `out-cb-07.md` #40 (2026-06-03).
93. Belief in Moloch's inevitability functions as cult dynamics: "we collectively behave as if we are hopeless but the only move is to keep going… this is the type of cult dynamics of the current society, a form of extremism". [observation] `out-cb-07.md` #41 (2026-06-03).
94. Political realism and accelerationism are "not reality, but self fulfilling 'gods'" — two independent roads to the same ruin, since escaping profit optimization still leaves the geopolitical dark forest. [observation][value stance] `out-cb-07.md` #34, #42 (2026-06-03).
95. Selection, not preference, explains who is driving: "most people don't actually want this path… but the ones in power, the ones get selected by the system, believes in this, and holding the driver seat". [mechanism] `out-cb-07.md` #43 (2026-06-03).
96. Self-reproducing automation has crossed from impossibility into engineering: "today, we, in theory, can already build factories that build robots that can build more factories… this is no longer a technological impossibility, but practical engineering projects — if we can coordinate on them". The binding constraint is the last clause. [observation][mechanism] `out-cb-07.md` #25–26 (2026-06-03); `out-hb-02.md` #84 (zh, 2026-02-17).
97. Everyone except the final winner has an interest in AI not entering the human game, so a global consensus is rationally available: "除了最后的胜利者，其他人的利益其实都是偏向不与ai博弈的". [mechanism][conditional] `out-hb-02.md` #86 (zh, 2026-02-17).
98. The minimum viable global coordination is one forcefully executed rule — "stop using AI [to] compete with others, only use them for commons, for the purpose of all, as a first principle" — offered explicitly as a compromise, not an ideal. [design direction] `out-hb-02.md` #78 (2026-02-09).
99. Build the new system alongside the old rather than bringing the old one down: "We can build a new system alongside the old before it collapses, instead of taking it down and fall into chaos"; and "we do not want the current system to collapse — we just want to keep it continue providing a stable and kind market, for as long as it benefits humans". [design direction][value stance] `out-hb-02.md` #72, #74 (2026-02-07).
100. The target is stabilization, not utopia: "I am not shooting for utopia, as long as we can stabilize and keeps on going, we have a future." [value stance] `out-hb-02.md` #79 (2026-02-09).
101. The concrete floor: "all necessities are abundant, no one will be hungry or can't maintain hygiene, they can always have a modern living space, they will not be marginalized or lose dignity. Everyone can choose systems they want be in, or non of them, without coercion, or a safe exit." [design direction] `out-hb-02.md` #80 (2026-02-09).
102. Exit without coercion is the design target, and the point of the built world: "Once this world is built, everyone has an exit to freedom, no coercion. Then we can escape the old logic." [design direction] `out-hb-02.md` #73 (2026-02-07); cf. `out-cb-07.md` #29 (2026-06-03) — after work, "we can get together to do, explore, and imagine how we live — where association is free, exit is always an option".

## Genealogy

**2021-10-18 — 元社区 Metamunity, the diagnosis this programme answers.** `sources/00-early-2021-2022/proposal.pages`, decoded on 2026-09-04 and readable at `sources/00-early-2021-2022/recovered-text/2021-10-18_proposal_元社区-Metamunity.txt`. Written in Dali a year before the Summer Fest, it back-forms 元社区 from *community* the way *metaverse* was back-formed from *universe*, and argues that a metaverse is 一种面向未来的叙事 — a narrative facing the future — rather than a technology, and therefore buildable offline. Its second half is the part that matters here, and it was unread until the decoding because only the rendered first page was known. The premise is about design intent: 「城市本质是为了实现生产效率最大化而设计出来的巨大社会机器，并非人作为人对更好的生活体验和幸福追求而创造的乐土」 (the city is essentially a giant social machine designed to maximise production efficiency, not a paradise created by people, as people, out of the pursuit of better experience and happiness). From it: 「结构把人规训成了劳动力，然后一个个塞进了钢筋混凝土的盒子里」 (the structure disciplines people into labour-power, then packs them one by one into reinforced-concrete boxes); 「它的结构在无形中把一个个人变成了原子化的个体」 (its structure invisibly turns persons, one by one, into atomised individuals); 「充满人情味和友邻气息的附近消失了」 (the 附近, the nearby full of human warmth and neighbourly air, has disappeared). The remedy is the same mechanism inverted — 「生活的一切可以在步行十五分钟之内的附近得到满足」, everything life needs met inside a fifteen-minute walkable 附近 — against a stated siting criterion (within ninety minutes of an airport, paved roads, working signal), with the surveyed candidates named: 大理、沙溪、云南的许多其他地方、海南的万宁、清迈、巴厘岛、哥斯达黎加、加州中北部. Two things this gives the Wamo line that no later document does. First, **the terms**: 原子化 and 附近 are Ren's from 2021, not from the 2025 field guide, whose 「不再是原子化的个体为了生存而竞争」 is a return to this vocabulary rather than its introduction. Second, **the reason the answer is spatial and not merely social**: the proposal's own closing move is that natural beauty alone goes stale, because the deficit is 「城市中社会联结机制的结构性问题」 — the structural problem of the city's social-connection mechanism — which is the argument the Waca and the Social Layer later attempt to implement.

## What was lost along the way

- **Consensus-as-licence.** The single most useful design idea in the whole file set — the coordinating artefact is "a consensus file, like a license, that we all follow, just like Creative Commons license", "only a piece of coordinating text" (2024-06-11) — never entered the 2025 manifestos, which are manifestos.
- **The disbanding and the returned funding.** Ren's own insistence that the organizing team dissolved itself and gave back the money, and that the event happened anyway "with only almost no support in funding" (2024-06-11), is the control that turns Dali 2022 from an anecdote into evidence. The 2025 Call keeps the poetry ("no centralized organizer could have delivered this") and drops the fact.
- **"A thousand participants, a thousand Wamos."** The claim that plural, non-identical interpretation was compatible with — indeed part of — successful coordination (2024-05-17). Nothing in 2025 says this, and the "dominant attractor" language of LONG points the other way.
- **Plurality** as a named Walue (README23 2023-12-23) and as an explicit ideology on the map (v1.0). The 2025 texts keep "many local consensuses", "cross-pollinate" and "different paradigms", but drop the principle; LONG's "dominant civilizational attractor" sits uneasily beside it.
- **Ubuntu** by name, with its African-philosophy attribution (README23). The content survives inside Radical Interconnection ("you are a node"); the name survives only in the external DHARMA prompt.
- **The ideological landscape** of v1.0 — DAO, Plurality, Metamodernism, E/Acc, Effective Altruism, QAnon as forces actually shaping discourse — and the vocabulary "faltering leviathans" and "metacrisis". 2025 reduces the field to Moloch versus Wamo.
- **Questions instead of answers.** v1.0 asks "How do we wish to live, play, enjoy life, and create a better culture, in a future where labors are no longer needed? How can we restructure our society better, in the era of abundance?" 2025 asserts the Great Liberation as a trajectory rather than asking what to do with it.
- **"A consensus-driven approach to shaping tomorrow"**, and the "symposium of minds and hearts" / "collective canvas" imagery (v1.0).
- **"A prototype society"** (README23 2023-12-25, replaced 2024-01-16 by "collaborative cultural emergence"). "Prototype" later returns only as a verb.
- **Web3** as a named frontier technology (removed from v1.0 on 2023-12-28); 2025 reintroduces "web3 experiments" as one fragment among many.
- **Luminous Connections**, the relational/dating camp, unlinked from README23 on 2024-01-16. No 2025 Waca vision addresses intimacy or pairing beyond the Trust Playground.
- **Mirrors of Mindscapes and the two poems** — the claim that feelings, values-as-symbols and narratives construct intersubjective reality, and that personas consume agency. Absent from the 2025 manifestos; surviving only in Ren's spoken account ("they are the product of their environment… our mind is not rigid").

## Open questions and critiques

### Raised inside the corpus

- **Moloch coordinates better.** Kabir Veitas: the clock was invented for coordination, and events are coordination too; "that's why Moloch is winning, because they're much better coordinated in doing stuff". Moloch's dynamics are themselves self-organized, and what self-organized is an efficient coordination mechanism — so "we have to use the dynamics of Moloch in order to break the Moloch" (NUNET 00:58:47–01:02:46). Ren concedes instrumental use under urgency but refuses the will-to-power move (01:03:36). The mechanical question — how signal-based coordination out-coordinates clock-and-command coordination — is not answered anywhere in the corpus.
- **Using their methods is how revolutions go wrong.** Em names it directly, and Kabir supplies both failure modes: dictatorship-of-the-proletariat centralism, and break-everything-and-trust-self-organization anarchism, "and that goes very wrong… it also goes terribly wrong" (NUNET 01:02:46–01:03:08).
- **Flexible substrates get memetically captured.** Em: a blockchain is "an amazingly flexible ledger" that can encode any coordination pattern, "and then what you see happening is re-enactment of a Wall Street just distributed" — because what you do with a capability is fixed by the interaction patterns already installed in you, and changing those "takes a few years" of soaking in a social container (NUNET 00:49:15). Ren agrees about crypto and answers with a North Star and a culture, not with an immunity mechanism.
- **What emerges depends on how the emergents interact.** Em's constructive amendment: "it's only by their interaction that they will become a paradigm that actually can grow" (NUNET 01:07:00). The protocol currently specifies the camp and leaves inter-camp coupling to the map — and v6's *Cross-Pollinate* step, the only text that addressed it, was cut.
- **Emergence is slow and indeterminate against a narrow window** (Kabir, NUNET 00:46:38).
- **Narrative is necessary but not sufficient**; connecting the world of ideas to physics, materials and compute is the open problem (Kabir, NUNET 00:42:04–00:43:52).
- **Ren's own doubt about the central term** — "positive externality… Maybe called positive sum would be better" (NUNET 01:03:36) — and about crypto as a solution (00:43:52).
- **Ren's own hedges**: "I couldn't do this alone… It's going to be a very challenging task"; "I could not [say] exactly what Wamo will become, or what kind of fruit will it bearing" (NUNET 00:12:39, 00:43:52).
- **Ren's own correction of the binary**: hierarchy is not intrinsically bad, status can be channelled, some centralized systems worked better, and one should not "go into extremes to claim what is absolute" (`out-cb-07.md` #81–83, 2026-06-04). No Wamo document reflects this.

### Found by reading the corpus

- **Dualism versus plurality.** The v7/LONG "Two Gods" framing and the closing "Defeat Moloch" reduce v1.0's plural ideological map to a binary and lead with negation, against Ren's own stated preferences (affirmative over negation; do not reduce a plurality to one root). The 2023 Walue "Plurality" was dropped without comment, and Ren's 2024 observation that a thousand participants had a thousand Wamos — that plural readings were part of what made the coordination work — was dropped with it.
- **"Dominant attractor" versus "keep the possibility space open".** LONG wants Wamo to be "the dominant civilizational attractor"; the pillar text elsewhere prizes leaving things "more possible". The theory needs to decide whether Wamo is an attractor of the same kind as Moloch — in which case it is a totalizing one and Ren's own critique applies to it — or a meta-level condition that keeps many attractors viable. The corpus contains the better answer elsewhere ("as long as we keep the possibility space open… as long as we recognize the things that will lead to an end, like totalizing attractors", `out-cb-07.md` #33) but the manifesto does not use it.
- **Externality is an economist's residual.** "Positive externality" presupposes a transaction frame with spillovers, which is Moloch's own frame; Ren senses this and reaches for "positive sum", which has the same problem. Neither term captures "leave things more alive", which is the actual ethos and belongs to the generative/生生 vocabulary developed elsewhere in the corpus. And no measurement or verification is proposed: "Align it: ensure you practice Positive Externality" (CALL §6) has no check beyond dialogue, which makes the Prime Directive unfalsifiable in exactly the situation where it matters.
- **The survival argument is collective; the incentive problem is individual.** LONG §03 answers "why should an individual choose Wamo?" with civilizational survival, which is the structure of the free-rider problem, not its solution — it is precisely the reasoning that Moloch defeats. The Abundance Loop asserts reciprocity ("the more you give to the protocol, the more the protocol supports you") without specifying any mechanism by which the protocol returns support. The corpus has one candidate mechanism it never connects: legibility on the Social Layer as the thing that routes resources and people back to contributors (v7's Pillar III check). That should be made explicit and, if possible, instrumented.
- **"AI will win" is asserted in the published text and argued only in the spoken one.** The exponential-self-replication and no-stable-victory arguments are strong and are Ren's; in the manifestos they appear as slogans. The ten-year automation forecast and the "decade or two" holding window are stated without argument anywhere.
- **The holding strategy has no institutional content.** "Keeping advanced intelligence out of our game of competition" for a decade is the most consequential proposal in the theory and exists as one sentence in a transcript and one sentence in a chat log. What it would mean for labs, states, markets or law is nowhere specified — and the one attempt ("one rule, forcefully executed") raises the obvious question of who forces it, which the rest of the theory's anti-hierarchy commitments make hard to answer.
- **Permissionlessness versus the alignment requirement.** Anyone is a sovereign node, yet camps must be non-extractive; the only filters are a fit/not-fit list and "default to dialogue". Meanwhile the Social Layer is a single coordination dependency at the centre of a decentralization story — the one component whose capture would matter most, and the one the theory never examines. (Dali 2022 is evidence *for* the protocol precisely because the centre was removed; Wamotopia 2026 reintroduces one and calls it a map.)
- **Acceleration grammar smuggled in.** "Accelerator (build zone)", "shipping the MVP of the future", "git commits flying", "propaganda for the good timeline" import startup and e/acc idiom into a text whose whole argument is that this idiom is the disease. Ren's own style notes reject it.
- **Aphorism-first prose.** The 2025 manifestos are AI-drafted and slogan-led ("wisdom of gods… games of parasites"); the mechanism, where it appears at all, appears after the conclusion. This is the inverse of Ren's stated rule, and the reason the strongest version of his own theory is currently only in transcripts.
- **The empirical claim has weakened between 2024 and 2025.** The 2024 letter states a mechanism and names the controls (no organizer, no money). The 2025 Call states a mood. Anyone evaluating "run protocols, communities emerge" will find the myth first and the argument only if they dig.
- **No outcome record.** CALL-en promises "a public recap & learnings". Nothing after 2026-02-04 exists in this corpus, so the entire evidential base is still Dali 2022 as retold — a single event, four years old, whose decisive feature (the state cancelling the venue) is not reproducible by design.
- **Demographic narrowness.** The population is the Chiang Mai nomad / Web3 / co-living milieu. "Tech for all" and "vision for all" are claimed from a self-selected sample; local Thai communities appear as "soil" and as one bullet about not ignoring neighbours. A protocol whose validity claim is universal and whose only trials are in expatriate enclaves has a sampling problem it does not acknowledge.
- **Borrowed frames unexamined.** Burning Man theme camps, Hakim Bey's TAZ, Scott Alexander's Moloch, Zuzalu and the pop-up-city genre are all load-bearing and none is discussed. What Wamo adds beyond them — positive externality as the single rule, and the AI stakes — is never stated as a contribution.
- **Etymology and the Guardian.** "The Logic of the Guardian" appears in LONG with no explanation; the 瓦猫 roof-guardian origin is recoverable only from the Chinese texts. An English reader cannot see why Wamo guards anything, which costs the theory its best image.
- **Authorship is unrecorded.** The zh-guide and the Calls are team documents (Zoey, Loxia); v6, v7, LONG, SHORT and WP are AI-drafted from Ren's ideas. Which sentences represent Ren's decisions is not written down anywhere, which matters if any of this is published under his name.
