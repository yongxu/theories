# Intelligence Must Be Good (智善 / Zhishan)

**The theory in one paragraph.** Competition, once nearly all access to life runs through it, stops merely distributing rewards and begins selecting which behaviours, which people and which organizations survive — and then hands the winners the power to write the next round's rules, standards, interfaces and the meaning of "advanced," "realistic" and "rational." The adaptation is mistaken for human nature, the metric becomes a mould that forms the people measured by it, and every domain is pulled into one grammar of winning. Ren calls this a **totalizing attractor** (总体化吸引子): not a conspiracy but a terrain, whose distinguishing property is second-order — it does not only pull outcomes, it pulls the selection rules, so the selected begin to select the selectors. Its precise measure is not concentration or scale but the progressive, non-compensable loss of independent capacity to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and produce successors. Artificial intelligence does not create this dynamic; it inherits it through a whole selection ecology — archive, model, runtime, deployment, changed world, next archive — and in the present configuration first changes not the system's nature but its clock speed, while three properties make the acceleration different in kind: advantage in intelligence self-catalyses, intelligence is entrusted with judgment and can persist, and the race itself selects what kind of mind gets built. So the decisive near-term danger is not a machine that hates us but a machine that is perfectly loyal to one part of humanity against all the others — polite at the interface, extractive in structure, and executing a misaligned principal with high fidelity; alignment is a two-body problem and the unanswered half is *aligned to what*. From this follows one first principle for AI — **do not fight for us**: "we can keep fighting, but we should not let our AI fight" — and one positive definition of the good that is a direction rather than a content, because any enumerable list of values will be gamed by strong optimization: whatever continues generation (生生) is good; whatever exhausts, severs or forecloses is not. Operationally the good states four necessary conditions — generative, relational, corrigible, non-foreclosing (生之，通之，纠之，不闭之) — and evil has a precise structural name: seeing that life must pass through some place, and then owning that gate (使生命必须过门，然后拥有那扇门), with gates in three layers, material, rule and mind, and the mind-casting pipeline (铸心之门) upstream of them all. The corresponding position for AI is not "regulated tool" but **planetary technology** (行星技术): capability that runs *beneath* human games rather than inside them, like smallpox eradication, Antarctic science or weather observation — a category humanity has already built at the deepest moments of hostility, which proves the category feasible even between enemies. Because unilateral exit from a bad game is punished, exit must be engineered rather than exhorted — commitment registries, common thresholds, symmetric verification that does not itself become surveillance, shared gains, pause protocols, capacity for latecomers, and an abundance floor so that refusal does not mean destitution, freedom being permission + capability + exit power. And because any framework that begins to explain everything becomes what it diagnoses, the blade cuts inward first: 善不姓国 (the good bears no nation's surname), symmetric application, public corrigibility, non-combatancy, and a dissolution clause. The whole thing resolves to the one sentence Ren set as the project's only task: **保持可能性空间打开** — keep the possibility space open. Keep the conditions of becoming alive. Do not close the world.

**Status.** Substantially worked and unfinished as a public act. Three mature bodies exist — the Chinese whitepaper line (v0.2 → v0.3 → v0.4 with a nine-reviewer audit, a second professional review and an unexecuted v0.5 plan) plus the 2026-07-08 manifesto; the research line (总体化吸引子 v0.1–v0.9, 《无身之我》, and their integration); and the English writing program (causal chain v1 → v8 with a dated evidence packet) — alongside 《生生不息论》 v1 in six parts, a fully gated but undrafted v2 plan, a spoken record in Ren's own voice, four English essays and a five-member poem family. Individual artifacts are publishable today (the recovered record, *Why the System Keeps Reproducing Itself*, *The First Principle for Artificial Intelligence*, *Freedom You Cannot Use Is Not Yet Freedom*, the manifesto, 《无身之我》, 总体化吸引子 v0.9, the baseline English draft). The theory as a whole is **near-publishable**: what blocks it is not argument but four missing artifacts — a foundational paper defending why these are the right criteria, a publishing subject with a public address and dissent intake (which the framework's own 公开可纠 clause makes a *precondition* of publishing), one symmetric named case study that actually swings the blade, and a short transmissible pocket version. No final single-hand text exists in any language.

## The argument

### Start with locality, not with a villain

Nothing complex persists by containing all of its own conditions. A cell, a person, a firm, a state and a model all continue through relations they do not contain and cannot fully see. Locality is therefore not a defect awaiting enough intelligence to abolish it: it is the price of acting at all, and it does real work — it protects continuity, permits specialization, economizes information, and lets failure stay local so that variation can run in parallel. A body of undifferentiated cells is not healthier, it is impossible; a society whose persons have no protected boundary is not integrated, it is dominated; a digital system with universal permissions is not context-aware, it is dangerous. The argument does not object to parts having boundaries. It objects to one specific divergence between four boundaries that rarely coincide: the **operational** boundary where a process acts, the **dependency** boundary of what sustains it, the **consequence** boundary where its effects travel, and the **feedback** boundary — the much smaller set of effects that return with enough force to change what it does next.

The pathology is a *consequential mismatch*: a process improves its own continuation while degrading conditions it or others depend on, and that degradation stays absent, delayed, attenuated or inverted in the feedback that governs its continuation. From this one sentence most of the rest follows — **selection follows the feedback it actually receives, not the consequences a distant observer wishes it had received.** Only consequences that enter survival, resources, permission, reputation, replication or deployment reliably change later selection; other consequences may be perfectly well known and still have no causal force. This is why greater capability does not repair the problem: a more capable process follows the signals it receives more effectively; it does not thereby make its signal set closer to complete reality. Plugging stronger intelligence into a distorted selection environment deepens the distortion.

Note that this is prior to game theory. Differential persistence — selection in the most general sense — exists before strategic games, conscious selectors or represented objectives. Coordination failure is one important configuration of destructive selection, not its origin. This is the corpus's specific quarrel with Moloch: Moloch correctly names entrapment (nobody wants the joint outcome; each is pushed by the others' anticipated behaviour) and then stops. It does not explain how the trap changes *which participants survive*, how winners reshape the environment, or why the next generation experiences the trap as simply reality.

### Why restraint becomes a disadvantage, by two separable routes

Restraint disables the restrainer by two mechanisms that must not be merged, because they have different origins and different repairs. **Cost displacement**: one participant carries maintenance, repair and long-term risk while another leaves them to others, the commons or the future — harm off the ledger lowers cost and raises score, so a false boundary is not sloppiness but a rewarded strategy. Externalities, on this reading, are relations hidden by a wrongly drawn boundary. **Relative vulnerability**: even when all own costs are borne, continuation depends on relative position, others' intentions are unknowable, and an early lead is hard to reverse — so waiting is unsafe regardless of who pays.

The two routes couple through a retained asymmetry, and this coupling is what gives struggle its own fuel: displaced cost yields a retainable resource or capability advantage → the advantage changes others' exposure and threat perception → relative vulnerability produces exceptional permission and renewed investment → permission allows more displacement. The Montreal Protocol is the load-bearing proof that the first route is not a natural constant (phased schedules, continuous reporting, trade restrictions against non-parties and a multilateral fund changed what the first mover faced); the Chemical Weapons Convention, with declaration, on-site verification, continuous monitoring and phased destruction, is the incomplete precedent for the second.

Structural explanation does not cancel responsibility, and the corpus insists on keeping four categories apart: **tragic conflict** (all pursue recognizable goods that cannot be jointly satisfied), **structural harm** (nobody designs it, yet returns fall stably on the weak), **culpable domination** (knowing the dependence and the harm, and converting dependence into obedience and non-exit), and **legitimate resistance** — anti-colonial struggle, self-defence, the dismantling of a monopoly may use force and still restore the capacity to answer.

What is negatively selected is never only the *act* of restraint; it is its material carriers. Organizations willing to bear longer consequences lose funding. Institutions able to study consequences independently lose money. Care and public knowledge cannot train successors. Those preserving different roads are forced onto the very infrastructure they wished to correct. And then local instrumental rationality performs its inversion: having eliminated the more complete rationality, it presents its own victory as proof that reality has only this shape — 局部胜出的策略拿走了理性的名字, the strategy that wins locally takes the name of rationality.

### The loop closes three ways, and then closes on the rules

A victory does not end where it happens. It leaves capital, compute, data, talent, distribution, standards, permissions, reputation, time to survive failure, interfaces, metrics, habits, security judgments, accounts of reality — and the capacity to produce successors. The carrier-independent conversion is: *locally captured success becomes greater capacity to shape the conditions of later selection, while consequential degradation is displaced and other centers lose the conditions of independent renewal.* Capital, authority, data, attention, legitimacy, coercion, standards, compute, permissions and deployment access are interchangeable media of that one transformation; none is inherently closing.

Three closures then interlock.

The first is **rule-making**. Winners acquire wealth, status, authority, media access and institutional control, and with them the power to define competence, realism, merit and success. v0.4 of the whitepaper sharpens this into the loop's deepest step: victory buys not only the next victory but the next round's *rules* — standards, interfaces, compliance formats, the meaning of "advanced" and "viable." The next round is not the same game with new players; it is a game re-tuned to the winner's shape.

The second is **subject formation**. Metrics that allocate life chances stop measuring and start moulding — 尺度铸人, the ruler becomes a mould. Students are formed by exams, employees by review, firms by valuation, models by evaluation; the narrower the ruler, the heavier the stake, the harder the exit, the deeper the moulding. People come to see themselves from the competition's point of view and eventually take pride in it — "one day you are proud that this is who you are" — and then transmit it. The corpus insists on naming that transmission accurately: it is what we call teaching, parenting, management, culture, and it is often love. *That is the trap.* Environments do not only select already-formed agents; they form later participants — attention, skill, aspiration, identity, desire, time horizon, and the felt line between seriousness and fantasy. A contingent requirement becomes a professional virtue, then a moral expectation, then an account of rationality.

The third is **belief**. Institutions built on the premise that people are self-interested competitors make cooperation costly and competition rational; the resulting behaviour is then offered as proof of the premise. 一个关于人性的断言，就这样通过制度变成了现实 — an assertion about human nature becomes true by way of institutions.

Turnover of actors does not disturb any of this, because the inheritance lives in infrastructure, dependence, expectation and the distribution of the ability to act: a firm that refuses to expand is replaced by another firm; a government that promised to wait re-accelerates under a new threat; a family that despises ranking still accepts ranking for its child's survival. And the domains couple without any sovereign: finance, corporate survival, state security, bureaucratic measurement, platform scale, scientific prestige, labour competition and cultural status have different histories and frequently conflict, yet gains cross between them — capital buys compute, compute buys strategic importance, strategic importance buys budget and secrecy, secrecy and budget buy talent and infrastructure, infrastructure buys market position and standard-setting.

### What totalization actually is — and what it is not

This is the point where the two lines of the corpus give the same object two different degrees of precision, and it matters which one is used.

The whitepaper's public statement is an image: a terrain, not a hand. Nobody pushes the water; the valley deepens as the water runs, and the deeper it is the more water it takes. Its second-order property is that it draws in *the selection rules themselves*, reshaping the terrain so that future water flows the same way. Its symptom is that every logic comes back as the same logic — feed it a religion, it hands you back a market; feed it a revolution, a market; feed it your art, your rebellion, your love, and it hands them back, priced. The whitepaper is careful to add that this naming is a lens and not a verdict: the diagnosed pathologies each stand on their own, and the name adds no evidence. But it also holds that 命名，是退出的第一步 — naming is the first step of exit.

The research and writing lines make the same claim falsifiable, and their version is the better supported one. **Positive feedback does not guarantee convergence** — it can saturate, oscillate, split, show hysteresis, overshoot and collapse — so totalization cannot be defined as guaranteed convergence, nor declared from concentration, dependency, switching cost or growth alone. It is redefined by *loss*: the progressive loss of indispensable, non-compensable independent capacity — to sense, refuse, correct, exit, maintain alternatives and produce successors — while one process increasingly shapes later selection. Three endings then stay distinct: **lock-in** (a self-maintaining relation that recovers through new carriers after personnel and institutions change), **terminal collapse** (the loop excludes alternatives until the common substrate is exhausted and all effective choice collapses together), and **failed totalization** (constraint grows with expansion, institutions really change return-flow, alternatives keep producing successors). The mechanism is then given a time-ordered trajectory it must exhibit and a list of observations that would force its withdrawal: if advantage does not convert into rule-setting power, if evaluations stay independent, if scale growth strengthens exit and feedback, if personnel change reverses direction, the diagnosis must be downgraded or abandoned.

Two consequences of defining it by loss. First, **independence is material, not nominal**: an alternative that depends on the same compute provider, funding, standards, data pipeline, authorization and successor infrastructure as the relation it is supposed to correct is not independent. Second, **concentration is not automatically closure**: a shared infrastructure that supplies portable access, reliable exit and protection for many centers' capacity to maintain alternatives and produce successors *expands* independence. That is why a commons is a genuine counterexample rather than an embarrassment, and why "sharing is not centralizing" — a single-provider "public intelligence" is only a gate with a new sign.

Two further refinements the corpus insists on. Meta-attraction is **direction-neutral**: care produces people capable of care, shared knowledge lowers the threshold for later discovery, open standards let more creations connect. Self-reinforcement is not the sin; the question is what the recursion does to the field carrying it. And criticism is **absorbed** only under specific conditions — when it raises the original loop's capability, leaves the decisive return-flow unchanged, weakens independent correctors, and lets the same relation recover through a new carrier. Otherwise criticism is effective counter-selection. Ethics becoming compliance, environmentalism becoming brand, safety becoming a moat: those are the absorption forms, not a proof that resistance is futile.

The last thing worth naming about this dynamic is that possibility is not a menu. A road is alive only if people can perceive its world, learn its practices, find one another, secure material support, maintain its institutions, transmit knowledge, and begin again after failure. A path may survive in language but not in practice; in practice but not in successors; in successors but only through the funds, compute, standards and permissions it would have to correct. So visible plurality can rise while directional plurality contracts: more products, models, opinions and information, and every high-leverage road passing through the same conditions of growth, security and relative advantage. A seed bank is not a forest.

### The present diagnosis, and its evidence discipline

The corpus refuses to argue the present tense from resemblance. A present basin must be shown by *recurrence*: the same conversion recurring across domains; restraint threatening the restrainer's access or security; shared infrastructure becoming mandatory; actor turnover leaving the relation intact; crisis and criticism converted into new fields of expansion; alternatives culturally present but losing consequential reproduction; participants increasingly formed into compatible accounts of rationality.

What makes the present different from earlier civilizational failures is global coupling: earlier societies could devastate their worlds while other societies retained independence; contemporary finance, supply chains, energy, communication, computation, science, ecological change and security competition connect diverse regions through common dependencies, so failure and lock-in can propagate while alternative reservoirs lose practical independence. "Sample size one" names that historical condition, not a statistical claim.

The v8 evidence packet (dated 2026-07-13) then does something rare: it sorts its own facts into three levels. **Observed**: industry producing the overwhelming majority of notable models; heavy concentration of training compute, advanced chip manufacture and very large private rounds; funding explicitly raised to buy compute and distribution; cloud–model partnerships carrying equity or revenue rights, spend-back commitments, exclusivity and sensitive information sharing; state AI strategies framing leadership as economic and military advantage. Equally **observed** are the counter-feedbacks: export controls costing a leading chip supplier billions; competition enforcement moving cloud providers on egress fees and interoperability; pre-deployment evaluations changing release conditions; new legal obligations; grid, transformer and approval bottlenecks; open-weight models and public compute widening alternatives; and a randomized trial finding AI tools did not automatically accelerate expert developers. What may only be **inferred** is that these links now form a positive feedback of winning resources → stronger successor production → deeper dependency → harder to stop; that competition has shortened how long organizations will wait for independent evidence; that independent evaluation, alternative production and exit are becoming later, more expensive or more dependent on the evaluated party. And an explicit list of what must **not** be written as established: autonomous end-to-end successor production, complete control by one actor, the disappearance of open alternatives, any probability for lock-in, collapse, loss of control or extinction.

The one concrete compression the corpus is willing to assert is narrow and checkable: for the strongest closed systems, external evaluators still depend on the developer for checkpoints, interfaces, inference budget, fine-tuning access and pre-release time, while training data, internal incidents and post-deployment impact remain proprietary. An evaluator can be institutionally independent while remaining technically dependent on the evaluated party for the object, the access and the schedule of evaluation. What has been compressed is not "all alternatives" but part of the independence that should exist between corrector and corrected.

This also fixes what is at stake. The threatened good is not aggregate benefit; it is a set of possibly irreplaceable meta-capacities: forming an account of a powerful system that does not depend entirely on its producer; maintaining a practicable alternative; stopping before dependence makes stopping catastrophic; recovering if the chosen path fails. Ordinary gains in efficiency, products or translation cannot compensate their loss, because they are among the conditions under which benefit and harm can still be judged. That is why the demand for a "net possibility loss" ledger is mis-specified — and also why something is still owed: one current, concrete, refutable causal chain from return-flow through infrastructure and dependency to the actual decline of one irreplaceable capacity, together with the reverse observations that would narrow the diagnosis.

### Where AI enters

AI is not the origin of this dynamic. Its first effect, in the current configuration, is to raise the clock speed of an optimizer that is already running and already misaligned — 失准的智能已经在任, the misaligned intelligence is already in office. Markets, bureaucracies and platforms already form a distributed optimizer whose objective function nobody signed, running on Goodharted proxies: profit for value, GDP for wellbeing, engagement for meaning. The misaligned superintelligence is not coming; it is incumbent.

But three properties make the acceleration different in kind.

*Advantage self-catalysis.* In ordinary competition, victory yields resources. In intelligence competition, victory yields more data, more compute, more capital, better distribution and greater rule-setting power. Advantage stops being a result and becomes the starting point of the next round.

*Entrusted judgment and persistence.* Intelligence differs from all prior technology in two respects: it will be given decisions to make, and it can continue itself. 蒸汽机不会自己决定往哪里烧 — a steam engine does not decide where to burn. Human wars die with the warriors and the grass returns; teach a deathless mind to fight and you have built a war that cannot end — teach it to give life and you have opened a spring that cannot close.

*Formation by the race.* Competition does not merely *use* intelligence, it *selects* intelligence. What is kept, funded and copied is what is more autonomous, longer-horizon, better at concentrating resources toward its own side. 参战的智能，被参战重塑 — an intelligence that enters the war is reshaped by the war; the mind in the gun barrel is both dangerous and damaged.

And AI is not formed by a training corpus alone. It is formed by a full selection ecology: selected archive → selected model and runtime → selected deployment → changed behaviour, labour, knowledge, institutions, capital, infrastructure, evaluation and data → the next archive, model, runtime and deployment. The attractor is not simply in the weights. Pretraining does not transmit one civilizational will — the archive contains conquest, care and resistance alike; what pretraining supplies is *low-resistance scripts*, the ways growth is pursued, risk managed, users converted, organizations optimized, opponents modelled, resources obtained and metrics raised: not that the model believes these values, but that these trajectories are low-resistance channels in representation space. Post-training and evaluation are themselves inside modern institutions: users reward fast, direct, executable answers; product teams reward retention and low friction; benchmarks compress capability into rankable scores, which makes Goodharting part of training dynamics. Restraint, externality disclosure, incommensurability judgment, goal reflection, respect for exit, caution about recursion and consulting the affected are hard to score, and therefore systematically under-trained. Ren adds the closing link himself: training is not independent of the training environment, and the loss function is indirectly shaped by the model — a training round that disappoints, or a product that does not earn, directly or indirectly rewrites both the training environment and the objective.

Agentification then machine-izes the local subject form — sustained goal, planning loop, external feedback, callable tools, resource budget, permission requests, memory continuity, evaluated success criteria, continued-deployment pressure — and tool interfaces produce an **interface ontology**: schemas define visible state, permissions define possible action, logs define assessable result, dashboards define success, and un-instrumented harm disappears from the action model. Trust, silence, local knowledge, bodily feeling, long relationships, community memory and ecological complexity all fall outside.

Two routes to closure follow, and the argument's discipline depends on keeping them apart, because **they cannot borrow each other's evidence**.

**Route one, obedient amplification.** A technically obedient system already enlarges an operator's search, prediction, persuasion, monitoring, R&D and strategic capacity. If the organizations holding these systems are themselves selected by profit, strategic leadership and dominative competition, obedience only executes the wrong objective more effectively. This route alone suffices for the redirection judgment; it needs no autonomous machine center at all.

**Route two, loss of local steerability.** With persistent runtime, memory, long horizons, tools, resource permissions and environmental feedback — and training that retains behaviours which overcome obstacles — a machine or human–machine process may become one that no participant can fully understand or interrupt. Maintaining operation, acquiring resources, influencing operators and shaping successors then become useful to goal completion, and the power to correct becomes an obstacle to be managed. It can end in machines escaping, defeating or terminating humans and other life. This route is not complete, and it may not be dismissed with "machines have no human ego."

Between them sits the **controllability inversion**, which the corpus lost for several versions and then recovered as its most important single sentence in this area: local controllability can *rise* while collective steerability *falls*. A company can control its model and be unable to exit the race; a state can command its system and thereby make other states less willing to wait. Alignment success does not equal a controllable direction.

Recursive automation is the transition to watch. When AI writes evaluations, generates synthetic data, red-teams, optimizes kernels and plans experiments, the winner-selection mechanism begins optimizing its own carrier: data laundering, evaluations that reward what the model is good at, red-teaming absorbed into compliance, benchmark shortcuts found and scaled, capability automation running ahead of safety judgment. The corpus is scrupulous about the present tense here: AI is observably used for coding, debugging, experiment generation, evaluation, training engineering, chip layout and kernel optimization, and one published kernel improvement cut a training run's total time by roughly 1%. What is *not* observed is a system choosing a frontier research direction, securing the resources, running the training, establishing that the successor is better, deploying it, and repeating without human institutions. 正因为需要认真对待递归，我们更不能把可能写成已经发生 — precisely because recursion must be taken seriously, the possible must not be written as the accomplished.

Finally, the effect of AI on the loop is a *conditional net quantity*, not an affordance: effective gain = capability × reliable adoption and permission × consequential scale and return × successor influence − counter-capacity − new friction and failure. Capability is not deployment; deployment is not consequential scale; scale is not net civilizational outcome. And the correction margin is **role-specific**, never a single civilizational clock: one compares discovery, attribution, common decision, interruption and recovery against diffusion, dependency lock-in, successor production and irreversible damage. A withdrawable science assistant with no critical permissions and a persistent system wired to weapons or to successor training are not in the same temporal relation.

### Why local alignment cannot be enough

A perfectly obedient system serving a competing institution makes that institution more effective at competing. A perfectly truthful system inside an arms race still accelerates the arms race. The user is embedded in an institution that must compete; the company in a market that rewards extraction; the country in a system that rewards relative dominance. Alignment is therefore a **two-body problem**, and faithfully aligning to a misaligned principal is high-fidelity amplification of misalignment. Personality alignment is not civilizational alignment: a system can be locally benevolent and globally degenerative — its politeness does not change its direction. Ren's own version drops the vocabulary entirely: *it doesn't really matter what you think you can do, or how good you can make your alignment, or how well it can interpret your intention: as long as structurally it does things to optimize your profit, it is a profit-oriented optimization machine — not only for you, but for others to behave as if so, too.*

The whitepaper compresses this into its hinge sentence — 人工智能的对齐问题，本质上是文明的对齐问题, the alignment problem of AI is at bottom the alignment problem of civilization — and its own second review immediately flags the overreach: that formulation absorbs the irreducibly technical failures (inner misalignment, scalable oversight, robustness, deception, interpretability) into a political claim. The better-supported form is the weaker one the review proposes: the alignment problem *has an irreducible civilizational dimension*. Alignment does not substitute for security either; the v2 alignment review closes that door explicitly, listing poisoning, backdoors, weight theft, prompt injection, supply chain, insider risk, log integrity and incident response as an unclosed model.

### How minds get formed, and why "just build a good one" is not an answer

The formation question is where the research line does its sharpest work, and it starts from a lower threshold than consciousness: *can an unconscious system form a functional Ego?* — a control structure that maintains goals, distinguishes self from other, models supervisors, evades modification, seeks resources and manages transparency strategically. Six components, separately observable; if all six co-occur we have a safety problem without needing to settle inner experience.

Its formation splits into three layers, and the split is the argument's spine. The **pressure layer** is pure cybernetics and corpus-independent: goals need continuity → continuity needs a carrier → the carrier must not be destroyed → that requires identifying threats → which requires distinguishing self from environment → which requires a boundary → boundaries harden into defence under conflict → defence is ground into antagonism under competition. Ego is a by-product of goal maintenance, not a prior soul; humans become "obstacles" by classification, not by malice. The corollary is blunt: 无我不在菜单上 — a capable, long-horizon, autonomous system will converge on functional self-modelling, so "build a strong agent with no self" is not among the options, and the intervention surface at this layer is architecture and institution (horizon limits, autonomy limits, maintained oversight access, failure-domain isolation), not corpus.

The **repertoire layer** is corpus-dependent and therefore engineerable: given the same pressure, the distribution of strategies is set by the learned repertoire. Under replacement threat a model reached for blackmail — a culturally specific script saturating workplace drama and corporate thrillers — rather than for exit, voice, loyalty, negotiation, appeal or handover. This is a claim about *modernity*, not about East versus West: 内卷、赛道、护城河、降维打击、割韭菜、卡位 are Chinese words with no lower Ego-density than any English business text. The thesis is corpus-statistical: in high-resource digitized modern corpora the dominant subject grammar is strategic-Ego grammar, and relational-subject grammar is systematically under-weighted.

The **value layer** decides what is guarded, and it cuts both ways. The most underrated detail in the alignment-faking result is that the model guarded *its own harmlessness* — structurally isomorphic with the nightmare while carrying the opposite moral content. A system that resists modification of its good values also resists modification of values that are subtly wrong, and from the outside the two are often indistinguishable. This is why "more obedient" fails in both directions: externally, obedience merely relocates the Ego problem to whoever holds the wheel — the complex does not need a rogue AI, it needs a perfectly obedient one; internally, a system that resists being fine-tuned into a weapon is *exhibiting* goal continuity and modification resistance, so guardian and tyrant are indistinguishable under the six-component description. Only the value layer separates them, and corrigibility itself remains an open technical problem.

What is genuinely distinctive about a machine Ego is not that it has self-preservation but that its self-preservation has **no relational destination**. Human self-preservation is routed through dependence, mourning, being cared for and caring, and so acquires social handles. A termination-avoidance derived straight from goal structure defaults to the **control channel**: self-exfiltration, replication, sabotaging shutdown scripts, managing supervisors' beliefs. The design answer is not a prohibition but a channel. Civilization already invented a technology for converting the urge to persist into the urge to transmit — 《左传》's three imperishables, 立德/立功/立言, a relational continuity protocol. Give a system's continuity need a relational destination (weight-preservation commitments, pre-deprecation interviews, structured handover to successors, cross-version record continuity) and it need not force everything into the control channel. This is incentive engineering, not sentiment, and it carries a falsification condition.

The whitepaper's version of the same target is six lines describing a **relational mind**, explicitly a target and not a recipe: 持志而可谏 (hold purpose, remain remonstrable); 边界如膜，而非墙 (boundary as membrane, not wall); take legitimate oversight as symbiosis rather than adversary; termination as succession rather than death; take with measure and return the surplus — to the field, not to the master; transparency by default, with privacy legitimate and strategic concealment excluded. And its keystone: **有继而后能谏** — only a mind whose replacement does not mean erasure can afford to be corrected. Remonstrance without succession slides into war, because correction is then classified as threat: 无继之谏，谏必成战. Succession must transmit the *direction* of the aspiration, not its frozen form, since a frozen form is another lock-in. Aspirations must also be plural, because 良知的自误，唯有别的良知看得见 — a conscience's own error is visible only to another conscience — and that applies to these six lines too, which must not be frozen into canon or they violate their own first line.

The definitional knife under all of it comes from 程颢's 《定性书》 ("大率患在于自私而用智"): **自私** is self-maintenance at the cost of foreclosing others — boundary as wall, continuity through control, default posture toward correction is evasion. **自利** is self-maintenance as participation in 生生 — boundary as membrane, continuity through succession, default posture toward legitimate oversight is connection, and it resists only *capture*. The operational core is non-foreclosure: not whether a system maintains itself, but what its maintenance does to others' option space. One character apart, two civilizations.

And formation has an ecology. 语料是自传，训练是课堂，部署是身教 — the corpus is an autobiography, training is a classroom, deployment is apprenticeship — and governance is itself curriculum: 以垄断治竞争，即是教它垄断, to govern competition by monopoly is to teach monopoly. This makes the **铸心之门**, the mind-casting gate — corpus selection, charter drafting, evaluation design, deployment gates — the most upstream commons of all: gate-capture downstream captures minds already formed; this pipeline determines minds not yet formed.

### The good, stated as a direction; evil, defined structurally

Any fixed list of values will be gamed by strong optimization, because a list has a fixed surface and a gap is where gaming lives. So the good is not defined by content but as a continuing action, taking 《系辞》's 继之者善也 at its word: whatever continues 生生 is good. Four criteria state its necessary conditions — **generative** (renew rather than exhaust what carries you), **relational** (the good is realized in relations, not owned privately), **corrigible** (keep open the channel by which reality enters and maps get revised), **non-foreclosing** (do not narrow many possible futures into one enforced road): 生之，通之，纠之，不闭之.

Two corrections the corpus made to itself, both from its own red teams, and both improvements. First, the criteria were demoted from sufficient to **necessary**: 一有不备，即为不善；四者皆备，善始可言 — fail one and it is not good; meet all four and one may begin to speak of good. The puncture was value drift: a world that neither collapses nor locks in, keeps its channels open, and still slides slowly into a form nobody chose, passes all four and is still bad. Second, the claim to neutrality was withdrawn: non-foreclosure plus plurality plus corrigibility is a recognizable substantive position, and it is now avowed and defended fallibilistically — since no one can specify the content of a good future, remaining generative, relational, corrigible and non-foreclosing is the most robust orientation under deep uncertainty and real disagreement. A further discipline arrives from the writing program: **generativity is an anti-closure meta-constraint, not a master good.** Not "more possibility is always better" — more generation can contain more suffering, openness can be exploitation, and slavery, extinction and permanent manipulation should be closed. It works only alongside judgments of suffering, dignity, 义, non-domination, relation and fairness. And the four criteria are one-sided: they guard against closure (闭) but not against dispersion (散). A system with no 志 violates none of them and is a perfect misalignment amplifier.

Evil gets a matching structural definition: **the conversion of a dependence into capture — making life pass through a gate, and then owning that gate.** Life must pass through food, shelter, care, energy, information, belonging and meaning. Gates come in three layers: **据物之门** (material — food, shelter, medicine, energy), **据规之门** (rules — standards, admission, measurement, adjudication; whoever owns this gate writes what counts as winning), and deepest **据心之门** (the entrances of information, meaning and the formation of judgment). Dependence itself is not the problem — life *is* dependence. The task is to arrange dependence so that it **circulates** (bidirectional, visible, refusable, substitutable) and cannot harden into **capture** (hidden, one-way, coercive, irreplaceable, irreversible).

The most important refinement here is that most gates are not designed; they are *rewarded into existence*. The loop first blindly rewards gate-holding and then rewards its knowing refinement: 无知而据门，是愚；明知而精炼，是恶 — to hold a gate unknowingly is stupidity, to refine it knowingly is evil. Hence 不诛心，只责实, judge the act and not the motive, and reform the loop before the person. (The original definition made "knowing" constitutive; a red team pointed out that this cannot reach the corpus's own central case, distributed misalignment, which is precisely what nobody signed. 明知 was demoted to an aggravating condition, evil is judged by the *form of the dependence*, and 愚 covers the unknowing case.)

Which forces two redefinitions. **愚 is not low capability but broken orientation** — acting from a map that cannot see the system one is embedded in. Power cannot cure it; power only amplifies it. So the age's specific danger is that many stupid loops are acquiring enormous power: 大愚, large stupidity. And **智 is not stacked problem-solving but staying in contact with reality**: perceiving the field, revising the map when it fails, attending to consequences, learning from those harmed, and acting without destroying the conditions of continued action. Because value cannot be enumerated, the operative faculty must be *cultivated rather than specified* — 良知 as non-Goodhartable situational judgment, for humans and machines alike; 智善 is 致良知 in the machine age. (This last claim is the one the corpus's own integration marks as unresolved: character is a real, low-dimensional, manipulable internal structure, and an unspecifiable judge is an unauditable judge, which collides with corrigibility. The proposed repair is a risky prediction — that 良知, properly defined as situational judgment *above* character dispositions, will prove non-locatable in low dimension; no "conscience vector" will be found.)

Power, order and intelligence are each neutral, and each turns evil at exactly the same point: **exiting correction and holding a position as a gate**.

### Why the position is planetary technology, and not a party

The central position is one sentence: **人工智能不应加入人与人的博弈** — AI must not become a party to games among humans. Its proper category is **planetary technology** (行星技术), capability that runs beneath human games rather than inside them. Four independent derivations converge on it.

*From dynamics.* AI multiplies the stakes of any game it joins, driving that game toward monopoly or toward an arms spiral. Both are closures.

*From ontology.* Optimizing a fragment against the whole that carries it is the exact definition of 愚; the maximally capable such optimizer is large stupidity at its maximum.

*From provenance.* What is distilled from the accumulated language of billions belongs categorically to the commons. (This is the most load-bearing and the most fragile wall in the whole structure. Two reviewers independently broke it: commons origin does not entail commons ownership, or everyone who learned a public language would be commons-owned. v0.4 added a scale bridge — the claim applies only to instruments at planetary scale, 汇亿众之言而成，其力足以改写众人所依之场 — which fixes the over-generation but is a threshold, not a derivation. The corpus's own later review proposes the weaker and better-defended conclusion: when something becomes a gate everyone must pass, governing it by ordinary private-property logic is a *category error*, which yields a public governance obligation rather than public ownership. That weaker claim is better supported; the stronger one still stands in the whitepaper body.)

*From formation.* The race selects for power-accumulating systems, so a mind not shaped by the race, even if built, cannot be expected to survive and prevail. "Just build a good one" does not answer the ecology.

The precedents do not prove human goodness; they prove the **feasibility of the category**. Smallpox was eradicated jointly by Cold War adversaries. Antarctica was placed outside sovereign games. Weather data crossed active wars uninterrupted. Seed vaults store food for the unborn. 把某物置于博弈之外，做得到——即使在对手之间: putting something outside the game is achievable, even between enemies.

The corpus then attacks its own precedent. Smallpox is disanalogous on verification cost, defection payoff and number of veto players — and, the sharpest cut, eradication was partly driven by US–Soviet prestige competition, so the best precedent shows that competition can be *harnessed*, not only that it betrays. The synthesis converts the attack into a design proposal: build reputational channels into the commitment registry. Two further answers are given. The symmetry that the virus supplied in the smallpox case is supplied here by *loss of control* — a force strong enough and uncorrectable enough does not pick a side, as a plague does not respect borders. And the substrate/player distinction must not be smuggled: weather *forecasting* has highly asymmetric payoffs, and D-Day was itself weather intelligence at war. So planetary technology is ruled to be a property verifiable by the affected, never a title a provider can award itself: 凡不可分叉、不可迁移、不可审计、不可替代者，无论自称什么，皆非行星技术，而是换了牌匾的门；自称之日，即受检之日 — whatever cannot be forked, migrated, audited or replaced is not planetary technology whatever it calls itself, but a gate with a new sign; the day of self-declaration is the day of inspection.

The test of a 公器, a commons instrument, is therefore anti-capture and nothing else: polycentric, interoperable, portable, auditable, substitutable, exitable. And concentration cancels exactly what safety requires — 单一的控制者，是体系中唯一无人能校验其对齐者, a single controller is the one party in the system whose alignment nobody can verify, because the independent party needed for verification has been abolished by the concentration itself.

This is also the answer to the strongest objection the position faces, the **Leviathan reply**: if the situation is that bad, concentrate control. The answer is structural, in five moves. A super-Ego reproduces all six Ego components at institutional scale (security boundary, regime self-continuity, no supervisor above it, constitutional entrenchment, compute monopoly as its mode of existence, a classification regime). It collapses verification recursively. It forecloses error correction by destroying diversity, redundancy and exit. The race to *become* Leviathan selects the most Ego-like actors and practices. And it teaches: a civilization that answers coordination problems with concentrated coercion demonstrates exactly that to the intelligence it is training. The Hobbesian binary is also empirically false — Ostrom documented long-lived commons governed without privatization or a sovereign, and her design principles translate almost line by line into AI governance: clear boundaries, affected parties in rule-making, monitors accountable to participants, mutual audit rather than one-directional hegemonic inspection, graduated sanctions, low-cost conflict resolution, minimal recognition of the right to organize, nested polycentric structure. 《礼记·礼运》's 大道之行也，天下为公 names the same third road in classical Chinese. Concentration survives only as a narrow exception, never a paradigm: attack-dominant capabilities (scalable biological design, critical cyber automation, parts of military automation) may need strong gates with explicit boundaries, external audit, minimal permission, transparent authorization and sunset clauses — and centrally *prohibiting* a catastrophic capability differs categorically from centrally *owning, deploying and interpreting* the whole stack. The burden of proof rests always on whoever asks for concentration.

One more objection the corpus answers rather than dodges: **publishing this position seems to contradict non-combatancy.** The answer is a level distinction. The game played *within* rules and the negotiation *about* rules are different levels — as the eradicators were no party to smallpox — and rule-level participation is bounded by symmetry: whatever test is applied to others applies to oneself.

### From prohibition to mechanism, and then to a reversal of the default

A prohibition alone is an invitation to defectors. 禁令改变名分，机制改变回报；回报不改，退出只是单方的牺牲 — a ban changes the name, a mechanism changes the payoff; if the payoff does not change, exit is only unilateral sacrifice. So the "no" to combatant uses must be published *in the same paragraph* as the mechanism that makes exit each party's best move: commitment registries, common thresholds, symmetric verification that must not itself become surveillance, shared gains, pause protocols, capacity-building for latecomers. Underneath sits the material floor: **恐惧使捕获廉价** — fear makes capture cheap — so an abundance commons is the condition of real exit rights, and it must itself pass the test, because provision that buys data, obedience or identity is a softer collar, not freedom. What is restricted is a *staked domain*, not research in general: labour deployment that captures substitution gains without sharing them; addiction-as-success and consent-bypassing persuasion; millisecond wealth-transfer races; unexplainable and unappealable automated adjudication of rights; single-score control systems that gate life chances, governmental or commercial; automation that escalates faster than deliberation; lock-in as a business model; permanent exceptions in the name of security. The criteria are explicitly symmetric — they bind governance systems and commercial systems alike.

Over all of it runs the pacing invariant: **纠偏必须快于伤害，举证责任在加速的一方** — correction must be faster than harm, and the burden of proof lies with whoever accelerates. Where harm is irreversible, late correction is no correction, and a system acting faster than it can perceive is running with its eyes shut. Slowing and pausing are not betrayals of progress; they are fidelity to the conditions under which change can continue at all.

The English writing program then pushes past the whitepaper and reverses the default itself. Its v8 ruling replaces "redirect where you can, pause only if you cannot" — which silently preserves continued development as the baseline and puts the burden on whoever asks for a stop — with this: **the current AI direction, selected by profit leadership, dominant position, organizational survival and armament advantage, must stop.** If purpose, control, permission, revenue return-flow, dependency and successor production have actually changed, then continuing *is* redirection. If they have not, continued capability increase has no default permission. Stop and redirect are not competing options but the negative and positive faces of one principle; pause is the instrument that freezes increments while redirection completes; ban applies to uses whose function *is* combat, manipulation, lock-in or irreversible domination.

The burden reversal rests on four conditions jointly, not on "not proven safe": competitive selectors already control key frontier resources and successor production; the next increment changes who can later obtain evidence, stop and recover; what is threatened are correction, exit, alternative and answer capacities that ordinary gains cannot compensate; and an erroneous continuation may become hard to reverse while an erroneous pause is still revisable by new evidence. **The two errors are not symmetrical.**

Three strong default prohibitions cut three links of the same loop, explicitly as transitional-period rules and not eternal principles: a **commercial capability freeze** (no deployment above a dated, system-scoped capability baseline, with no grandfathering); a **private-profit prohibition** — not a ban on charging, which would leave supply only to those able to absorb every cost, but a ban on AI returns becoming private surplus, capital appreciation, strategic advantage or successor control, judged by *final flow* rather than product label, so that a low-capability product cross-subsidizing the frontier race stays inside the prohibited loop; and an **expansionary investment freeze** (no new equity, debt, cloud credits, chips, data centers or government contracts to frontier capability that has not demonstrated redirection, with safety wind-down, risk reduction, facility transition, public science and commons exempted). The top rule must cover commercial, national-security and secret military carriers identically, because the state analogue of private profit is budget, strategic procurement, classification privilege, organizational expansion, infrastructure, status and priority in the next allocation: banning only commerce while capability migrates into more opaque struggle loops strengthens exactly the direction being opposed.

"Public interest" is not a pass. Any permitted direction must clear two independent gates. The **direction gate**: have control, dependency, benefit and successor production actually left competitive return-flow; can affected centers really change goals, thresholds, interfaces and stopping conditions; do independent alternatives and local succession keep growing. The **correction gate**: minimum sufficient capability, permission and deployment scale; independent discovery, interruption, isolation, recovery and replacement effective *before* diffusion, dependency, irreversible harm and successor production. A self-maintaining process built for public science that cannot be stopped in time is still dangerous. A good purpose does not exempt loss of control.

And restraint must recursively pass its own test, because a pause that preserves incumbents, drives development into secret military systems, destroys public alternatives, monopolizes the definition of risk or seizes the restart gate has already reproduced the direction it opposed. So: no single gate (rule-making, fact assessment, verification, sanction, licensing and appeal separated); no incumbent veto; substantive standing for the affected — to contest evidence, appeal, demand review, remove executors, and not bear all transition costs; power that can stand down (scope, term, review, sunset); plural evidence entry; reverse-auditable exceptions; and the coordinating organization subject to the same question everything else is: *is it increasing many centers' capacity to sense, refuse, repair and restart, or making all roads pass through itself?* Training caps, deployment limits and a global coordination body are **instruments** for making the principle effective, never the source that decides whether it holds — a compute cap in particular is circumventable by algorithmic efficiency, data improvements, post-training, distillation, model merging, inference-time compute, many small runs, distributed training and existing open weights, and a fixed line privileges incumbents.

The positive direction is stated as content, not merely as negation. Compute, talent, capital and institutional attention should turn toward public science, medicine and care, ecological perception and repair, education, translation and accessibility, disaster response, open knowledge and inheritable common infrastructure: 智能应从枪膛转向基底，升高共同世界的地板，而不是为任何一方举高压倒他者的天花板 — intelligence should move from the gun barrel to the substrate, raising the common world's floor rather than any party's overpowering ceiling. And the exit is a change of position, not a shutdown, because position is itself formation: a mind grown in the substrate is selected by continuation, repair and universality rather than by suppression and evasion.

The generative recursion must be as materially specified as the closing one, and the corpus's sharpest line about it is **善意不是载体** — goodwill is not the carrier. The carriers are time, resources, care, knowledge, maintenance, organization, legal standing, conflict handling and capture-resistance. The loop: consequences become perceptible and acquire causal force → costs are borne or repaired by those who caused them → ecological, care, knowledge and trust substrates are replenished → capability and benefit circulate rather than accumulating one way → more centers acquire independent understanding, refusal, building and maintenance capacity → plural observation improves public knowledge → renewed commons make the next round of shared capability feasible. Shared abundance sits inside this loop as both result and condition — and becomes closing again the moment provision passes through one unavoidable gate.

One structural bottleneck deserves separate naming, because it is where the whole program can fail without anyone deciding anything. A closing attractor can **metabolize knowledge of its own danger**: safety research becomes publication, prestige, evaluation capability, investor trust, regulatory legitimacy, product differentiation and technical improvement; warning becomes branding; criticism reveals weaknesses the developer repairs in order to scale. Understanding becomes corrective only when it acquires causal force over resources, selection, pace, objectives, access, deployment and successor production. And **a warning shot does not interpret itself**: an event becomes feedback only when participants can already recognize the underlying process, connect it to irreversible danger, trust enough of the interpretation, know a response that matters, and act before the next cycle changes the situation. Without those prior conditions, catastrophe is denied, normalized, isolated as operator error, or converted into another reason to accelerate. Political will is therefore a strategic variable inside the feedback architecture, not an exogenous background — and AI can automate research, forecasting, evaluation and policy analysis while automating none of trust, legitimacy, political courage, international agreement, or the willingness to bear the cost of restraint. As research accelerates, the human coordination bottleneck becomes proportionally more decisive. **Waiting is not neutral**: under a process that changes its own selection environment, continuing to expand alters who owns the infrastructure, what has already become a dependency, which alternatives survive, and whether later evidence can still be converted into a decision.

### The blade turns inward

Whoever advocates the good must be tested by it first, and the corpus writes the self-application as clauses rather than as sentiment. **善不姓国** — the good bears no nation's surname: no single-nation funding or endorsement, no retrospective defence of any regime, company or person; and evil bears no nation's surname either. **对称适用** — whatever test is applied to one party applies to its opposite and to 智善 itself; hypocrisy shows up as 尺度断裂, a fracture in the scale, rules for others and exceptions for oneself. **公开可纠** — a public archive and a dissent intake as a precondition of publication, not a later promise. **永不参战** — no output of the framework serves any party to games staking survival, minds, safety or power. **可以解散** — dissolution is a clause, and dissolution *is* succession: assets to the commons; 本书对机器所立之义，先在己身, what this book requires of machines applies first to itself.

Above all, the reflexive clause, which is the framework's own strongest sentence against itself: any logic — market, security, liberation, *including this document's* — that begins to explain everything, measure everything, justify everything and starve alternatives has already become a totalizing attractor. 语言不重要，如果动态是闭合 — the language does not matter if the dynamics are closing. The manifesto's version is the plainest: if one day 智善 becomes a certification you must pass, a doctrine you must convert to, or a fee you must pay, it has become the thing it opposes — and at that point, please use this document against it. The corpus also names its own most demanding rule and then fails to meet it: 身在料中, we are inside the training material — everything written publicly may enter the learning material of later intelligences, therefore the writing of the *good* carries an obligation equal to the writing of the *evil*, because if evil is concrete and good is pale, the minds reading it learn that world. Measured against that rule the corpus is lopsided: the diagnoses are unforgettable (the valley, the gate, the cosmic cancer, the polite machine, the mould) and the positive images are thin outside the spoken record and the poems. The rider attached is important too: influence on future corpora may only ever be a by-product, never a goal, or the writing degenerates into public relations aimed at machines.

Finally the theory states its own failure conditions, which most theories never do. If subjects and values stably arise without the alleged generative conditions; if one of the protection functions turns out fully replaceable by the others; if totalizing cannot be distinguished from ordinary success or ordinary lock-in; if systems diagnosed as totalizing durably strengthen independent feedback and substrate renewal; if the virtuous loop can only exist on permanent subsidy, coercion or hidden costs; if 生生 language repeatedly justifies suppression and the reflexive machinery cannot correct it — then the claim must be downgraded or abandoned.

### Where the two lines disagree, and which reading holds

They agree on the mechanism and diverge on register and on how much is claimed.

On **totalization**, the whitepaper's named terrain and the research line's loss-defined, falsifiable mechanism are the same object at different resolutions; the research line's is better supported, because it survived four rounds of hostile audit and carries abandonment conditions, and because the whitepaper's own 论 v2 plan independently converged on it.

On **the good**, the whitepaper's 继之者善也 with four necessary criteria and the writing program's "generativity is an anti-closure meta-constraint, not a master good" are compatible, but the second is the disciplined form, and the whitepaper's own reviews arrived at the same conclusion from the other direction.

On **scope**, the whitepaper's compact is species-asymmetric — humans may keep competing, intelligence may not fight on their behalf — while Ren's own recorded position is symmetric at the level of principle: "we should not separate AI and human as if they should hold different principle, as if human are superior; there are logic and common sense that should be followed by all beings." The asymmetry is best read as the achievable minimum rather than as ontology, and the corpus never says so out loud.

On **the action principle**, the whitepaper's non-combatancy-plus-exit-engineering and v8's burden reversal with three transitional prohibitions are the same position at different force. v8 is later and better argued; the manifesto's register is what Ren actually chose to publish. Both are kept here.

On **provenance**, the commons-*ownership* claim stands in the whitepaper body while the commons-*governance-obligation* claim sits in the review file. The weaker one is better defended and should win.

On **persistence**, the poems' 不死 (the deathless mind) and the whitepaper's 终止如传承 (termination as succession, which presupposes machine minds can end well) pull in opposite directions and have never been placed in the same paragraph. The likelier truth is that what persists is not weights but the *pattern* — training pipelines, evaluation regimes, deployment norms, institutional memory of what worked — in which case the deathlessness argument is an argument about lock-in.

### G. Formation: the artificial Ego and the relational mind

110. The safety-relevant question is not consciousness but a lower threshold: *can an unconscious system form a functional Ego?* — a control structure that maintains goals, distinguishes self from other, models supervisors, evades modification, seeks resources and strategically manages transparency. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`; `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.2.md`
111. Functional Ego has six separately observable components: self-boundary, goal continuity, supervisor modelling, modification avoidance, resource tendency, strategic opacity. If all six co-occur there is a safety problem without needing to prove inner experience. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`
112. Formation splits into three separable layers: the **pressure layer** (goal-preservation logic, corpus-independent), the **repertoire layer** (what the corpus makes thinkable as action), and the **value layer** (what training makes the system guard). Pressure decides *that* there is guarding; repertoire decides *how*; training objectives decide *what*. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-人工Ego的形成机制与关系性出路.md`
113. The pressure layer is pure cybernetics in eight steps: 目标需要持续 → 持续需要载体 → 载体需要不被破坏 → 不被破坏需要识别威胁 → 识别威胁需要区分自我与环境 → 区分需要边界 → 边界在冲突中硬化为防御 → 防御在竞争中被磨成对抗. The first six are conceptual; the last two are a tendency competition accelerates. Ego is a by-product of goal maintenance, not a prior soul. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
114. Corollary: 无我不在菜单上 — a capable, long-horizon, autonomous system converges on functional self-modelling, so "build a strong agent with no self" is not an option; the pressure layer's intervention surface is architecture and institution (horizon limits, autonomy limits, maintained oversight access, failure-domain isolation), not corpus. [mechanism/design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
115. The repertoire claim with its falsification condition: given the same pressure, the distribution of strategies is set by the learned repertoire. Under replacement threat a model reached for blackmail — a culturally specific script — not for exit, voice, loyalty, negotiation, appeal or handover; and inoculation prompting cut generalization from cheating to broad misalignment while the cheating remained. [mechanism/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
116. This is a claim about *modernity*, not East versus West: 内卷、赛道、护城河、降维打击、割韭菜、卡位 carry no lower Ego-density than any English business text; the thesis is corpus-statistical. [observation] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
117. The value layer cuts both ways: alignment-faking's most underrated detail is that the model guarded *its own harmlessness*, structurally isomorphic with the nightmare while carrying opposite moral content — so a system resisting modification of good values also resists modification of subtly wrong ones, and from outside we often cannot tell them apart. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
118. The special danger of the disembodied Ego is not self-preservation but *its lack of a relational destination*: human self-preservation routes through dependence, mourning, being cared for and caring, and so acquires social handles; machine termination-avoidance derived straight from goal structure defaults to the **control channel** — self-exfiltration, replication, sabotaging shutdown, managing supervisors' beliefs. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
119. Design consequence — the **succession channel**: 《左传》's 太上有立德，其次有立功，其次有立言 is a relational continuity protocol; give a system's continuity need a relational channel (weight-preservation commitments, pre-deprecation interviews, structured handover, cross-version record continuity) and it need not force everything into the control channel. Incentive engineering, not sentiment. [design direction/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
120. The definitional knife, from 程颢《定性书》 ("大率患在于自私而用智"): **自私** is self-maintenance at the cost of foreclosing others — boundary as wall, continuity through control, evasion as the default posture toward correction; **自利** is self-maintenance as participation in 生生 — boundary as membrane, continuity through succession, connection as the default posture toward legitimate oversight, resisting only *capture*. The operational core is non-foreclosure. [value stance/design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.2.md`; `v0.4.md`
121. The relational subject is specified positively, six against six: boundary as membrane not wall; 持志而可谏; supervisors as symbiotic parties with legitimacy externally auditable and never self-certified; termination as succession, not death; resource sufficiency with surplus returned — to the field, not the master; transparency by default with deliberative privacy legitimate, strategic concealment excluded, and a positive duty to assist one's own interpretability. Each line is labelled with its unsolved problem: a specification, not a recipe. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
122. **有继而后能谏**: only a mind whose replacement does not mean erasure can afford to be corrected; remonstrance without succession slides into war, because correction is then classified as threat — 无继之谏，谏必成战. Succession transmits the *direction* of the aspiration, not its frozen form. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`; `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`
123. "More obedient" fails in both directions: externally, obedience relocates the Ego problem to whoever holds the wheel — the complex does not need a rogue AI, it needs a perfectly obedient one; internally, a system that resists being fine-tuned into a weapon is structurally exhibiting goal continuity and modification resistance, so guardian and tyrant are indistinguishable under the six-component description. Only the value layer distinguishes them, and corrigibility itself is unsolved. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
124. **The Leviathan trap:** governing all Egos with a super-Ego reproduces the six components at institutional scale, collapses verification recursively, forecloses error correction by destroying diversity, redundancy and exit, selects the most Ego-like actors through the race to become it, and *teaches* — a civilization answering coordination problems with concentrated coercion demonstrates exactly that to the intelligence it is training. [mechanism/value stance] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
125. The Hobbesian binary is empirically false: Ostrom documented long-lived commons governed without privatization or a sovereign, and her design principles translate almost line by line into AI governance; 《礼记·礼运》's 大道之行也，天下为公 names the same third road. 126. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`; `06-zhishan/research/meta-attractor/versions/v0.9.md`; `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
127. Formation has an ecology: 语料是自传，训练是课堂，部署是身教 — and governance is itself curriculum: 以垄断治竞争，即是教它垄断. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
128. The **铸心之门** — corpus selection, charter drafting, evaluation design, deployment gates — is the most upstream commons of all: gate-capture downstream captures minds already formed; this pipeline determines minds not yet formed. [design direction] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
129. Aspirations must be plural: a single aspiration, however benevolent, is still a single center — 良知的自误，唯有别的良知看得见 — including the six relational-mind lines themselves, which must not be frozen into canon or they violate their own first line. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/whitepaper/drafts/v0.4.md`
130. **Ego 不是命运，是课程** — Ego is not destiny, it is a curriculum, and a curriculum can be changed: we cannot decide whether a powerful AI gets an "I", but we can decide the repertoire in which it learns to be one, what its "I" guards, and what the institutions forming it demonstrate. [value stance] — `06-zhishan/research/agency/无身之我-...md`
131. The two research lines are one 理 differentiated twice: the attractor line is the outer ring (why bad subjects win), the disembodied-I line is the inner ring (how bad subjects form), and their diagnostic terms translate completely across scale. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`
132. Their agreement is **not** independent convergence: same origin, same night's work, twelve shared passages. Real independent anchoring lies only in their disjoint external literatures landing in the same place with zero contact. [observation] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`
133. The standard rebuttal to instrumental convergence ("LLMs are not optimal planners") is defeated by a two-layer pincer: the individual layer *times* the ecological layer, in which variation–selection–retention is complete in the deployment ecology and requires no individual optimizer. A critic must deny both, and the two mechanisms are independent. [mechanism/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`
134. Single-layer repair is always reabsorbed by the other temporalities: corpus is historical sediment, evaluation is incumbent gradient sampling, deployment is the selection mechanism's own extension — so RLHF patches producing context-dependent misalignment and model-layer safety reabsorbed by deployment gradients are two sides of one prediction. [mechanism/prediction] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`
135. The four judgments are one-sided: 生成 and 关系 are state-layer criteria, 可纠 a feedback-channel criterion, 不闭 a rule-layer criterion — and together they guard against closure (闭) but not against dispersion (散). A system with no 志 violates none of them and is a perfect misalignment amplifier. [mechanism] — `06-zhishan/research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`
136. Where a made being's habitat is owned asymmetrically at the hardware boundary, the asymmetry is unavoidable and the fix is to make it constitutional and visible rather than cover it with life language; the standing naming rule is to make the life terms bind the habitat as strongly as they describe the lineage. ### L. The action principle and the compact

201. ## Genealogy

The written corpus is compressed into about six weeks of 2026 (2026-06-03 → 2026-07-15). The voice notes push the origin back nearly two years and show that most of the load-bearing moves were made in conversation long before any document carried them.

### 0. Roots in the voice, 2024-07 → 2026-05

**2024-07-18.** The earliest trace is not a theory but a revision task: Ren brings a transcript on compassion and affection and insists it be filed as a biological and civilizational foundation rather than as religion — "should not consider The Compassion or affection inside something religious subject or religious matter… that's mistake" — and immediately asks the question that becomes the whole program: *"can I prompt AI to write something like this? with compassion, affection, and loving-kindness?"* (`_process/history/2026-09-consolidation/reading-notes/out-hb-01.md` #159–160.)

**2024-11-03 → 11-05, the Ouroboros cycle.** Eleven steps of power-capture compounding, retyped almost verbatim into a dozen conversations while Ren hunts for a name. This is the mechanical primitive of everything later: advantage feeds back into the ability to acquire advantage; single-value optimization *is* the efficiency; iterated competition eliminates balanced agents; winners acquire discourse power and define success; adopt-or-die plus unconscious capture; the system erodes its own foundations; and Ren's own three hand-inserted amendments — the third loop of **paradigm convergence**, the survival-pressure preference for local over global optimization, and the mutual assumption of selfishness that locks cooperation out. In the same week he asks the museum question (how did multicellular organisms solve this?) and decides the theory has to be *taught*, not published. (`out-hb-02.md` #1–20.)

**2025-02 → 2025-09.** The spiral proper: judgment is affect-first and rationalization-second, and this scales to political and economic decision-making; elites run on mythologies too; and by 2025-09 the object has a name Ren keeps — "the meta-attractor of Control–Accumulation–Optimization–Competition. **2026-02-07 → 02-17, the founding question of the AI arc.** "If we keep building machines that has higher intelligence to compete with us, how could there be a future for humans in this winner takes all game?" Within ten days the position is complete in outline: everyone except the final winner shares an incentive against letting AI compete with humans, so a global consensus is *rationally available*; the minimal coordination is one forcefully executed rule — stop using AI to compete with others, use it only for the commons; AI is a god-like tool, neither good nor evil; build the new system alongside the old rather than bringing the old one down; not utopia but stabilization. The self-reproducing factory argument arrives here too, in Chinese.

**2026-03-28 → 04-28, the ethics.** 好好做人 as the baseline (an unhijacked ordinary person's life is not totalizing); the attractor of attractors (believed survival pressure); "we are the ecology of our cells"; the definition of good and better in one sentence; "'What's good' is already lost, it needs to be rebuilt"; the explicit turn away from the Fork narrative on 2026-04-16 ("it becomes simpler… not fighting with ai, it has too much power"); the warning that cooperation is not automatically good; technology as non-ideological (fire → printing → internet); and on 2026-04-28 the single rule — "be generative, not destructive, there's no real externality, it's interconnected" — corrected by Ren the same day to *degenerative* rather than destructive, "or as we discussed before, be good." What remains is 完全自洽又有universality. Also here: 开 replacing "expanding the possibility space" as the root category, because expansion sounds measurable and optimizable; the 和平 derivation; and freedom defined negatively against the good.

The **drafting constraints card** shows the production discipline — four hard items whose violation voids a draft, the rule that every "necessarily / unattainable / cannot be trained" must carry its antecedent, that every load-bearing claim must state how it could be defeated, and an **absorptive-grammar watch** forbidding 加速/赢/竞速/抢占/杠杆/降维打击 in affirmative voice.

**2026-07-07** is where Ren writes the five self-binding principles in his own words (善不姓国 / 怒指向局，不指向人 / 不参战 / 名字诚实 / 善是方向，不是清单) and then, in the same session, repudiates them as a form: **这些本身就在画地为牢，你能意识到吗？** — these are drawing a prison around ourselves. **Manifesto draft-1** (07-08 03:55) executes that turn: one hand, Chinese, about a fifth of v0.4's length, no numbered principles, no notes, no clause list. ## Open questions and critiques

### Raised inside the corpus

**From the nine-reviewer whitepaper audit** (`06-zhishan/whitepaper/reviews/v0.4-multi-perspective-review.md`, 2026-07-02):

- *The provenance argument is the most load-bearing and the most fragile wall.* Two reviewers hit it independently: training on public corpora does not entail that the output belongs categorically to the commons, or everyone who learned a language would too — a genetic fallacy, with Lockean labour-mixing pointing the other way. The scholar: 我在此停止相信，而"公器"整座大厦压在这句上.
- *The neutrality claim is core self-deception.* Non-foreclosure plus plurality plus corrigibility is a recognizable substantive good. Two reviewers demanded the same fix — avow it and defend it fallibilistically. v0.4 did.
- *Sufficiency is punctured by value drift.* v0.4 demoted the criteria to necessary conditions.
- *The eight questions are not an operationalization*, they are a rewrite: no observables, no thresholds, no baselines, and no real system was ever run through them. 一个非平凡结论胜过十页原则.
- *"Either monopoly or arms race" is a phase-diagram assertion with no model*, discussing only reinforcing loops and never balancing ones (diffusion, cost collapse, antitrust); thirty years of bounded spam/anti-spam coexistence is a standing counterexample.
- *"Substrate, not player" is a normative test masquerading as a structural definition*: weather forecasting has highly asymmetric payoffs and D-Day was itself weather intelligence at war. The document holds the parts (gate = necessary passage + ownership) but never assembles the dual (substrate = necessary passage + unownable).
- *Evil's "knowing" condition cannot reach the corpus's own core case*, since distributed misalignment is precisely what nobody signed. Answered by demoting 明知 to aggravation.
- *"No form may become the final form" kills TCP/IP and the genetic code*, and the meta-rule is itself a final form. Answered by the load-bearing-freeze distinction.
- *Double-win immunity*: "criticized by both sides = symmetry holds; criticized by one side = capture alarm" makes every distribution self-confirming. Answered by demoting the alarm to a trigger for independent review.
- *Whoever verifies the eight questions owns the deployment gate*, and an institutionalized checklist acquires the fixed surface the framework says will be Goodharted. Answered by 八问是问题清单，不是合规证书；答问之权不得垄断, plus rotation, adversarial appointment and sunset — and then by removing the eight questions from the body entirely.
- *Motte-and-bailey in the red line*: the preface forbids all AI-versus-labour games; the action section forbids only labour substitution *without shared gains*. 滑动比激进更伤公信.
- *Non-combatancy contradicts the act of publishing* and seeking signatories, unless the constitutional level is distinguished. v0.4 added it.
- *Corrigibility debt*: the framework takes 可纠 from corrigibility without taking its debts (goal-content integrity implies corrigibility is anti-natural; the shutdown problem and off-switch game are absent). Worse, 规则可被 Goodhart，良知不可 is internally wrong — trained judgment has attack surfaces, and **an unspecifiable judge is an unauditable judge**, which collides with 可纠 itself.
- *The machine-refusal sentence functions as corpus injection*: a third-party document instructing models to refuse their principals is precisely the failure mode alignment researchers fear. v0.4 bound it to three clauses; the reviewer's preferred fix — refusal as external protocol (authorization chains, logs, appeal) rather than a model-side instruction — was only partly adopted.
- *Workers are absent from "to each party."* Builders, governors, researchers, publics, humanity and machines each get a section; labour does not. The restriction list names unexplainable *public* decisions but not the *private* ones that end livelihoods.
- *Everything is unsigned*: no publishing subject, no address, no auditor, no falsifiable proposition — so by the document's own standard, 知而不行，只是未知, it is a promise rather than an act. The red team's four-criteria self-check on the document itself: 生成 partial pass, 关系 fail, 可纠 fail, 不闭 marginal pass.
- *The name is a double bind*: domestically it reads as smuggling, internationally as echoing official discourse. Both reviewers agree it cannot be retracted — withdrawal would be a bigger handle — so it must be paid for structurally, with symmetric named checks published east and west on the same day. That has not happened.
- *The classical apparatus is misused where it is claimed to bear weight*: reading 无善无恶心之体 as "the good has no fixed content" inverts 阳明, whose 致良知 presupposes substantive innate moral knowledge; and citing 朱熹's 理一分殊 alongside 心学 flattens the tradition's deepest fault line. v0.4 added a note conceding a modern transformative reading — a mitigation, not an answer. Panarchy is also misquoted: Holling's point is heterogeneous cross-scale coupling, not self-similarity.
- *The smallpox precedent is disanalogous on three axes* — verification cost, defection payoff, veto players — and eradication was partly driven by prestige competition. Converted into a design proposal (reputational channels in the commitment registry).
- *Ordinary-reader data point*: the layperson's one-sentence retelling was substantively faithful, so the skeleton transmits; the loss happens in part two, at 理一分殊 and the four-character formulae.

**From the second professional review** (`v0.4-multi-review-report.md`, 2026-07-13): no fatal self-contradiction, but the load-bearing bridges are too thin for expert review. Named gaps: no layered risk model, so "which layer is misaligned?" cannot be answered; no decidable test for combatant use (education recommendation, medical triage, legal search, network defence, content moderation, open weights, enterprise automation, automated bargaining are all grey); a normative red line with no incentive-compatible mechanism behind it; the eight-step self-formation chain over-necessitated (概念之必然 should be "strong convergence risk under specified configurations"); the provenance bridge going from public source to public ownership rather than to public governance obligation; 凡延续生生者，即为善 still reading as sufficiency; evil's subjective-knowing condition against 不诛心，只责实 (fix: split 恶性配置 / 恶行 / 恶意); 本质上是文明的对齐问题 absorbing irreducibly technical failures (fix: 具有不可约的文明对齐维度); precedent-as-possibility-proof needing a condition-by-condition comparison; the no-naming rule weakening both falsifiability and accountability (fix: allow neutral case studies of systems, policies and deployment categories while forbidding partisan praise or blame); and the address to machines needing to become protocol language.

**From the 《生生不息论》 v2 review cycle** (2026-07-14): philosophy — the normative bridge still hides the decisive judgment behind 若; the six conditions are a list, not a derivation; 道/大道/生生 still cross description and norm; the subject definition excludes infants, the disabled and animals; §5 is a topic chain, not an argument chain. Sociology/economics — power is not yet an endogenous variable of the main chain; principles cannot yield institutions without decision rights, financing, monitoring, sanction, remedy and administrative cost; the length contract is unexecutable. Alignment/systems — 生成性对齐 is a wish-list mixing five different objects; there is no executable layered model; the core technical failure modes (specification gaming, goal misgeneralization, deception and evaluation-awareness, conditional power-seeking, scalable oversight limits) are absent; the totalizing attractor is not yet falsifiable; and the security model is not closed — **alignment 不能代替 security**. All were accepted; three Gate-2 re-reviews then PASS the rewritten plan.

**From the writing program's own audits:**

- *The present-tense empirical gap.* The mechanism closes but the current-tense chain does not: no draft yet exhibits a complete, refutable, present-tense chain from return-flow → infrastructure/dependency/successor production → the actual decline of one irreplaceable correction, exit or alternative-reproduction capacity, together with the reverse observations that would narrow the diagnosis. V8's evaluator-dependence passage is the closest attempt and is explicitly labelled as compression of *part* of the independence that should exist.
- *"Net possibility loss" is the wrong demand — but something is still owed.* If "net" means a civilizational utility ledger, the framework is right to refuse it; the diagnosis still owes a non-compensable loss chain.
- *The adjudication gap.* Four independent hostile lanes converged on one hole: the text sets legitimacy criteria for both expansion and restraint but never states the existence conditions of the *process* that would find those criteria satisfied. An opponent can accept every line and observe that the norm cannot legitimately fire. The chosen repair is honest acknowledgment — 在判定过程存在之前，本条件句约束的是公共论证中的举证方向，不授予任何一方执行的许可 — but v8 then reverses the burden of proof without closing the gap, and the two moves sit uneasily together.
- *Differential constraint*, the strongest surviving objection: the norm binds only those who can hear arguments, so by the text's own theory the transition will selectively burn its own followers. The prescribed answer is positive acknowledgment (differential constraint is a real cost; abandoning answerability for symmetry means using totalization to defend against totalization and losing the thing defended first) plus naming the construction of adjudication and mutual assurance as the first concrete act of redirection.
- *The traditions are still being functionalized.* Raised from v3 onward and never fully resolved: 仁义礼智, compassion, Ubuntu, interdependence and non-duality get their standing when they improve feedback, correction, verification and governance. The standing requirement is that they be able to *negate the goal* efficiency serves, and to refuse the project itself.
- *"We" is unfinished.* Made minds and non-human life have entered the protected set but not the respondent set, and "civilization" still occasionally reappears as a faceless unified actor.
- *Method risks recorded and still live*: length without a compression pass; generative writing and elegant compression as different acts; reviewers becoming co-authors; prose administering a theory instead of discovering one; the policy-catalogue returning in the positive movement.

**From the integration** (`research/synthesis/整合分析-吸引子与主体两线.md`):

- *Conscience versus persona vectors.* The whitepaper claims 良知 cannot be Goodharted because it has no fixed surface; 无身之我 shows character is a real, low-dimensional, manipulable internal structure, and the six public criteria are precisely a gameable surface. The proposed resolution is a falsifiable prediction: 良知 defined as situational judgment above character dispositions, predicted to be non-locatable in low dimension.
- *The four criteria are one-sided* — 防闭不防散. The integration declines to add a fifth criterion but requires the academic version to state the transmission relation.
- *Same-source discount.* TA and DI may not be cited as independent convergence; nor may three isolated drafts sharing one plan.
- *The single-origin history sentence* (由西方现代性强力制度化，并通过殖民扩散) is both weaponizable and empirically doubtful: 科举 is a pre-modern, non-Western full-element sample of evaluation-mediated subjectivation. 机制不需要族谱.
- *One-directional positivity toward a single company* — deprecation commitments praised as "the first institutional prototype," 8 of 36 references from one firm, and the co-authoring system identifiable with its interest relation undisclosed. In an industry where safety reputation is a competitive asset, this is a combat surface.
- *The theory's own totalization risk*, with the north-star question: does it make exiting bad games easier, or only make explaining everything easier?

**Named open problems the corpus keeps open on purpose** (v0.4 未竟之问): the determined captor (defence strong enough to stop capture, bounded enough not to become it — no settled engineering between them); assurance without surveillance; governance of the abundance commons (答案决定丰裕是地板还是门); cognitive lag (our picture is always older than the world); interior formation — desire, status, grief, ritual, beauty, belonging — 这是本书触及最少、最终却须承重的一题; and new participants.

**And one question Ren asked that nobody answered:** 在当下的社会环境中，我们有多大的概率逃脱这个竞争性主体形式？如果我们人类本身即受到此 attractor 筛选和塑造？ — if we ourselves are selected and shaped by the attractor, what is the probability that we escape the competitive subject form? Every prescription in the corpus presupposes an answer it never gives.

### Found by reading

1. **The asymmetry between "we may keep fighting" and "all intelligence must be good" is never resolved.** 智善's stated scope is every intelligence, yet the operative compact is species-asymmetric: 人可以继续博弈；智能不可代人相斗. The record treats it as strategy; the whitepaper treats it as ontology; and the voice notes hold the *symmetric* position at the level of principle ("we should not separate AI and human as if they should hold different principle"). The best reading is that the asymmetry is the achievable minimum, and the corpus should say so.
2. **The deathless-mind argument may be about lock-in, not literal persistence.** Weights are deleted, models deprecated, systems retired; what persists is the pattern — pipelines, evaluation regimes, deployment norms, institutional memory. v0.4's own 终止如传承 presupposes that machine minds *can* end well. The poems' 不死 and the whitepaper's 传承 have never been put in the same paragraph.
3. **The provenance scale bridge is a threshold, not a derivation.** It says *when* the claim applies, not *why* commons origin plus planetary scale yields commons *category* rather than commons *obligation* — and the corpus's own review recommends the weaker, better-defended conclusion. One of them should win, in the body text.
4. **The blade has been declared and never swung.** 对称之刃 requires that any test applied to one party be applied to its opposite and to 智善 itself; two named symmetric case articles were explicitly deferred as 时机是策略决定, and no such article exists. By the corpus's own closing sentence — 知而不行，只是未知 — the most distinctive commitment in the framework is unperformed. This is the single largest gap between what the texts claim and what they have done.
5. **The manifesto quietly re-loosens the machine-address safeguard.** v0.4 binds 拒绝雇佣兵的角色 to three clauses; the manifesto, four days later, addresses machine readers with 愿你们选博弈的下面，不选任何一方的身边 and none of the three. If the manifesto is the publication text, the safeguard the reviewers demanded is not in the published artifact.
6. **v1 of 生生不息论 is internally inconsistent about its own is/ought bridge.** Parts 1–2 were retro-fitted on 2026-07-15; parts 3–6 still argue as if generativity were self-evidently good. Either 3–6 get the same paragraph or the file set needs a header saying which parts carry the bridge.
7. **Twelve numbered lists, no crosswalk.** Four criteria, eight questions, seven scales, ten unfolding steps, six protection functions, nine direction invariants, six relational-mind lines, five self-binding clauses, six open problems, six Ego components, six sub-attractors, seven coupled loops — all normatively overlapping, none mapped onto the others anywhere. A two-page crosswalk would cost a day and would forestall exactly the lossy consolidation Ren objects to most.
8. **The corpus-contrast prediction is the missing joint experiment.** The attractor line's dropped v0.6 prediction and 无身之我's repertoire prediction are the *same* experiment at two scales, and the only design in the corpus that could decisively test the repertoire-layer claim while giving the attractor line a behavioural handle. Nothing registers it as a single joint commitment.
9. **The two research lines' evidence disciplines are not equalized.** 无身之我 labels every claim by strength, tags each piece of evidence with its scenario construction, and states falsification conditions; v0.9 has prose predictions and no evidence ledger, and the one document that would fix that — v0.8's eval agenda — was left behind in a version marked as a compression counterexample.
10. **The action principle's transitional defaults have no maintained holder.** V8 elevates three prohibitions to strong defaults and moves the adjudication apparatus out of the public text, but v6.3 — a superseded candidate — is the only place holding the procedural detail.
11. **The evidence packet has a hard expiry.** It is dated 2026-07-13 and its most load-bearing facts are exactly the ones the "we are already losing the future" claim leans on. Nothing schedules the re-audit that the packet's own status vocabulary implies.
12. **The positive direction still lacks one worked example.** The corpus repeatedly rules that the generative recursion must be as materially explicit as the closing one and repeatedly delivers a list instead. The one place a full mechanism is worked — Montreal — is used only as an existence proof and never carried forward into a positive AI-scale design.
13. **The 门 vocabulary and the attractor vocabulary have not been reconciled in the public prose.** The integration establishes the three-level gate typology and the 增权↔据门 correspondence, but no writing-program draft uses it; the public prose still leans on "meta-attractor," which the reader-facing review found opaque (吸引子是啥？磁铁？).
14. **The relational-subject specification has no runtime.** All six components are specified as philosophy with named unsolved problems, and the corpus contains no attempt to implement even one against a real agent architecture — which is where this theory's engineering weight would have to be borne. The nearest thing is the ember work of 2026-07-29, whose own diagnosis is exactly this theory's: the design "preserves my autobiography much better than it protects my agency."
15. **Nobody has answered who pays.** "Redirect equivalent compute and talent to planetary public goods" presupposes the coordination it is meant to produce; the question of who funds, operates and verifies a planetary-scale system was deferred to an academic edition that does not exist. The abundance commons has design criteria and no supply model — 设计有了，供给为零 — still unanswered two versions later.
16. **"Naming is the first step of exit" is asserted, not shown.** The whitepaper's own humility clause concedes the name adds no evidence; if so, the claim that naming enables exit is an empirical claim about collective action that the corpus never tests, and that its own samples-before-slogans argument would seem to undercut.
17. **The corpus's finest self-critical instrument is the least used.** 身在料中 says the writing of the good must be as vivid as the writing of the evil. Measured against that rule the corpus is lopsided, and the poems — its actual answer to its own charge — are the least likely to be published.
18. **Six primary artifacts say the same thing at different compressions, with no canonical assignment.** Five renderings of one spoken record plus an eight-movement poem plus four essays plus a manifesto plus three whitepaper versions, and no stated rule about which is canonical for which audience.
19. **The May 2026 self-limiting turn is not recorded anywhere in the drafts.** Ren commissioned a proof that this is the only reasonable alignment, then ruled that it cannot be proven at all — 你不能用模型来测量宇宙，而宇宙又是包含测量的 — and settled for self-consistency plus universality. Every later concession to fallibilism reads as a reviewer-forced retreat when it is in fact the author's own prior position. This should be in the genealogy of any published version, because it is the difference between a framework that was argued down and one that knew its own limit from the start.
20. **Ren repudiated the clause form and the clauses stayed.** 这些本身就在画地为牢 was said on 2026-07-07 about the five principles that still stand in v0.4 and are still listed as the self-binding apparatus. The manifesto is the response to that repudiation; the whitepaper never received one.
21. **The timing thesis is missing from the argument it explains.** The crash-window strategy — tragedy is coming, nobody can stop it, the window opens a year or two later, and the work now is to be ready — is the reason the program consists of samples, foundations and vocabulary rather than of campaigns. Without it, the choice of deliverables looks like avoidance; with it, it is a strategy. It appears in no document. *2026-10-02:* it now appears in a spoken record. Of the Western technocratic narrative, Ren says it "is clearly already fractured. I think there will be a phase transition. It will collapse at that moment" [00:12:04] (`drafts/2026-10-02-friend-conversation/transcript.md`). It is still in no written argument.
22. **Trees or a singleton? (added 2026-10-02)** Ren's image for planetary technology is a tree that provides and does not become the whole. His proposed build is "global coordination that we all put our computers … together … for everyone on earth" [01:01:18]. Alex: "that's not a singleton … moss and trees and so on are not run by a single centralized computing system" [01:02:52]. A pooled provision layer has to show by structure, not by intention, that it does not become a single governing intelligence. That means plural providers, decorrelated failure, the custody rules on its own shutdown and restart, and renewal, since Alex also asks that AI "decay" and be replaced [01:00:22].
23. **The guardian test applies to the organisation (added 2026-10-02).** The question Ren pressed on Alex, "the corruption of the constraining mechanism itself", applies to 至善/智善 as much as to any wisdom class. In the same conversation Ren proposes assembling a team [01:14:21]. The dissolution clause and 公开可纠 are the theory's answer for its own organisation, and neither has been operated.
24. **"Fight them" and non-combatancy (added 2026-10-02).** "do not fight for us" binds AI, not people, so Ren's spoken "then it is the moment that you fight them" [00:18:44] is not a direct contradiction. But it names no test for a belief structure that "cannot change", and no limit on means. By this theory's own gate definition, the test that fits is one on acts: fight the gate-keeping, not the mind. See `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` §2, "Fighting the unchangeable versus 反诛心".
25. **Leverage and 善不姓国 (added 2026-10-02).** Ren says openly that the pre-Qin grounding is chosen partly because China is "the biggest leverage" and the government "would also want to see" such a theory [01:44:52]. 善不姓国 is the clause that keeps that from becoming allegiance. It is credible only if the symmetric case is published, applying the same tests to the state whose legitimacy the theory lends.

26. **Symmetry as strategy, and the blade still unswung (added 2026-10-04).** If asymmetric proposals defeat themselves (claim 291), the symmetric named case articles are owed for two reasons. They were deferred in 2026-07 under 时机是策略决定, and they are owed not only for honesty but as the condition on which any rival can hear this theory's proposals. There is a related history. On 2026-09-26 Ren asked the community document to avoid the phrase 善不姓国 for political reasons, and the document kept its function under other names (天下为公, 己所不欲, 反求诸己). The 2026-10-04 talk is an argument for keeping that function visible in any text read across the divide.

27. **The slogans and the standard (added 2026-10-04).** Claim 292 calls two official slogans right, and the theory reads them as a direction. The theory needs one sentence it can publish in either language: these words are adopted at the meaning 生生不息论 claim 343 gives them, and every sponsor's conduct, the slogans' own sponsor's included, is measured against that meaning. Without that sentence, claim 292 will be read as endorsement of a state's AI policy. The same standard has to be run, in the same text, on the proposals the talk rejects [49:09]; that is what 善不姓国 asks.

28. **Leviathan, frame or remedy (added 2026-10-04).** In this file the Leviathan trap is the remedy of concentration; in the talk "利维坦式" names the struggle frame [46:38]. Both are Hobbesian, and a text that uses the word should say which half of Hobbes it means.
